# Cambodian Labour Arbitration Award Briefs

**AI-generated five-point case briefs of 2,059 Cambodian labour arbitration awards — research notes,
not legal advice.** Written by an agent from the OCR'd Khmer and English originals of Arbitration Council
awards. Corrections are welcome and expected.

## Read this before using anything here

- These are **machine-generated research notes**, not law. The award controls, not the brief.
- Every brief is grounded in a numbered working text of the award, and every `[Para N]` was machine-verified
  to resolve against it. Resolution is not the same as correctness.
- OCR damage is widespread in these documents. Each brief discloses the specific defects it worked around
  in §6 instead of silently repairing them.
- These are first-instance arbitration awards, not binding precedent.

## What is in here

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `briefs/<year>/<doc-id>.md` | One brief per award, filed by the year of the award. Six sections: facts, issues, positions, award and reasoning, ratio, source defects. |
| `reports/index/` | Browsable index: `by-year/` and `by-topic/`, one page per group, each linking to the brief. |
| `reports/digest/` | Cross-case digest: `corpus.md`, plus `by-topic/` and `by-year/` pages with the ratios. |
| `METHOD.md` | How the briefs were produced, the citation scheme, and the acceptance gates. |
| `DISCLAIMER.md` | The full disclaimer in one place. |

The underlying award texts are **not** published here. They are public documents of the Arbitration Council
of Cambodia; only structured notes about them are reproduced.

## How each brief is structured

The last section is the one to read first if you plan to quote a figure: it lists the dates, amounts and
names the award contradicts in different places, or that OCR mangled.

## Privacy

Parties, witnesses, union officials and employer representatives are reduced to initials or roles. Only
arbitrators are named, as they are named on the face of the award.
