# Award No. 169/16 — Moning Glory Garment Inter Buy Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 11 August 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Nan, appointed by the workers; Kong Phallha, chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 139071

## 1. Facts
Moning Glory Garment Inter Buy Co., Ltd. (branch 2), a garment factory at Trapeang Krasang, Phnom Penh, employing about 420 workers, faced thirteen collective demands raised through four federations. [Para 1][Para 170] Conciliation on 08 July 2016 failed and the file reached the Council on 13 July 2016; the hearing was held on 21 July 2016 with both sides present. [Para 29][Para 34][Para 37] A union election on 23 June 2016 made Ms T Samoeun deputy president of the Khmer Workers' Consciousness Union; on 28 June 2016 the employer barred her from entering, treating her fixed-term contract as run. [Para 195][Para 276] Ing Sothy dissented on issue 01 and An Nan on issue 04(2). [Para 439][Para 453]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate Ms T Samoeun, a union deputy president dismissed on 27 or 28 June 2016, and pay her wages and benefits from dismissal to reinstatement? [Para 177]
3. Whether the employer must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riels a day, rather than the 1,000 riels paid? [Para 204]
4. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus to 15 USD a month and deduct it only pro rata to days of authorised leave? [Para 209]
5. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance from 7 USD to 12 USD a month? [Para 216]
6. Whether the employer must pay the overtime food allowance every Saturday for overtime worked from 04:00 to 06:00? [Para 221]
7. Whether the employer must allow pregnant workers one day a month to attend a health check with wages and benefits preserved? [Para 227]
9. Whether the employer must install fans in the cutting and warehouse buildings and provide hygienic drinking water? [Para 234]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Ms T Samoeun had never committed a fault; the employer dismissed her one day after the union announced its election results, which is anti-union discrimination, and she should be reinstated with back pay. [Para 182][Para 183][Para 194] On the other demands they pointed to other factories paying 15 USD bonuses, 2,000–3,000 riels of lunch money and daily overtime food money, and to heat of 35–40 degrees in the warehouse. [Para 207][Para 212][Para 238][Para 224]
**Employer (respondent):** the contract was fixed-term, expiring on 28 June 2016, and was simply not renewed; compensation was tendered under the Labour Law and refused, and staff had to be cut after 7,000 pairs of trousers were stolen. [Para 185][Para 187][Para 188] It denied anti-union discrimination, disputed the election, and said it lacked capacity to raise the allowances. [Para 190][Para 200][Para 208]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 01: reinstatement of Ms T Samoeun with back wages and benefits from the date of dismissal until return; issue 03, 04, 05 and 07: claims refused (the 15 USD bonus, the 4,000 riel lunch money, the 12 USD travel allowance and the pregnancy-check leave); issue 06: employer ordered to pay overtime food money weekly on Saturdays; issue 09: employer ordered to install exhaust fans or other means to reduce heat in the cutting and warehouse buildings and to clean the water tanks regularly. [Para 427][Para 428][Para 429][Para 431][Para 432][Para 433][Para 434][Para 435]

On standing, the panel held under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY that a federation president holding written rights-transfer from 82 workers could represent them, and refused to weigh undated rejection letters from 45 workers. [Para 256][Para 261][Para 264]

On issue 01 the panel applied Article 73(1) of the Labour Law: a fixed-term contract ends when its term arrives, and non-renewal is a party's own choice. [Para 269][Para 271] But it found the last contract ran to 28 June 2016, that Ms T Samoeun worked on 27 June, and that on 28 June the employer blocked her thumbprint and entry — so this was a dismissal, not an expiry. [Para 275][Para 276][Para 280] Under Articles 43 and 67 of the Trade Union Law an election candidate enjoys 45 days' protection either side of the poll and needs Labour Inspector authorisation to be dismissed; all three conditions were met — elected deputy president, dismissed five days after the poll, notice given through the security guard Mul Kun — with no authorisation, so reinstatement and back pay were ordered under Article 34 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 281][Para 296][Para 305][Para 309][Para 315][Para 318][Para 320][Para 321]

On issues 03, 04, 05 and 07 the panel found no law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or practice granting 4,000 riels of lunch money, a 15 USD bonus, 12 USD of travel money or a monthly pregnancy-check day, and classified each as a demand above the statutory minimum — an interest dispute. [Para 323][Para 346][Para 370][Para 396] Because the claimant union was not the most representative union under Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law, and an award on such a claim would replace the collective agreement for a year under Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099, the panel declined to consider them. [Para 335][Para 337][Para 340][Para 372][Para 398]

On the second limb of issue 04, the panel read point 2 of Notification No. 230 K.B/S.CH.N of 25 July 2012 as conditioning the bonus on attendance "without absence", held that authorised personal leave is a separate matter within the employer's discretion under Article 2, and refused the claim for pro-rata deduction. [Para 355][Para 358][Para 364][Para 366]

On issue 06 it ordered the overtime food money to be paid weekly on Saturdays, reading Notification No. 041/11 as requiring the money in time for the worker to buy food. [Para 375][Para 380][Para 393] On issue 09 it applied Article 229 and Prakas No. 147 SKBY on heat, finding the heat genuinely excessive, and Prakas No. 054 on hygienic drinking water, finding the tanks had not been cleaned. [Para 401][Para 404][Para 411][Para 425][Para 426]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a fixed-term contract has not yet reached its term, an employer that bars the worker from the workplace and stops her pay is dismissing her, not declining to renew; and if she is a union election candidate within the 45-day protection of Articles 43 and 67 of the Trade Union Law, the dismissal is unlawful unless the Labour Inspector authorised it in advance, and reinstatement with back pay follows. [Para 276][Para 280][Para 305][Para 318][Para 320]

An attendance bonus conditioned on attendance "without absence" is not earned by a worker who takes authorised personal leave; the employer may withhold it in full, and the panel will not rewrite the scheme to a pro-rata deduction. [Para 355][Para 364][Para 366]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's dictionary discussion of "ធុរៈ" (personal necessity) and its remark that Article 67's copy-to-the-Ministry requirement should not burden workers once the employer knows are wider than the orders required. [Para 294][Para 352][Para 353]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name appears as "Moning Glory", "Moning Kuri", "Moning Gli" and "Moning Thury" across the award and annexes. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 171][Para 437]
- The dismissal date is 27 June 2016 in the claim and the employer's account [Para 177][Para 186] but 28 June 2016 in the findings [Para 276][Para 304], which the panel adopted.
- The original complainant group is described inconsistently: 13 workers attended the hearing and 82 transferred rights [Para 173][Para 258], yet [Para 266] states the complainants are 46 workers present plus the 82.
- The bonus figure at [Para 344] is printed "១៣០ ១០ ដុល្លារ" (130 and 10), and the issue 4 heading at [Para 106] states a total of "១៥៦" (156) dollars.
- The decision block at [Para 430] repeats "បដិសេធ" twice and omits panel issues 02, 08, 10, 11 and 13, withdrawn or resolved by agreement at [Para 39][Para 233][Para 247][Para 254].
- The header date is 11 August 2016 [Para 1], after an agreed extension from 03 August [Para 43]; the annex date is garbled as "ថ្ងៃទី ១១ ខែ ឡី ទៅ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦ វិច្ឆិកា" [Para 451].
- Lines [Para 391]–[Para 392] are corrupted, with stray English and numerals replacing reasoning, so the order at [Para 393] was read with the annex at [Para 432].
