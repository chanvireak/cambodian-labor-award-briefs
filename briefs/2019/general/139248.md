# Award No. 093/19 — Ken Sport Shoe Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 November 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (president, chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139248

## 1. Facts
Ken Sport Shoe Co Ltd makes footwear at Chamkar Svay, Sethei commune, Samaki Meanchey district, Kampong Chhnang, and reports 9,955 employees. [Para 34] Its S3 building opened in May 2019 and holds about 1,600 workers, including 32 pregnant women and 8 persons with disabilities. [Para 36][Para 67] S3 has a single entrance which the company keeps shut in the morning; workers walk 500 to 800 metres from the bus park or main gate to reach it, and about 170 staff buses arrive each morning. [Para 37][Para 40][Para 43] The workers' union asked the Ministry of Labour to intervene on two points; one was settled at conciliation on 2 October 2019 and only the gate claim was referred to the panel. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must open the S3 building entrance and exit for workers when they start and finish work? [Para 35][Para 68]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the walk from the bus park takes 10 to 20 minutes, so workers reach the card reader after the shift has begun; those who arrive late are made to stand outside and are reprimanded in front of others by the Chinese supervisor, and a pregnant worker and one other were injured in the crush at the single narrow entrance, which also has no fire escape. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 48][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent):** the gate cannot be opened in the morning because there is no parking in front of S3 and 170 buses stopping there would block the road; a card reader has been placed near the parking area for late arrivals, no worker is warned or punished for slight lateness, and fire escapes and signage exist with evacuation drills twice a year. [Para 52][Para 55][Para 57][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim to open the S3 entrance for all workers is refused, but the employer must open it for pregnant workers and persons with disabilities at the start of the shift, or carry them by vehicle from the common parking area to S3 at that time. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95][Para 96]

The panel began from Article 2(2) of the Labour Law, which gives the employer the right to organise and manage the enterprise where that right is exercised lawfully and reasonably. [Para 68][Para 69] It accepted that routing buses to a common park and having workers walk to S3 is reasonable for the ordinary workforce: the arrangement keeps order on site and avoids the congestion that would otherwise delay everyone. [Para 78][Para 79] It found that a 500 to 800 metre walk does not by itself endanger the health of an ordinary worker, so the general claim failed. [Para 80]

The panel then applied Articles 248 to 250, which place responsibility for workplace accidents on the head of the enterprise and require all necessary precautions. [Para 71][Para 73][Para 74][Para 77] It found the employer had provided no means of easing the walk for the 32 pregnant women and 8 persons with disabilities in S3, and that the arrangement was unreasonable for them, as the injury to a pregnant worker hurrying in showed. [Para 83][Para 84]

Its own inspection of 21 October 2019 found unused space in front of S3 where a vehicle could stop, and it noted the number of affected workers is small, so letting their buses reach the S3 gate would not create congestion. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87] It also found the employer already lets these workers leave 15 minutes early, so the exit side needed no further order. [Para 88][Para 89]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer's right to manage access to its premises under Article 2(2) will be upheld where the arrangement is operationally justified and does not endanger the ordinary workforce, but it must be adjusted for pregnant workers and persons with disabilities, whose safety the head of the enterprise must secure under Articles 248 to 250; where a vehicle can reach the building, the employer must provide that access or transport rather than a long walk [Para 69][Para 80][Para 84][Para 86].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded the employer's undertaking that workers arriving slightly late because of the walk would not be warned or disciplined. [Para 91]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is heavily degraded. The number of pregnant workers in S3 is 20 at [Para 47] but 32 at [Para 67] and [Para 83], and persons with disabilities are 3 at [Para 47] but 8 at [Para 67] and [Para 83]. The panel's own question is garbled to "អគារ-S3-ត្រឹម" at [Para 71]. Headcount of 9,955 at [Para 34] is implausible on its face for a single plant and may be OCR of a smaller figure; the award does not reconcile it with the 1,600 workers in S3. The worker reprimanded for lateness is named as Khum Paly at [Para 46] and as Leat Channa at [Para 66]. Company name forms vary (Ken Sport Shoe, Khet Sport Shoe, Khen Sport Shoe). The operative order is stated twice, at [Para 93]–[Para 94] and again at [Para 95]–[Para 96]. The employer-appointed arbitrator signs as Ing Sothy, matching the header.
