# Award No. 107/19 — Lida Nit Thng (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 17 December 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Sok Matheung presiding; Kol Vathana, An Vireak) · Doc ID 139275

## 1. Facts
Lida Nit Thng (Cambodia) cuts and sews garments at Prek Samrong village, Ta Khmau, Kandal, with 258 workers. [Para 35][Para 36] Wages were contractually due on the 10th of each month and in practice paid on the 11th, 12th or 13th; the October 2019 wages of all 258 went unpaid, although only 92 workers signed the complaint of 21 October 2019. [Para 38][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] The employer promised payment on 15 November 2019 after the Water Festival, then asked for 20 October, then 20 November, then 25 November 2019, and paid nothing; from 19 November 2019 it had no work for them and they heard the factory might close. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] It did not appear at the hearing of 3 December 2019 and filed no documents. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay the October 2019 wages in full to all 258 workers, none of whom had received them? [Para 39][Para 53]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** wages were habitually paid on the 10th and, at the latest, the 13th; no October wages had been paid at all, each postponement having been requested by the employer and granted by the workers, and each worker earned about USD 300 a month; by the hearing date nothing had been received. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 43][Para 46][Para 56]

**Employer (respondent):** absent without explanation and without any documentary response; the panel recorded that it had abandoned its right to reply. [Para 19][Para 57]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to pay the October 2019 wages to every worker not yet paid, within 7 working days from the date the award becomes final; the award is non-binding and becomes final 8 (eight) days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]

The panel first asked whether it could proceed in the employer's absence. [Para 47] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council allows the panel to continue, or to close the file, where a party duly summoned fails to appear without valid reason; the secretariat had issued invitations No. 616 and No. 617 of 27 November 2019, telephoned and telegraphed the representative, and on the hearing day called twice, at 8:00 and 8:30, without answer. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17][Para 47][Para 50] The panel followed its earlier awards on the same article — 53/04, 148/07, 99/09, 173/11, 142/12, 109/13, 163/14, 250/15 and 022/16 — and continued in the employer's absence. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 52]

On the merits, Article 118 of the Labour Law places the burden of proving payment of wages on the employer, by the worker's signature on the payroll or, for an illiterate worker, two witnesses; the employer produced no payroll, no receipt and no witness. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 58] The panel therefore believed the workers' account that the October 2019 wages were unpaid, and relied on Article 34 of Prakas No. 099, which lets it order immediate payment of sums due, to fix a seven-working-day deadline running from the date the award becomes final. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer duly summoned neither appears nor produces payroll evidence, the panel may hear the case in its absence, and because Article 118 puts the proof of wage payment on the employer, the workers' uncontradicted account of non-payment must be accepted; a promise to pay at a later date does not discharge the obligation. [Para 47][Para 52][Para 53][Para 58][Para 59]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that only 92 workers had signed the complaint although 258 were affected, and nonetheless ordered payment to every worker still unpaid. [Para 38][Para 61]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Para 4 has the employer proposing payment on 20 October 2019 after it had promised 15 November 2019, so the earlier date follows the later one; the sequence at paras 43 and 55 (15, 20, 25 November) suggests November was meant, but the award is not corrected here. [Para 4][Para 43][Para 55] Para 11 names the province as "ឥណ្ឌា ស" where every other reference is Kandal. [Para 11][Para 12] Para 60 dates Prakas No. 099 to 21 April 2019, against 21 April 2004 at paras 6 and 47. [Para 6][Para 47][Para 60] Dates and figures are broken by stray characters: "26 November 20 2019" at para 14, "ឆ្នាំ ២០ ២០១៩" at para 53, "ប្រាក់ ឈ្នួល ៤០" at para 55, and the company appears once as "លីដា នីត ធីង ៩" at para 23. [Para 14][Para 23][Para 53][Para 55] No total sum is stated in riel or USD, only the monthly figure of about USD 300 per worker, and the workers' and employer's names survive only as Khmer script, transliterated here without expansion.
