# Award No. 065/19 — ខ្វាន់ ទុម ខ្លូស៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 29 August 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — chair គង់ ផល្លៈ, employer-appointed អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, worker-appointed ស៊ីន គឹម ស៊ាន · Doc ID 139111

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment manufacturer at Chrey Kaong, Chom Chao, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, employing 1,875 workers. The workers raised eight demands on 3 July 2019; conciliation on 19 July 2019 settled five, and the three remaining points went to the Council on 24 July 2019 under report No. 758 K.B/AK/VK of 23 July 2019. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 49][Para 16]

The hearing was held on 1 August 2019 and both parties attended; by agreement the award date was extended from 14 August to 29 August 2019. [Para 19][Para 24]

Both parties acknowledged that the company has no union holding exclusive representative status (ភាពជាតំណាងបំផុត), although a democratic workers' union is registered at the company. [Para 50][Para 41][Para 90]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must calculate pre-2019 seniority indemnity by dividing monthly wage by 22 days, as it does for the 2019 indemnity? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must restore one full day of paid sick leave as it applied before 2019? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must add one more dish to the midday meal, so that workers receive one dry dish and one soup dish? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company's internal rules, at Article 4 (working hours), fix Monday to Friday and therefore about 22 working days a month; using a 26-day divisor for pre-2019 indemnity produces a lower daily average and so deprives workers of benefit, and fairness requires the 22-day divisor used for 2019. Before April 2019 the employer granted one full day's sick leave, and since then only half a day where a worker merely collects medicine without being admitted. The company has grown and should add a second dish, which would also help workers who cannot eat pork. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 59][Para 71][Para 72][Para 80][Para 81]

**Employer (respondent):** it applies Instruction No. 057 of the Ministry of Labour, which expressly divides monthly wage by 26 days for pre-2019 indemnity and does not require an actual-working-day divisor; it grants half a day where the clinic is near the factory and a full day where the worker attends a distant hospital such as Calmette or Khmer-Soviet, and a full day where the illness is serious; adding a dish would create cost it cannot bear, and the present meal already exceeds what the law requires. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 69][Para 75][Para 82][Para 84]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three remaining demands were refused, the third without consideration on the merits; the panel found that the employer's application of Instruction No. 057, its sick-leave practice under the internal rules, and its one-dish midday meal did not depart from any binding standard. [Para 160][Para 161][Para 162]

On issue 1, the panel traced Article 89 (new) of the Labour Law, Article 2 and Article 3 of Prakas No. 443, and Instruction No. 057 of 10 June 2019, whose point II.2 computes the daily average by dividing the monthly average by 26 days. It found the employer had applied that method exactly. The internal rules do fix Monday to Friday and thus about 22 working days, and 22 would be more favourable than the law, but the rules nowhere prescribe a 22-day divisor for the indemnity; the panel therefore refused to read one in and dismissed the demand. [Para 103][Para 112][Para 113][Para 118][Para 119][Para 121][Para 123][Para 124][Para 125][Para 126]

On issue 2, Article 4 point 8 of the internal rules of 14 September 2018 requires a sick worker to attend the company clinic, and provides that where the company doctor certifies rest, the worker receives paid sick leave; absence without adequate documentation earns no sick leave. The panel found the employer's half-day and full-day practice conformed to those rules, and that nothing showed a breach, so the demand failed. It did not find a legal entitlement to a flat one day regardless of medical certification. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 131][Para 132][Para 134][Para 135][Para 136]

On issue 3, the panel first held that no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliged the employer to serve two dishes, so the demand was a claim for something better than the law — an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍), not a rights dispute. Under Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement and binds all workers for one year; the panel therefore held it could only adjudicate such a claim where the union bringing it holds exclusive representative status under Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law. No union here held that status, so the panel declined to consider the demand at all. [Para 137][Para 139][Para 140][Para 152][Para 153][Para 154][Para 157][Para 158][Para 159]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a ministerial instruction prescribes a divisor for computing the daily wage used in seniority indemnity, an employer who applies it exactly commits no breach, and a more generous divisor that appears in the company's internal rules only as working hours — not as a calculation rule — will not be imported into the indemnity. A demand that exceeds every legal or contractual standard is an interest dispute, and the panel will not adjudicate it unless the claimant union holds exclusive representative status in the enterprise. [Para 119][Para 125][Para 154][Para 158]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that a 22-day divisor would be "more favourable than the law", and its observations that an interest award would displace collective bargaining and the right to strike for a year, were not necessary once it had found the internal rules silent and the union unqualified. [Para 124][Para 153]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in at least four different spellings across the award — ខ្លូស៊ីង, ខ្លួស៊ីង, ខ្លួស៊ីង and ខ្ងូ ស៊ីង (Para 1) — and the award has been cited here under the spelling used in the header and case name. The report number appears as "៧៥៨ ក.ប/អក/វក" at Para 11 and as "៧៥៨ ភ.ប/អក/វក" at Para 16. Dates are truncated: Para 11 reads "ថ្ងៃទី ២៣ ខែ ឆ្នាំ កក្កដា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩" with the month missing, and Para 24 reads "ថ្ងៃទី ១៤ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩" with the month lost. Paragraph 60 states the claim as indemnity "before 2016" where every other reference is pre-2019, and Para 127 puts the sick-leave claim at "១០ ថ្ងៃ" where the demand was one day. The header of Para 86 carries a stray "២០", and Para 102 a stray "២០". The signature block (Para 164) omits the employer-appointed arbitrator's name, though អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី is named on the face of the award. Individual worker representatives named at Paras 99 and 100 are not reproduced here.
