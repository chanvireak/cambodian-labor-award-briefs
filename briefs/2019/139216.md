# Award No. 089/19 — Lam Lai Thai (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 2 October 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Yu Soun, appointed by the employer; Sin Kim Sean, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139216

## 1. Facts
Lam Lai Thai (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates at Kbal Damrey 2, Kakab, Por Sen Chey, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Two workers filed ten collective claims on 7 September 2019; conciliation failed on 9 September and the file reached the Council on 11 September. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18] The company obtained an order suspending the strike from 13 September, and workers returned on 14 September. [Para 21][Para 22] At the hearing of 23 September the claimants did not appear, and the panel closed the file without deciding any of the ten claims. [Para 25][Para 63]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must allow three days of annual leave to be taken on the Pchum Ben holiday? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company must pay the balance of the contract when it dismisses a worker before the contract ends? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must stop warning workers who occasionally forget their badge or scarf? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must stop warning workers who go to the toilet more than three times? [Para 6]
5. Whether the company must supply adequate medicine to workers? [Para 7]
6. Whether the company may deduct annual leave when there is no work? [Para 8]
7. Whether the company must build a nursery or, failing that, pay USD 30 per month as milk money? [Para 9]
8. Whether overtime must be kept voluntary? [Para 10]
9. Whether the company may deduct wages from a worker who is sick with a valid medical certificate? [Para 11]
10. Whether wages and other benefits must be maintained during a strike? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The ten claims above were filed through their representative, who told the secretariat by telephone that she was in her home province and that the other workers concerned had resigned, so none attended. [Para 16][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** Appeared through counsel and a general manager, and had asked the Council to suspend the strike; it filed documentary evidence but no submission on the merits of the ten claims. [Para 20][Para 26][Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the claims as abandoned and terminated the arbitration, making no order on any of the ten claims. [Para 59][Para 63][Para 64]
The panel identified a threshold question: whether it should continue in the absence of the claimant workers? [Para 37] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and rule 4.7 of the arbitration procedure allow it to proceed or to close the file by award, on condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39] Prior awards read that provision as requiring three cumulative conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and absence of a valid reason. [Para 40][Para 41]
On condition one, the panel found the claimants had been summoned by invitation No. 509 and also telephoned on 17 September, so notification was proper. [Para 42][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] On condition two, it held that appearing means both attending and taking part to the end, and the claimants did neither. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50] On condition three, the only explanation was that the representative was in her home province, which the panel did not accept as valid. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53] All three conditions being met, the panel held the claim abandoned. [Para 54][Para 59]
It added that a claimant bears the burden of adducing argument and evidence, and an absent claimant leaves nothing to be weighed; no facts on the ten claims could therefore be established. [Para 36][Para 56][Para 58]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant is properly summoned and neither appears nor offers a valid excuse, the panel may treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by award, since the burden of argument and evidence rests on the claimant and the merits cannot be assessed in that party's absence. [Para 39][Para 50][Para 57]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that both claimants and respondents are obliged to appear in person to present their arguments and the documents needed for the panel to decide. [Para 55]
