# Award No. 056/18 — Double U and D (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 4 January 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Yu Son Ti (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 138958

## 1. Facts
Workers of Double U and D (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (also written "W & D (Cambodia) Co., Ltd") filed a collective dispute on 24 December 2018 over three demands concerning seniority indemnity (ប្រាក់បំណាច់អតីតភាពការងារ) and wages during a strike. [Para 13] Ministry conciliation on 26 December 2018 failed, and the non-conciliation report No. 1187 ក.ប/អក/វក of 27 December 2018 reached the Secretariat the same day. [Para 14][Para 15] The panel was constituted on 31 December 2018 and, on the employer's request, issued Order No. 007/18 កបអ. of 31 December 2018 suspending the strike and ordering all strikers back to their shifts from the morning of 2 January 2019. [Para 16][Para 18] Both parties acknowledged that roughly 1,800 workers remained on strike and did not return. [Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay the workers their total seniority indemnity up to 10 January 2019, including wages and subsidiary benefits? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company must calculate seniority indemnity for periods before 2019 on the same basis as for 2019? [Para 5]
3. Whether the company must maintain wages and other benefits unchanged during the strike? [Para 6]

The panel noted that the conciliator had ceased work on issues 2 and 3 because issue 1 was not settled. [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The company must pay full seniority indemnity up to 10 January 2019, computed with wages and fringe benefits included, and must apply the pre-2019 calculation on the same footing as 2019. [Para 3][Para 5] Their representatives said they explained the Council's procedure to the strikers and told them to return to work, but the workers refused and did not follow that advice. [Para 22]

**Employer (respondent):** Seniority indemnity for all of them would be paid on 10 April 2019, in the manner prescribed by Ministry Notification No. 443 កប/ប្រ.ក.ខ.ល of 21 September 2018. [Para 4] The company also asked the Council for an order suspending the strike. [Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** proceedings terminated — the panel ordered the discontinuation (បញ្ចប់) of the arbitration in case No. 056/18; no sum was awarded on any of the three demands. [Para 43][Para 44]

The panel framed the dispositive question as whether arbitration should continue at all, and applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 សកបយ of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lock-out (មាត្រា 318 of the Labour Law) — and any other measure aggravating the dispute for the duration of the panel's work. [Para 34][Para 36] It read that provision, consistently with earlier awards (Nos 58-06-Samrou, 39-08-Chong Youv, 137-09 & 138-09-Phnom Kanh, 110/11-Simanou, 104/13-Sabrina (Cambodia), 3222/14-Chhim Ly (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, 071/15-MW Garment Kampong Cham and 021X/16-Star Light Carry Manufacturing), as meaning the Council will not proceed while workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 37][Para 38]

Because the workers were in fact striking, the employer had requested the suspension order, Order No. 007/18 had been issued, and the parties agreed that some 1,800 workers were still refusing to return, the panel found the precondition for continuing was absent and ended the proceedings under Article 20. [Para 39][Para 42]

No hearing was held, so the panel recorded that it made no findings of fact on the three disputed demands. [Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers are on strike, or the employer has locked out, at the time the panel considers procedure, the Arbitration Council will not continue the arbitration and will terminate the proceedings under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, whatever the merits of the demands. [Para 36][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recites that earlier awards had already interpreted Article 20 in this way and that it adopts their reasoning; and it notes the conciliator's withdrawal from issues 2 and 3. [Para 37][Para 7]
