# Award No. 017/19 — Jav Huy In Das Striel (Arbitration Council, 19 March 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phallha presiding; Seng Vouch Hun nominated by the employer, An Non nominated by the workers) · Doc ID 138860

## 1. Facts
Jav Huy In Das Striel, at Trapeang Chrey, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, makes footwear and employed about 190 workers. [Para 45][Para 46] Twenty workers filed a complaint on 5 January 2019, received on 11 January 2019, making eleven demands; conciliation on 13 February 2019 failed on all eleven, and at the hearing the workers withdrew demands 6 and 7, leaving nine. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 27][Para 28] The employer did not appear, and the panel proceeded in its absence under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 after finding it had been properly summoned. [Para 25][Para 79][Para 83][Para 85] The award is non-binding. [Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide a doctor and sufficient medicines during working hours? [Para 101]
2. Whether the employer must install a fingerprint scanner for attendance? [Para 125]
3. Whether the employer must keep clean, hygienic toilets for the workers? [Para 133]
4. Whether the employer must hold an election of worker delegates under the Labour Law? [Para 142]
5. Whether the employer must let pregnant women leave 15 minutes before the end of the shift? [Para 161]
8. Whether the employer must compute overtime on the base wage plus the seniority bonus and the position or function allowance? [Para 166]
9. Whether the employer must compute annual leave payouts at termination on the annual average wage? [Para 188]
10. Whether the employer must supply hot and cold water during working hours? [Para 194]
11. Whether the employer must allow one hour a day for a woman who has given birth to breastfeed? [Para 203]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the factory has no doctor, infirmary or medicines although workers have been injured; roll-call at the gate twice daily wastes their time; the eight toilets have no soap, broken doors and are not cleaned; after four years there are still no worker delegates; pregnant women get 10 minutes instead of 15; overtime is computed on base wage alone although the seniority bonus and function allowance are wages; and termination payouts use base wage rather than the annual average. [Para 51][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 61][Para 64][Para 66][Para 70] Four women have recently given birth, with no breastfeeding time and no nursing room. [Para 76][Para 78]
**Employer (respondent):** absent. It filed only a letter of 15 March 2019 objecting to the workers' documents, and told the secretariat by telephone that its representative was busy with Pchum Ben and later that a grandmother had died, without any written request to adjourn. [Para 25][Para 32][Para 83]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must keep a nurse on duty and a doctor present at least two hours during working hours with sufficient medicines (Prakas No. 330); keep hygienic toilets with soap (Prakas No. 052); hold the worker-delegate election; let pregnant women over three months leave 15 minutes early; compute overtime on base wage plus the function allowance; and give one hour a day for breastfeeding for one year; the fingerprint-scanner, annual-leave-average and hot-and-cold-water claims are dismissed. [Para 212][Para 214][Para 215][Para 216][Para 219]
On issue 1 Articles 238 and 242 require a permanent infirmary with a nurse for any establishment using at least fifty workers, and Article 3 of Prakas No. 330 sets one nurse and a doctor present two hours for 50 to 300 workers; with 190 workers the duty was engaged and unmet. [Para 104][Para 106][Para 110][Para 111] On issue 2 no law requires a fingerprint scanner, attendance recording being the employer's prerogative under Article 2. [Para 128][Para 130][Para 132] On issue 3 Article 229 and Articles 1 and 6 of Prakas No. 052 require clean toilets with soap. [Para 133][Para 136][Para 141] On issue 4 Articles 3, 32 and 35 of the Labour Union Law and Articles 1 and 6 of Prakas No. 302 require an establishment with eight or more workers to elect a delegate and a substitute for two years, the election being the employer's duty; none had been held. [Para 148][Para 153][Para 160]
On issue 5 Notification No. 294 of 27 September 2017 entitles a woman more than three months pregnant to leave at least 15 minutes early; the employer allowed 10. [Para 161][Para 163][Para 164] On issue 8 Article 139 (new) and Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 do not define the overtime wage; adopting award 74/08 (Generation), the panel held "wage in normal working time" to be the wage regularly received and the function or position allowance part of it. The payslips showed a USD 35 function supplement and the employer used base wage alone, so it was ordered to use base wage plus that allowance. [Para 168][Para 173][Para 179][Para 186][Para 187] On issue 9 the claim was premature, no worker having yet been dismissed; on issue 10 Prakas No. 054 requires only sufficient hygienic drinking water, not hot and cold taps; on issue 11 Articles 184 and 185 gave one hour a day for one year from the birth. [Para 189][Para 195][Para 198][Para 206][Para 211]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claim that an employer has fallen below a specific statutory minimum is granted once the standard and the non-compliance are shown, whatever the employer's absence: the panel matches the article and the prakas table to the headcount. [Para 106][Para 110] Where no provision imposes the thing demanded — a fingerprint scanner, hot and cold taps — the claim is dismissed, attendance and welfare arrangements being within management's prerogative. [Para 130][Para 132][Para 198] "Wage in normal working time" for overtime under Article 139 (new) includes the function or position allowance received regularly each month, so overtime computed on base wage alone is underpaid. [Para 173][Para 179][Para 187] A claim about how a future termination will be calculated is not arbitrable until the termination occurs. [Para 189][Para 192]

**Obiter dicta:** the employer's oral excuses carried no written adjournment request and did not prevent the panel proceeding, and the function and skill allowances do not differ in nature. [Para 83][Para 181]
