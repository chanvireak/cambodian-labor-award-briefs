# Award No. 001/19 — Thun Toch Plastic and Aluminium Processing Enterprise "GSY" (Arbitration Council, 11 February 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bunhea (chair) · Doc ID 138872

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a small plastics and aluminium processing enterprise trading as "GSY" at Ang Snuol, Kandal province, employing 45 workers [Para 46]. On 28 December 2018 the Kandal provincial department of labour received a request to settle a collective dispute raising two points, dismissal and working conditions [Para 16]. The two workers concerned, Mr P and Mr M, were engaged by oral agreement only, without a probation contract or any written employment contract, on a basic wage of USD 170 plus USD 20 attendance bonus, USD 10 travel and accommodation and USD 10 meal allowance [Para 50][Para 53]. Both were dismissed on 27 December 2018 without any prior notice of dismissal [Para 54].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer failed to respect the agreement of 20 November 2018 concluded with the workers and the Kandal provincial department of labour, in its eight listed points? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must take Mr P (employee no. 1036) and Mr M (employee no. 1032) back into work, and pay their wages and other benefits in accordance with the Labour Law? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** neither worker had ever committed any fault, and the employer should take both back into work and pay the wages and benefits owed [Para 49][Para 60].
**Employer (respondent):** both workers failed to cooperate — they led two strikes, on 27 September 2018 and 29 November 2018, disobeyed management instructions and were repeatedly absent without permission; the employer nevertheless agreed to pay severance and the other termination entitlements [Para 61][Para 62].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for reinstatement was dismissed, and the employer was ordered to pay both workers damages in lieu of notice, seniority indemnity, payment for unused annual leave and moral damages; no aggregate sum is stated in the award [Para 162][Para 163][Para 171]. The award is non-binding (សេចក្តីបង្គាប់ មិនជាប់កាតព្វកិច្ច) [Para 172].

**Contract type.** Article 67(7) of the Labour Law requires a fixed-duration contract to be made in writing, failing which it becomes a contract of indefinite duration [Para 81][Para 82]. Following Award 183/14 (San Long), the panel held that because neither worker had a written contract at the outset, both were employed under indefinite-duration contracts [Para 95][Para 96].

**The dismissal.** Under Article 74 an indefinite-duration contract may be terminated at either party's will, but no dismissal may be made without a valid reason related to the worker's capacity or conduct or to the needs of the enterprise, and Article 77 requires notice or payment in lieu [Para 99][Para 119]. The employer gave no notice and produced no evidence at all — not even the monthly payroll records it said it would rely on — to support its allegations of strikes, disobedience and absenteeism [Para 80][Para 106][Para 107]. The panel therefore found the dismissals unsupported by any valid reason and contrary to Article 74 [Para 108].

**Remedy.** Article 34 of Prakas 099 empowers the panel to order immediate payment of sums due [Para 114]. Applying Award 023/17 (Tien Sung Garment), the panel held that where the working relationship is no longer consensual and the employer refuses to take the workers back, reinstatement will not be ordered and compensation awarded instead; the workers had also not alleged union discrimination or protected status [Para 110][Para 111][Para 112].

**Entitlements.** Notice pay was awarded under Articles 75 and 77, calculated on actual seniority [Para 121]. Seniority indemnity was awarded under new Article 89 of the amending law and Prakas 443 — 7.5 days for one to six months' service and 15 days for more than six months, payable twice a year from 2019 but applicable to pre-2019 service [Para 137][Para 142][Para 146]. Unused annual leave was compensated under Articles 166 and 167 [Para 152]. Moral damages were awarded under new Article 91, the panel noting that this head is distinct from notice pay and seniority indemnity and requires no proof of specific loss [Para 154][Para 159].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contract of employment for a fixed term that is not reduced to writing is converted by Article 67(7) of the Labour Law into a contract of indefinite duration, so that ending it requires a valid reason and prior notice or payment in lieu; and where the employer refuses reinstatement and the relationship is no longer consensual, the panel will refuse reinstatement but award notice pay, seniority indemnity, unused annual leave and moral damages calculated on actual seniority [Para 82][Para 108][Para 112][Para 121].

**Obiter dicta:** the party making an assertion or accusation bears the burden of proof, and an employer that announces it will file payroll records but files nothing cannot be believed on the reason for dismissal [Para 103][Para 106].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Mr M's start date is printed as 25 April 2018 in the agreed facts and as 24 April 2018 in the reasoning [Para 50][Para 87]. Mr P's start date was never fixed: the workers said 1 May 2018, the employer said 31 July 2018 on the strength of payroll records it never filed, and the panel expressly found it could not determine the date, so his seniority remains unquantified [Para 57][Para 58][Para 93]. The panel found that neither worker had yet completed a year and so had no annual-leave entitlement, yet went on to order compensation for unused annual leave [Para 151][Para 152][Para 165]. The refusal of reinstatement is once expressed as applying to "all 4 workers" although only two were dismissed [Para 112]. The issue number in the procedural summary is corrupted to "ទី ២៦", a stray "៩៧" appears mid-sentence in the seniority finding, and the name of the workers' arbitrator is lost at the signature block [Para 30][Para 132][Para 173].
