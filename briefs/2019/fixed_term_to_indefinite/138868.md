# Award No. 008/19 — Indochina E-Beryl (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 31 January 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (chair) · Doc ID 138868

## 1. Facts
Indochina E-Beryl (Cambodia) Ltd, a Kandal province enterprise with 3,095 workers, faced a collective dispute over six demands; four were settled in conciliation on 22 January 2019 and two were referred to the panel. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 24] The unresolved demands were the dismissal of the company's human-resources officer, Ms S, and payment of the daily wage and benefits for the days spent on strike. [Para 2][Para 7] The workers struck, and on 25 January 2019 the Council issued order No. 003/19 requiring an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the morning of 26 January 2019. [Para 20] The workers did not return; the employer reported that only about 450 of 3,095 had come back. [Para 21][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must dismiss its human-resources officer, Ms S, from the company? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must maintain the workers' daily wage and other benefits for the days on which they struck? [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Ms S must be removed at once — the workers rejected the company's successive offers of six months, then three months, then seven days to reform her, and they will not end the strike while she remains in the company. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 23]

**Employer (respondent):** the company asked for six months to educate and reform Ms S and warned that if she did not change it would dismiss her; it offered to keep the 2016 wage and other benefits for the strike days if the workers returned from 23 January 2019, failing which it would apply the Labour Law and its internal rules. [Para 3][Para 8][Para 9]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no determination on the merits — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 008/19 and ordered nothing on either unsettled demand. [Para 43][Para 44]

The only question the panel reached was procedural: whether arbitration should continue at all. [Para 34] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council requires the parties, during the panel's work, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout, or any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 34][Para 35] The panel read that provision as barring it from proceeding while a strike or lockout is under way, and followed Awards 59/06, 137/09, 138/09, 110/11, 104/13 and 021/16 to that effect. [Para 37][Para 38]

On the facts, the workers were still striking when the panel sat: the employer confirmed only about 450 of 3,095 had returned, both parties told the pre-hearing meeting of 29 January 2019 that the strike continued, and the workers' representatives stated they would not stop unless Ms S was removed. [Para 39][Para 41][Para 42] Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the two demands. [Para 33] It therefore closed the file rather than rule on the merits. [Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers are on strike, or the employer has locked out, when the panel is seised, Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the panel to decline to conduct the arbitration and to terminate the proceedings, leaving the underlying demands undetermined until the dispute measure is suspended. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's record that it held no hearing and therefore made no findings of fact on the referred issues, and its account of how many workers had returned, were context for the procedural ruling rather than grounds of decision. [Para 33][Para 42]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The date on which the panel was constituted is printed three ways in one sentence — 25 January 2022, then 2019 twice — and the 2019 date is the only coherent reading. [Para 18] The conciliation report number appears both as "/K ២៩២/១៩" and as "២៩២/១៩". [Para 12][Para 17] The company name is spelled several ways (ឥណ្ឌូចិន អ៊ីភែរីល, អី ភែរីល, អ៊ីភែរឹល), and the provincial labour department is once mistranscribed as "មន្ទីរពេទ្យ" (hospital). [Para 1][Para 33] The employer's document list repeats its heading labels "ខ" and "គ", so the enumeration of exhibits does not run cleanly. [Para 27] The human-resources officer is identified only by a personal name, reduced here to Ms S; the eight worker representatives are named as individuals and are not reproduced.
