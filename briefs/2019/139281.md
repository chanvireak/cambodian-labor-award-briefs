# Award No. 110/19 — Kao Way Sport Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 15 January 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vuoch Hun for the employer party, Sin Kim Sean for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 139281

## 1. Facts
Kao Way Sport Ltd. is a shoe manufacturer at Thanh, Bavet, Svay Rieng, employing 2,308 workers. [Para 45][Para 46] Workers lodged eight demands on 28 October 2019; four were conciliated on 3 December 2019 and four came to the Council, which held a hearing on 19 December 2019 and issued a **non-binding** award. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 21] The remaining points were February 2019 wage recalculation, unpaid union dues of USD 8,880, Sunday and holiday pay at 300 per cent, and the timing of maternity payments. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must recalculate February 2019 wages and repay the amount workers lost that month? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay union dues from December 2016 to September 2017 totalling USD 8,880? [Para 3][Para 47]
3. Whether the employer must pay 300 per cent for work on Sundays and public holidays, as it did before? [Para 51][Para 70]
4. Whether the employer must pay 50 per cent of wages and allowances for the 90 days of maternity leave before the leave begins? [Para 64][Para 95]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 300 per cent rate had been paid since the factory opened, and Article 13 of the Labour Law means a benefit the employer has granted must continue; maternity pay is needed before the birth to prepare for delivery and buy necessities. [Para 52][Para 56][Para 65][Para 67]
**Employer (respondent):** the 300 per cent was a voluntary incentive to raise productivity, not an obligation; orders and profits have fallen, it has reverted to the statutory 200 per cent, it gave one day's notice to team leaders and workers volunteered without compulsion; and it pays maternity money monthly like other factories. [Para 54][Para 59][Para 60][Para 63][Para 68][Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for 300 per cent Sunday and holiday pay was dismissed; the employer was ordered to pay 50 per cent of wages and allowances for the 90 days of maternity leave **before** the worker stops work. [Para 113][Para 114] The February wage claim was withdrawn by the workers at the hearing, and the union-dues claim was withdrawn to be discussed with the buyer PLIMA, so neither was considered. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]

**Issue 3 — Sunday and holiday pay.** The panel began from the doctrine of past practice (ការអនុវត្ត កន្លងមក), supported by Article 65(2) and (3) of the Labour Law and defined in Award No. 95/09 Tak Fat as a practice long, regularly and consistently followed, clear and stable, and accepted by both sides. [Para 70][Para 74][Para 76][Para 77] It found that paying 300 per cent from the opening of the factory until shortly before September 2019 met that definition, and that both parties admitted it. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82] It then applied Tak Fat's holding that a past practice may lawfully end where the circumstances that grounded it have changed, where the parties agree a replacement, or where they decide to terminate it. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86] Here orders had fallen and profits were low, so the financial basis of the practice had changed, and the employer had told each team leader before reverting to 200 per cent, which the workers acknowledged; the workers also accepted there was now little Sunday or holiday work. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92] The panel therefore held there was no duty to continue the 300 per cent rate. [Para 93][Para 94]

**Issue 4 — maternity pay timing.** Article 182(1) gives women 90 days' maternity leave and Article 183 gives half wages including allowances, for women with at least one year's service. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 97][Para 99] The panel then asked when payment must be made, since neither article says. [Para 100] Article 115(3) forbids paying wages on a day the worker is entitled to rest and requires payment the day before where payday falls on a rest day, and Article 103 includes sums paid during maternity leave within "wages". [Para 101][Para 102][Para 103][Para 105] The panel held Article 115 applies to maternity payments too, following Award No. 139/08 Supreme Garment and the awards cited there, so paying monthly like ordinary wages did not comply; the employer must pay the 50 per cent for the full 90 days at least one day before the leave starts. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 110][Para 111]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A benefit paid at a rate above the statutory minimum becomes a binding past practice only while the circumstances that produced it persist: where orders and profits collapse, the practice may lawfully be ended by the employer, provided it first informs the workforce — here through the team leaders — and the change is accepted. [Para 81][Para 85][Para 91] The 300 per cent rate for Sunday and holiday work is therefore not entrenched by Article 13 where a genuine and notified change of economic circumstances is shown. [Para 59][Para 93] Separately, the half-wage maternity entitlement under Article 183 must be paid before the leave begins, not monthly during it, because Article 115(3) bars payment on a rest day and Article 103 treats maternity money as wages. [Para 105][Para 110][Para 111]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that there was little Sunday or holiday work left in the factory, and that other companies follow the same monthly maternity practice, are contextual observations that do not carry the order. [Para 69][Para 92]

- The date the 300 per cent practice ended is given as July 2019 at [Para 54] and as September 2019 at [Para 80] and [Para 83]; both are reported, and the panel's reasoning at [Para 91] rests on "shortly before September".

- The workforce figure is repeated and garbled ("ម៉ាន ... សូម ចំនួន ២៣០៨ នាក់ ... ជួល កម្មករ ... ២៣០៨ នាក់"), but 2,308 is consistent. [Para 46]
- Article 65 is cited at [Para 70] as "មាត្រា ៦៥៩៩" and the definition paragraph at [Para 74] is syntactically broken ("គឺជា ទម្លាប់ ឬ ទេ ការអនុវត្ត"); the sense taken is that of Award No. 95/09 quoted at [Para 77].
- The employer's representative at the hearing is a single initial-only name, "កញ្ញា Liuping", and the authorised director appears as Chen Jing transferring authority to Liu Ping. [Para 1][Para 24]
