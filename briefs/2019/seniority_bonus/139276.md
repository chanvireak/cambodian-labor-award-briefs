# Award No. 102/19 — Hirai Sin Auto (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 17 December 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Nyan Somunninth presiding; Yu Soun Ti, An Vireak) · Doc ID 139276

## 1. Facts
Hirai Sin Auto (Cambodia) cuts and sews clothing and bags at Phsar Trach village, Longvek commune, Kampong Tralach, Kampong Chhnang, with 7,856 workers. [Para 58][Para 59] On 8 October 2019 the provincial department received a request to settle seven demands; conciliation settled two, five went unresolved, and at the hearing the parties agreed four of the five, leaving issue 4 alone. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15][Para 20][Para 21] That issue concerns the calculation base for overtime: the employer pays only the base wage, without adding the monthly seniority bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់អតីតភាពការងារ) introduced by Notification No. 041/11. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 87] The claimants are 136 — 133 workers plus 3 representatives. [Para 60][Para 61]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must add the seniority bonus to the base wage as the basis for calculating overtime pay for the 136 claimant workers? [Para 62][Para 70]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the seniority bonus rewards long service and is itself part of the base wage, so overtime must be computed on base wage plus bonus; earlier awards of the Council have so ordered. [Para 64][Para 65]

**Employer (respondent):** no provision of the Labour Law or of any Prakas requires the seniority bonus to be added to the base wage for overtime, and the company applies Article 4 of its internal rules, which fixes overtime at 150% of the base wage. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 68]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to add the seniority bonus to the base wage as the basis for computing overtime for the 136 workers listed by name and thumbprint in the annex; the award is non-binding and becomes final 8 (eight) days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95]

The panel noted that new Article 139 of the Labour Law, and Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999, fix the 150% and 200% rates but do not say what wage the rate is applied to. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 74][Para 76] It then read Article 102, which defines wage as the remuneration payable under the contract of employment, and Article 103, which lists the components of wage including bonuses, and concluded that "ordinary wage" means the wage the worker regularly receives in normal working days. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 80][Para 81] It adopted the reasoning in award 74/08 (Generation), that "ordinary" means what is habitual, so the wage a worker regularly earns on normal days is the measure — not a sum that depends on good performance or targets in a given month. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 84][Para 85]

Applying that to the seniority bonus, the panel relied on Notification No. 041/11 and on award 255/13 (Pu Yien), which treat the bonus as remuneration for experience, effort and loyalty, paid in addition to the base wage; it therefore held the bonus to form part of the ordinary wage and to be included in the overtime base. [Para 87][Para 89][Para 90] It found as fact that the employer had used base wage alone, and ordered the recalculation for the 136 claimants. [Para 91][Para 92][Para 93]

Arbitrator Yu Soun Ti dissented, reasoning that neither Article 139 (new) nor Article 102 nor Prakas No. 80 defines the wage for overtime, that Article 103 lists overtime pay itself among the components of wage and so cannot serve as the base, and that the employer accordingly owes nothing. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 103][Para 104][Para 106]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the law fixes the overtime premium as a percentage but leaves the base undefined, the base is the worker's ordinary wage — the remuneration regularly received for normal working days — and a monthly seniority bonus paid for length of service, experience and loyalty forms part of that ordinary wage, so it must be added to the base wage before the premium is applied. [Para 81][Para 90][Para 92]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that Article 103 uses "wage" in a broad sense covering the worker's monthly income from the employment, which is wider than the contractual base wage. [Para 80]
