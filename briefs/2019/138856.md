# Award No. 024/19 — Village Culture Cambodia (ភូមិ វប្បធម៌ កម្ពុជា) (Arbitration Council, 8 April 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phalla presiding; Seng Vouch Hun, An Non) · Doc ID 138856

## 1. Facts
The respondent runs a cultural village and arts/tourism business in Siem Reap with 339 workers. [Para 110][Para 111] Its massage section was made redundant on 1 December 2018, 68 staff in all, after notice of 16 November 2018. [Para 118][Para 120] The tourism employees' union filed six demands; conciliation on 13 February 2019 settled one and left five, which reached the secretariat on 7 March 2019, and the hearing was held on 18 March 2019 with both parties present. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 23] No union was shown to be the most representative, and four workers recognised by the Siem Reap labour department represented the claimants. [Para 112][Para 116]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Demand 2) Whether the employer must pay 15 days' compensation in lieu of notice to the 17 massage workers? [Para 117][Para 189]
2. (Demand 3) Whether the employer must pay wages of USD 200 to probation workers and USD 250 to newly confirmed workers? [Para 138][Para 199]
3. (Demand 4) Whether the employer must raise salaries by USD 30 for workers earning under USD 150 and by USD 20 for those earning more? [Para 147][Para 223]
4. (Demand 5) Whether the employer must pay a food allowance of USD 50 a month? [Para 151][Para 231]
5. (Demand 6) Whether the employer must pay annual bonuses of 50 per cent at Chinese New Year and 25 per cent at Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben? [Para 159][Para 240]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the massage staff were given notice shorter than Article 75 allows, so under Article 76 the notice is void and the missing 15 days must be paid; wages of USD 100 and USD 25 food allowance are too low for Siem Reap living costs, where prices, food and rent are high; the employer has not raised the USD 25 food allowance since 2014 while other companies under the same parent group pay KHR 6,000 a day; and the parent company pays annual bonuses to all projects. [Para 117][Para 140][Para 142][Para 152][Para 154][Para 160]
**Employer (respondent):** the 17 claimants withdrew their complaints and signed letters confirming they were satisfied with the severance paid; wages follow skill and experience, with probation workers on USD 100 plus USD 25 food allowance and free lodging, water and electricity; the food allowance is a voluntary benefit the company cannot raise; annual bonus depends on project profit and this project made none. [Para 126][Para 130][Para 135][Para 144][Para 155][Para 162]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all five remaining demands were refused consideration, and the award is non-binding, becoming final 8 (eight) days after notification unless a party objects to the Minister. [Para 258][Para 259][Para 262][Para 263]
On issue 1 the panel checked the names in the 1 December 2018 authorisation against the withdrawal letters and found that all 17 massage workers had withdrawn, and that none of the 111 workers who signed the 18 January 2019 authorisation was a massage worker; with no claimant left, the panel declined to consider the claim. [Para 194][Para 195][Para 197][Para 198]
On issues 2, 3 and 4 the panel found no Prakas setting a minimum wage for the cultural and arts services sector, and no collective agreement, internal rule or past practice fixing USD 200/250 wages, a USD 30/20 increase or a USD 50 food allowance; each was therefore an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 199][Para 201][Para 224][Para 233] Applying Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099, which makes an award on an interest dispute a substitute collective agreement binding all workers for one year, and Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law, the panel held that only a most-representative union may bring such a dispute, found this union was not, and refused to consider the three claims. [Para 214][Para 217][Para 219][Para 221][Para 228][Para 236] On the food allowance it added that the USD 25 already paid is more favourable than the law requires. [Para 232]
On issue 5 the panel applied res judicata (អាជ្ញាអស់ជំនុំ): the same claimants and the same respondent had litigated the same annual bonus demand in award 040/17, where it was dismissed. [Para 241][Para 244][Para 250][Para 252] It noted that Cambodian arbitration procedure has no codified res judicata rule but adopted the three conditions used in earlier awards — same parties, same issue, issue fully decided — and found all three met. [Para 242][Para 244][Para 254][Para 255]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not decide an interest dispute — a demand for new wages or benefits that no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice already grants — unless the union bringing it is the most representative in the enterprise, because an award on such a dispute operates as a collective agreement binding every worker, a power the Trade Union Law reserves to the most-representative union. [Para 217][Para 219][Para 228] A demand already decided between the same parties on the same facts will not be reheard, even though the Council's procedure does not codify res judicata. [Para 244][Para 254]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the employer already pays a USD 25 food allowance, which is more favourable than the statutory minimum, and that the project had returned no profit. [Para 232][Para 163]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in several inconsistent Khmer forms — ខល ឆឺលេ វីដេច វីហ្សត in the header, ខល ឆឺរល វីលេច រី ហ្សត in the body and ខល ឆឺរល វីហ្សូត in the evidence list — with no Latin form anywhere, so no Latin name is asserted here. [Para 1][Para 72][Para 110] The chair is printed គង់ ផល្លៈ in the panel list and គង់ ផល្លី in the signature block, and the worker-nominated arbitrator អន ណន in the header but អន លាន at signature. [Para 1][Para 263] The decision on issue 2 prints the probation wage as "២០០០ ដុល្លារ" against USD 200 everywhere else. [Para 259][Para 199] The authorisation is said to bear 111 signatures at para 196 but "១១១៨" at para 114 of the same list. [Para 114][Para 196]
