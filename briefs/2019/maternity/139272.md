# Award No. 098/19 — Sheng Rong Zhi Yi (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 28 November 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair chosen by both) · non-binding award · Doc ID 139272

## 1. Facts
The company, a garment factory in Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, employs about 220 workers and pays a regular-attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់) of USD 10 a month plus a USD 5 fingerprint-scan bonus. Twenty workers filed eight demands on 21 October 2019; six were settled at the hearing, leaving two about the attendance bonus — whether it must be preserved during certified sick leave, and whether it must be deducted only in proportion to days of permitted special leave. [Para 36][Para 39][Para 17][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must keep wages and other benefits unchanged, including the attendance bonus, when a worker takes sick leave supported by a valid medical certificate? [Para 37]
5. Whether the employer must deduct the attendance bonus only in proportion to the number of days of permitted special leave taken with the employer's authorisation? [Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Because the employer already keeps wages during sick leave, it should keep the attendance bonus as well; and where a worker takes special leave with proper authorisation and complies with the internal rules, the bonus should be cut only in proportion to the days taken. [Para 44][Para 52]
**Employer (respondent):** It keeps wages for sick leave at state and partner hospitals; the attendance bonus is only for workers who come to work with no absence at all, so any absence forfeits it, and the $10 bonus is meant for regular attendance only. [Para 38][Para 45][Para 46][Para 54]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining demands were refused, and no payment was ordered. [Para 104][Para 105]

On issue 2 the panel worked from Article 2 of Notification No. 230 Kប/ស.ជ.ណ of 25 July 2012, under which a worker who attends on all the working days of a month with no absence receives an attendance bonus of at least USD 10. It read the purpose of the notification as encouraging punctual attendance and reducing absenteeism in order to sustain production, so that a worker who does not meet the stated condition cannot claim the bonus. It then searched for any provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules, contract, agreement or past practice requiring a pro-rata deduction for certified sick leave and found none, so the demand had no legal basis. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60]

On issue 5 the panel adopted the interpretation of the Full Bench (អាជ្ញាកណ្តាលរួមក្រុម) in its decision of 3 October 2017: the attendance bonus is payable at 100 per cent where the worker was present on every working day, counting statutory leave days, contractual leave days and days absent to perform a legal duty as attendance, and is not owed where the worker is absent beyond those categories, unless the employer has adopted a different policy or a separate agreement. The Full Bench set out the statutory categories — weekly rest under Article 145, paid public holidays under Article 161, annual leave under Article 166, sick leave, maternity leave under Article 182, and special leave of up to seven days under Prakas No. 267 of 11 October 2001 — and held that leave beyond seven days of special leave falls into another category, and that a bonus scheme cannot be used to pressure workers out of exercising legal rights. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 68][Para 73][Para 79][Para 81]

Applying that to the employer's practice of cutting USD 5 for one day and USD 10 for two days of permitted special leave, the panel held the practice was already more favourable than the law, since outside statutory, contractual and legal-duty leave the employer has discretion whether to grant the bonus at all, and that no legal basis supported the demand for proportional deduction. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 103]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The attendance bonus under Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 is conditional on attendance on every working day of the month, so an employer owes it neither for certified sick leave nor in proportion to days of permitted special leave; statutory leave, contractual leave and absence to perform a legal duty count as attendance for that purpose, but any other absence forfeits the bonus unless the employer's own rule or agreement provides better, and the Council will not order more than an employer's practice that already exceeds the legal minimum. [Para 58][Para 66][Para 81][Para 101]

**Obiter dicta:** That an incentive bonus must not be used to pressure workers into giving up statutory rights, and that a scheme penalising performance of a legal duty would be contrary to law; and that workers may by agreement extend statutory leave entitlements, such extensions counting as contractual leave. [Para 76][Para 78][Para 87][Para 92]
