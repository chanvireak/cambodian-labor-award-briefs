# Award No. 036/19 — Khan Thoren Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 30 May 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chea presiding; Ing Sothy appointed by the employer; An Non appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 138847

## 1. Facts
Khan Thoren Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment manufacturer at Trapeang Thleung, Chom Chao I, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, employing 1,080 workers. [Para 46][Para 32] On 2 April 2019 twenty of its workers filed a complaint setting out ten demands to improve working conditions; conciliation on 24 April 2019 settled four of the ten, and the six unresolved demands reached the Arbitration Council secretariat on 30 April 2019. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] At the hearing of 8 May 2019 the parties settled three more — issues 2, 4 and 6 — so the panel reasoned only on issues 1, 3 and 5. [Para 25][Para 26] No union with most-representative status existed at the company; the workers were represented by six persons recognised by the Labour Disputes Department, four of whom attended. [Para 49][Para 47][Para 48]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must install hot-and-cold water filter dispensers in the clinic for the workers to use, hygienically? [Para 50][Para 82]
3. Whether the employer must pay the meal allowance for overtime once every day, instead of once every ten days as it does now? [Para 55][Para 93]
5. Whether the employer must add the function allowance, the position allowance and the seniority bonus to the base wage when computing overtime pay? [Para 59][Para 105]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** workers need hot water in the clinic to take medicine when they feel unwell, and the single kettle of about one litre there is insufficient and is the doctor's own property. [Para 52][Para 53] Paying the overtime meal allowance only every ten days forces them to borrow or spend their own money for daily food, and the seniority bonus is itself part of wages, so it must be added to the base wage for overtime. [Para 57][Para 61]
**Employer (respondent):** a kettle of hot water is already supplied in the clinic every day and there is no evidence that it is insufficient; the accountants are too few to prepare money for daily payment; and overtime is calculated on the base wage in accordance with the labour inspector's authorisation and its attached schedule. [Para 54][Para 58][Para 62][Para 63]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 1 dismissed; on issue 3 the employer is ordered to pay the overtime meal allowance to the workers once every day; on issue 5 the employer is ordered to add the seniority bonus to the base wage as the basis for calculating overtime pay. [Para 126][Para 127][Para 128] The award is non-binding and becomes final 8 (eight) days after notification if no party objects to the Minister of Labour. [Para 129]
On issue 1, Article 1 of Prakas No. 054 on the supply of sanitary drinking water (10 February 2000) obliges the employer to provide sufficient sanitary drinking water, and the panel read it as not specifying hot or cold water at all. [Para 82][Para 84] Following its reasoning in Award No. 173/14 (Ison Cable), and finding that the employer already provides a kettle of about one litre in the clinic, the panel held there was no legal basis for the demand. [Para 83][Para 90][Para 91][Para 86]
On issue 3, clause 2 of Notification No. 041/11 of 07 March 2011 entitles a worker doing voluntary overtime to 2,000 (two thousand) riel per day or a free meal; the panel accepted the notification fixes no timing, but held that because a meal is a daily necessity the allowance must be paid every working day, following Awards 47/07, 79/07, 85/09 and 001/16. [Para 93][Para 97][Para 98][Para 99][Para 103]
On issue 5, Article 139 (new) of the Labour Law and Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999 fix the 150% and 200% rates but do not define "wage at normal working time". [Para 105][Para 107][Para 110] Reading Article 102 with Article 103, and following Award No. 255/13 (Pou Yien) on the purpose of the seniority bonus, the panel held the bonus is consideration for experience, effort and loyalty and therefore part of normal wage for the overtime base. [Para 112][Para 113][Para 117][Para 121][Para 124] The employer-appointed arbitrator, Ing Sothy, dissented on this issue alone, holding that adding the seniority bonus to the base rests on equity rather than legal basis. [Para 132][Para 136]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Prakas No. 054 requires only that the employer supply sufficient sanitary drinking water; it does not require hot-and-cold filter dispensers, and a demand for them has no legal basis where drinking water and a kettle of hot water are already provided. [Para 82][Para 84][Para 91] Where the employer chooses to pay cash rather than provide a meal for overtime under Notification No. 041/11, the 2,000 riel must be paid on each day overtime is worked, not accumulated and paid periodically, because the need it compensates is a daily one. [Para 93][Para 98][Para 103] The seniority bonus is part of "wage at normal working time" and must be added to the base wage when computing the overtime rate. [Para 121][Para 124]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that providing a meal in addition to wages gives workers the strength to continue doing overtime, since food is an unavoidable daily need; and the dissenting arbitrator reasoned that the base wage exists both to guarantee the legal minimum and to serve as the base for other payments. [Para 95][Para 133][Para 134]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is internally inconsistent on the conciliation report: para 16 attributes report No. 409 to "Sen Thoeun Bot Euro (Cambodia)" while every other reference is to Khan Thoren Apparel. [Para 16][Para 21] Para 22 prints the panel's constitution date as "3 May 2023 2019", two years in one date. [Para 22] The letter confirming the worker representatives is dated "09 December April 2019" at para 43. [Para 43] Notification No. 041/11 is dated 07 March 2011 at paras 93 and 117 but 2 March 2011 at para 96. [Para 93][Para 96][Para 117] The company's commercial registration number is given as MOC 13860959 at para 32 and as 025589 at para 46. [Para 32][Para 46] The worker-appointed arbitrator reads "An Non" in the panel list but is lost to OCR in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 130] The seniority-bonus rate at para 60 is corrupted ("2/dollar"). Individual workers, union officials and employer representatives appear only under partial OCR names and are not reproduced here. [Para 47][Para 48]
