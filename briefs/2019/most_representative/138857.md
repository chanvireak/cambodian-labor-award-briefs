# Award No. 022/19 — Green Tree Cam Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 3 April 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Aing Eng Tong presiding; Ing Sothy, An Non) · Doc ID 138857

## 1. Facts
Green Tree Cam Garment Co., Ltd. cuts and sews clothing at Prey Sandek, Chom Chao II, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, and employed about 693 workers at the hearing. [Para 36][Para 37] From 2013 it paid piece-rate wages (ស៊ីបុង) by adding the total piece-rate earnings to overtime pay; from January 2019 it changed to dividing total piece-rate earnings to find an hourly rate and multiplying that by overtime hours. [Para 41][Para 42] The workers filed five demands on 25 February 2019; conciliation on 26 February 2019 left one unresolved, which reached the secretariat the same day, and the hearing was held on 19 March 2019 with both parties present. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 15] No union was shown to be the most representative; five of the six workers recognised as representatives attended. [Para 40][Para 63]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must keep the pre-2019 piece-rate wage calculation for the workers? [Para 2][Para 66]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer should restore the former piece-rate method because it produced better pay than the practice now applied; they conceded at the hearing that the demand has no legal basis but asked for the earlier, more generous practice to be kept. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 78]
**Employer (respondent):** the former calculation was wrong; in February 2019 the owner checked the piece-rate computation and found the miscalculation, and the company then moved to a lawful method. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 76] It produced an annexe showing that a piece-rate worker doing overtime on a normal day is paid the number of pieces made in overtime × 1.5 × the piece price, and that a worker whose piece earnings fall short of the basic wage is paid on the basic wage instead. [Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the demand to restore the pre-2019 piece-rate calculation was refused; the award is non-binding and becomes final 8 (eight) days after notification unless a party objects to the Minister. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 83]
The panel first satisfied itself that the five representatives recognised by the Labour Disputes Department could act, no most-representative union existing. [Para 59][Para 64] It then applied Article 2(1)(2) of Prakas No. 465 on the 2019 minimum wage for the textile, garment and footwear sector, which fixes USD 182 a month, and the provisions on probationary workers and on piece-rate workers: a piece-rate worker receives the wage corresponding to actual output, and where that output falls below the minimum the employer must top the pay up to USD 177 for a probationary worker and USD 182 for a confirmed worker. [Para 66][Para 69][Para 71]
On the facts the panel found that the employer's new method — dividing total piece-rate earnings to obtain the hourly piece rate and multiplying by overtime hours — is consistent with Prakas No. 465, and that the workers themselves accepted their demand had no legal basis. [Para 73][Para 78][Para 80] It therefore held that the employer already calculates piece-rate wages lawfully and dismissed the claim. [Para 80][Para 81]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand to preserve an employer's former piece-rate practice has no legal basis, and will be refused, where the replacement method complies with the minimum-wage Prakas: the lawful entitlement of a piece-rate worker is actual output pay, subject to a statutory floor of USD 182 a month (USD 177 during probation), so a more generous former practice creates no enforceable right to continue it. [Para 69][Para 71][Para 80]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that where a worker's piece-rate output exceeds the basic wage, the employer pays on the excess, so the change of method does not by itself reduce earnings below the floor. [Para 77][Para 79]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The probationary minimum wage is printed as 177 in one sentence and 173 in the next line of the quoted Prakas, and both figures are reproduced here. [Para 67][Para 71] The award says the panel was constituted on "4 March 2023", which cannot be reconciled with a 2019 award. [Para 12] The employer is named in several inconsistent Khmer forms — ហ្គ្រីន ទ្រី ខេម ហ្គាមេន, ហ្គ្រេន ទ្រី ខេម ហ្គាមេន and ហ្គេន ទ្រី — with no Latin form on the face of the award, so no Latin name is asserted. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 25] The internal rules in evidence are attributed to "ធី នើ ហ្វេ សិន (ខេមបូឌា)" rather than to the respondent. [Para 23] Six representatives are listed at para 38 but five are said to have attended at paras 39 and 63, and the summary at para 65 is corrupted. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 65]
