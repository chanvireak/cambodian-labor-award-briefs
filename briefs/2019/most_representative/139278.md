# Award No. 106/19 — Mono Pha (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 December 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phalla presiding; Ieng Sothy, An Nonn) · Doc ID 139278

## 1. Facts
Mono Pha (Cambodia) Co Ltd makes bags at Krang Svay village, Rolang Kriel commune, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, with 998 workers. [Para 58][Para 59] On 14 November 2019 the provincial department received fourteen demands; the conciliation report of 18 November 2019 listed all fourteen as unsettled, and at the hearing of 29 November 2019 the workers withdrew issue 2 and the parties agreed issues 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13 and 14, leaving five. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24][Para 29][Para 30] On 8 November 2019 the employer suspended and then, on 25 November 2019 with the labour department's written authorisation No. 5079/19 of 19 November 2019, dismissed two workers who were the president and the treasurer of the independent union; the workforce struck from 14 to 25 November 2019 without notice to the employer. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 100][Para 102]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back the two dismissed union leaders, the dismissal being anti-union discrimination contrary to Articles 279, 266, 23 and 294 of the Labour Law and Article 43 of the Trade Union Law? [Para 61]
2. Whether the employer must deduct the attendance bonus (ប្រាក់ព្រីម) pro rata, rather than in full, when a worker takes leave other than statutory, contractual or public-duty leave? [Para 68][Para 125]
3. Whether the employer must pay a skill allowance of USD 10 a month to workers in every group? [Para 81][Para 144]
4. Whether the employer must reopen the north gate for workers to enter and leave the building? [Para 87][Para 166]
5. Whether the employer must keep paying wages during the strike? [Para 99][Para 174]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two leaders did no wrong and were dismissed for their union role, so they should be reinstated; the attendance bonus is deducted too heavily when leave is taken, and other factories do not deduct it at all; a skill allowance of USD 10 should go to every group, not only the sewing section; the single open south gate makes workers in the northern building walk about 100 metres, hard for the roughly 70 pregnant women and 23 disabled workers; and because the employer provoked the strike it should keep their wages. [Para 61][Para 67][Para 73][Para 74][Para 81][Para 94][Para 95][Para 103]

**Employer (respondent):** the two were dismissed for serious misconduct — lateness and breach of internal rules Articles 2 and 10 — with the labour department's authorisation; the bonus scheme of USD 13, USD 6.5 or nothing is its own policy and is more generous than the law; there is no policy of a skill allowance for all groups; the south gate is open permanently because it admits container trucks and controls visitors, and the other two gates are used from time to time; and workers who did not work during the strike cannot be paid. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 76][Para 86][Para 96][Para 97][Para 98][Para 104][Para 105]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all five remaining claims were refused, the panel declining to consider issue 1 while the Minister's review is pending, and refusing the bonus, skill-allowance, gate and strike-wage claims; the award is non-binding and becomes final 8 (eight) days after notification. [Para 184][Para 185][Para 186][Para 187][Para 188][Para 189]

On issue 1 the panel found that the dismissal followed the labour department's authorisation No. 5079/19 and that Article 43 of the Trade Union Law gives the Minister two months to review it; that period had not run by the hearing, so the dispute sat with the Minister and the panel would not consider it, following awards 162/11, 317/14, 024/17 and 097/19. [Para 108][Para 113][Para 118][Para 119][Para 122][Para 123]

On issue 2 the panel applied the second clause of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, which guarantees at least USD 10 a month to workers present on all working days, and the Full Bench decision of 3 October 2017, which extends that to statutory, contractual and public-duty leave but not beyond; outside those categories the employer has discretion, so a scheme paying USD 13, then USD 6.5, then nothing is more favourable than the law and there is no legal basis for pro-rata deduction. [Para 125][Para 128][Para 129][Para 132][Para 135][Para 140][Para 141][Para 142]

On issue 3 the panel held the claim to be an interest dispute beyond what any law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or past practice provides, and since no union at the factory has most-representative status, only such a union could bring an interest dispute to the panel under Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099; it therefore declined to consider it. [Para 145][Para 146][Para 151][Para 157][Para 159][Para 163][Para 164]

On issue 4 the panel treated the choice of gates as an exercise of the employer's management right under Article 2 of the Labour Law, which must be exercised lawfully and reasonably; the south gate is permanently open, the other two periodically, and the workers had not shown that the arrangement harmed them. [Para 167][Para 168][Para 170][Para 171][Para 172]

On issue 5 Article 332 suspends the contract during a strike and states that wages are not paid, and Article 72 confirms suspension removes the wage obligation; the panel followed award 27/10 and held that wages may be deducted for the days not worked. [Para 175][Para 176][Para 177][Para 178][Para 182]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the labour inspector has authorised the dismissal of a union leader and the two-month period for the Minister's review under Article 43 of the Trade Union Law has not expired, the panel will not examine the dismissal, because the decision then lies with the Minister. [Para 113][Para 118][Para 122] A claim for a benefit that no law, agreement, rule, contract or practice grants is an interest dispute, which only a most-representative union may bring; and an attendance-bonus scheme more generous than Notification No. 230 is lawful, so no pro-rata entitlement arises outside the protected leave categories. [Para 129][Para 142][Para 159]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that it found no evidence that any union at the factory held most-representative status, which it treated as a condition of its jurisdiction over interest disputes rather than as a fact decisive between these parties. [Para 60][Para 163]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning at para 124 both declines to consider issue 1 and orders the employer to take the two workers back, while the operative order at para 184 only declines to consider; the two cannot be reconciled and both are reported. [Para 124][Para 184] The list of surviving issues at para 30 reads "ទី១៩" for issue 10, and the panel's own numbering later uses 10. [Para 30][Para 87] The skill allowance is USD 10 in the claim but "100 ដុល្លារ" in the reasoning and the order. [Para 81][Para 145][Para 186] The company is printed ម៉ូណូ ភា, ម៉ូណូកា, ម៉ូតូ ណា ភា and ម៉ូណូតា, and the award date of creation reads "26 November 2023 2019". [Para 26][Para 54][Para 55][Para 59] Conciliation is described as settling four points at para 23 and as settling none at para 24, and the signature block omits the employer-appointed arbitrator's name and prints the president as "Kong Phalla" in the header and "Kong Phallak" in signature form. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 1][Para 190]
