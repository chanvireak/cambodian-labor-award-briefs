# Award No. 033/19 — Chun Sin Zi Yi Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 9 May 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ri, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalluk, chair) · Doc ID 138851

## 1. Facts
Ms D was hired on 26 February 2018 as head of the sewing section on a three-month fixed-duration contract at a basic wage of 350 dollars plus a regular attendance bonus of 15 dollars, 7 dollars travel and accommodation allowance and 2 dollars seniority bonus per month. [Para 47] Of the twelve demands raised by the workers' negotiating delegation, eleven were settled in conciliation and one remained: that the company take Ms D back as workers' representative with back pay from the date of dismissal to the date of return. [Para 2] The company terminated her employment by notice dated 1 March 2019 and notified the Kandal provincial department of labour, although it says the last three-month contract ran to 30 April 2019. [Para 50][Para 55]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take Ms D back as workers' representative and pay her back pay from the date of dismissal until the date she returns to work? [Para 46]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Ms D is a staff delegate and was dismissed without any misconduct on her part, so she must be reinstated; a delegate needs no proof of fault by the employer beyond the absence of her own. [Para 53]
**Employer (respondent):** Reinstatement is impossible; the company agrees to pay compensation under the Labour Law because she repeatedly committed faults, managed the production line badly, was absent without leave many times and did not follow management instructions. [Para 54]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to take Ms D back to work; the request for back pay (ប្រាក់រំលឹក) was not the subject of any order and no sum in riel or dollars was awarded. [Para 102]

On standing, the panel held that the five workers who appeared were entitled to represent the claimants under Prakas No. 362, since the provincial department of labour had recognised them by letter No. 009/19 of 8 April 2019 and no most-representative union existed at the company; Article 54 of the Trade Union Law and Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 leave collective disputes to workers' representatives where there is no most-representative union. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 71]

On the contract, the panel asked what type of contract Ms D held and answered that it was of indefinite duration. Only the initial three-month written contract existed; after it expired the employer made no further written contract and she simply carried on working, so under Article 67(8) of the Labour Law the relationship continued silently and became indefinite-duration. Article 67(7) reinforced this: a fixed-duration contract must be in writing, and a contract not reduced to writing becomes one of indefinite duration. [Para 72][Para 79][Para 80][Para 87]

On the dismissal, the panel found Ms D a staff delegate with a full mandate, and that the employer ended her contract on 1 March 2019 without authorisation from the labour inspector. Article 43 of the Trade Union Law of 17 May 2016 requires the employer to obtain the labour inspector's agreement before dismissing a delegate; here the company notified the department but no authorisation letter had issued. The dismissal was therefore procedurally improper. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 98][Para 99] Under Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 the panel may order an employer to take back a dismissed worker, and it did so. [Para 100]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer dismisses a staff delegate without first obtaining the labour inspector's prior authorisation under Article 43 of the Trade Union Law, the dismissal is improper regardless of the faults alleged against the delegate, and the panel will order reinstatement rather than leave the employer to pay compensation. [Para 93][Para 99]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified — each observation in the reasons goes to standing, contract classification or the validity of the dismissal. [Para 97]
