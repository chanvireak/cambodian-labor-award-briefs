# Award No. 088/19 — Khvaly T Text Style Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 14 October 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer; Sokh Mathoeung, chosen by the workers; An Non, chair) · Doc ID 139226

## 1. Facts
Khvaly T Text Style Co Ltd (ខ្វលលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអិលធីឌី), a garment factory at Choam Chau, Phnom Penh with 1,340 workers, has two local unions, neither of which holds most-representative status under the Law on Trade Unions. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] The workers' representatives filed for improved working conditions on 28 August 2019; conciliation on 6 September 2019 settled four points and left three, and at the hearing the parties agreed a further point, leaving two. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 18][Para 19] The disputed acts are the employer's refusal to pay a daily meal allowance for overtime, which it pays at 2,000 riel three times a month, and its refusal to pay 4,000 riel for work on public holidays and Sundays. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 6][Para 7] The parties chose a non-binding award. [Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance for overtime on each working day, rather than three times a month? [Para 53]
2. Whether the employer must pay a meal or 4,000 riel to workers who work on public holidays or Sundays? [Para 65]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The Ministry's prakas fixes 2,000 riel or one free meal for voluntary overtime, so the allowance must be paid immediately each time the employer cannot provide a meal; work on holidays and Sundays warrants 4,000 riel because wages are rising every year and workers' living standards are poor. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 51][Para 52]
**Employer (respondent):** The meal allowance is a wage component and the law requires payment twice a month, yet the company already pays three times a month, which is more generous than the law; it has too few staff to pay it daily, and for holiday work it has already applied the law. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 47][Para 51]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer ordered to pay the overtime meal allowance for each overtime day; the 4,000 riel holiday claim refused; the employer ordered to pay 2,000 riel for voluntary Sunday work and the 4,000 riel Sunday claim refused. [Para 114][Para 115][Para 116][Para 117]

On the meal allowance, point 2 of Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011 of the Ministry of Labour provides that a worker who voluntarily works overtime at the employer's request receives 2,000 riel a day or one free meal. [Para 53][Para 106] The panel read the notification as obliging the employer to provide the meal itself, because food is a daily necessity that cannot be forgone, and as not authorising a 2,000 riel cash substitute where no meal is given. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] Since the notification fixes no timing, the panel held it reasonable that the allowance be paid on each overtime day, and so ordered, following Awards 47/07, 79/07, 85/09 and 001/16. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 63]

On holiday work, Article 137 and Article 164 of the Labour Law and Articles 2 and 4 of Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999 give a worker who works on a holiday one day's normal wage in addition to his monthly wage, and the panel noted that holiday work is not overtime. [Para 65][Para 68][Para 70][Para 71][Para 73][Para 75] It then found that no provision of the Labour Law, a collective agreement, the internal rules, an employment contract, an agreement or past practice requires a meal or 4,000 riel for holiday work, and classified the claim as an interest dispute. [Para 77][Para 78] That classification decided the case: under Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interest dispute replaces the collective agreement for one year and applies to all workers, so only a most-representative union may bring such a claim. [Para 89][Para 91][Para 92] The claimant union was not most-representative, so the panel declined to consider the claim. [Para 93][Para 96][Para 97][Para 98]

Sunday work was treated differently: work beyond normal hours on a Sunday is overtime, so Notification 041/11 applies and 2,000 riel a day is payable, but nothing requires 4,000 riel. [Para 104][Para 105][Para 107][Para 108][Para 110][Para 113]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance accrues on each day overtime is worked, not once or twice a month, and the employer discharges it by providing a meal rather than by paying cash; but a claim for a benefit that no instrument and no past practice secures is an interest dispute, which the panel will not hear unless the union bringing it holds most-representative status, because an award on it would bind the whole workforce in place of a collective agreement. [Para 58][Para 78][Para 92]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remarks that holiday work is not overtime and that a worker on holiday work earns one extra day's wage, though correct on the instruments, were not the ground of decision — the claim failed on standing. [Para 73][Para 75][Para 97]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The signature block reverses the panel: the header names Sokh Mathoeung as the workers' arbitrator and An Non as chair, while the signatures give An Non to the workers and Sokh Mathoeung as chair. [Para 1][Para 119] The panel is said to have been constituted on "12 September 2019 2019". [Para 15] The claim is put at 4,000 riel throughout but printed as "៤០ រៀល" in one place and the monthly payment as "១០ ៣លើ" and "២លើ" in others. [Para 44][Para 62][Para 64] The issue heading in the reasoning is corrupted by stray characters and figures. [Para 37] The company name is spelled six ways across the face of the award (ខ្វលលី, ខ្វលី, ខ្វល់ ពី; ខូអិលធីឌី, ខូអែល ធីឌី, ខូអែល ធី ឌីឡា), and its Latin form here is a transliteration. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 25][Para 34][Para 35] The claim points are numbered 2, 4 and 6 in the conciliation report but 1, 2 and 3 in the award, so the two numbering systems do not correspond. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 19]
