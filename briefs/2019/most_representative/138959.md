# Award No. 053/18 — Rilay Abel South In Das Steel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 1 February 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Yu Soun Ty (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Aing Eng Thong (president) · Doc ID 138959

## 1. Facts
The company (printed variously as "រីឡាយ អេប៊ល សូស អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល (ខេមបូឌា)" and "Rilay Abel South In Das Steel") employs 2,550 workers in garment cutting and sewing. [Para 37][Para 38] On 20 November 2018 the workers filed six demands on working conditions; conciliation on 20 December 2018 settled none of four points, and non-conciliation report No. 1173 ក.ប/អក/វក of 24 December 2018 reached the Secretariat on 25 December 2018. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15] The panel was formed on 27 December 2018 and heard the case on 16 January 2019, the employer having obtained a postponement. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 19] At the hearing the workers withdrew demand 4, leaving three issues. [Para 21][Para 22] No union with most-representative status exists at the company. [Para 41]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must grant 90 days of maternity leave counted as working days, excluding public holidays and Sundays? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must pay an overtime meal allowance of 2,000 riel per hour to workers who voluntarily work overtime? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must allow pregnant workers one day per month for antenatal checks while preserving wages and all attendance bonuses? [Para 5][Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Article 182 of the Labour Law says 90 days, not three months, and does not say the 90 days include holidays and Sundays, so the 90 must be working days; other companies grant it that way. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 46] Half a day is too short for a pregnancy check given hospital queues and travel, and the attendance bonus should not be prorated. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 65] The 2,000 riel per hour overtime meal claim is to raise living standards. [Para 57]

**Employer (respondent):** It already applies the Labour Law — 90 days of calendar days including holidays and Sundays — and cannot go further because about 90 per cent of the workforce is female on a production line, so absences disrupt output and no substitute can be hired. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] It pays 2,100 riel per day in overtime meal money plus 2,000 riel lunch and one can of milk, which exceeds Prakas requirements. [Para 56][Para 58] Pregnant workers get half a day with wages kept, and a full day where a hospital letter shows an 8 a.m. to 1 or 2 p.m. visit. [Para 59][Para 66]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims were rejected; the award is a non-binding (មិនជាប់កាតព្វកិច្ច) order that becomes final eight days after notification absent written objection to the Minister. [Para 130][Para 131][Para 132][Para 133]

On issue 1 the panel held that Article 182 fixes 90 days without specifying working or calendar days, and that no Prakas or ministerial instruction defines the point; reading the article as a whole, its object being to give the mother time to recover and care for the newborn, 90 days longer than 15 days is conventionally counted in calendar days, and prior awards (Nos 23/08-IW 1, 25/08-SD, 152/08-Wilson, 041/18-BE Carry Will) so held. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 96][Para 97][Para 99] The employer's 90 calendar days therefore complied. [Para 100][Para 101]

On issue 2 the panel found the employer already pays 2,100 riel per day, above the 2,000 riel per day in Ministry Notification No. 041/11 ក.ប/ស.ជ.ណ of 7 March 2011, and that no law, collective agreement, internal rule or practice creates a duty to pay 2,000 riel per hour; the demand was therefore an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 103][Para 105][Para 106][Para 107] Under Article 96 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, an interest award replaces the collective agreement for one year and removes the right to strike, so the panel may decide such a dispute only where the claimant is the most-representative union or unions with over half the workforce; here it was not. [Para 108][Para 113][Para 114][Para 116] Following Awards 81/04-Evergreen, 98/04-Great Union, 169/11-Fortune TV, 02/11-Pou Yuen, 66/11-Inhansung and 278/13-Meng Da Fut Wear Industries, it declined to consider the claim. [Para 117][Para 118][Para 120]

On issue 3 the panel found no law, agreement or practice requiring one paid day per month with the attendance bonus preserved, that current practice is more favourable than the law, and dismissed on the same most-representative ground. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 125][Para 128][Para 129]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The 90 days of maternity leave under Article 182 of the Labour Law are calendar days, including public holidays and Sundays, because the article fixes a long period without specifying working days and no Prakas defines it otherwise. [Para 99] Separately, the panel will not decide an interest dispute unless the claimant is the most-representative union, or unions together representing more than half the workforce, since an interest award displaces the collective agreement and extinguishes the right to strike on that subject. [Para 116]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that a period over 15 days is conventionally read in calendar days, and its observation that the employer's meal and antenatal practice exceeds the legal minimum. [Para 98][Para 124]
