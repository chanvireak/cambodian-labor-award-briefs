# Award No. 009/19 — Tak Fook (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 21 February 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (chair) · Doc ID 138867

## 1. Facts
Tak Fook (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd, a Kampong Chhnang garment factory employing about 5,838 workers, hired staff on fixed-duration contracts of one year renewed each year, paying a 5% termination indemnity at each expiry and re-signing immediately. [Para 55][Para 59][Para 62] Of four demands raised in collective conciliation, one remained unsettled and came to the panel: conversion of the fixed-duration contracts of 117 long-serving workers — later particularised as 115 named workers — into undetermined-duration contracts (kiccanyā mīan thīr welā min kamnt). [Para 2][Para 9][Para 63] The award is non-binding. [Para 14]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to convert the fixed-duration contracts of the 115 named workers with more than two years' service into undetermined-duration contracts? [Para 99]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Successive one-year renewals ran without a break and the total exceeded two years, so Articles 67 and 73 of the Labour Law convert the contracts by operation of law; a 2012 document purporting to keep them temporary forever cannot defeat the statute. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 130]

**Employer (respondent):** Every decision was taken after consulting GMAC and the provincial labour department, which advised that a fresh one-year contract may be signed once the 5% indemnity is paid; the workers themselves agreed on 5 March 2012 to remain on one-year contracts, and from 2019 the company will move to undetermined-duration contracts only after three renewals. [Para 67][Para 69][Para 72][Para 74]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to convert the fixed-duration contracts of the 103 workers whose contracts total more than two years' service into undetermined-duration contracts (names annexed). [Para 137][Para 138]

On standing, the panel held that although neither party asserted a most-representative union in the enterprise, the four worker representatives accredited by the Kampong Chhnang provincial labour department on 18 January 2019 could represent the workforce under Article 54 of the Law on Trade Unions and Article 1 of Prakas No. 362, as it had held in Awards 223/16, 224/16, 008/17 and 237/16. [Para 83][Para 91][Para 92][Para 97]

On the merits the panel read Article 67(2) of the Labour Law as capping the total duration of a fixed-duration contract, including renewals, at two years, and Article 73(5) as deeming a silently renewed contract undetermined-duration once that total is passed; it followed Award 010/03 (Zaksintek) and Award 91/10 (Ny Wire) on the same reading. [Para 99][Para 102][Para 103][Para 123] Paying the 5% indemnity at each expiry did not sever the employment relationship, because the workers kept signing new contracts and kept working for the same company. [Para 129] The 5 March 2012 agreement with 17 shop-steward representatives, which purported to keep one-year contracts "forever", was contrary to Articles 67 and 73 and therefore void under Article 13 of the Labour Law. [Para 116][Para 118][Para 130]

Checking the list, the panel found that of the 115 claimants 103 had over two years' service, 7 had less than two years, 4 had incorrect identity-card numbers or hire dates, and 1 was not on the company roster; only the 103 were considered, and the four defective files were left for the parties to verify. [Para 78][Para 132][Para 134] The employer-appointed arbitrator appended a dissenting opinion, arguing that the choice of contract type belongs to the employer and that regular payment of the 5% indemnity should defeat conversion. [Para 142][Para 166]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer renews fixed-duration contracts so that the aggregate duration of the successive contracts exceeds two years, the contract becomes an undetermined-duration contract by force of Articles 67(2) and 73(5) of the Labour Law, and neither the payment of the 5% termination indemnity at each expiry nor an agreement with worker representatives to keep renewing one-year contracts can prevent that conversion, such an agreement being void under Article 13. [Para 112][Para 129][Para 130]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the Labour Law leans towards undetermined-duration contracts because job security benefits both sides, and its encouragement to the parties to verify the four defective files, were not necessary to the order. [Para 105][Para 134]
