# Award No. 023/19 — Kings Tech Stay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 March 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ri, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalluk, chair) · Doc ID 138854

## 1. Facts
Doung Bou Sam Meat (Cambodia) Co., Ltd transferred all of its workers to Kings Tech Stay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd by agreement of 27 February 2019, which kept their wages, benefits, positions and seniority unchanged. [Para 28][Para 43] The workers struck on 27–28 February 2019 demanding five termination payments from the new employer even though they continued working there. [Para 44] Kings Tech Stay accepted full responsibility for whatever the workers were owed under Prakas No. 443 and the Labour Law, but refused payment on the ground that nothing had been terminated. [Para 3][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the new employer must pay the five termination entitlements — pay in lieu of notice, seniority indemnity, the mental-hardship allowance (ប្រាក់សោហ៊ុយជំងឺចិត្ត), unused annual leave and unpaid or final wages? [Para 76]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the transfer dissolved their contracts with Doung Bou Sam Meat because they are now employees of a new company, so Article 116 of the Labour Law requires all wages and allowances to be paid within 45 hours; the old employer never met them before announcing the change on 26 February 2019. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56]
**Employer (respondent):** under Article 87 of the Labour Law a change in the employer's legal status leaves the contracts standing between the new employer and the workers, and it kept every contract, benefit and seniority date, so no severance is owed. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 57]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused all five heads of claim and awarded nothing in riel or dollars. [Para 90]

Before the merits the panel confirmed the six workers recognised by letter No. 005/19 of 27 February 2019 as the claimants' representatives, all of whom attended, since the company had no most-representative union. [Para 39][Para 72][Para 74]

On the merits the panel put Article 667 of the Civil Code beside Article 87 (new) of the Labour Law. Article 667 lets an employer assign its rights over its workers to a third party only with the workers' consent, and gives the worker a right to terminate where consent was absent; Article 87 provides that on a change in the employer's legal status — inheritance, sale, merger or incorporation — contracts in force continue between the new employer and the workers of the old enterprise. [Para 77][Para 80][Para 82]

The panel found that the workers had in fact agreed to work for the new employer, which maintained their contracts, benefits and seniority in full, and that from the transfer to the date of the award no worker had been dismissed or had a contract severed. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87] Following Award No. 045/08 Khvan Tum Chew Sing (Cambodia) Ltd, it held that where the change of employer leaves the contracts and benefits untouched, the new employer owes no termination indemnity. [Para 83][Para 88]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an undertaking is transferred with the workers' consent and the new employer keeps their contracts, benefits and seniority intact, the transfer does not terminate the employment relationship, so the new employer owes no severance or notice pay under the Labour Law. [Para 87][Para 88]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the transfer agreement itself makes the new company liable for seniority and other entitlements of anyone who declines to move, a point not needed since every worker here moved and continued working. [Para 51]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in at least six forms (ឃីនស៍ ថេភស្តាយ, ឃីនស៍ ថេក ស្តាយ, ឃីន ស៏ ថេក ស្ថាយ, ឃីន ស៊ី) and the transferor variously as ដុង ប៊ូ សាំ មីត and ដុង ប៊ូ សាំ មីត (ខេមបូឌា). [Para 1][Para 42][Para 50] The finding on the company's operating history is corrupted — "ក្រុមហ៊ុន 2 បាន បើក ដំណើរការ ផលិតកម្ម ចំនួន ៨ ឆ្នាំ" with a stray numeral and a damaged phrase on the contract type, so it is unclear whether the eight years belong to the old or the new company. [Para 41] The standing discussion at [Para 63] repeats and drops words ("ភាព ជា តំណាង ១០ យោង..."), and [Para 74] renders "មានសិទ្ធិ" as "ម្សិល សិទ្ធិ". The list of five claims is restated differently at [Para 77] and again, further garbled, in the disposition at [Para 90], where entries such as "ប្រាក់ បី" and "ប្រើ អ" survive only as fragments. The quoted Article 87 runs on into an unrelated string of article numbers. [Para 82]
