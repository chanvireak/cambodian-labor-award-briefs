# Award No. 031/19 — Min Rong Garment Factory Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 May 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalluk, chair) · Doc ID 138853

## 1. Facts
The company, a garment factory registered on 17 October 2017 and employing about 225 workers, let the three-month fixed-duration contracts of 120 workers expire on 31 March 2019 and did not renew them on 1 April 2019. [Para 60][Para 80][Para 178] Among those not renewed were three officers of the Khmer Workers' Heart Union and 51 other members, who brought seven demands through their union. [Para 70][Para 83] The complaint was signed by 57 workers, three without thumbprints and three already counted under the first demand, leaving 51 whose claims the panel considered. [Para 91]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must stop discriminating against the union and take back its three union leaders — Ms S, Mr P and Ms P — as before? [Para 130]
2. Whether the company must take back the 51 union members named and thumbprinted in the complaint? [Para 173]
3. Whether the workers may require the company to remove the general manager (អាចែ ធំ) from his post? [Para 197]
4. Whether the company must pay 2,000 riel a day as a lunch allowance, as it did before? [Para 208]
5. Whether the company must pay a machine-skill allowance of 3 dollars for one machine, 6 dollars for two and 9 dollars for three, as before? [Para 232]
6. Whether the company must build a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) in accordance with the Labour Law? [Para 238]
7. Whether the company must issue fixed-duration contracts of six months rather than three? [Para 246]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the three leaders are union officers enjoying protection under Articles 43 and 62 of the Trade Union Law, were never the subject of a labour-inspector request or agreement, and a team leader had warned that anyone joining the union would be dismissed; the non-renewals were therefore anti-union discrimination. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 89]
**Employer (respondent):** this was non-renewal at expiry, not dismissal; the company had no purchase orders and on 1 April 2019 a meeting decided not to renew 120 of 225 workers and to renew 105 on the basis of regular attendance and skill, so the selection did not turn on union membership. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 81]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all seven demands were refused; no sum in riel or dollars was awarded. [Para 272][Para 277]

On issues 1 and 2, the panel held that a three-month fixed-duration contract simply reached its term on 31 March 2019 and was not renewed. That is not a dismissal, so the protection against dismissal of staff delegates under Article 43 of the Trade Union Law does not apply, nor does the Article 95 procedure for layoffs, which the panel read as covering only staff cuts and not natural expiry. [Para 143][Para 144][Para 192][Para 195] Turning to discrimination, the panel applied Article 62 of the Trade Union Law and Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law, but placed the burden of proof on the workers, who produced nothing beyond a team leader's alleged remark; the employer's evidence of a company-wide non-renewal of 120 of 225 workers supported its account. [Para 145][Para 163][Para 169][Para 170]

On issue 3, the panel cited Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 311 of the Civil Code: only the parties to a contract may extinguish its obligations, so workers cannot compel the removal of a manager. Removal would require proof that the manager is dangerous and cannot be kept without endangering others; no evidence of the alleged shouting and insults was produced. [Para 200][Para 202][Para 205][Para 206]

On issues 4 and 5, the panel classified both claims as interest disputes — no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliged the company to pay the lunch money or the machine allowance. Because an award on an interest dispute replaces the collective agreement for one year under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, only a most-representative union may bring such a claim; the claimant union was not, so the panel declined to consider both demands. [Para 208][Para 210][Para 226][Para 231][Para 236][Para 237]

On issue 6, Article 186 of the Labour Law obliges an enterprise with at least 100 women workers to provide a breastfeeding room and a nursery; the company had 81 women among 105 workers, so no obligation arose and the claim was refused. [Para 238][Para 243][Para 244][Para 245]

On issue 7, a contract takes effect by offer and acceptance under Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code; neither party may impose a duration on the other, so the workers could not force six-month contracts any more than the company could force three. [Para 258][Para 268][Para 271]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer's decision not to renew a fixed-duration contract that has run to its term is not a dismissal; the delegate protection in Article 43 of the Trade Union Law and the layoff procedure in Article 95 of the Labour Law therefore do not apply, and a claim of anti-union discrimination against such a non-renewal fails unless the workers prove the union motive. [Para 143][Para 163][Para 195]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that no most-representative union existed at the company — a finding it needed only for the two interest demands, and which it also used to explain why an award there would bind all workers for a year. [Para 64][Para 225]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The header of [Para 1] is corrupted by a run of machine characters before the Council's name. Dates are printed twice or wrongly: the panel's constitution is dated "ឆ្នាំ ២០២១ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩" [Para 25], the company's evidence letter to "ឆ្នាំ ២១០៩" [Para 41], and the procedural rules to "ឆ្នាំ ២០២" [Para 17]. The number of claimants is given as 54 at [Para 83] but resolved to 51 at [Para 91]. The employer's name is spelled at least five ways (ហ្គាម៉ិន, ហ្គាមិន, ហ្វេក, ហ្វក, ថ្វេក ធើរ). The Article 186 threshold is damaged in the quoted text ("ប្រើប្រាស់ ក្តី ក្មេង ក្តីសុខ ចំនួន យ៉ាងតិច មួយ រយ នាក់") [Para 238], and [Para 243] renders the female headcount as "កម្មករ និយោជិត សហរដ្ឋ អាមេរិក ... ៨១ នាក់". The signature block and the annexed complaint form are illegible OCR noise, so the two side arbitrators' signatures and every worker's name in the annex are unrecoverable. [Para 279][Para 282]
