# Award No. 032/19 — Quinn Th Mejer In Das Strial Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 3 May 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Virak, appointed by the workers; Aing Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 138852

## 1. Facts
The Khmer workers' rights protection union at the company filed a collective dispute on 7 February 2019 raising four demands on working conditions and benefits: a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) or 25 dollars a month in milk money in lieu, easy leave for personal business without discrimination, and a 4,000 riel daily lunch allowance paid weekly each Saturday. [Para 2][Para 12] The fourth demand was withdrawn by the workers as already settled inside the company, leaving three, referred to the Council on 10 April 2019. [Para 15] The workers' representatives did not attend the hearing of 22 April 2019; the employer attended. [Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must build a nursery inside the factory for women workers, or instead pay 25 dollars a month in milk money? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must make it easy for workers to take leave for personal business without discriminating against them? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay a 4,000 riel daily lunch allowance and disburse it weekly every Saturday? [Para 5]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the four demands as filed with the Kandal provincial department of labour on 7 February 2019, of which only three survived conciliation; no representative appeared to argue them at the hearing. [Para 12][Para 20]
**Employer (respondent):** filed a written defence brief (សារណាការពារ) and appeared through its human resources manager and a GMAC officer, but the merits of its defence were never tested because the other side was absent. [Para 25][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel found the claimant had abandoned its claim and terminated the arbitration in case No. 032/19; nothing was ordered in riel or dollars. [Para 65][Para 69]

The panel framed the question as whether it should continue the arbitration when the complainant workers were absent. Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules allow the panel either to proceed in the party's absence or to close the file by issuing an award, provided the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 35][Para 36]

Following its earlier awards (No. 16/07 Lotus, No. 27/07 IM & W 3, No. 138/08 O'Malin, No. 11/11 Chhun Tech Thai, No. 131/12 Master & Frank, No. 108/13 Auto, No. 332/14 T & K Garment), the panel applied three cumulative conditions: the party was properly invited, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 38]

On condition one, invitation letter No. 218 dated 12 April 2019 clearly stated the hearing date of 22 April 2019, and secretariat staff also telephoned the union president and vice-president, who acknowledged the information. [Para 40][Para 42] On condition two, neither the complainant nor any representative attended any of the four stages of the hearing. [Para 50] On condition three, the reasons given — attending a relative's wedding, not remembering the date, and asking for a postponement only when the secretariat called — were not valid; a party wanting a postponement must contact the secretariat itself in advance. [Para 55][Para 56] All three conditions being met, the panel held the claim abandoned and closed the case. [Para 65][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the complainant party was properly summoned, fails to appear at any stage of the hearing, and offers no valid reason for its absence, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by award rather than decide the merits. [Para 65][Para 68]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that it could find no facts on the disputed issues at all, precisely because the complainant never joined the proceedings — a remark that explains the absence of any merits analysis. [Para 34]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award prints the employer's name only in Khmer (ឃ្វីន ធ៍ មេជើរ អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល) and spells it four different ways across the text (មេជើរ, មេជើវ, មេ ដើរ). [Para 26][Para 29] The statement of the demands is interleaved with the Council's letterhead and address block, so demand 1 breaks off mid-phrase and the period for the 25 dollars is lost. [Para 2][Para 4] The procedural history at [Para 14] is garbled and carries contradictory dates (1 March 2019 against a refusal letter of 4 April 2019). The evidence list at [Para 25] is scrambled with date fragments. Case numbers appear with a stray prefix ("១០ ០៣២/១៩") at [Para 69], and the signature block names only the chair, leaving the two side arbitrators' names unprinted at [Para 71].
