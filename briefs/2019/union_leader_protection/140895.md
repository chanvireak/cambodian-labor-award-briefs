# Award No. 096/19 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផូគី ថរ៉ា រី សត & ស្ប៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 19 November 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វួច ហ៊ុន appointed by the employer, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ appointed by the workers, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding) · Doc ID 140895

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផូគី ថរ៉ា រី សត & ស្ប៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) runs the Sofitel Phnom Penh Phokeethra hotel and employs 392 workers. [Para 47][Para 49] The workers' representative of the union សហជីព សាមគ្គី ឯករាជ្យ នៃ សណ្ឋាគារ សូហ្វី តែល ភ្នំពេញ ភូគី ត្រា filed a 2-point claim on 20 September 2019, which conciliation failed to settle, and the case came to the Council on 16 October 2019. [Para 2][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14] A 16 July 2018 agreement gave union leaders 18 days of union leave a year on top of annual leave. [Para 28][Para 50]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must keep paying the service charge to union leaders while they take union leave? [Para 50][Para 71]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay the 2018 breakage reserve to the nine workers who resigned before the reserve was distributed? [Para 63][Para 87]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព សាមគ្គី ឯករាជ្យ នៃ សណ្ឋាគារ សូហ្វី តែល ភ្នំពេញ ភូគី ត្រា:** the workers' representative argued that the 18 days of union leave must carry all benefits including the service charge, that clause 12.12 of the internal rules never withholds service charge from union leave, and that the workers who worked all of 2018 earned the 2018 reserve whether or not they were still employed when it was paid out. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 68][Para 69]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផូគី ថរ៉ា រី សត & ស្ប៉ា (ខេមបូឌា):** the company said the 18 days are already a benefit above the law, that staff on maternity or unpaid leave receive no service charge and union leave is the same, and that delaying the 2018 payout to March was deliberate strategy to stop workers resigning at the start of the year. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 70]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel dismissed the claim for service charge during union leave but ordered the employer to pay the 2018 breakage reserve to the nine workers who had resigned, within 48 hours of their last day. [Para 102][Para 103]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 134 of the Labour Law, which defines the service charge as a gratuity the employer collects from customers and pays to the staff who dealt with them, and followed Award 11/03 — Kampucheana Hotel and Award 042/16 — Victoria Angkor for the rule that only staff who actually serve customers share in it. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 75] Union leaders on union leave serve no customers, so they fall outside the class of beneficiaries. [Para 77] The claim that the employer promised otherwise at the pre-agreement meeting was unsupported, and the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof. [Para 81][Para 84][Para 85]
On Issue 2 the panel read clause 3 of the 16 July 2018 agreement and the 28 March 2019 breakage notice: the reserve is distributed at each year-end after breakage is deducted, and only staff who joined in 2019 or resigned during 2018 are excluded. [Para 87][Para 89][Para 91] The nine claimants had worked the whole of 2018, so their resignation before the payout did not defeat the entitlement. [Para 92][Para 94][Para 95] Because the reserve is deducted from the service charge it is part of wages, and Article 116 requires all wages and allowances to be paid within 48 hours of the last working day. [Para 96][Para 99][Para 100]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Service charge follows service: a worker who performs no customer-facing work, including a union leader on union leave, has no claim to a share of it, and a promise of such a share must be proved, not asserted. [Para 77][Para 81]
A breakage reserve carved out of the service charge is deferred wages, so a worker who completed the earning year keeps the entitlement even if she resigns before distribution, and Article 116 requires payment within 48 hours of her last day. [Para 99][Para 100]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that 18 days of union leave a year is itself a benefit above what the Labour Law requires, which framed but did not decide the service-charge question. [Para 78]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case number is printed 092/19 on the face of the award and corrected to 096/19 by an erratum appended to the same document. [Para 1][Para 105] The paragraph on the 2018 reserve counts 19 workers while the finding and the order count 9. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 101] The signature block inside the award lists two arbitrators and the closing page lists three. [Para 105][Para 110] Workers are named in full in the reasons and in the order, contrary to the Council's usual practice of initials. [Para 65][Para 100] The scanned heading carries machine debris and stray digits inside the quoted Article 134. [Para 1][Para 71]
