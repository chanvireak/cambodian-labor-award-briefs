# Award No. 20/07 — ស្តារ នីត ធីង អេន ហ្គាមេន ហ្វេក ធើរ៉ា អិលធីឌី (Star Nit Thing Garment Factory Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 16 March 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Thach for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Kong Chhala chair) · Doc ID 140592

## 1. Facts
Star Nit Thing Garment Factory Ltd (ស្តារ នីត ធីង អេន ហ្គាមេន ហ្វេក ធើរ៉ា អិលធីឌី) at National Road 115, Prek Samrong, Ta Khmau, Kandal, faced a collective complaint filed through the Kandal provincial labour office on 09 February 2007 by the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) and its local union at the factory. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 40]
The complaint carried 33 demands — correct back pay and bonuses for three years, one-year instead of three-month contracts, meal-allowance increases, no charge for lost identity cards, holiday and Sunday overtime at 1+1 and 1×2, dust masks, toilets and drinking water, a 10,100 riel medical-check refund, maternity and nursing facilities, and action against supervisors and an administrator accused of intimidating union members. [Para 2][Para 7][Para 10][Para 16][Para 22][Para 31][Para 33]
Conciliation failed (report of 12 February 2007, received 20 February 2007) and the hearing was held on 09 March 2007. [Para 37][Para 39] The employer produced a report dated 14 February 2007 listing 116 members said to have resigned from the union, each with a resignation letter. [Para 45] At the hearing the local union leadership and the worker members stated they had resigned from the Khmer Youth Union, no longer relied on the federation to represent them, and abandoned all 33 demands — so none of the demands was examined on its merits. [Para 48][Para 58]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation had legal standing to represent the local union and its worker members in resolving this dispute? [Para 53]
2. Whether the panel could still decide the 33 demands once the original complainants had withdrawn them at the hearing? [Para 58][Para 60]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ and its factory local union:** the 33 demands were pressed through the federation, whose officers appeared at the hearing to represent the workers. [Para 2][Para 47] At the hearing, however, the local leaders and members stated they had left the Khmer Youth Union and dropped every demand. [Para 48]
**Employer (respondent) — ស្តារ នីត ធីង:** all union members had resigned from the Khmer Youth Union, as shown by the 14 February 2007 report and 116 individual letters, so the federation no longer spoke for the workforce. [Para 45][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case No. 20/07 (Star Nit Thing) without any order on the 33 demands, because the complainants had abandoned them. [Para 61][Para 62]

On standing, the panel set out Article 268 of the Labour Law, which requires a professional organisation to deposit its statutes and the list of persons responsible for its administration with the Ministry in charge of labour in order to be registered, and Article 266 on the naming of worker organisations. [Para 50][Para 51] It noted that the Khmer Youth Union committee and its members had resigned and had given the federation no authority to settle their dispute. [Para 52][Para 55] Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires that anyone appearing for a disputing party hold written authorisation from that party; the federation held none, and so had no legal right to represent the local union and its members in this case. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 57]
On the demands, the panel held that where a complainant withdraws, the dispute that is the subject of the case no longer exists — no complaint, no award — and that the Council has no competence to decide a dispute the worker party has withdrawn. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60] It cited Award No. 45/06 (Spat Wear) as the approach followed, and ordered the file closed. [Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union federation cannot represent a local union and its worker members before the Arbitration Council without written authorisation from those parties, whatever the merits of the demands; and once the complainants withdraw their demands, the panel loses jurisdiction, so the case is closed with no ruling on the merits rather than decided demand by demand. [Para 54][Para 57][Para 60][Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the newly formed Khmer Youth Union at the factory had not yet been properly registered, and its quotation of Article 266 on union naming, are background to the standing question and were not themselves the ground of the closure. [Para 46][Para 51]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The chair's name is printed as គង់ ជល្លៈ in the header [Para 1] but គង់ ផល្លៈ in the signature block [Para 63]; both are reported rather than reconciled.
- The demand list is misnumbered: two consecutive items are numbered 3 [Para 4][Para 5] and two are numbered 6 [Para 8][Para 9], with stray opening and closing brackets carried over from the original document; the award elsewhere counts the same list as 33 points [Para 41][Para 43].
- The federation's name appears as សហព័ន្ឋ in the party block [Para 2] and សហព័ន្ធ in the reasons [Para 52]; the council's own name is likewise printed ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល and once as ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញាធរ [Para 62].
- Two complaint documents are described in the evidence list as 20 points (26 January 2007) and 13 points (16 January 2007) [Para 45], which does not obviously add to the 33 demands briefed; the award does not explain the discrepancy.
- Individual worker, union-official and employer-representative names are printed in full in the demand list and party block and are reduced to roles here; the employer administrator and the line supervisors identified in the demands are not named in this brief. [Para 1][Para 14][Para 33]
