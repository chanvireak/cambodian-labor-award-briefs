# Award No. 06/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃីង ឡែន ហ្គាមិន អិលធីឌី (Khing Laen Garment Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 12 February 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ប៉ែន ប៊ុនឈា presiding; ម៉ារ សំបូរណា, employer nominee; អន ណន, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140481

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃីង ឡែន ហ្គាមិន អិលធីឌី (Khing Laen Garment Co., Ltd), on Street 2, Prek Ta Long village, Chak Angre Krom, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, is a garment factory whose workers are organised by the garment section of សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា (the Democratic Union of Garment Workers and its confederation). [Para 1][Para 2] Conciliation of the union's demands failed on 28 December 2007 on two points, and the non-conciliation report No. 031 dated 08 January 2008 reached the Secretariat on 10 January 2008. [Para 8][Para 12][Para 13] The dispute followed the employer's refusal to carry out Award No. 109/07 and the dismissal of seven union leaders and activists between September and October 2007; some workers struck and the company began hiring replacements. [Para 16][Para 22][Para 23] The employer was invited twice and did not attend the hearing of 01 February 2008, giving no reason. [Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay 50 per cent of wages during maternity leave calculated on total monthly wage — overtime included — averaged over the last 12 months, rather than on basic wage alone? [Para 24][Para 69]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the seven dismissed union leaders and activists must be reinstated in their former posts with back pay from the date of dismissal to the date of return? [Para 27][Para 84]
3. (Additional issue) Whether the dismissal of four further workers in January 2008 was a direct consequence of this dispute, so that the panel could examine it under Article 312 of the Labour Law? [Para 55][Para 102]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and the workers argued that the 50 per cent maternity wage must be computed on total earnings including overtime averaged over the last 12 months, that the company had wrongly used only the last basic wage, and that the seven were dismissed for union membership and activity — attending the Council's hearing, recruiting members, checking conditions — with no real misconduct, so they should return with back pay. [Para 25][Para 28][Para 31][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃីង ឡែន ហ្គាមិន អិលធីឌី said it could pay only 50 per cent of basic wage, seniority pay and bonus, that it could not take the seven back at all, and it did not appear at the hearing to answer the claims. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 70]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — maternity pay of 50 per cent of the average monthly wage over the last 12 months, overtime included, for the 90-day leave (three months); reinstatement of the seven in their former posts with back pay from dismissal to return; the demands of the four workers dismissed in January 2008 refused. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 109]
On procedure, the panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099/04: a party duly summoned who fails to appear without valid reason does not prevent the panel from continuing and issuing an order, and it followed Awards 53/04 and 63/04 in hearing the case with the employer absent. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 68] On Issue 1, Article 182 gives 90 days' maternity leave and Article 183 half wage including ancillary benefits to a woman with one year's service; because Article 183 does not define wage, the panel read it with Article 103, which lists overtime pay among the components of wage, and concluded that the 50 per cent must be computed on total earnings including overtime. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 75][Para 77][Para 78] It adopted the formula used in Awards 68/04, 18/06 and 33/07: total wage of the last 12 months divided by 12, divided by two, multiplied by three for the 90 days. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 81]
On Issue 2, the panel noted that the seven held contracts of unspecified duration, so Article 74 required a valid reason for dismissal, and Articles 12 and 279 forbid an employer from using union membership or activity to decide hiring, discipline or termination. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 87][Para 88] Because the employer was absent without reason, the panel examined only the workers' evidence and treated their uncontradicted statements as proof that the dismissals were aimed at union members and that the infractions alleged were pretexts; it followed Awards 28/07 and 123/07 and ordered reinstatement with back pay from the date of dismissal. [Para 91][Para 94][Para 96][Para 99][Para 100]
On the additional issue, Article 312 confines the panel to the non-conciliation record and to later matters that are a direct consequence of that dispute; the four were dismissed in January 2008, after the record of 28 December 2007, but the panel found no evidence that those dismissals flowed from the two claims and refused to consider them. [Para 102][Para 104][Para 105][Para 106]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissal that occurs after the conciliation record satisfies the timing limb of Article 312 but not its causal limb: the panel will refuse to examine it unless the claimant shows it flows directly from the two claims in the record, so the mere sequence of events does not enlarge the Council's jurisdiction. [Para 102][Para 104][Para 106]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel added that where an employer stays away without valid reason, the workers' uncontradicted statements will be taken as evidence — a proposition that decided the discrimination issue rather than the jurisdiction question, and that also supported the maternity order. [Para 91][Para 95]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The face of the award prints the number as "០៦/០៨--- ឃីង ឡែន", with a run of dashes standing where the case name should be separated. [Para 1] The reasons at one point order maternity pay at "៥()" and then "៩០ ភាគរយ" (90 per cent), contradicting the 50 per cent given in the reasoning and in the operative order; the operative 50 per cent is reported here and both readings are cited. [Para 82][Para 107] The seven workers' names are spelled two or three different ways between the claim, the table of dismissals and the order (for example one activist appears as "ចាន់ ស៊ីម ហ៊ាន់" and as "ថាន់ ស៊ីម ហ៊ាន់"), and they are given here by role and initial only. [Para 27][Para 30][Para 103] One dismissal date in the additional issue is corrupted into "៤២៥/០១/០៨", and the union's registration certificate names the factory "បរឹង ឡែន" rather than Khing Laen. [Para 21][Para 55]
