# Award No. 340/14 — Kin Tay Gamain Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 31 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer, Liv Sovanna for the workers, Pen Bunhea chair) · Doc ID 139785

## 1. Facts
Kin Tay Gamain Co., Ltd, a Phnom Penh garment factory employing about 580 workers, did not renew the fixed duration contracts of three sewing workers (Ms N, Ms C and Ms P) in the first half of November 2014. [Para 34][Para 35] The Cambodia Union of the Kin Tay garment factory, registered on 19 May 2014, brought a collective dispute seeking reinstatement with unchanged position, wage and benefits plus back pay to the date of return; the third worker was taken back before the hearing, leaving two claimants. [Para 2][Para 37][Para 42]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must continue the employment contracts of the two remaining workers, keeping their position, wage and benefits, and pay back pay from the date of non-renewal until the date of return? [Para 88]
2. Whether the employer complied with point 4 of the agreement of 12 September 2014? [Para 114]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** neither worker committed any fault or ever received a warning, the employer gave no performance evaluation and never announced a staff reduction, yet it let go the longest-serving workers while keeping junior ones; non-renewal is in substance a dismissal, and point 4 of the 12 September 2014 agreement barred dismissing workers. [Para 47][Para 69][Para 74]
**Employer (respondent):** the dispute is not collective because it concerns only three workers; a company policy from November 2014 cut ten workers per group, from 95 to 85, across all sections without discrimination, to absorb the 2015 wage increase; the agreement barred dismissal only while the company had no work, and this was non-renewal while work existed. [Para 43][Para 48][Para 49][Para 68]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both claims rejected; no reinstatement, back pay or compensation ordered, and the award becomes final if no objection is filed within eight days. [Para 135][Para 136][Para 137]

On jurisdiction the panel applied Article 302 and found all three conditions of a collective dispute met — several workers against one employer, a subject matter of working conditions, and a dispute capable of disturbing the enterprise or social peace — holding that the employer bore the burden of showing otherwise, following Awards 45/07, 13/08 and 125/13. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 84][Para 86]

On issue 1 the panel held that under Article 73 a fixed duration contract expires automatically at its term and renewal depends on the parties' consent, which neither side can be compelled to give (Awards 100/07 and 64/09). [Para 90][Para 91] Both sides accepted that Ms C's contract expired on 12 November 2014. [Para 92] Ms N's contract expired on 3 November 2014; she said she was told on 4 November, the employer said 3 November, she produced no evidence, and the administration had completed her departure report and three-month wage statement on 3 November without objection, so the panel accepted the employer's date. [Para 103][Para 109][Para 110] The employer was therefore under no duty to take either worker back. [Para 112]

On issue 2 the panel read point 4 as barring dismissal only while the company had no work. Treating "dismissal" (បញ្ឈប់) as the employer's unilateral termination before expiry and "non-renewal" (មិនបន្តកិច្ចសន្យា) as the parties' decision not to make a new contract after expiry, and "has work" and "no work" as opposites, it found the agreement did not reach non-renewal while work was available, so there was no breach. [Para 121][Para 122][Para 126][Para 132]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed duration contract ends automatically at its term; a refusal to renew is not a dismissal, and an undertaking not to dismiss workers while the enterprise has no work does not extend to refusing renewal at expiry while work is available. The party asserting that the employer gave notice of non-renewal on a particular date bears the burden of proving it, and unchallenged contemporaneous personnel records outweigh unsupported testimony. [Para 90][Para 110][Para 123][Para 133]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that an employer challenging the collective character of a dispute must prove its case, and that re-employing one of the three workers did not change that character, were not necessary to the rejection of either claim. [Para 42][Para 77][Para 82]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The number of women dismissed is printed as "ចំនូន ០ញ៉ាំ7នាក់" [Para 65] but as three in the claim [Para 2]; the workers' reply elsewhere refers to 13 dismissed workers [Para 44].
- Doubled years: "ថ្ងៃទី ០៣ ខែវិច្ឆិកា ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៤" [Para 107] and "ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣ ២០១៤" for the 12 September 2014 agreement [Para 115].
- The year of Ms N's 4 August contract is lost to a stray "-៣-" [Para 19], and the three-condition list carries a stray "៤ ៣-" [Para 80].
- The panel finds the employer filed no letter notifying non-renewal [Para 40], then declines to rule on the workers' request to reject the employer's documents [Para 41]; the two passages do not read consistently.
- The header date (31 December 2014) is later than the hearing (17 December 2014) and the panel's constitution (11 December 2014) [Para 9][Para 12].
