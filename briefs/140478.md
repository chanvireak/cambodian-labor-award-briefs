# Award No. 028/20 — Cambodia Airports (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាកាសយានដ្ឋាន កម្ពុជា (CAMS)) (Arbitration Council, 22 June 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Mar Samborna (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair) · Doc ID 140478

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាកាសយានដ្ឋាន កម្ពុជា (CAMS) provides airport services at Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Preah Sihanouk, employing about 1,600 staff. [Para 107] As COVID-19 collapsed traffic, the company issued a memorandum on 8 April 2020 putting staff on home standby from 9 April to 31 May 2020 at 80% of base wage for those earning above USD 500, or 100% for those below, on condition of signing an acknowledgement annex. [Para 185][Para 111][Para 112] Two local unions — the independent union of Phnom Penh international airport and the tourism-industry union at Siem Reap — filed seventeen demands on 27 April; conciliation failed and five issues came to the panel, which heard the case twice, on 20 and 21 May 2020. [Para 26][Para 28][Para 24][Para 25] Of the 142 complainants on Issue 1, 83 signed the annex and 59 refused. [Para 248]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay 100% of wages and benefits for April and May 2020 to the workers placed on home standby? [Para 239]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must bargain a new collective agreement in good faith, without evasion and without postponing the negotiations? [Para 319]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay the 50% 2019 performance bonus to the union president? [Para 343]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the workers may have the new employment contract of 9 April 2020, providing only 80% wages, annulled? [Para 353]
5. (Issue 5) Whether five ramp supervisors were unlawfully paid less than a comparable non-union colleague, in breach of equal-pay rules? [Para 357]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត, through the two plant-level unions, argued that the employer never suspended contracts lawfully under Article 71(11), that the annex was signed under compulsion because refusal meant 50% wages, and that the union president was denied his bonus and the five supervisors paid less than a non-union colleague for identical work. [Para 121][Para 123][Para 211][Para 283]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាកាសយានដ្ឋាន កម្ពុជា, through its legal and HR managers, answered that the arrangement was not a suspension but a consensual variation of contract terms, that it had consulted the unions and explained the annex, that the 50% bonus was withheld for documented fault, and that the five supervisors sit at lower job levels. [Para 7][Para 132][Para 173][Para 213]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel rejected the 100% claim of the 83 workers who signed (order affirmed at 80%), ordered 100% wages for the 59 who did not sign, ordered the employer to bargain the collective agreement to completion within three months, ordered payment of the 50% 2019 bonus to the union president, and dismissed the annulment and equal-pay claims. [Para 380][Para 381][Para 382][Para 383][Para 384][Para 385]
The panel first rejected the workers' challenge to the employer-appointed arbitrator under Article 15 of Prakas No. 099, finding no evidence of conflict of interest, and noted that a dissenting arbitrator is entitled to record a dissent. [Para 231][Para 234][Para 236]
On Issue 1 it held that the arrangement was a variation of contract terms by agreement, not a suspension under Article 71(11): the employer had met the unions, explained the annex, and allowed time to consider before individual signature. [Para 279][Para 297][Para 298] Applying Articles 65, 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code, it found consent valid for the 83 signers, but that no agreement existed for the 59 who refused, so the unilateral cut to 50% for them was unlawful and they must receive 100%. [Para 301][Para 313][Para 316][Para 317][Para 318] It also found no proof of coercion, despite the letter of 20 April giving a deadline. [Para 300][Para 309]
On Issue 2 it applied Articles 53, 54, 69 and 71 of the Trade Union Law: the most representative union may demand bargaining and the employer must engage in good faith. [Para 329][Para 330] It found the postponement was caused by the pandemic and was justified, but held that an indefinite postponement is not permitted, and set a three-month limit running from the date the award becomes final. [Para 337][Para 339][Para 340][Para 342]
On Issue 3 it read the 31 March 2020 memorandum as promising the 50% company-result component to all staff without exception or individual assessment, and held that the employer's internal regulations reserve only the personal component to its discretion; the bonus was therefore owed. [Para 349][Para 350][Para 347][Para 351]
On Issue 4 it found the same consent that validated the annex, so there was no basis to annul it. [Para 354][Para 355]
On Issue 5 it found that the five supervisors held the same title but sat at different job levels (T5, AM1–AM4), that Article 106 requires equal conditions, equal skill and equal output together, and that no evidence showed the pay gap was union-related. [Para 213][Para 358][Para 371][Para 377][Para 379]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A wage reduction agreed individually in writing, after explanation and time for reflection, is a valid variation of the contract and binds the worker who signed it; but it cannot bind those who refused, and the employer that applies it to them unilaterally must restore their full wages. [Para 301][Para 303][Para 317]
An employer may postpone collective bargaining for a genuine economic reason but not indefinitely: the panel will fix a date certain for completion. [Para 339][Para 342]
A bonus announced to all staff without stated exception is owed to every employee, notwithstanding a general management-prerogative clause, where the internal rules reserve discretion only over the personal component. [Para 350][Para 351]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that suspending contracts under Article 71(11) would have left the workers with no wages at all, so the 80% arrangement was more favourable than the law, a point it used only to assess good faith. [Para 281][Para 282]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០," and the panel's constitution and hearing dates are printed as impossible composites ("15 May 20 20"). [Para 29][Para 24] Workforce figures conflict: 1,600 employees in the findings, 1,900 in the reasoning, 142 complainants, and a 150-name list. [Para 107][Para 186][Para 240][Para 248] The memorandum of 31 March 2020 refers to the company's "2029" results, evidently 2019. [Para 180][Para 344] Paragraph 371 is partly destroyed, leaving the reasoning on equal pay interrupted mid-sentence. [Para 371] The award is signed by Mar Samborna, Tuon Si Phan and Pen Bun Chhea, but the signature block is corrupted. [Para 387] Individual workers and union officers are named in the source and in the annexed staff list; this brief refers to them by role or job level only.
