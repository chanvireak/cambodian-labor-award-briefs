# Award No. 29/05 — Kang Ning Co. Ltd and the Khmer Youth Federation of Trade Union (Arbitration Council, 7 July 2005)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tayseng, Ann Vireak, chair Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140774

## 1. Facts
Kang Ning Co. Ltd, a hanger producer at Toulsangke, Phnom Penh, employed approximately 70 workers. [Para 24] On 28 March 2005 the Labour Inspection Department received a worker complaint; conciliation on 25 April 2005 failed on six demands, which reached the panel on 10 May 2005, and the hearing was held on 24 May 2005 with the employer absent. [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 27] Ms C, elected president of the Khmer Youth Union at Kang Ning Co. Ltd, began work on 27 November 2002 on an oral fixed-duration contract at a basic wage of US$45 plus a US$5 attendance bonus, and was dismissed on 6 February 2005. [Para 32] [Para 33] [Para 35] At the hearing the union added a claim for the dismissal of the union secretary, Ms S, on 20 May 2005. [Para 57] [Para 58]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must reinstate Ms C or pay her severance and termination entitlements in accordance with the Labour Law? [Para 65]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay each regular worker a seniority bonus of US$2 per month under Notification 17/00? [Para 45]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must pay overtime at 1.5 times and public-holiday and Sunday work at 2 times the base wage? [Para 48]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must reopen the factory's rear gate for workers living nearby? [Para 127]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the company must comply with the Labour Law when it dismisses workers? [Para 135]
6. (Issue 6) Whether the company must refrain from setting production targets beyond the workers' capacity? [Para 137]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that Ms C was dismissed for asking about a seniority bonus the company had agreed to pay, without prior notice and with indemnity below the law, and that they preferred severance to reinstatement. [Para 66] [Para 74] The union argued overtime and holiday pay fell below the legal multipliers from December 2004, that the rear gate was closed after the 2004 strike although it is only 30 metres from workers' homes against a one-kilometre walk, and that targets ignored differences between easy and difficult hanger models. [Para 48] [Para 52] [Para 54] [Para 137]
**Employer (respondent):** that the company did not appear at the hearing, did not join the Ministry's conciliation, and produced no evidence or argument at two opportunities offered by the panel. [Para 27] [Para 28] [Para 80]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must pay Ms C US$50.00 in lieu of one month's notice, US$57.69 as dismissal indemnity, and compensation for accrued unused annual leave, and must open the rear gate for workers living near the factory; the panel declined the seniority bonus, overtime, dismissal-compliance and production-target demands, and declined to consider Ms S's claim. [Para 153] [Para 155] [Para 157]
On Issue 1 the panel found the oral contract to be of unspecified duration under Article 74, so that termination required a valid reason relating to aptitude or behaviour. [Para 68] [Para 73] Asking about an agreed seniority bonus was neither misconduct under Article 74 nor serious misconduct under Article 83 paragraph 2, and with the employer absent and silent the workers' account was accepted, as in 18/04-Inter Continental. [Para 79] [Para 80] [Para 82] Entitlement therefore comprised last wages, notice compensation under Article 77, dismissal indemnity under Article 89 and unused leave under Article 166. [Para 85] [Para 99] Article 75 sets a minimum notice of one month for service between two and five years, and Article 89 sets 15 days of wages per year of service capped at six months. [Para 86] [Para 95] Following 51/04-Sam Han the monthly wage was fixed at US$50.00 (US$45 basic plus US$5 attendance bonus), giving US$50.00 in lieu of notice and US$57.69 indemnity for 30 days. [Para 96] [Para 98]
On Issue 2 the panel found no written agreement, and held that Notification 17/00 applies only to garment, textile and footwear enterprises in Phnom Penh. [Para 103] [Para 104] It had applied that notification by equity in 25/03-Advance Industry, but declined to do so here because hanger-making requires no specific skill and length of service did not improve skill. [Para 104] [Para 106] No other law provides a seniority bonus, so the demand was an interests dispute, and the union lacked most representative status under Article 277, an award on which would displace collective bargaining for a year under Article 43 of Prakas 99/04. [Para 107] [Para 112] [Para 114]
On Issue 3 the panel held it had no jurisdiction over a demand directed to future holiday and Sunday pay absent an actual violation, following 10/03-Jacqsintex. [Para 116] [Para 121]
On Issue 4 the panel noted that Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to manage the factory, but that Article 6 of Prakas 80/99 requires safety for workers leaving work at night. [Para 128] [Para 131] Unlike 11/05-South Bay, reopening the gate the company had itself opened before the 2004 strike cost nothing and the employer gave no security reason for closing it. [Para 130] [Para 133] [Para 134]
On Issue 5 the panel again held it had no jurisdiction over a demand about future dismissals. [Para 136]
On Issue 6 the panel found no law prohibiting production targets, following 28/04-Grand D'Angkor and 24/05-Wear Well, and no evidence of how targets were set. [Para 137] [Para 139]
On Ms S's added claim the panel applied Article 312: the dismissal came after the non-conciliation report but was not proved to be its direct consequence, since she and others in her group also missed the 100-dozen daily target. [Para 143] [Para 146] [Para 151] [Para 152]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An oral fixed-duration contract is of unspecified duration under Article 74, so dismissal requires a valid reason of aptitude or behaviour, and a worker's inquiry about an agreed bonus is not misconduct under Article 74 or Article 83. [Para 73] [Para 79] Where an employer stays away, refuses conciliation and files nothing after two opportunities, the panel accepts the workers' uncontradicted account and fixes entitlements under Articles 75, 77, 89 and 166 on the average-wage method. [Para 82] [Para 85] [Para 96] Notification 17/00 binds only garment, textile and footwear enterprises and is extended by equity only where service genuinely improves skill, not to unskilled work. [Para 104] [Para 106] A demand about future conduct, with no actual violation, is not a dispute and confers no jurisdiction. [Para 116] [Para 136] A gate the employer opened, then closed after a strike without any security justification, must be reopened for workers' night-time safety under Prakas 80/99. [Para 133] [Para 135] A claim first raised at the hearing is heard only if a direct consequence of the non-conciliation dispute under Article 312. [Para 143]

**Obiter dicta:** that a union without most representative status cannot bring an interests dispute, and that an award on interests would bind all workers and remove their right to strike for the same demands. [Para 113] [Para 114]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The dismissed worker's name is printed four ways across the award, including a form with a different final vowel and one split across a line break; only initials are used here. [Para 32] [Para 152] Her start date is 27 November 2002 in the facts but "September 2002" in the reasons, and her contract is described as lasting "two years and 3 months" for a period of about two years and two months. [Para 32] [Para 71] [Para 73] The workforce is "approximately 70 workers" in the case summary and "approximately 10 regular workers" in the seniority reasoning. [Para 24] [Para 44] The non-conciliation report is dated 9 May 2005 in the procedural history and "in April 2005" in the reasoning on the added claim. [Para 13] [Para 146] OCR drops medial "t" throughout ("themployer", "top en") and duplicating sentences recur in the reasons. [Para 128] [Para 132]
