# Award No. 63/05-Harta Co. Ltd — Harta Pakaging Industrial (Cambodia) Co, Ltd and Cambodia Federation of Independent Trade Union (CFITU) (Arbitration Council, 21 November 2005)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Suonty dissenting, Tuon Siphann, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140797

## 1. Facts
Harta Pakaging Industrial (Cambodia) Co, Ltd, at National road 4, Kontor village, Ang Snoul district, Kandal province, employs 220 workers [Para 22][Para 23]. Mr V, the local union president (name given in full in the source), began work in the shipping section on 5 June 2004 [Para 27]. In July and August 2005 he was warned for working without shoes, fined 500 riel and later 700 riel for lateness, and on 30 July 2005 refused a transfer to the Elastic section and went home without notice [Para 27][Para 30][Para 78]. On 30 August 2005 the union delivered its registration receipt to the employer, and on 31 August 2005 handed over the election minute and the list of elected leaders [Para 104][Para 110]. The company dismissed him on 1 September 2005, effective 2 September 2005, for not following management's instructions, without seeking Labour Inspector authorisation [Para 46][Para 48][Para 113].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must reinstate Mr V, President of the local union at Harta Company, who was dismissed without authorisation from the Labour Inspector? [Para 8]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), CFITU:** Mr V was elected union president on 7 August 2005, the union was registered and its receipt and leadership list delivered to the employer on 30 and 31 August 2005, and 45 fellow workers petitioned for his reinstatement [Para 19][Para 39][Para 104].
**Employer (respondent), Harta Pakaging Industrial (Cambodia) Co, Ltd:** the dismissal followed repeated lateness and refusal of a transfer; the employer received the leadership list only on 1 September 2005, the day it decided to dismiss, so no authorisation was needed [Para 111][Para 113].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to reinstate Mr V in the same place on the date the award takes effect and to pay full wages and other benefits for the dismissal period up to 30 November 2005 [Para 124][Para 125]. The award becomes binding 8 days after notification absent written opposition [Para 126].
The panel first disposed of the misconduct allegations: because the employer allowed the worker to work without shoes after seeing his sore leg, that act was not misconduct and the first warning did not count [Para 58][Para 59]; the 500 riel fine was already a disciplinary sanction, so Article 28 of the Labour Law barred a second sanction for the same lateness, and the company had no Internal Work Rules at the time [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]; the 30 July absence was regularised when the employer agreed not to count it as annual leave [Para 84][Para 86].
On protection, Article 282 and Article 293 paragraph 1 protect shop stewards and unelected candidates, requiring Labour Inspector authorisation for dismissal [Para 114][Para 115]. Article 3(3) of Prakas 305/01 of 22 November 2001 extends shop-steward protection to anyone running for union leadership from 45 days before the election, and Article 4(1)-(2) covers founding members and joiners from the filing of the registration application [Para 115][Para 117][Para 119]. The panel found the employer dismissed a protected union president without the Labour Inspector's permission, in violation of Article 279 of the Labour Law and Articles 3 and 4 of Prakas 305, so reinstatement with seniority and benefits followed [Para 121][Para 123].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker elected president of a union whose registration receipt and leadership list have been delivered to the employer is a protected shop steward, and dismissing him without prior authorisation from the Labour Inspector violates Article 279 of the Labour Law and Prakas 305, requiring reinstatement with full wages and benefits for the dismissal period [Para 121][Para 123].

**Obiter dicta:** the dissenting arbitrator, You Suonty, would have dismissed the claim: in his view Article 268 deems a union registered two months after filing even without a certificate, the employer received the leadership list only on the day of dismissal, and persistent lateness without Internal Work Rules still justified dismissal under Articles 78 and 83 [Para 151][Para 154][Para 135][Para 146].
