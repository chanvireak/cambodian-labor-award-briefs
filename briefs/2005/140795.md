# Award No. 61/05-Makalot — Makalot Garment (Cambodia) and Cambodian Labour Union Federation (CLUF) (Arbitration Council, 14 November 2005)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry, An Nan, Run Saray chair) · Doc ID 140795

## 1. Facts
Makalot Garment (Cambodia) is located in Vathanak Industrial Park, Street Veng Sreng, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Dangkor, Phnom Penh, and employs 1,200 workers [Para 19]. The Cambodian Labour Union Federation (CLUF, សហព័ន្ធសហជីពកម្មករកម្ពុជា) registered with the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training on 20 August 2005 [Para 19]. Three of its leaders at the factory — the president, the vice-president and the union advisor, referred to here as Mr H, Mr B and Mr K — were transferred from the Cutting section to the Numbering section on 14 October 2005 and dismissed on 18 October 2005 for alleged non-performance and causing disorder [Para 23]. The panel found the employer had not informed or sought authorisation from the Labour Inspector, and that the three had never received written warnings [Para 21][Para 22].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the suspension and dismissal of the three union leaders was lawful, and whether they must be reinstated and compensated? [Para 2][Para 25]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), CLUF and CLUF of Makalot Factory:** the contract suspension and dismissal were discrimination against the union and did not follow legal procedure; the three had done nothing wrong, and a petition of fellow workers supported their reinstatement [Para 2][Para 24].
**Employer (respondent), Makalot Garment (Cambodia):** the three were suspended because they stopped Cutting Section workers from going to work on 15, 17 and 18 October 2005, incited the strike and caused losses; the company would follow legal procedure once the problem was solved [Para 3][Para 26].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to reinstate Mr H (president of the union), Mr B (vice-president) and Mr K (union advisor) and to compensate them from the date of suspension to the date of reinstatement [Para 34]. The award is non-binding and becomes binding 8 days after notification unless a party lodges written opposition [Para 34].
Article 293 of the Labour Law allows dismissal of a shop steward or candidate only after authorisation from the Labour Inspector [Para 27]. Prakas 305 of 22 November 2001 extends the same protection from the moment the registration application is filed to founding members and to those who join during the application period, and renders void any measure taken without prior authorisation, with a fine under Article 373 [Para 27][Para 29]. The panel found the employer had not requested authorisation from the Labour Dispute Resolution Department before dismissing the three leaders [Para 30].
On evidence, the employer supplied documents but refused to let the panel disclose them to the other party or to examine the employer on them, so the panel treated them as invalid evidence under Article 27 of Prakas 099/04 [Para 31][Para 32]. With no admissible evidence, the employer was held to have proved nothing against the three workers [Para 33], which, combined with the procedural breach, required reinstatement and back compensation [Para 34].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union leader protected as a shop steward cannot be dismissed before the employer obtains prior authorisation from the Labour Inspector, and evidence the employer refuses to disclose to the other party cannot be relied on, so an unauthorised dismissal unsupported by admissible evidence requires reinstatement and compensation for the whole suspension period [Para 27][Para 32][Para 34].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted the parties settled the second issue, on wages and attendance bonus for the strike period, at the hearing and expressly made no decision on it [Para 9][Para 10].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The non-conciliation report is numbered 519 and described as concerning a collective dispute at "Tack Fat Factory" although the case is Makalot [Para 19][Para 1]. The conciliation date is printed as the unfilled placeholder "[date(s)]" [Para 6]. The decision paragraph drops the surname of the vice-president while the order names him in full, and the source gives no compensation figure [Para 34]. OCR has again dropped "th" throughout ("themloyer", "athe") and the Prakas number is printed inconsistently as 305 and 099/04 [Para 27][Para 32]. The order refers to a "suspension" while the facts describe a dismissal, and the panel uses both terms [Para 23][Para 30].
