# Award No. 004/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ (Ken Sport Shoe Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 9 March 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី for the employer, អន ណន for the workers, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140862

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ manufactures footwear at Chomkar Svay village, Setthei commune, Samaki Meanchey district, Kampong Chhnang, and employed 8,905 workers. [Para 46] [Para 1] On 22 January 2021 the workers' representatives filed 12 demands for improved working conditions with the provincial labour department; conciliation on 4 February 2021 settled 7 and left 5 unresolved, which were forwarded to the panel on 5 February 2021. [Para 12] [Para 13] [Para 14] At the hearing on 17 February 2021 the parties settled three further demands — the two requests to remove the heads of administration and of personnel from their posts, and the request for monthly lists of departed workers and for the 10th-of-month union-dues transfer — and agreed that the last two be treated as one, leaving the panel to decide demands 1 and 2. [Para 18] [Para 83] [Para 85] [Para 19] The award is non-binding. [Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must repay annual leave with pay that it wrongly deducted from workers, or pay the value of unused annual leave in money instead of allowing it to be carried forward? [Para 48] [Para 88]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer discriminated against the Khmer Youth Union by allowing members of other unions to film workers during working hours without sanction while withholding overtime from Khmer Youth Union members? [Para 67] [Para 91]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that the employer failed to arrange annual leave with pay, so that leave accumulated; that workers may carry over only 6 days a year for three years, that team and section heads refuse leave requests, and that leave wrongly deducted must therefore be paid back in money; and that the employer discriminated against the Khmer Youth Union, a member of a rival union having filmed workers during working hours with impunity while Khmer Youth Union members were refused overtime. [Para 48] [Para 53] [Para 55] [Para 71] [Para 76]
**Employer (respondent):** that it applies annual leave with pay in accordance with the Labour Law under agreements reached with all the unions; that if any worker's leave was wrongly deducted it will restore those days; that filming during working hours is banned for everyone and offenders were warned twice; and that the panel should not reconsider demand 1 because it was already decided in case No. 063/20, issue 7. [Para 60] [Para 61] [Para 65] [Para 66] [Para 68] [Para 81]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider demand 1 and rejected demand 2, issuing a non-binding award that becomes final if no party objects within 08 days. [Para 105] [Para 106] [Para 107]
On demand 1 the panel recorded what both sides accepted: annual leave with pay is announced and advised, up to 6 days may be saved for the following year and carried for no more than 3 consecutive years, no cash is paid in lieu while employment continues, and days wrongly deducted will be given back as leave. [Para 49] [Para 50] [Para 51] [Para 52] Because the employer conceded repayment at the hearing, the panel found the parties no longer in dispute on the point and declined to consider the claim. [Para 88] [Para 89] [Para 90]
On demand 2 the panel applied Article 12 of the 1997 Labour Law, which forbids an employer from using union membership or union activity as a ground for decisions on hiring, discipline or termination, and Article 62 of the 2016 Law on Trade Unions. [Para 92] [Para 93] It held, consistently with earlier awards it listed, that the worker alleging discrimination carries the burden of proving it, and that it will dismiss the claim where no specific evidence is produced. [Para 94] [Para 96] Turning to the facts, the panel found a standing internal ban on filming workers during working hours, meetings with all 8 unions on enforcing it, and two written and oral warnings already issued to offenders. [Para 68] [Para 70] [Para 81] It examined the evidence filed and found nothing showing that Khmer Youth Union members were treated unequally or that the employer discriminated against that union. [Para 100] [Para 102] The claim therefore failed. [Para 103] [Para 104]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the employer concedes at the hearing the substance of a demand and undertakes to correct any individual error, the dispute on that demand is spent and the panel will decline to decide it rather than issue an order; and an allegation of anti-union discrimination fails unless the claimant identifies comparable conduct that was treated differently, the burden of proof resting on the party alleging the discrimination. [Para 89] [Para 94] [Para 100]

**Obiter dicta:** that the panel endorsed the reasoning of a long line of earlier awards on the burden of proof in discrimination cases, and that the employer's invitation to treat demand 1 as already decided in case No. 063/20 was left unanswered. [Para 95] [Para 66]
