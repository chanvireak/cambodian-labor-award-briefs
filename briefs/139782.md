# Award No. 339/14 — Nouv Khob (Arbitration Council, 11 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; Tun Si Phan, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chea, chair) · Doc ID 139782

## 1. Facts
Four demands survived conciliation in a collective dispute at Nouv Khob, a company in Ang Snuol district, Kandal: a housing or transport allowance, a midday meal, a nursery and breastfeeding room with milk money, and adequate dining tables. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8] The workers struck from 1 December 2014; the Kandal provincial department of labour settled 8 of 12 points and sent the remaining 4 to the Council on 3 December 2014. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16] On 5 December 2014 the panel ordered the strike suspended and every worker back at her post from the morning of 6 December 2014; the workers stayed out. [Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay 15 dollars a month as a housing allowance or transport cost for the workers? [Para 2]
2. Whether the factory must provide one midday meal on every working day, or instead pay 26 dollars a month? [Para 4]
3. Whether the factory must set up a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) and a breastfeeding room for women workers and pay 15 dollars in milk money? [Para 6]
4. Whether the factory must arrange dining tables fit for the workers to eat at? [Para 8]

Before reaching any of them the panel put a threshold question to itself: whether it should continue the arbitration at all while the strike was still running? [Para 37]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** pressed all four demands as filed, and rejected the company's proposal to keep its old nursery practice; the company could meet only 12 of the 15 dollars sought and said it had no capacity for the meal. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 7] No representative attended the pre-hearing of 9 December 2014, though the union president told the secretariat by telephone that the strike was continuing. [Para 24][Para 26]

**Employer (respondent):** offered 12 dollars a month instead of 15, said it had no means to provide lunch, and asked to keep its existing arrangements for the nursery and feeding room. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7] It attended the pre-hearing, having earlier written on 3 December 2014 to ask the Council for an order suspending the strike that had begun on 1 December. [Para 19][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 339/14 without deciding any of the four demands, and ordered nothing in riel or dollars. [Para 57][Para 58]

The threshold question was decided on Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, Article 20 of which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure during the panel's proceedings — strike, lockout (ឡុកអៅ) under Article 318 of the Labour Law, or anything else that aggravates the dispute — and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 37][Para 38] The panel read the rule as a bar on proceeding, and pointed to its own earlier orders holding that it will not run an arbitration while workers are striking or an employer has locked out. [Para 39][Para 40] It followed, among others, award No. 110/11 (Simano), where arbitration was closed for exactly that reason, and a 2004 notice in case No. 04/04 treating the right to strike as unavailable for the duration of the proceedings. [Para 42][Para 47]

On the facts, the panel found that its order No. 063/14 of 5 December 2014 had required the strike to stop and all workers to return by the morning of 6 December 2014, that the strike nonetheless continued, and that the union president confirmed as much by telephone on 9 December 2014 while the workers' side stayed away from the pre-hearing. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 26][Para 56] That was a breach of Article 20 and of the order, so the panel terminated the proceedings instead of hearing the demands. [Para 57][Para 58]

Because no hearing on the merits was ever held, the panel made no findings of fact on the four demands. [Para 36]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party keeps a strike or lockout running after the panel has ordered it suspended for the duration of the arbitration, the panel will terminate the proceedings rather than decide the merits, and it may take the dispute up again only once the strike has stopped. [Para 40][Para 43][Para 57]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that it could make no findings of fact on the disputed issues at all, precisely because it never held a hearing on them — a remark that explains why the four demands went undecided. [Para 36] It also noted that the company's request for a suspension order had been granted on 5 December 2014. [Para 19][Para 20]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The year of order No. 063/14 is printed twice at [Para 20] — "ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣ ២០១៤" — and the same order is dated 5 December 2014 everywhere else. [Para 32] The pre-hearing date is likewise doubled at [Para 49], which reads "ថ្ងៃទី ២៤ ខែកញ្ញា ០៩ ខែធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៤" (24 September and 09 December); the rest of the text, and the telephone call of 9 December, show that 9 December is meant. [Para 26] The employer's prefix is fused to its name in the header and body ("ក្រុមហ៊ុនូវ ខប", elsewhere "នូវ ខប"), and the demand list at [Para 2] runs into the Council's letterhead and address block. [Para 1][Para 58] The list of earlier orders at [Para 41] carries OCR losses (for example "លេខ ១៣៧/០៩ &១៣៨/០៩"). The conciliation report is numbered 1797/14 throughout but with two different suffix codes (កប/កលា at [Para 11], ក.ប/ក.ណ at [Para 17]). The signature block at [Para 59] survives only as noise ("197 @,@ 2016 42 / muzzzone"), so the arbitrators' signatures are lost though their names appear in the header.
