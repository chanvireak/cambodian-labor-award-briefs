# Award No. 301/14 — Sing Tai Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 28 October 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 139751

## 1. Facts
Sing Tai Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, on Street 1019, Sangkat Tuek Thla, Khan Sen Sok, faced a collective dispute lodged on 13 October 2014 by five federations over 34 or 35 working conditions. [Para 1][Para 38] Conciliation failed on 17 October 2014 and the file reached the Council on 21 October; the workers struck, the employer asked for a suspension order, and on 24 October 2014 the Council issued Order No. 056/14 requiring the strike to stop and all strikers to return from the morning of 25 October. [Para 39][Para 43] The workers did not return and remained on strike through 27 October. [Para 44][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel must terminate the arbitration proceedings, without deciding the 34 claims, because the workers continued to strike in breach of Order No. 056/14 during the proceedings? [Para 53]
2. Whether the panel can make any finding of fact or award on those claims at all, given that no hearing was held? [Para 52]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the five federations, acting through a lawyer under a written transfer of rights dated 22 October 2014, pressed the 34 claims for improved conditions — reinstatement of the union's vice-president, a 2,000 riel hourly overtime meal allowance, 4,000 riel daily lunch money, unpaid 5% severance, and improved toilets, lighting, fans and medical arrangements. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 47] They confirmed at 2:30 pm on 27 October that they would continue the strike. [Para 45]
**Employer (respondent):** the company asked the Council on 22 October 2014 to order the strike stopped, and relied on that order; it made no submission on the merits. [Para 42][Para 46]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction to continue — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case 301/14 and made no order on any of the 34 claims or on any sum of money. [Para 64][Para 65]

The panel framed the only question it could reach as whether to continue at all. [Para 53] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council requires parties, while the panel is seised, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and to attend all meetings convened. [Para 54][Para 55] Earlier awards applied that rule by refusing to continue arbitration while a strike or lockout was under way, and by closing files where workers kept striking during proceedings — Awards 04/04 (IMSI), 28/06 (GDM) and 34/06 (Gold Fame), and later 97/07, 63/08, 110/11 (Simano) and 17/13 (Internal Fashion Royal). [Para 56][Para 57][Para 59][Para 60] Order No. 056/14 of 24 October 2014 had directed an immediate suspension of the strike until the arbitration was complete and a return to work by shifts from 25 October 2014. [Para 43] The panel found the workers had no intention of submitting the dispute to arbitration, since they kept striking and ignored the order even after the committee for the settlement of strikes and demonstrations intervened on the morning of 27 October. [Para 62][Para 63] Because no hearing was held, the panel recorded that it made no finding of fact on the reported issues. [Para 52]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Once a panel has ordered a strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, a workforce that keeps striking during the proceedings shows it does not intend to arbitrate, and the panel will terminate the proceedings without any finding on the merits; the same dispute may be brought again only after every union has stopped striking. [Para 58][Para 63][Para 64]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted it could take up the collective dispute afresh at any time once the unions ceased striking, which keeps the file revivable rather than dismissed on the merits. [Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claim count is printed as 35 points at the referral and 34 at the transfer to the Council. [Para 38][Para 40] The Prakas 099 citation is corrupted at the operative reasoning ("ប្រកាស លេខ្ជី ១៩"). [Para 64] Prior-award numbers are partly illegible ("៥៩០៦-សាង", "១៣៧០៩"). [Para 57] The award lists a company address and a union address as identical, and gives no riel or dollar total because nothing was ordered. [Para 1] Individual representatives are left unnamed here although the award prints their names.
