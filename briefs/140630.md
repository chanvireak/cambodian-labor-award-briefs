# Award No. 81/07 — Supreme Garments Pty Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 September 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Kong Phallack chairing) · Doc ID 140630

## 1. Facts
Supreme Garments Pty Ltd employs about 945 workers at Doeum Mean Village, Kandal Province [Para 23][Para 1]. Three unions — the Cambodian Labour Union (CLU), the Union of Solidarity and the Cambodian Union — brought a collective dispute of 29 demands; 20 were conciliated, and of the nine reported, one was settled at the hearing, three were withdrawn and one was already conciliated, leaving Issues 1, 6, 7 and 8 [Para 17][Para 20][Para 21]. None of the unions holds most representative status, while together they claimed 641 members, 67.83 percent of the workforce [Para 24][Para 78]. The surviving demands were annual leave around Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben, permission to listen to the radio at work, no wage deduction for under three days' leave a month, and a canteen [Para 27][Para 37][Para 44][Para 47].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must allow workers to take annual leave two days before and after Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben? [Para 27][Para 57]
2. (Issue 6) Whether the company must allow workers to listen to the radio at the workplace during working hours? [Para 37][Para 95]
3. (Issue 7) Whether the company must refrain from deducting wages when workers take fewer than three days' leave a month, other than special leave and certified sick leave? [Para 44][Para 108]
4. (Issue 8) Whether the company must build a proper canteen for the workers? [Para 47][Para 116]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — CLU, Union of Solidarity and Cambodian Union):** that Article 170 of the Labour Law let them take leave around festivals, their homes being far and travel costly; that freedom of information under the Constitution justified listening to the radio, which taught them new things, or alternatively that the company could play one radio for two hours; that special and certified sick leave were outside their demand, which covered family obligations and ceremonies; and that 60 to 70 percent of workers ate on factory premises without shelter [Para 27][Para 28][Para 40][Para 45][Para 48].
**Employer (respondent — Supreme Garments Pty Ltd):** that festive periods were peak production when goods had to be delivered to buyers; that radio would break concentration, drown out team leaders and create danger; that the law did not require payment for authorised absences, and its Internal Work Rules at Clauses 5(D) and 6(A) withheld wages for any leave; and that there was no space for a canteen [Para 29][Para 38][Para 46][Para 49][Para 84].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must allow workers with a year or more of service annual leave two days before and after Khmer New Year and one day before and after Pchum Ben, and must build a proper canteen under its Internal Work Rules; the radio demand and the no-deduction demand are rejected [Para 123].
On standing, the panel noted that an interests dispute may normally be brought only by a union with most representative status under Article 277, since such an award replaces the collective agreement for a year under Clause 43 of Prakas 099/04, citing Awards 57/04 Evergreen, 60/04 United Art (Issue 3), 08/07 Siu Quinh (Issue 3), 33/07 Gold Fame (Issue 2) and 51/07 Gold Fame (Issue 4) [Para 66][Para 67][Para 69]. It then applied Clause 9(2) of Prakas 305 dated 22 November 2001, allowing unions without that status to unite where their members are a majority, and found 641 of 945 workers sufficient standing for the three unions jointly [Para 70][Para 72][Para 73][Para 79][Para 80].
On Issue 1 the panel adopted Award 21/05 Sinomax (Issue 2) on Article 170: annual leave is normally given at Khmer New Year, the employer may determine timing and refuse leave in the first year of service, but must notify the Labour Inspector of any arrangement [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53][Para 55]. Because the company refused leave at Khmer New Year altogether, that practice was invalid, and leave of two days either side was ordered [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]. Pchum Ben is not mentioned in Article 170, making that part an interests demand, but being an equally important traditional festival it was granted in equity at one day either side [Para 60][Para 62][Para 86][Para 88].
On Issue 6 the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, the Constitution or the Internal Work Rules granting a right to listen to the radio during working hours, freedom of information not extending to working time, and followed Award 94/04 Eternity (Issue 6) in treating the request as an interests demand [Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 94]. Weighing knowledge gained against concentration, safety and the employer's duty of care, it found the employer's position more reasonable and rejected the demand [Para 96][Para 98][Para 102].
On Issue 7, Articles 71(6) and 72 make authorised absence a suspension of the contract, so no wage is owed absent a contrary provision, and Clauses 5(D) and 6(A) of the Internal Work Rules provide the opposite of payment [Para 102][Para 103][Para 104][Para 106]. The demand was an interests one, and the workers supplied neither evidence nor sufficient reasons, so it was rejected as a proper application of the rules [Para 107][Para 109][Para 112][Para 113].
On Issue 8, no law requires a canteen, following Award 24/04 South Bay (Issue 1), but Clause 8(C) obliges the company to provide designated eating places; since the rules were made under Article 24 and vised by the Labour Inspector, they bind the employer, and lack of space is no excuse, unlike the siting question in South Bay or the timing question in Award 35/04 Jacqsitex [Para 116][Para 117][Para 120][Para 121][Para 122].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where no union holds most representative status, several unions whose combined membership is a majority of the workforce may jointly bring an interests dispute, and the panel will decide it on equity [Para 73][Para 80]. On equity an award may exceed the strict text of Article 170: leave is granted for a festival the article does not name, at a reduced duration, while a demand that the law does not support at all and that the workers cannot evidence is rejected [Para 88][Para 109].

**Obiter dicta:** that a failure to deliver goods would cost the buyers' trust and ultimately the workers' jobs, and that the workplace is not a place to receive information [Para 85][Para 98].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The decision labels the radio issue "Issue 2", although the non-conciliation report and the reasons call it Issue 6, which the brief follows [Para 37][Para 123]. The employer is called Supreme Garments Pty Ltd in the header but Supreme Garment Company throughout the reasons, and the award date of 20 September 2007 postdates a hearing held on 29 August 2007 with the parties' agreed extension recorded [Para 1][Para 23][Para 27]. Issue 5 concerned a named union secretary's contract renewal and unpaid wages; the individual is not reproduced here [Para 7]. OCR has dropped the letter "t" extensively and one demand is garbled by a duplicated clause [Para 27][Para 54].
