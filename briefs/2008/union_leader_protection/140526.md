# Award No. 74/08 — Jenner e Sin International Signal Co Ltd (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 7 July 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-appointed), គង់ ជល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140526

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេនើរេ សិន អ៉ិន ធើណា សិនណល ខូអិលធីឌី, runs a factory of about 2,500 workers at Trapeang Kong, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu. The workers' side, សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ ជេនើរេ សិន, struck on 20 May 2008 over 14 demands [Para 20][Para 54]. Conciliation at the Kampong Speu labour department on 21 May failed on all 14; the panel's own conciliation settled 12 and left 2 — issues 1 and 13 — for decision [Para 21][Para 27][Para 28]. The union had held its leadership election on 20 April 2008 and given the company the result on 23 April; on 24 April the company dismissed the four elected officers and applied to the labour department that same day for permission [Para 174][Para 65].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must take back the four elected union leaders it dismissed on 24 April 2008, they being protected persons under Prakas 305? [Para 69][Para 154]
2. (Issue 13) Whether overtime pay must be calculated on the base wage plus the skill or position allowance, in the same way the company calculates deductions for absence? [Para 142][Para 203]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the four officers were elected on 20 April 2008, the company was notified on 23 April, and they were dismissed the next day; under Prakas 305 candidates enjoy protection from 45 days before to 45 days after the election, and under Article 293 of the Labour Law a protected worker cannot be dismissed without the labour inspector's and the Minister's approval [Para 139][Para 174][Para 138]. The company's dismissal for "serious misconduct" was disproportionate and discriminatory. On issue 13, the company deducts absence using base wage plus skill allowance but pays overtime on the base wage alone, which the union called unjust [Para 144][Para 148].

**Employer (respondent):** the dismissals had nothing to do with the union and rested on serious misconduct under Article 83 and the internal rules — collecting money during working hours, refusing a transfer from the water line to the cutting table, leaving early, playing games on a phone, and threatening the general manager [Para 64][Para 90][Para 100]. It also denied receiving the 23 April notice [Para 175]. On issue 13 it argued the skill payment is a discretionary support allowance and need not enter the overtime rate [Para 150].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — both of the two issues left for decision were resolved for the workers: the company must take the four officers back from 24 April 2008 and hold them in post pending the Minister's decision on the dismissal, and must pay overtime on the base wage plus skill or position allowance [Para 241][Para 242].

On issue 1 the panel applied Prakas 305 Articles 3(3) and 4 with Article 293 of the Labour Law. It took the three conditions from Awards 50/05, 64/05, 07/06, 09/06 and 148/07: the worker must fall within Prakas 305, the dismissal must fall inside the protected period, and the union must have notified the employer of the protected names [Para 154][Para 162][Para 164]. All three were satisfied — the officers were election candidates (protection 45 days either side) and the fourth was a founder protected for 30 days after registration; the dismissal fell within the window; and although the company refused the 23 April notice, the panel held that a refusal to accept a notice cannot defeat knowledge, since the company itself applied to the labour department for permission to dismiss exactly these four [Para 166][Para 174][Para 181][Para 182]. Article 293 then required the labour inspector's decision within one month and allowed the employer two months to appeal to the Minister; the department never replied, so refusal was deemed from 24 May 2008, and the company dismissed before any ministerial decision [Para 192][Para 197][Para 199]. The dismissal was therefore unlawful and reinstatement followed from the date of dismissal [Para 200][Para 201].

On issue 13 the panel found neither new Article 139 nor Prakas 80 Article 5 defines "wages during normal working time", consulted the Chuon Nath dictionary on "normal", and held that a skill or position allowance paid every month regardless of monthly output is part of normal wages, because the worker also deploys that skill during overtime [Para 204][Para 208][Para 214][Para 215]. The company's use of base plus skill to deduct absence but base alone for overtime was held contrary to equity [Para 221]. The panel also held the unregistered union could represent only the four named complainants, no written mandates from other members having been produced by the 16 June 2008 deadline [Para 232][Para 235][Para 240].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer deducts absence from a worker's base wage plus skill allowance, it must compute overtime on the same combined figure, because a monthly skill allowance paid irrespective of output forms part of the wage for normal working time and the worker continues to exercise that skill during overtime hours.

**Obiter dicta:** an employer's refusal to accept a union's notice of elected candidates does not prevent it from knowing their identity where its own conduct — here, a request to the labour department to dismiss those very four — shows that knowledge [Para 179][Para 182].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The president's name is printed as គង់ ជល្លៈ on the face of the award and as គង់ ផល្លៈ in the signature block, and the employer-appointed arbitrator's name is corrupted in the signature block. The company's name appears in at least four different spellings across the award, and the factory union is written with a different name order in the party heading and in the findings. The election date is given as 20 April 2008 in the findings but as 20 December April 2008 where the panel applies the protection window. Prakas 305 carries a stray extra digit in the year of one citation. Worker names are printed inconsistently, including a variant of the fourth officer's name in the reasoning, so the brief names them by office.
