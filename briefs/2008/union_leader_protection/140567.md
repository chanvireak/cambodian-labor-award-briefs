# Award No. 145/08 — Phan Tes Sa Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផាន ថេស សា ហ្គាមេន) and the Democratic Samaki Workers' Union (Arbitration Council, 31 December 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, An Virak, Kong Phallack chair) · Doc ID 140567

## 1. Facts
Phan Tes Sa Garment operates at Mal village, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, with close to 4,500 workers [Para 44]. On 24 October 2008 the Department of Labour Disputes received a complaint from the Democratic Samaki Workers' Union at the factory demanding better working conditions in eight points: retention of a USD 50 base wage and a USD 90 role allowance for one official, two volleyball courts, opening of the toilets at gates one and two, two hours a week for union meetings, a notice board, display of the union structure, proper language from Khmer team leaders, and a union representative at the monthly meeting [Para 5][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]. Conciliation failed on 6 November 2008 and non-conciliation report No. 1264 of 24 November 2008 came to the Secretariat the same day [Para 16][Para 22]. The panel never opened the merits hearing and made no findings on the eight demands; the award records that there are no such findings [Para 60].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Conditions 1–3, Prakas No. 099 Article 21) Whether the union was a lawful claimant, properly notified and absent without valid reason, so that the file could be closed? [Para 62][Para 66][Para 69]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Democratic Samaki Workers' Union at Phan Tes Sa Garment (សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ សំណាង កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ ផាន ថេស សា):** the union leadership and the factory's worker delegates maintained all eight demands [Para 4]. The union asserted it had elected a new leadership on 21 October 2008 and produced a letter recognising that leadership, but it never supplied the election records, its statutes or the workers' letters of authorisation [Para 52][Para 28].

**Employer (respondent), Phan Tes Sa Garment Company (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផាន ថេស សា):** the company said it was keeping the USD 50 base wage and a USD 15 role allowance for the mechanic, but that the USD 90 was an incentive bonus paid according to actual work, not a fixed entitlement [Para 6].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel held the complaint abandoned and closed Case No. 145/08 [Para 71][Para 73][Para 74].

The panel applied Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Article 21 of the same Prakas, which allow it either to continue in a party's absence or to terminate by issuing an award, and followed the three-condition test set in Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-IM & W 3, 95/08-Yong Va, 132/08-J H J and 138/08-Malin [Para 62][Para 64][Para 66][Para 67]. It found notification properly given, absence at the third hearing, and no reason offered, so all three conditions were met [Para 69][Para 70].

Its ground for closing the file, however, was standing. At the first and second hearings it could not proceed because the union had produced no letter authorising it to act for the workers, although the panel twice adjourned to let it file the election record, the union statutes, the letters of authorisation and the recognition letter [Para 25][Para 27][Para 28]. The facts recorded that the union had registered on 21 July 2008 with three office-holders, all of whom had left, and that the new leadership elected on 21 October 2008 was never notified to the employer [Para 46][Para 49][Para 52][Para 53]. At the third hearing on 22 December 2008 the employer attended and the claimant party did not [Para 31][Para 32]. The panel concluded that the union had never established its capacity as a lawful claimant, had forgone the right to act as claimant, and had not shown good faith in bringing the dispute [Para 61][Para 68][Para 71][Para 72].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that cannot produce its election records, its statutes and written authorisation from the workers it claims to represent is not a lawful claimant before the panel; where the panel has granted adjournments to cure that defect and the party then fails to appear without valid reason, the complaint is treated as abandoned and the file is closed under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules [Para 68][Para 70][Para 71][Para 73].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the union's conduct did not display genuine good faith in submitting the dispute to arbitration, and it made no observation at all on the merits of the eight demands [Para 60][Para 72].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The first two lines of the file are destroyed OCR, reduced to strings of digits and stray Latin characters, so nothing from the letterhead survives [Para 1][Para 2]. The award itself records that no findings were made on the substance because the merits hearing was never opened, which is why section 4 above reports only the procedural order [Para 60]. The factory name appears both as "ផាន ថេស សា" and, in the operative paragraph, as a run-together "ផានថេសសា" [Para 73][Para 74]. In the facts the names of the three former union office-holders and of the newly recognised leadership are partly garbled, and the individual worker whose allowance is claimed is reduced here to his role [Para 5][Para 46][Para 59]. The signature block is damaged, with one arbitrator's signature replaced by a digit string [Para 74].
