# Award No. 04/08 — Cambodia Apparel Enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ឹប ភែរ៉េល អ៉ឹន ដឺស ទ្រី) (Arbitration Council, 25 January 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (chair) · Doc ID 140477

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ឹប ភែរ៉េល អ៉ឹន ដឺស ទ្រី (Cambodia Apparel Enterprise), a garment factory on National Road 4 in Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu, employs 675 workers. [Para 1][Para 24] After a strike over working conditions, the Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) put seventeen demands, headed by the readmission of three union leaders dismissed on 28 December 2007 and including action against an administrative chief said to have taken USD 30 from each recruit plus a USD 10 card fee. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 9] Conciliation on 4 January failed because the company did not attend and the union would not proceed without it; the file was referred the same day and the panel convened for a pre-hearing meeting on 23 January. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 32] The union declined to attend that meeting, citing the cost of travelling from Kampong Speu, said it had filed the dispute with the provincial court, and asserted that the panel had no power to order the company to readmit the workers. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 55] The workers were still on strike on 24 January. [Para 49]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration where the workers remain on strike, and the employer has locked out, throughout the proceedings? [Para 58][Para 59]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the claimant union's refusal to attend the pre-hearing meeting, its filing of the same dispute in the provincial court and its disclaimer of the panel's authority show no genuine intention to have the dispute arbitrated? [Para 43][Para 55][Para 63]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត, through the Khmer Youth Union Federation, maintained all seventeen demands in the workers' favour and stayed on strike, refusing to appear before the panel on the ground that the journey from Kampong Speu to Phnom Penh was too long and costly, that the matter was already before the provincial court, and that the panel could not order the company to let the workers in. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ឹប ភែរ៉េល, through its administration head, attended the pre-hearing meeting; the company had also failed to appear at the conciliation of 4 January. [Para 29][Para 41][Para 46]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case 04/08 and closed the file, leaving all seventeen referred demands undecided. [Para 66][Para 67]
The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which obliges the parties to suspend every dispute measure such as strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law while the panel is functioning, and to attend all meetings it convenes. [Para 51][Para 52] It read the article, consistently with Award No. 04/04 (IMS), as meaning that the panel cannot resolve a collective dispute or issue an award while a strike continues, and may resume only once the strike ends. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]
It then noted its consistent practice of closing files where a party maintained a strike or lockout through the proceedings, citing Nos. 28/06 (GDM), 34/06 (Goldfame) and 22/07 (Park Yu). [Para 62][Para 64] It observed that the same parties' earlier case, No. 02/08, had been closed on 15 January for the same reason, that some of the issues here were identical to those in that file, and that the union had attended the pre-hearing there but not here. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 44][Para 56]
On the facts the panel found that the claimant had shown no genuine intention to have the dispute settled by arbitration: the strike continued, the union stayed away from the 23 January meeting despite telephone reminders, and it had taken the same dispute to the provincial court. [Para 47][Para 57][Para 63] It recorded that no findings of fact could be made on the referred issues because the hearing could not proceed. [Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate a collective dispute, and will close the file without deciding the referred demands, where one party maintains a strike or lockout throughout the proceedings or withholds the cooperation Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires, since the panel cannot make an award in that state and the parties' conduct shows no genuine intention to arbitrate. [Para 52][Para 59][Para 63][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the claimant had attended the pre-hearing meeting in the parties' earlier case, a comparison it used only to reinforce its finding of intent. [Para 44][Para 56]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case number is printed as 04/08 in the header and operative paragraph but as "04/07" in the reasoning, and the company name appears in five variants including a shortened "ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល" and a garbled "ខេមបូឌា អ៊ឹប តែ រ៉េល"; the district is given twice as "សំរោងទង" and once as "សំខាន់ រោង ទង". [Para 1][Para 24][Para 51][Para 67] The number of referred points is 17 in the reasons but the conciliation report is described as covering seventeen demands; the party list records the workers' representative as absent. [Para 1][Para 33] Paragraph 66 contains OCR debris ("NA, 1990 / 2016, 6") in place of cited text. [Para 66] The three dismissed union leaders and other individuals are named in the source; this brief refers to them by role only.
