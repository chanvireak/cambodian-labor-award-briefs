# Award No. 146/08 — I-Land Glory International (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាយ លែន គូរី អ៉ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល) and the Khmer Youth Union Federation at I-Land Glory Factory (Arbitration Council, 18 December 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, Liv Sovanna, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 140570

## 1. Facts
I-Land Glory International operates at Tuol Sangkae, Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, and registered in 2005 [Para 1][Para 18]. On 17 November 2008 the Department of Labour Disputes received a telephone complaint from the factory's Khmer youth union asking for better conditions; conciliation on 18 November 2008 left two of the three demands unresolved, and non-conciliation report No. 1286 of 26 November 2008 reached the Secretariat the same day [Para 8][Para 11][Para 12]. The two demands were that three-month fixed-duration contracts be changed to six-month contracts, and that a named worker be taken back with his wages and bonus preserved until he returned to work [Para 2][Para 4]. The hearing was fixed for 4 December 2008 at 14:30 and the claimant party did not attend [Para 10].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must convert three-month fixed-duration contracts into six-month fixed-duration contracts? [Para 2]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must reinstate the dismissed worker and keep paying his wages and bonus until he returns? [Para 4]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Khmer Youth Union Federation at I-Land Glory Factory (សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រ អាយ លែន គ្លូរី):** the union demanded that the company lengthen the three-month contracts to six months and reinstate the worker with his wages and bonus intact [Para 2][Para 4]. It filed no documents with the panel and sent no representative to the hearing [Para 26].

**Employer (respondent), I-Land Glory International:** the company asked the panel simply to apply the Labour Law, that is, to keep its existing practice on contract terms, and said it could neither take the worker back nor keep his wages and bonus [Para 3][Para 5][Para 16].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel held the complaint abandoned and closed Case No. 146/08 [Para 61][Para 63][Para 64].

The panel applied Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Article 21 of the same Prakas, following the three-condition test stated in Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-IM & W 3, 95/08-Yong Va, 132/08-J H J and 138/08-Malin [Para 30][Para 32][Para 33]. On condition 1 it found the party properly notified: the Secretariat asked the union to collect the summons, the union did not come for it, and the Secretariat telephoned a reminder on the hearing day, so notice of date, time and place was proved [Para 35][Para 37][Para 38].

On condition 2 it read "appearance" in Article 21 as requiring both presence and participation through all four stages of the proceedings — introduction, explanation of the procedure and issues, conciliation, and the hearing — and found the union attended none of them [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 43]. On condition 3 it found no valid reason: told by telephone that the union was busy, the Secretariat offered to receive another union leader instead, and the union refused; when advised to file a written request for postponement it did not do so, although it had earlier expressed willingness to have the hearing adjourned [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 48][Para 52]. All three conditions being met, the panel held it had power to issue an award, then treated the claimant's non-participation as abandonment of the complaint and closed the file without reaching the merits [Para 54][Para 55][Para 61].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant party properly summoned neither collects the summons nor attends any stage of the proceedings, refuses the panel's offer to hear another of its leaders, and declines to file the written postponement request it is invited to make, the panel may treat the complaint as abandoned and close the file under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules, without deciding the demands on their merits [Para 45][Para 48][Para 52][Para 63].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that a party which has already received notice of the hearing ought not to need the Secretariat to telephone and remind it of the time, and that the union's conduct did not show genuine good faith in bringing the dispute for resolution [Para 50][Para 62].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The letterhead and first line are destroyed OCR, reduced to stray Latin words, "<unk>" tokens and strings of @-@ characters, so nothing of the original heading survives [Para 1]. The findings section is printed as a single word meaning "none", so the award records no findings of fact at all and the two demands are known only from the non-conciliation report [Para 30]. The company name is printed in at least four different Khmer spellings across the file, alternating between "គូរី", "គ្លូរី", "ធ្នូរី" and "ផ្លូរី" [Para 1][Para 18][Para 21][Para 56]. The reasoning is organised by the three conditions of Article 21 rather than by the two numbered issues, so section 4 above cross-refers. Individual officers and representatives are reduced to initials and roles, and one arbitrator's signature line carries an English OCR fragment [Para 65].
