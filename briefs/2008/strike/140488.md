# Award No. 17/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីន ណឺ ណឹត ធីង (Vin Neu Neat Thing; no Latin company name appears in the source) (Arbitration Council, 20 February 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សុខ ម៉ាធឿង chair; មាំរ សំបូរ ណា, employer nominee; អន ណន, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140488

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីន ណឺ ណឹត ធីង, on National Road 21, Village 4, Svay Rolum commune, Sa'ang district, Kandal, faces the union federation សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ (C.CAWDU), whose official appeared for the workers. [Para 1][Para 2] On 8 May 2007 the company and the Khmer Youth Union Federation signed minutes agreeing to pay 500 USD to male workers who had worked five years and wished to resign, and the company says the agreement was made to end a strike and was never applied because no worker resigned. [Para 29][Para 31][Para 32] Conciliation on 16 January 2008 left one of the two demands unsettled, and the non-conciliation report No. 012/08 K.B/KN reached the Secretariat on 30 January 2008; the hearing was held on 13 February 2008. [Para 6][Para 9][Para 8] The single demand was that the company carry out the agreement on the 500 USD payment, which it had applied in the past. [Para 2]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must implement the agreement to pay 500 USD to workers who resign from their jobs, as it had done before? [Para 2][Para 38]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ, through its official, asked the company to honour the agreement under which 500 USD was paid to workers who resigned, and said that some workers wished to resign and had asked whether they would receive that sum as the minutes provided. [Para 2][Para 34]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីន ណឺ ណឹត ធីង, through its administration staff and its representative from the garment manufacturers' association, refused, saying the agreement had long since been set aside because the worker party did not respect it, that no worker had resigned so it had never applied, that no worker was currently resigning, and that the law on labour would be applied instead. [Para 1][Para 31][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the demand at all, holding that it raised a dispute that had not yet arisen. [Para 42][Para 45][Para 46]
The panel began from the evidence it would need in order to rule on the demand: the names of the workers claiming the money, the type of contract each held, how long each had served, and whether each was male or female. [Para 39] It found that the union official supplied none of this. He named no worker who had resigned and claimed the 500 USD, and the panel characterised the demand as one made on behalf of workers who might resign in the future, not of anyone who had already done so. [Para 39] The panel drew the consequence explicitly: there was as yet no dispute between employer and worker over the 500 USD. [Para 40]
It then followed its own earlier interpretation in Case No. 10/03, whose party name is garbled in the OCR, that the Arbitration Council was created to settle labour disputes and not to resolve problems that have not yet arisen, because nobody can predict what will happen, where, when, or which workers will be affected. [Para 42][Para 43] Applying that to the facts, the panel said it could not know how many workers would resign, and could not determine in advance whether a given worker would meet the conditions the agreement set for the payment. [Para 44]
It therefore declined to consider the demand, and the operative order refuses to examine the claim by សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ for the 500 USD payment to workers who resign. [Para 45][Para 46] The award is non-binding and becomes binding eight days after notification unless a party lodges a written opposition with the Minister of Labour. [Para 47]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The Arbitration Council is created to settle labour disputes that exist, not to resolve problems that have not yet arisen: a demand that depends on resignations by unidentified workers, whose contracts, service and eligibility are unknown, raises a future dispute over which the Council will not assume jurisdiction. [Para 40][Para 42][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the 500 USD agreement had been concluded in order to stop a strike and that the company had treated it as abandoned, facts it recorded without deciding whether the agreement remained binding. [Para 31][Para 32]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The file is Khmer only and gives no Latin rendering of the company, which appears as "វីន ណឺ ណឹត ធីង" in the title, "វីន ណឹ ណីត ធីង" in the evidence list and "វីន ណឹ ណឺត ធីង" in the report's title, so the spelling cannot be settled from the text. [Para 1][Para 14][Para 19] The 2007 agreement on the 500 USD was signed with the Khmer Youth Union Federation, not with the claimant federation សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ, and the award never addresses that mismatch. [Para 18][Para 29] The procedural section is partly destroyed: the number of demands conciliated is given as two with one unsettled, "០២ ចំណុច" and "០១ ចំណុច", while the rest of the case concerns a single point, and a stray figure "977" stands alone before the procedural heading. [Para 9] Paragraphs 22 to 27 are lost to the OCR and the operative order is a run of fragments ("សម្រេច និងបង្គាប់ ដែលទាមទារ... ៦ \" រាជ ៦៤ ប្រភេទ"), so the order's wording is reconstructed from the reasons. [Para 46] The employer's representatives, the union official and the four unions named in the reasons appear in full in the source and are described here by role or federation only.
