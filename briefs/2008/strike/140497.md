# Award No. 39/08-Zhong Yov — Zhong Yov Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 March 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, employer-chosen; Liv Sovanna, worker-chosen; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 140497

## 1. Facts
Zhong Yov Co., Ltd, at Phum Prey Rorka, Khum Chorkcheuneang, Srok Angsnoul, Kandal Province, employs 650 workers; the worker party is the Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (KTFYU) [Para 14][Para 1]. Nine points of collective dispute were assigned to a provincial conciliation officer on 17 March 2008 and none could be conciliated, after which both parties agreed not to negotiate the remaining points 2 to 9 [Para 14][Para 16]. The only demand actually articulated was point 1: that the employer provide full wages and perquisites when it has no work for the workers to do, the employer offering half [Para 10][Para 11]. The employer reported a strike on 19 March 2008 and asked for an interim order [Para 16]; the panel ordered the workers back to work from 20 March 2008, and on 23 and again on 25 March 2008 the Secretariat was notified that the strike continued [Para 19][Para 20][Para 26]. The merits of point 1 were never reached, and no arbitration hearing was held, so there is no fact-finding on the non-conciliation points [Para 28].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the Council will proceed to settle the workers' demand for full wages and perquisites while the workers continue to strike, contrary to the panel's interim order to return to work? [Para 10][Para 31]

Points 2 to 9 of the non-conciliation report were never before the panel, the parties having agreed not to negotiate them [Para 16].

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union, KTFYU, for the 650 workers of Zhong Yov Co., Ltd):** the employer must provide full wages and perquisites while it has no work for them; they stated at the pre-hearing meeting that they would not return to work and would continue the strike [Para 10][Para 22].
**Employer (Zhong Yov Co., Ltd):** it does not accept the demand and pays only half wages and perquisites; it said the company had opened its doors for the workers to return, and they had not [Para 11][Para 23].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the Council discontinued the arbitration process in case 39/08-Zhong Yov, leaving point 1 undecided, and the dispute may be settled again once the strike stops [Para 33][Para 32].
The panel applied Clause 20 of Prakas 99 dated 21 April 2004, which requires the parties during the arbitration process to abstain from strikes or lockouts within the meaning of Article 318 of the Labour Law and to attend all meetings the panel calls [Para 29][Para 30]. It read that clause as barring it from continuing the process where the worker party is on strike or the employer has locked out, and followed earlier awards to that effect: Case 04/04-MSI, where the Council held it could not settle a dispute referred to it while the parties had no right to strike, and Interim Order 2 of 24 December 2005 in Case 77/05-Franco Knitting Garment [Para 31][Para 32]. It also relied on Awards 28/06-GDM and 34/06-Goldfame, which were closed when the worker party kept striking (as recited in Arbitral Award 97/07-Suit Way) [Para 33]. On the evidence, the panel found the workers showed no intent to settle at the Council, having continued to strike and ignored the interim order of 19 March 2008 and the subsequent request to return, notified to it by both parties on 25 March 2008 [Para 19][Para 26][Para 33]. It therefore discontinued, and recorded that the Council may settle the collective dispute again when the workers stop the strike [Para 32][Para 33].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the worker party continues a strike after the panel has issued an interim order to return to work, the Council will not hear the merits and will discontinue the arbitration process, the dispute becoming capable of settlement again only once the strike ends [Para 31][Para 33].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that both parties had agreed not to proceed with non-conciliation points 2 to 9, and that a discontinuance leaves the substance of point 1 untouched; neither observation was necessary to the order [Para 16][Para 26].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The file is an unofficial English translation of a Khmer original, and its OCR has collapsed many word boundaries, producing forms such as "theemployer", "submitted" as "ubmitted" and "Training" as "Traing"; the employer's document list is printed as "N/A" and then garbled [Para 2][Para 14][Para 26]. Representative names are split across page breaks and survive only as a bare honorific at the end of a line, so the employer representative and the union staff cannot be named from this text [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3]. The document contains no fact-finding section, the panel stating expressly that none was possible without a hearing [Para 28].
