# Award No. 113/08 — New Way (Cambodia) Garment (ញូ វ៉ាយ ដ៏) (Arbitration Council, 23 September 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ang Eng Thong presiding; Mam Sambo Na, employer nominee; Vong Vanna, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140550

## 1. Facts
New Way (Cambodia) Garment — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូ វាយ ដ៏, registered with the CDC and the Ministry of Commerce on 1 March 2006 — at Tuol Pongro village, Chom Chao commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, employs about 1,125 workers and began cutting and sewing in 2006. [Para 1][Para 36][Para 37][Para 41] The claimant is the Khmer Democratic Workers' Federation at the New Way factory (សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព កម្មករ ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ ខ្មែរ រោងចក្រ វាយ ដ៏), registered under certificate No. 1216 and certified most representative on 30 January 2008, with more than 600 members. [Para 1][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] Its complaint of 13 August 2008 raised eight demands; conciliation failed on two on 21 August 2008 and report No. 943 of 29 August 2008 came in on 2 September 2008, and both parties attended the hearing of 9 September 2008. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 8][Para 15] The two open demands were conversion of six-month fixed-duration contracts into unfixed-duration contracts, and repayment of role bonuses to four team leaders. [Para 41][Para 57]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must abolish its six-month fixed-duration contracts and convert the workers to unfixed-duration contracts? [Para 41][Para 45][Para 77]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay back the role bonus claimed by four team leaders for the months in which it was not paid? [Para 57][Para 61][Para 103]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព កម្មករ ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ ខ្មែរ រោងចក្រ វាយ ដ៏:** an unfixed-duration contract gives more benefit, especially on termination and for future social-security entitlements; some workers have served since before 2007 on four successive six-month contracts, so they have worked more than two years and should be converted; and the four team leaders are members who asked the union to recover role bonuses they never received, totalling USD 85, USD 115, USD 160 and USD 60. [Para 45][Para 47][Para 49][Para 57][Para 62]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូ វាយ ដ៏:** it wants to keep six-month fixed-duration contracts to absorb fluctuations in the production chain and to renegotiate conditions at each renewal; each expiry was settled with 5 percent severance and annual leave paid; and the bonuses are discretionary — a USD 5 attendance bonus, a USD 30 "other bonus" for a month with no absence, and an output-based work bonus ranging from USD 0 to USD 60 — which the four team leaders did not receive because they did not meet the conditions. [Para 3][Para 51][Para 43][Para 65][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel dismissed the demand to convert fixed-duration contracts, and ordered the company to pay other bonus of USD 18 to one team leader for May 2008, USD 25 to the second for February 2008, USD 25 to the third for May 2008, and USD 60 to the fourth for April, May and June 2008. [Para 165][Para 166][Para 167][Para 168][Para 169]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 22 of Decree No. 38, holding a contract is the law of the parties and can be varied or extinguished only by their agreement, so neither side may impose a contract type on the other. [Para 77][Para 80][Para 83][Para 86] It noted that earlier awards ordered conversion where fixed-duration contracts had been renewed beyond two years, contrary to Article 67, but found that here the union gave no worker-specific evidence: it produced no names or documents and asked the employer to supply them, and could not say which of the 27 workers whose files the company produced were its members or claimants. [Para 92][Para 94][Para 95][Para 100][Para 101] It therefore dismissed the demand. [Para 102]
On Issue 2 the panel found no article of the Labour Law creates an incentive bonus, but that Article 2 gives the employer the right to manage and set reward policy, and a scheme tied to conditions the team leader must meet, leaving base wage and statutory bonuses untouched, is reasonable. [Para 103][Para 105][Para 112][Para 114] It nonetheless checked each worker's time cards and payroll against the claim and held that the attendance bonus and the USD 30 other bonus fall due once the worker attended regularly in the month, while the work bonus falls due only on proof that the team met the output target — proof the union did not provide. [Para 75][Para 119][Para 121][Para 124][Para 136] It ordered repayment of the other bonus only for the months where attendance was established. [Para 122][Para 129][Para 153][Para 164]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand to convert fixed-duration contracts into unfixed-duration contracts under Article 67 of the Labour Law must be proved worker by worker: where the claimant union cannot identify which of the workers whose contracts were renewed beyond two years are its members and claimants, the panel will not grant a collective conversion. Separately, where an employer's discretionary bonus scheme attaches stated conditions, the bonus becomes payable once those conditions are met, even though no statute requires such a bonus. [Para 92][Para 101][Para 114][Para 119]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that an unfixed-duration contract may bring greater benefit on termination and under a future social-security regime were the workers' arguments, not the panel's reason for refusal, which rested on the absence of worker-specific proof. [Para 45][Para 90]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears as ញូ វ៉ាយ ដ៏, ញូ វាយ ដ៏, ញូវ វ័យ ដ៏ and ញូវាយ ដ៏ across the header, findings and order, and the header date line is corrupted to "ភាលបរិច្ឆេទ". [Para 1][Para 32][Para 33][Para 36] The operative order at para 138 awards "USD 256" for the February bonus to the second team leader although the reasoning fixed the figure at USD 25 and the order repeats USD 25. [Para 129][Para 138][Para 167] One team leader's name is rendered in two different forms in the reasoning and again differently in the order paragraphs, and the fourth award repeats a doubled figure ("USD 300") in the claim list. [Para 58][Para 139][Para 156][Para 169] Para 105 breaks the text of Article 2 into three torn fragments with a stray "ល ។" line, and the signature block prints the presiding arbitrator's name twice in corrupted form with stray Latin characters. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 171] The four team leaders are named throughout the award; they are referred to here by initial only. [Para 58]
