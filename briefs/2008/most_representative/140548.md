# Award No. 111/08 — Yong Min (Cambodia) Apparel (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង មិន ខេមបូឌា អាប់ តឿរីល) (Arbitration Council, 26 September 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Kao Thach, employer nominee; An Non, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140548

## 1. Facts
Yong Min (Cambodia) Apparel — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង មិន ខេមបូឌា អាប់ តឿរីល — at Bal village, Dangkao commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, employed about 800 workers and had a single union, the claimant — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ យ៉ុង មិន, which the panel calls the Yong Min factory workers' union — with 300 members and no most-representative status. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] Its complaint of 24 June 2008 carried two demands: that the seniority bonus be folded into the base wage when overtime is calculated, and that the company refund USD 6 of living allowance it wrongly withheld in April and May 2008. [Para 13][Para 2][Para 4] Conciliation failed on both on 14 August 2008 and report No. 935 of 28 August 2008 reached the Secretariat on 29 August 2008. [Para 14][Para 10] The company stayed away from the hearing of 8 September 2008, citing pressure of work and telling the panel to sit without it, so the hearing proceeded in its absence; the workers then withdrew the allowance claim, leaving Issue 1. [Para 12][Para 17][Para 18][Para 19][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the seniority bonus must be added into the base wage when the company computes overtime pay, so that overtime is calculated on base wage plus seniority bonus rather than on base wage alone? [Para 28][Para 29][Para 31]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ យ៉ុង មិន:** the company divides the base wage by 26 and then by 8 and multiplies by 1.5 for overtime, leaving the seniority bonus out, and that bonus should be folded into the base wage for the overtime calculation because the company deducts seniority bonus proportionally for days of absence. [Para 29][Para 32][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង មិន ខេមបូឌា អាប់ តឿរីល:** absent from the hearing, but on the conciliation record it says it follows its old practice, cannot pay seniority in full, and that no law or declaration clearly forbids it from deducting seniority bonus on days of absence or from leaving that bonus out of the overtime calculation; on the USD 6 allowance it says notification No. 032 S.CH.N applies only from June 2008. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 6]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand that the company add the seniority bonus into the base wage when computing overtime pay. [Para 62]
The panel worked from notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000, point 5, which scales the seniority bonus from USD 2 a month after one year to USD 5 after four, and from point 3 of notification No. 745 K.B.V of 23 October 2006, which preserves those benefits. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 43] It found that notification 017 is unclear both on whether the bonus is cut for absence and on whether it enters the overtime calculation. [Para 44]
It noted its own earlier rulings — Awards 41/05-Way Olet, 66/06-Golida, 96/06-Wilson and 67/08-Yong Min — that the seniority bonus is a component of wage, and Article 103 of the Labour Law, which lists wage as a bundle including overtime pay, bonuses and allowances. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] Reading Prakas No. 80 SKBY of 1 March 1999, Article 5(a), with Article 137 of the Labour Law, it held that "ordinary wage" for overtime means the pay earned for eight hours a day or 48 hours a week according to the worker's skill and capacity, subject to at least the statutory minimum. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 52][Para 56]
Because the seniority bonus is already counted once inside the wage bundle under Article 103, adding it a second time to the overtime base would count the same money twice; the panel also observed that factories generally do not include the bonus in overtime. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61] It therefore rejected the claim. [Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A seniority bonus that is already counted once within the wage bundle under Article 103 of the Labour Law may not be added a second time to the base on which the overtime multiplier is applied; "ordinary wage" for overtime purposes is the pay for eight hours a day or 48 hours a week at the worker's skill level, subject to the statutory minimum, and nothing in notification 017 requires more. [Para 56][Para 58][Para 59]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that workplace practice generally excludes the seniority bonus from overtime computation, and its observation that notification 017 is silent on both the absence deduction and the overtime question, were supporting considerations rather than the operative rule. [Para 44][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is printed in full as យ៉ុង មិន ខេមបូឌា អាប់ តឿរីល in the header and only as យ៉ុង មិន in the body and case name. [Para 1][Para 21] The claimant is described in the header as a federation together with a factory union in one run-on line, and in the findings simply as the Yong Min factory workers' union, so the two bodies cannot be separated on the face of the award. [Para 1][Para 26] Para 23 carries a garbled insertion about an embassy and "អង្គរ" where the Secretariat's documents are listed, and para 28 quotes the earlier case 67/08-Yong Min with duplicated and corrupted text and an interpolated date in the middle of the sentence. [Para 23][Para 28] The date of notification 745 survives only as "ខែតុលា ឆ្នំ]២០០៦", and the signature block carries stray Latin OCR ("ungwer .22, A. Actic , 2017"). [Para 43][Para 64] The panel quotes Article 137 as limiting work to 48 hours a week in one line and to thirty-eight hours in the statutory text it reproduces, an inconsistency in the original. [Para 51][Para 52]
