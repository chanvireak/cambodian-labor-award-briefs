# Order No. 117/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ណាយ អ៉ិនធើណេស៊ីនណល / Sky Night International (Arbitration Council, 15 October 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ច for the employer party, អន លាន for the worker party, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140555

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ណាយ អ៉ិនធើណេស៊ីនណល (Sky Night International), a footwear factory at Kampoul village, Kampoul commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal province, employed 1,297 workers. [Para 2][Para 32] The union, សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស្កាយ ណាយ, registered on 08 September 2008 with about 700 members, pressed 27 demands; conciliation on 08 and 09 August 2008 settled 19, the parties settled six more at the hearing, and only issues 1 and 2 survived. [Para 3][Para 12][Para 14][Para 15][Para 33][Para 35] Both concern overtime: whether overtime workers must be let out for a half-hour meal break, and whether they get 1,500 riel or one free meal for the 16:00 to 18:00 overtime period. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must let workers who work overtime until 21:00 take a half-hour meal break from 18:00 to 18:30 without docking their hours or making them work it back? [Para 36][Para 49]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay 1,500 riel as a meal allowance, or provide one free meal, to workers who voluntarily work overtime from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 45][Para 72]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), members of សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស្កាយ ណាយ at the ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ណាយ អ៉ិនធើណេស៊ីនណល factory:** because the law does not allow overtime beyond two hours, a worker who stays until 21:00 must be given 18:00 to 18:30 to eat without losing the half hour; and the 1,000 riel the company pays no longer buys a meal now that market prices have risen, so 1,500 riel or a meal in kind is owed. [Para 39][Para 46][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ណាយ អ៉ិនធើណេស៊ីនណល:** it already lets those staying to 21:00 take half an hour to eat and gives two packets of noodles plus transport home, but that break cannot be counted inside overtime hours; and it pays 1,000 riel, plus 500 riel more for those who stay to 19:00, in line with Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 45][Para 48]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel declined to consider the half-hour paid break and the 1,500 riel meal allowance, but ordered the employer to provide one free meal to workers who voluntarily work overtime from 16:00 to 18:00. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 109]

On Issue 1 the panel accepted that the employer may allow a half-hour break to eat, but held that the break cannot be counted as overtime hours, because during it the worker is not working for the employer, so paying overtime for it would be unreasonable; no article of the Labour Law or any legal standard requires a paid meal break. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] Because the demand asked for more than the law gives, the panel classified it as a dispute over interests (វិវាទអំពីប្រយោជន៍) and, applying Article 96 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 and Article 277, held that a union bringing an interests dispute must be the most representative — more than half the workforce — which this union of 700 out of 1,297 was not, so it had no standing to conclude a collective agreement for the whole factory and the panel refused to consider the claim. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 61][Para 64][Para 65] It also noted that under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interests dispute would itself operate as a collective agreement for one year. [Para 67][Para 68] On Issue 2 the panel split the claim: notification No. 745 of 23 October 2006 preserved point 4 of Notification No. 017, fixing 1,000 riel or one free meal, and the employer was paying that, so no article, agreement or collective convention required 1,500 riel. [Para 75][Para 77][Para 79][Para 80][Para 81] Following cases No. 53/05, No. 39/07, No. 47/07-Chung Fai and No. 66/06-Golida, the panel found that point 4 aims primarily at providing a meal rather than money, and that 1,000 riel can no longer buy a meal as it could in 2000, so the workers may claim the meal instead; the demand for the meal, as an alternative to money, was therefore reasonable and granted. [Para 84][Para 94][Para 97][Para 99][Para 100][Para 104][Para 105]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that does not represent more than half the workforce is not the most representative union and cannot bring an interests dispute — a demand exceeding what the law guarantees — to the Arbitration Council; the panel will decline to consider it, so such a claim must first be pursued as a collective bargaining demand. Where a notification fixes a meal allowance as "1,000 riel or one free meal", the substantive obligation is the meal, and the worker may insist on the meal in kind once the money no longer buys one. [Para 57][Para 65][Para 97][Para 100]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that a half-hour break is not working time and that an award on an interests dispute would become a one-year collective agreement are general propositions that do not by themselves determine either order. [Para 54][Para 68]
