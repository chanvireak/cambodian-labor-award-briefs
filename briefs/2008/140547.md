# Award No. 108/08 — Hugo International (Cambodia) (ហ៊ូហ្គោ) (Arbitration Council, 17 September 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Ing Sothy, employer nominee; An Virak, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140547

## 1. Facts
Hugo International (Cambodia) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូហ្គោ អ៉ីនធើណេស៊ីណល អ៉ិន វេស មេន — at Kbal Damrey village, Kakab commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, employed about 1,149 workers. [Para 1][Para 51] The claimant was the Independent Solidarity Union of the Hugo factory (សហជីព សាមគ្គី ឯករាជ្យ), with 897 members and a certificate of most-representative status No. 039/07 K.B.V of 11 May 2007. [Para 2][Para 53] Its complaint of 13 August 2008 carried eight demands; conciliation on 19 August 2008 settled four and left four open in report No. 898 of that date, and after a strike the Council ordered a stoppage by order No. 010 KA of 20 August 2008, which the workers obeyed on 21 August 2008. [Para 16][Para 12][Para 18][Para 19] At the hearing of 28 August 2008 Issue 1 — abolition of fixed-duration contracts — was settled, leaving Issues 2, 3 and 4 for the panel. [Para 14][Para 22][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay a 15 percent wage increase each year to workers with ten years' service or more when they resign? [Para 54][Para 83][Para 85]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must resume supplying two tins of milk powder a month for workers' children aged one to eighteen months, as it did from 2002 to 2005? [Para 59][Para 100]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the workers may require the company to dismiss its head of administration for interfering in the union's internal affairs? [Para 72][Para 117][Para 118]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the Independent Solidarity Union:** workers with ten years' service deserve a 15 percent yearly increment, and long-serving workers should receive something when they resign; the milk powder was withdrawn in September 2005 without explanation or any written agreement, and should resume because prices have risen; and the head of administration interfered in union affairs, pressuring members to resign and having them followed, while remaining president of another federation. [Para 56][Para 60][Para 61][Para 64][Para 76][Para 77]
**Employer (respondent), Hugo International:** it says it cannot afford any of the three — it terminated the milk powder because it had no profit after a worker fainted and export conditions worsened, and will restore it only when it has buyers and profit again, without being able to say when; it also produced the officer's letter of resignation from the federation dated 1 December 2007 and denied that he interfered. [Para 5][Para 7][Para 66][Para 67][Para 124]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel dismissed the 15 percent claim, ordered the company to negotiate with the union over the milk powder within three months of the award taking effect, and dismissed the demand to dismiss the head of administration. [Para 141][Para 142][Para 143]
On Issue 2 the panel found that the Labour Law nowhere requires an employer to pay 15 percent to workers who resign, so the claim exceeded the statutory minimum and was an interest dispute. [Para 86][Para 87] Because the union was most representative it could bring such a dispute — Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305, following Awards 81/04-Evergreen and 48/07-ENT — but Article 312 bis paragraph 2 lets the panel decide other disputes only on equity, and the workers produced no credible evidence of a ground in equity for the increment. [Para 88][Para 93][Para 95][Para 98][Para 99]
On Issue 3 the panel found that no article of the Labour Law and no regulation requires two tins of milk powder, though nothing forbids an employer from granting better than the law requires; when such a benefit is withdrawn both sides must cooperate to explain why and find a solution. [Para 101][Para 102][Para 103] It noted three years had passed with only one meeting, whose minute recorded merely a promise to reconsider if profit returned, and held that forcing the company to pay regardless of profit would be unfair to it, while continuing to refuse because prices rose would be unfair to the workers; it therefore ordered negotiation within three months. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 108][Para 109][Para 116]
On Issue 4 the panel applied Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 22 of Decree No. 38 of 28 October 1988: only the parties to a contract may vary or end it, and no third party may compel them, so hiring and dismissal remain the employer's exclusive right. [Para 128][Para 133][Para 134][Para 135] The workers showed no evidence of interference, and the panel found nothing showing the officer endangered other workers' security, so the demand was dismissed. [Para 119][Para 123][Para 138][Para 140]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a union that is most representative brings an interest dispute for a benefit above the statutory minimum, the panel will grant it only on proof of a ground in equity, because Article 312 bis paragraph 2 confines such awards to equity; and workers cannot obtain an order compelling an employer to dismiss or retain a member of its staff, since only the parties to the employment contract may end it. [Para 95][Para 98][Para 133][Para 135]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that a benefit above the law may lawfully be granted, and that when it is withdrawn both sides must cooperate to explain the reason and seek a solution, is a general observation that did not itself decide Issue 3. [Para 102][Para 103]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is printed as ហ៊ូហ្គោ អ៉ីនធើណេស៊ីណល អ៉ិន វេស មេន in the header and in several shortened and differently spelled forms thereafter, and the union's registration certificate and most-representative certificate name the factory as "CHBBR VIEW" and "CHEER VIEW" rather than Hugo, so the certificates cannot be matched to the respondent on their face. [Para 1][Para 28][Para 30][Para 53] The milk powder period is printed as 2002–2005, as "២០០២២" in one line and as "ឆ្នាំ ២០០២៣" in another. [Para 59][Para 100] The officer's resignation letter is dated 1 December 2007 and acknowledged 2 December 2007 in the findings but 2 December 2008 in the evidence list. [Para 23][Para 74] Para 93 contains a stray "ក្រុមហ៊ុន C" fragment inside the reasoning, the signature block carries broken Latin OCR and a stray "1945", and the company officer, named throughout the award, is referred to here only by his role. [Para 93][Para 145]
