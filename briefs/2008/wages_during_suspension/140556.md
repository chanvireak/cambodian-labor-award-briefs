# Order No. 127/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង (សាខា) / Seng Yong (Branch) (Arbitration Council, 24 October 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វូច ហ៊ុន for the employer party, ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់ for the worker party, គង់ ផល្លៈ chair) · Doc ID 140556

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង (សាខា) (Seng Yong (Branch)), the branch opened in 2004 at Tuol Sangke village, Sangkat Tuol Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, employed about 390 workers and was the only unionised establishment of its kind there, with a single union affiliated to សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ. [Para 1][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] Workers struck from 15 to 18 September 2008, demanding above all to know whether the owner was changing, after the union's letter of 11 September 2008 asking for a meeting on 12 September went unanswered. [Para 2][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58] Of 14 demands, four were settled in conciliation, and at the hearing the parties agreed to take issues 1 to 9 to factory bargaining for a collective agreement, leaving only issue 10. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 33][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 10) Whether the employer must maintain wages and the regular attendance bonus for the period of the strike from 15 to 18 September 2008? [Para 52][Para 68]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), members of សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ at ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង (សាខា):** the strike was not their fault, because the company refused to meet over the change of owner, so the wages and attendance bonus deducted for those days should be repaid; the company already agreed to pay the half day of 15 September. [Para 56][Para 61][Para 62]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង (សាខា):** it asks to apply the Labour Law; it notified workers on 15 September at 3:00 pm to return or lose wages and the attendance bonus, and it agrees only to repay the half day worked on the morning of 15 September, which the workers refused. [Para 22][Para 60][Para 63]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim to maintain wages and the regular attendance bonus during the strike was dismissed, but the employer was ordered to repay the wages deducted for the part of 15 September 2008 before 2:15 pm, when no strike was taking place. [Para 103][Para 104]

The panel first recorded the facts: the morning of 15 September 2008 was normal work followed by an election of worker delegates, and the strike began at 2:15 pm that day and ran to 18 September 2008. [Para 54][Para 69] Applying Article 332 of the Labour Law, which suspends the employment contract during a strike and provides that no work and no wage is due, the panel held — following its order in case No. 22/04-Rafael Le Royal — that strikers cannot claim wages for the strike period even where the strike is lawful. [Para 72][Para 74][Para 75] Article 334 would require the employer to pay all strikers if it hired replacements during the strike, but the workers never alleged that replacements were hired, so that provision did not apply. [Para 76][Para 78] The panel then turned to the attendance bonus: Article 333 forbids any sanction against strikers, but that protection is fully effective only where the strike itself followed the legal procedure, and here it did not — there was no secret ballot under Article 323, no seven-day notice to the employer under Article 324, and the workers had not exhausted the conciliation and Arbitration Council route required by Article 320(k) paragraph 4 before striking. [Para 83][Para 84][Para 85][Para 91][Para 93][Para 96][Para 98] Point 3 of Notification No. 745 of 23 October 2006, preserving the 5 USD attendance bonus of Notification No. 017 point 3, requires attendance on the days due in the month, so a worker absent on strike did not attend regularly and cannot claim the bonus; the panel followed cases No. 04/03-Lida, No. 15/04-Lucky, No. 03/05 and No. 63/07. [Para 86][Para 88][Para 89][Para 100][Para 101] Because the employer had conceded the half day of 15 September and the panel found no strike before 2:15 pm, it ordered that deduction repaid. [Para 71][Para 102][Para 104]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Wages are not payable for days on strike, because Article 332 suspends the contract, and an unlawful strike — one launched without a secret ballot, without the seven-day notice and before conciliation and arbitration are exhausted — also forfeits the attendance bonus, since the worker did not attend on the days due. Where only part of a day was struck, the wage for the hours before the strike began remains payable, and the employer's own concession on that part will be enforced. [Para 74][Para 98][Para 100][Para 104]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that even a lawful strike earns no wages, and its reading of Article 334 on replacement workers, are propositions that do not change the outcome here because the strike was unlawful and no replacements were alleged. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Only issue 10 is decided; issues 1 to 9 were referred to factory bargaining, so §2 records the panel's own numbering with a single issue rather than renumbering.
- The strike start time is printed as "2:15 pm" at [Para 53] and [Para 82], but the employer's account at [Para 55] says the strike began after the midday meal without fixing an hour, so the 2:15 pm boundary rests on the workers' account alone.
- Wages are said to have been deducted "from the afternoon of 15 September" at [Para 70] but the order to repay covers the period "before 2:15 pm" [Para 104], and the owed half day is described both as a half day [Para 63] and as work before 2:15 pm [Para 102].
- The attendance bonus figure is printed only as "5 USD" at [Para 88], with no riel equivalent, unlike other awards.
- OCR losses: "ម៉ៅង" for "ម៉ោង" (hour) at [Para 102] and [Para 104], and the signature block at [Para 106] gives the employer-party arbitrator but leaves the worker-party arbitrator and chair without printed names.
