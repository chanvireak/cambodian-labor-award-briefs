# Award No. 70/08 — Kin Tay Garment Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន គីន តាយ ហ្គាមិន ខូ.អិលធីឌី) and the Khmer Youth Union Federation at the Kin Tay factory (Arbitration Council, 10 June 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, presiding) · Doc ID 140521

## 1. Facts
Kin Tay Garment Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន គីន តាយ ហ្គាមិន ខូ.អិលធីឌី), a Phnom Penh garment factory at Chak Angre Krom, faced four collective demands filed on 6 March 2008 through the Khmer Youth Union Federation at the Kin Tay factory (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រ គីន តាយ) [Para 1][Para 15]. The demands were holiday pay for October, November and December, the unpaid USD 5 monthly seniority bonus for October 2007, overtime for three finishing workers on 3 and 4 December 2007, and USD 5 in lieu of milk money plus childcare costs for a woman worker who was a union committee member [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8]. Conciliation failed on all four on 7 April 2008 [Para 16]. At the hearing the parties settled issues 1, 3 and 4, leaving only issue 2 [Para 20][Para 21]. The workers had struck throughout October, November and December 2007, complaining that the company had wiped their employment records; the panel found the strike unlawful [Para 26][Para 27]. A list of 264 workers with thumbprints supported the claim [Para 22][Para 29].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer owed the USD 5 monthly seniority bonus for October 2007 to workers who had struck for the whole of that month, given that Article 332 of the Labour Law suspends the contract of employment during a strike? [Para 33][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the seniority bonus is fixed by the number of years served — USD 2 after one year, one more dollar for each of years two, three and four — and is unrelated to the days or months actually worked or to the strike; it was therefore owed for October 2007 in full to workers with more than five years' service, and the company had not paid it [Para 28][Para 30][Para 39].

**Employer (respondent):** the company had paid the USD 5 seniority bonus to every worker who came to work, even one who came for only a few days, and had withheld it only from those who struck and did not work at all, because an unlawful strike means no work and no entitlement [Para 31][Para 32]. It also maintained that the bonus had been paid for October, November and December in line with Notification No. 017 [Para 3][Para 38].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand for the USD 5 seniority bonus for October 2007, holding that the employer's withholding of it from striking workers did not breach point 5 of Notification No. 017 [Para 54][Para 55].

The panel first settled the character of the payment. Relying on Article 103 of the Labour Law, which defines wages to include bonuses and allowances, and on Awards 41/05 (Violet), 66/06 (Golida) and 96/06 (Wilson), it held that the seniority bonus is a component of wages [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]. Article 332 then governs the strike: the contract of employment is suspended during a strike, no work is performed and no wage is paid [Para 40][Para 41]. Article 72(1) confirms that suspension relieves the employer of the duty to pay wages unless a contrary provision applies [Para 43]. The panel read those two articles to mean that on any day or in any month of a strike — whether the strike is lawful or not — no wage is owed, precisely because no work was done [Para 44]. Since the October 2007 strike lasted the whole month and no work was performed [Para 52], and since the seniority bonus is part of wages, the employer owed no bonus for that month [Para 50][Para 51]. The panel also noted its consistent line that workers who strike without complying with the Labour Law forfeit both wages and the regular attendance bonus, citing Awards 04/03 (Linda), 22/04 (Ramful Le Royal), 03/05 (Gong), 63/07 (Phnom Penh) and 110/07 (Nouv Khob) [Para 45][Para 46]. It accordingly found no violation of point 5 of Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000, which sets the seniority scale at USD 2, 3, 4 and 5 for one to four years' service, and dismissed the claim [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 53].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Because a strike suspends the contract of employment under Article 332 of the Labour Law, an employer owes neither wages nor any wage component — including a seniority bonus defined by length of service — for the days or months of the strike, irrespective of whether the strike was lawful, since no work was performed [Para 44][Para 50].

**Obiter dicta:** the finding that the 2007 strike was itself unlawful, and the recital of earlier awards on unlawful strikes, were unnecessary because the panel reached the same result even for a lawful strike [Para 27][Para 44][Para 45].
