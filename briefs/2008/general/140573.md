# Award No. 147/08 — Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd. / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូ អិលធីឌីជី (Arbitration Council, 18 December 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Kao Thach (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chea (president) · Doc ID 140573

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូ អិលធីឌីជី (Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd.) has 1,700 workers, of whom 800 sew inside the building of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ប្រូតិច ស្តាយ (Protextile Garment Co., Ltd.) while holding contracts with, being paid by, and keeping the working hours of Seng Yong [Para 43][Para 44]. The complainants are two of the three unions in the factory, សហព័ន្ធន សហជីព ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង and សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង [Para 1][Para 47][Para 48].

Seng Yong has operated since 1996 and in 2008 built a seven-storey block named Protextile, whose president is the owner's wife, separate from Seng Yong [Para 46]. Under a settlement of 14 October 2008 in case No. 129/08 the company agreed to delete the Protextile name between 6 October and 6 November 2008, to write to the relevant institutions within 30 days of the closure, to treat workers who do not sew after 30 days as not striking, to dissolve all workers under the Labour Law if it were found still using the Protextile name, and to preserve existing benefits [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56]. On 3 November 2008 the company notified workers at the new Seng Yong building to keep working in the same building, and warned that anyone who refused would be treated as having abandoned the job without compensation [Para 50][Para 51]. Conciliation on 20 November 2008 left one of five demands unresolved [Para 11].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company's notice of 3 November 2008 breached the settlement agreement of 14 October 2008 by failing to obtain an official reply confirming the closure of Protextile? [Para 49][Para 74]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សហព័ន្ធន សហជីព ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង and សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង say the agreement required the authorities to confirm the closure in writing, that merely filing letters is not enough, and that absent such a reply the company is in breach [Para 57][Para 76][Para 77].

**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូ អិលធីឌីជី says Protextile no longer operates, that it removed the signboard and now uses only the Seng Yong name, and that it filed the closure requests with the Council for the Development of Cambodia and the Ministry of Commerce on 23 October 2008, ahead of the 6 December 2008 deadline [Para 59][Para 67][Para 75][Para 86].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the workers' demand that the company be found in breach of the agreement of 14 October 2008 was rejected [Para 94][Para 95]. The order is binding and became final at once, both parties having agreed to adopt it at the hearing of 5 December 2008 [Para 96].

On point 1 of the agreement the panel read the text narrowly: it required the company to delete the Protextile name and contains no clause demanding a formal reply from any institution [Para 78][Para 79]. The evidence showed the name had in fact been removed — the signboard taken down, no shipping documents left in the Protextile name, staff cards collected and reissued in the Seng Yong name, with some workers declining to surrender the old ones — and the workers conceded that Protextile no longer operates [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82].

On point 2 the panel calculated that the 30-day period ran to 6 December 2008 and found the company had filed with the Council for the Development of Cambodia and the Ministry of Commerce on 23 October 2008 [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86]. The Council's reply of 27 November 2008 recorded that the request had been received, which the panel treated as reasonable compliance rather than as an outstanding authorisation [Para 87][Para 88][Para 89]. It also noted that the workers produced no evidence that Protextile was still trading [Para 70][Para 92]. The claim was therefore refused [Para 93].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a settlement obliges an employer to remove a second company's name and to notify the authorities within a fixed period, the obligation is discharged by actually removing the name from the premises, records and staff cards and by filing the closure requests in time; a demand for an official confirmation letter from those authorities adds a term the agreement does not contain [Para 79][Para 85][Para 91].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the company took reasonable steps in arranging the closure, and that the file now sits with the competent ministry, went beyond what the text of the agreement required [Para 87][Para 88].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The agreement date is printed as 14 October 2019 in one reason and as "14 October 2000្រ8" in another [Para 69][Para 91]. The company's registration certificate in the employer's evidence list is a Protextile certificate, not a Seng Yong one, and the independent union's registration is dated 27 March 1998 [Para 17][Para 36]. The notice of 3 November 2008 tells workers to continue "in the same building" while the surrounding text speaks of a new building, so which premises were meant cannot be settled from the text [Para 50]. Line 3 repeats the secretariat's telephone digits for several lines and ends in a corrupted web address [Para 3]. The claim as framed — breach by want of an official reply — is not recorded in the conciliation report, as the panel itself notes [Para 72].
