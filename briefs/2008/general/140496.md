# Award No. 33/08 — HS Ent (Cambodia) Co., Pte Ltd v Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (Arbitration Council, 19 March 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140496

## 1. Facts
HS Ent (Cambodia) Co., Pte Ltd, on National Road No. 4 at Tnort Muoy Deum Village, Bekchan Commune, Angsnuol District, Kandal, employs 940 workers. [Para 23] Of ten demands raised in conciliation, nine were settled, leaving one: that the company deduct a 1,000 riel union contribution from each member's monthly wage and pay it to the local Khmer Youth Trade Union (KYTU) of the Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (KYFTU). [Para 9][Para 12][Para 21] At the first hearing on 7 March 2008 both sides appeared and the company agreed to deduct once it received an accurate list of consenting members. [Para 13][Para 15] The union then filed no list, and appeared at neither the deadline nor the second hearing on 18 March 2008. [Para 18][Para 20][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Clause 21 / Rule 4.7) Whether the panel should proceed or close the case where the claimant union, duly invited and previously present, failed to attend the second hearing without good cause? [Para 34][Para 39]

The union contribution demand itself was not reached, the case being closed on the procedural question. [Para 40][Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, KYFTU and local KYTU at HS Ent):** the union asked the company to deduct 1,000 riel from each member's wage for union contributions, and at the first hearing agreed to produce the list of members consenting to the deduction; it submitted nothing, and its officer told the Secretariat by telephone that he was unwell and asked the panel to proceed according to law, assigning no representative. [Para 9][Para 17][Para 24][Para 25][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent, HS Ent (Cambodia) Co., Pte Ltd):** it does not refuse the deduction in principle but will act only on an accurate list of consenting members, having found irregularities in the earlier list, which it produced to the panel; if the claimant does not attend, it has abandoned the demand and the company will not revisit it. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 27][Para 28]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 33/08-HS Ent, treating the claimant union as having given up its demand. [Para 40][Para 41]

The panel relied on Clause 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which permits appearance in person or through a lawyer or a person authorised in writing. [Para 29] The union's officer had assigned no representative and no lawyer and told the Secretariat to proceed according to law. [Para 33] Clause 21 of the Prakas and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules allow the panel to proceed in the absence of a party or to terminate by award, provided notification of date, time and venue is established. [Para 34][Para 35] The panel found both parties properly notified by the invitations of 14 March 2008 for the second hearing, and reminded by telephone, and noted that the union knew the date because it had attended the first hearing and agreed it. [Para 23][Para 36][Para 37] Only the employer appeared on 18 March 2008. [Para 38] Following Awards 30/05-Maure and 16/07-Lotus, the panel closed the case rather than deciding the contribution claim in the claimant's absence. [Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union attends the first hearing, agrees a date for the second, submits none of the evidence it undertook to file, then fails to appear without notifying the Secretariat or seeking a postponement, the panel will close the case and treat the demand as withdrawn rather than decide it on the employer's material alone. [Para 26][Para 34][Para 39] The power to proceed in a party's absence exists, but its exercise is discretionary and termination by award is the appropriate course where the absent party is the claimant. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 40]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's acceptance of the company's position that a future repetition of the demand would not be entertained, and its remark that the union had not supplied sufficient evidence on the non-conciliation point, go beyond the procedural ground of closure. [Para 27][Para 28]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Hearing dates are printed throughout as 7 March 2007 and 18 March 2007 at [Para 1], [Para 13] and [Para 19], against 2008 in the header at [Para 12] and in the reasoning at [Para 23] and [Para 24]; the 2008 dates are used here.
- The invitation to the first hearing is dated 28 March 2008 at [Para 23] and [Para 21], three weeks after the hearing it convenes.
- The second hearing is set at 2:00 p.m. at [Para 12] but the reminder call is recorded at 1:30 p.m. at [Para 30].
- The company's list covers 1,150 workers at [Para 18] and [Para 21] although the factory employs 940 at [Para 23].
- The two union officers' names are split from their titles across [Para 2] to [Para 8], and the account of who was telephoned and who was ill is duplicated and inconsistent between [Para 24] to [Para 26] and [Para 30] to [Para 33]. Individual names are given by role here.
