# Award No. 138/08 — Malin Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន មាំលីន ហ្គា មិន) (Arbitration Council, 20 November 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · three-member panel: លី តៃ សេង (employer-nominated), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-nominated), គង់ ផល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140563

## 1. Facts
Ten demands by the workers of Malin Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន មាំលីន ហ្គា មិន), brought through សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព.សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន មាំលីន ហ្គា មិន, went unresolved through conciliation and were referred to the panel: special leave for workers in hardship, full pay when there is no work, a 5 per cent terminal payment on fixed-duration contracts, a 2,000 riel meal allowance for Sunday and holiday overtime, 50 per cent wage during 90 days' maternity leave, voluntary overtime, retention of wage and bonus during hospital treatment, a 1,000 riel monthly union-dues deduction, weekly payment of the meal allowance, and removal of a named security guard. [Para 3][Para 6][Para 7][Para 10][Para 12] Neither party attended the hearing of 7 November 2008; the union said its leaders were away at a ceremony in the provinces and left the matter to the panel. [Para 17][Para 40] No documentary evidence was filed by either side. [Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 1–10, as referred) Whether the panel may decide the ten referred demands at all, where both parties were duly summoned and neither appeared — that is, whether the three conditions of Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 are satisfied? [Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the Khmer Youth federation at Malin Garment (សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព.សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន មាំលីន ហ្គា មិន):** the ten demands stood as referred from the conciliation report, but the federation neither appeared nor filed reasons or evidence in support. [Para 3][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent), Malin Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន មាំលីន ហ្គា មិន):** likewise did not appear, and could not be reached by telephone on the day of the hearing. [Para 43]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed the case (សម្រេច បិទ សំណុំរឿង) without deciding any demand on its merits, treating the claimants' non-appearance as abandonment of the complaint. [Para 57][Para 58]

The panel applied Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules and Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which allow it to proceed or to terminate by award where a party does not appear. [Para 29][Para 30] Following Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07, 73/07-San Sin and 132/08-JHC, it treated Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions for closure: the party was properly notified, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 31][Para 32]

All three were met. Invitations were issued to both sides on 30 October 2008 for the hearing of 7 November 2008, and both were confirmed recipients. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 34] Neither appeared at any of the four stages of the proceedings. [Para 37][Para 39] On the third condition, the secretariat telephoned on the hearing day; the union answered that it could not attend and gave no reason, and the company could not be contacted at all. [Para 40][Para 43][Para 44]

The panel then addressed the consequence. A complainant bears the duty of giving reasons and evidence before the Council; a party that stays away loses the chance to do so. [Para 50][Para 52] It therefore held the claim abandoned and noted that such conduct did not show good faith in bringing the dispute for settlement. [Para 53][Para 56] It recorded that the union had been given every opportunity to defend the demands. [Para 48]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties are properly notified and neither appears, and neither gives a valid reason, a panel may terminate the file; a claimant that does not appear is taken to have abandoned its complaint, and the panel will not decide the referred demands on their merits in its absence. [Para 47][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** non-appearance is treated as evidence of a lack of good faith in seeking settlement, though the panel rested the closure on the procedural conditions alone. [Para 56]
