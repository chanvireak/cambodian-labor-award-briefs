# Award No. 112/08 — Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូច អេលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council, 22 September 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Kao Thach, employer nominee; An Non, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140549

## 1. Facts
Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូច អេលធីឌី — at building 4 on National Road 5, Tuol Sangke commune, Russey Keo district, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1996, employs 1,800 workers and hosts three unions, the claimant being the Seng Yong factory Free Workers' Union (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង) with 730 members. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] In 2008 the wife of the Taiwanese owner built a seven-storey building inside the factory under the separate name "Protect Stay (Cambodia)", of which she is president, and the company moved workers there and, the union says, changed their identity cards to the Protect Stay name. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] Three demands went to conciliation, which failed on one point on 22 August 2008 in report No. 938 dated 28 August 2008; the claim heard on 9 September 2008 was that the company keep the Seng Yong name on import and export documents and not use the Protect Stay name. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 29][Para 9]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must keep the name Seng Yong on documents for goods brought in or sent out, and must not use the name "Protect Stay" for that purpose? [Para 29][Para 40][Para 55]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊េង យ៉ុង:** they work for Seng Yong and want its name kept on import and export documents; they fear that Seng Yong will one day be dissolved and only Protect Stay left, so that they lose their seniority, and they produced documents said to show goods entering under the Protect Stay name; they accept that no worker has yet lost seniority or benefits and that identity cards still read Seng Yong. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 39][Para 34][Para 42]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូច អេលធីឌី:** it keeps the Seng Yong name and all workers remain its workers with their benefits intact; it did not change identity cards to Protect Stay; and Protect Stay has no import-export licence yet, its applications having gone to the ministries in June and July 2008 without official approval, so no goods can move under that name in any event. [Para 4][Para 33][Para 37][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the demand that the company keep the Seng Yong name on import and export documents. [Para 54][Para 55]
The panel found that the workers' concern was not a dispute that had arisen but one that might arise in the future: nobody had lost seniority or benefits, and the demand rested on a fear that Seng Yong would be dissolved later. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] It repeated its standing position that the Council exists to resolve labour disputes that have arisen, not problems not yet in existence, following Awards 10/03-Zaksin Tech, 47/04, 122/07-Yen Yuen and 77/08-Sin Roy, and Award 14/06-Meng refusing a demand about a future transfer because it was unclear when or whether it would occur. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]
It also held that a demand for the future, whose timing and factual basis are undefined, could not be adjudicated at all. [Para 47][Para 48] On the documents the workers filed, the panel held it had no jurisdiction to rule whether using the Protect Stay name for imports and exports is lawful; the company had filed its applications in June and July 2008 and still lacked authorisation, so goods could not move under that name. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] It advised the workers to take any concrete concern about irregular import or export to the ministries and agencies competent over trade. [Para 53]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The panel will not decide a demand that seeks to regulate a state of affairs that has not yet occurred: where the harm complained of is contingent on a future event whose timing and factual basis are undefined, there is no existing labour dispute to resolve, and the proper forum for an allegation of unlawful import or export practice is the ministry or agency competent over trade, not the Arbitration Council. [Para 44][Para 48][Para 52][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the company had filed its licence applications and that no goods could move under the Protect Stay name without authorisation were observations on the current facts, not reasons for declining jurisdiction over the legality of the name itself. [Para 37][Para 50][Para 51]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is named in full as ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊េង យ៉ុង ហ្គាមែន ហ្វាក់ តូរី ខូច អេលធីឌី in the header and simply as ស៊េង យ៉ុង throughout the findings, with a stray "Li" prefix producing "ក្រុមហ៊ុន Li សេង យ៉ុង" in para 49. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 49] The claimant is described in the header as the union of the factory and in the findings as the union of the company, two different formulations of the same body. [Para 1][Para 27] The Labour Department is said to have received the union's letter on 14 August 2008 although the letter is dated 22 August 2008. [Para 10] The evidence list repeats the entry "documents received from the worker party: none" twice, with the Ministry heading duplicated and corrupted ("ឥ--"), and the collective agreement registration letter is dated "22 November 200" with the final digit lost. [Para 20][Para 21] The signature block renders the worker arbitrator's name as "អនិ លាន" against "អន ណន" in the header and carries stray Latin fragments; individual union officers named in the header are not reproduced here. [Para 1][Para 57]
