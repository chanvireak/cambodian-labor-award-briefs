# Award No. 152/08 — Wil Sin Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. / ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីល សិន ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 12 January 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — You Soun Ty (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Nhan Somunind (president) · Doc ID 140575

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីល សិន ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត (Wil Sin Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.) is a garment factory at Prey Ta village, Sangkat Kab, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh, employing about 1,100 workers [Para 1][Para 40]. The claimant is សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីល សិន ហ្គាមេន, the local union of C.CAWDU, with 152 members [Para 1][Para 40]. The factory has three unions and none of them is most representative [Para 42][Para 43].

The union complained on 7 November 2008; final conciliation on 3 December 2008 failed on all five demands, which reached the panel on 16 December 2008 [Para 17][Para 18]. The first hearing was set for 23 December 2008 and was moved to 25 December 2008 at the employer's request [Para 16][Para 19]. The employer accepted a non-binding award [Para 23]. The five demands were pro-rating of the USD 5 monthly attendance bonus by days absent, an increase of USD 2 a month in seniority pay for each year up to 10 years, a 2,000 riel meal allowance for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00, reinstatement of one dismissed woman worker with back pay, and 90 days' maternity leave counted in working days only [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the workers may require the employer to deduct the USD 5 regular attendance bonus pro rata to the days each worker was absent? [Para 44][Para 54]
2. (Issue 2) Whether seniority pay must rise by USD 2 a month for each year of service, for up to 10 years? [Para 46][Para 65]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay a 2,000 riel meal allowance for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 46][Para 87]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the dismissed woman worker must be reinstated with back wages because the dismissal was unjustified? [Para 49][Para 104]
5. (Issue 5) Whether 90 days of maternity leave must be counted in working days, excluding Sundays and public holidays? [Para 53][Para 120]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីល សិន ហ្គាមេន asks that the USD 5 bonus be trimmed only in proportion to absences rather than forfeited whole, that seniority pay reach USD 20 a month after 10 years, that the meal allowance rise to 2,000 riel, and that the dismissed worker return with arrears [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 49].

**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន វីល សិន ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត applies Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000 on every point: it cannot deduct by day, pays USD 2 a year for four years, cannot pay 2,000 riel, complies with the law on maternity leave, and says the worker resigned voluntarily by her own letter [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all five demands were refused, three of them without any consideration of the merits [Para 129][Para 130][Para 131][Para 132][Para 133].

On issue 1 the panel read point 3 of Notification No. 017 as making the USD 5 an incentive for a full month worked, not a sum earned day by day, so a worker absent without leave earns nothing for that month; earlier awards 62/04, 15/05, 62/07 and 143/08 were followed [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 60]. It added that the employer may deduct the whole bonus where an absence is unauthorised [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64].

On issue 2 the panel found no text giving USD 2 for each year across 10 years: point 5 of the same notification stops at five years' service and USD 5 a month [Para 65][Para 68][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71]. A demand above that is a claim of interest, not of right, and only a union with most-representative status may bring such a claim, because under Article 277 of the Labour Law and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 an interest award stands as a collective agreement for a year binding the whole factory [Para 73][Para 74][Para 76][Para 79][Para 80][Para 82]. With 152 members out of 1,100 this union did not qualify, so the panel declined to consider the demand [Para 40][Para 86].

Issue 3 failed the same way. Point 4 of Notification No. 017 fixes 1,000 riel a day, or one free meal, for voluntary overtime, and earlier awards 53/05, 39/07 and 143/08 hold that the sum does not change with the length of the overtime [Para 88][Para 89][Para 90][Para 91]. The employer was paying 1,000 riel for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00, which discharged the obligation; no law and no agreement required 2,000 riel, so the claim was again one of interest and was declined for want of representativeness [Para 96][Para 98][Para 99][Para 100][Para 102][Para 104].

On issue 4 the panel asked first whether it was a collective or an individual dispute. Article 302 of the Labour Law requires a collective dispute to arise between an employer and a number of workers, to concern working conditions or union rights, and to be capable of disrupting the enterprise or public order [Para 104][Para 105][Para 106]. The first and third conditions failed because the matter concerned one worker alone, so the panel had no jurisdiction and followed awards 10/03, 41/04, 07/05, 45/07 and 13/08 in leaving the classification to the labour inspector and the Minister [Para 107][Para 110][Para 111][Para 112][Para 117][Para 119].

On issue 5 the panel found Article 182 of the Labour Law silent on whether the 90 days are working or calendar days, and no Prakas settling it [Para 120][Para 121]. Reading the provision against drafting practice — periods of 15 days or less counted in working days, longer periods in calendar days — it held the 90 days include Sundays and public holidays, which also matches the purpose of protecting the mother's and child's health [Para 123][Para 126][Para 127].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for more than the law or an existing notification grants is a dispute over interests, and where the union bringing it holds only a minority of the workforce the panel will not consider it, because an interest award would bind the whole factory as a collective agreement [Para 74][Para 79][Para 82]. A complaint about the dismissal of one worker is an individual dispute outside the panel's jurisdiction, and the 90 days of maternity leave under Article 182 run as calendar days including holidays [Para 110][Para 123].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that the increase in the price of a meal does not by itself create an entitlement to 2,000 riel, and that 90 calendar days is a sufficient period for recovery, were not necessary to the disposition [Para 97][Para 127].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The letter by which the worker is said to have resigned is dated 8 August 2008 in the evidence list and 28 August 2008 in the reasoning, and the reasoning also gives 30 August 2008 as the day she stopped work [Para 30][Para 52][Para 53]. The company name appears as វីល សិន, វិល សិន and វឹល សិន on different pages, and its suffix is printed លីមីធីត for "Limited" [Para 1][Para 40]. Line 96 loses the meal figure to a bare "10" before restating it as 1,000 riel [Para 96]. The quoted text of Article 182 is corrupted at the point where it refers to Article 1 of the Labour Law [Para 120]. The response section prints a stray "WW" in place of the employer's name [Para 11].
