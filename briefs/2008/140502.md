# Award No. 47/08-Grandtex — Grandtex International (Arbitration Council, 22 April 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-chosen; Tuon Siphann, worker-chosen; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 140502

## 1. Facts
Grandtex International, at Vatanak Garden, Building A, Chomchao Street, Dangkor, Phnom Penh, employs 1,100 workers; the claimant is the Coalition of Cambodian Apparel W.D.U (C.CAWDU), a registered union without most representative status [Para 19][Para 20][Para 2]. Of fourteen demands conciliated on 18 March 2008, ten were settled and four reached the Council; at the hearing of 4 April 2008 issues 1, 3 and 4 were resolved, leaving only Issue 2 [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]. That issue concerns the back door of the factory, which the company had long allowed workers to use at lunch and going home but closed five or six months before the hearing after workers stole company property through it [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]. The workers accepted that explanation but said the front door is further away, that they travel longer, and that this is unsafe when they work overtime at night [Para 24][Para 25]. The claims for a union meeting room, piece rates within four days and infirmary permission were settled or withdrawn at the hearing and were not decided [Para 15].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must reopen the back door for workers leaving at lunch and provide security at the entrances, where the door was closed after thefts and the workers say the detour is long and unsafe at night? [Para 8][Para 31]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (Coalition of Cambodian Apparel W.D.U, C.CAWDU, at Grandtex International):** the back door was convenient; using only the front door means a longer journey home, and there was a robbery, so the company should secure the workers' safety, particularly for those leaving after overtime at night [Para 24][Para 25].
**Employer (Grandtex International):** the door was closed because workers stole company property through it, and the company identifies the individuals involved; in five or six months of exclusive use of the front door no accident or security problem has occurred, and the company remains responsible for any work-related accident [Para 23][Para 26][Para 27].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the demand that the company open the back door for workers leaving at lunch and ensure their security on entering and leaving the factory [Para 38][Para 39].
The panel treated the question as one of management right under Article 2 of the Labour Law, which defines an enterprise as one or several establishments under the direct management of the employer, and followed Awards 17/03 and 18/03-Ho Hing, 28/04-Raffles Grand D'Angkor, 20/06-New Star, 17/07-Charm Textile and 116/07-Grace Sun in holding that this gives the employer the right to organise and manage the company reasonably and lawfully [Para 32][Para 33][Para 34]. On that basis it held the right to manage includes the right to determine the entrance and exit of the factory where reasonable and lawful [Para 34]. It found that opening the back door might again cause loss of property, as had already occurred, so closing it fell within that right [Para 35][Para 36]. It further found no evidence that the closure made workers late after lunch, caused accidents or affected safety; the workers gave reasons but no proof of an effect on work safety, and the panel could not find one [Para 37][Para 38]. The demand therefore failed.

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Choosing which gates workers use to enter and leave the factory is an exercise of the employer's right to organise and manage the establishment under Article 2 of the Labour Law, and the Council will uphold it unless the workers prove that the choice is unlawful or in fact endangers their safety [Para 34][Para 36][Para 37].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted the company remains responsible for workers who suffer work-related accidents, and that no security incident had occurred in the five or six months of exclusive front-door use; neither observation was necessary to the rejection [Para 26][Para 28].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The file is an unofficial English translation whose OCR has doubled whole clauses (the paragraph on the last unresolved issues repeats itself, as does the opening of the decision), fused words ("thecompany", "rejecthe", "withe"), and lost sentence openings throughout [Para 15][Para 39]. The exhibit list names three workers in theft announcements and a dismissed worker by name; those are individual names in the source and are not reproduced here [Para 17][Para 18]. The list of managerial rights is misnumbered, running from a stray "2-" to "4-" with items dropped between them, so the enumeration of prior awards cannot be reconstructed with confidence [Para 33][Para 34]. The case number in the header is printed as 47108 with the separator lost [Para 1].
