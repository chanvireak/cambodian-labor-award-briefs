# Award No. 115/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គា មិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 5 January 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed ឈីវ ភិរម្យ; worker-appointed លីវ សុវណ្ណា; chair គង់ ផល្លៈ) · Doc ID 140750

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គា មិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត, is a garment factory at Stung Meanchey, Meanchey district, Phnom Penh [Para 1]. Its workers' union, សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គា មិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត, put forward nine demands, of which four were settled in conciliation on 30 November 2006 and five were referred to the panel [Para 2][Para 17]. The five unresolved demands concerned fixed-term contracts of 6 months then 1 year, back pay for periods without work, retention of the $5 monthly attendance bonus during three days of legal absence and during up to five late arrivals caused by traffic jams, and re-election of worker representatives [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9]. At the hearing the union objected to the whole panel and walked out, saying it would not participate [Para 20][Para 21][Para 24].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural issue) Whether the panel could proceed and close the case in the absence of the claimant union, which walked out of the hearing without presenting any evidence? [Para 25][Para 27][Para 29]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គា មិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត):** The union objected to the composition of the panel, said the Council had repeatedly refused to consider its complaints in earlier cases and had been created only to collect money, and left the hearing room without arguing the five demands [Para 20][Para 23][Para 24].
**Employer (respondent — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គា មិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត):** The company maintained its position on each demand — first contracts of 3 months and second contracts of 6 months, no capacity to pay retroactive wages, and application of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000, which removes the $5 attendance bonus when a worker is late more than three times a month [Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 115/06 without examining any of the five demands, because the claimant union abandoned the hearing [Para 30].

The panel relied on rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council procedural rules annexed to Prakas 099, which provides that if a party fails to appear or has no representative in the proceedings, the panel may continue in that party's absence or may terminate the arbitration by issuing an award [Para 25]. It recorded that the union had been given the opportunity to defend the workers' claims and had instead chosen to walk out before the proceedings began and to abandon its chance to present evidence [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]. It also noted that the union gave no reason beyond its dissatisfaction with earlier awards in unrelated cases brought by the same federation [Para 27]. The panel therefore treated the union as having failed to appear and, under the rule, exercised its power to terminate the arbitration by award [Para 28][Para 29]. No documents were submitted by either party; the only material before the panel was the labour department's report 1832 and the conciliation minutes of 30 November and 15 November 2006 [Para 25]. No findings of fact were made [Para 25].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party that has been duly summoned walks out of the hearing before the proceedings begin and abandons its opportunity to present evidence, the panel may treat it as failing to appear and close the case by award without examining the merits, under rule 4.7 of the Prakas 099 procedural rules [Para 25][Para 28][Para 29].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the union's complaints about the Council's earlier decisions in other cases had no connection with the present file [Para 27].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The statement of the five demands is partly corrupted: the claim for fixed-term contracts is recorded as 6 months then 1 year against the company's 3 months then 6 months, and the fifth demand is numbered with a duplicated digit [Para 2][Para 3][Para 9]. The heading of the reasons reads "វិចាន ៤.៧" for what is plainly rule 4.7, and the operative paragraph repeats its own wording with a duplicated phrase [Para 25][Para 30]. The signature block trails off into unrelated OCR text [Para 31].
