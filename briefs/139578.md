# Award No. 126/14 — New Wise In Das Strial (Arbitration Council, 9 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom, appointed by the employer; An Nan, appointed by the workers; Ang Eng Thong, chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 139578

## 1. Facts
The company (ញូ វីស អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល), at Building BZ on Street 15, Sangkat Tuol Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, is a single-issue case: workers on strike demanded a 4,000 riel meal allowance for overtime worked between 16:00 and 18:00, against the 2,000 riel the employer said was its established practice. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4] The strike began on 19 May 2014; the employer wrote on 22 May asking for an order to stop it. [Para 14] Conciliation failed on one point. [Para 11][Para 12]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a 4,000 riel meal allowance for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 3][Para 10]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the allowance for the two overtime hours should be 4,000 riel, and the union's president told the secretariat he was negotiating with the building owner about the dispute rather than attending the pre-hearing meeting. [Para 3][Para 19]
**Employer (respondent):** it cannot pay that sum and continues to apply the old practice of 2,000 riel; it attended the pre-hearing meeting and reported that a minority had returned to work while most stayed out and pressured others not to return. [Para 4][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case 126/14 without deciding the meal-allowance claim, because the strike continued after its suspension order. [Para 48][Para 49]

The panel never reached the merits. It recorded that no hearing was held and therefore made no findings of fact on the issue in the non-conciliation report. [Para 29]

The decisive step was procedural. On 23 May 2014 the panel issued order No. 028/14 requiring the strike at the company to be suspended immediately and all strikers to return to work from the morning of 24 May 2014, in their own shifts, until the arbitration was complete. [Para 15] The workers did not comply: the strike continued, a minority returned and the majority stayed out, and the union reported that workers remained in front of the factory. [Para 16][Para 20][Para 22][Para 46]

Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the parties, while the panel is seised, to suspend every dispute measure — strike, lockout or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — and to attend every meeting the panel calls. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] The panel followed a long line of awards holding that it will not continue while a strike or lockout is under way: 59/06, 39/08, 137/08, 110/11, 104/13 and 119/13, and earlier 04/04 (IMS) and 18/04, the latter ordering strikers to stop and reserving the right to close the file if the order were breached. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] It also relied on 110/11 (Simano) and 17/13, where proceedings were terminated for the same reason. [Para 39][Para 40]

Finding that the workers were still striking and had not complied with order No. 028/14, the panel closed the file. [Para 47][Para 48]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered its suspension, the panel will not exercise its jurisdiction over the merits: Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the parties to suspend all dispute measures and attend every meeting during the arbitration, and breach of that obligation ends the proceedings rather than merely delaying them. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 47] The file may be reopened once the strike has actually stopped. [Para 35]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's reference to the 2004 IMS notification, under which the Council could not resolve a dispute while the parties retained the right to strike, is a historical illustration and was not applied to the present case, which turned on the breach of order No. 028/14. [Para 34]

- The conciliation report is numbered 515 in the procedural section and the ministry's list [Para 7][Para 24] but "514/14" where the referral is described [Para 12]; both carry 22 May 2014.
- The company name is transliterated inconsistently across the award (ញូ វីស អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល, ញូ វីស អ៊ិន ដាស្រៀ, ញ្ជូ វីស អ៊ិន ដាស្ទ្រៀល) and is evidently an OCR-damaged rendering of a Latin name that the award never prints [Para 1][Para 7][Para 49].
- The union president appears as ចេង ចំណែន once and ចេង ណេន three times [Para 19][Para 22][Para 43][Para 46]; the employer's administration head is given a female honorific in the header but "លោក" in the reasons [Para 1][Para 20].
- The pre-hearing time is printed "ម៉ោង ១ និង ៣០ នាទី" in the notice [Para 9] and as "ម៉ោង ១០០ និង ៤០ នាទី" and "ម៉ោង ១៖ ២០១៧ ៤០ នាទី" in the findings [Para 19][Para 43].
- The employer-side arbitrator's name is lost to OCR noise in the signature block, leaving only An Nan and Ang Eng Thong legible [Para 50].
