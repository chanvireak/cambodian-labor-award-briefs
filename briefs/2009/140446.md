# Dissenting Opinion in Award No. 140/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន (Arbitration Council, 10 November 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: តល់ វឌ្ឍនា (employer-appointed), ស៊ីន គឹម (worker-appointed, dissenting), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន (president) · Doc ID 140446

## 1. Facts
This document is the written dissent of the worker-appointed arbitrator, filed under Article 37 of Prakas No. 099/04 against the majority award in the same file; it states the majority's decision but argues the opposite result. [Para 1][Para 165][Para 200]
The respondent was ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន, a garment factory employing about 898 workers; the claimant was សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ, registered by certificate No. 1640 of 6 March 2009. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]
Of six unresolved demands, four were settled during the hearing, leaving issue 1 — reinstatement with back pay of the union president, Mr V — and issue 6 — reinstatement of the union treasurer, Mr N. Both held fixed-duration contracts that the factory did not renew. [Para 19][Para 48][Para 65]
Each had worked about two years. Mr V's final contract ran to 16 August 2009, and the company gave notice on 1 August 2009, at 03:52, offering 15 days' pay in lieu, July wages, five per cent contract damages and other benefits, a total of 260,645 USD. Mr N was offered 179,104 USD and had not collected it by the hearing. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 54][Para 69][Para 70]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the non-renewal of Mr V's contract, justified by a performance evaluation of 38/100, was in truth a dismissal before expiry and anti-union discrimination, so that reinstatement and back pay are owed? [Para 82][Para 99]
2. (Issue 6) Whether the non-renewal of Mr N's contract, justified by an evaluation of 37/100, was likewise anti-union discrimination requiring reinstatement? [Para 122][Para 148]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ):** neither man had ever been disciplined; the company negotiated only with សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ and not with this union; both were removed because of their union office and their help with workers' protests, and both should be reinstated with back pay. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 72][Para 98]
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន):** there was no anti-union discrimination; it discussed matters with both unions; each contract simply expired by effluxion of time and non-renewal followed evaluations showing poor performance, so the statutory benefits were paid and no reinstatement is owed. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 73][Para 112][Para 123]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the majority declined reinstatement and back pay for both men; the dissenting arbitrator would have granted both. [Para 121][Para 160][Para 200]
The majority reasoned that a fixed-duration contract expires of its own force under Article 73 of the Labour Law, that neither party can compel renewal absent agreement, and that the company had given 15 days' notice and paid the notice allowance, wages, five per cent contract damages and other benefits, so it had discharged its obligations. It accepted the evaluations of 38/100 and 37/100 as the real reason for non-renewal and found no proven discrimination, applying Articles 12 and 279 and holding that the burden of proving discrimination lies on the workers, who gave no specific reasons when objecting to the evaluation forms. [Para 96][Para 97][Para 117][Para 119][Para 156]
The dissent takes a different view of the facts. Mr V's contract ran to 16 August 2009 but notice was served on 1 August and he was released at once, which in the dissent's reading is termination before expiry, not expiry — the notice letter's own wording ("not able to continue") being decisive, and 15 days' pay in lieu cannot convert an early release into an expiry. [Para 166][Para 167][Para 181]
Because Mr V was a registered union president and the company knew it, Article 293 of the Labour Law required labour-inspector authorisation before any dismissal of a union leader; the company admitted it sought no authorisation and merely copied the notice to the Ministry, so the dismissal was unlawful and reinstatement followed. [Para 178][Para 181][Para 183]
The dissent also rejects the evaluations: each was a single, one-off scoring with no prior oral or written warning, no notification of the score to the worker and no evidence of how often evaluations were carried out, so they cannot be trusted as the ground for ending two years' service; Mr N, though not one of the three protected leaders, was caught by the same discriminatory pattern and also deserved reinstatement. [Para 186][Para 189][Para 195][Para 199]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The majority's rule: a fixed-duration contract expires by effluxion of time under Article 73 and a panel will not order its renewal or reinstatement where the employer paid the statutory terminal benefits and produced a performance evaluation supporting non-renewal, the burden of proving anti-union discrimination resting on the workers. [Para 96][Para 117][Para 121]

**Obiter dicta:** the dissent's view that an unsupported single-round evaluation is weak evidence of incapacity, and that a worker should be told his score so that he can improve before non-renewal. [Para 187][Para 189]
