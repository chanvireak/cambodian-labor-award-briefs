# Award No. 24/09 — Maxlin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 March 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phallack presiding; Chhiv Phyrum, employer nominee; Tuon Siphann, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140363

## 1. Facts
Maxlin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, at Trapang Krorsang village, Bek Chan commune, Angsnuol district, Kandal province, faced five demands from the Local Union of the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU): review of the skills bonus rate, expansion of the canteen and parking lot, more fans in the packaging section, and two further demands not reproduced in this translation. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 4][Para 5] Conciliation on 21 January 2009 settled two of the five; the three non-conciliated issues — skills bonus, canteen and parking, and packaging fans — reached the Council on 17 February 2009. [Para 8][Para 9] Both parties were absent without any reason from the hearing of 27 February 2009, so the panel made no finding of fact on any issue. [Para 10][Para 13] On each of the three issues the employer's recorded answer was that it would follow existing practice or would try to do so. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 6]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, both parties having been duly notified and neither appearing nor giving reasons, the panel should terminate the arbitration and close the case by award under Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules? [Para 14][Para 24]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the KYFTU local union at Maxlin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd demanded a review of the skills bonus rate, an expanded canteen and parking lot, and additional fans in the packaging section, and did not appear at the hearing or send a representative. [Para 1][Para 4][Para 5][Para 22]
**Employer (respondent):** the company answered the bonus demand by saying it would follow its existing practice and the other two by saying it would try to do so, and it too failed to attend and appointed no representative. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 6][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed Case 24/09-Maxlin, leaving the three demands undecided. [Para 24]
Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 permits the panel either to proceed in the absence of a party duly invited or to terminate the arbitration by award, and Rule 4.7 of the Annex to that Prakas says the same; in either case the panel must first be satisfied that the parties were notified of the date, time and venue. [Para 14][Para 15]
Applying Awards 30/05-Maurea, 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M & V (Branch 3), 95/08-Yung Wah, 132/08-GHG, 138/08-Marlyn, 151/08-Wilson Garment and 09/09-Nimbus Textile, the panel required three conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of reasons. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]
It found written notification sent to both parties in the Secretariat's regular form and confirmed by telephone, satisfying the first condition. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 21][Para 22] Neither the workers nor the employer appeared, and neither authorised a representative under Clauses 19 and 20 of the Prakas, satisfying the second. [Para 22][Para 23] Neither gave the Secretariat any reason, satisfying the third. [Para 23][Para 24]
The panel therefore ended the case without reaching the merits of the skills bonus, the canteen and parking, or the packaging fans. [Para 13][Para 24]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties, though duly notified, stay away from the hearing without explanation, the panel may close the file by award rather than adjudicate, once notification, non-appearance and absence of reasons are each established; the termination is procedural and leaves every demand undecided. [Para 15][Para 23][Para 24]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that two of the five demands had already been settled at the conciliation of 21 January 2009, a fact it did not need in order to close the case. [Para 8][Para 9]
