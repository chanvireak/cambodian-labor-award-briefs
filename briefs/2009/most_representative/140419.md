# Awards Nos. 101/09 and 106/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីមលី (ខេមបូឌា) ភីធី អ៊ី ("Jimaly") (Arbitration Council, 7 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ឹង សុទ្ធី, employer party; អាន វីរៈ, worker party; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាម chair) · Doc ID 140419

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីមលី (ខេមបូឌា) ភីធី អ៊ី, opened in 2006 at Trapeang Toul, Ang Snuol, Kandal, and employs about 250 workers. [Para 1][Para 90] The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព អភិវឌ្ឍន៍ ការងារ and its factory local, which claims about 900 members but has never been recognised as most representative. [Para 1][Para 93][Para 96] The two cases were heard together: case 101/09 raised 24 demands of which 18 went unresolved, and case 106/09 raised one further demand after the company suspended and dismissed 17 union leaders and committee members following the strike of 22 July 2009. [Para 29][Para 31][Para 145][Para 251] Most of the 101/09 issues were settled or withdrawn at the hearing, leaving issues 1, 3, 4, 5 and one limb of issue 7. [Para 35][Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1 of case 101/09) Whether the company must pay wages between the 5th day of each month rather than on the 10th? [Para 97][Para 182]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must add a 2,000 riel daily allowance for work performed on Sundays and public holidays? [Para 109][Para 200]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must pay a US$5 monthly skill allowance to skilled workers? [Para 118][Para 222]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the company must pay a US$5 monthly travel allowance? [Para 127][Para 226]
5. (Issue 7) Whether the company must install 6 fans in the staff canteen? [Para 136][Para 230]
6. (Issue 1 of case 106/09) Whether the company must take back the 17 union leaders and committee members it suspended and dismissed after the strike of 22 July 2009? [Para 145][Para 251]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** wages should come earlier to cover rent, food and remittances; Sunday and holiday work deserves an extra 2,000 riel a day beyond the statutory premium, skilled workers deserve US$5 and all workers US$5 for travel, as neighbouring factories pay; the canteen is a low tin roof with no airflow; and the 17 were dismissed only for their union role, without any warning or poor-performance record. [Para 103][Para 113][Para 121][Para 128][Para 231][Para 178]
**Employer (respondent):** wages are paid on the 10th because payroll runs through a Singapore account and bank clearance, and a March 2009 agreement fixed a deadline no later than the 10th; it already pays 200% for Sunday and holiday work; the law requires no skill or travel allowance and it gives discretionary bonuses instead; the canteen is open-sided and adequate at the 10:30 break; the 17 incited an unlawful strike and performed poorly. [Para 100][Para 107][Para 115][Para 123][Para 244][Para 252][Para 255]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the five claims in case 101/09 were dismissed, and in case 106/09 the company was ordered to take back all 17 suspended and dismissed union leaders and committee members. [Para 274][Para 275][Para 276][Para 277][Para 278][Para 279]

On the wage date, the panel held that Article 116(2) requires at least two payments a month for wage-earning staff and one for salaried staff but fixes no day, and that Article 2 gives the employer the right to organise the enterprise; the company's practice was regular and its explanation reasonable, so the claim failed. [Para 185][Para 187][Para 195][Para 199]

On issues 3, 4 and 5 the panel treated each as a demand for a benefit above what the Labour Law requires and therefore an interest dispute, which it refused to hear because the claimant union was not most representative and because an award would become a collective agreement binding all workers for a year under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 209][Para 212][Para 215][Para 221][Para 225][Para 229] On issue 3 it also found the 200% holiday and Sunday rates already satisfied Articles 139 as amended, 164 and Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999. [Para 207]

On issue 7 it read Article 229 and Prakas No. 125/01 and No. 147/02 as requiring adequate ventilation and temperature, found the canteen open-sided with no walls and used at 10:30 before the sun is fierce, and dismissed the fan claim. [Para 232][Para 238][Para 246][Para 250]

On case 106/09 the panel held that Article 330 makes violence during a strike serious misconduct but that leading or joining a strike is not, applied Article 333's bar on punishing workers for strike participation, relied on the Kandal labour department's letter No. 292/09 refusing the dismissals, and found no evidence of poor performance or prior warning; it ordered reinstatement of all 17. [Para 253][Para 258][Para 260][Para 266][Para 270][Para 273]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that is not the most representative in the enterprise cannot bring an interest dispute — a demand for benefits exceeding the Labour Law — because an award on it would operate as a collective agreement binding every worker for a year; such claims are refused without regard to their merits. By contrast, a dismissal grounded on participation in a strike is unlawful, and where the labour inspectorate has refused the dismissal request and no poor-performance evidence or warning exists, reinstatement of the dismissed union leaders must be ordered. [Para 215][Para 221][Para 263][Para 273]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that strike participation is protected activity and that the canteen's tin roof and open sides suffice in the morning are illustrative rather than necessary to the orders. [Para 246][Para 258]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed at least six ways, including "ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីមលី ( ខេមបូឌា) ភីធី អ៉ី", "ជី បលី ( ខេមបូឌា) ភីធី អិល ធី ឱ" and "ជិប លិ", so the registered form cannot be confirmed [Para 1][Para 55][Para 75].
- The panel's composition is contradicted by the signature block: the header assigns the employer seat to អ៉ឹង សុទ្ធី and the worker seat to អាន វីរៈ, while the signatures reverse them [Para 1][Para 281].
- The 17 affected workers are named individually in a table at [Para 160]; none is named in this brief.
- Issue numbering is unstable: the unresolved list is given as 18 points but the panel's remaining issues are numbered 1, 3, 4, 5, 7 and a second case's issue 1, and issue 3 of case 101/09 is described as an allowance "on top of" the statutory premium at [Para 109] but as the 200% premium itself at [Para 200].
- The demand is printed as "២០០០ រៀលើ" (a typographical corruption of 2,000 riel) at [Para 5] and "២០០០ រៀល" at [Para 221].
