# Award No. 96/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ូត វ៉េ ឡារី សាកា ហ្វេ សិន ហ្វេក ធើរី លឺ មឹតធីត ("Sout Vau") (Arbitration Council, 7 August 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (លឹ តៃ សេង, employer party; អាន វីរៈ, worker party; គង់ ផល្ហៈ chair) · Doc ID 140416

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ូត វ៉េ ឡារី សាកា ហ្វេ សិន ហ្វេក ធើរី លឺ មឹតធីត, employed about 600 workers at Toul Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh. [Para 1][Para 30] The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ ហជីព-សហជីព ការងារ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា, registered in 2002 with about 224 members and not the most representative union in the factory. [Para 1][Para 31][Para 32] Of eight demands, seven were settled at the hearing, leaving only the demand that the company recognise the union's newly elected second-term leadership, which the Ministry of Labour had endorsed by letter No. 116 of 19 February 2009. [Para 2][Para 20][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must recognise the union's new second-term leadership, which the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training has recognised by letter No. 116 dated 19 February 2009, or whether recognition lies elsewhere? [Para 2][Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union holds a 2002 registration certificate and a 2009 Ministry letter recognising its new leadership, so the company is bound to deal with that leadership. [Para 34]
**Employer (respondent):** it will not recognise the new leadership because the union has produced no fresh 2009 registration certificate from the Ministry. [Para 35][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim that the company must recognise the new union leadership was rejected, and the company was instead ordered to cooperate with the leadership recognised by the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training. [Para 51][Para 52]

The panel applied Article 268 of the Labour Law, which requires a professional organisation seeking the rights the law confers to deposit its statutes and the list of its responsible officers with the Ministry so that it may be registered, and to renew that deposit whenever the statutes or the leadership change. [Para 39][Para 40] It read Articles 1, 3 and 4 of Prakas No. 021 of 15 February 2006 as saying the same thing. [Para 41] On the evidence, the union held registration certificate No. 268 of 11 April 2002 and the Ministry's letter of 19 February 2009 recognising the second-term leadership, which the panel treated as a legally effective instrument confirming that the election had followed procedure. [Para 42][Para 45] The company's demand for a further registration certificate was therefore not a reasonable ground. [Para 46] The panel nonetheless held that recognition of a union's leadership is not within the employer's competence but the Ministry's, so the union's claim was misconceived, and it recast the relief as an order to cooperate with the recognised leadership in resolving workers' disputes. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the Ministry of Labour has recognised a union's new leadership under Article 268 and Prakas No. 021, the employer cannot insist on a fresh registration certificate as a condition of dealing with it; but an arbitration panel will not order an employer to "recognise" that leadership, because recognition is an act of the Ministry, and the appropriate order is that the employer cooperate with the leadership the Ministry has recognised. [Para 45][Para 47]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the union should seek the company's cooperation rather than its recognition, and that a cooperative relationship better protects workers' interests, is advice rather than a rule. [Para 49]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The header and the party blocks are heavily damaged: the composition line, the employer's address and the list of representatives at [Para 1] and [Para 2] contain bracketed fragments, stray digits and English text from the Council's letterhead, so the employer's registered name and one arbitrator's given name cannot be confirmed from the face of the document.
- The employer name is printed four different ways across the award — "ស៊ូត វ៉េ" [Para 1], "ស៊ូត វ៉ើ" [Para 30], "ស៊ូត វ៉ៅ" [Para 38] and "ស៊ូត វោី" [Para 22].
- The chair is printed "គង់ ផល្ហៈ" in the composition list but "គង់ ផល្លៈ" in the signature block [Para 54]; the employer-party arbitrator appears as "លឹ តៃ សេង" and "លី តៃ សេង" in the same two places.
- The award date is printed only as 7 August 2009 in the header [Para 1]; the hearing is recorded as 29 July 2009 [Para 14].
- The registration instrument is cited as Prakas No. 268 at [Para 36] and letter No. 116 at [Para 24] for the same 2009 recognition, so the two are not clearly distinguished.
