# Award No. 127/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត (Tack Faat Garment) and the Democratic Garment Workers' Union Federation of Cambodia (Arbitration Council, 6 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Ven Vev (worker-appointed), Koy Nam (president) · Doc ID 140437

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត, printed in the header as ភភេ ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន (ចាំ បូណុ) អែល អិទីឌី, is a garment factory on National Road 2, Chak Angre Leu, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh with about 1,834 workers, of whom about 131 are men. [Para 2][Para 28]
The **សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា**, the Democratic Garment Workers' Union Federation of Cambodia, with about 800 members in the plant but not the most representative union there, brought five demands; two points were settled in conciliation and a further point at the hearing, leaving issues 2 and 3 for decision. [Para 15][Para 29][Para 30]
Issue 2 asked the company to carry out point 5 of Award No. 59/05 of 27 October 2005, which ordered repayment of a 10,200 riel health-check fee; issue 3 asked it to stop charging idle days against annual leave. [Para 31][Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the panel has power to order the company to comply with point 5 of the Council's own Award No. 59/05 of 27 October 2005? [Para 31]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must stop deducting workers' annual leave to cover periods when it has no work for them? [Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), through the Democratic Garment Workers' Union Federation of Cambodia:** Award No. 59/05 was binding when issued, the company never complied and never appealed successfully, and this claim is a new one seeking performance of that binding order, which the Ministry itself referred to the Council; on annual leave, the employer's lack of work is its own responsibility and it may not shift that cost onto workers' leave, whether under Article 170 or otherwise. [Para 5][Para 32][Para 39][Para 40]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត:** it cannot comply with the 2005 award because it objected to it at the time, asked at that hearing for the order to be made non-binding, and its lawyer lodged an objection with the Minister; on annual leave, many workers had accumulated 20 to 50 unused days, so it arranged leave only up to 50% of the balance during the downturn, and it kept wages, the attendance bonus and the seniority allowance unchanged. [Para 6][Para 35][Para 41][Para 44]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel declined issue 2 for want of jurisdiction and ordered the company to stop deducting annual leave without the workers' agreement when it has no work to give; the award is non-binding and becomes permanent eight days after notification. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86]
On issue 2 the panel considered Article 42 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which deals with what follows when a party has accepted an award as binding, and found that once the period for objection has expired and the award has become final, the Council cannot itself enforce it; Article 46 instead allows the interested party to ask a court of competent jurisdiction to recognise and enforce the award. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 55][Para 57]
The panel noted the employer's account that in 2005 it had asked for its award to be non-binding and the arbitrator had refused because the company had already signed an agreement to accept it, so the company had lost its right of objection; even so, the panel held that compulsory enforcement is a court function, and it followed Awards 54/04 and 08/06 in declining the demand. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 58][Para 60]
On issue 3 the panel applied Article 170 of the Labour Law, under which annual leave is taken at Khmer New Year unless the parties agree otherwise and the employer must notify the labour inspector of any agreed alternative, and Article 171, which allows special leave to be charged against annual leave only in defined circumstances and only once the worker's leave has been used. [Para 62][Para 68][Para 71]
It found that the parties had no agreement authorising the practice, that the workers had not accepted it since April 2009, and that Article 71(11) requires the labour inspector's authorisation before a contract may be suspended for economic difficulty; the employer had not obtained that authorisation before imposing leave. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 77][Para 80][Para 82]
The panel accordingly ordered the practice stopped and held that, where leave is to be scheduled outside the Khmer New Year period, the employer must fix dates individually, obtain the workers' agreement and notify the labour inspector. [Para 76][Para 83]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The Council has no jurisdiction to compel a party to perform an award it has already issued: once the objection period expires and the award becomes final, recognition and enforcement belong to the competent court under Prakas No. 099, so a fresh claim framed as enforcement must be declined. Annual leave, by contrast, may be charged against idle time only with the workers' agreement and after the labour inspector has authorised the suspension of the contracts. [Para 50][Para 55][Para 60][Para 75]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the employer had described the leave arrangement as an act of goodwill prompted by mutual sympathy during an economic downturn, and that this did not create a legal basis for the deduction. [Para 44]
