# Award No. 141/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន (Hoya (Cambodia) Garment) (Arbitration Council, 28 October 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: លី តៃ សេង (employer-appointed), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន (president) · Doc ID 140445

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន began operating in August 2005 on National Road 2, Stung Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, and employed 989 workers, with two unions inside the plant — សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ and CCAWDU — neither of which held most-representative status. [Para 2][Para 34][Para 35]
The claimant was សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ, which the company recognised as having 367 members because it deducted their dues. [Para 36][Para 37]
Of four demands, one was settled at conciliation and a further point was settled at the hearing, leaving two: a 5,000 riel meal allowance for overtime worked on holidays and Sundays, and a further 1,000 riel on top of the allowance already paid for overtime. [Para 17][Para 21][Para 22]
Overtime ran from 16:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and the factory occasionally worked Sundays and holidays, most recently Sunday 27 September 2009; the company paid 1,000 riel a day for meals but paid it only once every two weeks. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 54][Para 47]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance of 5,000 riel for overtime worked on holidays and Sundays, paid on the same day? [Para 38]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay a further 1,000 riel on top of the meal allowance already paid for overtime? [Para 96]
3. (Issue 1, second limb) Whether the meal allowance for holiday and Sunday overtime must be paid once a week? [Para 64]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ):** holidays and Sundays are rest days, so work on them is overtime and merits 5,000 riel for food, paid the same day; no food is sold near the factory on those days, and the union dropped its demand for same-day payment in favour of weekly payment so that workers can buy meals. [Para 38][Para 40][Para 49][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមិន):** it pays the 1,000 riel fixed by Notification No. 017, in a lump sum every two weeks on the 15th or 16th as it has since 2005, and cannot pay 5,000 riel because of the global economic crisis, fluctuating orders and falling prices. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel dismissed the 5,000 riel and the additional 1,000 riel claims but ordered the employer to pay the meal allowance for holiday and Sunday overtime once a week. [Para 110][Para 112][Para 111][Para 113]
On issue 1 the panel applied Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000, which entitles a worker doing overtime to a 1,000 riel meal allowance per day or a free meal, and read it as entitling the worker to be able to buy food, not to any particular sum above 1,000 riel. A 5,000 riel allowance exceeding the notification is a dispute over interests, and the panel will not decide such a claim where the union bringing it is not most representative, under Prakas No. 099/04 Article 43 and its earlier awards. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 70][Para 77][Para 78][Para 81]
On issue 2 the panel found the company had already paid 1,000 riel for the 16:00 to 18:00 overtime, exactly as the notification requires, so the further 1,000 riel was likewise a claim beyond the law and was dismissed on the same most-representative ground. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 106]
On timing, however, the panel held the notification fixes no payment date, and that the allowance must reach workers within a reasonable period so they can buy food for the overtime they have worked; it therefore ordered weekly payment without requiring daily or monthly payment, following award 47/07. [Para 89][Para 91][Para 92][Para 95][Para 108]
The award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification unless a party objects. [Para 114]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker on overtime is entitled to a 1,000 riel meal allowance per day or a free meal, and no more; a demand above that figure is a dispute over interests that a panel will not decide unless the claimant union is most representative, but the panel may still order that the lawful allowance be paid within a reasonable time, such as weekly, so that the worker can buy food. [Para 66][Para 92][Para 81]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that no food is sold near the factory on holidays and Sundays, and its observation that paying the allowance often enough for a worker to eat supports the employer's own continuity of production. [Para 40][Para 87]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is severe. The same meal allowance is printed as 1,000 riel, as 10 riel and as 50 riel in different passages, and the claimed sum appears as both 5,000 and 500 riel. The hearing is recorded as starting at 15:00 in one place and 14:00 in another, and the invitation letters carry impossible dates (12 December 2009 and "412" October 2009). [Para 32][Para 33][Para 67][Para 82][Para 15][Para 19]
The company name appears as ហូយៀ, ហូបៀ, ហួយៀ, ហូហ្វេ and ហូរ, and the union's name is variously rendered. The conciliation report number is given in several forms. Individual workers, shop stewards and the employer's administrative officer survive only as corrupted strings and are identified here by role. [Para 2][Para 18][Para 29]
