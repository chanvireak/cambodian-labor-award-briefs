# Award No. 109/09 — USA Fully Field (Cambodia) Garment Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Youchhun, Liv Sovann, Ngann Somunneath presiding) · Doc ID 140423

## 1. Facts
The respondent is ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វុលលី ហ្វៀល (កម្ពុជា) ហ្គាម៉ិន អូ អិលធីឌី — USA Fully Field (Cambodia) Garment Co Ltd — on National Road 2, Chak Angre Krom, Phnom Penh, operating since 1999 with about 500 workers. Two unions organise the plant, the Free Workers Union and the Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ), neither union was found to be the most representative. The federation filed ten demands on working conditions. One was settled in conciliation, four more were settled at the hearing, and demand 6 was withdrawn by the workers, leaving eight issues for decision. [Para 3][Para 53][Para 54][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer may end the long-standing practice of letting workers take annual leave in advance, beyond the statutory days, without losing the USD 5 attendance bonus, and deduct wage and bonus for leave already taken? [Para 67][Para 130]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must keep piece rates (តម្លៃបុង) stable for a given style instead of changing them frequently without notice or explanation? [Para 169]
3. (Issue 3) Whether workers with four or more years' service are entitled to an extra USD 1 per month of seniority pay on top of the USD 5 set by Prakas 017? [Para 186]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the employer owes full wage for periods when it gave no work, having suspended operations without the labour inspector's authorisation? [Para 215]
5. (Issue 5) Whether workers who volunteer to work on a public holiday or Sunday are entitled to a meal or 5,000 riel, or only to the 1,000 riel meal allowance? [Para 231]
6. (Issue 7) Whether maternity leave pay of 50 per cent must be paid in a lump sum before the leave begins rather than month by month? [Para 263]
7. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must build a nursery and nursing room, or may instead pay 3 cans of milk and USD 20 a month? [Para 277]
8. (Issue 9) Whether the employer must deduct union dues of 1,000 riel per member on the strength of the union's list, or may require a fresh monthly list and take thumbprints itself? [Para 305]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Khmer Youth Union Federation):** the employer unilaterally destroyed practices the workforce had relied on for a decade — advance annual leave without loss of the USD 5 bonus, stable piece rates, full wage when no work was given — and the law entitled them to the extra dollar of seniority pay, a meal allowance of 5,000 riel on holidays, lump-sum maternity pay, a nursery, and dues deducted from the list the union supplied. [Para 70][Para 92][Para 98][Para 103]
**Employer (respondent):** it acted within its management right to set piece rates and to rebalance income across the production line, applied Prakas 017 as written (USD 5 seniority pay, capped), paid maternity benefit monthly under the 2008 agreement, had no space for a nursery, and could not deduct dues without a proper monthly list verified by the workers themselves. [Para 82][Para 187][Para 267][Para 316]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must restore the USD 5 attendance bonus to the 184 workers from whom it was cut in April 2009 and continue the advance-leave practice; fix piece rates for each style for a reasonable period after a seven-day study and consult before any change; pay 100 per cent wage to Khmer Youth Union members for unauthorised work suspensions; pay 50 per cent wage and allowances for 90 days of maternity leave before the leave starts; find a suitable room for a nursery and nursing room; and deduct dues on valid written requests. The seniority-pay claim, the 5,000 riel meal claim and the milk-and-USD 20 alternative were dismissed. [Para 322][Para 323][Para 326][Para 331][Para 333][Para 336]

On issue 1 the panel held that the practice, running since 1999 and accepted by both sides, was a past practice (ការអនុវត្តកន្លងមក) that could only be ended by lawful agreement or by showing that circumstances made it impossible to continue; the employer produced no notice of the change it said it had given, and the 11 May 2009 agreement it relied on was signed only by the Free Workers Union and five of its members, so it could not bind Khmer Youth members. [Para 142][Para 74][Para 78][Para 154]

On issue 2 the panel accepted that the employer may set piece rates lawfully and reasonably, but found it had changed rates from one day to the next for the same style with no notice or explanation, which was not reasonable; it ordered a study within seven days and stable rates per style, with consultation before any later revision. [Para 179][Para 181][Para 184]

On issue 3 the panel read Prakas 017 and found it clear: USD 5 a month for four years' service and no automatic annual increment, so the claim was for a benefit beyond the law; because the union was not the most representative in the plant it also lacked standing to bring an interest dispute, and the claim was dismissed on both grounds. [Para 194][Para 201][Para 214]

On issue 4 the panel applied Articles 71 and 72 of the Labour Law: suspension of the contract requires notification to, and authorisation from, the labour inspector, failing which the employer owes 100 per cent wage even when no work is given. It could not establish who was laid off, in which section or for how long, so it refused the retrospective reimbursement but ordered full wage for future unauthorised suspensions. [Para 219][Para 224][Para 226][Para 327]

On issue 5 the panel held that voluntary work on a public holiday is not overtime, so the 5,000 riel claim failed; the entitlement under Prakas 017 is one meal or 1,000 riel a day, which it ordered for Sunday work. [Para 247][Para 262][Para 329]

On issue 7 the panel applied Articles 182 and 183: 50 per cent of wage and allowances for 90 days, payable before the leave begins; the monthly payment under the 2006 agreement gave less than the law and could not be applied. [Para 265][Para 271][Para 276]

On issue 8 the panel applied Article 186: an employer with more than 100 women workers must provide a nursing room and nursery, and paying milk or cash does not discharge that duty; it dismissed the alternative claim but ordered the company to find a suitable room in or near the factory. [Para 281][Para 298][Para 333]

On issue 9 the panel applied Article 129: dues may be deducted only on each worker's written, revocable authorisation, and the employer may not demand a monthly list or collect thumbprints itself, that being interference in union affairs. [Para 309][Para 318][Para 336]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A practice that has been applied clearly, consistently and with the acceptance of both sides over many years becomes an enforceable term of the employment relationship; the employer cannot end it unilaterally but must either conclude a lawful agreement with the workers affected or show that circumstances make continued performance impossible. [Para 137][Para 142]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that an award resolving an interest dispute becomes a one-year collective agreement under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, and that a union lacking most-representative status cannot bring such a dispute, were not necessary to the outcome. [Para 205][Para 210]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan is heavily degraded: the award-number suffix, the company's Khmer name and several statutory citations are partly unreadable, and the presiding arbitrator's name is printed two ways. Worker and union-official names appear in the source and are reduced to roles here. Figures conflict: the claimant group is given as 184 workers in the reasons and order but as 18 in one passage; the meal allowance is quoted as 2,000 riel in one recital of Prakas 017 and 1,000 riel in the next; the 2008 agreement is dated 22 February in most places and 26 February in one; and the union's membership is printed as 240 with a damaged digit. The award date is 25 September 2009 throughout. [Para 167][Para 168][Para 233][Para 234][Para 215]
