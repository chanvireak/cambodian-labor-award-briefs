# Award No. 143/09 — Sino Fair (Cambodia) Ltd and the Free Workers' Union of the Sino Fair factory (Arbitration Council, 6 November 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (chair គង់ ផល្លៈ, printed also as កង់ ផល្លៈ; employer-party nominee លី តៃ សេង; worker-party nominee អន ណន) · Doc ID 140450

## 1. Facts
Sino Fair (Cambodia) Ltd — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ីណូ ភែរ (ខេមបូឌា) — is a garment factory at Kouk Chambak, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh, with about 100 workers. [Para 29] In March 2009 it closed the night shift (18:00–03:00) and moved 28 workers to 14:00–22:00; they refused, citing the danger of travelling home late, and struck from late August to 16 September 2009. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37] A union was formed by election on 02 September 2009 with about 50 participants, and the company was notified on 05 September 2009 through its security guard; the union was still unregistered at the hearing. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] Conciliation on 30 September 2009 left eight demands unresolved, including 50 per cent of wages during suspension with annual leave and the re-election of worker delegates; the panel never ruled on those two. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 7][Para 16] On 21 September 2009 the company dismissed three union office-holders without notice, saying only that they were floating workers. [Para 183][Para 212]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must return the 28 night-shift workers to the day shift from 07:00 to 16:00? [Para 2][Para 101]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must supply sufficient drinking water and water for use in the toilets, and keep the toilets clean? [Para 3][Para 115]
3. (Issue 4) Whether overtime must be organised on a voluntary basis, without warnings or discipline for refusal? [Para 4][Para 139]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the company must educate its Chinese team leaders to use appropriate language towards workers? [Para 5][Para 150]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the company must provide enough fans in all sections of the workplace? [Para 6][Para 163]
6. (Issue 8) Whether the company must reinstate the workers it dismissed? [Para 8][Para 180]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — the Free Workers' Union, សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ, acting for 24 workers who mandated it):** the shift change was unreasonable because a 22:00 finish leaves women travelling home at night; drinking water ran out three or four times a month and the toilets had none for four or five days a month; overtime was extracted by warnings; a machine supervisor harassed women workers with scissors and a tape measure; two or three fans cannot cool a hall holding 100 workers; and the dismissals, days after the union notified the company, were anti-union discrimination. [Para 36][Para 42][Para 43][Para 53][Para 62][Para 165][Para 211]
**Employer (respondent — Sino Fair (Cambodia) Ltd):** did not appear and gave no reason, filing no response to any demand. [Para 18][Para 57][Para 78]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the parties to negotiate the shift question, ordered the company to supply sufficient drinking water and toilet water and keep the toilets clean, to organise overtime voluntarily, to stop all improper conduct by its managers, to provide sufficient fans, and to reinstate the three named workers. [Para 219][Para 220][Para 221][Para 222][Para 223][Para 224][Para 225]

Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 allows the panel to proceed where a duly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason, as it had done in Awards No. 53/04 and 63/04; the company was summoned, stayed away and offered no explanation, so the panel heard the case in its absence and on the claimant's evidence alone. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 81][Para 84]

On standing, the only union that attended was unregistered, and under Article 268 of the Labour Law a professional organisation acquires the rights the Law recognises, including representation of members, only on registration; deemed registration after two months of ministerial silence had not run. [Para 86][Para 88][Para 91][Para 92] Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 nonetheless lets a non-party represent claimants under a written mandate, so the union could act for those who mandated it — but only 24 mandates were valid, the rest bearing no clear subject and only thumbprints, so the panel confined itself to those 24 workers. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 98][Para 100]

On the shift, Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to manage the enterprise, and earlier awards uphold shift changes that are lawful and reasonable; no reason for this change was established because the employer was absent, and Prakas No. 80 SKBY of 01 March 1999 requires a rest area or safe transport for workers finishing a night shift, so the panel directed the parties to negotiate between 07:00–16:00 and 14:00–22:00. [Para 104][Para 106][Para 109][Para 113] Drinking water is required by Prakas No. 054 of 10 February 2000 and toilet water by Article 229 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 052; the panel found the company in breach of both. [Para 116][Para 126][Para 131][Para 137] Article 4 of Prakas No. 80 makes overtime voluntary and forbids discipline for refusal, so the warnings were unlawful. [Para 142][Para 143][Para 148] The Labour Law contains no harassment offence, so the panel applied Article 31 of the Constitution and Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights to the supervisor's conduct and ordered it stopped. [Para 152][Para 154][Para 160][Para 162] Heat is governed by Article 229 and Prakas No. 147 SKBY of 11 June 2002, and two or three fans cannot serve 100 workers. [Para 166][Para 168][Para 179] Finally, the three dismissed workers held fixed-term contracts of under six months, so no notice was due on expiry; but dismissal days after the union notified the company, with no other worker dismissed then, showed an intent to remove union members contrary to Articles 12 and 279. [Para 182][Para 205][Para 208][Para 215][Para 217]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that is duly summoned and stays away without explanation loses its chance to answer the claim: the panel may hear the case in absentia on the claimant's evidence under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099. A union not yet registered under Article 268 of the Labour Law has no statutory right to represent workers, but may still act for those who give it a written mandate that identifies its object, under Article 19 of the same Prakas. Where union office-holders are dismissed within days of the employer being notified of the union's formation and no other worker is dismissed at that time, anti-union discrimination under Articles 12 and 279 is established and reinstatement follows. [Para 80][Para 95][Para 99][Para 208][Para 217]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the Labour Law has no offence covering harassment of women at work, so that the Constitution and the Universal Declaration must be applied, concerns a gap in the statute rather than a ground of the order. [Para 152][Para 153]

- The chair's name is printed as កង់ ផល្លៈ at the head of the award and គង់ ផល្លៈ at signature; the worker-party nominee's name is reduced at signature to Latin fragments and is not reproduced. [Para 1][Para 229]
- The panel's summary says the employer dismissed "4" workers while the findings and the operative order deal with 3. [Para 216][Para 218][Para 225]
- The third reinstated worker's surname is printed one way in the findings and another in the operative order; all three are rendered here by initials. [Para 218][Para 225]
- Dates and case numbers are unreliable: the strike start is printed as "៣49 ខែសីហា", dismissals appear as 21 September in the findings and 24 September in the reasoning, and cited award numbers are corrupted (for example 77/707 and 0570៩). [Para 37][Para 183][Para 212][Para 104]
- The award says conciliation produced agreement on eight points out of eight demands and then that all eight were unresolved. [Para 15][Para 16]
- Issues 3 (50 per cent wages during suspension and annual leave) and 7 (re-election of worker delegates) appear in the demand list but in neither the reasons nor the operative order. [Para 4][Para 7][Para 219][Para 225]
