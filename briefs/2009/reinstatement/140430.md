# Award No. 121/09 — Sky High (Vorn Reach) Co., Ltd / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ហាយ (វន រេច) v Khmer Youth Union Federation / សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Arbitration Council, 8 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ឹង សុទ្ធី; លីវ សុវណ្ណា; គង់ ផល្លៈ chair) · Doc ID 140430

## 1. Facts
Case No. 121/09 arises from a strike at the Phnom Penh factory of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ហាយ (វន រេច) (Sky High (Vorn Reach) Co., Ltd), where the claimant, សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Khmer Youth Union Federation), said about 3,000 workers were employed and 1,608 were on strike [Para 1][Para 22]. The strikers' demand was that the company implement Award No. 81/09 of 31 July 2009 in the same factory, which the company had objected to on 6 August 2009 [Para 2][Para 26][Para 32]. Conciliation on 27 August 2009 produced no settlement on any of the four demands [Para 12]. The union withdrew issues 2 and 3 by letter, leaving issue 1 and issue 4, both of which asked for that award to be applied [Para 28][Para 30][Para 31]. A pre-hearing meeting was held on 2 September 2009 which the union attended and the employer did not [Para 10][Para 15].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may order the company to apply the working conditions set by Award No. 81/09 of 31 July 2009 when the company has formally objected to that award? [Para 2][Para 25]
2. (Issue 4) Whether the company must take back Mr M into his former post, that demand being one already decided in Award No. 81/09? [Para 5][Para 31]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** that the company had to apply Award No. 81/09 in full, including reinstatement of the dismissed worker in his former post, because the award had been issued and only the employer's objection stood in the way; that they had notified the Ministry of Labour of the strike on 24 August 2009 and were exercising a lawful right [Para 24][Para 31][Para 32].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្កាយ ហាយ (វន រេច):** that it objected to Award No. 81/09, which it challenged on 6 August 2009, and it did not appear at the pre-hearing meeting or take part in the proceedings [Para 15][Para 26][Para 33].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel held that the demand to implement Award No. 81/09 fell outside its jurisdiction, and closed Case No. 121/09 without ordering the company to do anything [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42].

The panel began from Article 319 of the Labour Law, which grants the right to strike where one party refuses to carry out an arbitration decision, and from Prakara 40 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, under which an award cannot be implemented while a party's objection to it is pending [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]. It read the two provisions together as meaning that a party seeking enforcement of an award that the other side has objected to may either bring the matter to the competent court for a final ruling or resort to a strike conducted according to law, and it followed its earlier Award No. 45/06 on that point [Para 37][Para 38].

Applying that to the two remaining demands, the panel found that both were in substance the same request, since the reinstatement in issue 4 was itself part of Award No. 81/09 [Para 31][Para 39]. Because the company had objected to that award, the panel could not order its implementation; that was a matter for the court, and the union had instead chosen to strike [Para 39]. The panel therefore held the demand to be outside its jurisdiction and closed the file [Para 40][Para 41].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An arbitration award that a party has objected to cannot be implemented, and a fresh collective demand asking a new panel to apply that award does not confer jurisdiction: the aggrieved party's remedies are proceedings in the competent court for a final decision, or a strike conducted according to the Labour Law [Para 36][Para 38][Para 39].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the union had notified the Ministry of Labour of the strike on 24 August 2009, and treated the choice between court and strike as one for the union itself [Para 24][Para 39].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in three different Khmer spellings across the face of the award and the reasons, so the form used here is the one in the case heading. The withdrawal of issues 2 and 3 is dated 2 August in the operative paragraph and 2 September in the reasons [Para 28][Para 29]. The conciliation minutes are dated 27 August in the text and referred to elsewhere as 24 August [Para 23]. The signatures and the last page are partly unreadable, and the worker-appointed arbitrator's name appears with a stray diacritic on the face of the award. The names of the union's representatives and of the dismissed worker appear in full in the source; they are given here by role and initial only.
