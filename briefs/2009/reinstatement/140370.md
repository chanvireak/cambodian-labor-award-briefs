# Award No. 36/09 — Citadel (Cambodia) Pte., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 31 March 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Thach, Vong Vanna, Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 140370

## 1. Facts
Citadel (Cambodia) Pte., Ltd. began operating in 2004 at Salang Village, Samrong Krom Commune, Dangkor District, Phnom Penh and employs 75 workers, all members of the single union at the plant, the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU). [Para 22][Para 25] About 30% of the workforce rides motorbikes and 10% bicycles, and in June or July 2008 the employer built a fenced parking lot close to and visible from the workstations, attended by an employee who unlocks the gate. [Para 31][Para 37][Para 39] On 2 March 2009 the employer announced that all workers had to take care of their own vehicles; the next morning 20 workers, fearing theft, parked in the prohibited production area and refused to move despite the technical manager's requests, and at 10:15 a.m. the company director decided to dismiss them, with dismissal documents and indemnities prepared that afternoon and refused by the workers. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 47][Para 50] The workers' original demand for a parking lot was dropped at the hearing because the lot had been built, leaving only responsibility for vehicle losses plus reinstatement of the 20. [Para 10][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must take responsibility for the loss of the workers' motorbikes and bicycles? [Para 54]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the dismissal of 20 workers for parking their motorbikes in a prohibited area was a serious offence justifying dismissal, so that the employer may refuse reinstatement? [Para 66]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU):** although no vehicle has yet been lost, the workers fear loss because the employer announced on 2 March 2009 that they must look after their own vehicles, and parking in a prohibited area is not a serious offence warranting immediate dismissal, so the 20 should be reinstated. [Para 35][Para 42][Para 44]
**Employer (respondent) — Citadel (Cambodia) Pte., Ltd.:** it built a fenced, visible lot with a gatekeeper and Clause 8 of the Internal Work Rules states it is not responsible for parked vehicles; and the 20 committed misconduct under Article 83 Part B(3) of the Labour Law and Article 11 of the Internal Work Rules by parking in the production area after a warning, so they will not be reinstated. [Para 37][Para 41][Para 52][Para 53]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel declined to consider the demand that the employer answer for future losses of motorbikes and bicycles, and ordered the employer to reinstate the 20 workers. [Para 88]

On Issue 1, since no worker had yet lost a vehicle, the demand was a future demand. Following a line of awards (10/03-Jacqsintex, 14/06-Zheng Yong, 41/07-M & V Branch 3, 74/07-Global Apparels), the panel held it was created to resolve existing disputes, not events that may never occur, and it could not know whether, where, when or to whom a loss would happen or what responsibility would follow. [Para 59][Para 60] Award 119/07-Royal Crowntex, issue 14, added that no article of the Labour Law or regulation imposes such liability, and no evidence of employer responsibility during working hours was produced. [Para 61][Para 63][Para 64]

On Issue 2, the panel worked through Article 83 Part B(3) and Article 11 of the Internal Work Rules dated 7 November 2005, which grades misconduct: a first minor infraction brings a verbal warning, a second a written warning, a third dismissal; only serious misconduct allows immediate dismissal. [Para 67][Para 69][Para 74] Of the 20 dismissed, only two had received written warnings dated 24 February 2009, and nobody received a second warning or any warning for the 3 March parking; the employer thus skipped the graduated steps its own rules require. [Para 76][Para 78][Para 80] The infraction was therefore minor-to-medium misconduct, and dismissal as a serious offence was unsound and inconsistent with the rules. [Para 83][Para 84] Article 27 requires any sanction to be proportional to the misconduct, and Award 76/05-Global Footwear holds misconduct serious only when repeated, evidenced or specified; parking a motorbike after a half-hour-old notice did not meet that bar. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 88] Under Clause 34 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 the panel may order reinstatement, which it did for all 20. [Para 88]

A procedural point preceded the merits: the employer's technical manager signed no authorisation letter by 20 March 2009, but as production manager he asserted authority at the hearing, no party objected at the time, and the panel accepted him as representative. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 17]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand contingent on a loss that has not occurred is a future demand the Arbitration Council will not consider; and where Internal Work Rules prescribe graduated warnings before dismissal, an employer that dismisses on a first, uncontradicted infraction breaches those rules and Article 27's proportionality requirement, so reinstatement follows. [Para 59][Para 87]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that liability for parked vehicles appears nowhere in the Labour Law or the regulations. [Para 61][Para 63]
