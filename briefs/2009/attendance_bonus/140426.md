# Award No. 115/09 — Vay Oullet Co., Ltd / ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ អូឡែត v Khmer Youth Union at Vay Oullet Factory / សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Arbitration Council, 9 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborna; An Vireak; Kong Phalla chair) · Doc ID 140426

## 1. Facts
Case No. 115/09 concerns a collective dispute at the Vay Oullet factory of ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ អូឡែត (Vay Oullet Co., Ltd), Phnom Penh, brought by the Khmer Youth Union (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) at that factory, which was recorded as absent from the proceedings [Para 1]. The union had made 10 demands; conciliation on 18 August 2009 settled 4 and left 6 for the panel, namely a regular attendance bonus of USD 56, a seniority increment of USD 1 for four years' service, a share of profits, employer-funded health checks, a daycare centre meeting ministerial Prakas, and restoration of an incentive bonus said to have been deducted [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 11][Para 19]. The hearing was set for 4 September 2009 at 14:00 and neither the union nor any representative attended [Para 17]. The award was issued on 9 September 2009 [Para 1].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, the claimant union having been duly notified of the hearing, the panel could proceed and decide the case in its absence under Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules? [Para 35][Para 41]
2. Whether all three conditions in Prakara 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council were satisfied, so that the case should be closed as an abandonment of the claim? [Para 37][Para 54]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** the six unsettled demands recorded in the conciliation report, seeking improved conditions of work, and in particular the USD 56 attendance bonus and the USD 1 seniority increment for workers with four years' service [Para 2][Para 4][Para 18]. The union did not appear to argue them and gave no reason for its absence [Para 22][Para 50].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ អូឡែត:** that it could not grant the demands and asked that Notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2009 be applied instead; on the profit share it said there was no profit to distribute, and on the incentive bonus that it was discretionary and would be reviewed case by case [Para 3][Para 7][Para 12]. It attended the hearing and asked the panel to proceed according to law [Para 24][Para 51].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed Case No. 115/09, treating the claimant union's failure to appear as abandonment of its claim, and reached no finding on any of the six demands [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63].

The panel began from Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which allows it either to continue in the absence of a party or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an award, provided the absent party was properly notified of the date, time and place [Para 35][Para 36]. Prakara 21 of the same Prakas was read, consistently with Awards 16/07, 27/07, 95/08, 132/08 and 138/08, as requiring three cumulative conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and the absence of a valid reason [Para 37][Para 38][Para 40].

On the first condition the panel found that invitation letters Nos. 593 and 594 of 31 August 2009 had been sent to both sides and that the secretariat telephoned the union before the hearing to secure attendance [Para 29][Para 30][Para 41][Para 43]. On the second it found that the union attended none of the four stages of the arbitration — introduction, examination of the issues, conciliation and hearing — and so did not "appear" within the meaning of Prakara 21 [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 49]. On the third it found that on the hearing day the union telephoned to say it would not attend and asked the panel to proceed, offering no reason at all [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53].

Having found all three conditions met, the panel held that it retained power to issue an award notwithstanding the absence [Para 55]. It recorded that the union had been given a full opportunity to defend the workers' demands under the Labour Law but had chosen not to attend and so forfeited the chance to present evidence [Para 56][Para 57]. Noting that a claimant bears the burden of giving reasons and evidence, and that this claimant gave neither, the panel treated the non-appearance as abandonment of the claim [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union has been properly notified of the hearing, attends none of the stages of the arbitration, and offers no valid reason for its absence, the panel may close the case as an abandonment of the claim without examining the merits, provided it records that each of the three conditions in Prakara 21 is separately satisfied [Para 37][Para 54][Para 60].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that closing the case did not reflect a genuine intention on the union's part to have its labour dispute resolved before the Council [Para 61].
