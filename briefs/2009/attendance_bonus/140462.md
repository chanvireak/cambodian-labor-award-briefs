# Award No. 167/09 — Four Mark Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វូរ មាំក អ៊ិនធើប្រាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council, 18 December 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mam Sambona (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhean (president) · Doc ID 140462

## 1. Facts
Four Mark Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វូរ មាំក អ៊ិនធើប្រាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី) operates on National Road 4 at Damnak Ampil, Ang Snuol, Kandal. [Para 1] The Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) filed twelve demands with the Kandal labour office on 26 October 2009; conciliation settled five and left seven unsettled, which reached the panel. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16] The seven unreached demands were: full base wage during temporary stoppages, no backdating of workers' cards, preservation of wage and attendance bonus during sick leave, safety shoes for entering the commercial building, weekly meal money, more sewing machines without hasty removal, and a roof over the waiting area in front of the factory. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9] The employer answered that it could pay only 30% of base wage during stoppages and otherwise maintained its existing practice. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5] The complainants did not appear at the hearing; the federation telephoned at 8:45 to say the local union had not been told of the date and that one delegate was ill. [Para 18][Para 28][Para 42]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Preliminary) Whether the three conditions for closing the file in the complainant's absence — proper notification, non-appearance of the complainant, and no valid reason for that absence — were satisfied? [Para 31][Para 34]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ):** in the conciliation record they sought full base wages whenever the company had no work to give, a stop to backdating of identity cards so that seniority accrued from the original entry date, wages and the attendance bonus preserved during certified sick leave, safety shoes, weekly meal money, additional sewing machines with no precipitate removal, and a shelter roof. At the hearing they appeared neither in person nor by any authorised representative, and asked only for an adjournment. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 26][Para 41]
**Employer (respondent) — Four Mark Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វូរ មាំក អ៊ិនធើប្រាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី):** it attended through its management president and administration head; on the stoppage claim it could pay only 30% of the base wage, and on the cards it asked to keep the old rule under which seniority runs only from the date a worker attains full status. It opposed the sick-leave demand. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3][Para 5]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed the file in case No. 167/09, treating the complaint as abandoned, and decided nothing on the merits of the seven demands. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 51]
The panel cited Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council's procedural rules, under which it may proceed or close the case where a party fails to appear, provided the parties were properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] It drew from its earlier awards (Nos. 16/07, 138/08, 151/08, 09/09 and 45/09) three cumulative conditions: proper notification, non-appearance of the complainant, and absence of a valid reason. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33]
On the first condition the panel found that the secretariat had issued formal invitations No. 862 to both sides on 4 December 2009 and had also confirmed the date by telephone the same day, giving seven days to prepare. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 46][Para 47]
On the second, neither the complainants nor any representative — federation, lawyer or shop steward — attended when the hearing opened. [Para 41][Para 43]
On the third, the panel rejected the excuse: notice had been given, seven days was a reasonable period, and a party with a genuine impediment should have asked in writing for an adjournment beforehand; it also held that silence until the morning of the hearing meant the complainant had abandoned its complaint. [Para 45][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]
Having found all three conditions met, the panel closed the file and made no finding on wages, cards, sick leave, safety shoes, meal money, machines or shelter. [Para 50][Para 51]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a complainant union neither appears nor sends an authorised representative, and its only explanation is given by telephone on the morning of the hearing after proper written notice and a reasonable preparatory period, the panel treats the complaint as abandoned and closes the file without deciding the merits. [Para 41][Para 45][Para 48][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that a party with a genuine impediment must notify the secretariat in writing in advance to seek an adjournment, precisely to avoid unexplained absence. [Para 47]

[Para 14][Para 17][Para 51] One citation of Prakas No. 099 is printed with a doubled year ("2019 … 2004").
