# Award No. 26/09 — Kimsheng Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 13 March 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bunchhea presiding; Ing Sothy, employer nominee; An Nan, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140365

## 1. Facts
Kimsheng Garment Co., Ltd, at Samrong village, Ta Khmao commune, Ta Khmao district, Kandal province, faced nine demands from the Local Union of the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU): reinstatement of a worker dismissed on 19 January 2009, allegedly because he joined the union; an end to discrimination against union members; easier permission for personal and sick leave; a doctor during working hours and sufficient medical supplies; the piece rate announced a week in advance; a canteen with tables; proper language from Chinese group leaders and from security staff; and reinstatement of a second worker, who the union said was not dismissed according to legal procedure. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11] Conciliation on 17 February 2009 left all nine unresolved. [Para 17][Para 18] At 1:20 p.m. on 3 March 2009, forty minutes before the hearing, the Secretariat telephoned the parties; the union answered that it would not attend that hearing or any later one and gave no reason, while the employer appeared as summoned and asked the panel to apply the law. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, the claimant union having been duly notified and having refused to attend either this hearing or any further hearing without reason, the panel should close the case by award rather than decide the nine demands? [Para 29][Para 47][Para 52]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the KYFTU local union at Kimsheng Garment Co., Ltd demanded the reinstatement of two dismissed workers — one dismissed on 19 January 2009 for union membership and one dismissed outside legal procedure — along with the seven conditions above, then declined to attend the hearing or to send any representative. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 11][Para 20]
**Employer (respondent):** the company rejected all nine demands and, appearing at the hearing as summoned, asked the Arbitration Council to follow the law. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 7][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed Case 26/09-Kimsheng Garment, treating the nine demands as dropped and deciding none of them. [Para 50][Para 52]
Rule 4.7 of the Annex to Prakas No. 099 and Clause 21 of the Prakas both allow the panel, where a party duly invited fails to appear without good cause, either to proceed in its absence or to terminate the arbitration by award, after satisfying itself that notification was proper. [Para 27][Para 29][Para 30]
The panel followed Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M & V (Branch 3), 95/08-Yung Wah, 132/08-GHG, 138/08-Malin and 151/08-Wilson Garment on the three conditions. [Para 29][Para 30] On notification, the Secretariat dispatched a notice and telephoned before the hearing, so the first condition was met. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 34] On appearance, the panel read "appear" as requiring presence and participation in all four stages — introduction and disclosure of conflicts, explanation of the process and confirmation of the issues, conciliation if agreed, and arbitration — and the union took part in none of them. [Para 35][Para 38][Para 40] On reasons, none was given, the union having said in advance that it would not come. [Para 41][Para 43][Para 45][Para 46]
The panel added that a claimant must argue its claim with reasons and evidence, that the union had been given ample opportunity and had forfeited it, and that it therefore considered the claim dropped and the refusal to appear a lack of good will in resolving the dispute. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant union, though properly notified, announces in advance that it will attend neither the hearing nor any later one and offers no reason, the panel may treat the claim as abandoned and close the case by award under Rule 4.7 and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099, without deciding the merits, since a claimant that withholds reasons and evidence has failed to discharge the burden of arguing its claim. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel characterised the union's refusal to bring the dispute before it as a lack of good will, an observation on conduct that was not necessary to the closure of the case. [Para 51]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case is named "26/09-Kimsheng Garment" on the face of the award but the order closes "Case 29/09-Kimsheng Garment", so the case number is printed two ways. [Para 1][Para 52] The non-conciliation report is identified only as "No. special KB/KN" dated 18 February 2009, with no serial number. [Para 14][Para 18] The two dismissed workers whose reinstatement was demanded are named in full, with one employee number, in the list of demands; those names are not reproduced in this brief. [Para 2][Para 11] The evidence section was omitted from this English translation. [Para 23] The list of nine demands interleaves each demand with the employer's refusal on the following line, and one demand's numbering is lost entirely, so the printed numbering is not continuous. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 9]
