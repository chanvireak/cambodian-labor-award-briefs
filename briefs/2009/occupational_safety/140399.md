# Award No. 78/09-ជូន តិច ថា យ ល័ — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូន តិច ថា យល៍ (មិថុនា) (Arbitration Council, 13 July 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វូច ហ៊ុន for the employer, វ៉ែន ពៅ for the workers, chair តង់ ផល្លៈ) · Doc ID 140399

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូន តិច ថា យល៍ (មិថុនា) ("Juntechaya") operates on Russian Federation Boulevard, Sangkat Teuk Thla, Khan Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, employing about 4,500 workers. [Para 26][Para 27] In 2003 the company moved 136 sewing workers — 68 on the morning shift and 68 on the evening shift — from Group 10 to Group 7, without any objection at the time. [Para 30][Para 55] Their labour books, attendance books and health-check books continued to record Group 10. [Para 31] After conciliation failed on the single disputed point, the 136 workers, who appointed no union to represent them, brought the claim. [Para 28][Para 11]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the 136 workers of Group 7 are entitled to be reassigned to their former Group 10 because their labour, attendance and health-check books record Group 10? [Para 29][Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 136 workers of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូន តិច ថា យល៍ asked to return to Group 10, their original group, because every employment document they hold is registered there, which makes any leave application difficult, and because Group 7 is hot, dusty and noisy and they have trouble communicating there. [Para 33][Para 38][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent):** Juntechaya said the 2003 move was a mere change of location made to sequence production at the buyers' request, that wages, bonuses and hours were preserved, and that it is willing to correct the group name on the documents; it accepted that Group 7 is harsher but said ILO inspections graded the factory well and that ventilation, fan cleaning, added cooling and ear-plugs had been provided. [Para 35][Para 40][Para 57]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the demand that the 136 workers be moved from Group 7 (both shifts) back to Group 10. [Para 70][Para 71]

On the management right, the panel read Article 2(a), second paragraph, of the Labour Law (an enterprise may have several establishments, each employing a group of persons at one workplace) together with its earlier practice in Awards 39/07, 54/07, 47/08 and 49/09, and held that an employer's right to organise and manage the enterprise is lawful where it is exercised lawfully and reasonably. [Para 49][Para 53][Para 54] It found that in 2003 the workers raised no objection to the move and acknowledged that no benefit was lost, and that a wrong group name on a labour book causes the workers no loss, so the employer had reason to keep them in Group 7. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 58][Para 59]

On working conditions, the panel applied Article 229 of the Labour Law, which requires every establishment to keep workplaces clean and to maintain hygiene, safety and conditions necessary for workers' health, including ventilation and the control of noise and light. [Para 61][Para 63] It accepted that Juntechaya had cleaned dust from walls and fans, cleaned water-cooling pads, added fans and ice, greased the fan motors and was ordering ear-plugs, and that the workers neither denied these measures nor claimed any harm to their health. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 67] Their subjective discomfort therefore did not establish a breach of Article 229, and the employer's right to assign and reassign its workers stood. [Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may reassign workers between production groups, and may keep them in the new group, where wages, bonuses and working hours are preserved, the move causes no loss of benefit, and a stale group name on the workers' labour, attendance and health-check books is a clerical error that can be corrected; the assignment is set aside only where the new workplace fails the hygiene and safety standard of Article 229 of the Labour Law. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 63]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the distance from Group 10 to Group 7 is only about 16 metres, a point neither party contested. [Para 45][Para 46]
