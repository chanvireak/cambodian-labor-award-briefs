# Award No. 90/09 — Mak Lin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd and Khmer Youth Union Federation (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 25 July 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Chhiv Phirem ឈីវ ភិរម្យ (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean ស៊ីន គឹម ស៊ាន (worker-appointed), Ang Eng Pong អាំង អេង ផុង (president) · Doc ID 140408

## 1. Facts
Mak Lin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates at Tropeang Krasang village, Bek Chan commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal. [Para 1] On 19 June 2009 the Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ, the factory union at Mak Men) filed a 20-point complaint asking the company to improve working conditions; the Kandal provincial labour department received it on 22 June 2009. [Para 9] Conciliation on 3 July 2009 settled six of the seven points pressed, leaving one in dispute: the workers' demand for a 3,000 riel meal allowance for two hours of overtime worked, which the company refused. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 10]

The non-conciliation report No. 259/09 was forwarded on 8 July 2009 and the hearing was set for 20 July 2009 at 2:00 pm. [Para 6][Para 8] Written invitations were issued to both sides on 14 July 2009. [Para 16][Para 17] Neither party appeared: the company said some workers were ill and its president was abroad on mission, asking the panel to act as the law allows, while the union gave no reason at all. [Para 13] The panel recorded that it could find no facts on the claim because neither side was present to explain its position. [Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may close the file and issue an award where both parties, duly notified, fail to appear and give no valid reason? [Para 26]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**ភាគីកម្មករនិយោជិត — Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ, claimant):** the company should pay 3,000 riel as a meal allowance to workers who worked two hours of overtime; the union attended no hearing and sent no representative, and offered the secretariat no explanation for its absence. [Para 2][Para 32][Para 35]

**ភាគីនិយោជក — Mak Lin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (respondent):** it did not agree to the meal allowance, and did not appear either, explaining that some workers were ill and that its president was on mission abroad, and asking the panel to take whatever measures the law provides. [Para 3][Para 13]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 90/09 Mak Lin with no order on the 3,000 riel meal-allowance claim, because neither party appeared. [Para 39]

The panel treated the matter as procedural. Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council and Rule 4.7 of its procedural rules both allow the panel either to continue in a party's absence or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an award, and the panel identified three conditions that must be satisfied before it terminates: the party was properly notified, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 23] It noted that it had applied the same test in Awards 16/07, 138/08 (Mak Lin), 151/08 (Wil Sin), 09/09 (Nim Bus) and 45/09. [Para 22]

On the first condition, the secretariat had sent formal written invitations to both parties as a matter of routine, confirmed the date, time and place in writing and again by telephone, and the invitation letters of 14 July 2009 are on file. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29] This award itself recites the invitation letters for the employer and for the union. [Para 16][Para 17]

On the second condition, neither the union nor the company attended the hearing and neither appointed a representative — no federation officer, lawyer or member of management with authority to appear. [Para 32][Para 33]

On the third condition, the panel found the company's excuse (sick workers, president abroad) and the union's complete silence to be no valid reason, so the condition was met. [Para 35][Para 36] Having satisfied itself on all three, the panel closed the file. [Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been properly notified of the hearing and fails to appear without a valid reason, the panel will terminate the arbitration by award rather than adjudicate the merits; the claim is left undetermined because no evidence on it was ever presented. [Para 18][Para 23]

**Obiter dicta:** that written invitation, supplemented by telephone confirmation of date, time and place, satisfies the notification condition. [Para 28]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case number is printed two ways — a different number in the header from the number used in the body and in the operative order, both attached to the same party name — so the file may be indexed under either. The company's name also shifts between the party box and the report references, and the union is described once as attached to a differently transliterated company name. Staffing details for both sides are printed as "none", so no representative is identified at all. OCR damage is heavy: the English masthead is misspelled, the president's name in the header is corrupted by stray characters and is recoverable only from the signature page, and one citation to the 2004 Prakas carries a wrong year. The signature page holds stray digits and an English fragment. [Para 1][Para 30][Para 37][Para 40]
