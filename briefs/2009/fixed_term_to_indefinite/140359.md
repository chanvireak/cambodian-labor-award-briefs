# Award No. 16/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ប្លូម ថេម អ៊ីម ប្រយ ឌីរី (Plum Thm Eim Prey Diri Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 4 March 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phallack presiding; Ly Tai Seng, employer nominee; Liv Sovanna, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140359

## 1. Facts
Plum Thm Eim Prey Diri (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ប្លូម ថេម អ៊ឹម ប្រយ ឌីរី), a garment factory at Trapeang Thleng village, Choam Chao commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh with about 508 workers, issued a notice on 18 September 2008 converting the contracts of 105 workers from indefinite duration to fixed duration and paying each worker a sum in exchange. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 37] All 105 had held indefinite-duration contracts before September 2008. [Para 36] At the conciliation of 15 January 2009 the company agreed to annul the fixed-duration contracts at the workers' request, but refused to resume the indefinite-duration contracts, which it said had already been terminated and compensated. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 41] Only one of the two demands stayed non-conciliated and reached the Council — that the earlier indefinite-duration contracts be continued. [Para 15][Para 16] The claimant is the Plum Thm workers' union (សហជីព កម្មករ ប្លូម ថេម), representing the 105 workers. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether, the company having annulled the fixed-duration contracts it imposed in September 2008, it must continue the indefinite-duration contracts the workers held before that date, or whether the annulment freed it to conclude new fixed-duration contracts? [Para 3][Para 46]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the shift from indefinite to fixed duration was forced and did not express the workers' free will, and the compensation paid was not lawful under the Labour Law; under Article 21 of Kret 38 annulment returns the parties to their pre-September 2008 indefinite-duration contracts. [Para 3][Para 46][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent):** the fixed-duration contracts were concluded by mutual agreement, entitlements were paid to workers whose indefinite contracts ended, and company policy is fixed duration for new hires; "annulment" means destroying that contract and making a new one, so the indefinite contract cannot be revived. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 43][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to continue the indefinite-duration contracts "based on the will of each individual worker" (ផ្អែកតាមឆន្ទៈរបស់កម្មករនិយោជិតម្នាក់ៗ), with repayment of the conversion money left to each worker's own choice. [Para 70][Para 71]
The panel found no provision of the Labour Law defining annulment (mokhpheap), so it applied Article 65, which places the employment contract under civil rules, and through it Article 21 of Kret 38 of 28 October 1988: on annulment the situation existing before the contract is restored. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 54] It rejected the employer's reading that annulment means terminating the contract and replacing it. [Para 62]
Restoration yielded two pre-contract states — performance of the indefinite-duration contract, and receipt of a sum in exchange for the conversion. [Para 58][Para 59] The panel ordered performance of the indefinite contract to resume, and held that a worker who elects to return must pay the sum back, on the general principle of unjust enrichment codified in Article 736 of the Cambodian Civil Code, although that Code was not yet in force. [Para 60][Para 68][Para 69]
It declined to order restitution itself, because the conciliation record contained no such point and the employer had not raised it at the hearing; the choice belongs to each worker. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 67]
The employer-nominated arbitrator, Ly Tai Seng, dissented in an opinion annexed under Article 37 of Prakas 099: the panel presumed the settlement valid without testing whether consent was free, and since the Labour Law nowhere permits converting an indefinite contract into a fixed one, the September 2008 act was a termination followed by a new fixed contract, so the demands should have been rejected. [Para 73][Para 77][Para 106][Para 111]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the parties agree to annul a fixed-duration contract that had replaced an earlier indefinite-duration contract, annulment restores the pre-existing state under Article 21 of Kret 38: the employer must continue the indefinite-duration contract, and the compensating sum is repaid only where the individual worker elects to return, the panel leaving that election to the worker rather than ordering restitution. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 67][Para 70]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that Article 736 of the Civil Code, which codifies unjust enrichment, was not yet in force, and that the practical consequences of restoring the earlier situation were for the parties to discuss. [Para 66][Para 68]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case is numbered "១៦/០៩" with the employer's name in the title rather than a case name, and the company is spelled two ways in the body (អ៊ីម ប្រយ ឌីរី, អ៊ឹម ប្រយ ឌីរី). [Para 1][Para 30][Para 37] Article 21 of Kret 38 is dated "ថ្ងៃទី ២៥ ២៨ ខែ តុលា ឆ្នាំ ១៩៨៨" — two day numbers in one phrase — while the same article is cited elsewhere as 28 October 1988. [Para 54][Para 55] The chair arbitrator is printed "គង់ ផល្លៈ" in the panel list and "គង់ ផល្លា" in the signature block. [Para 2][Para 73] The sum paid for the conversion is never quantified, although the order turns on repaying it. [Para 37][Para 68] The employer's representative list is truncated mid-entry and cannot be paired with roles, so no representative is named here. [Para 2]
