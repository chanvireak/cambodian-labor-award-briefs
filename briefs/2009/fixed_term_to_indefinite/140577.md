# Award No. 155/08 — Yong Va In Dasriel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. / ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង វាំ អ៊ិន ដាស្វ្រៀល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 9 January 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vuoch Hun (employer-appointed), Ven Peov (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chea (president) · Doc ID 140577

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង វាំ អ៊ិន ដាស្វ្រៀល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី (Yong Va In Dasriel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.) runs a garment factory at Thmei village, Ta Khmao commune, Ta Khmao district, Kandal province, employing 5,000 workers, of whom 126 work in the inspection section [Para 1][Para 23]. The claimant is សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព C.CAWDU, the local union at the factory [Para 1][Para 24].

The company moved 20 of those 126 inspection workers from the garment-inspection section to garment inspection at the ironing section [Para 25]. Conciliation before the Kandal provincial department on 9 December 2008 failed on the single demand, which reached the panel on 17 December 2008 [Para 9][Para 10]. At the hearing of 25 December 2008 both parties appeared [Para 8][Para 12]. The panel recorded that the 20 women kept their wages, their posts and their other benefits, and that they had three to four years' service on undetermined-duration contracts [Para 27][Para 28].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer has the right to move the 20 inspection workers to the ironing section, and whether the way it chose which workers to move was fair and equal? [Para 40][Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព C.CAWDU says the selection was made by the section head ticking names on a list rather than by the rotation used before, that the new place has no chair so the women must stand for eight to ten hours, and that the move should be made equal, by rotation or by drawing lots [Para 26][Para 34][Para 36][Para 53].

**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង វាំ អ៊ិន ដាស្វ្រៀល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី says the production line required it, that the ironing section had 100 workers and only ten inspectors against 30 needed, and that the move follows the contract and the internal rules without touching wages, post or benefits [Para 33][Para 38][Para 27].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the company was ordered to make the transfer of the 20 inspection workers by rotation, one at a time, as it had done before, or to organise a draw, so that the arrangement is fair and equal [Para 70]. The award is binding and became final at once, both parties having adopted it at the hearing of 25 December 2008 [Para 71].

The panel began from Article 2 of the Labour Law, which it read with awards 84/08-Trinunggal Komara and 141/08 as giving the employer the right to manage and direct the enterprise so long as that management is lawful and reasonable [Para 41][Para 44][Para 45]. Following awards 17/03 and 18/03-Ho Hing, it held a transfer lawful where it does not cut wages, does not move the worker far from the original site, does not reverse day and night shifts, and does not require substantially different skills [Para 46][Para 47].

On the facts it found the move satisfied that test: wages, post and benefits were unchanged, the workplace stayed within the same factory, there was no shift change, and the inspection skills were of the same simple character [Para 48][Para 49]. It then separated the substance from the manner of the decision, holding that the supervisor's picking of names from a list of 126 workers, with no rotation and no chance of returning to the old section, was neither equal nor fair, all the more so because the old workplace allowed sitting and the new one required standing through eight to ten hours [Para 50][Para 51][Para 54][Para 56]. Article 312 of the Labour Law, which allows the panel to decide other disputes in equity, supplied the basis for the order [Para 65][Para 67][Para 69].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may transfer workers within the factory without their consent where wages, post, benefits, shift and the level of skill are unaffected, but the selection of who is moved must itself be equal and fair, and a transfer imposed on named individuals instead of by the rotation or draw applied to the whole section is set aside to that extent [Para 47][Para 49][Para 54][Para 58].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the old inspection workplace was easier because it allowed sitting, while the new one required standing throughout the shift, was not treated as a ground of illegality but as an aggravating feature of the unfair selection [Para 54][Para 56].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The invitation to the hearing is dated 25 December 2007 although the hearing it fixed took place on 25 December 2008 [Para 11]. The union's registration certificate and the workers' authorisation letters name the factory as "យ៉ុង វាំ ២", a different name from the respondent on the face of the award [Para 18][Para 19]. The company name is spelled four different ways across the face, the recitals and the findings [Para 1][Para 16][Para 23]. At line 44 the reasoning prints "human right" where the management right under Article 2 is meant, and a stray Latin word interrupts the operative paragraph [Para 44][Para 69]. The non-conciliation report number carries two different issuing codes in the recitals and the evidence list [Para 6][Para 20].
