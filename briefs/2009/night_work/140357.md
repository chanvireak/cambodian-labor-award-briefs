# Award No. 08/09 — Global Apparel Limited (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ក្លូប័ល អា ប៉ារែលីមីធីត) (Arbitration Council, 16 February 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-appointed, dissenting), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140357

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ក្លូប័ល អា ប៉ារែលីមីធីត (Global Apparel Limited, formerly registered as Jusca Garment) runs a garment factory at Trapeang Toul, Kampong Speu side of Angsnuol District, Kandal Province, with about 3,000 workers in two buildings, A and B, on day and night shifts; roughly 800 day-shift workers and 600 night-shift workers were in Building A. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 23] The company decided to move every Building A worker to two floors of the JIT building 500 to 600 metres away, which the company owns, converting night-shift workers to day shift first; it says the move cuts rent and administration costs during the world economic crisis. [Para 46][Para 52][Para 66] The workers, represented by five unions claiming 490, 250 and 530 members respectively, refused to move, citing health and safety, distance, the location of their skills tests and confusion with JIT's own workers. [Para 42][Para 44][Para 59] One issue reached the panel after conciliation failed. [Para 11]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer has the right to relocate the workers of Building A to the new JIT building, or whether the workers may insist on remaining in Building A? [Para 72][Para 146]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), ភាគីកម្មករនិយោជិត represented by five unions (Khmer Youth, Workers' Rights, Cambodian Women, Independent and Democratic, and Khmer):** the new building has no clinic, insufficient toilets or nursery, is hard to reach and high up, is 500 to 600 metres from their old workplace, was not where they sat their skills tests, and houses JIT workers whose identity cards look alike, so they should stay in Building A. [Para 59][Para 133]

**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ក្លូប័ល អា ប៉ារែលីមីធីត:** the move is a cost response to the economic crisis because the JIT building is company-owned while Buildings A and B are rented; wages, benefits and skills are preserved, day-shift workers stay on day shift, and the company will ask the labour authorities to inspect the new site and will put up signage and separate identity cards. [Para 52][Para 60][Para 64][Para 66]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the workers' demand to remain in Building A and upheld the employer's right to relocate them to the JIT building. [Para 113][Para 114]

The panel grounded the employer's power in Article 2, paragraph 2 of the Labour Law, read as a right to manage and organise the enterprise that must be exercised lawfully and reasonably, and adopted the four conditions it had set in Awards 153/08-Hitachi Garment, 137/07-MPA, 84/08-Trinunggal Komara and 17/03 and 18/03-Bo Hing: no wage cut, no transfer to a distant place, no change of shift from day to night or night to day, and no transfer far from the worker's skill. [Para 75][Para 77][Para 79]

Applying each condition, the panel found wages preserved and condition one met; the 500 to 600 metre distance not excessive, though it might affect arrival time, so it required a one-week trial period for workers to adapt; the shift direction preserved because Building A day workers moved to day shift at JIT, with night workers first converted to day shift at Building A, which the panel treated as a management matter with a right of protest if rights were infringed; and no change of skill. [Para 82][Para 86][Para 88][Para 91][Para 96]

On the objection that night workers lose 30% of pay when moved to day shift, the panel held the loss flows from the shift change itself, not from any deduction by the employer, and that the employer had reasonable economic grounds for both the shift change and the relocation. [Para 102][Para 103][Para 112] Arbitrator លីវ សុវណ្ណា dissented, in an annexed opinion, on the ground that the shift conversion does cut wages and so breaches two of the four conditions, and that the panel should have confined itself to the demand actually pleaded. [Para 147][Para 151][Para 162]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may relocate a workplace and change shift patterns under its Article 2 management right where the four conditions hold — no reduction in wages, no transfer to a genuinely distant site, no inversion of day and night shift, and no departure from the worker's skill; where the four are met the relocation is lawful and the workers cannot refuse it, and a pay difference that results from the worker's own shift moving from night to day is not a wage cut by the employer. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 103][Para 112]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel directed a one-week trial so workers could adjust travel time, and noted that any worker whose contractual conditions are affected by the move keeps a right to protest. [Para 88][Para 164]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The header of the Khmer original is largely destroyed by OCR, leaving a string of digits and fragments where the case number and date should be, so the file number is recovered only from the body and the annexed dissent. [Para 1][Para 116] The employer-appointed arbitrator's name is printed in two different forms, in the panel composition and again in the signature block, so one of them is an OCR corruption. [Para 116] The company appears under three names — the Khmer transliteration in the header, "Global Apparel Limited" in the evidence list, and its former name Jusca Garment — and the evidence list even includes internal rules bearing another factory's name. [Para 20][Para 23] The distance to the new building is given as 500 metres in one place and 500 to 600 metres elsewhere. [Para 52][Para 85] The number of workers in each building cannot be fixed because the parties agree night-shift workers move between buildings, and the union membership figures do not sum to the workforce. [Para 40][Para 45] The narrative is printed twice, once as the award's findings and again inside the dissent, with the two versions differing in detail. [Para 111][Para 133]
