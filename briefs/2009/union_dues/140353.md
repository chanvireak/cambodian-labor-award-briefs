# Award No. 09/09 — Nimbus Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 12 February 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (chair) · Doc ID 140353

## 1. Facts
Nimbus Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a garment factory at Prey Roka Village, Angsnuol District, Kandal Province, faced a collective dispute brought by the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU) over 21 demands: voluntary overtime without coercion, a doctor and medical supplies on site, conversion of two-month probationary contracts to permanent contracts, a day-care centre and breastfeeding room, free holiday meals, transport for long overtime, a canteen, a raised attendance bonus, annual-leave compensation twice a year, weekly meal allowance, paid sick leave, drinking water, full wages during work shortages, 15-minute personal exits, proper conduct by Chinese staff, wages paid by 4:00 p.m., music during work, terminal benefits within 48 hours, worker-delegate elections, and a 1,000 riel union dues deduction. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 4] The Kandal labour office conciliated the 21 issues on 9 and 15 January 2009 and resolved three, referring the rest under non-conciliation report No. 021/09 KB/KN dated 16 January 2009. [Para 7][Para 9] The Secretariat summoned both sides for 30 January 2009; the employer attended with its representatives, the union's representative was absent, and a telephone call drew the reply that the workers would attend neither that hearing nor any later one. [Para 7][Para 14][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural issue) Whether the panel must close the case, under Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099, where the claimant union was properly notified, failed to appear at any stage, and gave no reason for its absence? [Para 23][Para 35][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU):** the union submitted no evidence and made no submission; it had accused the employer's representative at conciliation of holding no authorisation letter from the company director, and by telephone before and during the hearing it stated that its members would not attend, then stayed away without written request or explanation. [Para 10][Para 13][Para 14]

**Employer (respondent), Nimbus Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd:** appearing through its manager, shipping officer and translator, it asked the panel to rule according to law, having filed its commercial registration, internal work rules, company statute, the conciliation minutes, the voluntary overtime roster and the resignation letters of three former union officers. [Para 15][Para 18]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the claim as dropped and closed case 09/09-Nimbus Textile without deciding any of the 21 demands. [Para 45][Para 46]

The panel could make no finding of fact on the demands at all, because the only party carrying the claim never appeared. [Para 21] It applied Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, both of which let a panel either proceed in a party's absence or terminate the proceedings by award, and adopted the three-condition test it had already formulated in Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M&V (Branch 3), 95/08-Yung Wah, 132/08-GHG, 138/08-Malin and 151/08-Wilson Garment: proper notification, non-appearance, and no reason given. [Para 23][Para 24]

On notification, the Secretariat's notices No. 050 and No. 049 and a reminder call established that the union knew the date, time and place. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 28] On appearance, the panel read "appear before the arbitration panel" as requiring both presence and participation across all four stages — introduction and conflict disclosure, explanation of the process and confirmation of issues, conciliation, and arbitration — and found the union present at none. [Para 29][Para 34] On reasons, the union offered none, either in writing before the hearing or by telephone when reminded. [Para 35][Para 36] All three conditions being met, the panel held it retained power to issue an award, but that the claimant had forfeited the opportunity to argue and prove its case. [Para 41][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel may close a case by award rather than decide the merits where the claimant was duly notified, was present at none of the four stages of the proceedings, and gave no reason for its absence; the claimant bears the burden of arguing and evidencing its claim, and non-appearance forfeits that opportunity, which the panel treats as abandonment of the claim. [Para 23][Para 43][Para 45]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that its power to proceed in a party's absence subsists even where that party had earlier accused the employer's representative of lacking authority to negotiate. [Para 10][Para 41]
