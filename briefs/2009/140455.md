# Award No. 155/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វូល លីហ្វ ហ្គាម៉ិន (កម្ពុជា) អិលធីឌី and សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ យូ អេស អេ
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia, Case No. 155/09, award dated 18 December 2009 (issued under Article 313 of the Labour Law).

## 1. Facts

The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វូល លីហ្វ ហ្គាម៉ិន (កម្ពុជា) អិលធីឌី, began operations in 1999 at Lot 790 on National Road 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Krom, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, and employed about 517 workers [Para 53][Para 54]. Two factory-level unions operated there, សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ and សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ, and at the time of the award neither had attained most-representative status [Para 55][Para 56]. The claimant union, សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ យូ អេស អេ, was registered with the Ministry of Labour on 14 December 2004 [Para 124]. Its representatives said about 300 workers were members, but only 60 had authorised the October 2009 dues deduction; the employer said it knew only of those 60 [Para 58][Para 59]. The collective dispute notice of 3 October 2009 carried 132 thumbprints from workers in the sewing, FQC, FT, FP, cutting table, QC, sanitation, thread-cutting, mechanic and pressing sections [Para 60][Para 66]. Conciliation failed and the case went to the Arbitration Council [Para 3][Para 49].

The demands before the panel were ten. The union also raised, but the panel did not reach as separate issues, its objection to the company's method of calculating the seniority increment, its claim that the meal allowance was under-paid, and its complaint about the notice given to the dismissed union secretary [Para 5][Para 7][Para 9].

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must grant one additional paid annual-leave day for every three years of seniority, rather than capping leave at 19 days? [Para 61]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must provide one free meal or 3,000 riel to workers who voluntarily work overtime on Sundays or public holidays? [Para 67]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must raise the seniority bonus by 2 US dollars for workers with five or more years of service? [Para 7]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay full wages during periods when it has no work for the workers to do? [Para 9]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must pay an additional 500 riel meal allowance, 1,500 riel in total, for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00 and from 18:45 to 21:00? [Para 357]
6. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must pay wages twice a month, at least 16 days apart? [Para 259]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the employer must state clearly, before terminating a fixed-duration contract, its reason for not renewing? [Para 105]
8. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must take back the dismissed union secretary and continue his contract? [Para 117]
9. (Issue 9) Whether fixed-duration contracts running beyond two years must be converted to contracts of unlimited duration? [Para 129]
10. (Issue 10) Whether the employer must restore the 5 US dollar incentive allowance? [Para 138]

## 3. Positions of the parties

The union (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ យូ អេស អេ) argued that the employer added only one leave day at the three-year mark and then stopped, so a worker with six or ten years of service still received 19 days; that notice 017 of 18 July 2000 promised a free meal or 3,000 riel for holiday and Sunday overtime; that the 2 dollar seniority increase and the 500 riel meal supplement were owed; that full wages were due during work shortages; that wages should be paid twice monthly; that the reason for non-renewal should be stated; that the union secretary should be reinstated; that contracts beyond two years should become indefinite; and that the 5 dollar incentive should be restored [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 67][Para 130][Para 137][Para 140].

The employer (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វូល លីហ្វ ហ្គាម៉ិន (កម្ពុជា) អិលធីឌី) answered that it gave 19 days to anyone with over three years and would follow the law if more were required [Para 64]; that holiday and Sunday work already carried a high rate and notice 017 was not mandatory [Para 68][Para 69]; that it could not meet the meal, seniority and full-wage demands and asked to apply notice 017 [Para 6][Para 8]; that it paid once a month and asked to keep that practice [Para 104][Para 105]; that it terminated fixed-duration contracts lawfully under Article 73 [Para 116]; that the secretary's contract had simply expired [Para 118]; that it would keep hiring on fixed terms [Para 131][Para 135]; and that the incentive was suspended because of the global economic crisis [Para 345].

## 4. Award and reasoning

On Issue 1 the panel read Articles 166 and 167 of the Labour Law and Article 4 of the company's internal rules, which provide 18 days rising by one day for each three years worked, and held that the flat 19-day cap for years four to six was unlawful [Para 155][Para 156][Para 157].

On Issue 2 the panel found that voluntary work on a public holiday is not overtime, following prior awards, and that no agreement promised a meal or 3,000 riel for up to eight hours of holiday work; the demand was a interests dispute and was refused [Para 175][Para 176][Para 177]. On Issue 5 it likewise refused the extra 500 riel [Para 357].

On Issue 6 the panel applied Article 6 of the Labour Law and its earlier reasoning on who is a "worker", found the claimants were manual and skilled production staff rather than office employees, and ordered wages twice a month at least 16 days apart [Para 252][Para 257][Para 258][Para 259].

On Issue 7 the panel held that the law requires advance notice but not a statement of reasons for non-renewal, save where discrimination under Articles 12 and 279 is shown, and refused the demand [Para 277][Para 278][Para 281]. On Issue 8, finding no evidence of the claimed 2005 start date and only a 12-month contract from 14 October 2008, it refused reinstatement [Para 286][Para 287][Para 288]. On Issue 9 it ordered conversion of contracts exceeding two years to unlimited duration, noting the employer did not contest the demand [Para 130][Para 131][Para 132]. On Issue 10 it accepted the economic-crisis justification and refused restoration of the incentive [Para 347][Para 349][Para 351].

**Outcome:** Mixed. The panel ordered one extra paid leave day per three years (19 days for years 4–6, 20 for years 7–9, 21 for years 10–12) [Para 352]; full wages during work shortages [Para 356]; and wages paid twice monthly [Para 358]. It refused the holiday meal or 3,000 riel [Para 353][Para 354], the 2 dollar seniority increase [Para 355], the 500 riel meal supplement [Para 357], the statement of reasons [Para 359], the secretary's reinstatement [Para 360], and the 5 dollar incentive [Para 362]. The award was non-binding and would become final eight days after notification absent objection [Para 363].

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Annual leave accrues by statute in steps of one day per three years of service; a flat cap for long-serving workers is unlawful [Para 155][Para 352]. Voluntary work on a public holiday is not overtime, so the overtime meal allowance does not attach to it [Para 175][Para 353]. Factory production workers fall within Article 6 and must be paid at least twice monthly [Para 257][Para 358]. An employer need not give reasons for not renewing a fixed-duration contract unless discrimination is shown [Para 277][Para 359]. Fixed-duration contracts whose total duration exceeds two years convert to contracts of unlimited duration [Para 361]. A benefit granted outside the law may be withdrawn for sound and honest reasons, including a genuine economic downturn [Para 349][Para 351].
