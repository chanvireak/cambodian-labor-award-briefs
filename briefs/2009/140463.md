# Award No. 169/09 — PCCS Garment Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្គារ មិន លីមីធីត) (Arbitration Council, 30 December 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — In Sothy (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhean (president) · Doc ID 140463

## 1. Facts
PCCS Garment Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្គារ មិន លីមីធីត), on Russian Federation Boulevard, Tuek Thla, Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1997, employs about 3,500 workers, runs 38 sewing teams and hosts 16 unions. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 54] Facing falling orders, the company told the 16 unions on 9 September 2009 that teams would be consolidated, and a 6 October meeting with the labour inspectorate confirmed that teams 27 and 28 would be dissolved, with the 69 remaining full-status workers moved to teams short of staff on unchanged position and benefits. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 60][Para 63][Para 64] On 8 and 10 October 2009 the move was carried out: 18 workers accepted, 51 refused and demanded to stay in teams 27 and 28. [Para 65][Para 66] The 51 were mostly long-serving — in the factory since 1999 or 2000, ten years or more — and had just left another union to join the Khmer Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ខ្មែរ), which filed for them. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 71] The company sued in the Phnom Penh court, which on 13 November 2009 issued protective order No. 329 declaring the strike unlawful and ordering return to work within 48 hours. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74] The workers stayed out; the company did nothing until 30 November, then treated them as having abandoned their jobs and paid out wages and end-of-year indemnity. [Para 105][Para 118] Of two demands, the second (a penalty on the company for breaching Articles 18, 278, 279 and 280) was settled at the hearing, leaving Issue 1. [Para 5][Para 17][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must take the 51 workers back into teams 27 and 28 and, failing that, pay compensation under Articles 75, 89, 91, 116, 166 and 167 plus October and November 2009 wages? [Para 52][Para 89]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — Khmer Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ខ្មែរ):** the 51 had just moved from another union into the Khmer Union Federation, and the selection of their two teams for dissolution was union discrimination and a disguised sanction; they should be restored to their own workstations, and if not, the company must pay statutory compensation including two months' unpaid wages. [Para 52][Para 68]
**Employer (respondent) — PCCS Garment Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្គារ មិន លីមីធីត):** the consolidation followed the world economic crisis and lost orders and was agreed at the 9 September and 6 October meetings; position and benefits were preserved; and the matter is now before the court, so the panel lacks jurisdiction and should await the court's ruling. [Para 4][Para 70][Para 82][Para 88][Para 90]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to take the 51 workers back to work under its assignment to other teams, keeping their skill position, wage and other benefits unchanged; their claim for statutory compensation was dismissed. [Para 154][Para 155]
On jurisdiction the panel held that Regulation 32 of Prakas No. 099 gives it power over collective labour disputes, and that the court's protective order decided only the legality of the strike, not the reinstatement claim, so the two proceedings were distinct and the panel could rule. [Para 91][Para 93][Para 94][Para 96]
On the merits the panel applied Article 337: once a strike is declared unlawful, workers must return within 48 hours, and those who do not without good reason commit serious misconduct. [Para 98][Para 100][Para 116] The 48 hours ran from 13 November 2009 when the order was served, and the workers did not return. [Para 104][Para 105]
The misconduct, however, had to be acted on. Under Article 26(2) the employer loses the right to dismiss if it does not act within seven days of learning of the serious misconduct, that is by 22 November 2009. [Para 111][Para 113][Para 117] The company did nothing until 30 November, when it declared the 51 to have abandoned their jobs, so it had forfeited the right to dismiss and the abandonment finding was wrong; the 51 remained lawful employees. [Para 118][Para 119][Para 120][Para 121]
Because the contracts subsisted, no severance was owed and the compensation claim was refused. [Para 124][Para 125]
On union discrimination the panel applied Articles 12 and 279 and its consistent rule that the worker bears the burden of proof; no evidence showed that the choice of teams 27 and 28 was motivated by the workers' move to the Khmer Union Federation, and the panel found the consolidation, with position, wages and benefits preserved, lawful and reasonable. [Para 127][Para 129][Para 130][Para 135][Para 137][Para 152]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers fail to return within 48 hours of a court declaring their strike unlawful, the employer acquires a right to dismiss for serious misconduct that must be exercised within seven days; if it waits and later treats the workers as having abandoned their jobs, the dismissal is unlawful and the workers remain employees entitled to reinstatement, though not to severance while their contracts subsist. [Para 113][Para 117][Para 120][Para 121][Para 124]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that a court order on the legality of a strike does not decide the reinstatement claim, so the two proceedings can run separately; and that an employer may regroup teams and reassign workers provided skill, wage and benefits are preserved. [Para 93][Para 152]
