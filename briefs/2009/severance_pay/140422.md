# Award No. 111/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លីមីធីត ("Hou Yie Garment") (Arbitration Council, 11 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (លី តៃ សេង, employer party; វ៉ែន ពៅ, worker party; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាម chair) · Doc ID 140422

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លីមីធីត, began operating in 2005 on National Road 2, Chhak Angrae Leu, Khan Mean Chey, and employs about 898 workers. [Para 1][Para 51] Two unions exist in the factory, the C.CAWDU local and the Khmer Youth Union; the claimant is the local of សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា, with about 70 members and no dues deduction yet arranged. [Para 2][Para 52][Para 54][Para 55] Of nine demands, six were settled in conciliation and one was withdrawn at the hearing, leaving the demand that occasional workers be converted to permanent status. [Para 14][Para 18][Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must convert occasional workers who have worked from one to over five years into permanent staff with full rights? [Para 2][Para 58][Para 73]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** 20 to 30 occasional workers in the packing, warehouse, finishing and cutting sections have worked 8 hours a day, 26 days a month, for more than three months and some for over a year, yet remain occasional; the company even renews their cards every two months to keep them on that footing. [Para 58][Para 62][Para 74]
**Employer (respondent):** occasional workers are taken on without a written contract to absorb periodic production peaks and are assessed by their team leaders; conversion depends on that assessment, and the company could not convert 20 to 30 workers at once or run two payroll systems. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand for wholesale conversion of occasional workers to permanent status was dismissed, but the company was ordered to give occasional workers the same statutory rights and benefits as permanent staff. [Para 91][Para 92]

The panel applied Articles 9 and 10 of the Labour Law, which distinguish permanent from occasional work but provide that occasional workers owe the same duties and enjoy the same rights as permanent workers. [Para 74][Para 79] It followed award 97/08, which held that occasional and permanent workers receive identical rights and duties under the law, so that the label matters less than the entitlements attached to it, and that a change of label has no legal effect. [Para 81][Para 84][Para 86] On the facts it found that the company had hired occasional workers over a long period, that some had over a year's service, that it paid them the same basic wage of US$50, a US$5 attendance bonus and a US$6 living allowance, but withheld the severance of 5% and the seniority bonus owed on fixed-term contracts; that was a breach of Article 10, which required the employer to grant occasional workers the same benefits as permanent staff. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 90] The panel therefore refused to order conversion, holding that it would be legally ineffective, and ordered equal treatment instead. [Para 84][Para 91][Para 92]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Under Article 10 occasional and permanent workers have the same rights and duties, so an arbitration panel will not order an employer to relabel occasional workers as permanent — the relabelling has no legal effect — but it will order that occasional workers receive the same statutory rights and benefits, including severance and the seniority bonus, as permanent staff of the same employer. [Para 79][Para 84][Para 91][Para 92]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the conversion of occasional workers into permanent staff is not itself problematic, and its remark that the union had not yet asked the company to deduct dues, are not part of the rule. [Para 83][Para 55]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed "ហូយៀ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លីមីធីត" in the party block but "ហូយៀ", "ហូយ្យំ", "ហូល" and "ហូ យៀង" elsewhere, so the registered form cannot be confirmed [Para 1][Para 43][Para 44][Para 51].
- The arbitrator selected by the employer is printed "លី តៃ សេង" in the composition list; the chair appears as "ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាម" in the composition list and as "ប៉ែន ប៊ុនឈាម" in the signature block [Para 1][Para 94].
- A line of scanned Latin text and fragments ("and the state … the control") interrupts the document at [Para 51], and the exhibits list at [Para 22] to [Para 41] is a run of dismissal letters whose dates conflict — the same worker appears with letters dated 15, 30 and 31 July and 5, 15 and 29 August 2009.
- The wage figures in the reasons are partly corrupted: the basic wage of US$50 and the bonuses of US$5 and US$6 at [Para 71] and [Para 88] are legible, but the severance rate is printed "៥ភាគរយ" (5%) while the statutory rate for fixed-term severance is expressed differently at [Para 71] and [Para 88], so the exact contractual term cannot be read.
- The workers cited as examples of long-serving occasional staff are named individually in a list at [Para 59]; none is named in this brief.
