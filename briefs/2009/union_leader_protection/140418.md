# Award No. 99/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃឹង ឡែន ហ្គា មិន ("Khing Lan Garment") (Arbitration Council, 14 August 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (យូ សួន ទី, employer party; អន ណន, worker party; រុន សារ៉ាយ chair) · Doc ID 140418

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃឹង ឡែន ហ្គា មិន, a garment factory on National Road 2, Khan Mean Chey, employs about 505 workers; it did not attend conciliation or the hearing and filed no evidence. [Para 1][Para 17][Para 26][Para 34][Para 37] The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា, registered in 2002 with over 230 members. [Para 35][Para 36] Seven demands remained unresolved after conciliation, covering rest-day changes, holiday pay, the dismissal of one worker, leave procedures, a deducted day's wage, medicines and medical-check fees. [Para 2][Para 10][Para 16]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must consult the union and workers before moving Sunday or holiday rest days to other days? [Para 2][Para 38]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay double wages to workers who volunteer to work on holidays or Sundays, and stop team leaders threatening those who decline? [Para 3][Para 44]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must recompute the terminal benefits of the worker it dismissed, including moral damages, severance, annual leave and notice? [Para 4][Para 47]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must make it easier for workers to obtain leave for personal business? [Para 5][Para 58]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the company may deduct a day's wage for 27 May 2009, the day it told workers to stay home for a bridge inauguration? [Para 8][Para 64]
6. (Issue 6) Whether the company must stock sufficient medicines for sick workers? [Para 9][Para 71]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the company must reimburse the 12,000 riel medical check each worker paid on recruitment? [Para 10][Para 73]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company repeatedly moved rest days without discussion, paid no premium for voluntary holiday work, dismissed a union member without cause and paid her only US$12.79, made leave applications go to the owner in person, deducted the 27 May wage without warning, kept no adequate medicines, and made recruits pay their own 12,000 riel medical check. [Para 38][Para 46][Para 53][Para 58][Para 67][Para 71][Para 73]
**Employer (respondent):** absent throughout; the record contains no position, though the conciliation report shows it declined to discuss the demands. [Para 17][Para 25][Para 37]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 1, 2 and 7 were refused in part or in whole while orders were made on issues 3, 4, 5 and 6 and on holiday rest days: severance and terminal pay for the dismissed worker, facilitation of leave, one day's wage for 27 May 2009, sufficient medicines and 12,000 riel per worker for medical checks. [Para 219][Para 220][Para 221][Para 222][Para 223][Para 224][Para 225]

On issue 1 the panel read Articles 146 to 148 and 160 as allowing an employer to move the weekly rest day but only with the labour inspector's authorisation, and found no evidence that authorisation was sought; it refused to order prior consultation over Sunday rest, yet ordered the company to discuss any change to holiday rest days with workers and the union. [Para 84][Para 91][Para 100][Para 104][Para 219]

On issue 2 the panel held that voluntary holiday or Sunday work attracts 200% of normal wages under Article 139 as amended, Article 164 and Article 4 of Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999, but only where the employer has not obtained the Ministry's prior permission; since the employer was absent and the workers proved nothing more, it ordered the 200% rate conditionally. [Para 115][Para 118][Para 119][Para 220]

On issue 3 the panel found the dismissal unlawful for want of a valid reason and want of warning, applied Articles 74, 75, 77, 89, 91, 166 and 167, and awarded notice compensation, severance of 15 days, moral damages equal to severance and unused annual leave. [Para 124][Para 126][Para 131][Para 137][Para 143][Para 148][Para 160][Para 166]

On issue 4 it held that Article 2 gives the employer managerial discretion over leave but that discretion must be exercised lawfully and reasonably, and ordered the company to make access easier. [Para 171][Para 181][Para 222]

On issue 5 it found the 27 May absence was authorised by the employer, that authorisation did not imply unpaid leave, and that Articles 71 and 72 did not apply because the workers had not agreed to a deduction, so the wage was owed. [Para 187][Para 191][Para 192][Para 223]

On issue 6 it applied Article 242(4) and Prakas No. 330 of 6 December 2000 and ordered an adequate stock of medicines. [Para 194][Para 200][Para 224]

On issue 7 it applied Article 247(a) and (c) and Article 2(d) of inter-ministerial Prakas No. 1191 of 21 November 2006, which fixes the fee at 12,000 riel for a Khmer worker, and ordered reimbursement for workers recruited within the three years before the award, Article 120 barring older claims. [Para 203][Para 204][Para 213][Para 218]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer who moves the weekly rest day off Sunday must obtain the labour inspector's prior authorisation under Articles 148 and 160; absent it, work performed on the substituted day is work on a rest day attracting 200% of normal wages under Article 139 as amended. And where an employer itself tells workers not to attend on a given day, the absence is authorised, so the wage for that day remains payable unless the workers agreed to forgo it. [Para 91][Para 119][Para 221][Para 223]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the employer's absence meant it had abandoned its right to answer the workers' allegations, and that wages may not be deducted without consent, are general observations rather than operative rules. [Para 97][Para 210]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed "ឃឹង ឡែន" in the party block but "ឃីង ឡែន" throughout the reasons and order [Para 1][Para 34][Para 219]; the union appears both as សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and with an extra "រោងចក្រ ឃីង ឡែន" qualifier [Para 1][Para 36].
- The parties' representatives at [Para 1] include a garbled fragment "មជ្ឈមណូល" and the union president's name is partly lost.
- The dismissed worker is named in the award; her name is not reproduced here, and she appears as a bare initial in places ("តោ ចាន់ អុន").
- The amount of the medical check is printed "១២០០០ រៀល" in the demand and "១២.០០០ រៀល" in the order [Para 10][Para 225]; the dismissal date is given as 8 May 2009 [Para 52] and the last wage as US$12.79 [Para 53].
- The chair's name is broken in the signature block, where only "promon" and initials survive [Para 227].
