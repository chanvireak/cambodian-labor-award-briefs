# Award No. 156/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេច អិម ប្លាស់ស្ទិច and the Free Workers' Union at the plant (Arbitration Council, 02 December 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា; employer nominee យូ សួន ទី; worker nominee អន ណន) · Doc ID 140454

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេច អិម ប្លាស់ស្ទិច (HM Plastic) has operated since about April 2003 at Borei Kamkar village, Bek Chan commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, and employs about 80 workers. [Para 1][Para 50][Para 51] Its only union, សហជីព សេរីភាព កម្មករ រោងចក្រ អេក អឹម ផ្លាស្ទិច, registered on 28 December 2007, has 60 members. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 55] On 28 October 2009 workers waited for the company to say whether 29 October would be a paid holiday, and from 15:30 to 16:30 they sat idle; when the three union leaders went to ask the administration chief, the company allowed only the president in and refused the others, and the workforce then stopped work. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 63][Para 68][Para 74] On 30 October 2009 the company suspended the three leaders and asked the Kandal labour department to approve their dismissal under Article 293; the letters reached them and the department on 5 November 2009. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 81] The strike ran from 28 October to 13 November 2009, and the company hired 26 replacement workers between 5 and 10 November before letting them go on 16 November. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 151]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may order the company to take back the three union leaders while the labour inspector is still seized of the request to dismiss them? [Para 55][Para 115][Para 120]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must repay the October 2009 wages it deducted from the three leaders? [Para 84][Para 124]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must pay wages and the attendance bonus for the strike period from 05 to 13 November 2009? [Para 101][Para 143]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — សហជីព សេរីភាព កម្មករ រោងចក្រ អេក អឹម ផ្លាស្ទិច):** the leaders never told anyone to strike and only went to ask about the holiday notice; the company had never docked their pay before and gave no reason for doing so in October; and because it brought in replacements during the strike it must pay them for those days. [Para 61][Para 94][Para 95][Para 111]
**Employer (respondent — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេច អិម ប្លាស់ស្ទិច):** the leaders led and incited an unlawful strike from 28 October to 13 November 2009, which is serious misconduct under Article 83 and article 10(c) of the internal rules, so their dismissal has been referred to the inspector and the company awaits the decision; the October deduction was because their work was poor and the sum paid is an incentive, not base wage; and no wages or bonus are due for an unlawful strike. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 97][Para 112]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must repay the deducted October wages of USD 73 to one leader, USD 27 to the second and USD 37 to the third; the panel declined to consider reinstatement while the labour inspector is seized; and it dismissed the claim for wages and attendance bonus during the strike. [Para 169][Para 168][Para 170]

On Issue 1 the panel held that Article 293(1) makes the labour inspector's approval a precondition for dismissing a staff delegate or union leader, that the company filed its request on 05 November 2009, and that no decision had issued by the hearing; following Case No. 79/06 it declined to act while the matter lay within the inspector's competence. [Para 115][Para 119][Para 120][Para 121][Para 123]

On Issue 2 the panel applied Articles 102 and 103 of the Labour Law, which define wage to include bonuses, incentives, seniority payments and overtime; it found the sums paid to the three were wages agreed orally and paid consistently since late 2007, that the company produced no evidence they were purely discretionary, and that its payroll ran from the 26th of one month to the 25th of the next, so the October wage covered a period ending 25 October — three days before the strike began. [Para 126][Para 127][Para 129][Para 132][Para 137][Para 138] A deduction imposed as discipline must be proportionate under Article 27, and this one had no basis, so repayment was ordered. [Para 133][Para 139][Para 142]

On Issue 3 the panel applied Article 332, under which the employment contract is suspended during a strike and wages are not paid, and Article 334, under which an employer that hires replacements must nevertheless pay the strikers. [Para 144][Para 150] It found the company had hired replacements, which on its own reading would entitle the workers to strike pay. [Para 151][Para 152] It then held the strike unlawful because the union gave no seven-day notice as Article 324 requires and had not exhausted conciliation as Article 320(4) requires, and concluded that the workers could not claim the wages and bonus. [Para 154][Para 155][Para 157][Para 163][Para 167]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A deduction from wages imposed as a disciplinary sanction must rest on a proven fault and be proportionate under Article 27; where the pay period closed before the misconduct alleged, the deduction is unfounded and the sum must be repaid. While a request to dismiss a union leader is pending before the labour inspector under Article 293, the panel will not order reinstatement. Strike pay is not owed where the strike was called without the seven-day notice of Article 324 and without exhaustion of conciliation under Article 320(4). [Para 139][Para 120][Para 157]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's reading of Article 334 — that hiring replacements obliges the employer to pay strikers — was stated but not applied, because it went on to find the strike unlawful. [Para 150][Para 152]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award contradicts itself on strike pay: one passage holds that, because replacements were hired, the workers may claim wages for the strike period, while a later passage and the operative order hold that they may not. [Para 152][Para 159][Para 170] One passage states the second and third leaders' monthly wage as USD 1,200, where the findings and the order use USD 200. [Para 127][Para 124][Para 169] The company name is printed in at least six forms (អេច អិម ប្លាស់ស្ទិច, អេច រិម ហួស់ ស្ទិច, រេរច អិម ប្ឆាស់ ស្ទិច, អេច អិប្ឆាស់ ស្ទិច), and the chair appears as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា and ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 50][Para 172] The union leader's name is printed as ប៊ុន តុល, ប៊ុន តុ and ប៊ុន តុលេខា. [Para 55][Para 113][Para 169] The strike start is given as 28 October in the findings and 05 November in the claim, and Prakas No. 099 is cited once as "99/04". [Para 101][Para 109][Para 140]
