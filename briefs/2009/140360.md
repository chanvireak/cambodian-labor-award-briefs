# Award No. 19/09 — Galaxy Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 May 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phallack presiding; Chhiv Phyrum, employer nominee; Ann Vireak, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140360

## 1. Facts
Galaxy Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, at O Andong village, Prek Pra commune, Meanchey district, Phnom Penh, faced ten demands from the Local Union of the Free Trade Union of Workers of the Kingdom of Cambodia (FTUWKC): reinstatement of 18 workers, a US$6 living allowance per worker, 50 percent of wages for women on maternity leave, an infirmary with a doctor, clean and hygienic toilets with sufficient water, medical fees for work-related accidents, hygienic drinking water, 18 days of annual leave, conversion of long-serving workers to permanent contracts, and twice-monthly wage payment under the newly promulgated Prakas. [Para 1][Para 2] Conciliation on 21 January 2009 settled none of them and the ten issues reached the Secretariat on 11 February 2009. [Para 4][Para 6] The employer was absent from the first and second hearings of 20 February and 2 March 2009; the workers attended, then asked for a suspension to negotiate while the factory had suspended employment, and both parties were absent without explanation from the third hearing of 20 May 2009. [Para 7][Para 9][Para 10][Para 13] Because no party appeared, the panel made no finding of fact on any demand. [Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, both parties having been duly notified of the date, time and venue of the hearing and neither appearing nor giving reasons, the panel should terminate the arbitration and close the case by award under Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules? [Para 15][Para 22]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the FTUWKC local union at Galaxy Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd pursued the ten demands above, attended the first two hearings, and on 2 March 2009 wrote asking that the case be suspended so that negotiations could continue with the employer, whose operations were then suspended. [Para 1][Para 8][Para 10]
**Employer (respondent):** the company entered no appearance and sent no representative to any of the three hearings, giving no reason for its absence. [Para 7][Para 9][Para 13][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed Case 19/09-Galaxy, leaving all ten demands undecided. [Para 22]
Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 lets the panel proceed in the absence of a party duly invited or terminate the proceedings by award, and Rule 4.7 of the Annex to the same Prakas repeats the choice. [Para 15] Following Awards 30/05-Maurea, 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M & V (Branch 3), 95/08-Yung Wah, 132/08-GHG, 138/08-Malin, 151/08-Wilson Garment and 09/09-Nimbus Textile, the panel held that three conditions must be met before a case is closed: proper notification, non-appearance, and no reason given for the absence. [Para 15][Para 16]
On notification, the Secretariat sent written notices in the regular form and also confirmed the date, time and venue by telephone, so the first condition was met. [Para 17][Para 18] On appearance, neither the workers nor the employer attended the third hearing and neither appointed a representative, whether from the federation, the union or a lawyer, so the second condition was met. [Para 19][Para 20] On reasons, both sides were silent before the Secretariat, so the third condition was met as well. [Para 21]
The panel did not weigh the merits of the ten demands and made no order on reinstatement, allowances, maternity pay, the infirmary, sanitation, drinking water, annual leave, contract conversion or wage frequency. [Para 15][Para 22]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party duly notified of the hearing fails to appear without good cause, the panel may terminate the arbitration by award instead of deciding the merits, provided each of three conditions is independently established — proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of reasons — and the resulting award closes the case without adjudicating the demands, which remain undecided unless revived by a fresh complaint. [Para 15][Para 21][Para 22]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that the workers had earlier sought suspension pending negotiations and that the employer had gradually resumed operations, without treating either fact as an explanation for the absence at the third hearing. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The face of the award names the case "19109-Galaxy Textile" while the order closes "Case 19/09-Galaxy". [Para 1][Para 22] The evidence section was omitted from this English translation, which is stated on its face to be unofficial, the authoritative text being the Khmer original. [Para 1][Para 14] In the list of demands the fourth and fifth demands share one numbered line, so the printed numbering runs to ten over nine lines. [Para 2] The award date of 22 May 2009 post-dates the third hearing of 20 May 2009, but the file contains no note of any notification of the panel's intention to proceed in the parties' absence. [Para 1][Para 13]
