# Award No. 108/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យីង ចាង ("Ying Chang") (Arbitration Council, 10 September 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វូច ហ៊ុន, employer party; លីវ សុវណ្ណា, worker party; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140420

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន យីង ចាង, at Boeng Kak, Khan Tuol Kork, Phnom Penh, employs about 450 workers, roughly 80% of them on fixed-term or occasional contracts. [Para 1][Para 44][Para 69] The claimant is the សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ local at Ying Chang, which had lodged a registration application and held only a receipt dated 7 July 2009 when the case was heard. [Para 1][Para 46][Para 101] Of 12 demands, five (issues 1, 5, 8, 10 and 12) were withdrawn, leaving issues 2, 3, 4, 6 and 9; the company never attended. [Para 24][Para 30][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay maternity allowance of 50% of wages and allowances for 90 days, and pay it in advance of the leave? [Para 6][Para 47]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must build a nursery and a breastfeeding room? [Para 7][Para 54]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must preserve wages, the attendance bonus, the seniority bonus and the US$6 living allowance during sick leave supported by a proper medical certificate? [Para 8][Para 62]
4. (Issue 6) Whether the company must convert occasional workers with two months' service into permanent staff and raise their basic wage above US$50? [Para 10][Para 69]
5. (Issue 9) Whether the company must issue pay slips showing each worker's wage components on payday? [Para 13][Para 81]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company pays only 50% of the last basic wage for maternity leave and pays it after the worker returns rather than in advance; there is no nursery or breastfeeding room, so mothers go home to feed their children; wages, bonuses and the US$6 allowance are all cut during sick leave; workers with 2 to 5 years' service remain "occasional" with their cards renewed every two months; and wages are handed out by team leaders with no slip. [Para 47][Para 56][Para 62][Para 74][Para 83]
**Employer (respondent):** absent from conciliation and from the hearing, and filed no evidence or position on any demand. [Para 17][Para 24][Para 88]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to pay maternity allowance at 50% for 90 days in advance of the leave, to build a nursery and breastfeeding room, to pay the US$6 monthly living allowance during certified sick leave, and to issue pay slips; the claims for full wages during sick leave and for wholesale conversion of occasional workers were refused. [Para 233][Para 234][Para 241][Para 242][Para 244]

On issue 2 the panel applied Articles 182 and 183(1), which grant 90 days' maternity leave at half pay including allowances, and held that the 12-month average wage must be the basis of calculation — not the last basic wage as the company did — and that Article 115 requires payment before the leave begins. [Para 115][Para 119][Para 121][Para 125][Para 130]

On issue 3 it applied Article 186, which requires a breastfeeding room and a nursery where the enterprise employs at least 100 women, and ordered both, adding that where the company cannot take children over 18 months it must pay the cost of an outside nursery against receipts. [Para 133][Para 136][Para 141][Para 142]

On issue 4 it held that Article 71(3) suspends the contract during certified sick leave and Article 72(1) removes the wage obligation, so full wages were refused; but since the attendance and seniority bonuses are part of wages under Article 103, they must be reduced in proportion to days absent and the US$6 living allowance must be paid in full because the governing instrument attaches no condition. [Para 144][Para 147][Para 157][Para 165][Para 185][Para 202][Para 203]

On issue 6 the panel followed award 97/08: occasional and permanent workers have the same statutory rights, but relabelling them has no legal effect, so the conversion claim was refused while the company was ordered to give occasional workers the same rights and benefits as permanent staff. [Para 205][Para 209][Para 218][Para 243]

On issue 9 it read Article 112(b) as requiring clear wage information each pay period, found that slips impose no real burden because payroll is already computerised, and ordered slips on payday. [Para 220][Para 228][Para 232]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Maternity allowance must be calculated on the average of the 12 months' wages preceding the leave rather than the last basic wage, and must be paid before the leave starts. Sick leave suspends the contract so full wages are not owed, but because attendance and seniority bonuses are components of wages their reduction must be proportional to days absent, while an allowance granted without conditions cannot be withheld. [Para 125][Para 130][Para 165][Para 202]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that relabelling occasional workers as permanent staff has no legal effect, and that pay slips improve transparency, are explanatory rather than operative. [Para 209][Para 229]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed "យឹង ផាង" in the party block, "យើង ចាង" in the case records and "យិង ចាង" / "យីង ថាង" in the reasons and order [Para 1][Para 33][Para 44][Para 234].
- The union's name is likewise unstable — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ and "សមាជិព យុវជន ខ្មែរ" — and its registration status is described as pending at [Para 46] while the panel later treats reliance on Prakara 19 as sufficient [Para 106][Para 108].
- The arbitrator selected by the employer is named "សេង វួច ហ៊ុន" in the composition list but the employer's signature line at [Para 246] carries no name at all, only stray Latin and digits.
- Pages 246 to 270 of the source are almost wholly destroyed, containing an appendix of workers' thumbprints reduced to fragments of Latin words, digits and punctuation, so the list of workers covered by the award cannot be read.
- The living allowance instrument is cited with contradictory numbers — "សេចក្តីជូនដំណឹង លេខ ០៣២/០៨" at [Para 191] and "ប្រកាស លេខ ១៤ ចុះ ថ្ងៃទី ១៦ ខែសីហា ឆ្នាំ ២០០២" at [Para 148].
