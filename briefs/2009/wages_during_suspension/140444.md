# Award No. 137/09 and 138/09 — ញ៉ូ ភាញ់ វិល ត្រេត (ខេមបូឌា) ចំកាត់ (Arbitration Council, 13 October 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: រ៉ឹង សុទ្ធិ (employer-appointed), ពិរវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-appointed), កង់ ផល្ហៈ (president) · Doc ID 140444

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញ៉ូ ភាញ់ វិល ត្រេត (ខេមបូឌា) ចំកាត់, a cutting and garment plant on National Road 5 in Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, employed about 501 workers. [Para 1][Para 24]
សហជីព សេរីកម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា filed ten demands in case 137/09 and two more in case 138/09; none was settled at conciliation, and the parties agreed to have both files heard by one panel. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 28]
The live demands were that the company pay 50 per cent of wages during a two-month suspension of contracts running from 1 October to 30 November 2009, that it pay 100 per cent if there was still no work on return, or else terminate the contracts lawfully, and that it stop discriminating against the union and start deducting union dues. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 12][Para 14]
About 300 workers struck from 1 October 2009. On 6 October the panel ordered them back to work on 7 October pending the merits; they did not return and the strike continued to 12 October. [Para 29][Para 32][Para 33][Para 40]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural issue) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration while the workers remain on strike in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 and of the panel's own return-to-work order? [Para 48][Para 57]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហជីព សេរីកម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា):** the company owed wages for the two-month suspension, the workers would not accept the 13 USD a month offered in place of staying at work, and their final demand was 16 USD a month; they stayed out because the employer had not met the claim. [Para 12][Para 37][Para 38]
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញ៉ាញ់ វិល ត្រេត (ខេមបូឌា) ចំកាត់):** with no cloth to sew it could offer only 13 USD a month, it kept the gate open for workers who had not finished their contracts, and it asked the panel to order the strike stopped because it was disrupting the production line. [Para 13][Para 29][Para 38][Para 39]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to continue the arbitration and closed both files, leaving the demands undecided, with liberty to take the case up again once the strike ended. [Para 57][Para 58]
The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04, which requires parties to suspend every act of dispute — strike or lockout — for the duration of the arbitration and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. It read the article, as it had in earlier awards, to mean that the panel will not run an arbitration while the workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 48][Para 50]
It followed award 04/04, which held that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during the proceedings and that the panel cannot decide a collective dispute referred to it in that situation, and awards 77/05, 28/06 and 34/06, in which files were closed for the same reason. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 55]
On the facts, the panel found the workers had not shown a genuine intention to resolve the dispute through arbitration, because they kept striking and ignored both the panel's order and its request to return. It therefore made no findings on any of the twelve demands and issued no order on wages, discrimination or dues. [Para 47][Para 56][Para 57]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate, and will close the file without deciding the merits, where the workers continue a strike during the proceedings in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 and of a return-to-work order; the file may be reopened once the strike ends. [Para 50][Para 53][Para 58]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it had no factual findings to make on the referred issues because the hearing could not proceed, and its observation that the employer had left its gate open to workers whose contracts had not ended. [Para 39][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is heavy. The conciliation minutes for the company are dated 29 October 2009, after the award itself (13 October 2009), and the non-conciliation report is dated both 2 October and "2/21" October 2009. The second case is described as covering "402 points" where the recital plainly means two. [Para 18][Para 27][Para 26]
The company name appears in at least four forms (ញ៉ូ ភាញ់, ញ្ញូ ភាញ់, ញ៉ូ ភញ៉េ, ញ៉ាញ់), and the union's name is rendered both as សហជីព សេរីកម្មករ and សហភាព សេរីកម្មករ. The three arbitrators are printed under different names in the header and in the signature block, so the header forms are used here. [Para 1][Para 59]
Individual workers, shop stewards and the employer's representatives survive only as corrupted strings and are identified by role. [Para 2]
