# Award No. 144/09 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្កាមិន លីមីធីត (PCS Garment Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 28 October 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: អ៉ីង សុម្លឹ រ (employer-appointed), សុង វណ្ណ ស៊ីន (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140447

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្កាមិន លីមីធីត, a garment factory on Federation of Russia Boulevard, Tuek Thla, Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, faced a collective claim brought through សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ខ្មែរ on behalf of 69 sewing workers in groups 27 and 28. [Para 1][Para 2]
The workers put three demands: that the company keep groups 27 and 28 as they were, that it merge them into a single group, and, if it would do neither, that it pay the terminal benefits due under the Labour Law. All three remained unresolved at conciliation on 13 October 2009. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 14]
The workers were already on strike when the file reached the panel, and they stayed out through the pre-hearing meeting of 22 October 2009, at which the panel explained that it cannot arbitrate while a strike continues. [Para 17][Para 18]
On 27 October 2009 the panel issued an order requiring the strike to stop at once; the workers did not comply and were still striking when the award issued. [Para 21][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural issue) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration while the workers remain on strike in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 and of the panel's order of 27 October 2009? [Para 30][Para 38]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ខ្មែរ):** the 69 workers of groups 27 and 28 insisted that their groups be preserved, or merged into one, failing which the company should pay them the compensation the Labour Law provides; they maintained the strike to press those demands. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 29]
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ភីស៊ី អេស ហ្កាមិន លីមីធីត):** it could not keep groups 27 and 28 unchanged and could not merge them, and it asked the panel to intervene in the strike, which it said was disrupting production. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 13][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to continue the arbitration in case 144/09 and closed the file without deciding any of the three demands. [Para 38][Para 39]
The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04, under which the parties must suspend every act of dispute — strike or lockout — for the duration of the arbitration and must attend all meetings convened by the panel. It read the provision, consistently with its earlier practice, to bar it from arbitrating while the workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]
It followed award 04/04, which held that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during the proceedings and that the panel cannot decide a collective dispute referred to it while a strike continues, and awards 77/05, 28/06 and 34/06, in which files were closed on the same ground; it also relied on the parallel closure orders in cases 137/09 and 138/09. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]
On the facts, the panel found the workers had shown no genuine intention to have the dispute resolved by arbitration, because they kept striking after being told the proceedings could not run and after being ordered to stop. It made no findings on the group structure or on compensation. [Para 28][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate, and will close the file without deciding the merits, where the workers continue a strike during the proceedings in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 and of an order to stop striking; the file may be taken up again once the strike ends. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement at the pre-hearing meeting that the parties' written submissions due by 26 October 2009 were not filed, and its observation that a collective dispute cannot be resolved while production is halted. [Para 19][Para 20]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is heavy. The conciliation minutes are dated "13 Dulda 2009", a month that does not exist, and a second reference gives the minutes as 13 October 2009. The company name appears in at least five forms (ភីស៊ី អេស, ភិស៊ី អេស, រ៉ាស៊ីបរស, រ៉ាស៊ី ស៊ិរេរ, ភីស៊ិស៊ី រេរស), and the union's name is rendered both as សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ខ្មែរ and សហភាព សហជីព ខ្មែរ. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 30]
The file is only 40 lines long because the award terminates proceedings; the merits, the evidence and the parties' factual positions were never reached, and no reasoning on the three demands exists in the source. Individual workers and the union's representatives survive only as corrupted strings and are identified here by role. [Para 23][Para 2]
