# Award No. 147/09 — Tak Fat Garment (Cambodia) Ltd v. seven factory unions (Arbitration Council, 6 November 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (chair គង់ ផល្លៈ; worker-party nominee ប៉ែន ពៅ; employer-party nominee's name lost to OCR) · Doc ID 140449

## 1. Facts
Tak Fat Garment (Cambodia) Ltd — ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន (ខេមបូឌា) អែល ធី ឌី — runs a garment factory at Building 1159, National Road 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Leu, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, with about 1,823 workers. [Para 59] Seven factory unions, among them the federations of the Cambodian workers' movement, jointly demanded that the company terminate their members' labour contracts and pay severance under Articles 75, 77, 89, 91, 116, 166 and 167 of the Labour Law, on nine grounds: switching from state electricity to rented generators, selling off stored raw materials and fabric, channelling funds into a new factory registered in another person's name, provoking an unlawful strike, transferring key office staff away, a disputed handover of the factory to a former production manager whose wages went unpaid, and repeated unlawful suspension of work while neighbouring factories worked overtime. [Para 5][Para 7][Para 8][Para 12][Para 13] The unions refused the USD 12 per month suspension allowance the company offered for the two-month suspension from 08 October to 07 December 2009, having asked for 50 per cent as previously paid. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 17] Conciliation failed on one point, referred to the Council by non-conciliation report No. 989 of 21 October 2009. [Para 21][Para 25] The unions then declined to attend the hearing, and the panel closed the file without reaching the compensation claims. [Para 30][Para 61][Para 68]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may terminate the arbitration and close the file where the claimant unions decline to attend the hearing, on the ground that the lawyer acting for the company holds no valid authority to represent it? [Para 24][Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — the seven factory unions at Tak Fat Garment):** the document delegating authority to the company's lawyer is not lawful, because the officer who signed it does not appear in the company's statutes; in any dispute the workers may insist on dealing with the person truly responsible or his lawful representative, and as they could not identify who had transferred authority to the lawyer they decided to sit out the hearing. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 40][Para 42][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent — Tak Fat Garment (Cambodia) Ltd):** it rejected the demands in their entirety, saying the dispute arose because it had no work for the workers, that on 25 October 2009 it applied to suspend work under labour-inspector supervision, and that it had already raised the suspension allowance from USD 10 to USD 12 per month including a living allowance. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 147/09 (បិទសំណុំរឿង) without any ruling on the compensation claims. [Para 68][Para 69]

The panel grounded its competence in Chapter 12, Articles 309 to 317 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 with its annexed Rules of Procedure, and Prakas No. 190 of 02 September 2009 on the seventh mandate. [Para 19] It recorded that when the hearing opened the union party objected that the company's lawyer had no lawful representation, and that the pre-hearing refusal to attend could be treated as a party failing to appear or appearing without a representative. [Para 37][Para 66] Rule 4.7 of the Rules of Procedure annexed to Prakas No. 099 provides that where a party does not appear or has no representative, the panel may continue in the party's absence or terminate the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 63] The panel had explained the procedure and given the unions the option of proceeding lawfully, and had allowed the lawyer time to produce evidence of his authority; the unions nonetheless persisted in staying away. [Para 64][Para 65] It examined the authority itself and found that the name of the officer who delegated it does not appear in the company's statutes of 26 October 2009. [Para 65] On that basis it held it could terminate the proceedings by award and closed the file. [Para 67][Para 68] It also noted that no facts on the merits had been established, since no hearing on the substance was ever opened. [Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party chooses not to appear, or appears without a lawful representative, the panel is not obliged to hear the merits: Rule 4.7 of the Rules of Procedure annexed to Prakas No. 099 SKBY lets it either proceed in the party's absence or terminate the arbitration by award, and closing the file is then the procedural consequence of the party's own decision rather than a ruling on its claims. [Para 63][Para 66][Para 68] Whether a lawyer's authority to represent an employer is lawful turns on whether the officer who delegated it is shown as a company officer in the statutes. [Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that it had explained the conciliation and arbitration procedure to the unions and offered them a lawful route to proceed, and that it had given the company's lawyer time to prove his authority, explain why the non-appearance was treated as voluntary rather than as a denial of justice. [Para 33][Para 64][Para 65]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The OCR is badly degraded throughout: the party block of the award is a run of fragments, and several grounds of the demand are unrecoverable — ground 6 survives only as "ការប្រមូល" with stray digits. [Para 10]
- The panel's names are printed differently in the two places they appear: the head of the award gives the chair's name in one Khmer form and the worker-party nominee as វ៉ែន ពៅ, while the signature block gives the chair as គង់ ផល្លៈ and the nominee as ប៉ែន ពៅ; the employer-party nominee's name is corrupted to a Latin fragment and is not reproduced here. [Para 1][Para 70]
- The company's name is printed four ways across the file — តាក ហ្វាត, តារ ហ្វាត, រោក ហ្វាត and ពាក ហ្វាត — and its statutes are dated 23 June 1994 in the evidence list. [Para 15][Para 45][Para 47][Para 49]
- The panel cites the statutes as dated 26 October 2009 when testing the delegation of authority, while the delegation letter it examines is dated 26 October 2009 and the statutes in evidence are dated 23 June 1994; both dates are reported as printed. [Para 44][Para 47][Para 65]
- Dates are internally inconsistent: the objection letter is dated "២៣ ខែធ្នូ តុលា ឆ្នាំ ២០០៩" (December and October in the same line), the suspension request is dated "២៥ ០៥ ខែតុលា", and the hearing is listed at 02:00 in the afternoon but the parties are recorded as attending at 14:00. [Para 23][Para 34][Para 15][Para 51]
- The award sits at odds with itself on what was decided: the panel states there are no facts relating to the dispute because no hearing was held, yet the record shows both parties present at the hearing. [Para 35][Para 61]
