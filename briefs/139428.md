# Award No. 236/15 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន យី ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 23 September 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, appointed by the employer party, dissenting; អន ណន, appointed by the worker party; គង់ ផល្នៈ, chair) · Doc ID 139428

## 1. Facts
Jin Yi Garment (Cambodia) is a cutting and sewing factory at Sla Phnou, Sethey, Samaki Mean Chey, Kampong Chhnang, employing about 3,716 workers. [Para 1][Para 57][Para 58] Six of eleven collective demands remained unresolved after conciliation on 27 August 2015; at the hearing on 8 September the parties settled four and the workers withdrew a fifth, leaving only demand 6 — reinstatement of two hygiene-section workers. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] Both had been hired on 15 July 2013 on two-month probation and then four successive six-month fixed duration contracts, the last expiring 15 September 2015; the employer gave notice of non-renewal on 31 August 2015. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must remove the hygiene manager (name redacted) from his post? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay all workers full compensation when it closes a production building? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must share profits equal to one month's wage each year? [Para 4]
4. Whether the employer must apply employment contracts of one year rather than six months? [Para 5]
5. Whether the employer must raise the piece rate by 20 per cent? [Para 6]
6. Whether the employer must take back the two hygiene-section workers whose contracts it did not renew? [Para 7][Para 61]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two workers had worked hard and never absented themselves; under the internal rules dismissal is possible only after four light faults or two medium faults, and neither worker reached that threshold; the faults alleged — sitting under the stairs during working hours and slow work, and an unclean work area — do not warrant dismissal. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 75]
**Employer (respondent):** the contracts simply expired, and under the Labour Law an employer may renew or not renew a fixed duration contract according to the quality of the worker's work; it did issue education letters and a warning for the faults, and it renewed 38 of the 41 workers whose contracts ended that month. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — five of the six demands were settled or withdrawn at the hearing; on demand 6 the employer was ordered to take back the two hygiene-section workers and let them work normally. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21][Para 135][Para 136]

As a preliminary point the panel held that the elected worker delegate (name redacted) who attended had standing under Article 283 of the Labour Law to represent all the company's workers, both parties accepting his election. [Para 83][Para 86][Para 87][Para 88]

On demand 6 the panel first characterised the contracts. Under Article 67(2) a fixed duration contract may not exceed two years and may be renewed so long as the total stays within that limit; under Article 73(5) a fixed duration contract becomes one of undetermined duration where there is no notice of termination and the total duration exceeds the two-year ceiling. Reading those provisions with its own earlier award in Zaksintech, the panel held the ceiling applies to the aggregate and not to each renewal. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 92][Para 95][Para 96] Since probation plus four six-month contracts took both workers well beyond two years, their contracts had converted to undetermined duration, and the August non-renewal was therefore a dismissal under Article 74, not the expiry of a fixed term. [Para 102][Para 103][Para 104][Para 105][Para 106]

The panel then applied Article 74: an undetermined duration contract may be terminated by either party, but no dismissal may occur without a valid reason relating to the worker's aptitude or conduct or to the needs of the enterprise. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 109] Turning to Article 10 of the internal rules, it found dismissal permissible only after four consecutive light faults or two medium faults, and found that the employer had proved only education letters and one warning, none of them classified as light, medium or serious. [Para 77][Para 117][Para 118][Para 119][Para 121]

The faults themselves — sitting under the stairs during working hours, slow work, and an unclean area — were held not to be of a kind that justifies dismissal, and the sanction imposed was disproportionate to the fault, contrary to Article 27. [Para 122][Para 123][Para 126][Para 128] Reinstatement in the old or a suitable post being available under Article 34 of Prakas No. 099, the panel so ordered. [Para 132][Para 135]

Arbitrator អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី dissented in an annexed opinion: the employer may choose the contract type; the conversion to undetermined duration at each expiry was compensated because the employer had always paid the 5 per cent termination payment under Article 73(6), so its Article 73 duty was discharged and reinstatement was wrong. [Para 138][Para 140][Para 143][Para 154][Para 160][Para 162]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Successive fixed duration contracts whose aggregate term exceeds two years convert by force of Articles 67(2) and 73(5) into a contract of undetermined duration, so a refusal to renew at expiry is a dismissal that must rest on a valid reason under Article 74. Faults that the enterprise's internal rules grade as light or medium, and which do not reach the rules' own threshold for dismissal, cannot supply that reason even when proved, and the sanction must remain proportionate to the fault under Article 27. [Para 96][Para 105][Para 121][Para 128]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that an elected worker delegate may represent all workers in the enterprise under Article 283 [Para 87], and that an employer should first make a written evaluation of the fault before imposing discipline [Para 127], were not necessary to the reinstatement order.
