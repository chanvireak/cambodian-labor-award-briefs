# Award No. 052/20 — Quin Th Merjer In Das Striel Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Sin Kim Sean, Pen Bun Chhea presiding) · Doc ID 140809

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃ្វីន ធ៏ មេជើរ អ៉ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល ខូអិលធីឌី operates at Trapeang Chheu Neang, Peuk commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal. [Para 1]
Workers lodged a three-point collective demand with the Kandal provincial labour department on 17 June 2020; conciliation on 24 July 2020 left one point unsettled, which was transmitted by non-conciliation report No. 541/20 of 3 August 2020. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14]
The unsettled demand was that the company cease discriminating against the workers' democratic union at the plant, the workers alleging that the company had terminated members and activists after suspending contracts for want of work. [Para 2][Para 4]
The first hearing was adjourned because the workers said a demand point was missing from the file; both hearings were held on 25 September and 12 October 2020, and the workers then boycotted the second. [Para 17][Para 10][Para 11][Para 80]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the arbitration proceedings must be continued, or terminated, where the claimant worker party has been validly summoned and declines to attend? [Para 69]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers denied that the company had any basis to end their contracts and insisted the company had dismissed union members and activists, so that the demand for an end to anti-union discrimination stood. [Para 4] They refused to attend on the ground that the provincial report transmitted only three of the four points in their original complaint and that page 4 of the 24 July 2020 conciliation minutes was missing. [Para 21][Para 23]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃ្វីន ធ៏ មេជើរ អ៉ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល ខូអិលធីឌី says it did not discriminate against the union, that it ended the contracts of all workers because it had no work for them, and it attended the second hearing through counsel. [Para 3][Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 052/20 on account of the absence of the worker party, treating the claim as abandoned. [Para 83][Para 85]
The panel put the question to itself as one of procedure: Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council requires the panel to summon the disputing parties to state their claims in person and to supply documents, requires the parties to attend every meeting, and permits the panel either to proceed in the absence of a party or to close the case by an award. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 71]
Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 was read the same way, on condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 72][Para 73]
The panel held that appearing "before the panel" means both presence at the hearing and participation until the proceedings conclude, and it followed earlier awards Nos. 16/07, 27/07, 138/08, 11/11, 131/12, 108/13 and 332/14 terminating proceedings where a claimant failed to appear. [Para 76][Para 78]
It rejected the workers' justification, holding that the omission of a demand point from the conciliation file could not license a boycott, and drew from that the conclusion of abandonment. [Para 81][Para 82]
The award records that it becomes final eight days after notification if no written objection is filed with the Minister of Labour through the Council secretariat. [Para 86]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claimant party that, having been properly notified, stays away from a scheduled hearing without a valid reason is taken to have abandoned its claim, and the panel will close the proceedings rather than decide the merits in its absence. [Para 81][Para 82]
A procedural grievance about the conciliation file — a demand point dropped from the transmission report or a missing page — is not such a reason, because the panel's own duty is to summon the parties and hear their claims in person. [Para 74][Para 76]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel described the four stages of an arbitration hearing and noted that in earlier cases it had ended proceedings where a claimant was summoned but neither appeared nor participated; that description was not applied to any fact beyond the boycott. [Para 77]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is printed as "ឆ្នាំ ២០" with the final two digits missing, including the hearings of 25 September and 12 October 2020 and the award date; the panel is also said to have been constituted on "១៥ ខែកញ្ញា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០", giving two conflicting year forms on the same line. [Para 1][Para 11][Para 15]
The respondent's name is printed in at least six variants across the document, including "ឃ្វីន ធ៏", "ឃ្វីន ធ៍" and "ឃ្វីន ធ៍ មេ ដើរ", and one entry substitutes "JICA" for the company name. [Para 2][Para 8][Para 21][Para 59]
One item in the employer's evidence list is an invitation to a Phnom Penh Capital Council consultation forum addressed to the company's lawyer, which has no apparent connection to this dispute. [Para 46]
At the signature block the employer-appointed arbitrator's name is overlaid by stray OCR characters, though the same arbitrator is legible in the panel composition. [Para 86][Para 1]
