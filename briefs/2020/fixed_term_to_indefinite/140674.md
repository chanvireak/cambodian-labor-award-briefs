# Award No. 039/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គ្រោន សឺវីស អ៉ិនធើណេសិន ណល (Ground Service International) (Arbitration Council, 23 July 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (Ing Sothy), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (Liv Sovanna), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (Pen Bun Chea, Chair) · Doc ID 140674

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គ្រោន សឺវីស អ៉ិនធើណេសិន ណល, at Kruos village, Siem Reap, supplies labour and employs 57 workers, including airport cleaning staff placed at Siem Reap International Airport [Para 1][Para 52][Para 57]. The union, សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត វិស័យ ទេសចរណ៍ កម្ពុជា, registered in January 2020, and the workers complained to the Siem Reap Labour Department on 18 May 2020 over four demands [Para 13][Para 41]. Two of them, Issues 1 and 4, were withdrawn at the hearing, leaving Issue 2 (reinstatement of dismissed workers with back pay) and Issue 3 (annual leave) [Para 19][Para 20]. All workers were on rolling three-month fixed-duration contracts paid by days worked, 21 to 23 days a month [Para 57][Para 76]. The employer declined to renew the contracts of four union members, three of whom it took back, and paid five percent severance pay [Para 56][Para 59]. The employer admits it has never granted paid annual leave [Para 74].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the non-renewal of the fixed-duration contracts of the four workers was lawful, or was instead union discrimination entitling them to reinstatement and back pay? [Para 81]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must grant and apply paid annual leave under the Labour Law? [Para 111]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The four were a union founder and members who voted to establish the union, the employer knew of the union from the earlier case No. 003/20, notice of the founder and of the 20 October 2019 election with 21 participants was served, and the employer renewed others while dropping these four, so the non-renewal was union discrimination [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 65].

**Employer (respondent):** The four were on three-month contracts that reached their end dates; the airport has not signed a new service contract because of COVID-19, the four worked inside the airport, notice of non-renewal was given in advance, and over 40 other workers were also let go regardless of union membership, so there was no discrimination; annual leave is hard to schedule because the work is seasonal [Para 67][Para 68][Para 69][Para 79].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand for reinstatement of the four workers and back pay was dismissed, and the employer was ordered to grant and apply paid annual leave in accordance with the Labour Law [Para 128][Para 129]

On Issue 2 the panel applied Article 73(1) of the Labour Law: a fixed-duration contract terminates at its stated end date, and whether to renew is the right of the contracting parties, no party being able to force renewal without consent, as held in Award 100/07 — Ho Yea Cambodia and others [Para 81][Para 83][Para 85]. It found the four workers' last contracts ended on 31 March and 30 April 2020, that notice was given before those dates, and that three of them received and accepted the five percent severance pay [Para 71][Para 72][Para 86].

The workers nevertheless argued that Article 12 and Article 279 of the Labour Law, read with Article 62 of the Trade Union Law of 2016, make non-renewal of successive fixed-duration contracts a form of dismissal that can amount to union discrimination [Para 89][Para 90][Para 93][Para 97]. The panel accepted that reading in principle: the general rule of contractual freedom cannot apply where the non-renewal is shown to be linked to union discrimination [Para 101][Para 102]. But the worker party bears the burden of proving discrimination, as held in Awards 148/07 — Pay Her, 182/16 and 214/16, and here it offered no evidence beyond the timing and its own assertion, while conceding that the employer had taken back some union members [Para 103][Para 107][Para 108]. The panel therefore found no link between the non-renewals and union discrimination and dismissed the claim [Para 109][Para 110].

On Issue 3, Article 166 of the Labour Law gives every worker paid annual leave of one and a half working days per month of continuous service, and Article 167 requires one year of service before the right arises, as interpreted in Award 31/12 — B & N [Para 111][Para 115][Para 116]. The panel rejected the employer's seasonal-work argument: the workers averaged 21 to 23 days a month, which counts as continuous service even in irregular occupations [Para 119][Para 122][Para 123]. It calculated the entitlement at one and a half days a month, or 18 days a year, and ordered the employer to grant and apply it [Para 121][Para 125][Para 127][Para 129].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Article 62 of the Trade Union Law extends the protection of Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law to the non-renewal of successive fixed-duration contracts, so an employer cannot rely on the contract's expiry where the non-renewal is shown to be union discrimination; but the worker party must prove that link, and proof of timing alone will not do [Para 97][Para 101][Para 103][Para 107]. Paid annual leave accrues at one and a half days a month once a year of service is completed, and a seasonal or day-paid work pattern averaging 21 to 23 days a month does not defeat it [Para 121][Para 123].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted the employer's point that the airport's own service contract had not been renewed during the COVID-19 period, without treating it as a justification for the non-renewals [Para 67].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is spelled several ways: ហ្គ្រោន សឺវីស អ៉ិនធើណេសិន ណល on the face of the award, ហ្គោន សឺវីស អ៊ីនធើណេសិន ណល in the evidence list, and ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គ្រោន សឺវីស អ៉ិនធើ ណេសិន ណល in the body [Para 1][Para 53][Para 29]. The claim is pleaded for seven dismissed workers but is narrowed first to four named workers and the panel speaks once of "all 40" [Para 55][Para 81][Para 90]. Years are truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, including the award date, the hearing date and the opposition deadline, so some dates cannot be fixed [Para 12][Para 23][Para 130]. One decision paragraph orders annual leave for "2015", a year that has no apparent connection to the facts [Para 127]. The signature block is partly lost to OCR, and the panel's chair is recorded twice [Para 131][Para 132]. Latin fragments ("AM", "To", "MV", "Yook") intrude into the Khmer text at several points [Para 56][Para 106][Para 118].
