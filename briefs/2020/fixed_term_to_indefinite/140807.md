# Award No. 043/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 11 August 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, employer-appointed, dissenting; អន ណន, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លៈ, chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 140807

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត, a garment factory in Kandal Province with 4,050 workers, suspended employment contracts in two rounds — 3,136 workers from 5 March to 30 April 2020 and 762 from 1 May to 31 May 2020 — for want of orders during the COVID-19 outbreak, and from April 2020 terminated contracts in successive batches until 2,017 workers had left by agreement. [Para 87][Para 94][Para 96][Para 160] The workers' representatives of C.CAWDU brought three demands; at the hearing of 17 July 2020 they withdrew the second, on fixed-duration contracts, leaving issues 1 and 3. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 19][Para 20] Issue 1 concerned five termination entitlements under undetermined-duration contracts, including seniority back-pay calculated on base wage plus allowances; issue 3 sought reinstatement of three union leaders with back pay, five termination entitlements, and moral damages for two other workers. [Para 90][Para 136]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (ចំណុច ទី១) Whether the employer must calculate seniority back-pay on base wage plus allowances averaged over the 12 months before termination, and pay moral damages equal to that seniority payment? [Para 90][Para 163]
2. (ចំណុច ទី៣) Whether the employer must reinstate three union leaders with back pay and pay them five termination entitlements plus moral damages for two other workers? [Para 135][Para 136]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — អ្នកតំណាង ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត):** The employer's seniority back-pay used base wage alone, whereas Article 110 requires base wage plus allowances averaged over the preceding 12 months; the three union leaders should be taken back with wages from the day they were stopped, and the two other workers were owed moral damages. [Para 92][Para 135][Para 152]
**Employer (respondent — ក្រុមហ៊ុន គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត):** Prakas 443 and Instruction 057/19 require pre-2019 seniority back-pay to be computed on net wage average and cap it at six months, so paying on base wage was correct; the terminations were by mutual agreement carrying four entitlements, and it never dismissed the three leaders, whose file is on appeal to the Labour Minister. [Para 110][Para 116][Para 154][Para 225]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to recalculate seniority back-pay by taking base wage together with seniority or attendance allowances averaged monthly over the 12 months before the worker stopped work; the moral damages claim and the claim to seniority compensation were rejected, the reinstatement demand was not considered, and the five-entitlement claim was rejected. [Para 251][Para 252][Para 253][Para 254]

On issue 1 the panel held that where an undetermined-duration contract ends, Article 110 (new) requires the calculation to include seniority or attendance allowances averaged over the 12 months before leaving, and that a computation on base wage alone caused workers to lose the benefit the article guarantees. [Para 191][Para 196][Para 199][Para 200] It examined Prakas No. 443 of 21 September 2018 and Instruction No. 057/19 of 10 June 2019 and found their pre-2019 formula, based on average net wage and capped at six months, addressed workers still in service and set no method for termination cases; where an agreement is silent on the formula, the statutory method applies. [Para 183][Para 187][Para 189][Para 190] It followed Awards 054/19 and 068/19. [Para 185][Para 193]

On the union leaders, the panel found the employer had asked the Kandal labour department on 19 May 2020 to authorise termination, that the department refused on 25 June 2020, and that the employer appealed to the Minister on 8 July 2020; under Article 43 and Article 67 of the Trade Union Law the inspector's decision may be appealed within two months and the Minister has two months to decide. [Para 137][Para 138][Para 139][Para 216][Para 218] Because that period had not run by the hearing, the demand was still within the Minister's competence and the panel declined to consider it, following Awards 162/11, 317/14, 024/17, 097/19 and 106/19. [Para 222][Para 226][Para 227]

On the five termination entitlements the panel applied Article 65 of the Labour Law and Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code: a contract is formed, modified or extinguished by matching offer and acceptance, so neither party may force the other to terminate, and the workers' demand of five entitlements could not be imposed on an employer unwilling to terminate at that price. [Para 231][Para 240][Para 247][Para 249][Para 250] On moral damages it applied Article 91 (new), which requires termination by one party without valid reason; here termination followed a signed agreement for four entitlements, so no damages were due. [Para 202][Para 207][Para 208][Para 209]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an undetermined-duration contract ends, seniority back-pay must be computed on base wage together with seniority or attendance allowances averaged over the 12 months before the worker stopped, and the Prakas 443 formula, being confined to workers still serving, does not displace that method. [Para 196][Para 189][Para 200] A panel will not entertain a demand concerning the dismissal of union leaders while the employer's appeal against the labour inspector's refusal is still within the Minister's statutory two-month period, and no worker can compel an employer to terminate the contract in order to obtain severance. [Para 227][Para 249]

**Obiter dicta:** The dissenting opinion of arbitrator អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, who considered that Instruction 057/19 permitted calculation on base wage and that an order departing from it would be improper. [Para 258][Para 261]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, and the hearing date is printed as 17 July 2019 where the case is a 2020 file, the sitting having been moved from 16 to 17 July. [Para 1][Para 17][Para 23] The company name is printed in at least three different Khmer spellings, one of them dropping words entirely. [Para 78][Para 135] The claimant count is given as 245 thumbprints, then 40 in the list filed on 24 July, while the employer says only 52 contracts were terminated and the annex lists 32; these figures are not reconciled. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 128][Para 42] The operative part repeats the whole of issue 1 twice, at paragraphs 210-211 and again at 251-252. [Para 210][Para 251] The signature block attributes the employer-appointed seat to arbitrator អន ណន, whom the face of the award names as worker-appointed, and carries no signature for the chair. [Para 256]
