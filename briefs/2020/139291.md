# Award No. 007/20 — Regency Company Ltd (The Great Duke Phnom Penh) (Arbitration Council, 20 February 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 139291

## 1. Facts
Regency Company Ltd operated The Great Duke Phnom Penh hotel with 147 employees, of whom 139 filed this collective claim after the hotel closed. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 68] On 20 December 2019 the employer met all staff, announced closure on 31 December 2019 for financial reasons, told them they were dismissed, had them collect their belongings, and shut the doors from 21 December 2019; December 2019 wages were paid in full. [Para 69][Para 70] It notified the labour inspectorate of the closure on 28 January 2020. [Para 71] Audited accounts showed losses of USD 318,264 in 2018 and USD 404,119 in 2019. [Para 103][Para 104]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay damages in lieu of notice under Article 75 of the Labour Law to the workers? [Para 69][Para 105]
2. Whether the employer must pay for unused annual leave under Articles 166–167? [Para 81]
3. Whether the employer must pay pre-2019 seniority back pay calculated on the base wage together with allowances? [Para 84][Para 128]
4. Whether the employer must pay moral damages equal to the pre-2019 seniority back pay? [Para 96][Para 155]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** notice was given one day before termination although Article 75 requires notice graduated by seniority; the seniority back payment must be computed on base wage plus allowances, because Instruction No. 057/19 is inferior to the law, which prevails; and the abrupt closure caused damage warranting moral damages under Article 91. [Para 72][Para 87][Para 88][Para 96]
**Employer (respondent):** it gave notice on 20 December 2019 and closed on 31 December 2019; under Article 87(4) of the amended Labour Law, read with Prakas No. 288, a closure notified to the labour inspectorate extinguishes the duty to pay damages in lieu of notice and moral damages; it accepted paying annual leave and the pre-2019 back pay on average net wages under Instruction 057/19, in three instalments. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 76][Para 98]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay damages in lieu of notice calculated on each worker's actual seniority, and to pay pre-2019 seniority back pay computed on base wage and allowances; the claims for annual leave were disposed of by agreement and the claim for moral damages was dismissed. [Para 172][Para 173][Para 174]

The panel recorded that issue 2 was settled at the hearing, the employer agreeing to pay unused annual leave and the workers accepting, so it made no ruling on it. [Para 81][Para 83]

On issue 1 the panel read Article 87(3) of the amended Labour Law as providing that suspension of an enterprise's activity does not release the employer from the obligations in Part 3, including notice; only a closure meeting conditions set by ministerial prakas is exempt, and the panel found that no prakas yet prescribes those conditions and procedure. [Para 74][Para 117][Para 121][Para 123] Prakas No. 288 was held to create only a duty to notify the labour inspectorate within 30 days of closure, saying nothing about exemption from damages in lieu of notice. [Para 120][Para 122] The employer had therefore not given notice equal to the workers' actual seniority, and Article 77 required damages equal to the wages and benefits for the unobserved notice period. [Para 125][Para 126][Para 172]

On issue 3 the panel held Instruction No. 057/19 inapplicable: by its terms it covers only textile, garment and footwear enterprises, and a hotel is outside that sector. [Para 141] Instruction No. 042/19 governs other enterprises, deferring pre-2019 back payments to December 2021 but requiring the whole amount to be paid at once where a worker is dismissed for a reason other than serious misconduct. [Para 142][Para 143] Applying that instruction to a closure, and Article 110 (new) on including allowances in the calculation, the panel ordered the back pay computed on base wage plus allowances. [Para 144][Para 153][Para 154][Para 173]

On issue 4 the panel held Article 91 (new) requires both an unjustified unilateral termination and proof of damage; the tourism downturn was an external factor beyond the employer's control, the losses were documented, and the workers proved no damage, so no moral damages were due. [Para 160][Para 166][Para 168][Para 169][Para 170]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Notification of a closure to the labour inspectorate under Prakas No. 288 satisfies only a reporting duty: it does not exempt the employer from Article 75 notice or Article 77 damages in lieu, because the exemption in Article 87(4) operates only on conditions fixed by ministerial prakas, and absent such a prakas the employer must pay damages measured by each worker's actual seniority. [Para 121][Para 122][Para 126]

**Obiter dicta:** a documented closure driven by an external collapse in tourist arrivals, and not by management fault, is a proper reason that defeats a claim for moral damages even where the notice obligation was breached. [Para 166][Para 168]

The award is non-binding, becoming final eight days after notification. [Para 175] OCR damage is severe and repeated: the year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, the hearing date is garbled, and stray Latin fragments interrupt the text ("MarkAT105%", "Jung", "Aft", "VO"). [Para 13][Para 80][Para 121][Para 150] Instruction No. 042/19 is dated 22 March 2019 at one point and 22 June 2019 at another, and Instruction 057/19 is described in some passages as covering garment workers and in others as inapplicable to them. [Para 92][Para 142][Para 141] The company appears as Regency Company Limited, "រេហ្សិន ស៊ី", and once as "ក្រុមហ៊ុន Lis ហ្សិន ស៊ី". [Para 42][Para 63][Para 67] Article 89 is quoted as "១៥៦ ថ្ងៃ" where 15 days is meant, and the notice table at Article 75 is split across lines by broken brackets. [Para 105][Para 131]
