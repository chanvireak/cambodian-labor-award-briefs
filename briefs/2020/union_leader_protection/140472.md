# Award No. 033/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជុវ៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី (Juva (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.) v ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជុវ៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី / សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ (Arbitration Council, 29 June 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · Panel: គង់ ផល្លា (Chair), អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, អន ណន · Doc ID 140472

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជុវ៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី, a garment manufacturer at Phsar Trach village, Longveak commune, Kampong Tralach, Kampong Chhnang, employs 646 workers; the claimant is the workers' representative body at the company, organised through សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ, registered there since August 2017 [Para 47][Para 36][Para 1]. On 25 May 2020 the provincial labour department received a complaint raising nine demands on working conditions; three were settled in conciliation, six were referred to the Council, and at the hearing of 12 June 2020 four more (Issues 2, 3, 5 and 6) were settled, leaving Issues 1 and 4 [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20][Para 24][Para 25]. Issue 1 concerns prior notice to union leaders before a worker's contract ends; Issue 4 concerns the workers' demand that the head of administration, Mr H, be removed from the company for insulting workers, including pregnant women, and for refusing to take responsibility for his words at meetings [Para 48][Para 55][Para 57][Para 64].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer is obliged to give prior notice to union leaders when it terminates a worker's labour contract? [Para 69]
2. (Issue 4) Whether the workers may require the employer to dismiss the head of administration from his job? [Para 80]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជុវ៉ា and សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ:** the Labour Law and the Law on Unions require the employer to notify union leaders before ending a worker's contract, and in 2019 the employer agreed to do so; notice lets the union protect the worker's rights, and union leaders are also staff delegates [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]. The head of administration called pregnant workers "ពួក ក្បាល ខ្មៅ", promised then refused annual leave for 2 May 2020, and never accepted responsibility, causing a strike on 12 and 13 May 2020 and lost wages; many workers back his removal [Para 57][Para 58][Para 63][Para 65].
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជុវ៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី:** a labour contract binds only the employer and the worker, so it owes notice only to the worker whose contract ends, not to the union; the head of administration did say workers could take annual leave on 2 May 2020 under administrative procedure, and the employer cannot dismiss him because the grievance is a matter between the employer and him [Para 54][Para 66][Para 67][Para 68].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining demands were rejected, although the panel directed the employer to instruct and correct the head of administration's conduct; the award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification [Para 110][Para 111][Para 108].
On Issue 1 the panel applied its standing rule that the party asserting a claim bears the burden of proof, following Awards 79/05-Evergreen, 101/08-J&D, 168/09-Touk Thla Plaza (issue 2), 115/10-G-Fumos (issue 18), 148/11-Dai Young and 276/15-Mitthpheap Garment [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 74]. The workers referred to a 2019 agreement but produced no copy or other specific evidence of it, and the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules or past practice imposing a duty to notify the union before a contract ends; the employer's established practice was to notify the worker concerned directly [Para 75][Para 76][Para 77][Para 78].
On Issue 4 the panel held that the head of administration has his own labour contract with the company, so the workers are third parties to it; under Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 311 of the Civil Code only the parties to a contract may vary or extinguish its obligations, and a third party cannot force its termination, following Awards 116/10, 83/12, 04/13, 112/14, 215/14 and 058/15 [Para 81][Para 83][Para 84][Para 85][Para 87][Para 89]. Article 2 of the Labour Law additionally reserves to the employer the organisation and running of the enterprise, and the panel had repeatedly declined to order the removal of a worker, section head or team leader as an internal management matter, following Awards 62/06, 108/06, 33/07, 119/09, 156/13 and 201/14 [Para 90][Para 94][Para 95]. Absent proof that the man was dangerous to others or had committed a fault affecting their safety, the demand failed [Para 97][Para 102][Para 103].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand that the employer notify the union before a worker's contract ends fails where no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or established practice imposes that duty and the union cannot produce the agreement it invokes [Para 75][Para 78]. Workers, as third parties to the contract between the employer and a supervisor, cannot require the employer to dismiss that supervisor, since only the parties may extinguish contractual obligations and staffing is the employer's management prerogative [Para 84][Para 89][Para 95].

**Obiter dicta:** although the demand failed, the panel observed that the head of administration's abusive language towards pregnant workers was improper and that the company should instruct and correct him so as to keep a peaceful workplace, following Award 29/08-Thairong (issue 2) [Para 104][Para 105][Para 107][Para 108].

Long stretches of Latin garbage are embedded in the Khmer text, and one recital is interrupted by an unreadable string of English words [Para 48][Para 82]. The Prakas number in the jurisdiction clause is truncated to "185/20" with its year missing [Para 13]. The award states that six issues remained after conciliation and four were settled at hearing, but never explains why the numbered demand list contains only six items [Para 20][Para 24]. The head of administration and the union officers are named in full in the award and are reduced to roles or initials here.
