# Award No. 003/20 — Gron Services International (Arbitration Council, 18 February 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 139290

## 1. Facts
Gron Services International, a labour-supply company in Siem Reap with 57 employees, deployed workers to Siem Reap International Airport. [Para 53][Para 54] On 12 November 2019 it terminated the fixed-duration (three-month) contracts of five workers ahead of expiry; all five were union founders or candidates in the union election of 20 October 2019, and three had been elected. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 123] They sought reinstatement and back wages from dismissal to reinstatement, expressly waiving any claim for terminal compensation. [Para 56][Para 84] The employer said demand had fallen at the airport and that the contracts had been ended lawfully with notice and compensation paid. [Para 76][Para 83]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The workers originally filed two claims, which the panel recorded as one after the workers merged them at the hearing. [Para 18][Para 19]
1. Whether the employer must take the five workers back into work and pay them back wages from the date of dismissal until the date of reinstatement? [Para 55][Para 92]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the dismissals were union-motivated and procedurally improper — all five were union leaders or election candidates, and the company, twelve years old, had never had a union; a fixed-duration contract may be ended early only by mutual agreement under Article 73 of the Labour Law. [Para 64][Para 66][Para 67]
**Employer (respondent):** the dispute was individual, not collective, because the company carried on normally after the five left; it had given notice, computed final wages and paid the contractual compensation; and falling tourist traffic through Siem Reap airport meant it could not guarantee job stability. [Para 55][Para 83][Para 76]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim for reinstatement and for back wages from dismissal to reinstatement was rejected. [Para 150][Para 151]

The panel first tested its own jurisdiction, since Prakas No. 099 (Article 32) confines it to collective disputes. [Para 92][Para 93] Applying Article 302 of the Labour Law, it found all three statutory conditions met: the claim concerned five named workers supported by a petition signed by 40 fellow employees; it arose from the termination of employment, a matter of employer-worker relations; and, unresolved, it could disrupt the enterprise's operation or social peace. [Para 102][Para 104][Para 105][Para 107][Para 109] That the company continued trading was therefore not decisive.

On the protection claimed, the panel applied Article 43 and Article 67 of the 2016 Trade Union Law and the three-condition test in Award 169/16. [Para 112][Para 120] Conditions one and two were satisfied: the five were candidates or founders, and they were dismissed on 12 November 2019, within the protected window around the election and while their registration application was pending. [Para 122][Para 126] Condition three failed: notice had to go to the employer and, by copy, to the Ministry; here the workers sent it by Cambodia Post to a manager at Siem Reap International Airport who was neither an employee nor a representative of Gron Services. [Para 128][Para 131] The burden of proving that he represented the employer lay on the party asserting it, and the workers produced no specific evidence; the panel followed its earlier rulings refusing unsupported claims. [Para 134][Para 135][Para 137] Protection under Article 67 was therefore unavailable. [Para 138][Para 140]

Turning to Article 73(3) of the Labour Law, the panel held that an employer may end a fixed-duration contract before expiry but must then pay damages at least equal to the wages due to the contract's end date. [Para 141][Para 142][Para 145] Because the employer refused reinstatement and had offered compensation, reinstatement could not be ordered; the panel noted the workers had not claimed terminal payments, so it did not address them. [Para 146][Para 147][Para 149]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Union-office protection under Article 67 of the Trade Union Law depends on notice of the protected persons reaching the employer itself, or an authorised representative of it; notice delivered to a third party who merely supervises the workers at the client's site does not satisfy the third condition, and the workers bear the burden of proving that the recipient represented the employer. [Para 131][Para 137]

**Obiter dicta:** that the enterprise in fact kept operating normally after the dismissals does not answer the third condition of Article 302 — the panel treated potential future disruption, not present disruption, as the test. [Para 55][Para 107]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is non-binding, becoming final eight days after notification absent objection to the Minister. [Para 152] The document is heavily degraded: the hearing date and hour are lost (line 11 reads only "វ៉ាង ១៤ និង ០០ នាទី រសៀល"), the year appears as "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, and the panel is "created" on a date printed as "១៧ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០". [Para 11][Para 15] The union election notice is dated 19 October 2018 in one passage and 19 October 2019 everywhere else, and the notification letter is variously "០០១/២០" and "០០១-០១/២០". [Para 122][Para 40] Article 67 is quoted twice with the protection period rendered once as 48 and once as 45 days, and stray tokens ("៤០", "AKP", "AFA") interrupt the reasoning text. [Para 115][Para 120][Para 103][Para 143] The company name appears as Gron Services International and, once, as "វហ្គ្រាន សឺវីស". [Para 54]
