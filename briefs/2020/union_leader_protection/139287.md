# Award No. 115/19 — Double U and D (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 31 January 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-nominated), Sin Kim Sean (worker-nominated), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 139287

## 1. Facts
Double U and D (Cambodia) Co., Ltd sews garments and employed about 1,400 workers; the award also refers to a strike of some 1,800. [Para 153][Para 154][Para 393] The workers struck from 24 December 2018 over unpaid seniority payments; the Council ordered them back on 31 December 2018 and the Municipal Court issued a temporary injunction the same day, which the panel found they ignored. [Para 236][Para 237][Para 402] On 4 January 2019 the company struck roughly 1,100 names from its roll for failing to return, then reinstated them in late March 2019 without back pay. [Para 393][Para 251][Para 408] The workers' representatives lodged 14 demands on 3 December 2019; five were conciliated and nine came to the panel, which dismissed six and granted three. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 36] The award is non-binding. [Para 37][Para 430]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must deduct the monthly attendance bonus in proportion to the number of days a worker is absent on properly authorised personal leave? [Para 273][Para 155]
2. Whether the employer must recognise medical certificates from private hospitals recognised by the Ministry of Health and partnered with the National Social Security Fund, and keep wages and benefits during that leave? [Para 321]
3. Whether the employer must allow nursing mothers to leave at 17:30? [Para 330]
4. Whether the employer must restore workers to their original production teams? [Para 341]
5. Whether the employer must transfer union dues into the union's bank account? [Para 366]
6. Whether the employer must keep paying the travel and accommodation allowance during the strike from 24 December 2018 to 3 January 2019? [Para 376]
7. Whether the employer's dismissal of workers who did not return to work on 4 January 2019 was procedurally valid, and whether back pay is owed for 4 January to 29–30 March 2019? [Para 386]
8. Whether the employer must keep wages and benefits while union leaders and members attend seminars on a proper federation invitation? [Para 410]
9. Whether the employer must accept the union's request for negotiation meetings and admit a C.CAWDU representative to them? [Para 417]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The attendance bonus is part of wages under Article 103, and a worker who takes authorised leave should not lose $5 twice in one month. [Para 162][Para 164] Private-hospital cards are ignored and wages cut even where a doctor's note exists. [Para 177][Para 178] Nursing mothers must travel home and back, taking nearly 30 minutes, so the second half hour should move to 17:30. [Para 203][Para 204] Travel and accommodation allowance is not wages, so it should survive the strike. [Para 232]
**Employer (respondent):** The attendance bonus exists to encourage punctual attendance, so an absent worker cannot earn it, and paying it twice a month already exceeds the law. [Para 165][Para 167][Para 168] It accepts every hospital certificate that carries a proper doctor's note and keeps wages accordingly. [Para 322][Para 327] Breastfeeding follows Article 184. [Para 206] Team reorganisation is a managerial right made necessary after teams fell from 16 to 13. [Para 209][Para 219] The strike was unlawful and the dismissals followed the court injunction. [Para 235][Para 256]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 2 and 5 were granted, issue 9 was met with an order to decide case by case, and issues 1, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8 were dismissed; no sum was ordered. [Para 422][Para 423][Para 426][Para 429]

On issue 1 the panel adopted the Full Bench decision of 3 October 2017 interpreting Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012: the attendance bonus is earned at 100% only where the worker is present on every working day, and days of leave that are a right by law, by contract, or by legal duty count as presence. [Para 277][Para 281][Para 293] An incentive may not penalise the exercise of a legal right, but leave beyond those categories carries no entitlement. [Para 292][Para 313] Because the company already kept the bonus for sick and special leave and cut $5 only for other personal leave, its practice was more generous than the law and the claim failed. [Para 315][Para 317][Para 320]

On issue 2 the panel read Article 7(5) of the internal rules and the employer's own concession as accepting any hospital's certificate so long as a doctor has certified the period, without distinguishing public from private. [Para 322][Para 323] The employer was therefore ordered to keep wages and benefits on production of a certificate from a private hospital recognised by the Ministry of Health or partnered with the NSSF. [Para 327][Para 329][Para 423]

On issue 3 Article 184 gives one hour a day inside working hours, split into two thirty-minute periods at times agreed with the employer, and at the midpoint of each work period absent agreement. [Para 330][Para 331][Para 334] The company's 11:00–11:30 and 15:30–16:00 slots comply, and no agreement moved the second slot to 17:30, so the claim was dismissed. [Para 335][Para 337][Para 340]

On issue 4 the panel considered res judicata, since Award No. 037/19 covered the same demand, but held the first condition unmet because the claimants there were team-4 workers and here team-5 workers. [Para 341][Para 348][Para 350] On the merits, Article 2 gives the employer management power exercised lawfully and reasonably, subject to four limits — no wage cut, no distant relocation, no day/night switch and no change beyond the worker's skill. [Para 355][Para 359][Para 363] The regrouping met those limits and was dismissed. [Para 364][Para 365]

On issue 5 Article 26 of the Trade Union Law and Article 2 of Prakas No. 300 require the employer, once a member consents in writing, to deduct dues and transfer them into the union's bank account, not hand cash to a treasurer. [Para 366][Para 369][Para 373] The employer conceded the point, and the order issued. [Para 374][Para 375][Para 426]

On issue 6 the $7 monthly travel and accommodation allowance under Notification No. 230 is an incentive owed to workers who actually come to work, so it does not accrue during a strike. [Para 377][Para 383][Para 385]

On issue 7 the panel found the strike unlawful and continued despite the injunction, which is serious misconduct under Article 337; the dismissals on 4 January 2019 came within the seven-day window of Article 26, and the employer owed no wages for the stoppage. [Para 386][Para 403][Para 405][Para 406][Para 408] On issues 8 and 9 it held no rule obliged the employer to pay for federation seminars, since it may first check whether they concern company work, and ordered it to allow a representative where a case genuinely requires one. [Para 410][Para 415][Para 421]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An attendance bonus may be withheld only where absence falls outside leave that is a right by law, by contract or by legal duty: days of annual, sick, special or legally compelled leave count as presence, because an incentive may not penalise the exercise of a statutory right. [Para 293][Para 307] Where the workers who bring a demand are not the workers who brought the earlier one, res judicata does not bar the panel from deciding it again. [Para 348][Para 350]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that paying the bonus twice a month is more favourable than the law requires, and that the parties may agree to extend special leave beyond seven days. [Para 168][Para 286][Para 299]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR dropped the last two digits of years written "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, so 2020 is inferred from context. [Para 24][Para 39] The strike is dated 24 December 2018 in most places but "24 December 2019" once. [Para 382] The Council's return-to-work order appears as 31 December 2019 where the context is 2018. [Para 237][Para 243] Headcount is given as 1,400 in the findings and 1,800 strikers later, and the travel allowance is printed as "77 dollars" in one passage and $7 elsewhere. [Para 154][Para 393][Para 383] The company also appears as "W&D" and "Double U and Digital" in the file. [Para 124] Individual workers and union officers are reduced to initials here. [Para 1]
