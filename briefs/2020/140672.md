# Award No. 038/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផេក ធី ក (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី (Pek Thi Ka (Cambodia) Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 22 July 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (Ing Sothy), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (Liv Sovanna), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (Pen Bun Chea, Chair) · Doc ID 140672

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផេក ធី ក (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី, operates in Siem Reap province producing towels, spectacle cloths and bags, with about 500 workers [Para 1][Para 108]. The workers, represented by the CTWUF-affiliated union at the factory and by a lawyer, brought three demands: payment of pre-2019 seniority back pay, full wages after the company suspended contracts, and reinstatement of 75 workers with back pay [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6]. A further demand concerning documents for 34 workers was withdrawn [Para 7]. At the hearing the worker party withdrew Issue 2 and the parties reached agreement on Issue 3, leaving only Issue 1 for the panel [Para 21][Para 22]. The company does not dispute that it has paid no pre-2019 seniority back pay and says it deferred payment to 2021 under Instruction No. 042/19, which applies outside the garment, textile and footwear sector [Para 111][Para 3].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay pre-2019 seniority back pay to the workers under Article 89 (new) of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 443 and Instruction No. 057/19? [Para 109]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The company cuts and sews cloth for towels, spectacle cloths and bags and is listed by the Ministry of Labour as a garment-sector enterprise at entry No. 56 in the government's suspension support annex, while the Siem Reap Labour Inspector has already required it to apply garment-sector minimum wages, so Instruction No. 057/19 governs and Instruction No. 042/19 has no application [Para 117][Para 118][Para 143].

**Employer (respondent):** The company's statute and tax patent describe handicraft production of towels and bags from microfibre, not garment cutting; it is registered with the Ministry of Industry and Handicraft as a handicraft and GMAC lists it only as an associate member, so it falls outside the garment sector and may postpone payment to 2021 under Instruction No. 042/19 [Para 121][Para 122][Para 123][Para 124].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to pay pre-2019 seniority back pay to the workers in accordance with Instruction No. 057/19 of the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training [Para 148]

The panel began from the statutory framework: the second article of the Labour Law amendment, amending Articles 87 to 122, provides that seniority for work performed before the law took effect is calculated and paid under a ministerial Prakas, and Prakas No. 443 of 21 September 2018 fixes the method [Para 132][Para 133]. Clause 3 of Prakas No. 443 requires garment, textile and footwear enterprises to pay 30 days of total seniority pay each year, 15 days in June and 15 days in December, and Instruction No. 057/19 of 10 June 2019 adds that this back pay is paid with the second June and December wage instalments, alongside 7.5 days of current seniority pay per half-year [Para 133][Para 135][Para 137][Para 138].

The only question that remained was whether this company is inside the sector, because Instruction No. 042/19 — the instrument that defers payment to 2021 at six days a year — applies only to enterprises outside garment, textile and footwear [Para 3][Para 120][Para 139]. The panel answered that question by looking at what the company actually does rather than at how its patent labels it: its production chain passes through cutting and sewing to make towels, spectacle cloths and bags, and the "garment sector" in Instruction No. 057/19 is not confined to clothing, since any production chain involving cutting and sewing inside the enterprise falls within it [Para 142]. The Prakas authorising the factory, issued by the Ministry of Industry and Handicraft on 25 January 2018, classifies its output under industrial codes 1399 and 1512, which are textile and bag codes [Para 127][Para 140][Para 141]. The Siem Reap Labour Inspector had already required the company to apply garment, textile and footwear working conditions, including minimum wages, and the Ministry of Labour's own suspension letter placed the company in the garment sector [Para 143][Para 144]. The panel therefore held the company to be in the textile, garment and footwear sector and ordered payment of the pre-2019 back pay under Instruction No. 057/19 [Para 145][Para 146][Para 147].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Whether an enterprise owes pre-2019 seniority back pay immediately or may defer it to 2021 turns on whether it is in the garment, textile and footwear sector, and that question is answered by the enterprise's actual production chain — if cloth is cut and sewn there, it is in the sector even when the product is not clothing — not by the wording of its tax patent or its GMAC associate membership [Para 120][Para 142][Para 145].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the Ministry of Labour had not yet replied to the employer's request to clarify which sector the company belongs to [Para 116].
