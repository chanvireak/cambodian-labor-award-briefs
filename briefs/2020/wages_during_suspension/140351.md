# Award No. 027/20 — Hulu Garment Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូលូ ហ្គាមេន) (Arbitration Council, 24 June 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), រុន សារ៉ាយ (president) · Doc ID 140351

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូលូ ហ្គាមេន (Hulu Garment Co., Ltd) is a Phnom Penh garment factory employing 1,025 workers, of whom 224 signed the collective complaint filed through their union federation representatives. [Para 62][Para 64] From 2 March to 30 April 2020 the employer suspended 663 employment contracts after COVID-19 removed its orders; on 22 April 2020 it met the workforce and offered a choice between continued suspension and resignation on four payments — notice, seniority, unused annual leave and final wages. [Para 72][Para 75] More than 400 workers thumbprinted resignation letters and received those payments; one of the two conciliation demands settled, leaving the second for the panel. [Para 76][Para 14]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the resignation letters thumbprinted on 22 April 2020, and the resignation agreement of that date, are valid, or were vitiated by fraud and threat? [Para 135][Para 165]
2. (Issue 2, point a) Whether the employer owes additional pay in lieu of notice commensurate with each worker's length of service under Article 75? [Para 166][Para 172]
3. (Issue 2, point b) Whether the employer owes seniority back-pay for the years 2005 to 2008? [Para 173]
4. (Issue 2, point c) Whether the employer owes moral damages (ប្រាក់សោហ៊ុយជំងឺចិត្ត)? [Para 175][Para 178]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), ភាគីកម្មករនិយោជិត, represented by the coalition of factory union federations:** the 22 April meeting explained the factory's order shortage but never disclosed the four-point resignation package; administration staff then had them thumbprint a covered document without telling them what it was, warning that no money would be released otherwise, so no genuine consent exists. They offered to return the four payments if reinstated, and claimed notice pay under Article 75 plus the untransferred 2005–2008 seniority increments. [Para 78][Para 80][Para 82]

**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូលូ ហ្គាមេន:** the meeting set out both options openly, union and worker-delegate representatives attended, workers who did not wish to resign simply kept their contracts suspended, and every worker was shown the letter and pay slip and given a chance to query it; there was no concealment or coercion, 15 days' notice was paid, and the 5% seniority entitlement for 2005–2008 had already been settled in 2009. [Para 85][Para 89][Para 95]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims rejected; the panel declined to order any payment and declared the resignation letters valid. [Para 179][Para 165]

On validity, the panel treated the resignation letter as a contract and applied Article 65 of the Labour Law and Articles 664, 311, 345 and 350 of the 2007 Civil Code, following the interpretation in Award No. 170/11-IM (Kampong Chhnang): consent obtained by fraud, mistake, threat or exploitation is voidable at the instance of the party deceived. [Para 135][Para 138][Para 147] Both sides' witnesses confirmed the 22 April meetings happened and that the four-payment package was offered; the panel found that workers who could read checked the letter themselves, that those who could not had the contents read to them, and that the cash receipt was clipped to the letter for comparison. [Para 148][Para 149][Para 113] The workers called no witness and produced no evidence of concealment, so the burden of proof, as applied in earlier awards, defeated the allegation of fraud. [Para 157][Para 160][Para 162]

On notice pay, Article 77 obliges the employer to pay in lieu only where it unilaterally terminates without observing the notice period; here the contract ended by consent, and 15 days' notice money had been paid, so nothing further was owed. [Para 166][Para 171][Para 172] On the 2005–2008 increments, documents and witnesses showed the 5% entitlement had been paid by instalments in 2009, extinguishing the claim. [Para 173][Para 174] On moral damages, the new Article 91 requires a termination without valid reason; a termination by mutual resignation engages no such liability. [Para 175][Para 177][Para 178] Arbitrator អន ណន dissented, holding that the thumbprinting occurred before the worker representatives' meeting had concluded and that the workers never understood the choice, so the employer should pay compensation if it does not reinstate them. [Para 187][Para 190][Para 195]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer facing a lawful contract suspension convenes the workforce, offers a documented choice between continued suspension and resignation on the four statutory terminal payments, and the workers thumbprint resignation letters after the contents were read or shown to them, the resulting termination is by mutual consent; a claim that the consent was procured by fraud fails unless the claimant produces evidence, because the party asserting fraud bears the burden of proof, and Articles 77 and 91 of the Labour Law are not engaged. [Para 160][Para 171][Para 177]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that workers who refused to thumbprint simply remained on a third suspension, which it treated as confirmation that the resignation was optional rather than imposed. [Para 92]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR truncates every year to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", so the award date, hearing dates and payment dates survive only partially. [Para 22] Paragraph 73 dates the recall telephone call to 21 April 2002 where the surrounding narrative requires 2020, an internal contradiction the panel's reasoning does not resolve. [Para 73] The size of the resigning group is printed four ways — 224 signatories to the complaint, "more than 400" thumbprints, 436 resignations in the conciliation minute, and 440 workers in the resignation agreement — and the panel adopts none of them expressly. [Para 64][Para 76][Para 125][Para 121] The case number appears as 027/20 in the header and in the annexed dissent, while paragraph 23 prints the same file under a Khmer-numeral form. Several individual workers named in the evidentiary list are reduced to fragments, and the worker-appointed arbitrator's signature block is largely lost to garbled characters in the annex. [Para 182]
