# Award No. 057/20 — Sin Tri (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Venh Pov, Pen Bun Chhea presiding) · Doc ID 140812

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ីន ទ្រី (ខេមបូឌា) is based at house 442D, Tonle Bassac, Chamkar Mon, Phnom Penh, and employed 1,912 workers. [Para 1][Para 21]
Workers demanded five termination entitlements: the last month's wage, pay in lieu of notice, outstanding annual leave, seniority back pay for the period before 2019 and severance for 2020, and compensation for moral injury. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 6]
Conciliation on 5 October 2020 settled nothing and the five points were transmitted by report No. 1058 of 8 October 2020. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 9]
The workers struck; on 9 October 2020 the panel issued injunction No. 004/20 ordering the strike suspended from the afternoon of 10 October, the workers did not return, and about 10 per cent of the 1,912 had gone back by the pre-hearing meeting of 13 October, which the worker party did not attend. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 21][Para 44][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration while the worker party is on strike and absent from the proceedings, or must terminate them? [Para 37][Para 46]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers' position appears only in their written demands: that the company owed the final month's wage, notice pay, remaining annual leave, seniority back pay and severance, and moral damages for the manner of termination. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 5][Para 6] They notified the secretariat that they would not attend the hearing and continued the strike. [Para 27][Para 42]
**Employer (respondent):** The company asked the panel to issue an order suspending the strike, attended the pre-hearing meeting through its human resources executives, and reported that the strike continued with only about 10 per cent of the workforce back at work. [Para 26][Para 21][Para 44]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 057/20 without ruling on any of the five demands. [Para 47]
The panel framed the only question as whether the arbitration could continue, and answered it under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout and to attend all meetings during the arbitration. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]
It followed earlier awards Nos. 59/06 (Sang Vo), 39/08 (Chung You), 137/09 and 138/09 (Nou Kanh), 110/11 (Simano), 104/13 (Sabrina (Cambodia)), 322/14 (Jimly (Cambodia) PTE Ltd), 071/15 (MW Garment International, Kampong Chhnang branch) and 021/16 (Star Light Angel Manufacture), which held that the panel does not continue arbitration while workers are striking or an employer is locking out. [Para 40][Para 41]
Because no hearing was held, the panel recorded that it made no finding of fact on the transmitted issues at all. [Para 36]
The award notes that the strike order had already been issued and disobeyed, that the worker party was absent from the pre-hearing meeting, and that the employer confirmed the strike was continuing. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
While a strike or lockout is actually in progress and the party promoting it stays away, the panel will not arbitrate the underlying demands; it terminates the proceedings under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 rather than decide claims in that party's absence. [Para 39][Para 41][Para 46]
A party cannot obtain an award on its demands while simultaneously using industrial action to press them. [Para 42]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that it had made no finding of fact on the transmitted issues because no hearing took place, leaving the merits untouched for any future proceeding. [Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" with the final digits lost, including the award date of 16 October and the injunction date of 9 October 2020. [Para 1][Para 16]
The panel's constitution date appears as "៩ ខែតុលា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០", printing two different year forms on the same line. [Para 15]
Paragraph 20 repeats the sentence on employer attendance and then contradicts itself as to whether the worker party was present at the pre-hearing meeting, which paragraphs 44 and 21 put beyond doubt. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 44]
The presiding arbitrator's surname is printed once as ឈា and once as លា in the signature block, so the two spellings of the same name cannot be reconciled. [Para 48][Para 1]
Stray Latin fragments such as "AKP" and "ASA" appear mid-sentence in the reasoning and in the decision, the residue of a corrupted page header. [Para 40][Para 46]
The evidence section is numbered "គេ-" where the heading should be "គ-", and one report is dated "ថ្ងៃទី ០៧ ខែតុលា ឆ្នាំ ២០ ២០" with the year duplicated. [Para 28][Para 25]
