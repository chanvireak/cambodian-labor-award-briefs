# Award No. 037/20 — JS Leader Call Link Sin Phnom Penh Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេអេស លេឌើរ ខល លិក សិន ភ្នំពេញ ឯ.ក) (Arbitration Council, 9 July 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair) · Doc ID 140473

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេអេស លេឌើរ ខល លិក សិន ភ្នំពេញ ឯ.ក (JS Leader Call Link Sin Phnom Penh Co., Ltd) is a bag-manufacturing company in Phnom Penh employing roughly 1,702 workers, 502 of whom supported the negotiating delegation. [Para 39][Para 41] On 1 June the workers, through the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union federation, lodged eleven demands on working conditions; conciliation on 15 June settled ten and referred one unresolved point to the Arbitration Council. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] The unresolved demand asked the company to transfer the head of its electrical workshop to another section, on the ground that he had used working hours and company premises to modify an air gun and rubber items contrary to law. [Para 2][Para 42] The panel was constituted on 19 June and heard the case on 26 June. [Para 12][Para 8]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer is under a legal obligation to transfer the head of the electrical workshop to another section at the demand of the complainant workers? [Para 60]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត, represented by the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union, contended that the section head abused working time and premises, that four team members had photographed the modifications and sent the images to the union on 22 May, and that the employer should move him elsewhere. [Para 42][Para 47][Para 79]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេអេស លេឌើរ ខល លិក សិន ភ្នំពេញ ឯ.ក, through its human-resources manager and assistant, maintained that it could not comply because it had appointed the section head for his competence and reliability, had learned of the incident only during conciliation, had instructed him to stop, and had obtained his promise to do so; dismissal or transfer decisions follow its internal evaluation of each worker. [Para 3][Para 50][Para 52][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected (បដិសេធ) the workers' demand that the employer transfer the head of the electrical workshop to another section; no compensation or other order was made. [Para 83][Para 84]
On the contract question the panel applied Article 65 §1 of the Labour Law and Article 311 of the Civil Code, holding that only the parties to a contract may vary or extinguish the obligations it creates, so that third parties have no such right. [Para 60][Para 64] Because the complainant workers were not parties to the employment contract between the company and the section head, they could not demand its alteration. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70]
The panel then relied on Article 2 of the Labour Law and a long line of its own awards for the proposition that an employer's right to organise and manage the enterprise stands so long as it is exercised lawfully and reasonably, and that the panel cannot order an employer to transfer, suspend or dismiss a worker, section head or team leader at the demand of other workers, because staffing is internal company management. [Para 71][Para 75][Para 76][Para 77]
On the evidence the panel found that the workers had proved only that the section head modified the items and took them out of the company; they produced nothing showing he was a dangerous person whose retention endangered other workers. [Para 79][Para 80] It also weighed the employer's warning and the section head's undertaking to stop. [Para 81][Para 82] The demand therefore failed on both standing and proof.

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Workers who are strangers to another employee's contract of employment cannot compel the employer to transfer, suspend or dismiss that employee: the demand fails at the threshold, and it fails again on the merits unless the claimants adduce evidence that the employee is dangerous in a way that threatens the safety of fellow workers, staffing being an internal management prerogative exercised lawfully and reasonably. [Para 70][Para 77][Para 80]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the company's appointment of the section head rested on his competence and that his team members' grievance did not displace the employer's evaluation of him; it also noted that the section head had been warned and had promised to stop, conduct that would sound only in the employer's disciplinary discretion, not in the claimants' right of action. [Para 50][Para 81]
