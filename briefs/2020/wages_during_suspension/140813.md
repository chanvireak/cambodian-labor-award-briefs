# Award No. 048/20 — Khvaly Thy Tex Style Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 13 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Kong Phalla presiding) · Doc ID 140813

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធីឌី cuts and sews garments at Trapeang Thleung, Chom Chao, Posenchey district, Phnom Penh, and employed 900 workers. [Para 38][Para 1]
Workers raised six demands; conciliation on 1 September 2020 settled two and referred the other four by report No. 925 of 3 September 2020. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 12]
At the hearing of 22 September 2020 the parties agreed a further point, on drinking water and hot water, leaving Issue 1 on cooling, Issue 3 on twice-monthly wage payment and Issue 4 on paid-holiday wages during suspension. [Para 20][Para 22][Para 52][Para 14]
The employer had removed five cooling fans from the rear building in June 2020 for repair, found them rotten beyond reinstallation, and suspended the contracts of over 400 workers from May to June 2020 with the labour inspectorate's authorisation. [Para 41][Para 65][Para 75]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must reinstall cooling fans in the rear building so that the working temperature is tolerable for the more than 200 workers there? [Para 40][Para 77][Para 134]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay wages twice a month as the Ministry's Prakas requires? [Para 55][Para 92][Para 135]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay 100 per cent wages for paid public holidays falling inside the period of lawful contract suspension in May and June 2020? [Para 63][Para 106][Para 136]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers say that after the fans were removed the building became hot and airless, dust blew in from the carton store through the empty fan openings, two extra fans would not be enough and would draw in more dust, and masks cannot be worn in the heat. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] They say the employer unilaterally reverted from twice-monthly pay under an agreement that was only for one month, that the USD 70 they received monthly during suspension is wages, and that the law allows no deferral. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 66]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធីឌី says reinstalling the fans costs more than six thousand US dollars, that it is waiting for a budget because COVID-19 has cut orders, and that the suspension was lawful with the labour inspectorate's approval, so no wages are owed under Article 71. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 60][Para 71][Para 72]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to take measures to make the building temperature acceptable and to resume paying wages twice a month, and dismissed the claim for 100 per cent holiday wages during suspension. [Para 134][Para 135][Para 136]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 229 of the Labour Law, requiring every workplace to be kept clean with the sanitary and safety conditions necessary for workers' health, and Prakas No. 147 of 11 June 2002 on heat, which obliges an employer to use means such as fans or extractors when heat affects health. [Para 77][Para 78] It followed Awards Nos. 94/04 (ETC Angel), 143/09 (Cino Kia) and 047/19 (Heng Jing Tech Style Factory), and held that a promise to reinstall when funds allow, with no date, satisfies nothing. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 83][Para 91]
On Issue 3 the panel applied Article 116 of the Labour Law, requiring wages to be paid at least twice a month with no more than sixteen days between payments, and Article 1 of Prakas No. 442 of 21 September 2018, fixing payment in the second and fourth weeks of each month. [Para 92][Para 93][Para 94] Following Award No. 36/11 (Cambodia Commercial Broadcasting), it held the employer's practice of paying monthly to be contrary to Article 116, found no legal basis for deferral, and dismissed the argument of economic difficulty. [Para 96][Para 102][Para 103][Para 104][Para 105]
On Issue 4 the panel applied Articles 71 and 72 of the Labour Law and the Ministry's letter No. 051/20 of 1 May 2020 authorising suspension of 462 workers from 1 May to 15 June 2020, and followed Awards Nos. 58/07-08 (Scar Sport Wear) and 283/15 (MW Garment International) in holding that Prakas No. 10 of 1999 on paid holidays does not apply while activity is suspended. [Para 106][Para 108][Para 116][Para 117][Para 126][Para 130] It therefore refused the demand for 100 per cent holiday wages. [Para 132][Para 133]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer must keep the temperature of the workplace tolerable by every available means, and its intention to reinstall cooling equipment when its budget permits, with no date fixed, does not discharge that duty. [Para 77][Para 80][Para 91]
Statutory wage timing is not a term the parties may vary: an agreement between an employer and worker representatives to pay monthly instead of twice monthly cannot displace Article 116 of the Labour Law, and economic difficulty under COVID-19 is no defence. [Para 103][Para 104][Para 105]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that where suspension is fully lawful the employer owes no other leave either, including paid public holidays, an observation that supported but did not itself decide Issue 4. [Para 127]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", including the award date of 13 October and the audits of May and June 2020, and several lines print the year twice, for example the panel's constitution on "០៩ ខែកញ្ញា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០". [Para 1][Para 17][Para 25]
The respondent's name appears in at least four renderings, including ស្ទីល, ខូអែល, ខូអ័ល and the corruption "រតក្ស", so the name on the face of the award is not stable. [Para 1][Para 39][Para 41][Para 75]
The panel's price schedule submitted by the employer was excluded because it was in a foreign language, yet no translation or summary of the amounts appears anywhere in the file. [Para 50][Para 51]
Paragraph 69 states the workers' legal conclusion as a bare fragment, "suspension of the contract for paid holidays 100 per cent", with the verb missing, and one line of the decision at paragraph 128 contains nothing but an ellipsis and a full stop, so those two passages carry no reconstructable meaning. [Para 69][Para 128]
The two worker representatives named in the representation certificate appear as a degraded name list; this brief records the defect and does not reproduce the names. [Para 39][Para 1]
