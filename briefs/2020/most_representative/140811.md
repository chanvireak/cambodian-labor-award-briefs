# Award No. 017/20 — Meng Da Footwear In Das Striel Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Pen Bun Chhea presiding) · Doc ID 140811

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង ដា ហ្វូត វៀ អ៊ិន ដាស់ ស្ទ្រីល ខូអិលធីឌី manufactures footwear at Chom Chao I, Posenchey district, Phnom Penh, and employed 5,000 workers. [Para 60][Para 1]
Workers filed a six-point demand on 20 February 2020; conciliation on 4 March 2020 settled two points and left four, transmitted by report No. 274/20 of 9 March 2020. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19][Para 14]
At the hearing of 28 September 2020 the parties settled Issue 1 as well, leaving issues 2 to 4: whether the role allowance and support allowance enter the overtime base, whether a nursery must be built, and whether a USD 50 annual bonus is owed when the factory is profitable. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 16]
This was an interest dispute and the award was issued as a binding one. [Para 30][Para 137]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must add the role allowance and support allowance to the base wage as the basis for calculating overtime pay for team leaders and section heads? [Para 62][Para 133]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must build a nursery on the factory grounds for women workers who are members of the union and must send their children out to a nursery? [Para 74][Para 135]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay a USD 50 annual bonus to workers when the factory makes a profit? [Para 83][Para 136]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers argued that the role and support allowances should be consolidated into the base wage used to compute overtime, relying on Articles 102 and 103 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 090 of 1999 and Award No. 092/19, and that Article 186 obliges the employer to build a nursing room and nursery. [Para 70][Para 79] They said the USD 50 annual bonus was owed under Notification No. 06 of 1997 and that the employer had bought another factory and ordered heavy overtime, which shows profit. [Para 83][Para 84]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង ដា ហ្វូត វៀ អ៊ិន ដាស់ ស្រីល says it computes overtime exactly by the ministry formula in the labour inspectorate annex, that the support allowance is not wages, and that the allowance ranges from USD 30 to USD 1,000 a month for roughly 300 heads. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73] It says it offered workers the choice in 2018 between a nursery and USD 10 a month, that most chose the cash, that it agreed this with every union except សហជីព បេះដូង កម្មករ ខ្មែរ, and that it made no profit. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 76][Para 85]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider all three demands, on the role allowance as overtime base, on construction of a nursery, and on the USD 50 annual bonus. [Para 133][Para 135][Para 136]
On Issue 2 the panel applied Article 19 of Prakas No. 099, requiring written authorisation for representation, and followed Award No. 181/14 (Sung Yin (Cambodia) B-Tech Style) and Award No. 092/19 (Burry Arkel). [Para 89][Para 91][Para 95][Para 52] The workers had promised a list naming the four team leaders but filed nothing by the evidence deadline, and the original complainant list named no one holding such a post, so the demand failed for want of an identified beneficiary. [Para 68][Para 92][Para 93][Para 99]
On Issue 3 the panel relied on the same absence of identification: no document named the women members of សហជីព បេះដូង កម្មករ ខ្មែរ who had to place children in an outside nursery and no evidence supported the claim, so it declined to consider the demand. [Para 101][Para 103][Para 104][Para 105]
On Issue 4 the panel held that the party asserting a fact carries the burden of proof, found no evidence of profit beyond the assertion, noted the bonus had never been paid before and was not in the internal regulations, and classified the claim as an interest dispute. [Para 108][Para 111][Para 112][Para 113][Para 114] It then held that only a most representative union may bring an interest dispute, under Article 96 paragraph 2 of the Labour Law, Article 54 and Article 72 of the Trade Union Law and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, found the claimant union did not hold that status, and declined to consider the demand. [Para 115][Para 122][Para 126][Para 130][Para 131]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that is not the most representative union in the enterprise cannot bring an interest dispute to the panel, because an award on an interest dispute operates as a collective agreement binding all workers and only the most representative union is empowered to conclude one. [Para 122][Para 126][Para 130]
Separately, a demand that names no identifiable beneficiary and is supported by no document by the evidence deadline fails, whatever its legal merit. [Para 92][Para 103]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel reasoned that treating an arbitration award on an interest dispute as a collective agreement removes from all workers the right to bargain and to strike over that interest in future, an observation that did not itself decide any issue. [Para 125]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", including the award date of 20 October and the hearing of 28 September 2020. [Para 1][Para 16]
The panel is described as constituted on "១២ ខែមីនា ឆ្នាំ ២ ២០", printing the year twice. [Para 20]
The respondent's name is rendered inconsistently throughout, appearing as ស្ទ្រីល, ស្ទីល, ស្រីល and "វវៀរ" in different passages, so it cannot be matched character for character. [Para 1][Para 34][Para 74][Para 100]
Paragraph 28 repeats the recitation of settlement twice, leaving the count of points settled at the hearing unclear between one and nineteen. [Para 28]
The signature block loses the presiding arbitrator's name to OCR, leaving only stray Latin characters where the name should be. [Para 138]
The four team leaders and section heads named in the claimant's demand list appear in the record only as an OCR-degraded list of individual workers; this brief describes that defect and does not reproduce the names. [Para 61]
