# Award No. 060/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តារ ឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង (Arbitration Council, 23 November 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer, អន ណន appointed by the workers, chair អាំង អេង ថុង) · Doc ID 140816

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តារ ឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង, Star Light Apparel Manufacturing, cuts and sews garments at Seda village, Vihear Suor commune, Khsach Kandal district, Kandal, with 2,000 workers. [Para 1] [Para 30] Workers of the factory filed 12 demands on 3 September 2020; conciliation on 16 October 2020 settled 10 of them, and the two remaining demands came to the Council secretariat on 23 October 2020. [Para 12] [Para 13] [Para 14] The panel sat on 3 November 2020, and the parties agreed to extend the award date from 18 to 23 November 2020. [Para 11] [Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must keep wages and other benefits unchanged and grant paid special leave without deducting those days from the worker's annual leave? [Para 32] [Para 48]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay a skills allowance to sewing workers of US$3 for one machine, US$6 for two, US$10 for three and US$15 for four or more? [Para 40] [Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that a worker who takes special leave — for a wedding, a sick child, or a sick or deceased parent — must keep his wage and benefits and must not have the days cut from his annual leave, because wages are low and living costs high. [Para 3] [Para 32] [Para 36] They also sought the machine-skills allowance, saying the employer asks about machine skills at recruitment but pays skilled and unskilled workers the same. [Para 42] [Para 43]
**Employer (respondent):** that it already complies with the Labour Law and its internal rules, which allow special leave to be deducted from annual leave, and that no law obliges it to pay a skills allowance. [Para 4] [Para 37] [Para 44] It added that COVID-19 and the loss of EBA preferences have hurt it, and that other factories in the same special economic zone would resent a concession. [Para 38] [Para 39]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand for special leave free of deduction from annual leave, and declined to consider the skills-allowance demand at all. [Para 84] [Para 85]
On the first issue the panel read Article 171 paragraphs 1 to 3 of the Labour Law as allowing deduction of special leave from annual leave while the worker has not used his annual leave, and permitting the employer to require repayment once it is used. [Para 48] [Para 49] [Para 50] Article 1 of Prakas No. 267 SKBY of 11 October 2001 grants up to seven days of paid special leave a year for personal or family events, and Article 2 of the same Prakas allows deduction from annual leave. [Para 51] [Para 52] The panel found the employer's practice consistent with those texts and, following Award No. 24/13 (Cambodia Data Systems Organisation) and Award No. 055/20 (Bou Keo Garmen Factory), held that no law requires special leave to be granted free of deduction. [Para 53] [Para 55] [Para 57] [Para 58] The demand was therefore held to have no legal basis. [Para 60] [Para 61]
On the second issue the panel treated the skills allowance as a demand for a benefit above the law, and so an interest dispute rather than a rights dispute. [Para 64] It held that under Article 96 paragraph 2 of the Labour Law, Article 54 (new) of the Trade Union Law and Article 72 of that law only a most-representative union, or several local unions together, may bring an interest dispute, because an award on such a dispute replaces the collective agreement for a year under Article 43 paragraph 1 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY. [Para 65] [Para 73] [Para 76] [Para 77] The claimant union was found not to be the most representative union in the enterprise and so not entitled to bring the demand; the panel followed earlier awards 81/04, 98/04, 02/11, 66/11, 118/16 and 203/16 to the same effect. [Para 79] [Para 81] [Para 82] It therefore declined to consider the merits. [Para 83]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that grants seven days of paid special leave a year under Article 171 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 267 SKBY but deducts those days from annual leave acts lawfully whenever the worker has not yet taken his annual leave, and no award will order the leave free of deduction. [Para 53] [Para 58] A demand for a benefit that the law does not require is an interest dispute, and the panel will not hear it on the merits unless the union bringing it is the most representative union in the enterprise or a group of local unions jointly entitled to conclude a collective agreement, because the resulting award replaces the collective agreement. [Para 64] [Para 76] [Para 78]

**Obiter dicta:** that the employer's difficulties from COVID-19 and the loss of EBA preferences, and the resentment of other factories in the same special economic zone, were noted but played no part in the reasoning. [Para 38] [Para 39]
