# Award No. 045/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អ៉ី ភី អាយ អេ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន (Arbitration Council, 31 August 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, employer-appointed; អន ណន, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លា, chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 140806

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អ៉ី ភី អាយ អេ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន, a cutting and sewing company at Trapeang Chrey, Khan Porsenchey, Phnom Penh, employed 210 workers. [Para 1][Para 39] On 17 July 2020 the labour dispute department received a seven-point demand, conciliation on 23 July failed, and report No. 746 ក.ប/អក/វក dated 27 July was sent the same day; the seventh point, on suspension of employment contracts, was kept back by the conciliator, leaving six. [Para 10][Para 13][Para 14][Para 15][Para 16] At the hearing of 5 August 2020 the parties settled points 2, 4 and 6, so only points 1, 3 and 5 remained; 46 workers had thumbprinted the original complaint and 30 were verified as claimants. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 40][Para 76] The factory had suspended the contracts and restarted only in July 2020. [Para 54]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (ចំណុច ទី១) Whether the employer must pay compensation in lieu of unused annual leave for February 2020, as it had done in past practice? [Para 43][Para 77]
2. (ចំណុច ទី៣) Whether the employer must pay 5 per cent severance for February 2020? [Para 56][Para 94]
3. (ចំណុច ទី៥) Whether the panel has jurisdiction over, and the employer must pay, National Social Security Fund contributions regularly? [Para 61][Para 106]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត ក្នុង ការដោះស្រាយ វិវាទ ការងារ រួម នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អ៉ី ភី អាយ អេ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន):** In February and March 2020 they worked full time but received no payment in lieu of annual leave, and no date was fixed for it; they also sought the 5 per cent severance for February 2020 and regular remittance of social-security contributions, without which they cannot use their NSSF cards at partner hospitals. [Para 48][Para 64][Para 65]
**Employer (respondent — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អ៉ី ភី អាយ អេ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន):** The absence of purchase orders left it in financial difficulty, so it could not pay the annual-leave compensation or the contributions, and it could not promise a date; it had sought deferral of contributions from January to April 2020 and would pay when orders returned, and some workers had only two or three days' work a week. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 68][Para 55]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the demand for payment in lieu of unused annual leave was rejected, the demand for 5 per cent severance was rejected, and the demand that the employer remit social-security contributions was not considered. [Para 114][Para 115][Para 116]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 167(2) of the Labour Law, which requires compensation for leave accrued where the contract is cut short or ends, and Article 167(3), which voids any agreement to waive annual leave; payment in lieu therefore arises only on termination of the employment relationship. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80] Because the contracts here had not ended, an annual payment during employment, even one the employer had made in the past, conflicts with those provisions and is void under Article 13, whose rules are of public order. [Para 81][Para 85][Para 87][Para 91] The panel followed Awards 94/04-Internet, 45/05-BNN, 88/12-Magnet Textile and 73/13-Yak Jin. [Para 84]

On issue 3 the panel read Article 73(6) as requiring 5 per cent severance at the end of a fixed-duration contract, when the working relationship with the employer is severed, and found that none of the claimants had had a contract terminated; the money was therefore not due. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 102][Para 103][Para 104] It followed Awards 60/06, 99/06, 85/09, 124/09, 105/11 and 131/15. [Para 98]

On issue 5 the panel asked first whether it had jurisdiction, and answered no: Article 91 of the Social Security Law 2019 sends disputes over the application of social-security rules between members, employers and the Fund to the Fund's own dispute committee, then to the social-security regulator for the member's workplace, and only then to the court. [Para 106][Para 108][Para 110][Para 112] It followed Awards 44/17 and 042/20. [Para 109]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Payment in lieu of unused annual leave under Article 167(2) and 5 per cent severance under Article 73(6) fall due only when the employment contract ends, so an employer owes neither while the contract subsists, and its own former practice of paying annually cannot create the obligation because Article 167(3) and Article 13 void it. [Para 80][Para 91][Para 102] A demand that an employer remit contributions to the National Social Security Fund is outside the panel's jurisdiction, Article 91 of the Social Security Law 2019 routing it to the Fund's dispute committee and then the regulator. [Para 108][Para 112]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that the Labour Law nowhere provides for payment in lieu of annual leave during the currency of a contract, so the employer's past practice was itself unlawful rather than a benefit to be continued. [Para 81][Para 91]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" in the award date, the report and nearly every instrument, so 2020 is inferred from context only. [Para 1][Para 10][Para 13] The file is cited once as case 046/20 in a reference to an earlier summary, though the award is 045/20. [Para 25] The claimant count is printed five different ways — 46 thumbprints, 12 representatives, 6 named in the department's letter, 32 in the list filed, and 30 after verification. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 74][Para 76] Two claimants appear twice in the annex with different identity numbers, and the very first annex entry has no entry date, so the list is unreliable on its face; names are not reproduced here. [Para 75][Para 120] The operative part labels the social-security item inconsistently with the numbering used in the issues and reasons. [Para 113][Para 116]
