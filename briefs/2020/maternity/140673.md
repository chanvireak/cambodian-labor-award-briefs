# Award No. 042/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃាំង ដុង ១ ខូអិលធីឌី (Khiang Dong 1 Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 27 July 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators: យូ សួន ទី (Yu Soun Ti), អន ណន (An Nan), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (Pen Bun Chea, Chair) · Doc ID 140673

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃាំង ដុង ១ ខូអិលធីឌី, at Kbal Damrei village, Khan Po Sen Chey, Phnom Penh, cuts and sews garments and employs 180 workers [Para 1][Para 46]. The workers, represented through សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ (registered 6 July 2018) and by five authorised representatives, filed a complaint on 1 July 2020 in the name of 184 workers [Para 29][Para 47][Para 48]. Of seven demands three were conciliated and the worker party withdrew Issue 4 at the hearing, leaving Issues 1 to 3 [Para 13][Para 24][Para 25]. The employer sent no representative with authority and the panel heard the case in its absence [Para 22][Para 23][Para 71]. The demands were 50 percent of wages for three months of maternity leave plus the 70 percent social security maternity allowance, a US$20 monthly waiting allowance for June and July 2019 for about 14 casual workers, and regular payment of social security contributions [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay back 50 percent of wages for the three months of maternity leave, and the 70 percent social security maternity allowance, to women who have taken maternity leave? [Para 85]
2. (Issue 2) Whether an agreement existed obliging the employer to pay US$20 a month as a waiting allowance for June and July 2019 to workers kept waiting for work? [Para 111]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the panel has jurisdiction over a demand that the employer pay social security contributions in full so that workers can use social security services? [Para 124]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** About 20 women had not received the 50 percent maternity wage, although the employer paid it in 2018 and stopped from 2019; the employer promised US$20 a month for June and July 2019 but never paid it after releasing the casual workers; and it has paid no social security contributions since July 2019, so pregnant women alone can still use the scheme [Para 50][Para 52][Para 57][Para 63].

**Employer (respondent):** The employer did not appear at conciliation or at either hearing and filed no evidence or argument in answer [Para 1][Para 22][Para 92].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay back 50 percent of wages for three months of maternity leave to the women who took it; the demand for the 70 percent social security allowance was refused for want of jurisdiction, as was the Issue 3 demand on contributions, and the Issue 2 demand for a US$20 waiting allowance was dismissed [Para 130][Para 131][Para 132][Para 133]

The panel first held it could proceed in the employer's absence: Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 lets the panel continue or terminate by award where a duly invited party fails to appear without good cause, as in Awards 53/04, 148/07, 99/09 and others, and the secretariat had served and telephoned the employer [Para 66][Para 68][Para 69][Para 71]. It also held that only one of the five listed representatives attended and she was on the Labour Department's list, so she had standing under Prakas No. 362 [Para 75][Para 79][Para 80].

On Issue 1, Article 182 of the Labour Law gives women 90 days' maternity leave and Article 183 gives half wages including benefits during that leave, but only to a woman who has worked in the enterprise for at least one year [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87]. Checking the claim list against the thumbprinted complaint, the panel found only three claimants who both thumbprinted and were named on the list, and each had more than one year of service, so only those three were considered [Para 83][Para 84][Para 93][Para 94]. Since the employer was absent and had paid nothing, the panel ordered payment of the 50 percent wage for three months to them [Para 96][Para 130].

On the 70 percent maternity allowance, Article 91 of the Social Security Law assigns disputes over the application of social security rules between members, employers and the Fund to the Fund's own dispute committee, then to the social security regulator, before any court, as the panel had held in Award 44/17 [Para 98][Para 101][Para 102]. Failing to pay contributions is therefore a dispute between the Fund and the employer, outside the panel's jurisdiction [Para 103][Para 105]. The same reasoning disposed of Issue 3, which sought regular contributions [Para 128][Para 129].

On Issue 2, the panel applied Articles 311 and 336 of the Civil Code: a contract arises from the parties' mutual consent, offers and acceptances, and only those claimants with a verifiable name on the original complaint were considered, seven of the 14 [Para 109][Para 111][Para 113][Para 114]. The worker party produced no specific evidence of an agreement to pay US$20 a month, so the burden it bore was not discharged and the claim was dismissed [Para 117][Para 121][Para 122][Para 123].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for the social security maternity allowance, or for payment of social security contributions, lies outside the panel's jurisdiction because Article 91 of the Social Security Law reserves such disputes to the Fund's dispute committee and then the regulator; only the statutory half wage owed directly by the employer under Article 183 of the Labour Law can be ordered here [Para 91][Para 101][Para 105]. A demand resting on an alleged oral promise must be supported by specific evidence of the agreement, since the party asserting it bears the burden of proof [Para 117][Para 121].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the employer's own administrative chief could not represent it without a written delegation of authority, a point not necessary to the finding that the employer had been properly invited [Para 22][Para 23].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed inconsistently: the face of the award reads ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃាំង ដុង ១ ខូអិលធីឌី, while the procedural history and evidence list use សាំង ដុង ១ and យ៉ាំង ដុង ១ [Para 9][Para 14][Para 47]. Years are truncated throughout — dates appear as "ឆ្នាំ ២០" with the last digits lost, including the award date and the deadline for opposition [Para 11][Para 28][Para 134]. The arithmetic of the claimant group does not reconcile: the complaint is said to be signed by 184 workers, 20 women are said to claim maternity pay, eight names are listed, and only three are finally considered [Para 48][Para 81][Para 82][Para 84]. The number of authorised representatives is given as five in the narrative but the list at the head of the award shows four [Para 2][Para 47]. The signature block is garbled, losing the chair's name and title [Para 135].
