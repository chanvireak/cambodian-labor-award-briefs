# Award No. 005/20 — Siem Reap International Airport (Cambodia Airports Services) (Arbitration Council, 19 February 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mar Samborna (employer-appointed, dissenting), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 139292

## 1. Facts
The employer operates Siem Reap International Airport with 563 staff. [Para 57][Para 58] Mr R was president of the airport's tourism-industry workers' union and simultaneously "mekar" — team leader of ground services — supervising twelve baggage handlers. [Para 2][Para 61][Para 62] Two baggage incidents occurred: one bag left off a flight on 9 February 2019, and eighteen bags weighing 244 kg left behind on flight AK542/543 on 15 November 2019. [Para 67][Para 70] The employer issued a reminder letter on 14 February 2019 and a final warning on 21 November 2019. [Para 69][Para 72] The union demanded both be withdrawn and the accusations dropped. [Para 61]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether this is a collective labour dispute falling within the panel's jurisdiction, rather than an individual dispute? [Para 199]
2. Whether the employer must drop the accusations and withdraw the February 2019 reminder letter and the final warning issued to Mr R? [Para 61]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the first incident was caused by a subordinate who miscounted, not by Mr R, who had asked for a recount; the second was caused by a broken conveyor belt at the sorting area and an unserviceable radio, and the employer never investigated whose fault it was; singling out the union president for discipline is anti-union discrimination under Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law and Article 62 of the Trade Union Law. [Para 76][Para 84][Para 96][Para 97][Para 99][Para 146]
**Employer (respondent):** the dispute concerns one worker only and is therefore individual; the warning followed repeated negligence of the same kind and was issued under Article 19.3.2 of the internal rules after a prior reminder, and a team leader bears responsibility for his team's work and cannot deflect fault onto subordinates. [Para 58][Para 105][Para 116]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to cancel the reminder letter concerning the one-bag incident and to issue instead a written warning for the eighteen-bag incident, valid for 12 months under Article 19.3.2 of the internal rules. [Para 280]

On jurisdiction the panel applied Article 302 of the Labour Law and the three-condition test. [Para 211][Para 213] Condition one was satisfied despite the claim touching a single worker, because Mr R was the most representative union leader in the company and many union members supported the claim; condition two was satisfied because the disciplinary measure concerns employer-worker relations; and condition three was satisfied because the dispute involved that union leadership and could unsettle the enterprise if left unresolved. [Para 216][Para 218][Para 219][Para 221] Arbitrator Mar Samborna dissented, holding that all three conditions failed — the claim concerned one worker, turned on the interpretation of his job description, and had no demonstrated effect on the company — so the panel should have declined jurisdiction and rejected the claim. [Para 297][Para 299][Para 300][Para 302]

On the one-bag incident the panel found the miscount was the subordinate's: Mr R asked him to recount when the figure of 100 bags did not match, the recount produced 101, and no rule requires a team leader to count personally. [Para 234][Para 235][Para 242] The reminder letter therefore rested on no fault of his, and the employer had no right to issue it. [Para 244][Para 245]

On the eighteen-bag incident the panel accepted that the belt had broken and the radio was faulty, but held that as team leader Mr R still had to verify the bag count against the sorting-area figure before allowing the cargo door to be closed and the aircraft pushed back; his subordinates had dispersed and reported nothing, and he never confirmed the number. [Para 248][Para 252][Para 253][Para 256][Para 257] The fault was his and discipline was open to the employer. [Para 258] The measure was nonetheless mislabelled: the internal rules provide an oral warning, then a written warning, then suspension or termination for minor faults, and a written warning then suspension or termination for a first and second medium fault — they contain no "final warning" step, so the letter was to be corrected to a written warning valid 12 months. [Para 260][Para 261][Para 262]

On discrimination the panel held the workers bore the burden of proving anti-union motive, found no specific evidence beyond assertion, and held the warning was grounded in a breach of the internal rules, not in union activity. [Para 271][Para 275][Para 277]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A disciplinary letter must have both a factual basis and the correct legal character: where the worker did not commit the fault alleged the letter must be cancelled outright, and where the fault is established but the employer's internal scale provides only a written warning for a first medium-level fault, the sanction must be corrected to that step and cannot be escalated to a "final warning" the rules do not create. [Para 242][Para 245][Para 260][Para 262]

**Obiter dicta:** a claim by a single worker may still be a collective dispute where that worker is the company's most representative union leader and the claim is supported by the union's members; and an employer may not rely on a team leader's supervisory role to attribute to him a counting error committed by a subordinate where he did in fact require a recount. [Para 216][Para 241]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is non-binding, final eight days after notification, and carries a dissenting opinion annexed under Article 37 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 282][Para 284][Para 287] The document is badly degraded: the year is cut to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, hearing and filing dates are partly destroyed, and Latin garbage intrudes ("MarkAT105%", "5770 %", "Li", "Li ១០"). [Para 8][Para 36][Para 160][Para 248] The 18 bags are given as 244 kg in the reasoning and 232 kg and 245 kg at other points. [Para 70][Para 104][Para 168] The employer is named both as Siem Reap International Airport and as "ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ែរ ផត មែនីជ្ជមែន សឺវីស" (Cambodia Airports Services). [Para 57] The final warning is variously dated 21 November 2019 and described as issued "ក្នុងអំឡុងពេលបំរើជីងហោះហើរ", and the union's own election notice appears once as 19 October 2018 and once as 2019. [Para 72][Para 122] Individual workers who gave statements are named in the source; they are reduced here to roles.
