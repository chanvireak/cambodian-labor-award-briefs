# Award No. 061/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធី ឌី (Arbitration Council, 4 December 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer, អន ណន appointed by the workers, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140819

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធីឌី, Khmer Time Tex Style Co., Ltd, cuts and sews garments at Trapeang Thleung 4 village, Chom Chao 1 sangkat, Pur Senchey, Phnom Penh, with 850 workers. [Para 1] [Para 41] The claimant is the local union សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ ការពារ កម្មករ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធីឌី. [Para 1] [Para 31] Workers of the plant filed five demands on 13 October 2020; conciliation on 27 October 2020 settled one, and four reached the Council on 3 November 2020. [Para 15] [Para 16] [Para 17] At the hearing of 13 November 2020 the parties agreed the fourth demand, leaving demands 1, 2 and 3, and the panel issued its non-binding award on 4 December 2020. [Para 14] [Para 21] [Para 22] [Para 25]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay wages twice a month, regularly, and complete payment by 3:30 pm on the payday? [Para 45] [Para 72]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay the US$12 monthly milk allowance for small children regularly, between the 10th and the 15th of each month, as before? [Para 58] [Para 92]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay the 2,000 riel daily meal allowance for overtime every ten days, on the 13th, the 23rd and the 3rd of the following month, as before? [Para 65] [Para 108]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ ការពារ កម្មករ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វលី ធី តេក្ស ស្ទីល ខូអែល ធីឌី:** that the employer stopped paying wages twice a month, as the Ministry of Labour requires, and began paying once a month, irregularly and late, sometimes only by the 14th; that paying at 3:30 pm, the moment they leave work, hurts pregnant workers and their travel time; and that the milk allowance and overtime meal allowance are now paid late or two months in arrears. [Para 45] [Para 49] [Para 56] [Para 58] [Para 65]
**Employer (respondent):** that it accepts paying once a month and late, because COVID-19 cut its income and it had to meet other costs; that it tried to reschedule payments and asked the workers to understand; and that it never withheld money owed. [Para 51] [Para 52] [Para 62] On wages it said it began paying at 3:30 pm because that is the hour workers leave. [Para 55] [Para 57]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to pay wages twice a month and regularly, to complete payment by 3:30 pm, to pay the US$12 milk allowance between the 10th and 15th of each month, and to pay the 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance every ten days on the 13th, 23rd and 3rd. [Para 127] [Para 128] [Para 129]
On the frequency of wages the panel applied Article 116 of the 1997 Labour Law, which requires wages at least twice a month with a gap of no more than sixteen days, and Article 1 of Prakas No. 442 K.B/Pr.K.Kh.L of 21 September 2018, which from January 2019 fixes the first payment in the second week and the second in the fourth week of each month. [Para 72] [Para 73] [Para 74] [Para 75] It found the employer had changed to monthly payment on its own in April 2020, and followed Award No. 085/19 (Jin Yi Garment) and Award No. 111/19 (Syun Yin) in holding that no provision lets an employer change or postpone the statutory pay cycle. [Para 47] [Para 77] [Para 79] [Para 80]
On the hour of payment the panel read Article 115 paragraph 3 of the Labour Law, which forbids paying wages on a rest day and requires payment a day earlier where the due day is a rest day, together with Article 137 fixing eight hours a day and forty-eight a week, as meaning that wages must be paid and the payment completed inside working hours; paying at the closing hour therefore breached both articles. [Para 83] [Para 85] [Para 86] [Para 89] [Para 90]
On the milk allowance the panel held that an employer's management right under Article 2 of the Labour Law must be exercised lawfully and reasonably, and that the employer produced no evidence of hardship and no agreement with the workers to postpone; the allowance must therefore be paid between the 10th and 15th as before. [Para 96] [Para 101] [Para 102] [Para 106] [Para 107]
On the overtime meal allowance the panel applied Article 2 of Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, which gives 2,000 riel a day or one free meal to workers who volunteer for overtime, found the notification silent on timing, and held that the money must reach workers in time to buy food, so that the long-standing dates of the 13th, 23rd and 3rd bind the employer. [Para 108] [Para 110] [Para 113] [Para 121] [Para 125] [Para 126]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may not replace the statutory twice-monthly wage cycle with monthly payment, however genuine its cash-flow difficulty, because Article 116 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 442 of 21 September 2018 fix the cycle and no provision allows a unilateral change or postponement. [Para 75] [Para 79] Wages must be paid, and payment completed, within working hours, so paying at the hour work ends breaches Article 115 paragraph 3 and Article 137. [Para 86] [Para 90] A benefit the employer has long paid on fixed dates, such as a milk allowance or an overtime meal allowance, must continue on those dates unless the employer proves a lawful and agreed reason to postpone it. [Para 102] [Para 113]

**Obiter dicta:** that the free meal is itself an encouragement to volunteer for overtime, since food is a daily necessity the employer must provide. [Para 111] [Para 112]
