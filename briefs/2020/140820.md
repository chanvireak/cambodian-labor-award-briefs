# Award No. 062/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃែន ធើរេន អឹភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 18 January 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី appointed by the employer, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ appointed by the workers, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140820

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឃែន ធើរេន អឹភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី, Kan Thaeren Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, cuts and sews garments at Trapeang Thleung village, Chom Chao 1 sangkat, Pur Senchey, Phnom Penh, with 670 workers. [Para 1] [Para 50] Workers filed five demands on 26 October 2020; conciliation on 23 November 2020 settled two, and three reached the Council on 25 November 2020. [Para 15] [Para 16] [Para 17] The hearing set for 1 December 2020 was suspended at the parties' joint request after the COVID-19 community outbreak, and the panel sat instead on 28 December 2020. [Para 19] [Para 20] [Para 22] The award issued on 18 January 2021. [Para 1] [Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay wages twice a month and regularly, as the Labour Law requires? [Para 53] [Para 92]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must install hot-water and cold-water dispensers in the factory for the workers? [Para 70] [Para 106]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must add the seniority bonus to the base wage as the basis for calculating the overtime rate? [Para 80] [Para 118]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that since April 2020 the employer has paid once a month instead of twice, and incompletely, so that a worker waits a month and a half for wages; that late wages brought bank penalties, unsympathetic landlords, and no money for children's milk; and that the employer calculates overtime without the seniority bonus although it does add the bonus for supervisors and TS staff. [Para 53] [Para 57] [Para 60] [Para 85] They also asked for hot and cold drinking water in the factory. [Para 70]
**Employer (respondent):** that under COVID-19 it asked to pay monthly, having met the workers' and union representatives and section heads until December 2020, and that it will return to twice-monthly payment once its finances improve; that it cannot afford water dispensers, having already provided two filtered-water tanks; and that overtime is calculated on the labour inspectorate's guidance, which excludes the seniority bonus. [Para 3] [Para 5] [Para 64] [Para 87]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay wages twice a month and regularly; the water-dispenser demand was refused; and the seniority-bonus demand was declined without reconsideration. [Para 140] [Para 141] [Para 142]
On wages the panel applied Article 116 of the 1997 Labour Law, requiring payment at least twice a month with no more than sixteen days between payments, and Article 1 of Prakas No. 442 K.B/Pr.K.Kh.L of 21 September 2018, fixing the first payment in the second week and the second in the fourth week of each month. [Para 92] [Para 93] [Para 94] [Para 95] It found the employer had switched to monthly payment from April 2020 with only one day's notice and no agreement, and followed Award No. 085/19 (Jin Yi Garment), Award No. 111/19 (Syun Yin) and Award No. 36/11 (Cambodia Broadcasting) in holding that no provision permits an employer to change or postpone the statutory cycle. [Para 97] [Para 98] [Para 100] [Para 103] [Para 104]
On the water dispensers the panel applied Article 1 of Prakas No. 054 SKBY of 10 February 2019, which requires the employer to supply sufficient hygienic drinking water, and read it, following Award No. 173/14 (Ay Son Cable), as requiring water and not hot and cold dispensers. [Para 106] [Para 107] [Para 108] The employer's two filtered-water tanks and the shared kettle allowed in the clinic satisfied that duty, so the demand had no legal basis. [Para 71] [Para 110] [Para 115] [Para 116]
On the seniority bonus the panel applied the principle of res judicata: the same issue between the same parties had been decided in Award No. 086/19 (Kan Thaeren Apparel), where the panel ordered the seniority bonus added to the base wage for overtime, and the three conditions of identical parties, identical issue and a full decision on the merits were met. [Para 121] [Para 124] [Para 128] [Para 133] [Para 135] [Para 137] It therefore declined to reconsider the demand, leaving the earlier order to be enforced through the courts. [Para 90] [Para 138] [Para 139]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may not move from the twice-monthly wage cycle fixed by Article 116 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 442 of 21 September 2018 to monthly payment, even with notice and a pandemic, because the law allows no unilateral change or postponement. [Para 95] [Para 104] A duty to supply sufficient hygienic drinking water is satisfied by clean drinking water and does not require hot-and-cold dispensers. [Para 108] [Para 110] Where an issue between the same parties has already been decided on the merits by an earlier award, the panel will decline to decide it again and the earlier award must be enforced in court. [Para 124] [Para 138]

**Obiter dicta:** that res judicata serves to avoid contradictory outcomes on the same dispute and to ensure a dispute ends in one final decision. [Para 126]
