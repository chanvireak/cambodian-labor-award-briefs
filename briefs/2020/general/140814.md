# Award No. 053/20 — Mery Dan In Das Stri Garment (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 23 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Hem Hou Narith, An Non, Run Saray presiding) · Doc ID 140814

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន មេរី ឌាន អ៉ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រី ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីតធីត knits garments at Kampong Pring, Sitbo, Ta Khmau, Phnom Penh, and employed 725 workers. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 1]
Workers raised four demands on 6 July 2020; conciliation on 22 July 2020 settled none, and the four points were transmitted by report No. 598/20 of 23 July 2020. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19][Para 14]
At the hearing of 2 October 2020 the parties settled Issues 1 to 3 by agreement, so only Issue 4 remained: the USD 5 monthly incentive bonus for May 2020. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 16]
Since the factory opened the employer had paid that bonus monthly to workers with no absence; workers met the conditions for May 2020 and the employer issued a letter to pay it, then on 20 June 2020 notified postponement from May to September and later until COVID-19 eased. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 67][Para 68]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay the USD 5 incentive bonus for May 2020 to the workers who met the conditions for it? [Para 63][Para 93]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers say the employer never gave notice before postponing the incentive, that they had already worked to the standard that earns it for May 2020, and that about 300 of them claim it, though they acknowledged that those 300 do not appear by name or thumbprint in the authorisation delegating representation. [Para 70][Para 77]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន មេរី ឌាន អ៉ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រី ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីតធីត says the USD 5 is a benefit above what the law requires, meant to buy workers a snack, that two large buyers cancelled orders of about 5 million garments and pushed payment terms from 60 to 160 days, and that 600 workers thumbprinted rejecting the claim. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 75]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the demand for the USD 5 incentive bonus for May 2020. [Para 93]
The panel based itself on Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which allows a party to appear through a representative only where that person holds written authorisation from the party, and it followed Awards Nos. 161/09 (Prek Treng Handicraft), 43/10 (Moeng Chhean), 144/12 (I-Garment) and 120/15 (Pacific Gross Brai Vet Co., Ltd), together with Award No. 181/14 (Sung Yin (Cambodia) B-Tech Style). [Para 84][Para 85][Para 87]
Checking the letter delegating representation of 22 June 2020, the panel found 174 named and thumbprinted workers, while the roughly 300 workers now claiming the May bonus appeared nowhere in it, and it noted that the workers did not contest this. [Para 55][Para 77][Para 82][Para 90]
It accordingly held that there was no identified complainant claiming the bonus and refused to consider the demand. [Para 91][Para 92]
The panel did not reach the merits of whether the bonus had vested for May 2020, on the correspondence in which 174 workers accepted the temporary suspension of the USD 5 payment until the COVID-19 outbreak ended. [Para 78][Para 80][Para 81]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand cannot be arbitrated unless the person claiming it is identified and has authorised the representative in writing; where the workers actually seeking the benefit are absent from the delegation letter, the panel will decline to consider the demand however clear the underlying entitlement appears on the documents. [Para 85][Para 90][Para 91]
The panel will not supply a claimant class of its own motion, even a class whose identity the parties agree on in substance. [Para 77][Para 92]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded that the employer had paid the USD 5 bonus from the start of operations and that 467 additional workers had thumbprinted refusal of the claim, facts that did not enter the ground of decision. [Para 64][Para 81]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", including the award date of 23 October, the hearing of 2 October and the May 2020 bonus month. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 63]
The month of the claim is printed two ways: the reasoning and the order speak of May 2020, while the reasons at paragraph 83 date the demand to "ខែឧសភា ឆ្នាំ ២០០២". [Para 63][Para 83]
The respondent's name is rendered inconsistently, appearing once as "ស្ទី ហ្គាមេន" and once without its final "ខេមបូឌា", so the corporate name cannot be matched character for character. [Para 1][Para 35]
One line of the issues section consists only of a Khmer full stop, and the employer's exhibit list jumps from item 5 to "១៦-" before returning to 7. [Para 10][Para 34]
The citation to the earlier award numbered 161/09 is split across two paragraphs by a quotation mark, so the award number appears detached from the case name. [Para 86][Para 87]
The signature block prints the worker-appointed arbitrator's name with a stray figure appended, and the presiding arbitrator's name is preceded by a stray "3.". [Para 95]
