# Award No. 047/20 — Long Met Agri Khol Choer Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 September 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Pen Bun Chhean presiding) · Doc ID 140808

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឡុង មេត អេហ្គ្រី ខល ឈ័រ ឯ.ក is an agricultural company cultivating bananas at Trapeang Phleang, Chhuk district, Kampot, employing 470 workers as of the hearing. [Para 44][Para 45]
Since 2018 the employer assigned each worker 2,000 banana trees and paid a USD 50 monthly bonus to those who completed the quota. [Para 47]
From July 2020 the employer began applying a new scheme of 2,500 trees over a maximum of nine months, with bonus paid at harvest rather than monthly. [Para 48]
Workers filed a four-point demand with the Kampot provincial labour department on 14 July 2020; three points were settled at conciliation and the remaining one was referred to the Arbitration Council by non-conciliation report No. 252/20 of 13 August, with the single issue heard on 1 September 2020. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 5]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must abandon the new 2,500-tree scheme and restore the old policy of 2,000 trees per worker with a USD 50 monthly bonus for every worker, as the workers demand? [Para 46]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers say the quota forced them to work without rest, on holidays and rest days, and that some brought children and siblings to help finish 2,000 trees, so no worker can carry more than 2,000. [Para 51][Para 52] They add that the employer introduced the new scheme without approval from the labour inspectorate and without consulting the union, and would eventually dismiss those who refused and dissolve the union. [Para 53][Para 55]
**Employer (respondent):** The employer relies on Article 2 of the Labour Law, arguing an employer may organise and manage the enterprise, and that the new scheme was studied on comparable banana farms in Ratanakiri, Mondulkiri, Kampong Cham and abroad. [Para 57][Para 58] It attributes the change to prolonged drought that destroyed over half the plantation and to COVID-19 depressing export prices, and says workers who do not consent stay on the old terms. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the demand that the employer restore the old policy of 2,000 trees per worker and the USD 50 monthly bonus. [Para 85]
On the single issue the panel found no act yet committed: no worker who refused the new scheme had been relocated or dismissed, and the employer applies the new terms only to those who agree to them. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 75] It therefore held the dispute had not yet arisen and was a dispute about the future. [Para 79]
The panel grounded that holding in Article 312 paragraph 1 of the Labour Law, which limits it to the issues recorded in the non-conciliation minutes or arising directly from that dispute. [Para 80]
It followed its own earlier rulings declining to examine future disputes, citing Award No. 68/04 (CTI), Award No. 36/06 (Mondoltech), Award No. 58/07-08 (Scar Sport Wear) and Award No. 155/13 (KAT), and adopted their reading that the panel exists to settle labour disputes, not problems that have not yet occurred. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 81]
The award states it is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour through the Council secretariat. [Para 86]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand that the panel condemn an employer's announced policy is not justiciable while the policy has been applied only to workers who consented to it; until an objecting worker is actually transferred, penalised or dismissed under it, the claim concerns a future dispute that the panel must decline to consider. [Para 78][Para 79]
The corollary the panel drew is that continuance of the old terms for non-consenting workers is what keeps the dispute speculative. [Para 75][Para 83]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that a substantial majority of the workforce had accepted the new scheme and that only about thirty workers refused it, though it treated those figures as background rather than as a ground of decision. [Para 72][Para 73]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year in the document is printed as "ឆ្នាំ ២០" with the final two digits lost, including the award date, the 2018 starting date and the July 2020 policy change; the header date of 22 September and the hearing date of 1 September are legible but the year must be read from context. [Para 1][Para 7][Para 48]
The respondent's name appears twice in abbreviated form, once as "AKP ឡុង មេត អេហ្គ្រី ខល ឈ័រ ឯ.ក", and the company is also written "ឡង មេត" in the tax patent entry. [Para 33][Para 22]
The reasoning at the head of the decision restates the demand as being for four workers ("៤ ម្នាក់") where every other passage says one worker per quota, an internal contradiction left unresolved. [Para 74]
One arbitrator's signature line is corrupted by OCR so that the panel member's name is partly replaced by digits, though the same name is legible in the panel composition. [Para 87]
