# Award No. 049/17 — Gawon Apparel Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 29 December 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (appointed by the employer), Venh Pov (appointed by the workers), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 138952

## 1. Facts
The respondent is the same garment factory in Ta Khmao, Kandal, that was the subject of Award No. 047/17. The workers claimed that during their November 2017 strike the company should keep their wages and other benefits for seven days at the existing rate; the company would agree to only three and a half days, deducting the remaining three and a half days from annual leave. Conciliation on that single point failed on 14 December 2017 and the case reached the Council on 18 December 2017. The strike was still running when the panel was constituted on 20 December 2017. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 10][Para 12]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must maintain the wages and other benefits of the workers for the seven days of the November 2017 strike, rather than deducting part of that period from their annual leave? [Para 2][Para 3]

The panel did not reach that question. It framed the determinative issue as whether the arbitration could continue at all, given that the strike was still in progress. [Para 38]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers sought payment in full for the seven strike days, rejecting the company's offer of three and a half days plus a three-and-a-half-day deduction from annual leave. No worker attended the pre-hearing meeting as a claimant: the five persons named in the labour department's representative letter of 8 December 2017, together with five other workers who did attend, all denied knowing of this case and denied receiving any mandate from the workers to represent them in it, saying they had been summoned for case No. 047/17 instead. [Para 2][Para 21][Para 23][Para 45]
**Employer (respondent):** On the morning of 25 December 2017, 652 of 920 workers scanned their fingerprints into the factory but did not work even though work was available; by that afternoon 703 had scanned in but only 144 actually worked, the rest sitting idle. The company treated this as non-compliance with the Council's order to suspend the strike. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction to proceed — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 049/17 without determining the wage claim, and made no order for payment. [Para 51]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 (21 April 2004), which requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures, including strike and lock-out, throughout the arbitration and to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. It followed its earlier awards (Nos 59/06, 39/08, 137/09, 138/09, 110/11, 104/13, 119/13, 019/14, 322/14) holding that the Council will not continue arbitration while workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 38][Para 41][Para 42]

It found that order No. 006/17 of 20 December 2017 had directed the workers to stop striking and return to normal work from the morning of 21 December 2017; that the workers did not comply; and that on 25 December 2017 they scanned in but refused to work. Because no claimant worker appeared and the named representatives disclaimed any mandate, the panel accepted the employer's uncontradicted account of the strike's continuation. It recorded that, no hearing having been held, it had made no findings of fact on the wage claim, and closed the file. [Para 14][Para 44][Para 47][Para 48][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the Council has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the Council will terminate the arbitration proceedings instead of deciding the claims on their merits. Where no claimant worker attends and the persons named as their representatives deny receiving any mandate, the panel may act on the respondent's uncontradicted account of the strike and need make no findings on the substance. [Para 44][Para 48][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's recording that the ten workers who attended said they had been summoned for case No. 047/17 rather than this one was noted but not relied on beyond the finding that no claimant party was present. [Para 23][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The date of order No. 006/17 is printed as "24 20 December 2017" at [Para 43] where [Para 14] gives 20 December 2017. The representative letter No. 012 is dated 8 December 2017 at [Para 19] but 14 December 2017 at [Para 31], and the two passages name different lists of five representatives. The attendance figures at [Para 26] are corrupted, reading "703 ... but only 197 workers, 144 of them" in a sentence that cannot be parsed as printed. The employer's name appears in several variants (Gawon Apparel / Gawon Ei Pheril), and [Para 37] inserts a stray Latin fragment ("មន្ទី Sang") into the reference to the provincial labour department. The employer-appointed arbitrator's signature block at [Para 52] is partly lost to OCR, though Ing Sothy is named on the face of the award [Para 1]. Workers are identified here by role only.
