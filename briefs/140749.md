# Award No. 114/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាង ឆឹង (Chang Cheng) (Arbitration Council, 2 January 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed អ៊ុក រី; worker-appointed លីវ សុវណ្ណា; chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140749

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាង ឆឹង, is a subcontracting garment factory at Stung Meanchey, Phnom Penh, with about 90 workers [Para 21][Para 22]. Two workers hired on 8 and 14 July 2006 under direct contracts were dismissed on 16 November 2006 with their wages paid but without prior notice [Para 25][Para 37]. They had been elected president and secretary of the Khmer Youth Union at the factory, សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាង ធីង, on 10 November 2006 [Para 2][Para 27][Para 28]. Of 14 demands, 10 were settled in conciliation and 4 were referred to the panel — 100% wages during periods without work, correct rounding of wages to the last cent, meal allowance of 1,000 riel for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00, and reinstatement of the two union leaders [Para 2][Para 4][Para 8][Para 15]. At the hearing issues 1, 2 and 4 were settled, leaving only the reinstatement claim [Para 18][Para 19].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 3) Whether the two dismissed workers were union leaders entitled to the special protection against dismissal, so that their reinstatement could be ordered? [Para 23][Para 44]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — សហព័ន្ឋ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាង ធីង):** The union representatives said the two had been elected as union leaders and were dismissed because of their union role, so the company had to take them back [Para 23][Para 38].
**Employer (respondent — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាង ឆឹង):** The company said both were ordinary piece-rate workers, that it had decided to dismiss them, and that it never received notice of the union election because the person who signed the notice was not its employee [Para 24][Para 34].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel refused to consider the reinstatement claim because the dispute was not a collective labour dispute within its competence [Para 75][Para 76].

On the protection claim, the panel set out Article 293 of the Labour Law, under which dismissal of a staff representative or a candidate requires the labour inspector's agreement [Para 44], and Article 4 of Prakas 305, which extends the same protection to union founders and members for 30 days after registration [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48]. Following awards 50/05, 64/05, 09/06 and 43/06, it held that protection requires three cumulative conditions: the worker must belong to a protected category, the dismissal must fall within the protection period, and the employer must have been notified of the protected status [Para 49][Para 50]. It found none satisfied: the workers produced no election minutes, member list, registration receipt or notification, and did not answer the company's point that the signatory of the notice was not an employee [Para 51][Para 32][Para 56][Para 60][Para 61]. The panel gave them until 22 December 2006 to file further evidence, but nothing arrived [Para 41][Para 42][Para 55]. It then asked whether the case was a collective dispute under Article 302, which requires a dispute between an employer and some of its workers over working conditions or union rights that could disrupt the enterprise or social peace [Para 68]. Conditions (a) and (b) were met, but condition (c) was not, because the two workers were not supported by a lawfully registered union or by other workers in the enterprise [Para 70][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 74]. Having jurisdiction only over collective disputes, the panel declined to consider the claim [Para 66][Para 75][Para 76]. The award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification [Para 77].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissal of alleged union leaders is a collective labour dispute only if the union is lawfully registered, the workers can prove their protected status with election and registration documents, and the employer was notified; absent that proof the panel has no jurisdiction and will not examine the dismissal [Para 49][Para 60][Para 66][Para 75].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the labour inspector and the Minister decide whether a dispute is individual or collective before referral, and that the panel will normally follow that classification absent clear reason to depart from it [Para 62][Para 63].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name is printed both as "ឆាង ឆឹង" and "ឆាង ធីង" [Para 21][Para 2]. The application dates of the two workers and the dismissal period (nearly four months) are rendered with stray brackets in the OCR [Para 25][Para 26]. The signature block garbles the employer-appointed arbitrator's name and gives the chair's name twice [Para 78].
