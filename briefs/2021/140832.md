# Award No. 015/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន គួ តាយ ហ័រ សេង ស៊ីន ហ្វេង ជីង (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន (Arbitration Council, 16 September 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · binding award under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (president) · Doc ID 140832

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន គួ តាយ ហ័រ សេង ស៊ីន ហ្វេង ជីង (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន is a garment factory employing 620 workers [Para 119]. The workers' representatives filed 11 demands on 25 June 2021 [Para 16]; conciliation on 21 July 2021 failed and report No. 511 K.B/AK/VK of 28 July 2021 sent four unresolved points to the Council [Para 13][Para 17]. At the hearing the workers withdrew Issue 4, leaving three [Para 24]. The company had replaced three incentive schemes on 1 June 2021 with a monthly wage of 192 USD plus a 2 to 5 USD skills addition, and had stopped the 6 USD cloth-change bonus [Para 142][Para 153].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer had to restore and maintain the benefits lost from 1 June 2021 — (a) the kta khien production-board bonus, (b) the piece-rate wage (ប្រាក់ ដេរ ស៊ី បុង), and (c) the 6 USD cloth-change bonus? [Para 181][Para 198][Para 226]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer had to pay 100% of wages for 1–14 April 2021 and 50% for 15–30 April 2021 during the Phnom Penh and Ta Khmau lockdown? [Para 233]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer had to pay 100% of the May 2021 wage to workers who could not attend because of the lockdown? [Para 242]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន គួ តាយ ហ័រ សេង ស៊ីន ហ្វេង ជីង (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន argued that the new three-consecutive-days rule destroyed the board bonus, that workers struck in June 2021 against it [Para 131][Para 132], and that the company cut the 6 USD cloth-change bonus contrary to the collective agreement [Para 154]. It relied on Instruction No. 043/21 as encouraging 50% wage support and on nearby factories that paid it [Para 161][Para 163].
**Employer (respondent):** the new scheme pays more, 4,000 to 42,000 riel a day instead of 2,000 to 16,000 riel, and targets are reduced when team members are absent or machines break [Para 136][Para 137]; the shift from piece rates to a monthly wage falls within its management right [Para 213]; Instruction No. 043/21 only makes wages for 1–14 April mandatory [Para 238].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to restore and maintain the 6 USD cloth-change bonus from 1 June 2021; the claims on the kta khien bonus and the piece-rate wage were dismissed, and so were the April and May 2021 wage claims [Para 251][Para 253][Para 254][Para 255].
The panel first held the award binding because clause 2(3) of the collective agreement of 19 March 2019 lets either party elect a binding award, and the employer had so elected [Para 177][Para 180]. On the board bonus it applied Article 2's management prerogative [Para 181], and the rule that an employer may set incentive bonuses beyond the statutory minimum unilaterally [Para 183]; the wage tables showed 9 of 14 production lines earned more under the new scheme [Para 189][Para 191], and targets were reduced for absent members or broken machines [Para 194], so the claim was dismissed [Para 197]. On the piece rate it applied the "past practice" doctrine of Award No. 95/09-Tak Fat [Para 199], read with Article 65(2) and (3) [Para 200], and held that how production is organised lies under employer control and is not a practice binding the company [Para 210][Para 213]; the workers also failed to prove the 200 to 300 USD they said they had earned, and the burden of proof lay on them [Para 219][Para 222]. On the cloth-change bonus, clause 8(3) of the collective agreement promised 6 USD per change without any condition, so the employer could not make it depend on the piece-rate system [Para 226][Para 230].
On Issue 2 the panel read points 2 and 3 of Instruction No. 043/21 as making wages for 1–14 April mandatory and leaving 15–30 April to an additional allowance paid according to capacity, so no 50% entitlement arose [Para 233][Para 236][Para 240]. On Issue 3, Article 102 ties wages to work actually performed, so workers who did not work during the May lockdown had no wage claim, the cause being neither their fault nor the employer's [Para 242][Para 248][Para 249].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A benefit written into a collective agreement, such as the 6 USD cloth-change bonus, is a contractual entitlement the employer cannot withdraw or condition unilaterally when it changes the pay system [Para 230][Para 231]. By contrast, an incentive bonus above the statutory floor and the choice between piece rates and a monthly wage are exercises of management prerogative, lawful where the evidence shows workers generally earn at least as much and targets are adjusted for absences and breakdowns [Para 183][Para 196].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that neither party could be blamed for the lockdown, a fact it recorded without letting it create a wage entitlement [Para 248].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer appears under two corporate identities, the registered name ក្រុមហ៊ុន គួ តាយ ហ័រ សេង ស៊ីន ហ្វេង ជីង (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន and the CBA counterparty ខេមបូស៊ីន ហ្វេង ជីង ហ្គាម៉េន ខូអិលធីឌី [Para 42][Para 178]. The workforce is 620 in the findings but 700 in the reasons [Para 119][Para 149]. The CBA is dated 19 March 2019 in one citation and 19 March 2021 in another [Para 123][Para 226], one evidence deadline (26 July 2021) precedes the 19 August hearing [Para 28], and the signature block records only two arbitrators, omitting the president named on the face of the award [Para 257].
