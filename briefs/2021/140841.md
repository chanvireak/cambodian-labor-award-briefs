# Award No. 025/21 — Ken Sport Shoe (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ) (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 30 November 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Vireak (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 140841

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ manufactures shoes at Village Chamkar Svay, Sethey, Samaki Mean Chey, Kampong Chhnang, and employs 8,311 workers, of whom about 7,000 are women [Para 66][Para 160]. The workers' representative filed ten demands on 24 August 2021; conciliation on 13 September 2021 settled two, and seven unresolved points came to the Council, of which two were settled at the hearing and one was withdrawn, leaving four for decision [Para 22][Para 23][Para 33][Para 34]. A further demand concerning overtime on the first vaccination day of 3 July 2021 was held to fall to the labour inspector, and the panel considered only the seven referred points [Para 31][Para 32]. The disputed acts are the refusal of overtime to the union's deputy president, the compulsory use of annual leave while there was no work, the absence of a nursery, and unequal payment of the functional allowance [Para 70][Para 87][Para 102][Para 110].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer discriminated against សហជីព កម្ពុជា by refusing its deputy president overtime and assigning him ladder work alone, and whether it must pay him overtime for 16 days? [Para 121]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer may require workers to use their annual leave when there is no work, rather than pay 100 per cent of wages? [Para 137]
3. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must build a nursery and provide a comfortable breastfeeding room under the Labour Law? [Para 152]
4. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must pay a functional allowance of 6 USD to every worker alike? [Para 172]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union argued that the employer refused its deputy president the overtime that other cooling-system technicians received, that it allowed him to do 16 days of overtime and then refused to pay, and that it had broken its own agreement of 15 June 2021 to end discrimination against him [Para 77][Para 79][Para 80]. It added that workers were pushed onto annual leave when work ran short, that some who took unpaid personal leave lost 8 USD, and that the factory has 7,000 women but no nursery [Para 90][Para 143][Para 160].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ replied that it knew nothing of the overtime because the team leader never reported it, that technicians in his grade do no overtime, and that paying him would invite others to self-authorise overtime [Para 83][Para 84][Para 85]. It said workers choose freely between annual leave and coming to work, that it pays a 5 USD child allowance in place of a nursery and is studying construction for 2022, and that the functional allowance follows each worker's role and evaluation [Para 98][Para 104][Para 115].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to build or arrange a nursery for women workers with small children, and dismissed the claims for overtime pay, for a bar on using annual leave during work shortages, for a comfortable breastfeeding room, and for a uniform 6 USD functional allowance [Para 189][Para 190][Para 191][Para 192][Para 193].
On issue 1 the panel applied Article 139 (new) of the Labour Law, which sets overtime at a 50 per cent premium, together with Article 4 of Prakas No. 80 SKBy of 25 March 1999 on the voluntary principle, and followed Award 150/14 in holding that the worker decides whether to work overtime and the employer may neither compel nor discipline a refusal [Para 121][Para 123][Para 124]. It found that the deputy president filled in the overtime sheet himself without his team leader's approval, that the administration refused it, and that colleagues in the same grade did no overtime, so there was no discrimination and no duty to pay the 16 days [Para 130][Para 131][Para 132][Para 134].
On issue 2 it applied Article 170 of the Labour Law, under which annual leave is normally fixed at the Khmer New Year unless the parties agree otherwise and the employer must notify the labour inspector of any arrangement [Para 137][Para 138]. Since the workers had agreed voluntarily to use annual leave and the employer had told those who preferred to work to approach the administration, the panel refused the demand [Para 147][Para 149][Para 150]. On issue 5 it applied Article 186, which requires a breastfeeding room and a nursery in any enterprise employing at least 100 women, and ordered a nursery; the claim about the comfort of the existing breastfeeding room failed for want of evidence, since the employer produced photographs that the workers did not rebut [Para 152][Para 168][Para 169][Para 170][Para 171]. On issue 6 it held that a functional allowance is an incentive not required by law, that Article 2 of the Labour Law leaves its terms to the employer's management, and that the workers had not shown the 6 USD was fixed in the internal rules or contracts, so the claim failed [Para 172][Para 174][Para 175][Para 185][Para 186].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Overtime is voluntary: an employer owes the statutory premium only for overtime it authorised, and a worker who records his own hours without his supervisor's approval cannot found either a wage claim or a discrimination claim on the refusal [Para 124][Para 134]. By contrast, an enterprise employing at least 100 women must provide a nursery under Article 186 regardless of any child allowance it pays in substitution, and an incentive allowance that neither law nor the internal rules fix remains within the employer's managerial discretion [Para 152][Para 175].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the employer must still keep the breastfeeding room clean, even though Article 186 sets no standard of comfort for it [Para 170]. It also observed that the employer had undertaken to study building a nursery in 2022 [Para 159].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR is badly degraded in places. It prints the overtime claim as 160 days in one recital and 16 days elsewhere, and the statutory citation reads "Article 139 ... Labour Law 2019 1997" with two dates in one phrase [Para 70][Para 121]. The award records the number of women workers as "15 7000" in one sentence and 7,000 in another [Para 158][Para 160]. A sentence in the reasoning degenerates into digit strings ("89872017") where the Khmer is unrecoverable [Para 148]. The list of prior awards relied on in the nursery reasoning breaks into untranslatable Latin fragments [Para 157]. The demand count is given as ten in the complaint but seven referred points are decided [Para 22][Para 32]. Individual workers, union officers and employer representatives are named in the source and appear here by role only.
