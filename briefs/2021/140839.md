# Award No. 032/21 — Yong Rong Han Bak Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង រ៉ុង ហ៊ែន ប៊ែក ឯ.ក) (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 26 November 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Hem Hou Narith (employer-appointed), Huon Chun Dy (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 140839

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង រ៉ុង ហ៊ែន ប៊ែក ឯ.ក operates at Village Krong Svay, Svay Rompear, Mukh Kampul, Kandal [Para 1]. The workers' representative, through សហជីព សិទ្ធិ ប្រយោជន៍ កម្មករ, filed 13 demands on 27 September 2021, conciliation on 21 October 2021 failed, and all 13 were referred to the Council on 1 November 2021 [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]. The demands covered a 15 USD monthly rice-and-milk allowance in place of a nursery, correct payment of holiday and Sunday wages, weekly payment of meal allowances for overtime, overtime rates for rework, sick-leave wages, access to National Social Security Fund services, respectful conduct by supervisors, correct final settlement, twice-monthly wages, union dues deduction, no discrimination against pregnant workers, suspension instead of dismissal, and correct payment on layoffs [Para 2][Para 3][Para 6][Para 7][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]. Neither the claimant nor any representative attended; the employer's administrative head appeared and reported that the original complainants had left the company and been paid their wages and benefits, so they would not attend [Para 1][Para 31]. The panel opened the hearing in their absence [Para 32].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural issue) Whether the panel must continue the arbitration when the claimant party, duly invited twice and contacted by telephone and Telegram, fails to appear and offers no valid reason? [Para 44][Para 53]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union's demands are recorded in the conciliation report as the 13 points summarised above, notably the 15 USD allowance, correct holiday and Sunday wages, and payment of meal allowances with overtime [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4]. No representative appeared at the hearing and no documentary evidence was filed by the workers' side [Para 37][Para 43].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ុង រ៉ុង ហែន ប៊ែក ឯ.ក submitted its internal transfer-of-authority letter, wage payment schedules and termination and resignation letters for the complainants, and reported that those complainants had already left and been paid, so they would not attend [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 31].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the claim as abandoned and ordered the termination of the arbitration proceedings in Case No. 032/21 [Para 79].
The panel applied Article 18 and Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBy of 21 April 2004, which require parties to appear and let the panel continue or close the file where a duly invited party stays away, together with Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules [Para 44][Para 46][Para 47]. It distilled three conditions from earlier awards — proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of a valid excuse — and found each satisfied: the second invitation of 12 November 2021 fixed the hearing for 23 November 2021 and the parties acknowledged receipt by telephone and Telegram, the claimant did not attend any of the four stages, and no excuse was given [Para 49][Para 53][Para 55][Para 59][Para 61][Para 63]. Because the claimant bore the burden of giving reasons and evidence for its demands and gave none, the panel held the claim abandoned and declined to continue [Para 69][Para 71][Para 73][Para 74].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant party has been notified of the hearing by a written invitation acknowledged by telephone or Telegram, then stays away without valid reason and files no evidence, the panel may treat the demand as abandoned and close the case by award under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBy and Rule 4.7, after expressly verifying the three conditions of notification, non-appearance and lack of excuse [Para 46][Para 47][Para 73].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that both claimant and respondent normally carry a duty to appear and to produce the documents needed for a decision, and that an absent claimant cannot be taken to have proved its demands [Para 68][Para 69].
