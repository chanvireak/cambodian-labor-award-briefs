# Award No. 005/21 — Sofitel Angkor Phokeethra Golf & Spa Resort (Arbitration Council, 15 December 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី; ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន; គង់ ផល្លៈ presiding) · Doc ID 140847

## 1. Facts
The employer is សណ្ឋាគារ សូហ្វី តែ ល អង្គរ ភូគីត្រា ហ្គោហ្វ និង ស្ប៉ារី ហ្សូត (Sofitel Angkor Phokeethra Golf & Spa Resort), a Siem Reap hotel employing 189 workers [Para 87]. The claimant is សហជីព និយោជិត សណ្ឋាគារ សូហ្វី តែ ល អង្គរ, the hotel's employee union [Para 1]. Conciliation at the Siem Reap provincial labour department ended without agreement, and report No. 033 M.K.B of 18 February 2021 listed four unsettled demands of which the individual-termination issue was referred elsewhere [Para 13][Para 18]. Three demands reached the panel [Para 19]; the first, on lawful termination procedure, was settled by the parties' agreement at the hearing [Para 31], leaving two issues for decision [Para 32].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must stop assigning hotel workers to serve at locations where no service charge (ប្រាក់ធនានុគ្រោះ) is collected? [Para 92]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must implement the collective bargaining agreement by paying the 13th-month salary, the Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben and Water Festival bonuses, the annual staff party, the annual excursion and the annual base-wage increase that it withheld in 2020? [Para 126]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Article 6(b) of the collective agreement forbids moving a worker to another location, so one-sided assignment to the golf course without the worker's consent breaches the agreement [Para 99]; the employer's duty to pay the 13th-month salary and festival benefits under Articles 9(c), 8(f), 13(j), 8(b.4) and 9(ng) survives the ACCOR subsidy, which does not discharge it [Para 138][Para 140].
**Employer (respondent):** Clause 3 of each employment contract reserves the right to change the workplace according to hotel needs [Para 103]; Article 6 addresses only permanent transfer, not occasional assignment during working hours [Para 104]. COVID-19 removed all revenue and the hotel lost 10,157,311,885.00 riel, so payment was impossible [Para 141][Para 152].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand in issue 2 was dismissed [Para 292]; on issue 3 the employer was ordered to pay the 13th-month salary for 2020 to workers who are members of the union [Para 293], while the claims for festival bonuses, the annual party, the annual excursion and the annual base-wage increase were dismissed [Para 294].

The panel first excluded the employer's foreign-language invoices and work schedules, because Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires Khmer as the language of proceedings and untranslated exhibits are not considered [Para 169][Para 170]. On the agreement itself, the panel found the collective agreement of 18 December 2019 was never registered at the Ministry of Labour, so under Article 73 of the Law on Trade Unions of 17 May 2016 it did not acquire legal effect over all staff and operates only as an ordinary contract between its signatories [Para 182][Para 187][Para 203].

On issue 2, Article 6 of the agreement was read as governing permanent change of work or transfer to another region, not ad hoc assignment during working hours [Para 218]. Because the Labour Law of 1997, Article 2, gives the employer managerial authority exercised lawfully and reasonably, and because the hotel redistributed all service charge collected equally among staff, the rotation to the golf course was lawful and the demand was dismissed [Para 205][Para 223][Para 225].

On the 13th-month salary, Article 9(c) of the agreement obliged payment each 31 December [Para 232]. The panel accepted that Civil Code Articles 389, 392 and 415 discharge a debtor for impossibility without fault [Para 241][Para 246], but held the single accounting sheet too unclear to prove the alleged loss [Para 255][Para 256], so the obligation stood [Para 258]. The festival bonuses failed because Article 8(f) merely asks the company to "strive to improve" the gifts and creates no enforceable duty [Para 261]. The annual party and excursion did create duties under Articles 8(b.4) and 13(h) [Para 268], but government COVID-19 prohibitions on gatherings made performance impossible without fault, excusing the employer [Para 279][Para 280]. The base-wage increase was refused because Article 9(ng) ties it to business results and individual appraisal, a prerogative the panel would not displace [Para 287][Para 289].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective agreement never registered with the Ministry of Labour under Article 73 of the Law on Trade Unions 2016 binds only its signatories as an ordinary contract, and so cannot ground a demand by non-member workers [Para 187][Para 204]. Where a contractual benefit is withheld for alleged economic impossibility, discharge under Civil Code Article 415 requires proof of the impossibility and absence of fault; an unclear accounting sheet does not meet that burden [Para 241][Para 255].

**Obiter dicta:** Untranslated foreign-language exhibits are disregarded under Article 23 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY even where they would otherwise support the tendering party [Para 170].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case number is printed three ways: 005/21 in the header, "4005/21" in one exhibit list and "4521-1" in another [Para 1][Para 65][Para 67]. The 2020 net loss appears as 10,157,311,885.00 riel in the defence, 10,157,311,855 riel in the accounting sheet and 10,167,311,585 riel in the annexed statement [Para 152][Para 153][Para 299]. The panel's constitution date reads "23 February 2019 2021" [Para 20]. The employer's name is spelled several ways across the face of the award, including a variant reading ស្ប៉ាវឺ ហ្សូត [Para 54]. Long stretches of the reasoning and the evidence lists are corrupted by interspersed Latin fragments and stray characters, and one exhibit bundle is attributed to three individuals without stated roles while another handwritten note carries no author at all [Para 42][Para 43][Para 101].
