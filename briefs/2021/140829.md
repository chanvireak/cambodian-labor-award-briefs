# Award No. 022/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូហ្វ៊ូ ម៉ា២ ខ សធ្យម (ខេមបូឌា) / Nhu Fou Ma 2 Kh Sathym (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 2 September 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · order under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140829

## 1. Facts
The workers' representatives of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូហ្វ៊ូ ម៉ា២ ខ សធ្យម (ខេមបូឌា) filed a collective dispute on 9 July 2021 asking the company to improve 20 points of working conditions [Para 27]. Conciliation on 29 July 2021 failed because the employer did not attend [Para 28], and the Department of Labour Disputes' report No. 533 K.B/AK/VK of 6 August 2021 sent the 19 unresolved points to the Council [Para 31]. Demand 1, reinstatement of the union president with back wages, had already been withdrawn so that it could be brought separately before the labour inspector under Article 43 of the Trade Union Law [Para 30]. The 19 remaining demands ranged from an end to union discrimination and unlawful dismissals [Para 1] to a company doctor, a canteen, drinking water, an NSSF card for every worker, a nursing room, voluntary-only overtime and a 1,000 riel increase in the meal allowance [Para 16].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel should continue the arbitration when the claimant workers and their representatives failed to appear at the hearing? [Para 52]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the three conditions for terminating proceedings in a party's absence — proper notice, non-appearance, and no valid excuse — were satisfied? [Para 57]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the original claimants had abandoned their claims, so that the arbitration could be closed by order? [Para 78]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the collective workers of the company demanded that the company stop discriminating against the union and stop dismissing workers and union representatives unlawfully [Para 1], together with the nineteen further conditions-improvement points carried forward from conciliation [Para 5]. Their federation official told the secretariat by Telegram that all the worker representatives had been dismissed, were working at a new company, and could not obtain leave to attend [Para 34].
**Employer (respondent):** the company's two administration staff appeared only to state, without representing the employer in the case, that the workers had sued under the wrong company name [Para 36]. The employer had earlier refused to join conciliation [Para 45] and filed corporate documents, an internal-rules inspection record and a transfer-of-authority letter dated 24 August 2021 [Para 37][Para 39].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 022/21, treating the claimants' claims as abandoned, and ordered no relief on any of the nineteen demands [Para 82][Para 83].
Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 lets the panel either continue in the absence of a party or close the proceedings by order, provided the party was properly notified [Para 53]. Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules repeats that choice and adds the requirement of notice of date, time and place [Para 54]. Following a line of earlier awards beginning with No. 30/05-Mouria and including No. 132/08-GHG and No. 270/13-8 Star Sport Wear, the panel reduced the test to three conditions [Para 58]. On condition one, the secretariat issued an invitation letter on 18 August 2021 fixing the hearing for 25 August 2021 and also telephoned two worker representatives [Para 59][Para 61]; proper notice was therefore proved [Para 62]. On condition two, no worker or representative was present at the hearing [Para 65]. On condition three, the excuse relayed by telephone and Telegram — dismissal followed by new employment — was not a valid reason, and no written postponement request had been filed in time [Para 68][Para 69]. The panel accordingly held that the claimants had abandoned their claims, that it need not continue the arbitration, and closed the case by order [Para 78][Para 79][Para 81].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant in a collective dispute has been properly notified of the hearing date, time and place, does not appear, and files no timely postponement request on valid grounds, the panel may treat the claims as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by order under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY without examining the merits [Para 72][Para 81]. A claimant's duty to appear and to produce reasons and evidence is a condition of having its demands decided, so non-appearance forfeits the claim rather than suspending it [Para 74][Para 76].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the respondent likewise bears a duty to appear and to answer the claim with reasons and evidence, and that the employer's assertion of a wrong company name was not relied on for the outcome [Para 36][Para 75].
