# Award No. 011/21 — Thien Sung Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ធៀន ស៊ុង ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 18 October 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Mar Somborna (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed, dissenting), Pen Bun Chha (president) · Doc ID 140837

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ធៀន ស៊ុង ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី is a garment-sewing company in Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh with 1,097 workers [Para 69]. The most representative union, សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ធៀន ស៊ុង ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី, filed a notice of non-violent strike on 7 June 2021 raising four demands, conciliation failed on 17 June 2021, and all four points came to the Council [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]. At the hearing the parties settled demands 3 and 4 and merged demands 1 and 2 into a single issue [Para 29]. The disputed act is the employer's deduction of 1.50 USD from each non-member worker in July 2020 as a one-off agency service fee under clause 13(b) of the collective bargaining agreement of 29 May 2020 [Para 75][Para 113].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 1 and 2) Whether the employer must deduct 50 per cent of every benefit a non-member worker receives under the collective agreement, month by month and on each payment, to remit an agency service fee to the union, or whether the fee is payable once only and the July 2020 deduction of 1.50 USD was lawful? [Para 72][Para 105]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ធៀន ស៊ុង ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី argued that clause 13(b) of the collective agreement applies to each benefit received, that Article 26(2) of the Trade Union Law 2016 fixes no period for the fee, and that the agreement runs for three years, so a single 36 USD payment could not be right; it also demanded the fees withheld since 29 May 2020 be paid over [Para 79][Para 83][Para 103].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ធៀន ស៊ុង ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី replied that the fee is paid once only, that workers had already paid it in July 2020, and that deduction for a second time would be unlawful; it also relied on the union's own letter refusing deduction for overtime pay [Para 77][Para 93][Para 98].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the union's claim that the employer deduct 50 per cent of benefits month by month and pay over agency fees allegedly withheld since 29 May 2020 [Para 118].
The panel read point 2 of Article 26 of the Trade Union Law 2016 together with point 1 of clause 7 of Prakas No. 300 K.B/Pr.K.K.B.K of 2 July 2018: the most representative union may set a single reasonable agency fee that does not exceed its own members' monthly dues, and that fee "is paid once only" [Para 105][Para 107][Para 106]. It followed the reasoning in Award 059/20 (Cambodia Airports Services, CAMS), which held that only the most representative union may negotiate such a fee, that it cannot exceed members' monthly dues, and that it is payable once when the agreement starts to apply [Para 109][Para 110].
Applying that to clause 13(b) of the collective agreement, which fixes the fee at 50 per cent of benefits received and contains no words "on each occasion", the panel held the employer's duty was to deduct once only, when the agreement began to apply [Para 111][Para 145]. It found on the parties' own admission that the employer had deducted 1.50 USD in July 2020, equal to 50 per cent of the regular attendance bonus and travel allowance then paid, and remitted it, so the obligation was discharged [Para 113][Para 114][Para 116].
The panel also rejected the view that the fee might reach beyond union dues and other benefits: the ceiling is the member's monthly dues, and the recorded figure of 1 USD monthly dues against a 36 USD three-year total shows the scale [Para 94][Para 78][Para 83]. Arbitrator Sin Kim Sean, appointed by the workers, attached a dissenting opinion under Article 37 of Prakas No. 099 SKBy of 21 April 2004, arguing that the majority failed to explain its rejection of each head of claim and that Article 26(2) and Prakas No. 300 clause 7(1) allow a claim for benefits not yet implemented; the majority's order stands, and the award is binding and final at once [Para 122][Para 150][Para 119].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An agency service fee negotiated under point 2 of Article 26 of the Trade Union Law 2016 and point 1 of clause 7 of Prakas No. 300 K.B/Pr.K.K.B.K is capped at the union's own monthly dues and is payable once only, at the moment the collective agreement begins to apply; a clause fixing the fee at 50 per cent of benefits received therefore authorises one deduction, not a recurring monthly deduction on every benefit, and a second deduction would exceed the statutory ceiling [Para 110][Para 112][Para 115].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that members of the most representative union pay 1 USD monthly dues, while non-members would pay the agency fee once, and it declined to treat that difference as unfair because the statute fixes the terms [Para 78][Para 89]. It also observed that if the law later required monthly deduction the employer would comply, since the fee is the workers' money and the employer is only the conduit [Para 96].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR mangles several percentages into invented figures: the summary of the decision prints the claim as "1950 per cent" where the operative order says 50 per cent, and one passage on monthly deduction prints "3500 per cent" [Para 117][Para 118][Para 86]. References to the year of the collective agreement are truncated to "year 20" throughout, so 2020 is inferred from the date of 29 May and the 27 July registration given elsewhere [Para 73][Para 111]. The panel's constitution date is printed as "25 June 2023 2021" [Para 19]. The Prakas number appears both as 300 and once as 303 in the same quotation [Para 81][Para 107]. The company name is rendered variously (ខូលិធិឌី, ខូអិលធិឌី, ធៀន ស៊ុន) and the union's certificate of most-representative status is dated only "9 January year 20" [Para 66][Para 70].
