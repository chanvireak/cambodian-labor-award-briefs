# Award No. 028/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហឹរ៉ាយហ្សិន អៅដរ (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 16 December 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (យូ សួន ទី; អាន វីរៈ; គង់ ផល្លៈ presiding) · Doc ID 140848

## 1. Facts
The employer is ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហឹរ៉ាយហ្សិន អៅដរ (ខេមបូឌា), a garment and bag factory at Longvek, Kampong Tralach, Kampong Chhnang, employing 9,047 workers of whom 8,526 are women [Para 65]. The claimants are the worker representatives of that factory, acting with the union សហជីព សិទ្ធិ ប្រយោជន៍ កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ហឹរ៉ាយហ្សិន អៅដរ (ខេមបូឌា) [Para 1]. Seven demands went to conciliation, which failed by report No. 675 K.B/AK/VK of 1 October 2021 [Para 16][Para 19]. One demand, overtime pay during first-dose vaccination, was referred to the labour inspector, leaving six [Para 21][Para 22]; two were then settled at the hearing, leaving five for decision [Para 30][Para 31]. The dispute arose during a COVID-19 outbreak in the factory, when the provincial administration restricted communal eating [Para 94][Para 100].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must issue warnings to workers who took emergency leave under Article 10 of the internal rules rather than under Article 17 of the collective agreement? [Para 68][Para 141]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must let workers leave the factory premises to eat lunch outside during the working day? [Para 174]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must allow workers to exit through the front gate at lunchtime? [Para 190]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must build a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) and provide a comfortable breastfeeding room staffed by a carer? [Para 203][Para 204]
5. (Issue 6) Whether a worker with an urgent family matter may seek permission only from the team leader to come out to the factory gate? [Para 219][Para 222]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The employer warns workers who take emergency leave without following the internal rules, and the collective agreement signed with another union cannot be applied to their members [Para 72][Para 74]; being barred from eating outside the gate is an unlawful restriction, since other nearby factories allow it [Para 95][Para 96]; the breastfeeding room has no hygiene standard and no carer [Para 122].
**Employer (respondent):** Warnings follow Article 17 of collective agreement No. 010 K.B/AK/VK and Article 10 of the internal rules [Para 75][Para 78]; the ban on eating outside implements instruction No. 147 S.N.N of the Kampong Chhnang administration, which has no end date [Para 100][Para 182]; the nursery is under study and the breastfeeding room was inspected without criticism [Para 124][Para 126].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demands in issues 1, 3, 4 and 6 were dismissed [Para 228][Para 229][Para 230][Para 233]; on issue 5 the employer was ordered to build or provide a nursery for workers with small children [Para 231], while the demand for a comfortable breastfeeding room with a carer was dismissed [Para 232].

The panel first held the collective agreement enforceable: the union that signed it is the most representative union under Article 54 of the Law on Trade Unions 2016, and the agreement was registered on 18 February 2021 and runs to 2023, so its Article 4 scope binds the parties [Para 156][Para 169][Para 172]. Workers in this case fall outside the Article 4 exclusions [Para 171].

On issue 1, Article 10 of the internal rules only classifies misconduct and disciplinary penalties and says nothing about warnings for emergency leave taken by three or more workers in one day, so it could not be applied [Para 151][Para 152]. Article 17 of the collective agreement expressly governs that situation and the panel found the employer had followed it, hence dismissal [Para 153][Para 173].

On issue 3, the panel applied Article 4 of the Law on Measures against COVID-19 of 2021 and Article 11 of Sub-Decree No. 57 ANKr.BK [Para 174][Para 176], together with instruction No. 147 S.N.N of 26 May 2021 and letter No. 880/21 of 4 June 2021 [Para 179][Para 182]. Because an employer cannot act contrary to a provincial administrative instruction, the lunch restriction was upheld [Para 188][Para 189].

On issue 4, Article 2 of the Labour Law of 1997 and Article 9 of the internal rules left the choice of gate to the employer's management right, exercised lawfully and without loss to any right of the workers [Para 194][Para 197][Para 200][Para 201].

On issue 5, Article 186 of the Labour Law requires an establishment employing at least 100 women to provide a breastfeeding room and a nursery [Para 204], and where a nursery is impossible, the employer must pay for outside care [Para 205]. With 8,526 women employed and no nursery in place, the panel ordered one [Para 210][Para 217]. The further demand for a staffed, comfortable room failed for want of evidence and because Article 186 sets no such standard [Para 215][Para 216].

On issue 6, Article 7(a) of the collective agreement requires written permission from the administration before leaving during working hours, and the panel found no loss of rights in that rule [Para 222][Para 226][Para 227].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer's internal rules and a registered collective agreement are applied in that order of specificity: where the internal rules do not address a situation, the collective agreement's procedure governs, and a demand that the employer follow the internal rule instead fails even though the agreement binds [Para 152][Para 153]. A management measure that implements a live provincial administrative instruction on epidemic control cannot be found unlawful while that instruction remains in force [Para 188].

**Obiter dicta:** The duty under Article 186 of the Labour Law to provide a nursery is satisfied by building one; the statute prescribes no standard of comfort or staffing for the breastfeeding room [Para 216].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Issue 4 is recorded as settled by agreement at the hearing but is then listed among the issues remaining for decision and is dismissed in the operative section [Para 30][Para 31][Para 230]. The year of collective agreement No. 010 K.B/AK/VK is truncated to "18 February 20" wherever it appears, and the panel's constitution date reads "18 October 2019 2021" [Para 3][Para 23]. The employer's name is spelled several ways across the award, including ហឺរ៉ាយ ហ្ស៊ិន and ហើរ៉ាយហ្សិន [Para 36][Para 66]. The workforce figure is restated in garbled form where only the female count survives intact [Para 210]. Several passages of the reasoning and the evidence lists carry stray Latin fragments and duplicated clauses [Para 115][Para 224].
