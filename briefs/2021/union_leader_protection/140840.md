# Award No. 024/21 — Han Tong 2 (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាន តុង ២ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council of Cambodia, 1 October 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed, dissenting on Issue 8), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 140840

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាន តុង ២ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី sews bags at Village Ta Ngao, Teuk L'ak, Kong Pisey, Kampong Speu, and employs 400 workers [Para 58]. The claimant is សហជីព បេះដូង កម្មករ ខ្មែរ, registered on 6 July 2021 [Para 46]. Of eleven demands filed on 28 July 2021, conciliation on 12 August 2021 settled six (issues 2, 6, 7, the meal and travel part of 8, 10 and 11), leaving six for decision (issues 1, 3, 4, 5, the attendance-bonus part of 8, and 9) [Para 24][Para 25][Para 32][Para 33]. The disputed acts are the employer's refusal to accept the union's notice and registration, the dismissal of the union president on 26 July 2021 and of its vice-president on 29 July 2021, one month of maternity leave on half pay, the absence of a nurse, doctor and medicines, the deduction of the attendance bonus, and heat in the work building [Para 65][Para 66][Para 169].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer discriminated against សហជីព បេះដូង កម្មករ ខ្មែរ and its members by refusing the union's notice and dismissing its leaders? [Para 108]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the Council has jurisdiction over the demand that every worker be enrolled in the National Social Security Fund? [Para 133]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must grant 90 days' maternity leave with 50 per cent of wages including allowances, paid in one sum before the leave begins? [Para 142]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must provide sufficient medicines and a duty nurse and doctor for every working hour, under Article 238 and Article 242 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 330? [Para 173]
5. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must preserve the regular attendance bonus when a worker takes other lawful leave? [Para 193]
6. (Issue 9) Whether the employer must take all means to reduce the heat in the workers' building to an acceptable level? [Para 246]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សហជីព បេះដូង កម្មករ ខ្មែរ produced its notice of 6 March 2021, an express-mail envelope marked refused, and the Ministry's letter of 19 August 2021 ordering reinstatement, and argued that the administration told workers not to join and threatened dismissal when they thumbprinted membership forms [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78][Para 69]. It claimed the company gives one month of maternity leave at 50 per cent of one month's wage only, and keeps no nurse, no sick bay and no medicines [Para 84][Para 87].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាន តុង ២ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី replied that it never knew of the union and had no documents for it, that the two dismissals were individual matters for poor performance and unrelated to union activity, and that it does grant three months' maternity leave but cannot afford further benefits [Para 71][Para 74][Para 86]. It said it has medicines but no duty doctor, that it sends accident cases to a partner hospital, and that the building is low and narrow so it cannot cool it [Para 92][Para 93][Para 107].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to stop discriminating against the union and its members, to grant 90 days' maternity leave with half wages including allowances paid before the leave, to appoint one nurse and a doctor present at least two hours and to stock sufficient medicines under Prakas No. 330, and to take all means to reduce the heat; it declined to consider the Fund demand for want of jurisdiction and dismissed the attendance-bonus claim [Para 259][Para 260][Para 261][Para 263][Para 264][Para 265][Para 266].
On issue 1 the panel applied Article 12 and Article 279 of the Labour Law of 1997 and Article 62 of the Trade Union Law 2016, which forbid an employer to use union membership or union office as a ground for hiring, discipline or termination, and followed Award 182/16 in holding that Article 62 adds to rather than contradicts Articles 12 and 279 [Para 108][Para 109][Para 112][Para 114]. It found the union's notice refused, the express envelope marked undelivered, and the Ministry's reinstatement letter itself refused, while the employer disputed none of the documents, and so accepted the discrimination claim [Para 128][Para 129][Para 130][Para 131].
On issue 3 the panel held that Article 91 of the Social Security Law 2019 sends disputes over the application of the Fund's rules to the Fund's own dispute commission and then to the social security regulator before any court, and that a failure to enrol workers is such a dispute, so it declined to consider it [Para 133][Para 136][Para 138][Para 140]. On issue 4 it applied point 1 of Article 182 and Article 183 of the Labour Law, which give 90 days' maternity leave and half pay including allowances to women with one year of service, plus point 3 of Article 115 on payment before a rest day, and Article 103 defining wages to include allowances; it followed Award 216/14 and found the employer's practice of one month on half pay unlawful [Para 142][Para 143][Para 148][Para 150][Para 157][Para 169].
On issue 5 it applied Article 238 and Article 242 of the Labour Law and Article 3 of Prakas No. 330 SKBy of 6 December 2000, which for 301 to 600 workers require one duty nurse and a doctor for two hours, and ordered a nurse and a doctor present two hours plus sufficient medicines [Para 173][Para 179][Para 183][Para 184][Para 192]. On issue 8 it adopted the Full Bench decision of 3 October 2017: the attendance bonus must be paid at 100 per cent where absence falls on lawful, contractual or legally-required leave, and the employer retains discretion for other leave; the claim for a general preservation was therefore refused, and Arbitrator An Non dissented, reading the order as too broadly worded [Para 198][Para 199][Para 240][Para 244][Para 272]. On issue 9 it applied Article 229 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 3 of Prakas No. 147 on heat, and held the employer had not exhausted the means to make the workplace acceptable [Para 246][Para 249][Para 256][Para 258].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that refuses a registered union's notice, refuses a Ministry letter ordering reinstatement of its leaders and imposes no discipline on managers who threaten them has discriminated on the ground of union membership, contrary to Article 12 and Article 279 of the Labour Law and Article 62 of the Trade Union Law 2016, and must cease; and the statutory attendance bonus must be preserved only where the absence is lawful, contractual or a legal duty, never for leave the employer merely permits [Para 112][Para 240].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that a doctor's presence is measured against the 301-600 worker band of Prakas No. 330, and that referral to a partner hospital does not displace the duty to keep a sick bay [Para 183][Para 93]. It noted that the union's registration certificate of 6 July 2021 was issued by the Ministry and that the employer's ignorance of it could not be credited [Para 47][Para 129].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR prints the number of demands as "1199 points" in the procedural recital where the file elsewhere has eleven, and garbles several statutory references, including a page-number fragment inside the citation to Article 54 and an "Article 145" intrusion in the list of amended articles [Para 24][Para 135]. The company name is rendered in at least six variants (ហាន តុង ២, ហុន តុង ២, AKP ហាន តុង ២) and the union's registration certificate is given two different numbers, 105211015 and 166/21 [Para 38][Para 46][Para 37]. The award date is printed as 1 October 2021 in the header and again at the foot, but the recital extending the deadline prints "14 September 2019 2021" [Para 36]. Individual workers, union officers and managers are named in the source and are reduced to roles here.
