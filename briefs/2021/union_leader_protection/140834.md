# Award No. 012/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ណាហ្គាវើល ដ៏ លីមីធីត (NagaWorld Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 10 September 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · order under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: យូ សួន ទី (employer-appointed), អាន វីរៈ (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លា (president) · Doc ID 140834

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ណាហ្គាវើល ដ៏ លីមីធីត runs a hotel and casino in Khan Chamkarmon, Phnom Penh, in the tourism sector [Para 450]. The workers' representatives filed five demands on 22 January 2021 [Para 50]; one was settled at conciliation and four were referred to the Council by report No. 450 K.B/AK/VK of 2 July 2021 [Para 46][Para 51]. The panel held two hearings, on 15 July and 12 August 2021 [Para 48][Para 49], and merged Issues 1 and 2 [Para 59]. The company had suspended operations from 1 March 2021 because of COVID-19 and was restructuring 373 posts into a collective-dismissal procedure [Para 8][Para 340]. The surviving claims were about that plan, about the calculation of severance for staff let go at the end of 2020 and early 2021, and about seniority indemnity for serving staff [Para 471][Para 472][Para 473].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 1 and 2) Whether the employer had to stop the staff-reduction plan and the meetings at which staff were invited to accept termination packages, and let those who did not resign voluntarily return to work when operations resumed? [Para 342]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer had to recalculate severance — seniority indemnity, mental-health indemnity, pre-2019 back indemnity, unused annual leave and notice indemnity — correctly for staff whose contracts ended in late 2020 and early 2021? [Para 348]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer had to resume paying seniority indemnity, and recalculate it, for staff still serving? [Para 425][Para 426]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត ក្នុង ការដោះស្រាយ វិវាទ ការងារ រួម នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ណាហ្គាវើល ដ៏ លីមីធីត, supported by សហជីព ទ្រង់ សិទ្ធិ ការងារ បុគ្គលិក កម្មករ ខ្មែរ, argued that the company had made a net profit above 102 million USD in 2020 [Para 2], that staff had already accepted shift rotation and shorter hours [Para 3], that more than 380 staff refused the package, and that every union leader, activist and employee delegate was on the cut list, contrary to ILO Convention C87 [Para 6][Para 7]. It also argued that severance was computed on the garment-sector Instruction No. 057 instead of Article 110-new [Para 28].
**Employer (respondent):** business was suspended from 1 March 2021 and tourism revenues fell 50% in 2020, so some roles were no longer needed [Para 8][Para 335]; it consulted the employee delegates and the union and notified the labour inspector [Para 13]; meetings with listed staff were voluntary and no one was forced to leave [Para 20][Para 21]; severance was paid in full on seven heads [Para 31], and pre-2019 back indemnity remains postponed for non-garment enterprises [Para 42].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand to stop the reduction plan was refused; the employer was ordered to recalculate severance, mental-health indemnity, pre-2019 back indemnity, annual-leave compensation and notice indemnity for staff dismissed at the end of 2020 and in early 2021, and to continue paying the 2020 and 2021 seniority indemnity to serving staff; the demand to resume the pre-2019 back indemnity now was refused [Para 471][Para 472][Para 473][Para 474].
On Issues 1 and 2 the panel held Article 95 permits collective dismissal for a genuine reduction of the establishment's activity or internal reorganisation [Para 332][Para 338], subject to ranking staff by professional merit, seniority and family burden and notifying the labour inspector [Para 339]. Since the 373-worker plan was still under the labour inspector's review, the panel could not yet examine its substance [Para 346], and refused the claim [Para 347].
On Issue 3, Article 110-new requires allowances and bonuses to be included in severance, at the average of the 12 months preceding departure [Para 366][Para 367]. Applying Instruction No. 058/19 [Para 372], the panel found the employer's two-step method wrong on five heads: it used basic wage rather than total average earnings for severance [Para 381], mis-based the mental-health indemnity [Para 384], mis-based the pre-2019 back indemnity [Para 394][Para 398], excluded allowances from the annual-leave compensation [Para 405][Para 414], and used basic wage for the notice indemnity [Para 420][Para 423].
On Issue 4 the panel found the employer had already resumed the 2020 and 2021 indemnity on 1 July 2021 through its staff portal [Para 427], and ordered that it continue [Para 429]. It refused the pre-2019 back indemnity for now, because Instruction No. 042/19 postpones that payment for non-garment enterprises to December 2021 and the company is a hotel and casino in tourism [Para 444][Para 451][Para 453]. It also refused the challenge to the calculation method under Instruction No. 058/19 for want of supporting facts [Para 465][Para 466].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective-dismissal plan under Article 95 is an exercise of management prerogative where the reduction is genuine, but its legality is supervised by the labour inspector; while the plan is pending before that inspector the panel will not decide its substance and the demand to stop it fails [Para 338][Para 346]. Severance and the related indemnities must be computed on the average of wages plus allowances and bonuses over the 12 months in which the worker actually worked, excluding months not worked, so a computation on basic wage alone understates the entitlement [Para 367][Para 461]. For a non-garment enterprise the pre-2019 back indemnity is not payable before December 2021 [Para 451][Para 452].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that neither the employer's suspension of business nor the workers' refusal of the package was itself unlawful, since the lockdown was imposed by public authority [Para 458][Para 459].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Year figures are repeatedly truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", so 2020 and 2021 are indistinguishable in the recitals [Para 3][Para 36]. The 2020 net profit is printed as "102 million USD" in one place and "40 102 million USD" in another [Para 2][Para 14]. Two meeting dates are printed as 2012 instead of 2021 [Para 87][Para 88], the cut list is described as 373 staff in the findings and as more than 380 in the demands [Para 6][Para 340], and Instruction No. 057/19 and No. 058/19 are cited interchangeably for the calculation method [Para 364][Para 372]. The signature block omits the president named on the face of the award [Para 478].
