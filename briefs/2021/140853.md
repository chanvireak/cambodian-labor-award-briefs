# Award No. 042/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តាឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល ម៉េនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង ខូអិលធីឌី (Stallion Apparel Manufacturing Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 27 January 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three — អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន បុន ឈា (president) · Doc ID 140853

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តាឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល ម៉េនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង ខូអិលធីឌី (Stallion Apparel Manufacturing Co., Ltd.) cuts and sews garments in Kandal province and employed 4,567 workers. [Para 49] On 27 November 2021 the workers' representative filed 12 demands; conciliation on 6 December 2021 settled eight and left four in dispute. [Para 19][Para 20] Conciliation report No. 831 dated 23 December 2021 reached the Secretariat on 27 December 2021. [Para 16] At the hearing of 11 January 2022 the parties settled issues 1 and 3 as well, so the panel decided only issues 2 and 4. [Para 29][Para 30] Both sides acknowledged that the regular attendance bonus stood at 10 USD a month. [Para 53]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must cut the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់ព្រីម) only pro rata, rather than cancel it in full, when a worker arrives late or is absent? [Para 51]
2. (Issue 4) Whether the workers may require the employer to remove the security supervisor in charge of building 4? [Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the bonus should be deducted pro rata because Stallion and Crystal Martin (building 8) are one group under one logo and Crystal Martin never cut the bonus for unpaid leave; the security supervisor walked the factory photographing workers, used improper language, pulled workers aside and took 5 to 10 USD from them when they came to collect their pay. [Para 56][Para 64][Para 65]
**Employer (respondent):** the bank the company pays through carries no small dollar notes, so amounts under 100 USD are paid in riel at the exchange rate; the bonus is earned only by regular attendance and the company has already been generous over annual, special and sick leave; the security supervisor photographs breaches on the employer's instruction and cannot be removed. [Para 5][Para 58][Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining demands rejected, the order being non-binding and becoming final 8 days after notification if no objection is filed. [Para 161][Para 164]

On issue 2 the panel found that Notice No. 230 K.B/S.J.N of 25 July 2012, which sets a minimum 10 USD monthly bonus for regular attendance, says nothing about how the bonus is granted or withheld. [Para 72][Para 73] It followed the Full Bench decision of 3 October 2017: the bonus is paid at 100 per cent where the worker attends every working day, counting statutory, contractual and legally mandated leave days as attendance, and is not payable for absence beyond those. [Para 76][Para 77] Reading Articles 145, 161, 166, 71 and 182 of the Labour Law with Prakas No. 267 on special leave, the panel held that lateness beyond the 30 minutes a month the company already tolerates falls outside the protected categories, so the employer keeps its discretion. [Para 80][Para 84][Para 120][Para 121]

On issue 4 the panel applied Article 65(1) of the Labour Law and Articles 664 and 311 of the Civil Code, under which only the parties to a contract may vary or dissolve it. [Para 131][Para 133][Para 134] The security supervisor is supplied by an outside security firm under a service contract, so the workers are third parties to that contract and cannot demand his removal. [Para 140][Para 141] Article 2 of the Labour Law leaves staffing to the employer, and following Award No. 15/15 and Award No. 08/18 the panel declined to order a change of personnel, finding no evidence that the supervisor was dangerous. [Para 143][Para 137][Para 158]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Only absence on statutory, contractual or legally mandated leave counts as attendance for an attendance bonus; any other lateness or absence, however good the reason, lets the employer withhold the bonus, because a bonus may not be used to suppress the exercise of a legal right yet need not subsidise leave outside those categories. [Para 94][Para 119][Para 121]

A worker cannot obtain an order removing an employee of another company who works on site under a service contract, since the worker is a third party to that contract and staffing is the employer's prerogative absent proof that the person is dangerous. [Para 136][Para 149][Para 158]

**Obiter dicta:** the parties remain free to extend leave by agreement, and the security supervisor had worked at the company for two years. [Para 87][Para 70]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The header names the worker-appointed arbitrator អន ណន while the signature block prints អន លាន, one of them lost to OCR. [Para 1][Para 165] The company name appears in several OCR variants (អឹភែរឹល, អឹកែរឹល, ស្តារ ឡៃ ថ៍), which obscures whether the entity sued is the apparel or a related group company. [Para 1][Para 35] The panel formation date is printed "២៩ ខែធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០២១" with two years in sequence. [Para 22] The citation of Article 538 of the Penal Code prints its fine range in descending order, "១០០,០០០ រៀល ទៅ ១,០០០ រៀល". [Para 105] The security supervisor is identified throughout only by a nickname and the administrative officer named in the settled issue 3 only by a personal name; neither is reproduced here.
