# Award No. 063/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ (Arbitration Council, 5 February 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer, អន ណន appointed by the workers, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140821

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ, Ken Sport Shoes, manufactures footwear at Chamkar Svay village, Sethey commune, Samakki Mean Chey district, Kampong Chhnang, and employed 8,808 workers. [Para 1] [Para 65] Workers filed 12 demands on 12 November 2020; conciliation on 30 November 2020 settled one, and after removing one non-collective point and one already applied the nine remaining points came to the Council on 4 December 2020. [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 27] At the hearing of 8 January 2021 the parties agreed demand 2, which the panel recorded in the award, the workers withdrew demand 9 and part of demand 1, leaving seven issues. [Para 24] [Para 33] [Para 34] [Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must restore four card-scanning machines in each building and four at the factory gate? [Para 69] [Para 144]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer may deduct wages, the attendance bonus and the function allowance from workers who arrive up to 15 minutes late because of traffic or a broken vehicle? [Para 94] [Para 163]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must keep a vehicle on standby to carry workers who fall ill or faint at work? [Para 106] [Para 190]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must pay US$25 a month for one year in place of the one-hour breastfeeding break? [Para 110] [Para 200]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must restore the annual leave it deducted on 3 November 2020? [Para 115] [Para 209]
6. (Issue 7) Whether the employer must pay out the unused annual leave of workers still in service? [Para 129] [Para 229]
7. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must issue early-leave cards to ten workers with travel difficulties? [Para 136] [Para 241]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by five unions including សហជីព សេរី ស្ត្រី កាត់ដេរ and សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** that the employer cut card machines from four to three in each building, so workers queue and are marked late; that it deducts wages, the attendance bonus and the function allowance for lateness the traffic caused; that no standby vehicle exists for workers who faint; that the breastfeeding hour is unpaid in practice; and that annual leave was deducted for 3 November 2020 and unused leave is written off. [Para 69] [Para 74] [Para 94] [Para 110] [Para 115] [Para 132] A total of 278 workers thumbprinted the claim. [Para 68]
**Employer (respondent):** that card machines were moved into the buildings to shorten queues and that Adidas compliance found no problem; that its internal rules allow deduction of wages and the attendance bonus, but say nothing about the function allowance; that no law requires a standby vehicle, only a social-security ambulance; that Article 184 gives an hour, not money; and that Article 167 bars paying out annual leave while the contract runs. [Para 79] [Para 83] [Para 170] [Para 171] [Para 109] [Para 204] [Para 232]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to stop deducting the function allowance, recorded the parties' agreement on meal-money payment, and declined or refused every other demand. [Para 245] [Para 246] [Para 247]
On Issue 1 the panel applied res judicata: Award No. 019/20 had already refused the same demand about card machines and gate repairs between the same parties, so the issue was not decided again. [Para 144] [Para 145] [Para 162]
On Issue 3 the panel held, under Article 23 of the Labour Law as interpreted in Award No. 58/08 (Yong Wa 2), that internal rules are the company's own binding standard; point 1.8 allows deduction of wages and the attendance bonus for lateness but says nothing about the function allowance, so deducting that allowance was unlawful. [Para 164] [Para 167] [Para 170] [Para 171] [Para 172] [Para 175] [Para 176]
On Issue 4 the earlier Award No. 042/17 concerned paying for an ambulance service, not keeping a vehicle, so res judicata did not apply and the panel decided on the merits: Articles 248 to 250 put the duty on the enterprise director to prevent occupational accidents but do not require a standby vehicle, so the claim failed, though the panel observed the employer should take suitable measures. [Para 185] [Para 186] [Para 187] [Para 190] [Para 196] [Para 197] [Para 198] [Para 199]
On Issue 5 Article 184 gives a breastfeeding mother one hour a day within working hours for a year and provides no cash alternative, so the US$25 demand had no legal basis. [Para 200] [Para 204] [Para 207] [Para 208]
On Issues 6 and 8 the panel found no named claimant and no written authorisation, following Award No. 181/14, and declined to decide either demand. [Para 210] [Para 214] [Para 215] [Para 242] [Para 243] [Para 244]
On Issue 7 Article 167 paragraph 2 allows payment in lieu of unused annual leave only when the contract ends, and paragraph 3 voids any waiver or agreement to forgo annual leave, so paying serving workers was contrary to the law. [Para 229] [Para 230] [Para 232] [Para 237] [Para 240]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a demand has already been refused by an earlier award between the same parties on the same facts, the panel will decline to decide it again. [Para 145] [Para 162] A deduction is lawful only to the extent the employer's internal rules provide for it, so deducting an allowance the rules never mention is unlawful even when the lateness is proved. [Para 171] [Para 172] The statutory breastfeeding hour cannot be converted into money, and unused annual leave cannot be paid out while the contract continues; it must be taken. [Para 204] [Para 232] A union cannot bring a demand for individual workers without naming them and filing their written authorisation. [Para 214] [Para 243]

**Obiter dicta:** that the employer should nevertheless arrange suitable measures to help workers who fall ill at work, and should let workers take their annual leave. [Para 198] [Para 239]
