# Award No. 018/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្មែរ អេឡិកទ្រី ខល ផៅវ័រ (Khmer Electricity Call Power) (Arbitration Council, 30 August 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · order under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: គល់ វឌ្ឍនា (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140830

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្មែរ អេឡិកទ្រី ខល ផៅវ័រ generates electricity and sells it on contract to Électricité du Cambodge, employing 57 workers [Para 49]. Forty workers on indefinite-duration contracts, acting through the party representing the workers in the collective dispute (ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត), signed the claim asking for improved conditions [Para 52]; conciliation failed and the Department of Labour Disputes' report No. 522 K.B/AK/VK of 2 August 2021 referred four unresolved points to the Council [Para 9]. The employer did not appear and filed no documents, so the panel proceeded in its absence [Para 24][Para 93]. The four demands concerned severance pay owed before 2019 and for 2020 and the first half of 2021, two wage-scale increases, and the 30% night-work premium [Para 3][Para 6].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer had to pay the pre-2019 seniority indemnity and the indemnity for 2020 and the first six months of 2021, as required by Ministry Notification No. 003/21? [Para 94]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer had to raise wages by role by 15 to 25 USD per month from April 2021? [Para 132]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer had to back-pay a role-based increase of 10 to 20 USD per month from April 2020 to March 2021? [Para 163]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the employer owed the 30% supplement bringing night-shift pay to 130% for the two years from 1 August 2018 to 31 July 2021? [Para 172]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត ក្នុង ការដោះស្រាយ វិវាទ ការងារ រួម នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្មែរ អេឡិកទ្រី ខល ផៅវ័រ — the workers' representatives in the collective dispute — argued that the company was not harmed by COVID-19 because it held a contract with Électricité du Cambodge and was paid throughout [Para 54][Para 55]; the Ministry's Notification No. 003/21 required resumption of the indemnity payments [Para 58]; the employer had issued a role-based increase every year from 2006 to 2019, then in 2020 granted only a flat 5 USD without any letter [Para 64][Para 67]; night-shift work was paid at 100% like day work [Para 81].
**Employer (respondent):** absent from the hearing and represented by no one; the company submitted no documentary evidence at all [Para 24][Para 91].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to resume the pre-2019 seniority indemnity and the indemnity for 2020 and the first half of 2021 under Notification No. 003/21, and to pay the 30% night-shift supplement for 1 August 2018 to 31 July 2021 to 19 workers; the two wage-increase claims were dismissed [Para 191][Para 192][Para 194].
Article 89-new requires 15 days' wages per year of service, paid in two instalments every six months [Para 94][Para 95]. Prakas No. 443 of 21 September 2018 fixed the pre-2019 catch-up at 7.5 days in June and 7.5 days in December [Para 105], and Instruction No. 042/19 postponed that catch-up for non-garment enterprises to the end of December 2021 [Para 107][Para 110]. The panel found the employer had in fact paid 30 days for 2018–2019 in the garment manner, so it was not entitled to the postponement [Para 111][Para 112]. Notification No. 018/20 of 2 June 2020 then deferred the payments during COVID-19 [Para 113], but Notification No. 003/21 of January 2021 ordered them to restart during 2021 on a set schedule [Para 118][Para 123]; the panel therefore ordered resumption from the first half of 2021 [Para 131].
On the wage increases, the panel applied the "past practice" doctrine, which it grounded in Article 65(2) and (3) on oral contracts and in Award No. 95/09-Tak Fat [Para 135][Para 142]: an increase granted yearly at the employer's discretion after a performance evaluation is not a practice binding the employer [Para 150]. Article 2's management prerogative and clause 5 of the internal rules left the amount to the company's capacity [Para 151][Para 159], and no evidence showed the 2021 decision was improper, so both claims failed [Para 160][Para 171].
On night work, Article 144-new defines night work as 22:00 to 05:00 and requires 130% of the day wage, confirmed by Circular No. 185 of 14 August 2007 [Para 172][Para 176]. Paying rotating night-shift workers 100% was unlawful [Para 183]. Although Article 120 allows a three-year look-back, the workers limited the claim to two years [Para 83][Para 187], and the panel awarded only to the 19 workers shown on the 25 August 2021 list to work 22:00–05:00, not the 20 who claimed [Para 86][Para 87].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A discretionary annual wage increase, granted after a performance assessment and tied to the company's capacity, never becomes a binding "past practice" merely because it was repeated for years; only a regular, consistent practice recognised by both parties creates an entitlement [Para 141][Para 150]. A Ministry instruction that postpones severance payments for a sector does not help an employer that has already been paying on the standard schedule, and a later notification restarting payments binds it from the stated instalment [Para 112][Para 124].

**Obiter dicta:** an employer's absence does not relieve the panel of the duty to verify that notice was properly served, which it did before continuing [Para 91][Para 93].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name is printed in at least four spellings across the order (ផៅវ័រ, ផៅអ័, ផៅរ, ផៅវរ័) [Para 9][Para 32][Para 43]. Dozens of year figures lost their last digit and print as "២០" instead of 2020 or 2021, including the two-year night-shift period [Para 6][Para 172][Para 188]. Notification No. 003/21 is dated both 21 and 25 January 2021 [Para 118][Para 123]. The signature block is garbled, naming "ក្រុម ផ្កា កណ្តាល" instead of the panel [Para 196], and stray "AFC" insertions appear in cross-references to prior awards [Para 90][Para 156].
