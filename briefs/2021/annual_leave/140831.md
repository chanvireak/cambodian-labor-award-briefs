# Award No. 019/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ត្រាយ អាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក (Arbitration Council, 31 August 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · order under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), រុន សារ៉ាយ (president) · Doc ID 140831

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ត្រាយ អាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក manufactures footwear with 1,749 workers [Para 42][Para 43]. The workers filed 11 demands on 6 July 2021 [Para 11]; conciliation on 26 July 2021 settled eight of them, leaving three for the Council under report No. 515 K.B/AK/VK of 29 July 2021 [Para 12][Para 13]. The three unresolved demands were compliance with an earlier award on antenatal health-check leave, a 2 USD base-wage increase for supervisors, and a 5 USD health allowance [Para 45][Para 49][Para 55]. Both sides attended the video hearing, but the employer's representatives waived their right to reply, stating they had no authority to decide anything in the case [Para 17].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel has competence to order the employer to comply with Arbitration Award No. 049/20 — ត្រាយអាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន? [Para 62]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer had to add 2 USD to the base wage of team leaders, section heads and supervisors already earning a base of 200 USD or more, under the 2021 minimum-wage Prakas? [Para 68]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer had to restore the 5 USD health allowance it had previously paid? [Para 74]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the party representing the workers, អ្នកតំណាង ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត ក្នុង ការដោះស្រាយ វិវាទ ការងារ រួម នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ត្រាយ អាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន, argued that the company had applied Award No. 049/20 for only three or four months before cutting the monthly paid day for antenatal checks to 5 hours, with any excess deducted from annual leave [Para 46][Para 47]. It also argued that the minimum-wage Prakas for 2021 required the 2 USD addition for every worker, supervisors included [Para 51], and that the 5 USD health allowance paid since 2017 was withdrawn in early 2021 when old two-year contracts were replaced [Para 56][Para 57].
**Employer (respondent):** its human-resources manager, an administrative assistant and a translator attended but waived the right to reply, saying they had no power to settle the case at the hearing [Para 17][Para 48].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims were rejected: the panel declined to consider the compliance claim for want of jurisdiction, refused the 2 USD increase, and declined to consider the health allowance [Para 81][Para 82][Para 83].
On Issue 1 the panel relied on Article 353 of the 2006 Civil Procedure Code, under which even an arbitral decision, domestic or foreign, needs a court order before it can be enforced [Para 62]. Only the courts may compel a party to perform an Arbitration Council award, so the panel had no competence to enforce its own earlier award [Para 63][Para 64]. It followed a consistent line of prior awards, including No. 18/13-IM W, No. 352/14 and No. 043/19, that had refused the same request [Para 65][Para 66].
On Issue 2 the panel cited Article 107(2) of the Labour Law, which leaves the guaranteed minimum wage to a ministerial Prakas [Para 68], and Prakas No. 303/20 setting the 2021 minimum for the garment, textile and footwear sector at 192 USD a month, being 190 USD plus 2 USD [Para 69][Para 70]. Because the supervisors and team leaders were already paid a base of 200 USD or more, above that floor, the employer's practice was more favourable than the Prakas and the claim for a further 2 USD failed [Para 72].
On Issue 3 the panel held the assertion that the 5 USD allowance had ever been paid was unsupported by sufficient facts and evidence, and it declined to examine whether withdrawing it was lawful [Para 78][Para 79].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The Arbitration Council cannot enforce its own awards: a party seeking performance of an award must obtain an enforcement order from the court under Article 353 of the 2006 Civil Procedure Code, and a fresh demand for compliance is not arbitrable [Para 63][Para 64]. A minimum-wage Prakas fixes a floor only; where the employer already pays above the prescribed minimum, the workers cannot claim the annual increment as a separate entitlement [Para 70][Para 72].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that a health allowance alleged to have been paid historically was not established on the evidence, so the lawfulness of withdrawing it was left undecided rather than decided against the workers on the merits [Para 78].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The health allowance is printed as 4,500 USD in the list of demands but as 5 USD throughout the reasoning and the operative order [Para 6][Para 74][Para 83]. The hearing is dated 20 August 2021 in the procedural narrative while the invitations are dated 13 August 2021 [Para 11][Para 40], and one contract letter is dated "36 June 2021" [Para 32]. The employer's human-resources manager appears only in Latin script, partly damaged, and is given here by role [Para 1]. Stray OCR words ("Lin", "Ling", "VNA") interrupt sentences in the reasoning [Para 61][Para 67][Para 78].
