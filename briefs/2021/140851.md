# Award No. 037/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ព្រីន អិកស៍ ជីភី (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្វេក ធើរី ឯ.ក (bag and accessories factory) (Arbitration Council, 11 January 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Houn Chun Dy, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chea, chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 140851

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ព្រីន អិកស៍ ជីភី (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្វេក ធើរី ឯ.ក makes bags and accessories at Trapeang Thlong village, Damnak Reang commune, Oudong district, Kampong Speu province, and employed 680 workers; the claimant party was អ្នកតំណាងភាគីកម្មករ និយោជិត, the representatives of the workers organised in សហជីពស្មារតីកម្មករ ខ្មែរ, the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union. [Para 1][Para 48][Para 33][Para 49]
Workers struck from 24 November 2021 over the administration's termination of a worker, and the Council ordered the strike suspended by order No. 002/21 K.B.A. of 30 November 2021, with everyone back at work from the afternoon of 1 December 2021. [Para 36][Para 16][Para 17]
Two demands survived conciliation: that the employer not deduct the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់, "prime") for four days a month of compassionate leave, and that the head of administration, Mr Ch, be removed. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 51][Para 66]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must stop treating four days a month of compassionate leave as a deduction from the regular attendance bonus? [Para 51][Para 78]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the workers may require the employer to remove the head of administration, Mr Ch, from his post? [Para 66][Para 122]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The regular attendance bonus of USD 10 a month, paid since the factory opened in July 20.., is now cut by USD 5 for one day of leave and USD 2.50 per day thereafter, a change from a four-day tolerance to two days that was never notified or discussed with the union; the head of administration uses improper language towards women workers in the hygiene section, cuts bonuses without notice, and terminated pregnant women without proper notice. [Para 52][Para 55][Para 57][Para 58][Para 67][Para 68]
**Employer (respondent):** It never applied a four-day tolerance and needs no notice for a rule change that follows Ministry guidance; the bonus is discretionary and the law does not require it; the head of administration used no improper language, the allegation is unproven, it gave a week's notice before ending three-month contracts, and it has already taken back the two pregnant workers with their wages and seniority preserved. [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63][Para 71][Para 72][Para 77]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both demands were rejected, the panel declining to order any change to the attendance-bonus deductions or any removal of the head of administration. [Para 153][Para 154]

On Issue 1 the panel applied Notification No. 230 K.B/S.J.N of 25 July 2012, which entitles a worker who attends on the full number of working days in a month without absence to a regular attendance bonus of at least USD 10, but which does not itself state how the bonus is earned or cut. [Para 78][Para 79] It therefore followed the Full Bench decision of 3 October 2017, which held that 100 per cent of the bonus is due when the worker attends every working day, including days off that are a legal right, days off under the contract, and days absent by legal obligation, and that no bonus is due for absence beyond those. [Para 80][Para 82][Para 83] The Full Bench divided leave into four categories: leave by legal right (weekly rest, public holidays, annual leave, sick leave, maternity leave and special leave of up to seven days), leave granted by contract, absence compelled by law, and leave merely permitted by the employer; a bonus scheme may not be used to pressure a worker out of exercising the statutory rights. [Para 86][Para 91][Para 96][Para 112]

The panel accepted that reasoning, but found the workers had not said which category their compassionate leave fell into — sick child, sick parent, wedding, housewarming or birthday — so their claim rested on no established fact. [Para 117][Para 119][Para 120] It rejected the demand.

On Issue 2 the panel held that, under Article 65(1) of the Labour Law and Articles 664 and 311 of the Civil Code, only the parties to a contract may vary or end it, and the workers are third parties to the contract between the company and its head of administration. [Para 123][Para 126][Para 127] Article 2 gives the employer the managerial prerogative, and earlier awards allowed the panel to order removal only where the person is shown to be dangerous and a risk to others' safety. [Para 133][Para 137][Para 139][Para 145] The panel found no evidence that Mr Ch was such a person, noting that the two pregnant workers had been reinstated. [Para 149][Para 150][Para 77]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The regular attendance bonus must be paid in full where the worker's absence falls within leave that is a legal right, leave granted by contract, or absence compelled by law, and may be withheld only for other absence, unless the employer's own policy or a separate agreement is more generous; a claim that does not identify which category of leave was used fails for want of factual basis. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 119]
A panel will not order an employer to remove a manager at the workers' demand, since the workers are strangers to that manager's contract and removal is available only where the manager is proved dangerous to others' safety. [Para 127][Para 139][Para 145]

**Obiter dicta:** A bonus or incentive may not be designed so as to pressure workers out of exercising their statutory leave rights, and leave in the three protected categories is treated as attendance for bonus purposes even though the worker was not at the machines. [Para 96][Para 97]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Year figures are truncated throughout, the factory's opening month printed as "July 20..", so hire and opening dates cannot be fixed from the text. [Para 52][Para 30] Paragraph 94 contains a run of OCR noise inside a citation of the Civil Code, and paragraph 107 states a fine range whose upper figure is lower than its lower figure. [Para 94][Para 107] The head of administration's name is printed with a silent inconsistency in its final syllable between the issue list and the operative order, and is reduced to an initial here. [Para 4][Para 154] The award also repeats whole blocks of reasoning verbatim across the two issues, including citations to the same prior awards twice. [Para 138][Para 140][Para 146][Para 147]
