# Award No. 285/13 — Jandly Garment (Arbitration Council, 23 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វូច ហ៊ុន for the employer, អន ណន for the workers, គង់ ផល្ហៈ chair) · Doc ID 139932

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment factory at National Road 5 in Kandal province employing about 287 workers, whose contracts are in practice all of six months. [Para 57][Para 62] The factory union was registered on 20 June 2013 and its three officers recognised by the Ministry the same day. [Para 44][Para 60] The union filed 16 demands; by the end of conciliation and the hearing eleven were settled outright, one was settled in part, four were withdrawn, and one element remained: whether workers on fixed-term contracts of under a year, with less than a year's seniority, may take annual leave. [Para 24][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must let workers whose fixed-term contract is under one year, and whose seniority is under one year, take annual leave? [Para 61][Para 75]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** all workers should have annual leave, and the employer's practice is unequal, since some workers with about three months' service may take leave while others in the same position may not. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 84]
**Employer (respondent):** the law grants annual leave only to workers who have completed a year with the company, so it cannot grant it to those on contracts of under a year; it had in the past allowed workers with three months' service 1.5 days a month, but that was not a general practice, and it would now follow the Labour Law. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67][Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the sole remaining claim was refused. The employer had already agreed at the hearing to pay compensation for unused annual leave whenever a contract ends, and the 5% contract-termination indemnity was being paid, so those points were no longer in dispute. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 93][Para 94]

Article 166(1) of the Labour Law entitles all workers to paid annual leave at 1.5 working days per month of continuous service, unless a collective agreement or individual contract gives more, and Article 167(1) provides that the right arises only after a full year's work. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 77] The panel read the two provisions together, as it had in Award No. 31/12 (B&N), and held that a worker acquires annual leave only after completing one year, at which point the entitlement is 18 days a year. [Para 78][Para 81][Para 82]

The workers' alternative argument was past practice. The panel adopted the definition of "past practice" from Award No. 95/09 (Tak Fat): a practice long observed, repeated and consistent, clear and stable, and recognised and accepted by both sides. [Para 85] Here the employer admitted allowing 1.5 days a month to some workers after three months, but said the concession was not general and was not extended to everyone with three months' service. [Para 88][Para 89] The workers could not identify which workers received the benefit, why some did and others did not, when the practice began or how long it had lasted, and offered no evidence on any of those points. [Para 87][Para 90] Without that, the panel held, the practice was neither clear nor stable nor mutually accepted, and did not qualify as a past practice capable of grounding a claim. [Para 91][Para 92]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Article 166 sets the rate of annual leave at 1.5 days a month but Article 167 makes the right conditional on a full year's service, so a worker on a six-month fixed-term contract with less than a year's seniority has no enforceable annual leave entitlement, and the accumulated leave becomes payable only as compensation when the contract ends. [Para 75][Para 77][Para 81] An inconsistent concession granted to some workers and not others, whose scope, start date and duration the claimants cannot prove, does not become a binding past practice. [Para 89][Para 91][Para 92]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the 5% termination indemnity and unused-leave compensation had ceased to be disputed because the employer conceded them at the hearing was not a ruling on their merits. [Para 71][Para 73]

- The employer's name appears as ជេនឡី ហ្គាមេន, ជេន ឡី ហ្គាមិន and ជេនឡី ហ្គាមិន, and its commercial-registration number is given as both "So. 0646 E/2012" and "6633 ពណ.ចបព". [Para 1][Para 38][Para 56]
- The past-practice finding refers to workers with "03 months July" service, an OCR corruption of the three-month period used elsewhere. [Para 83]
- One worker's name is printed លី ភីម៉ា and លី ហ្វ៊ីម៉ា in the same evidence item. [Para 42]
