# Award No. 111/14 — JSD Tech Style (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 22 May 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Tuon Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chea, president) · Doc ID 139567

## 1. Facts
This is a closure order, not a merits award. Workers at JSD Tech Style (Cambodia), a garment factory in Takeo province, struck from 28 April 2014; conciliation at the provincial labour department failed on 3 May, the parties refused to sign the minutes, and thirty-six demands — nineteen from the Khmer Workers' Federation and seventeen from the Khmer Youth Federation — were referred to the panel on 5 May 2014. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] The company asked the panel to order the strike suspended; on 6 May the panel issued order 025/14 requiring the strike to stop immediately and all strikers to return from the morning of 7 May 2014. [Para 51][Para 52] The strike continued. [Para 53][Para 56]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel decided no substantive claim. Its only question was:
1. Whether the arbitration procedure should continue at all, given that the strike had not stopped after order 025/14? [Para 74][Para 96]
The undecided demands included, for the record:
2. Whether the company must maintain 100% of wages and benefits during the strike? [Para 6]
3. Whether the company must reinstate the worker it dismissed, Mr C? [Para 8]
4. Whether the company must pay a 4,000 riel daily lunch allowance and a 15 USD monthly travel and accommodation allowance? [Para 5][Para 10][Para 11]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two federations maintained their demands in full and, through their officials, said the strike was continuing because no solution had been reached and they could not persuade workers to return. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 92]
**Employer (respondent):** it applied on 5 May for an order suspending the strike, on the ground that it was disrupting the production chain, and pressed that application after the order was ignored. [Para 51][Para 64]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction / proceedings terminated — the panel ordered the arbitration in case 111/14 closed, without deciding any of the thirty-six demands. [Para 97][Para 98]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas 099 of 21 April 2004: while arbitration is running the parties must suspend every disputed measure, including strike or lockout, and must attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 75][Para 76] It read that provision, consistently with earlier awards, as barring it from continuing arbitration while workers are striking or an employer has locked out. [Para 77][Para 78]

It then checked the facts. Both parties accepted at the pre-hearing meeting of 8 May 2014 that the strike was still running after order 025/14 of 6 May. [Para 56][Para 88] Statements taken on 12 May from the secretaries-general of both federations and from the company's administrative assistant confirmed that the strike continued, that only some workers had returned on the Saturday, and that a further union had entered the plant and was leading the workers. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 92][Para 94] On that evidence the panel found the order had not been respected. [Para 96]

Because it never held a hearing, the panel expressly recorded that it made no finding of fact on any of the disputed issues referred by the labour dispute department. [Para 73] It relied on a line of earlier closures — Awards 04/04, 18/04, 77/05, 110/11, 17/13, 119/13 and 066/14 — where files were closed for the same reason, and reserved the right to close proceedings again if a party breached any term of its order. [Para 79][Para 82][Para 84][Para 85]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike, or an employer continues a lockout, after the panel has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas 099, the panel will not proceed to hear the merits and will close the arbitration; the dispute may be brought back before it once the strike has actually ended. [Para 77][Para 80][Para 97]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it keeps the right to terminate proceedings whenever a party breaches any term of an interim order, and its approval of the hotel-sector orders of 2004, are general observations that did not decide this file. [Para 82][Para 85]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Order 025/14 is dated "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦ ២០១៤" at [Para 52], two years printed in one date, although the order is elsewhere dated 6 May 2014. [Para 67][Para 96] The number of information minutes from the company's administrative assistant is given as four at [Para 61] and as ten at [Para 93]. The unions party to the case are the Khmer Workers' Federation and the Khmer Youth Federation, yet the strike is described at [Para 57] and [Para 89] as led by C.CAWDU, a union that is not a party and appears nowhere in the party list at [Para 1]. [Para 1][Para 57] The signature block is largely destroyed, leaving a fragmentary line in place of the arbitrators' names. [Para 99]
