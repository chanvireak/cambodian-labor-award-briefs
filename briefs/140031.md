# Award No. 63/11 — Jocam Footwear Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 June 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vuoch Hun, Sin Kim Sean, Nhean So Munin) · Doc ID 140031

## 1. Facts
Jocam Footwear Co., Ltd is a shoe factory at Street 2004, Trapang Chourk Village, Teouk Tla Commune, Sen Sok District, Phnom Penh [Para 1]. The workers, through the Local Union of the Voice Khmer Youth Union Federation (VKYUF), submitted ten demands, including reinstatement of two union committee members and an end to the use of security guards against workers [Para 2]. The workers went on strike; the Council issued Interim Order 008/11 KBA on 3 June 2011 ordering them to cease striking and return to work on 4 June 2011, but the order was not complied with [Para 10][Para 11]. The claimants and their union were absent from both prehearings [Para 13][Para 19].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate Chun Setha and Chan Sopharath, committee members of the Local Union of VKYUF? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must refrain from using security guards to threaten workers and the competent authority must investigate? [Para 2]
3. Whether the employer must provide a 1,000 riel meal allowance for overtime from 7:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.? [Para 2]
4. Whether the employer must set up a day-care centre and breastfeeding room? [Para 2]
5. Whether the employer must allow all workers to leave at 4:00 p.m. every Saturday? [Para 2]
6. Whether the employer must rectify underpayments of the seniority bonus? [Para 2]
7. Whether the employer must pay wages before 4:00 p.m. on payday? [Para 2]
8. Whether the employer must refrain from discriminating against the leaders, committee and members of the Local Union of VKYUF? [Para 2]
9. Whether the employer must dismiss David, a manager, for his bad attitude towards workers? [Para 2]
10. Whether the employer must deduct union contribution fees from members' wages? [Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers did not attend either prehearing or the conciliation session, so no position on the merits was presented; their complaint of 28 May 2011 carried the ten demands [Para 1][Para 6][Para 19].
**Employer (respondent):** The employer attended both prehearings, stated that the Local Union of VKYUF had not been formally registered, and asserted that the workers continued to strike until 14 June 2011 despite the interim order [Para 16][Para 28]. It had asked the Council for an interim order to stop the strike [Para 10].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** arbitral process terminated — no award was made on any of the ten demands [Para 32]
The panel applied Clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 dated 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to abstain from strikes or lockouts during arbitration and to attend all meetings to which the panel calls them [Para 24][Para 25]. It followed earlier awards in cases 04/04-MSI and 77/05-Franco Knitting Garment Factory, where the Council held that it would terminate the process if workers continued to strike [Para 26][Para 27]. The panel also relied on cases 28/06-GDM and 34/06-Goldfame, in which awards were issued to close cases because the workers continued to strike during the process [Para 28]. On the facts, the workers continued to strike after the interim order, failed to appear at the first prehearing on 8 June 2011 and the second prehearing on 14 June 2011, and did not show a genuine intention to resolve the dispute [Para 29][Para 30]. The panel therefore ruled that the arbitral process be terminated [Para 31]. Separately, the panel noted doubts about representation: VKYUF did not acknowledge involvement because the concerned workers were from the Forever factory, the union was not formally registered, and no authorisation letters from the seven named workers had been received [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where claimant workers continue a strike in breach of an interim order and fail to attend the panel's prehearings, the Arbitration Council terminates the arbitral process without deciding the merits; the right to strike is suspended while arbitration is pending. [Para 24][Para 30]

**Obiter dicta:** The doubts over VKYUF's registration and the absence of authorisation letters were noted but did not found the decision, which rested on the strike and non-attendance. [Para 17]

The text is an unofficial English translation with heavy OCR loss: "themployer", "athearing", "Sethand" for "Setha and", "Fax: NIA" for "N/A", and "Voice Khmer Youth Union Federation" is abbreviated inconsistently [Para 1][Para 2]. The complaint is described as received by telephone on 2 June 2011 but also as dated 28 May 2011 [Para 4][Para 1]. Demand 9 names "David, a manager" while the employer's representatives include "Mr D Jen", so it is unclear whether the same person is meant [Para 1][Para 2]. The award records no evidence or facts on the merits, because none were presented [Para 24].
