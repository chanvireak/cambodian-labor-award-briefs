# Award No. 31/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅធន៍ ប៊េរ (Sao Thon Borey Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 20 March 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ឈីវ ភីរម្យ, employer-chosen; លីវ សុវណ្ណា, worker-chosen; គង់ ផល្លៈ, chair) · Doc ID 140499

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅធន៍ ប៊េរ is a company at Soun Vattana Building, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh, employing approximately 600 workers; the complainant is the local branch of សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា (the Cambodian workers' union federation) at the company [Para 1][Para 46]. On 28 January 2008 the federation filed complaint No. 1935 seeking improved working conditions; of 15 demands, eight were conciliated on 12 February 2008 and seven non-conciliated points went to the Council on 26 February 2008 [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]. At the hearing of 12 March 2008 points 1 and 6 (the wage part) were conciliated and points 4, 5 and 7 were withdrawn, leaving issues 2, 3 and 6 for decision [Para 20][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]. The union had applied for registration on 6 March 2008 but had received no certificate by the hearing, and it produced no written authority from the 600 workers by the deadline of 13 March 2008 [Para 46][Para 47][Para 71].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must allow workers to take three days of paid annual leave outside the Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben periods whenever they have a personal necessity? [Para 54][Para 58]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must add US$ 5 to the regular monthly attendance bonus already paid? [Para 62][Para 63]
3. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must keep paying the US$ 5 attendance bonus to a worker absent on sick leave with a valid medical certificate? [Para 65][Para 137]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (the local branch of សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា at ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅធន៍ ប៊េរ, for 600 employees):** annual leave must be usable at any time of personal necessity, and three days beyond the festival periods should be available; the attendance bonus should rise by US$ 5 because goods have become dearer and workers work hard; and the bonus must survive certified sick leave, since the company deducts it entirely [Para 4][Para 58][Para 63][Para 65].
**Employer (ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅធន៍ ប៊េរ):** annual leave is granted only around Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben and other festivals requested by workers, and beyond that workers receive payment instead; the bonus cannot be increased because fuel and goods prices have risen; and it already pays full wages during certified sick leave, so the bonus cannot also be kept [Para 54][Para 55][Para 64][Para 68].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to allow three days of annual leave for personal necessity with prior notice to the employer and to stop immediately paying cash in lieu of annual leave (Issue 2); it rejected the further US$ 5 attendance bonus (Issue 3); and on Issue 6 it rejected the demand to maintain the bonus during sick leave but ordered the employer to deduct that bonus in proportion to the days of absence [Para 156][Para 157][Para 158][Para 159].
On standing, the panel held that under Article 268 a union acquires the rights the Labour Law recognises only when registration is granted, and this union held only a receipt for its application of 6 March 2008, so it could not yet represent its members [Para 70][Para 74][Para 77][Para 80]. It then applied Article 19 of Prakas 099, which lets a person who is not a party act as representative on written authority, but the union failed to file the workers' letters of authority by 13 March 2008, so the panel found it had no right to represent all 600 workers and could decide the dispute only for the complainants themselves [Para 81][Para 82][Para 85][Para 86][Para 87].
On Issue 2, Articles 166 and 167 entitle a worker who has completed one year to one and a half days of paid leave per month, and Article 170 requires leave in principle to be taken at Khmer New Year but does not forbid other timing by agreement; Article 167(3) makes void any agreement to forgo leave or to pay it in cash [Para 88][Para 91][Para 103][Para 102]. Following Award 21/05-Sinomik, the panel held leave may be taken at other times by agreement, and found three days out of eighteen a reasonable allowance provided the worker gives prior notice so production is not disrupted [Para 94][Para 97][Para 106][Para 112]. On Issue 3, Point 3 of Notification 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000 requires at least US$ 5 for regular attendance, both sides accepted that US$ 5 was paid, and no law or agreement required more, so the demand was rejected [Para 116][Para 117][Para 118][Para 121]. On Issue 6, Article 103 treats bonuses as part of wages, but Articles 71(3) and 72(1) suspend the contract during certified sickness and relieve the employer of paying wages for that period; the bonus rewards attendance, so it is not earned while absent [Para 144][Para 145][Para 147][Para 150]. The panel nonetheless ordered a pro rata deduction rather than total loss, as in prior awards [Para 151][Para 155][Para 159].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union whose registration certificate has not yet issued holds no right to represent workers before the Council; it may act only on written authority from each worker, and absent that authority the panel will decide the dispute for the named complainants alone [Para 80][Para 86][Para 87].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that three days of leave out of eighteen is a modest allowance, and that prior notice is required so the employer can arrange replacement labour; neither observation was necessary to the order [Para 111][Para 112].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The Khmer text is heavily degraded: word boundaries are broken throughout, and at the type-of-award clause an entire English phrase unrelated to the award has been inserted into the Khmer, which the translation cannot recover [Para 160]. Arbitrator names are printed in more than one form between the panel list and the signature block, with the chair's name and the worker-chosen arbitrator's name each appearing in two variants [Para 1][Para 161]. The non-conciliation figures are internally inconsistent: the procedural summary states that seven non-conciliated points remained and that points 1 and 6 were conciliated, while the findings say 600 of 600 workers were claimants and the panel later speaks of the seven complainants only [Para 26][Para 49][Para 87]. The company name is spelled សៅធន៍ and សោធន៍ in different places, and the union's registration evidence is described as a receipt, not a certificate [Para 1][Para 46][Para 75].
