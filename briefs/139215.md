# Award No. 077/19 — Cy Clu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 September 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Hem Huonarith, appointed by the employer; Tun Si Phan, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139215

## 1. Facts
Cy Clu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd manufactures bags at Chbar Mon, Kampong Speu, with 6,253 workers. [Para 63][Para 64] Workers brought nine claims; one was withdrawn and four agreed at the hearing, leaving issues 2, 5, 7 and 8 for decision. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] Issue 7 concerned Mr S, elected deputy president of the factory union on 25 December 2018 and not re-engaged three days later. [Para 75][Para 147]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must merge the USD 5 incentive bonus into the regular attendance bonus, making USD 15 per month, and pro-rate it by days worked? [Para 65]
5. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance for voluntary overtime of 1,500 riel per hour? [Para 68][Para 100]
7. Whether the non-renewal of Mr S's fixed-duration contract was anti-union discrimination, requiring reinstatement and back pay? [Para 72][Para 121]
8. Whether the employer must deduct 2,000 riel per month from the wages of members who thumbprinted consent, as union dues? [Para 161]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** A worker with near-perfect attendance loses the whole incentive bonus for one late arrival, which is unjust; overtime meal money should be 1,500 riel per hour because travel is costly; and Mr S was warned only once, for posting the union's notice, and was then dropped for union activity. [Para 66][Para 70][Para 76][Para 143]
**Employer (respondent):** The USD 5 is a discretionary special bonus to build punctuality habits, so it may not be merged; it cannot afford 1,500 riel per hour and already pays 2,000 riel per day under Notification 041/11; and Mr S was absent without permission seven times, was warned four times, and the company did not know of his election. [Para 67][Para 71][Para 80][Para 81][Para 83]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — reinstatement of Mr S ordered [Para 183]; deduction of 2,000 riel per month as union dues ordered for members who consented [Para 185]; the incentive-bonus merger and the 1,500 riel hourly meal allowance refused [Para 181][Para 182]; back pay refused [Para 184].
On issue 2 the panel held that article 2 of the Labour Law confirms the employer's right to organise and manage the enterprise so long as it acts lawfully and reasonably, and that a bonus not created by law may be conditioned unilaterally by the employer, following Awards 62/06, 108/06, 33/07, 119/09 and 24/05. The claim was therefore dismissed. [Para 92][Para 93][Para 97][Para 99]
On issue 5 the panel read clause 2 of Notification 041/11 as requiring 2,000 riel per day, or a free meal, for voluntary overtime; the employer paid that, so no 1,500 riel hourly entitlement existed, and the claim exceeded the law and was an interest dispute that the claimant union, lacking most-representative status, could not bring. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 103][Para 116][Para 118]
On issue 7 the panel applied articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law and article 62 of the Trade Union Law, which reach the non-renewal of successive fixed-duration contracts, following Award 182/16. [Para 121][Para 122][Para 123][Para 127] The burden of proving discrimination lay on the workers [Para 130][Para 132]; they discharged it with a Cambodia Post delivery slip showing the employer received the election notice on 25 December 2018 [Para 133][Para 136][Para 137]; the non-renewal followed three days later, and the written warnings bore no signature or thumbprint of Mr S and he acknowledged only one [Para 142][Para 143][Para 146]. Discrimination was found, and article 34 of Prakas 099 allowed reinstatement. [Para 148][Para 149][Para 153] Back pay was refused because his fixed-duration contract expired on the day of non-renewal, ending the employment relationship. [Para 158][Para 159]
On issue 8 the panel applied article 129 of the Labour Law, article 26 of the Trade Union Law and clauses 1 and 4 of Prakas 300: deduction is lawful on written consent, with ten days' notice, once the union is registered — which this union was on 12 February 2019. [Para 161][Para 164][Para 166][Para 169][Para 172]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Non-renewal of a fixed-duration contract is a termination for article 62 purposes, so where it closely follows the employer's receipt of notice that the worker holds union office and the employer's disciplinary record is unsigned and unproven, anti-union discrimination is established and reinstatement follows; but because the relationship ends with the contract term, no wages accrue past that date. [Para 127][Para 147][Para 159]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the employer's managerial prerogative extends to designing bonus schemes beyond those the law requires, and that most-representative status is a precondition for any interest claim, since such an award replaces the collective agreement. [Para 94][Para 113]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award prints the company name only in Khmer; the Latin form above is a transliteration. The Trade Union Law is dated 13 May 2016 in one passage and 17 May 2016 in another. [Para 106][Para 123] Notification 041/11 is dated 7 March 2011 with "2016" inserted. [Para 100] The workers' account puts the dismissal on 8 January 2019 while the employer's notice is dated 28 December 2018. [Para 78][Para 82] Consent signatories are counted as 1,207 and later as 1,189. [Para 88][Para 177]
