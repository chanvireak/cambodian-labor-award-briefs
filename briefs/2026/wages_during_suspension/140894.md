# Award No. 003/26 — Great New Wing Garmin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 23 April 2026)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; Liv Sovanna, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 140894

## 1. Facts
Great New Wing Garmin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (ហ្គ្រេត ញូ វីង ហ្គាម៉ិន (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក) sews garments in Takeo province and employed 946 workers, 668 of them women. [Para 46] In early January 2026 it had no work for sewing groups 7, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16, and on 3 or 4 January 2026 it met section heads and worker representatives and had them notify workers orally that 5 and 6 January 2026 would be taken as two days of paid annual leave; no written acknowledgement was signed. [Para 51] Of the 15 demands filed on 18 February 2026, 14 were settled in conciliation on 12 March 2026, leaving one unresolved claim for those two days. [Para 17] [Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay back two days of paid annual leave (5 and 6 January 2026) that it deducted when it had no work to give the workers? [Para 49] [Para 102]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 18 days of annual leave granted ahead of the statutory year were a benefit, and the employer may deduct annual leave only when workers take special leave; where work runs out the employer must apply to suspend the contracts under the law, and notice given at the end of the working day left no time to object. [Para 56] [Para 57] [Para 114]
**Employer (respondent):** it grants 18 days of paid annual leave before a full year of service — more favourable than the law — and no worker objected when the sections were briefed; assigning annual leave when work runs out is the employer's prerogative, as other factories in the area also do. [Para 59] [Para 60] [Para 61]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay back two days of paid annual leave (5 and 6 January 2026) to 4 remaining complainants, the award leaving the sum at two days' wages with no riel or dollar figure printed; the award is non-binding and becomes final 8 days after notification unless a party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour through the Council secretariat. [Para 118] [Para 119] [Para 120]

Who was left: 16 workers thumbprinted the 16 February 2026 request and 2 more attended the hearing, so 18 were claimed; checking names and thumbprints, the panel found 17 complainants, one name on the request having no match. [Para 78] [Para 89] Article 19 of Prakas 099 of 21 April 2004 requires written authorisation for a representative, and the panel repeated that the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof: the workers gave no evidence that the 21 February 2026 withdrawal letters were signed out of fear of their section heads or were unclear, and 13 of the 17 had signed them, leaving 4 complainants. [Para 82] [Para 96] [Para 101]

On the merits, Article 170 of the Labour Law of 1997 grants annual leave in principle at Khmer New Year unless the employer and workers agree on another time, requires the employer to notify the labour inspector in advance of any such arrangement, and lets the employer fix the remaining days where leave exceeds fifteen days. [Para 102] [Para 104] Following Award 53/04, the panel held that an employer may not use workers' annual leave to cover a period in which the company has no work, and that any change of timing needs the workers' consent and prior report to the labour inspector. [Para 107] The employer's more favourable practice of advancing leave before one year of service did not confer a right to impose annual leave unilaterally when work ran out, and no agreement was proved — only oral briefings through section heads. [Para 115] [Para 117]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that runs short of work may not discharge the resulting idle time by deducting workers' paid annual leave: annual leave may be fixed outside the Khmer New Year only by agreement with the workers and with prior notice to the labour inspector, and a practice more generous than the statute — such as advancing leave before a year of service — does not supply the consent that Article 170 requires. [Para 107] [Para 115] [Para 117]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted without endorsing the employer's point that neighbouring factories in the area also assign annual leave when work runs out, and it resolved differing ID numbers for the same worker names as a question of evidence rather than of substance. [Para 61] [Para 76]
