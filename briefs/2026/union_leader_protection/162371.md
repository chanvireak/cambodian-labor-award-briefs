# Award No. 006/26 វ.រ — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ុន (Yak Jin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 28 July 2026)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី; ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding) · Doc ID 162371

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ុន is a garment factory employing 2,708 workers, 2,126 of them women. [Para 73]
On 6 December 2023 the employer met workers' representatives and union leaders and agreed to suspend the employment contracts on nine Saturdays — 30 December 2023, 2, 13, 20 and 27 January 2024, and 3, 10, 17 and 24 February 2024 — paying 50% of base wage. [Para 79]
The claimants, ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ុន acting through three unions, filed two demands on 1 June 2026, withdrew the first, and pursued a further 50% of wages for those nine days on behalf of 1,447 workers. [Para 8][Para 10][Para 78]
Conciliation failed and the single unresolved issue was referred to the panel on 26 June 2026. [Para 11]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay a further 50% of wages for the nine Saturdays suspended between 30 December 2023 and 24 February 2024 to the workers recognised as claimants? [Para 78][Para 146]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត (claimant workers):** suspension of an employment contract requires the labour inspector's authorisation under Articles 71 and 72, and the employer never applied for it; workers in Awards 012/24 and 001/26 already received the extra 50%, so the same treatment is owed here. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 96]
**ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ុន (respondent employer):** the leaders of all three claimant unions signed and fingerprinted the 6 December 2023 agreement and cannot reopen the point, and paying every employee would cost about USD 100,000. [Para 97][Para 99]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to pay a further 50% of wages for the nine suspended Saturdays (30 December 2023; 2, 13, 20, 27 January 2024; 3, 10, 17, 24 February 2024) to 1,193 workers [Para 168]
On representation, the panel applied Prakas No. 362 ក.ប/ប្រ.ក of 29 August 2016 and Article 14 of Prakas No. 098/26 of 13 April 2026: eight of the nine recognised worker representatives appeared and held written delegated authority, so they could act for the workers who fingerprinted. [Para 125][Para 130][Para 133]
On the claimant list the panel found that of 1,564 names, 29 bore no fingerprint and 9 were duplicated, leaving 1,526; but the employer's own table recognised 1,193 as the remaining entitled workers after deducting 28 already paid under Award 012/24, 170 who had left, 145 hired after the suspension and 16 duplicates, and the workers contested neither the list nor the number. [Para 101][Para 114][Para 142][Para 144]
The panel therefore decided the issue for 1,193 workers only. [Para 145]
On the merits the panel read Article 71 of the Labour Law as permitting suspension only under the labour inspector's supervision and for no more than two months, and Article 72(1) as removing the wage obligation only where the suspension was itself lawful. [Para 147][Para 149][Para 151][Para 153]
It found that the employer never requested authorisation for the nine Saturdays, so Article 72(1) did not apply. [Para 158][Para 164]
Under Article 13 of the Labour Law the agreement was void because it fixed 50% pay without the inspector's authorisation. [Para 161][Para 162]
The employer could not rely on that agreement to escape its statutory duty, and owed the further 50% for the nine days. [Para 165][Para 166]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A suspension of employment contracts agreed with union leaders or worker representatives, without the prior authorisation and supervision of the labour inspector, does not shift the wage risk to the workers: the employer owes 100% of wages for the suspended days and any clause fixing 50% is void as contrary to the Labour Law. [Para 151][Para 162][Para 165]
Where the employer's own schedule recognises a specific number of entitled claimants and the workers do not contest that number, the panel decides the issue on that number rather than on the larger list originally filed. [Para 142][Para 144][Para 145]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified. [Para 168]
