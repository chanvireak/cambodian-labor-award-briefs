# Award No. 70/11 — Goldfame Enterprises (Int'l) Knitters Ltd (Arbitration Council, 13 July 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Tuon Siphann, Run Saray) · Doc ID 140039

## 1. Facts
Goldfame Enterprises (Int'l) Knitters Ltd produces winter coats at Kampong Pring Khum Sithbo, S'ang, Kandal, and employs 6,000 workers; four unions operate there, and the claimant Local Union of VKYUF represents 285 workers without most representative status [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]. The employer began using fixed-duration contracts in early 2007 to handle orders rising from May to December; 65% of workers hold undetermined-duration contracts and 35% fixed-duration contracts of one, six, eight or 10 months [Para 20][Para 22]. About 10% of fixed-duration workers have over two years' service [Para 21]. On expiry the employer pays severance of 5% of wages and payment in lieu of unused annual leave, and renews either immediately or after one week, one month or four months, changing the workers' ID numbers [Para 23][Para 25][Para 26]. Of seven demands, five were conciliated and two referred to the panel [Para 6][Para 7].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must convert the fixed-duration contracts of workers with over two years' service to undetermined-duration contracts? [Para 2][Para 18]
2. Whether the employer must rectify underpayments of the seniority bonus dating back to March 2011? [Para 3][Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Article 67 makes a contract undetermined-duration once the total length of fixed-duration contracts exceeds two years; the practice causes loss of seniority bonuses and future retirement payments [Para 26][Para 27]. The employer must apply Notification No. 041/11 and pay US$ 9, 10 and 11 for nine, 10 and 11 years' service, with back pay from March 2011, and any inconsistent agreement must be abrogated [Para 30][Para 31].
**Employer (respondent):** Its practice does not affect seniority bonuses and it contributes to social security for both contract types [Para 27]. It reads Articles 67 and 73 to allow renewal without limit where prior notice is given, and relies on the Labour Inspection's explanation that each renewal need not exceed two years [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]. It refuses the seniority demand [Para 32].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** The panel ordered conversion of the fixed-duration contracts of VKYUF members with over two years' service where contracts are immediately renewed, but rejected conversion where workers are re-employed after a gap; it ordered seniority bonuses of US$ 9, 10 and 11 and back pay from 1 March 2011 [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53].

The panel first limited the claim to union members, because non-members did not attend the hearing and VKYUF produced no written authorisations for them, as Clause 19 of Prakas No. 99 requires [Para 33][Para 34]. On Issue 1, it applied Article 67(2) and its earlier awards (10/03-Jacqsintex, 36/06-Mondotex, 57/06-Evergreen, 155/09-USA, 17/11-JRB) holding that renewals taking the total beyond two years create an undetermined-duration contract [Para 34][Para 38]. It reasoned that an employer which immediately needs the same workers for its production line is not responding to occasional production increases, so immediate renewal beyond two years converts the contract [Para 41][Para 42]. Where the employer lets a week, a month or four months pass before re-employment, fixed-duration contracts remain lawful even beyond two years of service [Para 42]. It found no intention to evade obligations because the employer paid severance and seniority payments on expiry, but warned it would order the practice stopped if the employer waited deliberately to prevent conversion [Para 43][Para 44].

On Issue 2, the panel held that Notification No. 041/11 requires US$ 9, 10 and 11 for nine, 10 and 11 years' service, and that the 20 February 2010 agreement with the Local Union of C.CAWDU paying only US$ 8 was null and void under Article 13 because it gave less than the law and the notification [Para 45][Para 50]. It ordered back pay from 1 March 2011 [Para 51].

**Dissent:** Arbitrator Ing Sothy recorded a dissent, stating that the employer may choose either contract type, that each renewal did not exceed two years so the contracts remained fixed-duration, and that conversion would make the employer pay termination payments twice after already paying 5% severance [Para 56][Para 59][Para 61][Para 62]. He would not order conversion [Para 67].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Fixed-duration contracts that are renewed immediately on expiry become undetermined-duration contracts once their total length exceeds two years; where the employer waits for production needs to fall and re-employs the worker after a gap, the contracts remain fixed-duration. [Para 42][Para 44]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel warned that it would order the employer to stop its practice if it maliciously delayed re-employment to prevent conversion. [Para 44]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is described as an "unofficial English translation" with pervasive OCR fusion ("themployer", "fixeduration", "length", "Traing") [Para 1][Para 3]. The employer's Internal Work Rules are cited as "No. 058 SKBY dated 12 September 2011", a date after the 13 July 2011 award [Para 13]. The dissent is headed "issue 6" although the award contains only two issues [Para 55]. The document title on the face reads "70/11-Goldfame" without the company suffix, while the annex uses "Goldfame Enterprises (Int'I) Knitters Ltd" with a capital I for "Int'l" [Para 1][Para 54]. The notification number appears as both "No. 041/11" and "No. 041/11 KB/SCN" [Para 45][Para 51].
