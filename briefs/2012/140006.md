# Award No. 76/12 — The Blue Pumpkin Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 22 May 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambona, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chia, chair) · Doc ID 140006

## 1. Facts
The Blue Pumpkin Co., Ltd. operates a restaurant branch in Siem Reap among 250 staff company-wide [Para 53][Para 54]. Mr B, deputy president of the pro-company union, and Mr E, a cook, were dismissed in March 2012; the union federation filed a collective dispute alleging anti-union discrimination in promotions and seeking reinstatement of both men [Para 72][Para 89]. The employer said both were dismissed for serious misconduct [Para 73][Para 98].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer discriminated against union members in decisions on promotion? [Para 57]
2. Whether the employer must reinstate Mr B? [Para 72]
3. Whether the employer must reinstate Mr E? [Para 89]
4. Whether the employer's internal regulations should be annulled as contrary to the Labour Law? [Para 111]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** no union member had ever been promoted; Mr S's promotion was withdrawn and Mr C, a non-member, was promoted over the more senior Mr E [Para 58][Para 60]. Both dismissals were on grounds of union membership [Para 72][Para 89].
**Employer (respondent):** it never received a union membership list, so could not discriminate [Para 63]; Mr B punched a colleague's attendance card [Para 73]; Mr E falsified his attendance and had prior warnings [Para 103]; the internal regulations were duly registered [Para 113].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed in part and not considered in part — all four claims refused; no compensation or reinstatement ordered [Para 180][Para 181][Para 182][Para 183].

Issue 1: Article 12 of the Labour Law; the claimant bears the burden of proof. No further evidence was filed by the 3 May 2012 deadline, and the bare assertion that no union member was promoted did not establish discrimination [Para 114][Para 130][Para 131]. Issue 2: because the dismissal of a union leader was under appeal to the Minister under Article 293 and the two-month period had not expired, the panel declined to consider the reinstatement claim [Para 143][Para 144]. Issue 3: Mr E asked Mr B to punch his card on 4 March 2012 while arriving at 8:15 rather than 7:59; the panel held this was intentional falsification during the contract, serious misconduct under Article 83(2), so dismissal without prior notice was lawful under Article 82 [Para 152][Para 160][Para 163][Para 170]. Issue 4: under Article 302 and Article 32 of Prakas No. 99, annulment of already-registered internal regulations is not a collective labour dispute within the panel's jurisdiction [Para 176][Para 178].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union-discrimination claim over promotion fails where the worker produces no specific evidence beyond the absence of union members among those promoted; the burden remains with the claimant [Para 130]. Attendance fraud committed by having a colleague punch one's card is serious misconduct under Article 83(2) and justifies summary dismissal [Para 163].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the employer's promotion procedure was not in writing and that Mr C had received no prior warning, yet still found no discrimination [Para 69][Para 126].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Paragraph 145 states that the panel both refused to consider the reinstatement claim and ordered Mr B reinstated, contradicting the operative order at Para 181; the operative order is followed. Several lines are duplicated or garbled by OCR (Paras 39–40, 48–50), and the union membership figure in Para 56 is lost.
