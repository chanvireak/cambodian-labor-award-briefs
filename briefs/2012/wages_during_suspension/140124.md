# Award No. 085/12 — Vori Garmen Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 May 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Ing Sothy appointed by the employer, An Non appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 140124

## 1. Facts
Workers at Vori Garmen Co., Ltd, represented by the Khmer Youth Voice Union Federation and its union at the company, struck from 25 April 2012 over four demands left unresolved by conciliation at the Ministry of Labour [Para 1][Para 18]. The Ministry referred the four non-conciliated points to the Arbitration Council by report No. 463 K.B/AK dated 30 April 2012 [Para 12][Para 17]. On 2 May 2012 the Council issued order No. 014/12 K.B.A. requiring the strikers to suspend the strike and return to their shifts from 3 May 2012 until the arbitration was complete [Para 19]. The workers did not return; the worker party then failed to attend the pre-hearing of 4 May 2012 and gave no reason [Para 20][Para 37].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration may continue at all, given that the worker party kept striking and stayed away from the pre-hearing? [Para 29]

The panel recorded four non-conciliated demands — an equal wage for 20 probationers, the dismissal of the head of administration, reinstatement of two union committee members, and the attendance bonus and wage rates (paras 2-8) — and made no order on any of them. They are described in §1, not listed here as decided issues.

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — Khmer Youth Voice Union Federation at Vori Garmen):** the 20 probation-completed workers are owed a flat USD 66 a month; the head of administration must be removed; the two union committee members must be reinstated; and the attendance bonus and wage rates must be kept as they are [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8].

**Employer (respondent — Vori Garmen Co., Ltd):** a flat USD 66 is impossible because pay follows a scheme distributed by grade and work capability; the administrator will not be dismissed, but will be counselled or warned in writing if fault is proven; the two committee members will not be rehired because one resigned and the other's contract had expired; and the company simply applies the law on bonuses and wages [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided and ordered that the arbitration proceedings in case No. 085/12-Vori shall not continue, making no order on the four demands [Para 39][Para 40].

The panel put the dispositive question to itself as whether to carry on with the arbitration, not as the merits of the four demands [Para 29]. It read Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 as requiring the disputing parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law — and any other measure aggravating the dispute for as long as the panel is seized, and as requiring both parties to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them [Para 29][Para 30]. Measured against its own order of 2 May 2012, the workers had not returned to their shifts and kept striking, and the worker party had stayed away from the 4 May pre-hearing without explanation [Para 35][Para 37]. Because the referral itself sprang from a strike still running, the panel recorded that it made no findings of fact on the disputed points at all: the complainant party was absent and no hearing was held [Para 28]. It reinforced that reading with earlier awards — in case No. 04/04-MSI the Council had held it could not resolve or order on a collective dispute where the party had no right to strike during the proceedings — and cited orders No. 110/11-Simano and No. 59/12-Vincentith to the same effect [Para 31][Para 32]. It therefore declined to continue [Para 38][Para 39]. The four substantive demands were never adjudicated and remain unresolved by this award [Para 40].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party to a collective dispute keeps a strike running, or refuses to attend the panel's pre-hearing, in defiance of an order to suspend dispute measures and return to work, the Arbitration Council will not continue the arbitration and will make no findings on the merits. Access to arbitration in a collective dispute is conditional on the party suspending strike action and attending the proceedings [Para 29][Para 38].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the four non-conciliated points had been transmitted to the Secretariat, but expressly declined to reach them [Para 28][Para 40].
