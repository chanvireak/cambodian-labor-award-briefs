# Award No. 106/12 — Bino (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 13 July 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Uk Ri, An Vireak) · Doc ID 140141

## 1. Facts
Bino is a garment factory at Phnom Penh with about 950 workers, sued by the Democratic Union of Khmer Apparel Workers, whose factory branch was not yet registered. [Para 83][Para 84][Para 85] Seven demands were left unsettled by conciliation; the union withdrew issue 5, merged issues 3 and 4, so five were decided at two hearings on 14 June and 9 July 2012. [Para 26][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 22][Para 23] The union held written authorisations from 194 workers. [Para 86][Para 185] The employer pays a $15 monthly attendance bonus, $5 of housing allowance from June 2012, free transport home, 2,000 riel of meal money for overtime between 16:00 and 18:00 and 1,500 riel more between 18:00 and 20:00. [Para 162][Para 163][Para 171][Para 223]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay 4,000 riel in meal money to workers who volunteer to work on Sundays and public holidays? [Para 87][Para 187]
2. Whether the employer must reduce the attendance bonus in proportion to the number of days a worker is absent on leave authorised by the employer? [Para 92][Para 227]
3. Whether the employer must take back the five dismissed workers — among them union leaders — and recognise the union leadership and stop intimidating and threatening them? [Para 97][Para 246][Para 248]
4. Whether the employer must pay a further $10 a month of housing and travel allowance, making $15? [Para 162][Para 297]
5. Whether the employer must pay 5,000 riel in meal money for overtime worked between 16:00 and 20:00? [Para 171][Para 303]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Sunday and holiday work is overtime, so 4,000 riel of meal money is due; the bonus should be cut only pro rata to authorised absence; the five dismissals were union discrimination and the leaders must be recognised; prices and rents have risen and zone factories pay $10 to $15; noon-to-8pm overtime merits 5,000 riel. [Para 89][Para 96][Para 166][Para 167][Para 173]

**Employer (respondent):** Sunday and holiday work is not overtime — only 8 hours, paid at 200% and 300%, above the law; a worker absent once loses the whole $15 except on annual or special leave; the five were not dismissed for union activity — some resigned with their statutory payments, others were stopped for insubordination; $5 plus free transport exceeds the law; it pays 3,500 riel meal money and will not raise it. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 225][Para 241][Para 273][Para 299][Para 304]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to reduce the attendance bonus pro rata to the days of leave it has authorised (issue 2), and refused to consider the claims for 4,000 riel holiday meal money, for reinstatement of the five workers and recognition of the leadership, for a further $10 housing and travel allowance, and for 5,000 riel of overtime meal money. [Para 309][Para 310][Para 312][Para 313][Para 314]

On representation, the panel held that the federation could act for the 194 authorising workers under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 — enough for the rights disputes, not for the interests disputes. [Para 183][Para 185][Para 186]

On issue 2 it held the bonus to be a wage under Article 103, and applied Articles 71(6) and 72: leave granted with the employer's consent suspends the contract, so no wage and therefore no bonus is due for those days. [Para 234][Para 235][Para 236][Para 237][Para 241] Being earned day by day, the bonus may be cut only in proportion to the days absent, as earlier awards ordered. [Para 242][Para 245]

On issue 1 it held that holiday work is not overtime: Article 137 fixes 8 hours a day and 48 a week, and Article 139 with Article 4 of Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999 gives a day's wage for holiday work rather than the 50% premium, as Award No. 115/08 held. [Para 191][Para 193][Para 195][Para 196][Para 198] Sunday work is different, Monday to Saturday being the normal week; but the notice guarantees only 2,000 riel or a free meal, and 4,000 riel exceeds it. [Para 210][Para 214][Para 216][Para 217] Both demands were therefore interests disputes. [Para 199][Para 218]

On the interests disputes (issues 1, 4, 5) the panel applied Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, under which an interests award replaces the collective agreement for a year, and Article 277, which requires a union to be registered; only a most representative union may bring such a claim. [Para 202][Para 203][Para 204] The branch was unregistered and 194 of 950 workers had authorised the federation, so it lacked the standing to bring those claims, which were not considered. [Para 205][Para 206][Para 207][Para 302][Para 308]

On issue 3 the panel took the five separately. [Para 248] Two were absent from the hearing and had signed no authorisation, so their claims were refused. [Para 247][Para 249][Para 250] A third resigned in writing on 26 May 2012 and took his terminal payments; the claim of pressure had no date, time or witness, and his letter promised to stop unlawful strikes. [Para 251][Para 255][Para 257][Para 258] For the last two, the 45-day protection of Article 293 and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 did not attach, because the panel could not establish when the union notified its candidacy: the letter was dated 22 June 2012 though the events occurred in May, witnesses disagreed whether the election was on 21 or 22 May, and the attendance records showed both present only on the morning of 21 May. [Para 264][Para 267][Para 269][Para 271][Para 274][Para 278] On discrimination, the evidence was that one refused the security gate, refused to sign for a phone charger and spoke against the company, and that the other was stopped for being his relative; the panel found no union discrimination. [Para 281][Para 282][Para 285] Article 73 allows early termination of a fixed-term contract with compensation, which had been paid, so the panel found the obligation discharged and refused reinstatement. [Para 293][Para 294][Para 295][Para 296]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An attendance bonus is a wage earned by attendance: leave granted with the employer's consent suspends the contract, and the bonus may be reduced only in proportion to the days absent. Work on a public holiday is not overtime and earns a day's wage under Article 139 and Prakas No. 10, whereas work on the weekly rest day is overtime. A demand above the guaranteed minimum is an interests dispute the Council will not consider unless the union is registered and most representative. A candidate's 45-day protection requires proof of the election date and the notification. [Para 196][Para 214][Para 242][Para 206][Para 278]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations on the difference between holiday pay and the 50% overtime premium, and on the purpose of Article 43 in replacing the collective agreement for a year, are explanatory rather than necessary to the orders. [Para 197][Para 204]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The corporate name is OCR-garbled and printed in several forms ("Bino E B Peril", "Bino E B Feril"), so only "Bino" is reliable. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 83]
- The notification of the union election is dated 22 June 2012 while the events it concerns occurred in May, and the panel itself records that the month was altered on the letter. [Para 137][Para 139][Para 271]
- The attendance records show presence only on the morning of 21 May, though the union pleaded work on 22 May; twelve supporting statements were found unreliable, being written by one person, undated, and listing a woman as male. [Para 119][Para 146][Para 147][Para 160]
- The claim for issue 4 is pleaded both as a further $10 and as a total of $15, and the operative paragraph orders refusal of $15. [Para 165][Para 297][Para 313]
- Sentences are duplicated wholesale by OCR at [Para 43], [Para 46], [Para 208] and [Para 311].
- Latin debris interrupts the Khmer text throughout ("Mad-", "Mark", "Artica", "Sea", "Lig", "AKP", "Sin"), and page numbers and stray years ("2017") split sentences. [Para 10][Para 27][Para 191][Para 215][Para 220][Para 227]
- The signature block is corrupted: the worker-party arbitrator's name is printed twice and the chair's signature line reads "vecebon". [Para 316]
