# Award No. 04/12-USA Fully Field — USA Fully Field (Cambodia) Garments Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 6 February 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Suonty, Tuon Siphann, chair Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 139936

## 1. Facts
USA Fully Field (Cambodia) Garments Co., Ltd is a garment factory on National Road 2, Meanchey District, Phnom Penh, operating since 1999 with about 618 workers, 592 of them women. [Para 28] [Para 29] The claimant is the Local Union of FTUWKC, registered 14 December 2004, representing 90 workers and holding no certificate of most representative status (MRS). [Para 31] Its complaint of 8 December 2011 raised eight demands; conciliation on 28 December 2011 settled two, non-conciliation report No. 022 KB/RK/VK dated 5 January 2012 transmitted six, and the hearing of 17 January 2012 settled one more. [Para 10] [Para 12] [Para 13] [Para 15] Both parties signed the MoU of 28 September 2010 and chose binding arbitration of rights disputes, non-binding for interests disputes. [Para 16] [Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay 6,500 riel instead of 5,900 riel as overtime payment and meal allowance for overtime from 6:00 to 9:00 p.m.? [Para 32]
2. Whether the employer must offer undetermined-duration contracts to probationary workers who complete the two-month probation? [Para 38]
3. Whether the employer must pay an additional US$ 3 attendance bonus? [Para 42]
4. Whether the employer must provide a monthly US$ 10 transportation or accommodation allowance, or build housing and pay its electricity and water? [Para 46]
5. Whether the employer must provide a US$ 5 incentive bonus? [Para 49]
6. Whether the employer must provide an additional US$ 5 milk allowance to women returning from maternity leave? [Para 55]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that continuous overtime from 4:00 to 9:00 p.m. deserves an extra 600 riel; that successive three-month contracts create insecurity and deny seniority rights; that rising costs and a US$ 10 bonus elsewhere justify more; that a subcontractor earning from frequent overtime can afford US$ 5 incentive and US$ 5 extra milk money. [Para 35] [Para 40] [Para 41] [Para 44] [Para 49] [Para 58]
**Employer (respondent):** that it is a subcontracting company for Sun Tex with no direct buyers and little profit; that it pays 5,900 riel and issues fixed-duration contracts under Articles 67 and 73; that it complies with Notification No. 041/11 on the US$ 7 attendance bonus and recruits workers living nearby; and that Award 155/09-USA already allowed it to end the incentive bonus. [Para 37] [Para 44] [Para 45] [Para 49] [Para 51] [Para 52]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 2 rejected, and on issue 6 the employer was ordered to set up a day-care centre (failing which, to pay external centre charges on receipts) and the extra US$ 5 milk allowance declined; issues 1, 3, 4 and 5 declined as interests disputes. Issues 2 and 6 were binding; the rest non-binding. [Para 115] [Para 116] [Para 117] [Para 119]

The panel classed as rights disputes only issues 2 and 6, because they touch contract formation and Article 186 of the Labour Law, and treated the rest as interests disputes exceeding the law's requirements. [Para 61] [Para 62]
On issue 1, applying the definition in Awards 05/11-M & V (Branch 1) and 13/11-Gold Kamvimex, it found no agreement and no legal basis, then declined for want of MRS, following Awards 02/11-Pou Yuen and 66/11-In Han Sung; Clause 43 of Prakas No. 099 means an interests award becomes a one-year collective agreement binding all workers, which only a most-representative union may seek. [Para 60] [Para 66] [Para 68] [Para 70]
On issue 2 it reasoned through Articles 65 and 311 of the Labour Law and Articles 664, 336, 337 and 342 of the Civil Code: a contract needs matching intentions, and an acceptance that alters the offer is a new offer. The workers' wish for an undetermined-duration contract did not match the employer's three-month offer, so it was a counter-offer the employer was free to reject; neither side can force a contract type on the other. [Para 72] [Para 75] [Para 77] [Para 79] [Para 83] [Para 84] [Para 85]
On issue 6, Article 186 requires any enterprise employing at least one hundred women to set up a nursing room and day-care centre, and following Award 81/09-Wonrex, where it cannot, women with children over eighteen months may use an outside centre at the employer's expense on receipts; a voluntary US$ 5 monthly payment does not discharge that duty. [Para 105] [Para 107] [Para 110] [Para 111] [Para 112]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An enterprise employing one hundred or more women must provide a nursing room and day-care centre; paying a monthly cash sum in lieu does not satisfy Article 186, and the substitute obligation is reimbursement of an external centre's charges against receipts. Separately, a worker's acceptance that substitutes an undetermined-duration contract for the fixed-duration contract offered is a counter-offer under Article 342 of the Civil Code, which the employer may refuse, so no party can compel the other to adopt a contract type or duration it did not offer. A demand above the legal minimum, absent agreement, is an interests dispute that a union without MRS has no standing to bring. [Para 70] [Para 85] [Para 111]

**Obiter dicta:** that an award settling an interests dispute operates as a one-year collective agreement for all workers at the enterprise, so that the right to strike cannot be used to revise an unexpired agreement. [Para 70]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Paragraph 12 says two of eight issues were conciliated and paragraph 15 that one more was resolved at the hearing, which should leave five, yet six issues are reasoned and decided. [Para 12] [Para 15] [Para 32] The non-conciliation report is cited as "No. 022 KB/RKIVK", "No. 022 KB/RK/VK" and "No. 022 KB/RK/VK". [Para 9] [Para 13] [Para 26] The employer's commercial registration is dated 15 March 2012, after the award date of 6 February 2012, and one overtime pay slip is dated 11 January 2011 where the others are 2012. [Para 1] [Para 24] The issue 6 milk-allowance paragraph duplicates itself ("The workers demand that the employer provide an additional US$ 5"), and the OCR drops "th" throughout and splits "day-care" as "day. care". [Para 55] [Para 105] Individual workers, union officers and the employer's representative are reduced to initials per convention.
