# Award No. 209/12 — M&V International Garment (Kampong Chhnang) (Arbitration Council, 14 November 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea chair; Mar Samborna appointed by the employer; Liv Sovanna appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 140204

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment factory in Kampong Chhnang province, a branch of M&V International, reported at conciliation as employing about 5,951 workers and at the hearing about 4,900, some 80% of them women. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 47] The Cambodia Workers' Union Federation, through its M&V 4 factory union, filed three demands; conciliation on 18 October 2012 left all three unresolved, and at the hearing the third — removal of a named male administrative officer — was settled, leaving Issues 1 and 2. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 14][Para 15] Issue 1 asked the employer to comply with the Council's earlier Award No. 163/12 on pro-rata deduction of the attendance bonus for days of authorized leave. [Para 2][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel has jurisdiction to decide a claim that the employer must implement the Council's earlier Award No. 163/12 on pro-rata deduction of the attendance bonus? [Para 51][Para 53]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must build a nursery and a breastfeeding room under Article 186 of the Labour Law, or may instead pay a cash allowance in lieu? [Para 58][Para 61]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer had not applied Award No. 163/12, and the union gave notice of a strike on 5 October 2012 for that reason; the factory had never had a nursery or nursing room although the law and the Prakas require one, and if the employer would not build them it should pay a monthly allowance instead. [Para 34][Para 45][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** it had already complied with the earlier award, and in any event Notification No. 230/12 took effect on 1 September 2012, releasing it from an award that had enforced the older Notification No. 041/12; on the nursery, it had the space and the means to build one and was willing to do so, but would not pay cash in substitution. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 48][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel declined to consider Issue 1 for want of jurisdiction, and ordered the employer to build a nursery and a breastfeeding room; no cash allowance was ordered. [Para 68][Para 69]

On Issue 1, the panel first held the claim to be a rights dispute, since it rested on a decision already issued rather than on a new benefit. [Para 52] It then turned to Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004: Article 40 gives each party eight calendar days to object to an award, and Article 46 provides that once that period has run and a party refuses to comply, the other party may ask the competent court to recognize and enforce the award. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] The Council, the panel concluded, has no jurisdiction to hear objections or complaints about the implementation of its own awards, and so refused to consider the demand. [Para 56][Para 57] It also recorded that the earlier award was dated 31 August 2012, not 14 August 2012 as the demand stated. [Para 44]

On Issue 2, Article 186 of the Labour Law requires any enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a nursing room on or near the premises and a nursery as well; where the enterprise cannot itself run a nursery for children over eighteen months, the mother may place the child in an outside nursery at the employer's expense, on production of the invoice. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 63][Para 64] Because the employer conceded it had both the space and the means, the duty was to build, and the panel ordered it to do so. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] The workers' alternative claim for an allowance was not reached, the panel confining itself to the demand for facilities since the employer had elected to provide them. [Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The Council will not hear a complaint that a party has failed to carry out one of its awards: once the eight-day objection period under Prakas No. 099 has expired, recognition and enforcement lie with the competent court, not with a fresh arbitration. An employer that employs at least 100 women and has the space and means to do so must build a nursery and nursing room; the duty converts into paying outside nursery fees only where the employer cannot provide a nursery itself. [Para 56][Para 61][Para 63]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's treatment of the outside-nursery alternative — reimbursement on production of the nursery's invoice, following Award No. 81/09 — was not applied, the employer having undertaken to build on site. [Para 64][Para 66]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The earlier award is dated two ways: 14 August 2012 where the demand is recited, and 31 August 2012 where the panel examines it. [Para 2][Para 44][Para 51] The workforce is given as about 5,951 from the conciliation report and as about 4,900 at the hearing. [Para 31][Para 32] The Article 186 passage is corrupted at the point where the number of nursing rooms is stated, and one line of the Issue 2 reasoning is garbled beyond recovery. [Para 58][Para 60][Para 61] Name defects: the demand for removal named a male administrative officer of the company in full — redacted here as a role; the employer's representatives and the union officers are given as roles. [Para 4][Para 1][Para 2] The signing block and page numbering are partly lost, leaving no legible signature for the chair. [Para 71]
