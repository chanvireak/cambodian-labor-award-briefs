# Award No. 06/12-Ou Seang — Ou Seang Gamen (អូសេអង់ ហ្គាមិន) (Arbitration Council, 16 February 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, chair Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 139938

## 1. Facts
Ou Seang Gamen, a garment factory at Trapeang Thloeng, Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1999 and employs about 2,700 workers. [Para 1] [Para 54] The claimant is the Cambodian Workers Union at the Ou Seang factory, which reports 351 members but collects dues from only 301, and holds no certificate of most representative status (MRS). [Para 55] [Para 56] [Para 57] Its complaint of 5 December 2011 raised seven demands; conciliation on 4 January 2012 left six, one more was settled at the hearing, and five remained for decision. [Para 19] [Para 20] [Para 25] The hearing was held on 19 January 2012 at 2:00 p.m. and both parties attended. [Para 18] [Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must let workers draw on annual leave before completing one year of service? [Para 58]
2. Whether the employer must maintain wages and benefits, and reduce the attendance bonus only pro rata, where the worker's sick leave is certified by a private clinic or hospital as well as by a state hospital? [Para 65] [Para 69]
3. Whether the employer must remove Mr L, its head of administration, from employment? [Para 75]
4. Whether the employer must pay each worker a US$ 10 monthly accommodation allowance? [Para 86]
5. Whether the employer must open the factory's rear gate whenever workers work overtime from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m.? [Para 91]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that a worker who has not served a year but needs special leave should be allowed to use next year's annual leave without losing wages or the attendance bonus; that certificates from private clinics licensed by the Ministry of Health must be accepted; that the head of administration interferes in union work, leaks union information and treats unions unequally; that rents, transport and food prices justify US$ 10; and that workers' rented rooms lie behind the factory and the rear gate would shorten a one-kilometre walk. [Para 61] [Para 68] [Para 75] [Para 76] [Para 87] [Para 95] [Para 97]
**Employer (respondent):** that annual leave is earned only after a year and special leave is deducted or repaid; that only state hospital certificates are recognised, private ones leading to loss of wages and the whole attendance bonus; that Mr L committed no fault and all unions enjoy equal freedom; that it cannot pay US$ 10; and that the rear gate serves generators and fuel drums and is dangerous at night. [Para 63] [Para 67] [Para 74] [Para 79] [Para 92] [Para 93]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 1 rejected save for a repayment order, issue 2 granted, issues 3 and 5 rejected, and issue 4 declined as an interests dispute; issues 1, 2, 3 and 5 being rights disputes, binding. [Para 205] [Para 207] [Para 208] [Para 209] [Para 211]

The panel first classified the demands as rights or interests disputes on the definition used in Awards 05/11-M & V (Branch 1) and 13/11-Goul Kamvimex, treating only issue 4 as an interests dispute. [Para 101] [Para 104] [Para 105]
On issue 1, Article 167 of the Labour Law confines paid annual leave to workers who have completed a year, and Article 171 lets the employer grant special paid leave, deducting it from annual leave or requiring repayment; Prakas No. 267 of 11 October 2001 caps special leave at seven days a year, requires repayment within 90 days, at no more than 10 hours a day or 54 hours a week, at the normal hourly rate. [Para 107] [Para 110] [Para 114] [Para 116] [Para 118] The panel therefore refused the borrowing of future annual leave but ordered that workers under one year who take special leave repay the employer on those terms. [Para 121] [Para 126] [Para 206]
On issue 2, Article 71(3) suspends the contract during certified sick absence, and the factory's internal rules, Article 4 point 6, promise paid leave on an "official doctor's" certificate; following Award 102/07 on that phrase, an official doctor is one trained and licensed by the Ministry of Health, whether practising in a state or a licensed private facility. [Para 132] [Para 134] [Para 135] [Para 137] An employer that finds a rule inconvenient must amend or annul it, not keep it and refuse to perform it; claiming the rule mistranslates the law was no answer while it stood unamended. [Para 141] [Para 143] The panel ordered full wages preserved and the attendance bonus reduced only pro rata for sick days certified by a private clinic or hospital. [Para 160] [Para 207]
On issue 3, the head of administration has his own employment contract with the employer; under Articles 65 of the Labour Law and 664 and 311 of the Civil Code only the parties to a contract may vary or end it, and workers are third parties. [Para 162] [Para 166] [Para 167] [Para 174] Following Awards 54/08 and 124/10, workers may demand removal only on proof that the person is dangerous and cannot be kept; the panel found no such evidence and no unequal treatment of the unions. [Para 169] [Para 177] [Para 178] [Para 179]
On issue 4, no law or agreement requires the allowance, so it is an interests dispute, and a union with 301 members out of 2,700 and no MRS cannot bring one. [Para 183] [Para 184] [Para 192] [Para 193] [Para 194]
On issue 5, Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to organise the enterprise lawfully and reasonably; the rear gate opens onto generators and fuel drums, the employer already allows daytime use, and the demand serves convenience only, so it was refused. [Para 195] [Para 198] [Para 201] [Para 203] [Para 204]
Arbitrator Ing Sothy dissented on issue 2, on the ground that a bonus belongs to the "bonus owner", who alone sets its conditions. [Para 215] [Para 222]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An internal work rule that promises paid sick leave on an "official doctor's" certificate must be applied as written: a doctor licensed by the Ministry of Health qualifies wherever he practises, so an employer cannot refuse private-clinic certificates while leaving the rule in force, its remedy being to amend or annul the rule. Statutory paid annual leave is earned only after a year, and a worker who takes special leave earlier must repay it under Prakas No. 267 rather than draw on leave not yet earned. Workers, being strangers to the contract between the employer and a supervisor, cannot compel that supervisor's dismissal without proving him dangerous to the workforce. [Para 143] [Para 121] [Para 169]

**Obiter dicta:** that a demand above the legal minimum and outside any agreement is an interests dispute only a union with most representative status may bring, an award on which stands as a one-year collective agreement binding all workers. [Para 184] [Para 190] [Para 191]
