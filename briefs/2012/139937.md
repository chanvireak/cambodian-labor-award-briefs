# Award No. 08/12-Roo Hsing — Roo Hsing Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 9 February 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Nan, chair Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 139937

## 1. Facts
Roo Hsing Garment Co., Ltd is a garment factory at Toul Sanke, Russei Keo District, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] [Para 2] The Khmer Workers Pride Federation Union (KWPFU) complained by telephone on 13 December 2011; conciliation left four issues, transmitted by non-conciliation report No. 054 KB/RK/VK dated 16 January 2012. [Para 9] [Para 10] [Para 11] The hearing was fixed for 30 January 2012 at 8:30 a.m. and both parties were notified on 24 January 2012, but the union did not attend and gave no proper reason, saying only that the workers were unable to come; the employer attended. [Para 11] [Para 12] [Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide a monthly US$ 30 milk allowance? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must provide a 4,000 riel meal allowance for work on public holidays and Sundays? [Para 2]
3. Whether the employer must provide a monthly US$ 15 accommodation allowance to each worker? [Para 3]
4. Whether the employer must refrain from transferring workers from one section to another? [Para 5]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** no position was argued; the union was absent and filed no evidence, so its demands survive only as recorded in the non-conciliation report. [Para 15] [Para 17] [Para 37]
**Employer (respondent):** that it refused the US$ 30 milk allowance on the basis of its existing practice, refused the holiday and Sunday meal allowance and the US$ 15 accommodation allowance outright, and refused to stop transfers because its production needs require them. [Para 2] [Para 3] [Para 4] [Para 6]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 08/12-Roo Hsing without deciding any of the four issues, holding that KWPFU had dropped its claim. [Para 38] [Para 39]

The panel asked only whether it could proceed at all. Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 dated 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Procedural Rules allow it either to continue in the absence of a party or to terminate by award, provided notification was proper. [Para 17] [Para 18]
Following Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M & V (Branch 3), 138/08-Maxlin and 11/11-June Textile, it applied three conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and no proper reason for absence. [Para 19] [Para 20]
On the first, the Secretariat had telephoned KWPFU and informed it of the hearing, so notification was proper. [Para 21] [Para 23] On the second, "appear before the arbitration panel" means both presence and participation in the whole process — introduction and conflict disclosure, explanation of the process and confirmation of issues, conciliation, and arbitration — and KWPFU attended none of the four steps. [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 29] [Para 30] On the third, the union said only that it would not attend and left the matter to the Council, which is no proper reason. [Para 31] [Para 34]
All three conditions being met, the panel held it was empowered to issue an award. [Para 35] [Para 36] It then noted that a claimant must argue its claim with reasons and evidence, that KWPFU had been given ample opportunity and passed it up, and concluded that the union had dropped its claim. [Para 37] [Para 38]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union, properly notified, neither appears nor takes part in any stage of the hearing and offers no proper reason, the panel may close the case by award and treat the claim as dropped, without weighing the merits — the burden of presenting reasons and evidence lies on the claimant, and non-appearance forfeits it. Proper notification is assessed on the Secretariat's actual contact with the party, not on formal service alone. [Para 19] [Para 23] [Para 37] [Para 38]

**Obiter dicta:** that the panel retains a discretion to proceed in the absent party's stead rather than close the case, a course it did not take here. [Para 17] [Para 36]
