# Award No. 134/12 — Top World Garment (Cambodia) Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 26 July 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Virak (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 140150

## 1. Facts
Top World Garment (Cambodia) Ltd., registered on 30 December 1999, is a garment factory at Chrey Ampil, Kbal Koh, Meanchey, Phnom Penh, employing about 800 to 830 workers, and appeared through its administration chief, Mr L [Para 1][Para 43]. The Cambodian Democratic Garment Workers' Union Confederation filed on 30 May 2012 for better working conditions; conciliation ended on 28 June 2012 with 4 of 10 demands settled and 6 left over [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21]. At the hearing of 12 July 2012 both sides attended and no further point was settled [Para 18][Para 24]. Only two workers appeared for the complainant, and the union's registration certificate on file dates from 23 July 2009 [Para 34][Para 44].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the two workers present were lawfully recognised union leaders for the current term and so able to represent the complainant? [Para 96][Para 100]
2. Whether the company must pay a housing and travel allowance of USD 15 a month to each worker? [Para 102]
3. Whether the company must contribute 4,000 riel a day towards the midday meal? [Para 113]
4. Whether the company must pay USD 20 a month towards infant milk for mothers? [Para 117]
5. Whether the company must raise the regular attendance bonus from USD 7 to USD 15 a month? [Para 121][Para 126]
6. Whether the company must pay a USD 5 a month skills bonus to every skilled worker? [Para 130][Para 137]
7. Whether the company must install air conditioning in the factory clinic? [Para 139][Para 157]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** prices have risen and wages do not cover daily needs, so the company should pay USD 15 for housing and travel, 4,000 riel for lunch, USD 20 for infant milk until the child is 18 months, and a USD 5 skills premium; other factories in the Svay Rieng special economic zone already pay USD 10 to 15 [Para 47][Para 48][Para 61][Para 62][Para 78][Para 79]. For the clinic, they said the room is hot and airless and only an air conditioner will do [Para 91][Para 95].

**Employer (respondent):** no law requires these payments, and the company will apply the Labour Advisory Committee's statement of USD 7 for travel and housing from 1 September 2012 and USD 10 attendance bonus from the same date [Para 49][Para 58][Para 65][Para 71][Para 73]. On skills, the company already pays USD 5 to 28 workers selected by section heads, which is its management prerogative [Para 80][Para 81][Para 84]. On the clinic it offered to refit the room with extractor fans and one extra fan [Para 93][Para 145].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all six demands were refused, four of them without any examination of the merits [Para 158][Para 163].

First the panel tested who was before it: the union's certificate was dated 23 July 2009, a two-year term had long expired under Prakas No. 305, and the union never filed the ministry letter recognising a new leadership, so the two individuals could not represent members and the panel considered the demands for those two workers alone [Para 96][Para 98][Para 99][Para 101].

On the housing and travel allowance, the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, no collective agreement and no agreement between the parties fixing USD 15, so the demand was a dispute over interests rather than rights [Para 102][Para 103]. Invoking Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, it reasoned that an interest award replaces the collective agreement for a year and binds all workers, and that only a union with majority representativeness — or several unions together holding more than half the workforce — may bring such a claim [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107]. No representative union existed here and only two workers were complainants, so it declined to rule [Para 109][Para 111][Para 112]. The lunch, infant-milk and attendance-bonus demands were disposed of on the same ground by cross-reference [Para 114][Para 115][Para 118][Para 127][Para 129].

The skills bonus failed on evidence: the complainant never identified how many workers were skilled enough to qualify, despite promising the list by 16 July 2012, and the burden of proof lies on the party making the allegation [Para 130][Para 132][Para 133][Para 137].

On the clinic, the panel applied Article 229, which requires sanitary and healthful conditions and adequate ventilation, and Article 2 on management prerogative [Para 139][Para 141][Para 148]. It found the room about 16 square metres with three fans, accepted that extractor fans plus one more fan would remove the heat and stuffiness, and held that the employer's choice was lawful and reasonable; the workers offered nothing to show that only an air conditioner could work [Para 143][Para 152][Para 155][Para 156].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for a benefit that no law, collective agreement or agreement between the parties provides is a dispute over interests, which the panel will only adjudicate where the claimant union — alone or with others — represents more than half the workers in the enterprise; absent that representativeness the claim is refused unexamined [Para 103][Para 107][Para 111]. Where Article 229 is in issue, a lawful and reasonable measure that actually removes the unhealthy condition satisfies the employer's duty, and the workers' preference for a specific installation does not [Para 153][Para 155].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that a majority union's interest award displaces the collective agreement for a year and removes other workers' right to strike on the same subject was not applied, since no such union existed [Para 106].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The clinic is measured two ways — about 14 square metres at first description and about 16 square metres in the reasoning [Para 88][Para 143]. The documentary list repeats items under the same numbers and duplicates the conciliation minutes [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]. OCR runs of Latin garbage break the text at lines 16, 52, 96 and 147, and the quotation of Article 2 in line 147 is partly destroyed [Para 16][Para 52][Para 96][Para 147]. The company appears variously as "ថប វើលដ៍" and "ថប់ វើលដ៍", and the file also carries stray letters such as "Li" and "Chy" inside the operative paragraphs [Para 43][Para 112][Para 120]. The union's registration date is given in two places consistently, but its 2009 term is the pivot of the award and rests on a partly corrupted paragraph [Para 97].
