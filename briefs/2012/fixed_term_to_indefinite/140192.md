# Award No. 195/12 — Vy Py (WVP) (Arbitration Council, 25 October 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Socheath, employer-appointed; Tuon Siphann, worker-appointed; Pen Bunhea, chair) · Doc ID 140192

## 1. Facts
The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia brought two demands against Vy Py (WVP): that the company cease discriminating against the formation of the union and against union activity, and that it take back the union's three leaders — its president, vice-president and secretary — in normal work [Para 1][Para 3]. Conciliation on 20 September 2012 settled nothing on either point, and the file reached the Council on 28 September 2012 [Para 6][Para 10][Para 11]. The hearing was fixed for 10 October 2012 at 14:00 and neither party attended, nor sent a representative [Para 8][Para 12][Para 13]. The only document before the panel was the non-conciliation report, because neither side filed evidence [Para 14].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel should nonetheless close the file by award, or continue the proceedings, when both parties properly summoned have failed to appear? [Para 21][Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Absent from the hearing; the union explained afterwards that the workers concerned had already been paid their wages and for that reason did not come [Para 38].
**Employer (respondent):** Also absent; the company explained that it did not attend because the workers had already agreed with the company and been paid [Para 39].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 195/12 by award, without ruling on either demand [Para 42][Para 43].

The panel put the question to itself as whether the arbitration should continue, and answered it under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed arbitration rules, both of which allow the panel either to proceed in a party's absence or to terminate the proceedings by award, provided the party was properly notified of the date, time and place [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]. It took from its earlier awards — No. 30/05 (Moria), No. 16/07 (Lotus Tex), No. 27/07 (IM & W 3), No. 95/08 (Young Va I), No. 132/08 (GHG), No. 138/08 (Malin), No. 151/08 (Wil Sen), No. 09/09 (Nim Bus Tech Thai), No. 01/10 (Kinner Text) and No. 30/10 (Global Apparel) — three conditions for closing a file: proper notice, non-appearance, and no valid reason for the absence [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26].

On notice, the secretariat issued written invitations No. 1019 and No. 1020, both dated 3 October 2012, one to the employer and one to the workers, in its usual form, and also contacted both sides by telephone about the date, time and place [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]. The panel therefore found condition one satisfied [Para 31]. On condition two, neither the company nor the union appeared and neither appointed a federation or lawyer to represent it, so condition two was satisfied [Para 34][Para 35]. On condition three, the panel recorded the explanations offered — wages already paid, and a prior agreement with the company — and held that neither was a valid reason for staying away, so condition three was satisfied too [Para 40][Para 41].

Having found all three conditions met, the panel closed the case rather than deciding the merits, and it made no finding of fact on either demand, stating expressly that it could find none because no hearing took place [Para 19][Para 42]. The result is that the demands for an end to anti-union discrimination and for the reinstatement of the three leaders were left undetermined [Para 1][Para 3].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties have been properly notified of the hearing and neither appears nor appoints a representative, and neither offers a valid reason for the absence, the panel may terminate the arbitration by award without examining the demands on their merits [Para 21][Para 22][Para 41].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's recording of the parties' explanations — that wages had been paid and that an agreement had been reached — as facts it noted but did not treat as a valid excuse for non-appearance [Para 38][Para 39].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
No facts were found at all, so the file contains no account of the alleged discrimination or of the three dismissals beyond the one-line demand [Para 3][Para 19]. The demand list breaks off mid-sentence where the company's address is run into the text, so the names of the three leaders survive only inside a garbled passage. The employer is named in different forms in the header and in the body, and the two parties' registered addresses are printed identically, which cannot be right. The number of claimants appears as four employees where the demand speaks of three leaders, the section heading for the ministry's documents is duplicated, and the award date is printed with a doubled year in the same line as the case number.
