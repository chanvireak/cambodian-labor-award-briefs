# Award No. 62/12 — TASK (Trong and Development of Poor Communities) (Arbitration Council, 30 April 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sophanna, employer-appointed; Sin Kim Sean, worker-appointed; Kong Phalha, chair) · Doc ID 139988

## 1. Facts
TASK is a registered non-governmental organisation with 33 staff, funded by foreign donors and organised into separate projects. Five project staff — Ms S, Ms O, Ms K, Mr S and Ms T — had their fixed-term contracts terminated in November 2011 after their projects closed. Conciliation at the Ministry of Labour failed on six demands, which the panel merged into one issue. [Para 1][Para 87][Para 89][Para 96]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer owes the five dismissed employees severance pay, three months' notice pay, accrued annual leave, emotional-distress damages, study support of USD 1,800 for Ms O and outstanding maternity pay for Ms S and Ms T? [Para 2][Para 7][Para 90]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The five had worked continuously for years on rolling yearly contracts, so their contracts were indefinite-term; the dismissals were therefore unlawful and entitled them to severance, contractual notice, leave, distress damages, the study reimbursement and the maternity balance. [Para 92][Para 100][Para 101]

**Employer (respondent):** The contracts were fixed-term one-year contracts that expired when donor funding was withdrawn and the projects closed; the employer gave the 15 days' notice required by law and had no money left after donors reclaimed project funds. [Para 96][Para 97][Para 98][Para 109]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered payment in lieu of notice of 1 month 15 days for Mr S, Ms K and Ms T and 1 month 3 days for Ms S and Ms O; dismissal damages of 90 days for Ms S, 30 days for Ms O, 180 days for Ms K, 75 days for Mr S and 135 days for Ms T; all outstanding annual leave for the five; and maternity balances of 1 month 2 days for Ms S and 3 days for Ms T. It dismissed the distress claim and the USD 1,800 study-support claim. [Para 209][Para 210][Para 211][Para 212][Para 213][Para 214]

On the contract type, Article 67 of the Labour Law caps a fixed-term contract at two years and provides that a renewal pushing the total beyond two years converts it into an indefinite-term contract. The panel found all five had been renewed yearly with no break between contracts and all had more than two years' seniority, so their contracts were indefinite-term. [Para 136][Para 142][Para 143][Para 144]

On the termination, the panel rejected the employer's funding argument: donors reclaiming funds does not make a dismissal lawful, and the employer had promised but failed to file written evidence of the reclamation. Because the contracts were indefinite-term, the dismissals required a valid reason and the notice actually given (15 days) fell short of the three months the contracts stipulated. [Para 109][Para 112][Para 113][Para 98][Para 99]

On annual leave, the employees did not quantify their remaining days, but the employer did not contest that leave remained, so the panel ordered payment for the balance. [Para 104][Para 105][Para 106]

On maternity pay, the panel found the women had taken 90 days' maternity leave but received only part of the statutory pay — Ms S was short 1 month 2 days and Ms T short 3 days — and ordered the balance. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 126][Para 208]

The study-support claim failed because Ms O produced no written approval from the director or board, only her own request letter; the distress claim was rejected outright. [Para 121][Para 212][Para 213]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Successive fixed-term contracts renewed without any gap, whose combined duration exceeds two years, convert by operation of Article 67 into an indefinite-term contract; terminating such a contract for project closure or donor withdrawal is an unlawful dismissal attracting severance and notice compensation. [Para 138][Para 144]

**Obiter dicta:** An employer's assertion that donors reclaimed all project funds does not excuse statutory severance, and an unproven assertion of that kind is given no weight. [Para 110][Para 113]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated 30 April 2012 in the header, while para 19 records that it was to issue on 09 April 2012 and was postponed by agreement to 30 April 2012. The staff table at para 96 is badly garbled by OCR, with dates and column labels interleaved. Para 135 says "5,00" employees where five are meant. The worker's name appears as "Ouk Li Vanna" at para 132 and "Ouk Vanna" elsewhere. Para 213 contains stray repeated years ("2022, 2019, 2019, 2019") inside the study-support figure. Para 208 renders the maternity balance as "នៅស ព័រ Mangan". The employer is also called "Trong and Development of Poor Communities" and "Trong and Development of Health of the Poor" in different documents.
