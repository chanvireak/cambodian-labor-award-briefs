# Award No. 37/12 — Soun Ousakamm Mean Hat Then (Arbitration Council, 14 March 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Sin Kim Sean, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 139959

## 1. Facts
The employer operates a factory at Ampil Leu village, Ampil commune, Kampong Siem district, Kampong Cham, and the claimant is the Free Trade Union of Workers of the Kingdom of Cambodia (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា) together with its local union at the enterprise. [Para 1] Of eight demands taken to conciliation, points 6 and 8 were settled there and point 3 was settled later, leaving five points unsettled; the non-conciliation report No. 09/12 KB.K of 16 February 2012 reached the Secretariat on 20 February 2012. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 13][Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] Both parties were listed as absent, and neither attended the hearing of 5 March 2012 at 2:00 p.m. or sent a representative. [Para 1][Para 21][Para 54]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must convert the fixed-duration contracts of workers who have worked more than two years into contracts of undetermined duration? [Para 4]
2. Whether the company must pay two months' compensation to workers on undetermined-duration contracts who resign? [Para 5]
3. Whether the company must apply point 1 of Article 5 of its internal rules, granting a bonus equal to one month's salary or more to workers with full attendance and no lateness or early departure? [Para 8]
4. Whether the company must pay termination indemnity of 15% of the wages received during the contract to workers on fixed-duration contracts? [Para 10]
5. Whether the company must pay seniority pay (ប្រាក់ អតីតភាព ការងារ) to workers whose fixed-duration contracts were renewed without any gap? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** No position was recorded. The union appeared at no stage of the hearing and supplied only the agreement concluded at the Garment Manufacturers Association office on 29 February 2012, which settled some points and left others, including point 1, open. [Para 29][Para 38][Para 39]
**Employer (respondent):** No position was recorded. The company was represented as absent on the face of the award and filed only the same 29 February 2012 agreement. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 54]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — no order was made on any of the five claims; the panel closed the file in case No. 37/12 (បិទ សំណុំរឿង) because both parties were absent from the hearing. [Para 59][Para 60]
The panel never reached the merits. It recorded that it could not establish the facts, since the union had handed over nothing but the 29 February 2012 agreement before the hearing. [Para 37][Para 38] It then applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council (21 April 2004), which allows a panel to continue in a party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award where that party, duly invited, fails to appear without valid reason. [Para 41] Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules attached to the same Prakas was read the same way, subject to the requirement that the party be properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 42][Para 43] Following a line of earlier awards (30/05-Mouria, 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-Im Vey, 95/08-Young Wah, 132/08-GHG, 138/08-Malin, 151/08-Well Sin, 09/09-Nim Bith Tech Thai, 01/10-Khinnetich, 30/10-Global Apparel), the panel identified three cumulative conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of any valid reason. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]
All three were satisfied. Invitations Nos. 221 KB/AK/VK/LKA to the employer and 222 KB/AK/VK/LKA to the workers, both dated 27 February 2012, were issued in writing in the Secretariat's ordinary procedure and confirmed by telephone, so notification was proper. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51] Neither party attended nor appointed a federation or lawyer to represent it, satisfying the second condition. [Para 54][Para 55] Neither gave any reason to the Secretariat, satisfying the third. [Para 57][Para 58] The panel accordingly closed the case rather than deciding the five claims. [Para 59]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties have been duly notified of the hearing date, time and place by written invitation confirmed by telephone, and neither appears or sends an authorised representative and neither offers a valid reason, the panel may close the arbitration by award under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 without determining the claims, leaving them unresolved rather than dismissed on their merits. [Para 41][Para 44][Para 59]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it could not find the facts because the union supplied only the 29 February 2012 agreement, which itself left some points unsettled. [Para 38][Para 39]

[Para 22][Para 23] The two conciliation tables are inconsistent: one records 2 points settled and 6 unsettled, the other 1 settled and 3 unsettled, and point 3 appears as both unsettled and settled.
