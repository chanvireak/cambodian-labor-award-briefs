# Award No. 59/12 — Win Singtex (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 March 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Sin Kim Sean, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139985

## 1. Facts
Win Singtex (Cambodia) Ltd is a garment factory in Chom Chao, Phnom Penh. On 7 March 2012 the Federation of Trade Unions (five unions) filed a complaint seeking improved working conditions; conciliation resolved 2 of 16 demands and the report on the 14 unresolved points reached the Council secretariat on 16 March 2012. On that same day the workers went on strike, and the Council issued Order No. 009/12 suspending the strike and requiring a return to work from 17 March 2012. The workers did not comply. [Para 1][Para 34][Para 36][Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration should continue where the workers remain on strike in defiance of the Council's order suspending the strike? [Para 49]

The 14 unresolved demands underlying the referral were: a $15 monthly travel and lodging allowance; a $10 monthly attendance bonus; a $308.5 social welfare fund; a union meeting room; paid leave for union leaders to attend seminars; proper procedure on factory relocation and closure; severance on closure or transfer; a 1,000 riel meal allowance for overtime beyond two hours; lawful termination procedure; a $20 monthly milk allowance for mothers; conversion of fixed-term contracts to undetermined duration; removal of three administrative staff; and continuation of the collective agreement. [Para 2][Para 6][Para 8][Para 17][Para 21][Para 23][Para 25][Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** all 14 demands stand as filed; the workers maintained the strike and, at the pre-hearing meeting, the union present confirmed that the workers would not follow the union's advice to stop, and still held their position. [Para 2][Para 10][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent):** the company can pay only $3 a month for travel and lodging, deducts 50% of the attendance bonus for an absence under one day and 100% from one day up, cannot provide a union room, and applies Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 on seminar leave; overtime from 16:00 to 18:00 follows Notification 041/11 and 1,000 riel an hour is paid only for overtime to 20:00. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 9][Para 11][Para 18]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in Case No. 59/12 against Win Singtex. [Para 61]

The panel framed a single procedural question, whether the arbitration should proceed, and answered it under Article 318 of the Labour Law and Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike, lockout or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — throughout the arbitration and to attend all meetings convened. [Para 49][Para 50]

It followed its own prior orders on the same point: Order 04-MSI of 29 January 2004, Order 18/04-Hotel of 22 April 2004, Order 77/06-Franco Nighting of 24 December 2005 and Case 110/11-Simanou, in each of which the Council declined to arbitrate while a strike continued. It found that the workers had shown no genuine intention to bring the collective dispute to the Council for settlement, and that the strike had not been called off despite the suspension order and the secretariat's telephone check on 19 March 2012. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]

The panel reached no view on the merits of the 14 demands, and received no evidence documents from either party. [Para 44][Para 48]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the Council has ordered it suspended, the panel will not conduct the arbitration, because a party that keeps up industrial action has not submitted the dispute to arbitration; proceedings may resume only once the strike ends. [Para 54][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the suspension duty extends to lockouts and any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, and both parties must attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The pre-hearing meeting date is printed as 20 March 2011 while every surrounding event is dated 2012 [Para 33]. The conciliation report number appears as 321 K.B/AK/VK in one place and 321/11 K.B/AK/VK in another [Para 36][Para 44]. Names are partly lost to OCR: the demand to remove the administrator and assistants reads "យ៉ង់ លោក Whan នៅ ស៊ីណា", and the strikethrough text in the strike narrative is garbled [Para 25][Para 37]. The award records that no documentary evidence was received from either party [Para 44].
