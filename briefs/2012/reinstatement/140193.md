# Award No. 201/12 — Sing Seung Yean (Arbitration Council, 5 November 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouchhun, employer-appointed; An Virak, worker-appointed; Aing Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 140193

## 1. Facts
The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia brought twelve demands against the Sing Seung Yean factory in Sen Sok district, Phnom Penh, of which two remained unresolved: that the company take back Mr C and Mr S in their work, and that it maintain wages and the regularity bonus during the strike days [Para 2][Para 4][Para 12]. The company's position was that it could not take the two back because both were still on probation, and that it could not pay the wages and bonus claimed [Para 3][Para 5]. Conciliation at the factory on 1 October 2012 failed on those two points and the file reached the Council on 5 October 2012 [Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]. The hearing was fixed for 19 October 2012 at 14:00; neither party attended and neither sent a representative [Para 10][Para 15]. Neither side filed any evidence, the non-conciliation report being the only document [Para 16].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel should close the file by award when both parties, properly summoned, failed to appear and gave no valid reason? [Para 22][Para 28]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia):** Absent from the hearing and from the earlier conciliation meeting at the panel; the file records no reason for their absence and no representative was appointed for them [Para 15][Para 35].
**Employer (respondent, Sing Seung Yean):** Also absent, having stated at conciliation only that it would not reinstate the two probationary workers and would not pay wages or the regularity bonus for the strike period [Para 3][Para 5][Para 15].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 201/12 by award, deciding nothing on the merits of the two demands [Para 41][Para 42].

The panel framed its question as whether all three conditions for closing a file were met, and took those conditions from Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed arbitration rules, under which the panel may either proceed in the absence of a party or terminate the proceedings by award, provided the party was properly notified of the date, time and place [Para 22][Para 23][Para 28]. It cited its earlier awards No. 30/05 (Moria), No. 16/07 (Lotus Tex), No. 27/07 (IM & W 3), No. 95/08 (Young Va I), No. 132/08 (GHG), No. 138/08 (Malin), No. 151/08 (Wil Sen), No. 09/09 (Nim Bus Tech Thai), No. 01/10 (Kinner Text) and No. 30/10 (Global Apparel) for the same three-condition test [Para 25][Para 26].

On the first condition the panel found that the secretariat had issued written invitations No. 1037 to the employer and No. 1038 to the workers, both dated 9 October 2012, in its usual form, and had also telephoned both sides about the hearing arrangements [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]. Condition one was therefore met [Para 32]. On the second, neither side appeared and neither appointed a federation or a lawyer to represent it, so condition two was met [Para 35][Para 36]. On the third, the panel found that neither party had given any valid reason to the secretariat for not attending, so condition three was met as well [Para 39][Para 40].

The panel did not reach the substance of the demand for reinstatement, and so made no ruling on whether probationary status justified refusing to take Mr C and Mr S back [Para 3][Para 41]. It likewise made no ruling on wages and the regularity bonus for the strike days [Para 4][Para 42]. It stated only that it could find no facts relating to the demands, since no hearing was held [Para 21].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties have been properly notified and neither appears nor appoints a representative, and no valid reason for the absence is given to the secretariat, the panel will close the file by award and will not decide the demands; proper notification is judged by the secretariat's standard written invitations, corroborated by telephone contact [Para 28][Para 31][Para 40].

**Obiter dicta:** none identified — the panel confined itself to the procedural test and expressly declined to make any finding of fact [Para 21][Para 41].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel recorded that it could find no facts at all, so the file contains nothing on the two dismissals beyond a one-line demand and the company's one-line refusal [Para 3][Para 21]. Several passages are printed twice in succession, in particular the ministry's document list and the paragraph on the second condition, and one date carries a doubled year. The factory is named in three different forms across the header, the report title and the operative paragraph, and the number of demands is given as twelve while only two are set out. The two workers' names survive only inside the demand sentence, and their probation status is asserted by the company without any contract being exhibited. [Para 16][Para 21][Para 41]
