# Award No. 108/12 — Gold Fame Inter Bright Six (Inter) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 2 July 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy — arbitrator for the employer party, Tun Si Phan — arbitrator for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea — chair) · Doc ID 140136

## 1. Facts
Gold Fame is a garment and apparel factory at Kampong Pring village, Setbo commune, Sa'ang district, Kandal, employing about 5,000 workers. [Para 44][Para 45] The factory-level union of the Democratic Union of Khmer Apparel Workers (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ) is the claimant; it had raised its monthly dues from 1,000 to 2,000 riel and on 13 September 2011 sent the employer a list of 1,610 new members with thumbprints, asking that 2,000 riel be deducted from each. [Para 46][Para 48][Para 50][Para 74] The employer refused, saying the list carried about 600 duplicate names and that some workers denied having consented or denied membership at all. [Para 53] At the hearing the union undertook to rebuild the list, and on 27 June 2012 filed names and thumbprints for 1,362 workers, a list the employer did not contest. [Para 76][Para 77][Para 78]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must deduct 2,000 riel a month from the wages of the 1,362 workers claimed as new union members and pay the amount over to the Democratic Union of Khmer Apparel Workers? [Para 47][Para 67]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Article 129(2) of the Labour Law and section 5 of Prakas No. 305 of 2001 oblige the employer to make the deduction once a proper written request is made, and the 1,362 thumbprints now filed are voluntary and unopposed. [Para 62][Para 66][Para 68][Para 70]

**Employer (respondent):** it withheld the deduction because the first list was unreliable — roughly 600 names duplicated, workers saying they had thumbprinted for other purposes or had never joined — and it asked the union to re-verify the membership; it would deduct once the list and thumbprints were genuine and clear. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to deduct 2,000 riel from the wages of union-member workers and pay it to the Democratic Union of Khmer Apparel Workers, but only for those workers whose thumbprints it found clear and correct. [Para 83][Para 84]

On the character of the claim, the panel held that a rights dispute is one turning on an entitlement written in a law, agreement or collective convention, and that this demand fell within Article 129 of the Labour Law, which obliges the employer to deduct union dues where a valid request is made — hence a rights dispute, not an interests dispute. [Para 63][Para 66] Because both parties had signed the Memorandum of Understanding of 28 September 2010 making rights-dispute awards binding, the decision takes effect immediately and cannot be appealed. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 85]

On the merits, Article 129(2) permits a worker to authorise deduction of wages to pay dues to the union of which he or she is a member, the authorisation to be in writing and revocable at any time, and section 5 of Prakas No. 305 requires the written request to reach the employer at least 15 days before the pay day. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71] Following Award No. 60/05 (Evergreen), the panel described the purpose of the writing requirement as protecting each worker against deduction contrary to his or her will and protecting the company against mistakes in making deductions. [Para 72][Para 73]

The panel then weighed the evidence. The union's first list of 1,610 was compromised by duplication and disputed consent, which justified the employer's refusal to act on it. [Para 74][Para 75] The replacement list of 1,362 was not contested by the employer, and the employer had promised at the hearing to deduct if the names and thumbprints proved genuine. [Para 59][Para 78] Counting the prints itself, however, the panel found some clear and correct but others torn in half, faded almost beyond recognition, blurred, or printed twice over one another so that identity could not be fixed. [Para 81][Para 82] It therefore confined the order to the verifiable prints rather than grant the claim for all 1,362. [Para 83]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer is obliged under Article 129(2) of the Labour Law and section 5 of Prakas No. 305 of 2001 to deduct union dues from a member's wage once a written, revocable authorisation reaches it at least 15 days before pay day; but the panel will enforce the obligation only worker by worker, on authorisations it can itself verify, and a thumbprint that is torn, faded, blurred or superimposed proves nothing and supports no deduction. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 81][Para 83]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's explanation of why the writing requirement exists — shielding workers from unwilling deduction and employers from error — is general reasoning drawn from an earlier award rather than a step in the order; so too its observation that the withdrawn second claim needed no ruling. [Para 14][Para 72]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The corporate name is garbled by OCR at every occurrence, printed as "ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៊ិន ធើ ប្រាយ ស៊ីស (អ៊ីន ថ៍) នីត ធើរ លីមីធីត" and rendered here as "Gold Fame Inter Bright Six (Inter) Co., Ltd"; the true suffix is not recoverable from this text. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 44]
- The membership figures are printed four ways that cannot be reconciled: 1,610 new members on the September 2011 list, 1,362 in the June 2012 list, 1,559 "old" members of the rival union at 1,000 riel, and 1,240 old members at 1,000 riel. [Para 37][Para 46][Para 50][Para 61][Para 77]
- Paragraph 8 of the internal work rules is set out twice under the same numeral, the second copy with a different spelling of the company and of the month. [Para 29][Para 30]
- Latin fragments of a second column survive inside the Khmer text ("Dock", "Salle", "The", "Mast", "BA", "San", "CC"), and page numbers interrupt sentences mid-line. [Para 1][Para 5][Para 47][Para 63][Para 68][Para 71]
- The signature block records no signature by any of the three arbitrators, only dashes, and its labels are corrupted to "muntityple" and "muccabac". [Para 86]
- The award is dated 2 July 2012 on its face, the issue date having been postponed by agreement from 27 June to 2 July. [Para 1][Para 21]
