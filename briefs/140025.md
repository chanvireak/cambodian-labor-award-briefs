# Award No. 52/11 — Kingdeer (Cambodia) Knitting Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 4 July 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-chosen; Tuon Siphann, worker-chosen; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 140025

## 1. Facts
Kingdeer (Cambodia) Knitting Co., Ltd, at Diem Mean Village, Takhmao City, Kandal Province, faced a ten-point demand lodged by the VKYUF local union on 15 March 2011 covering reinstatement, contract renewal, a day-care centre, delegate elections, non-discrimination and several allowances. [Para 1] [Para 5] None of the ten issues was conciliated and all were referred to the panel on 28 April 2011. [Para 6] [Para 7] The workers attended the first hearing on 12 May 2011 but were absent, without proper reason, from the second hearing on 16 June 2011; the employer attended. [Para 9] [Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate the local union president, Mr P Bunthoeun, the vice-president, Mr S Vanthy, and eight other workers, with back pay from dismissal to reinstatement? [Para 1]
2. Whether the employer must renew the workers' contracts once a year? [Para 1]
3. Whether the employer must set up a day-care centre and nursery room? [Para 1]
4. Whether the employer must arrange an election to re-elect the worker delegates? [Para 1]
5. Whether the employer must refrain from discriminating against members of the local union? [Para 1]
6. Whether the employer must increase the seniority bonus by US$ 1 for each year over five years of service? [Para 1]
7. Whether the employer must provide an additional 3,000 riel meal allowance for overtime work from 6:00 p.m.? [Para 1]
8. Whether the employer must provide a monthly US$ 10 accommodation or transport allowance? [Para 1]
9. Whether the employer must provide a monthly US$ 10 attendance bonus? [Para 2]
10. Whether the employer must maintain daily wages and benefits when workers take periodic leave? [Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union pressed all ten demands, but at the first hearing asked for an adjournment because it was not well informed of the issues, and then did not appear at the second hearing, leaving the demands unargued. [Para 8] [Para 9] [Para 34]
**Employer (respondent):** The employer attended both hearings as invited and submitted its brief statement, commercial registration, internal work rules and the employment contracts and CVs of the two union leaders. [Para 11] [Para 12] [Para 29] It advanced no position on the merits, because the workers did not appear to present their claim. [Para 35]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 52/11-Kingdeer without ruling on any of the ten demands, treating the claim as dropped by the worker party. [Para 36] [Para 38]

The panel relied on Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council Procedural Rules and clause 21 of Prakas No. 099, both of which allow the panel to proceed or to terminate the proceedings by award where a duly invited party fails to appear without good cause. [Para 17] Following Awards 16/07-Lotus and 27/07-M&V 3, it applied a three-condition test: proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of reasons. [Para 18] [Para 19] On notification, the Secretariat had sent notice No. 373 KB/AK/VK/LKA dated 8 June 2011, so the first condition was met. [Para 19] [Para 20] On appearance, the panel read "appear before the arbitration panel" as requiring presence and participation in all four stages — introduction and conflict disclosure, explanation of process and confirmation of issues, conciliation, and arbitration — and the workers attended none. [Para 21] [Para 22] [Para 23] [Para 24] [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 27] [Para 28] On reasons, the workers said only that they had prior arrangements and left the matter to the Council, which was not a proper reason. [Para 28] [Para 30] [Para 31]

The panel added that the claimant had ample opportunity to argue its claim and submit evidence, and that its non-appearance and failure to discharge the burden of arguing the claim showed it did not intend to pursue the dispute through the legal procedure. [Para 32] [Para 33] [Para 34] [Para 35] [Para 37] It therefore closed the case. [Para 38]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been duly notified, is absent from the hearing without participating in any stage of the proceedings, and offers no proper reason, the three conditions for proceeding in absentia are satisfied and the panel may terminate the arbitration by award, treating an unargued claim as dropped. [Para 18] [Para 27] [Para 31] [Para 36]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the worker party's conduct reflected an unwillingness to bring the dispute to the Council for resolution, and that a claimant is ordinarily obliged to argue its claim with reasons and evidence. [Para 34] [Para 37]
