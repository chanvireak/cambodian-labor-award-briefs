# Award No. 71/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី / Chu Shing Garment (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 6 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators ឈីវ ភិរម្យ, លីវ សុវណ្ណា, អាំង អេង ថុង (president) · Doc ID 140290

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី ("Chu Shing Garment") employed about 3,100 workers and had five unions on site, none of them most representative [Para 43][Para 44]. The claimants were សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្ពុជា អាស៊ី ("Cambodia Asia Trade Union Confederation") and the in-company union សហជីព ធម្មាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ រោងចក្រ ជូ ស៊ីង [Para 1][Para 47]. Of the workforce, 304 held undetermined-duration contracts (UDC), and 104 of them thumb-printed a request that those contracts be converted to fixed-duration contracts against 20% of annual wages [Para 48][Para 49]. A collective agreement registered on 25 March 2010 had already been signed by the employer and four other unions and provides 4.5% for such conversions; the claimants were not parties to it [Para 45][Para 47][Para 63]. Two demands survived conciliation and were referred on 7 July 2010 [Para 9][Para 14].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must convert undetermined-duration contracts to fixed-duration contracts and pay 20% of annual wages as severance? [Para 48]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay 3,000 riel or provide one free meal for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 56]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimants, សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្ពុជា អាស៊ី and សហជីព ធម្មាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ រោងចក្រ ជូ ស៊ីង):** factories have been closing in the economic crisis without paying termination compensation, so workers want their UDC converted now, with 20% of total annual wages, rather than risk losing everything [Para 51][Para 52]. On meals they argued that 1,000 riel, fixed in 2000, no longer buys a meal, so 3,000 riel or a free meal is owed [Para 57][Para 58].
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី):** conversion is voluntary and is governed by the 23 March 2010 collective agreement, which offers 4.5% of base wages, spread at ten workers a month by seniority; workers who refuse may keep their UDC [Para 53][Para 54]. It pays the Prakas 017 allowance of 1,000 riel for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00 and says low unit prices leave no room for 3,000 riel [Para 56][Para 60].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the conversion claim is dismissed, the 3,000 riel claim is refused, and the company must provide one free meal to workers doing overtime from 16:00 to 18:00 [Para 115][Para 116][Para 117].

On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 65 of the Labour Law, which subjects the employment contract to the general law of contract, and Article 22 of the 1988 decree-law on contracts, under which a contract is the law of the parties and may be modified or extinguished only with their consent [Para 64][Para 68][Para 72]. No party may force a contract type on another, and the panel, as a third party, will not impose an obligation contrary to the will of the parties [Para 78][Para 79]. Since the employer refused the 20% figure and stood on 4.5%, the 104 workers' UDCs stand unchanged unless a further agreement is reached [Para 80][Para 81].

On Issue 2 the panel split the demand. It found that Prakas 017 point 4 fixes 1,000 riel per day of overtime or one free meal, that the company was paying exactly that, and that 3,000 riel therefore exceeded the instrument and raised a benefits dispute [Para 84][Para 85][Para 87]. The claimant union held no most-representative certificate, so the panel declined to decide that benefits claim, following its consistent practice [Para 90][Para 91][Para 100]. It nevertheless held that the wording of Prakas 017 — "1,000 riel per day or one free meal" — does not say who chooses; reading its object, the panel held the meal is the primary obligation and that 1,000 riel can no longer buy a meal, so a free meal may be claimed in substitution [Para 101][Para 105][Para 107][Para 108]. A free meal was therefore ordered for the 16:00–18:00 overtime slot [Para 113][Para 117].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The panel will not alter the type of an employment contract, nor fix a severance figure for a conversion, unless both parties consent: under Article 65 of the Labour Law read with Article 22 of the 1988 contract decree, the contract is the law of the parties, and a demand that the employer convert undetermined-duration contracts to fixed-duration contracts against its refusal fails even where a majority of affected workers have requested it [Para 64][Para 72][Para 78][Para 81].

**Obiter dicta:** the 1,000 riel meal allowance fixed in 2000 no longer buys a meal, so the free-meal option in Prakas 017 may be claimed in substitution; and a union without most-representative status cannot obtain a ruling on a benefits claim [Para 100][Para 107].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR is poor throughout and the award is only 119 lines, so several passages of reasoning are fragmentary. The collective agreement is dated 15 March 2010 in the summary of the dispute but 23 March 2010 in the evidence list and the reasons [Para 3][Para 19][Para 40]. The employer's name appears as ជូ ស៊ីង, ជូ ស៊ឹង and ជូ ស៊ឹង ហ្គាមីន, and one arbitrator's name is printed both អាំង អេង ថុង and អាំង អេង ថូង [Para 1][Para 119]. The reasoning states that five unions exist but then refers to four co-signatories of the collective agreement [Para 43][Para 45]. Amounts are printed inconsistently as "1.000 riel", "១,០០០" and "3.000 riel" [Para 82][Para 84][Para 116].
