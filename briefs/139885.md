# Award No. 227/13-CIDG — CIIDG Erdos Hongjun Electric Power Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 6 November 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Tang Tri presiding; Mar Samborana, employer nominee; An Non, worker nominee) · Doc ID 139885

## 1. Facts
CIIDG Erdos Hongjun Electric Power Co., Ltd, at Kampenh commune, Stung Hav district, Preah Sihanouk province, engaged a body of trainees (សិក្ខាកាម) whose collective dispute reached the provincial Department of Labour on 10 October 2013 as fifteen demands. [Para 1][Para 12] Conciliation on 14 October 2013 settled three points and left twelve unresolved; the Department forwarded only four of them to the Secretariat in report No. 1185/13 of 16 October 2013, received on 18 October 2013, declining to resend the eight it had already transmitted once. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15] At the hearing of 28 October 2013 both sides appeared, but in the final stage the worker party abandoned its claim by walking out of the hearing room. [Para 11][Para 18][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company may refuse to amend the scholarship agreement, and instead conclude a fresh employment contract with the trainees once that agreement ends? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company discriminates against the union, as the trainees and the union allege including in the examination process, or practises no such discrimination as it maintains? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company may suspend the trainees' work without issuing a written notice stating a time limit? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company may re-set the examination for trainees who failed only after sixteen months, or must re-examine within three months and, on a second failure, dissolve the trainee contract with repayment of training costs? [Para 5][Para 6]
5. Whether, the claimant having abandoned its claim by leaving the hearing, the panel must continue the arbitration or terminate it? [Para 29]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union asked the panel to continue and decide all twelve points left unresolved in the provincial conciliation record, not merely the four that were transmitted. [Para 48]
**Employer (respondent):** the panel should examine only the four unresolved points contained in the report the Secretariat had received, and the company's defence of 25 October 2013, filed through its attorney, denied the discrimination alleged. [Para 20][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration in Case No. 227/13-CIDG; nothing was ordered on the merits. [Para 62][Para 63]
The panel framed the single question it had to answer as whether Article 18, Article 20 and Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, together with Rule 4.7 of the arbitration rules annexed to it, required it to continue or to close a case in which the claimant had walked out. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] It read Article 21 and Rule 4.7 as allowing either course when a party fails to appear, but as directing termination where the absentee is the claimant, since the claimant bears the burden of giving reasons and evidence to the panel. [Para 37][Para 38] It then distilled three conditions for termination: the parties were properly invited, the claimant did not appear before the panel, and the claimant gave no valid reason for not appearing. [Para 39]
Condition one was met. The Secretariat issued invitation No. 1135 Kb/AK/VK/LKAK of 22 October 2013 to the federation and the workers' representatives with the date, place and contact numbers, and a federation official signed for it on 22 October 2013 at 1:30 p.m. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 44]
Condition two was met because "appearing" in Article 21 means both presence at the hearing and participation in the arbitration through to its end, and the panel identified four stages — introduction and conflict-of-interest explanation, explanation of procedure and confirmation of the issues, re-conciliation if the parties agree, and the hearing on the merits. [Para 45][Para 46] The workers attended stages (a), (b) and (c) only and left before any finding of fact was sought on the four issues, so in substance they did not appear. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54]
Condition three was met because the workers offered no valid reason for dropping out, merely asking the panel to issue an award without their participation. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57] Having found all three conditions satisfied, and having recorded that it had given the worker party sufficient opportunity and time, the panel terminated the arbitration. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party is properly invited, attends the opening and conciliation stages of the hearing but withdraws before the stage at which facts are examined, and gives no valid reason for doing so, the panel will treat that as a failure to appear under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and terminate the arbitration by award instead of deciding the claims on their merits. [Para 39][Para 52][Para 57][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded, without needing it for the outcome, that the company-level union of Khmer workers had not yet received its union registration certificate and that no letter delegated the trainees' right to pursue these demands to the federation; it also noted the Department's refusal to resend the eight points already transmitted once. [Para 15][Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's creation date is printed "21 October 2019 013" in the OCR of para 16, two year figures fused. [Para 16] The rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 are dated "22 21 April 2004" in para 32 while the Prakas itself is dated 21 April 2004 elsewhere, and the re-examination interval appears as "016 months" in para 5, its leading digit unexplained. [Para 5][Para 7][Para 32] The company's name is rendered both in Latin "CIIDG Erdos Hongjun Electric Power Co., Ltd." and in Khmer transliteration, and once as "Hun CIIDG" in para 50, so the entity's registered Khmer form cannot be fixed from this text. [Para 1][Para 50] The federation is named សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព អំណាច កម្មករ ខ្មែរ on the face of the award and the company-level union as "union of Khmer workers" in para 50, leaving unclear whether one or two bodies are meant. [Para 1][Para 50] The former eight demands appear both as already settled and as already sent to the Council in para 12, and as not forwarded again in para 15; individual trainees are identified only by the collective term សិក្ខាកាម, and the employer's side is identified by office (administrative assistant, attorney, translator) rather than by party representative. [Para 2][Para 12][Para 15]
