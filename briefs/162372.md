# Award No. 029/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ ធី ស៊ី ខប ប៉ូរេ សិន (Arbitration Council, 7 December 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុង សុទ្ធី; អាន វីរៈ; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding) · Doc ID 162372

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ ធី ស៊ី ខប ប៉ូរេ សិន is a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise employing 2,513 workers, of whom women make up between 65% and 68%. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 86]
For about ten years it paid a bonus for sewing above a set target, four times a month, to teams that exceeded their quota; from August 2021 it replaced this with a twice-monthly average-based scheme shared out 65% to sewing and 35% to other sections, so that every worker received 2 or 3 US dollars a payment whether or not the target was met. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 60]
The claimant workers, ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ ធី ស៊ី ខប ប៉ូរេ សិន, filed seven demands on 15 September 2021; conciliation on 13 October 2021 settled four, and the three remaining were referred to the panel on 18 October 2021. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16]
At the hearing the workers withdrew the part of the first demand seeking an industrial-engineering measurement with their representatives present. [Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay the target-sewing bonus according to the practice applied before August 2021? [Para 51][Para 94]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay the 29 team heads, sub-line heads, cutting-table heads, warehouse heads and finishing heads the same bonus as sewing team and sub-line heads? [Para 74][Para 120]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must establish a nursery and breastfeeding room to standard, with childminders, and if it cannot, pay the actual cost of outside childcare? [Para 85][Para 132]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត (claimant workers):** the target bonus is part of wages, workers who met the target earned 20,000 to 57,000 riel a payment before August 2021 and only 2 or 3 US dollars after, and it is unfair to take the bonus earned by productive workers and share it with those who missed the target. [Para 59][Para 62]
**ភាគី និយោជក (respondent employer):** the bonus is discretionary and set by section according to production strength, technical input and skill, sewing being the core section, and the scheme cannot be reversed because the former owner sold his shares and the new owner applies one policy across its factories abroad. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 82][Para 91]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay the target-sewing bonus as under the earlier practice, the claim for the 29 section heads was dismissed, and the employer was ordered to establish a nursery compliant with the Labour Law [Para 149][Para 150][Para 151]
On Issue 1 the panel treated "past practice" as a legal term of art, defined in Award 95/09 as a practice long and consistently followed and accepted by both sides, and held that a change in how a production bonus is calculated is a management decision over the production chain and therefore not itself past practice. [Para 94][Para 101][Para 108]
Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to organise and manage the enterprise only where that right is exercised lawfully and reasonably, and the panel accepted that an employer may set bonus schemes outside the statutory bonuses. [Para 109][Para 110][Para 111]
Here, however, the employer produced no evidence that the new scheme was lawful or reasonable, and the stated wish to match its factories abroad was not a valid reason. [Para 115][Para 116]
The panel held the bonus reasonable only for workers who exceeded the target, so spreading it to those who did not was improper, and ordered payment according to the earlier practice. [Para 117][Para 118][Para 119]
On Issue 2 the panel found that section-head bonuses fall within the employer's discretion, set by reference to each worker's production strength, technical input and skill, so no obligation arose to equalise the 29 heads with sewing heads. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 125]
On Issue 3 Article 186 of the Labour Law requires any enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a breastfeeding room and a nursery, and where the enterprise cannot take children over 18 months the employer must pay the cost of an outside nursery. [Para 132][Para 133]
Article 187 leaves the standards to a Prakas of the labour ministry, which had not yet been issued. [Para 142][Para 143]
The panel nevertheless found no nursery, room or childminder in the factory and ordered the employer to set up a nursery compliant with the Labour Law, since children cannot be left without a carer. [Para 146][Para 147][Para 148]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may unilaterally replace a discretionary production bonus, but the replacement stands only if it is lawful and reasonable; a scheme that pools the bonus earned by workers who met their target and pays it to those who did not is neither, so the earlier target-based practice is restored. [Para 110][Para 118][Para 119]
Bonus payments for section or team heads remain within management discretion where they are fixed by reference to production strength, technical input and skill, and the panel will not equalise them. [Para 124][Para 125]
Where an employer employing at least 100 women has no nursery and no Prakas has yet fixed the standards under Article 187 of the Labour Law, the Article 186 duty still binds, and the panel will order a nursery with childminders rather than wait for the Prakas. [Para 143][Para 147][Para 148]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified. [Para 149]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR corruption is heavy and page furniture ("A COUN HEARBITRATION CO", "MARBITRATION COUNCIL", "HEARBITRATION COUNCIL") is spliced into sentences, most damagingly at lines 90 and 139, where the claim about outside childcare is partly illegible. [Para 90][Para 139]
The company name is printed in at least four variants across the award, including a form in which a syllable is duplicated, and the employer's representatives are named in two different Latin orders plus Khmer transliteration, so no individual is named here. [Para 47][Para 14]
The 15 US dollar childcare allowance is printed both as "15 dollars" and with an extra syllable before "dollars". [Para 87][Para 138]
Article 65(2) and (3) are quoted in support of the past-practice doctrine although their text concerns the form of the employment contract; the panel did not explain the link. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 98]
