# Award No. 47/05 — Chinarear Garment (Cambodia) Limited (Arbitration Council, 12 September 2005)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ouk Ry (employer-chosen), An Nan (worker-chosen), Koy Neam (chair) · Doc ID 140786

## 1. Facts
Chinarear Garment (Cambodia) Limited operates in Sangkat Phnom Penh Thmei, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, and employs 650 workers [Para 11]. On 11 August 2005 the Labour Dispute Resolution Department received a verbal complaint from the workers demanding improved working conditions, and conciliation at the factory settled four of the five issues [Para 12][Para 13]. The remaining issue, forwarded to the panel on 19 August 2005, was the union's demand that the company reinstate a dismissed worker whom the company said was still in her probationary period [Para 1][Para 2][Para 14]. Conciliation on 18 August 2005 failed and non-conciliation report No. 101 reached the Secretariat on 19 August 2005 [Para 3]. The worker party, the Union Federation of Independent and Democratic (UFID), then sent a letter dated 24 August 2005 withdrawing its claim [Para 9][Para 14]. At the hearing of 29 August 2005 the company appeared through its administrative director while the worker party was absent [Para 3][Para 9][Para 14].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must reinstate the dismissed worker, the company contending that she was still within her probationary period? [Para 1][Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Union Federation of Independent and Democratic (UFID)):** the company should reinstate the dismissed worker; the union later withdrew the claim by letter dated 24 August 2005 and did not appear at the hearing [Para 1][Para 9][Para 14].
**Employer (Chinarear Garment (Cambodia) Limited):** it refused reinstatement because the worker was in a probationary period, and it appeared through an authorised representative to contest the demand [Para 2][Para 9].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed Case No. 47/05-Chinarear without deciding the merits, the claiming party having withdrawn its demand [Para 14].
The panel's reasoning rests entirely on the letter from the union and the local union of the factory dated 24 August 2005 asking that the case be closed [Para 14]. It treated that letter as a withdrawal by the claiming party and held that the case is therefore automatically closed, without any examination of the probationary-period argument the company had raised [Para 2][Para 14]. The operative decision records simply that Case No. 47/05-Company Chinarear is closed [Para 14]. No costs, remedies or findings on the working conditions complaint were made [Para 13][Para 14].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claiming party formally withdraws its demand before the hearing, the panel does not proceed to examine the merits and the case closes automatically; the withdrawal disposes of the dispute in the same way a discontinuance would, leaving nothing for the panel to decide [Para 14].

**Obiter dicta:** none identified.
