# Award No. 109/10 — Pou Yien (Cambodia) Enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ពូ យៀន (ខេមបូឌា) អេនធើ ប្រាយ) (Arbitration Council, 28 October 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 140315

## 1. Facts
Pou Yien (Cambodia) Enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ពូ យៀន (ខេមបូឌា) អេនធើ ប្រាយ) began operating in May 2010 in Sangkat Tuek Thla, Khan Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, and employed about 1,200 workers under four unions. [Para 1][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] The Workers' Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត) and the Pou Yien workers' union complained on 21 September 2010; of eight demands only one remained unresolved — that the company return a worker to his original post. [Para 2][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] That worker, a staff delegate and union official, had been hired on 8 May 2010 on a two-month probation contract as a sewing worker and theory instructor, moved to the warehouse as a materials issuer, and on 17 September 2010 was moved back to sewing without prior consultation, the day after he joined a strike over compensatory leave for Pchum Ben. [Para 37][Para 42][Para 44][Para 46][Para 48][Para 51]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must take the worker back into his original warehouse post, as the union demanded, or was entitled to transfer him to the sewing section? [Para 2][Para 37][Para 63]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the Workers' Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត) and the Pou Yien workers' union (សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត ភូ យៀន):** the worker refused the transfer because he had spent months in the warehouse and had the skills for it; he was moved without consultation the day after he struck, after his group leader threatened to transfer anyone who struck, so the move punished his union activity. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 48][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent), Pou Yien (Cambodia) Enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ពូ យៀន (ខេមបូឌា) អេនធើ ប្រាយ):** the move only reorders the production line; it is a management prerogative, applied to more than this worker, meant to train him in a real skill so that he can later become a group leader training new workers; his wage, benefits, delegate status and union role are all untouched, and the warehouse post carried no defined duties. [Para 3][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand that the company return the worker to his original post in the warehouse, holding the transfer to the sewing section lawful. [Para 77][Para 78]

The panel framed the question as whether the employer had the right to move the worker from the warehouse to sewing. [Para 63] It relied on Article 2 paragraph 2 of the Labour Law, under which an enterprise may have several establishments, each with a group of people working together in one place such as a factory or workshop under the employer's management and direction. [Para 63][Para 65] Following Awards 54/07-Young Waty, 47/08-Gen Tich, 39/09-Green Meadow and 42/10-Tak Fat, it held that this article gives the employer the right to organise and manage the company, including transferring workers from one section to another, provided the right is exercised lawfully and reasonably. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 71]

It then set out the conditions drawn from earlier awards: the transfer must not cut wages, must not move the worker too far away, must not switch shifts from day to night or night to day, and must not impose work wholly outside the worker's skills. [Para 68] Applying those conditions, the panel found the wage and all other benefits had been kept, the delegate and union positions were preserved, the transfer affected more than this one worker, and the contract of employment had never named a post, while the worker had himself started as a sewing worker and instructor for about a month before moving to the warehouse. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 75] The purpose of training him was a proper one, so the transfer was a lawful and reasonable exercise of management authority and did not prejudice what he had been receiving. [Para 76]

The panel did not accept that the timing proved anti-union motive: it recorded the allegation that a group leader had threatened that anyone joining the strike would be transferred, and that the worker was moved the next day, but weighed that against the absence of any post in the contract, the preserved benefits and the training purpose, and concluded the transfer was within the employer's prerogative. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 51][Para 76]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may transfer a worker from one section to another as part of its right to organise and manage the enterprise under Article 2(2), and the panel will not order a return to the former post where the wage, allowances, seniority-linked benefits and any delegate or union role are preserved, the contract specifies no particular post, the work is within the worker's experience, and the transfer is applied to more than one worker for a genuine operational or training purpose. A transfer that cuts pay, shifts day to night work, moves the worker far away, or assigns work wholly outside his skill remains open to challenge, and proximity in time to protected union activity will be weighed but is not by itself enough to make the transfer unlawful. [Para 66][Para 68][Para 73][Para 76]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the union had about 420 members but had not asked the company to check off union dues, and that the company said it did not know the membership numbers because no list had been given to it — observations that did not bear on the transfer. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The worker at the centre of the demand is named in full throughout the source, including in the list of documents filed; his name is withheld here and he appears only by his post. [Para 2][Para 20][Para 21] One passage inserts a stray Latin syllable into the reference to Chapter 12 of the Labour Law. [Para 5] The employer's name is printed both "ពូ យៀន" and "ភូ យៀន", including in the union's own title. [Para 1][Para 2] The heading of the single issue is truncated into "ចំណុច វិវា ទៈ" and is not numbered, so the brief treats it as issue 1. [Para 37] Several sentences end in isolated fragments — a bare "ល ។" and a bare "គាត់ ។" — where text was lost. [Para 50][Para 64][Para 69] One arbitrator's name appears in two different spellings in header and signature block. [Para 1][Para 80]
