# Award No. 033/21 — គ្រឹះស្ថាន មីក្រូ ហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ ជោគជ័យ ហ្វាយ នែន ភី អិល ស៊ី (Arbitration Council, 15 December 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ម៉ារ សំបូរ ណា for the employer, អាន វីរៈ for the workers, គង់ ផល្លា presiding) · Doc ID 140845

## 1. Facts
គ្រឹះស្ថាន មីក្រូ ហិរញ្ញវត្ថុ ជោគជ័យ ហ្វាយ នែន ភី អិល ស៊ី is a microfinance institution lending to customers and employing 254 staff [Para 168]. On 13 October 2021 the workers' representatives filed eight demands; conciliation on 1 November 2021 settled one, and seven came to the Council on 3 November 2021 under conciliation report No. 721 [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95]. At the hearing the workers withdrew issues 3 to 7, leaving only the Pchum Ben bonus and the salary deductions for decision [Para 101][Para 102]. The award is non-binding [Para 103][Para 279]. The nine affected employees had been demoted in 2021 with salaries reduced to the rate of their new posts [Para 176][Para 257].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay the 2021 Pchum Ben bonus of 50 per cent of salary calculated on the employees' former positions? [Para 171][Para 223]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must repay the salary it deducted from the nine employees following their demotion? [Para 195][Para 256]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Clause 4.4 of the company's HR policy summary promised a 13th-month payment of 50 per cent before Khmer New Year and 50 per cent before Pchum Ben to every employee past probation, and it set no condition allowing the bonus to be withheld; the employer's practice of paying only the five Sen Sok staff nothing, and the four Ta Khmao staff on the reduced rate, breached it [Para 3][Para 177][Para 179][Para 183]. On the deductions, they argued Article 28 of the Labour Law forbids an employer from imposing a fine or cutting wages, and that the demotions were imposed in a single letter without any prior discussion or consent [Para 197][Para 200].

**Employer (respondent):** The nine had committed serious faults — inflating collateral values and failing to verify borrower identity — and admitted them, so the company applied clause 4.13 of the 2018 HR policy and clause 26 of the internal regulations to demote them and pay the wage of the new post [Para 184][Para 185][Para 201][Para 206]. It denied withholding the bonus as a punishment, saying payment depended on performance results and was its own prerogative under clause 5.3.1 [Para 186][Para 187].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to pay the 2021 Pchum Ben bonus at 50 per cent of salary calculated on the former positions to the five workers at the Sen Sok branch and to make up the difference for the four at Ta Khmao, and to repay the deducted wages to all nine [Para 276][Para 277][Para 278].

On which document governed, the panel applied Article 23 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, which makes Khmer the language of proceedings, and declined to rely on untranslated foreign-language documents; it also relied on Article 25, which leaves the weight of evidence to the panel, and held the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof [Para 226][Para 227][Para 228][Para 229]. Because the employer produced no Khmer version of the 2018 policy while both sides filed the May HR policy summary, the panel took the summary as the governing text [Para 232][Para 233][Para 234][Para 235]. Clause 4.4 of that summary entitles staff past probation to the 13th-month payment, and the panel found no clause permitting it to be cut [Para 245][Para 246][Para 247]. It followed Award No. 24/05 (Ware World) that a bonus outside the Labour Law may be set unilaterally by the employer, but held that once granted, the employer may attach only reasonable and lawful conditions [Para 240][Para 244][Para 248][Para 249].

On the deductions, the panel applied Article 27 of the Labour Law, requiring disciplinary penalties to be proportionate to the weight of the fault, following Award No. 109/07 (King Lan) [Para 258][Para 262]. Having found no provision in the internal regulations of 22 December 2017 or in the HR summary allowing demotion and wage reduction for fault, the panel held the demotions and cuts unlawful, and ordered repayment [Para 267][Para 268][Para 270][Para 275]. It relied on Article 34 of Prakas No. 099, which empowers the panel to order civil compensation, following Award No. 036/20 (Chhun Vay) [Para 272][Para 273].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer relies on an internal HR policy to justify a decision but files only a foreign-language version, the panel will not apply it, and the Khmer policy summary filed by both parties becomes the governing instrument; a bonus promised there is payable, because although the Labour Law does not compel a Pchum Ben bonus, an employer that grants one may attach only lawful and reasonable conditions and cannot convert it into a penalty [Para 227][Para 233][Para 244][Para 247]. A demotion with wage reduction is unlawful unless the internal regulations or the contract authorise that penalty for the fault found, and any penalty must be proportionate to the fault under Article 27 [Para 258][Para 267].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the employer may unilaterally design incentive payments outside the Labour Law to encourage higher output, and that the workers' claim rested on the May summary rather than the 2018 policy because only the summary was before the panel in Khmer [Para 240][Para 235].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The HR policy year is printed inconsistently: most references truncate it to "ឆ្នាំ ២០", para 245 dates the summary book to May 2016, and the employer's competing policy is dated 2018 throughout, so the two policies cannot be reconciled from the text [Para 232][Para 245]. The 2018 policy is said at para 172 to set the bonus at 25 per cent while the workers plead 50 per cent under the summary [Para 172][Para 177]. Branch worker lists number their entries 1, 2, 4, 4, dropping the third [Para 171][Para 255]. The operative paragraphs repeat the reasoning text verbatim [Para 254][Para 276]. OCR has destroyed the header telephone block into runs of digits and mangles several clause cross-references, and a stray year "២០១៥" interrupts para 223 [Para 2][Para 223].
