# Order No. 004/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាន ហឺ ដា មេន ញូ ហ្វេក ឈើ រីង ឯ.ក (Arbitration Council, 15 February 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 140869

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាន ហឺ ដា មេន ញូ ហ្វេក ឈើ រីង ឯ.ក cuts and sews garments on National Road 4 at Chom Chao III, Porsenchey, Phnom Penh, and employed 1,950 workers [Para 80]. Its workers' representatives filed a complaint on 20 December 2022 raising seven demands [Para 17]; conciliation on 5 January 2023 settled three, and the four remaining points were referred to the Council on 11 January 2023 under report No. 021 [Para 18][Para 19]. The instrument is an order under Article 313 of the Labour Law [Para 1]. At the hearing of 23 January 2023 the parties settled issue 3 (monthly union-member lists) and asked the panel to record the settlement, leaving issues 1, 2 and 4 [Para 23][Para 24]. Those concerned payment of the 90-day maternity allowance before leave [Para 83], the incentive and responsibility bonuses withheld for 2022 [Para 242], and the monthly productivity bonus [Para 175].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay the 90 days of maternity allowance before the worker stops work, calculated on the average of the last twelve months, to the women named in the complaint? [Para 83][Para 197]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay the incentive bonus and the responsibility bonus for 2022 to the six workers whose bonuses it deducted when they took certified sick leave? [Para 242][Para 252]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay the 2022 monthly productivity bonus it deducted from workers on certified sick leave, and maintain the productivity bonus during any absence? [Para 175][Para 293]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត:** women on maternity leave have no money for medicine, food and milk, so the whole 90 days should be paid in advance as the Council had ordered in earlier cases [Para 96][Para 99]. The incentive and responsibility bonuses are wages under Article 103 of the Labour Law, shown on the payslip, so they cannot be cut for certified sick leave [Para 146][Para 148]. The productivity bonus is a payment to smooth the production chain and must be maintained during absence [Para 177][Para 179].

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាន ហឺ ដា មេន ញូ ហ្វេក ឈើ រីង ឯ.ក:** the switch to monthly maternity payments followed a tax audit finding that wages must be declared in the month paid, and all 65 pregnant workers voted for it in a secret ballot on 6 January 2023 [Para 87][Para 104][Para 125]. The bonuses are discretionary incentives tied to full attendance, better than the law requires [Para 152][Para 155]; the productivity bonus likewise depends on attendance and is shared only among those who came to work [Para 181][Para 182].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the maternity claim for either named worker [Para 233][Para 242], and rejected the bonus claims for the five workers [Para 319] and the productivity claims [Para 321][Para 322].

On Issue 1 the panel first tested standing. Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 and article 1 of Prakas No. 362 of 29 August 2016 let the Labour Disputes Department recognise worker representatives [Para 200][Para 202][Para 204], and three representatives were recognised by letter No. 055/23 for 36 workers who had thumbprinted the complaint [Para 206][Para 212]. Of the six women named, only two appeared on that list [Para 215]. One had signed a letter of 10 February 2023 withdrawing the claim and accepting monthly payment, so she was no longer a party [Para 222][Para 226][Para 227], following Award 181/14 [Para 228]. The other had already completed her maternity leave and been paid in January 2022 and was not pregnant [Para 234][Para 236]; following Award 81/11, only a woman actually on leave and still unpaid for the 90 days may bring this claim [Para 237][Para 238][Para 239]. Both claims were therefore refused without reaching the merits [Para 233][Para 242].

On Issue 2 the panel held that Article 103 of the Labour Law defines wages broadly to include bonuses but sets no conditions of entitlement [Para 243][Para 244]; Article 2 gives the employer the right to manage the enterprise lawfully and reasonably [Para 257][Para 259], and an employer may unilaterally design bonus schemes beyond those fixed by law [Para 260], following Awards 06/06, 18/06 and 108/06 [Para 260] and Awards 24/05, 03/13 and 029/15 [Para 261]. Under Policy No. 5 of 2 May 2019, amended 1 October 2022, the incentive is USD 10 for full attendance, USD 5 where one day of annual leave is taken, and nothing above that or for sick or unpaid leave [Para 266][Para 267][Para 269]. The payslips showed the five claimants had taken such leave, so they failed the conditions [Para 276][Para 277][Para 282] and no obligation arose [Para 283]. On the responsibility bonus, the governing policy pays it only for full attendance [Para 286][Para 287], and the claimant concerned had two absences in May 2022 [Para 288][Para 289][Para 290].

On Issue 4 the panel noted Article 103(2) excludes health treatment, family allowance, travel costs and benefits granted specially to facilitate the performance of duties from the wage [Para 311][Para 312][Para 313], and that no legal provision requires an employer to pay a productivity bonus [Para 315][Para 297]. Setting it with attendance conditions is a managerial prerogative under Article 2 [Para 316], the claimants showed no impact on their rights and no unreasonableness [Para 317], and the claim failed [Para 318].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker who has already received the full 90 days of maternity allowance, or who has withdrawn her claim in writing, is not a proper claimant in a collective dispute about advance payment of that allowance, and the Council will not decide the merits for her [Para 227][Para 239][Para 242].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the switch to monthly maternity payments was made to satisfy the tax authority that wages are declared in the month of payment, and that all 65 pregnant workers chose it by ballot, although that finding was not needed once the claimants were held to lack standing [Para 87][Para 123].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is extensive in the evidence lists. Paragraph 24 states that "3.00" issues remained where three were meant, and the operative paragraphs repeat the digits "490" for "90 days" [Para 24][Para 319]. Paragraph 212 dates the complaint "13 December 2019 2022", and paragraph 307 refers to April and May 2023 where the rest of the award says 2022 [Para 212][Para 307]. Prakas No. 303 is dated 2 July 2018 at paragraph 200 and 2 July 2015 in parallel awards [Para 200]. The company name is printed in at least four variants, alternating ឈើ and ឈឺ and adding or dropping the ឯ.ក suffix. Several individuals, including a treating doctor and a number of women workers, appear under full names in the Khmer text; they are reduced to initials or roles here. The order is expressly non-binding and becomes final only if no written objection reaches the Minister within eight days [Para 323].
