# Award No. 008/23 — Yonj Fashions Sporting Good (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (យ៉ញ ហ្វសស៍ ស្ព័ត ធីង ហ្គដ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក) (Arbitration Council, 31 March 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-selected, dissenting), Liv Sovanna (worker-selected), Pen Bun Chea (president) · Doc ID 140871

## 1. Facts
The respondent is ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ញ ហ្វសស៍ ស្ព័ត ធីង ហ្គដ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក, a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise employing 986 workers [Para 47]. The workers' side (ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត) filed a one-point demand on 15 February 2023; conciliation on 27 February 2023 failed and the case was referred to the Council on 2 March 2023 [Para 12][Para 13]. From 12 January 2023 the company stopped paying the monthly bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ប្រចាំខែ) in cash by hand on the 12th or 13th of the following month and began transferring it on the 12th through Wing accounts [Para 57][Para 58]. The workers demanded the old practice be restored, and in the alternative that the bonus be paid together with wages on the 10th of the following month through Wing [Para 53].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must continue paying the monthly bonus in cash directly into workers' hands by the 12th of the following month, or, failing that, pay the bonus together with wages on the 10th of the following month through a Wing account? [Para 53][Para 86][Para 90]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** cash payment is easier for daily living, whereas the Wing agent sits far from the factory, queues are long, workers quarrel in line, the agent serves larger balances first, and some wait until the working bell; Articles 113 to 116 of the Labour Law require wages and bonus to be paid in cash at the workplace unless the worker agrees otherwise [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67].

**Employer (respondent):** the switch was made to ensure the bonus reaches each worker once, to let workers verify it, and to avoid wrong amounts, exchange-rate loss, torn or missing notes and theft [Para 72][Para 73]. One-dollar and five-dollar notes were unavailable [Para 75]. The bonus is a performance evaluation paid to roughly 60 workers, and it cannot be added to the 10th-of-month payroll because attendance for the 1st to the 7th must first be processed [Para 55][Para 76][Para 80]. It offered cash or Wing payment on the 25th of the following month instead [Para 79].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to pay the monthly bonus together with wages on the 10th of the following month through a Wing account, refusing the demand for direct cash payment by the 12th [Para 132].

On representation, the panel held that under Article 54 (new) of the Law on Trade Unions only the most representative union holds the exclusive right to bargain and to bring collective disputes [Para 93], but under Article 59 (new) a minority union may represent its own members in a collective dispute that does not arise from a collective agreement [Para 97][Para 99]. The Cambodia Union at the factory held certificate No. 108/22 with 168 members, a minority, and the dispute did not arise from a collective agreement, so it could represent its members, together with the 113 workers who thumbprinted the referral [Para 100][Para 101][Para 114].

On the merits, Article 116 of the Labour Law requires wages to be paid at least twice a month with no more than sixteen days between payments [Para 117], and Prakas No. 442 of 21 September 2018 requires the first payment in week two and the second in week four of each month [Para 118]. The panel read the second instalment as covering remaining basic wage, allowances and all other benefits, which includes the monthly bonus [Para 123]. The company's internal rules, filed 11 July 2022, fix wage days at the 10th and 25th [Para 124], and paying the bonus on the 12th of the following month was therefore contrary to the Prakas [Para 127]. The panel nonetheless accepted the workers' alternative: since the 10th is a lawful wage day, the bonus should fall due with that payment through Wing [Para 130][Para 131].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A monthly bonus is one of the "other benefits" that Article 116 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 442 require to be paid with the second wage instalment of the month, so a practice of paying it on a later fixed day such as the 12th of the following month is unlawful and the panel will align it with a lawful wage payment date [Para 123][Para 127][Para 130]. A union that is not the most representative may still represent its own members in a collective dispute that does not arise from a collective agreement [Para 99][Para 101].

**Obiter dicta:** the arbitrator selected by the employer, Ing Sothy, filed a dissenting opinion under Article 37 of Prakas No. 099: because the 10th falls in week two and the 25th in week four, a demand to pay the bonus on the 10th is itself inconsistent with Prakas No. 442 and with Article 7 of the internal rules [Para 136][Para 146][Para 147].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company's name is printed in several variant Khmer spellings across the header, the evidence list and the findings, and the award's English masthead is garbled in the scan. Paragraph 14 gives two possible years for the panel's constitution, printing ២០១៩ and ២០២៣ together. Paragraphs 127 to 132 first hold that the bonus must be paid in week four under Prakas No. 442 and then order payment on the 10th of the following month; §4 reports the operative order at paragraph 132. The number of workers who thumbprinted the demand is given as 107 in one place and 113 in another [Para 82][Para 114]. The list of delegates in paragraph 49 is partly lost to bracketed initials and a missing sixth name.
