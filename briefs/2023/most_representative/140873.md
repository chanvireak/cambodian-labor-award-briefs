# Award No. 013/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ (Ken Sport Shoes Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 2 June 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Tang Tri presiding) · Doc ID 140873

## 1. Facts

ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ (Ken Sport Shoes Co., Ltd.) of Chamkar Svay village, Sethei commune, Samakki Meanchey district, Kampong Chhnang, makes shoes and employs 8,032 workers. [Para 43][Para 44]

In April 2023 the representatives of its workers filed 18 demands on working conditions with the Labour Disputes Department. [Para 21]

Conciliation on 2 May 2023 settled 13 of the 18, and the five unresolved points reached the Arbitration Council on 11 May 2023. [Para 22][Para 23]

At the hearing of 19 May 2023 the parties settled issues 1, 2 (shoes), 3 and 5, and the workers withdrew the demand for shirts every six months and the demand that pregnant workers be kept out of the paint building, leaving part of issue 4 alone. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Issue 4) Whether workers in the paint building (អគារ អូស ពណ៌) are entitled to a USD 25 incentive payment from the employer? [Para 45][Para 57]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The representatives of the workers of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ conceded that no law requires the payment, but argued that paint-section workers stand and walk for up to 10 hours a day in a three-storey building and face toxic chemicals, and that other sections receive USD 5 to USD 10 while the paint section receives at most USD 0.80. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]

**Employer (respondent):** The company submitted that it has its own procedure and policy for incentives, already pays a USD 6 function allowance to the paint-section workers, and that incentives elsewhere depend on reaching company targets and are redistributed by percentage, so it cannot grant the demand. [Para 54][Para 56]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the claim that the employer pay a USD 25 incentive to the workers in the paint building. [Para 79]

The panel first characterised the demand as an interest dispute (វិវាទ អំពី ផលប្រយោជន៍) rather than a dispute over rights. [Para 60]

It found no provision of the Labour Law, no collective agreement, no internal rule and no past practice obliging the employer to pay the USD 25. [Para 59]

Under Article 54 (new) of the amended Law on Trade Unions and Article 72 of the Law on Trade Unions, only a most-representative union, or one with sufficient representative characteristics, may negotiate a collective agreement. [Para 70][Para 73]

The claimant union was expressly found not to be most representative, and no union in the company had yet obtained that status. [Para 49]

As the claimant was neither most representative nor sufficiently representative, the panel held that it could not decide the interest dispute at all. [Para 76]

It added, citing Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council, that an award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement and binds every worker for one year. [Para 71]

Such an award would therefore have cost all the company's workers their right to bargain collectively and to strike over future interest disputes. [Para 72]

The panel followed six earlier awards to the same effect, among them No. 118/16-E-Cheng (Cambodia) Union and No. 055/19-Cambodian Tech Style World. [Para 75]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A panel may decide an interest dispute, including a demand for an incentive that no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice supports, only where the claimant union is the most representative union in the enterprise or has the sufficient representative characteristics required by Article 72 of the Law on Trade Unions. [Para 73]

Where that condition is absent the panel must decline to consider the demand, because granting it would substitute for a collective agreement binding all workers. [Para 72]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded, without needing to rely on them once jurisdiction failed, that the employer already pays a USD 6 function allowance and distributes other sections' incentives by percentage against production targets. [Para 54][Para 56]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

- The demand list is misnumbered: the fifth heading is printed "៥-" while its own text calls it demand six, and the fourth heading prints its number twice. [Para 11][Para 14]
- The summing-up paragraph joins "refuse to consider" with "and order the employer to provide USD 25", which read alone suggests a grant; the operative order and the reasoning both refuse the claim. [Para 78][Para 79]
- Two citations lose their year: the Prakas appointing Council members ends "ឆ្នាំ ២០" and the Trade Union Law amendment is dated "ឆ្នាំ ២០". [Para 16][Para 64]
- The date the panel was formed prints two years in sequence ("ឆ្នាំ ២០២១ ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣"). [Para 24]
- The workers'-side arbitrator's name appears in one form in the panel list and with a different final element in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 81]
- The claimant union is never named, and the six worker representatives survive only as broken OCR initials. [Para 45]
