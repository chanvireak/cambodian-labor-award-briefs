# Award No. 015/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វាន់ទុម អឹភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត v សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ (Arbitration Council, 7 July 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Tang Tri presiding) · Doc ID 140874

## 1. Facts

ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខ្វាន់ទុម អឹភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត is a garment factory at Tuol Pongro, Chom Chao 1, Posenchey, Phnom Penh, with 1,474 workers, 1,314 of them women. [Para 62]

On 12 May 2023 the representatives of its workers, organised in សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ (the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union), filed ten demands on working conditions. [Para 14][Para 45]

Four were withdrawn and conciliation of the remaining six on 31 May 2023 settled three, leaving three points for the panel, which heard them on 12 June 2023. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay a skill allowance of USD 10, USD 15 and USD 20 to workers able to operate two, three and four machines respectively? [Para 64][Para 65]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must restore the piece-rate time allowance (នាទីដេរ តាមបរិមាណផលិតផល) from 2 minutes 38 seconds to 5 minutes 40 seconds? [Para 82][Para 83]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must let the union's leader leave the factory premises for 15 minutes every Thursday in the afternoon break without a written permission slip? [Para 103][Para 104]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** Workers operating two machines or more always exceed target yet receive only the USD 200 base wage, the same as a one-machine worker, while the employer moves them to cover absent workers and they lose out by it. [Para 71][Para 72] Other factories pay a skill allowance, and the union pointed to one paying USD 5 for two needles and USD 10 for three. [Para 73] On the time allowance, the rate fell from 5 minutes 40 seconds to 2 minutes 38 seconds although the sewing technique did not change, and more than half the piece-rate workers cannot reach it. [Para 93][Para 95] The 15-minute break is the workers' own time. [Para 110]

**Employer (respondent):** No law, contract, internal rule or practice obliges it to pay a skill allowance. [Para 74] Piece-rate times come from an industrial-engineering study and vary with style and fabric, and any worker earning below USD 200 is topped up to that figure. [Para 97][Para 175] On issue 3, a slip signed by the section head protects security, and the employer is liable if a worker is hurt outside the gate. [Para 183]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — all three demands were refused. [Para 189][Para 190][Para 191]

On issue 1 the employer first pleaded res judicata, the same demand having been brought in Award No. 044/18 against the same company. [Para 67][Para 68] The panel held that the third res judicata condition was not met, because the earlier panel had refused to consider the demand, which is not a ruling on its substance. [Para 129]

On the merits it found no provision of the Labour Law, no collective agreement, no agreement between the parties, no internal rule and no past practice requiring a skill allowance. [Para 131] A demand that goes beyond what the law sets is an interest dispute. [Para 132] The claimant union held a certificate of most-representative status, so it was entitled to bring such a dispute. [Para 146][Para 148] The workers nevertheless produced no evidence to support the claim, and it was dismissed. [Para 157][Para 158][Para 159]

On issue 2, Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to organise and manage the enterprise, exercised in accordance with the law and reasonably. [Para 162] Article 7 of the Law on Minimum Wage requires piece rates to be set so that an average-skilled worker working normal hours earns at least the guaranteed minimum wage. [Para 164] Prakas No. 247/22 fixes the 2023 minimum at USD 200 a month. [Para 168][Para 169] The panel found the employer tops up shortfalls to USD 200 and so breached no duty. [Para 177][Para 178]

On issue 3, the same management right under Article 2 allowed the employer to require a written request before a worker leaves the premises during working time. [Para 185][Para 186] The demand was refused, though the panel added that the procedure should be simplified so workers do not wait long. [Para 185]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A refusal to consider a demand (បដិសេធ ពិចារណា) is not a decision on the merits, so the third condition of res judicata is not satisfied and a later panel may decide the same demand afresh. [Para 129]

A piece-rate or time-allowance setting is lawful where a worker of average skill working normal hours still earns at least the statutory minimum wage and the employer tops up any shortfall; the panel will not substitute its own rate for the employer's. [Para 164][Para 177]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the leave-permission procedure ought to be simplified, and that most-representative status is what entitles a union to bring an interest dispute. [Para 185][Para 148]
