# Award No. 009/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ ធី ស៊ី ខប ប៉ូរេសិន (Arbitration Council, 13 April 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three — អ៊ុក រី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (chair) · Doc ID 140875

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន វ៉ាយ ធី ស៊ី ខប ប៉ូរេសិន is a garment-sewing enterprise employing 2,461 workers, 1,706 of them women. [Para 33]
The workers' representative filed ten demands; conciliation on 13 March 2023 settled seven of them, and the three unresolved demands were forwarded to the Council on 23 March 2023 in conciliation report No. 174 កប/អក/វក. [Para 14][Para 15]
The three unresolved demands were light work for pregnant workers, for workers who miscarried and for women returning from maternity leave; annual-leave compensation averaging bonus, seniority payment and other benefits; and a 2,000 riel health-food allowance, or one health-food meal, for workers doing overtime. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6]
At the hearing of 31 March 2023 the parties reached agreement on the first two, leaving only the overtime food demand for the panel to decide. [Para 19][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 3) Whether the employer is obliged to provide workers with food and drink containing sugar at each overtime session? [Para 44]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The employer must supply a sweet snack or sweetened beverage at every overtime session as the Ministry of Labour's instruction No. 003 of 10 February 2004 requires, because workers doing overtime suffer fatigue, dizziness and fainting. [Para 40][Para 41]
**Employer (respondent):** The company already pays a 4,000 riel overtime meal allowance per day and a USD 10 monthly lunch allowance folded into the base wage, which is better than the law requires, and it cannot do more given the economic situation and the suspension of employment contracts from 9 to 18 March 2023. [Para 42][Para 43]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to provide a sweet food supplement, such as a sweetened beverage, to workers performing overtime, in compliance with instruction No. 003 of 10 February 2004. [Para 55]
On Issue 3 the panel held that Notification No. 003 សកបយ of 10 February 2004, point 4, obliges factories to give workers a sugar-based supplement at each overtime session in addition to the overtime wage. [Para 45][Para 46]
It followed its own earlier awards No. 031/22, No. 035/22, No. 041/22 and No. 006/23, which had applied the same notification. [Para 46][Para 48]
The panel accepted that the 4,000 riel daily overtime meal allowance and the USD 10 monthly lunch allowance were more favourable than the statutory minimum, but held that the employer had still not performed the separate duty of providing the sweet supplement. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53]
The award was declared non-binding and would become final if no party objected within 08 days. [Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that pays a cash meal allowance above the statutory minimum still owes the distinct obligation under Notification No. 003 សកបយ to provide a sugar-based food or sweetened drink at every overtime session. [Para 51][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's observation that the 4,000 riel overtime meal allowance and the USD 10 monthly lunch allowance are "better than the law" did not itself decide the case. [Para 50]
It was also noted that only finishing-section workers worked two hours of overtime, from 4pm to 6pm. [Para 39]

The paragraph recording constitution of the panel carries two years, "២០១៩ ២០២៣", so 2023 is recoverable only from context. [Para 16]
The company name appears in several OCR variants, and the conciliation report number is printed both as 174 កប/អក/វក and in a garbled second form. [Para 34]
Stray Latin and numeric tokens survive where OCR dropped Khmer text, including the opening word of the reasons on Issue 3. [Para 44]
