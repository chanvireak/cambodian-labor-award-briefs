# Award No. 017/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គ្រេត យូញៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន ខូ អិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 20 September 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three — គល់ វឌ្ឍនា (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លៃ (chair) · Doc ID 140876

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គ្រេត យូញៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន ខូ អិលធីឌី is a garment-sewing enterprise employing 750 workers. [Para 145]
A group of workers filed ten demands on 6 July 2023; the final conciliation on 9 August 2023 failed, and the file reached the Council on 10 August 2023 under conciliation report No. 481 ក.ប/អក/វក. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56]
At the hearing the workers withdrew demand 2, merged demands 3, 4 and 5 into one, and the parties agreed demands 7 and 8, asking that the agreement be written into the award. [Para 60][Para 61]
On 13 July 2023 the employer dismissed six workers who had acted as their representatives in this dispute, for alleged serious misconduct. [Para 172]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the workers' seniority must be preserved from the date each first entered employment, despite the employer's 2012 re-registration and new contracts? [Para 240]
2. (Issues 3, 4 and 5) Whether the dismissal of the six workers was lawful, and if not, whether they must be reinstated with back wages and severance? [Para 288][Para 291]
3. (Issue 6) Whether the first-half 2023 seniority payment must be recalculated on total wage including bonus and allowances rather than the bundle wage? [Para 372]
4. (Issue 9) Whether the employer owes the seven workers holiday and Sunday work pay on the same basis as other workers? [Para 385]
5. (Issue 10) Whether the employer owes the seven workers the regular attendance bonus, the travel and accommodation allowance and overtime wages? [Para 399]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Seniority runs from first entry into the company because the employment relationship never ended; the 2012 contract change was presented as a mere card renewal. [Para 151][Para 246]
**Workers (claimant), on dismissal:** The six were not guilty of serious misconduct — they only claimed benefits due under the law. [Para 290]
**Employer (respondent):** The company registered commercially anew in August 2012 and the workers signed new contracts from that year, so seniority before 2012 is not recognised. [Para 248][Para 269]
**Employer (respondent), on dismissal:** The six were dismissed for serious misconduct under Article 83(b)(3) and (b)(5), and the bundle wage already absorbs the bonuses and allowances now claimed. [Para 178][Para 407]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must preserve seniority from first entry to date for all seven workers, reinstate five of them with back wages from dismissal to reinstatement, and pay the sixth full severance including pre-2019 seniority back pay, notice in lieu and moral damages; issues 6, 9 and 10 were dismissed. [Para 411][Para 412][Para 413][Para 414]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 87-new of the Labour Law, which keeps existing contracts with the new employer where the employer's legal status changes, and found no evidence that the workers had agreed to a fresh seniority start. [Para 242][Para 281]
Because the workers received no termination payment when the contracts were reissued, any such agreement would fall foul of Article 13, which voids arrangements less favourable than the law; the panel followed awards No. 045/18, No. 023/19 and No. 004/22. [Para 279][Para 284][Para 243]
On Issues 3, 4 and 5 the panel held that the employer bears the burden of proving serious misconduct, citing award No. 107/04, and that no evidence supported the Article 83(b)(3) or (b)(5) allegations. [Para 293][Para 296][Para 297]
The dismissal was therefore made on the employer's will alone, without valid reason and without notice, contrary to Article 74; under Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 the panel ordered reinstatement of five workers and back wages to the date of return. [Para 305][Para 308][Para 313]
The sixth worker did not seek reinstatement, so the panel ordered her severance pay, pre-2019 seniority back pay calculated under Article 89-new and Prakas No. 443, notice in lieu under Articles 75 and 77, and moral damages under Article 91-new. [Para 329][Para 354][Para 363][Para 370]
On Issues 6, 9 and 10 the panel found the bundle wage had absorbed the bonuses, holiday pay and allowances for more than ten years without any vitiating element, so the claims failed. [Para 382][Para 395][Para 409]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that re-registers and reissues contracts cannot restart workers' seniority unless it proves the workers freely agreed and were paid their termination entitlements; an agreement to the contrary is void under Article 13 of the Labour Law. [Para 281][Para 284][Para 286]

**Obiter dicta:** Moral damages under Article 91-new do not require the worker to prove actual loss. [Para 351]
The panel also observed that a bundle wage paid unchallenged for over ten years evidences the parties' intent to fold statutory allowances into it. [Para 379][Para 382]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award prints most worker names as unspaced initial strings, and the same worker appears under two or three different spellings across the document. [Para 148][Para 163]
In two places a worker's name is printed in full where the rest of the award uses initials; those workers are identified by role here, not by name. [Para 314][Para 404]
The constitution date of the panel carries two years, "២០២២ ២០២៣". [Para 57]
The panel records the remaining issues as five but lists six numbers (1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 10), and the merged 3-4-5 block is counted as one. [Para 62]
Stray English fragments survive mid-sentence where OCR dropped Khmer text, including in the operative summary. [Para 248][Para 287]
