# Award No. 020/23 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហួយ ស៊ីន ទូ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្វេ សិន ឯ.ក (Arbitration Council, 3 November 2023)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three — អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន (worker-appointed), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140877

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហួយ ស៊ីន ទូ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្វេ សិន ឯ.ក is a garment-sewing enterprise in Khan Kombol, Phnom Penh, employing 386 workers. [Para 50]
The workers' representatives filed eleven demands on 4 October 2023; conciliation on 9 October 2023 settled ten, and the single unresolved demand reached the Council on 11 October 2023 under report No. 639 ក.ប/អក/វក. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14]
Workers struck on 29 September 2023 from 10:42 until 4pm, and again all day on 30 September 2023, to press for paid annual leave during the Pchum Ben holiday. [Para 63][Para 64]
The employer deducted USD 11.53 in wages and USD 15 in regular attendance bonus for the one and a half strike days. [Para 65]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay back the wages of USD 11.53 and the attendance bonus of USD 15 for the strike days to the 309 workers who signed the claim? [Para 94]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Paid annual leave may be taken during Pchum Ben, and only a court can declare a strike unlawful, so no deduction was justified; on 30 September they wanted to return but the employer shut the gate and barred 13 union leaders. [Para 66][Para 75]
**Employer (respondent):** The strike was unlawful, so the wages and the regular attendance bonus were properly withheld. [Para 77]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim for USD 11.53 in wages and USD 15 in attendance bonus for 309 workers was refused. [Para 113]
As a threshold point the panel held that the four workers recognised by the Labour Disputes Department under letter No. 058/23 were entitled to represent the workers, applying Article 1 of Prakas No. 362 and Article 16 of Prakas No. 303. [Para 82][Para 88]
On the merits the panel found the strike unlawful on two grounds: no seven-day prior notice as Article 324 requires, and no exhaustion of conciliation as Article 320(4) requires. [Para 99]
It followed its award No. 27/10 holding that Article 332 suspends the employment contract during a strike, so no wage is payable for strike days whether or not the strike was lawful. [Para 105][Para 106]
The regular attendance bonus failed for the same reason, since a striking worker is not attending regularly; the panel also cited point 2 of Notification No. 230 on the bonus. [Para 108][Para 109]
Only the 309 workers whose names and thumbprints were attached to the 30 September 2023 claim were covered, not the 389 named at the hearing. [Para 92][Para 93]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Wages and the regular attendance bonus are not payable for days on which workers strike, because Article 332 of the Labour Law suspends the employment contract for the duration of a strike. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 111]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the employer's deduction was limited to the 1.5 strike days and expressed no view on any wider penalty. [Para 74]
It also observed that only a court decides whether a strike is lawful, yet still assessed lawfulness itself for the wage question. [Para 76][Para 99]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's constitution date again carries two years, "២០១៩ ២០២៣". [Para 15]
The start of the 29 September strike is printed as 10:42 in one place and as "10 and 20 42" minutes in the reasoning. [Para 63][Para 111]
The workforce is given as 386 while 389 workers are said to have struck, and 373 on the first day. [Para 50][Para 63][Para 64]
Worker representatives appear under several inconsistent initial strings, and their names are partly lost to OCR. [Para 51][Para 86]
