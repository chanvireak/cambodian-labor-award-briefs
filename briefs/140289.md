# Award No. 68/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វ៊ូល លីហ្វៀល (កម្ពុជា) / YSA Full Life Garment (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 29 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators យូ សួន ទី, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (president) · Doc ID 140289

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វ៊ូល លីហ្វៀល (កម្ពុជា) ("YSA") began operating in 1999 and employed 508 workers, with three unions on site, none holding most-representative status [Para 42][Para 43][Para 45]. The claimant was សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ ("Khmer Youth Voice"), whose in-company branch registered on 1 June 2010 and counted about 80 members [Para 35][Para 46]. Its 30 April 2010 complaint raised 19 demands; 9 were settled in conciliation and 10 were referred to the panel after the 30 June 2010 non-settlement report [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]. The disputes concerned meal allowance for Sunday and holiday overtime, the US$5 attendance bonus, wages during work shortages, contract duration, seniority pay, wage increments, a canteen, union dues, night transport and worker delegates.

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether 4,000 riel meal allowance is owed for Sunday and holiday overtime? [Para 47]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the US$5 attendance bonus must be kept when a worker is absent for half a day? [Para 57]
3. (Issue 3) Whether full wages are owed when the company has no work? [Para 62]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must issue one-year fixed-duration contracts? [Para 71]
5. (Issue 5) Whether seniority pay must rise by US$1 per month for each year beyond four? [Para 78]
6. (Issue 6) Whether wages must rise annually by skill grade? [Para 81]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the company must build a midday canteen? [Para 86]
8. (Issue 8) Whether the company must deduct 1,000 riel union dues from each member? [Para 93]
9. (Issue 9) Whether the company must provide safe transport for night overtime? [Para 104]
10. (Issue 10) Whether worker delegates must be elected promptly? [Para 111]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ):** 1,000 riel no longer buys a meal, and eight hours of Sunday or holiday work should bring 4,000 riel [Para 50][Para 52]. They said the company cut the whole US$5 bonus for any half-day absence, paid only 50–75% when work ran short, and kept them on three-month contracts to avoid renewal [Para 57][Para 63][Para 76]. They also said the company's practice of re-questioning each member who signed a dues authorisation frightened workers into refusing [Para 101].
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វ៊ូល លីហ្វៀល (កម្ពុជា)):** Sunday and holiday work is not overtime, so no 4,000 riel is owed; the company applies Prakas 017's 1,000 riel or one meal [Para 54][Para 56]. It admitted paying 75% on the first idle day and 50% afterwards without seeking any suspension [Para 63][Para 66]. It refused one-year contracts, extra seniority and skill-based increments as beyond its means, and said the site has no space for a canteen [Para 77][Para 85][Para 86].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must pay 1,000 riel or provide one free meal for Sunday overtime; must pay 100% wages during work shortages because it never lawfully suspended the contracts; must deduct 1,000 riel union dues once a valid written request is received and must not re-interrogate each member; and must provide transport after 7:00 p.m. The 4,000 riel claim, one-year contracts, extra seniority, skill increments and the canteen were refused, and the panel reaffirmed the delegate election order in Award 54/10 [Para 311][Para 314][Para 319][Para 320][Para 321].

On Issue 1 the panel held holiday work is not overtime under Article 139 or Prakas 080, so the 4,000 riel claim exceeded the law and, the union lacking most-representative status, was refused as a benefits dispute [Para 136][Para 137][Para 150]. It nevertheless found Sunday work is overtime, since the company's normal week runs Monday to Saturday, and ordered the Prakas 017 allowance of 1,000 riel or a free meal [Para 153][Para 160][Para 165]. On Issue 2 it allowed deduction of the attendance bonus pro rata to authorised absence days but not its total forfeiture [Para 181][Para 182].

On Issue 3 the panel applied Article 71(1)(11): suspension requires notification to, and supervision by, the labour inspector, and where the employer suspends on its own the wage obligation survives [Para 185][Para 200]. Because YSA never filed the request, 100% wages were ordered for the idle periods [Para 201][Para 202]. Issues 4 to 6 failed: contract terms rest on mutual consent under Article 65 and the Civil Code, and the panel cannot impose terms on an unwilling employer [Para 205][Para 213][Para 215]; Prakas 017 fixes seniority pay at US$5 and requires no further increment [Para 224][Para 225]; and no law or agreement requires skill-based wage rises [Para 235][Para 237]. Issue 7 failed because no law requires a canteen and the site has no space, though the panel encouraged the parties to approach the owner of vacant land 200 metres away [Para 246][Para 252]. On Issue 8 the panel held that re-questioning each signatory interfered with union activity, contrary to Article 129 and Article 271 [Para 259][Para 272][Para 274].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that stops providing work without the suspension procedure in Article 71(1)(11) — a written request and supervision by the labour inspector — remains liable for 100% of wages for the idle days, even though no work was performed, and cannot substitute its own 50% or 75% scale for the statutory obligation [Para 185][Para 200][Para 202].

**Obiter dicta:** holiday work is not overtime, so Article 139's premium does not apply to it; the attendance bonus may be reduced in proportion to lawfully authorised absences; and a canteen, though desirable, is not legally required [Para 136][Para 181][Para 246].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is heavily degraded OCR. The employer's name appears in at least six variants, and one arbitrator's name is printed with a stray digit, so the panel composition above follows the face of the award only in part and one individual is identified by initial. The union's registration date, the number of settled demands and the operative numbering collide: conciliation is said to have settled 9 of 19 demands, while the reasons treat Issue 1 as both refused and partly granted [Para 25][Para 124][Para 126]. Amounts are printed inconsistently as "4 000 riel", "៤០០០" and "1.000 riel" [Para 126][Para 162][Para 165]. Pages of the annexed dissent by the worker-appointed arbitrator are spliced into the operative text, and the file ends without a signature page [Para 323][Para 339].
