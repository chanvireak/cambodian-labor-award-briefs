# Award No. 049/20 — Tri Amfin Dragon (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 6 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Ing Sothy dissenting on Issue 2) · Doc ID 140810

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ត្រាយ អាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន (ខេមបូឌា) ឯក makes footwear at Khnar Tong, Komar Reachea commune, Bati district, Takeo, and employed 1,404 workers at the hearing. [Para 51][Para 1]
Workers filed nine collective demands; seven were settled at the provincial conciliation of 14 August 2020 and two were transmitted by non-conciliation report No. 1778/20 of 28 August 2020. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]
Those two survive: the demotion and wage cut of four section heads, and the employer's reduction, from January 2020, of paid antenatal check-up leave for pregnant workers from one full day a month to half a day. [Para 53][Para 68][Para 69]
The employer had allowed the full day, with wages and benefits preserved, throughout the four years before that change. [Para 68]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must restore the positions and the pay and benefits of the four section heads it demoted and whose wages it reduced? [Para 53][Para 122]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must again allow pregnant workers one full paid day a month to attend antenatal check-ups, as it did before January 2020? [Para 88][Para 123]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers say the demotions came without prior notice: section heads were called in, told they were reduced to ordinary team leaders, and told that anyone who would not thumbprint acceptance could resign, so they thumbprinted without a choice. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59] On the leave, they say half a day is not enough because the Bati district health centre is far away and crowded, and that the employer neither wrote the new rule into its internal regulations nor notified them of it. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ត្រាយ អាំហ្វិន ដ្រេហ្គន (ខេមបូឌា) ឯក says it applied a clear internal policy after in-house meetings, with a table of the different levels and pay of each section head and team leader, that it told the workers of their faults first, and that they agreed by thumbprint. [Para 61][Para 62] On the leave it says the half-day rule is in the internal regulations, that administration always granted a second half-day on request, and that falling production forced the change. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 77]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — Issue 1 was dismissed, and on Issue 2 the panel ordered the employer to allow pregnant workers one full paid day a month for antenatal checks as before. [Para 122][Para 123][Para 121]
On Issue 1 the panel relied on Article 19 of Prakas No. 099, which allows a party to be represented only by a person holding written authorisation, and on Award No. 181/14 (Sung Yin (Cambodia) B-Tech Style), which applied that rule. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82] Checking the original complainant list, the panel found no worker identified as a section head and no evidence supporting the claim, so it refused to consider the demand. [Para 86][Para 87]
On Issue 2 the panel applied the "past practice" doctrine, grounded in Articles 65 of the Labour Law on contracts formed by practice, and followed Award No. 95/09 (Tak Fat), which defined a past practice as conduct repeated over time, clear, stable and accepted by both parties. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 97][Para 109]
It held that a past practice may end lawfully only where the parties agree to replace it, one side terminates it, or the circumstances underlying it change, and it placed the burden of proving an assertion on the party making it. [Para 103][Para 112][Para 113]
Inspecting the internal regulations and the file, the panel found no half-day rule in those regulations and no notice to the workers, and no evidence that the full day had depressed production. [Para 107][Para 116][Para 117] The employer-appointed arbitrator, Ing Sothy, filed a dissent relying on the employer's Article 2 management right and on the absence of any legal provision on antenatal leave. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A benefit that an employer has granted openly and consistently for years becomes a condition of employment under the past practice doctrine, and it cannot be withdrawn unilaterally by an unpublished internal rule: the employer must show agreement, notice to the workforce, or a change in the circumstances that produced the practice. [Para 109][Para 112][Para 117]
A demand brought on behalf of particular individuals fails where the claimant cannot identify them in the file, because an unnamed beneficiary cannot be the subject of an order. [Para 79][Para 86]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that past practice awards have been made on annual leave use, wage payment, bonuses and meal allowances, a catalogue that did not itself decide either issue here. [Para 98]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Every year in the document is truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" with the last two digits lost, including the award date of 6 October and the January 2020 change; one line prints the year as "ឆ្នាំ ២៩ ២០". [Para 1][Para 69][Para 52]
The hearing dates are irreconcilable: the notice fixes the hearing at 23 September, the reason for adjournment is a holiday on 15 September, and the adjournment order moves the hearing from "15 July" to 24 September. [Para 10][Para 16][Para 17]
The respondent's name is printed in several corrupt forms, including "ខេមឃ្នះ", "ខ្ទមបូឌា" and "ត្រាយ អ៊ី", and one exhibit is attributed to "AKP" with a district name corrupted. [Para 35][Para 27][Para 52][Para 34]
Paragraphs setting out the panel's conclusion on Issue 2 are badly broken by OCR, leaving fragments of the operative reasoning unreadable, though the order itself is clear. [Para 118][Para 119]
The list of the four section heads appears in the record only as an OCR-degraded name list of individual workers; this brief describes the defect and does not reproduce the names. [Para 52]
