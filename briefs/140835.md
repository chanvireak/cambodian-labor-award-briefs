# Award No. 017/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វីនិក ស៊ី ឃ្យួរី ធី ស៊ី ស្ទឹម (Arbitration Council, 22 September 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · order under Article 313 of the Labour Law · panel: យូ សួន ទី (employer-appointed), អន ណន (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (president) · Doc ID 140835

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វីនិក ស៊ី ឃ្យួរី ធី ស៊ី ស្ទឹម registered in 2008 [Para 39]. The workers' representatives filed eight demands on 29 June 2021 [Para 18]; conciliation on 21 July 2021 settled three and report No. 512 K.B/AK/VK of 28 July 2021 referred the remaining five to the Council [Para 19][Para 20]. The hearing was held on 20 August 2021 [Para 17], and the panel refused the employer's late request to extend the evidence deadline on 31 August 2021 because the parties had signed a schedule on 20 August [Para 32]. The five demands were about unexplained wage deductions, pay for work on public holidays, weekly rest days, annual leave, and alleged union discrimination in discipline [Para 355][Para 364].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer had to stop deducting wages without a stated basis and repay the sums deducted? [Para 355]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer had to pay the additional day's wage for work on paid public holidays and repay the shortfall since 2020? [Para 358][Para 359]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer had to grant weekly rest days and pay 200% for rest days worked since 2020? [Para 360][Para 361]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the employer had to grant annual leave and convert the 18 days not granted in 2020 into money or days? [Para 362][Para 363]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the employer had discriminated against union leaders in imposing discipline? [Para 364]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the party representing the workers, ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត ក្នុង ការដោះស្រាយ វិវាទ ការងារ រួម នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វីនិក ស៊ី ឃ្យួរី ធី ស៊ី ស្ទឹម, argued that wages were deducted without any document showing the reason [Para 2], that holiday and weekly-rest compensation had never been paid since 2020 [Para 4][Para 6], that annual leave was withheld [Para 8], and that since the union appeared the company imposed photo and thumbprint checks on arrival as pressure [Para 10]. Staff worked seven days a week with no rest day and still received only a 165 USD base wage for a 30 or 31-day month [Para 280].
**Employer (respondent):** the company never deducted any worker's wage [Para 3]; it would start implementing the holiday and rest-day entitlements from October 2021 [Para 5][Para 7]; it could not pay annual leave in cash [Para 9]; and there was no union discrimination, since rest days had been given and workers asked to work them [Para 11][Para 264].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to repay the deductions, to pay the additional day's wage for holiday work, to pay 200% for rest days worked since 2020, and to give back the annual leave days withheld in 2020; the union-discrimination claim was dismissed [Para 355][Para 358][Para 360][Para 362][Para 364].
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 118, which puts the burden of proving that wages were paid on the employer [Para 216]. The employer produced no payroll evidence for the deduction it alleged was a performance penalty [Para 217], so the panel accepted the claim and ordered repayment of 10 USD a month from March to July 2021 to the affected worker [Para 220], plus recalculation for the four area supervisors and repayment of amounts over-deducted in July 2021, and a written rule for any future deduction [Para 221][Para 223].
On Issue 2, Article 164 and Article 4 of Prakas No. 100 SKBY give 200% of the normal wage to staff who work on a paid public holiday [Para 226][Para 230]. The parties agreed only nine holidays a year were compensated [Para 232], and the payroll showed no holiday payment [Para 237][Para 238]; the panel ordered the additional day's wage and back pay from 2020 [Para 247].
On Issue 3, Articles 146 to 148 require 24 consecutive hours of weekly rest, normally on Sunday [Para 252][Para 253]. Working seven days a week breached those provisions [Para 271][Para 281], and no evidence showed the 200% was paid [Para 282], so the panel ordered the rest days and the 200% back pay [Para 283].
On Issue 4, Article 166 gives 1.5 days a month plus one extra day per three years of service, so a worker with one year's service has 18 days [Para 287][Para 292]. Because Article 167(2) and (3) forbid paying annual leave in cash while the contract subsists [Para 314][Para 315], the panel refused the money claim but ordered the withheld 2020 days to be granted as days [Para 322][Para 326].
On Issue 5, Articles 279 of the Labour Law and 62 of the Trade Union Law bar using union membership or activity in disciplinary decisions [Para 329][Para 331], but the complainant must prove discrimination [Para 334]. No evidence was produced [Para 340], and the new attendance policy predated the union's registration on 9 June 2021 [Para 341], so the claim failed [Para 344]. A separate demand to reinstate an area manager was not decided: it was absent from the conciliation report and was already before the ministry [Para 350][Para 353].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The employer bears the burden of proving that wages and statutory benefits were paid, so a bare denial of unlawful deductions, unaccompanied by payroll records, does not discharge it and the claimed sums are ordered repaid [Para 216][Para 220]. Where staff work seven days a week the employer owes 200% for the rest day worked, and the same premium is due for work on a paid public holiday [Para 230][Para 283]. Annual leave cannot be converted into money while the employment relationship continues; the remedy is to grant the withheld days [Para 314][Para 322].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that it will not decide a demand raised for the first time at the hearing where that demand is absent from the conciliation report and is already being handled by the ministry [Para 350][Para 353].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Year figures are truncated to "ឆ្នាំ ២០" throughout, so 2020 cannot be distinguished from 2021 in most of the reasoning [Para 4][Para 247]. The award date is printed as "30 July August 2021" before being postponed to 22 September 2021 [Para 35], and the company name alternates between ស្ទឹម and ស្ទីម [Para 15][Para 38]. The holiday Prakas is cited as No. 10 in one passage and No. 100 in another [Para 228][Para 239], and stray OCR text interrupts the quotation of Article 166 [Para 293]. The operative order names five individual workers, who are identified here by role only [Para 221].
