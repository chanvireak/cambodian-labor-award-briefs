# Award No. 36/06 — Bon Duti Chrana Thing & Garment Factory Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 29 May 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ច for the employer party, ទូន ស៊ី ជា នំ for the worker party, គង់ ជល្លៈ chair) · Doc ID 140700

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ប៉ុន ដូតិ ច្រា នីត ធីង និង ហ្គាមិន ហ្វាត់ ថូរ លីមីធីត, a factory at Toul Kork village, Chak Angre Krom, Mean Chey, with about 1,200 workers, has hired on fixed-duration contracts since 2003 — first one year at a time, later six or three months — and some 500 workers have total service above two years through successive renewals. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29] On 31 March 2006, 130 to 200 workers stopped work for about an hour and asked why payment in lieu of annual leave was not being made, having had unclear information from their union president; the company treated the stoppage as a strike and deducted one quarter of the USD 5 weekly attendance bonus from those workers. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 44] The union is សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ម៉ុន ដូតិ ច្សា, and of the six unresolved demands three (issues 1, 3 and 4) were settled at the hearing. [Para 2][Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether fixed-duration contracts whose successive renewals exceed two years must be converted into contracts of indefinite duration? [Para 3][Para 28]
2. (Issue 5) Whether the company must pay 100 per cent wages for the periods when it has no work to give, instead of the contractual 50, 25 or 0 per cent? [Para 6][Para 35]
3. (Issue 6) Whether the 200 workers who stopped work on 31 March 2006 must be paid the USD 5 attendance bonus that the company deducted for that day? [Para 7][Para 83]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** contracts renewed beyond two years in total have become contracts of indefinite duration; wages must be paid at 100 per cent when the company has no work; and the USD 5 bonus must be restored for the 31 March 2006 stoppage, which they say was not a strike but a protest. [Para 3][Para 6][Para 7][Para 42][Para 84]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ប៉ុន ដូតិ ច្រា នីត ធីង និង ហ្គាមិន ហ្វាត់ ថូរ លីមីធីត rejects the panel's earlier reading of Article 67(2), relying on letter No. 2216 K.B.V/AK of 13 August 2004 of the Labour Inspectorate, and says the stoppage was a strike that forfeited the week's bonus. [Para 34][Para 42][Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the employer to convert to contracts of indefinite duration the contracts of every worker whose service exceeds two years, from the date the award takes effect, and refused both the 100 per cent wage claim and the USD 5 bonus claim. [Para 103][Para 104][Para 105][Para 106]

On Issue 2 the panel read Article 67(2) against Article 73(5): a fixed-duration contract may not exceed two years, may be renewed, and becomes indefinite if no notice of termination is given and the total duration exceeds the statutory maximum. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 51] It found the Khmer text ambiguous on whether "renewal" means each renewed term or the aggregate, so it consulted the French original, which caps the total at two years, and ILO Recommendation No. 166 of 1982, which says fixed-term contracts should not be used for long-term work and should become indefinite where renewals exceed two years in total. [Para 52][Para 56][Para 62][Para 63][Para 68] It held the Inspectorate's letter No. 2216 to be one official's personal view with no legal force, and followed its own award 10/03 (Zaksintec). [Para 70][Para 71][Para 72]

On Issue 5 the panel found no evidence of any lack of work, and none at the time of filing, so the demand concerned only a future situation; a rights dispute over an event that may never occur is not decided, following 10/03 (Zaksintec), 19/06 (Singhong) and 32/06 (Thop Vorn). [Para 74][Para 75][Para 79][Para 82]

On Issue 6 the panel applied the three elements of Article 318 — a group of workers, a stoppage of work, and a purpose of obtaining a solution to a demand — and found all three present; the stoppage was therefore a strike. [Para 83][Para 85][Para 86] The workers had given no notice seven working days in advance under Article 324, so the strike was unlawful. [Para 97][Para 98] Under Article 332 the contracts were suspended during the strike and no wages were due; Article 334 did not apply because no replacement workers were hired; and the protection against sanctions in Article 333 does not cover an unlawful strike, so the deduction of the attendance bonus was not an unlawful penalty, following cases 04/03, 03/05, 07/05, 22/04 and 49/05. [Para 87][Para 89][Para 90][Para 92][Para 99][Para 101]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where fixed-duration contracts are renewed so that the aggregate duration exceeds two years, they become contracts of indefinite duration: Article 67(2) read with Article 73(5), the French original text and ILO Recommendation No. 166 all make the two-year cap apply to total service, not to each renewed term, and a letter of the Labour Inspectorate cannot displace that reading. A stoppage that meets the three elements of Article 318 is a strike even if brief and even if the workers call it a protest; if it was called without the seven working days' notice required by Article 324 it is unlawful, wages are not payable under Article 332, and the protection of Article 333 does not prevent the employer from withholding an attendance bonus. [Para 51][Para 61][Para 68][Para 86][Para 98][Para 101]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that an indefinite-duration contract promotes job security valuable to workers and also benefits employers by encouraging commitment were contextual support for its reading of Article 67(2), not part of the order. [Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The claim preamble states that only issue 6 remains for decision, yet issues 2 and 5 are also reasoned and decided, and the text at [Para 20] is corrupt at that point.
- The employer's name is unstable across the award (ប៉ុន ដូតិ ច្រា នីត ធីង, ម៉ុន ដូតិ, ម៉ុន ដូពិច្ស, ម៉ុន ដូតិ ច្សា), so the corporate identity cannot be fixed from the face of the award [Para 1][Para 20][Para 27].
- A date is printed as 31 March "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៣" at [Para 84] where every other reference is 2006, and the number of participating workers varies between 130, 200 and "about 500" workers [Para 40][Para 29][Para 85].
- At [Para 31] a 50 per cent severance payment is printed as "ប្រាក់ បំណាច់ ៥ភាគរយ", an evident digit loss.
- The arbitrator for the workers is ទូន ស៊ី ជា នំ in the composition but ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ at signature, and the chair's name is printed twice in corrupted forms [Para 1][Para 108].
- The operative orders are duplicated and interleaved with fragments of themselves at [Para 103] to [Para 106], so their scope was taken from the reasoning.
