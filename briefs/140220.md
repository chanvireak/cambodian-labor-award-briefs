# Order No. 228/12 — Nex-t Apparel (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 23 November 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mr Ing Sothy for the employer party, Mr An Nan for the worker party, Mr Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 140220

## 1. Facts
The Coalition of Cambodian Apparel Workers' Democratic Union (CCAWDU) brought a collective dispute at Nex-t Apparel (Cambodia) Co Ltd, a garment factory in Dangkao, Phnom Penh, after a strike over working conditions; conciliation failed on all ten points and the Ministry's non-conciliation report No. 1336 of 15 November 2012 reached the Council the same day. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 19] The panel issued Order No. 032/12 of 15 November 2012 requiring the strike to be suspended at once and all strikers to return to their shifts from the morning of 16 November 2012. [Para 20][Para 21] The panel was then told the company was refusing entry to workers who had struck, and the workers in turn refused to return. [Para 22][Para 23] Ten demands are recorded — readmission of a union president and adviser and two named workers with back pay, a regular attendance bonus, voluntary overtime, sick-leave pay, annual leave on request, an end to abusive language by Chinese supervisors, 50 per cent of wages during 90 days' maternity leave, a canteen, no retaliation against strikers, and contract renewal of six months to one year. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 9][Para 10][Para 12] None was reached on the merits.

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel should continue the arbitration when the workers keep striking during the proceedings? [Para 33][Para 43]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they would continue the strike unless the employer let the barred workers return to work, and they confirmed that position at the pre-hearing meeting of 16 November 2012. [Para 26]
**Employer (respondent):** by letter of 16 November 2012 the company would take back only workers it had not dismissed; those it had dismissed from the strike were not to re-enter, and it confirmed this at the meeting. [Para 24][Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case No. 228/12, terminating the proceedings without any order on the ten demands. [Para 44][Para 45]

Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure, such as a strike or lockout, for the duration of the panel's work, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 33][Para 34] The panel read that article as preventing it from proceeding at any time the worker party is on strike, and followed its own earlier orders: the notice of 29 January 2004 in Order No. 04/04-IMSI, the second order of 22 April 2004 in case 18/04 (the Kambodiana, Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Grand d'Angkor, Sunway, InterContinental and Sofitel Angkor hotels) at points 9 and 11, the second order of 24 December 2005 in case 77/06 at point 4, and case 110/11 (Simano). [Para 36][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42]

On the facts, the panel found the workers had carried on the strike without respecting Order 032/12 and had shown no genuine intention of bringing the collective dispute to arbitration, so it declined to continue. [Para 43][Para 44] Because no hearing was ever held, the panel expressly made no findings of fact on the ten demands and weighed only the employer's letter and the Ministry file. [Para 27][Para 32]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended during the proceedings under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not hear the dispute and will terminate the arbitration, treating the continuing strike as conduct showing that the party does not intend to submit the dispute to arbitration; the demands are then left undecided rather than dismissed on their merits. [Para 33][Para 43][Para 44]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that the parties must attend every meeting it convenes, and its repetition of the earlier ruling that the Council may take up the same collective dispute again once the strike has ended, are general observations on procedure and do not qualify the termination ordered here. [Para 34][Para 37]

- The number of workers barred from re-entry is printed as "២ ២ នាក់", readable either as 2 or as 22; the panel later speaks of "both workers", which suggests 2. Both readings are reported. [Para 22][Para 26]
- The employer's name is printed in several different Khmer spellings across the order, including a variant in the case name itself, and appears in Latin script in the library catalogue as Nex-t Apparel (Cambodia) Co Ltd. [Para 1][Para 27][Para 45]
- The evidence list repeats the Ministry heading designator, so the Ministry documents appear under two "គ-" headings. [Para 28]
- Individual workers and union officers are named only in the demands and in the attendance list, in places lost to OCR or to a bare initial; they are recorded here by role. [Para 3][Para 12]
