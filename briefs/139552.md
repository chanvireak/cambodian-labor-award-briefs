# Award No. 094/14 — Six Plus Industry Street Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 9 May 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, employer-appointed; Sin Kimsan, worker-appointed; Pen Bunchea, chair, chosen by both) · arbitration terminated for want of a claimant · Doc ID 139552

## 1. Facts
Six Plus Industry Street Co., Ltd operates at Kandal village, Prek Anhchanh commune, Mukh Kampul district, Kandal. Two demands reached the panel through the provincial labour department's non-conciliation report No. 457/14 of 22 April 2014: that the government and the employer release the 21 people detained after a strike, and that the company raise the basic wage to USD 160 a month. Conciliation on 21 April 2014 had failed because the worker side was absent without notice. When the secretariat took the hearing summons to the factory on 28 April 2014, no worker accepted it or claimed to be the claimant, and workers at the factory said nobody was pursuing either demand. At the hearing of 2 May 2014 the employer appeared and no worker representative did. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 7][Para 12][Para 22][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the government and the employer must release the 21 people detained in connection with the strike? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must set the basic wage at USD 160 a month? [Para 3]
3. Whether the panel can conduct an arbitration at all where no claimant comes forward? [Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Nothing was put before the panel. The demands exist only as recorded in the non-conciliation report; no worker attended the conciliation or the hearing, none accepted the summons, and workers present at the factory denied that anyone was pursuing the two points. [Para 12][Para 23][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent):** The company accepted the summons and attended through its administration representative, Mr S, producing a letter of 30 April 2014 transferring the director's authority to him; it advanced no argument on the merits, and none was needed. [Para 11][Para 14]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no claimant, arbitration terminated — the panel ordered that the arbitration of case No. 094/14 be brought to an end, with no order on either demand and no award of money. [Para 34][Para 35]

The panel recast the matter as procedure: what is a panel to do where there is no claimant? Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council requires the panel to summon the parties in dispute to explain their claims in person and to produce the documents and information needed; from that the panel drew the ordinary rule that a claimant must present its reasons and evidence before the panel to support its demand, and the respondent must answer once the claimant has done so. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]

Applying it, the panel found the factual premise missing: the secretariat had gone to the factory, no worker claimed to be a party or accepted the invitation, and those at the factory said the two points were not being demanded by anyone. With no claimant present and no demand being made, the panel held there was no claimant in the case, declined to open the hearing, and found itself unable to establish any facts on the two points in report No. 457/14. It therefore terminated the arbitration rather than deciding the demands on a one-sided record. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 26][Para 34]

Two features of the demands went unaddressed because the case never reached the merits: the first was directed at the government as well as the employer, and the second, a basic wage of USD 160, was a minimum-wage question. The panel said nothing about either, and nothing in the award should be read as approval or rejection of them. [Para 2][Para 3]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a collective dispute is referred to the panel but no worker comes forward to claim the demand, refuses the summons, and those at the workplace deny pursuing it, there is no claimant and no case; the panel must terminate the arbitration instead of ruling on the demands recorded in the non-conciliation report. [Para 31][Para 33][Para 34]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's general statement of the burden of proof — that a claimant must give reasons and evidence first, and the respondent's duty to answer arises only afterwards — was not applied to any evidence, since none was heard. [Para 29][Para 30]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel is recorded as constituted on "24 April 2019 2014" (para 9), the same doubled-year slip found elsewhere in this file. The case number is printed as 094/14 in the style of cause but as 94/14 and 9/14 in the body text (paras 21, 25, 35), and the company appears as "Six Plus X Industry Street" in the invitation quoted at para 21. The award is short and ends at page 5, with no reasons on the merits; the two demands are known only from the conciliation report, whose own text is not reproduced. The employer's representative is named in full in the header but is given here as Mr S. [Para 1][Para 9][Para 21][Para 36]
