# Award No. 353/14 — Jin Yi Garmen (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 27 January 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer party; An Non, chosen by the worker party, dissenting in part; Tang Tri, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139798

## 1. Facts
Jin Yi Garmen (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment factory in Kampong Chhnang province employing about 3,600 workers, 449 of whom mandated four federations to represent them. [Para 86][Para 88] On 5 December 2014, between noon and 2 p.m., thirteen union leaders and committee members walked from building to building telling other workers to stop work over the administration's refusal to end the deduction of union dues; about 200 workers in four buildings downed tools, and all thirteen were dismissed that day. [Para 135][Para 142][Para 183] Of nine demands, conciliation on 17 December 2014 settled none and the hearing of 25 December 2014 settled issues 2 and 6, leaving seven. [Para 17][Para 22][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back the thirteen union leaders and committee members and let them work again with their benefits preserved? [Para 90][Para 182]
2. Whether the employer must dismiss its administration head, Mr L? [Para 150][Para 238]
3. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus by 5 US dollars to 15 US dollars a month, and whether it must pay that bonus pro rata for days of absence permitted by the employer? [Para 156][Para 259]
4. Whether the employer must dismiss the manager of groups 1 to 4 in building 11, Ms C? [Para 162][Para 317]
5. Whether the employer must add 28 US dollars to the base wage for 2015? [Para 167][Para 330]
6. Whether the employer must compute the overtime rate by including the seniority bonus? [Para 171][Para 340]
7. Whether the employer must preserve wages and benefits during the strike of 11 to 22 December 2014 and refrain from suing any person or body? [Para 176][Para 352]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the thirteen were dismissed for union discrimination, without valid ground, notice or the labour inspector's authorisation; the administration head delayed ending the dues deduction for 704 workers who had resigned, and the group manager pressured workers who refused overtime, used abusive language and made leave hard to obtain; the 28 US dollar increase matches neighbouring factories; seniority bonus is wage and must enter the overtime rate; strikers' wages must be preserved because the employer hired replacements. [Para 91][Para 151][Para 164][Para 173][Para 177]

**Employer (respondent):** the thirteen committed serious misconduct under Article 83 and clauses 2 and 10 of the internal rules by parading, inciting others to stop work and stalling production; it already pays above the law, with a 10 US dollar attendance bonus and a 2, 4, 6 and 10 US dollar sliding deduction for permitted absences; managers essential to sample checking cannot be dismissed, and the group manager was already transferred; no decision has been taken on 2015 wages; it hired no replacements and stopped recruiting on 12 December 2014; it will not sue anyone who breaks no law. [Para 137][Para 178][Para 181][Para 312][Para 324][Para 331]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — every demand was refused, five on the merits and two for want of jurisdiction or standing; the award is non-binding subject to the eight-day objection period. [Para 372][Para 380]

On reinstatement the panel set out Article 293 of the Labour Law, barring dismissal of staff delegates and election candidates without the labour inspector's consent, and Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, protecting candidates 45 days either side of a union election. [Para 207][Para 209] Protection of elected leaders continues beyond 45 days only for a reasonable period, such as until the union applies for registration. [Para 214] Four of the thirteen were elected on 30 August 2014 and dismissed 97 days later while the union had still not applied to register, a period the panel found long enough to organise; eight held office without having been candidates, so only the closed 45-day window could apply; the thirteenth's role was never stated. [Para 221][Para 226][Para 230][Para 233] None was protected and the demand failed. [Para 237]

On the two dismissal demands the panel held that only the parties to an employment contract may terminate it, so workers cannot require a manager's dismissal unless they prove the person is dangerous to others. [Para 241][Para 246] No evidence met that burden, and the employer had already moved the group manager to the sample section. [Para 257][Para 328]

On the attendance bonus the panel split the demand. The increase was an interests dispute, and because the claimant union was not the most representative it refused to consider it, applying Article 96 of the Labour Law, Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 264][Para 271][Para 278] On the pro-rata claim it held on the merits that point 2 of Prakas No. 230 of 25 July 2012 makes the bonus conditional on attendance "without absence", that the 2012 text deliberately tightened the earlier Prakas No. 041, and that absence with permission but without pay fails the condition; the sliding deduction was more favourable than forfeiting the whole 10 US dollars. [Para 283][Para 288][Para 314]

On the 28 US dollar claim no dispute yet existed: the employer had taken no decision on 2015 wages, and the Council resolves disputes that have arisen, not future ones. [Para 333][Para 338]

On the overtime rate the panel found that Article 139, Article 144, Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 and point 3 of Prakas No. 041 require computation on the ordinary wage and say nothing of the seniority bonus. [Para 342][Para 349][Para 350]

On the strike it applied Article 332, under which the contract is suspended and no wage is payable during a strike, and Article 334, which requires wages only where the employer hired replacements; no evidence of replacement hiring was produced, and recruiting had stopped on 12 December 2014. [Para 354][Para 359][Para 369]

Arbitrator An Non dissented on issues 1, 4(2) and 8, holding that Article 333 forbids any sanction for taking part in a strike, that the strike was never declared unlawful and ended within 48 hours, and that the seniority bonus should enter the overtime rate. [Para 390][Para 400][Para 431][Para 433]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The protection against dismissal that Article 293 of the Labour Law and Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 give to union election candidates runs 45 days either side of the ballot, and for elected leaders lasts only a reasonable period after it — no longer than is needed to apply for the union's registration; a leader dismissed after that window, the union never having applied, cannot claim the protection. [Para 209][Para 214][Para 233] Workers have no standing to demand a manager's dismissal, because only the parties to that manager's contract may end it, unless they prove the manager endangers others. [Para 243][Para 246] A demand for a wage increase the employer has not yet decided on is a future dispute the panel will not hear. [Para 333][Para 338]

**Obiter dicta:** the remarks that 97 days was long enough for the union to prepare its registration file, and that absence with permission still breaches the attendance condition, were reasoning toward conclusions reached on other grounds. [Para 230][Para 297]
