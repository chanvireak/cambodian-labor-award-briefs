# Award No. 225/16 — Jin Yi Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 21 November 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun appointed by the employer, An Non appointed by the workers, Kuy Nam chair) · Doc ID 139379

## 1. Facts
Jin Yi Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. sews garments at Spien Po village, Samakki Mean Chey district, Kampong Chhnang, and employs about 4,336 workers through four unions, none of which held a certificate of most-representative status. [Para 1][Para 46][Para 47][Para 57] Two workers, Moun Touch (ironing, later assistant team leader) and Him Vanny (warehouse, later carton scanner), had each worked since February 2013 on successive six-month fixed-duration contracts; in October 2016 the employer let the final contracts expire with 15 days' notice instead of renewing. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 88][Para 89][Para 90] Seven days of Moun Touch's October wages and twelve days of Him Vanny's remained unpaid. [Para 64][Para 91]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take Moun Touch back — that is, whether the ending of her contract was a dismissal or a non-renewal of a fixed-duration contract, and if a dismissal whether it was lawful? [Para 143]
2. Whether the employer must take Him Vanny back on the same two questions? [Para 183]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** both had worked continuously for more than two years, so their fixed-duration contracts had become undetermined duration and the employer needed a valid reason; each family is poor and work is hard to find locally. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 93][Para 94]
**Employer (respondent):** the contracts simply reached their expiry dates, so this was non-renewal and not dismissal; each worker had been warned repeatedly for breaching internal rules — four times for Moun Touch, five for Him Vanny — and neither will be taken back. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 95][Para 96][Para 269]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to take back Moun Touch and Him Vanny and to give them work again; the majority ordered no money, reinstatement being the remedy. [Para 220][Para 221]

The panel first settled who could bring the claim. Under Article 54(1) of the Law on Trade Unions of 17 May 2016 only the most-representative union holds exclusive standing to raise and argue a collective dispute; none of the four unions held that certificate, and the mandates of the Khmer and Worker Benefit union leaders had expired, with no membership lists or recognition letters filed. [Para 106][Para 115][Para 132][Para 135] Following Award 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting), where no most-representative union exists the workers concerned may represent themselves or mandate a union or another person; under Article 19 of Prakas 099 the eleven workers present at the hearing were therefore the claimants. [Para 116][Para 137][Para 141]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 67(2): a fixed-duration contract may not run longer than two years, and successive renewals may not exceed two years in total, failing which the contract becomes one of undetermined duration. [Para 143][Para 147] Moun Touch started on 07 February 2013 on successive six-month contracts whose total exceeded two years, so her relationship was of undetermined duration and the expiry argument failed; the act was a dismissal. [Para 157][Para 158] Article 74 then required a reason connected with her aptitude or behaviour or with the needs of the enterprise, and the employer relied on four breaches of its internal rules. [Para 165][Para 166] Prakar 10 of those rules permits dismissal for four consecutive minor breaches or two medium breaches, and defines minor breaches by reference to Prakars 2 and 12. [Para 167][Para 173] The proven acts — pressing and looking at a telephone, sleeping at work, mis-sorting labels — appear in neither Prakar, and no other rule classified them as medium or serious; the panel also noted that the missing labels were later found. [Para 75][Para 176][Para 177] Finding no valid reason, it ordered reinstatement under Prakar 34 of Prakas 099. [Para 179][Para 182]

Issue 2 ran the same course: Him Vanny began on 13 February 2013 on successive six-month contracts totalling more than two years, so his contract too was of undetermined duration; [Para 183][Para 186][Para 187] the five warnings did not correspond to faults defined in Prakars 2 and 12, [Para 198][Para 201] and the warning of 28 May 2016 for incorrect carton scanning was not shown to have affected the company's operations, so it was invalid. [Para 208][Para 214] Absent a valid reason under Article 74, reinstatement followed. [Para 216][Para 219]

Arbitrator Seng Vouch Hun dissented in part: he agreed both terminations were dismissals without valid reason but would have declined reinstatement and ordered notice compensation, severance pay, moral damages, unused annual leave and final wages instead. [Para 231][Para 317]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed-duration contract renewed successively until its total term exceeds two years converts into a contract of undetermined duration under Article 67(2); letting it then expire is a dismissal that must rest on a reason connected with the worker's aptitude or behaviour or the needs of the enterprise under Article 74. A breach can support dismissal only if the enterprise's own internal rules classify it as minor, medium or serious in the terms those rules use, and a warning issued without stated reasons or any explanation of its effect on operations is invalid. [Para 143][Para 165][Para 177][Para 208]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the absence of a most-representative union can obstruct workers' rights of representation and engage Article 39 of the Constitution, and its survey of earlier awards on union standing, are general observations not necessary to the order. [Para 114][Para 112] The dissent's calculations of compensation never became the award. [Para 271]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award date is printed two ways: 21 November 2016 in the header and at the signature [Para 1][Para 532], but the dissenting appendix recites an award "issued 18 November 2016" [Para 223][Para 381]. The panel had earlier extended its own deadline from 10 to 21 November 2016 [Para 21]. Both dates are reported above as printed.
- Start dates carry OCR debris: Moun Touch's appears as 07 February 2013 [Para 59] and as "07 February 2019 2013" [Para 157]; Him Vanny's as 13 February 2013 [Para 88] and as "13 December February 2013" [Para 183].
- The document prints the award and then re-prints the whole reasoning inside a dissent appendix with its own pagination, so the same paragraphs appear twice at different line numbers (for example [Para 165] and [Para 392]). The appendix's compensatory orders are the minority view, not the decision.
- The conciliation file is described as carrying "9 4" unsettled points [Para 13] and four at [Para 12]; fault counts are printed as "05 0 times" [Para 345] and "05 0 times" again at [Para 500].
- Names are transliterated inconsistently (Moun Touch/Muon Touch, Him Vanny/Him Vanny/Him Vanny), and the employer's representatives appear as "HSU SHIH CHUAN" and "HSHIH CHUAN" [Para 1][Para 22].
