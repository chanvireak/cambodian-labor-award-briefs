# Award No. 333/15 — Yan Darong Foung (Cambodia) Tech Style (Arbitration Council, 20 January 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phalha, president), with two dissenting opinions annexed · Doc ID 139499

## 1. Facts

The company knits garments in Takeo province with about 1,000 workers. [Para 54] The Solidarity Labour Union of Cambodia (សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព ការងារ កម្ពុជា) held a leadership election on 06 November 2015 at which Mr Y (Yun Sokha, staff No. D53, warehouse) became president; the union notified the employer on 23 November 2015, which refused the notice. [Para 58][Para 71] Workers struck from 05 to 23 November 2015. [Para 114] On 07 December 2015 the employer told Mr Y he was dismissed because there was no work for him, and paid him nothing; the employer did not attend the 12 January 2016 hearing, sought an adjournment to February, and its administration assistant then left the room. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 38][Para 62]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the employer must reinstate Mr Y with back pay and end anti-union discrimination? [Para 63]
2. Whether 4,000 riel monthly union dues must be deducted? [Para 190]
3. Whether the attendance bonus is owed pro rata for authorised personal leave? [Para 202]
4. Whether the employer must stop compelling overtime? [Para 225]
5. Whether wages and the bonus continue during certified sick leave? [Para 246]
6. Whether hot and cold drinking water is owed? [Para 275]
7. Whether pregnant workers may leave 15 minutes early? [Para 287]
8. Whether a canteen must be built? [Para 294]
9. Whether toilets must be clean and hygienic? [Para 304]
10. Whether night-shift wages are miscalculated? [Para 316]
11. Whether aggregated late-arrival minutes may cancel the bonus? [Para 333]
12. Whether one paid antenatal day a month is owed? [Para 344]
13. Whether leave procedures must be simplified? [Para 351]
14. Whether the hot buildings need more fans? [Para 376]
15. Whether masks, strike wages and protection from retaliation are owed? [Para 387][Para 396]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** Mr Y was elected president on 06 November 2015 and dismissed on 07 December 2015 without reason or the Labour Inspector's authorisation, so the dismissal is void; the employer also compelled overtime by requiring "voluntary" letters and punishing refusal, cut the USD 10 bonus for any absence, and withheld water, fans, masks, clean toilets and a canteen. [Para 68][Para 82][Para 241]

**Employer (respondent):** The employer did not appear, sending only a letter of 11 January 2016 saying its director was abroad and its administration chief had resigned, and asking that the hearing be moved to February 2016. [Para 61][Para 36]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to reinstate Mr Y in his former post with wages from the dismissal date to reinstatement, to stop compelling overtime, to provide sufficient clean cold drinking water, to keep the toilets clean and hygienic, to install sufficient fans or other cooling in the pressing building and warehouse, and to provide masks to all workers; all other claims were dismissed. [Para 433][Para 437][Para 440][Para 443][Para 448][Para 449]

As a threshold, the panel held it could proceed in the employer's absence under Articles 21 and 30 of Prakas No. 099, the employer having been properly summoned, not appeared and given no valid reason; the Article 313 two-month limit expired on 20 January 2016 and the workers refused postponement, so the adjournment was refused and the employer treated as having waived its right to reply. [Para 129][Para 132][Para 164]

On reinstatement, the panel applied Article 293 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 305: a union leader is protected like a staff delegate, and the protection attaches from candidacy and continues at least 45 days after the election. [Para 134][Para 135][Para 153] Mr Y had been elected on 06 November 2015 and dismissed on 07 December 2015, within that period, and the employer had not sought the Labour Inspector's prior authorisation, so under Article 4 of Prakas No. 305 the dismissal was void and reinstatement with back pay followed. [Para 152][Para 170][Para 171][Para 174] The panel dismissed the anti-discrimination claim for want of any evidence, noting that the union's vice-president and secretary remained at work. [Para 186][Para 187][Para 188]

On union dues, Article 129 of the Labour Law and Article 5 of Prakas No. 305 require the worker's written authority to reach the employer at least 15 days in advance; the panel found that the 208 thumbprinted requests of 09 November 2015 had been addressed to the union president and never sent to the employer, so the employer owed no deduction. [Para 190][Para 192][Para 199][Para 200]

On the attendance bonus, the panel applied point 2 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, which requires attendance on the working days of the month "without absence"; it held the bonus is a conditional entitlement payable only when those conditions are met, that authorised personal leave still breaks the condition, and that the claim was in any event one for better-than-law benefits which the union, not being the most representative, could not bring; the claim was dismissed. [Para 206][Para 212][Para 223][Para 272]

On overtime, the panel applied Article 4 of Prakas No. 80 of 25 March 1999: overtime must be voluntary, and an employer may request but not compel it or impose discipline for refusal; it found the practice of demanding signed "voluntary" letters and sending refusers to administration for warning or dismissal contrary to that rule and to Notification No. 030/15, and ordered the practice stopped. [Para 225][Para 244][Para 245]

On sick leave, the panel applied Article 71(3) and Article 72(1) of the Labour Law: certified sickness suspends the contract, so the employer owes neither wages nor the attendance bonus. [Para 246][Para 248][Para 255][Para 256]

On facilities, the panel granted clean cold water under Article 1 of Prakas No. 054 (sufficient hygienic drinking water, not hot water) and clean toilets under Article 229 of the Labour Law and Articles 1, 2 and 6 of Prakas No. 052; fans under Article 229 and Prakas No. 147 on workplace heat; and masks under Article 229, finding dust, fabric particles, chemical vapour and kiln ash affect health and that masks had gone only to cleaning staff. [Para 281][Para 284][Para 315][Para 386][Para 395] The canteen, early departure for pregnant workers, the antenatal day and the simplified leave procedure were rejected as interest disputes or for want of evidence; night-shift pay and the late-minute aggregation failed for want of evidence and because the practice was better than the law. [Para 289][Para 327][Para 346][Para 374] Strike wages were refused under Articles 332, 334 and 72, no unlawful replacement hiring being proved. [Para 399][Para 409][Para 417]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A union leader is protected from dismissal from candidacy until at least 45 days after the election; a dismissal within that window without the Labour Inspector's prior authorisation is void, and reinstatement with wages from dismissal to return follows, whatever reason the employer gives. Separately, a union that is not the most representative in the enterprise cannot bring interest disputes — claims for benefits beyond the law — and the panel will decline to consider them. [Para 171][Para 274]

**Obiter dicta:** An employer that sought but was refused an adjournment and then stayed away made itself the author of its own absence, extinguishing its right of reply. Two arbitrators dissented: Ing Sothy on the back-pay order, An Non on the attendance bonus. [Para 131][Para 455][Para 465]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The panel records 8 of 23 points withdrawn, leaving 19 — but 23 minus 8 is 15, and only seven are listed as withdrawn. [Para 39][Para 40] The second hearing is dated "02 February 2015" though it occurred in 2016. [Para 35] The award is dated 20 January 2016 in the header and signature block, while An Non's annexed dissent is dated "20 January 2015". [Para 464][Para 470] Para 152 says Mr Y was dismissed "nearly 30 months" after the election, an interval in fact of one month. [Para 152] The employer's name varies between Yan Darong Foung, Yan Darong Foung Tech Style and Yan Darong Houng. [Para 1][Para 27] Individual workers and the team leader are reduced to initials per redaction policy.
