# Award No. 31/10 — Prek Treng Trading (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 12 April 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Song Vann Sin for the worker party, chair named Tang Phalla on the face and Kong Phalla in the signature block) · Doc ID 140259

## 1. Facts
Workers of the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation at the Prek Treng Trading (Cambodia) enterprise in Saang district, Kandal province, filed 24 demands for improved working conditions on 28 January 2010; the provincial labour department conciliated on 25 February 2010 without settling any of the 24, and non-conciliation report No. 153/10 of 16 March 2010 reached the secretariat on 19 March 2010. [Para 1][Para 27][Para 30][Para 31] A hearing was fixed for 1 April 2010 at 14:00, and both parties were formally invited on 25 March 2010. [Para 29][Para 33][Para 39][Para 40]

At 9:10 on the morning of the hearing, before the session opened, the Council received a letter dated 30 March 2010 by which the workers withdrew their complaint. [Para 35][Para 43] Neither party then appeared. [Para 34] By an earlier letter of 7 October 2009 the workers had transferred authority to ten representatives to negotiate with the company, the ministry, the Council, the courts and the authorities; the withdrawal letter carried the fingerprints of 18 people, and the panel identified six named representatives among them who held that delegation. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 44][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the withdrawal letter submitted by the representatives to whom the workers had transferred authority was legally sufficient to close the file without examining the 24 unresolved demands? [Para 46][Para 47]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation and the representatives holding the transfer of authority had power to decide whether to pursue the demands or withdraw them, and by the letter of 30 March 2010 they chose to end the case. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent):** absent from the hearing and filed no documents with the panel. [Para 1][Para 34][Para 36]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel ordered the closure of case No. 31/10-Prek Treng Trading, the claimants having withdrawn their complaint. [Para 50][Para 51]

The panel began from the transfer of authority: because the workers had delegated to their representatives the decision whether to continue or to withdraw the demands, the only question was whether the letter of 30 March 2010 was a valid exercise of that power. [Para 45][Para 46] It verified that the letter bore the fingerprints of the representatives and found it sufficient in form to allow the file to be closed, since the workers had expressed the will to bring the case to an end. [Para 44][Para 47] It followed its earlier Award No. 47/05, where a union and local union had asked for the file to be closed and the panel held that a party which withdraws its claim ends the case automatically. [Para 48][Para 49] Applying that reasoning, it concluded that the withdrawal terminated the 24 demands, which were therefore never examined on their merits. [Para 49][Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the persons to whom workers have transferred authority notify the panel in writing that the complaint is withdrawn, the panel closes the file: a withdrawal by an authorized claimant ends the dispute automatically, and the panel neither examines the demands nor issues any ruling on their merits. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 49][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's verification of the fingerprints on the withdrawal letter, and its identification of six of the eighteen signatories as holders of the delegation, exceed what the closing order required once the authorized representatives had signed. [Para 44][Para 45]
