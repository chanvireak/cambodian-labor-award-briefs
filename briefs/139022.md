# Award No. 092/16 — Da Rong Printing & Im Pray Diri Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 12 May 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy appointed by the employer, Soung Sophal appointed by the workers, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · binding award made by the parties' agreement of 26 April 2016, with a dissenting opinion annexed · Doc ID 139022

## 1. Facts
The company embroidered and printed on garments at Trapeung, Peuk commune, Ang Snuol, Kandal, employing about 442 workers. [Para 58][Para 59] The Democratic Union of Cambodian Garment Workers received transfers of rights from 280 workers by letter of 24 February 2016. [Para 60] Three printing-section workers entered on 03 December 2013 on two months' probation, were then given a two-year fixed-term contract, and on its expiry were given a three-month contract running 03 December 2015 to 03 March 2016; the employer ended their employment on 03 March 2016. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72] By the date of the last contract they had more than two years' service. [Para 111]

## 2. Issues in dispute
Three claims reached the panel; issue 2 was withdrawn at the hearing, so two were decided in the panel's own numbering. [Para 18][Para 19]
1. Whether the employer must take the three named workers back into work and pay them back wages from the day they were stopped until they resume? [Para 63][Para 89]
3. Whether the employer must convert the fixed-term contracts of workers whose aggregate contract period exceeds two years into contracts of indefinite duration? [Para 74][Para 164]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they had worked more than two years and had never been at fault or warned, so their contracts had become contracts of indefinite duration and their stopping was a dismissal; a worker whose successive fixed-term contracts total more than two years is entitled to an indefinite-duration contract under the Labour Law and the panel's earlier awards. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 75][Para 85]
**Employer (respondent):** this was not a dismissal but the non-renewal of fixed-term contracts that had reached their term, under Article 73; the employer renews for everyone and declined only for these three, who had been absent 72 days across 2014 and 2015, and it warns or terminates at term so that the company can keep operating through economic difficulty. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 78][Para 81][Para 82][Para 88]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel refused reinstatement and back wages but ordered the employer to pay each of the three workers compensation in lieu of notice of 1 month (Articles 75 and 77), dismissal damages (Article 89), unused annual leave (Articles 166 and 167), moral damages (Article 91) and final wages if not yet paid, the sums to be computed on their seniority; and it ordered the conversion of fixed-term contracts to indefinite duration for all workers whose aggregate contract period exceeds 02 years. [Para 158][Para 159][Para 160][Para 161][Para 162][Para 163][Para 169][Para 171][Para 176]

On issue 1 the panel began with Article 67(2): a fixed-term contract may not exceed two years and may be renewed only for periods each not exceeding two years, and any practice contrary to that rule turns the contract into one of indefinite duration. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91] It followed Award No. 10/03 in reading the provision as converting the relationship once the aggregate duration of all the contracts exceeds two years. [Para 95][Para 104] It found the three workers had served their probation, a two-year term and then a three-month term, so their aggregate service already exceeded 02 years by the time the last contract ended and their contracts had become indefinite. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 111][Para 112] The ending on 03 March 2016 was therefore not a non-renewal at term but a dismissal under an indefinite-duration contract. [Para 113][Para 121] Under Article 74 such a dismissal is lawful only for a valid reason going to the worker's aptitude or character or to the necessities of the enterprise's operation. [Para 114][Para 116] The employer relied on 72 days' absence in 2014 and 2015 but never said which worker was responsible and produced no document or evidence at all, and the panel held that the party making an allegation carries the burden. [Para 122][Para 128][Para 129][Para 130][Para 131] It found the dismissal unsupported by any valid reason and applied Articles 75, 77, 89, 91, 166 and 167 to the compensation. [Para 132][Para 144][Para 145][Para 148][Para 154] It did not state why it declined reinstatement and back wages, though it dismissed that part of the claim. [Para 158]

On issue 3 the panel applied the same reading of Article 67(2), together with Article 73(5), and observed that the Labour Law leans towards indefinite-duration contracts because job security matters to workers and long service also serves the employer. [Para 92][Para 96][Para 97][Para 98] It cited ILO Recommendation No. 166 of 1982, which states that fixed-term contracts should not be used for work of a permanent nature and should become indefinite where successive contracts total more than two years. [Para 100][Para 101] It held that an employer may not keep concluding fixed-term contracts with a worker whose aggregate fixed-term service already exceeds two years, and must instead conclude a contract of indefinite duration. [Para 167][Para 168]

**Dissent:** the employer-appointed arbitrator, Ing Sothy, recorded a dissent in an annex under Article 37 of Prakas No. 099, arguing that the three contracts remained fixed-term, that the 5% end-of-contract compensation under Article 73(6) was paid at each expiry, and that the panel should not order conversion. [Para 178][Para 180][Para 181][Para 183][Para 188][Para 201]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where successive fixed-term contracts with the same employer total more than two years, the relationship becomes one of indefinite duration by operation of Article 67(2) of the Labour Law, and ending it when the last short renewal expires is a dismissal, not a non-renewal at term; the employer must then show a valid reason under Article 74, and the party alleging absenteeism must identify the worker concerned and produce evidence of it. Correspondingly, an employer may not continue to issue fixed-term contracts to a worker whose aggregate fixed-term service already exceeds two years, and must conclude a contract of indefinite duration with that worker. [Para 91][Para 104][Para 113][Para 130][Para 168]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general observations that the Labour Law leans towards indefinite-duration contracts, and that long service benefits the employer as well as the worker, are reasoning of principle that was not necessary to either order. [Para 96][Para 97]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- No sum is stated anywhere for any head of compensation; the order names the heads of payment and leaves the amounts to be computed from seniority, so the operative figures cannot be reported. [Para 159][Para 171]
- The panel gives no reason for refusing reinstatement and back wages while granting severance for the same unlawful dismissal; the operative paragraph simply rejects that part of the claim. [Para 158][Para 170]
- The panel limits its decision to workers who transferred rights and those present at the hearing [Para 62], yet orders conversion for all workers with more than two years' aggregate service [Para 176]; 42 workers had such seniority [Para 50] while 37 claimants on this issue did not attend [Para 76].
- Attendance and representation figures differ: about 442 workers employed [Para 59], 280 transfers [Para 60], 05 workers at the hearing [Para 61].
- The conciliation record is dated 03 March 2016 in one place [Para 9], 30 March 2016 in another [Para 14][Para 52] and 29 March 2016 in a third [Para 30], against a final conciliation held on 28 March 2016 [Para 13].
- The company's registration certificate is dated "22 July 2019 2002" [Para 58], two years printed in one date; the employer's name appears in at least five forms (ដា រ៉ុង ព្រីន ធីង & អ៊ីម ប្រយ ឌីរី, ដារ៉ុង ព្រីន អែន អ៊ិម ប្រោយ ឌីរី, ដា វ៉ុង ព្រីន ធីង) [Para 1][Para 24][Para 27][Para 58].
- The dissent's signature is illegible ("ហត្ថលេខា របស់ អាជ្ញា កំណាល?") and the dissent annex is numbered inconsistently within the award [Para 202].
