# Award No. 010/21 — Ken Sport Shoe (Arbitration Council, 12 August 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy appointed by the employer party, An Nan appointed by the worker party, Kong Phalla chair; An Nan dissented on issue 7) · Doc ID 140828

## 1. Facts
Ken Sport Shoe (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ), a shoe factory at Chamkar Svay, Samakki Mon Chey district, Kampong Chhnang, employed 8,870 workers. [Para 70][Para 71] On 2 April 2021 the provincial Labour Department received 15 demands on working conditions, and conciliation on 12 April 2021 settled six of them, leaving nine for the panel. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19] At the online hearing the parties agreed issues 1 and 2 and asked that the agreement be written into the award, merged issues 3 and 9, leaving six issues to be decided. [Para 26][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 3 and 9) Whether the employer must install three more card-swipe machines at the front gate and open the building machines at 6:20 a.m.? [Para 80][Para 128]
2. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must stop deducting wages, function allowance and attendance bonus from workers who arrive less than ten minutes late? [Para 93][Para 142]
3. (Issue 5) Whether the employer owes meal allowance for overtime, having paid for only four of five overtime days a week? [Para 104][Para 152]
4. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must add at least two more doctors to the infirmary? [Para 109][Para 161]
5. (Issue 7) Whether the employer must restore the section-chief salary and allowances of a worker it moved to translator after she became union secretary? [Para 112][Para 169]
6. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must fix the number of production lines and staff them adequately without change? [Para 124][Para 188]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត):** congestion at only two gate machines made workers two or three minutes late, and the employer then docked wages, function allowance and attendance bonus; meal allowance for overtime was paid for four days when five were worked; one doctor and four nurses cannot serve 8,300 workers; and the union secretary lost USD 40 of basic wage, USD 10 of function allowance, USD 70 of section-chief allowance and USD 80 of translator allowance after 19 September 2019 because she took the union post. [Para 86][Para 106][Para 110][Para 116]
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេន ស្ព័ត ស៊ូ):** 39 to 40 swipe machines remained across the plant though the workforce had fallen from ten thousand to eight thousand, and the two gate machines are opened at 6:55 for latecomers; deductions follow clause 1.8 of the internal regulations and letter SGC-2-HUM-005 and are proportionate; five days of overtime meal allowance were paid; the infirmary meets Prakas No. 330; and the demotion was made at the worker's own written request of 14 September 2019, effective 1 October 2019. [Para 83][Para 88][Para 98][Para 107][Para 111][Para 117][Para 122]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — every remaining claim was rejected, and the award issued as a non-binding order. [Para 197][Para 199][Para 204][Para 205]

On issues 3 and 9 the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law, holding that the employer may arrange and manage the enterprise so long as it does so lawfully and reasonably; the workers produced no concrete evidence of the congestion they alleged, and the panel found the arrangement affected no right or benefit. [Para 128][Para 129][Para 137][Para 139][Para 140] On issue 4 it held that clause 1.8 of the internal regulations of 7 June 2016 and clause 4.6.3.2 of letter SGC-2-HUM-005 expressly authorise proportionate deductions of wage, attendance bonus and function allowance for lateness, so the deductions were lawful. [Para 142][Para 145][Para 148][Para 150][Para 151]

On issue 5 the panel relied on Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, which fixes meal allowance for voluntary overtime at 2,000 riel a day or a free meal, and found the employer had in fact paid for five days a week; no debt was proved. [Para 153][Para 157][Para 159][Para 160] On issue 6 it applied Article 239 of the Labour Law and Article 3 of Prakas No. 330 of 6 December 2000, whose staffing table requires one doctor and four nurses for an enterprise of more than 2,000 workers; with 8,300 workers and one doctor plus four nurses on duty the employer complied. [Para 161][Para 162][Para 166][Para 167][Para 168]

On issue 7 the majority found that the worker had herself signed a request of 14 September 2019 to move from section chief to translator, that the employer accepted it, and that she did not dispute her signature or produce contrary evidence; the reduced pay simply followed the new post. [Para 179][Para 181][Para 182][Para 183][Para 186]

On issue 8 the panel again applied Article 2 and found no evidence that the line and staffing levels were unreasonable or affected workers' rights. [Para 188][Para 194][Para 195][Para 196]

**Dissent:** arbitrator An Nan would have ordered the four allowances restored, holding that the cut took effect after the worker became union secretary and so breached Article 279 of the Labour Law and Article 62 of the Trade Union Law; she had denied agreeing to the reduction at the hearing, and the employer gave no specific reason for a cut of about USD 200 a month. [Para 208][Para 209][Para 210][Para 215][Para 216]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may deduct wage, function allowance and attendance bonus in proportion to lateness where its registered internal regulations and a compensation policy expressly provide for it, and may set production-line numbers and staffing, so long as the measure is lawful, reasonable and does not remove a statutory benefit. [Para 129][Para 148][Para 195]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the two gate machines open at 6:55 a.m. specifically to serve latecomers is a factual observation about the current arrangement, not a ruling that the arrangement must continue. [Para 131][Para 133]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The headcount is printed as 8,870 in the findings but 8,300, ten thousand and eight thousand at other points, and one passage gives "1 หมื่น" for the earlier figure. [Para 71][Para 89][Para 109][Para 166]
- The employer's name is printed with an extra word in two places, as "Ken Sen Sport Shoe", and the registration number appears twice as 09862 in unrelated contexts. [Para 142][Para 145]
- The year 2020 is truncated to "20" wherever it appears, including in the pay tables and the disciplinary approvals. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]
- Pieces of English OCR noise sit inside the Khmer text of the findings, including tables of numbers that cannot be read. [Para 77][Para 153][Para 162]
- Workers, union officials and representatives are named in full at many points; they are identified here by role and issue only. [Para 72][Para 116]
