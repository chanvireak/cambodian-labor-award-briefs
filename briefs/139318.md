# Award No. 41/04 — MiCasa Hotel Apartments (Arbitration Council, 6 August 2004)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phallack presiding; Mar Samborana appointed by the employer, chosen by ballot; An Nan appointed by the workers) · binding award by agreement of the parties · Doc ID 139318

## 1. Facts
MiCasa Hotel Apartments employs 200 workers in Phnom Penh. [Para 5] On 31 March 2004 the Department of Labor Inspection received a union letter of 29 March 2004 complaining that the hotel had dismissed and suspended 7 employees; conciliation on 28 May 2004 settled one of three issues. [Para 6][Para 7] Two demands remained: reinstatement of Mr S, security manager of the night shift, dismissed on 24 March 2004, and of Mr K, Mr E and Mr V, dismissed on 25 March 2004. [Para 8][Para 11][Para 12][Para 34][Para 39] Mr S was elected union president on 26 March 2004 and the three others were candidates on the list delivered to the hotel on 25 March 2004. [Para 12][Para 54]

## 2. Issues in dispute
Preliminary — Whether the dispute is collective, so that the panel has jurisdiction, the hotel contending that it is an individual dispute? [Para 54][Para 55]
1. Whether the hotel must reinstate Mr S, dismissed on 24 March 2004? [Para 8][Para 11]
2. Whether the hotel must reinstate Mr K, Mr E and Mr V, dismissed on 25 March 2004? [Para 8][Para 34][Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the dismissals punished union activity — Mr S was told at a meeting on 22 March 2004 to resign from the union, and the three others had just put their names forward for the union leadership; none had been given the warnings the internal rules require. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 38][Para 81]
**Employer (respondent):** the terminations rested on misconduct recorded in the internal work rules — sleeping on night shift, drunkenness and playing Tong Tin for Mr S, repeated faults for the others — and the hotel did not know of his union office until 25 March 2004, the day after he was dismissed; it was willing to reinstate Mr E on a public apology and a new contract. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 20][Para 30][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the hotel must reinstate Mr S, Mr E, Mr K and Mr V immediately once the award comes into force; the three dismissed on 25 March 2004 must repay the severance pay they received that day; the hotel must pay full wages to Mr S from 24 March 2004 and to the other three from 25 March 2004. [Para 87]
On jurisdiction the panel followed the Minister of Labour and the Labour Inspectorate, as it does unless there is clear reason to object (cases Jacqsintex 10/03 and Cambodiana 02/04), and found the three conditions of Article 302 satisfied: a dispute between a number of employees and an employer, concerning working conditions and the exercise of recognised rights of professional organisations, capable of disrupting the hotel. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 61]
On Mr S the panel applied Article 74: an undetermined-duration contract may be terminated with notice only for a proper reason relating to aptitude, behaviour or the needs of the enterprise, failing which damages are owed under Article 91, and Articles 12 and 279 forbid dismissal for union affiliation or activity. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66] The hotel said nothing against his behaviour or aptitude and relied on a misconduct report describing events that occurred "a very long time ago"; under Article 26 the employer must act within 7 days of knowing of serious misconduct, or 15 days for other penalties, and it had lost that right. [Para 66][Para 69][Para 70] The general manager did not attend to answer the account of the 22 March meeting despite invitation, and the panel concluded that the employer had dismissed him more for anti-union reasons than for the misconduct it alleged. [Para 75][Para 76]
On the three the panel found no evidence of misconduct during 2004, no final written warning as section 4, clause 3 of the internal rules requires before dismissal without notice, and no proof of serious misconduct; their names were on the union candidate list, and the hotel's stated unwillingness to restore two of them to team leader posts betrayed the discriminatory ground. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 81][Para 83] Article 279 forbids taking union affiliation or participation into account in disciplinary measures and dismissal. [Para 85]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that knows of a worker's misconduct must dismiss within 7 days for serious misconduct, or 15 days for other penalties, under Article 26 of the Labour Law, so a dismissal built on stale misconduct is unlawful; a dismissal of union leaders or candidates, made without evidence of misconduct in the relevant period and without the warnings the internal rules require, breaches Articles 12 and 279, and reinstatement with wages from the date of dismissal follows, subject to repayment of severance already received. [Para 70][Para 81][Para 85][Para 86]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel will generally defer to the Labour Inspectorate and the Minister of Labour on whether a dispute is individual or collective unless there is clear reason to object. [Para 57][Para 58]

[Para 44][Para 12] Paragraph 50 runs two sentences together ("Mr Va requests Mr Ea Kimhun, an electrician, was dismissed...").
