# Award No. 247/12 — Decoro (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambor Na; Oun Non; Pen Bunhea presiding) · Doc ID 140231

## 1. Facts
Decoro (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment factory at Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu [Para 1]. The Khmer Youth Voice union federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ) and its factory local brought 12 demands, headed by reinstatement of a factory union leader dismissed on 17 November 2012 with payment of wages from dismissal to return, and including a canteen and motorcycle shed, travel and accommodation allowance, one-year contracts, immediate 5% contract compensation, a nurse on duty, sick-leave pay, drinking water and soap, an equal quality bonus, health checks at the employer's expense, an end to anti-union discrimination, and monthly 1,000 riel dues deductions [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]. Conciliation failed, the company not attending the session of 12 December 2012, and all 12 points were referred [Para 21][Para 22]. At the hearing of 4 January 2013 the employer attended with counsel, but neither the dismissed leader nor any other factory union leader nor anyone with power to decide for the union appeared; a federation officer attended only to ask for an adjournment and stated he had no authority over the case [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]. No findings of fact on the demands were therefore possible [Para 55].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (First procedural issue) Whether the panel should postpone the hearing at the request made on the day of hearing by a federation officer whose stated reason was the urgent business of the factory union leader? [Para 58]
2. (Second procedural issue) Whether the panel should continue the arbitration in the absence of the claimant union? [Para 65]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The federation officer asked for an adjournment because the factory union leader had urgent business, and said the other factory leaders could not attend either; he confirmed he had no right to decide the disputed points and did not know the history of the complaint [Para 26][Para 27].
**Employer (respondent):** The company appeared through counsel and did not agree to any postponement of the hearing [Para 24][Para 63].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the claim as abandoned and terminated the arbitration, leaving all 12 demands undecided [Para 85][Para 87][Para 88].

On the first issue the panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules, which allow it either to proceed in a party's absence or to close the arbitration by award where an invited party fails to appear without a valid reason [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]. Because the employer objected, and because the reason advanced was neither explained nor substantiated, the panel declined to adjourn [Para 63][Para 64]. On the second issue it adopted the three conditions settled in Awards 16/07, 27/07, 138/08, 11/11 and 131/12: the party must have been properly invited, must not have appeared, and must have offered no valid reason [Para 68][Para 69]. The first was satisfied — the secretariat telephoned the claimant and sent invitation 1318 of 26 December 2012, which a federation officer signed for [Para 70][Para 72][Para 73]. On the second, the panel held that "appearing before the panel" means both being present at the hearing and taking part through all four stages — introduction, explanation of procedure, conciliation and the hearing to judgment — and neither the dismissed leader nor any other factory leader nor anyone authorised to decide attended [Para 74][Para 75][Para 77][Para 78]. On the third, the officer could not say what the urgent business was, and lack of means of travel was not a valid excuse [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82][Para 83]. All three conditions being met, the panel treated the complaint as abandoned and terminated the proceedings [Para 84][Para 85][Para 88].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an invited claimant neither attends nor sends anyone empowered to decide, and offers no substantiated reason, the panel will treat the complaint as abandoned and close the file rather than decide the demands; a request for adjournment made on the day by an officer without decision-making authority does not alter that. [Para 77][Para 82][Para 85]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that its proceedings have four stages and that mere presence at the opening does not amount to appearance for the purposes of Article 21 [Para 74][Para 75].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan is badly degraded: fragments such as "AKP", "Kin", "Sea", "Netter" and "modecasessan" cut into Khmer sentences, and several passages repeat themselves verbatim [Para 55][Para 64][Para 66][Para 77][Para 89]. The company name is rendered variously as Decoro, Dekoro and Dekouro across the file [Para 30][Para 51]. One evidence item dates a payroll table to "17 August 2023" where 2012 is meant [Para 40]. The conciliation report is cited with slightly different reference numbers and issuing offices in different passages [Para 16][Para 22][Para 51]. The claimant's factory union leader is named throughout the text, as is the federation officer who attended; both are described by role here and their names are not reproduced. The award decides no demand, so the file contains no evidence assessment on the merits [Para 55].
