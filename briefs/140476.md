# Award No. 02/08 — Cambodia Apparel Enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល អ៉ឹន ដឺស ទ្រី) (Arbitration Council, 15 January 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (chair) · Doc ID 140476

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល អ៉ឹន ដឺស ទ្រី (Cambodia Apparel Enterprise), a garment factory on National Road 4 in Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu, employs 675 workers. [Para 1][Para 13] The Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) raised eleven demands on 24 October 2007; five were settled in conciliation on 21 December and the parties agreed not to pursue demand 7, leaving five referred to the Council — points 2 (action against two administrative staff said to have taken USD 30 from each new worker), 5 (wages by the 5th of each month and payment finished by 4 pm), 8 (a copy of the contract to each worker), 9 (a child-minder for workers who bring children) and 11 (return of the USD 10 card fee). [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 7][Para 15][Para 17]
The union's three leaders had their contracts terminated with notice on 28 December expiring 31 December 2007. [Para 30][Para 36] The panel ordered the strike stopped on 3 January and the workers back at work on 4 January; they stayed out because the three leaders were not readmitted, and were still out on 14 January. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 34][Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration while the workers remain on strike during the proceedings? [Para 40][Para 41]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the parties showed a genuine intention to have the dispute settled by the panel, having disregarded its orders and its request of 10 January? [Para 48][Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត, through the Khmer Youth Union Federation, refused to return to work until the company readmitted the three union leaders whose contracts it had ended, and maintained the eleven original demands. [Para 28][Para 35]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល, through its administration staff, said it had opened the gates to all workers, that the workers simply would not come in, and that the three leaders' contracts had already been terminated. [Para 29][Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case 02/08 and closed the file, leaving the five referred demands undecided. [Para 49][Para 50]
The panel grounded the decision on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires parties to suspend every dispute measure, including strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, while the panel is functioning, and to attend all meetings it convenes. [Para 38][Para 39] It read that article, consistently with its own earlier awards, as barring it from resolving or issuing an award on a collective dispute while a strike continues, and as allowing proceedings to resume only once the strike ends. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42]
It then reviewed its jurisprudence on non-compliance: Award No. 04/04 (IMS) and No. 77/05 (Franco Nitting Garment), where files were closed because the strike went on, and Nos. 28/06 (GDM) and 34/06 (Goldfame), closed for the same reason, together with No. 22/07 (Park Yu), closed where the employer locked out during proceedings. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 47]
On the facts it found no genuine intention on either side to have the dispute settled by the panel: both held to their positions, neither obeyed its orders, and the strike continued after the 14 January deadline it had set. [Para 48][Para 32][Para 34] Having heard the parties at the pre-hearing meeting of 10 January, it recorded that no findings of fact could be made on the referred issues because the hearing could not proceed. [Para 37][Para 12]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not continue to arbitrate a collective labour dispute, and will close the file without deciding the referred demands, where a party maintains a strike or lockout throughout the proceedings, because Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires all dispute measures to be suspended while the panel functions and the panel cannot make an award in that state. [Para 38][Para 41][Para 49]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that it had offered a staged return to work, with all workers back by rotation, precisely to preserve the possibility of continuing to arbitrate. [Para 31]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company's name is printed in at least four variants — ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល, ខេមបូឌា អ៊ីប ភែរ៉េល, ខេមបូឌា អាភែរ៉េល and a garbled "ក្រុម ហ៊ុន" — and the case name is rendered "ខេមបូឌា អ៉ីប ភែរ៉េល" in the header. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 50] The notice of expiry annexed by the employer is dated 28 December for a 31 December expiry, but the termination of the three leaders is described elsewhere as taking effect at a date not stated. [Para 30][Para 36] Paragraph 37 is internally contradictory, recording that no findings of fact exist while later paragraphs make findings; the strike notice, the number of referred points and the conciliation report number are inconsistently transcribed. [Para 17][Para 37] Several individual workers and union officers are named in the source; this brief refers to them by role only.
