# Award No. 073/19 — Glowing Well You (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 23 August 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chia, chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 139116

## 1. Facts
Workers at the employer's factory at Trapeang Russey village, Tumnob Thom commune, Ponhea district, Kandal, raised 8 demands through a strike notified for 1 August 2019, seeking improved working conditions; conciliation at the provincial labour department on 2 August 2019 failed on all 8, and the file reached the Council on 5 August 2019. [Para 1][Para 17][Para 18][Para 19] On the employer's application the Council issued order No. 010/19 on 5 August 2019 suspending the strike, and the workers returned to their shifts from the afternoon of 6 August 2019. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] A hearing was set for 15 August 2019 with written invitations of 12 August 2019 reconfirmed by telephone; the employer attended with two representatives and the worker party did not appear at all. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr N, worker No. 104 in the union section, whom it dismissed on Wednesday 31 July 2019? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must make it easier for workers to take sick leave for unrelated illnesses? [Para 5]
3. Whether overtime must be worked on a voluntary basis only? [Para 6]
4. Whether the employer must preserve the wage and other benefits of workers who submit a sick note from a state-recognised doctor? [Para 7]
5. Whether the employer must refrain from discriminating against the union? [Para 8]
6. Whether the employer must refrain from discriminating against pregnant women? [Para 9]
7. Whether the employer must refrain from dismissing workers without fault or without clear reason? [Para 10]
8. Whether the employer must open the gates wide at the start and end of shifts? [Para 11]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Their demands were the eight points carried by the strike of 1 August 2019, headed by the reinstatement of Mr N, dismissed on 31 July 2019 while serving in the union section; the employer refused every point at conciliation, which is why the file came to the Council. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5] They then did not appear: on the morning of the hearing their representatives told the secretariat by telephone that they were busy, that "the matter is finished", that the workers had stopped demanding and that nobody would attend. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]
**Employer (respondent):** It attended through its manager and head of administration, with a delegation letter of 5 August 2019 conferring authority on the latter, and asked the Council to order the strike suspended. [Para 1][Para 28][Para 29] It filed no substantive pleading on the eight demands, and no document at all was received from the worker party. [Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 073/19 and issued no order on any of the eight demands, having treated the claim as abandoned. [Para 72][Para 75][Para 76]

The panel framed a single procedural question — whether it could continue the arbitration where the claimant party was absent — and answered it from Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004. Article 18 requires the panel to summon the parties to present their claims in person and to file the necessary documents; Article 20 requires the parties to attend every session to which they are summoned; Article 21 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules allow the panel, where a party duly invited fails to appear without valid reason, either to continue in that party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 41][Para 43][Para 46]

The panel applied the three conditions it had drawn from its earlier awards — due notification, non-appearance, and absence of a valid reason. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] On the first, invitations No. 413 and No. 414 of 12 August 2019 stated the date, time, venue and contact numbers, and the secretariat telephoned both sides the same day. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54] On the second, it read "appearance before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both attendance and participation through all four stages of the hearing — introduction, explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation, and hearing — and found the claimant absent from all of them. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 58][Para 59][Para 61] On the third, the representatives gave no valid reason: one said he was busy and the case was over, the others that the workers had dropped their demands. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]

Because the claimant neither appeared nor produced argument or evidence, and had on its own account abandoned the demands, the panel held the burden of proof had not been discharged and treated the claim as withdrawn, closing the case. [Para 68][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72] It recorded that no factual finding on the eight issues was possible because no hearing had been held. [Para 36]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claimant party that has been duly notified of the hearing, fails to appear without valid reason, and states that it no longer pursues its demands is treated as having withdrawn the claim, and the panel closes the arbitration without any decision on the merits; the panel's power to continue in the party's absence is discretionary, not obligatory. [Para 41][Para 43][Para 48][Para 72]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's four-stage description of an arbitration hearing, and its statement that a party may appear in person, with an accompanying person, through a member of the Bar, or through another person authorised in writing, were set out although the claimant appeared in none of those ways. [Para 55][Para 56]
