# Award No. 081/15 — Seav Kinh (MFI) (Arbitration Council, 30 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Hem Hour Narith for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139173

## 1. Facts
Seav Kinh (MFI), a garment factory in Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, faced a strike by its workers, who telephoned the Labour Disputes Department on 20 March 2015 complaining of working conditions and demanding improvements on 129 points. [Para 1][Para 19] Conciliation that day left six of the demands unsettled, and the file reached the Council on 24 March 2015. [Para 20][Para 21] The employer asked the Council on 23 March for an order stopping the strike; the panel issued Order No. 012/15 on 25 March 2015 requiring the strike to be suspended immediately and all strikers to return to their shifts that afternoon, and the strike continued regardless. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] The secretariat confirmed by telephone on 26 March and the employer confirmed by report on 27 March that the strike was still on. [Para 27][Para 28] No hearing was ever held, so the panel made no findings on any of the six demands. [Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must raise the regular attendance bonus by a further USD 5? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must raise the base wage of piece-rate sewers from USD 75 to USD 88 per month? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel per day? [Para 6]
4. Whether the employer must raise the base wage of the QC section to USD 140 per month? [Para 8]
5. Whether the employer must restore the 6 per cent payment it previously made and then abolished? [Para 10]
6. Whether the employer must maintain wages and other benefits during the strike? [Para 13]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the six demands were pursued through conciliation and were not settled; the workers struck to obtain them and continued the strike after the Council ordered it suspended. [Para 20][Para 26][Para 53]
**Employer (respondent):** it applies the Labour Law; it has no capacity to grant the attendance bonus, the wage increases or the lunch allowance; it asks time to consider the QC wage; it has abolished the 6 per cent policy; and it asked the Council to order the strike stopped because the workers would not comply. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 24][Para 29]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings without deciding any of the six demands. [Para 56][Para 57]

The panel framed the only question it reached as whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 38] It applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, which requires the parties to a dispute to suspend every dispute measure — strike, lockout or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — during the proceedings of the arbitral panel, and to attend every meeting to which the panel invites them. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] It noted that its own prior awards had read that provision as barring the panel from continuing whenever workers are striking or the employer has locked out, and it surveyed a line of decisions to that effect, including cases in which arbitration was terminated because the strike persisted: No. 04/04 (MSI), No. 18/04 (the hotel cases), No. 77/05 (Franco Nitting), No. 110/11 (Simano), No. 17/13 (Internal Fashion) and No. 066/14 (Callisto Apparel (Cambodia)). [Para 42][Para 44][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50] It also relied on the earlier order in this very case, which had warned that the panel would not adjudicate while the strike continued. [Para 45][Para 52]

On the facts the panel found that the strike had begun before the file reached it, had continued after Order No. 012/15 of 25 March 2015 directed its immediate suspension and the return of all strikers to their shifts, and was still running when the secretariat telephoned the worker party on 26 March and when the employer reported on 27 March. [Para 23][Para 25][Para 27][Para 28][Para 53][Para 54] It therefore held that it could not proceed to a hearing and terminated the proceedings under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered its suspension under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the merits, because the statutory precondition for the proceedings — that the parties suspend all dispute measures — is not satisfied; the dispute may be brought back once the strike has ended. [Para 41][Para 45][Para 56]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that it made no factual findings on any of the six demands because no hearing took place, so the merits of each demand remain undetermined and would fall to be heard afresh. [Para 37]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The scale of the dispute is printed two ways: the strike is said to concern 129 demands at [Para 19], while only 10 demands were conciliated and six left unsettled at [Para 20]; the two figures are never reconciled.
- The employer's strike report is dated "27 March 2016" in one place and 2015 in the surrounding narrative; 2015 is used here. [Para 30][Para 54]
- The jurisdiction paragraph is duplicated verbatim with a stray "វិល ៣៥៩៦" inserted mid-sentence. [Para 15]
- A quoted 2004 notice is dated "29 Khmer March 2004", the month being corrupted. [Para 44]
- No hearing date and no worker-party evidence are recorded; the list of documents from the worker party reads "none". [Para 34][Para 37]
- The company's Latin name is nowhere printed; "Seav Kinh (MFI)" is a transliteration of "ស៊ាវ គិញ (អឹម អេហ្វ យី)", which also appears with the parenthetical spelled "អីម អេហ្វ យី". [Para 1][Para 18]
- The signature block is corrupted ("mazzove", "20222"), though the three arbitrators named on the face of the award are those listed in the Forum line. [Para 58]
