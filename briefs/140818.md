# Award No. 059/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ែរ ផត មែនី ជ្ជមែន សឺវីស (CAMS) (Arbitration Council, 23 November 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (លី តៃ សេង appointed by the employer, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ appointed by the workers and dissenting, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140818

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌា អ៊ែរ ផត មែនី ជ្ជមែន សឺវីស (CAMS), Cambodia Airports Management Services, provides airport management services at Phnom Penh, Siem Reap and Preah Sihanouk and employed 1,315 workers. [Para 1] [Para 110] [Para 113] Its 2014-2016 collective agreement ran from 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2016, and on 22 August 2016 the unions wrote rejecting the old agreement and asking for a new one for 2017 to 2019. [Para 116] [Para 117] [Para 185] The claimants are សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត ឯករាជ្យ ព្រលានយន្តហោះ អន្តរជាតិ ភ្នំពេញ and សហជីព កម្មករ ឧស្សាហកម្ម ទេសចរណ៍ កម្ពុជា នៃ ព្រលានយន្តហោះ សៀមរាប, the two most-representative unions at the airport. [Para 1] The two unions filed three demands; conciliation failed on 14 October 2020 and the three unresolved points reached the Council on 19 October 2020. [Para 24] [Para 25] [Para 26] The panel heard the parties on 26 October 2020 and issued a non-binding award on 23 November 2020. [Para 28] [Para 220]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must reapply the 2014-2016 collective agreement for a provisional three years from 2020 to 2022, with all its benefits, working conditions and practices, from 1 January 2020? [Para 116] [Para 174]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must deduct an agency fee for the use of the collective agreement from workers who are not union members, equal at least to the monthly union dues members pay? [Para 132] [Para 191]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay 100 per cent wages, a 100 per cent 13th-month allowance and a US$90 monthly food allowance to the workers who did not sign the new working conditions? [Para 141] [Para 200]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត ឯករាជ្យ ព្រលានយន្តហោះ អន្តរជាតិ ភ្នំពេញ and សហជីព កម្មករ ឧស្សាហកម្ម ទេសចរណ៍ កម្ពុជា នៃ ព្រលានយន្តហោះ សៀមរាប:** that the employer negotiated the new agreement dishonestly, suspending it without the workers' consent and setting no completion date, contrary to Articles 51 and 63 of the Trade Union Law; that the union's 2016 letter rejected only the procedure, not the content, so the old agreement should be applied again; and that non-members who share the agreement's benefits should pay an agency fee under Article 26 of the Trade Union Law and Prakas No. 300. [Para 119] [Para 120] [Para 124] [Para 138] On the third demand they said the employer cut their wages to 50 per cent for refusing the new conditions without a lawful suspension under Article 71(11). [Para 151] [Para 234]
**Employer (respondent):** that the 2014-2016 agreement expired on 31 December 2016 because the unions denounced it in writing on 22 August 2016, and cannot be revived; that it has nevertheless raised wages each year and pays 13th-month allowance in two instalments; and that only a most-representative union may put an agency fee into a collective agreement, which the old agreement never contained. [Para 4] [Para 5] [Para 12] [Para 139] It added that revenue fell 95 per cent over eight months and it used company reserves. [Para 153]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the reinstatement of the old collective agreement, refused the agency-fee deduction, and declined to consider the wage and allowance claim. [Para 217] [Para 218] [Para 219]
On the first issue the panel applied Article 70 paragraph 2 of the Trade Union Law of 17 May 2016, under which an agreement of fixed duration continues for the same period unless a party gives three months' notice of denunciation, and found that the 22 August 2016 letter was such notice: read as a whole it rejected the content of the 2014-2016 agreement and asked for a replacement, so it was not a purely procedural objection. [Para 176] [Para 177] [Para 188] The agreement therefore lost effect on 1 January 2017 and could not be applied again, following Award No. 021/18 (Siem Reap International Airport). [Para 181] [Para 189] [Para 190]
On the agency fee the panel held that only a most-representative union may negotiate such a fee into a collective agreement, under Article 26 paragraph 2 of the Trade Union Law and Article 7 paragraph 1 of Prakas No. 300 of 2 July 2018, that the fee may not exceed the monthly dues of its own members and is paid once only. [Para 191] [Para 193] [Para 195] The old agreement contained no agency-fee clause and had expired, so no deduction was owed. [Para 197] [Para 198] [Para 199]
On the third issue the panel held that even a most-representative union may only bring a claim on behalf of workers who have authorised it in writing or by thumbprint, and that the lists filed on 28 October 2020 carried neither; the claim was therefore not decided. [Para 212] [Para 214] [Para 215] That reasoning also disposed of the related demand for holiday pay, which the workers did not argue at the hearing. [Para 166]
Arbitrator ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ dissented, recording that he would have ordered full 100 per cent wages for 59 unsigned workers from June 2020 and a full 13th-month allowance, relying on Award No. 028/20 (CAMS). [Para 224] [Para 225] [Para 252] [Para 253]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective agreement of fixed duration continues in force for a further identical term unless a party denounces it at least three months before expiry; where the union's notice rejects the agreement's content and asks for a replacement, the agreement ends at its term and cannot be revived by an award, even for a provisional period. [Para 177] [Para 188] Only a most-representative union can negotiate an agency fee into a collective agreement, the fee cannot exceed its members' monthly dues, and no deduction is owed where the agreement contains no such clause. [Para 193] [Para 195] A union, however representative, cannot bring a money claim for individual workers without a written request or thumbprint from each of them. [Para 214] [Para 215]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the employer had raised wages yearly and paid the 13th-month allowance in two instalments, and that government guidance on 13th-month pay did not alter the contractual position. [Para 5] [Para 12]
