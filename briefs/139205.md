# Award No. 129/15 — Veng Hour Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 22 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vuoch Hun, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Aing Eng Tong, presiding) · Doc ID 139205

## 1. Facts
Veng Hour Garment Co., Ltd. (វេង ហួរ ហ្គាមេន ខូ អិលធីឌី) is a garment manufacturer employing over 200 workers, four of whom — Mr H, Ms S, Ms C and Ms V — transferred their claims to the Free Workers' Union of Cambodia. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] The company paid severance at USD 55 per year of service on 28 April 2015, after a strike on 27 April 2015, then shut its gates on 29 April 2015 without notice and became unreachable. [Para 45][Para 50][Para 76] Three workers refused the payment; Ms V accepted USD 110 for two years' seniority and then claimed the statutory entitlements. [Para 51][Para 52]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may proceed to hear the case in the absence of the employer? [Para 57]
2. Whether the employer is obliged to pay the four workers the statutory severance package — notice pay, dismissal compensation, moral damages, unused annual leave and final wages? [Para 67]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The company closed without notice on 29 April 2015 and cannot be contacted; all four held indefinite-duration contracts and were terminated without prior notice, and the USD 55 per year paid was not computed in accordance with Articles 75, 89, 91, 166 and 116 of the Labour Law. [Para 45][Para 48][Para 53] They submitted a seniority-calculation table which the employer never contested. [Para 54][Para 55]
**Employer (respondent):** Appeared at neither hearing and filed no evidence; the panel records that the employer thereby abandoned its right of reply and did not discharge the burden of showing a valid reason for termination. [Para 14][Para 79][Para 82]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer must pay all four workers dismissal compensation, moral damages, notice pay in lieu, unused annual leave and final wages; Ms V must return the USD 110 she received. No sum certain is stated in riel or dollars for the individual entitlements. [Para 111][Para 112]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099, which allows proceedings to continue and an award to be issued where a properly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason; it found the secretariat had served the company in person, by Cambodian Post (returns marked "Not occupied"), by EMS and by telephone, and that summons had been published in newspapers was considered but rejected as harmful to the employer's reputation and private life. [Para 57][Para 15][Para 20][Para 65]

On issue 2 the panel held that paying severance and shutting the business on 29 April 2015 amounted to terminating the contracts, and that the employer had shown no valid reason connected with the worker's aptitude or conduct or the operation of the enterprise under Article 74. [Para 81][Para 83] Dismissal compensation under Article 89 was therefore due, and because the termination was without valid reason, moral damages equal to that compensation under Article 91 were also due, Article 91 being expressly distinct from notice pay and dismissal compensation. [Para 88][Para 93] Notice pay followed under Articles 75 and 77 since no notice period was observed, and unused annual leave and final wages under Articles 166 and 116. [Para 98][Para 104][Para 108]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that pays out seniority-based severance and closes its premises without notice has terminated the contracts, and where it then defaults at the hearing it fails to discharge the burden of proving a valid reason under Article 74, so Articles 89, 91, 75 and 166 entitlements all accrue together. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 93] Moral damages under Article 91 are cumulative with, not alternative to, dismissal compensation under Article 89. [Para 89][Para 90] A properly summoned party's unexplained absence does not stay the panel; Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 permits an award in absentia. [Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** The refusal to summon the employer by newspaper publication, on the ground that publicity would harm its private life, honour and investor relations, though service had already been validly effected by other means. [Para 20]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The hearing dates are printed two ways: 8 and 12 June 2015 in the procedural summary, but 8 and 12 May 2015 where the secretariat describes the invitations. [Para 7][Para 13] Line 19 dates the first hearing "08 June 2014". The panel's constitution date carries OCR noise ("29 May 2 2015"). Ms V's name appears as both ផ្លុង វត្តី and ផ្ទុង វត្តី, and the workforce is given only as "over 200" with no verified figure. [Para 12][Para 41][Para 44] The award orders the five heads of compensation without quantifying any amount, so the sums cannot be checked against the seniority table. [Para 111]
