# Award No. 323/14 — San Fong International (Arbitration Council, 28 November 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), Tun Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair) · Doc ID 139769

## 1. Facts
San Fong International (សាន ហ្វូង អន្តរជាតិ) employed the claimants, who were represented by the Federation of Unions for Labour Rights. On 17 November 2014 four workers telephoned the Department of Labour Disputes about a strike, begun on 14 November 2014, demanding improved working conditions; conciliation that day left 5 of 8 demands unresolved, and the strike continued after the file reached the Council on 20 November 2014. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 19][Para 21] On 20 November 2014 the panel issued Order No. 060/14 ordering the strike suspended and every striker back at his or her shift from the morning of 21 November 2014. [Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់) while work is suspended? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 5]
3. Whether the company must raise the child milk allowance to USD 15 a month? [Para 7]
4. Whether the company must remove the Chinese department head and the two mechanics named in the claim? [Para 9]
5. Whether the company must pay the compensation due under the Labour Law, given the shortage of work and the workers' long seniority? [Para 11]
6. Whether the arbitration could continue at all, the workers remaining on strike after Order No. 060/14? [Para 34][Para 51]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company should pay the regular bonus during suspension, 4,000 riel a day for lunch, USD 15 a month for child milk, dismiss three named Chinese supervisors, and pay statutory compensation because there is little work left and seniority is long. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11]
**Employer (respondent):** it already pays 50% of wages with the regular bonus shared pro rata (ចែកជាសមាត្រ); it cannot pay the lunch allowance or compensation; it pays USD 5, not USD 15, for milk; and it asked only to "advise" the three supervisors rather than remove them. It separately petitioned for an order suspending the strike. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration (បញ្ចប់ដំណើរការអាជ្ញាកណ្តាល) in case 323/14 without deciding any of the five demands; no money was ordered. [Para 52][Para 53]
The panel recorded expressly that it made no findings of fact on the disputed points and held no hearing on the merits, so nothing in the award determines the bonus, lunch, milk, dismissal or compensation claims. [Para 33] It framed the only question it answered as whether the arbitration should proceed. [Para 34]
Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires the parties, throughout the arbitration, to suspend every dispute measure — strike, lockout or anything else aggravating the dispute — and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 34][Para 35] The panel read that article as barring it from continuing while a strike or lockout is running. [Para 36] It followed its own earlier awards to the same effect, including Nos 59/06, 137/09 and 138/09, 110/11-Simana, 104/13-Sabrina (Cambodia), 119/13-Pou Yia and 019/14-Dongdu Tech Style (Cambodia). [Para 37][Para 38] It also relied on Notice No. 4 of 29 January 2004 in case 04/04, which stated that the Council cannot resolve or rule on a collective dispute while the parties have no right to strike and may take the dispute up again once the strike stops [Para 39][Para 40]; on the second order in case 18/04-Hotel, which reserved the right to terminate if a party breached the order [Para 41][Para 42]; on Order No. 2 of 24 December 2005 in case 77/05 [Para 43]; and on the termination order in case 110/11-Simana [Para 44].
On the facts, both parties acknowledged at the 25 November 2014 pre-hearing that the strike was still on, after Order No. 060/14 had required it to stop. [Para 46][Para 47] The workers asked for one extra day to discuss with the strikers whether to return. [Para 48] On 27 November 2014 the Secretariat was told by the company's lawyer that many workers had re-entered but a few strikers were shouting slogans and disturbing those working, and by the federation president that all had entered yet none was working, waiting for a settlement, with no reply to his request to negotiate. [Para 49][Para 50] The panel concluded that the workers had no intention of bringing the dispute to the Council, because the strike continued in defiance of Order No. 060/14 and of legal procedure, and terminated the case under Article 20. [Para 51][Para 52]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate a collective labour dispute while the workers are striking or the employer is locking out: where a party carries on a strike or lockout after the Council has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel terminates the arbitration without reaching the merits, and the dispute may be brought back once the strike has ended. [Para 36][Para 40][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the Council may resolve the dispute again when all the workers stop striking [Para 40], and its account of returning workers shouting slogans at colleagues still working [Para 49], which it recorded but did not use as a ground.

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The signature block is badly corrupted: only the chair's name, Pen Bun Chhea, survives legibly, the employer-side arbitrator's name appears as the nonsense string "Zuzzel" and the worker-side arbitrator's name is lost, although the face of the award names them as Seng Vouch Hun and Tun Si Phan. [Para 1][Para 54] The Secretariat minute recording the federation president's information is dated 27 November 2015 where every surrounding event and the award itself are dated 2014. [Para 33] A stray year "2017" is inserted into the case number in the reasoning, which reads "case 2017 No. 323/14". [Para 51] The employer's name is printed three ways — San Fong, San Foung and San Houng (សាន ហ្វូង / សាន ហ្វុង / សាន ហួង). [Para 19][Para 26][Para 28] Order No. 060/14 is printed once as "10 060/14" with the digits split. [Para 46] The procedural narrative at [Para 23] is garbled to the point of being unreadable. Finally, conciliation is said to have left 5 of 8 demands unresolved, yet only five demands are itemised. [Para 19][Para 3]
