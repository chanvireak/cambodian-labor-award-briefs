# Award No. 18/07 — Trinungal Komara Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កូមារា) v Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា) (Arbitration Council, 6 March 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 140590

## 1. Facts
Trinungal Komara Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កូមារា) employs about 1,200 workers near Phnom Penh and has two unions; the claimant is the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា), with about 700 members and most-representative status in the factory. [Para 19][Para 20]
Of the nine demands filed on 12 January 2007, six were settled in conciliation and three were referred: removal of the administration head from his post, retention of a daily bonus, and a nursery or a milk allowance; the third was withdrawn at the hearing, leaving two. [Para 12][Para 14][Para 18]
Since 2005 the company had paid every worker an extra 500 riel a day; from 1 January 2007 it stopped that payment and replaced it in the cutting section with team incentives of 1,000 to 2,000 riel per person. [Para 25][Para 27][Para 30][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must move the head of administration out of the administration section? [Para 21]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must keep paying the 500 riel daily bonus to the sections that received it before? [Para 25]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia / សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា):** they are dissatisfied with the administration head's character and conduct because he does not keep the promises he makes to workers, and the 500 riel daily bonus should continue for the sections that used to receive it rather than be replaced. [Para 21][Para 33][Para 35]
**Employer (respondent, Trinungal Komara Garment / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កូមារា):** it cannot change the administration head's role, and it stopped the bonus from 1 January 2007 because of an economic downturn that cut production from 28 lines to 18, offering team incentives instead, with new methods to be devised for the remaining sections. [Para 24][Para 27][Para 34]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand to move the administration head was rejected, and the company was ordered to keep paying the 500 riel daily bonus to the workers who received it before, until the parties agree on a new system. [Para 67][Para 68]
On issue 1 the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law and Article 22 of the Law on Contracts: assignment of work is the employer's exclusive right as a party to the employment contract, and only the parties may seek to modify it; it also found no evidence of the alleged conduct, since the union named no specific incident and conceded there was no abusive language. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 49][Para 23]
On issue 2 the panel first confirmed that the claimant union held most-representative status and so could bring the demand, then held that the employer may change bonus policy but must discuss it with the workers. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 57][Para 62]
Because the 500 riel payment had been applied generally since 2005 without any written agreement or notice of its conditions, and because a new method was agreed only in the cutting section, fairness required the old payment to continue elsewhere until a new system is agreed. [Para 61][Para 65][Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A bonus paid generally and over time without written terms becomes an established entitlement that the employer may replace only by agreement with the workers; until a new method is agreed, the old payment must continue for the sections not covered by it, and the panel will not order an employer to reassign its own management staff. [Para 49][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the employer's right to reorganise bonus policy is not unlimited but must be exercised after discussion with the workers' representatives. [Para 62]
