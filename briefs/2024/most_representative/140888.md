# Award No. 008/24 — I TAO PET SUPPLY (CAMBODIA) CO., LTD / ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាយ តាវ ផេត សឹប ផ្លាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក (Arbitration Council, 22 July 2024)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, Liv Sovanna, Nhan Somonnith) · Doc ID 140888

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាយ តាវ ផេត សឹប ផ្លាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក (I TAO PET SUPPLY (CAMBODIA) CO., LTD), a factory producing animal feed and supplying sterilisation services in Prey Nob district, Preah Sihanouk, employed 1,485 workers [Para 66]. The representative of the workers at the company filed a collective dispute on 24 April 2024 raising 13 demands [Para 24][Para 70]. Two demands (10 and 13) were withdrawn, 11 went to conciliation on 20 May 2024 where four were settled and seven were referred to the panel [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]. At the hearing the parties agreed four further points (issues 1, 5, 6 and 7), leaving issues 2, 3 and 4 for decision [Para 32][Para 33].
The disputed acts were the smell from sterilisation exhaust at the meat-twisting section, the employer's unilateral cut of 160 riel per trai from the over-quota bonus from 2 March 2024, and the non-renewal of union members' fixed-duration contracts [Para 8][Para 121][Para 146]. Union membership fell from 317 in February 2024 to 39 in May 2024 [Para 149][Para 367].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must arrange the sterilisation exhaust so that no foul smell affects workers' health, have experts inspect on Fridays, and allow a union or worker representative to attend the inspection? [Para 85][Para 270]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must maintain the over-quota bonus at 840 riel per trai of three-wheel meat and 960 riel per trai of hat dok as its past practice? [Para 117][Para 292]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must take the seven non-renewed workers back into employment? [Para 145][Para 328]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the delegates of the workers at ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាយ តាវ ផេត សឹប ផ្លាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក:** the smell at the meat-twisting section made breathing difficult and two workers fainted in December 2023, while experts tested only air quality and noise, never smell [Para 86][Para 87]. The 840 riel and 960 riel rates had been paid since the factory opened and could not be reduced; the cut destroyed every worker's incentive [Para 122][Para 125]. The non-renewed workers were union members who refused to resign, and none had received a warning [Para 146][Para 153].
**Employer (respondent), through counsel:** the company would negotiate expert inspections on Fridays but could not let worker representatives attend, and the union was not the most representative [Para 92][Para 93]. The bonus was discretionary, unlike the minimum wage, and its reduction applied company-wide because input prices rose and sales fell [Para 127][Para 130][Para 131]. The workers held fixed-duration contracts that expired and were not renewed because supervisors rated their performance poor [Para 155][Para 159].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to have experts inspect the smell at the workplace on Fridays with the union or worker representative informed or present, within at most three months [Para 392]; to maintain the over-quota bonus at 840 riel per trai of three-wheel meat and 960 riel per trai of hat dok as in past practice [Para 393]; and to take five workers back into work [Para 394].
On representation, Prakas No. 362 lets the Labour Disputes Department recognise worker representatives, and Article 11 of the 2016 Law on Trade Unions lets a registered union's leadership represent its members [Para 233][Para 246]. Reading Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 with Article 54(2) of the Civil Procedure Code, the panel held a representative may appoint a sub-delegate unless the principals forbid it, so counsel could act for the 65 fingerprinted claimants [Para 252][Para 266][Para 269].
On issue 2, Article 229 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 125 require premises free of odour harmful to health, following Award No. 255/13 [Para 270][Para 272][Para 274]. The environmental letter had assessed air and noise but never the weekly cleaning smell, so the statutory duty required prompt action [Para 281][Para 284][Para 286].
On issue 3, Article 13 and Article 65(2)–(3) of the Labour Law mean a practice more favourable than the statutory minimum stays valid [Para 294][Para 303][Para 306]. The 840 riel and 960 riel rates were fixed, stable and accepted by both sides from opening until February 2024, and the employer showed neither a ground for unilateral change nor the workers' consent [Para 312][Para 321][Para 324].
On issue 4, Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law and Article 62 of the 2016 Law on Trade Unions bar using union membership to end or not renew a contract, as held in Award No. 182/16 [Para 348][Para 352][Para 358]. The work-analysis table stated no rating criteria, and the panel found the non-renewal of the five union members was linked to membership [Para 386][Para 388]. Two of the seven claimants had not fingerprinted the 10 April 2024 authority, so the panel decided for the five who had [Para 341][Para 343].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Non-renewal of a fixed-duration contract at expiry counts as a termination under the union anti-discrimination rules, so an employer who declines to renew union members while renewing others must prove a genuine, evidenced performance ground or reinstate them [Para 358][Para 359][Para 389].
A bonus rate paid consistently and accepted by both parties over years is a past practice that prevails over the statutory minimum and cannot be cut unilaterally without a demonstrated ground or the workers' consent [Para 313][Para 327].

**Obiter dicta:** a claimant's account at the hearing is a party explanation rather than testimony, because a claimant cannot witness for itself [Para 344].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Worker and union names survive only as fragmentary Khmer strings with digits in place of letters, so individuals appear here by role or initial [Para 341][Para 394]. The conciliation report is numbered 361 ក.ប/អក/វក in the procedural history and 316 ក.ប/អក/វក in the evidence list [Para 27][Para 188]. The number of claimants is given as 63 in one passage and 65 in another [Para 229][Para 254]. The date of the meeting at which a worker was told her contract would not be renewed appears as 25 April and as 25 March 2024 [Para 175][Para 183]. The union registration certificate is dated 21 September 2022 in one place and shows a corrupted year in another [Para 54][Para 68].
