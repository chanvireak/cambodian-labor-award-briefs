# Award No. 012/24 — YAK JIN (CAMBODIA) IN / ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៉ីន (Arbitration Council, 8 January 2025)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Kong Phalla) · Doc ID 140889

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៉ីន (YAK JIN (CAMBODIA) IN), a garment factory in Khan Kombol, Phnom Penh, employed 2,827 workers, 2,183 of them women [Para 120]. The workers' representatives filed a collective dispute on 11 October 2024 raising eight demands [Para 24]. Three demands were settled at conciliation on 12 November 2024 and five were referred to the panel [Para 25][Para 26]. At the hearing the parties agreed two further points (issues 3 and 5), leaving issues 1, 2 and 4 for decision [Para 30][Para 31].
Three wage questions remained: the shortfall in holiday pay for 18 June 2024, the 50% wage paid during nine Saturdays of suspended work from 30 December 2023 to 24 February 2024, and pay for 12 and 13 April 2022, days swapped for 14 and 19 May 2022 [Para 4][Para 7][Para 13].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay a further 100% on top of the 200% already paid for work on the public holiday of 18 June 2024, as its earlier practice, to 55 workers? [Para 127][Para 226]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay back the missing 50% of wages for the nine days on which it reduced working days between 30 December 2023 and 24 February 2024, to 220 workers? [Para 168][Para 275]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay double the ordinary day's wage to the 326 workers who did not agree to swap the 12 and 13 April 2022 holidays for 14 and 19 May 2022? [Para 201][Para 307]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by the two independent unions at ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៉ីន and their federation, through an ACILS legal officer:** the 200% paid on top of the base wage on holidays had been the practice since 2007, so the reduction to 100% plus 100% breached it, and the 55 claimants were outside the group that accepted the change [Para 131][Para 142][Para 144]. The company cut working days without the consent of each worker and without the labour inspector's authorisation [Para 173][Para 174]. The 326 workers who never agreed to the swap were still owed double wages [Para 204][Para 205].
**Employer (respondent), through its administration and compliance head and counsel:** the change was announced at a meeting with the union leadership and staff delegates on 17 February 2023 and confirmed on 17 August 2024, applying the statutory 100% plus 100%, and workers had accepted it [Para 129][Para 137][Para 138]. The work-day reductions were agreed with worker and union representatives on 6 December 2023, notified to the Ministry, and compensated at 50% of base wage plus travel, attendance and seniority allowances [Para 9][Para 175][Para 176]. Double wages for the swap had already been paid to 233 workers who chose the shorter break or did not sign [Para 207][Para 208].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for a further 100% of holiday pay for 18 June 2024 was dismissed [Para 339]; the employer was ordered to pay a further 50% so as to complete 100% of wages for the nine reduced days to 220 workers [Para 340]; the double-wage claim was dismissed for the 17 workers already paid but granted for 147 workers [Para 341][Para 342].
On issue 1, the panel held that the 200% supplement on top of the base wage paid from 2007 until 17 February 2023 was a past practice, but that a past practice may lawfully end where the parties agree to a new one or the circumstances change [Para 256][Para 258]. It found the 17 February 2023 meeting, announced to the union leadership and staff delegates and applied without objection since, had replaced the old practice with the statutory 100% plus 100%, and rejected the workers' argument that the change did not bind the 55 claimants [Para 245][Para 269][Para 270][Para 271].
On issue 2, the panel applied Articles 71 and 72 of the Labour Law: suspension of a contract for serious economic difficulty must be authorised and supervised by the labour inspector and may not exceed two months [Para 282][Para 284]. Because no authorisation letter covered those Saturdays, the suspension was unlawful and the employer had to pay the missing 50% for the nine days [Para 299][Para 300][Para 303].
On issue 4, Article 118 of the Labour Law puts the burden of proving wage payment on the employer [Para 319][Para 337]. Applying Award No. 361/14, the panel dismissed the claim for the 17 workers shown as paid and ordered double wages for the 147 for whom no payment was evidenced [Para 321][Para 335][Para 336][Para 342].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A past practice more favourable than the statutory minimum ends lawfully when the employer replaces it through an announced agreement with the union leadership and staff delegates and the new practice is then applied consistently without objection, and the workers' later claim to the old rate fails [Para 258][Para 269][Para 271].
A reduction of working days made without the labour inspector's prior authorisation is an unlawful suspension, and the employer must pay the withheld portion of wages for those days [Para 299][Para 300][Para 303].
Where wages are disputed, an employer that cannot produce payroll evidence of payment must pay the claimed amount [Para 337][Para 338].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel treated a list of 289 thumbprints containing 46 duplicate names as proof of only 243 claimants, showing that an authority document is counted by distinct worker [Para 230][Para 231].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears only in Khmer, ending in a fragment that reads as "អ៉ីន" and corresponds to no company suffix, while the case number prints it in Latin as "YAK JIN (CAMBODIA) IN" [Para 1][Para 120]. The conciliation report No. 846 is dated 29 November 2024 in the procedural history but 25 November 2024 in the evidence list [Para 21][Para 115]. Claimant numbers shift between passages: 55 for issue 1, 220 for issue 2, 326 claimed and 164 or 147 allowed for issue 4 [Para 234][Para 281][Para 317][Para 336]. The summary paragraph before the operative part renders the double-wage order as "2ម៉ឺន ដុល្លារ", a figure the panel nowhere else uses [Para 339]. Individual worker names and staff numbers survive only as fragments with digits in place of letters, so they are given here by role or initial [Para 327][Para 329].
