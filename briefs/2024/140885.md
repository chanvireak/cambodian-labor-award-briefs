# Award No. 009/24 — Kala Khem Invesment Co., Ltd. (ក្រុមហ៊ុន កាឡា ខេម អ៉ិន វេស មេន) (Arbitration Council, 8 August 2024)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ម៉ារ សំបូរណា, លីវ សុវណ្ណា, ប៉ែន ប៊ុនឈា) · Doc ID 140885

## 1. Facts
Kala Khem Invesment Co., Ltd. (ក្រុមហ៊ុន កាឡា ខេម អ៉ិន វេស មេន) ran a garment-cutting and sewing factory in Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu, employing 131 workers, 106 of them women [Para 50]. On 31 May 2024 it announced a phased closure and began terminating indefinite-duration contracts (UDC), giving each worker written notice scaled to length of service [Para 88][Para 59]. Conciliation at the Labour Disputes Department failed on 5 July 2024 [Para 11]. Of the original demands, severance, final wages and annual-leave balances were settled by acceptance of the employer's payment [Para 54]; the 88 workers who had thumb-printed the complaint pressed only notice indemnity and sick leave pay [Para 55][Para 96].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1, head ក) Whether the employer owed notice indemnity (ប្រាក់ទូទាត់ជួសការជូនដំណឹងមុន) to the 88 claimant workers on the ground that its termination notice did not state the reason for termination? [Para 100]
2. (Issue 1, head ខ) Whether sick leave pay (សោហ៊ុយជំងឺចិត្ត) is 7.5 days as the employer paid, or the whole accrued seniority indemnity up to six months' wages as the workers claimed? [Para 127]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the notice letters named no reason for termination, which makes the notice improper under Article 75 and Article 77 of the Labour Law, so notice indemnity is owed [Para 63][Para 64]. Sick leave pay must be 15 days per year of service, capped at six months' wages, not the 7.5 days the employer paid [Para 71][Para 127]; the workers in that class had not withdrawn even where severance was received [Para 97].

**Employer (respondent):** it gave advance notice in accordance with each worker's seniority — one month for 2–5 years, two months for 5–10 years and three months beyond 10 years — as Article 75 requires, and the closure was caused by insolvency [Para 66][Para 68][Para 69]. No provision obliges an employer to state a reason in the notice in order to escape notice indemnity [Para 66]; on sick leave pay it applied a 7-day rule and paid 7.5 days as an additional benefit [Para 77][Para 112].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for notice indemnity for the 88 workers was dismissed, and the employer was ordered to pay sick leave pay to those 88 workers equal to their total seniority indemnity, capped at six months of wages and allowances [Para 132][Para 133]

On notice indemnity the panel read Article 75 of the Labour Law, which sets minimum notice periods of 7, 15 and 30 days and then 2 and 3 months by length of service, together with Article 77, which makes the employer liable to pay wages and benefits for the notice period it failed to observe [Para 101][Para 103][Para 106]. Following its own reasoning in Award No. 086/09-Wilson Garment, the panel held that where the employer in fact observed the Article 75 periods, no indemnity arises, and that no provision of the Labour Law requires the employer to state the ground of termination in order to be released from that payment [Para 107][Para 108]. It checked the notice letters against service bands of 1, 2 and 3 months and found compliance for all 88 [Para 110][Para 111].

On sick leave pay the panel applied Article 91-new of the Labour Law, under which termination at the will of one party without valid reason entitles the other to claim compensation for the injury suffered [Para 115], together with section V of Instruction No. 057/19 dated 10 June 2019 [Para 121]. It held the measure is the worker's total seniority indemnity accrued from first employment to termination, and that the worker need not prove actual loss [Para 122][Para 124]. It distinguished this sum from notice indemnity and from seniority indemnity itself [Para 116]. The panel confined the award to the 88 workers whose names and thumbprints appear on the complaint, relying on Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council, which requires written authority for any representative [Para 93][Para 98].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that observes the Article 75 minimum notice periods owes no notice indemnity under Article 77, and its failure to state a reason for termination in the notice does not convert compliant notice into non-compliant notice, because the Labour Law imposes no duty to give reasons [Para 108][Para 106]. Sick leave pay under Article 91-new is measured by the total seniority indemnity accrued over the whole employment, capped at six months of wages and allowances, and is a separate head from both notice indemnity and seniority indemnity [Para 124][Para 128][Para 116].

**Obiter dicta:** acceptance of the employer's severance payment does not withdraw a worker from the complaint, and the panel therefore still counted workers who had already been paid [Para 97][Para 99]. The panel also noted that the meeting of 3 June 2024 with the labour inspectorate produced no minutes [Para 58].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan repeats itself: the statement of positions and part of the reasoning appear twice in near-identical wording, once before and once inside the reasons section [Para 55][Para 92][Para 100]. Sick leave quantum is printed three ways in the same document — 7.5 days as the employer's practice, 15 days per year under Article 91-new, and an unexplained 20-day reference in the calculation paragraph [Para 76][Para 77][Para 128]. Several representative names survive only as bare initials or stray Latin fragments, and one reasoning paragraph carries a leftover personal-name fragment that does not belong to this case; both are described here rather than reproduced [Para 1][Para 124]. The complaint annexed at the end carries mangled dates, showing 2017 against 2024 elsewhere in the file [Para 135]. The award number is printed with a Khmer-numeral date "០០៩/២៤" only, and the signing block is overlaid by form text [Para 1][Para 135].
