# Award No. 258/15 — Bloomsfield (Cambodia) Knitters Ltd (Arbitration Council, 30 September 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Sin Kim Sean for the worker party, Kong Phallack chair) · Doc ID 139440

## 1. Facts
Bloomsfield (Cambodia) Knitters Ltd, a knitting enterprise at Sorvong Village, Kompong Trorlach District, Kampong Chhnang, faced a collective dispute filed by the FTUWKC at the Kampong Chhnang Department of Labour on 17 September 2015 demanding four improvements to working conditions; conciliation on the same day produced no resolution and the four issues were referred to the Secretariat on 22 September 2015 by report no. 1478 dated 18 September 2015. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 9] Two hundred workers struck from 17 September 2015, and at the employer's written request the panel issued Interim Return to Work Order no. 35/15 dated 25 September 2015 requiring the strike to stop and all strikers to return to their shifts from 26 September 2015. [Para 12] Information from both sides up to 28 September 2015 showed the strike continuing unabated. [Para 12][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The four issues referred from the non-conciliation report:
1. Whether the employer must reinstate the dismissed workers? [Para 4]
2. Whether the employer must convert workers whose employment contracts run from six months to one year into permanent workers? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must refrain from dismissing the two administrative staff members named in the report? [Para 4]
4. Whether the employer must stop making workers redundant? [Para 4]

The panel raised for itself a prior question:
5. Whether the panel may proceed with the arbitration hearing while strike action continues in breach of its Interim Return to Work Order? [Para 16][Para 24]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the four demands were improvements to working conditions that conciliation had not settled; the worker party refused to select a panel member by telephone on 22 September 2015, objecting to the selection notice on the ground that employer and workers had not resolved their issues, and the local union's suggested arbitrator was then drawn by lot. [Para 7][Para 10][Para 11]
**Employer (respondent):** by letter of 22 September 2015 it asked the Council for an interim return to work order suspending the strike of 200 workers, and by letter of 26 September 2015 notified the Council that the strike was continuing; it answered each of the four demands in the negative in the non-conciliation report. [Para 4][Para 12][Para 13]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration process of dispute no. 258/15 and made no order on any of the four substantive demands. [Para 24]

Clause 20 of Prakas no. 099 on the Arbitration Council, dated 21 April 2004, requires parties to abstain from any strike or lockout, as defined in Article 318 of the Labour Law, or any other action likely to aggravate the situation during the arbitration process, and requires them to attend all meetings to which the panel calls them. [Para 16][Para 17] The panel read that clause, as it had in earlier awards, to mean the Council will not continue an arbitration while workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 17] It listed the awards in which it had taken that position — 59/06 Sangwoo, 39/08 Zhong Yov Co., Ltd., 137/09 and 138/09 New Point, 110/11 Shimano (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., 104/13 Sabrina, 119/13 Pou Yuen (Cambodia) and 019/14 Dongdu Textile (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. — and the reasoning of its notice of 29 January 2004 in 04/04 MSI, that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during the Council process and the Council therefore cannot resolve or award on the dispute, though it can resume once the strike ends. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20] It also relied on the second interim orders in 18/04 Hotel (Cambodiana and others), whose clauses 9 and 11 required the strike to discontinue from 23 April 2004 and reserved the right to terminate the process on breach, and in 77/05 Franco Knitting, whose clause 4 said the Council would not proceed if workers continued striking; and on 110/11 Shimano, 17/13 International Fashion Royal and 66/14 Callisto Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., where the process was terminated for continued strike action. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] On the facts, the Council had information from both parties on 28 September 2015 that the strike was ongoing in direct disobedience of Interim Return to Work Order no. 35/15, and it therefore held it could not proceed with the hearing. [Para 23][Para 24]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue strike action after the panel has issued an interim return to work order, the panel will not hear the collective dispute and will terminate the arbitration process under clause 20 of Prakas no. 099, whatever the merits of the referred issues; the Council may begin resolving the dispute again once the workers stop striking. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 24]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that it understood strike action to have been ongoing after the case had been referred to the Council, and that the facts on the four issues submitted by the Department were "not found due to the termination of the arbitration hearing process" — observations explaining why no findings were made, not part of the order. [Para 11][Para 16]

- The evidence lists are partly lost: the employer's list ends in "NIA" [Para 13], the heading "Provided by the workers" is followed by nothing but "C." [Para 14], and "Provided by the MoLVT" runs into a numbered item beginning "Report on collective labour dispute resolution" [Para 15].
- Two individual names, the administrative staff whose dismissal forms the third issue, are printed on the face of the award; they are reduced here to "the two administrative staff members" under the naming convention. Representatives appear only as "Miss." followed by a name at the end of one line and an office at the start of the next [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3][Para 4].
- The employer's address is given as "Kompong Trorlach District" while the provincial department is "Kampong Chhang"/"Kampong Chhnang" elsewhere [Para 1][Para 7].
