# Award No. 002/25 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូ យូនី សូល អិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 31 March 2025)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Yu Soun Ty, An Non, Kong Phalla) · Doc ID 140890

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូ យូនី សូល អិលធីឌី, a garment factory in Khsach Kandal district, Kandal, employed 1,769 workers, 1,611 of them women [Para 53]. On 4 February 2025 the workers filed a collective dispute listing nine demands [Para 19]. Demands 1, 3 and 7 had already been settled by the labour inspector, in an agreement of 22 October 2024 and minutes of 14 June 2024, which the company had not implemented [Para 21]. Conciliation on 3 March 2025 settled two of the remaining five points, leaving three for the panel [Para 23][Para 24].
The three live demands were that all gates of the single production building be opened wide at the midday break, that machine-section workers receive an annual incentive of 10 to 15 US dollars like team leaders and supervisors, and that workers with two or more skills receive a monthly skill allowance of 5 to 10 US dollars [Para 57][Para 97][Para 112].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must open both leaves of every gate of the production building when workers leave for the midday break, so that they are not congested and do not lose time? [Para 57][Para 58][Para 121]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must raise the annual incentive for machine-section workers from 10 to 15 US dollars, equal to team leaders and supervisors, without discrimination? [Para 97][Para 140]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay a monthly skill allowance of 5 to 10 US dollars to the 109 union members with two or more machine skills? [Para 112][Para 179]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), members of សហជីព អភិវឌ្ឍន៍ កម្ពុជា ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូ យូនី សូល អិលធីឌី, represented by two delegates:** one gate leaf per gate is not enough at the 45-minute lunch break, workers crowd and push, and one worker fell and broke an arm and a leg at the end of 2024 [Para 58][Para 62][Para 63]. Machine workers do the same work as team leaders yet receive a smaller annual incentive, only some are assessed each year, and the union's reading of the buyer's checklist finds no instruction to narrow the gates [Para 95][Para 107][Para 108]. Other factories pay a skill allowance, and 80 to 90 per cent of the workforce has two or more skills but is paid as single-skilled [Para 117][Para 118].
**Employer (respondent), through its legal and administrative staff:** closing one leaf keeps insects out of the cartons; a buyer inspection in 2024 found insects in the product and forced re-sorting, and the buyer instructed that one leaf stay closed, which ended the problem [Para 68][Para 69][Para 71][Para 73]. The annual incentive depends on the Korean section head's yearly grading and company finances allow no increase for machine workers, who differ from team leaders and supervisors [Para 9][Para 109]. There is no policy of a skill allowance and there is not enough work, contracts having been suspended from 12 February to 7 March 2025 [Para 13][Para 119].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims were refused: the order to open both gate leaves was refused [Para 217]; the increase of the annual incentive from 10 to 15 US dollars for the four verified machine-section claimants was refused [Para 218]; and the panel declined to consider the skill-allowance claim of the 109 union members [Para 219].
On issue 1, the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law, holding that the employer's right to organise and manage the enterprise must be exercised lawfully and reasonably [Para 122]. Article 9 of the internal rules, approved in 2014, requires workers to use the gates the company designates, and the panel read that as power to fix which gate leaves are used, including for product quality [Para 134][Para 135]. Keeping one 2.5-metre leaf open was lawful and reasonable and did not harm workers' rights, so no obligation to open both leaves arose [Para 136][Para 137].
On issue 2, only four of the eleven machine workers claimed could be verified as claimants against the 100 thumbprints filed, so the panel decided for those four [Para 145][Para 148]. Article 12 of the Labour Law bars discrimination on the listed grounds, and the panel found unequal incentive pay was not among them [Para 151][Para 153]. Article 8 of the Minimum Wage Law requires equal pay only for equal working conditions, equal professional skill and equal output, and the machine worker's role differs from that of a team leader or supervisor [Para 155][Para 156][Para 170][Para 171]. The incentive is nonetheless part of wages under Articles 102 and 103, so the employer's discretion over it must be lawful and reasonable, which the grading scheme was [Para 158][Para 164][Para 174][Para 176].
On issue 3, the panel found no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice creating a skill allowance [Para 198][Para 199]. Because the claim sought a new benefit it was an interest dispute, and under Article 54 (new) of the amended Trade Union Law and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interest dispute replaces the collective agreement for all workers; the panel therefore had to ask whether the claimant union was the most representative [Para 204][Para 210][Para 211]. It held it was not, so it declined to consider the claim [Para 214][Para 215][Para 219].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may narrow the gate leaves used at shift breaks where a buyer's inspection found contamination and the restriction is tied to product quality, because the managerial right under Article 2 of the Labour Law is exercised lawfully and reasonably and does not remove any benefit the workers held [Para 122][Para 136][Para 137].
A claim for a benefit that no law, agreement, internal rule or past practice provides is an interest dispute, and the panel will not decide it unless the claimant union is the most representative union in the enterprise, since its award would bind every worker and foreclose future bargaining [Para 199][Para 211][Para 214].
Unequal incentive pay between job roles is not discrimination where the roles differ in conditions, skill and output, even though the incentive counts as part of wages [Para 153][Para 164][Para 171].

**Obiter dicta:** although the claim failed, the panel observed that a worker had fallen in the crush and that the employer should arrange safety measures so workers can leave on time without crowding [Para 138].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR is badly degraded in the headers and page breaks, which render the Council's name as strings such as "TE ARBITRATION CO DUNCAN" and "HEAL SITRATION COUNCIL" [Para 1][Para 2]. The size of the machine section is given as 12 workers in one passage and 11 in another, and only 4 were accepted as claimants [Para 105][Para 140][Para 148]. The authority list carries 100 thumbprints plus 2 negotiators, yet the panel counted two further claimants from the 100 who were machine workers, so the arithmetic of the claimant group cannot be reconciled from the text [Para 56][Para 145][Para 147]. One evidentiary passage dates the insect-control measures to September 2028 [Para 132]. Individual workers, delegates and union officers appear only as initials or fragmentary Khmer, and the employer's representatives are given by initials only [Para 1][Para 146].
