# Award No. 005/25 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូ គឺ ថរ៉ា រី សត & ស្ប៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 30 October 2025)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង រួច ហ៊ុន appointed by the employer, អន ណន appointed by the workers, គង់ ផល្លៈ presiding) · Doc ID 140892

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជូ គឺ ថរ៉ា រី សត & ស្ប៉ា (ខេមបូឌា) operates the Sofitel Phnom Penh Phokeethra hotel and employs 350 workers, 110 of them women. [Para 61] The workers' representative, organised through the union សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត វិស័យទេសចរណ៍ នៃសណ្ឋាគារ សូហ្វីតែល ភ្នំពេញ ភូគីត្រា, filed a 3-point claim on 12 June 2025, thumbprinted by 173 workers. [Para 18][Para 64][Para 50] The Department settled 2 points and referred 1 to the Council on 3 September 2025. [Para 20][Para 21] At the hearing the parties agreed that the employer would publish the monthly service-charge figures on the union and hotel notice boards, leaving the deduction itself in dispute. [Para 26][Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer may withhold 7.5% of the monthly service charge collected from customers as a reserve against breakage, instead of paying the whole service charge to the workers? [Para 65][Para 114]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the workers' representative argued that Article 134 of the Labour Law makes the service charge the staff's money, that no Prakas permits the employer to carve out a breakage reserve, and that the employer had continued the 7.5% deduction since 31 December 2022 with no agreement at all. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 79][Para 102]
**Employer (respondent):** the company said the deduction follows the 2018 and 2021 collective agreements with the union, is a sector-wide custom under Article 136, is spread proportionally across all departments so no individual bears the loss, and is better for workers than cutting individual wages for breakage. [Para 6][Para 84][Para 86][Para 94]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to pay the workers the entire service charge collected from customers and to stop deducting 7.5% for a breakage reserve, with effect from the date of the award. [Para 146][Para 147]
On the framing issue the panel read Article 134 of the Labour Law as defining the service charge as a gratuity paid by customers to staff, which the employer must collect and hand over in full to the staff who served those customers. [Para 114][Para 116][Para 117] It followed its own reasoning in Award 11/03 — Kampucheana Hotel, which held that a 10% service charge must be paid 100% to the workers. [Para 118][Para 119]
On the claimed custom the panel accepted Article 136, which allows distribution to be set by professional custom, but found that no custom and no Prakas authorises retaining a proportional reserve for breakage. [Para 125][Para 126] Clause 18 of the hotel's internal rules makes the individual worker who causes loss liable, so a collective deduction offends that rule as well as Article 127, which limits wage deductions to the materials a worker has actually been entrusted with. [Para 127][Para 129][Para 130][Para 139] The panel relied on Prakas 099 Article 34 to order the practice stopped immediately. [Para 144][Para 145]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A service charge collected from customers is the workers' remuneration, so an employer may not retain any part of it for its own purposes; a breakage reserve deducted proportionally from every department is unlawful unless the worker who caused the loss is individually identified and the deduction stays within Article 127. [Para 134][Para 140]
Where a collective agreement that authorised such a deduction has expired, its continued unilateral application is void under Article 13 of the Labour Law, because an employer may improve on the law but not fall below it. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 112]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that most Phnom Penh hotels deduct 10% for breakage and only three deduct nothing, and that 7.5% was therefore a comparatively favourable rate — a point that did not save the practice. [Para 86]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer-appointed arbitrator is named one way on the face of the award and differently in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 149] The 7.5% figure is printed as a garbled numeral where the annual redistribution is described. [Para 71] The 2018 agreement is dated "16 July 1018" in the respondent's summary. [Para 6] The internal rules exhibit is cited with two different dates, and the second collective agreement is referred to both as "Article 2" and "point 3". [Para 42][Para 107][Para 109] Pages of the hearing and evidence sections are partly overlain by scanner lettering. [Para 62][Para 97]
