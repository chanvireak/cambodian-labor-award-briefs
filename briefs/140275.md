# Award No. 54/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ ហ្វ៊ូល លីហ្វៀល (កម្ពុជា) ហ្គាម៉ឺន ខូ អិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 30 June 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Suonty, appointed by the employer; Tuon Si Phan, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 140275

## 1. Facts
The company, a garment factory at Lot 790 on National Road 2, Chak Angre Krom, Meanchey, Phnom Penh, opened in 1999 and employs 508 workers; three unions exist and none holds a majority. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Khmer Youth Union Federation) and its factory union, with about 184 members. [Para 43] The union filed eight demands on 2 April 2010, conciliation failed on all eight on 19 May 2010, the file reached the Council on 31 May 2010 and the hearing was held on 8 June 2010. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 22] At the hearing the workers withdrew Issue 7, which asked the company to comply with Award No. 677 of 25 September 2009, leaving seven issues for decision. [Para 14][Para 28][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must organise a fresh election of worker delegates whose terms expired on 15 September 2009? [Para 44][Para 45]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must restore the USD 5 monthly incentive it stopped paying from February 2009 and pay it again? [Para 50][Para 55]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must settle final wages and other lawful benefits immediately on termination of a fixed-duration contract? [Para 60]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company discriminated against the union by refusing to meet it while meeting another union? [Para 67][Para 68]
5. (Issue 5) Whether temporary workers must receive the same wages and benefits as permanent workers? [Para 77][Para 78]
6. (Issue 6) Whether the company must renew three-month fixed-duration contracts as six-month contracts? [Para 87][Para 190]
7. (Issue 8) Whether the company must add one day of paid annual leave for every three years of service? [Para 92][Para 203]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ / សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** the delegates' terms expired long ago and no election has been held; the USD 5 incentive was a practice that cannot simply be withdrawn; the company meets only the Free Workers Union and ignores the Khmer Youth Union's letters; temporary staff are paid less than permanent staff; and since it once gave one-year contracts it should renew for at least six months. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 49][Para 58][Para 68][Para 80][Para 87]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូ អេស អេ:** no election was held because work was short and workers too few; the incentive was suspended from February 2009 because of the world economic crisis and falling buyer prices; it meets any union that writes; it never employed temporary workers beyond 21 days; and contract length depends on its situation. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 72][Para 86][Para 89][Para 91][Para 200]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to organise an election of worker delegates; to pay final wages and benefits within 48 hours of termination; to give temporary workers the same paid public-holiday and other benefits as permanent workers; and to grant 19 days of annual leave for years 4-6, 20 days for years 7-9 and 21 days for years 10-12; the incentive, discrimination, contract-length and arrears claims were dismissed. [Para 227][Para 229][Para 231][Para 233]

On Issue 1 Article 292 of the Labour Law makes organising the election the employer's task, and Prakas No. 286 of 5 November 2001 requires a new election within 15 days before the old term ends; earlier awards held that a fresh election may be held once the term has expired. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 109][Para 110][Para 112] The delegates' terms expired on 15 September 2009 and no election followed; with 508 workers, Article 285 requires delegates, so the employer's excuse of few workers was wrong and did not relieve it of the duty. [Para 45][Para 113][Para 115][Para 116][Para 117]

On Issue 2 the panel held that an employer may unilaterally modify a bonus not required by law if it acts reasonably and in good faith; the 2009 suspension answered a genuine economic crisis and past practice need not continue. [Para 128][Para 132][Para 133][Para 140][Para 142] On Issue 3 Article 116(5) requires wages and all allowances to be paid within 48 hours of the last working day, and the company admitted paying in three days, so it was ordered to comply with the 48-hour rule. [Para 145][Para 146][Para 150][Para 154]

On Issue 4 the panel held that the worker party bears the burden of proving anti-union discrimination with clear evidence; the union promised letters showing refused requests but produced none by 11 June 2010, while the company showed it had met the union at Khmer New Year 2010. [Para 169][Para 170][Para 172][Para 173] On Issue 5 Articles 9 and 10 give temporary and permanent workers the same rights and duties, so paying a five-day temporary worker nothing for a public holiday falling in that period was unlawful; the panel ordered equal benefits, but only for the future, arrears having been dropped. [Para 176][Para 177][Para 178][Para 188][Para 189]

On Issue 6 the panel held that a contract may be varied only by consent of both parties and that it cannot impose terms on an unwilling employer; contract length depends on the company's real situation, so the claim to convert three-month contracts into six-month contracts was refused. [Para 196][Para 199][Para 201][Para 202] On Issue 8 Article 166(1) and (4) and Article 4 of the company's internal rules give 18 days of paid annual leave plus one day per three years of service; the company stopped at 19 days after three years, which was unlawful, and the panel set the scale at 19, 20 and 21 days. [Para 211][Para 214][Para 221][Para 222][Para 225] The award is not binding and becomes final eight days after notification absent objection to the Minister. [Para 233]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer cannot escape the statutory duty to hold a worker-delegate election by pointing to a small workforce or to the lateness of the election: Article 292 makes the election the employer's task and Prakas No. 286 fixes the timing, and where the enterprise has enough workers to require delegates the duty is enforced. Discretionary bonuses not required by law may be reduced or stopped for a reasonable and bona fide economic reason, but statutory entitlements — payment within 48 hours under Article 116(5), equal treatment of temporary workers under Articles 9 and 10, and the annual-leave scale in Article 166 — are enforced whatever the employer's practice. [Para 105][Para 110][Para 132][Para 146][Para 178][Para 211]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the words "temporary" and "permanent" in Article 9 merely describe the duration and frequency of work, and that a change of category has no legal effect on statutory rights, are general observations beyond the four orders made. [Para 181][Para 185]
