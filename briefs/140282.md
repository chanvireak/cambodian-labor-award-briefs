# Award No. 62/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ចុង យ៉ូវ ខូអិលធីឌី (Chong You Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 19 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: សេង វូច ហ៊ុន (employer-nominated), ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ (worker-nominated), វុន សារ៉ាយ (chair) · Doc ID 140282

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ចុង យ៉ូវ ខូអិលធីឌី is a garment factory at Prey Runka, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, employing about 428 workers of whom roughly 381 are women, working in two buildings (A and B). [Para 48][Para 53] សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Khmer Youth Voice Union Federation) filed a 15-point demand on working conditions which the Kandal labour department received on 6 May 2010; all 15 points failed conciliation and the hearing was held on 1 July 2010. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 27] Two points fell away before argument — point 7 was agreed and point 13 withdrawn — leaving 13 contested demands. [Para 27] The union had applied for registration on 26 April 2010 without a certificate, and the panel first directed it to file the certificate, which it did on 14 July 2010; the panel then held the union competent to represent its own members only. [Para 44][Para 47][Para 137][Para 138]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must build a nursery and a breastfeeding room for its workers? [Para 139]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must stop requiring workers to carry a pass card when moving between buildings A and B? [Para 156]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must pay wages twice a month? [Para 176]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must provide transport home for workers whose overtime runs to 20:30? [Para 194]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the company must issue the wage schedule to workers one week before payday? [Para 230]
6. (Issue 8) Whether the company must take measures to reduce heat in building B? [Para 253]
7. (Issue 14) Whether maternity pay of 50 per cent including supplementary pay must be paid for the three months before a worker's maternity leave begins? [Para 314]
8. (Issue 15) Whether the company must refund two months of union dues deducted in 2010 to the 43 workers who had resigned from the old union? [Para 334]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** with 381 women and about ten births a month, Article 186 requires a nursery and breastfeeding room, and the 2008 cash-for-benefit agreement has expired and is less favourable than the law; pass cards are too few (four or five for fifty workers) and block access to water and the clinic; wages must be paid twice monthly and the schedule issued a week ahead so underpayments can be challenged; night transport is unsafe and costly; and the company continued deducting dues for an old union after 43 workers resigned. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 63][Para 66][Para 126][Para 150]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ចុង យ៉ូវ ខូអិលធីឌី:** the 2008 agreement with the then-dominant union gave $5 a month for twelve months after childbirth in place of building a nursery, has no fixed term and no replacement, and remains binding; the building owner forbids alterations and the pass cards are needed to stop workers drifting between buildings; wages cannot be paid twice monthly because buyers remit once a month; heat is manageable with one large and forty ceiling fans; and deducted dues cannot be repaid. [Para 50][Para 64][Para 67][Para 98][Para 99][Para 148][Para 335]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to keep applying the 2008 benefit-in-place-of-nursery agreement until replaced, to issue more inter-building pass cards, to pay wages twice a month at intervals of no more than 16 days, to provide night transport for workers finishing overtime at or after 19:00, to reduce heat when it is extreme, to pay 50 per cent of wages and supplementary pay for the three months before maternity leave, and to refund two months of union dues; the demand to abolish pass cards, to build a nursery, and to issue the wage schedule a week early were refused. [Para 343][Para 344][Para 346][Para 347][Para 348][Para 350][Para 352][Para 356][Para 357]

On Issue 1 the panel read Article 186 as allowing the employer to satisfy the duty in one of three ways — a room on site, a room nearby, or a cash payment agreed with the workers — and found the 2008 agreement still in force because nothing had replaced it, so the demand to build was refused but the agreement ordered continued. [Para 145][Para 153][Para 154] On Issue 2 the panel held that Article 2 gives the employer the right to organise and manage the enterprise so long as the exercise is lawful and reasonable, and that a pass-card system was within that right; because only four or five cards served fifty workers it ordered more cards rather than abolition. [Para 161][Para 171][Para 173]

On Issue 3 the panel applied Article 116 and Circular No. 1903 K.B. of 24 November 2008, distinguishing និយោជិត (monthly) from កម្មករ (twice monthly under Article 6), and held the production workers were កម្មករ, so twice-monthly payment at intervals not exceeding 16 days was ordered. [Para 178][Para 183][Para 192][Para 193] On Issue 4, Prakas No. 80 SKBY of 1 March 1999 article 6 and Article 144 (new) §1 required transport home for night work; the panel fixed night as starting at 19:00, when darkness falls in Cambodia, so transport is owed from that hour. [Para 196][Para 199][Para 204][Para 206]

On Issue 6 the panel applied Article 229 and Prakas No. 147 SKBY of 11 June 2002 (articles 1 and 3), found neither side had proved the actual temperature in building B, and nonetheless ordered means of reducing heat during extreme heat. [Para 254][Para 257][Para 264][Para 265] On Issue 14 the panel held Article 183(1) and (4) entitle a woman with one year's service to half wages including supplementary pay, that Article 115(3) requires payment before the leave starts, and that under Article 13 the parties' agreement to defer supplementary pay for 10 days after return was void as less favourable than the law. [Para 319][Para 323][Para 330][Para 331][Para 333]

On Issue 15 the panel applied Article 129 §2: a worker may authorise deduction of dues but may revoke at any time, so the 43 workers who resigned in March 2010 were entitled to the two months' dues the company still deducted for the old union. [Para 337][Para 340][Para 341] On Issue 5 the panel found Article 112 required clear notice of the wage rate but set no deadline, and that issuing payslips on payday satisfied it, so the demand was refused. [Para 234][Para 242][Para 248][Para 250]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may satisfy a statutory welfare duty by an agreed cash equivalent, but such an agreement binds only while it stands unreplaced — and where an agreement gives a worker less than a mandatory statutory entitlement, Article 13 voids it, so maternity pay and its timing follow the statute rather than the parties' bargain. [Para 154][Para 330] A management rule such as a pass-card system is valid only if lawful and reasonable; where the rule itself creates a practical obstacle to basic needs, the remedy is to make the rule workable, not to strike it down. [Para 162][Para 173]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed generally that where a union lacks most-representative status the Council will not entertain interests disputes at all, and that an award in such a dispute would stand in place of a collective agreement for one year — a statement of consequence rather than of decision. [Para 219][Para 221]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The chair's name is printed differently in the header and in the signature block, where the employer-nominated arbitrator's signature line is also blank. [Para 1][Para 359] The decision section repeats each issue number inside its own operative sentence, and issue 13 is skipped without explanation. [Para 347][Para 356] Amounts are printed inconsistently (1.50 riel against 1,500 riel in the reasons), and one quoted article is reproduced as "at least two times a year" where the next line says once a month. [Para 178][Para 203][Para 224] The evidence schedule prints more than forty individual workers' names in two variant spellings; they are given here as roles only. [Para 32][Para 33]
