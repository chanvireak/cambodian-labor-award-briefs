# Award No. 013/22 — Rui Sing Garment Co., Ltd. (រូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ែន) (Arbitration Council, 24 May 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Eing Sothy for the employer, Tuon Si Phan for the workers, Pen Bun Chhea presiding) · Doc ID 140864

## 1. Facts
Rui Sing Garment Co., Ltd. (រូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ែន), a Phnom Penh manufacturer of garments and protective masks, employed 1,332 workers. [Para 71] With orders collapsing during the COVID-19 pandemic, it notified the labour inspector on 15 February 2022 of terminations in two phases, 962 workers and then 370. [Para 87] Conciliation of the collective dispute failed on 24 March 2022 and the unresolved demands were referred to the panel. [Para 12][Para 13] A strike followed; the panel issued Order No. 001/22 suspending it and requiring the workers back to their shifts from 11 April 2022. [Para 19] The workers ultimately pressed three heads of compensation after withdrawing Issues 1 and 2. [Para 25][Para 78]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 3) Whether the employer owes the 600 named workers damages in lieu of notice, moral damages (សោហ៊ុយជំងឺចិត្ត) and seniority back-pay for 2013–2018? [Para 136][Para 153]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), through the CCAWDU union (សហជីពប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា):** the employer bypassed Article 95 and the suspension rules in Articles 71 and 72; notice was broadcast collectively and never delivered to each worker, and a collective notice is no notice at all. [Para 90][Para 92]
**Employer (respondent), Rui Sing Garment Co., Ltd. (រូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ែន):** loss of orders left no work to give; notice letters were signed and thumbprinted by each worker, and where a worker refused the letter it was posted publicly. [Para 105][Para 108]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — every head of the Issue 3 claim was rejected and nothing was ordered payable to the 600 workers. [Para 202]
On notice, the panel applied Articles 75 and 77 of the Labour Law and found the employer had issued written notice scaled to each worker's seniority, one, two or three months, and had posted the letters publicly where workers declined them; the burden sat on the party asserting the breach. [Para 155][Para 174][Para 163]
On moral damages, Article 91 (new) opens them only for termination without a valid reason. Following Award 026/20-Vallong (Cambodia) Footwear, the panel treated the COVID-19 order collapse as an external factor beyond the employer's control and the termination as justified. [Para 176][Para 180][Para 189]
On seniority back-pay, Instruction No. 057/19 and Notification No. 023/19 provide that where a fixed-duration contract ran past the legal maximum and the 5% end-of-contract indemnity was paid at each renewal, no seniority back-pay follows; the panel found both facts proved for 2013–2018. [Para 192][Para 196][Para 200]
Before reaching the merits the panel satisfied itself on representation: 692 workers had thumbprinted the claim, but only 600 were pressed at the hearing. [Para 148][Para 150]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that pays the 5% end-of-contract indemnity at each renewal of a fixed-duration contract used beyond the legal maximum cannot afterwards be ordered to pay seniority back-pay for the pre-2019 period, since that indemnity already discharged the entitlement the ministerial instruments recognise. [Para 199][Para 200]

**Obiter dicta:** the union held most-representative status for the enterprise but had no collective bargaining agreement with the employer, which the panel treated as limiting its exclusive right to carry a collective dispute for non-members. [Para 146][Para 149]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The header date is 24 May 2022, yet the panel's constitution is printed as "31 March 2023 2022" [Para 15], and the claimant list is dated "8 April 2019 2022" [Para 148]. The count of workers given two months' notice appears as 1,961 in one table and 961 in another [Para 131][Para 168]. The panel disposes of the claims of 600 workers but one line prints "60" [Para 151]. Worker names in the notice samples and in the union leadership list survive only as fragments and initials, so individuals are identified by role only [Para 72][Para 73]. The company name is printed in several forms (រូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ែន, រូបសំ, វូ ស៊ីង).
