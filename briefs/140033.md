# Award No. 62/11 — Ocean Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 July 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140033

## 1. Facts
Ocean Garment Co., Ltd, a Phnom Penh garment manufacturer employing 2,700 workers, faced seven demands from the Local Union of CLUF, which represented 429 workers but did not hold most representative status. [Para 24][Para 25] Issues 3 and 4 were resolved or withdrawn at the hearing, leaving issues 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7. [Para 14][Para 15] The claims concerned transport and accommodation allowance, maternity pay, attendance bonus, milk formula and deduction of the attendance bonus for personal-commitment leave. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide an accommodation and transportation allowance of US$10 per month? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay pregnant workers 50% of their wages, based on average wages over the previous 12 months including overtime, before maternity leave? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must increase the existing attendance bonus by US$2? [Para 5]
4. Whether the employer must pay US$15 per month for three years for milk formula in lieu of building a day-care centre? [Para 6]
5. Whether the employer must deduct from the attendance bonus in proportion to days of leave taken for personal commitments? [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** New workers received no transport benefit although pre-existing workers did; overtime should be included in maternity pay; the bonus should rise because the new notification set US$7; milk formula should be US$15 given rising prices and the US$10 paid to another union; and the bonus should be reduced only proportionally for personal leave. [Para 28][Para 35][Para 39][Para 44][Para 45][Para 48]
**Employer (respondent):** It could not afford the transport allowance; it paid 90 days at 50% of full wages including perquisites but excluded overtime; it was already paying the US$7 bonus and would not add US$2; and it paid US$10 milk formula following an agreement with CUMW. [Para 31][Para 33][Para 34][Para 40][Para 43]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the rights disputes in Part II were granted in part and the interests disputes in Part I were declined; the Part II award was binding. [Para 94][Para 96][Para 97]
Issue 1 was an interests dispute because no agreement or law required a transport allowance for workers recruited at the new location. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] The union lacked MRS, and under Article 277 and Arbitral Awards 07/06-Dai Young, 71/09-Hytex and 84/10-Ming Da Footwear only an MRS union has standing to bring an interests dispute. [Para 56] The panel declined to consider it. [Para 56]
Issue 2 was a rights dispute. Article 183 requires half of wages and perquisites during 90 days' maternity leave, and Article 103 includes overtime payments in wages. [Para 59][Para 61] Following Arbitral Awards 33/07-Goldfame, 68/04-City New and 18/06-GHG, the payment is the previous 12 months' total wages including overtime, divided by 12, then 50% multiplied by three. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64] The employer was ordered to include overtime in the calculation. [Para 94]
Issue 5 was an interests dispute because the employer already paid the US$7 required by Notification No. 041/11 and no agreement required an extra US$2. [Para 65][Para 67][Para 68] It was declined. [Para 69]
Issue 6 was a rights dispute under Article 186. [Para 70][Para 73] The law requires a day-care centre or payment of external childcare fees, but does not require milk formula; the demand to raise the allowance from US$10 to US$15 was rejected and the employer was ordered to set up a day-care centre or pay fees on receipts. [Para 79][Para 80]
Issue 7 was a rights dispute. Because the attendance bonus is part of wages, the employer may deduct proportionally for authorised personal-commitment leave, but not for annual, sick or special leave. [Para 89][Para 91][Para 92][Para 93]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An interests dispute can be brought only by a union with most representative status; a union representing 429 of 2,700 workers without MRS has no standing. For rights disputes, maternity pay under Article 183 must include overtime, and the attendance bonus may be reduced proportionally for authorised personal-commitment leave because the bonus is part of wages. [Para 56][Para 62][Para 89][Para 91]

**Obiter dicta:** Arbitrator Ing Sothy dissented on issue 7, arguing that the attendance bonus is a conditional bonus owned by the employer and that workers who take leave fail the condition, so no proportional deduction is owed. [Para 99][Para 105][Para 117]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award labels the rights-dispute decision "Part II" before the interests-dispute "Part I", reversing the usual order. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 97] The demand is for 50% of wages, but the order speaks of including overtime in "wages and perquisites" without restating the 50% multiplier. [Para 3][Para 94] OCR losses include "themployer", "annualeave", "fixeduration" and "remaing"; "Fax: NIA" appears for N/A. [Para 1][Para 2]
