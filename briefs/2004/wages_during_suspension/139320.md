# Award No. 46/04 — M & A Cambodia Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 5 July 2004)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators: Kao Thach (employer party), An Nan (worker party), Ang Eng Thong (chair) · Doc ID 139320

## 1. Facts
M & A Cambodia Garment Co., Ltd, Tuol Kok district, employed 180 workers, some on specified-duration contracts. When orders were short the company sent workers home for a few days and paid them 50% of basic salary, relying on a clause in the specified-duration contracts. On 26 May 2004 the workers demanded full basic salary when there is no work, repayment of lost wages for specified-duration workers, and better ventilation on the fourth floor; conciliation on 31 May 2004 settled nine of eleven issues. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay full basic salary when there is no work to do, and repay specified-duration workers whose wages fell short? [Para 11][Para 26]
2. Whether the company must install more equipment to reduce the heat on the fourth floor? [Para 11][Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They cannot survive on 50% of basic salary when there is no work; if the company suspended the contracts lawfully they would receive nothing, so they ask instead that full basic salary be preserved for specified-duration workers. The fourth floor is unbearably hot, the tissue stored there gives off a chemical smell, and exhaust fans are needed for air circulation. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 29][Para 30]

**Employer (respondent):** Payment of 50% of basic salary when there is no work is expressly written into the employment contracts, and the contract and the Labour Law should be followed. Water thrown on the roof had sufficiently reduced the heat, though the company promised to throw more water and install fans on the fourth floor. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** Granted — the employer must pay employees full basic salary except where the employment contract is suspended in conformity with Article 71 of the Labour Law, and must install more pipes and three or four more fans on the fourth floor of the factory. No monetary sum beyond the minimum wage figures discussed in the reasons is quantified. [Para 42]

On the first issue the panel started from Notification No. 017 of the Ministry of Social Affairs and Labour, which requires a minimum salary of USD45 per month, and from the employer's right to suspend the employment contract in economic difficulty, which is exercisable only where the suspension is under the control of the Labour Inspection Department and conforms to the procedure in Article 71(11). Where the employer has not suspended the contract in that way, the obligation to pay the minimum wage survives. The specified-duration clause by which the worker accepts 50% wages when work stops temporarily was read as less favourable to workers than the entitlements set out in the Labour Law; under Article 13 the actual employment contract therefore cannot be applied, except where the employer follows the requirements of Article 71. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]

The claim for repayment of past shortfalls was withdrawn by the workers during the hearing, so no order was made on that part. [Para 26][Para 35]

On the second issue the employer had already agreed to the demand, promising to install more pipes and three more fans; the panel recorded that agreement and ordered the pipes and three or four additional fans. [Para 32][Para 41][Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A contractual term that pays specified-duration workers only 50% of basic salary whenever work stops is less favourable than the Labour Law and cannot be applied under Article 13 unless the employer first suspends the employment contract in accordance with Article 71, under the control of the Labour Inspection Department; absent such a suspension the full basic salary, and at least the notified minimum wage, remains payable. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 41]

**Obiter dicta:** That the employer retains a right to suspend the contract in economic difficulty or difficult circumstances, provided the statutory procedure is followed — a right the panel accepted in principle while finding it unused here. [Para 38]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The two disputed issues are run together in a single numbered line of the non-conciliation report, so the numbering followed here is the panel's own division into a first and second issue [Para 11][Para 37][Para 41]; OCR loss throughout ("themployees", "specifieduration", "theat", "not enough"), and duplicated or garbled sentences in the findings [Para 32][Para 35]; the minimum wage notification is cited only by number and date, not by article; the award gives the number of fans as "three" in the findings and "three or four" in the decision [Para 36][Para 42]; the employer's address and telephone appear twice and inconsistently in the header, and one worker representative is printed as "M." without a title [Para 6][Para 7]; the panel's decision on the dropped repayment claim is recorded only in the findings, not in the formal decision [Para 35][Para 42].
