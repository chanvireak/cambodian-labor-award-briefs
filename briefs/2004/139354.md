# Award No. 97/04 — Grace Sun (Cambodia) Garment (Arbitration Council, 15 December 2004)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Thach, Huon Chundy, Koy Neam chair) · Doc ID 139354

## 1. Facts
Grace Sun (Cambodia) Garment, at building No. 1223, National Road No. 2, Sangkat Chakanreleu, Meanchey district, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1998 and reports 662 workers. [Para 24][Para 25] From 2001 it placed workers on one-year and then two-year fixed duration contracts without terminating the earlier contracts, drawing a complaint to the Labour Inspector on 8 November 2004. [Para 26][Para 30][Para 33] The disputed act is the substitution of those fixed duration contracts for relationships the workers say were already open-ended. [Para 38]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel recorded that the two non-conciliated points were combined into one because both concerned the same employment relationship. [Para 13]
1. Whether the fixed duration contracts imposed in 2001, 2003 and 2004 were lawful, where the employer never terminated the undetermined duration contracts existing since 1998? [Para 40][Para 42]
2. Whether the workers are entitled to severance pay on the footing that their undetermined duration contracts were terminated? [Para 14][Para 44]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the earlier contracts were undetermined duration; signing fixed duration contracts terminated them, so severance pay was due before any new fixed duration contract from 2005 onward. [Para 38]
**Employer (respondent):** it maintained the seniority of all employees, would cancel the fixed duration contracts and apply undetermined duration contracts instead, and could not pay severance because it had terminated nobody and the factory was still operating. [Para 15][Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the severance pay demand was rejected and the fixed duration contracts made in 2001 and 2003, together with the two-year contract signed on 27 December 2003, were voided, the workers retaining the right to continue under the criteria of the undetermined duration contract. [Para 44][Para 45]

Article 74 allows an undetermined duration contract to be terminated at will only with written prior notice and a valid reason relating to the worker's aptitude, behaviour or the requirements of the enterprise. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] The panel found the company never gave such notice in 2001, 2003 or 2004, never paid the benefits attaching to termination under Articles 166, 75, 89, 167 and 91, and kept employing the same workers without any intention to dismiss them. [Para 40][Para 34][Para 36] Because the open-ended contracts were never terminated, the fixed duration contracts layered over them were irregular, and the 2004 contract was further vitiated by a signature date that did not match its stated date — an argument the employer conceded. [Para 41] The change of contract type was therefore not legal and was voided. [Para 42][Para 43]

On severance the panel reasoned in the opposite direction: since the employer could not terminate, and did not terminate, the undetermined duration contracts, no termination occurred and no severance pay fell due. [Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed duration contract signed over a live undetermined duration contract, without the written prior notice and valid reason Article 74 requires, does not alter the character of the employment: the open-ended contract continues and the fixed-term document is void. [Para 40][Para 42][Para 44]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted the employer's explanation that it sought signatures to show buyers and satisfy their wishes, and that the employer did not wish to change working conditions; neither observation was relied on for the outcome. [Para 33][Para 34]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The one-year contract's end date is printed both as 1 December 2001 and as 31 December 2001 in different passages. [Para 23][Para 30] The 2004 contract is dated 27 December 2003 in the findings and described as signed with a wrong date of 27 September 2004 in the reasons, while the workers' signatures are said to be 23 September 2004. [Para 33][Para 41] The findings give 662 workers while the employer's evidence is a list of 701 workers. [Para 23][Para 25] OCR also splits representatives' names across numbered lines and prints the establishing Prakas as 338/02. [Para 18]
