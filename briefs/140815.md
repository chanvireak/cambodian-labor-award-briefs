# Award No. 055/20 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន បូវ ឃើរ ហ្គាមេន ហ្វេក ធើរី (ខេមបូឌា) ខម ភេនី លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 23 October 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer, អន ណន appointed by the workers, chair អាំង អេង ថុង) · Doc ID 140815

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន បូវ ឃើរ ហ្គាមេន ហ្វេក ធើរី (ខេមបូឌា) ខម ភេនី លីមីធីត, Bou Keo Garmen Factory (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, cuts and sews garments at Poung Teuk village, Bek Chan commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, and employed 3,800 workers. [Para 1] [Para 43] [Para 44] The union សហជីព កម្ពុជា ឯករាជ្យ (NITUFE) complained to the Kandal labour department on 28 August 2020 about working conditions; conciliation on 25 September 2020 settled four of its five demands, and the single remaining demand reached the Council secretariat on 30 September 2020. [Para 10] [Para 11] [Para 20] The panel was constituted on 2 October 2020, heard both parties on 8 October 2020 and issued a non-binding award on 23 October 2020. [Para 8] [Para 12] [Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1(a)) Whether the employer must let workers take the seven days of paid special leave a year without deducting those days from their annual leave? [Para 46] [Para 61]
2. (Issue 1(b)) Whether the employer must pay one to three days of special leave according to each worker's seniority, without deducting them from annual leave, as it had previously granted? [Para 46] [Para 84]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that Article 169 of the Labour Law gives every worker seven days of paid special leave a year, and that where a worker has a proper reason and a supporting certificate the employer may not deduct those days from annual leave. [Para 49] [Para 50] They added that the employer had earlier given one to three days by seniority without deduction and should continue. [Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** that Article 171 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 2 of Prakas No. 267 SKBY of 11 October 2001 entitle it to deduct special leave from annual leave. [Para 3] [Para 55] It maintained that it already gives seven days plus one to three days by seniority, which it called better than the law requires. [Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused both the demand that special leave be granted without deduction from annual leave and the demand for payment of one to three days by seniority, the second without reaching the merits. [Para 97] [Para 98]
On the first head the panel read Article 171 paragraphs 1 to 3 as permitting the employer to deduct special leave from annual leave while the worker has not yet used all his annual leave, and to claim repayment once it has been used. [Para 61] [Para 62] [Para 63] Article 1 of Prakas No. 267 SKBY allows up to seven days of paid special leave a year for personal or family events, and Article 2 of the same Prakas lets the employer deduct those days from annual leave. [Para 64] [Para 65] Article 169 was held to deal only with the calculation of service for the annual-leave entitlement, requiring periods of suspended contract and of paid leave to be counted, and not to forbid deduction of special leave. [Para 72] [Para 73] [Para 80]
The panel therefore found no provision obliging the employer to grant special leave free of deduction and held that demand to have no legal basis, following Award No. 329/15 (Orange Trading (Cam) Co., Ltd) and Award No. 027/16 (Hong Kong Van Fair Garment Cambodia Ltd) on the same reading of Article 169. [Para 75] [Para 78] [Para 81] [Para 82]
The second head failed for want of a party: no named claimant was identified, and Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, read with Award No. 181/14, was held to require written authorisation from an identified party before the panel can decide a demand. [Para 85] [Para 87] [Para 88] [Para 89] The workers were given until 12 October 2020 to file the list of names and amounts and did not file it, so the panel declined to consider the demand at all. [Para 93] [Para 96]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer grants seven days of paid special leave a year under Article 171 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 267 SKBY but deducts those days from the worker's annual leave, the panel will not order the leave to be granted free of deduction: Article 169 only requires special-leave time to be counted as service when the annual-leave entitlement is computed, and creates no independent right. [Para 80] [Para 81] A demand for money owed to a group of workers whose names are never filed cannot be decided on the merits, because Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY requires written authorisation from an identified party. [Para 88] [Para 93]

**Obiter dicta:** that an employer which adds one to three days of special leave by seniority gives more than the law requires, an observation recorded when stating the employer's position. [Para 58]
