# Award No. 92/12-SL — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អិល ហ្គាមេន ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) (SL Garmen Processing (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 23 May 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — សេង រួច ហ៊ុន (employer-nominated), អន ណន (worker-nominated), អាំង អេង ថុង (chair) · Doc ID 162373

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អិល ហ្គាមេន ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) — SL Garmen Processing (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. — was the employer; the claimants were សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អិល [Para 1].
On 12 May 2012 the labour dispute department received a telephone complaint from the company that its workers were striking, demanding the reinstatement of a dismissed union president with back pay from dismissal to reinstatement and a package of working conditions [Para 29].
Conciliation that same day left 11 of 28 demands unresolved — a $10 monthly travel allowance, a $15 monthly rent allowance, a 200 per cent night-shift rate for old and new workers alike, a 1,500 riel meal-hour top-up, retirement at 55, a $5 premium increase taking the premium to $12, a 4,000 riel lunch allowance, a covered motorbike shelter and equal conditions on the SL I and SL II lines [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 10][Para 12][Para 14][Para 16][Para 8][Para 18].
Those 11 points were referred to the panel on 17 May 2012 [Para 31].
The panel issued Order No. 015/12 on 17 May 2012 ordering an immediate stop to the strike and a return to shifts from the morning of 18 May 2012; the workers did not return, and no hearing was ever held [Para 32][Para 33][Para 43].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the arbitration should continue when the workers remained on strike throughout the proceedings, disobeyed the panel's order to suspend the strike and return to work, and did not attend the pre-hearing meeting? [Para 44][Para 52]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អិល pressed the eleven demands by strike and sought an equal 200 per cent night-shift rate for all workers regardless of seniority [Para 6]. They sent no representative to the pre-hearing meeting of 21 May 2012 and filed no documents [Para 35][Para 38].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេស អិល ហ្គាមេន ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) attended through an administrative assistant and asked the panel to order the strike suspended [Para 1][Para 37]. On the merits it said old workers already received 200 per cent and new workers 130 per cent under the amended law [Para 7], asked the panel to hold the travel and rent allowances over for later consideration [Para 3][Para 5], and said it could not yet build the covered motorbike shelter [Para 9].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided and ordered that arbitration not continue in case No. 92/12-SL Garmen, so none of the eleven demands was ever ruled on [Para 53][Para 54].
Its authority to sit at all rested on Chapter 12, Part 2(b), Articles 309 to 317 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 and Prakas No. 136 K.B./Pr.K of 7 June 2011 [Para 24].
The only question it put to itself was whether the proceedings should go on, and it answered through Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout for the duration of the arbitration and to attend all meetings the panel convenes [Para 44][Para 45].
That rule, as the panel had already construed it, bars it from continuing whenever the workers keep striking [Para 47].
It followed its own earlier files: case 04/04-MSI, where it declared it could neither settle the dispute nor issue an award while the strike ran [Para 48]; cases 28/06-GDM and 34/06-Gold Game, both closed for that reason [Para 49]; and cases 110/11-Simanor and 59/12-Winsing Chic [Para 50].
On the record, the workers stayed on strike, refused to return to work under Order No. 015/12 and were absent from the pre-hearing, which the employer alone attended [Para 52][Para 35].
Because no hearing took place, the panel made no factual findings on the referred demands [Para 43].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A party that keeps up a strike or other dispute measure while arbitration is under way acts contrary to Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, and the Council will decline to continue the arbitration rather than decide the merits, whatever the strength of the demands [Para 46][Para 51][Para 53].
The trigger is the party's own conduct: continuing the strike, ignoring the interim order to suspend it and return to work, and staying away from the pre-hearing [Para 52].
