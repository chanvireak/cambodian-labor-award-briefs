# Award No. 035/17 — Khem Vanna Fashion Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 31 October 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 138937

## 1. Facts
Khem Vanna Fashion Co., Ltd. (ខេម វើណា ហ្វេ សិន ខូអិលធីឌី) cuts and sews garments in Khsach Kandal district, Kandal, and employed about 612 workers as at September 2017. [Para 61][Para 62] On 16 September 2017 the employer terminated all 612, paying each a flat $50 per year of service said to replace notice compensation, dismissal damages and moral damages; 547 workers thumbprinted an agreement to that effect on 13 September 2017. [Para 69][Para 72][Para 74][Para 89] 368 workers delegated representation to the union leadership, which the Labour Department certified on 3 October 2017. [Para 64][Para 65] The company has no most-representative union. [Para 67][Para 118] Two workers were treated separately: the factory union president, dismissed in February 2017, and a second worker whose claim is already before the Kandal Provincial Court. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78] Of the two demands, both went to the panel. [Para 16][Para 139]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the workers on indefinite-duration contracts — 277 by the claimant's table — are entitled to compensation in lieu of notice under មាត្រា 75 and 77, dismissal damages under មាត្រា 89, and moral damages under មាត្រា 91? [Para 97][Para 98][Para 139][Para 152][Para 185]
2. Whether the workers on fixed-duration contracts — 65 by the claimant's table — are entitled to moral damages equal to the wages they would have earned to the end of the contract under មាត្រា 73(3), and to 5 per cent severance under មាត្រា 73(6)? [Para 100][Para 101][Para 231][Para 232][Para 237]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer gave no written notice of termination, gave only oral notice through section and team leaders on 13 September 2017, and paid nothing for dismissal damages or moral damages; the $50-per-year agreement offers less than មាត្រា 75, 77, 89 and 91 require, and the workers say they did not know what they were thumbprinting — they were told the paper was to take their claims to the Ministry; and terminating a fixed-duration contract early entitles them to wages to the end of the term. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82][Para 87][Para 88][Para 108][Para 109]
**Employer (respondent):** it faced a financial crisis and a lack of orders causing serious losses, which is why it ended all contracts; it admits it cannot pay the statutory amounts in full and can offer only $50 per year of service; it did give notice orally; and it declined to produce accounts because they are confidential company documents. [Para 83][Para 84][Para 99][Para 102][Para 110][Para 149]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay the indefinite-contract claimants notice compensation, dismissal damages and moral damages, and the fixed-duration claimants moral damages and 5 per cent severance; the claims of the two individually-named workers were refused consideration. [Para 255][Para 256][Para 257][Para 258][Para 259]
The panel first applied មាត្រា 74: an indefinite contract may be terminated by either party, but not without a valid reason going to the worker's aptitude or conduct or to the necessities of the enterprise. [Para 140][Para 142] The employer asserted a financial crisis, but the panel had ordered it to produce supporting evidence and it refused, calling the documents confidential; by the evidence deadline nothing had been produced, so the panel found the asserted crisis unproven and the terminations contrary to មាត្រា 74. [Para 149][Para 150][Para 151]
On the $50 agreement the panel applied មាត្រា 13: statutory rules are mandatory, and any unilateral decision, contract or agreement giving less than the law is void. [Para 193][Para 194][Para 196] Because $50 per year fell below what មាត្រា 75, 77, 89 and 91 require, the agreement was void and the statutory amounts were ordered instead. [Para 200][Para 201]
On quantum, the panel applied the notice scale in មាត្រា 75 (7, 15 or 30 days and upwards by length of service) and មាត្រា 77 for pay in lieu. [Para 152][Para 157][Para 166] It fixed the daily wage as average monthly earnings over the last 12 months divided by 26, following Award 51/04 (Samhan). [Para 168][Para 171][Para 180] Under មាត្រា 89 it awarded 15 days' wages and allowances per year of service, capped at six months, with a part-year of six months or more counted as a full year. [Para 173][Para 174][Para 176] Under មាត្រា 91 it awarded moral damages equal to the dismissal damages. [Para 185][Para 187][Para 190]
For fixed-duration workers the panel held that where the employer ends such a contract before term, មាត្រា 73(3) entitles the worker to moral damages at least equal to the wages due to the end of the contract, and មាត្រា 73(6) to 5 per cent severance. [Para 232][Para 233][Para 237][Para 238] It rejected the claimant's method of computing the 5 per cent on the wages that would have been earned to the end of the term, and instead ordered it computed on wages actually earned from the start of the final three-month contract to the termination date, following Award 107/04. [Para 245][Para 248][Para 254]
The two individual claims were left undecided on their merits: the union president's dismissal under មាត្រា 43 of the Law on Trade Unions was still inside the Minister's two-month review after the employer's appeal of 4 September 2017, so the panel declined to consider it; and the second worker's identical claims were already before the court, so the panel declined those too. [Para 204][Para 219][Para 220][Para 226][Para 227]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An agreement in which workers accept a flat sum per year of service in place of statutory notice pay, dismissal damages and moral damages is void under មាត្រា 13 where the sum is less than the law requires, and the panel will award the statutory amounts regardless of the thumbprints; an employer that pleads financial collapse but refuses to produce its accounts has not discharged its burden and the dismissals stand without valid reason under មាត្រា 74. [Para 151][Para 196][Para 200]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's lengthy handling of the disputed delegation letters — 96 names not matching the payroll, 20 duplicated names and unreadable thumbprints — ended in an instruction that the parties re-verify names together rather than in any ruling, and it then proceeded on the verified lists. [Para 135][Para 137][Para 139] Its observation that the employer and workers should jointly check names before the panel hears such claims is guidance, not an order. [Para 138]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The number of fixed-duration claimants is given three ways: 45 by the claimant at the hearing, 65 by the employer, and 65 in the claimant's own table. [Para 95][Para 112][Para 113] The panel states at [Para 132] that it considers the case for "20" workers, which matches neither the 277 indefinite-duration names nor the 65 fixed-duration names. Article numbers are misprinted in the reasoning: [Para 177] cites "មាត្រា ៨" for the six-month cap that មាត្រា 89 contains, and [Para 94] cites មាត្រា 89 for moral damages where the reasoning elsewhere uses 91. [Para 185] The award records the company director as "Bai Jian Feng", described as being of Korean nationality, at [Para 52]. The company's Khmer name and that of the provincial department are printed inconsistently across the document list. Worker and union representative names are redacted here.
