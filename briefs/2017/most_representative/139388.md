# Award No. 238/16 — Ever Well Knitting Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 5 January 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three — Hem Hunarith (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair) · Doc ID 139388

## 1. Facts
A garment factory at Treay Sla, Sa'ang, Kandal, employing about 200 workers [Para 63][Para 64]. The plant union was registered on 14 March 2016 [Para 54][Para 65]. On 23 August 2016 the employer told a group of workers, whose three-month fixed-duration contracts expired on 31 August 2016, that their contracts would not be renewed [Para 86][Para 85]. Among them were the union's deputy president, Mr S, an activist, Ms T, and two candidates who had lost the union's 31 July 2016 election [Para 88][Para 97]. Mr C, a member, was instead cut off on 31 August 2016 although his contract ran to 11 November 2016 [Para 123][Para 124].

## 2. Issues in dispute
Six demands were filed; one was settled and one withdrawn, leaving four [Para 21][Para 22].
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr S, deputy union president, and Ms T, union activist, with back wages, ending its discrimination against union leaders and activists? [Para 154]
2. Whether the employer must take back the two unsuccessful election candidates, Ms Y and Ms H? [Para 194]
3. Whether the employer must take back the nine union members named, with back wages? [Para 202]
4. Whether the employer must issue one-year fixed-duration contracts to all workers instead of three-month contracts? [Para 130]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the non-renewals came immediately after the union notified the employer of its new leadership election, and struck only workers connected with the union, while the factory worked daily overtime and hired replacements [Para 69][Para 70][Para 76]. They would produce the new hires' contracts and staff numbers [Para 181].
**Employer (respondent):** there was simply no work for these workers, so their contracts were not renewed; three-month contracts need no renewal notice, but seven days' notice was given as a kindness [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]. It denied knowing who the leaders and activists were [Para 74][Para 95]. Of the thirteen claimants, ten had already accepted severance [Para 81].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the reinstatement claims for all the non-renewed workers are dismissed; the employer must pay Mr C his damages, 5% end-of-contract indemnity, unused annual leave and final wages; the one-year-contract demand is dismissed [Para 288][Para 292].

On standing, the panel found no most-representative union, but the provincial labour department had certified four representatives under Prakas 362, three of whom attended, and they could represent the workers [Para 145][Para 149][Para 152].

On issue 1, the panel followed Award 185/16 in holding that the special protection requiring labour-inspector consent applies only to dismissal, not to the expiry of a fixed-duration contract [Para 159][Para 162]. Article 62 of the Trade Union Law nevertheless forbids discrimination by reason of union office or activity, expressly including improper non-renewal of successive fixed-duration contracts [Para 167]. Following Award 182/16, that article adds to rather than contradicts Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law [Para 173][Para 178]. But the burden lay on the workers, and the panel found the August non-renewals covered 19 workers including non-union members, that the replacements were only in the spray section, and that no documentary evidence was produced [Para 186][Para 187][Para 190]. Discrimination was not proved [Para 191][Para 193].

Issues 2 and 3 (the eight non-renewed members) turned on the same reasoning and were dismissed [Para 200][Para 210]. Mr C's case differed: his contract ran to 11 November 2016 and the employer ended it on 31 August 2016, which is a dismissal, not a non-renewal [Para 216][Para 218]. Under Article 73(3), unilateral termination before term without serious misconduct entitles the worker to damages of at least the wages due to the contract's end [Para 229][Para 234]; he was also owed the 5% end-of-contract indemnity under Article 73(6), unused annual leave under Articles 166–167, and final wages under Article 116 [Para 241][Para 249][Para 253].

On issue 4, a contract is formed by offer and acceptance, and neither party may compel the other to accept terms it rejects, so the demand for one-year contracts was dismissed [Para 270][Para 284][Para 287].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Article 62 of the Trade Union Law makes improper non-renewal of a fixed-duration contract a form of discrimination [Para 167], but the worker must prove it: where the same non-renewal sweep hits non-union workers and the evidence is only the timing of the union's election notice, discrimination is not established [Para 186][Para 191]. Where the employer ends a fixed-duration contract before its expiry for want of work rather than serious misconduct, that is a dismissal, and the worker is owed damages at least equal to the wages to the contract's end [Para 218][Para 234], alongside the 5% indemnity, unused annual leave and final wages [Para 241][Para 249][Para 253].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the labour inspector's prior consent under the Trade Union Law protects against dismissal only, so a fixed-duration contract may lawfully be allowed to expire [Para 162]; and that no party can force another into a contract term it does not accept [Para 284].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award extends its issue date from 21 December "2022" to 5 January 2017, an obvious OCR corruption of 2016 [Para 25]. The conciliation report is cited as No. 209716 in one place and 2097/16 in another, and the conciliation date as 18 November and 21 November [Para 12][Para 16][Para 17]. The employer says thirteen workers claimed and ten took severance, yet issue 3 names nine members and the panel speaks of eight [Para 81][Para 202][Para 210]. One line calls the provincial labour department a hospital [Para 152]. The employer's first representative carries a garbled name [Para 1].
