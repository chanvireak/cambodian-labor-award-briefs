# Award No. 033/17 — Thien Sung Gamem (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 21 November 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 138938

## 1. Facts
A garment manufacturer at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, which hired production workers on successive three-month fixed-duration contracts. [Para 1][Para 137] The union federation's collective-dispute complaint No. 108/17 of 7 August 2017 went to conciliation on 14 September 2017, which settled 6 of 10 points and left 4 unsettled for the panel. [Para 15][Para 16] At the hearing one of the four was settled for Mr C and two were withdrawn, leaving two live points. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] The individual claimants appear here as Ms M, Mr Y, Mr N, Mr A and Mr C.

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Ms M, Mr Y, Mr N and Mr A and pay them wages from the date of dismissal until reinstatement? [Para 144]
2. Whether the employer must preserve the seniority and other benefits of Ms H and Ms N, who had corrected their names for social-security registration? [Para 198]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** reinstatement at the same workplace with seniority from first hire and back wages; repeated renewals had converted the contracts to indefinite duration; the five-per-cent incentive should continue; the non-renewals were union discrimination. [Para 2][Para 176][Para 206]

**Employer (respondent):** it cannot take them back; the fixed-duration contracts reached their term and were settled according to law; reinstatement is possible only on new contracts wiping seniority; there is no discrimination, since it had worked with their union for over a year. [Para 3][Para 155][Para 182]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — reinstatement refused for all; the employer was ordered to pay Ms M and Mr Y notice pay under Articles 75 and 77, dismissal damages under Article 89, pay for unused annual leave under Articles 166 and 167, moral damages under Article 91 and final wages under Article 116, and may recover the five-per-cent end-of-contract payments; seniority was ordered preserved for Ms H and Ms N. The award prints no riel or USD figure for any head. [Para 346][Para 355][Para 414]

On contract type, the panel held that Article 67(2) caps a fixed-duration contract at two years and converts renewals beyond that into an indefinite contract, following Award 10/03 Zaksintec and ILO Recommendation 166, and that the five-per-cent payment at each renewal does not prevent the conversion (Award 91/10 New Wide). [Para 241][Para 248][Para 264][Para 269][Para 270] Termination was therefore a dismissal, which Article 74 permits only for a reason relating to capacity or conduct or the needs of the enterprise. [Para 271][Para 273]

For Ms M the sole reason advanced was that she worked more slowly than others, with no disciplinary step taken; she also declined both options the employer offered, so the employment relationship had broken down and compensation replaced reinstatement. [Para 275][Para 277][Para 282][Para 283] For Mr Y the written warning did not state which level of warning it was or which internal-regulation offence it recorded, and the carton report relied on was in English, which Prakas 099 Article 23 excludes because Khmer is the working language; the dismissal thus breached Prakas 10 of the internal rules and Article 74. [Para 288][Para 289][Para 297]

Quantum was fixed by the panel's usual heads: one month's notice because both had between two and five years' service [Para 302][Para 308]; Article 89 dismissal damages on the twelve-month average wage [Para 318][Para 321]; accrued annual leave [Para 328][Para 331]; Article 91 moral damages, claimable without proof of loss where the dismissal lacked a valid reason [Para 338][Para 341]; and final wages [Para 343].

Mr N and Mr A failed because their fixed-duration contracts expired naturally on 3 May 2017 and 17 April 2017; expiry operates automatically and renewal is a matter of the parties' agreement, so there was no duty to reinstate. [Para 357][Para 359][Para 362][Para 392] Ms M's union-discrimination allegation failed for want of evidence beyond her own assertion, the burden being on the claimant, notwithstanding six new hires and the inspection of her documents. [Para 376][Para 384][Para 385]

On issue 2 the panel applied Ministry Notification No. 127 of 2 May 2016, which treats identity irregularities as not the worker's fault and preserves benefits including seniority on re-registration; since the employer never terminated the two workers, their seniority had never been cut. [Para 406][Para 408][Para 413][Para 414]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where successive fixed-duration contracts are renewed so that their aggregate exceeds two years, the contract becomes indefinite by force of Article 67(2) whatever five-per-cent payments were made at each renewal; ending it is then a dismissal that must satisfy Article 74, and a breach entitles the worker to notice pay, dismissal damages, accrued annual leave, moral damages and final wages, subject to the employer's right to recover the five-per-cent sums already paid. [Para 258][Para 269][Para 355] A correction of a worker's name for social-security registration does not interrupt seniority, which runs from first entry into service until the worker actually stops working for the company. [Para 398][Para 405][Para 414]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that many enterprises have no most-representative union, that this can obstruct workers' representation rights and touch rights guaranteed by the Constitution, and that affected workers may then represent themselves or mandate a local union or another person. [Para 223][Para 224][Para 225]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR degradation is extensive. The employer's name is printed in several forms across the award. Individual names shift between spellings paragraph by paragraph: the worker recorded as Nhem Pov appears as ញ៉ែ ម ពៅ, ញ៉ែម ពៅ and ញ៉ែម ពៅ, and paragraph 2 lists "An Ly" with staff number 16428 where the rest of the award names An Si with the same number. Paragraph 144 inserts a stray digit into Yeng Sothun's staff number ("២ ៥៧៩៥"). Paragraph 145 dates Ms M's entry into service to 4 April 2013, while the contract summary at paragraph 53 dates her first contract from 04/02/2013. No sum in riel or USD is printed for any of the compensation ordered, so the operative part is unquantified on its face.
