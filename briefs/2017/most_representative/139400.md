# Award No. 246/16 — Seng Hong Yan Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 12 January 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Inng Sothy appointed by the employer party, An Non appointed by the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139400

## 1. Facts
Seng Hong Yan Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. is a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, employing about 250 workers at the time of the award. [Para 39][Para 40] On 8 November 2016 the Coalition of Cambodian Apparel Workers' Democratic Union (C.CAWDU) filed a collective dispute claiming seven improvements to working conditions; conciliation failed on all seven and the non-conciliation report No. 1558 was forwarded to the Council on 21 December 2016. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 11] At the 29 December 2016 hearing the parties settled five demands by agreement, the workers withdrew one, and only the fixed-duration contract demand remained for decision. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] The grievance: workers were hired on three-month contracts renewed repeatedly so that about 80 of them had accumulated more than two years' service without conversion to an indefinite-duration contract. [Para 48][Para 53]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reduce the attendance bonus according to law when a worker takes leave with the employer's agreement? [Para 2] (withdrawn by the workers at the hearing [Para 21])
2. Whether the employer must pay the output-based bonus every Wednesday as before? [Para 3] (settled by agreement [Para 20])
3. Whether the employer must use an indefinite-duration employment contract for workers who have worked more than two years? [Para 4][Para 48]
4. Whether the employer must abstain from dismissing any worker without cause, and apply the procedure and law on labour to every dismissal? [Para 5] (settled by agreement [Para 20])
5. Whether the employer must station a nurse during every hour that workers perform overtime? [Para 6] (settled by agreement [Para 20])
6. Whether the employer must provide a doctor and medical practitioner on duty? [Para 7] (settled by agreement [Para 20])
7. Whether the employer must provide a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន)? [Para 8] (settled by agreement [Para 20])

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** three-month contracts renewed indefinitely leave workers exposed to non-renewal, and the employer uses the threat of non-renewal to pressure workers who refuse overtime or ask for sick or annual leave; renewal delivers no security and forfeits the workers' entitlements. [Para 50][Para 51] About 80 claimants have more than two years' cumulative service, and the employer never converted them. [Para 53][Para 54]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer applies the Labour Law on fixed-duration contracts and pays the 5 per cent termination indemnity at each expiry, so it has done nothing unlawful. [Para 56] Fixed-duration contracting is convenient at the point of termination, and in any event workers do not stay long because they move to new factories opened near their homes. [Para 57][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted on issue 3 — the panel ordered the employer to use an indefinite-duration employment contract where a worker's cumulative fixed-duration service exceeds 2 years. [Para 119][Para 118] Issues 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7 were recorded as settled by agreement and issue 1 as withdrawn, so no order was made on them. [Para 20][Para 21]

On standing, the panel first had to fix who the claimant was, because neither party disputed that the enterprise had no union holding most-representative status. [Para 43][Para 69] It held that only a most-representative union holds the exclusive right to bring and represent a collective dispute, following its reasoning in Case 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting), [Para 59][Para 61][Para 63] but that where no such union exists workers may represent themselves or delegate their rights to a local union or another person. [Para 70] Prakas No. 099 on Council procedure, Article 19, allows a party to be represented by any person it has authorised in writing, [Para 72][Para 74] and Prakas No. 362 delegates to the Labour Disputes Department and provincial offices the function of certifying worker representatives. [Para 77][Para 79] Three named delegates were certified, were among the 268 workers who thumb-printed the 21 September 2016 delegation, and appeared at the hearing; the panel therefore found the claimant to be 268 workers. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 83][Para 84]

On the substance, Article 67(2) of the Labour Law caps a fixed-duration contract, and any renewal of it, at a maximum of two years, and provides that contrary practice turns the contract into one without a fixed term. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87] Article 73(5) treats a contract whose total renewed duration exceeds the Article 67 limit as a contract without a fixed term. [Para 88][Para 90] The panel adopted its reasoning in Award 10/03 (Zacksintek) that the trigger is the cumulative duration of all the contracts, not each renewal taken alone, [Para 92][Para 97] and cited ILO Recommendation No. 166 of 1982, paragraph 3, against using fixed-term contracts for long-term work. [Para 98][Para 99] It found as fact that the employer had renewed three-month contracts to the present and that the cumulative total exceeded two years for the claimants, and that the employer did not contest this at the hearing. [Para 105][Para 107]

The employer's answer — that the 5 per cent indemnity paid at each expiry keeps the relationship lawful — was rejected: payment on expiry and renewal cannot prevent a fixed-duration contract from converting, [Para 112] and the money paid at each expiry does not mean the workers' contracts remained fixed-duration. [Para 116]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer renews fixed-duration contracts so that a worker's cumulative service exceeds the two-year maximum in Article 67(2) of the Labour Law, the contract becomes one of indefinite duration by operation of law, and the employer may not continue to issue fixed-duration contracts to that worker. [Para 87][Para 103][Para 117][Para 118] Payment of the 5 per cent termination indemnity at each expiry is not a defence and does not restart the two-year calculation, because the test is the total duration of the successive contracts, not the length of any single term. [Para 97][Para 112][Para 116]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that the absence of a most-representative union may obstruct workers' right of representation and may affect the constitutional right to complain and claim compensation under Article 39 of the Constitution are general remarks, not necessary to the order on issue 3. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]
