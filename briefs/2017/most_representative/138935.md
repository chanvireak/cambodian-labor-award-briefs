# Award No. 032/17 — Zhong Yin (Cambodia) B Textile Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 23 November 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 138935

## 1. Facts
Zhong Yin (Cambodia) B Textile Co., Ltd. is a garment-cutting and sewing company employing about 1,200 workers. [Para 60][Para 61] The Democratic Union of Garment Workers of Cambodia (registered under certificate No. 3363, 15 September 2015) filed 12 demands for improved conditions; none were settled at conciliation, issues 11 and 12 settled at the hearing, and issues 3 and 6 were merged, leaving a long list for the panel. [Para 18][Para 23][Para 24][Para 54] 726 workers signed a letter delegating authority to three worker representatives. [Para 64] The company also has the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union, which held a most-representative certificate No. 048/16 dated 13 July 2016 and valid to 13 July 2018. [Para 65][Para 146] On 17 October 2017 the claimant representatives withdrew 11 of the remaining demands; the employer objected. [Para 66][Para 67]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back the 33 dismissed workers, all members of the claimant union, whose non-renewal and termination is said to breach មាត្រា 12 of the Labour Law? [Para 69][Para 70]
2. Whether the employer must issue piece rates for every series and every section within 3 to 7 days of a new series? [Para 81]
3. Whether the employer must restore the meal allowance of 1,500 riel for general workers, 5,500 riel for day-shift auto-section workers and 6,500 riel for night-shift auto-section workers? [Para 83]
4. Whether the employer must restore the monthly $10 transport and accommodation allowance? [Para 98]
5. Whether the $12 monthly attendance bonus may be deducted only pro rata for absences that the employer has authorised? [Para 103]
6. Whether the employer must pay an incentive or machine-handling allowance to workers in all sections? [Para 112]
7. Whether fixed-duration contracts whose cumulative length exceeds two years must convert to indefinite-duration contracts? [Para 116]
8. Whether the employer must raise the PN series piece rates in the fashion section to the 2016 rates? [Para 125]
9. Whether the employer must raise the hem-section piece rate to $0.35 for all series? [Para 134]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 33 terminations hit only members of one union, which is discrimination contrary to មាត្រា 12; benefits granted above the law become lawful entitlements that cannot be withdrawn unilaterally under មាត្រា 13, so the meal, transport and incentive payments practised since 2013 must continue; and contracts renewed without gap for over two years automatically become indefinite under មាត្រា 67. [Para 70][Para 87][Para 88][Para 101][Para 119][Para 120]
**Employer (respondent):** orders have fallen sharply, 340 workers' contracts were suspended in 2016 with further suspensions in 2017, so it cannot restore the extra-statutory benefits or raise piece rates; the terminations were lawful under មាត្រា 73, two were for serious misconduct, five were casual workers and one had already been paid severance; and the 5 per cent severance paid at the end of each fixed-duration contract prevents conversion. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 75][Para 76][Para 79][Para 89][Para 122][Para 123]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the case at all, on the ground that the claimant party had no standing to bring a collective labour dispute before it. [Para 148][Para 149]
The panel began with the scope of its own authority over the claimant. [Para 137] Reading មាត្រា 54(1) of the Law on Trade Unions of 17 May 2016, it held that only the most-representative union holds the exclusive right to negotiate a collective agreement and to conduct a collective labour dispute, and that this right extends to bringing the dispute at every stage, including before the Arbitration Council. [Para 137][Para 141][Para 142] It followed its own reasoning in Award 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting) and Award 171/16 (Jewellyn). [Para 140][Para 143]
On the facts the panel found the company did have a most-representative union — the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union — holding a valid certificate to 13 July 2018. [Para 146] The three workers designated by the provincial Labour Department under letter No. 006/17 dated 15 September 2017 were therefore not entitled to represent the workforce in bringing this collective dispute, and the panel declined to consider the case. [Para 145][Para 147] It recorded, before reaching that conclusion, that the claimant's unilateral withdrawal of 11 demands could not take effect without the respondent's consent under មាត្រា 217(2) of the Civil Procedure Code, the hearing having already been held. [Para 66][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an enterprise has a union holding a valid most-representative certificate, a group of workers — even one authorised by the Labour Department to act as dispute representatives — cannot bring a collective labour dispute to the panel; the panel will refuse to hear the case without reaching the merits, because the exclusive right under មាត្រា 54 belongs to that union alone. [Para 142][Para 146][Para 147]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's treatment of the withdrawal under មាត្រា 217(2) of the Civil Procedure Code was unnecessary once the case was refused for want of standing, and no order turned on it. [Para 68] It also noted, without deciding, that the employer's method of computing piece-rate wages by dividing total lot value by total hours and comparing it with the hourly base wage had been accepted by the Ministry and the ILO. [Para 132][Para 133]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated 23 November 2017 on its face, but [Para 28] records the parties' agreement to postpone issuance to 24 November 2017 — two dates are printed and neither is reconciled. The list of remaining issues at [Para 21] is introduced as nine points but enumerates ten entries out of order ("ទី១, ទី២, ទី៣ និង ទី៦, ទី៤, ទី៥, ទី៧, ទី៨, ទី ៩, និង ទី១០"). The withdrawal is described at [Para 66] as covering 11 issues although only eight numbers are listed, and the same paragraph and [Para 67] refer to case No. 034/17 while the file is No. 032/17. The name and identity-number tables at [Para 2] and [Para 69] are badly duplicated by OCR, with fragments repeated and interleaved so that several card numbers cannot be relied on. The company appears variously as Zhong Yin, Zhong Ying and Zhong Yiv (and as ស្យុង យិន / ស្យុង មិន in Khmer). Worker and union representative names are redacted here.
