# Award No. 009/17 — Im We Garment International (Branch 3) (Arbitration Council, 22 February 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Sin Kim Sean for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 138898

## 1. Facts
Im We Garment International (Branch 3) is a garment factory at Prek Ta Long, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employing about 2,300 workers. [Para 38][Para 39] On 28 January 2016 the factory director asked the Labour Inspection Department to allow the contracts of the 245 workers in Building A to be suspended for two months, from 8 February to 31 March 2016; on 9 February 2016 the Department head replied by letter No. 024 កប/អក/អធក. [Para 23][Para 24] The company actually suspended from 16 February to 31 March 2016 and paid each worker a USD 50 monthly accommodation allowance in the meantime. [Para 42] Seventeen workers — four of them the recognised worker representatives — filed the claim for 100% of wages, attendance bonus, transport allowance and other benefits for the whole suspension period, arguing the Department's letter was only an invitation to negotiate and not an authorisation. [Para 35][Para 40][Para 41][Para 44] The same claim had already been decided against the union in Award No. 091/16. [Para 51][Para 52]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay 100% of wages, attendance bonus, transport allowance and other benefits to the Building A workers for the suspension period of 16 February to 31 March 2016? [Para 42][Para 76]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the Department's letter No. 024 was merely a notice telling the employer to discuss the matter with the union and the workers, not a permission to suspend, so the suspension is void under Article 13 of the Labour Law; the production line was running normally and lorries kept coming and going, and the employer never obtained the workers' agreement. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent):** the suspension was authorised by the labour inspector under Article 71, which does not require the workers' consent, and the accommodation allowance was paid under Article 72; the same issue was already decided in Award No. 091/16, which has become final because nobody objected. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 51]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim for 100% wages and benefits for the suspension period was refused for the two claimants who were union members as already decided, and refused on the merits for the other fifteen. [Para 110][Para 111]

The panel first fixed who was before it. There is no most representative union at the plant, but the Labour Disputes Department had certified four worker representatives under Prakas No. 362, and seventeen workers attended, so those seventeen were the claimants. [Para 68][Para 72][Para 73][Para 75] It then split them. Two of the seventeen, Ms S and Ms K, were members of the Democratic Union of Khmer Garment Workers at Branch 3 — the union that had brought Award No. 091/16 — and their claim was identical to issue 2 of that award. [Para 57][Para 84][Para 89] Applying res judicata, the panel found the same parties, the same issue and a decision on the merits, and refused to hear them again. [Para 87][Para 93][Para 94] The remaining fifteen were not union members and had not joined the earlier case, so the first condition failed and the panel could decide afresh. [Para 96][Para 97][Para 99][Para 100] On the merits it read letter No. 024 of 9 February 2016 as an authorisation: the text — "the director is expected to suspend this employment contract lawfully under the Labour Law" — was held to permit the suspension rather than merely to invite negotiation, and the panel expressly rejected the workers' contrary reading. [Para 103][Para 104][Para 105] It found the actual suspension ran from 16 February to 31 March 2016, which is not longer than two months, and so complied with Article 71 of the Labour Law; and it adopted its own reasoning in Award No. 091/16 that the employer therefore owed no wages, attendance bonus, transport allowance or other benefit for that period. [Para 107][Para 108]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A letter from the labour inspector which states that the employer is expected to suspend the contract lawfully is an authorisation for the purposes of Article 71 of the Labour Law, and a suspension that stays within two months and follows such a letter discharges the employer from paying wages and benefits for the suspension period. [Para 104][Para 105][Para 107][Para 108] Where the same claim is later brought by workers who were neither members of the union that litigated it nor parties to that case, res judicata does not bar them and the panel will decide the claim afresh on its merits; it bars only those who were parties or members of the party union. [Para 97][Para 99][Para 100]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations about whether the production line continued to operate during the suspension, and about the lorries entering and leaving the factory, did not affect the outcome once the authorisation was found to exist. [Para 47][Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The claim is stated as "2000 per cent" in the summary of the dispute but as 100 per cent everywhere else; the Building A headcount is 246 in the summary and 245 in the procedural history and the claim itself. [Para 3][Para 12][Para 27][Para 42]
- The authorisation letter's date is given as 09 February 2016 in the evidence and findings, but as "ថ្ងៃទី ០ ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦" — day missing — at paragraph 103.
- The prior award is cited as "លេខ ០៩១៦" at paragraph 5 and as "លេខ ០៩១/១៦" elsewhere, and the company is printed as អ៊ឹម វ៉េ, អ៊ីម វេ, អ៊ីម វ៉េ, អឺម and អ៉ីម across the document.
- Paragraph 14 is badly corrupted ("ចំនួន 09 6006", "ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញាធរ ផ្កាយ veligima 1997 @.@ 000 @.@ 000"), and paragraphs 44 and 67 contain embedded English filler text ("iones albood invilionationaliagamon @-@ interical", "untilar exuad @-@ 2017").
- The number of claimants fluctuates: 17 attend the hearing, but the order at paragraph 111 names 15 individuals while paragraph 109 lists a partly different set, with សុខី and សំ សុខី used interchangeably.
- The letter of request is dated 28 January 2016 and asks for suspension from 8 February 2016, but the employer suspended from 16 February 2016; the eight-day gap is unexplained on the face of the award.
