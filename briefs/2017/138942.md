# Award No. 039/17 — Cambodia Airport Management Services (CAMS), Siem Reap International Airport (Arbitration Council, 13 December 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: Mar Sambor Na (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kuy Neam (president) · Doc ID 138942

## 1. Facts
CAMS operates the international airports of Siem Reap, Phnom Penh and Sihanoukville with about 558 staff. [Para 78][Para 106] It engages GSI (formerly UGS) as a labour contractor to supply baggage handlers, under a service agreement of 24 June 2015. [Para 109][Para 146][Para 213] A five-point collective dispute went to conciliation on 26 September 2017, which settled two points and left three; the point concerning Mr T was withdrawn at the hearing, leaving two. [Para 18][Para 24][Para 25] The union holds a registration certificate, but the panel found no most-representative union at CAMS; two of the four certified worker representatives attended. [Para 81][Para 83][Para 172][Para 174]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must adopt a new set of internal regulations for CAMS, drafted in consultation with the union and the employee delegates? [Para 176]
3. Whether the employer CAMS must pay termination benefits to the three baggage handlers, Mr T, Mr D and Mr Th? [Para 186]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the common internal regulations were put into force without consultation with the employee delegates or the union leadership, several provisions are unlawful, restrict workers' rights or are unclear, and workers at Siem Reap cannot be governed by rules shared with two other airports whose working conditions differ; CAMS must also pay the three handlers their termination benefits because they worked under its direction and GSI refused to pay. [Para 92][Para 93][Para 96][Para 122][Para 123]

**Employer (respondent):** revising internal regulations is managerial prerogative; the common regulations were approved in writing by the provincial labour department after three meetings with the delegates of CAMS and a three-day period for their comments; and the three handlers are GSI's employees, with no contract of any kind with CAMS. [Para 3][Para 99][Para 100][Para 128][Para 154]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining points were refused, and no payment was ordered. [Para 235][Para 236]

On issue 1 the panel asked whether it had jurisdiction at all. Article 302 of the Labour Law confines a collective labour dispute to one arising between an employer and some of its own workers over working conditions or the exercise of rights granted to a professional organisation, and Prakas No. 099 Article 32 confines the panel to collective disputes referred to it under Article 309. [Para 178][Para 179][Para 182] A complaint that the labour department should not have approved the common internal regulations is not a dispute of that kind, and at the hearing the workers identified no specific provision said to be unlawful, rights-restricting or unclear. [Para 180][Para 181] The panel accordingly held it had no jurisdiction and dismissed the point. [Para 183][Para 184][Para 185]

On issue 3 the panel first rejected the employer's objection to representation: a union president who is not an advocate may nonetheless represent workers who have mandated him in writing, since Prakas No. 099 Article 19 and Article 53 of the Civil Procedure Code allow a party to be represented by any person authorised in writing. [Para 189][Para 193][Para 194] It also held the three handlers to be within the words "some of its workers" in Article 302, because Article 48(2) lets a worker of a contractor sue the entrepreneur directly. [Para 216][Para 219][Para 220]

On the substance, Article 45 makes GSI a labour contractor: it recruits, pays and disciplines the three men, while CAMS is the entrepreneur, directs their work, records their hours and receives the contractor's performance assessments. [Para 209][Para 211][Para 214][Para 215] Article 48(1) makes the entrepreneur liable in substitution only where the contractor has no funds or has not fully performed its obligations. The three men produced no evidence that they had ever demanded payment from GSI, or that GSI was unable to pay, although GSI is a registered company still trading. [Para 223][Para 228][Para 230] Article 48 could not therefore be applied and the claim against CAMS failed. [Para 231][Para 232]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An entrepreneur that engages a labour contractor is liable for the contractor's severance debts only where the contractor has no funds or has failed to perform its obligations; the worker must first claim against the contractor and prove that default, and the absence of that proof defeats the claim against the entrepreneur. [Para 223][Para 230][Para 232] A demand that the labour department's approval of common internal regulations be set aside is not a collective labour dispute within Article 302, and the panel has no jurisdiction over it. [Para 180][Para 183][Para 184]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general statement that the labour inspector and the Minister in principle decide whether a dispute is individual or collective before referral, and that the panel will normally defer to that classification absent clear reason to depart from it. [Para 199][Para 200]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is heavy throughout. The employer's name appears in many permutations of ខេមបូឌា អ៊ែរ ផត មែនី ជួមែន សីវីស (including ជ្ជមែន, សឺវីស and សីវីស), and its registration number is given as 09862 at paragraph 77 but as MOC-22109205 at paragraph 35. Paragraph 106, describing the three airport offices, is largely destroyed. The contractor is called UGS in the 2015 agreement and GSI thereafter, with the award noting the change of name only in passing. The numbering is internally inconsistent: the award has three issues, yet the panel writes "issue 2" at paragraphs 206 and 220 when it means the fifth claim, and the point withdrawn at the hearing is described as both issue 2 and claim 4. The president's name is printed កុយ នាម in the header and ក្មួយ នាម in the signature block.
