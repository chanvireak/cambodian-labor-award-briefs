# Award No. 19/11 — 8 Star Sportswear Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 February 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Kong Phallack, chair) · Doc ID 139992

## 1. Facts

8 Star Sportswear Ltd began operating in 2006 and employs about 1,100 workers at Damnak Thom Village, Stung Meanchey Commune, Meanchey District, Phnom Penh. Two unions exist at the factory, the Local Union of CUMW and the Local Union of FTUWKC; neither holds a certificate of most representative status. The Local Union of CUMW, representing 148 workers, is the claimant. [Para 1][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]

Nine demands were filed on 24 December 2010 and conciliation failed on all of them; five were settled at the hearing, leaving issues 3, 5, 8 and 9, of which 3, 8 and 9 were combined. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the employer must maintain the monthly US$ 7 bonus when workers arrive late (other than for traffic jams) or feel faint and must leave early? [Para 29]
2. Whether the employer must provide an additional 500 riel meal allowance, or a free meal, for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.? [Para 38]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The former director never deducted the US$ 7 bonus for lateness, fainting or flat tyres; the bonus should be maintained. The 1,200 riel meal allowance no longer buys a meal since prices rose, so 300 riel more is needed or a free meal provided. [Para 7][Para 38][Para 40]

**Employer (respondent):** The US$ 7 is a special incentive under its written policy of 24 November 2007 for workers who attend regularly without lateness, early departure or leave; it is conditional and not the statutory attendance bonus. It already pays 1,200 riel, above Notification No. 017, and most workers prefer an allowance to a meal. [Para 8][Para 9][Para 31][Para 42]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** partly granted — the US$ 7 demand is rejected; the panel declines to consider the 300 riel increase, but orders the employer to provide a free meal on each day a worker works two hours of overtime. [Para 78][Para 79]

Issues 3, 8 and 9: no provision of the Labour Law fixes a US$ 7 monthly bonus. The employer's written policy of 24 November 2007 defines it as conditional on regular attendance without lateness, early departure or leave, and the employer measures lateness against normal working hours; the panel found the employer would not maintain the bonus for lateness, early departure or hospitalisation from faintness, except for traffic jams. As the bonus is conditional, the employer was not obliged to grant the demand, and the panel rejected it. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 51][Para 52][Para 54]

Issue 5 was split into two demands. On the 300 riel increase, the employer's 1,200 riel already exceeded the 1,000 riel of Notification No. 017, and no law, agreement or collective agreement required more; the demand was therefore an interests dispute. Because the Local Union of CUMW holds no MRS, it lacks legal capacity to conclude a collective agreement covering the factory and standing to bring an interests dispute, so the panel declined to consider it. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 62][Para 64][Para 65][Para 71]

On the free meal, the notification's "1,000 riel meal allowance per day or a free meal" is ambiguous. The panel read its purpose as guaranteeing a meal to workers on overtime, since the allowance no longer buys one, and held the workers' demand legitimate and consistent with the notification's purpose. It ordered a free meal for each day of two hours' overtime. [Para 72][Para 77][Para 78]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A contractual bonus conditioned on regular attendance is not payable when the condition fails, and the panel will not rewrite it. A union without most representative status cannot bring an interests dispute, so a demand for a benefit above the statutory minimum is declined for want of standing — but the statutory free-meal alternative is an entitlement the employer must honour. [Para 52][Para 65][Para 77]

**Obiter dicta:** A union with MRS gains the capacity to negotiate a collective agreement and legal standing for interests disputes; an award settling an interests dispute stands as a one-year collective agreement, and the right to strike cannot be used to revise an unexpired agreement. [Para 65][Para 68][Para 69]
