# Award No. 128/11 — Mean Hathen (Sun Industrial) (Arbitration Council, 7 November 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Tang Tri presiding; Hem Hu Narith, Tuon Si Phan) · Doc ID 140082

## 1. Facts
Mean Hathen, a garment factory in Kampong Siem district, Kampong Cham, employed about 5,300 workers; the factory's free trade union and the FTUWKC filed four claims with the provincial labour department on 6 September 2011 after conciliation left three of them unresolved. [Para 11][Para 46] Both parties are signatories to the 28 September 2010 memorandum of understanding on professional relations in the garment industry, so the panel split the claims into rights disputes and interest disputes before deciding them. [Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may decide again the claim for a two-month allowance on resignation that Award No. 132/10 already decided between the same parties? [Para 50][Para 106]
2. Whether the employer must keep applying point 2 of the 26 January 2008 agreement, under which workers are transferred only with their consent? [Para 114]
3. Whether the employer must provide adequate parking and answer for motorcycles lost on the premises? [Para 135]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** point 8 of the 2008 agreement promised two months' allowance to workers on unlimited-duration contracts who resign, and point 2 promised that transfers would follow the worker's own wishes; the employer stopped honouring both after 2010 and moved an older worker to a section where she had to stand all day. [Para 50][Para 67]
**Employer (respondent):** the allowance was a temporary incentive offered when orders were scarce, not a permanent entitlement, and the 2008 agreement no longer fits present conditions; the union refused to renegotiate; the worker concerned was returned to her original section and the planned building transfer has not been implemented. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 66][Para 124]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for the resignation allowance dismissed as res judicata; the employer ordered to continue applying point 2 of the 26 January 2008 agreement; the parking and motorcycle claims dismissed. [Para 162][Para 163][Para 166]
Claims 1 and 2 concern rights written into a binding agreement and are therefore rights disputes; claim 4 is not, because neither the Labour Law nor any agreement obliges an employer to provide parking or to bear the loss of workers' vehicles. [Para 104][Para 105]
On claim 1 the panel applied res judicata: the same parties had brought the same claim in Award No. 132/10 of 29 December 2010, the panel there disposed of it fully, and the three conditions stated in Award No. 42/07 were met, so re-examining it would risk contradictory outcomes and defeat finality. [Para 106][Para 108][Para 113]
On claim 2 the panel held that Article 22 of Kret No. 38 of 28 October 1988 makes a contract the law of the parties, alterable only by their consent; the 2008 agreement fixed no expiry and no termination method, so it remains in force and the employer's wish to renegotiate does not release it. [Para 131][Para 133][Para 134]
On claim 4 the union holds 2,500 of 5,300 workers and is not the most representative union under Article 277, so the interest dispute could not be resolved by the panel; the claim for a motorcycle lost on 10 August 2011 also failed because no article or regulation imposes a duty of care on the employer for workers' vehicles. [Para 147][Para 149][Para 157][Para 158][Para 159]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claim that the same parties already brought, and that a prior award disposed of in full, cannot be re-examined: res judicata bars it even where the panel has no statutory rule of procedure, because contradictory awards on one dispute would defeat the purpose of arbitration. [Para 107][Para 112]
An agreement that fixes no expiry and no termination method binds the parties until they agree otherwise; changed business conditions are not by themselves a ground to stop applying it. [Para 125][Para 126][Para 133]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel praised the employer's 2006 security measures, encouraged it to enforce the September 2011 notice effectively, and observed that posting a notice without enforcing it is no answer to liability. [Para 160][Para 161]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The 2008 agreement is dated 26 January 2008 at paras 50 and 130, "26 July January 2008" at para 134, and "26 January 2019 2008" at para 3; the panel is citing one agreement throughout. Para 147 reads "2,500 of 30 workers, 5,300 in total", a stray "30" that does not reconcile with the 5,300 total. Para 106 repeats a clause twice. Individual names appear in the OCR (worker, union representative and employer representatives); they are reduced to initials here, and no name has been reconstructed. [Para 3][Para 134][Para 147]
