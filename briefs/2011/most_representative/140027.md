# Award No. 56/11 — Star Knitting & Garment Factory Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 23 June 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, employer-chosen; Liv Sovanna, worker-chosen; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 140027

## 1. Facts
Star Knitting & Garment Factory Ltd., at Prek Samrong Village, Takhmao, Kandal Province, began operating in 2004 and had passed through four owners; the current owner had run it for two months and employed about 393 workers, 275 of them women. [Para 20] [Para 21] The VKYUF local union held most-representative status under certificate No 003/11 dated 24 January 2011. [Para 22] Of ten demands, four were settled in conciliation, two more at the hearing, leaving issues 1, 2, 4 and 5. [Para 13] [Para 17] [Para 18] The factory had never had a day-care centre or breastfeeding room. [Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must set up a day-care centre and a breastfeeding room to the standard set by the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training? [Para 2] [Para 22]
2. Whether the employer must pay three months' wages to pregnant workers taking maternity leave under the Labour Law? [Para 3] [Para 25]
4. Whether the employer must deduct from the attendance bonus in proportion to the days of authorised leave taken, rather than forfeiting the whole bonus? [Para 6] [Para 26]
5. Whether the employer must inform workers of a new piece rate one week at the most after setting new samples? [Para 7] [Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The employer is required by law to provide the day-care centre and breastfeeding room and should do so despite the recent change of owner; it should pay maternity wages and maintain wages and bonuses during antenatal checks; the attendance bonus should be reduced only in proportion to authorised leave; and workers need the new piece rate in advance to know what difficult samples will earn. [Para 23] [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 28]
**Employer (respondent):** It acknowledged the statutory childcare requirement but said it could not afford it after only two months under the new owner and had no space; it had set no maternity payment policy though no worker had taken leave in that period; it deducted the full attendance bonus for any leave; and it announced piece rates one day before pay day because cheaper rates might trigger complaints. [Para 23] [Para 24] [Para 25] [Para 26] [Para 28] [Para 29]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted in full on all four remaining issues — the employer must set up a breastfeeding room and a day-care centre in or nearby the factory, with external day-care charges reimbursed on actual receipts for children over 18 months; pay 50% of ninety days' wages and perquisites before maternity leave begins; reduce the attendance bonus pro rata for authorised sick leave and pay it in full for paid annual leave and special leave taken from annual leave; and inform workers of the new piece rate seven days after they begin work on a new mode. [Para 73] [Para 74] The award was non-binding. [Para 74]

On issue 1, Article 186 requires enterprises employing at least 100 women to set up a nursing room and day-care centre, and the panel followed Awards 63/04-Shine Well, 68/04-City New, 103/08-Vivatino Design and 115/08-Top One. [Para 32] [Para 33] [Para 36] It held the breastfeeding room mandatory and the day-care obligation dischargeable by paying an external centre's charges on receipts for children over 18 months. [Para 37] [Para 38] [Para 39] [Para 40] The new owner's two-month tenure and lack of space did not excuse compliance. [Para 36]

On issue 2, Articles 182 and 183 give ninety days' maternity leave at half wages and perquisites, and the employer's refusal to pay that half was improper; following Awards 57/06-Evergreen, 97/06-New Max Garment and 08/08-Hytex, payment must be made before leave commences. [Para 42] [Para 43] [Para 44] [Para 45] [Para 46]

On issue 4, the panel noted the ambiguity in Notification No. 041/11 KB/SCN and Notification No. 017 SKBY about authorised leave, and applied Awards 57/07-Seratex, 106/07-M & V (Branch 3), 128/08-Wei Hua and 31/11-Quint Major Industrial to require pro-rata deduction for sick leave and full payment for annual and special leave. [Para 49] [Para 50] [Para 51] [Para 54] [Para 61] [Para 62]

On issue 5, Article 112(a) obliges the employer to inform workers of wage terms before a job is assigned or when terms change; the employer had failed to do so, and Awards 14/03-Chu Hsing and 71/08-River Rich supported the order. [Para 63] [Para 64] [Para 66] [Para 67] The panel found three to seven days adequate for testing and fixed seven days. [Para 72] [Para 73]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer with at least 100 women workers must provide a breastfeeding room and a day-care centre regardless of how recently it acquired the business; where it cannot provide a centre for children over 18 months it must pay the charges of an external centre against receipts, and the obligation is not excused by cost or lack of space. [Para 33] [Para 36] [Para 39] [Para 40]

**Obiter dicta:** The attendance bonus must be treated as earned in proportion to authorised leave, because workers permitted to take leave should not lose the whole bonus; and the employer's fear that cheaper piece rates will provoke complaints does not justify withholding the rate until pay day. [Para 29] [Para 50] [Para 51]
