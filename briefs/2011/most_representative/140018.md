# Award No. 45/11 — Zhong Yov Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 May 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vuoch Hun for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140018

## 1. Facts
Zhong Yov Co., Ltd, a Kandal Province garment factory that began operating in 2008, employed approximately 250 workers and had two unions: the Local Union of CUF (the claimant, registered on 12 January 2011 and representing 26 workers) and the Local Union of KYFTU, which held a certificate of most representative status (MRS) valid until 15 July 2012. [Para 24][Para 25] Six demands were referred; issues 2, 3, 5 and 6 were resolved at conciliation or hearing, leaving issues 1 and 4. [Para 14][Para 15] Issue 1 sought an additional US$ 5 of wages and US$ 6 of living allowance for August and September 2010; issue 4 sought back pay of seniority bonuses of US$ 3 deducted in 2008 and 2009 from 17 workers. [Para 5][Para 8][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay the additional US$ 5 of wages and US$ 6 of living allowance for August and September 2010 to workers who were not members of the MRS union? [Para 5][Para 25]
2. Whether the employer must back pay the US$ 3 seniority bonus deducted from 17 workers in 2008 and 2009 and pay the bonus in accordance with their seniority? [Para 8][Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they held the same positions as the KYFTU members and should receive the same benefits; the employer discriminated against the Local Union of CUF by paying the extra benefits only to workers who withdrew from CUF and joined KYFTU; because KYFTU held MRS, its agreement should apply to all workers. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent):** the agreement was made with KYFTU and applied only to its members; after Notification No. 049 took effect in October 2010 it paid all workers the US$ 5 increase and US$ 6 living allowance, so it had complied with the law. [Para 26][Para 29][Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel rejected the demand for back pay of the US$ 5 wage increase and US$ 6 living allowance for August and September 2010, but ordered the employer to back pay the US$ 3 seniority bonuses to the 17 listed workers and to pay the bonus in accordance with their seniority. [Para 69]

On issue 1 the panel held the demand was a rights dispute because it concerned an agreement. [Para 37][Para 38] Under Article 65 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 22 of Decree 38, a contract binds only its parties and cannot be extended to non-parties without consent (AAs 131/09 Medcrest, 54/10 USA, 68/10 USA). [Para 42][Para 43][Para 49] An MRS union may represent all workers, but its agreement becomes a collective agreement applicable to all only when registered under Clause 4 of Prakas No. 287; KYFTU told all workers the agreement covered only its members and did not apply for registration, so it bound members only. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58] The non-payment to CUF members was therefore not discrimination, because CUF was not a party to the agreement. [Para 60][Para 61]

On issue 4, Point 5 of Notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000 set seniority bonuses of US$ 2, US$ 3, US$ 4 and US$ 5 per month for one, two, three and four years' service. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64] The employer deducted US$ 3 per month from 17 workers in 2008 and 2009 under an agreement with KYFTU; Article 13(2) permits agreements more favourable than the law but not less favourable, so the deduction agreement did not comply and was invalid. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] The panel ordered back pay and future payment by seniority. [Para 68][Para 69] Arbitrator Liv Sovanna dissented on issue 1, reasoning that an MRS union's agreement must cover all workers, that the employer failed to produce the agreement when ordered, and that the workers should not lose benefits because of that failure. [Para 70][Para 76][Para 78][Para 80]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An agreement between an employer and the MRS union that is not registered as a collective agreement binds only the union's members; refusing the same benefits to non-members is not union discrimination because they are not parties to the agreement. Separately, an agreement authorising the employer to deduct a statutory seniority bonus is invalid under Article 13(2), and the bonus must be back paid according to seniority. [Para 54][Para 58][Para 65][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that registration is what converts an MRS-union agreement into a collective agreement applicable to all workers, and that the July 2010 notification was not involved because it post-dated the agreement, are general observations on the legal framework rather than findings necessary to the orders. [Para 41][Para 54]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award date is printed as 11 May 2011 in the header but as "20 April 2011" in the decision and signature block; both are reported and neither is treated as authoritative. [Para 1][Para 69]
- The award is an unofficial English translation; OCR drops the leading "t" from "the" throughout ("themployer", "hemployer", "outling"), and "remember" appears as "rember". [Para 1][Para 27]
- The 17-worker table at [Para 35]–[Para 36] is scrambled: names, ID numbers and commencement dates run together, and some entries lack an ID number. It was not relied on for any individual's identity.
- The employer's representative was absent from the second hearing on 9 May 2011, and the first hearing was on 20 April 2011. [Para 14]
- The MRS certificate is described as "No. 030/10 KBIVB dated 15 July 2010" and as valid until 15 July 2012. [Para 21][Para 25]
- The seniority table heading is garbled ("Employment Commencement Date") and the text describes "17 claimant workers" while the table appears to list 17 entries with duplicated numbering. [Para 34][Para 35]
