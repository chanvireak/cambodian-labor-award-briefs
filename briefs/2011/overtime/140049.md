# Award No. 86/11 — Reliable Appel Source In Das Strial (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 18 August 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mr Mar Sambor Na (employer party), Mr Vaen Pov (worker party), Mr Pen Bunchhea (chair) · Doc ID 140049

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment factory at Kbal Damrey village, Kap commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, employing more than 1,900 workers and hosting four unions. [Para 68] The Khmer Youth Union, with about 774 members, complained on 8 June 2011 and pressed eleven demands; conciliation on 30 June 2011 settled none. [Para 22] The non-conciliation report reached the Secretariat on 12 July 2011 and the panel heard the case on 25 July and 1 August 2011. [Para 24] The parties agreed issues 5, 9 and 11 and the workers withdrew issues 2 and 6; the employer refused to sign but asked that the agreement be written into the award. [Para 33] Six issues remained: exit security checks, hot drinking water, an extra trolley worker, discipline of an administrative officer, voluntary overtime, and warehouse heat. [Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must post additional security guards to check each building exit when workers leave? [Para 70]
2. Whether the employer must provide sufficient hot water for workers to drink? [Para 79]
3. Whether the employer must assign an additional worker to push cloth trolleys from the cutting tables to the ironing section on the second floor? [Para 89]
4. Whether the employer must take disciplinary measures under its internal rules against Ms T, the administrative officer, for interfering in union affairs? [Para 95]
5. Whether the employer must organise overtime on a genuinely voluntary basis, with workers signing for themselves? [Para 105]
6. Whether the employer must take measures to reduce the heat and stuffy air in the warehouse section? [Para 115]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** checks at the common exit take 10–30 minutes and cause crowding, lost belongings and fainting; women cannot push more than 60 kg; hot water is hard to reach because medical staff are hostile and "no entry" signs are posted; Ms T forced workers into another union and threatened closure if they joined the Khmer Youth Union; overtime consent was signed by group leaders and bonuses were cut for refusal; and the warehouse is hot and airless for 25 workers. [Para 106]
**Employer (respondent):** checks at each building exit would cause queues and danger among machines and cannot be staffed; checks take 10–12 minutes and have caused no problems; hot water is available in 11 tanks and workers may use any; it would discipline Ms T only if the union supplied evidence; signatures did not matter because overtime was voluntary; and it acknowledged the warehouse heat and planned roof fans without a date. [Para 77][Para 122]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel rejected the demands for guards at every exit and for an extra trolley worker; ordered the employer to announce its hot-water policy and remove all prohibition signs; ordered disciplinary action against Ms T only if the workers produce evidence, plus compliance with the Prakas No. 124 weight limits; ordered an immediate end to signatures on workers' behalf for overtime; and ordered immediate measures against heat and stuffy air in the warehouse. [Para 213]

Issue 1 was a rights dispute about the employer's managerial right. Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer authority to manage the enterprise, and the common-exit check had operated for over three years; two guards were added after the protest, and the claims of fainting and lost property were unsupported. [Para 145]
Issue 3 rests on Prakas No. 054 on hygienic drinking water. The employer had 11 hot-water tanks, so supply was adequate for the workforce; the real obstacle was access — hostile medical staff and "no entry" signs — so the employer must announce the policy and remove the signs. [Para 159]
Issue 4: the employer may discipline workers who breach internal rules, but the union produced no evidence of Ms T's misconduct; the order was therefore conditional on proof, together with compliance with the 60 kg limit for a four-wheel trolley under Prakas No. 124. [Para 183]
Issue 7: under Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999 overtime must be voluntary, without coercion or penalty; signatures by group leaders on workers' behalf were improper and must stop, with personal signature or thumbprint. [Para 199]
Issue 8: Article 229 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 147 on workplace temperature require acceptable conditions and every means — fans, extractors, cooling — where heat affects health; the employer admitted the warehouse was hot and stuffy, so it must act immediately. [Para 212]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The duty to supply hygienic drinking water under Prakas No. 054 is not discharged by tanks alone where access is blocked by hostile staff or prohibition signs; the employer must publish the right and remove the signs. [Para 151] Overtime is voluntary under Prakas No. 80, so a group leader's signature for a worker is invalid and must give way to the worker's own signature or thumbprint. [Para 193] Under Article 229 and Prakas No. 147, once an employer admits that heat and stuffy air affect health, it must use every available means to reduce them. [Para 205]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that an employer may discipline any worker who breaches internal rules, and its observation that access to the medical room was a practical rather than a legal obstacle, explain the orders rather than state independent rules.

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The company name is printed variously: "រីឡាយ អេប៊ល សួស អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រៀល" [Para 2], and "Reliable Source Sewing Industrial" in the library title; the romanisation in the heading is the brief's own.
- The administrative officer's name appears as "ថុល ផល្លីន", "ថុល ជល្លីន" [Para 168], "ថុល ជល្ទីន" and "ថុល ផល្លី"; she is recorded here as Ms T.
- Decision numbering is garbled: "ចំណុច វិវាទ ទី ៧៦" [Para 217] and "ចំណុច វិ វាទ ទី ៨" [Para 218] stand for issues 7 and 8, and the award refers to "ក្រុមហ៊ុន Lin" [Para 145], apparently a transcription error for the respondent.
- The weight table at [Para 166] is corrupt ("រទេះ រុព្ទ កង់ ១ ៣២ គីឡូក្រាម [៨០ គីឡូក្រាម ៤០ គីឡូក្រាម"), so the categories cannot be read; [Para 4] duplicates the letterhead in Khmer and English.
- The reasons cover only issues 1, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 10 of the eleven demanded; the award records that 5, 9 and 11 were agreed and 2 and 6 withdrawn but makes no order on the agreed points.
