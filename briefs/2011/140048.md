# Award No. 83/11 — Qicksew (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 27 July 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitral panel: Mr S Vuoch Hun (arbitrator chosen by the employer party), Mr An Virak (arbitrator chosen by the worker party), Nhân Somuntheary (chair, chosen by the two arbitrators) · Doc ID 140048

## 1. Facts
Qicksew (Cambodia) Co Ltd operates a garment factory at Building J, Damnak Thom village, Stung Meanchey commune, Meanchey district, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] The free workers' union at the factory and the workers brought a collective dispute over four unresolved demands: plastic sheeting to block the heat, a rice allowance of Riel 500 on top of the Riel 2,000 in Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, reinstatement of Mr O Chanram, and a USD 20 increase on the existing USD 10 bonus. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8] The Department of Labour Disputes received the complaint on 2 June 2011, conciliation on the same day settled none of the four points, and non-conciliation report No. 735 KB/AK/VK, dated 4 July 2011, reached the Secretariat on 5 July 2011. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] A hearing was fixed for 14 July 2011 at 08:30. [Para 14][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must place additional plastic sheeting in the factory to block the heat? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must add Riel 500 to the rice allowance, making it Riel 2,500? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must take back Mr O Chanram at his original workplace? [Para 6]
4. Whether the employer must add USD 20 to the existing USD 10 bonus? [Para 8]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union insisted on plastic sheeting because the heat inside the factory is intense every day; it claimed the employer had previously added Riel 500 on top of the Riel 1,000 rice allowance under Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000 but had added nothing to the Riel 2,000 set by Notification No. 041/11; and it demanded Mr O Chanram's reinstatement and the USD 20 bonus increase. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8]
**Employer (respondent):** it could not add more plastic sheeting because the factory was not hot; it would apply Notification No. 041/11 without any top-up; it could not take Mr O Chanram back because his individual labour dispute was being handled through the individual dispute procedure; and it would keep the bonus at USD 10. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** the case was closed without any ruling on the four demands; the panel treated the claim as abandoned. [Para 55]

At the hearing the employer appeared, but the local union and the workers who were the direct claimants did not. [Para 28] Only two officials of the free workers' union confederation, Mr K Channa and Mr Y Rath Keo Pisey, attended to give information; they did not show that they had been validly authorised to represent the claimant workers, and they said the local union no longer had members because they had resigned. [Para 19][Para 29] The employer said it had received letters asking it to stop deducting union dues from wages and had been told the workers had resigned from the union. [Para 30]
The panel applied clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and rule 4.7 of the Annex Procedural Rules, under which a party duly summoned that fails to appear without good cause may have the proceedings continued in its absence or terminated by an award. [Para 31][Para 32] It followed the three conditions established in Award No. 11/11: the party was properly summoned, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 34] On the first condition, the Secretariat had notified the claimant workers and the local union; only the confederation officials attended. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] On the second, the claimants were absent from all four stages of the proceedings. [Para 39][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43] On the third, the only explanation offered through the attending officials was that the union leaders and workers had resigned from the union. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 47][Para 48]
The panel held that even with the claimants absent it had power to issue an award, since a claimant bears the burden of presenting reasons and evidence, and here neither the union nor any worker was still pursuing the four demands. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] It therefore treated the local union and the two workers as having abandoned their claim and found sufficient reason and legal basis to close the case. [Para 53][Para 54]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union and the claimant workers, though properly summoned, fail to appear at any stage of the arbitration proceedings and offer no valid reason, the panel may treat the claim as abandoned and close the case under clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 and rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules, without ruling on the merits; the three conditions are proper summons, non-appearance, and absence of a valid excuse. [Para 31][Para 34][Para 53][Para 54] Officials of a federation who attend without proof of authority to represent the local union and its members do not cure the claimants' absence. [Para 29][Para 36]
**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it retains power to issue an award despite the claimants' absence, and its observations that the local union had no remaining members, explain why it proceeded to close rather than adjourn; they are not independent rules. [Para 29][Para 50]
