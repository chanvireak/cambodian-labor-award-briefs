# Award No. 131/11 — Good People (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 2 November 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (chair Pen Bunchhea; Ing Sothy for the employer, Liv Sovanna for the workers) · Doc ID 140085

## 1. Facts
Good People (Cambodia) Ltd, a Phnom Penh garment factory with no registered factory union at the time, faced a strike by more than 100 workers from 7 October 2011 over 12 demands on wages, bonuses, contracts and health checks. [Para 19][Para 20] Conciliation failed, the panel issued interim order 014/11 on 11 October 2011 suspending the strike and requiring all strikers back from 12 October 2011, which the workers obeyed. [Para 24][Para 25] At the hearing of 17 October 2011 the employer attended, the other unions withdrew their claims, and the claimant federation did not appear. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the case should be closed, with the claims treated as abandoned, where the claimant federation was duly summoned, failed to appear at any stage of the proceedings and gave no valid reason? [Para 40][Para 44]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The claimant federation — the Federation of Unions for Workers' Rights — did not attend the hearing; on the hearing day it merely notified the secretariat that it was busy and would not attend, giving no valid reason. [Para 29][Para 38][Para 39] The other unions present, appointed by the National Confederation for the Protection of Workers, withdrew their claims from the case. [Para 28]
**Employer (respondent):** The company attended as summoned. [Para 27] It had earlier asked the panel to intervene so that the workers would return to work, and had objected at conciliation to dealing with federations, saying it negotiated only with its own workplace representatives because no union yet existed in the factory. [Para 20][Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** case closed (បិទសំណុំរឿង) — no relief was granted on the 12 demands; the file was closed without a merits determination. [Para 63][Para 64]

The panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules, both of which allow the panel to proceed or to close with an award where a party, duly invited, fails to appear without valid reason. [Para 40][Para 41] It drew on its earlier awards in Case 16/07 (Lotus) and Case 27/07 (M&V 3) for the three conditions: valid notification, non-appearance, and absence of valid reason. [Para 42][Para 43]

Testing each condition, the panel found the claimant federation had been properly summoned by letter 732 of 12 October 2011, that it attended none of the four hearing stages (introduction, procedure, conciliation, evidence), and that it gave no valid reason, merely a same-day message that it was busy. [Para 44][Para 47][Para 50][Para 51] It held that a claimant bears the duty to present reasons and evidence before the panel, that non-attendance forfeits that opportunity, and that the conduct amounted to abandonment of the claim and showed no genuine intention to pursue the dispute through the Council. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] It accordingly closed Case No. 131/11. [Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant party has been validly notified, fails to appear at any stage of the arbitration hearing, and offers no valid reason for its absence, the panel may close the case and treat the claim as abandoned, without examining the merits. [Para 42][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that non-appearance deprives the defaulting party of the chance to give reasons and evidence in support of its claims, and that this is inconsistent with a genuine intention to have the dispute resolved by the Council. [Para 60][Para 62]
