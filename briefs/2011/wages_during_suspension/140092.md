# Award No. 146/11 — Huy Chhean (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Mean Chey Branch) (Arbitration Council, 20 December 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators · Doc ID 140092

## 1. Facts
The worker, a union activist and warehouse employee, was suspended from his job on 25 August 2011 and again on 10 September 2011 without any time limit. The employer accused him of stealing two cans of glue (10 kg) on 11 August 2011 and filed a police complaint on 24 August 2011. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer's suspension of the worker without a time limit for alleged theft is lawful? [Para 55]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The suspension is unlawful and discriminatory; the employer has no evidence, and the worker demands reinstatement and back wages from the date of suspension. [Para 104][Para 106]
**Employer (respondent):** The worker stole company glue; the employer filed a police complaint and suspended him pending the investigation. [Para 57][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** Granted — the employer must end the suspension, reinstate the worker, and pay back wages from 25 August 2011 until reinstatement. [Para 158]
The panel found the employer's evidence contradictory: the employer claimed 20 cans of glue (10 kg) while witnesses claimed 20 kg; witness accounts also differed on the distance to the warehouse (10 m vs 30 m) and the number of people present during questioning (2 vs 3). [Para 115][Para 116][Para 117][Para 120][Para 124] The panel further noted the employer failed to submit the police report. [Para 154] The suspension without a time limit violates Article 71 and Article 27 of the Labour Law because it cannot be proportional to the alleged fault. [Para 155][Para 156]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A suspension without a clear time limit is unlawful as a disciplinary measure because it cannot be proportional to the fault as required by Article 27 of the Labour Law. [Para 156]

**Obiter dicta:** None identified.

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The source contains contradictions in the quantity of glue alleged to have been stolen: the employer claimed 2 cans (10 kg) [Para 56] but later claimed 20 cans [Para 115], while witnesses stated 20 kg [Para 116]. Witnesses also gave conflicting accounts of the distance between the work area and the warehouse (10 m vs 30 m) [Para 120][Para 121] and the number of people present during questioning (2 vs 3) [Para 124].
