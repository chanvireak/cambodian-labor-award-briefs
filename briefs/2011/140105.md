# Award No. 162/11 — Cambo Handsome Ltd, Branch 1 (Arbitration Council, 27 December 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (I. Sothy, employer-appointed; A. Nan, worker-appointed; K. Phallack, chair) · binding award (unofficial English translation) · Doc ID 140105

## 1. Facts
Cambo Handsome Ltd (Branch 1), a garment factory of 2,000 workers at Chom Chao, faced a strike led by the Worker Friendship Union Federation (WFUF) from 26 to 30 November 2011, which the workers say drew 1,000 participants and the employer did not dispute. On 25 November 2011 the employer suspended three union leaders — the WFUF local president (accused of taking shirts from the factory and hiding them under his motorbike seat), its vice-president, and the president of the Cambodian Federation Labour Union local (both accused of urging work stoppages and insulting team leaders) — and on 6 December 2011 asked the Labour Inspector for authorisation to dismiss them. At the date of the award the Inspector had not decided and his one-month period had not run. Of six demands, three were settled at the hearing of 16 December 2011. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 7][Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 19][Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may order the reinstatement of the three suspended union leaders before the Labour Inspector has ruled on the employer's request for authorisation to dismiss them? [Para 26][Para 28]
2. Whether the demand that the employer withdraw its criminal complaint against the WFUF local president is a collective labour dispute within the panel's jurisdiction? [Para 33][Para 35]
3. Whether the employer must pay wages and benefits to the workers for the days of the strike? [Para 39][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The three suspensions rest on the employer's manipulation of the facts, and the theft accusation against the WFUF local president is false and denied; the strike was a response to that accusation, so the suspensions must be lifted and the three leaders reinstated, the court complaint withdrawn, and wages maintained for the striking days. They withdrew the claim for benefits once the employer had paid them. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** It has referred the three leaders to the Labour Inspector for dismissal and relies on the statutory procedure; it will not withdraw its complaint to the Phnom Penh Court of First Instance; and the striking workers are not entitled to wages whether the strike is lawful or unlawful, because a strike suspends the contract of employment. [Para 16][Para 20][Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the reinstatement demand and the demand to withdraw the criminal complaint, and rejected the demand for wages during the strike. The award is binding and enforceable, the parties having chosen binding arbitration. [Para 44][Para 45]

On issue 1 the panel set out Article 293, under which a shop steward or candidate may be dismissed only with the Labour Inspector's authorisation, the Inspector must decide within one month, and the parties may then appeal to the Minister within two months; Clause 4 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2004 extends that protection to the three union leaders. Since the employer applied on 6 December 2011 and the Inspector had neither decided nor exhausted his month, the panel held the matter still lay within the Inspector's jurisdiction and declined to act, following Awards No. 79/06 (Woo Su) and 16/11 (Cambo Handsome). [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 31]

On issue 2 the panel held that its jurisdiction under Chapter 12 is confined to collective labour disputes as defined by Article 302 — working conditions, the exercise and recognition of professional organisations' rights, and employer-worker relations — and that a demand to withdraw a criminal complaint over an alleged theft of company property fell outside that list, so the panel had no jurisdiction over it, following Awards No. 99/04, 81/06 and 83/06. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]

On issue 5 the panel applied Article 332, under which a strike suspends the labour contract so that no wage is payable for strike days, as it had already held in Awards No. 04/03 (Lida) and 63/07 (Phnom Penh Garment). Article 334 creates an exception: an employer that recruits replacements for strikers, other than for minimum service, must pay the strikers' wages for the duration of the strike. No evidence of replacement recruitment was produced, so the demand was rejected. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 43][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer has referred the dismissal of a protected union leader to the Labour Inspector and the Inspector's one-month period has not expired, the dispute remains within the Inspector's jurisdiction and the panel will not order reinstatement; a demand that an employer withdraw a criminal complaint about alleged theft of its property is not a collective labour dispute under Article 302; and strikers are entitled to no wages for strike days unless the employer has recruited replacements, in which case Article 334 obliges it to pay them for the whole strike. [Para 28][Para 31][Para 37][Para 40][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** None identified beyond the panel's remark that it would have power to order strike wages had replacement recruitment been proved. [Para 43]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
This is an unofficial English translation of a Khmer original and is corrupted throughout by a dropped "t" ("themployer", "athearing"), with some sentences losing their verbs. The strike is dated 29 November 2011 in the procedural section and 26 to 30 November 2011 in the reasons. The number of strikers is given as "more than 400" in one place and 1,000 in another. Prakas No. 305 is dated 22 November 2004 in the text though it is elsewhere cited as 2001. The witness statements in the exhibit list are individual security guards and workers whose names are not repeated here, and the three union leaders and the lawyers are reduced to roles. The exhibit list is truncated in the source at several points. [Para 3][Para 11][Para 23][Para 28]
