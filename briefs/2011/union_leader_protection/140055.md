# Award No. 92/11 — Zongtex Garment Nouveau Co., Ltd (Branch) (Arbitration Council, 19 August 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Houn Chundy, Pen Bunhea presiding) · Doc ID 140055

## 1. Facts
The Khmer Youth Union Federation, representing the Khmer Youth Union at the Zongtex Garment Nouveau (Branch) factory at Bamprek South village, Sangkat Kab, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh, filed 15 claims to improve working conditions. The employer did not attend conciliation, and the case reached the panel, which heard it on 28 July and 2 August 2011. Thirteen points settled at the hearing, one was withdrawn, leaving two disputes: daily overtime meal allowance and the reinstatement of two dismissed union leaders. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay the overtime meal allowance for every single day overtime is worked, rather than every two weeks? [Para 68][Para 104]
2. Whether the employer must reinstate Mr L, the union president, and Ms K, the union vice-president, whom it dismissed before their fixed-term contracts expired? [Para 75][Para 125]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The meal allowance must be paid daily because workers eat every day and need the money to buy food; the law fixes no payment interval, and Prakas 041 requires immediate payment to workers who volunteer for overtime. Mr L and Ms K were elected union leaders, were dismissed within the 45-day protection window, and the union had notified the employer of the election, so their dismissal without the labour inspector's approval was unlawful. [Para 69][Para 71][Para 89][Para 125]

**Employer (respondent):** It pays the meal allowance every two weeks because the factory has a new owner in Taiwan, remittance is difficult, and there is only one accountant who also handles wages and office purchases. Ms K was dismissed for poor and inaccurate sewing after a one-week notice and lawful wage payment; Mr L was dismissed for inciting workers to stop work over Ms K's dismissal without first raising it with the employer, and was also paid lawfully. It denies ever receiving notice of the union. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74][Para 78][Para 80][Para 87][Para 88][Para 91][Para 93][Para 141]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** Granted. The panel ordered the employer to pay the overtime meal allowance every day overtime is worked, and to reinstate Mr L and Ms K to their original jobs with back wages from the date of dismissal to the date of reinstatement. Both orders are rights disputes, binding immediately under the 28 September 2010 Memorandum of Understanding. [Para 150][Para 151][Para 152]

On the meal allowance, the panel read Prakas 041 (7 March 2011) as requiring either 2,000 riel a day or one free meal for workers who voluntarily work overtime at the employer's request, and held that the Prakas fixes no payment interval but the purpose of the benefit requires daily provision; prior awards ordered weekly payment, and the panel went further because the workers could not finance two weeks of meals in advance. It acknowledged the employer's staffing difficulty but told it to hire additional staff or reorganise so payment is timely. [Para 106][Para 108][Para 109][Para 112][Para 120][Para 122][Para 123][Para 124]

On reinstatement, the panel applied Article 293 of the Labour Law and article 3 of Prakas 305, which protect union leaders and candidates from dismissal without the labour inspector's prior approval. It distilled three conditions: the worker must be in a protected category, the dismissal must fall inside the protection period, and the union must have notified the employer of the candidacy by any effective means. [Para 126][Para 127][Para 131] Both workers were candidates elected on 21 May 2011 and were dismissed on 11 and 13 June 2011, within 45 days of the election, satisfying the first two conditions. [Para 133][Para 134][Para 136][Para 137] On notification, the panel accepted that the union tried to deliver the notice on 25 May 2011, that company security refused it, and that the union then sent it by post; the employer's denial did not defeat the union's genuine efforts. [Para 139][Para 140][Para 143] Because the employer dismissed them without notice to and approval from the labour inspector, the dismissal was procedurally invalid, and the panel ordered reinstatement with back pay. [Para 144][Para 145][Para 146][Para 149]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union leader or candidate who is dismissed inside the statutory protection window without the labour inspector's prior approval is entitled to reinstatement with back wages, provided the union made a genuine attempt to notify the employer of the candidacy by any effective means; an employer cannot rely on its own security staff's refusal to accept the notice. [Para 127][Para 143][Para 145]

**Obiter dicta:** The overtime meal benefit under Prakas 041 is not a monthly or fortnightly allowance but a daily entitlement tied to each day of overtime, because its purpose is to fund the meal the worker must eat that day. [Para 109][Para 121]
