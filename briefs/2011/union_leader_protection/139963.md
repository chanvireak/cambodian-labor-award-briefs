# Award No. 04/11 — Zhen Yun Cambodia Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 7 February 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Ann Vireak, Kong Phallack chairing) · Doc ID 139963

## 1. Facts
Zhen Yun Cambodia Co., Ltd. sews garments at Tuol Sangke, Phnom Penh, and reported 250 workers in the non-conciliation report while telling the panel it employed 162 [Para 17][Para 18]. A local union of FTUWKC was elected on 3 November 2010, with Mr S as President and Mr K as Vice-President, and the union notified the employer on 11 November 2010 [Para 19]. Mr K had worked in the sewing section on a verbal contract since 8 February 2010 at a last wage of US$ 66; on 25 November 2010 the employer moved him to the cutting section and on 13 December 2010 warned him for two days' unauthorised leave, then suspended him from 15 to 22 December 2010 [Para 25][Para 27]. The workers say he was turned away by security on 22 December 2010; the employer says he never returned and had abandoned his post [Para 27][Para 29].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate Mr K, the workers alleging that his termination resulted from union discrimination and the employer maintaining that it cannot reinstate him? [Para 3][Para 21]
2. Whether the employer must place a physician on standby during working hours? [Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr K's transfer to cutting was unsuitable to his sewing skills, his absence was explained, his home is near the factory, and the misconduct was not serious enough to justify termination; the administration staff who hand out medicine are too busy and sometimes absent, so a physician must be on standby for first aid [Para 34][Para 38][Para 44].
**Employer (respondent):** Mr K was moved because the sewing section was overstaffed, was lazy after transfer, was suspended so he could reconsider, and abandoned his work when he failed to appear on 22 December 2010 as the Internal Work Rules require; it did not dismiss him [Para 29][Para 31]. It offered to reinstate Mr S but not Mr K, and said it would build an infirmary without setting a timeframe [Para 21][Para 41].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer is ordered to reinstate Mr K once the award takes effect, and remains free to impose a disciplinary sanction for the unauthorised leave; it is further ordered to place a nurse and physician on standby for a minimum of two hours during each shift [Para 85][Para 86].
On issue 1 the panel first tested special protection for union leaders under Article 293 paragraph 1 of the Labour Law and Clause 3 paragraph 3 of Prakas No. 305 SKBY (22 November 2001), applying the three conditions drawn from Arbitral Award 71/09-Hytex: candidacy, termination inside the protection period, and notification to the employer by all reliable means [Para 46][Para 49]. Candidacy was established, and the panel extended the protection of an elected candidate beyond 45 days after the election while the union was applying for registration, following Arbitral Award 40/10-Meng Yan, so the dismissal of 27 December 2010 fell inside the period [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56]. The third condition failed: the employer denied receiving the 11 November 2010 notice and the workers produced neither election minutes nor a signed acknowledgement, so the protection did not attach [Para 57].
The panel then examined lawfulness. Article 27 requires any disciplinary sanction to be proportional to the misconduct, and the employer had to show that four days' unexplained absence was serious misconduct under Article 83, which it could not do because it never submitted the Internal Work Rules it relied on; the panel held that, absent such a rule, Article 84 required the employer to obtain a court determination of the magnitude of the offence, and no court decision was produced [Para 60][Para 67][Para 68][Para 70][Para 71]. The sanction was therefore neither reasonable nor proportional and the termination unreasonable, and Clause 34 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY empowered the panel to order reinstatement [Para 72][Para 74].
On issue 2 the panel applied Clause 1 of Prakas No. 330 SKBY (6 December 2000), requiring a permanent infirmary in enterprises of 50 or more employees, and the Clause 3 staffing table, which at 162 employees mandates one physician or nurse on standby for two hours per shift [Para 76][Para 78][Para 83]. It accepted that medicine for minor illness was stocked but held that a physician on standby is essential for workplace accidents and sudden illness, and ordered accordingly [Para 79][Para 81][Para 85].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer characterises repeated absence as serious misconduct justifying dismissal without prior notice, but cannot produce an internal work rule defining that conduct as serious misconduct and holds no court determination under Article 84, the sanction is disproportionate under Article 27, the termination is unreasonable, and the panel will order reinstatement while leaving the employer free to impose a lesser sanction [Para 68][Para 70][Para 72][Para 75].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded that the local union was not yet formally registered [Para 19], and it never resolved the conflict between the 250 workers in the non-conciliation report and the 162 stated at hearing, simply using 162 for the staffing table [Para 17][Para 84].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The translation is machine-degraded throughout: "the employer" appears as "themployer", "the hearing" as "athearing", "the election" as "thelection" and "his" as "his/ihis" (e.g. "hisick brother" at Para 27), so a few sentences are ambiguous. The dismissal date is printed inconsistently: the panel finds the employer dismissed Mr K on 27 December 2010 [Para 55] while the workers' account places the return to work on 22 December 2010 and Mr K later said he attended on "Friday 27 December 2010" [Para 38]. Headcount is printed both as 250 and 162 [Para 17][Para 18]. Paragraph 84 repeats a garbled fragment ("According to the facts improve their health") from the preceding line. No arbitrator name was lost.
