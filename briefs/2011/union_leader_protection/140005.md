# Award No. 23/11-June Textile — June Textile Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 9 March 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phyrum, Sin Kim Sean, Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 140005

## 1. Facts
June Textile Garment Co., Ltd. runs a garment factory on Russian Federation Boulevard, Phnom Penh. Its workers, organised in the FTUWKC local union, struck in the Cutting Section and raised five demands. Conciliation on 9 February 2011 failed on all five issues, which were referred to the Arbitration Council on 14 February 2011. The hearing was set for 25 February 2011; the worker party did not attend and had sent a letter dated 22 February 2011 refusing to attend, while the employer attended. [Para 2][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay workers' wages before holidays? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must provide an allowance of US$5 per month for transportation or house rental? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must pay hourly wages to workers in Groups 2, 5 and 8 who went on strike? [Para 4]
4. Whether the employer must retain the previous work arrangement in the Cutting Section? [Para 5]
5. Whether the employer must stop deducting union contribution fees from members' wages without consulting union leaders? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** demanded the five items and, by letter dated 22 February 2011, told the Secretariat it "would not attend the dispute resolution at the Arbitration Council's request", leaving the issues for the Council to decide. [Para 14][Para 37]
**Employer (respondent):** attended the hearing; said it would follow its existing practice on pre-holiday wages, was unable to meet the US$5 allowance, could not meet the hourly-wage demand, regarded the Cutting Section arrangement as its prerogative, and would follow the Labour Law and Prakas No. 305 on union-fee deductions. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 15]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim was treated as dropped and the panel ordered "Close case 23/11-June Textile", without ruling on the merits of the five demands. [Para 46]

Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules (Annex to Prakas No. 099, 21 April 2004) and Clause 21 of that Prakas permit the panel, where a party duly invited fails to appear without good cause, to proceed in its absence or to terminate by award. [Para 21][Para 22] Awards 16/07-Lotus and 27/07-M&V 3 read that clause as requiring three conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and no proper reason. [Para 23][Para 24]

Condition 1 was met: the Secretariat contacted the worker party and was told it would not attend, confirmed by the 22 February letter. [Para 25][Para 26] Condition 2 was met: "appear" means being present and participating in all four steps — introduction and disclosure of conflicts, explanation of the process and issues, conciliation if agreed, and arbitration; the worker party attended none and did not choose an arbitrator. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33] Condition 3 was met: the letter gave no proper reason and merely refused to attend. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 39][Para 40]

The panel noted the claimant had ample opportunity to argue its case but did not, failed its obligation to provide reasons and evidence, lost that opportunity, and should be treated as having dropped its claim. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been properly notified of an arbitration hearing, fails to appear or participate in any stage, and offers no proper reason for its non-appearance, the panel may treat the claim as dropped and close the case by award under Rule 4.7 and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 46]

**Obiter dicta:** the observations that non-appearance showed an unwillingness to resolve the dispute through the Council and that the claimant had lost the chance to present reasons and evidence are explanatory rather than necessary to the three-condition test. [Para 42][Para 45]
