# Award No. 137/11 — Reliable Apparel (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 16 November 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed Mar Sambona, worker-appointed On Non, chair Sok Mathoeung) · Doc ID 140087

## 1. Facts
The company, operating since 2005 with 2,100 employees and five unions on site, faced a six-point demand lodged by the Federation of Unions for Cambodian Workers (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ដើម្បី កម្មករ កម្ពុជា), which has 400 members at the factory and no most-representative certificate. Conciliation on 5 October 2011 settled five of the six points; the sole unsettled point was the demand that the employer play music during working hours or the midday break so workers could listen and relieve boredom and drowsiness. Both parties elected a non-binding award. [Para 1][Para 26][Para 28][Para 29][Para 10][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to play music for workers during working hours or the midday break? [Para 30][Para 42]
2. Whether the principle of res judicata bars the panel from deciding this issue again, it having been decided in Award No. 24/10? [Para 34][Para 35]
3. Whether the claimant union may bring an interest dispute without most-representative status? [Para 46][Para 47]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer should play music during working hours or at the midday break because it helps workers dispel boredom and drowsiness; the demand was pressed as a matter of right and practice. [Para 30]
**Employer (respondent):** it cannot arrange music as demanded because no law requires an employer to do so, and it asked to discuss the matter with company management first. [Para 3][Para 31]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider (បដិសេធ ពិចារណា) the demand that the employer play music during working hours or the midday break; the award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

On the threshold, the panel noted the identical issue had been decided in Award No. 24/10 of 8 April 2010 and asked whether res judicata applied. Applying the three conditions set out in Award No. 42/07 — same parties, same issue, and a prior decision on the merits — the panel found the claimant here is the Federation of Unions for Cambodian Workers, not the Khmer Youth Union which brought 24/10, so one condition failed and res judicata did not apply. [Para 34][Para 37][Para 39][Para 40]

On the merits, the panel found the demand is not provided for in any law, in past practice, or in any agreement between the workers and the employer; neither party had ever agreed on or practised the playing of music. It therefore classified the demand as an interest dispute (វិវាទ អំពី ផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 32][Para 43][Para 44]

For interest disputes the panel ordinarily examines the union's most-representative status. It found this union did not yet hold that status at the factory, and that prior awards refuse to consider an interest dispute brought by a union lacking it. Although a most-representative union enjoys legal advantages and may lawfully bring interest disputes, this union could only bring the demand jointly with other unions representing more than 50 per cent of the total workforce. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 50][Para 54]

The panel also observed that under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 an award resolving an interest dispute substitutes for a collective agreement for one year from the date it becomes final, and that a collective agreement applies to all workers in the factory, so the right to strike cannot be used to renegotiate one before it expires. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will decline to consider an interest dispute over a demand that no law, collective agreement, agreement or past practice supports, where the claimant union is not the most representative at the factory and does not bring the demand jointly with unions representing more than 50 per cent of the workforce. Res judicata does not bar a second claim on the same issue where the claimant union in the second case differs from the one that brought the first. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 54]

**Obiter dicta:** that the purpose of refusing to reconsider an identical demand is to avoid conflicting outcomes and to bring disputes to a final decision; and that an award on an interest dispute stands as a collective agreement for one year, during which the right to strike cannot be used to reopen it. [Para 38][Para 51][Para 53]
