# Award No. 11/11 — June Textile Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 28 January 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Ven Pov for the worker party, Kong Phallack chair) · Doc ID 139971

## 1. Facts
June Textile Co., Ltd operates on Russian Federation Boulevard, Teouk Thla Commune, Sen Sok District, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] The workers of Group 2 demanded that the employer increase their piece rate or, failing that, change the purchase order assigned to them; the single non-conciliated issue was referred to the panel by report No. 051 KB/RK/VK dated 17 January 2011, and the hearing was set for 20 January 2011 at 2:00 p.m. [Para 1][Para 4][Para 6] The local union of FTUWKC, the claimant, did not attend and gave no reason, having told the Secretariat by telephone that it would not appear and would leave the issue to the Council; the employer attended. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 13][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must increase the piece rate for the workers of Group 2 or change their purchase order? [Para 1]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they did not appear at any stage and offered no argument or evidence, telling the Secretariat on the day of the hearing that they would not attend and left the issue to be addressed by the Council. [Para 13][Para 26][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent):** it attended as summoned and stated that it could not provide what the workers demanded and would follow its existing practice. [Para 2][Para 8][Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel found the claim dropped and closed case 11/11-June Textile, making no order on the piece rate. [Para 34][Para 35]

The panel began from Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council's Procedural Rules, an annex to Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, and Clause 21 of the same Prakas, both of which allow the panel either to proceed in the absence of a party or to terminate the proceedings by an award where a duly invited party fails to appear without showing good cause. [Para 14] It adopted the three conditions for closing a case stated in awards 16/07-Lotus and 27/07-M & V (Branch 3): the party was properly notified, the party did not appear, and the party gave no reason for its absence. [Para 15] On the first condition, the Secretariat had contacted the workers and they had answered that they would not attend, which the panel treated as proper notification. [Para 16][Para 17] On the second, it read "appear before the arbitration panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation in the whole process, which it described as four stages — disclosure of conflicts by the arbitrators, explanation of the process and confirmation of the issues, conciliation if the parties agree, and arbitration — none of which the workers attended. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 23][Para 24] On the third, no reason was given, and the panel noted that the Secretariat had telephoned a reminder on the hearing day itself and had been told the union would not attend. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 29] Having found the three conditions satisfied and its power to award established, the panel held that a claimant must argue its claim with reasons and evidence, that the union had not done so, and that it had therefore lost the only opportunity to support its claim. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 33]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union is duly notified of the hearing but attends none of its stages and gives no reason for its absence, the panel will treat the claim as dropped and close the case under Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099, even though the respondent appeared and the merits of the demand remain unresolved; the burden of arguing a claim with reasons and evidence lies on the claimant. [Para 15][Para 23][Para 32][Para 34]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's four-stage description of the arbitral process, and its remark that the union's choice "reflects its unwillingness to bring its labour dispute to the Council", are explanatory observations rather than findings needed for the closure. [Para 18][Para 35]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The facts section is printed as "FACTS NIA" and every evidence list is "N/A", so the award contains no account of the factory's practice on piece rates beyond the employer's one-line position. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 12]
- The union's telephone number is truncated as "08833 5" and its representative is recorded only as "Absent", leaving no individual to name. [Para 1]
- The hearing date is printed twice in the same line ("20 January 2011 at 2:00 p.m" then "Date of hearing: [20 January 2011 at 2:00 p.m"). [Para 4]
- The union is abbreviated FTUWKC at the head of the award and FTUKWC in the Secretariat's letter reference. [Para 1][Para 12]
- The non-conciliation report reference appears as "051 KB/RK/VK" and as "051 KBIVK", and the Ministry is consistently misspelt "Traing" for Training. [Para 4][Para 12]
- The OCR drops "e" after "th" throughout ("themployer", "thearing", "outling"), so several sentences cannot be quoted verbatim. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 5]
