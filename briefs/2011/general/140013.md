# Award No. 32/11 — Pou Yuen (Cambodia) Enterprise Ltd v Rights and Profit Workers Federation of Trade Unions Local Union (Arbitration Council, 25 March 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Ven Pov, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140013

## 1. Facts
Pou Yuen (Cambodia) Enterprise Ltd faced a seven-point demand from the Local Union of RPWFTU; six issues were conciliated on 25 January 2011 and only the demand for one paid day off each month for pregnant workers to attend health checks remained. The non-conciliation report was forwarded on 4 March 2011 and the hearing set for 16 March 2011. The employer attended; the worker party did not, and gave no reason beyond prior arrangements. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer should allow pregnant workers one day off each month with wages and benefits maintained to have their health checked? [Para 1]
2. Whether the case should be closed because the worker party failed to appear at the hearing without proper reason? [Para 11]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union demanded a full paid day off each month for pregnant workers to attend health checks. It did not appear at the hearing and left the issue to be addressed by the Council, stating only that the workers had prior arrangements. [Para 1][Para 21]
**Employer (respondent):** The employer offered only half a day off each month with wages and benefits maintained, and attended the hearing as invited. [Para 1][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 32/11-Pou Yuen without deciding the merits. [Para 31]

The panel applied Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099, which allow it to proceed in a party's absence or to terminate by award. Following Awards 16/07-Lotus and 27/07-M&V 3, closure requires three conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and no proper reason. [Para 11][Para 12]

On the first, the Secretariat sent the workers notice No. 195 KB/AK/VK/LKA and the panel found notification proper. On the second, it read "appear" as requiring presence and participation across all four stages — introduction and conflict disclosure, explanation of process and issues, conciliation, and arbitration — and the workers attended none. On the third, the workers offered only that they had prior arrangements and decided to leave the issue to the Council, which the panel did not accept as good cause. All three conditions were therefore met. [Para 13][Para 19][Para 21][Para 24]

The panel added that the union had ample opportunity to argue its claim and present evidence, and that a claimant is obliged to do so. Having missed the hearing, it had lost that opportunity, and the panel treated the claim as dropped and the union as unwilling to pursue the dispute through the legal procedure. It closed the case. [Para 25][Para 27][Para 29][Para 31]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a duly notified claimant neither appears nor participates in any stage of the proceedings and gives no proper reason, the panel may treat the claim as dropped and close the case by award, leaving the merits undecided. [Para 11][Para 24][Para 29]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the worker party's conduct showed an unwillingness to bring the dispute to the Council for resolution. [Para 30]
