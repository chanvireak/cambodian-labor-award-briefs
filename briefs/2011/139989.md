# Arbitral Award No. 16/11 — Cambo Handsome Ltd (Branch 1) (Arbitration Council, 11 February 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 139989 · Binding award

## 1. Facts
Cambo Handsome Ltd (Branch 1), a garment factory at Prey Tea Village, Chom Chao Commune, Dangkor District, Phnom Penh, employs about 2,000 workers and has three unions: CWFDFU (324 members' dues), WFUF (43 members) and FTUWKC, which holds most-representative status. CWFDFU and WFUF jointly filed 12 demands. Conciliation failed on 20 January 2011; at the hearings six issues were settled and issue 4 withdrawn, leaving issues 1, 2, 3, 6 and 12. [Para 1][Para 9][Para 14][Para 15][Para 11][Para 12]

The trigger events were two dismissals and a strike. Mechanic Nhget Morn (employed from 14 August 2009 on an indefinite-term contract) was dismissed on 7 January 2011 over an altercation with cutting-section worker Pum Muon. Union activist Yi Davy (employed from 2 September 2009, indefinite-term) was dismissed on 12 January 2011 for allegedly inciting a work stoppage. On 12, 13 and 14 January 2011 about 400 workers struck for three days. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 33][Para 39][Para 54][Para 119]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate Nhget Morn and Yi Davy and back-pay their wages from dismissal to reinstatement? [Para 16]
2. Whether the employer must reinstate the five suspended union leaders — Ly Bunnarith, Suong Won, Plork Bunly, Oeun Sarath and Thok Senghun? [Para 48]
3. Whether the employer must pay wages and attendance bonuses to workers for the three-day strike of 12–14 January 2011? [Para 54]
4. Whether the employer must raise the overtime meal allowance from 1,500 to 2,000 riel for the 6:00–8:00/8:30 p.m. shift? [Para 58]
5. Whether the employer must dismiss administration staff Chan Davuth and Bun Borin? [Para 66]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (CWFDFU and WFUF):** Nhget Morn did not commit serious misconduct — he took out his phone to check the time and Pum Muon attacked him, and it was unfair to dismiss only him. Yi Davy merely told co-workers that Nhget Morn had been dismissed and did not call a stoppage; in any event participation in a strike is not serious misconduct. The five union leaders were suspended for union activity and must be reinstated. The strike was caused by the employer's own conduct, so wages and attendance bonuses should be maintained. The meal allowance should reach 2,000 riel because the law limits overtime to two hours and prices have risen. Chan Davuth and Bun Borin oppress workers and discriminate against the union. [Para 17][Para 21][Para 22][Para 34][Para 54][Para 55][Para 60][Para 61][Para 66][Para 67]

**Employer:** Nhget Morn photographed Pum Muon and then grabbed his collar and stabbed him with a screwdriver; witnesses who saw the incident from the start confirm this, and Pum Muon only received a warning because he was not the instigator. Yi Davy shouted for workers to come out and about 100 stopped work for 4–10 minutes, which is incitement to serious misconduct. The five union leaders were suspended and the Labour Inspector's authorisation to dismiss them was requested on 12 January 2011. The strike was illegal and no wages or bonuses are owed. The 1,500-riel allowance already exceeds the law and any increase is an interests dispute the claimants lack standing to bring. Chan Davuth and Bun Borin have never threatened workers. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 27][Para 28][Para 35][Para 38][Para 39][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 58][Para 62][Para 68][Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** the panel rejected reinstatement for Nhget Morn; ordered reinstatement of Yi Davy with back pay from dismissal to reinstatement; rejected reinstatement of the five union leaders; rejected wages and attendance bonuses for the strike; rejected the 2,000-riel meal allowance; and rejected the demand to dismiss Chan Davuth and Bun Borin. Binding award. [Para 158][Para 159][Para 160][Para 161]

**Nhget Morn:** the panel found the employer's account of the photograph credible. Two employer witnesses who saw the incident from the beginning said Nhget Morn photographed Pum Muon; Nhget Morn himself admitted taking out his phone; the worker-side witnesses only saw the fight and not its origin. The panel held the altercation began with Nhget Morn's photograph, making him the instigator, so dismissing him while warning Pum Muon was reasonable. This was serious misconduct under Article 83 and Article 84(B)(4) (assault on other workers). [Para 85][Para 86][Para 88][Para 91][Para 92][Para 95]

**Yi Davy:** the panel accepted that he called for workers to stop working, but held that a work stoppage meeting the Article 318 definition of a strike is not serious misconduct, following Awards 76/04-M & V, 70/04-Hanna and 125/09-Wincam. Article 83(B)(5) does not cover incitement to strike unless the employer proves incitement to violence during the strike, which it did not. The dismissal was therefore incorrect and reinstatement with back pay was ordered. [Para 107][Para 109][Para 110][Para 111][Para 112][Para 113]

**Five union leaders:** as union leaders they enjoy the Article 293 protection extended by Clause 4 of Prakas 305 SKBY (22 November 2001), so dismissal requires Labour Inspector authorisation. The employer had requested authorisation but the Inspector had not decided. Following Award 79/06-Woosu, the panel held the matter still lay with the Inspector and declined jurisdiction. [Para 115][Para 116][Para 118][Para 119]

**Strike wages:** Articles 332 and 72(1) suspend the contract and the pay obligation during a strike regardless of legality. The strike was in any event illegal — no seven-day notice under Article 324 and no exhaustion of peaceful settlement under Article 320(4) — and workers in illegal strikes forfeit wages and attendance bonuses under Notification 017 SKBY. [Para 120][Para 121][Para 122][Para 123][Para 126][Para 129][Para 131][Para 132]

**Meal allowance:** Notification 017 SKBY point 4 requires only 1,000 riel per overtime day; the employer pays 1,500. A demand above the legal minimum is an interests dispute, and neither claimant union holds most-representative status (FTUWKC does), so neither has standing. [Para 135][Para 141][Para 143][Para 145][Para 146][Para 147]

**Dismissal of staff:** only the parties to a contract may terminate it; workers cannot demand another worker's dismissal absent proof of danger to workplace safety. No such evidence was produced. [Para 148][Para 149][Para 150][Para 152][Para 157][Para 158]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Calling workers out on strike, without more, is not "inciting other workers to commit serious offences" under Article 83(B)(5) of the Labour Law, because participation in a strike is not serious misconduct; the employer must prove incitement to violence. [Para 109][Para 111]

Union leaders may be dismissed only with Labour Inspector authorisation under Article 293; until the Inspector decides, the Arbitration Council has no jurisdiction over their reinstatement. [Para 115][Para 119]

**Obiter dicta:** workers participating in an illegal strike are entitled to neither wages nor attendance bonuses [Para 129][Para 131]; and a union without most-representative status lacks standing to bring an interests dispute on behalf of workers [Para 145][Para 147].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The text is an unofficial English translation of the Khmer original and is heavily OCR-mangled throughout: "the employer" is routinely printed as "themployer", "the" as "the"/"he", "determined" as "determined", "meal" as "me", "strike" as "gon strike". The evidence section was omitted in the English version (Para 13). The conciliation date is given as 17 January 2010 at Para 9 though the report is dated 20 January 2011. The name alternates between "Pum Muon" and "Pum Morn", "Nhget Morn" and "Nghet Morn", and "Chan Davuth" and "Chan David". Para 32 lists a witness "Kimly, ID5 2145" whose ID is garbled. Para 60 says workers on the first shift get 1,500 riel and the second shift "an additional 1,500", which conflicts with the 2,000 total described elsewhere. [Para 9][Para 13][Para 24][Para 32][Para 60]
