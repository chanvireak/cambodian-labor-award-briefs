# Award No. 68/11 — B & N Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 June 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, Liv Sovanna, Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140037

## 1. Facts
B & N Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates a garment factory at Toul Sangke, Russei Keo, Phnom Penh; its Head of Administration is Mr M Leng [Para 1]. On 19 May 2011 the Department of Labour Disputes received a complaint from the Rights and Profit Workers Federation of Trade Unions setting out seven demands to improve working conditions [Para 4]. An expert officer conciliated at the factory on 7 June 2011 and none of the issues was resolved; the seven non-conciliated issues went to the Secretariat on 10 June 2011 under non-conciliation report No. 614 KB/RK/VK dated 9 June 2011 [Para 4][Para 6][Para 7]. The hearing was set for 23 June 2011 at 8:30 a.m.; the workers' representatives did not attend and the employer did [Para 8][Para 9]. The source's "Facts" section records "NIA" and no evidence was produced by the worker party [Para 12][Para 14].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide chairs and tables and set up a canteen? [Para 1]
2. Whether the employer must install a proper door for the restroom? [Para 1]
3. Whether the employer must implement a system to reduce heat in the Cutting Section and other heat-affected sections? [Para 1]
4. Whether the employer must block sunlight in the infirmary? [Para 1]
5. Whether the employer must pay an additional US$ 10 to each scissor chief and US$ 5 to each scissor apprentice? [Para 1]
6. Whether the employer must pay 50% of wages and perquisites to women workers on maternity leave? [Para 1]
7. Whether the employer must establish a proper and hygienic place to drink water? [Para 1]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The seven demands in the non-conciliation report were the workers' claim; the worker party's representative was recorded as absent and the workers filed no evidence or written argument [Para 1][Para 12].
**Employer (respondent):** The employer attended through Mr M Leng under an authorisation letter dated 23 June 2011 and asked the Council to proceed; it produced no substantive position on the seven demands in the award [Para 9][Para 11].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** The panel closed case 68/11-B & N Garment without ruling on any of the seven demands [Para 35].

The panel treated the case as one of non-appearance. Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council's Procedural Rules and Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 permit the panel, where a party duly invited fails to appear without good cause, to proceed in its absence or to terminate the proceedings by award [Para 16][Para 17]. Following awards 16/07-Lotus and 27/07-M & V 3, the panel applied three conditions: proper notification, non-appearance, and no proper reason [Para 17][Para 18]. The workers had been notified by notice No. 390 KB/AK/VK/LKA dated 14 June 2011, so the first condition was met [Para 18][Para 19]. The panel read "appear before the arbitration panel" to require presence and participation in all four stages — introduction and conflict disclosure, explanation of process and confirmation of issues, conciliation, and arbitration — and the workers attended none of them [Para 20][Para 25][Para 26]. The workers gave no reason at all for their absence and left the matter to the Council, satisfying the third condition [Para 27][Para 29][Para 30]. The panel found the worker party had dropped its claim and did not intend to engage in dispute resolution, and closed the case [Para 34][Para 35].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant has been properly notified, fails to appear at any stage of the hearing, and gives no proper reason for its absence, the panel may treat the claim as abandoned and close the case by award without deciding the merits. [Para 16][Para 20][Para 27][Para 35]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the claimant had been given ample opportunity to argue its claim and had lost the opportunity to submit reasons and evidence. [Para 31][Para 33]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The "Facts" section is printed as "NIA" with no narrative, and the worker party's representative is recorded as "Absent" [Para 1][Para 14]. "Ministry of Labour and Vocational Traing" recurs throughout, and "Fax: NIA" is used for N/A [Para 1][Para 13]. The hearing date is printed twice in the same sentence, "23 June 2011 at 8:30 a.m. Date of hearing: _23 June 2011 at 8:30 a.m." [Para 4]. The complaint is attributed to the Rights and Profit Workers Federation of Trade Unions, but that federation is not named on the face as the disputant party, whose representative is listed only as "Workers' representatives" [Para 1][Para 4].
