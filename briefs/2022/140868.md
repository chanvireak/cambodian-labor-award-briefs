# Order No. 023/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យី ដា មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរើ ខូ អិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 30 May 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; Tuon Si Phan, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 140868

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន យី ដា មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរើ ខូ អិលធីឌី manufactures garments at Chheu Teal commune, Kien Svay district, Kandal, and employed 3,975 workers [Para 52]. Its workers' representatives filed a complaint on 28 February 2022 raising nine demands on working conditions [Para 17]; conciliation on 20 April 2022 settled five, and the four remaining points were referred to the Council on 27 April 2022 under conciliation report No. 278/22 [Para 18][Para 19]. The instrument is an order under Article 313 of the Labour Law [Para 1]. At the hearing of 6 May 2022 the parties settled issue 2 by agreement, leaving issues 1, 3 and 4 for decision [Para 25][Para 26]. The disputed acts were the employer's refusal to dismiss or transfer a section chief and a relative of his whom workers accused of insulting them [Para 55], its deduction of the loyalty allowance (ប្រាក់ប៉ូវ) from assistants who took sick or personal leave [Para 77], and its refusal to grant a full day of sick leave where the medical certificate covered less [Para 94].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the workers may require the employer to dismiss or transfer a section chief and another worker for grave misconduct under Article 83 of the Labour Law, where those two had abused workers in groups 306, 308 and 309? [Para 55][Para 116]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer is obliged to maintain the loyalty allowance for assistants when they take sick leave or personal leave, rather than deducting it by percentage? [Para 77][Para 143]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must grant one full day of sick leave whenever a worker attends hospital for treatment, even where the medical certificate covers less than a day? [Para 94][Para 163]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត:** the two supervisors had repeatedly insulted and humiliated workers during working hours, with several written warnings already on file, so Article 83 grave misconduct applied and the employer should remove them [Para 59][Para 61]. On the allowance, assistants cover the whole workload of absent colleagues and are hard to replace, so their loyalty allowance should be preserved [Para 84][Para 85]; and the employer should allow a full sick-leave day on a hospital visit, as it once did [Para 95][Para 97].

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន យី ដា មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរើ ខូ អិលធីឌី:** it had already met, minuted and warned both supervisors in writing, and had transferred one of them to other groups, since it cannot dismiss at the workers' request [Para 3][Para 4][Para 57]. The loyalty allowance is a discretionary incentive of USD 30 to USD 50 by section, set outside the internal rules, and the company applies it as before [Para 78][Para 90][Para 146]. Sick leave follows the hours certified by a Social Security Fund clinic or hospital, which is already more generous than the law [Para 102][Para 103].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three remaining claims were rejected: the demand to dismiss the section chief and the other worker [Para 184], the demand to preserve the loyalty allowance for assistants on leave [Para 185], and the demand for a full day of sick leave on a shorter certificate [Para 186].

On Issue 1, the panel held that only the parties to a labour contract may dissolve the obligations it creates. Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 311 of the Civil Code were applied to the effect that a third party has no right to terminate an employment contract [Para 116][Para 118][Para 120][Para 122]. Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to manage the enterprise, provided the right is exercised lawfully and reasonably [Para 123][Para 126]; the panel followed Awards 116/10 and 83/12 [Para 123], Awards 62/06, 108/06, 33/07, 119/09, 156/13 and 201/14 [Para 127], and Awards 04/03, 14/03, 17-18/03, 15/04, 16/04 and 50/07 [Para 128], holding that the Council may order a transfer or dismissal only where a worker is a danger to the health or safety of others, for instance through violence [Para 129]. Insult and abuse of fellow workers is grave misconduct under Article 83(b)(4) and article 10 of the internal rules [Para 133][Para 134], but the employer had already transferred, minuted and warned, and the panel declined to order dismissal while noting the employer should act under Article 83 if the conduct recurred [Para 138][Para 141].

On Issue 3, the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law to hold that an employer may unilaterally design incentive payments beyond those fixed by law [Para 144], following Awards 24/05, 03/13 and 029/15 [Para 145]. Neither party produced the policy governing the allowance [Para 147], and the deduction ran at 60% for leave under four days and 80% above that [Para 158]. Since the claimant bears the burden of proof [Para 150][Para 151], the payslips did not identify who was an assistant [Para 154][Para 155], and no evidence showed the arrangement was unlawful or unreasonable [Para 160], no obligation to preserve the allowance was found [Para 161].

On Issue 4, article 3 of Article 71 of the Labour Law suspends the contract for certified illness for up to six months, and Article 72(1) relieves the employer of the wage obligation during suspension absent a contrary rule [Para 163][Para 164][Para 170][Para 171]; the panel followed Award 171/13 [Para 167][Para 173] and Awards 016/15 and 204/16 [Para 176]. Because paying wages for the certified visiting hours already exceeds the statutory minimum [Para 180], a demand for a whole day on a shorter certificate was a claim better than the law and without legal basis [Para 181].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A workers' collective may not obtain an order requiring the employer to dismiss or transfer a supervisor or fellow worker: discipline and workplace organisation are the employer's managerial prerogative under Article 2 of the Labour Law, and the Council will intervene only where the person's conduct creates a danger to the health or safety of other workers [Para 126][Para 129][Para 132].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel added that some illnesses leave a worker needing rest at home after a consultation, so the employer should also weigh the actual symptoms when granting sick leave, even though the claim itself was refused [Para 182].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR losses are moderate but consequential. Paragraph 20 dates the panel's constitution "29 April 2019 2022", and paragraph 165 dates the internal rules "1 July 2019 2015", both impossible readings [Para 20][Para 165]. Paragraph 153 says the workers filed payslips for 1,267 workers while paragraph 92 says ten, so the figure is unreliable [Para 92][Para 153]. The company name is printed in several spellings, including forms ending ខូ អិលធីឌី, ខូ អិល ធី ឌី and លីមីត, and one address line is garbled. Several workers and the section chief appear under full or partly recovered names in the Khmer text; they are reduced to initials or roles here. The order is expressly non-binding and becomes final only if no written objection reaches the Minister within eight days of notification [Para 187].
