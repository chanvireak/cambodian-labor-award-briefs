# Award No. 011/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក (Jin Bei Pha Les Ek Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 26 April 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី for the employer, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ for the workers, chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 140861

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក runs a hotel, restaurant and casino at Sangkat No. 4, Preah Sihanouk City, and employed 4,534 workers at the time of the dispute. [Para 85] [Para 1] On 31 January 2022 the workers filed 14 demands for improved working conditions with the Preah Sihanouk provincial labour department; conciliation on 28 February 2022 left 10 of the 14 unresolved, and those 10 were forwarded to the panel on 10 March 2022. [Para 19] [Para 20] [Para 21] The unresolved demands covered 12-hour shifts against a claimed 8-hour day, a workplace nursery or nursery allowance, election of staff representatives, counting of tips, contractual wage increases, pay for public holidays and other leave, meetings with the local union, and application of the Labour Law. [Para 2] [Para 4] [Para 5] [Para 6] [Para 7] [Para 9] [Para 10] [Para 11] [Para 12] [Para 13] At the opening of the hearing the employer asked the panel to terminate the case on the ground that the workers had sued the wrong company. [Para 94]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Jurisdiction) Whether the panel may consider the ten unresolved demands against ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក when the non-conciliation record forwarded to it names ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី គ្រុប ឯ.ក as the employer? [Para 94] [Para 157]
2. (Identity of the employer) Whether the two companies are in truth a single employer, so that a demand pressed against the group company may be heard against the company named in the record? [Para 91] [Para 154]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that their original complaint to the provincial labour department was always against ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី គ្រុប ឯ.ក (ជីន ប៉ី កួយ ជូ), and still is; that they were moved to ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក without being told, that their old contracts were never terminated and no termination pay was paid, and that the new contracts, drafted in English and incompletely translated, were signed under pressure and should be set aside. [Para 100] [Para 105] [Para 108] [Para 116] [Para 134]
**Employer (respondent):** that the workers work under valid contracts with ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក, that they have no dispute with it, that the company is not a party to this dispute, and that of those attending the second hearing only four work for it at all; it therefore asked the panel to terminate the case. [Para 94] [Para 135] [Para 136] [Para 137] [Para 139]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to consider case No. 011/22 against ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក, issuing a non-binding award that becomes final if no party objects within 08 days. [Para 158] [Para 159]
Article 312 of the 1997 Labour Law confines the panel to the issues stated in the non-conciliation record, or to later issues that are a direct consequence of the dispute, and Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, Article 33, repeats that limit. [Para 144] [Para 145] Article 34 of the same Prakas lets the panel award full compensation for breaches of the Labour Law, but the panel read "party to the dispute" there as the worker and employer named in the record sent to it. [Para 146] [Para 149] [Para 150] The record forwarded, report No. 102/22, was between the workers and ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក. [Para 151] [Para 152]
On the identity question the panel found that the two companies are distinct: ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក holds commercial-register certificate No. 00046064 of 16 August 2019, while ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី គ្រុប ឯ.ក holds certificate No. 00023956 of 22 March 2017. [Para 154] It accepted that they share one location, but held they are not one company because each has its own licences, statutes, registration certificates and workforce. [Para 97] The panel then found that the workers have no dispute with the employer of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី ផា លេស ឯ.ក in this case, and that their demand runs against the employer of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីន ប៉ី គ្រុប ឯ.ក (ជីន ប៉ី កួយ ជូ), which is not a party in the record. [Para 153] [Para 155] [Para 156] Applying Article 312 and Articles 33 and 34 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY, it held it had no jurisdiction to resolve a dispute between workers and an employer who is not a party, and refused to consider the case. [Para 157] [Para 158]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel's jurisdiction is fixed by the employer named in the non-conciliation record that was forwarded to it: where the workers press their demands against a differently registered company — even one trading from the same premises with the same management and a shared workforce — that company is a non-party, and the panel must decline to consider the case rather than pierce the registration to reach the earlier employer. [Para 154] [Para 156] [Para 157]

**Obiter dicta:** that the employer chose to raise the objection at the very opening of the hearing rather than in its written defence, and that the panel noted the point without treating the timing as a waiver. [Para 94]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The conciliation report is dated 2 March 2022 where first cited and again in the panel's narrative, but 24 March 2022 at the paragraph that identifies it as the record of the non-conciliation, and 2 March 2021 at another. [Para 16] [Para 21] [Para 151] [Para 78] The paragraph recording the panel's constitution repeats four unrelated years in succession, so the date of constitution cannot be read from it. [Para 22] The commercial-register number for the same 16 August 2019 certificate is printed as MOC-62578402 in the employer's document list and as 00046064 in the findings. [Para 35] [Para 88] The respondent's name is printed in at least four different Khmer forms across the award, including one in the operative paragraph that inserts a stray letter before the company name. [Para 110] [Para 158] [Para 159] The shift description in the first demand reads "8 hours normal, 20 hours overtime or a further 2 hours of rest" within a 12-hour shift, so one figure has been lost or corrupted. [Para 2] The employer's HR manager is printed under two different Latin spellings in the transfer letters, and individual workers, union officers and representatives are reduced to initials or roles by the briefing convention. [Para 31] [Para 32] [Para 137]
