# Order No. 021/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាន់ សឺខល ហ្គា មេន ឯ.ក (Sun Senkhle Garmen Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 24 May 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Vireak, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 140867

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាន់ សឺខល ហ្គា មេន ឯ.ក (Sun Senkhle Garmen Co., Ltd) cuts and sews garments at Ta Du village, Roka Krau commune, Daun Keo city, Takeo, and employed 938 workers [Para 56]. On 19 March 2022 twenty of its workers filed a complaint raising 21 demands on working conditions [Para 19]; conciliation settled 15 of them, and the 5 unresolved points reached the Council on 19 April 2022 under conciliation report No. 258/22 [Para 21][Para 22]. The instrument was issued as an order under Article 313 of the Labour Law [Para 1]. At the hearing of 29 April 2022 the parties agreed issues 1, 2 and 3 (shelter for motorcycles and for meals, fingerprint scanners, weekly meal allowance) and the workers withdrew issue 4 (full pay where no work is available), leaving only issue 5 [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]. On 3 and 4 January 2022 the company had no work and rested the sewing-group workers by charging those days against their paid annual leave [Para 61][Para 64].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 5) Whether the employer's resting of workers on 3 and 4 January 2022 by deducting those days from their paid annual leave, at a time when it had no work for them, was lawful under Article 170 of the Labour Law, so that no reimbursement of the deducted leave is owed? [Para 60][Para 109]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត:** the claimant side said the workers did not understand how their annual leave was being used and had nobody to ask, so they simply followed the employer's instruction [Para 68], and that the notice could not bind them because section and group heads had no authority to agree on the workers' behalf [Para 76]. They also maintained that wages were owed when the employer gave them no work [Para 74].

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាន់ សឺខល ហ្គា មេន ឯ.ក:** COVID-19 disrupted materials and the company had been operating only two months, so it consulted its section heads, agreed a short-term arrangement resting workers on paid annual leave, and met the workers to explain it without protest [Para 79][Para 80][Para 81]. It conceded that no individual worker had thumbprinted consent [Para 82].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim that the employer repay the paid annual leave deducted for 3 and 4 January 2022 was rejected [Para 129].

The panel first fixed who was claiming. Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 K.B/Pr.K of 2 July 2015 provides for settlement through worker representatives where an enterprise has no most representative union, and article 1 of Prakas No. 362 K.B/Pr.K of 29 August 2016 delegates recognition of those representatives to the Labour Disputes Department [Para 91][Para 93]; following Awards 223/16, 224/16, 237/16 and 008/17, and Awards 161/09, 43/10, 144/12 and 120/15 on authorised representation [Para 97][Para 103], the panel held that the four representatives recognised by letter No. 04/22 of 29 March 2022 had standing [Para 98][Para 100]. On the claimant list, only 27 of the 38 workers who had thumbprinted the original complaint were actually filed, 16 of those were in sewing, and only one attended and confirmed she worked in sewing group 4, so the panel decided the issue for that sole claimant [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107][Para 108].

On the merits, Article 170 of the Labour Law requires annual leave to be taken at Khmer New Year unless the employer and worker agree otherwise, on prior notice to the labour inspector, and lets the employer fix the remaining days beyond fifteen at another time [Para 109][Para 110][Para 111]; the panel followed Awards 21/05, 81/07 and 123/09 [Para 114]. The employer produced its notice of 27 December scheduling the SR4 group's rest on 3 and 4 January 2022 [Para 88], and a table of 31 workers with names, staff numbers and signatures, including the claimant's signature agreeing to the deduction [Para 121][Para 122]. The workers filed no evidence in support of their account [Para 118], the panel found no coercion of the claimant [Para 125][Para 126], and it concluded that the arrangement conformed to Article 170 [Para 127].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may schedule paid annual leave outside the Khmer New Year where the individual worker, or a representative the worker has authorised, agrees to the specific days and the agreement is recorded in writing, the labour inspector having been notified in advance; a worker's later assertion that she did not understand the arrangement does not undo a consent she signed [Para 123][Para 127].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's reading of article 16 of Prakas No. 303 on enterprises without a most representative union was not needed to decide this case, a union being registered at the company [Para 91][Para 46].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR losses are heavy throughout the Khmer text. Paragraph 60 states the disputed dates as "3 and 19 January 2022" while the reasons and the operative order use 3 and 4 January [Para 60][Para 129]. Paragraph 23 gives the panel's establishment date as "21 April 2019 2022", and the operative paragraph prints the year as "20 2022" [Para 23][Para 129]. The employer's notice is dated 27 December 2022 in the evidence list although it schedules days in January 2022 and the reasons treat it as 2021 [Para 88]. Worker, representative and union-official names survive only as bare initials or garbled fragments (one appears as a single syllable followed by a bracket), so no individual is named here. The company's own name is printed in at least three variant spellings. The instrument is styled a សេចក្តីបង្គាប់ (order) under Article 313, expressly non-binding, and becomes final only if no written objection is filed with the Minister within eight days of notification [Para 130].
