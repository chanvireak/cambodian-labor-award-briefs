# Award No. 039/22 — Yak Jin (Cambodia) Inc (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ីន) (Arbitration Council, 5 September 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ouk Ry (employer-selected), An Non (worker-selected), Pen Bun Chea (president) · Doc ID 140872

## 1. Facts
The respondent is ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ីន, a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise employing 2,376 workers, of whom 1,793 are women [Para 75]. The workers' side (ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត) filed seven demands on 2 July 2022; conciliation on 20 July 2022 settled two, and five were referred to the Council [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19]. The employer had exchanged working days, giving workers rest on 12 and 13 April 2022, ordinary working days, in return for work on 14 and 19 May 2022, paid public holidays, and paid only the normal day's wage for that holiday work [Para 97][Para 98]. The employer also uses three-month fixed-duration contracts renewed without any non-renewal notice [Para 82].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay compensation in lieu of advance notice under Article 73 of the Labour Law to nine workers whose fixed-duration contracts were not renewed, and give such notice in future? [Para 79][Para 252]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay an additional two times the normal day's wage to the 19 workers who worked on the paid public holidays of 14 and 19 May 2022? [Para 173]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must grant seven days of special leave each year without deducting them from annual leave? [Para 201]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must write and post a public apology to the two unions for its notice about their refusal to sign? [Para 224]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must pay the A/B bonus to the nine sample-sewing workers as it does to sewing workers? [Para 238]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** three-month contracts renewed without a break for 15 to 18 months mean continuous service, so the Article 73 notice duty is owed and holiday work should attract 1 plus 2 wages [Para 85][Para 86]. The employer's notice caused members to insult the unions and membership fell from 200 to about 20 or 30 [Para 129][Para 130]. Sample-sewing workers are skilled and were selected, so they deserve the bonus [Para 147].

**Employer (respondent):** three-month fixed-duration contracts carry no notice duty under Article 73 [Para 90]. The day exchange followed a free vote in which 68 percent agreed and was announced to all workers [Para 108]. No rule requires an apology [Para 136]. Sample-sewing workers already receive a higher base wage plus function and technical allowances of about 40 to 50 USD each [Para 149].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay an extra two times the normal day's wage to the workers who worked on 14 and 19 May 2022; issues 1, 3, 4 and 5 were refused [Para 254][Para 252][Para 255][Para 256].

Issue 1 was refused on two grounds. Under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 a representative needs written authorisation, and the nine workers never thumbprinted a delegation nor furnished identity documents, so there was no identifiable claimant [Para 153][Para 158][Para 160]. Independently, Article 73(5) of the Labour Law applies only to fixed-duration contracts longer than six months, so three-month contracts attract no notice, and notice is measured by each contract's own term rather than accumulated service [Para 162][Para 165][Para 166][Para 170].

Issue 2 was granted. Article 2 of Prakas No. 277/21 allows paid-holiday rest days to be moved only by agreement of the individual worker, or under internal rules or a collective agreement [Para 183][Para 185]. Many workers did not agree, so the exchange was improper and the work counts as holiday work [Para 187]. Article 164 of the Labour Law and Article 4 of Prakas No. 10 give an extra payment equal to one normal day's wage [Para 188][Para 189][Para 190]. Because the employer's own practice of 1 plus 2 is more favourable, and Article 13(2) preserves more favourable terms, the panel ordered two times the normal day's wage and limited the order to the 19 claimants [Para 193][Para 195][Para 197][Para 177].

Issue 3 was declined. Seven un-deducted special-leave days are a benefit above the law, and no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice grants it, so the demand is an interest dispute [Para 203][Para 204][Para 205]. Article 96(2) of the Labour Law defines a collective agreement [Para 209], and Article 54 (new) of the Law on Trade Unions reserves the exclusive right to the most representative union [Para 211]. Under Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement for one year and removes the right to strike on that subject [Para 216][Para 217]. No union at the factory was most representative, so the panel declined to decide [Para 78][Para 222][Para 223].

Issue 4 was dismissed: Article 63 of the Law on Trade Unions lists the employer practices treated as unlawful, posting the notice fell outside that list, and the notice stated the facts truthfully [Para 225][Para 231][Para 233][Para 236]. Issue 5 was dismissed: Article 2 of the Labour Law gives the employer the right to manage, exercised lawfully and reasonably [Para 241][Para 246]; bonuses beyond those fixed by law are at the employer's discretion [Para 243]; no evidence showed the omission was unlawful or harmed any worker's rights, and sample-sewing workers already earned more [Para 247][Para 248][Para 251].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that moves paid public holidays onto ordinary working days without the individual worker's agreement breaches Article 2 of Prakas No. 277/21, and the panel will order the extra holiday-work payment at the rate the employer itself applies to other holiday work [Para 185][Para 187][Para 199]. Where a demand seeks a benefit above the law with no basis in any instrument or practice, it is an interest dispute that the panel will decline to decide unless the claimant union is the most representative, because its award would displace a collective agreement for a year and extinguish the right to strike under Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 [Para 205][Para 217][Para 222].

**Obiter dicta:** workers should also be entitled to be told the reasons for any change to their wages [Para 235]; and Article 73(5) notice runs from each contract's own term, not from total service [Para 166].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Paragraphs 14 and 20 print two possible years for the referral and the panel's constitution, giving both ២០១៩ and ២០២២ [Para 20]; the referral report number is legible as ៤៩៩ in paragraph 19 but garbled in paragraph 14 [Para 19]. The company's name appears in several variant Khmer spellings (យ៉ាក ជីន, យ៉ាក់ ជីន, យ៉ាក ជ័ន) across the header, evidence list and findings. The award prints a union president's full name in paragraphs 138 to 139 where the rest of the document uses initials, so this brief identifies that person by role only. Several delegate lists are reduced to bracketed initials with a missing sixth name [Para 109].
