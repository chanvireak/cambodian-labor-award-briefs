# Award No. 006/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តាឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល ម៉េនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង ខូអិលធីឌី (Stally Thai Apparel Menu Food Cheryng Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 29 March 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, employer-appointed; អន ណន, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លៈ, chair) · Doc ID 140859

## 1. Facts
The respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្តាឡៃ ថ៍ អឹភែរឹល ម៉េនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង ខូអិលធីឌី, sews garments at Ksach Kandal, Kandal province, with 4,567 workers on its books. [Para 55] On 2 February 2022 fifty workers, through their representative body, demanded the settlement of 11 working conditions; conciliation on 23 February 2022 resolved 6 and left 5, which reached the Secretariat on 28 February 2022 under non-conciliation report No. 146 ក.ប/អក/វក dated 25 February 2022. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 20] The hearing was held online on 9 March 2022 under COVID-19 measures. [Para 17][Para 24] The five surviving demands concerned one day of paid annual leave deducted on 14 January 2022, payment of wages in United States dollar notes, consent forms for union dues, leave for workers who could not take the third vaccine dose, and drinking water inside Building 3. [Para 59][Para 79][Para 91][Para 105][Para 119]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must restore the one day of paid annual leave it deducted from the Building 2 workers for 14 January 2022? [Para 59]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay wages in US dollar notes of $20 and above? [Para 79]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must stop requiring workers to write and thumbprint consent forms before the administration on union dues, and deduct dues on a union list with workers' consent? [Para 91]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must restore one day of paid annual leave to workers who could not receive the third COVID-19 vaccine dose on 13 and 14 January 2022? [Para 105]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the company must let workers in Building 3 bring drinking-water bottles inside the building? [Para 119]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហភាព សហជីព ការងារ ដើម្បី កម្ពុជា through Mr V and Mr S:** the 14 January 2022 shutdown followed eight workers fainting after their booster doses, and the deduction of annual leave was never agreed by the workers themselves — the agreement relied on was made with staff representatives and a different union. [Para 4][Para 60][Para 66] Payment in riel forces workers to absorb exchange losses when they must repay bank loans, contrary to their contracts. [Para 83][Para 84] Questioning workers about union membership in the administration office, and requiring a fresh thumbprint even where consent letters existed, interferes in union affairs. [Para 96][Para 98] Workers ineligible for the booster attended work and were then sent home with a day of leave deducted, without prior notice. [Para 109][Para 112] The water point for Building 3 is about 50 metres away, so fetching water slows the line and draws reprimands. [Para 123][Para 124]
**Employer (respondent), through Mr M and Mr S:** the company merely honoured agreements signed with the staff representatives and unions, bodies elected and recognised by the Ministry of Labour. [Para 4][Para 8][Para 75] Its bank could not supply small dollar notes and other exchange points were exhausted, with about one million dollars to be handled. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 88] Its questions were only to verify consent to the dues deduction, not union membership, and the forms serve as evidence for buyers' compliance audits. [Para 101][Para 102] The buyer's standards for the Uniqlu brand forbid damp objects on the sewing floor to prevent mould. [Para 127][Para 129]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 1, 3 and 4 were granted and issues 2 and 5 dismissed: the company must repay one day of paid annual leave to the Building 2 workers for 14 January 2022, must stop requiring written and thumbprinted consent before the administration while deducting dues once the union lodges a compliant list, and must repay one day of paid annual leave to workers who could not take the third dose on 13 and 14 January 2022; the dollar-note and water-bottle claims were refused. [Para 220][Para 222][Para 223][Para 224][Para 221][Para 225]

On issue 1 the panel held that Article 170 of the Labour Law allows annual leave to be fixed outside the Khmer New Year holiday only by agreement, and followed awards 21/05-Sinomek, 81/07-Soup Prim and 123/09-Sutwe. [Para 149][Para 153] The right belongs to the worker personally or to a representative he has authorised, so agreement with staff representatives and another union could not consume it. [Para 157][Para 159] Only workers on the original complaint list of 50 qualified, not the 150 first asserted or the 76 listed later. [Para 146][Para 147][Para 148]

On issue 2 the panel read Article 2 of the Labour Law as conferring a management right that must be exercised lawfully and reasonably, and found no law or regulation obliging an employer to pay wages in dollar notes of $20 and above. [Para 162][Para 163][Para 168] The riel actually paid matched the dollar wage at the bank rate, so the claim failed for want of legal basis. [Para 169][Para 170]

On issue 3 the panel applied Article 129 of the Labour Law and Article 26 of the Law on Trade Unions 2016, with Prakas No. 300 ក.ប/ប្រ.ក.ប.ក of 2 July 2018. [Para 173][Para 176][Para 182] Deduction requires a dated, signed or thumbprinted consent from the member worker and ten days' notice, but nothing requires that consent to be executed in front of the employer. [Para 183][Para 187] Requiring a second thumbprint before the administration was therefore unlawful, while a valid union request does oblige the employer to deduct. [Para 188][Para 191]

On issue 4 the panel again applied Article 170: the ineligible workers had come to work and never agreed to spend annual leave, so the deduction was unlawful and one day was repayable. [Para 204][Para 205][Para 206]

On issue 5 the panel held that a party alleging unreasonableness carries the burden of proof, and that placing the water point 50 metres outside Building 3 was a reasonable exercise of the Article 2 management right that also preserved the buyer's standards and hence the jobs. [Para 214][Para 218]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Paid annual leave may be scheduled outside the Khmer New Year period only with the agreement of each worker personally or of a representative that worker has authorised to agree on his behalf; an agreement with staff representatives or with a union the claimant did not authorise cannot be set against him, and leave deducted without that consent must be restored. [Para 157][Para 158][Para 159] A union dues deduction is lawful on a dated consent of the member plus ten days' notice to the employer, and the employer may neither demand that the consent be re-executed before it nor refuse to deduct once a compliant list is lodged. [Para 183][Para 187][Para 191] An employer's Article 2 management right covers the placement of drinking water and the currency in which wages are tendered, provided the exercise is lawful and reasonable and the sum paid equals the contractual wage. [Para 163][Para 168][Para 218]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that no union in the company enjoyed most-representative status, and that the Labour Ministry may recognise worker representatives for arbitration where no such union exists; [Para 133][Para 137]
