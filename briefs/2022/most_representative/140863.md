# Award No. 009/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហឺរ៉ាយហ្សិន អៅដរ (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 29 April 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុង សុទ្ធី, employer-appointed; អាន វីរៈ, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លា, chair) · Doc ID 140863

## 1. Facts
The respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហឺរ៉ាយហ្សិន អៅដរ (ខេមបូឌា), sews garments and bags at Kampong Tralach, Kampong Chhnang, with 9,174 workers. [Para 94] On 18 February 2022 the workers' representatives Mr H, Ms N and Ms S demanded the settlement of 13 working conditions; conciliation on 3 March 2022 settled 2, and non-conciliation report No. 166 ក.ប/អក/វក of 9 March 2022 brought the remaining 11 to the panel. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] At the hearing of 21 March 2022 the parties settled four further heads — issues 2, 4, 7 and 8 — leaving seven for decision: issues 1, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11. [Para 37][Para 38] Three minority unions — សហជីព អានុភាព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា, សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត — brought the claims, the most-representative union being សហជីព សិទ្ធិ ប្រយោជន៍ កម្មករ, holder of certificate No. 501/19 ក.ប/វ.ប.វ.ក of 28 November 2019 under a collective agreement valid to 2023. [Para 96][Para 100][Para 218]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must deduct union dues for the three claimant unions once workers join them and thumbprint a resignation from another union? [Para 102]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must pay the ABC bonus to members of the three claimant unions as before? [Para 136]
3. (Issue 5) Whether the company must stop warning workers when a fingerprint scan fails? [Para 148]
4. (Issue 6) Whether the company must let workers eat breakfast in the area around the buildings? [Para 172]
5. (Issue 9) Whether the company must provide hot and cold water in every building? [Para 183]
6. (Issue 10) Whether the company must limit carton-holders to four sewing teams each and pay US$30 extra to those holding five? [Para 191]
7. (Issue 11) Whether the company must maintain wages for sick leave certified by the NSSF even where the worker gave no notice? [Para 198]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), through the three minority unions and Mr H, Ms N and Ms S:** most members of the most-representative union wish to resign and join one of the three, and on 25 December 2021 the unions lodged resignation letters, new membership forms and dues-deduction requests which the employer refused to accept. [Para 102][Para 103] The employer wrongly insists the outgoing union must first countersign the resignations, and has returned the letters by post. [Para 104][Para 114] The ABC bonus was paid before and should resume now that orders have returned. [Para 137][Para 139] Meal areas are far away, so late arrivals eat near the buildings and security throws their food away. [Para 173][Para 174]
**Employer (respondent), through Ms T and colleagues:** clause 10 of the collective agreement requires verification of names with the most-representative union before any deduction, and clause 12 requires workers whose scan fails to notify the administration. [Para 120][Para 158] The ABC bonus is an irregular bonus under clause 23, payable at the employer's discretion. [Para 141][Para 143] It provides a canteen and adequate drinking water, and hot water is available in the clinic. [Para 176][Para 183][Para 189]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all seven heads were refused: issues 6, 9 and 10 on the merits, and issues 1, 3, 5 and 11 because the claimant minority unions lacked standing to pursue them. [Para 329][Para 330][Para 331][Para 332][Para 334][Para 335]

On standing, the panel held that under Article 54 new of the Law on Trade Unions only a most-representative union holds the exclusive right to represent workers in collective disputes, while Article 59 new and Article 14 of Prakas No. 303 ក.ប/ប្រ.ក.គ.ប of 2 July 2018 let a minority union represent its own members only in disputes not arising from the application of a collective agreement. [Para 208][Para 213][Para 217][Para 220] The three claimant unions were minority unions and could therefore act within that limit. [Para 219][Para 222]

On issue 1 the panel applied res judicata: the same union had already litigated dues deduction against this employer in case 007/21, but the present demand fixed neither date nor amount, so the second condition was not met and the panel could still hear it. [Para 235][Para 241][Para 244][Para 246][Para 248] On the merits, Articles 129 and 102 of the Labour Law, Article 26 of the Law on Trade Unions and Article 4(2) of Prakas No. 300 ក.ប/ប្រ.ក.ក.ប.ក of 2 July 2018 require a dated written consent from each worker and ten days' notice; all three unions were properly registered, but no membership forms or consent letters were produced. [Para 249][Para 252][Para 259][Para 261][Para 272] The panel also held the employer's practice of requiring the outgoing union to verify the resignations to be wrong, since resignation needs no consent. [Para 270]

Issues 3, 5 and 11 turned on the same standing rule: the ABC bonus is fixed by clause 23, and the scan-warning and sick-notice rules by clauses 12 and 19 of the collective agreement concluded with the most-representative union, so the minority unions could not reopen them. [Para 276][Para 279][Para 283][Para 287][Para 325][Para 327]

On issue 6 the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law and Article 229, which require the employer to keep the premises clean, hygienic and orderly, and found the designated canteen a lawful and reasonable arrangement; lateness from eating elsewhere was a personal matter. [Para 291][Para 292][Para 295][Para 299][Para 300]

On issue 9 the panel applied Article 1 of Prakas No. 054 សកបយ of 10 February 2000, which requires adequate hygienic drinking water but specifies neither hot nor cold water, and refused the demand for a hot-water tank in each building. [Para 302][Para 304][Para 310]

On issue 10 the panel held that the number of teams per carton-holder and the percentage bonus fall within the employer's management prerogative, no law or agreement fixing US$30, and the workers had shown no illegality or unreasonableness. [Para 313][Para 318][Para 320][Para 321]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Only a most-representative union may bring a collective dispute over the interpretation or application of a collective agreement; a minority union may represent its own members solely in disputes that do not arise from applying the agreement, so a claim that merely repeats or varies a clause already bargained for by the majority union is inadmissible at its instance. [Para 213][Para 217][Para 279] A union dues deduction requires a dated written consent and thumbprint of each member worker plus ten days' notice to the employer, and a union producing neither cannot obtain an order to deduct. [Para 261][Para 272]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that an employer may not make resignation from a union conditional on the outgoing union's consent, and that a repeat demand escapes res judicata where it omits the dates and rate fixed in the earlier case. [Para 270][Para 245]

[Para 1][Para 26][Para 42][Para 101][Para 131] The header dates the award 29 April 2022, but the hearing is printed "ថ្ងៃទី ២១ ខែមីនា ឆ្នាំ ២០២១", a year earlier than every other reference. [Para 1][Para 28] The procedural record is inconsistent: 13 demands on 18 February, 2 settled, yet 11 forwarded. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] The same three unions are printed both "អានុភាព" and "អនុភាព", with 96, 115 and 135 members. [Para 96][Para 97][Para 99] The number of resignation letters is given as 105 in the reasoning and 20 in the employer's document list. [Para 49][Para 230] The operative paragraphs are split across page breaks and omit issues 2, 4, 7 and 8 because they were settled. [Para 273][Para 333] The signature block carries stray Latin OCR fragments around the arbitrators' names. [Para 337]
