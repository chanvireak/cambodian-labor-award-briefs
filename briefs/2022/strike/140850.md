# Award No. 041/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ដារ៉ុង ព្រីន ធីង & អ៉ីម ប្រយ ឌើរី ខូអិលធីឌី (Darong Printing & Im Propy Dery Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 10 January 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Sin Kim Sean, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chea, chair) · non-binding award terminating proceedings · Doc ID 140850

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ដារ៉ុង ព្រីន ធីង & អ៉ីម ប្រយ ឌើរី ខូអិលធីឌី (Darong Printing & Im Propy Dery Co., Ltd.) is a printing enterprise at Trapeang village, Peuk commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal province, and the worker side was the group of employees' representatives (អ្នកតំណាងភាគីកម្មករ និយោជិត) at that factory. [Para 1][Para 2]
Workers struck on 13, 14 and 15 December 2021 and put two demands: that the company preserve their wages and other benefits for the strike days, and that it remove Mr L, the company manager, from his post; the stated grievance behind the first demand was the company's termination of a worker in the water-filtration and printing section under the Labour Law, which the company said it had already settled as an individual dispute. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 6]
The provincial labour department conciliated on 13 to 15 December 2021 without agreement, issued non-conciliation report No. 291/21 of 16 December 2021, and the panel was constituted on 20 December 2021 for a hearing set by letters No. 253 and 254 of 21 December 2021. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must maintain the wages and other benefits of the workers who joined the strike of 13, 14 and 15 December 2021? [Para 2][Para 14]
2. (Issue 2) Whether Mr L must be removed from his post as company manager? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The two demands were that strikers keep their wages and benefits for the three strike days and that the manager, Mr L, be removed; the workers' representatives did not attend the hearing and instead wrote on the morning of the hearing that the dispute had ended on 16 December 2021 and asked the panel to close case 041/21. [Para 2][Para 6][Para 19][Para 39]
**Employer (respondent):** The company did not agree to either demand and asked that the Labour Law simply be applied; it said the dismissal of the worker in the water-filtration and printing section was an individual dispute already resolved according to law; it attended through representatives authorised by transfer-of-authority letters of 28 December 2021. [Para 4][Para 5][Para 7][Para 22][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel ordered the termination of the arbitration proceedings in case No. 041/21, leaving nothing to decide on the merits. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42]

The panel first asked whether it should continue the four-stage arbitration when the complainant workers were absent. [Para 31] It read article 18 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council as requiring the panel to invite the parties to explain their claims in person and to file documents, article 20 as requiring parties to attend every session, and article 21 as allowing the panel either to continue in the party's absence or to close the case by award where a duly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33] Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules repeats that choice, provided the party was properly notified of date, time and place. [Para 34][Para 35]

The panel interpreted "appearance before the panel" in article 21 as requiring both physical presence at the hearing and participation in the proceedings through to the end, so that a party who stays away has not appeared in the sense the Prakas intends. [Para 36] It noted that earlier awards had terminated proceedings where the complainant, though properly summoned, neither appeared nor joined the hearing. [Para 37][Para 38]

On the facts, the workers' representatives were absent at the hearing of 28 December 2021 at 14:00 despite the secretariat's invitation, and on the same morning filed a letter stating the dispute had been closed since 16 December 2021 and asking that case 041/21 be terminated. [Para 39] The employer appeared and was heard. [Para 20][Para 21] Because the complainants neither attended nor pursued any claim, and had asked in writing for closure, the panel found no dispute left for it to consider and terminated the proceedings rather than ruling on the two demands. [Para 30][Para 40][Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the complainant party, duly notified of the date, time and place, fails to appear and confirms in writing that it no longer pursues the dispute, the panel has no dispute before it and will terminate the arbitration proceedings by award instead of deciding the demands in the party's absence. [Para 33][Para 36][Para 40][Para 42]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's observation that "appearance" requires participation in all four stages of the hearing, not mere presence at the opening, which explains why it did not proceed to hear the employer's case alone against the absent claimants. [Para 36][Para 37]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a short procedural order and much of the reasoning is missing at the joins: the employer's answer to the second demand survives only as a truncated fragment ("the company does not agree"), and several sentences are cut mid-word where OCR dropped text. [Para 7][Para 8] The hearing date appears as 28 December 2021 in the procedure section but as "28 December 2019 / 2021" in the reasoning. [Para 13][Para 19][Para 39] The company name is printed in at least five different spellings across the face of the award and the reasons, and the case number is once rendered as "041/" with the year detached from the number. [Para 1][Para 11][Para 16][Para 41] The worker whose dismissal triggered the strike is named in the Khmer text by a single given name only, and is not identified here. [Para 3][Para 5]
