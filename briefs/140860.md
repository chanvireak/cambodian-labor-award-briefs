# Award No. 007/22 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្សិត.ហ្វើនី ឈ័រ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក (F.P. Set Finery Chair (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.) (Arbitration Council, 22 April 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វួច ហុន, employer-appointed; អន ណន, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លៈ, chair) · Doc ID 140860

## 1. Facts
The respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្សិត.ហ្វើនី ឈ័រ (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក, makes furniture components at Prey Nob, Preah Sihanouk, with 800 workers. [Para 65] On 5 February 2022 forty-five workers, through សហជីព ការងារ ដើម្បី កម្ពុជា and their representatives Mr C and Ms S, demanded the settlement of 14 working conditions; conciliation failed on 14 February and non-conciliation report No. 147 ក.ប/អក/វក of 25 February 2022 reached the Secretariat on 28 February 2022. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 66] A hearing fixed for 11 March 2022 was postponed to 18 March at the employer's request after a COVID-19 case, and held online. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 30] At the hearing the parties settled five heads — issues 1, 8, 10, 13 and 14 — and asked that the settlement be written into the award, leaving nine heads for decision: issues 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 12. [Para 32][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay a seniority bonus to workers with one year's service or more and back-pay the arrears? [Para 72]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must restore the wages and allowances deducted for 31 January 2022, the Chinese New Year holiday? [Para 82]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must obtain an overtime permit from the labour inspector before requiring overtime? [Para 102]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the panel has jurisdiction over the demand for a fresh election of staff representatives? [Para 110]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the company must pay the overtime meal allowance weekly rather than monthly? [Para 116]
6. (Issue 7) Whether the company must consult the union and obtain a majority before changing working days to holidays or weekly rest days? [Para 119]
7. (Issue 9) Whether the company must pay an overtime meal allowance of 8,000 riel or US$2 for work on holidays and Sundays? [Para 129]
8. (Issue 11) Whether the company must install a hot-water tank for the sections? [Para 136]
9. (Issue 12) Whether the company must pay 50% of wages and allowances for the 90 days of maternity leave in one payment before the leave begins? [Para 148]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហជីព ការងារ ដើម្បី កម្ពុជា through Mr C and Ms S:** the company never paid the seniority bonus it claims to pay, since no such line appears on the pay slips. [Para 73][Para 74] The 30–31 January swap to 30 January was never agreed, and Sunday work should carry double pay. [Para 86] Overtime ran to 9 or 10 p.m. beyond the permit, and the election was announced only three days ahead with the local union excluded. [Para 106][Para 110][Para 112] Meal allowance must come weekly, and a hot-water tank is needed for drinking. [Para 117][Para 145]
**Employer (respondent), through Mr M:** the seniority bonus has been paid, at US$0.50 a month rising to US$6, above the statutory US$2 from month 13, though mislabelled "ប្រាក់ឧបត្ថម្ភបន្ថែម" through a translation error. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78] It held the required overtime permits, and the 7 March 2022 election was recognised by the Ministry. [Para 102][Para 113] Weekly meal allowance is impossible for lack of staff, and the company has no hot-water tank even in the director's office. [Para 118][Para 146]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 12 was granted: the company must pay 50% of wages and allowances for the full 90 days of maternity leave at least one day before the leave begins; issues 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 11 were dismissed, and issues 5 and 9 were refused for want of jurisdiction and as an interest dispute. [Para 312][Para 313][Para 314][Para 315][Para 316][Para 317][Para 318][Para 319][Para 320]

The panel first fixed who it could decide for. No union in the company held most-representative status, so under Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 ក.ប/ប្រ.ក.គ.ប.ក of 2 July 2018 and Article 1 of Prakas No. 362 of 29 August 2016 the recognised worker representatives stood in the union's place, and only the 45 workers on the original complaint of 5 February 2022 were covered. [Para 167][Para 168][Para 170][Para 181][Para 184]

On issue 2 the panel found the company outside the scope of Notification No. 041/11 ក.ប/ស.ជ.ណ of 7 March 2011, which covers textiles, garments and footwear, because it makes furniture components; the seniority bonus paid, though labelled an additional allowance, was more favourable than the law, so no arrears were due. [Para 185][Para 189][Para 193][Para 194]

On issue 3 the panel applied Article 664 of the Civil Code and Article 102 of the Labour Law: wages are earned by work performed, and the workers neither came on 30 January 2022 under the swap nor on 31 January, so the deduction stood. [Para 198][Para 203][Para 218][Para 219]

On issue 4 the panel applied Article 1 of Prakas No. 80 សកបយ of 1 March 1999 and Notification No. 030/15 of 27 February 2015, and found permits E10TN20220102 and E1OTN20220994 covering 670 workers, so the demand was already met — while noting the company must still work within the hours those permits allow. [Para 221][Para 225][Para 228][Para 229]

On issue 5 the panel held itself without jurisdiction: Article 47 of the Law on Trade Unions of 2016 and Article 298 of the Labour Law send disputes over elections, candidacy and electoral validity to the labour court. [Para 231][Para 235][Para 242]

On issues 6 and 11 the panel applied the Article 2 management right, lawful and reasonable exercise, and found no rule requiring a weekly meal allowance or a hot-water tank. [Para 247][Para 252][Para 287][Para 293] Issue 7 was refused as a claim about a future change that had not occurred. [Para 260][Para 261][Para 265] Issue 9 was refused because the demand exceeded the law and the claimant union was not most representative, so the panel could not make an interest award binding on the workforce. [Para 269][Para 281][Para 286]

On issue 12 the panel applied Articles 182 and 183 of the Labour Law, which give 90 days' maternity leave on half pay including allowances, and Article 115(3), which forbids payment on a rest day and requires it the day before. [Para 296][Para 297][Para 301] Article 103 treats maternity pay as wages, so the monthly instalment practice breached Article 115. [Para 304][Para 305][Para 309]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Half-pay maternity wages including allowances for the 90-day leave are wages within Article 103, so Article 115(3) applies to them and the whole sum must be paid at least one day before the leave starts; a practice of paying it month by month during the leave is unlawful. [Para 304][Para 305][Para 310][Para 311] A demand that exceeds what the law requires is an interest dispute, and the panel may decide it only where the claimant union holds most-representative status or the qualifying characteristics of Article 72 of the Law on Trade Unions. [Para 269][Para 281] Disputes over the election, candidacy and validity of staff representative elections lie outside the panel's jurisdiction. [Para 231][Para 242]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that an employer remains bound to observe the hours stated in the overtime permit even where a permit exists, and that no union in the company had most-representative status. [Para 229][Para 167]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company's name appears in at least six forms — អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្សិត.ហ្វើនី, អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្សិត.ហ្វីនី, អេហ្វ.កី.ហ្សិត.ហ្វីនី, អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្សូត and អេហ្វ.ភី.ហ្ស៊ិត — and "ឈ័រ" alternates with "ឈ័រ (ខេមបូឌា)". [Para 1][Para 19][Para 24][Para 65] The panel's constitution date is printed "ថ្ងៃទី២ ខែមីនា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០២២", a duplicated year. [Para 25] The award date in the header, 22 April 2022, is echoed by a postponement to that date from 23 March. [Para 1][Para 36] The operative part on issue 2 is garbled, repeating "បដិសេធ ការទាមទារ" twice mid-sentence. [Para 312] The worker count shifts between 45 on the complaint, 200 union members claimed and 45 considered. [Para 180][Para 182][Para 184] The chair is printed គង់ ផល្លៈ on the face but the signature block shows "អន លាន" for the worker-appointed arbitrator where the face gives អន ណន. [Para 1][Para 322] The thumbprint annexes at the end are largely destroyed OCR and were not relied on. [Para 343][Para 348]
