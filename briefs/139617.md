# Award No. 162/14 — Meng Da Footwear Industrial (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vuoch Hun, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Aing Eng Tong, president) · Doc ID 139617

## 1. Facts
Meng Da Footwear Industrial (Cambodia) Co., Ltd ("Meng Da") runs a shoe factory registered as Inv. 1357 E/2008 and employing about 4,100 workers. [Para 58][Para 59] The Khmer Workers' Conscience Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករខ្មែរ), holding transfer-of-rights letters from 56 workers dated 20 May 2014, brought a collective dispute over 11 demands; 5 were settled in conciliation and 6 came before the panel. [Para 63][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a health allowance of $5 per month to the workers? [Para 66]
2. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of $15 per month — that is, $8 above the $7 already paid? [Para 77]
3. Whether the employer must maintain the full regular attendance bonus when workers arrive slightly late, by up to 15 minutes in one instance? [Para 89]
4. Whether the employer must pay a regular attendance bonus of $15 per month — that is, $5 above the $10 already paid? [Para 115]
5. Whether the employer must pay 2,000 riel per day in place of the one midday meal it currently provides? [Para 127]
6. Whether the employer must pay the overtime meal allowance weekly rather than monthly? [Para 150]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The $5 health allowance was paid before Notice 103 and other factories still pay it; house rents rose 30–40 per cent and transport costs rose, so $7 no longer covers them; the federation's own inquiry found most workers dissatisfied with the canteen meal. [Para 67][Para 79][Para 148]

**Employer (respondent):** The $5 was folded into the minimum wage when it rose from $61 to $80; the company has no capacity to pay more; the deduction policy exists to enforce punctuality and discipline; and the meal allowance is bound into the monthly payroll system, so weekly payment would require splitting the system and extra staff. [Para 74][Para 83][Para 103][Para 155]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 1 to 5 dismissed; on issue 6 the employer was ordered to pay the overtime meal allowance once a week, every week. [Para 253][Para 258]

**Issue 1.** On the merits the panel held that Notice No. 103 of 9 April 2013 merged the $5 health allowance into the minimum wage, and that Prakas No. 317 of 31 December 2013 — raising the minimum from $80 to $100 for 2014 — derogated that notice, so no separate obligation survived; it followed its own reasoning in case 197/13. [Para 158][Para 164][Para 167] Having found the demand to exceed what the law requires, it treated it as an interest dispute and also held the federation not to be the most representative union. [Para 171][Para 172][Para 182]

**Issue 2.** Point 1 of Notice No. 230 of 25 July 2012 requires $7 per month for travel and accommodation; the employer was paying exactly that, so it had discharged the obligation, and no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliged $15. [Para 185][Para 186][Para 188]

**Issue 3.** Point 2 of Notice No. 230 conditions the $10 attendance bonus on coming to work punctually on the working days of the month; the panel read that as including arrival at the fixed hour. Lateness of any length therefore means the worker did not attend punctually, and rounding every instance of lateness up to 15 minutes is a reasonable way of computing the deduction, given the bonus is tiny when counted by the minute. [Para 194][Para 196][Para 201]

**Issue 4.** The employer was paying the $10 bonus required by Notice No. 230, so nothing further was owed; no instrument or practice in the company set $15. [Para 206][Para 208][Para 209]

**Issue 5.** No law, collective agreement or internal rule required a 2,000 riel cash payment in place of the meal. Article 2 of the Labour Law leaves the organisation of the enterprise to the employer, and the panel held the choice between providing food and paying cash to be within that managerial prerogative where exercised lawfully and reasonably — support here coming from the 26 January 2013 memorandum with another union providing one meal, and from the employer's survey of 400 workers. Workers who can prove the food is poor may demand improvement, but not conversion to cash. [Para 215][Para 228][Para 232]

**Issue 6.** Point 2 of Notice No. 041/11 requires 2,000 riel or a free meal for voluntary overtime but fixes no payment interval. The panel held the money must reach workers often enough that they can buy food when working overtime, and accepted that the workers' weekly need — ordinary weekly expenses and debt interest — was genuine; the employer's payroll-system difficulty did not displace that need. [Para 234][Para 239][Para 251][Para 252]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍) is admissible only where the union bringing it is the most representative in the enterprise — that is, where it or the unions acting together have more than half the workers as members. A minority union cannot put a new entitlement to the panel, however real its members' need. [Para 179]

**Obiter dicta:** That workers who can prove the canteen food is of poor quality may demand that it be improved but cannot require the employer to substitute a cash allowance, the form of the benefit remaining a managerial choice. [Para 232]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The federation's membership is printed as 56 workers at Para 63 but as 30 at Para 181, in the passage holding it not most representative. The presiding arbitrator is printed "អាំង អេង ថុង" in the header at Para 1 and "អាំង អេង ថ្ម" in the signature block at Para 260. The order at Para 252 carries a stray "ក្នុង មួយ ឆ្នាំ" ("in one year") that Para 258 does not repeat. Minimum-wage figures appear two ways: $56 + $14 + $5 = $75 for probationary and $61 + $14 + $5 = $80 for full workers at Paras 159–160, against the employer's "$61 to $80" at Para 74; stray "២០១៥" insertions recur in the reasons. [Para 63][Para 181][Para 252][Para 258]
