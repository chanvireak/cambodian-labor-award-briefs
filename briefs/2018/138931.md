# Award No. 044/18 — ខ្វាន់ ទុម អី ភែរីល (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត (Arbitration Council, 12 December 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chheam, president) · Doc ID 138931

## 1. Facts
The company is a garment cutting and sewing enterprise at Chom Chao, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, with 1,790 workers, and neither party asserted that a most-representative union existed there [Para 41][Para 42][Para 44]. Twelve workers' delegates raised nine demands on 12 November 2018; conciliation on 13 November left three unresolved, and the workers struck until the Council's order of 16 November 2018 required them back to work [Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 19][Para 20]. At the hearing the workers withdrew demand 1 — seniority indemnity from 2008 to 2018 calculated on a USD 170 base — leaving two claims for the panel [Para 2][Para 23][Para 24]. The employer had already undertaken to apply Prakas No. 443 of 21 September 2018 on seniority indemnity in full [Para 4][Para 30].

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must pay a function (position) allowance of USD 20 per person to group assistants and department assistants? [Para 45][Para 81]
3. Whether the employer must pay a skill or incentive allowance of USD 10 a month to workers who can sew on two machines and USD 15 to those who can sew on three? [Para 52][Para 107]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** assistants and group assistants are paid a fixed monthly wage, receive no piece-rate or other bonus, and so earn less than the workers they supervise [Para 47]; they do more work than group heads, whose tasks the heads decline to perform and leave to them, yet their pay is unchanged [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]. The employer repeatedly moves workers onto two- and three-machine work precisely because they have that skill, while workers without it are left alone, so the extra skill should attract an allowance [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56].
**Employer (respondent):** there is no policy of paying a function allowance and no agreement creating one; the existing "កាតុង" target bonus already rewards these staff [Para 45][Para 51]. Sewing workers are paid by output, so multi-machine workers already earn more through volume; the company moreover provides a free midday meal, USD 12 a month in travel and accommodation support, better-than-statutory seniority bonuses and 60% of wages during maternity leave [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining claims were refused and no sum was ordered; the award was issued as non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification absent written objection to the Minister [Para 113][Para 114][Para 115].

On standing, the panel began from Article 54(1) of the Trade Union Law 2016, under which only a most-representative union holds the exclusive right to conclude a collective agreement and to conduct collective disputes, including before the Council [Para 61][Para 64][Para 65]. Following awards 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting) and 171/16 (Jewelry Line), it held that the absence of such a union does not extinguish representation: Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 (2 July 2018) provides that where an enterprise has no union or no most-representative union, collective disputes are handled by worker representatives, and Article 1 of Prakas No. 362 lets the Labour Disputes Department recognise them [Para 66][Para 67][Para 72]. Six representatives were recognised on 12 November 2018 and four attended, so the panel accepted that they could conduct the case [Para 75][Para 77].

On the merits, both claims failed at the characterisation stage. For the function allowance the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules, contract, agreement or past practice obliging the employer to pay it, and noted that the demand in fact sought more than the law requires [Para 81][Para 82]. It therefore classified the claim as an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍) [Para 83]. The skill allowance received identical treatment: no instrument or practice created the entitlement, so it too was an interest claim [Para 107][Para 108]. Because an interest award substitutes for the collective agreement for one year under Article 43 of Prakas 099, and because the claimant was not a most-representative union, the panel declined to consider either claim [Para 97][Para 99][Para 100][Para 103][Para 104]. The recognised delegates' competence to appear did not cure that absence of standing to bring an interest claim, and the panel said so expressly [Para 101][Para 105][Para 112].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective claim for a new allowance that no statute, agreement, internal rule or past practice supports is an interest dispute, and only a most-representative union may bring it to the Council; worker representatives recognised under Prakas No. 362 and Prakas No. 303 may conduct the proceedings but cannot confer standing the claimant lacks [Para 100][Para 103]. Where the employer already provides benefits above the statutory floor — free meals, travel support, seniority bonuses, maternity pay at 60% — the panel will not treat the existence of those benefits as creating a duty to add a further allowance [Para 58][Para 60][Para 82].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's recital of the employer's benefits package and its remark that the workers' claim "seeks more than the law requires" go beyond what was needed, since the claim was refused on standing [Para 58][Para 82].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears only in Khmer on the face of the award and is spelled several ways within the document (អី ភែរីល, អី ភែរឹល, អ៊ីភែរឹល, អ៊ីភែរីល); no Latin form is printed, so no English rendering has been adopted here [Para 1][Para 18][Para 41][Para 42]. The claim is variously described as nine demands and as "9 points" with three unsettled, while the panel lists demands 2 and 3 only because demand 1 was withdrawn [Para 14][Para 15][Para 23][Para 24]. Issue 3's amount is printed as "១៥៦ ដុល្លារ" in one recital and "១៥ ដុល្លារ" elsewhere; the operative order uses USD 15 [Para 106][Para 107][Para 114]. The judgment contains repeated garbled passages (for example "កាពព្វកិច្ច" for កាតព្វកិច្ច and a corrupted recital of the Prakas 099 text), and the president's name appears as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាម on the face and ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា at the signature [Para 1][Para 106][Para 116]. The award date is stated twice, consistently, as 12 December 2018 [Para 1][Para 26].
