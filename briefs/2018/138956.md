# Award No. 055/18 — Seduno Inn Wes Men Kambo Five Sin (Arbitration Council, 10 January 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — You Suon Ty (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Kong Phalha (chair) · Doc ID 138956

## 1. Facts
Seduno Inn Wes Men Kambo Five Sin is a garment manufacturer at Khva village, Dangkao commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] On 26 December 2018 the workers petitioned for better working conditions on four points and struck; one point — a demand that the company pay all accumulated seniority compensation (ប្រាក់បំណាច់អតីតភាពការងារ) to each of them — failed conciliation the same day and reached the Council on 27 December 2018. [Para 2][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] The panel suspended the strike by Order No. 006/18 of 31 December 2018, ordering a return to normal shifts from the morning of 2 January 2019. [Para 14]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all where the worker party that filed the complaint did not attend the pre-hearing conference? [Para 37]
2. Whether the company must pay the workers the whole of their accumulated seniority compensation? [Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They maintained the demand that the company settle all accumulated seniority compensation in full. [Para 2] Their representative Mr M Chanthou told the secretariat by telephone that he would not attend because he was very busy and because a number of workers had already quit. [Para 19][Para 61]

**Employer (respondent):** The company applied Prakas No. 443 of 21 September 2018 of the Ministry of Labour. [Para 3] By the pre-hearing stage, about 100 workers had refused to return to work under the Council's order and the court's protective injunction, and those workers had accepted severance pay from the employer and had therefore ceased to be employees; no strike continued at the factory. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to continue the arbitration and terminated its proceedings in Case No. 055/18; it never reached the merits of the seniority-compensation claim. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 76]

The panel applied Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council and Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules, which allow an award to be issued in a party's absence provided three conditions are met: the party was properly notified, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 41][Para 43][Para 47] It found all three satisfied. Notice was properly given: the secretariat sent invitation letter No. 013 of 4 January 2019 to the worker representatives, stating the date, time and place of the 8 January 2019 pre-hearing conference, and telephoned the three complainants Mr M Chanthou, Mr P Romduol and Mr B Sava. [Para 16][Para 51][Para 52][Para 54] Neither the representatives nor the workers appeared. [Para 18][Para 58][Para 59] On the third condition, the secretariat telephoned Mr M Chanthou at about 9:16 on the morning of the conference and he said he was too busy and that some workers had already resigned, and no request to postpone was ever filed. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63][Para 65]

The panel also set out the ordinary allocation of burdens — the complainant must come forward and prove its claim, and the respondent must then answer it — and found the complainants had done neither, so it treated the claim as abandoned. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 73]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A complainant that has been properly notified of a pre-hearing conference, fails to appear, and offers no valid reason is treated as having abandoned its claim, and the panel will terminate the arbitration without examining the merits — being "too busy" and pointing to other workers' resignations is not a valid reason and does not substitute for a request to postpone. [Para 63][Para 73][Para 74]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the right to appear may be exercised in person, through an accompanying person, a lawyer of the Bar Association of Cambodia, or another person holding the party's written authority. [Para 56] It also recorded, without deciding anything on it, the employer's statement that roughly 100 workers had accepted severance and were no longer employees. [Para 20][Para 21]
