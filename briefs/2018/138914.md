# Award No. 035/18 — Jimly (Cambodia) PTY LTD (Arbitration Council, 14 September 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chiv Phirom, appointed by the employer; An Non Chey, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalha, president, appointed by both) · Doc ID 138914

## 1. Facts
Jimly (Cambodia) PTY LTD operates a garment factory at Trapaing Thul village, Kambol commune, Posen Chey district, Phnom Penh [Para 1]. One collective demand remained unresolved after conciliation on 3 September 2018: that the company settle the old employment contracts in advance should it relocate or change its name [Para 2][Para 10]. The company answered that it had already paid out the contracts under an agreement dated 01 August 2018 [Para 3][Para 24]. While the case was pending the workforce struck; the panel issued Order No. 002/18 of 7 September 2018 directing an immediate suspension of the strike, and both sides later acknowledged that only some workers returned [Para 15][Para 19].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay out the old employment contracts in advance in the event it relocates its premises or changes its name? [Para 2][Para 9]
2. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration when the strike it ordered suspended is still being carried on? [Para 34][Para 43]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They maintained the demand for advance settlement of the old contracts, and at the pre-hearing meeting stated that those still refusing to return to work were pressing for payment of severance on termination of their contracts [Para 41].

**Employer (respondent):** It had already paid each worker's contract termination in full in accordance with the agreement of 01 August 2018, and asked the panel to order the strike stopped [Para 3][Para 14].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated its own arbitration proceedings in case No. 035/18 without holding a hearing or making any finding of fact, leaving the wage demand undetermined [Para 33][Para 46].

The panel framed the question as whether it should continue at all [Para 34], and applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, under which the parties must suspend every dispute measure — strike or lock-out under Article 318 of the Labour Law — for the duration of the panel's work, and must attend all meetings to which the panel summons them [Para 34]. It noted its settled practice, stated in earlier awards it cited by number, of refusing to continue arbitration while workers are striking or an employer has locked out [Para 37][Para 38]. The panel recorded that, after Order No. 002/18 required workers to stop striking and resume work from the afternoon of 7 September 2018, the Secretariat was told the workers had not returned and were continuing the strike [Para 15][Para 16]. At the pre-hearing meeting of 13 September 2018 both sides agreed some had returned and some had not, and the panel found the strike still ongoing [Para 19][Para 42]. Concluding that it could not proceed to hearing on that footing, it terminated the proceedings [Para 43][Para 44]. Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the merits [Para 33].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where, after an order suspending a strike, workers persist in striking during the panel's proceedings, the panel will not continue the arbitration: Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 makes suspension of dispute measures a precondition of the panel's work, so non-compliance requires termination of the proceedings rather than adjudication of the merits [Para 34][Para 44].

**Obiter dicta:** none identified. The panel's references to its earlier awards on the same point describe consistent practice rather than adding a rule, and its observation that some workers had resumed work while others had not was made only to establish that the strike continued [Para 38][Para 41].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Both parties' names appear in several corrupted forms — "Jimly", "Jim Li", and "Ji Balin" for the company, with "PTY" rendered variously as ភីធី/អិលធីឌី in the same document [Para 1][Para 6][Para 11]. The signature block attributes An Non to the employer-appointed seat, contrary to the recital naming Chiv Phirom to that seat, and prints the president as គង់ ផល្លៈ while the recital reads គង់ ផល្ជៈ [Para 1][Para 47]. The award number of the conciliation report is printed both as 757 and as ក.ប/អុក/វក against ក.ប/អក/វក elsewhere [Para 6][Para 27]. Line numbering survives as a fragment at [Para 45], and the date on which workers were required to return to work is garbled in the order's operative sentence [Para 15]. No findings of fact were recorded, so nothing on the merits could be verified against the file [Para 33].
