# Award No. 001/18 — HR Green Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 January 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mar Sambor Na (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kuy Nam (chair) · Doc ID 138874

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment-cutting factory at Kien Svay, Kandal province, employing about 1,021 workers, where the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union (សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) has been registered since 4 March 2016 [Para 44][Para 45]. On 8 December 2017 the Kandal provincial department of labour received the union representatives' complaint raising 11 points; conciliation on 27 December 2017 settled 8 and referred 3 to the Council on 2 January 2018 [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]. At the hearing the parties settled points 1 and 3 by agreement, leaving only point 2 for decision — the pro-rating of the USD 13 monthly attendance bonus [Para 19][Para 20].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay USD 20 as a milk allowance to women workers with small children in place of building a nursery? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pro-rate the USD 13 attendance bonus (13 divided by 26 days) for each day a worker takes leave for personal reasons, instead of withdrawing the whole bonus? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must allow workers to borrow against annual leave whenever their annual entitlement is exhausted? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer deducts the attendance bonus far too often, and a worker absent for two days in a month loses the whole USD 13; they asked the panel to reconsider the point because the earlier award had refused it [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56].
**Employer (respondent):** it rejected all three claims [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7]. It confirmed the practice of paying USD 13 and of deducting by degree — USD 3.5 for half a day, USD 6.5 for one day, USD 9.5 for a day and a half, and the whole USD 13 for two days — and applied that deduction only to leave taken for personal reasons not arising from the contract, the Labour Law or other rules [Para 50][Para 51].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the attendance-bonus claim at all, on the ground that it had already been decided between the same parties; points 1 and 3 were recorded as settled. The award is non-binding (សេចក្តីបង្គាប់ មិនជាប់កាតព្វកិច្ច) [Para 93][Para 94][Para 21].

**Standing.** No most-representative union existed in the enterprise, and following Awards 171/16 and 180/16 the absence of such a union must not obstruct workers' representation. The Kandal department's letter 014 of 28 December 2017 recognised five representatives, of whom those present had standing under Article 1 of Prakas 362 [Para 61][Para 62][Para 72].

**Res judicata.** The same claim had already been brought and decided in Award 025/17 (HR Green Garment) [Para 75]. Applying the principle of អាជ្ញា អស់ជំនុំ (res judicata), the panel found all three conditions satisfied: the parties were the same, the point was the same one previously brought to the Council, and the Council had decided it on the merits and in full [Para 83][Para 85][Para 87][Para 88]. No provision of the arbitration procedure provides otherwise, and the panel noted that the principle exists to avoid contradictory outcomes on the same dispute and to ensure finality [Para 77][Para 78][Para 81].

**Effect.** Because the conditions were met, the panel did not examine the claim again and issued no order on the bonus [Para 92][Para 93].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective dispute point that the Arbitration Council has already decided on the merits between the same parties will not be reconsidered by a later panel, even where the arbitration procedure contains no express rule on the subject; the earlier award is treated as finally settling the point, and a fresh claim raising it is refused consideration rather than decided again [Para 80][Para 88][Para 92].

**Obiter dicta:** the absence of a most-representative union in an enterprise does not bar workers from being represented in collective dispute resolution by representatives recognised by the provincial labour department or by a union to which they transfer their mandate [Para 62][Para 63].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The earlier award is dated 18 July 2017 in the employer's evidence list and 19 July 2017 in the res judicata analysis [Para 27][Para 88]. The company's name is printed in at least five different transliterations across the document (អេច អរ, អ៊េច អរ, អើច អរ, អ្ហ្រេន, អ៊ច អរ), and its registration number appears both as Inv.0351E/2015 and as MOC-26133766 [Para 26][Para 43]. The number of recognised representatives is given as five in the recognition letter, four attending at the hearing in one passage and three in another, the panel resting its ruling on three [Para 46][Para 47][Para 71]. Stray numerals interrupt the reasoning ("កម្មករ និយោជិត ១៦"), and one page of the findings is replaced by English OCR debris [Para 23][Para 68].
