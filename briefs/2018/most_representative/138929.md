# Award No. 043/18 — Quantum Clothing (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 December 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; Liv Sovanna, appointed by the workers; Aing Eng Thong, president) · Doc ID 138929

## 1. Facts
The company, a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise at Chom Chao, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, employs 2,383 workers [Para 62]. After a change of shareholders and of senior management, a group of workers struck and demanded that the employer terminate their contracts and pay five heads of compensation, saying they had lost confidence in the new management [Para 71][Para 73]. The Ministry's conciliation of 8 November 2018 failed on the single unresolved point, and the Council issued order No. 004/18 to stop the strike, after which the workers returned [Para 15][Para 16][Para 19][Para 23]. The dispute reached the panel as one issue [Para 130].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to terminate the workers' labour contracts and pay severance-type compensation — payment in lieu of notice, dismissal damages, mental-health costs, indemnity for unused annual leave — and final wages? [Para 130]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 6 January 2017 shareholders' agreement was followed by a restructuring that stripped benefits: 75 piece-rate sewers were moved onto a line system, daily earnings fell from USD 12 to USD 7, hours moved from Monday–Friday to Monday–Saturday, and the annual incentive fell from 3% to 2% after protest; some workers were moved from indefinite to fixed-duration contracts [Para 73][Para 77][Para 78][Para 83][Para 84]. They fear the employer will abandon them, and say compulsory overtime breaches the internal rules [Para 75][Para 76].
**Employer (respondent):** the company is trading normally and has no intention of terminating anyone; the line-system change was a three-month trial that lifted output 30%, with wages topped up to the old piece-rate level, and it will be abandoned because of the protests [Para 88][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 103]. The 2017 agreement preserved seniority, contracts and benefits [Para 105].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the claim that the employer must terminate the contracts and pay payment in lieu of notice, dismissal damages, mental-health costs, annual-leave indemnity and final wages; the award was issued as non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification absent written objection to the Minister [Para 147][Para 148][Para 149].

On standing, the panel applied Article 54(1) of the Trade Union Law 2016, under which only a most representative union holds the exclusive right to conduct collective disputes, and found that neither party asserted such a union existed at the company [Para 112][Para 115][Para 117]. Article 16 of Prakas No. 303 (2 July 2018) and Article 1 of Prakas No. 362 then supply the answer: where there is no union or no most representative union, collective disputes are handled by worker representatives recognised by the Labour Disputes Department [Para 118][Para 123]. Eight representatives had been recognised on 8 November 2018 and five attended, so the panel held those five entitled to represent the workers before it [Para 126][Para 128]. The employer's objection to the federation official Ms Y Leap was upheld only in part: she could attend, but could not act as representative, because the letter of authorisation ran to the federation rather than to her as an individual [Para 67][Para 69][Para 68].

On the merits the panel applied the new Article 87 of the Labour Law, as amended by the law promulgated 26 June 2018 and in force from that date, which provides that where the employer's legal status changes — succession, sale, merger, conversion into a company — contracts in progress continue between the new employer and the workers of the old enterprise [Para 130][Para 132][Para 133]. It found the share transfer real but the contracts, seniority and benefits preserved, including the 2017 agreement's promise that the transfer would not affect prior benefits and seniority and its USD 4-per-year-of-seniority incentive [Para 107][Para 108][Para 134][Para 136]. The line-system trial did not breach that article because wages were topped up to the previous piece-rate level and the trial was to be discontinued [Para 144][Para 145]. Since no change of legal status had prejudiced the contracts, no obligation to terminate with compensation arose [Para 146].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A change of shareholders or of management that leaves the contracts, seniority and benefits intact is not a change in the employer's legal status within the meaning of new Article 87 of the Labour Law, and therefore creates no right for workers to demand termination with severance, damages or notice pay; the continuity rule protects the contract, it does not entitle the workers to cash it out [Para 133][Para 146]. Where an enterprise has no most representative union, only the worker representatives recognised by the Labour Disputes Department may conduct the collective dispute, and an official of an outside federation cannot substitute for them [Para 123][Para 128].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the line-system trial raised output by 30% and that 44 of the 75 affected workers did not wish to return to piece-rate work, neither of which was necessary to the holding [Para 90][Para 93].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears on the face of the award only in Khmer (ខ្វាន់ ទុម ខ្លួស៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) លីមីធីត), spelled several different ways across the document, and the Latin form "Quantum Clothing (Cambodia) Ltd" appears only in a transfer-of-authority letter signed by the employer's deputy director; the two have not been independently reconciled [Para 1][Para 31]. The procedural recital says the workers claimed five heads of relief at conciliation but that only one point was conciliated and left unresolved, and the statement of claim is described as having "5 points" while the single issue before the panel lists five money heads [Para 14][Para 15][Para 130]. The list of 41 worker representatives at Para 5 is truncated in the source text. The signatures show the worker-appointed arbitrator's name twice, as លីវ សុវណ្ណា and លវ សុវណ្ណា [Para 150].
