# Award No. 041/18 — ប៊ឺរី អី ភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី (Arbitration Council, 29 November 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី, អន ណន, គង់ ផល្ហៈ); dissenting opinion by អន ណន · Doc ID 138921

## 1. Facts
The employer, a garment factory employing about 3,019 workers, faced eight demands from the Independent Union of the factory, registered on 8 December 2017 with a three-year mandate for its president Ms L, vice-president Ms Kh and secretary Ms H. [Para 19][Para 62] Six points were referred after conciliation failed; two were settled at the hearing, leaving the meal allowances, the overtime meal money, maternity leave and the team-leader bonus. [Para 20][Para 25][Para 26] Ten unions exist in the factory and none holds most-representative status. [Para 65] The award is not binding. [Para 27][Para 251]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must restore the previous piece-rate pricing without recording quantities? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must add 2,000 riel a day to the 2,000 riel lunch allowance, making 4,000 riel a day? [Para 66][Para 143]
3. Whether the employer must pay 3,500 riel a day as overtime meal money instead of the 3,000 riel paid? [Para 70][Para 170]
4. Whether the employer must use unfixed-duration contracts for workers employed more than two years? [Para 8]
5. Whether the employer must give 90 working days of maternity leave rather than 90 calendar days? [Para 181]
6. Whether the employer must restore the 80 US dollar monthly team-leader bonus of Ms L and repay the amounts already deducted? [Para 196][Para 201]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** 2,000 riel a day no longer covers a meal because food prices rose; the overtime meal money should match what parent and sister companies pay at 3,500 riel; the law's 90 days must mean working days, since a ministry announcement sets 26 working days a month; and Ms L was told to resign from the union and then lost her bonus despite returning to regular attendance. [Para 68][Para 72][Para 76][Para 89]
**Employer (respondent):** it cannot afford any increase; it pays 3,000 riel for overtime meals, already above Notice No. 041/11, and does not know what other companies pay; Article 182 means 90 calendar days, which it grants, with an extra month available; and Ms L missed work repeatedly and ignored two warnings, so the discretionary bonus stopped. [Para 69][Para 74][Para 78][Para 93][Para 220]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — every remaining claim was refused, with no sum ordered. [Para 248][Para 249][Para 250]

On standing, the panel held that Article 54 of the Law on Trade Unions and Prakas No. 303 reserve the conduct of interest disputes to a most-representative union, because an award on interests replaces the collective agreement for one year and removes the right to strike over the same demand. [Para 125][Para 160][Para 163] The three recognised representatives could act for the workers in a rights dispute under Prakas No. 362, but no most-representative union exists in the factory. [Para 65][Para 130][Para 141]

On the lunch allowance and overtime meal money, the panel found no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice requiring either increase: the 2,000 riel lunch allowance already exceeds the law, and the 3,000 riel overtime meal money exceeds Notice No. 041/11, which requires 2,000 riel. [Para 145][Para 146][Para 173][Para 174] Because the demands went beyond what the law guarantees, the panel classified both as interest disputes and declined to consider them for want of a most-representative claimant. [Para 148][Para 169][Para 175][Para 180]

On maternity leave, Article 182 grants 90 days without saying whether they are working or calendar days, and no ministry regulation defines the point. [Para 182][Para 186][Para 187] Reading the article as a whole and following earlier awards, the panel held that 90 days means calendar days, counting public holidays and Sundays, because a longer period is conventionally counted in calendar days. [Para 189][Para 191][Para 192] The employer already granted 90 calendar days, so the claim failed. [Para 193][Para 194]

On the team-leader bonus, the panel held that the 80 dollar incentive is not a statutory entitlement but a discretionary scheme under the employer's managerial prerogative in Article 2, conditional on regular attendance and good team supervision. [Para 205][Para 213][Para 214] It found the parties agreed that Ms L had not met those conditions, and that the workers produced no evidence that she was asked to leave the union or that other team leaders were treated differently. [Para 224][Para 225][Para 243][Para 244] Since the party asserting union discrimination bears the burden of proof, the claim and the discrimination allegation were rejected. [Para 241][Para 245][Para 247]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand that goes beyond what the Labour Law, a collective agreement or established practice already gives is an interest dispute, and only a most-representative union may bring it, because an award on interests substitutes for the collective agreement for one year and extinguishes the right to strike over the same demand; recognised worker representatives may therefore pursue rights claims but not interest claims. [Para 160][Para 163][Para 164]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel set out a drafting convention for periods the law leaves unqualified — 15 days or fewer are read as working days, longer periods as calendar days — and observed that an employer's discretionary bonus scheme must still be applied consistently and lawfully. [Para 191][Para 204][Para 208]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed at least three ways in the same document — "ប៊ឺរី អី ភែរឹល", "ប៊ីរី អី ភែរីល" and, in the body, "ប៊ឺរី អ៊ីភែរីល" — so no single Latin rendering is settled by the face of the award. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 62] The number of claimants is unstable: paragraph 142 refers to "2 workers" as the claimants while paragraph 63 certifies three representatives. [Para 63][Para 142] Paragraph 163 repeats its own sentence twice in corrupted form and paragraph 191 loses a numeral to OCR ("/១៥"). [Para 163][Para 191] The dissenting opinion is appended as an annex that repeats the award number in two spellings and describes the dissenting arbitrator as chosen by the workers' party, which matches the face of the award but leaves the chair's name absent from the signature block. [Para 252][Para 1] Issue numbering jumps from 1 to 6 because points 1 and 4 were settled at the hearing without their terms being recorded. [Para 25][Para 26]
