# Award No. 046/18 — Rousing Gamem (Branch 1) (Arbitration Council, 13 December 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Chan (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 138941

## 1. Facts
A garment factory at Tuol Sangke, Russey Keo, Phnom Penh. [Para 2] The company notified the labour inspectorate of workforce reductions in three tranches: 211 workers on 25 June 2018, 150 workers on 21 August 2018 and 220 workers on 5 September 2018. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 34] Thirty-three workers, who had mandated three of their number as representatives, claimed reinstatement at the same workplace together with wages from the date the company stopped them working until reinstatement. [Para 3][Para 21] The employer answered that their contracts had reached their term and that it had no orders; it also asked that the matter be treated as an individual dispute. [Para 4][Para 6]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take the dismissed workers back at the same workplace and pay them wages from the date of dismissal until reinstatement? [Para 3]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company stopped them working and must reinstate them with back wages; they attended to the extent of mandating three certified representatives. [Para 3][Para 23]

**Employer (respondent):** it cannot take them back because their fixed-duration contracts had expired and it has no customer orders, and it has already paid wages and other benefits according to the Labour Law. [Para 4][Para 5]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel did not adjudicate the claim; it treated the claim as abandoned and closed the proceedings by order. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 84]

The panel's only question was whether it could proceed, or must close the file, when the claimant party is absent. It took Prakas No. 099 Articles 18, 20 and 21 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Rules of Procedure as permitting it either to continue in the absence of a party or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an order, provided the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 54] It then applied the three-condition test it had stated in earlier awards. [Para 56]

Condition one, proper notification, was met: invitation letter No. 302 of 3 December 2018 set out the hearing date, venue and the secretariat's telephone and fax numbers, and both the representatives and the claimants received it. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61] Condition two, non-appearance, was met: none of the three certified representatives attended, so none took part in the four stages of the hearing (introduction and conflict check, explanation of procedure, renewed conciliation, and hearing to the end). [Para 63][Para 66][Para 67] Condition three, absence of a valid reason, was met: the secretariat telephoned Mr C, who said he could not attend because he was busy with a festival in his home province and that he had sent the federation officer in his place, and no adjournment was ever requested. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 72]

The panel added that the applicant bears the burden of first presenting its claim, its reasoning and its evidence; the respondent's burden to answer arises only after that. Since the claimants never appeared and gave no valid reason, the claim was deemed abandoned and the file closed. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 79][Para 81] The two federation officers who did attend had told the panel that they were not the claimants and had no authority to represent them. [Para 20]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant workers have been properly notified and none of their certified representatives appears, and the only explanation offered is that a representative was busy with a festival in his home province and sent a federation officer without a mandate, the panel will find the claim abandoned and close the proceedings by order instead of adjudicating it. [Para 56][Para 70][Para 81]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified.

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The operative paragraph cites the case as No. 046/14 while the header and every earlier reference give 046/18. The signature block loses the worker-appointed arbitrator's name to OCR (printed as ម៉ឺន ណា), although the header names Tuon Si Chan. Paragraph 12 speaks of claims by two workers while paragraph 21 records thirty-three claimants and three mandated representatives. The three reduction notices record 211, 150 and 220 workers but the award never reconciles those figures with each other or with the size of the workforce, and the claimants' individual contracts, wages and dates of dismissal are not stated anywhere in the document, so the substance of the claim cannot be assessed from the face of the award.
