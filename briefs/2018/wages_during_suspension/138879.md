# Award No. 006/18 — First Gawon Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 20 February 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Sin Kim Sean, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 138879

## 1. Facts
The union federation filed complaint No. 140/17 on 12 December 2017 seeking improvement of a series of working conditions at the company's garment factory. Conciliation on 26 January 2018 covered 11 issues, of which 5 remained unsettled and were referred to the Arbitration Council by report No. 986 of 1 February 2018. The panel was constituted on 6 February 2018, but the employer did not attend the hearing of 12 February 2018, and the panel went on site on 13 February 2018 to establish who had padlocked the production buildings while a strike was running. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 27][Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company should pay wages twice a month? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company should organise a fresh election of worker delegates for a first mandate? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay 100% of wages and other benefits for the suspension of work from 24 to 27 March 2017 (group 1: 24 workers, group 6: 25 workers, group 8: 21 workers, and further groups)? [Para 6]
4. Whether the employer must allow workers two months' suspension of work when they have personal matters to attend to? [Para 10]
5. Whether the employer must pay wages and other benefits for the strike day of 11 November 2017? [Para 12]
6. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration at all while a strike and a lockout are in progress? [Para 51]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** pressed all five claims and denied that the January 2018 strike was connected to them, saying the complaint was filed before the strike began; they asserted that December 2017 wages were unpaid and that it was the employer, not the workers, who padlocked the production buildings. [Para 24][Para 30][Para 55]
**Employer (respondent):** did not appear; by telephone it said the company was applying the "old policy", asked for time to consult the owner (on one issue 20 days), and stated that the workers had chained the main gate and locked the production building so that neither the director nor a representative could attend. [Para 3][Para 9][Para 27][Para 28]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 006/18 without hearing the merits and ordered nothing on the five claims. [Para 68]

On the threshold question the panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires parties to suspend any dispute measures such as a strike or lockout (as defined in Article 318 of the Labour Law) during the panel's proceedings and to attend every meeting convened. Following its own earlier awards (No. 5906-Sang Vo, 3908-Chung Yu, 110/11-Simano, 104/13-Sabrina (Cambodia), 322/14-Jim Li (Cambodia) PTE Ltd, 071/15-Umm Wei International Garment Kampong Cham branch, 021/16-Starlight Carrel Manufacturing), the panel read that provision as barring it from continuing whenever workers are striking or the employer is locked out. [Para 51][Para 53][Para 54]

The evidence was contradictory on who had closed the factory: the employer blamed the workers, the workers blamed the employer. Exercising its investigative power under Article 24 of Prakas 099, the panel went to the factory on 13 February 2018 at 9 a.m. without giving the parties advance notice, and the security guard on duty — supported by a shop steward (name redacted) — confirmed that the employer had padlocked every production building and that office staff were not working either. [Para 34][Para 36][Para 38][Para 61]

The panel held that the closure of the whole enterprise by the employer in the course of a pending labour dispute is a lockout within Article 318(2) of the Labour Law, and that it had occurred while the parties already had a case before the Council. Being unable to continue under Article 20, the panel ended the proceedings. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where, at the time it is seised of a collective dispute, the panel finds that the enterprise has been closed by the employer while a labour dispute is pending — a lockout under Article 318(2) of the Labour Law — or that the workers are on strike, Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the panel to suspend its proceedings and terminate them without ruling on the merits, whatever the strength of the underlying claims. [Para 51][Para 65][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statements that Article 24 of Prakas 099 empowers it to investigate the economic situation of the enterprise and to require documents from the parties, and that it may carry out a site investigation without informing the parties in advance; its observation that workers were taking turns guarding the factory for fear the employer would remove machinery, and that the parties declined to attend the site visit. [Para 31][Para 36][Para 39][Para 56][Para 59]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in several OCR variants (ហ្វីស, ហ្វឹស, ហ្វឺស, ហ៊ឹស ហ្គាវ័ន្ត អី ភែរីល), rendered here as First Gawon Apparel (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.; the union's name shows the same variation. OCR corruption is visible at paras 25 ("Datz"), 39 ("LKZZZ"), 47 ("Ka---") and 55 (a Latin-script garbage run inside the Khmer text), and para 66 repeats the phrase "ដំណើរការ សវនាការ បាន ទេ". Para 34 prints "មិន មាន ១០ ភាពស៊ីសង្វាក់" where "no consistency" is plainly meant, and the panel is referred to at para 64 as "ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល ៤០" without explanation. The strike is dated 11 January 2018 at para 30 but issue 5 concerns a strike on 11 November 2017 at para 12; both are reported as printed. Individual worker and union names are given as bare initials here.
