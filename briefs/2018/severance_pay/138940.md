# Award No. 045/18 — Kvan Tum Chhloesing (Cambodia) Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 18 December 2018)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Aing Eng Tong (president) · Doc ID 138940

## 1. Facts
A garment manufacturer at Chom Chao, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, with about 2,383 workers. [Para 50] Its shareholders changed by agreement dated 6 January 2017. [Para 24][Para 56] From October 2018 the company trialled a single "flow line" in place of piece-rate sewing, moving 75 sewers onto it; it ended the trial after the workers protested. [Para 67][Para 113][Para 116] The workers filed three claims on 13 November 2018; one remained unsettled and came to the panel: payment of seniority cut at 6 January 2017 together with notice pay, seniority severance under the amended Article 89 and moral damages. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 102]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay out the workers' seniority as at 6 January 2017, the date of the shareholder-change agreement, and pay the termination benefits claimed: notice pay under Article 75, seniority severance under the new Article 89, and moral damages? [Para 102]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** after the share transfer the employer replaced the management and changed working practice, moving 75 piece-rate sewers onto a flow line that pays only the base wage, without consulting the worker representatives; the change of owner therefore entitles them to a payout of their accrued seniority to 6 January 2017. [Para 56][Para 60][Para 62]

**Employer (respondent):** the flow line is a three-month trial to raise output, and any worker earning less than before would be topped up to the old wage; despite the share transfer it kept every worker's contract, seniority, wage and benefits unchanged, so no severance is due. [Para 67][Para 70][Para 77][Para 110]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the whole claim and ordered no payment. [Para 122][Para 123]

The panel first established that the amended law was in force: Article 87 (new) and the amending instrument were promulgated on 26 June 2018. [Para 102][Para 106] It then read Article 87(1) as providing that where the employer's legal status is altered — by succession, sale, merger, transfer of undertaking or conversion into a company — the employment contracts in force on the date of that change continue as between the new employer and the workers of the former enterprise. [Para 103][Para 107]

Applying that to the facts, both parties accepted that the shareholding had changed; the panel found that the employer had nonetheless maintained the contracts in being and preserved seniority, wages and benefits. [Para 108][Para 110] A change of legal status that leaves the contracts intact does not infringe Article 87(1), and consequently does not trigger a settlement of accrued seniority. [Para 120][Para 121] The claim for notice pay, seniority severance and moral damages therefore lapsed with its premise. [Para 122]

On the flow line, the panel found the trial was a genuine measure to raise output, that workers earning less than their piece rate would be topped up to the previous wage, that the employer would end the trial in the face of the protest, and that 44 of the 75 workers concerned did not want to return to piece-rate work. [Para 117][Para 118][Para 119]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A change in the employer's legal status, including a transfer of shares, does not by itself terminate the employment contracts or mature a claim to accrued seniority: under Article 87 (as amended, in force 26 June 2018) the contracts continue with the new employer, and severance is owed only where the employment relationship is actually severed. [Para 107][Para 110][Para 121]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's findings on the flow-line trial — that a temporary change of production system was within the employer's managerial discretion, that a top-up undertaking protected earnings, and that the workers' own signatures showed a preference for the new system — none of which was necessary to disposing of the severance claim. [Para 117][Para 118]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award nowhere states the sum claimed or any figure in riel or USD, so the value of the dismissed claim cannot be read off the document. The company name is OCR-corrupted into several forms in the body (ខ្វាន់ ទុម ខ្លួស៊ីង, ខ្លួស៊ីង, ខ្ពូ ស៊ីង, ខ្ជាន់ ទុយ), and the same variation affects party and staff names. Paragraph 12 records three claims received while paragraph 13 records only one point unsettled, and the intervening settlement of the other two is not described. The quoted text of Article 87(1) is partly garbled and its promulgating instrument is cited with an unreliable number. Paragraph 102 refers to "the new Article 89" without ever setting out its terms, so the quantum rule the workers invoked is not reproduced.
