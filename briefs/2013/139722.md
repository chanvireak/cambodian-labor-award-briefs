# Award No. 97/13 — Master & Frand (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 7 June 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed Ing Sothy; worker-appointed An Virak; president Pen Bun Chhea) · Doc ID 139722

## 1. Facts
Master & Frand (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a garment manufacturer at Ang Snuol, Kandal, faced eighteen demands from the Samaky Kampuchea Labour Union, covering reinstatement of eight union leaders, wage and allowance increases, bonus rules, toilet access, transfers, medical staffing, contract length and anti-union discrimination. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 16] Conciliation settled ten of the eighteen points on 7 and 8 May 2013, but the workers walked out of the negotiation table, and the employer then declared the whole settlement void and treated all eighteen points as unresolved. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] The panel summoned both sides for 23 May 2013; neither attended and neither gave a valid reason. [Para 31][Para 37][Para 56]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back the eight union leaders and let them work normally? [Para 1]
2. Whether the base wage must rise by USD 20 while the USD 8 incentive bonus is preserved? [Para 3]
3. Whether the transport allowance must rise by USD 10, to USD 17? [Para 4]
4. Whether a lunch allowance of USD 16 per worker per month must be paid? [Para 5]
5. Whether wages and bonuses must be left undeducted for the two-hour break on 12 April 2013 for the Khmer New Year party? [Para 6]
6. Whether the USD 2 incentive bonus must not be deducted when a worker is warned? [Para 7]
7. Whether the group production bonus must be paid according to actual output? [Para 8]
8. Whether the company must stop card and fingerprint scanning for toilet and meal breaks? [Para 9]
9. Whether the outside doors must open after lunch so workers need not wait until 11:40? [Para 10]
10. Whether workers may not be transferred to work outside their skill? [Para 11]
11. Whether a USD 30 technical allowance must be paid equally to mechanics, electricians, cutters and medical staff? [Para 12]
12. Whether team mechanics must receive a bonus equal to their team's workers? [Para 13]
13. Whether the cutting section must receive a bonus per garment cut? [Para 14]
14. Whether medical staff must be present during all working and overtime hours? [Para 15]
15. Whether short-term contracts must be replaced by contracts of at least six months? [Para 16]
16. Whether the company must refrain from discriminating against the union? [Para 17]
17. Whether wages must be paid regularly on the fifth of each month? [Para 18]
A further demand on maintaining wages and benefits appears as an OCR-mangled eighteenth point. [Para 19]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demands sought better conditions, union protection and payment of bonuses already earned; the workers refused to continue conciliation and did not appear at the hearing. [Para 27][Para 53]
**Employer (respondent):** it treated the ten settled points as void once the workers left the table and stood on all eighteen points as unresolved; it also did not appear. Its only filing was a letter of 9 May 2013 asking that strike action be suspended. [Para 28][Para 32][Para 53]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration and closed case 97/13 without deciding any of the eighteen demands; no payment or order was made. [Para 60][Para 61]

The panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules, which allow proceedings to continue or to be closed by award where a duly notified party fails to appear without good cause. [Para 39][Para 40] Reading those provisions with Awards 30/05 Mouria, 116/07 Lotous Ty, 27/07 Im and Ve 3, 95/08 Yong Wah 1, 132/08 GJG, 138/08 Malin, 151/08 Wil Sin, 09/09 Nimbus Tech Thai, 01/10 Kinetix and 30/10 Global Apparel, it identified three cumulative conditions: valid notification, non-appearance, and absence of a valid excuse. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]

It found all three satisfied. The Secretariat issued written invitations No. 503 and No. 504 of 16 May 2013 to the employer and the workers respectively and confirmed the date, time and place by telephone, so notification was valid. [Para 46][Para 48][Para 49] Neither party attended nor appointed a representative. [Para 53][Para 54] Neither explained the absence. [Para 58][Para 59]

The panel also recorded that on the hearing day the employer telephoned the Secretariat to say the parties had agreed on all the disputed points, but no written withdrawal or settlement ever reached the panel before the award issued, so the case could not be closed as settled. [Para 57] Having no evidence on any demand — the claimants having filed nothing — the panel could not make findings of fact. [Para 33][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties are duly notified, neither appears, and neither gives a valid reason, the panel may close the arbitration by award without adjudicating the merits; a telephone assertion that the parties have settled does not terminate the case, because only a written withdrawal or settlement filed with the Secretariat does. [Para 57][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that a settlement reported only by telephone cannot bind it before the award issues, and its remark that the ten points already agreed at conciliation were declared void by the employer rather than by any ruling [Para 28][Para 57].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The document is badly degraded: the heading runs the case number as 97/13 and later "97/13-1", and one line prints "28" where the eighteenth demand should be, so the last demand's content is lost. [Para 19][Para 60] The conciliation arithmetic is internally inconsistent — eighteen points, five settled on 7 May, five more on 7–8 May, ten settled and eight unresolved, yet eighteen were referred as unresolved. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 29] The hearing date is given as 23 May 2013 in the procedure section but the panel elsewhere refers to the invitations of 16 May 2013 and a hearing "at 2 p.m." without date. [Para 23][Para 30] The union's name appears as both សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព ការចារ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព ការងារ កម្ពុជា, and the arbitrator's name is printed both អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី and អ៉ឹង សុទ្ធី. [Para 1][Para 62]
