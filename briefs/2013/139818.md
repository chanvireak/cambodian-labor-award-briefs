# Award No. 130/13 — Canter Apparel (Cambodia) Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 12 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Nan for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 139818

## 1. Facts
Canter Apparel (Cambodia) Ltd. is a garment factory at Trapaing Thleung Village, Veng Sreng Road, Chom Chao, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Four demands were conciliated on 20 June 2013; one was settled and three were referred in non-conciliation report No. 740 of 21 June 2013, received by the Secretariat on 24 June 2013. [Para 15][Para 16] A strike was under way: on 24 June 2013 the employer asked the Council for an order to stop it, and the Council issued order No. 028/13 the same day directing the strikers back to work on the morning of 25 June 2013, an order the workers initially did not obey. [Para 19][Para 20] The hearing was fixed for 8 July 2013 at 2 p.m.; the worker party did not attend and gave no reason beyond being busy. [Para 13][Para 25][Para 57]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay seniority compensation of US$120 per year? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must provide 4,000 riel per day towards the midday meal? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must pay US$12 per month for transport and accommodation? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the three unsettled demands above were maintained — US$120 a year in seniority pay, 4,000 riel a day for lunch, and US$12 a month for transport and lodging [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6]; the workers had been on strike and returned to work after the Council's order was issued. [Para 18][Para 22]
**Employer (respondent):** seniority pay is paid as the Labour Law requires [Para 3]; it has no capacity to fund the daily meal [Para 5]; and it pays US$7 a month for transport and accommodation in line with the Ministry notification, not the US$12 demanded [Para 7]. At the hearing it added that at the 25 June 2013 conciliation the workers had already withdrawn the first demand, but that both sides forgot to record it in the minutes. [Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel found the complaint abandoned and ordered the arbitration in case 130/13 terminated; none of the three demands was decided on the merits and no money was ordered. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]

The panel never reached the substance. It recorded that it could not establish any facts on the demands because the worker party was absent from the hearing without a valid reason. [Para 36][Para 37] It then applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Rules of Procedure, which allow the panel either to proceed in a party's absence or to close the case by award. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] Both are conditioned on the party having been properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 42]

The panel adopted the three-condition test laid down in earlier awards — 30/05-Mourian, 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-IM & V 3, 95/08-Young Va, 132/08-G, 138/08-Malin, 151/08-Wil Sin, 09-Nim Beas Tich Tha Y, 01/10-Kinetic and 30/10-Global Apparel: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid excuse. [Para 43][Para 45][Para 46]

All three were satisfied. The Secretariat invited the workers by letter No. 657 of 1 July 2013, confirmed the date, time and place by telephone the same day, and called again on 8 July 2013 at 2 p.m.; the workers did not collect the letter but were properly informed. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51] They neither appeared nor appointed a representative under Article 19 of the same Prakas. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] Their only explanation, given by telephone on the day, was that they were busy — which the panel held was no valid reason. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]

The panel noted that the workers had been given a full opportunity to bring and defend their claims and had chosen not to take part. [Para 61] It therefore treated the complaint as abandoned and closed the file. [Para 62][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party is properly notified of the hearing by letter and telephone, neither appears nor appoints an authorised representative, and offers only that it is busy, the panel will treat the complaint as abandoned under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 and terminate the arbitration rather than hear the demands in its absence. [Para 42][Para 57][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that it had given the workers a full opportunity to pursue their claims, and that the 25 June 2013 minutes failed to record the withdrawal of the first demand which the employer said had been agreed. [Para 26][Para 61]
