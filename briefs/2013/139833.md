# Award No. 147/13 — Sino Garment Industry Strike Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 01 August 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer, Soung Sophal for the workers, chair Pen Bun Chhea) · Doc ID 139833

## 1. Facts
Sino Garment Industry Strike Co., Ltd, a garment-cutting factory at Samrong village, Ta Khmau district, Kandal, operating since 2011 with about 300 workers, faced 14 demands from the Federation of Union of Workers' Hope (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ក្តីសង្ឃឹមកម្មករ) and its company union, registered No. 2644 on 11 October 2012. [Para 1] [Para 58] [Para 60] Conciliation on 26 June 2013 settled none of the 14; at the hearing the workers withdrew points 1 to 6, and further conciliation settled six more, leaving points 8 and 14 — a canteen, and the rate of the overtime meal allowance. [Para 33] [Para 38] [Para 40]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Panel's issue 8) Whether the employer must build a proper canteen for workers to eat in? [Para 62]
2. (Panel's issue 14) Whether the employer must pay an overtime meal allowance of 1,500 riel per hour, that is 3,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 pm and 6,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 8:00 pm? [Para 68] [Para 90]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that the factory has no canteen at all, so workers eat under the concrete stilts of the building, in the yard and under trees; and that prices have risen and workers do not eat enough, which damages their health, so the meal allowance should rise to 1,500 riel per hour of overtime. [Para 63] [Para 72]

**Employer (respondent):** that it is renovating the factory in stages and the canteen is part of that plan — it is now building a new office block, toilets and a clinic — so it needs a reasonable period and cannot yet fix a date; and that it cannot afford the higher allowance. [Para 64] [Para 65] [Para 67] [Para 73]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — on issue 8 the panel ordered the employer to build a suitable canteen for the workers within six months of the award taking effect; on issue 14 it declined to consider the demand. The award is non-binding. [Para 108] [Para 109] [Para 110]

On issue 8, the panel followed its earlier awards 35/04-Zak Sin Tech, 55/07-Seav Kinh and 81/07-Sub Prim, where a canteen was ordered on the basis of the parties' agreement and the internal rules, and where the absence of a place to eat was treated as affecting workers' health. Here the employer conceded that workers do face difficulty eating and that it intended to build a canteen, and the workers' account of eating under the building and under trees was not contested. The panel accepted that the renovation was staged and that the employer needed time, but an open-ended period was impermissible: the employer had agreed to build the canteen and must therefore fix a reasonable time. Following Award 96/11-Top World, the panel held six months from the date the award takes effect to be that reasonable period. [Para 77] [Para 78] [Para 83] [Para 85] [Para 87] [Para 89]

On issue 14, the panel recorded that the employer already pays 2,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 pm and 4,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 8:00 pm, which is more favourable than point 2 of Ministry Notification No. 041/11 of 07 March 2011 (2,000 riel per day or one free meal). [Para 91] [Para 92] [Para 90] No law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice required 3,000 and 6,000 riel, so the demand went beyond what the law fixes. [Para 93] [Para 94] The panel characterised it as an interests dispute and applied Article 96 of the Labour Law with Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, under which only the most representative union may pursue an interests claim, an interests award standing in place of the collective agreement for one year under Article 43 of Prakas 099. [Para 96] [Para 98] [Para 100] [Para 101] Because the company union was not the most representative in the factory — a point the panel had already found — the federation lacked standing to bring the claim and the panel declined to consider it. [Para 61] [Para 105] [Para 106] [Para 107]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer accepts that it must provide a canteen, it cannot leave the timing at large: the panel will fix a reasonable period, six months being reasonable for construction, because eating in the yard or under the building is a health matter and not merely a comfort. A demand for an overtime meal allowance above the 2,000 riel fixed by Notification 041/11 is an interests claim which only the most representative union in the enterprise may bring. [Para 85] [Para 87] [Para 93] [Para 102]

**Obiter dicta:** that an employer which already pays more than Notification 041/11 requires — here 2,000 riel for two hours and 4,000 riel for four — is not thereby obliged to pay more again on demand. [Para 91] [Para 92]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasons say six of the remaining points were settled at the further conciliation but list only five (7, 9, 10, 12 and 13), point 11 being unaccounted for; the award nevertheless proceeds on points 8 and 14 alone. [Para 39] [Para 40] The panel is said to have been constituted on 12 July "2019 2013", and the award date of 01 August 2013 sits oddly with a hearing on 19 July and evidence deadlines of 22 and 24 July. [Para 35] [Para 42] The chair is printed as Pen Bun Chhea in the header and as "Men Bun Chheam" and "Pen Bun Chhea" in the signature block. [Para 1] [Para 112] The signature block is otherwise destroyed — long runs of Latin OCR noise replace the employer and worker arbitrators' signatures, though their names survive — and stray insertions such as "2015", "2017" and "20006" appear in the decision and header. [Para 111] [Para 112] [Para 109] [Para 1] Individual workers and union officials are reduced to initials under the briefing convention.
