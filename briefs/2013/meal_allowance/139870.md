# Award No. 200/13 — Sun Keng Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 October 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Yu Sounti, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phallak, chair) · Doc ID 139870

## 1. Facts
Sun Keng Garment (Cambodia) is a garment factory on National Road 4 in Samrong Tong district, Kompong Speu. [Para 1] Two federations — the Khmer Workers' Power Federation and the Khmer Free Workers' Federation — filed a joint collective dispute with 19 demands covering sick-leave deductions, meal money, milk money, Pchum Ben advances, the thumbprint system, a USD 15 premium and travel allowance, salary payment before the 8th, anti-union discrimination and the withdrawal of an administrative manager. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 12][Para 18] Conciliation on 19 September 2013 failed on all 19 points. [Para 24] At the hearing on 20 September 2013 the workers' side walked out at 17:10, one by one, without notice or permission, before the session had finished. [Para 30]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel put its own single procedural question, the substantive demands never being reached:
1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration or terminate it, given that the worker party left the hearing room before the proceedings were complete? [Para 55]

The 19 demands are listed in the non-conciliation report but not adjudicated; the panel had narrowed its hearing to demands 9 to 17, and the parties had agreed six of those (12 to 17) without yet signing. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 54]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the Khmer Workers' Power Federation did not appear at all and sent no explanation; the Khmer Free Workers' Federation attended with three federation officials and eight workers, and its representatives left the room while the panel was still deliberating on the demand that wages be preserved during the strike. [Para 29][Para 48][Para 49][Para 54]

**Employer (respondent):** appeared at the hearing as summoned and, on the strike-wage demand, asked for time to consider and reply; the strike failed for that reason. [Para 28][Para 54]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — no decision on the merits; the panel terminated the arbitration in case 200/13 and closed the file. [Para 81][Para 82]

The panel framed the question under Prakas 099. Article 18 requires the panel to summon the parties to present their claims in person and to supply the necessary documents [Para 55]; Article 20 requires the parties to attend every session to which they are summoned [Para 56]; and Article 21 lets the panel either proceed in the absence of a party who fails to appear without good reason or terminate the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 57] Rule 4.7 of the annex to the same prakas repeats that choice. [Para 58] Whichever route is taken, the panel must first be satisfied that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 59]

Applying that to the facts, the panel found notification proper: invitation 1014 of 18 September 2013 went to both federations and to the workers' representatives, naming the hearing, its venue and the contact numbers. [Para 45][Para 64] Mr S Huot, treasurer of the Khmer Workers' Power Federation, received it on 19 September 2013 at 08:44 and signed for it. [Para 46][Para 65] The first condition was therefore met. [Para 67]

The second condition — failure to appear — was also met, and the panel gave "appearance" a two-limb meaning drawn from Article 21: presence at the hearing and participation in the proceedings until they end. [Para 68] It followed its own award 115/06, which held that leaving the hearing room before the arbitration starts counts as non-appearance or as having no representative in the proceedings. [Para 69] It also followed award 27/07, which held that the panel may not decide disputes other than those recorded in the non-conciliation report. [Para 70] Here the worker party attended only stages (a) and (c) and did not complete stage (d), and neither federation asked for an adjournment. [Para 74][Para 75] The panel found the worker party had not appeared within the meaning of Article 21. [Para 76][Para 77]

The third condition — no valid reason — was met too, since the party left without notice to the panel or the employer and gave no explanation. [Para 78][Para 79] All three conditions being satisfied, the panel terminated the arbitration under Article 21 and rule 4.7 rather than proceeding in absentia. [Para 80][Para 81]

The panel noted that the six agreed points had not been signed and the remaining points had not been heard on the merits, so nothing was enforceable and nothing was decided on the 19 demands. [Para 54]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Attendance at an arbitration hearing means both presence and participation to the end; a party that leaves the room mid-hearing without notice or an adjournment request has failed to appear without valid reason, and the panel may then terminate the arbitration rather than decide the claims in its absence. [Para 68][Para 76][Para 80]

The panel may not decide any dispute not recorded in the non-conciliation report. [Para 70]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified — the panel decided nothing on the merits of the 19 demands. [Para 82]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award date is printed 10 October 2013 in the header but the reasons refer to 2013 throughout with OCR damage at the reconstitution date, which reads "16 September 2019 2013" and "16 September 2013" in the same line. [Para 26] The federation names are inconsistent: "Khmer Workers' Power Federation" and "Khmer Free Workers' Federation" on the face, but the agreement document at line 32 names four different federations (Smorotei, Youth Voice, Labour of Cambodia, Labour for Cambodia). [Para 32] The invitation numbers are printed as 1013 for the employer and 1014 for the workers, and the telephone number appears as both 023 881 824 and 855-23 881 824. [Para 2][Para 42][Para 45] Line 50 states the hearing covered demands 9 to 17 but describes them as "09 to 17" in one place and "9 to 17" in another, and the operative decision section carries only "decide and order" with no numbered orders. [Para 82] The signature block is heavily degraded. [Para 82]
