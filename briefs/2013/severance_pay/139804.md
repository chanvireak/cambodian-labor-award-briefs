# Award No. 115/13 — Harta Park K Jing In Das Stri (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Ang Eng Tong, chair) · Doc ID 139804

## 1. Facts
Workers at the garment factory went on strike demanding that the company terminate their employment contracts and pay the compensation due under the Labour Law, on the ground that the factory had changed owner and changed its logo and uniforms from "HP" to "OSI". [Para 2][Para 10] The company denied that either the owner or the company name had changed. [Para 3] Twelve workers had asked the national trade-union federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ជាតិ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា) to take up their working conditions. [Para 16] Conciliation resolved one of the two demands; the single unresolved demand came to the Council on 7 June 2013. [Para 12][Para 13]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must terminate the workers' employment contracts and pay severance and other compensation under the Labour Law because it changed owner and changed the company logo from HP to OSI? [Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The company changed owner and changed the uniform and logo from HP to OSI, so the contracts should be terminated and the statutory compensation paid. [Para 2] The demanding workers did not attend the second hearing. [Para 52]
**Employer (respondent):** There was no change of owner and no change of company name; the corporate documents, internal rules and patent certificate filed with the panel were produced to show continuity. [Para 3][Para 30][Para 37] By the second hearing the employer stated it had already negotiated a settlement with the demanding workers and that those workers had resigned. [Para 25]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel declined to decide the demand on the merits and terminated the arbitration of Case No. 115/13, without any order for compensation. [Para 55][Para 56]

The panel began from Article 312(1) of the Labour Law, which limits the Council to the issues stated in the non-conciliation report, or issues arising afterwards as a consequence of that dispute. [Para 47][Para 48] The only non-conciliated issue was the demand for contract termination and compensation sent to the Council on 7 June 2013 in report No. 666. [Para 53] At the second hearing the employer asserted, and the federation's representative did not contest, that the employer had settled that demand directly with the workers who raised it, and that those workers had resigned and no longer worked at the company. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 49][Para 50] The federation also said it no longer held a mandate from those workers, since a union can only pursue a demand for workers still employed. [Para 28] On that basis the panel found the dispute had been resolved by agreement between the parties, leaving no non-conciliated issue for it to decide. [Para 51][Para 53][Para 54] It recorded that it could not establish the facts of the dispute because the workers who made the demand did not appear at the second hearing. [Para 46] The proceedings were therefore closed rather than decided. [Para 55][Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The panel decides only the issues listed in the non-conciliation report and issues arising after it as a consequence of that dispute; where the parties have settled the sole listed issue by agreement and the workers who raised it have left the employer, there is nothing left to arbitrate and the panel must terminate the proceedings instead of ruling on the demand. [Para 48][Para 53][Para 54]

**Obiter dicta:** A union's standing to pursue a collective demand depends on the workers concerned still being employed at the enterprise; the federation accepted it had lost its mandate once those workers resigned. [Para 28] The panel also observed that a party's absence from the merits hearing leaves it without a factual basis to decide the demand. [Para 46]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The document is the Khmer original and, unusually for this library, no English translation exists; quotations here are translated by the brief's author. The employer's name appears only in Khmer transliteration (ហាតា ផាក ខេ ជីង អ៊ិន ដាស ស្ទ្រី) and is rendered phonetically above; no Latin-script form is printed anywhere in the award. [Para 1] The worker-appointed arbitrator is printed "លីវ សុវណ្ណា" on the face of the award and "លឹម សុវណ្ណា" at the signatures, i.e. Liv or Lim Sovanna; both forms are reported. [Para 1][Para 57] The date of award is 15 July 2013 in the header, yet the reasons record the parties' agreement deferring issuance from 1 July to 19 July 2013; both dates are printed and neither reconciles with the other. [Para 1][Para 29] The company is referred to in some passages as "Harta" alone and the logo is also written "HP" and "OSI" without further explanation of either mark. [Para 31][Para 34] Sentence numbering in the evidence section is duplicated and broken (two items numbered "១" under the ministry's documents, run-on numerals such as "១៥ ១១៥/១៣"), and stray OCR artefacts ("Ji", "A", bracketed digits) interrupt sentences 22, 46 and 55.
