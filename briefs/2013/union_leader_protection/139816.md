# Ruling No. 129/13 — Global Apparel Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 12 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Nan for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 139816

## 1. Facts
Global Apparel Ltd. is a garment factory at Trayeung Krasaing Village, Chom Chao Commune, Pou Senchey District, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Ten demands went to conciliation; five were settled on 17 June 2013 and five were not, producing non-conciliation report No. 742 KB/AK/VK of 21 June 2013, received by the Secretariat on 24 June 2013. [Para 20][Para 21] A hearing was fixed for 28 June 2013 at 14:00 and the worker party was invited by letter No. 625 of 24 June 2013, signed for by a federation officer on 25 June 2013 at 9:08 a.m. [Para 33][Para 44] The factory union did not appear; its president and secretary had resigned, and its remaining vice-president, Ms S Vanna, sent no written authorisation and pleaded illness. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 37][Para 38]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must raise the base wage by US$14 per month above what each worker already receives? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must pay a bonus to workers who work only 8 hours a day? [Para 6]
3. Whether the employer must provide one midday meal every day? [Para 8]
4. Whether the employer must pay US$15 per month in transport costs? [Para 10]
5. Whether the employer must refrain from deducting wages and the attendance bonus during a strike? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the five unsettled demands above were maintained before the panel; the factory union was the complainant but its president and secretary had resigned, leaving only the vice-president, who was ill. [Para 3][Para 10][Para 23][Para 37]
**Employer (respondent):** it already pays base wages no lower than the law requires, at about US$85 a month for roughly 80% of the workforce [Para 4][Para 5]; the bonus follows the old principle that only workers reaching 10 hours and meeting the quota earn the incentive [Para 7]; it has no capacity to provide a daily meal or the US$15 transport payment [Para 9][Para 11]; and it pays no wage or attendance bonus to workers who strike [Para 13].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel held the complainant to have abandoned its complaint and ordered the termination of the arbitration proceedings in case 129/13. Nothing was decided on the merits and no money was ordered. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]

The panel never reached the five demands. It recorded that it could not establish the facts on the disputed issues because the factory union leadership that filed the complaint did not attend. [Para 35] Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 lets a party appear in person or through an authorised representative, and "authorised in writing" means the representative must hold the party's written authorisation; the panel followed its earlier awards in Case 161/09 (Prek Treng Handicraft), 43/10 (Meung Chia) and 144/12 (E-Garment). [Para 56][Para 57] Here neither the Secretary-General nor the other federation officials held any written authorisation or transfer of rights from Ms S Vanna or from the other workers. [Para 58]

The panel then applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council procedural rules, each of which allows the panel either to continue in the party's absence or to close the proceedings by ruling. [Para 39][Para 40] Those provisions are conditioned on the party having been properly notified of the date, time and place, and the panel adopted the three-condition test it had already laid down in Cases 16/07 (Lotus), 27/07 (IM & V 3), 138/08 (Malin), 11/11 (Chun Tech) and 131/12 (Master & Freng): proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid reason for the non-appearance. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43]

All three conditions were met. The invitation was served and signed for on 25 June 2013, three days before the hearing. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 59] The worker party did not appear, only federation officials attended. [Para 46][Para 50] No valid reason was given: the Secretary-General said only that Ms S Vanna was ill, without saying what illness, and she had three days in which to issue a written authorisation and did not. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 60] The panel therefore treated the complaint as abandoned and closed the file. [Para 61][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the complainant party is properly invited and fails to appear without a valid reason, and no representative holds its written authorisation, the panel will treat the complaint as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by ruling rather than decide the demands on the merits; a federation official's attendance without written authorisation from the local union or the individual workers does not constitute appearance by the complainant. [Para 42][Para 57][Para 58][Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the three-condition test derives from its consistent earlier interpretation of Article 21, and that the invitation expressly stated the date, time and place of the hearing — recited as proof of proper notification rather than as a rule. [Para 42][Para 59]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Khmer-language original with no English version; party and arbitrator names are transliterated here, and the claimant's vice-president is reduced to "Ms S Vanna" although the award prints her full name. [Para 53]
- The tribunal is said to have been constituted on "24 June 2022 2013" — a stray year inserted before the correct one. [Para 22]
- "Mr P Tori" at [Para 3] appears as "Mr C Tori" in the hearing list at [Para 23]; the federation officer who signed for the invitation is printed "Pen Chan Rith" at [Para 44] and "Pen Chan Rithi" at [Para 26]. Names are redacted in this brief.
- The company name is printed "Global Apparel Ltd." in the title and "ក្លូប័ល អា ប៉ារែលី មីធីត" in the body, with several further variants across the text. [Para 1][Para 23][Para 63]
- The case number is printed "129/13" in the header but "129/13-1" in the operative paragraph. [Para 1][Para 62]
- The award never states the merits of the five demands, so the file contains no findings on them at all. [Para 35]
