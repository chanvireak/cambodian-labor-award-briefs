# Award No. 46/13 — Cam F Ever Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 March 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Ang Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 139662

## 1. Facts
Cam F Ever Co., Ltd ("Khem F Ever") is a garment company at Steung Mean Chey, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh. [Para 1][Para 36] The Cambodian Workers' Spirit Federation lodged 22 demands on 5 November 2012; conciliation resolved all but seven, which reached the panel. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25] The employer-appointed arbitrator was absent from the first hearing for personal reasons, so a second hearing was set for 12 March 2013, at which the claimant union did not appear. [Para 28][Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The seven unresolved demands were, in the conciliation report's numbering:
1. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 15 a month? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must pay a "prim" (attendance incentive) of USD 15 a month? [Para 5]
3. Whether the base wage of every worker must be raised to USD 70? [Para 7]
4. Whether the employer must add 1,000 riel for each hour of overtime worked, on top of the existing meal money? [Para 9]
5. Whether special leave must be granted under the Labour Law with immediate five-day payment charged against the annual leave entitlement? [Para 11]
6. Whether the infirmary must be moved to the ground floor, closer to the workers? [Para 13]
7. Whether wages must be advanced three months to pregnant workers who cross the river, and calculated on the whole of their wage? [Para 15]

The panel reached none of them, terminating the arbitration on the claimant's non-appearance. [Para 88][Para 90]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demands were maintained in full at conciliation; on the day of the second hearing the union's local president was said to be ill and unable to attend, and no federation official was available either. [Para 3][Para 29][Para 31]
**Employer (respondent):** it complies with Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 on the travel and "prim" allowances, applies its existing practice on overtime meal money, cannot meet the base-wage, infirmary or maternity-advance demands, and grants special leave only as the law requires. [Para 4][Para 10][Para 12][Para 16]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel held the claimant had abandoned its complaint and terminated the arbitration; no order was made on any of the seven demands. [Para 88][Para 90][Para 91]

The panel proceeded under article 21 of Prakas 099 of 21 April 2004 and rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council's procedural rules, which allow the panel to continue or to close the case and issue an award where a properly summoned party fails to appear, provided the party was validly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] Following awards 16/07 Lotus, 27/07 Im and Vey 3, 138/08 Malin, 11/11 Choun Tich Thai and 131/12 Master & Frank, it applied the three conditions those awards derive: valid summons, non-appearance, and absence of a valid excuse. [Para 68][Para 69]

Condition 1 was satisfied: the second hearing was notified both orally after the first hearing and by invitation 260 K.B/A.K/V.K/L.K.A. of 6 March 2013, signed for by the UFIWR president. [Para 70][Para 73][Para 74] Condition 2 was satisfied on the panel's reading of "appearing before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction, explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation, and hearing on the merits — none of which the claimant attended. [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78][Para 79]

Condition 3 turned on the excuse. The federation president telephoned to say the local union president was ill but could not say what illness it was, and gave no reason why nobody else attended, when the local union had three office-holders legally entitled to represent their members and two of them could have come. [Para 80][Para 84][Para 85] The panel also noted the postponement request had not been made in writing before the hearing began. [Para 60][Para 64] It found no valid reason and treated the complaint as abandoned. [Para 83][Para 86][Para 88]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant has been validly summoned, does not appear, and offers no substantiated excuse, the panel treats the complaint as abandoned and closes the case without ruling on the demands. An assertion that one local officer is ill is not a valid excuse where the union has other officers who could attend and the claimant cannot particularise the illness or explain their absence. [Para 68][Para 84][Para 85]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's four-stage description of an arbitration hearing, and its remark that the two federations in this case sit under a single national confederation, are general statements not necessary to the termination. [Para 32][Para 76]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name appears in at least five forms — Cam F Ever, Khem F Ever, "Khem Ho Ever", "Cam Ho Ever" and "Cambodia Ho Ever" — and one paragraph of the findings is printed as an unrelated fragment. [Para 1][Para 23][Para 57][Para 90]
- The union acronym is given as UFIWR at [Para 3] and UFTWR at [Para 70], and the federation is named in three different Khmer renderings; the local union president's name is lost to "Allander" at [Para 61], an OCR substitution, so it cannot be reported.
- The non-conciliation report is dated 21 February 2013 at [Para 19] but 19 February 2013 at [Para 25], and the conciliation minute 14 February 2013.
- The panel's invitation to the first hearing reads "6 March 2019 22 2013" [Para 26], and the paragraph numbering in the demand list loses a numeral at [Para 8].
- The award disposes of no substantive issue, so the seven demands are reported from the conciliation list and not from the panel's own findings; the reasons for the employer's refusals are recorded only in those recitals. [Para 4][Para 16]
