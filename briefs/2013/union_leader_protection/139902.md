# Order No. 248/13 — Glad New Electronic (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 26 December 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · Arbitrators Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Taing Tri (chair) · Doc ID 139902

## 1. Facts
Glad New Electronic (Cambodia), a factory in Phnom Penh, faced a strike from 1 November 2013 over 15 demands covering union discrimination, wage payment, attendance bonus, transport and food allowances, night-shift pay, medical care, overtime, and the treatment of union leaders. [Para 2][Para 24] Conciliation failed and the 15 non-conciliated issues were referred to the Secretariat on 13 November 2013. [Para 25][Para 26] The strike continued, and on 20 November 2013 the panel issued order 045/13 requiring the strike to be suspended and all workers to return from 21 November. [Para 27][Para 28]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the Arbitration Council could continue its arbitration process in this case? [Para 60]
2. Whether the parties' conduct — the continued strike, mutual accusations and failure to file evidence — showed an intention to resolve the dispute? [Para 73][Para 81]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they had ended the strike and returned to work, but the employer gave them no work. [Para 39][Para 70]
**Employer (respondent):** the workers continued the strike and refused to work, and some had drained water in the toilets and broken bowls, toilets and knife-sharpening stones; the employer had video evidence. [Para 37][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration process in Case 248/13. [Para 82][Para 83]
Clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures such as strikes or lockouts and to attend all meetings convened by the panel. [Para 60][Para 61] The strike continued after referral, in breach of order 045/13. [Para 27][Para 29] The panel convened a pre-hearing meeting on 27 November and hearings on 5 and 12 December 2013, ordered both parties to file evidence by 11 December, and received none. [Para 30][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43] The parties made contradictory accusations — the workers saying the employer provided no work, the employer saying the workers refused to work — and neither proved its case. [Para 79][Para 80] The panel found this showed no goodwill to resolve the dispute and that the situation was worsening. [Para 73][Para 81] Having found no facts on the 15 issues, it terminated the process under Clause 20. [Para 59][Para 82]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the parties fail to suspend strike action as ordered and fail to submit evidence, and instead exchange unproven accusations, the panel may find no intention to resolve the dispute and terminate the arbitration process under Clause 20 of Prakas No. 099. [Para 60][Para 81][Para 82]

**Obiter dicta:** the party that makes an assertion bears the burden of proof. [Para 76]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed three ways: "គ្លែត ញូស៍", "ភ្លែត ញូសំ" and "ត្អែត ញូស៏" (Glad New Electronic (Cambodia)). [Para 1][Para 49] The award date is 26 December 2013 in the header, but the parties agreed to defer issuance to 27 December 2013. [Para 1][Para 47] The award records the complaint as received on 1 November 2013 and the final conciliation on 7 November 2013, while the non-conciliation report is dated 13 November 2013. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] OCR losses include "បេន្ត" for "បន្ត" and "ចេច" for "ចេញ". [Para 47][Para 60]
