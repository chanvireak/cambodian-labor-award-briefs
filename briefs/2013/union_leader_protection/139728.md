# Award No. 107/13 — Sun Well Shoes Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 June 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, employer-chosen; An Nan, worker-chosen; Kong Phallack, chair) · Doc ID 139728

## 1. Facts
Sun Well Shoes Co., Ltd is a shoe manufacturer registered under a corporate charter of 22 May 2008 and business registration certificate No. 3940 of 24 August 2010, employing 1,200 workers at Cham Chao, Pur Senchey, Phnom Penh. [Para 21][Para 23][Para 24] The claimant is the local union of the Workers Union Federation (WUF), whose new leadership was recognised by the Department of Labour Disputes in letter No. 1080 of 13 September 2012. [Para 25][Para 26] The union filed fifteen demands on 3 May 2013; five remained non-conciliated on 16 May 2013, and at the hearing of 6 June 2013 issue 1 (a USD 10 payment in lieu of milk formula for new mothers) was settled, issues 2, 3 and 5 were withdrawn, leaving only issue 4. [Para 7][Para 9][Para 13][Para 14] Team supervisors receive a base wage of USD 80 — the same as ordinary workers — plus a position bonus of USD 85 a month granted on promotion for good performance, with no change to the written contract. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 36][Para 55][Para 56]

## 2. Issues in dispute
4. Whether, in calculating overtime pay for team supervisors, the employer must use the sum of the base wage and the position bonus, divided by 26, rather than the base wage alone? [Para 27][Para 39][Para 68]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** a team supervisor should not earn less than a non-supervisor, other factories do not separate the position bonus from the base wage, and the bonus is not severable from the base wage because the supervisor exercises that position and competency to meet the employer's objectives. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 32] In the example raised, Mr S receives the same USD 80 base wage as ordinary workers plus a USD 85 position bonus, yet only the base wage enters the overtime calculation. [Para 33][Para 34]
**Employer (respondent):** it takes only the base wage into account for overtime, whether for workers or team supervisors, and provides the position bonus in addition to a base wage equal to that of normal workers; the head of administration conceded he was unclear how the position bonus relates to the base wage, and said the employer fixes the bonus amount, base wage alone having always been used in his time at the company. [Para 27][Para 38][Para 39]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel orders the employer to take the sum of the base wage and the position bonus as the basis for calculating overtime pay for team supervisors. [Para 68][Para 70] The award is a binding award under the Memorandum of Understanding on Improving Industrial Relations in the Garment Industry, dated 3 October 2012 in the text and 28 September 2010 in the order. [Para 15][Para 18][Para 70]

The panel first classified the demand as a rights dispute, because it concerns the interpretation and enforcement of law: Article 312(2) of the Labour Law gives it jurisdiction over such disputes and leaves other disputes to equity, and Clause 43 of Prakas 099 provides that an interest award stands in place of a collective agreement, following Awards 05/11-M & V (Branch 1), 13/11-Gold Kamvimex, 14/11-GXG, 31/11-Quint Major Industrial and 62/11-Ocean Garment. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 43][Para 44] The demand turned on new Article 139 of the Law on Amendment of Articles 139 and 144 of the Labour Law of 20 July 2007 and Clause 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999 on overtime work, which fix premium rates of 150 per cent for normal working days and 200 per cent at night. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 50]

Neither text says whether the base wage is the calculation base. [Para 48][Para 50] The panel found that Clause 5 uses only the phrase "wages during normal working hours" and does not define it, so the meaning of that phrase was decisive. [Para 51][Para 52] Article 102 of the Labour Law defines "wage" broadly as the remuneration for employment or service, convertible in cash and fixed by agreement or legislation, owed under a written or oral contract for work done or to be done; the panel read the spirit of that definition as remuneration paid in exchange for a particular work performance. [Para 53]

Applying that to the facts, the panel found the position bonus is additional wage paid to workers holding the team-supervisor position in exchange for the daily performance of assigned work, granted on top of the base wage and without any condition written into the contract beyond fulfilling the position. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59] It followed Award 74/08-Generation, Issue 13, that a monthly skill bonus which workers come to expect as part of the benefits package for performing their work counts as part of wages paid in normal working hours, and Award 78/13-G Formost, Issue 4, on the same point. [Para 59][Para 61][Para 62] It saw no difference in nature between a skill bonus and a position bonus, both being paid as compensation for the skill or responsibility a worker exercises, and held both to be an indivisible element of the wage. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]

The panel therefore concluded that "wage during normal working hours" in Clause 5 of Prakas No. 80 includes both the base wage and the position or skill bonus, so the employer must use their sum as the overtime base. [Para 65][Para 66] Since the employer in fact used the base wage alone for everyone, worker and supervisor alike, the practice was wrong and the order followed. [Para 67][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
"Wages during normal working hours" in Clause 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999 includes the base wage together with any position or skill bonus paid regularly for performing the job, because such a bonus is an indivisible element of remuneration under Article 102 of the Labour Law; overtime for a team supervisor must therefore be calculated on the sum, not on the base wage alone. [Para 53][Para 65][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the employer reserves the power to set the amount of the position bonus, and that a bonus of this kind is not made conditional on any particular monthly performance or target in the written contract. [Para 38][Para 59][Para 60]
