# Award No. 17/13 — International Fashion Royal (Arbitration Council, 30 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Tang Tri, chair) · arbitration not continued · Doc ID 139601

## 1. Facts
Workers at International Fashion Royal, represented by the Cambodian Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation and its local union, struck from 11 January 2013 over six demands on wages, allowances, leave, attendance bonus, reinstatement of five dismissed workers and withdrawal of complaints against union leaders. Conciliation failed on 17 January 2013 and the six issues were referred under non-conciliation report No. 092 dated 18 January 2013. The panel issued order No. 002/13 on 21 January 2013 directing the strike to stop and all workers to return to their shifts from the afternoon of 22 January 2013; the strike continued, and at a meeting on 24 January 2013 the workers confirmed it would go on. No hearing was held and no evidence was taken. [Para 19][Para 21][Para 15][Para 25][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must raise the basic wage to US$75 a month? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of US$15 a month? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must grant special leave, chargeable against remaining annual leave, without cutting bonuses and other incentives? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must reduce the attendance bonus in proportion to the days of lawful absence taken by workers? [Para 7]
5. Whether the company must take back the five named workers into employment? [Para 9]
6. Whether the company must stop lodging complaints against the leaders of the Cambodian Workers' Spirit Trade Union? [Para 11]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union members maintained all six demands and, at the 24 January 2013 meeting, confirmed that the strike would continue; they did not comply with the panel's order to suspend it and return to work. [Para 25][Para 42]
**Employer (respondent):** On the basic wage it said it applied Notification No. 230 dated 25 July 2012 of the Ministry of Labour; on each of the other five demands it said only that it would take the request for discussion with the factory management. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case 17/13, so none of the six demands was examined and nothing was ordered on the merits. [Para 43][Para 44]

The only question the panel reached was whether to continue at all. It cited clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 on the Arbitration Council, which requires the parties, while the panel is sitting, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout (as contemplated by Article 318 of the Labour Law) or any other measure that could aggravate the situation, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 32][Para 33]

The panel read that clause as meaning it will not continue an arbitration while the workers are on strike, a reading it had already adopted in earlier awards. In case 04/04 (IMI) it issued a notice dated 29 January 2004 stating that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during the panel's proceedings, that the panel cannot settle the dispute or issue an award in that situation, and that it may take the case up again once all the workers stop striking. In case 18/04 (hotels: Cambodiana, Rafles Le Royal, Raffles Grand Angkor, Sunway, Intercontinental and Sofitel Angkor) its second order of 22 April 2004 directed that from 23 April 2004 all workers stop striking, demonstrating or disturbing the hotel premises, and reserved the right to terminate the proceedings if a party breached the order. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]

It also relied on case 77/06 (Frankonia? printed "ហ្វ្រង់កូ នីត ធីង"), whose second order of 24 December 2005 stated at point 4 that where the workers continued to strike the panel would not proceed with the collective dispute, and on case 110/11 (Simano), where it likewise declined to continue for the same reason. The panel said it agreed with those decisions. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]

On the facts, the workers were still striking and were refusing to obey the panel's order, so the conditions for continuing were not met and the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in this case. Its formal decision repeats that outcome and nothing more. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
While a strike continues in defiance of the panel's order to suspend it, the panel will not continue the arbitration or rule on the demands, because clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the parties to suspend all dispute measures and attend the panel's meetings during proceedings; the dispute may be taken up again once the strike ends. [Para 32][Para 36][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the recital that the employer said it would refer five of the six demands to factory management for discussion, and that on the basic wage it relied on Notification No. 230 dated 25 July 2012 — recorded without any finding. [Para 4][Para 10]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a Khmer original with no English version, and the employer's name is transliterated five ways within the document ("អ៊ិនធើណាស្យូណាល់", "អ៊ីនធើណា", "អ៊ិន ធើណាល់", "អ៊ិន ធើណា ស្យូណាល់"), while the final order prints the case name with stray characters. Paragraph 44 carries a stray figure "47" and OCR debris around the signatures, and the worker-appointed arbitrator is printed "អន ណន" on the face but "វន ណន" at the signature. The five dismissed workers are named in the issue list and are redacted here. The six demands are set out only in the non-conciliation report; the panel recorded that it made no findings of fact because it held no hearing. [Para 1][Para 9][Para 31][Para 44]
