# Award No. 236/13 — Alim (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 November 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139893

## 1. Facts
Alim (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a garment factory in Phnom Penh, faced five unsettled demands from the Khmer Workers' Movement Union: removal of a Korean sewing supervisor, of the administration head, and of the group leaders of groups one and three, plus a doubled overtime meal allowance and a daily lunch allowance. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10]
Conciliation on 30 October 2013 settled three of eight points; the five remaining points came to the Council on 31 October 2013 and the hearing was held on 7 November 2013. [Para 14][Para 18][Para 19][Para 23]
After the hearing, about 40–50 per cent of the workforce struck from 11 November 2013 over the same demands; the panel ordered the strike stopped and a return to work from the morning of 13 November 2013. [Para 25][Para 28][Para 31]
On 15 November 2013 both parties confirmed the strike was still continuing past that date. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 64][Para 65]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must remove the Korean sewing supervisor from the factory? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must remove the head of administration? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must remove the group leaders of groups one and three? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must raise the overtime meal allowance from 2,000 riel to 4,000 riel for the hours 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel per day? [Para 10]
6. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all, given the strike staged after the hearing? [Para 50]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union maintained all five demands and, through its federation officer, confirmed by telephone that the strike was continuing and concerned four of the demands in this case. [Para 2][Para 31]
**Employer (respondent):** the company could not remove the supervisor, the administration head or the two group leaders, and had no capacity to grant either meal allowance; it asked the panel to order all workers back to work. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 40]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case 236/13-Alim (Cambodia), leaving all five demands undetermined. [Para 67][Para 68]
The panel framed the only question it would answer as whether the arbitration should proceed, since it had heard the case on 7 November 2013 but the workers struck afterwards and the award was due on 26 November 2013. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 61]
It applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires parties to suspend all dispute measures such as strikes during the panel's proceedings, and Article 318 of the Labour Law to which that clause refers; the parties must attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. [Para 51][Para 52]
Following its own earlier practice in cases 04/04-MSI, 18/04-Hotel (Cambodiana), 77/05-France Nitting and 110/11-Simano, the panel held that it will not continue arbitration while workers are striking during the proceedings, and that it may resume only when the strike ends. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60]
Because the strike began after the hearing, continued past the 13 November 2013 return date fixed in order No. 044/13 and was still running on 15 November 2013, the panel also held it could not rely on the facts it had found at the hearing, and discontinued. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 49][Para 66][Para 67]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a union strikes during the panel's proceedings, after the hearing and against an order to return to work, the panel will decline to continue the arbitration and will not decide the merits on facts found at the hearing; the case may be taken up again only once the strike has ended. [Para 55][Para 60][Para 67]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's reference to the 40–50 per cent participation reported by the employer's general manager was not tested and played no part in the decision. [Para 31]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The evidence section, headed "អង្គហេតុ", is displaced: it begins at para 49 with the strike narrative rather than with findings, so the facts on the five demands are never reached. [Para 49]
Dates are printed corruptly in several places: the panel is said to have been formed on "01 November 2 2013", the phone record is dated "ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣ ២០១៣", and the employer's strike report ends "ថ្ងៃទី ១៥១៩ ខែវិច្ឆិកា". [Para 20][Para 30][Para 33]
Order No. 044/13 of 12 November 2013 is printed as "លេខ ១០ ០៤៤/១៣" at para 63, and stray "2017" tokens appear in paras 44 and 63. [Para 44][Para 63]
The signature block at para 69 is largely destroyed by OCR, with only the chair's name, Pen Bun Chhea, legible; the employer and worker arbitrators' names appear on the face of the award but their signatures are unreadable. [Para 1][Para 69]
