# Award No. 67/13 — Mekong Footwear (Arbitration Council, 25 March 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139679

## 1. Facts
Mekong Footwear, a shoe factory at Kraing Kam, Tbaeng, Kandal Stueng district, Kandal province, faced a strike by its workers from 15 March 2013 over 15 claims on wages and conditions, of which six were not conciliated. [Para 2][Para 19][Para 21] The workers' representatives did not attend the pre-hearing meeting; the employer's director and administrative officer did. [Para 2][Para 17] The panel issued Order No. 011/13 of 20 March 2013 requiring the strike at Branch 2 to be suspended immediately and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 21 March 2013, and the workers did not comply. [Para 22][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to raise the basic wage by USD 80 per month? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer is obliged to pay a transport and accommodation allowance of USD 15 per month? [Para 5]
3. Whether the employer is obliged to install additional card-scanning machines? [Para 7]
4. Whether the employer is obliged to pay a regular attendance bonus of USD 15 per month? [Para 8]
5. Whether, when the company bus breaks down and no replacement collects them, absent workers must keep their wages, bonus and other benefits? [Para 9]
6. Whether the employer is obliged to raise the basic wage of every section team leader to USD 100 per month? [Para 11]

The panel decided none of these. It set itself one threshold question instead: whether the arbitration proceedings should continue at all. [Para 32][Para 43]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the federation filed the complaint after the strike began, seeking improved working conditions; it did not appear at the pre-hearing meeting and did not comply with the order to suspend the strike, which continued at Branch 2. [Para 18][Para 2][Para 23]

**Employer (respondent):** it asked the panel to apply its existing scale — USD 61 for probationary workers and USD 66 for workers who have completed probation — and offered USD 9 of the USD 15 travel claim and USD 11 of the USD 15 attendance-bonus claim, refusing the rest. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12] It also wrote on 16 March 2013 asking the panel to have the workers end the strike. [Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in Case No. 67/13, leaving all six claims undetermined. [Para 43][Para 44]

The panel grounded the decision on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every contentious measure, including strikes or lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, while the panel is sitting, and to attend all meetings to which it summons them. [Para 32] It read that article, as earlier panels had, to mean that the Council will not proceed with arbitration while the workers are striking. [Para 34] It followed Award 04/04 ISI, where an award of 29 January 2004 held that a party has no right to strike during its own arbitration and that the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on such a dispute, though it may reopen the case once all unions cease the strike. [Para 35][Para 36] It also followed Award 18/04 against a group of hotels, which ordered the strike and any disturbance on hotel premises stopped from 23 April 2004 and reserved the right to terminate proceedings if a party breached the order, and Award 77/06 Franco Nithing, which declined to proceed where the strike continued. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39] Awards 110/11 Simano, 17/13 Internal Fashion Royal and 49/13 Eternity Global Sporting had reached the same result. [Para 40] Because the workers here were still striking and would not obey the order, the panel declined to proceed and made no findings of fact, since no hearing had been held. [Para 31][Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not continue the arbitration or rule on the merits; it closes the case and may reopen it only if the strike ends. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it retains the power to reopen the dispute once all unions stop striking is not part of the order, which simply declines to proceed. [Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The arbitrator chosen by the employer party is named "Ing Sothy" on the face of the award [Para 2] but signs as "Din Sody Bai" [Para 45]; the two cannot both be right and neither name is expanded.
- The non-conciliation report is dated 19 March 2013 in the procedural history [Para 15] and in the evidence list [Para 25], but 18 March 2013 at [Para 20], where its number is also corrupted ("200/13" and "20%13").
- Conciliation is placed on 15 March 2013 [Para 19], the same day the strike began [Para 21], yet a pre-hearing meeting is recorded for 22 March 2013 [Para 17] after the order of 20 March.
- The panel states at [Para 31] that it held no hearing and made no findings, while its own procedural section records a pre-hearing meeting attended by the employer [Para 17].
- [Para 18] contains unreadable Latin-script fragments; [Para 37] splices two separate orders together with stray "2017" inserted; [Para 41] refers to "the Arbitration Council 40" for what appears to be a citation to prior awards.
- The company appears as "Mekong Footwear", "Mekong Foot", "Mekong Footwear Branch 2" and other spellings across [Para 1], [Para 21], [Para 24] and [Para 44]; the union federation is spelled four different ways between [Para 2], [Para 18] and [Para 27].
