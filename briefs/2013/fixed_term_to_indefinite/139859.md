# Award No. 183/13 — Idal Pacific (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 21 October 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom, chosen by the employer party; An Nan, chosen by the worker party; Aing Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 139859

## 1. Facts
Idal Pacific (Cambodia), a handbag factory at Troyeung village, Peuk commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, registered as Inv.0169 E/2013 and operating from 9 April 2013, employed about 356 workers. [Para 67][Para 68] Of eight demands, three were conciliated and four withdrawn, leaving the claim that the employer take back its union leaders; the panel heard the case twice, on 11 September and 14 October 2013. [Para 18][Para 21][Para 24][Para 25] A union was elected on 19 June 2013 — Mr S as president, Mr R as vice-president, Mr O as treasurer — and the employer dismissed all three on 21 June 2013, two days later. [Para 199][Para 267]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back all the union leaders and stop discriminating against the union committee and against workers who volunteered for the strike? [Para 74][Para 138]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the three were dismissed without notice and without cause, the union having told the employer of the election result on 19 June 2013 by letter, courier and post; the dismissals were anti-union reprisals. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 111][Para 219]
**Employer (respondent):** orders were placed wrongly for June, July and August, production stalled and poor performers had to be cut; it never received the notification letter and considers the three to have been probationers or fixed-term staff. [Para 221][Para 248][Para 268][Para 270]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to take back Mr S, Mr R and Mr O to work as normal, although the panel found that none of them held protected status at the date of dismissal. [Para 242][Para 278][Para 279]

First the panel fixed the contract type. Article 67 (point 7) requires a fixed-duration contract to be in writing and makes an unwritten one indefinite. [Para 152][Para 153] The probation contract produced for Mr S bore no term, no date, no thumbprint or signature and no name, and the internal rules only require probation of no more than three months, not the two months claimed, so no probation contract was proved. [Para 142][Para 143][Para 146][Para 151] The three-month contracts said to follow probation for Mr R and Mr O were never signed in writing, so both also held indefinite contracts. [Para 159][Para 163][Para 164][Para 171][Para 172][Para 173]

On protection, Article 293 bars dismissal of a staff delegate without the labour inspector's agreement, and Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 extends that to union election candidates — Article 3(3) for 45 days before the poll and until 45 days after if not elected, Article 4 for members from the date of the registration application until 30 days after registration. [Para 175][Para 176][Para 180] Following earlier awards, the panel applied three conditions: the worker must be one entitled to protection, the dismissal must fall inside the protected period, and the union must have notified the employer of the names by any reliable means. [Para 196] The first was satisfied. [Para 203][Para 204] The second was satisfied for Mr S and Mr R, dismissed inside the window that runs from election day to registration, but not for Mr O, because the panel could not establish the date the registration application was filed. [Para 211][Para 212][Para 217][Para 218] The third failed: the letter of 19 June was sent by EMS and signed for by "security" on 21 June with no time recorded, and the panel could not find that it reached the employer before the 8 a.m. dismissal. [Para 224][Para 225][Para 227][Para 234][Para 240][Para 241]

The dismissal was nonetheless unlawful. Article 74 allows an indefinite contract to be ended by either party on written notice, but forbids dismissal without a valid reason connected with the worker's aptitude or conduct or the needs of the enterprise. [Para 243][Para 244][Para 245] The employer never stated what the three had done wrong, produced no evidence of poor work, gave no prior warning, and showed no other worker dismissed for poor performance at the same time. [Para 252][Para 254][Para 255][Para 275] Dismissing all three leaders two days after the election, having been told of the result, was anti-union discrimination contrary to Articles 12 and 279. [Para 258][Para 259][Para 267][Para 277]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An unwritten fixed-duration contract, or a probation contract lacking signature, term and date, is an indefinite-duration contract under Article 67, so the employer must show a valid reason for dismissal under Article 74. [Para 152][Para 151][Para 163] Where the employer bears the burden and offers no reason, no evidence of poor performance and no comparable dismissals, the dismissal of union leaders days after their election is anti-union discrimination under Articles 12 and 279, and reinstatement follows even if the protected-status claim fails for want of proof of timely notification. [Para 255][Para 277][Para 278]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's holdings that protection of elected leaders runs from election day until the union is registered, extending Prakas No. 305 beyond its 45-day rule, and that a union member's protection under Article 4 begins with the registration application, were not applied because the notification condition failed. [Para 188][Para 195][Para 213]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel's conclusion is printed as "the two workers ... did not receive protection" where three were in issue throughout, and the operative order repeats itself verbatim. [Para 242][Para 279]
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "29 August 2023 2013", and the extension of the award date reads "from 30 September 2013 to 1 [day] 21 October 2013". [Para 20][Para 26]
- Worker names are printed inconsistently: "សំ សូយ" and "សំ សួយ", "អ៊ុំ ឌី" and "អុំ ឌី", and the union is also written "អាយ ឌុល" and "អាយ ឌាល". [Para 199][Para 279]
- Article 67 is cited throughout as "Article 67, point 7", and Article 293 is quoted with a stray "4" breaking the sentence. [Para 152][Para 175]
- Untranslatable fragments interrupt the reasoning at [Para 182], [Para 234] and [Para 271], and the internal-rules quotation at [Para 89] ends mid-sentence.
