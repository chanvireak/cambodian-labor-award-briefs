# Order No. 268/13 — Berry Ei (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 20 December 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun appointed by the employer party, An Vireak appointed by the worker party, Aing Eng Tong chair) · Doc ID 139915

## 1. Facts
Workers of Berry Ei (Cambodia), a company at Chambou Vin village, Chom Chao sangkat, Pou Senchey khan, Phnom Penh, held contracts of indefinite duration (កិច្ចសន្យា គ្មាន ថិរៈ វេលា កំណត់) and demanded that the company pay them the 5% that workers on fixed-duration contracts receive. [Para 2] The company answered that it cannot grant the payment because it still applies the indefinite-duration contracts as written. [Para 3] Conciliation of the collective dispute on 11 December 2013 failed on this single point, and non-conciliation report No. 1593 dated 12 December 2013 referred it to the Council. [Para 10][Para 11] The workers struck from 11 December 2013; by order No. 047/13 of 13 December 2013 the panel directed them to suspend the strike and return to their shifts from the morning of 14 December 2013, and they did not comply. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether workers employed on contracts of indefinite duration are entitled to the 5% payment that the company pays to workers on fixed-duration contracts? [Para 2]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration when the workers remain on strike despite the panel's order suspending the strike? [Para 26]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 5% is owed to them on the same footing as colleagues on fixed-duration contracts; they struck to obtain it, sent no documents, did not attend the 17 December meeting, and confirmed by telephone that the strike continued. [Para 2][Para 20][Para 40]
**Employer (respondent):** the company is unable to make the payment because it continues to apply the indefinite-duration contracts, and the strike continued in disregard of the order of 13 December 2013. [Para 3][Para 41]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel ordered the termination (បញ្ចប់) of the arbitration in case No. 268/13, leaving the 5% claim undecided. [Para 42][Para 43]

The panel did not reach the merits. It recorded that it held no hearing and therefore made no finding of fact on the dispute referred to it. [Para 25] It framed the operative question for itself as whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 26] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to a dispute to suspend every contentious measure — strike or lockout, as contemplated by Article 318 of the Labour Law — for the duration of the panel's work, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 26][Para 27] The panel read that provision as an absolute bar: it held that the parties must suspend such measures and that the Council will not proceed while the workers are striking or the employer has locked out. [Para 28][Para 29] It followed its own earlier orders — among them 5906-Sang Vo, 3908, 110/11-Simanou and 104/13-Sabrina — and the notice of 29 January 2004 in case No. 04/04-MSI, which stated that the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on a collective dispute while the party before it has no right to strike, and may take the dispute up again once all the workers' unions stop striking. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32] The second order of 22 April 2004 in case No. 18/04 (Cambodiana, Raffles Le Royal and others) was also invoked: point 9 required all workers to stop strike action and demonstrations in the hotel grounds, and point 11 reserved the Council's right to close the proceedings if either party breached the order. [Para 33][Para 34] On the facts, the employer attended the 17 December 2013 meeting, the workers did not, and at 14:00 that day a secretariat officer telephoned them and was told they would not come and that the strike continued. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] The panel therefore closed the file. [Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike during the panel's proceedings after an order directing them to suspend it, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the merits, because Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY requires all contentious measures to be suspended while the panel is seised; the dispute may be brought back once the strike has ended. [Para 26][Para 28][Para 32][Para 42]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it agrees with the earlier line of orders on strikes during arbitration is an endorsement of authority rather than a ruling on these facts [Para 37]; its note that no findings of fact were made because no hearing was held explains why the 5% claim stands undetermined rather than dismissed [Para 25]. The employer's position that indefinite-duration contracts exclude the payment was left unexamined. [Para 3]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed four different ways in Khmer across the document — "ប៊ឺរី អី ភែរឹល", "ប៊ឺរី អី តែ រីល", "ប៊ឺរី អ៊ីភែរីល" and "ប៊ឺរី អី ភែរីល" [Para 1][Para 13][Para 19][Para 20]; "Berry Ei (Cambodia)" is used here as the face-of-award spelling.
- The date of the order is 20 December 2013 in the header [Para 1], but the signature block carries a stray "2014" [Para 44]; the year of order No. 047/13 is garbled as "ឆ្នាំ ២០១ ២០១៣" [Para 14] and the 17 December meeting as "ឆ្នាំ ២០ ២០១៣" [Para 38].
- The worker-party arbitrator is named "អាន វីរៈ" in the panel composition [Para 1] but appears in the signature block as "អាន * Ambolical" [Para 44]; the chair's signature line likewise carries "memont".
- Case numbers in the string of precedents are partly destroyed: "១៣៧០៩ ១៣៨ ០៩-ព្លំ កាញ់" and "៥៩០៦-សាង វ៉ូ" [Para 30]; only the legible ones are cited above.
- Two paragraphs repeat the file reference "ឃ- ឯកសារ បាន ទទួល ពី លេខាធិការដ្ឋាន" verbatim [Para 22], and "ផ្ស៖ផ្សា" appears as an OCR corruption of "ផ្សះផ្សា" (conciliation) [Para 21].
