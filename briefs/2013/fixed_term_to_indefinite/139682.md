# Award No. 75/13 — Chheng Yiok Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 5 April 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Kong Phalha chair) · Doc ID 139682

## 1. Facts
Chheng Yiok, a factory at Trapeang Thleung, Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, faced a strike by its workers over a single demand: that they be allowed one day's leave before Khmer New Year and one day after — 13 and 18 April 2013 — with wages and the attendance bonus preserved. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 9] The employer says it grants both days but counts them against the workers' annual leave. [Para 3] Conciliation on 29 March 2013 failed on this one point, the file reached the Secretariat on 1 April 2013, and the strike continued after referral. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12] The union's representatives did not attend the pre-hearing meeting on 3 April 2013; the employer's manager, HR manager, safety chief and interpreter did, and reported the strike continuing. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 17][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must allow workers one day's leave before Khmer New Year and one day after, on 13 and 18 April 2013, while preserving their wages and regular attendance bonus? [Para 2][Para 9]

The panel decided nothing on this question. It set itself a threshold question instead: whether the arbitration proceedings should continue at all. [Para 26][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two days around Khmer New Year should be granted as leave that does not cost the workers their wage or their attendance bonus for the month. [Para 2] Their representatives did not appear at the pre-hearing meeting and did not appear when the panel summoned both parties on 3 April 2013 to clarify the strike. [Para 18][Para 38][Para 39]

**Employer (respondent):** it already allows both days, but the leave is set off against the workers' annual leave entitlement, so no wage or bonus question arises beyond that. [Para 3] It wrote to the panel on 1 April 2013 asking for an order suspending the strike. [Para 13][Para 19]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in Case No. 75/13, leaving the single claim undetermined. [Para 40][Para 41]

Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties, while the panel is sitting, to suspend every contentious measure, including strikes or lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] Earlier panels had read the article to mean the Council will not proceed while the workers are striking or the employer is locking out, and this panel agreed. [Para 29][Para 37] It relied on Award 04/04 ISI, where a notice of 29 January 2004 held that a party has no right to strike during its own arbitration and that the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on the dispute, though it may reopen the case once all unions cease the strike. [Para 31][Para 32] It also relied on Award 18/04 against a group of hotels, which ordered the strike and any disturbance on hotel premises stopped from 23 April 2004 and reserved the right to terminate proceedings if a party breached the order, on Award 77/06 Franco Nithing, which declined to proceed where the strike continued, and on Awards 110/11 Simano and 17/13 Internal Fashion Royal, which declined for the same reason. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]

The panel had already issued Order No. 013/13 of 1 April 2013 at the employer's request, requiring the strike at Chheng Yiok to be suspended immediately and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 2 April 2013 until the arbitration was complete. [Para 13][Para 14] The workers did not obey, and the panel recorded that the strike was continuing. [Para 15][Para 39] Because no hearing took place, the panel made no findings of fact on the leave claim. [Para 25]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not continue the arbitration or rule on the merits, even on a single narrow claim; it closes the case and may reopen it only if the strike ends. [Para 29][Para 32][Para 40]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it may resolve the dispute afresh once all the workers' unions stop striking is not part of the order, which merely declines to proceed. [Para 32]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel dates its own suspension order No. 013/13 to "016 April 2013" at [Para 14], which is after the award date of 5 April 2013; the same order is dated 1 April 2013 at [Para 13], [Para 22] and [Para 39], and [Para 22] leaves the day and month blank. None of the three can be reconciled with the others.
- The meeting of 3 April 2013 is printed as "3 April 2019 2013" at [Para 38].
- The citation list at [Para 30] is corrupted into fragments ("5906-Sang Vo", "3908 Chong You", "13709 138/09-Plum Phanh") with no readable award numbers for three of the five.
- [Para 26] attributes Prakas No. 099 to the Ministry of Social Affairs and adds a stray "20" before "Youth Rehabilitation"; [Para 29] inserts a stray "4" into "the Arbitration Council will not continue".
- The employer's name appears as "Chheng Yiok", "Chhing Yiok" and "Chheng Yiok Co Ltd" across [Para 1], [Para 19] and [Para 41]; the file name on the face of the award is "75/13-Chheng Yiok" while the letter of 1 April 2013 is called a letter of the company "Chhing Yiok".
- The award records no findings of fact and no hearing [Para 25], which is consistent with its procedural history but leaves the leave claim, and the employer's set-off against annual leave, entirely unresolved.
