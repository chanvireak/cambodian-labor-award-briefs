# Award No. 246/12 — Master & Frang (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 4 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy; Oun Non; Kong Phalla presiding) · Doc ID 140228

## 1. Facts
Master & Frang (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment manufacturer at Ang Snuol district, Kandal province [Para 2]. A garment workers' union federation submitted 22 demands, including acceptance of two union leaders back to work with back pay [Para 4], a USD 20 monthly milk allowance for children, a 4,000 riel lunch allowance, retention of the attendance bonus for sick leave certified by a doctor, free work shoes, additional fingerprint scanners and an end to salary deductions for union dues [Para 6][Para 9][Para 11][Para 18][Para 20]. Conciliation on 20 December 2012 failed on all 22 points, which were referred to the Council on 21 December 2012 [Para 55][Para 56]. The workers had struck from 19 December 2012 and the strike was still running when the case arrived [Para 57]. The panel issued Order No. 033/12 on 25 December 2012 requiring the strike to be suspended immediately and all strikers to return to work from the morning of 26 December 2012 [Para 57]. The strike nevertheless continued [Para 58], and neither party attended the meeting the panel convened for 27 December 2012 [Para 60]. No hearing was held, so no findings of fact were made on any of the 22 demands [Para 65]; the panel decided only whether to continue the arbitration [Para 66].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Preliminary issue) Whether the panel should continue the arbitration proceedings when the workers had kept striking after Order No. 033/12 directed them to suspend the strike and return to work? [Para 66][Para 77]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The federation pursued all 22 demands, principally reinstatement of two union leaders with payment of wages from the date the company stopped them until they resumed work [Para 4], and it maintained the strike launched on 19 December 2012 [Para 57].
**Employer (respondent):** The company did not agree to the demands put to it during conciliation [Para 7][Para 10][Para 12] and did not attend the pre-hearing meeting the panel convened [Para 2][Para 60]; it had agreed only to arrange three extra fingerprint scanners from January 2013 and to deduct union dues once proper membership forms were produced [Para 19][Para 21].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case 246/12, leaving all 22 demands undetermined [Para 78][Para 79].

The panel framed a single threshold question: whether the arbitration should proceed at all [Para 66]. It relied on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council, which requires the parties to a dispute before the panel to suspend every contentious measure, including strikes and lockouts, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes [Para 66][Para 67]. It read that provision as settled practice: in Award No. 04/04 (IMI) the Council held it could not hear or decide a collective dispute while the workers were striking and could take the case up again once the strike ended [Para 69][Para 70], and the same result followed in the hotel case of 2004, in Award No. 77/06 and in Award No. 110/11 [Para 72][Para 74][Para 75]. On the facts, the workers had continued their strike and had not complied with Order No. 033/12, and neither side appeared on 27 December 2012 [Para 58][Para 60][Para 77]. Having taken no evidence, the panel made no finding on the merits [Para 65] and discontinued the proceedings, expressly adopting the approach of the earlier awards [Para 76][Para 78].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a collective dispute has been referred to the panel and the workers carry on a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended, the panel will not continue the arbitration; it may take the case up again once the strike has ended, but it decides nothing on the merits in the meantime. [Para 66][Para 70]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that Order No. 033/12 bore a technical error in its text, the date being written as 21 December 2012 instead of 25 December 2012, and treated the order as effective notwithstanding [Para 57].
