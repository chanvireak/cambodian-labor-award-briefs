# Award No. 118/13 — Tripos International (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 19 June 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mae Nimmith presiding; Mar Samborana, employer nominee; An Virak, worker nominee) · Doc ID 139808

## 1. Facts
Tripos International (Cambodia) is a factory at Chrok Romeat village, Chhuk Sa commune, Kampong Tralach district, Kampong Chhnang, employing a workforce represented by two unions, the local of the Federation of Development Workers' Unions and the Workers' Welfare Union. [Para 1][Para 2] Six demands were conciliated on 10 June 2013 and three were settled, leaving three. [Para 11] While the case was pending the workforce struck, and on 11 June 2013 the employer asked the Council to order the strike stopped. [Para 13] The Council issued order No. 023 of 11 June 2013 requiring the strike to be suspended at once and all strikers to return to their shifts from the morning of 12 June 2013; the strike continued. [Para 14][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide a place on the premises where workers of the Muslim faith may pray? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must stop deducting the attendance bonus (ប្រាក់ព្រីម) when a worker takes sick leave? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must pay the USD 5 health allowance separately again, now that it has been folded into the base wage? [Para 4]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they sought a prayer space, an end to attendance-bonus deductions for sick leave, and restoration of the USD 5 health allowance as a separate payment; they also maintained the strike after the Council's order, a fact their representatives confirmed at the pre-hearing meeting of 14 June 2013. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 17][Para 38]
**Employer (respondent):** it asked the Council by letter of 11 June 2013 to issue an order requiring the workers to end the strike; it did not put a position on the three demands, and none is recorded. [Para 13][Para 18]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to continue the arbitration and closed case 118/13 without deciding any of the three demands, so no order for or against the employer was made. [Para 39][Para 40]

The panel framed the only question it would answer as whether the arbitration should proceed at all. [Para 25] It applied Article 20 of Prakas 099, under which the parties to a dispute must suspend every dispute measure — a strike or a lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, or anything else that would aggravate the dispute — throughout the panel's proceedings, and must attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]

It read that provision, as it had in earlier awards, to mean that the panel will not continue an arbitration while the workers are striking or the employer has locked them out, and it listed the cases in which it had issued an order and then stopped: case 04/04, where it notified the parties on 29 January 2004 that a party with no right to strike during proceedings could not have its dispute decided; case 18/04, where its second order of 22 April 2004 required strikers at six hotels to cease all strike, demonstration or disturbance on hotel premises from 23 April 2004 and reserved the right to terminate if a party breached the order; case 77/05, which stated that a continuing strike would end the proceedings; and cases 110/11 and 17/13, discontinued on the same ground. [Para 28][Para 30][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]

On the facts, both the employer and the union representatives attended the meeting of 14 June 2013 and agreed that the workers were still striking in disregard of order No. 023. [Para 37][Para 38] The panel therefore decided not to continue, and made no findings on the prayer space, the attendance bonus or the health allowance — it recorded expressly that it had made no findings of fact because it never held a hearing. [Para 24][Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a workforce continues its strike after the panel has ordered the strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas 099, the panel will not continue the arbitration and closes the case without deciding the demands; the strike, not the merits, disposes of the case, and the same demands may be brought again once the strike has ended. [Para 27][Para 31][Para 39]

The order suspending a strike is enforceable within the arbitration itself: a panel that has issued one may terminate its proceedings on proof that the strike continued, without examining whether the underlying demands were well founded. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 38]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted, quoting its earlier order in case 18/04, that it retains the right to terminate proceedings where a party breaches any term of an order it has made — a general power it invoked here only in its strike-specific form. [Para 33] It also recorded that the case file contained no findings of fact because no hearing was held, so the record on the three demands is empty. [Para 24]

[Para 1][Para 6] The award is internally inconsistent on which province dealt with the dispute: the parties' addresses and the conciliation are in Kampong Chhnang, but the closing finding attributes the non-conciliation report to the Kampong Speu labour department. [Para 3] Prakas 099 is described as issued by the Ministry of Social Affairs, Labour, Vocational Training "and 40 Youth Rehabilitation". [Para 25] The quotation from case 04/04 is broken by stray "កម្ពុជា" and "The", and one passage names the deciding body as the "National Arbitration Council". [Para 30][Para 36] The pre-hearing meeting is dated "14 June 2023 2013". [Para 37] The signature block carries the fragments "Am Yeel-<" and "March" in place of signatures. [Para 41] The employer's name appears only in Khmer (ត្រី ប៉ូស អ៊ីនធើណេសិន ណល), so "Tripos International (Cambodia)" is a transliteration, not a printed name. [Para 1]
