# Award No. 182/13 — Nichi T I Pearl (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 9 September 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kav Tha Ch, chosen by the employer party; An Nan, chosen by the worker party; Kong Phalla, chair) · Doc ID 139856

## 1. Facts
Nichi T I Pearl (Cambodia), a garment factory at Mal village, Sangkat Dangkao, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh, faced a strike that began on 22 August 2013 over 11 demands put by the Democratic Confederation of Garment Workers' union; none of the 11 was conciliated. [Para 2][Para 17][Para 18] On 3 September 2013 the employer asked the Council to order the strike suspended; the Council issued Order No. 035/13 that day requiring the strikers to stop immediately and return to their shifts from the morning of 4 September 2013. [Para 21][Para 23] The workers did not comply, the strike continued on 4 September, and the union filed no report in answer to the Secretariat's letter No. 948 of 5 September, due by 11:30 on 9 September 2013. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must deduct the attendance bonus in proportion to the days of lawful leave taken? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must stop setting production targets above the workforce's capacity? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must make it easier for workers to request leave? [Para 5]
4. Whether the employer must pay a 4,000 riel midday meal allowance for each meal? [Para 6]
5. Whether the employer must renew employment contracts for one year at a time? [Para 7]
6. Whether the employer must remove the Chinese managers from the company? [Para 8]
7. Whether the employer must apply overtime on a voluntary basis? [Para 9]
8. Whether the employer must make the workplace cool enough for work? [Para 10]
9. Whether the employer must include male workers in employment contracts after three months, on becoming permanent? [Para 11]
10. Whether the employer must stop deducting union dues from wages without the workers' consent? [Para 12]
11. Whether the employer must maintain wages and the attendance bonus during a strike and not discriminate against strikers? [Para 13]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 11 demands were pressed as the object of the strike begun on 22 August 2013, and the union did not answer the Secretariat's request for a report on the strike's current state. [Para 17][Para 26]
**Employer (respondent):** the company applied to the Council for an order suspending the strike, relying on the strike's continuation and on photographs of the strike on 3 and 4 September 2013. [Para 21][Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration of case No. 182/13, and made no order on any of the 11 claims. [Para 49][Para 50]

The panel recorded that it held no hearing and therefore made no finding of fact on any of the disputed issues. [Para 36] Its reasoning turned on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure, including strikes and lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, throughout the panel's proceedings, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 37][Para 38] Earlier awards had read that provision as barring the Council from proceeding while the workers are striking: in case No. 04/04-IMS the Council stated that where a party has no right to strike during the proceedings it cannot settle the dispute or issue an award, and may take the dispute up again once the strike ends. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] The panel also relied on case No. 18/04 (a group of Phnom Penh hotels), which ordered strikers to stop and reserved the right to terminate proceedings if a party breached the order, and on cases No. 77/05 and No. 110/11-Simana, where proceedings were not continued because the strike went on. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] Applying that line, the panel found the order of 3 September 2013 had been disobeyed on 4 September and that the strike was still running, and so declined to proceed. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a panel has ordered a strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 and the workers keep striking, the panel will not continue the arbitration and will issue no award on the merits; the dispute may be brought back once the strike has ended. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 49]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark, drawn from case No. 18/04, that it reserves the right to terminate proceedings whenever a party breaches one of its orders is a general statement of the Council's practice rather than a ground of this decision, which rested on the continuing strike. [Para 43]

- Dates are printed twice over: the panel is said to have been constituted on "28 August 2022, 2013" and Order No. 035/13 is dated "3 September 2019, 2013". [Para 20][Para 23]
- The chair appears as "គង់ ផល្ទៈ", "គង់ ដស្ទះ (AXUS 2777" and "គង់ ផល្លៈ" across the face and signature pages. [Para 2][Para 51]
- The company is printed as "ក្រុមហ៊ុនី ច-ធី អី ភែរឹល (ខេមបូឌា)", "នីច ធី អ៊ីភែវីល" and "នីចធី អ៊ីភែរីល"; the header gives "នីច-ធី អី ភែរឹល". [Para 1][Para 35][Para 50]
- Evidence items 3 and 4 of the Secretariat's file are duplicated and the order's date is split across the repetition ("3 September 2013" / "2013"). [Para 34]
