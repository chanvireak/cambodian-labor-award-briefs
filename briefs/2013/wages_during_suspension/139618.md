# Award No. 22/13 — Kbal Koh Garmen Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 30 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Tun Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139618

## 1. Facts
Kbal Koh Garmen Co., Ltd runs a garment factory on National Road 1, Sangkat Kbal Koh, Khan Meanchey, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Conciliation of a 12-point collective dispute failed on 9 points, reported on 23 January 2013, and the workers struck from 22 January 2013. [Para 26][Para 28] The Council ordered the strike suspended at once and all strikers back at work from the afternoon of 24 January 2013; the strike nonetheless continued. [Para 29][Para 30] The union undertook that members would stop from 26 January and report on 28 January, then gave no report and could not be reached. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must provide 2,000 riel per day as lunch money, every day? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must change short-term contracts from 30 months to one year? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must raise the base wage from $70 to $100 per month? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must provide 2,000 riel per day as meal money on public holidays and Sundays? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must pay severance to workers with long service? [Para 10]
6. Whether a worker absent one day without permission should have a $5 regular attendance bonus deducted? [Para 12]
7. Whether the company must provide a bonus to the assistants of every group and section? [Para 14]
8. Whether the company must pay an allowance to workers whose function is changing needles? [Para 16]
9. Whether the company must provide a bonus for the cutting-table team? [Para 18]
10. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all, the strike having continued after the Council's suspension order? [Para 45]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The nine unsettled demands stood as formulated; the union stated that from 26 January 2013 its members would stop striking and return to work, and would report the position on 28 January. [Para 2][Para 33]

**Employer (respondent):** It could not meet the demands — no lunch money, no wage rise beyond what the law requires, no severance — and asked for time to study the contract and holiday-meal points; it reported that the strike was still continuing although some strikers had returned. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to continue the arbitration and made no order on any of the nine demands. [Para 62][Para 63]

The panel recorded at the outset that it had held no hearing, so it made no findings of fact on the disputed points. [Para 44] It framed the only question it could decide as whether the arbitration should proceed, and applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council: during the panel's proceedings the parties must suspend all dispute measures, including strikes, and must attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. [Para 45][Para 46] Earlier awards had read that provision to bar the Council from continuing whenever the workers were striking — citing cases 04/04-IM SI, 18/04-Hotel (Cambodiana and others), 77/06-Franco Nith Ting and 110/11-Simanor. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 52][Para 53] On the facts, the panel accepted the employer's account that workers were still striking up to 28 January 2013, the union having failed to report as promised and having become unreachable. [Para 61][Para 60] It therefore declined to continue. [Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers continue a strike after the Council has ordered dispute measures suspended, the panel will not continue the arbitration or rule on the demands; the statutory bar on dispute measures during arbitration is a precondition of the panel sitting, not a matter going to the merits. [Para 45][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** That the Council may take up the same collective dispute again once the strike has ended, as the earlier awards quoted record. [Para 49]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award records that no hearing was held and that no facts were found on the nine demands, so sections 3 and 4 rest on the parties' written positions only. [Para 44] The signature block lost the worker-appointed arbitrator's name to OCR and prints only the employer-appointed name and the president's. [Para 64] The company appears as "ក្បាលកោះ" and as "ក្បាល កោះ ហ្គាមេន" within the same document. [Para 1][Para 63] Stray numerals ("១៩", "២០") are inserted mid-sentence in the passages quoting earlier awards. [Para 51][Para 54]
