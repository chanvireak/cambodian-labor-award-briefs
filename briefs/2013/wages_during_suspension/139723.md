# Award No. 104/13 — Sabrina (Cambodia) Garment Manufacturing Corp (Arbitration Council, 31 May 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed Ing Sothy; worker-appointed Huon Chun Dy; president Pen Bun Chhea) · Doc ID 139723

## 1. Facts
Sabrina (Cambodia) Garment Manufacturing Corp, on National Road 4 at Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, faced a strike led by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia over nine unresolved demands. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 17] On the employer's request the panel issued Order No. 021/13 on 23 May 2013 directing an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the morning of 25 May 2013; the workers ignored it and stayed out. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] The panel then summoned both sides to an information meeting on 29 May 2013; the employer attended and said the strike was continuing, the workers did not attend. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must add USD 14 to the base wage in line with the Government's notification? [Para 2]
2. Whether workers with two months' service must receive the same benefits as longer-serving workers? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must provide one midday meal or 4,000 riel per day, including holidays and Sundays? [Para 4]
4. Whether Sunday work and night overtime must be paid at triple the wage rate? [Para 5]
5. Whether an overtime meal allowance of 1,000 riel per hour must be paid? [Para 6]
6. Whether the transport or accommodation allowance must rise by USD 8? [Para 7]
7. Whether a milk allowance of USD 25 per month for 20 months must be paid? [Para 8]
8. Whether the attendance bonus must rise by USD 10? [Para 9]
9. Whether the company must honour its agreement with the union of 30 January 2013? [Para 10]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the nine demands were pressed through a strike that continued after the case reached the panel; the union did not attend the pre-hearing meeting or file any document. [Para 19][Para 25][Para 27]
**Employer (respondent):** it asked the panel to order the strike suspended and reported that the workers were still striking in defiance of Order 021/13. Its sole filing was the letter of 22 May 2013. [Para 20][Para 24][Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction to proceed — the panel declined to continue the arbitration and closed case 104/13 without deciding any of the nine demands; no payment or other order was made. [Para 47][Para 48]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, which requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures such as strikes or lockouts, and any other measure that could aggravate the dispute, throughout the arbitration, and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35] It read that provision, consistently with Awards 59/06 Sang Vo, 39/08 Choung Youv, 137/09 and 138/09 Pla, and 110/11 Simano, as barring the panel from arbitrating while a strike or lockout is in progress. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 43]

It then applied the earlier rulings directly: in Award 04/04 MSI the panel held that a party striking during arbitration has no right to strike and that the panel cannot settle or issue an award on the dispute until the strike ends; in Award 18/04 (hotels: Cambodiana, Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Hotel Le Royal, Sokha Angkor, Sunway, InterContinental and Sofitel Angkor) the panel ordered the strike stopped and reserved the right to close the case if any party breached the order. [Para 38][Para 40][Para 41] Award 77/06 Franco Night Thing and Awards 110/11 Simano and 17/13 Internal Fashion-Royal reached the same result where workers kept striking during the proceedings. [Para 42][Para 43]

On the facts, notice of the 29 May 2013 meeting was given, the employer confirmed the strike was still running, and the workers neither appeared nor explained their absence, so the conditions for closing the arbitration were met. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not arbitrate the dispute at all; it closes the case without ruling on the merits, and the demands can be taken up again only once the strike has ended. [Para 35][Para 39][Para 47]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that it retains the right to terminate proceedings where a party breaches its order, and that the dispute may be re-filed when the strike stops [Para 39][Para 41].
