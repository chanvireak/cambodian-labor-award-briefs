# Award No. 136/13 — Southland (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Vong Vanna, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 139822

## 1. Facts
Southland (Cambodia) Co Ltd, in Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, faced a strike from 1 July 2013 over four claims; three were settled at conciliation and one remained — the reinstatement of Mr S Chanrithy, whom the company had dismissed for serious misconduct. The company asked the Council for an order halting the strike, and on 2 July 2013 the Council issued order No. 029/13 requiring the strike to be suspended immediately and all strikers to return to their shifts from the morning of 3 July 2013. The strike continued. Neither party attended the pre-hearing meeting of 4 July 2013, and no hearing was ever held. [Para 2][Para 10][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take Mr S Chanrithy back into normal work, he having denied the serious misconduct alleged against him? [Para 2]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all, the strike having continued in breach of the Council's suspension order? [Para 24][Para 26]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr S Chanrithy maintained that he committed no serious misconduct of the kind alleged, and the workers continued their strike in support of the unresolved claims. [Para 2][Para 11]
**Employer (respondent):** The company refused reinstatement on the ground of serious misconduct, and added that it had already paid his wages and unused annual leave and that he had accepted that compensation. It asked the Council to order the strike stopped. [Para 3][Para 12]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** proceedings terminated — the panel ended the arbitration in case No. 136/13 without any award on the merits. [Para 40][Para 41]

The panel treated the case as a procedural one from the start. Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 requires the parties, throughout the Council's proceedings, to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law — and any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, and requires them to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. The panel read that article as meaning it will not continue arbitration while the workers are striking or the employer has locked out. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27]

It then applied a line of its own decisions. In case No. 04/04 (MSI) the Council declared that where a party has no right to strike during the proceedings it cannot decide the dispute or issue an award at all, and may take the case up again only once the strike ends. In the 2004 hotel cases the Council ordered strikers to cease all strike action, protest and disruption on hotel premises from 23 April 2004 and reserved the right to close the proceedings if either party breached the order. In case No. 77/05 (Franco Nitting) it stated that if the workers continued to strike it would not process the collective dispute, and it reached the same result in cases No. 110/11 (Simano) and No. 17/13 (International Fashion 2 Royal). [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34]

On the facts the panel found that its order of 2 July 2013 had not been obeyed: the strike went on after the case was referred to it, and on 4 July 2013 secretariat staff telephoned the employer, who confirmed the strike was continuing and the order was not being respected, while the workers could not be reached at all. Neither party had appeared at the meeting convened for that afternoon. Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the reinstatement claim in the non-conciliation report, and it terminated the proceedings. [Para 11][Para 23][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the party workers continue a strike, or the employer a lockout, after the Council has ordered it suspended, the panel will not arbitrate the underlying dispute: Article 20 of Prakas No. 099/04 obliges the parties to suspend all dispute measures and to attend every meeting, and breach of that obligation ends the proceedings rather than producing an award. The dispute may be taken up again only once the strike has stopped. [Para 26][Para 30][Para 34]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel expressly recorded that it made no finding of fact on the reinstatement claim because it never held a hearing, so nothing in the award touches the merits of the alleged serious misconduct. [Para 23]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The reasoning at one point states that the panel terminates the proceedings with a negative particle attached, which if read literally says the opposite of the operative order; the operative paragraph and the surrounding reasoning show termination was intended. The meeting time is given as 16:00 in one passage and 14:00 in another, while the telephone calls are timed at 4:25 and 4:30 in the afternoon. Two dates carry a duplicated year ("2 July 2019 2013", "4 July 2019 2013"). The president's name appears as Kong Phalla in the signature block and in a variant spelling in the header, and the Prakas on panel appointments is dated 17 June 2013 in the jurisdiction paragraph but 2012 elsewhere in the same series of awards. The conciliation report number appears both as 783/13 and 783. [Para 4][Para 13][Para 15][Para 36][Para 40][Para 42]
