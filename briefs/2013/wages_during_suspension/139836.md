# Award No. 151/13 — Te Fou Am Preley Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer, Liv Sovanna for the workers, chair Pen Bun Chhea) · Doc ID 139836

## 1. Facts
Te Fou Am Preley Co., Ltd, a factory at Tuek Chea village, Peuk commune, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, faced a collective dispute brought by the Federation of Union of Solidarity of Labour of Cambodia (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព ការងារ កម្ពុជា) over working conditions. [Para 1] Conciliation on 11 July 2013 settled none of the points, and the non-conciliation report No. 551/13 of 12 July 2013 was sent to the Council. [Para 19] [Para 23] The workers struck. On 15 July 2013 the company asked the panel for an order to stop the strike, and the panel issued order No. 031/13 that day requiring the strike to be suspended at once and all strikers to return to their shifts from the afternoon of 16 July 2013. [Para 25] [Para 26] The workers did not comply. [Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration proceedings should continue when the workers remain on strike during the arbitration, contrary to the panel's order? [Para 38]
2. Whether the points left unsettled at conciliation should be decided — that the company not transfer the head of the sewing section or other workers to work outside their skills, that the car allowance rise by US$5 to US$12, that workers receive 3,000 riel for two hours of overtime, 2,000 riel a day for lunch paid on Saturdays, no deduction of wages or attendance bonus for certified sick leave, work assigned according to ability, and probation limited to two months followed by longer fixed-term contracts? [Para 3] [Para 5] [Para 7] [Para 9] [Para 11] [Para 16]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that the company should improve the conditions listed in the nine-point demand, and, through their union representative, that although they understood the Council's procedure they maintained their position and would continue the strike until the employer offered a suitable solution. [Para 3] [Para 53]

**Employer (respondent):** that it opposed every demand at conciliation, that it asked the Council on 15 July 2013 to order the workers back, and that at the information meeting it asked the strikers to return to work and offered to settle with them afterwards. [Para 4] [Para 25] [Para 52]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 151/13 without reaching the merits, having held no hearing and therefore made no findings of fact. [Para 37] [Para 55] [Para 56]

The panel framed the only question it decided as whether the arbitration should continue. [Para 38] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the parties, throughout the panel's proceedings, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout or any other measure that could aggravate the dispute, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 38] [Para 39]

The panel had already interpreted that article in earlier awards — 5906-Sang Rou, 3908-Chong Yuv, 13709 and 13809-Plam Phanh, and 110/11-Simanou — as meaning that it will not continue arbitration while workers are striking or the employer has locked out. [Para 41] [Para 42] In Award 04/04-IMSI the panel had issued a notice of 29 January 2004 stating that a party has no right to strike during the proceedings, that the panel cannot then decide the dispute, and that it may take the dispute up again once the strike ends. [Para 43] [Para 44] In Award 18/04 (the hotel cases) the panel had ordered the strike stopped and reserved the right to terminate if any party breached its order. [Para 45] [Para 46] Award 77/05-Franco Nithing had provided that the panel would not deal with the dispute where the strike continued, and Awards 110/11-Simanou and 17/13-International Fashion-Royal had terminated on the same ground. [Para 47] [Para 48]

On the facts, the panel summoned both parties to an information meeting on 17 July 2013, at which the employer and the union both confirmed that the strike was continuing in defiance of the order of 15 July 2013. [Para 50] [Para 51] The employer asked the strikers to return and offered to settle afterwards, but the union representative said the workers understood the procedure and would keep striking until a suitable solution was offered. [Para 52] [Para 53] Because the strike continued during the arbitration, the panel held it could not continue to resolve the dispute and terminated the proceedings. [Para 54] [Para 55]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A party that keeps a strike or lockout running after the panel has ordered it suspended deprives the panel of the conditions in which it can arbitrate: under Article 20 of Prakas 099 the panel will terminate the proceedings rather than decide the dispute, and it may take the dispute up again once the strike has ended. [Para 39] [Para 44] [Para 47]

**Obiter dicta:** that the panel reserves the right to terminate the arbitration where a party breaches any term of its order; and that, having held no hearing, it made no findings of fact on the nine demands, so nothing in the award touches their merits. [Para 46] [Para 37]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Only eight demands are set out under "បញ្ហាក្នុងវិវាទ", yet the procedure records nine points conciliated and nine referred as unsettled, so one demand is missing from the record. [Para 3] [Para 16] [Para 23] [Para 24] The non-conciliation report is said to have reached the secretariat on "២៤ ១២ ខែកក្កដា", and the jurisdiction paragraph reads "ជំពូក ទី ២៤ ១២ ផ្នែក ទី២ (ក)"; the panel's order is dated 15 July "២០ ២០១៣". [Para 24] [Para 18] [Para 26] The company appears as "តេ ហ្វូ អាំ ប្រេឡឺ" and once as "ប្រេឡី", and stray Latin and numeral tokens ("New", "CL", "៤០", "១៩") intrude into the reasoning. [Para 1] [Para 38] [Para 30] [Para 41] [Para 46] The chair's signature line is replaced by OCR noise, though his name survives. [Para 57] Individual workers, union officials and representatives are reduced to initials under the briefing convention.
