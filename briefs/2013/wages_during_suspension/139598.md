# Award No. 12/13-Vin Son In Thl Gaming — Vin Son In Thl Gaming (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-chosen; Huon Chun Dy, worker-chosen; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 139598

## 1. Facts
Vin Son In Thl Gaming (Cambodia) Ltd is a garment factory at Rumduol Village, Trapeang Kong Commune, Samraong Tong District, Kampong Speu. [Para 1] A strike began on the morning of 12 January 2013, led by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia (សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា); of ten demands, conciliation on 14 January 2013 settled Issues 3 and 4, leaving eight. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] The strike continued after referral, the employer obtained a protection order from the Kampong Speu First Instance Court, and the workers did not attend the 21 January 2013 pre-hearing. [Para 18][Para 22][Para 19][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back Ms H, Ms D and Mr K into employment? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must raise the base wage to US$70? [Para 3]
5. Whether the company must calculate pay correctly for night-shift work? [Para 4]
6. Whether the company must stop deducting the attendance bonus when a worker takes statutory leave? [Para 5]
7. Whether the company must stop deducting pay when a worker is absent on sick leave with a medical certificate? [Para 6]
8. Whether the company must stop making deductions when a worker comes to iron/press? [Para 7]
9. Whether the company must collect workers when the staff vehicle breaks down? [Para 8]
10. Whether the company must stop deducting pay during the strike? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** their demands, as recorded in the non-conciliation report, were reinstatement of three dismissed workers, a US$70 base wage, correct night-shift pay, and no deductions of the bonus, of pay during certified sick leave, on coming to iron/press, or during the strike, plus transport when the vehicle breaks down. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 9] They did not appear at the pre-hearing or at any sitting, and filed no documents. [Para 21][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** it attended and stated that the workers were still on strike as of 21 January 2013; it relied on the Kampong Speu First Instance Court protection order No. 06(NG) dated 15 January 2013 and on the court's notice of enforcement dated 16 January 2013. [Para 20][Para 22][Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to continue the arbitration in Case No. 12/13-Vin Son In Thl Gaming. [Para 38][Para 39]

The panel put a single question to itself: whether the arbitration should proceed at all. [Para 30] Clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 dated 21 April 2004 requires the disputing parties to suspend all dispute measures — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, or any other measure aggravating the dispute — throughout the panel's proceedings, and to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. [Para 30][Para 31] Reading that clause, the panel held it will not continue an arbitration while the worker party is striking or the employer has locked out. [Para 32][Para 33] It followed Awards 59/06-Sang Vo, 3908-Chong You, 13709 and 13809-Plum Phnom and 110/11-Simanou on the same point. [Para 34][Para 35]

On the facts, the panel summoned both parties to clarify the strike on 21 January 2013, but the workers did not attend, and on the employer's statement the strike was continuing to that date. [Para 36][Para 37] It therefore decided not to continue the arbitration. [Para 38] Because no hearing on the merits took place, the panel expressly made no findings of fact on the disputed issues. [Para 29]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers continue a strike, or the employer a lockout, after the dispute is referred, and the worker party fails to attend the panel's summons, the panel will decline to continue the arbitration under Clause 20 of Prakas No. 099 rather than decide the demands. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 37]

**Obiter dicta:** the obligation to suspend dispute measures is reciprocal — it binds the employer against lockout as much as the workers against strike. [Para 30][Para 32]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
No findings of fact were made, the section stating only that no factual findings exist because no hearing was held, so the demands rest entirely on the non-conciliation report. [Para 29] The workers filed no documents at all, so their position is reconstructed from the report alone. [Para 25] Issues carry double numbering (for example "៣(៥)", "៤(៦)") inherited from the original ten-demand list. [Para 4][Para 5] The summons date prints as "២០២៣ ២០១៣" (2023 and 2013) in the same sentence. [Para 36] The company name is spelled several ways, including "វីន សុន អ៉ិន ធល ហ្គាមិន", "វីន សុន អ៊ីន ធល ហ្គា មិន" and "វីន សុន អ៊ីន ផល ហ្គាមិន". [Para 1][Para 26][Para 25]
