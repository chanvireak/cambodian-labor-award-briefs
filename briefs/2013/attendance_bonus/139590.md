# Award No. 01/13 — Alim (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 23 January 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed, dissenting; An Non, worker-appointed; Ang Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 139590

## 1. Facts
The company sews garments at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, with about 556 workers, and the Alim garment factory branch of the Workers' Movement Union was the claimant, registered on 4 December 2012. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 33] One demand survived conciliation out of six: that the employer deduct the 10 USD monthly attendance bonus in proportion to the days a worker takes authorised unpaid leave, rather than cancelling the whole bonus for any leave. [Para 11][Para 34] The employer's practice was to withhold the entire 10 USD whenever a worker took even half a day of permitted personal leave for a family ceremony or wedding. [Para 34][Para 37] Both parties accepted that this had happened to union members in the past. [Para 39]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must deduct the attendance bonus proportionally to the number of days of authorised unpaid leave taken, instead of withholding the whole 10 USD? [Para 34][Para 82]
2. Whether the phrase "without absence" added by point 2 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 covers leave that the employer has authorised without pay? [Para 52][Para 61][Para 71]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** wages are so low that losing the whole 10 USD for half a day's permitted leave destroys morale; the panel's 2011 awards ordered proportional deduction in Cases 136/11, 154/11, 169/11, 171/11, 173/11 and 177/11, and the employer's practice contradicts them. [Para 37][Para 38]
**Employer (respondent):** the bonus is earned only by workers who attend regularly, so any absence forfeits it; paying the full bonus despite absence would be unfair to the employer. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 46]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to deduct the attendance bonus proportionally to the number of days of authorised personal leave taken, rather than cancelling it in full. [Para 82][Para 83]
The panel first noted that Notification No. 041 of 7 March 2011, which set a minimum bonus of 7 USD for regular attendance, had been revoked and replaced by Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, setting 10 USD, applicable from 1 September. [Para 44][Para 49][Para 51] It observed that the later notification adds the words "without absence", which the earlier text did not contain, so the term required construction. [Para 52][Para 61][Para 63]
On the nature of the bonus the panel reasoned that while bonuses are ordinarily a matter of employer discretion, point 2 of Notification No. 230 has ceased to be a management tool and has become a general legal rule binding on all textile, garment and footwear employers, so it must follow the legal regime of wages under Article 103 of the Labour Law. [Para 54][Para 56][Para 59][Para 60]
On the meaning of "absence" the panel distinguished paid from unpaid leave, and within unpaid leave distinguished authorised from unauthorised leave. [Para 68][Para 69] It held that "absence" in the notification means absence without permission — ឈប់ឥតច្បាប់ — because authorised unpaid leave is a prior agreement between employer and worker that reduces that worker's working days for the month, with no wage for those days and no disciplinary consequence. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 78] Since wages are calculated on an aggregate monthly basis, a worker who works fewer hours is paid proportionally and cannot claim the full bonus; paying the full bonus despite authorised unpaid leave would be unfair to the employer. [Para 79][Para 81] The correct result was therefore proportional deduction, not total forfeiture. [Para 82]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Authorised unpaid leave is not "absence" within point 2 of Notification No. 230; a worker who takes such leave keeps a proportionate share of the 10 USD attendance bonus, and an employer that cancels the bonus in full for permitted leave acts contrary to the notification and to Article 103. [Para 59][Para 71][Para 78][Para 82]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel declined to interpret the notification's silence on the consequences of absence generally, saying it would not decide points unrelated to the dispute before it. [Para 64]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The procedural history states that one of six demands was unsettled, then records "09 points" as referred. [Para 11][Para 12] The replacement notification's effective date is printed as "1 September 2015" in one place and 2012 elsewhere, and the old notification is described as revoked by a "2012" notification in another sentence. [Para 49][Para 50] The award carries a dissenting opinion by the employer-appointed arbitrator arguing that the bonus is discretionary and that any absence defeats entitlement; it is annexed after the signatures and is not reflected in the dispositive order. [Para 85][Para 88][Para 105] The signature block is partly corrupted. [Para 85]
