# Award No. 228/13 — Prai Sen International Nol Khob Corporation (Arbitration Council, 5 December 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Tang Tri presiding; Hem Hou Narith, employer nominee; An Vira, worker nominee) · Doc ID 139887

## 1. Facts
Prai Sen International Nol Khob Corporation, a shoe manufacturer at Oknha Teap village, Trapeang Kong commune, Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu, employs a workforce whose collective dispute reached the provincial Department of Labour on 23 September 2013 as ten demands on conditions of work and benefits. [Para 1][Para 13][Para 49] Conciliation on 11 October 2013 settled four points and left six; the panel settled a fifth (issue 7) at the hearing and the workers withdrew issue 6, leaving four for decision. [Para 15][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] The claimant party was the Samakki Kamkar Kampuchea federation together with its factory union, which was not yet registered as a professional organisation although the federation had signed a memorandum of understanding with the company on 3 October 2012. [Para 1][Para 24] Both parties appeared at the hearing of 12 November 2013 and agreed to a non-binding award. [Para 20][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់) pro rata to the days a worker took lawfully authorised unpaid leave, or may withhold it in full? [Para 94][Para 95]
2. Whether the company must stop using three-month fixed-duration contracts and use contracts of at least six months? [Para 3][Para 194]
3. Whether the company must pay an extra 500 riel per overtime hour as a meal allowance, on top of the 2,500 riel already paid? [Para 6][Para 178]
4. Whether the company must pay 3,000 riel per day as a midday meal allowance, or instead provide a meal of a quality equal to 2,000 riel? [Para 7][Para 179][Para 185]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** authorised unpaid leave should cost only the bonus for the days actually taken, not the whole monthly bonus; short three-month contracts should be replaced by contracts of six months or more; overtime should carry an additional 500 riel per hour and the midday meal should be worth 3,000 riel, or 2,000 riel in quality, as the employer had promised. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 186]
**Employer (respondent):** the company already provides one midday meal a day in its own canteen since 1 August 2013, which is better than the Labour Law requires and was introduced because workers had fainted during working hours; paying cash instead would let workers save the money and eat inadequately, and the company will review food hygiene. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 93]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected all four remaining demands; nothing was ordered to be paid. [Para 193][Para 194][Para 195][Para 196][Para 197]
On the claimant's standing, the panel held that the factory union had not yet acquired the status of a professional organisation under the Labour Law because it was not registered, and that the federation's presence at the hearing, whether as representative of members or as assignee of some workers' rights, could not make the claimants the signatories of the memorandum of understanding of 3 October 2012. [Para 24][Para 25]
On the attendance bonus, the panel read Point 2 of Notification No. 230 Kab/SChN of 25 July 2012, in force from 1 September 2012, which added the words "without absence" to the earlier Notification No. 041 Kab/SChN of 7 March 2011, as requiring attendance on every working day of the month for the bonus to be earned. [Para 95][Para 99][Para 100][Para 104] It declined to follow its earlier awards because those rested on the superseded 2011 notification. [Para 101][Para 102] It held that authorised unpaid leave is still absence, but that leave authorised by the Labour Law or other regulations remains a valid excuse that preserves the bonus, and that permission and bonus are separate matters. [Para 112][Para 113][Para 116]
On the three-month contracts, the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules or past practice requiring contracts of six months. [Para 194]
On the overtime meal allowance, it found no legal or contractual basis for an additional 500 riel per hour above the 2,500 riel already paid. [Para 178][Para 166]
On the midday meal, it found the daily practice of one meal in the company canteen since 1 August 2013 already exceeds the legal standard, and no law, collective agreement or practice obliges the employer to pay 3,000 riel. [Para 179][Para 180][Para 181] The workers produced no evidence of the agreement they said promised a meal worth 2,000 riel, despite the panel's request, so that claim also failed. [Para 186][Para 187][Para 188] Each of these four demands was classified as an interest dispute, which the panel declined to determine. [Para 166][Para 182][Para 190]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a demand concerns a benefit the law does not require and no contract, collective agreement or established practice provides, the panel treats it as an interest dispute and declines to determine it, leaving the level of the benefit to collective bargaining rather than awarding it. [Para 166][Para 182][Para 190] A demand for a benefit is proved only by the document said to grant it; a party that asserts an agreement and produces none fails on that issue even after the panel has asked for the document. [Para 187][Para 188]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the company's canteen meal was introduced after workers had fainted during working hours, and that paying cash instead might leave workers eating less adequately — a policy view it did not need for the dismissal. [Para 90][Para 91]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award gives the employer only in Khmer script, and in two spellings — ប្រាយសិន in the title and ទ្រាយសិន in the party details — so no Latin form can be fixed and the rendering above is provisional. [Para 1] The demand count does not close: ten demands were conciliated, four settled and six unsettled, yet the six unsettled are then reduced by one settlement and one withdrawal to four, and the surviving numbering skips 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 9. [Para 15][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] The order's numbering departs from the reasons', dismissing "ចំណុចវិវាទទី ០៤" and "ទី ០៥" while the reasons discuss issues 4, 5, 8 and 10. [Para 94][Para 193][Para 194] The reasoning on the attendance bonus says authorised leave preserves the bonus, yet the order dismisses the workers' demand — the two cannot both be read as a ruling for the workers. [Para 113][Para 193] The employer's 2,000-riel figure is printed as "២ ៣០ រៀល" in para 89, a digit lost. [Para 89] The award date is 5 December 2013, the parties having postponed issue from 14 November 2013. [Para 1][Para 28] The union's own registration status is stated only in the standing section and never resolved. [Para 24]
