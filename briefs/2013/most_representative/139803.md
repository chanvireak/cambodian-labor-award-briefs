# Award No. 113/13 — Global Apparel Limited (Arbitration Council, 3 July 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 139803

## 1. Facts
Global Apparel Limited is a garment manufacturer with 2,230 workers, registered on 13 July 2011. [Para 17][Para 18] The claimant union, the Local Union of WUF, has 65 members and does not hold a certificate of most representative status (MRS) at the company. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] The union brought eight demands on bonuses, payslips, transport and overtime allowances, early departure for sick and elderly workers, and wage docking. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3] Issue 6 was withdrawn at the hearing and Issues 7 and 8 merged, leaving six issues for decision. [Para 10]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay the same team-work daily bonus to workers who meet production targets in an eight-hour shift and leave before team-mates who work ten hours? [Para 22]
2. Whether the employer must provide payslips three days before payday? [Para 33]
3. Whether the employer must raise the transport allowance from US$7 to US$15 per month? [Para 41]
4. Whether the employer must raise the overtime allowance from 2,000 riel to 4,000 riel? [Para 42]
5. Whether the employer must permit sick, elderly and less mobile workers to leave work five minutes early each day? [Para 43]
6. (Issues 7 and 8, merged) Whether the employer must refrain from docking wages when workers leave fifteen minutes early and allow them 10–15 minutes extra to arrive at and depart from work? [Para 45]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The demand for the eight-hour bonus rests on the company's meeting minutes on the daily bonus, and on the practice of other factories that pay on production targets alone; the workers had justifiable reasons to leave at 3:15 p.m. [Para 57][Para 24] The employer had tolerated early departures before February 2013 and only began docking wages then. [Para 46][Para 47]
**Employer (respondent):** The daily bonus is a team bonus conditional on the whole team reaching the target and leaving together at 3:15 p.m. or 5:15 p.m.; workers leaving earlier forfeit it. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 57] It could not raise the transport or overtime allowances because it had recently increased the minimum wage, and its accounts department was too busy to issue payslips early. [Para 38][Para 42][Para 43]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted in form, dismissed in substance — the three rights issues were decided (Issues 1 and 7–8 rejected; Issue 5 declined for want of evidence), and the three interests issues (2, 3 and 4) were declined because the union lacks MRS. No money was ordered. [Para 98][Para 100][Para 101]

Issue 1: The panel first characterised the demand. Under paragraph 2 of Article 312 of the Labour Law and Clause 43 of Prakas No. 099, disputes over the interpretation and enforcement of law or a collective agreement are rights disputes, all others being decided in equity. [Para 51][Para 54] Following Award No. 98/13-Ming Fu Garment, the panel treated the claim as a rights dispute because it was said to arise from the company's bonus minutes. [Para 55][Para 57] Reading the minutes of 31 December 2012 together with the parties' agreement at the hearing, the panel found the bonus conditional: eligibility requires the team to reach the target and leave together at 3:15 p.m. or 5:15 p.m. [Para 61][Para 63] Workers who meet the target in eight hours but leave ahead of team-mates still working ten are therefore not entitled, and the demand was rejected. [Para 64][Para 65]

Issue 2: Article 112(b) requires the employer to inform workers precisely of the items making up their wage whenever those items change, but says nothing about timing; no law, agreement, internal rule or past practice requires a payslip three days before payday. [Para 67][Para 69][Para 71] The dispute is therefore an interests dispute. [Para 72] Because an award on interests disputes takes the place of a collective agreement and removes the right to strike, the panel held it cannot decide such a dispute unless the union holds MRS or represents over half the workforce, following Awards 81/04-Evergreen, 98/04-Great Union, 169/11-Fortune Teo, 02/11-Pou Yuen and 66/11-In Han Sung. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 75] The union holds no MRS, so the demand was declined. [Para 75][Para 76]

Issues 3 and 4: No provision of law, agreement, internal rule or past practice obliges the employer to pay an additional US$8 transport allowance or an additional 2,000 riel overtime allowance, so both are interests disputes and were declined on the same MRS ground. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80]

Issue 5: The panel found this a rights dispute, as it concerned the employer's existing practice of letting workers with certified health problems leave early. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 84] The panel had ordered the workers to file, by 17 June 2013, a list of workers with health issues and of those involved in the alleged pushing incident; nothing was filed. [Para 85] Following a line of awards (63/04-Shine Well, 99/06-South Bay, 74/07-Global Apparel, 91/07-J K, 94/07-Fortune Garment, 65/09-Nugget), the panel declined to consider a demand unsupported by evidence. [Para 86][Para 87]

Issues 7 and 8: Wages under Article 102 are remuneration for work performed, as interpreted in Award 107/13-Sun Well Shoe. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 92] Workers who leave fifteen minutes early do not perform that work, so they are not entitled to wages for those fifteen minutes and the employer breaches no obligation by docking them; the demand was rejected. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 97]

The Part I rights award is binding and enforceable under the MoU of 3 October 2012; the Part II interests award is non-binding and becomes binding eight days after notification absent written opposition. [Para 99][Para 101]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A team production bonus that the employer's minutes and the parties' practice make conditional on the whole team meeting the target and leaving together is earned only on those conditions; a worker who meets the target in fewer hours but leaves the team early has no right to it. [Para 61][Para 64] A demand that is not grounded in law, a collective agreement, internal rules or established practice is an interests dispute, and a union without MRS cannot bring one, because the resulting award would substitute for a collective agreement and extinguish the right to strike. [Para 72][Para 74][Para 76]

**Obiter dicta:** Article 112(b) does not fix when wage information must be given, only that it must be precise and comprehensible when the wage items change. [Para 68][Para 69] The panel also observed that a party's failure to comply with an order to file evidence leaves it with no basis to decide the demand. [Para 86]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The English text is an unofficial translation and the OCR is badly degraded throughout: initial "th" is consistently lost, so "the employer" prints as "hemployer", "the" as "he", "this" as "his", and "eight" as "ight". The issues list is partly unnumbered and misnumbered, with item "8." appearing inside the first block while the fourth demand (4,000 riel overtime allowance) carries no number. [Para 1] The bonus minutes are dated 31 December 2013 in one passage and 31 December 2012 in two others; both are reported as printed. [Para 28][Para 57][Para 60] The workers' account of when they leave prints "between 3.15 a.m. to 5.15 a.m." where 3:15 p.m. is plainly meant. [Para 26] The Part II decision labels Issue 4 "Issue 42". [Para 101] The Issue 4 disposition describes the declined sum as "payment in lieu of lunch", contradicting the overtime allowance framing used elsewhere. [Para 80] Issues 7 and 8 were pleaded as a complaint about lateness docking but were clarified at the hearing as concerning early departure only, and one passage describes attendance "15 minutes late" rather than early. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 48] The employer's two representatives and the union's three representatives are recorded by name in the source; they are redacted here.
