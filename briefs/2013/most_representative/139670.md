# Award No. 54/13 — Im & Ve International Manufacturing Ltd (Kampong Chhnang branch) (Arbitration Council, 2 April 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mr Kao Thach (chosen by the employer), Mr Liv Sovanna (chosen by the workers), chair Mr Ang Eng Thong · Doc ID 139670

## 1. Facts
Im & Ve International Manufacturing Ltd, a garment factory in Kampong Chhnang town and province, employed 4,823 workers. [Para 1][Para 32] The Im & Ve workers' union, an affiliate of the Cambodian Workers' Union Federation with 1,035 members and a Ministry letter of 13 February 2012 recognising its second-term leadership, was the claimant but held no certificate of most-representative status. [Para 26][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35] Of the demands raised, one remained unresolved at conciliation on 7 March 2013: two work uniforms per worker per year. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 36] The employer had never supplied uniforms to workers, though it gave one shirt a year to every team leader. [Para 37][Para 48]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must supply each worker with two sets of work clothing a year, free of charge? [Para 36][Para 43]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Other factories in the sector, such as Ying Dong and Can Sport Shoes, already provide uniforms, and about 60 percent of companies in Cambodia do so; Prakas of 2007 and a notification of 2006 require the employer to supply work clothing. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]
**Employer (respondent):** It has no capacity to provide uniforms, and other garment companies have not yet applied such a practice. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 57][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to provide two sets of work clothing a year to each worker under Prakas No. 307 K.B/Pr.K of 14 December 2007; as a rights-dispute order it became final immediately and enforceable against the parties under the memorandum of understanding of 3 October 2012. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]

The panel first classified the claim as a rights dispute, since it turned on an entitlement written into a Prakas rather than on a demand for a new benefit, and it followed its earlier awards No. 05/11, No. 13/11 and No. 14/11 on that distinction. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 46][Para 47] Article 6 of Prakas No. 307 requires the employer to supply, free of charge, at least two sets of work clothing to each worker per year; the first set must be given within 15 days of the worker's first day, and each set must consist of at least two parts — trousers or a skirt and a shirt — sized to the worker's body and to the movements the job requires. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 55] The panel rejected the employer's incapacity defence outright: a Prakas is law and binds employers and workers alike, so an employer cannot escape the duty to supply work clothing on grounds of cost, and the fact that other factories have not yet complied does not excuse this one. [Para 59][Para 60] It noted that the employer supplied shirts to team leaders only, which does not meet the obligation owed to every worker. [Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The duty to supply work clothing is statutory, not contractual or discretionary: Article 6 of Prakas No. 307 obliges every employer to give each worker at least two sets a year free of charge, the first within 15 days of engagement and each set comprising at least trousers or skirt and shirt sized to the worker, and neither a claim of financial incapacity nor the non-compliance of other employers is a defence. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded the union's comparative argument — that some 60 percent of Cambodian companies already provide uniforms and that named factories do so — without deciding anything on that basis, resting the order solely on the Prakas. [Para 38][Para 39]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The procedural summary records 10 demands received at the Ministry but says conciliation left 1 unresolved out of 6 claims, and the demand list itself carries only the single uniform claim; both figures are reproduced as printed. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 36] The employer's name is printed in several forms in the same award — "Im & Ve International Manufacturing Ltd", "Im & Ve International Cutting", and the shorthand "M & V 4" in the union's title — while the order and signature block use the heading form. [Para 1][Para 22][Para 23][Para 33] Article 6 of the Prakas is quoted twice with slightly different wording ("two sets" and "two sets at least per year"), and para 53 inserts "2015" into the citation of the 2007 Prakas. [Para 50][Para 53][Para 55] The signature block carries no printed names for the employer-chosen and worker-chosen arbitrators beyond "Kao Thach" and "Liv Sovanna", and the chair's name is spelled two ways in the heading and the signature block. [Para 1][Para 64] Individual union officers are recorded only by name and are not reproduced here.
