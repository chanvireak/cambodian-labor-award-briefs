# Award No. 181/13 — Alim (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 September 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer party; An Nan, chosen by the worker party; Tang Tri, chair) · Doc ID 139857

## 1. Facts
Alim (Cambodia) Co Ltd, a garment factory at Trapeang Thleung, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, employed 584 workers. [Para 1][Para 35][Para 37] The Labour Movement Union, which collected dues from 354 members in July and holds no certificate of most-representative status, gave seven days' notice of a peaceful strike on 5 August 2013 and put three demands; none was conciliated. [Para 2][Para 13][Para 28][Para 38][Para 39] The employer began withholding wage tax from July 2013 wages, after the minimum wage was raised to USD 80 a month, and pays 2,000 riel as an overtime meal allowance. [Para 42][Para 45] At the hearing of 4 September 2013 both parties agreed to accept a binding award. [Para 12][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must stop withholding tax from the workers' monthly wages? [Para 41]
2. Whether the employer must add a further 1,000 riel meal allowance when workers work overtime from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 44]
3. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of USD 15 a month? [Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** several factories near Alim pay the wage tax on their workers' behalf, so those workers take home full wages; 2,000 riel does not buy a meal; other factories pay USD 8 to 10 a month as a midday meal allowance. [Para 41][Para 45][Para 50]
**Employer (respondent):** it applies the tax law and cannot bear the tax in place of the workers, the factory having made no profit since it opened; the 2,000 riel allowance follows the Ministry's notice and its base wage is already at least USD 85 a month. [Para 43][Para 46][Para 51][Para 52]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims were refused, the first for want of jurisdiction and the other two as interest demands the union lacked standing to bring. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 90]

Issue 1: the panel set out the three conditions of a collective labour dispute under Article 302 of the Labour Law — a dispute between workers and one or more employers, over working conditions or the exercise of professional-organisation rights, capable of disrupting the enterprise or social peace. [Para 54][Para 55] It held the condition requiring a dispute between workers and employer was not met, because withholding wage tax for the state is an obligation running between the worker and the state, represented by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, not a dispute with the employer. [Para 57][Para 58] The claim was therefore not a collective labour dispute and the panel had no jurisdiction over it. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]

Issues 2 and 3: the panel found no law, agreement or collective agreement obliging the employer to pay either allowance, so both were interest disputes (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 66][Para 67][Para 84][Para 86] Point 2 of Prakas No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011 requires 2,000 riel a day, or a free meal, for workers who volunteer for overtime at the employer's request, and the panel found the employer's current practice compliant. [Para 64][Para 65] Because Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 makes an interest award stand in place of the collective agreement for a year and removes the right to strike on that subject, the panel will only arbitrate such a dispute where the union is most representative or unions together hold over half the workforce. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 76] The Labour Movement Union held no certificate of most-representative status, so it lacked standing and the claims were not considered. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 87]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand that the employer stop withholding wage tax is not a collective labour dispute within Article 302, because the tax obligation runs between the worker and the state; the panel has no jurisdiction over it however the claim is framed. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 60] Where a statutory instrument such as Prakas No. 041/11 already fixes the overtime meal allowance at 2,000 riel a day, payment at that rate satisfies the rule, and a claim for more is an interest dispute that only a most-representative union may bring. [Para 64][Para 76][Para 79]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's survey of how neighbouring factories treat the wage tax [Para 41] and its observation that the employer's base wage already exceeds USD 85 [Para 52] were noted but played no part in the three refusals, which rested on jurisdiction and standing.

- The jurisdiction recital at [Para 8] is corrupted by inserted text ("after 20 September 2017 ... 2019") and by an unrelated phrase about motor vehicles; the Prakas number and date had to be taken from the later references at [Para 74].
- The employer's answers at [Para 43] and [Para 46] are each printed twice, with the stray tokens "Chinic" and "Chin" inside them.

- The invitation to the employer at [Para 32] carries no date ("ចុះ ថ្ងៃទី ឆ្នាំ ២០១៣"), and the conciliation minute is dated 23 August 2013 in one entry and 23 August in another with the month misspelt. [Para 31]
