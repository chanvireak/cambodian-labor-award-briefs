# Award No. 25/13 — Mee Rei Ei Pearl (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 February 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, selected by the employer; Liv Sovanna, selected by the workers; Kong Phallaph, chair) · Doc ID 139623

## 1. Facts
Mee Rei Ei Pearl (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment factory in Steung Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, with about 580 workers. [Para 54] On 19 December 2012 the Khmer Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ), holding transfers of authority from 60 workers, filed 13 demands; conciliation on 16 January 2013 settled none of seven remaining points, and the non-conciliation report No. 106 of 22 January 2013 came to the Council. [Para 21][Para 58][Para 23] At the hearing the workers withdrew demand 6, a US$9 rise in the base wage to US$70 a month, leaving six live claims. [Para 27][Para 87][Para 88]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide one free midday meal or 5,000 riel a day to every worker? [Para 59][Para 117]
2. Whether the employer must hold an annual party with a prize draw before the 8 March Women's Day holiday? [Para 66][Para 137]
3. Whether the employer must pay the overtime meal allowance once a week? [Para 73][Para 142]
4. Whether the employer must give every pregnant worker one day a month for antenatal checks on full wage and allowances, regardless of distance from home? [Para 80][Para 163]
5. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance of 1,500 riel per overtime hour? [Para 83][Para 171]
6. Whether the employer must restore the skill allowance as previously paid and reimburse the amounts withheld since December 2012? [Para 89][Para 179]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** market food is expensive and monthly wages are small, so the 1,500 riel lunch money is not enough; overtime is worked every day, so a daily meal is needed, and several named factories pay the overtime meal allowance weekly; the skill allowance of US$5, US$15 and US$20 by sewing grade was paid until December 2012 and its withdrawal was never explained. [Para 61][Para 74][Para 77][Para 90]
**Employer (respondent):** the company has paid 1,500 riel lunch money since June 2011 and keeps its old policy; orders have fallen so it cannot fund a party or a rise to 5,000 riel; it already pays 2,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 pm and 1,300 riel more from 6:00 to 8:30 pm; the skill allowance was an incentive outside the contract, exchanged for the 5% severance payment and the move to an indefinite-term contract. [Para 63][Para 65][Para 72][Para 173][Para 94]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — claims 1, 2, 4 and 5 dismissed; the employer ordered to pay the overtime meal allowance once a week to workers who work overtime, and to restore the skill allowance as before and reimburse it from the date it was stopped. [Para 215][Para 217][Para 220] Arbitrator Ing Sothy appended a dissent on issue 6 only. [Para 223]

The panel first held the union entitled to represent members: the Ministry received its registration application on 5 December 2012 and had not replied by the hearing, and under Article 268(1) and (2) of the Labour Law silence for two months deems the union registered. [Para 113][Para 116]

On claims 1, 2 and 4 the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement or party agreement fixing a 5,000 riel lunch payment, an annual party and draw, or a full day a month for antenatal checks; each was a demand above what the law gives, hence an interest dispute (វិវាទ អំពី ផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 120][Para 166][Para 167] Applying Article 96 and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, and Prakas No. 099 Article 43, the panel held it may decide an interest dispute only where the claimant union is the most representative in the enterprise; the local union here was not, so the claims were refused consideration. [Para 127][Para 134][Para 136]

On claim 3, point 2 of Ministry Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011 requires 2,000 riel a day or a free meal for workers who volunteer for overtime, but fixes no interval of payment. [Para 144][Para 146] Following Award No. 76/11 Chong Thich and others, the panel held the allowance need only be paid at an interval that lets the worker actually buy food, but since the employer gave no reason why weekly payment was impossible, and the workers' demand was reasonable and necessary, it ordered weekly payment. [Para 150][Para 156][Para 160][Para 162]

On claim 5 the panel found the employer already paid 2,000 riel for the 4:00–6:00 pm stretch plus 1,300 riel for 6:00–8:30 pm, meeting and exceeding the notification, so the claim for 1,500 riel an hour was refused. [Para 173][Para 174]

On claim 6 the panel found the skill allowance paid continuously from 1 September 2011 to December 2012 — about a year and three months — openly and accepted by both sides. [Para 191][Para 192] Under Article 65(2) and (3) of the Labour Law an unwritten agreement binds the parties, so the practice had become an oral contract conferring a benefit above the law. [Para 186][Para 188][Para 193] A past practice may end only by agreement, by a replacement practice, or by a change in the circumstances that produced it, and the party asserting the agreement bears the burden of proof. [Para 195][Para 203] The employer produced a probation contract, an undated notice and two thumb-printed consent letters, but none mentioned the skill allowance; they covered only the 5% severance payment and the conversion to an indefinite-term contract, and the workers protested at once. [Para 205][Para 206][Para 209] The employer therefore could not withdraw the practice unilaterally; the panel ordered restoration with back pay. [Para 212][Para 214]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A continuous, open employer practice granting workers a benefit beyond what the law requires, accepted by both sides over a sustained period, becomes an oral contract binding them under Article 65 of the Labour Law; the employer cannot revoke it unilaterally, and may end it only by agreement, by a substitute practice, or on proof that the circumstances underlying it have changed — the burden of that proof lying on the party asserting it. [Para 186][Para 193][Para 195][Para 212]

**Obiter dicta:** remarks that an award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement for one year under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, and that deciding interest claims is confined to the most representative union, are general observations on the Council's jurisdiction rather than holdings on these facts. [Para 127][Para 129]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Issue numbering is inconsistent: the reasons run 1 to 5 and then jump to 7, because demand 6 was withdrawn [Para 87][Para 179][Para 219]. The decision section also heads the final order "ទី ៧" after omitting 6.
- The probation contract is dated 15 November 2011 for a term of 15 November 2011 to 15 January 2012 at [Para 205] but 15 November 2012 to 15 January 2012 at [Para 102], which runs backwards.
- Letter No. 035 of the Federation appears as dated 11 December 2013 at [Para 30], though the complaint it precedes is dated 19 December 2012 [Para 21][Para 45].
- The hearing date is printed as 6 February "ឆ្នាំ ២០២" — the year is truncated to three digits at [Para 114].
- The two sides give different skill-allowance scales: the workers say US$5, US$15 and US$20 by sewing grade [Para 90], the employer's written statement says US$3, US$5, US$7 and US$20 [Para 98]; the panel noted the discrepancy and preferred the employer's version [Para 100][Para 101].
- The employer-side arbitrator's name is lost in the signature block, appearing only as "mountone ( <unk> )" at [Para 222]; it is recovered from the face of the award at [Para 1].
- The company name is printed four ways: ម៉ី រ៉េ អី ភែរឹល, មី រ៉េ, ម៉ីរ៉េ អី ភែរីល and, in the dissent heading, ម៉ី រ៉េ អី ភរីល [Para 1][Para 223].
- The order on claim 5 omits the word បាយ ("rice"), reading only "ប្រាក់ ថ្លៃ បន្ថែម ម៉ោង" at [Para 219] where the claim was a meal allowance.
- Stray numerals intrude at [Para 16], [Para 28] and [Para 205], consistent with OCR noise across the original.
