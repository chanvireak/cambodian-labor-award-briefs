# Award No. 55/13 — Tae Young (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 2 April 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Sok Mathoeung (president) · Doc ID 139674

## 1. Facts
Tae Young (Cambodia) is a garment factory employing about 780 workers, of whom roughly 300 belong to the Cambodian Union of Workers at Tae Young, the most representative union in the plant. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] The union claimed a 3 US dollar incentive for January 2013 for some 600 workers who left at 4:00 pm on 31 January 2013 without working overtime until 6:00 pm. [Para 39] An agreement of 5 February 2013 between the union federation and the factory made the 3 dollar payment conditional on full attendance and on completing the month's overtime from 4 to 6 pm. [Para 45] Both parties had signed the memorandum of understanding of 3 October 2012, so the award on a rights dispute binds them immediately. [Para 17][Para 73]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay each worker a 3 US dollar incentive for January 2013 where the worker left at 4:00 pm without performing overtime from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm? [Para 39]
2. Whether that claim is a rights dispute (វិវាទអំពីសិទ្ធិ) or an interest dispute, and therefore whether the award binds the parties at once or only after the eight-day objection period? [Para 60]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The employer had only just begun paying the incentive, and workers wanted to take the shared transport home with employees of other factories who finish at 4:00 pm; 31 January 2013 was a special day because 1 February 2013 was the procession of the late King's coffin, and the union asked twice for an early release without loss of the bonus. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 48]
**Employer (respondent):** The 3 dollars is a conditional payment tied to overtime from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm; team leaders and the union were briefed on the condition, and the workers' requests for release were refused. [Para 42][Para 46][Para 49]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the claim for 3 US dollars per worker for January 2013, as a binding award on a rights dispute effective immediately between the parties. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]

The panel began by classifying the claim, applying article 312(a) paragraph 2 of the Labour Law and article 43 of Prakas No. 099: disputes over the interpretation or application of a law, agreement or collective agreement are rights disputes, decided according to law, while all others are interest disputes decided in equity. [Para 52][Para 55] Because the claim rested on clause 3 of the 5 February 2013 agreement, the panel held it to be a rights dispute. [Para 60][Para 56]

On the merits, clause 3 provides 3 US dollars to workers who worked the full month without absence and who helped with overtime for the full month from 4 to 6 pm, except where the company granted permission. [Para 45][Para 64] The panel found the employer had told team leaders to publicise the condition, and the workers themselves admitted they had been informed through their team leaders on 1 January 2013 that leaving at 4:00 pm would forfeit the payment. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 47] It also found that the decisive condition was performing overtime from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm, so that only workers who did so could receive the money. [Para 67] Since the claimants did not meet the condition, they had no right to the incentive for January, and the claim failed. [Para 69][Para 70]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a collective agreement makes an incentive payment conditional on completing the month's overtime, a worker who leaves at the normal finishing time instead of working the extra hours does not acquire a right to the payment, and the panel will enforce the condition as written rather than relax it because the workers had a good personal or symbolic reason to leave early. [Para 45][Para 67][Para 70]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded that the parties disputed only the conditions attached to the overtime, not the existence of the 3 dollar incentive itself, which the employer had begun paying in the previous month. [Para 44][Para 40]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The file is short and comparatively clean, but several defects remain. The date the panel was constituted is printed as "13 March 2023 2013", and the operative paragraph prints the claimed sum as "20 03 dollars" before settling on 3 dollars. [Para 13][Para 72] The company appears variously as Tae Young, TAE YOUNG (CAMBODIA) CO., LTD and "TAE LYOUNG", including in one quoted ministerial letter; the award name and the registration documents do not match. [Para 1][Para 23] The signature block is incomplete, with no signature printed for any of the three arbitrators and a marginal note recording that fact. [Para 74]
