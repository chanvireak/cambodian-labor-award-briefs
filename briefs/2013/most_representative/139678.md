# Award No. 61/13 — Global Apparel (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 23 April 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Ang Eng Thong chair) · Doc ID 139678

## 1. Facts
Global Apparel is a garment factory at Kambol, Phnom Penh, employing about 2,600 workers. [Para 57][Para 58] The Cambodian Workers' Development Union Federation and its factory union, registered 4 March 2013, filed 15 claims to improve working conditions; 10 points were not conciliated, three (points 8, 9 and 10) were settled at the hearing, point 1 was withdrawn, and six remained for decision. [Para 27][Para 29][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] At the date of the award the claimant union was not the most representative union in the factory. [Para 61]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer is obliged to pay a midday meal allowance of 2,000 riel per day to each worker? [Para 119]
3. Whether the employer is obliged to add USD 3 per month to the existing USD 7 transport and accommodation allowance, bringing it to USD 10? [Para 133]
4. Whether the employer must pay seniority bonus to workers who accepted 5% of total wages and signed a new fixed-duration contract? [Para 137]
5. Whether the employer is obliged to pay a further 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance, bringing the daily total to 4,000 riel? [Para 149]
6. Whether the employer is obliged to issue payslips three days before the monthly pay day? [Para 153]
7. Whether the employer is obliged to give each worker a box of cakes worth about USD 3 at Khmer New Year? [Para 174]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** a meal allowance would free part of their wage for other needs and other factories provide one; USD 7 does not cover travel that costs some workers up to USD 12 a month; the 5% payment should not extinguish seniority bonus; 2,000 riel for an overtime meal is too little as food prices rise; an advance payslip would let them verify and challenge miscalculations, as one worker's four missing hours in 2011 showed; and only 300 lottery prizes are offered for 2,600 workers. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 74][Para 86][Para 90][Para 102][Para 104][Para 110][Para 111]

**Employer (respondent):** the company cannot meet any of the claims; it has never paid a midday meal allowance; it complies with Notification No. 230 and has no duty to top up the overtime meal; the 5% option was chosen voluntarily with all conditions explained and no coercion; and it has had no time to issue payslips early in nearly ten years of practice. [Para 68][Para 77][Para 81][Para 83][Para 95][Para 106][Para 108][Para 112]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — every remaining claim (points 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7) was refused, and the award is non-binding and becomes final if no party objects within eight days. [Para 178][Para 179][Para 180][Para 183][Para 184]

The panel treated claims 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 as interest disputes, since no provision of the Labour Law, no collective agreement and no agreement between the parties granted what was claimed. [Para 119][Para 120][Para 133][Para 134][Para 149][Para 150][Para 170][Para 171][Para 174][Para 175] Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 reserve conclusion of a collective agreement to the most representative union, and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 makes an award on an interest dispute stand in place of the collective agreement for one year. [Para 121][Para 123][Para 125][Para 126] Following Awards 81/04 Evergreen, 98/04 Great Union, 169/11 and 02/11 Puyean, only a most representative union, or unions together holding over half the workforce, may bring such a dispute; this union was not, so the panel declined to consider the claims. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129][Para 130][Para 131][Para 132]

On claim 6 the panel additionally held that Article 112(b) obliges the employer to notify wages clearly and understandably whenever they change, but does not require the payslip to be handed over before the pay day; issuing it on the pay day satisfied the article. [Para 153][Para 162][Para 165][Para 166][Para 168][Para 170][Para 173] On claim 4 the panel found the workers had given no particulars — length of service, duration of the new fixed-duration contract, when the bonus stopped — for the 15 workers said to claim, and dismissed for want of evidence, consistent with Awards 63/04 Sapywell, 33/07 and 51/07 Gold Fame. [Para 141][Para 142][Para 143][Para 144][Para 145][Para 147][Para 148]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claim that exceeds what the Labour Law, a collective agreement or an actual agreement confers is an interest dispute, and the panel will decline to consider it unless the union bringing it is the most representative in the enterprise or, with others, represents more than half the workforce — because the resulting award replaces the collective agreement for a year and binds all workers. [Para 126][Para 127][Para 131]

Article 112(b) requires that the wage be notified clearly and understandably when it changes; it does not require the payslip to be delivered before the pay day. [Para 165][Para 170]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that itemised payslips are transparent and remove workers' suspicions of their wages, and would improve professional relations, go beyond what Article 112(b) requires and are not part of the order. [Para 163][Para 164]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The count of non-conciliated points is given three ways: "10 of 15 claims" at [Para 29], "08 points" at [Para 30], and 10 again at [Para 33]; the arithmetic only reconciles with 10 minus three settled minus one withdrawn giving the six decided at [Para 38].
- The seniority payment is "550 percent" in the list of demands [Para 8] and 5 percent everywhere else [Para 9][Para 78][Para 137].
- The evidence list includes the internal rules of "Faska Garment (Cambodia) Ltd" [Para 47], a company not party to the case; the non-conciliation report names the employer "Global Garment" [Para 53].
- Registration is dated twice: certificate No. 3984 of 13 July 2011 [Para 46] and registration No. Inv. 810E/2003 [Para 57].
- The panel's constitution is dated "18 March 2023 2013" [Para 32], and the file number in the transfer letter is followed by a stray 2013 date [Para 45].
- The quotation of Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 is badly corrupted at [Para 125], and the paraphrase at [Para 126] has interest-dispute awards stripping all workers of the right to strike, where Award 57/13 on the same article speaks of losing the right to claim.
- [Para 174] contains unreadable Latin-script fragments ("2016 everourong in Shownn and 1970") in the middle of the Khmer text.
