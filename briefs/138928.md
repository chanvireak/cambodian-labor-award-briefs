# Award No. 025/17-H A Green Gemin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 18 July 2017)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chiv Phirom, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Palha, president) · Doc ID 138928

## 1. Facts
H A Green Gemin (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (អេច អ ហ្គ្រេន ហ្គាម៉ិន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី) is a garment-cutting and sewing enterprise at Kien Svay, Kandal, employing about 820 workers, of whom the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union claims some 590 as members [Para 45][Para 47]. The federation filed seven demands on working conditions; four were settled at conciliation on 26 June 2017 and the remaining three were referred to the Council by report No. 1497 [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14]. The disputed acts are the employer's refusal to pay a 3,000 riel daily lunch allowance, a sewing-skill allowance, and its deduction of the monthly attendance bonus for authorised personal leave [Para 50][Para 54][Para 63].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to pay 3,000 riel per day as a lunch allowance to the workers? [Para 88]
2. Whether the employer is obliged to pay a skill allowance of USD 5, USD 10 and USD 15 per month to workers able to operate one-, two- and three-needle sewing machines? [Para 113]
3. Whether the employer is obliged to deduct the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់) pro rata to the days a worker is absent on authorised personal leave (ធុរៈ)? [Para 118]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** neighbouring factories (Yida, E-Cheng, Wing Star Shoes, Olive, CXC, Reliable Source) pay 1,000–2,000 riel a day for lunch, so 3,000 riel is reasonable and reduces living costs; the skill allowance would reward workers who can run several machines; the attendance bonus is wages, so it must be cut in proportion to days absent [Para 51][Para 57][Para 66]. The union also relied on its registration certificate of 4 March 2016 and a Ministry letter confirming its lawful status [Para 32][Para 33].
**Employer (respondent):** it has no capacity to meet any of the three demands; neither the internal rules nor any prior agreement provides for a lunch or skill allowance [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56][Para 59]. The attendance bonus is conditional on regular attendance, and pro-rating it would encourage absenteeism that disrupts the production line [Para 67][Para 68].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three claims were refused and no sum was ordered against the company; the award was issued as non-binding (សេចក្តីបង្គាប់មិនជាប់កាតព្វកិច្ច) and becomes final eight days after notification unless a party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour [Para 138][Para 139][Para 140][Para 141].

The panel dealt first with representation, because Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law 2016 reserve to the most representative union the exclusive right to conclude a collective agreement and to conduct interest disputes before the Council [Para 70][Para 73][Para 102]. The claimant union had around 590 members but had never applied for a most-representative certificate, a fact the workers conceded [Para 48][Para 110][Para 115]. Following its own awards in 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting) and 171/16 (Jewelry Line), the panel held that absence of a most-representative union does not destroy representation — workers may act directly or delegate, and Prakas 362 allows the provincial labour office to recognise worker representatives, as it did here on 26 June 2017 [Para 76][Para 80][Para 83][Para 87]. That recognition cured the procedural defect in who spoke for the workers, but it did not supply the substantive standing the union needed [Para 111].

On issues 1 and 2 the panel found no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice creating a lunch or skill allowance, and therefore classified both demands as interest disputes rather than rights disputes [Para 89][Para 90][Para 113]. It then declined to entertain them because the union bringing them was not most representative: an interest award displaces the collective agreement for a year under Article 43 of Prakas 099, so only a union entitled to conclude such an agreement may trigger it [Para 103][Para 107][Para 108]. Prior awards 118/16 (E-Cheng) and 223/16, 224/16, 008/17 and 237/16 were followed to the same effect [Para 109][Para 85].

Issue 3 was decided on the merits but against the workers. Point 2 of Notification No. 230 (25 July 2012) requires the employer to pay at least USD 10 a month to workers with no absences, and the panel read the condition as regular attendance, not merely authorised absence [Para 118][Para 123][Para 124]. Granting personal leave is an exercise of managerial discretion under Article 2 of the Labour Law, distinct from the obligation to pay the bonus, so permission for leave does not certify that the bonus condition was met [Para 126][Para 127][Para 129][Para 130]. The employer's actual scheme — USD 13 a month, with deductions of USD 3.5, 6.5 and 9.5 for half-, one- and one-and-a-half-day absences and total loss at two days — was found more favourable than the statutory floor and consistent with a 2016 agreement with the union whose text neither side produced [Para 132][Para 135][Para 136]. The claim for pro-rata deduction was therefore refused [Para 137].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A local union that has never obtained a most-representative certificate cannot bring an interest dispute to the Arbitration Council, because an award on interests substitutes for the collective agreement and only a union competent to conclude one may set that process in motion; recognised worker delegates may conduct the case, but they cannot confer standing the union lacks [Para 107][Para 111]. Separately, where the employer's attendance-bonus scheme pays more than the USD 10 minimum in Notification No. 230 and deducts on a published graduated scale, the panel will not order pro-rata deduction for authorised personal leave, because permission to be absent and satisfaction of the bonus condition are separate matters [Para 129][Para 136].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's resort to the 1989 Chuon Nath dictionary to define ធុរៈ (personal business unconnected with contractual duty) and its remark that internal leave procedures exist to protect the production line, neither of which was necessary given the outcome [Para 120][Para 127].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award date is printed three ways: 18 July 2017 in the header and at the signature block, but 19 July 2017 as the date of issuance in the procedural recital [Para 1][Para 21][Para 149]. The company name is OCR'd inconsistently throughout (អេច អ ហ្គ្រេន, អេច អរ ហ្គ្រេន, អេហ្វ្រេន), and the president's name appears as គង់ ផល្ហៈ on the face and គង់ ផល្លូវ at the signature [Para 1][Para 142]. The list of worker representatives at Para 49 is corrupted by Latin garbage and an embedded date, so individual names there cannot be relied on. A stray figure "$74472" appears where a document reference belongs [Para 40]. The 2016 bonus-deduction agreement was never produced to the panel, and its terms are recorded only as the parties' joint recollection [Para 134][Para 135]. Arbitrator An Non filed a dissenting opinion on issue 3, arguing that the majority departed from a line of prior awards (223/2012, 216/2012, 214/2012, 211/2012, 204/2012, 203/2012, 180/2012) without reasons [Para 144][Para 147][Para 148].
