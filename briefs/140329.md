# Order No. 119/10 — Jing Cheung (Ying Feng) garment factory (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីង ចឹង (យិង ហ្វេង)), Dangkao, Phnom Penh (Arbitration Council, 25 November 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding; សេង វួច ហ៊ុន, employer nominee; លីវ សុវណ្ណា, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140329

## 1. Facts
Jing Cheung (ជីង ចឹង), the new name of the Ying Feng (យិង ហ្វេង) garment factory at Ang Keo village, Choam Chao commune, Dangkao district, Phnom Penh, employs about 320 workers, most of them long-serving Ying Feng staff. [Para 1][Para 28][Para 43] The claimant was the Khmer Workers' Power Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព អំណាច កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) and its factory branch, whose three leaders were president, vice-president and secretary. [Para 1][Para 59] Its complaint of 15 September 2010 pressed fourteen demands; conciliation on 4 October 2010 settled none. [Para 24][Para 25] The employer did not appear at the hearing of 12 November 2010, saying the factory is named Jing Cheung and not Ying Feng, so the dispute did not concern it. [Para 28][Para 58] The panel decided issues 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 13 and 14; the demands on soap and toilets, lot prices, accident law, wage deductions, cash in lieu of leave, consultation before dismissal and early departure for pregnant workers were left undecided. [Para 3][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must take back its three dismissed union leaders and pay them back wages? [Para 126][Para 127]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must keep a nurse and a doctor on duty while its workers work? [Para 151][Para 152]
3. (Issue 3) Whether overtime must be voluntary? [Para 159][Para 160]
4. (Issue 8) Whether the company must pay the seniority bonus set by Notification No. 017? [Para 170][Para 171]
5. (Issue 9) Whether the company must grant paid annual leave? [Para 182][Para 183]
6. (Issue 13) Whether the company must pay half wages during 90 days' maternity leave? [Para 193][Para 195]
7. (Issue 14) Whether nursing mothers must be allowed one hour a day to breastfeed? [Para 205][Para 206]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព អំណាច កម្មករ ខ្មែរ:** the company dismissed its three union leaders after the Ministry registered them on 7 September 2010, using guards to bar them from the gate and withholding wages, without stating any fault or giving notice; the factory has no doctor on duty, only cola oil and bandages, and a worker who fainted was sent home with that day's wage docked; overtime is forced, with the fingerprint clock locked away so that anyone leaving at 4:00 pm loses USD 2 of wages and the USD 5 attendance bonus; and the company has never paid the seniority bonus, paid leave, maternity pay or a feeding hour. [Para 59][Para 75][Para 83][Para 89][Para 91][Para 99][Para 105]
**Employer (respondent) — Jing Cheung (Ying Feng):** it did not attend and gave no answer on the merits; its position was that the factory is Jing Cheung, that the union's papers name the old factory Ying Feng, and that the fourteen disputes therefore have nothing to do with it. [Para 17][Para 58][Para 112]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — all seven issues decided were granted: reinstatement and back wages for the three union leaders; a nurse and doctor on duty at least two hours of each eight-hour shift; voluntary overtime; and the seniority bonus, paid annual leave, maternity half pay for 90 days and one feeding hour a day. [Para 213][Para 214][Para 215][Para 216][Para 217][Para 218][Para 219]
The panel first held the hearing in the employer's absence under Article 21 of Prakas 099, which permits it to proceed where an invited party fails to appear without proper cause, and found the employer's reason no proper cause; it decided on the workers' evidence alone. [Para 109][Para 115]
It then held that the change of name did not release the employer: under Article 87(1) of the Labour Law, where the employer's legal status changes, contracts in force continue between the new employer and the workers of the old enterprise, force majeure aside. [Para 116][Para 120][Para 121] The workers were old Ying Feng staff under one employer, their contracts never terminated and their seniority preserved, so Jing Cheung remained liable. [Para 122][Para 125]
On issue 1 the panel applied Article 293, under which a union leader may not be dismissed without the labour inspector's agreement, and Article 4 of Prakas 1305 of 22 November 2001, which protects three union leaders. [Para 127][Para 129][Para 130] Dismissing them without that authorisation was contrary to the Labour Law; on discrimination, the worker normally bears the burden, but the company had neither appeared nor produced evidence to rebut the charge, so the panel held the dismissals connected to anti-union discrimination. [Para 141][Para 149][Para 150]
On issue 2 it applied Article 238, requiring first aid, and Prakas 330 of 6 December 2000: with 320 workers a nurse on duty and a doctor for at least two of each eight-hour shift were required, and no first aid had been arranged. [Para 152][Para 156][Para 158] On issue 3 it applied Article 4 of Prakas 80 of 1 March 1999, which makes overtime voluntary and forbids pressure or discipline against workers who refuse it; the docking of wages proved compulsion. [Para 160][Para 165][Para 169]
On issue 8 it applied point 5 of Notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000, preserved by point 3 of Notification No. 745 KKBV of 23 October 2006: a worker of one to four years' service receives USD 2 to 5 a month, and the employer had never paid it. [Para 171][Para 176][Para 180] On issue 9 it applied Articles 166 and 167: paid annual leave of one and a half working days a month, plus one day for each three years of service, available after a full year's work. [Para 183][Para 186][Para 192]
On issue 13 it applied Articles 182 and 183: 90 days' maternity leave with half wages and allowances, for women with at least one year's service. [Para 195][Para 199][Para 204] On issue 14 it applied Articles 184 and 185: one hour a day within working hours for a year after birth, splittable into two thirty-minute periods and separate from ordinary leave. [Para 206][Para 209][Para 212] The order is not binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects to the Minister. [Para 220]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A change in the employer's name or legal status does not extinguish its obligations: contracts continue with the new employer under Article 87(1) of the Labour Law, so an employer cannot escape a dispute by asserting that the claimant's papers bear the factory's former name; and where an employer, duly invited, stays away without proper cause, the panel will proceed under Article 21 of Prakas 099 and treat its failure to rebut the evidence as establishing the anti-union dismissal and the entitlements claimed. [Para 115][Para 121][Para 125][Para 149]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the employer should have attended to explain whether Jing Cheung bore responsibility, and its earlier invitation to it to do so, were not necessary to the orders made. [Para 113][Para 114]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is named "ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជីង ចឹង (យិង ហ្វេង)" on the face but the reasons use "រោងចក្រ យ៉ុង ថា ហ្វេង", "ជីង ទីង" and "យិង ហេង" for the same factory, and the facility is variously called a company and a workshop. [Para 1][Para 28][Para 44][Para 112] The reasons take the fourteen demands out of order and skip issues 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11 and 12 entirely, so only the seven decided are recorded above. [Para 2][Para 126] Notification No. 017 is cited as "លេខ ២០ សកបយ" in one paragraph and as "លេខ ០១៧ សកបយ" elsewhere, and the seniority table breaks off mid-sentence. [Para 171][Para 176] The foot signature block is overlaid with stray characters. [Para 221] The three dismissed leaders are named in full throughout the source; they are given here only by office, as are the worker representatives. [Para 1][Para 59]
