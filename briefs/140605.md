# Award No. 42/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សោធន៍ បេ (Arbitration Council, 11 June 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (លី តៃ សេង for the employer party, អន ណន for the worker party, គង់ ជល្ហៈ chair) · Doc ID 140605

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សោធន៍ បេ, a garment factory at Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh with 638 workers, of whom 200 signed the complaint, required every worker to pass a medical check after two months of service and, on the workers' account, left them to pay the 10,100 riels themselves. [Para 1][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 27] The workers are organised in សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ រោងចក្រ (C.CAWDU) សៅធន៍ បេ, which is registered. [Para 1][Para 43] Three demands were referred after conciliation failed on 20 April 2007: reimbursement of the medical-check fee, removal of the card-code machine at the toilets, and a rule that no worker may be moved to another section without consent; the second was settled at the hearing, leaving the first and third. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 19][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must reimburse the 10,100 riels medical-check fee to all workers? [Para 25][Para 48]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must obtain a worker's consent before transferring him or her from one section to another, and take the two named ironing workers back into the ironing section? [Para 59][Para 81]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ រោងចក្រ (C.CAWDU) សៅធន៍ បេ:** the employer must repay the health-check fee the workers paid themselves since 2001, and must not move workers between sections, or back to the ironing section, without their agreement, since the workers who protested most were moved to the warehouse where pay is fixed and lower than piece-rate ironing. [Para 25][Para 31][Para 32][Para 36]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន សោធន៍ បេ:** its internal rules make a medical check a condition of being hired; the warehouse needed staff and the two workers were moved for that reason and for refusing instructions, though no evidence of that was produced; transfer is the company's right. [Para 3][Para 34][Para 35]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider both the claim for reimbursement of the 10,100 riels medical-check fee and the claim for consent before transfer and for the return of the two workers to ironing. [Para 80][Para 81]

On issue 1 the panel first held that a registered union may represent its members, but only its own members, since the union produced nothing to show that non-members had asked it to act. [Para 41][Para 44][Para 47] It then found the demand identical to the one already decided in case 15/05 (and pleaded at [Para 28] as case 11/05), and applied the principle of res judicata — គោលការណ៍ អាជ្ញា អស់ ជំនុំ — holding that a dispute finally decided between the same parties cannot be reopened, a principle it said applies both inside and outside the court system. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 52][Para 53][Para 57] On issue 3 it applied Article 312 of the Labour Law, which limits the panel to the issues recorded in the non-conciliation minutes or to matters arising after them as a direct consequence of the dispute, and found the transfer demand was neither, because the transfers happened before the conciliation and the report. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 78][Para 79] It also noted that a demand about transfers in the future lacks the concrete character a panel can decide. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand between the same parties that an earlier arbitration award has already decided will not be decided again, whatever the merits, and a demand that appears neither in the non-conciliation record nor as a direct consequence of the recorded dispute falls outside the panel's jurisdiction under Article 312 of the Labour Law. [Para 53][Para 57][Para 74][Para 79]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that the arbitration council exists to settle disputes that have arisen, not problems that have not yet arisen, and that the union's right to represent is confined to its own members, were not necessary to the two dismissals. [Para 47][Para 70]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The prior award is cited as case 11/05 in one passage and case 15/05 in three others; the operative section also refers inconsistently to "ចំណុច ទី ១៦". [Para 28][Para 48][Para 56][Para 80]
- The referral report is dated "ថ្ងៃទី៣០ ខែមេសា/ឆ្នាំ២០១៧" and a page header reads "2017", years that do not exist in this case. [Para 11][Para 12]
- The fee is printed as 10.100 riels in the demand and reasons but as "១០០ រៀល" once in the summary of the workers' claim. [Para 2][Para 25]
- The chair's name appears as គង់ ជល្ហៈ at the header and គង់ ផល្លៈ at the signature block, and the worker-party signature line is lost to OCR characters. [Para 1][Para 83]
- Two ironing workers are named in full throughout the reasoning; they are described by section and role here rather than reproduced, and the operative paragraphs on issue 3 are corrupted by OCR into unrelated fragments. [Para 29][Para 81]
