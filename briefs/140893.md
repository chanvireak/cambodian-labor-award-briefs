# Award No. 004/25 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន រ៉ាយ លីង (ខេមបូឌា) អឹភែរឹល ឯ.ក (Arbitration Council, 30 September 2025)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ឈីវ ភិរម្យ appointed by the employer, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ appointed by the workers, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding) · Doc ID 140893

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន រ៉ាយ លីង (ខេមបូឌា) អឹភែរឹល ឯ.ក is a garment factory in Thpong district, Kampong Speu, whose workers bargained through the union សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ, affiliated to the K.V.Y.U.F. federation. [Para 1][Para 47] The workers' representative filed a 17-point claim on 28 July 2025; the Department settled 15 points on 14 August 2025 and referred 2 to the Council on 20 August 2025. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12] At the hearing the workers dropped the demand for a copy of their contracts, leaving the sewing-target and contract-duration issues. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 78]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must set a fixed, unchanging sewing target for the workers in each sewing team? [Para 51][Para 104]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must conclude fixed-duration employment contracts of at least six months with the 18 claimants rather than three-month contracts? [Para 80][Para 131]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the workers' representative argued that the employer raised the weekly target week after week — 130, then 140, 150 and 160 pairs of trousers per hour — while the target bonus stayed at 2,000 riel, that workers who missed the target were warned orally and made to thumbprint a warning letter, and that three-month contracts leave workers afraid of non-renewal. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 61][Para 63][Para 83]
**Employer (respondent):** the company said targets are set by its IE unit with the Chinese section heads after timing each operation, rise by only 10 pieces an hour when a team meets the target, are held steady when a team cannot, and cause no loss of base wage or disciplinary action; targets and bonus schemes are a unilateral management right. [Para 67][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72][Para 106]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both the demand for a fixed sewing target and the demand for six-month fixed-duration contracts were rejected. [Para 161][Para 162]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law, which it read as giving the employer the right to manage the enterprise, subject to legality and reasonableness, and followed its earlier awards 06/06, 18/06, 108/06, 33/07 and 119/09 on unilateral bonus schemes. [Para 104][Para 105][Para 106] The employer's time-study tables and bonus schedules showed targets set by operation, machine and headcount, and both sides accepted that falling short costs no base wage, no warning and no dismissal. [Para 123][Para 125][Para 127] The claim that workers were warned and pressured was uncorroborated, and the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof. [Para 112][Para 115]
On Issue 2 the panel held Article 73 of the Labour Law governs termination only and says nothing about contract length, and that the six-month example in Ministry Guidance 050/19 of 17 May 2019 is illustrative rather than mandatory. [Para 133][Para 134] Under Article 65 and Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code a contract needs matching offer and acceptance, so neither side can force a duration on the other. [Para 135][Para 138][Para 142]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Targets and incentive schemes that the Labour Law does not regulate are within the employer's managerial power under Article 2, and the panel will not fix them by award where the scheme is set by time study, keeps base wages intact, and imposes no disciplinary consequence for shortfall. [Para 105][Para 127]
Guidance 050/19 illustrates how to classify contracts; it does not oblige an employer to sign six-month terms, because a fixed duration is a term of the contract and a contract requires the consent of both parties. [Para 134][Para 155]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that even the employer cannot impose a three-month term on a worker who does not agree, so the freedom it affirmed runs both ways. [Para 158]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The workforce figure is printed as 120 workers while the same sentence gives 432 women. [Para 47] The employer-appointed arbitrator's name differs between the face of the award and the signature block. [Para 1][Para 164] The reasoning refers to awards "2017" and stray fragments of an earlier template interrupt paragraphs in the facts and the annex. [Para 78][Para 179] The claimant's letter is dated 25 July 2025 but the annexed list of thumbprints is described elsewhere as bearing 15 names and the representative list as 3 names, while the panel counts 18 claimants. [Para 50][Para 103][Para 130]
