# Award No. 046/20 — សណ្ឋាគារ ឡឺ ម៉េរី ឌាន អង្គរ (Hôtel Le Meridien Angkor) (Arbitration Council, 4 September 2020)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (សេង វួច ហ៊ុន, employer-appointed; ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន, worker-appointed; គង់ ផល្លៈ, chair) · arbitration award under Article 313 · Doc ID 140805

## 1. Facts
The employer, ឌិ អ៉ីម ភៀ រៀល អង្គរ តែ លេស ហូ ថេល (សណ្ឋាគារ ឡឺ ម៉េរី ឌាន អង្គរ), operates the Hôtel Le Meridien Angkor at Svay Dangkum, Siem Reap; the workers of the hotel were represented in the collective dispute by their dispute-settlement representatives, who did not attend the hearing. [Para 1][Para 20] On 22 July the Siem Reap provincial department of labour received a complaint listing several demands, conciliation on 3 August failed, and report No. 056 ម.ក.ប dated 04 August reached the Council on 11 August. [Para 5][Para 8][Para 9] The report split the case in two and treated the first dispute point — reinstatement of three union activists with back pay — as an individual dispute, so only two points were sent for arbitration. [Para 10][Para 11] On 25 August the workers asked for a postponement pending a meeting at the provincial administration, the employer refused, and on the morning of the hearing the workers wrote that they would not attend. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (ចំណុច ទី២) Whether the employer must raise the wage of ordinary staff at levels 1, 2 and 3 from 65% to 80% of the base wage? [Para 2]
2. (ចំណុច ទី៣) Whether the panel should take legal measures against several named hotel managers for threats, abusive gestures, harassment, discrimination and pressure on staff to resign? [Para 3]
3. Whether the panel must continue the arbitration when the claimant workers, having been properly summoned, fail to appear and refuse to participate? [Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** By their demand statement they sought a wage rise for level 1, 2 and 3 staff because the monthly wage no longer covered daily living costs, and action against managers they accused of threatening and pressuring many staff to resign; at the hearing they put no position, having withdrawn from the process to await a decision of the provincial administration. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 18]
**Employer (respondent):** Present through counsel, it did not consent to an adjournment and asked the panel to proceed. [Para 16][Para 19][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration of case 046/20 and issued an award closing the file, without reaching the merits of either demand. [Para 52][Para 53]

The panel framed the question as whether it should continue when the complainant is absent, and read Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 សកបយ of 21 April 2004 as requiring the parties to appear in person and to submit the necessary documents. [Para 41] Article 20 obliges the parties to attend every meeting convened by the panel, and Article 21 allows the panel, where a properly invited party fails to appear without valid reason, either to continue in that party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 42][Para 43] Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to the same Prakas repeats that choice, subject to the condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 44][Para 45] "Appearing before the panel" was read to mean both presence at the hearing and participation until the proceedings close, through the four stages of introduction, explanation of procedure, conciliation and hearing. [Para 46][Para 47] The panel followed its earlier awards in cases 16/07, 27/07, 138/08, 11/11, 131/12, 108/13 and 332/14. [Para 48] It rejected the workers' ground for staying away — disagreement with the provincial department's decision to split the file — because a party must attend every meeting convened under Article 20, so that objection could not justify absence. [Para 50] Non-appearance by the complainant was therefore treated as abandonment of the complaint, since the party that bears the burden of giving reasons and evidence for its claim had not come to present them. [Para 51]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A complainant union that has been properly notified and then stays away from the hearing abandons its complaint, and the panel may close the arbitration by award without examining the merits; disagreement with a labour department's decision on the scope of the reference is not a valid reason for absence, because Prakas No. 099 សកបយ Article 20 requires attendance at every convened meeting. [Para 43][Para 50][Para 51]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's four-stage description of its own procedure, and its observation that "appearance" includes participation to the end of the hearing and not mere presence at its opening. [Para 46][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The year of the award, of the report and of nearly every instrument is printed truncated as "ឆ្នាំ ២០", so the date of 4 September is only inferable as 2020 from context; the award date and the hearing date are both incomplete as printed. [Para 1][Para 5][Para 7] The employer's name appears in at least four different Khmer spellings across the face, the exhibits and the collective agreement, and the collective agreement is attributed to a Marriott company, which the award does not reconcile. [Para 1][Para 22][Para 26] One date is printed as "26 July August", fusing two months. [Para 49] The three managers named in the second demand are individuals and are reduced here to their roles; their job titles are also printed separately from their names, so the pairing is uncertain. [Para 3] The signature block carries names for only two of the three arbitrators and the employer-appointed arbitrator's line is empty. [Para 54]
