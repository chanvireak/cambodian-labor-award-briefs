# Case No. 142/14 — Teratx Knit Tinh and Gamin Internal Factory Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139605

## 1. Facts
The employer operates a garment factory at National Road 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Krom, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employing 850 workers. [Para 80] Ms K began work on 3 May 2012 as a sewing-machine operator on a two-month probation, then on successive fixed-duration contracts, the last running from 1 January to 31 March 2014. [Para 97][Para 99] On 18 March 2014 at 4:00 p.m. the administration told her the contract would not be renewed and asked her to sign a notice, which she refused. [Para 105][Para 106] She was a candidate elected as factory president of the Cambodia Union Confederation, and 25 workers asked the confederation president on 29 April 2014 to intervene on four demands. [Para 82][Para 116]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take Ms K back as union president and pay back wages from the date of dismissal until her return? [Para 96]
2. Whether the employer must stop discriminating against the union, having refused the notice of elected union leadership and dismissed union leaders in succession? [Para 96][Para 107]
3. Whether the Chinese group leaders must stop pressuring workers who go frequently to the toilet? [Para 125]
4. Whether the employer must top up the basic wage of piece-rate sewers who cannot reach their target? [Para 127]

Issues 1 and 2 were merged by the workers at the hearing; issues 3 and 4 were settled by agreement between the parties, so the panel considered only issues 1 and 2. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the non-renewal was union discrimination — Ms C An, a deputy union president, had already been dismissed in case No. 93/14, the employer refused to accept the notice of elected union leadership, no fault of Ms K was ever raised, and she was given no time off to look for work. [Para 107][Para 197] The dispute was collective, and the confederation president held a written mandate from 25 workers. [Para 95][Para 179]
**Employer (respondent):** there was no discrimination; the employer did not know of Ms K' union role until the conciliation meeting, the security guard refused the letter, and non-renewal of a fixed-duration contract is the employer's right. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 110] The letter of authority post-dated the complaint, and some of the 25 had resigned or were never its employees. [Para 84][Para 170]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the claims for reinstatement of Ms K and for back wages from the date of dismissal until return; no sum was ordered. [Para 220][Para 221]
On representation, the panel found the complaint was filed on 1 April 2014 by the confederation president without any written transfer of authority, but the 25 workers ratified her mandate in writing on 29 April 2014. Reading Article 57 of the Civil Procedure Code with Articles 369 and 370 of the Civil Code, the panel held that ratification operates from the date of the act ratified, so the representation was valid from the filing. [Para 163][Para 164][Para 167] On jurisdiction, the panel applied Article 302 of the Labour Law and found all three conditions of a collective labour dispute met, the third because the four demands concerned working conditions and could disturb the enterprise or social peace. [Para 180][Para 181][Para 187]
On the merits, the panel applied Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law, which forbid an employer to use union membership or activity as a ground for discipline or termination, and held that a fixed-duration contract may be non-renewed unless the non-renewal is tainted by such discrimination. [Para 188][Para 189][Para 210] The complainant bears the burden of proof. [Para 192] The workers could not prove the employer knew of Ms K' elected candidacy: they failed to identify the security guard who supposedly refused the letter, they never collected the returned postal item, and they produced no evidence of receipt. [Para 199][Para 204][Para 205][Para 218] On the notice period, the panel read Article 73(5) (ten days' notice applies only to contracts longer than six months); her last contract ran three months, so no notice was owed and the notice given on 18 March 2014 was more favourable than the law. [Para 212][Para 213][Para 215]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer's decision not to renew a fixed-duration contract stands unless the worker proves that the employer knew of the worker's union office or elected candidacy when it decided; a notice of elected union leadership that is never shown to have reached the employer does not establish that knowledge, and the burden of that proof lies on the complainant. [Para 205][Para 218][Para 192]
A complaint filed by a union federation without written authority is cured retroactively where the workers later ratify the mandate in writing, the ratification dating back to the filing. [Para 164][Para 166]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that a three-month contract attracts no statutory notice period, so the employer's voluntary notice was "more favourable than the law", was not needed to decide discrimination. [Para 215] The panel also declined to analyse whether thumbprints of non-employees were genuine, holding it lacked competence to do so. [Para 171]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed several ways across the OCR (Teratx / Terat Chh / Teratx Tinh), and the case is captioned only by number, 142/14, with no separate award number. [Para 1][Para 80] The filing date appears as 1 April 2014 but is corrupted to "២០១" in one recital. [Para 84][Para 161] The award says four workers had irregularities yet lists five names, and separately says 25 workers mandated the union although only about ten were cited at the hearing. [Para 88][Para 95] The chair's signature block is partly lost to OCR noise. [Para 223] The notice of non-renewal is dated 18 March 2014 in the evidence list but the contract expiry is given as 31 March 2014, which the panel reconciled without comment. [Para 28][Para 99]
