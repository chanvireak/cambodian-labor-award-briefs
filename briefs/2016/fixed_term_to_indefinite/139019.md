# Award No. 090/16 — Sour Sisk Sess (Cambodia) In Das Steel Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 May 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chiv Phirum appointed by the employer, Liv Sovanna appointed by the workers, Ang Eng Thong chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 139019

## 1. Facts
The company manufactures at the Sihanoukville Special Economic Zone and employed about 300 workers, all of whom it engaged on fixed-term contracts of 03 months at a time. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 39] On 17 March 2016 a federation organised an election that formed a local union at the company, but the union was never registered with the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training. [Para 35] 60 workers signed a letter asking the Khmer Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation for assistance over 11 working conditions; the federation brought the remaining unresolved claim, for contracts of 06 months or longer, after conciliation settled 10 of the points. [Para 36][Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must apply employment contracts of a fixed duration of 06 months or more to the workers? [Para 65][Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** a 03-month contract lets the employer cut the contract at will without prior notice, throws the worker into at least a month of job-hunting, and is used to discriminate against union leaders — the employer stopped the work of Ms S, a union leader. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]
**Employer (respondent):** the company has only recently opened and is not yet stable, and it may close or relocate, which is why 03-month contracts are used; with a 03-month fixed-term contract no prior notice is owed on termination, and most workers accept that arrangement. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the workers' claim that the employer apply fixed-term contracts of 06 months or more. [Para 93][Para 94]

Before the merits the panel examined who the claimant was. Article 268 of the Labour Law requires the founders of a professional organisation to deposit its statutes and the list of its responsible officers with the Ministry in order to be registered, and registration is deemed granted if the Ministry does not reply within two months. [Para 49][Para 50] The panel found that the local union at this company had not completed that registration, so it held no lawful right to represent the workers who were its members. [Para 56][Para 57] Prakas No. 099/04 on the Arbitration Council, Article 19, allows a party to be represented by another person only on written authorisation, [Para 58][Para 59] and the letter signed by the 60 workers contained only a request for assistance and help — no transfer of rights and no written authority to represent them — so the federation could not act for those 60 workers either. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] Only the 03 workers who appeared at the hearing were claimants. [Para 63][Para 64]

On the claim itself the panel read Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 664 of the Civil Code as making the employment contract a creature of mutual promise of work for wages. [Para 65][Para 67][Para 68] It applied the general law of contract in the Civil Code — Article 311 (consent to create, modify or extinguish obligations), Article 336 (offer and acceptance) and Article 337 — to hold that neither party may impose a form, type or content of contract the other has not accepted. [Para 70][Para 72][Para 74][Para 77] It followed its earlier awards on the same point, including 56/06, 96/13, 154/13, 164/13, 188/13, 208/13 and 016/14. [Para 83][Para 85] It found as fact that the employer contracts on 03-month terms and that the workers were asking for 06 months or more. [Para 86][Para 87] The demand itself was held not contrary to the law, because making an offer is the worker's right, but the employer's refusal was equally lawful, because rejecting an offer is its right; the panel expressly held that the workers cannot compel a 06-month contract and the employer cannot compel a 03-month one against a worker's will. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 90][Para 91]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A local union that has not been registered — or deemed registered — under Article 268 of the Labour Law has no legal right to represent workers before the Arbitration Council, and a federation holding nothing more than a signed request for assistance, without written authority under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099/04, cannot represent those workers either; the claimants are confined to the workers who appear in person. On the merits, a demand for a fixed-term contract of six months or more and an employer's insistence on three-month terms are both lawful exercises of the right to make and to refuse an offer, since a fixed-term employment contract requires mutual consent under Article 65 of the Labour Law and Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code, and neither party can be compelled to accept a duration it has not agreed. [Para 57][Para 62][Para 77][Para 88][Para 89]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general statement that once the parties have agreed a fixed-term contract they must perform it until its expiry date unless they agree to modify it was not engaged, because no such contract was ordered or found. [Para 92]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel is recorded as constituted on "08 April 2019 2016" [Para 11], two years printed in one date; the same doubling appears in the date of Prakas No. 099, given as "21 April 2019 2004" [Para 58].
- The procedure section gives the original claim as 11 points [Para 8], the final conciliation as covering 19 points [Para 9], with 10 settled and 01 left [Para 9][Para 10] — the figures are irreconcilable and the award proceeds on a single remaining issue.
- The claim is consistently "06 months", but the reasoning prints "16 months" and a stray "03 months" in the same sentence [Para 91], and the operative order repeats its own wording, ordering the employer twice over in one paragraph [Para 94].
- The employer's name is printed at least six different ways in Khmer (សួរ៍ សឹក សេស, សូរ៍ សីក សេស, ស្ទ័រ សឹក សេស, ស្ម៍រ សីក សេស, ស្ង័រ សីក សេស) [Para 1][Para 5][Para 29][Para 33][Para 36]; the union's name likewise (ស្មារតី / ស្ទារតី / ស្ម័រ) [Para 1][Para 35]. The company's stated main activity, "ការផលិត ក្រូណូ", is not recoverable as a product name [Para 33].
- The employer-side arbitrator is named Chiv Phirum in the header [Para 1] but his name is absent from the signature block, which reads only "contont . 4547" under the chair [Para 96].
- Line 32 is an orphaned date fragment and line 33 opens with OCR debris ("pylapal"), losing the start of the findings [Para 32][Para 33].
