# Award No. 336/15 — Kuva Hara (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 January 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Sngoun Ty, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139497

## 1. Facts

Kuva Hara (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a quality-inspection service company, not a garment factory, holding commercial registration No. Co. 1764 E/2012 of 09 July 2012; its in-house rules were filed with the Labour Inspectorate under letter No. 142 of 06 April 2015. [Para 52][Para 31] The Federation of Trade Unions of Workers (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត) filed a demand on 11 December 2015 for improved working conditions on eight points; conciliation at the Ministry of Labour produced a report No. 1669 of 30 December 2015 recording no agreement. [Para 21][Para 23] At the hearing the panel settled four of the eight points, leaving only points 1 and 3 for decision. [Para 27][Para 28] The disputed acts are the separation of two workers, Mr R (Ros Langdy) and Mr P (Pouch Bounthoeun), and the company's refusal to pay the USD 10 monthly incentive without an overtime condition. [Para 57][Para 189]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company must refrain from dismissing workers at will and reinstate Mr R and Mr P, or instead pay them compensation in lieu of notice, severance pay, moral damages, annual-leave indemnity and final wages? [Para 2][Para 57]
2. Whether the company must pay the USD 10 monthly attendance/incentive allowance to all workers meeting 26 working days of 8 full hours, without the condition of two days' overtime per month? [Para 6][Para 189]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The union is a duly registered professional organisation under certificate No. 3351 of 07 September 2015 and may represent its members; Mr P's dismissal was a pretext, since on 21 November 2015 a meeting with Japanese management and the union had decided not to dismiss him, and the company thereafter delayed beyond the seven-day limit for acting on serious misconduct. [Para 53][Para 84][Para 89] The USD 10 is in substance the statutory attendance bonus under Prakas No. 230 of 25 July 2012 and cannot be conditioned on overtime, which must be voluntary. [Para 100][Para 99]

**Employer (respondent):** Mr R resigned in writing and was paid his final wages, so no further indemnity is owed; Mr P threatened and attempted violence against team leader Mr B (Bi Sophanit) on 21 November 2015, which is serious misconduct under Article 83 and Article 25.1 of the in-house rules, and his final payment was tendered but refused. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60] The USD 10 is a voluntary overtime-cooperation incentive tied to two overtime days a month since 2012, and the company is a service company outside the scope of Prakas No. 230. [Para 101][Para 104]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim of Mr R was dismissed; the company was ordered to pay Mr P, over and above the final wages and annual-leave indemnity it had already offered, compensation in lieu of notice under Article 75, severance pay under Article 89 and moral damages under Article 91; the USD 10 claim was rejected. The panel fixed no sum, leaving the amounts to be computed on the Article 89 averaging method (12-month total divided by 12, then by 26). [Para 218][Para 217][Para 177][Para 178]

On the union's standing, the panel applied Article 268 of the Labour Law and its own earlier awards, holding that a registered professional organisation acquires the rights the Law recognises, including the right to represent members before the Council; the union's certificate and leadership letter were in evidence, so the union was a proper claimant. [Para 112][Para 119][Para 120] It added that the four individual workers present were also claimants under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099/04, which allows a party to appear in person. [Para 124][Para 125][Para 126]

On Mr R, the panel found no evidence that he was a union member, noted his absence from the hearing and the absence of any written authority for the Federation to represent him, and declined to consider his demand at all. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129]

On Mr P, the panel applied Article 67(k) paragraph 7: a fixed-duration contract must be in writing, failing which it becomes an indefinite-duration contract; as no written contract existed, Mr P held an indefinite contract. [Para 130][Para 134] Under Article 74 an indefinite contract may be terminated by either party, but not without a reason relating to the worker's capacity or conduct or the operational needs of the enterprise. [Para 135][Para 137] Weighing the evidence, the panel found the alleged threat — "if you are a man, hit me" — unproven, including on the video submitted by the employer; only the table-slapping and pointing were admitted, which the panel held was not serious misconduct. [Para 151][Para 152] At most it was the medium misconduct of refusing a supervisor's instruction under Article 25.2 of the in-house rules, which attracts suspension, not dismissal. [Para 158] The dismissal also breached Article 29.1 of those rules, because the investigation questioned only the administrative chief and staff delegates and never the worker himself, denying him the right to answer the charge. [Para 161][Para 162][Para 163] That procedural defect, combined with the absence of a valid reason, opened the claim in lieu of notice under Article 75 and the severance under Article 89, and because the termination lacked a valid reason it also opened moral damages under Article 91. [Para 172][Para 182][Para 185]

On the USD 10, the panel noted that both sides agreed the company paid USD 10 to workers completing 26 days of 8 hours tied to two overtime days a month, and that the 01 May 2013 meeting minutes had abolished the old USD 10 incentive and re-granted it only to workers volunteering overtime at least twice a month. [Para 199][Para 201][Para 202] Applying Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code and its award No. 271/14, the panel held the employer was bound by that agreement; the workers' claim sought to strip the overtime condition, which contradicted the agreement, and was therefore rejected. [Para 203][Para 210][Para 216] The panel also followed award No. 188/14 holding that Prakas No. 230 applies only to textile, garment and footwear enterprises, which this quality-inspection company is not. [Para 191]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where an employer terminates an indefinite-duration contract, the panel will test the stated ground against the Labour Law and the enterprise's registered in-house rules and, if the ground fails or the disciplinary procedure denied the worker a hearing, will order compensation in lieu of notice, severance and moral damages — even where the underlying conduct (insubordination to a team leader) would only have justified suspension; and a contractual incentive that the employer itself re-defined in written meeting minutes will be enforced on its own terms, not recast as the statutory attendance bonus. [Para 163][Para 214]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that a fixed-duration contract not reduced to writing converts to an indefinite one regardless of the parties' intent, and referred approvingly to the practice of averaging severance over 12 months and 26 days per month. [Para 131][Para 177]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The procedural account is internally inconsistent: para 27 states that six of the eight unsettled points were settled but names only four (points 2, 5, 7 and 8, itself "ចំនូន ៦ ចំណុច"), and para 28 then says two points remained — the arithmetic yields six, not eight. [Para 27][Para 28] The panel's constitution is dated "04 January 2019 2016 2016" in the OCR. [Para 24] The employer's registration appears as "Co. 1764 E/2012" at para 52 while its statutes bear No. 2184/2012 at para 32, and para 191 prints the 2012 Prakas number as both "230 ក.ប/ស.ជ.ណ" and a garbled variant. [Para 52][Para 32][Para 191] Parts of paras 189–191 contain untranslated OCR noise in Latin characters. Individual workers and the team leader are reduced to initials per redaction policy; the arbitration panel is named as printed.
