# Award No. 078/16 — Fumay (Cambodia) Import Export (Arbitration Council, 17 May 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; Sin Kim Sean, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, chair appointed by the two) · Doc ID 139013

## 1. Facts
The Cambodia Labour Union (សហភាពការងារកម្ពុជា) filed 12 collective demands against Fumay (Cambodia) Import Export, a dairy producer employing about 60 workers; conciliation at the Provincial Department of Labour failed on all 12 points and the file reached the Council on 23 March 2016 [Para 17][Para 94]. At the hearings 6 points were settled, 4 were withdrawn, and points 1 and 5 were merged, leaving a single live dispute [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]. The disputed act is the employer's notice of 29 February 2016 dismissing Mr K, president of the company's democratic workers' union and head of the dairy-filling section (employee no. 2013) [Para 113][Para 145].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 1 & 5, merged) Whether the dismissal of Mr K — the union president elected on 11 February 2016 — fell within the statutory protection period and was therefore void for want of the Labour Inspector's prior authorisation, entitling him to reinstatement and back pay from the date of dismissal until his return to work? [Para 100][Para 121]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr K never committed a fault and was never warned; the internal rules the employer relied on were unenforceable because they had not been vised by the Ministry of Labour; the dismissal, 18 days after the union election, was plain anti-union discrimination, compounded by transfers between work stations [Para 101][Para 102][Para 103].
**Employer (respondent):** Reinstatement and back pay were refused because Mr K committed serious misconduct — leading a strike on 5 February 2016 that spoiled many cases of milk, inciting workers on 26 February 2016 not to sign employment contracts, and disobeying instructions on 27 and 29 February 2016; the employer denied knowing he was a union leader and said the dismissal followed Article 4 of the internal rules [Para 104][Para 105][Para 109].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to take Mr K back into employment and to pay him back wages from the date of dismissal until the date he resumes work; the award is binding and, by the parties' agreement, final and enforceable between them alone [Para 169][Para 170][Para 171].
On the governing law, the panel applied Article 293 of the Labour Law, under which no staff delegate or candidate may be dismissed without the Labour Inspector's agreement, extended to union officials by Article 3 ¶3 of Prakas No. 305 SKBY of 22 November 2001 [Para 121][Para 122]. It read that Prakas as protecting election candidates for 45 days before the ballot and 45 days after it where they are not elected [Para 124], and, following its own Award No. 40/10 — Meng Yang, as protecting a successful candidate at least until he files the application to register the union [Para 135][Para 136].
The panel then tested three cumulative conditions drawn from its earlier awards [Para 137]: (1) that the worker belongs to the protected category — satisfied, because the CLC letter of 18 February 2016 records Mr K elected president [Para 140][Para 141][Para 142]; (2) that the dismissal occurred inside the protection period — satisfied, because the election was on 11 February 2016 and the dismissal on 29 February 2016, 18 days later [Para 145][Para 146][Para 147][Para 148]; (3) that the union notified the employer of the candidacy by any workable means — satisfied, because notice was sent through Cambodia Post on 19 February 2016 and the postal officer confirmed the employer refused delivery, which the panel treated as knowledge on the employer's part, following Award No. 283/14 — Lien Fa (Cambodia) Hor Min Co Ltd [Para 149][Para 153][Para 155][Para 156].
On the employer's misconduct case, the panel held that even if serious misconduct were proved, Article 295 of the Labour Law obliges the employer to obtain the Labour Inspector's prior authorisation before dismissing a protected worker; no such request was shown [Para 161][Para 167][Para 168].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker elected to union office acquires the protection against dismissal from the date of the ballot, and continues to hold it at least until the union files its application for registration; a dismissal inside that window is void unless the employer first obtained the Labour Inspector's authorisation, and proof of serious misconduct does not dispense with that authorisation [Para 143][Para 158][Para 167].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that Prakas No. 305 says nothing about candidates who win office before the union is registered, and filled that gap by adopting its earlier interpretation rather than by reading the text [Para 135]. It also observed that the employer had switched from indefinite-duration contracts written in Khmer to fixed-duration contracts in Latin script, a fact not relied on for the outcome [Para 112].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR damage is heavy. The employer's name appears variously as "Fumay (Cambodia) Import Export", "FUMAY CAMBODIA CO. LTD" and, in the findings, "Houmi" / "Fumi" (ហូម៉ី, ហ្វ៊ូម៉ី) [Para 23][Para 92][Para 17]; the union president is printed as "Kan Sokha", "Kan Sota", "Kan Sotā" and "Phan Sokha" in different passages [Para 96][Para 99][Para 101][Para 120]. The date of hire is contradicted within the award: the workers assert 27 February 2012 while the employer's February 2016 payroll list shows 4 May 2012, and the panel recorded both without resolving them [Para 115][Para 116]. Numbered sub-paragraphs in the evidence lists are duplicated out of sequence (two "៣-" entries and a repeated "៦-") and several bracketed quotations — including the text of Prakas 305 — are truncated mid-sentence where the OCR dropped columns [Para 87][Para 88][Para 91][Para 129][Para 130]. The arbitrators' names survive on the face of the award but the signature block is largely illegible [Para 172].
