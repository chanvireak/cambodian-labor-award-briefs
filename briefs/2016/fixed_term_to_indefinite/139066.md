# Award No. 162/16 — Yong Si (Cambodia) Garment (Arbitration Council, 28 July 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; On Non Di, worker-appointed; Kong Phalloua, presiding) · Doc ID 139066

## 1. Facts
The Federation of Unions for Workers' Rights (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សិទ្ធិ ប្រយោជន៍ កម្មករ) filed a collective dispute on 7 June 2016 seeking improvement of nine conditions of work at Yong Si (Cambodia) Garment, a garment manufacturer of about 400 workers. Forty-three workers had transferred their representation rights to the federation on 4 June 2016; 328 others signed a letter refusing to recognise it. Conciliation failed on 29 June 2016 because the employer did not attend. [Para 16][Para 51][Para 53][Para 55][Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the federation retained standing to represent the workers, given that the 43 workers who had transferred their rights later resigned and withdrew their reliance on it? [Para 66][Para 67]

The nine substantive claims — a 2,000 riel daily lunch allowance, hygienic drinking water, easier leave procedures, weekly meal-money payment, wages paid before 4 pm, no discrimination against pregnant workers plus one paid health-check day a month, 50 per cent wages plus allowances for three months on maternity leave, no double deduction for one day's unauthorised absence, and conversion of indefinite contracts to fixed-term with severance paid — were never reached, because six of them (points 2 to 7) had already been agreed at the hearing and the panel then found no claimant remained. [Para 2][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 24]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the federation relied on written transfers of rights from 43 workers and asked the company to improve the nine conditions listed in its letter of 7 June 2016. [Para 45][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** 328 workers had signed a letter dated 7 July 2016 expressly refusing to recognise the federation as their representative, and the company asked the panel to determine the case fairly on the workers' own request rather than through the federation. [Para 29][Para 55]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel ordered the termination of the arbitration proceedings in case 162/16, the award being non-binding. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 70]

Representation: Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 permits a party to be represented by another person only where that person holds written authorisation from the party. The panel read "written authorisation" strictly and held the federation could represent the 43 workers only on the strength of their written transfers. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 66]

The decisive fact was that those 43 workers had all resigned from and withdrawn their reliance on the federation, filing letters to that effect on 11 July 2016, and the federation did not contest those letters. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 61]

Consequently there was no longer any claimant before the panel. The federation had lost both its right to represent the workers in this case and its right to choose the type of award, so the panel followed Award 131/14 (Te Yong Cambodia) and terminated the proceedings for want of a complainant instead of deciding the merits. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 71][Para 72]

The panel had earlier ordered both sides to verify the fingerprints of the 328 objecting workers against the 43 who had transferred rights, which is what exposed the collapse of the claimant's mandate. [Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a union's authority to bring a collective dispute rests solely on written transfers of rights from individual workers, and every one of those workers has since resigned from the union and withdrawn the transfer without the union contesting it, no claimant remains; the panel must terminate the proceedings rather than decide the claims, and the union loses both its standing and its right to elect a binding award. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 69]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that representation before it requires written authorisation in every case, and cited four earlier awards to that effect without depending on any of them for the outcome. [Para 63]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's constitution date is printed as "08 July 2019 2016" and cannot be reconciled from the text. [Para 19] The presiding arbitrator is named គង់ ផលួា on the face of the award but signs as គង់ ផល្លៈ (Kong Phalla). [Para 1][Para 76] The case number appears as "117 162/16" in the summary, and stray numerals ("1 6", "14 - 888") interrupt the conciliation report references. [Para 73][Para 13] The number of withdrawing workers is printed as "43-0" in one place and 43 elsewhere. [Para 57] The hearing time is given as "02:00 pm" while the same sitting is elsewhere recorded as 14:00. [Para 20][Para 15] The award states that six of the nine points were settled at the hearing but the list of nine claims on the face of the award begins at "1" without identifying which single point had previously been resolved.
