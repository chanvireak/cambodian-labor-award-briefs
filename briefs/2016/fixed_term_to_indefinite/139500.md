# Award No. 337/15 — Adzyai Sweet Toe (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 14 January 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; Toun Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139500

## 1. Facts

Three federations — the Democratic Progressive Workers' Federation, the ASEAN Workers' Federation and the Cambodia ASEAN Trade Union Confederation — filed eight demands on 28 December 2015 for workers at the company's factory in Chbar Mon, Kampong Speu. [Para 2][Para 14] Conciliation on 29 December 2015 covered fifteen points, seven of which were settled, leaving eight for the Council. [Para 15][Para 16] At the hearing of 12 January 2016 the employer attended through its human-resources manager, Ms C (Chin Meiling), holding a written delegation of 11 January 2016, but none of the three claimant federations appeared. [Para 19][Para 27] The employer told the panel that about 50 per cent of workers were then on strike, led by two other unions that are not parties to this case. [Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the panel must continue the arbitration or may close it by award where the claimant federations were duly summoned and did not appear? [Para 40]
2. Whether the three conditions in Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 — proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid excuse — were met, and whether non-appearance amounts to abandonment of the eight demands? [Para 43][Para 61][Para 69]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The three federations sought meal allowances for overtime of two hours (USD 1) and four hours (USD 2), USD 1 for Sunday and holiday overtime meals, the transfer of two administrative staff, an extra USD 15 transport and housing allowance, an extra USD 15 attendance bonus, written contracts of six months or more, a nursery or milk allowance for children aged one to eighteen months, and maintenance of wages and allowances during the strike. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9] They did not appear. By telephone on the hearing day, the Cambodia ASEAN Confederation said it had never received the case file and the Democratic Progressive Federation said it had mistaken the time and believed the hearing was in the afternoon; the ASEAN Workers' Federation could not be reached. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

**Employer (respondent):** The employer attended, produced its delegation, registered in-house rules approved by the Kampong Speu labour department under letter No. 1457 of 29 April 2015, its statutes and commercial registration, a collective agreement with the ASEAN Workers' Federation of 26 October 2015, and transfer-of-rights letters from workers to the Democratic Progressive Federation; it said it did not know why the claimants were absent. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 33][Para 35][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 337/15 without ruling on any of the eight demands, holding that the claimants had abandoned their claims. [Para 69][Para 72][Para 73]

The panel began from Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules, which allow it either to proceed in a party's absence or to close the case by award where that party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42] It distilled three cumulative conditions from Article 21 and its earlier awards: proper summons, failure to appear, and no valid reason for the failure. [Para 43][Para 44]

On the first condition, the Secretariat issued invitation No. 027 of 06 January 2016 to the presidents of all three federations, stating the hearing date, venue and contact telephone and fax numbers, and telephoned the worker party the same day to confirm; the panel found proper notification. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 49][Para 50] On the second, it read "appearing before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict check, explanation of procedure and issues, optional re-conciliation, and the merits hearing; the three representatives did not attend at all. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54] On the third, none of the claimants gave any specific valid reason: misreading the time as an afternoon hearing, and asserting that the file was never received, were not accepted as valid excuses. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60]

With all three conditions satisfied, the panel reasoned that a claimant must appear in person to present its claim, its argument and its evidence, and that the respondent's burden arises only once the claimant has done so; a claimant that does not appear abandons its claim, and the panel need not continue and may close the file by award. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 66][Para 67][Para 69] It accordingly terminated the proceedings, and recorded that the award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification absent written objection to the Minister of Labour through the Secretariat. [Para 71][Para 74]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where a claimant union or federation is properly summoned, fails to appear at the arbitration hearing, and offers no valid excuse — confusing the hearing time, or claiming never to have received the file, are not valid — the panel will treat the claims as abandoned and close the case by award without examining the merits, leaving the demands undetermined rather than dismissed. [Para 60][Para 69]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the employer's own attendance and production of a written delegation, in contrast with the claimants' absence, was sufficient to establish that the employer had received the summons; and it noted, without deciding anything, that the strike then in progress was led by unions that were not parties to the case. [Para 21][Para 30]
