# Award No. 126/16 — Lan Hao Garment (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 27 June 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phallha, president) · non-binding award · employer absent · Doc ID 139038

## 1. Facts
Lan Hao Garment is a Phnom Penh clothing manufacturer employing about 196 workers. [Para 1][Para 33][Para 34] Eight demands went to conciliation on 27 May 2016; the workers withdrew points 3, 6 and 8 and merged point 5 into point 7, leaving four. [Para 15][Para 22][Para 23] The employer was validly summoned by letter and twice by telephone but did not appear, saying it had gone abroad and had delegated no representative. [Para 19][Para 21] Only the 38 workers present at the hearing were treated as claimants, since the thumbprint mandates of the 72 who signed were never filed. [Para 34][Para 58][Para 59]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay monthly wages in full by 04:00 pm on payday? [Para 36][Para 70]
2. Whether the employer must pay the overtime meal allowance to the workers once a week? [Para 40][Para 84]
3. Whether the employer must provide hot drinking water and maintain the cold-water containers in a hygienic condition? [Para 43][Para 100]
4. Whether the employer must pay end-of-contract compensation under the Labour Law to the 16 dismissed workers? [Para 46][Para 118]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Wages are usually paid between 04:00 and 06:00 pm, sometimes at 09:00 pm, which is unsafe and has caused them to lose money, and other factories pay by 04:00 pm; the overtime meal allowance is paid only every half month or later, although provision is 1,000 riel an hour; the steel drinking-water tank is rusty, wormy, mossy and sometimes empty; and the 16 were dismissed on 06 May 2016 without notice, without fault and because they were union members. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 41][Para 44][Para 47][Para 48][Para 50]
**Employer (respondent):** Absent and therefore in default of any reply; by not attending it abandoned its right to contest the workers' account, which the panel accordingly accepted as established. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 97][Para 98]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — wages ordered paid in full by 04:00 pm; the overtime meal allowance ordered paid weekly; the employer ordered to keep the cold-water containers clean, hygienic and adequately supplied, with the claim for hot water dismissed; and end-of-contract compensation ordered for 15 of the 16 workers under Articles 89, 91, 75/77, 166/167 and 116. No sums in riel or USD were fixed. [Para 186][Para 187][Para 188][Para 189][Para 195]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 115(3), which bars payment on a rest day and requires payment the day before, and Article 137 on working time, reading them together to mean wages must be paid during and at the end of working hours, that is by 04:00 pm. [Para 70][Para 72][Para 73][Para 81]

On issue 2 the panel applied clause 2 of Notification No. 041/11 K.B/ស.ជ.ណ of 07 March 2011, which gives 2,000 riel a day or a free meal for voluntary overtime, and held that the notification fixes no timing for payment; what matters is that the worker can buy food for the overtime day, so weekly or monthly payment is acceptable. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 88][Para 92][Para 93] It ordered weekly payment. [Para 99]

On issue 3 the panel applied clause 1 of Prakas No. 054 SKBY of 10 February 2000, holding that it requires only sufficient and hygienic drinking water, not hot water, and dismissed the hot-water claim; but because the cold water was kept in an unhygienic metal tank, it ordered the employer to provide clean and sanitary water in adequate quantity. [Para 100][Para 113][Para 114][Para 115][Para 116][Para 117]

On issue 4 the panel found the 15 claimants held contracts of undetermined duration, that the employer terminated by its own will on 06 May 2016 without any reason relating to their aptitude or to the needs of the enterprise under Article 74, and without prior notice. [Para 122][Para 129][Para 130][Para 157] It therefore awarded dismissal damages under Article 89 (none for those under six months), moral damages equal to the dismissal damages under Article 91, notice pay under Articles 75 and 77, unused annual leave under Articles 166 and 167, and the six remaining days of May 2016 wages under Article 116. [Para 136][Para 150][Para 158][Para 167][Para 178] One claimant was excluded for non-attendance and absence of a mandate. [Para 119][Para 120]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Wages must be paid within working hours and completed at the end of the working day, so payment after the 04:00 pm close of work breaches Articles 115(3) and 137 even where the sum is eventually paid in full. [Para 81][Para 186] The obligation under Notification No. 041/11 is to ensure the overtime meal allowance actually lets the worker buy food for the overtime day; the notification does not require daily payment, and a weekly cycle satisfies it. [Para 88][Para 93][Para 99]

**Obiter dicta:** Prakas No. 054 requires only that drinking water be sufficient and hygienic; it creates no duty to supply hot water, so a demand framed as hot water fails even where the cold-water arrangement is itself unhygienic. [Para 113][Para 115]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer was absent throughout, so every finding rests on the claimants' unopposed account, and the panel recorded that the mandates of the 72 workers who thumbprinted were never filed, reducing the claimant group to the 38 who attended and, for the dismissal claim, to 15. [Para 34][Para 57][Para 59][Para 119] The claimant's identity at the centre of that exclusion is printed both as "ប៉ាង ស្រេន" and "បាំង ស្រេន" (lines 55 and 119). The service table at line 51 is badly corrupted and its dates are unreliable. The panel ordered compensation by reference to statutory formulas without computing any figure, and its water reasoning is internally awkward: it holds the container unhygienic and then orders a clean container while dismissing the hot-water claim on the ground that the prakras does not require hot water. The signature block (line 197) is largely destroyed, and the header date and the award date both read 27 June 2016, consistent with line 26. Individual workers are redacted here.
