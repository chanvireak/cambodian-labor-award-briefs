# Award No. 016/16 — AJ Svet Thu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 January 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Virak (worker-appointed), Kong Phalha (president) · Doc ID 138969

## 1. Facts
AJ Svet Thu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (អ៊ែដ្យាយ៍ ស្វេត ថឹ) is a factory at Borey Kamkar village, Chbar Mon, Kampong Speu [Para 1]. On 6 January 2016 the Kampong Speu Department of Labour received a collective complaint from two union confederations pressing nine demands on working conditions; conciliation failed and the report was referred to the Council on 20 January 2016 [Para 13][Para 12][Para 15]. The workers were on strike, and on 21 January 2016 the Council issued order No. 004/16 requiring the strike to stop immediately and all strikers to return to their shifts from the afternoon of 22 January 2016 [Para 16]. Both parties then reported by telephone that the workers remained on strike in front of the Ministry of Labour, in disregard of that order [Para 17][Para 35]. No hearing was held and neither party filed any document [Para 18][Para 19][Para 23].

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel framed one question and never reached the merits of the nine referred demands [Para 24][Para 37]:
1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration proceedings at all, given that the strike ordered suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 continued? [Para 24][Para 36]
The demands left undecided, numbered as filed, were: (1) removal of the head of administration for past harassment; (2) permission for workers to take leave when necessary; (3) a USD 3 increase in the travel allowance, then USD 7; (4) withdrawal of the complaints filed against workers and their representatives; (5) no dismissal of workers during the strike; (6) conversion of three-month contracts to six months or one year; (7) an increase in the piece rate; (8) a further 2,000 riel for the evening meal; (9) payment of wages on holidays [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 8][Para 9].

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They maintained the nine demands and, after the Council's suspension order, continued the strike in front of the Ministry of Labour [Para 1][Para 17][Para 35]. They filed no documents with the secretariat [Para 19].

**Employer (respondent):** The employer likewise filed no documents and no evidence, and joined in the telephone report that the strike was continuing [Para 18][Para 35]. Its position on the nine demands is therefore not recorded anywhere in the award [Para 18].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការ អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល) the arbitration proceedings in case No. 016/16, leaving all nine demands undecided [Para 37][Para 38].

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every contentious measure — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law — and every other measure that could aggravate the dispute during the proceedings, and requires them to attend all meetings convened by the panel [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]. It read that provision as meaning the Council will not continue proceedings while a strike or lockout is under way, and followed a line of its own awards to that effect: Nos 58/06 (Sang Vo), 39/08 (Choung Youv), 137/09 and 138/09 (Prou Phanh), 103/10, 110/11 (Simano), 104/13 (Sabrina) and 019/14 (Dongdu Tek Sthay), together with the notice of 29 January 2004 in case No. 04/04 (IM SI), the second order of 22 April 2004 in case No. 18/04 (hotels), case No. 77/05 (Frank Nid Thing) and case No. 040/15 (FY Cambodia) [Para 27][Para 28][Para 30][Para 32][Para 33].

Finding that the dispute had not returned to its original calm because the workers were still striking in disregard of order No. 004/16, the panel held it could not hold a hearing or decide the collective dispute, and closed the file [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party continues a strike (or a lockout) after the panel has ordered it suspended, in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will terminate the arbitration proceedings rather than hear the dispute; the case may be brought again once the strike has stopped [Para 27][Para 29][Para 36][Para 37]. The obligation is procedural and applies whatever the merits of the underlying demands, none of which the panel examined [Para 23][Para 26].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it could reopen a collective dispute once all the workers' unions stop striking describes a future step rather than the disposal of this case [Para 29].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award date is 26 January 2016 in the header, but the reasons rest on a strike still continuing after 22 January and no hearing was ever held, so the file was closed on the papers [Para 1][Para 16][Para 23]. The conciliation date is irreconcilable: the last conciliation is given as 19 December 2015, yet the complaint was only received on 6 January 2016 and the non-settled report is No. 166/16 dated 19 January 2016 [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14]. That report number's issuing code is printed three ways — "កប/កស្ត", "កប/ភសុព" and "ភប/កស្ត" [Para 12][Para 15][Para 20]. The employer's name varies between "អ៊ែដ្យាយ៍ ស្វេត ថឹ", "អ៊ែដ្យាយ៍ ស្វេត ថឺ" and "អ៊ែដ្យាយ៍ ស្វេត ថឹ" with the Khmer spelling of the last element changing between paragraphs [Para 1][Para 12][Para 15]. The ninth demand is partly lost: "ថ្ងៃ បុណ្យ គុណ និង ពីរ" cannot be resolved into a single holiday name [Para 9]. Union names are garbled, appearing as "សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ" in the party list and "សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចលនាកម្មករ" in the procedure section [Para 1][Para 13]. Prior award numbers are OCR-mangled into forms such as "៥៨០៦-សាង វ៉ូ" and "១៣៧០៩"; §4 gives them as printed where legible [Para 28]. The signature block lost two arbitrator names entirely, retaining only "គង់ ផល្លៈ" with stray years "2009 . 2007" [Para 39]. The panel composition in the header is legible and is reproduced in the Forum line [Para 1].
