# Award No. 007/16 — Hwa Sin Tay Ltd (Arbitration Council, 18 February 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ri, employer-appointed; Tuon Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 138966

## 1. Facts
Hwa Sin Tay Ltd, a shoe manufacturer at Chom Chao, Porsenchey, Phnom Penh, employed about 1,968 workers, of whom 195 were men [Para 70][Para 71]. The Khmer Workers' Pride Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព មោទនភាព កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) filed 13 demands in December 2015; two were settled in conciliation and five more at the hearing, leaving issues 1, 2, 5, 7, 8 and 11 [Para 30][Para 31][Para 35][Para 36]. A local union was elected on 19 December 2015 with Pich Vuthy as president and Chin Pha as vice-president, but it was never registered with the Ministry of Labour, and 46 workers had transferred their rights to the federation by letter of 1 December 2015 [Para 72][Para 74][Para 75].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must build a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) and nursing room, or instead supply three 1.5 kg tins of milk powder and USD 30 per month for childcare? [Para 3][Para 78]
2. Whether the employer has discriminated against workers who join or form a union? [Para 168]
3. Whether the employer must deduct the attendance bonus pro rata when a worker takes authorised special leave? [Para 188][Para 191]
4. Whether the employer must pay a USD 15 monthly bonus to the roughly 90 workers operating the three cutting lines? [Para 110][Para 229]
5. Whether workers who finish two months' probation must be made permanent on six-month contracts rather than three? [Para 234]
6. Whether the employer must pay the lunch allowance, the overtime meal allowance, weekly rest-day wages and public-holiday wages weekly, every Saturday? [Para 241]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the nursing room exists but no nursery does, so mothers cannot leave their children at the factory; the vice-president Chin Pha was not renewed one day after the election result was delivered, which shows anti-union discrimination; the three-month contracts are too short and let the employer dismiss easily; and paying meal and rest-day money monthly forces workers to borrow at interest [Para 80][Para 86][Para 117][Para 122].
**Employer (respondent):** it already provides a nursing room and USD 5 a month in lieu of a nursery, under a 1 July 2015 agreement with two unions; it has three unions so there is no discrimination, and Chin Pha was not renewed for inability to account for materials lost from the warehouse, after oral and written warnings [Para 79][Para 88][Para 178][Para 179].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted in a narrow sense — the panel dismissed the nursery claim, the discrimination claim, the USD 15 machine bonus and the six-month contract claim, but ordered the attendance bonus to be deducted only pro rata to authorised leave days [Para 298][Para 299][Para 300][Para 301][Para 302] and ordered the overtime meal allowance paid weekly each Saturday, while dismissing the weekly payment of the lunch allowance, rest-day wages and holiday wages [Para 303][Para 304].

Preliminarily, the panel applied Article 268 of the Labour Law and clause 19 of Prakas 099: an unregistered union cannot represent its members, and the federation officer's mission letter authorised him only to assist, not to represent the 46 transferors [Para 124][Para 135][Para 148][Para 149]. Only Pich Vuthy, present at the hearing, remained a claimant [Para 150][Para 165].

On issue 1, the panel applied Article 186, which requires an enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a nursing room and a nursery, and read it with earlier awards allowing the employer to pay for a place at an outside nursery where it cannot build one [Para 151][Para 152][Para 154]. Because the claimants produced no list of mothers who had transferred rights, the claim failed [Para 164][Para 167].

On issue 2, the panel applied Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law and the standing burden-of-proof rule [Para 168][Para 169][Para 173]. It found Chin Pha's non-renewal was a fixed-duration contract expiring on 31 December 2015, notified seven days earlier, based on lost warehouse materials that he himself admitted and on prior warnings, and that the workers never produced the postal receipt proving the election result reached the employer before the decision, so anti-union motive was not established [Para 182][Para 183][Para 186][Para 187].

On issue 5, the panel applied Notification 230/12, holding that the USD 10 attendance bonus is a statutory minimum, not a discretionary gift, and that authorised absence suspends the contract under Articles 71 and 72 so the bonus may be reduced only in proportion to the days absent, not forfeited entirely [Para 196][Para 222][Para 225][Para 226][Para 227].

On issue 4, it found no law or practice creating a USD 15 bonus, only daily and weekly output-target bonuses, and no claimant with standing [Para 228][Para 232]. On issue 6, it held that Articles 103 and 116 fix only the minimum frequency of wage payment and that the employer's monthly practice for the lunch allowance, rest-day and holiday wages was lawful, but that the overtime meal allowance under Notification 041/11 exists to let workers buy food during overtime and is therefore payable weekly [Para 250][Para 258][Para 271].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An unregistered local union cannot represent its members before the panel, and a federation officer holding only a letter to "assist" cannot represent workers who transferred their rights, so the claimant set is limited to workers personally present [Para 135][Para 148][Para 150]. Where the employer's practice for a statutory attendance bonus is to deduct it, the deduction must be proportionate to the days of authorised absence, because authorised leave suspends the contract and does not extinguish the entitlement [Para 225][Para 227].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that Article 186 can be satisfied either by building a nursery or by paying for an outside place, and that Article 116 sets only a minimum payment frequency, so a monthly cycle for meal and rest-day money is lawful [Para 152][Para 250]. It also observed that the Labour Advisory Committee has not defined "without absence" in Notification 230/12, so each case must be assessed on its facts [Para 199][Para 204].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR losses are extensive: "Issue 9" of the original list is printed as "១៩៩-", and the operative order for issue 7 is truncated mid-sentence so that only the amount and the group of 90 machine operators survive [Para 19][Para 301]. The panel's formation date reads "19 January 2022 2016" [Para 32]. The award date on the face is 18 February 2016, the parties having postponed it from 08 February 2016, which is itself printed as "08 February 2019 2016" [Para 1][Para 39]. The federation officer is named "Hul Sokhon" on the party list and "Hil Sokhon" in the reasoning [Para 2][Para 148]. The company name appears as "Hwa Sin Tay" and in several Khmer variants including "Hwa Sin Chanthou" [Para 1][Para 72]. The union's election is dated 19 December 2015 in the findings but 20 October 2015 is also referred to as the date the employer learned of it [Para 77][Para 95].
