# Award No. 074/16 — GHI Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 29 March 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mr Seng Vouchhun, appointed by the employer; Mr Liv Sovanna, appointed by the workers; Mr Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139012

## 1. Facts
GHI Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates on National Road 2 at Prek Ho, Ta Khmau, Kandal. [Para 1] The workers filed 15 demands — medicine, contracts of three months or more, sick leave, enforcement of a 04 March 2016 agreement, reinstatement of workers dismissed before the strike, Khmer New Year holidays, union dues deductions, antenatal breaks, final payments, seniority in overtime, drinking water, a nursery or $20 a month, and two sets of uniforms. [Para 2] [Para 5] [Para 10] [Para 17] Conciliation on 18 March 2016 failed on all 15 points and they were referred to the Council on 21 March 2016. [Para 25] [Para 26] A strike had begun on 23 February 2016 and was still running when the referral arrived. [Para 28] [Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration proceedings should continue, given that the strike continued after the panel ordered it suspended? [Para 43] [Para 60]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that the 15 demands stood and that the strike continued; they filed no documents or evidence with the Council. [Para 26] [Para 37]
**Employer (respondent):** that the strike was unlawful and should be stopped, asking the Council by letter of 21 March 2016 to issue an order suspending it. [Para 28] [Para 36]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings (បញ្ចប់ដំណើរការអាជ្ញាកណ្តាល) in case No. 074/16 without deciding any of the 15 demands on the merits. [Para 59] [Para 60]
The panel's route was procedural. Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures — strikes, lockouts or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — throughout the arbitration, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 43] [Para 44] [Para 45] Acting on that provision the panel issued order No. 007/16 on 22 March 2016 requiring the strike to stop immediately and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 23 March 2016. [Para 29] The strike did not stop. [Para 30]
The panel then convened both parties before the hearing on 25 March 2016, where both confirmed the strike was continuing, and it gave the workers until 26 March 2016 to return. [Para 33] [Para 34] On 28 March 2016 the secretariat telephoned both parties, who again confirmed the strike was continuing. [Para 35] The panel therefore held that the workers had not complied with its order and that it could not proceed to a hearing, and terminated the proceedings. [Para 59] It followed its own earlier practice in Awards No. 04/04 — IMSI, No. 18/04 — hotels, No. 77/05 — Franco Nitting, No. 110/11 — Simano, No. 17/13 — International Fashion, and No. 066/14 — Calysto Apparel (Cambodia), where continuing strike action during arbitration led it to end the case. [Para 47] [Para 51] [Para 52] [Para 53] [Para 54]
Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the demands themselves. [Para 42]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers keep striking, or the employer keeps locking out, after the panel has ordered the dispute measures suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will terminate the arbitration and give no decision on the merits of the demands; compliance with that order, and attendance at the panel's meetings, is a precondition of the panel continuing rather than a matter of discretion. [Para 45] [Para 54] [Para 59]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that Article 20 covers lockouts as well as strikes, although only a strike was in issue here, and its statement that it retains the right to terminate proceedings where a party breaches its order. [Para 45] [Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The order number appears both as No. 007/16 and as No. 007/06, and its date is printed as 22 March 2016 in one place and garbled as "22 មនា" in another. [Para 29] [Para 39] [Para 55] The conciliation report is numbered 50196 in one reference and 461/16 in another. [Para 21] [Para 26] The company name is printed as ជី អេច អាយ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា), ជឺ អេច អាយ, ជី អេច អាច and ជី អេច អាយុ; the Latin form in this brief is a transliteration of the Khmer. [Para 1] [Para 28] [Para 36] [Para 60] The reasoning at one point inserts the words "ជំងឺ កូវីដ" (COVID) into the finding that the panel could not continue the hearing, which cannot belong to a March 2016 award. [Para 59] The strike's start date is given as 23 February 2016 but the month is lost in the OCR and only the day survives. [Para 28] The operative part is printed twice with the case name repeated and truncated. [Para 60] [Para 61] Individual workers and union officers do not appear by name in this award at all.
