# Award No. 110/16 — New Archid Garment Factory Limited (Arbitration Council, 25 May 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed សេង វូច ហ៊ុន; worker-appointed ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់; chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា) · Doc ID 139031

## 1. Facts
New Archid Garment Factory Limited ("the company") faced a collective dispute brought by four union federations on fifteen demands covering reinstatement of three worker representatives, travel, health, attendance and machine-skill allowances, meal money for overtime, twice-yearly bonuses, annual-leave practice, a footbridge, a staff party, and deductions for lateness, illness and strike periods. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 11][Para 13][Para 15][Para 16] Conciliation on 4 May 2016 failed on all fifteen and the file reached the Council on 5 May 2016; the workers were then on strike, and on 9 May 2016 the Council issued Order No. 008/16 suspending the strike, with all workers back at work by 12 May 2016. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 25][Para 27][Para 28] The federations held authorisations from only five workers and none of them attended the hearing of 23 May 2016. [Para 31][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration when the claimant federations were properly summoned, did not appear, and gave no valid excuse? [Para 44]
2. Whether the fifteen non-conciliated demands could still be decided on their merits in the claimant's absence? [Para 43][Para 63]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the federations did not appear and filed no written submissions; by telephone on the hearing day their officials said the case had already been settled with the employer and that they would send a letter withdrawing the case, which never arrived. [Para 33][Para 51][Para 59]
**Employer (respondent):** the company attended through its administration head and asked the Council, by letter of 5 May 2016, to issue an order suspending the strike; it filed no case on the fifteen demands. [Para 26][Para 30][Para 35]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed the arbitration without deciding any of the fifteen demands, treating the claimant's claims as abandoned. [Para 69][Para 73][Para 74]

The panel first noted that the file's merits could not be established at all: because the claimant did not join the proceedings, it could find no facts relating to the disputed points raised in the Ministry's non-conciliation report. [Para 43] It then framed the procedural question under Clause 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which allows the panel either to continue in the party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 72] Following Awards 16/07, 27/07, 138/08, 131/12, 108/13 and 332/14, the panel applied three cumulative conditions: the party was properly summoned, the party failed to appear before the panel, and the party gave no valid reason for its absence. [Para 47][Para 48]

On the first condition, the secretariat issued invitation No. 644 dated 18 May 2016 stating the hearing date of 23 May 2016, and telephoned the federations on 18 May 2016 (and the fourth federation on 5 May 2016); the claimant was therefore properly summoned. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 52][Para 53] On the second condition, the panel read "appearance before the panel" as requiring both attendance at the hearing and participation through all four stages — self-introduction and conflicts, explanation of procedure and issues, renewed conciliation, and the hearing itself; none of the four federations attended or took part. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

On the third condition, the federations' telephone statement that they had settled and would withdraw did not qualify, because by 25 May 2016 no withdrawal letter had been received, and a stated intention to withdraw that is never carried out is not a valid reason for absence. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] With all three conditions met, the panel held that the claimant had abandoned its claims and that it need not continue the arbitration. [Para 63][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71]

The panel added that a party's duty to appear and to present its arguments, documents and information in person is the premise of the whole procedure, and that a respondent's duty to answer arises only once the claimant has discharged its own. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67][Para 68] It therefore terminated the arbitration in case 110/16 by award. [Para 73][Para 74]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant has been properly summoned, fails to appear before the panel, and offers no valid excuse, the panel treats the claim as abandoned and closes the arbitration by award without reaching the merits; an announced intention to withdraw, unaccompanied by a withdrawal letter, is not a valid excuse for non-appearance. [Para 47][Para 61][Para 69][Para 72]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that "appearance" under Clause 21 means both physical attendance and participation through all four procedural stages, not attendance alone. [Para 54][Para 55]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The hearing date is printed as 23 April 2016 in the procedure section, while the invitations, the hearing record and the reasoning all fix 23 May 2016; the May date is used here. [Para 20][Para 29][Para 49][Para 51] The panel's constitution is dated 9 May "2023 2016". [Para 24] The fourth federation told the secretariat on 5 May 2016 that it was withdrawing, yet the panel continues to count four absent federations. [Para 32][Para 58] The company's registered address line is corrupted, and the prior-award citations are partly garbled with an impossible reference ("2016 ៧៦,ខែ ១១ ជូន តិច"). [Para 1][Para 47] The claim states fifteen demands but the authorisation base is given as only five workers. [Para 31]
