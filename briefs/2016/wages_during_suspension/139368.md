# Award No. 216/16 — Stung Star (Cambodia) Garment Factory Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 16 September 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, An Nan, Run Saray presiding) · Doc ID 139368

## 1. Facts
Stung Star (Cambodia) Garment Factory Co., Ltd. ("Stung Star"), a garment factory at Damnak Thom, Stung Mean Chey, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, dismissed three union representatives — Mr S, Ms O and Mr P — after notifying them on 17 August 2016. [Para 2] Seven demands went to conciliation on 30 August 2016; five were settled and two referred to the Council. [Para 11][Para 12] A strike followed, and on 5 September 2016 the Council issued injunction No. 020/16 ordering it suspended until arbitration concluded and all workers back at their shifts from 6 September; the workers ended the strike and returned on 7 September 2016. [Para 16][Para 17] The union federation and the workers' representatives did not appear at the 14 September hearing. [Para 19][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back the three union representatives it dismissed after notice given on 17 August 2016? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must maintain premium pay, daily wages and other benefits during the strike period? [Para 4]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the federation maintained that the three representatives had been dismissed and demanded their reinstatement, and that wages and benefits should continue through the strike. [Para 2][Para 4] No representative attended to develop either claim, and the federation told the Secretariat by telephone that it had not attended and could not reach the workers, and asked the Council to close the file. [Para 54]
**Employer (respondent):** the company would not take the three back, and said it had applied the law on the strike-period pay claim; it asked the Council to order the strikers back to work. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 15]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no determination on the merits — the panel terminated the arbitration of Case 216/16, treating the claimant as having abandoned its claims. [Para 65][Para 68]

The panel framed the only question as procedural: whether it should continue when the claimant party was absent. [Para 27] It applied Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Procedural Rules. [Para 27][Para 29][Para 31][Para 33] Article 21 allows the panel to proceed in a party's absence or to close the case by award, provided three cumulative conditions are met: the party was duly invited, did not appear, and gave no valid reason for its absence. [Para 38][Para 40]

On condition one, the panel found the Secretariat had invited the workers' party by letter No. 1177 dated 7 September 2016, stating the date, place, telephone and fax details, and had also telephoned the federation on 7 September at about 14:00 and again on the hearing day to check attendance. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] The invitation was therefore validly served. [Para 46]

On condition two, the panel read "appearance before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages of arbitration — introduction and conflict-of-interest explanation, procedure and issue definition, conciliation if the parties agree, and the hearing to the end. [Para 47][Para 48] The claimant was present at none, and appointed no authorised representative under Article 19 of Prakas 099. [Para 50][Para 51] This condition was met. [Para 52][Para 53]

On condition three, the panel recorded that at about 08:00 on the hearing day the federation answered by telephone that it was not attending, could not contact the claimant workers, and proposed that the file be closed; no other reason was offered. [Para 54][Para 55] The condition was met. [Para 56][Para 57]

With all three conditions satisfied, and because the complainant bears the burden of presenting its allegations, reasons and evidence, and had not done so, the panel held the claims abandoned and declined to continue. [Para 58][Para 61][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party, though duly invited with full particulars of the hearing and reminded by telephone, neither attends nor appoints an authorised representative, and offers no valid reason, the three conditions of Article 21 of Prakas 099 are satisfied and the panel may close the file by award treating the claim as abandoned rather than deciding it on the merits. [Para 38][Para 47][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's observation that the respondent's duty to answer arises only after the complainant has first discharged its own burden of allegation and evidence was explanatory of the sequence of proof, not necessary to the closure. [Para 62]
