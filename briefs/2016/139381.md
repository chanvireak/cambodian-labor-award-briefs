# Award No. 229/16 — Ou Thong Development (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 24 November 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun appointed by the employer, Ang Eng Thong appointed by the workers, An Virak chair) · Doc ID 139381

## 1. Facts
Ou Thong Development (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. runs a tyre-and-tube factory in Prey Boeng village, Kantouk, Phnom Penh, with about 47 workers. [Para 1][Para 50][Para 51] Chhin Navy, president of the Workers' Voice union at the plant, and Yim Vannara, its vice-president, began work on 20 May 2014 on a three-month probation and were then kept on without any written contract. [Para 52][Para 58][Para 119] On 24 September 2016 the employer posted notices of non-renewal on the company notice board naming 07 October 2016 as the last working day; on that day it called both in to pay contract compensation, which they refused pending resolution of the dispute. [Para 59][Para 61]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Chhin Navy and Yim Vannara — that is, whether their contracts were of undetermined duration and whether dismissing union leaders without the labour inspector's prior consent was lawful? [Para 106][Para 126]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they were dismissed because they led the union, which is discrimination; both had worked since 2014 with no written contract and do not want to look for new jobs. [Para 57][Para 107]
**Employer (respondent):** there was no anti-union discrimination; the two simply reached the end of their contracts and were not renewed. [Para 68][Para 108] The employer did not attend the hearing, sending documents instead and saying its director was abroad with no authority delegated. [Para 17][Para 78]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to take back Chhin Navy, the union president, and Yim Vannara, the vice-president, and give them work again. [Para 145][Para 146]

The panel first held it could sit in the employer's absence: Article 21 of Prakas 099 permits that where a duly invited party stays away without valid reason, and here the secretariat had telephoned on 02 November and emailed the invitation of 02 November 2016 with the hearing date and venue. [Para 72][Para 76][Para 77] Travel abroad without delegating authority was not a valid reason. [Para 80][Para 81] On representation it followed Article 54(1) of the Law on Trade Unions and Award 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting): the plant has no most-representative union, so the workers could act for themselves or mandate a union, and the local union — registered as No. 3442 K.B/V.K on 07 December 2015 with its leadership recognised the same day — was the claimant. [Para 55][Para 92][Para 102][Para 105]

On the character of the contracts the panel applied Article 67(1) and (7) of the Labour Law: a fixed-duration contract must be in writing and must state a clear end date, failing which it becomes a contract of undetermined duration. [Para 115][Para 116][Para 117] No written contract was produced by either side, and the employer filed nothing to contradict the workers, so its right of reply was treated as waived; the contracts were therefore of undetermined duration and the ending of them was a dismissal, not a non-renewal. [Para 121][Para 122][Para 123][Para 124]

On lawfulness the panel turned to the Law on Trade Unions of 17 May 2016: Article 43 forbids dismissing a staff delegate or candidate without the labour inspector's prior agreement, and Article 67 extends the same protection to union leaders — president, vice-president and secretary — for the stated period. [Para 126][Para 127][Para 131] Following Awards 170/16 (Jahu I Metal Manufacturing), 181/16 (Ou Thong) and 197/16 (Sin Chithan), that protection applies to union office-holders as it does to delegates. [Para 128][Para 133][Para 136] The employer produced no document showing it had sought the inspector's consent before dismissing either leader, so the procedure was not followed. [Para 139][Para 142] Under Prakar 34 of Prakas 099 the panel ordered reinstatement rather than compensation. [Para 140][Para 141]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where no written contract stating an end date exists, the employment relationship is of undetermined duration under Article 67(1) and (7) of the Labour Law, so ending it is a dismissal and not the expiry of a fixed term. Dismissing a union president or vice-president without the labour inspector's prior agreement breaches Article 43 as extended by Article 67 of the Law on Trade Unions, and the remedy is reinstatement; the employer bears the consequences of filing nothing, since an unanswered assertion stands admitted. [Para 117][Para 136][Para 139][Para 143]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the absence of a most-representative union may obstruct workers' representation rights, and its recital of earlier awards on proceeding in default, are general observations not required by the order. [Para 92][Para 74] The award is expressly non-binding. [Para 18]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The operative paragraph names only the union president: "order the employer to take back Chhin Navy, union president" [Para 146], although the reasoning and the summary immediately before it order reinstatement of both Chhin Navy and Yim Vannara [Para 143][Para 145]. The reasoning is followed here; the operative text as printed is narrower.
- The conciliation report is dated twice over: 27 October 2016 in the procedural recital [Para 6] but "19 27 October 2016" where the day number is duplicated [Para 11], and the conciliation meeting itself as "19 September October 2016" [Para 10].
- Prakas 099 is dated 21 April 2004 on the face of the award [Para 4] and 21 April 2005 in the reasoning [Para 140].
- The company appears as Ou Thong Development (Cambodia), Ou Thong Divelop Men, and "Ou Thong Divelop Men (Cambodia)" for the same entity [Para 1][Para 9][Para 51]; the union appears once as "Samleng Kummok" instead of "Samleng Kamkar" [Para 1].
- Names are unstable across the document: the vice-president is Yim Vannara, Yim Vanna and "Khim Vannara" [Para 1][Para 56][Para 145]; the worker-side arbitrator is Ang Eng Thong at [Para 1] but "An Virak" is printed as chair in both the header and the signature block [Para 148].
- A block of unrelated Khmer and Latin OCR noise interrupts the closing formalities at [Para 147].
