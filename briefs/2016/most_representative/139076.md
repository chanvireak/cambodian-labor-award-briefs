# Award No. 175/16 — Denim Khon Sip Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 9 August 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party — dissenting in part — and Kong Phalla chair) · Doc ID 139076

## 1. Facts
Denim Khon Sip Co Ltd ("Denim") is a garment factory at Dangkao, Phnom Penh, with about 500 workers. [Para 46][Para 47] The Khmer Workers' Rights Union Federation held an election at the factory on 12 June 2016 and formed a local union with Mr K as president, Ms C as vice-president and Mr C as secretary; the local union was not yet registered with the Ministry of Labour. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 89] On 15 June 2016, three days later, the employer dismissed those three and a fourth worker, Mr C V, without stating a reason. [Para 85][Para 88][Para 316] The employer did not appear despite two valid summonses, one by hand and one by EMS which it refused. [Para 27][Para 98][Para 99] Of 16 demands, five were withdrawn (9, 11, 12, 14, 16) and 10 and 15 merged, leaving ten for decision. [Para 30][Para 32]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer may set quotas the workers cannot meet and discipline them for falling short? [Para 53][Para 114]
2. Whether the employer must make overtime genuinely voluntary and stop warning or dismissing those who decline it? [Para 55][Para 129]
3. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus to USD 20 a month and stop pro-rata deduction for leave days? [Para 58][Para 142]
4. Whether the employer must instruct supervisors to use appropriate language? [Para 63][Para 190]
5. Whether the employer must fit locks to the toilet doors? [Para 65][Para 198]
6. Whether the employer must spray water on the sewing-room roof to reduce heat? [Para 68][Para 209]
7. Whether the employer must permit telephone calls at the workplace? [Para 71][Para 222]
8. Whether the employer must pay the full overtime meal allowance weekly? [Para 74][Para 237]
9 (issue 13). Whether the employer must pay a USD 15 monthly lunch allowance? [Para 79][Para 258]
10 (issues 10 and 15, merged). Whether the employer must reinstate the four dismissed leaders with back pay? [Para 82][Para 265]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** quotas rise from 1,000 to 1,200 and 1,600 garments a day and those who cannot keep up are warned; overtime runs 16:00–18:00 and those leaving at 16:00 are warned or dismissed outright; the four leaders were dismissed right after the election result reached the administration, which is anti-union discrimination. [Para 54][Para 56][Para 88][Para 122]
**Employer (respondent):** no position — it never attended, forfeited its right to answer, and each factual assertion was treated as admitted. [Para 120][Para 191][Para 314]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — ordered: voluntary overtime, the language instruction, toilet locks, heat-reduction measures, full weekly overtime meal allowance and reinstatement of all four leaders with back pay; dismissed: the quota, USD 20 bonus, telephone and USD 15 lunch demands. [Para 324][Para 327][Para 329][Para 331][Para 333]

Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 lets the panel proceed where a party was properly summoned and failed to appear; refusing the EMS letter was no excuse. [Para 95][Para 99][Para 101] Article 11 of the Trade Union Law confers rights only on registration, and the local union was unregistered, so the federation acted instead on written delegations from four workers plus one complainant attending in person. [Para 104][Para 108][Para 113]

On the dismissal, Article 67(7) of the Labour Law makes a fixed-term contract never put in writing an indefinite one, which these four held. [Para 265][Para 271] Special protection under Article 67 of the Trade Union Law and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 requires three cumulative conditions: that the worker be a protected leader, that the dismissal fall inside the protected period, and that the union have notified the employer and copied the Ministry. The first two were met (elected 12 June, dismissed 15 June), but the notice was merely handed to an administration that refused it, was never posted, and no proof showed the Ministry received it. [Para 272][Para 295][Para 305][Para 306] Article 74 nonetheless forbids dismissal without a reason related to aptitude, conduct or the needs of the enterprise, and none was given, so the dismissal was unlawful and reinstatement with back pay was ordered. [Para 311][Para 316][Para 317][Para 322]

On the quota demand, Article 2 of the Labour Law (the employer's right to manage) and the burden of proof applied; the workers produced no evidence of how far the quota exceeded their capacity or how many failed it. [Para 117][Para 123][Para 125][Para 128] On overtime, Article 4 of Prakas No. 80 requires the voluntary principle and forbids discipline for refusal, so warnings and dismissals for leaving at 16:00 were improper. [Para 129][Para 141] On the attendance bonus, the employer already paid the USD 10 minimum under Notification No. 230; a higher figure is a benefit above the statutory floor, hence an interest dispute this union could not bring, and that notification's express "no absence" wording does justify pro-rata deduction for leave days. [Para 146][Para 147][Para 174][Para 189]

Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 lets the panel order cessation of unlawful conduct; the phrases used ("you lot", "worse than animals") were improper and the employer must instruct the three named supervisors. [Para 195][Para 197] Article 229 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 3 of Prakas No. 052 require hygienic toilets with closeable, internally locking doors, and three of the ten had no lock. [Para 199][Para 207] Articles 1 and 3 of Prakas No. 147 require every means to keep the workplace cool but no specific method, so roof spraying was ordered only as one such means. [Para 211][Para 221] Telephone use was refused: the ban is a lawful management measure under Article 2. [Para 225][Para 230][Para 236] The overtime meal allowance was granted: Notification No. 041/11 fixes Riel 2,000 a day regardless of daily or weekly payment, and four days had been withheld in the first week. [Para 237][Para 255][Para 257] The USD 15 lunch demand was refused on the same interest-dispute ground. [Para 260][Para 264]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Special protection is not automatic: the union must have notified the employer in writing and copied the Ministry, and a notice refused by the administration and never posted does not satisfy that condition. But where the leader's contract was never reduced to writing and is therefore indefinite, Article 74 still requires a reason related to aptitude, conduct or the needs of the enterprise, and dismissal three days after an election with no reason stated is unlawful, entitling the worker to reinstatement and back pay. [Para 271][Para 305][Para 311][Para 317] An employer's absence after valid summons does not suspend the hearing, and above-minimum benefit demands fail on the burden of proof and the most-representative-union rule. [Para 101][Para 125][Para 147]

**Obiter dicta:** the encouragement that the employer let workers take urgent family calls by some suitable means, and that workers keep work and family time distinct, went beyond the refusal of the telephone demand. [Para 234][Para 235]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Arbitrator An Non's dissenting opinion is annexed, objecting only to rejection of the pro-rata attendance-bonus claim; he argued the panel departed from earlier awards (Nos. 223/2012, 214/2012, 211/2012, 204/2012, 203/2012, 180/2012) without good reason. [Para 334][Para 337][Para 339][Para 340]
- The award date is 9 August 2016 in the header and reasons [Para 34], but the signature block prints the month as a fragment [Para 341]; the panel's constitution date reads "20 July 20169" [Para 26] and Prakas No. 099 is dated "21 April 2019 2004" at [Para 95] against 2004 at [Para 109].
- The protected-period conditions are three at [Para 289] but "13 conditions" at [Para 291].
- The employer's name is printed both "Denim Khon Sip Co Ltd" and "Denim Un Sim" [Para 46]; the remaining list at [Para 32] names eleven numbers while calling them ten because 10 and 15 were merged.
- Individual names appear in several spellings ("Khem Van Dy"/"Khem Vandy", "Cheng Chan Sok"/"Theng Chan Sok"); names here are redacted. Notification No. 230 is dated 25 July 2012 but cited "230/12" elsewhere, and the award also relies on the superseded Notification No. 041/11 [Para 169][Para 172][Para 337].
