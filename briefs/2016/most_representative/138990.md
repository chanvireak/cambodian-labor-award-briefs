# Award No. 038/16 — Kao Tek Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 March 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea, chair; Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer, dissenting in part; An Non, appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 138990

## 1. Facts
Kao Tek Garment Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន កៅ ថេក ហ្គាមេន ខូអិលធីឌី), a garment factory in Ang Snuol district, Kandal province, employed about 400 workers. [Para 101][Para 102] The Cambodia-ASEAN Union Federation and its enterprise union, elected on 2 December 2015 and notified to the company on 11 December 2015, filed an eleven-point complaint; three points were settled at the hearing, leaving eight for decision. [Para 19][Para 25][Para 103] On 6 February 2016 the company terminated Ms L D, a cutting-table head elected union president, and Ms M, a mechanic elected union treasurer, both of whom had worked since 16 December 2013 on successive three-month fixed-duration contracts, the last due to expire on 15 February 2016. [Para 109][Para 110][Para 111][Para 112][Para 230] A strike ran from 8 to 16 February 2016 without prior notice. [Para 162][Para 163]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (points 1 and 2, merged by the claimants) Whether the company must take back Ms L D and Ms M and recognise the Cambodia-ASEAN Union in the company? [Para 109][Para 185]
4. Whether the company must pay 20 US dollars a month as a milk allowance to women workers until their child reaches 3 years? [Para 138][Para 302]
5. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 3,000 riel a day per worker, paid weekly? [Para 143][Para 319]
7. Whether the company must set the target bonus at 2,000 riel or more when a worker reaches the target? [Para 148][Para 325]
8. Whether the company must pay 5,000 riel as an overtime meal allowance for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00, on top of the existing sum? [Para 153][Para 332]
9. Whether the company must pay a skill allowance of 5 dollars for one machine, 10 dollars for two and 15 dollars for three or more? [Para 157][Para 342]
10. Whether the company must maintain wages and other benefits unchanged during the strike? [Para 162][Para 348]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two were dismissed on 6 February 2016 without notice and because of their union activity; Ms L D had been elected president and Ms M stood for the leadership and was elected treasurer, and the company knew of the result from 11 December 2015. [Para 115][Para 126][Para 127] On the allowances, other factories pay 3,000 riel for lunch and more than 3,000 riel for overtime meals, and the 1,000-riel target bonus is too little for the effort. [Para 145][Para 146][Para 150][Para 155]
**Employer (respondent):** this was expiry of a fixed-duration contract, not a dismissal, and the two were not taken back because their work was poor, their conduct inappropriate, their evaluation scores low and their absences frequent; in the same month the company ended the contracts of eight other workers and did not touch the union's vice-president or secretary, so there was no anti-union motive. [Para 116][Para 121][Para 122][Para 188][Para 274] It also objected that the union's registration papers were defective and said it already grants more than the law requires. [Para 128][Para 156]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to take back Ms L D and Ms M and to pay them the wages and other benefits from the day of dismissal until the day they return; points 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10 were refused. [Para 375][Para 376][Para 377][Para 378][Para 379][Para 380][Para 381]

On standing, the panel found that under Article 268 of the Labour Law a professional organisation acquires the rights the Law recognises once registered, and registration is deemed complete if the Ministry does not answer within two months; the union's second receipt was dated 10 February 2016, so two months had not run by the hearing. [Para 169][Para 170][Para 176][Para 177] The claim nonetheless proceeded because Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 allows representation by any person holding a written authorisation, and the federation held mandates signed by 151 workers. [Para 178][Para 179][Para 182][Para 184]

On points 1 and 2, the panel held that although each contract was for three months, renewal since 16 December 2013 pushed the total beyond two years, so under Article 67(2) and Article 73(5) of the Labour Law the contracts became indefinite-duration; terminating them was therefore a dismissal, not a non-renewal. [Para 189][Para 192][Para 200][Para 204][Para 205] Ms L D, as a candidate elected union president, enjoyed delegate protection under Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305; although the 45 days after the election had run, the panel treated the period up to the date of the registration application as a reasonable extension of that protection. [Para 208][Para 218][Para 246][Para 248] Because Article 293 requires the labour inspector's prior authorisation to dismiss a protected worker and the employer admitted it never sought one, her dismissal was unlawful and reinstatement with back pay followed. [Para 206][Para 259][Para 260][Para 264] Ms M, a losing candidate, had only 45 days of protection after the poll, which had expired, so she was not protected; but the employer still had to show a valid ground under Article 74, and it failed — the November 2015 evaluation was nearly three months old, never repeated, the minutes did not name her, and no warning was issued. [Para 209][Para 250][Para 285][Para 290][Para 295][Para 301]

On points 4, 5, 7, 8 and 9, the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract, agreement or past practice creating the claimed benefit, classified each as an interest dispute, and declined to consider it because the claimants were individual workers, not a most-representative union; on the overtime meal it noted the company already paid 3,000 riel, above the 2,000 riel required by Notification No. 041/11. [Para 303][Para 320][Para 327][Para 334][Para 343][Para 314][Para 317]

On point 10, Article 332 of the Labour Law suspends the employment contract during a strike and provides that no wages are paid, so the company owed neither wages, lunch money nor the attendance bonus for 8 to 16 February 2016; the strike had also begun without the prior notice Article 324 requires. [Para 349][Para 350][Para 369][Para 373][Para 381]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Successive fixed-duration contracts that together exceed two years convert into an indefinite-duration contract under Articles 67(2) and 73(5) of the Labour Law, so ending them is a dismissal requiring a valid ground under Article 74 and, where the worker is a protected union officer, the labour inspector's prior authorisation under Article 293; absent that authorisation or a proven ground, the worker is reinstated with back pay. [Para 200][Para 205][Para 260][Para 296]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's extension of a candidate's post-election protection beyond the statutory 45 days, up to the date the union applied for registration, was framed as a reasonable-period reading of earlier awards, not a fixed rule, and was not needed for Ms M. [Para 219][Para 246][Para 250]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Arbitrator Ing Sothy filed a partial dissent: he agreed that Ms L D should be reinstated but disputed the back pay, and dissented entirely as to Ms M, on the view that both held fixed-duration contracts that had simply run their term and that they had received 5 percent termination pay at each renewal. [Para 385][Para 387][Para 391] The claimant count does not reconcile: 150 mandates plus six workers present, one of whom signed nothing, is resolved by the panel to "១៥១ នាក់". [Para 106][Para 183][Para 184] Dates are corrupted: conciliation is dated 09 February "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៤" [Para 18], the panel's constitution "ថ្ងៃទី ១៥ ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០២២ ២០១៦" [Para 20], and the election "ថ្ងៃទី ០២ ខែមីនា ធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" [Para 228] against 2 December 2015 elsewhere. [Para 103] Registration is given as No. 5341 dated 29 August 2013 at [Para 30] and Inv. 1885 E/2013 dated 23 August 2013 at [Para 101]. The lunch allowance reads 1,000 riel at [Para 143] but "២០ ១០០០ រៀល" at [Para 319], and the milk claim "២០១ ដុល្លារ" in the operative order. [Para 376] The skill claim is 15 dollars at [Para 159] but "១៥៦៦" at [Para 342]. Passages at [Para 290] and the signature block at [Para 383] are OCR noise, so the signatures are lost.
