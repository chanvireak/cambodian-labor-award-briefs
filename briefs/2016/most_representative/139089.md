# Award No. 189/16 — Khaing Dong 1 (Arbitration Council, 6 September 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ri, employer-appointed; Tuon Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · non-binding award · Doc ID 139089

## 1. Facts
Khaing Dong 1 is a garment factory at Kbal Damrey, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, employing about 220 workers, roughly 200 of them in one production building that is hot, dusty and full of flying cloth scrap. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 56][Para 57] The Khmer Workers' Spirit Union Federation filed nine demands on 4 July 2016; conciliation on 27 July 2016 settled six, and the three remaining demands reached the Council on 3 August 2016. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] At the hearing 87 workers were represented through written transfers of right, while Ms Y attended having transferred nothing. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 113]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to install sufficient dust- and cloth-scrap-extraction fans and run them during every working hour? [Para 56][Para 122]
2. Whether the employer must reimburse the Khmer New Year travel allowance for 2016 and restore the skill/incentive bonus of USD 10, USD 15 and USD 20 a month by needle grade to eight workers as before? [Para 67][Para 132]
3. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 3,000 riel a day to the workers? [Para 95][Para 187]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the building is hot and dusty with no air circulation, making breathing difficult and damaging health; the employer always paid a Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben travel allowance and a monthly skill bonus of USD 10, USD 15 or USD 20 by needle grade, and cut both after the February 2016 strike; neighbouring factories such as VOA Hun Sin Tai pay 1,000 riel a day for lunch. [Para 60][Para 79][Para 81][Para 96]
**Employer (respondent):** it intends to install extraction fans but the building is old, and agreed with a technician to trial one unit at the end of August 2016; pay slips show the 2016 Khmer New Year travel allowance was paid to all workers including the eight, with receipts signed before the group leader; the incentive bonus depends on 1% of company profit and on attendance and cooperation, and was reduced because output fell; it cannot afford a meal allowance. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 75][Para 88][Para 98][Para 160]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must install sufficient dust- and cloth-scrap-extraction fans or otherwise keep the workplace clean; must pay the 2016 Khmer New Year travel allowance of USD 20 to Ms H and Ms T and USD 10 to Ms Y; must pay Ms H, Ms T and Ms Y their incentive bonus "in accordance with the company's principles"; the claim for fixed bonuses of USD 10/15/20 was dismissed; the meal-allowance claim was refused consideration. [Para 208][Para 209][Para 210][Para 211][Para 212]

On representation the panel held that Article 54(1) of the Trade Union Law of 17 May 2016 reserves exclusive standing to settle collective disputes to the most representative union, and followed Award No. 180/16 (Cement Cambodia Chakrey Ting) that where none exists workers may bring a dispute themselves or transfer their rights; Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires written authorisation. [Para 99][Para 104][Para 114][Para 115] The federation therefore represented the 87 transferring workers, and Ms Y could represent herself. [Para 119][Para 120][Para 121]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 229(1) of the Labour Law and Article 01 of Prakas No. 125 SKBY of 15 June 2001, requiring a clean workplace free of dust and fumes harmful to health, following Award No. 064/16 (Im H B O N G). [Para 122][Para 123][Para 125][Para 127] Both parties admitted no extraction fans had yet been installed and the workshop was hot and dusty; a planned trial of one unit did not discharge the duty, so the employer was ordered to comply. [Para 128][Para 129][Para 130][Para 131]

On issue 2 the panel split the demand. For the travel allowance it applied the rule that the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof; the employer said it had receipts but produced none by the deadline, so the panel assumed the allowance had not been paid for 2016 and ordered reimbursement of the amounts the three workers had previously received. [Para 138][Para 141][Para 142][Para 144][Para 147] For the incentive bonus it found that such a bonus is not provided by the Labour Law, so the employer may set its conditions under its managerial discretion (Article 2(2) of the Labour Law), following Award No. 24/05 (Wear Well). [Para 152][Para 155][Para 157][Para 162][Para 163] But the employer had cut the bonus on the ground that the three workers' output fell and produced no report or evidence to that effect, so it exercised that discretion without valid reason. [Para 164][Para 169][Para 170] It further held that the contract of 7 April 2016, restoring the bonus and travel allowance in full only if the workers did not strike a second time, imposed a penalty for strike participation and was void under Article 333 of the Labour Law, following Award No. 3/14 (Tru Tham, Phnom Penh). [Para 171][Para 173][Para 178][Para 179][Para 180]

On issue 3 the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules, contract, agreement or past practice creating a midday meal allowance, so the demand was an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 188][Para 189] Under Article 96(2) of the Labour Law, Articles 54 and 72 of the Trade Union Law and Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099, an interest dispute may only be brought by the most representative union; the claimant was not one, so the panel declined to consider the claim at all. [Para 199][Para 200][Para 203][Para 205][Para 206]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the union bringing a collective dispute is not the most representative union in the enterprise, the panel has no jurisdiction over a demand that is an interest dispute — one asserting a benefit no law, agreement or past practice yet grants — because only a most representative union may conclude a collective agreement that would bind all workers; the same union may nonetheless bring a rights dispute on behalf of workers who transferred their rights in writing. [Para 199][Para 202][Para 205] Separately, an employer that withholds a discretionary incentive bonus in reliance on a stated ground — here, falling output — must prove that ground with documents; failing that, the reduction is an exercise of managerial discretion without valid reason. [Para 163][Para 169][Para 170]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's agreement with Award No. 180/16 that the absence of any most representative union can obstruct workers' constitutional right to complain is a general observation on the Trade Union Law, not the ground of any order here. [Para 105][Para 112] Its remark that receipts signed by the group leader would not have been believed even had they been produced is likewise unnecessary to the findings. [Para 143]

- The workforce is "about 220" at [Para 51] but "about 200" in the production building at [Para 56]; the transfers of right read "87" at [Para 113] with a stray "១៥" inserted in the same line. Worker names vary in spelling and are reduced here to initials.
