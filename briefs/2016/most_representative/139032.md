# Award No. 109/16 — He Cai Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 13 June 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed ឈីវ ភិរម្យ; worker-appointed ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់; chair ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន) · non-binding award · Doc ID 139032

## 1. Facts
He Cai Garment Co., Ltd. ("He Cai") cuts and sews garments under licence No. 157756 of 21 September 2015 and employs about 240 workers. [Para 36][Para 38] The Khmer Workers' Spirit Union Federation holds authorisations from 88 of them dated 23 April 2016. [Para 32][Para 39][Para 40] A strike complaint over working conditions reached the labour dispute department on 27 April 2016; of nine demands, seven were settled in conciliation and two remained: a permanent on-duty medical service, and an increase of the travel and housing allowance from USD 7 to USD 12 per month. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15] The parties chose a non-binding award and extended its issue date from 30 May to 13 June 2016. [Para 21][Para 23]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide a doctor on duty throughout working hours, sufficient medicines, and a larger infirmary? [Para 41][Para 56]
2. Whether the employer must add USD 5 to the travel and housing allowance, bringing it to USD 12 per month? [Para 81]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the infirmary is only 2 metres by 3 metres with no space to rest or to be treated for wind illness (កោសខ្យល់), medicines run short, and workers who faint or fall ill need a doctor for first aid; the USD 7 allowance does not cover USD 20 in monthly transport and USD 40 to USD 60 in rent, while other employers pay USD 10. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 53][Para 54]
**Employer (respondent):** it will consult the building owner about a suitable site for a bigger infirmary and stock sufficient medicines, but the premises are too small to build on and it cannot say when the work could be done; it will recruit a doctor once the infirmary is ready. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50] On the allowance, it says it lacks the capacity and already pays benefits above the law, such as a USD 4 attendance bonus on top of the USD 10 monthly attendance bonus. [Para 55]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to enlarge the infirmary to at least 20 m², to stock medicines in accordance with Prakas No. 330, and to have one nurse on duty plus one doctor or intermediate nurse present for at least 2 hours during working hours; the allowance claim was refused. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 103][Para 104]

**Issue 1 — size.** Article 238 of the Labour Law requires every enterprise to provide primary treatment for its workers, and Article 242(1) requires an enterprise with at least 50 workers to maintain a permanent infirmary. [Para 57][Para 58] Clause 1 of Prakas No. 330 of 6 December 2000 repeats that duty above 50 workers, and Clause 2 requires a location near the workplace and a size suited to the workforce but at least 20 m². [Para 58][Para 59] The parties agreed the infirmary measures 2 m by 3 m — 6 m² — which is below the minimum. [Para 41][Para 56][Para 60][Para 62] The panel ordered it enlarged to at least 20 m². [Para 63][Para 64]

**Issue 1 — medicines.** Article 242(4) requires dressings and medicines sufficient for urgent treatment of accidents and work-related illness, and Clause 4 of Prakas No. 330 lists the minimum furniture, steriliser, equipment and medicines. [Para 65][Para 67] Following Award 62/08 (Paow Da) on its second issue, the panel found the medicines insufficient: workers who fall ill must ask the administration head, the sewing head or the packing head for medicine, and the administration head has no medical training. [Para 47][Para 68][Para 71][Para 72] It ordered the employer to stock medicines in line with Prakas No. 330. [Para 73]

**Issue 1 — staff.** Article 242(2) and (3) require one or more nurses to staff the infirmary, with at least one nurse permanently on duty during both day and night working hours. [Para 75][Para 76] Clause 3 of Prakas No. 330 fixes staffing by workforce: for 50 to 300 workers, one nurse on duty and one doctor or intermediate nurse present for 2 hours per 8-hour shift; for 301 to 600 workers, 1 nurse and a doctor for 2 hours. [Para 77][Para 79] He Cai has 240 workers and no on-duty medical staff, the employer having conceded it had not yet arranged one. [Para 78] The panel ordered one nurse on duty and one doctor or intermediate nurse present for at least 2 hours during working hours. [Para 80][Para 102]

**Issue 2.** Point 1 of Ministry Notification No. 230 K.B/S.J.N of 25 July 2012 sets the travel and housing allowance for textile, garment and footwear workers at USD 7 per month, and the employer pays exactly that. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 83] The panel found no statute, collective agreement, internal rule, agreement or past practice requiring USD 12, so the demand exceeded the law and was an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86] Under Article 96 and Clause 9 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 only the most representative union may conclude a collective agreement, and under Clause 43 of Prakas No. 99 an award on an interest dispute binds all workers for one year in place of such an agreement. [Para 87][Para 89][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92] Following Awards 169/11, 402/11 and 66/11, an interest dispute may be arbitrated only where the claimant is the most representative union, or unions together holding more than half the workforce. [Para 93][Para 94] The claimant was 88 individual workers, not such a union, so the panel refused to consider the demand. [Para 96][Para 97][Para 98][Para 99]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer with at least 50 workers must provide a permanent infirmary of no less than 20 m², stocked with the medicines prescribed by Prakas No. 330, and staffed by a nurse on duty with a doctor or intermediate nurse present for at least two hours of every shift; the statutory USD 7 travel and housing allowance satisfies the law, so a demand for more is an interest dispute that only the most representative union may bring. [Para 59][Para 63][Para 79][Para 82][Para 93]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel accepted the employer's undertaking to consult the building owner and to recruit a doctor once the infirmary is enlarged, but held that an inability to say when the work could be done could not postpone a statutory duty. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 64]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The chair arbitrator is printed as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន on the face of the award and as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា in the signature block. [Para 2][Para 105] Prakas No. 99 is dated "21 April 2019 2004" in the body, and stray years ("2017", "2019") are embedded inside quoted Prakas text. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 77][Para 91] The panel's constitution line reads "ថ្ងៃទី ០៥ ខែឧសភា ឆ្នាំ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦". [Para 16] The opening complaint is described as covering four working conditions while the conciliation record speaks of nine demands. [Para 13][Para 14] The operative paragraph prints the doctor's number as "09" where one person is meant. [Para 102] The annexed Prakas No. 330 text is heavily corrupted, with its staffing table partly unreadable, though the rows for 50–300 and 301–600 workers remain legible. [Para 77][Para 109]
