# Award No. 135/16 — Ou Thong Development Cambodia Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 14 July 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer party; Tuon Si Phan, chosen by the worker party; Pen Bun Chhea, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139046

## 1. Facts
Ou Thong Development Cambodia Co., Ltd manufactures tyres and inner tubes at Prey Boeng, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, with about 60 workers, permanent and floating. [Para 40][Para 74][Para 191] Its Cambodia Asia Union, registered on 23 July 2015 with three leaders, claimed about 53 members. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] The union referred eleven demands; four were settled at conciliation, the union withdrew the payroll check-off demand, and two others were joined. [Para 14][Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] Six issues remained, none about dismissal. [Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance of 1,500 riel for each hour of overtime worked? [Para 75]
2 and 5. Whether the employer must pay the remaining annual leave of the two workers whose contracts ended, plus the regular attendance bonus and the travel and accommodation allowance for May 2016? [Para 108][Para 133]
3. Whether the employer must pay a regular attendance bonus of USD 15 per month instead of the USD 10 applied? [Para 159]
4. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 10 per month instead of the USD 7 applied? [Para 169]
7. Whether the employer must provide a permanent infirmary, a duty nurse and doctor, and adequate medicine under Prakas No. 330? [Para 179]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** prices for food have risen, so 1,000 riel per overtime hour is not enough and the meal allowance should be 1,500 riel; the attendance bonus should rise from USD 10 to USD 15 as rents have risen; the travel allowance should be USD 10; and the factory has neither a clinic nor a medicine box although it employs more than 60 workers. [Para 46][Para 63][Para 71][Para 155]
**Employer (respondent):** it pays what the Labour Law and the Ministry's Prakas require — a 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance, a USD 10 attendance bonus and USD 7 travel allowance — and will not go beyond them; it has set up a first-aid box in the office since a week before the hearing and sends injured workers to a nearby hospital. [Para 47][Para 64][Para 68][Para 72][Para 73]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must pay the actual remaining annual leave of the two departed workers and their travel and accommodation allowance for May 2016, and must open a permanent infirmary with a nurse on duty, a doctor or mid-level doctor present at least two hours per eight-hour shift, and adequate medicine; the meal allowance and the USD 15 and USD 10 claims were refused. [Para 211][Para 213][Para 215][Para 217][Para 210]

Issue 1. Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011 fixes a 2,000 riel meal allowance for overtime, but by its terms applies only to textile, garment and footwear workers; this company makes tyres, so it falls outside. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 79] Even so, the employer pays 2,000 riel for the 16:00–18:00 period, which the panel read as an intention to follow the Notification. [Para 80][Para 86][Para 87] No law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or past practice obliges payment of 1,500 riel per hour, so the demand is for a benefit above the statutory floor — an interest dispute. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 90]

Issues 2 and 5. Annual leave: Article 166(1) gives one and a half working days per month of continuous service, and Article 167(2) requires payment for the unused balance when the contract ends; the employer accepted it owed the balance. [Para 108][Para 110][Para 118][Para 211] Attendance bonus: Notification No. 230/12 requires the worker to have worked the full number of working days in the month without absence; these two worked only part of May 2016, so the bonus was refused. [Para 122][Para 139][Para 141] Travel allowance: the same Notification provides USD 7 per month and, following Award No. 216/12 (Wing Star), it is payable whether or not the worker attended and is not payable where the employer supplies transport or housing; it was not paid for May 2016, so it was ordered. [Para 142][Para 143][Para 149][Para 153][Para 155]

Issues 3 and 4. The employer already applies the Prakas rates of USD 10 attendance bonus and USD 7 travel allowance. [Para 163][Para 173] No law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or practice requires USD 15 or USD 10, so both demands seek benefits above the statutory floor and are interest disputes. [Para 165][Para 166][Para 175][Para 176][Para 177]

Issue 7. Article 238 requires primary medical care for the workforce; Article 242 requires any enterprise with at least 50 workers to have a permanent infirmary on site, staffed by a doctor with one or more nurses on duty day and night, with dressings and sufficient medicine, all at the employer's cost. [Para 179][Para 181][Para 184][Para 185] Article 1 of Prakas No. 330 repeats the 50-worker threshold, and its Article 3 table requires, for 50–300 workers, one nurse on duty and a doctor or mid-level doctor present at least two hours per eight-hour shift. [Para 187][Para 188][Para 194] With 60 workers and no clinic, the employer was ordered to comply in those terms and to stock the medicines Article 4 lists. [Para 200][Para 201][Para 206][Para 209]

**Interest disputes.** Article 96(2) of the Labour Law and Article 54 of the 2016 Trade Union Law reserve the bringing of an interest dispute, and the conclusion of a collective agreement, to the most representative union; Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 makes an award on an interest dispute stand in place of a collective agreement for one year. [Para 91][Para 94][Para 101] The panel found the claimant neither the most representative union nor otherwise qualified to bring an interest dispute, and declined to consider those demands. [Para 105][Para 107]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for an allowance higher than any law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or established practice provides is an interest dispute, which the panel will not decide unless the referring union is the most representative union in the enterprise; an employer who already pays the Prakas rate commits no breach merely because the union asks for more. Statutory minimum standards are different and are enforceable without any question of representativeness: unused annual leave must be paid on termination, the travel allowance is not conditional on attendance, and an enterprise of 50 or more workers must maintain an on-site infirmary with the nurse and doctor coverage and the medicines Prakas No. 330 prescribes. [Para 105][Para 149][Para 188][Para 201]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's reading of the 2,000 riel payment as an intention to comply with Notification No. 041/11 although that Notification does not cover a tyre plant, and its remark that a Prakas or notification is a legal instrument of the Ministry of Labour, are not necessary to the orders. [Para 84][Para 86][Para 130]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel states that six issues remained but lists five labels — "01, 02 and 05, 03, 04 and 07" — since issues 2 and 5 were joined. [Para 21]
- Annual leave is calculated three ways: the workers claim 7.5 days remaining [Para 49], their own arithmetic gives 11.5 days accrued and 1.5 used, leaving 10 [Para 57][Para 58], and the employer offers 7.5 days [Para 59].
- Money is corrupted by OCR: the attendance bonus appears as "1,000 USD per month" [Para 126][Para 163], and the annual-leave order carries the year "2022" [Para 211].
- The union is recorded as having about 53 members [Para 44] in a workforce the employer puts at 60 [Para 74], yet the panel found it was not the most representative union without showing the calculation [Para 105]; the workforce is also given as "more than 600" in one passage [Para 190].
- The Prakas reference is printed as No. 230/12 and 230 k.p/s.ch.n [Para 121][Para 123]; the conciliation report is dated 07 June 2016 at [Para 11] but 07 June 2019 at [Para 35].
- The face of the award lists the first workers' representative twice [Para 2], and the annexed Prakas No. 330 text at [Para 220]–[Para 232] is reduced to fragments.
