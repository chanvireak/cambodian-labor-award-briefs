# Award No. 015/16 — In Chia German Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 February 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kong Phalha (president); two dissenting opinions annexed · Doc ID 138971

## 1. Facts
In Chia German Co., Ltd (អ៊ិន ចៀ ហ្គាមេន) is a garment factory employing about 1,900 workers; the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) had just won a leadership election whose union was not yet registered [Para 50][Para 51][Para 54][Para 55]. Of twelve demands sent to conciliation, four were settled and eight came unresolved to the Council; point 4 was withdrawn at the hearing, leaving seven [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 30]. Sixty-eight workers had transferred their rights and 70 were treated as parties [Para 56][Para 59]. The disputed act is the employer's practice of paying an attendance bonus of USD 10 a month and cutting it in full for any absence [Para 60][Para 65].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus by USD 5, to USD 15 a month? [Para 60][Para 101]
2. Whether the employer must pro-rate the attendance bonus by the days of absence, (a) for leave granted by the employer and (b) for sick leave covered by a valid medical certificate? [Para 65][Para 121][Para 143]
3. Whether the employer must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 164]
5. Whether the employer must let pregnant workers leave 15 minutes early every day? [Para 171]
6. Whether the employer must pay a skill allowance to workers who can operate two or more machines? [Para 178]
7. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance to USD 12 a month? [Para 184]
8. Whether the employer must pay overtime meal money at 1,500 riel an hour, and weekly rather than monthly? [Para 196][Para 206]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Market prices for food, rent and transport have risen, so the attendance bonus should rise to USD 15 and a 4,000 riel lunch allowance be paid [Para 62][Para 77]. Cutting the attendance bonus in full for a single day's authorised absence, or for sick leave with a medical certificate, is unjust, and many factories keep it [Para 69][Para 71]. Pregnant workers need the extra 15 minutes to buy food before the crowd leaves, and overtime meal money is needed weekly to buy meals [Para 83][Para 97].

**Employer (respondent):** It has no capacity to grant the increases; the USD 10 attendance bonus follows Ministry of Labour Notice No. 230 and is provided for in the internal rules, and Notice No. 230 requires regular attendance without absence [Para 63][Para 64][Para 73][Para 104]. Travel and accommodation of USD 7 is paid as the notice requires, the overtime meal allowance of 2,000 riel a day is paid with the monthly wage, and sick leave does not attract wages [Para 93][Para 198].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the attendance bonus must be pro-rated for days of sick leave covered by a proper medical certificate, and overtime meal money must be paid one week at a time; all other demands are dismissed or refused consideration [Para 163][Para 223][Para 224][Para 227][Para 233]. The award is non-binding and becomes final if no party objects in writing within 08 days [Para 234].

On points 1, 3, 5, 6 and 7 the panel held each claim to be an interest dispute (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍) — a demand for a benefit beyond what the law sets — and applied Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9(1) of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, under which only the most representative union may request a collective agreement; by Article 43(1) of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 an award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement for one year [Para 106][Para 111][Para 114][Para 116]. Because the complainants here were employees and not the most representative union, the panel held they lacked standing to bring an interest dispute and declined to consider those claims [Para 118][Para 119][Para 120][Para 168][Para 192]. On point 7 it added that the employer already complied with Notice No. 230, which provides USD 7 and excludes workers given transport or lodging [Para 185][Para 187][Para 195].

On point 2(a), the panel found that Notice No. 230 of 25 July 2012, which replaced Notice No. 041/11, conditions the bonus on regular attendance "without absence", so a worker absent on leave granted by the employer has not met the condition; the practice of cutting the bonus was lawful and the claim was refused [Para 125][Para 126][Para 131][Para 141][Para 142].

On point 2(b) the panel held that entitlement to sick leave with pay under Article 169 of the Labour Law, read with Article 72 of the Constitution and Articles 25 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and 12 of the ICESCR, means absence through certified illness cannot be treated as breaking regular attendance [Para 152][Para 154][Para 155][Para 157]. Cutting the whole monthly bonus in that case destroys the purpose of the bonus and pushes sick workers to attend, so the panel ordered the bonus to be pro-rated by the days of certified sick leave [Para 158][Para 160][Para 162][Para 163].

On point 8, Notice No. 041/11 requires 2,000 riel a day or a free meal for voluntary overtime but fixes no time of payment; the panel reasoned that the money must reach the worker early enough to buy food for the overtime, and ordered payment one week at a time in line with the workers' needs, following Awards Nos 76/11, 146/12, 248/12 and 162/14 [Para 197][Para 207][Para 211][Para 219][Para 222][Para 223].

Two arbitrators dissented: An Non would have pro-rated the bonus for authorised leave as well, on the strength of prior awards Nos 223/2012, 216/2012, 214/2012, 211/2012, 204/2012, 203/2012 and 180/2012; Ing Sothy would have refused pro-rating for sick leave, treating the bonus as a conditional reward rather than wages [Para 240][Para 241][Para 247][Para 256].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Only the most representative union in an enterprise, or unions together representing more than half the workforce, may bring an interest dispute; individual workers cannot, and their demands for benefits beyond the law will not be considered [Para 116][Para 119]. Where a bonus is conditional on attendance without absence, the condition is not met by authorised unpaid leave, but certified sick leave counts as attendance for this purpose, so the bonus must be pro-rated by the days of certified sick leave rather than forfeited in full [Para 126][Para 157][Para 162]. Meal money for overtime must be released early enough to be used for that overtime, which a weekly payment satisfies [Para 211][Para 222].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's survey of the practice of other factories, and its remark that a full cut would depress productivity, support the reasoning on sick leave but are not part of the rule [Para 78][Para 160].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The number of demands shifts between twelve in the complaint, thirteen at conciliation and eleven in the union's letters, and eight or nine unresolved points are referred to in different passages [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 42]. The complainants are given as 68 transferring rights, 7 present at the hearing and 70 accepted as parties, with two named workers falling outside the transfer letter [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]. The award date of 10 February 2016 is consistent in the header, the signature block and both dissents, but the panel is recorded as constituted on "ថ្ងៃទី ២១ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៦" [Para 1][Para 26][Para 242][Para 257]. The employer's name and the union's name vary in spelling across the document ("អ៊ិន ចៀ", "អ៉ិន ចៀ", "អ៊ិន ចៀ ហ្គាមេន" and "អ៉ីន ចៀ ហ្គាមិន"), and the arbitrators' names appear as "គង់ ផល្ហៈ" in the header and elsewhere as "គង់ ផល្នៈ" [Para 1][Para 235][Para 242]. The operative section is badly degraded: point 2 is split into two duplicated sub-paragraphs with repeated text, the point 5 and 6 orders are printed twice in corrupted form, and the dissent annexes repeat their own headings [Para 225][Para 226][Para 229][Para 230][Para 242][Para 244]. The prior-award citations in the dissent are OCR-mangled, e.g. "សំណុំរឿង លេខ ២២៣/២០១២- ៨ស្ពារ ស្ពត វែរ" [Para 240].
