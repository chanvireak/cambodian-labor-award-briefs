# Award No. 039/16 — Cambo Young Sing Garment (Arbitration Council, 10 March 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Chhiv Phirom (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kong Phallu (president) · Doc ID 138987

## 1. Facts
The National Federation of Unions of the Textile and Garment Industries of Cambodia brought a collective dispute for workers at Cambo Young Sing Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូ យ៉ុន ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ិន), whose fixed-duration contracts were expiring between 08 and 15 February 2016, claiming the right to a further three-month renewal [Para 2]. The employer answered that the factory had closed since 07 February 2016 and could not renew, though for contracts ending between 08 and 29 February 2016 it offered one month of base wage and benefits [Para 3][Para 4]. Conciliation on 09 February 2016 failed on the single point, which was forwarded to the Council on 15 February 2016 [Para 12][Para 13]. The employer attended the 09 March 2016 hearing with its director and an interpreter; the complainant federation did not appear [Para 10][Para 15][Para 16].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether workers whose fixed-duration contracts expired between 08 and 15 February 2016 have a right to have those contracts renewed for a further 03 months? [Para 2][Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The affected workers claim the right to a three-month extension of their contracts; the federation did not appear to argue the point or file any document [Para 2][Para 20][Para 27].
**Employer (respondent):** The company closed on 07 February 2016, so it cannot renew any contract; nevertheless, for workers whose contracts ran out between 08 and 29 February 2016 it has offered base wage and other benefits for one month [Para 3][Para 4].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 039/16 without reaching the merits, treating the complainant's claim as abandoned [Para 58][Para 61][Para 62]. No sum was ordered and no other relief granted.

The panel framed its only question as procedural: whether it could continue in the absence of the worker party that had filed the complaint [Para 28]. It read Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 — where a party has been duly summoned and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may continue or may close the arbitration by issuing an award — together with Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules, which requires the panel to satisfy itself that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place before deciding [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]. Following earlier awards, it reduced Article 21 to three conditions: proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid reason given [Para 31][Para 33].

On the first condition, it found the secretariat's letter of 29 February 2016 addressed to the federation president, specifying the hearing date of 09 March 2016 at 08:30, the venue and the Council's contact numbers, and confirmed by telephone the same day [Para 34][Para 36][Para 39]. On the second, it held that "appearing before the panel" in Article 21 means both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflicts, procedure and issues, attempted conciliation, and hearing to verdict — and the complainant was present at none [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 45]. On the third, it recorded that at about 08:10 the federation's acting president telephoned to say he had confused the hearing date and would send an official, asking for roughly one hour's adjournment; the panel waited until 09:00 and no one came, and no specific valid reason was ever given [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50].

The panel then addressed the consequences. A complainant carries the burden of presenting argument, reasons and evidence to support its claim, and the respondent's burden arises only once the complainant has discharged its own [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55]. Because the complainant never appeared, never supplied argument or evidence, and had no valid excuse, the panel held the claim abandoned and concluded it need not continue, closing the arbitration by award [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 60].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
"Appearing before the panel" under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 means being present at the hearing and taking part through every stage of the arbitration, not merely being summoned; where the complainant is duly summoned, stays away through all stages without a valid reason, and files no argument or evidence, it has abandoned its claim and the panel will close the arbitration in its favour-free absence without examining the merits [Para 42][Para 43][Para 58]. A bare claim of having confused the hearing date, followed by failure to attend after a one-hour indulgence, is not a valid reason [Para 47][Para 49].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's account of the four-stage structure of an arbitration hearing, and its statement that a respondent's evidential burden arises only after the complainant has discharged its own, are general procedural explanations not necessary to the termination order [Para 41][Para 55].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The document is heavily degraded. Two arbitrator names conflict between the face of the award and the signature block: the worker-appointed arbitrator is "អន ណន" in the header but "អន ឈិន" at the signature, and the president is "គង់ ផល្ហៈ" in the header and "គង់ ផល្លៈ" at the signature [Para 1][Para 63]. The employer's name is printed in several forms ("ខេមបូ យ៉ុន ស៊ីង ហ្គាម៉ិន", "ខេមបូ យុទ្ធ សុង ហ្គាម៉ន", "ខេមបូ យ៉ុន ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន") [Para 1][Para 11][Para 13]. The jurisdictional paragraph cites "ជំពូក ទី ១២ ផ្នែក ទី ៧(ខ)" where sister awards cite Part 2, and the Prakas citation dissolves into "អាជ្ញាធរ ជាតិ 08 សករថយ" [Para 5]. Whole sentences are duplicated or garbled, particularly in the reasoning (for example the repeated hearing date at paras 34–36) [Para 34][Para 35][Para 59]. The panel records an employer offer of one month's wages for contracts ending 08–29 February 2016 but makes no finding on it, so it forms no part of the order [Para 4][Para 62]. The award is dated 10 March 2016 in the header, and no separate non-binding notice paragraph appears in the text [Para 1].
