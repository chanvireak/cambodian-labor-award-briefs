# Award No. 193/16 — Dong Du Tek Stay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 August 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139093

## 1. Facts
Dong Du Tek Stay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates a garment factory in Vattanac Industrial Park 2, Tuek Thla village, Krang Pongro, Dangkor, Phnom Penh. [Para 1][Para 36] Its workers struck from 1 August 2016, in a strike of about 200 people, over seven unresolved demands — reinstatement of terminated union activists, removal of four named administrative and QC staff, a lower daily production target, an end to union discrimination, re-admission of outside rice sellers, removal of the C4 group leader Mr E, and maintenance of wages and benefits during the strike. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12][Para 14][Para 25] Conciliation on 3 August 2016 left seven points unsettled and they were referred to the Council on 5 August 2016. [Para 22][Para 23] The employer then asked the Council for an injunction; Order No. 016/16 of 9 August 2016 required the strike to stop immediately and all strikers to return to their shifts from the morning of 10 August 2016. [Para 25][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration at all, given that the workers kept striking after the order of 9 August 2016 required them to stop? [Para 44][Para 47][Para 60]
2. Whether the employer must reinstate the terminated union activists with back pay, remove the four named administrative and QC staff, lower the daily production target, cease union discrimination, admit outside rice sellers, remove the C4 group leader Mr E, and maintain wages during the strike? [Para 3][Para 4][Para 12][Para 14]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer terminated the contracts of union activists, though it says only that the contracts expired and that it never received documents showing who was a union member; the four administrative staff named should be removed, the daily target lowered, discrimination ended, the rice sellers readmitted as before, the C4 leader Mr E removed, and wages and benefits maintained during the strike; the workers would keep striking until the 17 detained or excluded workers were allowed back to work like everyone else. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 6][Para 8][Para 9][Para 10][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent):** it never dismissed or terminated any union activist, since their contracts simply reached their stated term and it has no record of their union status; it has no power to decide on removing its own staff; it applies the old production policy, has never discriminated against the union, cannot readmit the rice sellers, cannot remove the C4 group leader, and applies the law on wages during a strike; it allowed all other workers back but excluded 17, of whom three were union leaders and four had been dismissed. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 13][Para 15][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration without deciding any of the seven demands. [Para 62][Para 63]

The panel framed the question as whether the arbitration could proceed at all, and applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004: during the panel's proceedings the parties must suspend every dispute measure, including strikes and lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and must attend every meeting the panel calls. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] It followed a line of earlier awards — No. 59/06 (Sang Vo), No. 39/08 (Chung You), No. 110/11 (Simano), No. 019/14 (Dong Du Tek Stay (Cambodia)) and No. 066/14 (Callisto Ivory (Cambodia)) — for the rule that the panel will not run an arbitration while workers are striking or an employer has locked out. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57] On the facts, the order of 9 August 2016 was issued, yet by the morning of 11 August 2016 the workers were still striking; the panel summoned both parties to a pre-hearing meeting on 12 August 2016, where the workers said they still chose to strike as a means of resolving the dispute. [Para 26][Para 58][Para 59] It therefore found the workers had no intention of submitting the collective dispute to arbitration and could not continue the hearing in that situation, terminating the proceedings. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the seven demands. [Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended, the panel has no basis to proceed: Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures for the duration of the arbitration, and a panel that hears a case in breach of that requirement cannot issue an award, so it must terminate the arbitration, leaving the demands undecided. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 61][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's references to the earlier orders terminating arbitration in Simano and Callisto Ivory, and its observation that continuing the strike shows the workers did not intend to have the dispute settled by arbitration, are explanatory of its practice rather than separate rulings. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The header dates the award "16 August 2014", which cannot be right: the strike, the order and the pre-hearing meeting all fall in August 2016. Both the printed year and the 2016 sequence are reported; the 2016 date is used in the title.
- The injunction is numbered "016/16" at [Para 26] but "016/14" in the evidence list at [Para 40].
- [Para 22] records the conciliation result as "15 points settled and 07 unsettled" with a stray "300" inserted; [Para 23] confirms seven points were referred.
- The record contains material that cannot belong to this award: [Para 63] opens with a sentence naming "លោក ហ៊ុន សែន" before the operative wording, and [Para 64] repeats the case number as a standalone paragraph. Both were disregarded as OCR corruption.
- The company name appears as ដុង ឌូ ថេក ស្ថាយ, ដុង ឌូ ថេ ក ស្តាយ, ដង ខ្ញុ ថេក ស្ថាយ, ដងឌូ ថេកសាយ and ដុងឌូឡាវ; the union is printed as សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ការចារ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and elsewhere សហភាព សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ជាតិ កម្ពុជា.
- The worker-appointed arbitrator is An Nan in the header but "អន លាន" in the signature block, which also carries untranslated English noise ("<unk>", "and the train") [Para 1][Para 66].
- [Para 27] and [Para 29] record the workers' report that the employer shut the factory gate against them, while [Para 28] records the employer's version that the workers had not returned by 08:00; the panel made no finding on this conflict, because it held no hearing [Para 43].
