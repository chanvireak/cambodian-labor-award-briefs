# Award No. 014/16 — Hong Sen Thek Sthay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 February 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 138968

## 1. Facts
Hong Sen Thek Sthay (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ហុង សេន ថេក ស្តាយ) is a garment-cutting enterprise at Ta Du village, Takeo, employing about 1,500 workers [Para 44][Para 45]. A union was formed by election on 20 November 2015 but its registration had not been granted when, on 19 December 2015, the employer dismissed the three elected leaders and an activist [Para 46][Para 47][Para 59]. Of nine demands, three were withdrawn, three settled, and the panel took only points 1, 6 and 8 to decision [Para 18][Para 23][Para 24]. The parties chose a non-binding award [Para 25].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the three elected union leaders — Mr S (president), Mr P (deputy) and Mr T (secretary) — were workers enjoying special protection against dismissal when they were dismissed on 19 December 2015, 29 days after the election? [Para 97][Para 108][Para 116]
2. Whether the employer's dismissal of those three without prior authorisation from the labour inspector complied with the procedure required for protected workers, and whether they should be reinstated with back pay? [Para 127][Para 131]
3. Whether Ms M, an activist named in the claim but not among the elected candidates, was a party to the case and entitled to reinstatement and back pay? [Para 138][Para 143]
4. Whether the employer was obliged to continue paying the USD 25 monthly incentive allowance to Mr P, who had received it for over a year (the panel's point 6)? [Para 146][Para 160]
5. Whether the employer was obliged to enlarge the infirmary from its existing area to 5 m × 6 m (30 m²) (the panel's point 8)? [Para 169][Para 182]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** On 16 December 2015 the employer told the three leaders that if they wished to keep working they must resign from the union, otherwise they could take compensation and leave; they refused and were dismissed three days later, while Ms M was dismissed for her activity in the election [Para 61][Para 66]. The union notified the election result by letter No. 134/15 of 23 November 2015 by hand and by post, and the employer refused delivery [Para 73][Para 74]. The USD 25 allowance was paid monthly to Mr P for over a year and stopped after the October 2015 strike while two teammates continued to receive it [Para 87][Para 88].

**Employer (respondent):** The three were called in but only their work was discussed, never the union, and they were dismissed because their work was not good [Para 64][Para 65]. The employer knew nothing of their union roles and received no notice of the election [Para 76][Para 77]; it sought no inspector authorisation because it regarded them as ordinary workers [Para 78][Para 129]. The USD 25 is a discretionary reward, may be given or withheld, and was cut after the strike because the employer lost heavily [Para 90][Para 91].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer is ordered to take back Mr S, Mr P and Mr T and to pay them back wages from the date of dismissal until the date of return; the claims of Ms M and the USD 25 allowance and the infirmary enlargement are dismissed [Para 183][Para 184][Para 185][Para 186]. The award is non-binding (សេចក្តីបង្គាប់មិនជាប់កាតព្វកិច្ច) and becomes final if no party objects in writing within 08 days of notification [Para 187].

On the status of the three leaders, the panel applied Article 293 of the Labour Law, which forbids dismissing a staff delegate or candidate without the labour inspector's consent, read with Article 3(A) paragraph 3 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, which extends that protection to election candidates for 45 days before and after the ballot [Para 97][Para 99][Para 100]. Since the Prakas does not expressly cover winners before the union is registered, it followed Award No. 40/10 (Meung Yan), extending protection at least to the date the union files its registration application [Para 103][Para 104][Para 106]. It set three cumulative conditions — protected candidate, dismissal inside the window, notice to the employer by any practicable means — and found each satisfied: the election was on 20 November 2015, the dismissals on 19 December 2015, 29 days later, and notice by hand and post was refused, which cannot be treated as non-notification [Para 108][Para 113][Para 116][Para 119][Para 123][Para 125].

On procedure, Article 293 required the inspector's authorisation before dismissing protected candidates; the employer admitted it never applied, its view that they were ordinary workers being wrong [Para 127][Para 129][Para 136]. Applying Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which empowers the panel to order reinstatement and immediate payment, and following Awards Nos 71/09, 09/10 (Chung Hao), 91/11 (Khlyk Su) and 337/14 (Chin Ten Yan), the panel ordered reinstatement with back pay [Para 131][Para 132][Para 134][Para 137].

On Ms M, Article 19 of Prakas No. 099/04 allows representation only on written authority; the transfer-of-rights letter of 21 December 2015 named only the three leaders, no authority from her was produced when asked, and she did not attend, so she was not a party and the claim was refused [Para 51][Para 138][Para 142][Para 145].

On the USD 25 allowance, an employer may decide unilaterally on incentives not required by law and may alter or withdraw them if it acts reasonably and in good faith, following Awards Nos 95/09 (Takfat), 10/03, 28/05 and 119/09 [Para 152][Para 154][Para 157]. The claimant bore the burden of proof and produced nothing on the two colleagues said to still receive it, so no unreasonableness was established [Para 161][Para 166][Para 168].

On the infirmary, Article 238 of the Labour Law requires primary care on site, and Articles 1 and 2 of Prakas No. 330 of 6 December 2000 require a permanent infirmary of at least 20 m² for enterprises with 50 or more workers [Para 169][Para 170][Para 171]. The existing room met that minimum and no evidence showed it unsuited to the workforce, so the demand for 30 m² was refused [Para 173][Para 181][Para 182].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A candidate elected to union leadership is protected from 45 days before the ballot until at least the date the union files its registration application, though unregistered; if the dismissal falls in that window and the union gave the employer notice by any practicable means, an employer that dismissed without the labour inspector's prior authorisation must reinstate and pay wages from dismissal to return [Para 104][Para 108][Para 136][Para 137]. An employer's refusal to accept a notice tendered by hand and post does not defeat notification [Para 123]. A worker named in the claim but absent from the transfer-of-rights letter and from the hearing is not a party [Para 143]. Incentives not required by law lie within the employer's unilateral discretion absent proof of unreasonable withdrawal [Para 157][Para 167].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's account of the gap the 45-day window leaves for winners before registration explains its choice of Award No. 40/10 rather than deciding anything itself [Para 103][Para 104].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears three ways: "ហុង សេន ថេក ស្តាយ" in the header and evidence list, "ហុង សេង ថេក ស្តាយ" in the worker-party description, and "ហុង សេន ថេក ស្ថាយ" in the reasons [Para 1][Para 2][Para 44]. The infirmary is 4 m × 5 m = 20 m² in the findings and final reasoning but 4 m × 6 m at one point, and the panel cites "ប្រការ ២០" (Article 20) of Prakas No. 330 where Article 2 is meant [Para 94][Para 172][Para 180]. Numerals are corrupted: "ទាំង ២០៣* នាក់" (203) for three workers, a workforce printed as "១៥០១", team sizes as "២ ០៣" and "០២៣" [Para 96][Para 117][Para 160]. The date extension prints "ឆ្នាំ ២០២ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦", though 16 February 2016 is consistent in header and body [Para 1][Para 27]. Prior awards are garbled into strings such as "០៧០៦-ដា យ៉ាំង" and "១៤៨០៧-ប៉ាយ ហឺ"; §4 gives only those legible [Para 109][Para 152]. The signature block carries stray years "2017 2022" and "យុវតី" above the employer-appointed arbitrator's signature, but the three arbitrator names match the header [Para 1][Para 187].
