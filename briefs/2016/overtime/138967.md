# Award No. 013/16 — Dai Young (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 12 February 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Sin Kim Sean, worker-appointed; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 138967

## 1. Facts
Dai Young (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment manufacturer in Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, employing about 600 workers, 80 to 90 per cent of them women [Para 39][Para 40][Para 57]. Its local union of the Democratic Garment Workers' Confederation was registered on 9 March 2015 with Heng Sotha as president, Heng Han as vice-president and Ieng Veasna as secretary [Para 41][Para 42]. Of 11 demands filed in December 2015, only two remained unresolved: enforcement of point 1 of an agreement of 23 April 2015 promising an annual staff party if the company secured the Thageat order in May 2015, and the provision of a nursery for workers' children from 18 months of age [Para 2][Para 4][Para 12].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must hold the annual party for 2015 under point 1 of the agreement of 23 April 2015? [Para 45][Para 65]
2. Whether the employer must provide a nursery so that women workers with small children can leave them there? [Para 85]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company had held an annual party every year from 2005 to 2012 and, after winning the Thageat order, refused to perform the agreement it had signed; work and overtime had increased since November 2015 and Thageat production rose from four to six sewing lines, so the condition was met [Para 45][Para 49][Para 51].
**Employer (respondent):** the agreement was expressly conditional on securing the Thageat order in May 2015, which did not happen — no answer came until October 2015, the orders were trial-scale, and no formal contract exists; the site is too cramped for a nursery, so it pays USD 7 per month in milk money instead, which the workers accepted [Para 53][Para 55][Para 62].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the annual-party claim was dismissed [Para 97], and the employer was ordered to provide a nursery for workers' children, or, where it cannot, to let mothers place children from 18 months at an outside nursery with the employer paying the care costs against actual invoices [Para 98][Para 99].

On issue 1, the panel applied Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code and Award 271/14: a contract binds the parties, but only on the terms they agreed [Para 65][Para 71][Para 72]. It read the phrase "ជាប់ Thageat" in the agreement as meaning that the company obtained an order from the buyer Thageat in May 2015 [Para 76][Para 77]. On the facts, no answer came in May 2015 and no Thageat goods were produced until October 2015, so the condition was not satisfied in the stipulated month and no obligation to hold the party arose [Para 80][Para 82][Para 83].

On issue 2, the panel applied Article 186 of the Labour Law, which requires an enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a nursing room in or near the premises and a nursery [Para 85][Para 86]. It held that the purpose of the provision is to keep mother and child close, support breastfeeding in the first six months and keep the child safe while the mother works [Para 92]. The USD 7 milk payment did not meet that purpose and could not release the employer from the duty [Para 93]. Following earlier awards, however, it accepted that where the employer cannot run a nursery for children over 18 months, the mother may use an outside nursery at the employer's expense on production of actual invoices [Para 88][Para 94].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a collective agreement grants a benefit subject to an express condition and a stated deadline, the benefit is owed only if the condition is met within that period; a party cannot be ordered to perform on the basis of later, different circumstances [Para 77][Para 80][Para 83]. A cash allowance in lieu of a nursery does not discharge the Article 186 duty to provide one, though an employer unable to build may satisfy it by paying actual outside nursery invoices [Para 93][Para 94].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the increased workload and additional sewing lines since November 2015 were irrelevant, because the agreement tied the party to the May 2015 order and not to later production volume [Para 51][Para 82].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's formation date reads "20 January 2022 2016" [Para 14]. The award date on the face is 12 February 2016, postponed from 09 February 2016, and the postponement paragraph is the only record of that change [Para 1][Para 20]. Both operative paragraphs are corrupted at the opening ("រូប ដ៏ សេធ" for the dismissal and "រដ្ឋមន្ត្រី បង្គាប់" for the order), so the outcome was taken from the reasoning at Paras 84 and 95 [Para 97][Para 98]. The employer's name appears as "Dai Young" on the face, "Dai Yang" in the union's title and "Dai Hong" in the union's letter [Para 1][Para 23][Para 39]. The buyer's name is printed variously "Thageat", "Thageath" and "Thageat" within the same paragraph, and one reference dates the union's fourth document to "21 November 2015" while another gives the same document as 21 February [Para 25][Para 53][Para 76]. The USD 7 figure appears both as a milk allowance and, in the panel's summary of the employer's case, as a substitute for the nursery, which is the ambiguity the panel had to resolve [Para 58][Para 91][Para 93].
