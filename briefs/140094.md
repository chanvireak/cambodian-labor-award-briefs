# Award No. 148/11 — Dai Yang (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 30 December 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators · Doc ID 140094


The panel comprised Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Vira (worker-appointed) and Kong Phalou (chair, chosen by the two arbitrators). The employer is Dai Yang (Cambodia). [Para 1]

## 1. Facts

The workers' federation and its Dai Yang factory union filed a complaint on 13 October 2011 seeking improved working conditions. Conciliation on 4 November 2011 failed on all 14 demands, and the non-conciliation report No. 1246 was forwarded to the Arbitration Council on 14 November 2011. A hearing was convened for 13 December 2011; both parties attended. At the hearing the worker party could not produce the Ministry of Labour's certificate recognizing the union leadership, promised to file it by 14 December 2011, and never did. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company should honour its notice of 26 August 2011 on disputed points 1, 2 and 3? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company should comply with the Arbitration Council's order of 15 August 2011? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company should provide adequate water in the toilets? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company should stop pressuring workers who cannot sew the required quantity? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company should stop retaliating against workers who joined the past strike? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company should remove buckets and cameras from the route into the restrooms? [Para 7]
7. Whether the company must discuss each holiday with the representative union two days in advance? [Para 8]
8. Whether the company should provide a meeting room for the representative union? [Para 9]
9. Whether the company should warn the Chinese supervisor who used inappropriate language toward workers? [Para 10]
10. Whether the company should stop oppressing workers from time to time? [Para 11]
11. Whether the company should pay wages to a female worker who has worked nine months and applies for maternity leave? [Para 12]
12. Whether the company should stop transferring workers from one place to another? [Para 13]
13. Whether the company should honour its promise to maintain wages for workers who joined the past strike? [Para 13]
14. Whether office staff should be required to work clearly? [Para 14]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The union pressed all 14 demands, relying on the company's 26 August 2011 notice and the Arbitration Council's 15 August 2011 order, and asked the panel to order improved conditions, non-retaliation, adequate facilities and prior holiday consultation. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4]

**Employer (respondent):** The company, represented by its administration chief and a company adviser, did not concede the demands and asked the union to show its leadership-recognition letter. [Para 1][Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 148/11-ដាយ៉ាំង for lack of a lawful complainant, without ruling on the 14 demands. [Para 61][Para 62]

The panel treated the threshold question as whether the worker party present at the hearing was the legally recognized leadership of the union federation, authorized to represent the complainants. Applying the burden-of-proof rule used in prior awards, it held that the party making a claim must prove it. The worker party promised on 14 December 2011 to file the Ministry of Labour's leadership-recognition letter but produced nothing, so the panel could not accept it as the union's representative. Without a lawful original complainant, the panel assumed no facts were established and closed the case. [Para 51][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A union representative cannot bring or sustain a labour-dispute claim before the Arbitration Council unless it produces the Ministry of Labour's certificate recognizing it as the union's leadership; absent that proof the claim has no lawful complainant and is closed without reaching the merits. [Para 57][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the employer also asked the union to show the recognition letter, and that both parties attended the hearing as invited. [Para 24][Para 26]
