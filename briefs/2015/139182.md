# Award No. 90/15 — Forfic Cross Bright Way Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 5 May 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy chosen by the employer, An Virak chosen by the workers, Tang Tri chair) · Doc ID 139182

## 1. Facts
Forfic Cross Bright Way Co Ltd cuts and sews garments at Svay Chrom, Baek Chan, Ang Snuol, Kandal, employing about 2,500 workers. [Para 27][Para 28] The workers struck from the afternoon of 1 April 2015 until 9 April 2015 without prior notice and without exhausting the conciliation and arbitration procedure. [Para 32][Para 49][Para 55] The employer deducted wages and the regular attendance bonus in proportion to the days each worker joined the strike. [Para 33] Conciliation on 2 April resolved three of four points; only the demand to preserve wages and the attendance bonus for the strike period reached the panel, which considered it for the two workers who attended. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 30][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must preserve one and a half days of wage and the regular attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ធ្វើការទៀងទាត់) for the strike on the afternoon of 1 April and on 2 April 2015? [Para 32][Para 40][Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the claim is a request for the employer's understanding — almost all workers joined the strike, so the employer should not deduct their wages and attendance bonus, which are an incentive to keep working actively. [Para 35][Para 36] They rejected the employer's proposal to offset strike days against annual leave and insisted on the claim as made. [Para 39]
**Employer (respondent):** the claim is contrary to law and the employer will apply the law; it had already offered to deduct the strike days from each worker's annual leave — lending further days if leave was exhausted — so no wages or bonus were in fact lost. [Para 37][Para 38]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the claim for one and a half days of wage and the regular attendance bonus for the strike of 1–2 April 2015. [Para 62][Para 63]

The panel applied Article 332(1) of the Labour Law, under which the employment contract is suspended during a strike and no work is given and no wage is paid, together with Article 72(1), under which suspension affects the principal obligations — the worker's duty to work and the employer's duty to pay. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43] Following Award 27/10 (Hat Enterprises), it held that on any day a worker strikes, whether the strike is procedurally lawful or not, the employer is not required to pay wages or bonuses. [Para 44][Para 45] It further found that workers who strike outside the legal procedure do not attend work regularly, so they cannot earn the regular attendance bonus, consistently with Awards 04/03, 15/04, 07/05, 63/07, 54/08, 81/08 and 204/12. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 60] The strike here was unlawful on its face: no prior notice as Article 324 requires, and no exhaustion of peaceful dispute resolution as Article 320(4) requires. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57] The employer's pro-rata deduction was therefore lawful and no order was made. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
During a strike the employment contract is suspended under Articles 332(1) and 72(1) of the Labour Law, so the employer owes neither wages nor the regular attendance bonus for the days struck, regardless of whether the strike was procedurally lawful; and a worker who strikes without the notice and prior-negotiation steps required by Articles 324 and 320(4) is not in regular attendance, so the attendance bonus cannot be claimed for that month. [Para 42][Para 44][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's closing observations that the strike is a worker's right coupled with conditions that protect the worker's own benefits and give the employer time to consider the demands, and its treatment of the employer's offer to charge strike days against annual leave, were not necessary to the dismissal — the panel decided the case on Article 332 alone and made no order about the leave offset. [Para 37][Para 61]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Article 324 is quoted as requiring notice of "ប្រាំ ពីរ ថ្ងៃ" ("five two days") at [Para 56] — the figure is unrecoverable from the OCR, so the panel's notice finding cannot be checked against the text it quoted.
- The claim describes a strike running 1–9 April 2015 [Para 32][Para 49], but the demand and the order cover only the afternoon of 1 April and 2 April, one and a half days [Para 34][Para 62]; the award never explains the narrowing beyond the parties' agreement that the strike began on the afternoon of 1 April.
- The employer's name appears in Khmer only, in several inconsistent transliterations ("ភ័រហ្វិក គ្រោស៍ ប្រាយ វេត" and "ភ័រហ្វិក គ្រោស៏"); the Latin form used in the title is a transliteration and is not confirmed anywhere in the source.
- The award date is 5 May 2015 in the header [Para 1], the hearing was 21 April 2015 [Para 7], and the parties extended the deadline from "29 April 2015" printed as "ឆ្នាំ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" to 5 May [Para 18].
- The worker-party arbitrator's name is printed both as "អាន វីរៈ" and "អាន វ័រៈ" at [Para 65]; the two claimants are abbreviated here as Ms H and Ms J, and the four worker representatives as Mr R, Mr P, Ms H and Ms J.
