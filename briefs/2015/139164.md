# Award No. 071/15 — Im Wey Garment International Co., Ltd., Kampong Chhnang Branch (Arbitration Council, 23 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mar Samborna (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Ang Eng Thong (president) · Doc ID 139164

## 1. Facts
Workers at Im Wey Garment International Co., Ltd., Kampong Chhnang Branch, represented by the Free Trade Union of Workers of the Kingdom of Cambodia, demanded that the company raise the piece rate by USD 0.25 per lot ("ឡូត៍") [Para 1]. Conciliation at the Kampong Chhnang Provincial Department of Labour failed on 11 March 2015 and the non-conciliation report No. 548 Kb/KCh of 12 March 2015 reached the Council on 13 March 2015 [Para 5][Para 10]. The workers struck, and continued to strike after the case was referred to arbitration [Para 11][Para 14]. The panel convened a pre-hearing on 20 March 2015 at 2:00 pm, at which the employer appeared and the workers did not [Para 17][Para 18].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration proceedings should continue at all, given that the workers were still on strike during the proceedings? [Para 28]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** No position was put to the panel: the workers' representatives did not attend the pre-hearing of 20 March 2015 [Para 18]. By telephone on 19 March 2015 their union official confirmed only that the workers had continued the strike at the company [Para 41].
**Employer (respondent):** That the strike was continuing notwithstanding the Council's order, and that the workers had neither suspended the strike nor returned to work as ordered [Para 13][Para 19].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in Case No. 071/15, without adjudicating the wage demand [Para 42][Para 43].
Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to a dispute to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout — and any other measure that could aggravate the situation, for the duration of the arbitration proceedings, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes [Para 28][Para 29]. On the employer's submissions at the 20 March 2015 pre-hearing, and the union official's telephone confirmation of 19 March 2015 that the strike continued [Para 41], the panel found that the workers were in breach of that obligation [Para 30].
The panel read Article 20, as it had in earlier orders, to mean that the Council will not proceed with arbitration whenever the workers are striking or the employer has locked out [Para 31]. It followed its own prior orders — Case No. 04/04 (IMSI), Case No. 18/04 (hotels), Case No. 77/05, Case No. 110/11 (Simano) and Case No. 17/13 — in each of which proceedings were not continued because the workers remained on strike during the arbitration [Para 32][Para 33][Para 37][Para 38]. It also noted that its order No. 011/15 KbA of 17 March 2015 had already directed an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the afternoon of 18 March 2015, and that this order had not been obeyed [Para 13][Para 14].
The panel recorded that it took no evidence and made no findings of fact on the merits of the non-conciliation report, because it held no hearing [Para 20][Para 27].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers continue a strike, or the employer imposes a lockout, while a collective labour dispute is before the Arbitration Council, the Council will not continue the arbitration proceedings, and will terminate them, until the strike or lockout has ceased [Para 30][Para 31][Para 42].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel reserved its right to end proceedings where a party breaches any term of its order, and noted that it could take up the dispute again once the strike ended [Para 36][Para 34].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed four different ways in the source — "អ៊ឹម វ៉េ" in the case title [Para 1], "អ៊ឹម វ៉ឹម" in the party details [Para 1], "អ៊ីម វ៉េ" in the orders and correspondence [Para 13][Para 20]; the title form is used above. Paragraph 8 attributes the complaint to the company seeking improved working conditions, which contradicts the workers' demand recorded at paragraph 1 and the non-conciliation report at paragraph 10. Paragraph 26 prints the invitation date as "១៩ ខែធ្នូ មីនា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" — two months, December and March, in one date; paragraph 25 dates the parallel employer invitation 19 March 2015. Paragraph 39 is a duplicated, contentless stub ("នៅក្នុង សំណុំរឿង នេះ នៅក្នុង សំណុំរឿង នេះ"), and paragraph 34 contains OCR garbage in Latin script. The signature block [Para 44] is badly corrupted, with stray "2016" tokens and lost signatures; the header date of 23 March 2015 is the only date of the order that survives intact. Prior-award citations at paragraph 32 are partly illegible (numbers run together, e.g. "១៣៧០៩ ១៣៨ ០៩").
