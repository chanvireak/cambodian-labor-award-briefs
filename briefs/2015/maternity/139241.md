# Award No. 168/15 — Fai Star Khot Thin Garment (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 July 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-appointed), វង្ស វណ្ណា (worker-appointed), រុន សារ៉ាយ (chair) · Doc ID 139241

## 1. Facts
The respondent operates a garment factory at Trapeang Chhuk village, Po Angrong commune, Baset district, Kampong Speu, and its workers raised 11 demands through two federations after conciliation failed on 3 July 2015. [Para 20] The Council convened a hearing for 16 July 2015 at 14:00 and summoned both sides; the employer attended, the workers and their representatives did not. [Para 25] Neither party filed any documentary evidence, so the file held only the non-conciliation report and the conciliation minutes. [Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel did not reach the 11 demands, which ranged from re-running the worker representative election and a "sibling" allowance for every department to maternity pay and conduct of Chinese administrative staff. [Para 3] It framed a single procedural question, then tested it against three statutory conditions: [Para 31]
1. What procedure must the panel apply where the worker party is absent on the hearing day? [Para 31]
2. Whether the party was properly summoned? [Para 44]
3. Whether the party failed to appear and to participate in the hearing without an authorised representative? [Para 47]
4. Whether the party gave a valid reason for its non-appearance? [Para 52]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** no position was presented — the claimant federation and its representatives were absent from the hearing and appointed no representative, so its contentions survive only as the 11 demands recorded in the non-conciliation report. [Para 50]
**Employer (respondent):** appeared at the hearing as summoned but filed no evidence and put no substantive case on the merits; no position of substance is recorded beyond its attendance. [Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed the arbitration in case No. 168/15 and made no order on any of the 11 demands, treating them as abandoned. [Para 64]

Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 requires the panel to summon the parties to state their claims in person and to lodge the necessary documents, Article 20 requires parties to attend every meeting convened, and Article 21 allows the panel, where a properly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason, either to continue in its absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award; rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules is to the same effect, provided the party had proper notice of date, time and place. [Para 33][Para 35]

Condition one was met: the Secretariat summoned the workers by letter No. 885 dated 10 July 2015, stating the hearing date, venue and contact numbers, and followed it with a telephone reminder. [Para 44] Condition two was met: the workers were absent, took part in none of the four hearing stages, and appointed no authorised representative. [Para 49] Condition three was met: the only explanation offered was that the workers had forgotten the hearing date, which the panel held was not a valid reason. [Para 54] Having found all three conditions satisfied, the panel concluded that the claimant had abandoned its claims, that it need not continue the proceedings, and that it could close the file by award. [Para 56][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant has been properly summoned and neither appears nor sends an authorised representative, and offers no valid reason — forgetting the date is not one — the panel treats the claims as abandoned and closes the arbitration by award without examining the merits; the duty to present reasons and evidence rests on the claimant first. [Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the respondent's duty to answer and disprove the claim arises only once the claimant has discharged its own burden of explanation and proof. [Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
No evidence was taken from either side, so the merits of all 11 demands are unreviewable on this record. Demand 6 is printed twice in the list of demands at line 8. The company name appears as ហ្គាមេន in the title but ហ្គាមិន at lines 63 and 64, and as ហ្វាយ_ស្តារ at line 16. The signature block is badly corrupted at line 66, where the worker-appointed arbitrator's name is followed by stray text ("Mund", "-១១៩៩៣៧ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៧"), so the panel's signatures cannot be confirmed from the text.
