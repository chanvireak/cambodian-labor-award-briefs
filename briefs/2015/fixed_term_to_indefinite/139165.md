# Award No. 072/15 — Meroson (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 24 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ingh Sothy (employer-appointed), Venh Pav (worker-appointed), Kong Pollakha (president) · Doc ID 139165

## 1. Facts
Some 20 workers of Meroson (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., backed by three union federations, struck from 11 March 2015 over four demands on working conditions: conversion of all fixed-duration contracts to indefinite-duration contracts, facilities for lawful leave, an end to forced overtime and punitive threats, and no termination without valid reason [Para 3][Para 4][Para 13]. Conciliation on 12 March 2015 left all four demands unresolved, and non-conciliation report No. 268 Kb/Ak/Vk of 13 March 2015 reached the Council the same day [Para 11][Para 14][Para 15]. The strike continued after referral, and the panel met no hearing on the merits [Para 16][Para 30].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the arbitration proceedings should continue, where the workers remained on strike through the proceedings? [Para 31]
2. Whether the workers' four substantive demands — indefinite-duration contracts, leave facilities, overtime and penalties, and termination — could be reached on the merits? [Para 4][Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The four demands stood as referred: abolish fixed-duration contracts across the workforce, ease the taking of lawful leave, stop forcing overtime and threatening penalties, and stop terminating workers without proper reason during this period [Para 4][Para 6][Para 7]. They filed no documents with the panel and put no submission at a hearing [Para 25].
**Employer (respondent):** That the strike had run since 11 March 2015 and continued after the Council's order, and that the workers were not complying with Order No. 010/15 KbA [Para 17][Para 22][Para 24].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in Case No. 072/15 without ruling on any of the four demands [Para 47][Para 48].
The employer's letter of 13 March 2015 asked for an order suspending a strike that had begun on 11 March 2015 [Para 17]. On 17 March 2015 the panel issued Order No. 010/15 KbA directing an immediate suspension of the strike until the arbitration was complete and a return to work from the afternoon of 18 March 2015 on the workers' own shifts [Para 18]. The workers did not comply and the strike continued [Para 19].
The panel then verified the position by telephone twice — on 18 March 2015 at about 14:25 and on 19 March 2015 at about 08:02 — and on both occasions both parties confirmed the strike was continuing; the workers added that they would march on the Ministry of Labour [Para 20][Para 21][Para 44]. The employer confirmed the same by letter of 20 March 2015 [Para 22][Para 45].
Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout — and any measure that could aggravate the dispute for the duration of the arbitration, and to attend all meetings convened [Para 31][Para 32]. Reading that article as it had in earlier orders, the panel held that it will not continue arbitration while workers are striking or the employer has locked out [Para 33][Para 34]. It aligned itself with its prior orders — Case No. 04/04 (IMSI), Case No. 18/04 (hotels), Case No. 77/05, Case Nos. 110/11 (Simano), 104/13, 119/13, 019/14, 322/14, 17/13 and 066/14 (Calisto) — in each of which proceedings were ended or not continued because the strike persisted [Para 35][Para 36][Para 40][Para 41][Para 42]. Because the panel held no hearing, it made no findings of fact on the non-conciliation report [Para 30]. Its conclusion was that the continuing, non-compliant strike made it impossible to continue the proceedings or hold a hearing [Para 46].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A strike or lockout maintained in breach of the Council's suspension order deprives the panel of the ability to proceed: the Council will terminate the arbitration rather than adjudicate the demands while the dispute measure continues [Para 34][Para 46][Para 47].

**Obiter dicta:** The Council may take up the same collective dispute again once the union and workers cease the strike [Para 37]. The panel also reserved the right to end proceedings where a party breaches any term of its order [Para 39].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The fourth demand is numbered "២៤-" ("24") at paragraph 7 where "៤-" ("4") is meant, following demands 1–3 at paragraphs 4–6 [Para 6][Para 7]. The employer appears as "មេរ៉ូសាន់ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី" in the Khmer text but as "Meroson (Cambodia) Co., Ltd" in the document list [Para 1][Para 23]. The presiding arbitrator is printed "គង់ ផល្ហៈ" on the face of the award and "គង់ ផល្ទុះ" in the signature block [Para 2][Para 49]. Paragraph 20 contains a stray year token "2017" inside a 2015 chronology, and the signature block at paragraph 49 carries "2016" tokens. Paragraph 23 repeats its own opening clause ("ធ្វើ ការពិចារណាស់ ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល ធ្វើ ការពិចារណា"), and paragraph 30 is truncated mid-sentence at "កណ្តាល មិន បាន ដំណើរការ សវនាការ". Paragraph 45 drops the negative — it reads that the workers "still continued the strike in compliance with the order", where the reasons require "in non-compliance"; paragraph 43 states the point correctly. Paragraph 39 merges the quoted point 11 of Case No. 18/04 with the following paragraph on Case No. 77/05, so the two are hard to separate. The worker-side arbitrator is printed as "វ៉ែន ពៅ" twice in the signature block, with no signature mark surviving.
