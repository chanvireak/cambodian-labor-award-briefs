# Award No. 294/15 — Foucs Met (Cambodia) Le Doux Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 4 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bunchea presiding; Ing Sothy appointed by the employer, Ven Pov appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 139468

## 1. Facts
The company cuts and sews garments at Pong Tuek, Damnak Ampil, Ang Snuol, Kandal. [Para 1] On 28 October 2015 the Khmer Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation filed three demands: reinstate the dismissed union leaders with their wages and benefits, remove the head of administration, Mr V, for insults, incitement to violence and anti-union discrimination, and pay September 2015 wages owed to the leaders and other workers. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 13] Conciliation failed on 12 November 2015 and report No. 2039/15 reached the secretariat on 13 November 2015. [Para 14][Para 9] The employer attended the information session of 18 November and the hearing of 19 November 2015; the union did not appear and told the secretariat by telephone that morning that it would not. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 19][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel must continue the arbitration when the claimant workers' party, duly invited, does not appear? [Para 29]
2. Whether the union's three demands (reinstatement, removal of Mr V, September wages) could be examined at all? [Para 3][Para 28]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union sought reinstatement of its company-level leadership with wages and benefits preserved, the removal of Mr V for assaulting a worker while security guards removed her ID card, pocketing union dues and dismissing union members, and payment of September 2015 wages. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5] It then did not attend the hearing and gave no reason for its absence. [Para 19][Para 44]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer appeared through its HR manager and purchasing liaison, holding a transfer of authority dated 19 November 2015, but the record contains no substantive answer to the three demands. [Para 1][Para 21][Para 28]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration, holding that the claimant had abandoned its complaint; no order was made on the three demands. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 55]
Because the claimants were absent, the panel found it could establish no facts on the merits and turned to its own procedure. [Para 28] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 lets a panel either continue or terminate where a duly invited party fails to appear without good reason, and Rule 4.7 of the arbitration rules requires that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] Following awards 138/08 (Malin), 11/11, 131/12 (Master & Freng), 108/13 (Otto), 332/14 (T & K Garmen) and 232/15 (Blue Field), the panel applied three conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid reason. [Para 32][Para 33]
On the first, invitation No. 1494 of 18 November 2015 was sent to the federation president with the date, place and contact numbers, fixing the hearing for 19 November 2015 at 14:00. [Para 34][Para 36] On the second, the panel read "appearance before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation in all four stages — introduction, procedure, conciliation and hearing — and the union did neither. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 41][Para 43] On the third, the federation gave no reason at all for staying away. [Para 44][Para 45] All three conditions being met, the panel treated the complaint as abandoned and closed the file. [Para 47][Para 52][Para 53]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union has been properly notified and neither appears at the hearing nor takes part in any of its four stages, and offers no valid reason, the panel will treat the complaint as abandoned and terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the demands in the claimant's absence. [Para 38][Para 47][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel stated the general allocation of burdens — the claimant must first give reasons and evidence for its claim, and the respondent's duty to answer arises only afterwards — though it decided nothing on the merits here. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 9][Para 15] Para 16 dates the panel's constitution "17 November 2023 2015", printing two years, and para 30 garbles the citation of Prakas No. 099 into "099 Sakabay activity at home 2016". [Para 16][Para 30] Article 21 is cited as "Article 212" at para 29 and correctly at paras 38 and 42. [Para 29][Para 38][Para 42] Para 48 refers to "case 10 collective labour disputes" when the file concerns three demands. [Para 48] The employer's name is OCR-damaged throughout and printed in at least five forms ("Foucs Met (Cambodia) Le Doux", "Foukhue Sa Met ... Le Doux", "Le Doux Manufacture Garment", "Le Doux Manu Fact Garment", "Le Der Goud Meny Fak"); the romanisation in the title is approximate and is flagged rather than resolved.
