# Award No. 040/15 — F.Y (Cambodia) Fashion Limited (Arbitration Council, 19 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer; Sin Tim Sean, chosen by the workers; Pen Bunchhea, chair) · Doc ID 139143

## 1. Facts
F.Y (Cambodia) Fashion Limited (អេហ្វ វ៉ាយ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្វាស្ហិន លីមីធីត) makes garments at Tonle Bassac, Chamkarmon, Phnom Penh, and employs 20 workers. [Para 1][Para 8] The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia filed four demands on 06 January 2015 — a 4,000 riel daily meal allowance, a USD 15 attendance bonus, a USD 14 travel and accommodation allowance, and a USD 28 wage increase for all 20 workers from January 2015 — and conciliation failed on all four on 28 January 2015. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 15] The non-conciliation report of 05 February 2015 reached the Secretariat on 06 February, and a pre-hearing meeting was set for 13 February 2015 at 15:00. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 16] On 09 February 2015 the workers stopped the machines and struck; on 11 February the panel issued Order No. 006/15 requiring the strike to be suspended at once and all strikers to return to their shifts from the morning of 12 February 2015. [Para 17][Para 18] The employer reported that the workers stayed out on 12 February, entering the plant only to sit idle. [Para 19][Para 23][Para 48]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay a regular-attendance bonus of USD 15 a month? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 14 a month? [Para 6]
4. Whether the employer must add USD 28 to the base wage of all 20 workers from January 2015? [Para 8]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The union pursued the four claims through conciliation and referral, then struck when they were refused; it did not attend the pre-hearing meeting of 13 February 2015 and sent no explanation. [Para 14][Para 21][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** It could not meet the meal allowance and instead applies the Labour Law; it pays the USD 10 attendance bonus and USD 7 travel allowance set by ministerial notification rather than the sums claimed; it can meet the wage demand only within the notification and the law; and it asked the panel to order the workers back to work because they had shut the machines. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel discontinued the arbitration without ruling on any of the four claims, and ordered nothing on the merits. [Para 50][Para 51]
Because no hearing was ever held, the panel recorded that it made no findings of fact on the four referred issues. [Para 34] It framed the only question it decided as whether the arbitration should continue, and answered it under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law — and any other step that could aggravate the dispute, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37] It followed a long line of its own awards (59/06, 39/08, 137/09, 138/09, 103/10, 110/11, 104/13 Sabrina and 019/14) holding that it will not conduct an arbitration while a strike or lockout is running. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 44] It also relied on its notice of 29 January 2004 in case 04/04, which stated that a party has no right to strike during its own arbitration and that the panel could not decide a dispute referred to it in those circumstances, and on the second orders in the hotel cases 18/04 and 77/05, which reserved the right to end proceedings where a party breached the order. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43] On the facts it found that the workers were still striking on 12 February 2015 in defiance of Order No. 006/15, and that the union had ignored the invitation of 12 February to the pre-hearing meeting without explanation, while the employer attended. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] It therefore terminated the arbitration under Article 20. [Para 49][Para 50]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate a collective dispute while a strike or lockout is in progress: where a party continues a strike in defiance of an interim order to suspend it and return to work, and ignores the pre-hearing meeting, the panel discontinues the proceedings without any decision on the merits, leaving the claims undetermined. [Para 37][Para 49][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it retains the right to end proceedings whenever a party breaches one of its orders, drawn from the hotel awards, is a general remark not needed for this discontinuance. [Para 42][Para 43]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed in at least four Khmer forms, the title reading អេហូវ៉ាយ where the body reads អេហ្វ វ៉ាយ, and the Latin rendering used here follows the body. [Para 1][Para 12][Para 25] The worker-chosen arbitrator is named ស៊ីន តឹម ស៊ាន in the header but ស៊ីន គីម សាន in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 52] The recital of the procedural history is garbled to the point of duplication and states both that the department sent the four issues and that it did not. [Para 34] The list of precedents runs its numbers together ("លេខ ១៣៧ ០៩ ១៣៨/០៩"), so some case numbers are unrecoverable. [Para 39] The signature block and final page are corrupted, ending in placeholder tokens and stray characters. [Para 52][Para 53] No monetary order was made, so no figure is reported as awarded. [Para 51]
