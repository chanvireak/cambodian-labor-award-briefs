# Arbitral Order No. 319/15 — Vinny Fay Sen Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 9 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer party; Liv Sovanna, chosen by the worker party; Pen Bun Chhea, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139485

## 1. Facts
Two union federations (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ ជឿនលឿន) filed a collective complaint, numbered 20, at the Kandal provincial department of labour on 2 December 2015, raising four demands against Vinny Fay Sen Co., Ltd, a factory at Trapeang Krasang, Ang Snuol. [Para 1][Para 12] Conciliation on all four points was held the same day and failed; the non-conciliation report 2231/15 was transmitted to the Council on 3 December 2015. [Para 11][Para 13] The workers had been on strike since 1 December 2015; the employer wrote on 4 December 2015 asking for an order suspending the strike, and the Council issued order 038/15 on 7 December 2015 requiring the strike to stop immediately and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 8 December 2015. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 18] They did not return.

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must convert the fixed-duration employment contracts into undetermined-duration contracts? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must take back all the workers and union leaders it dismissed on 30 [month printed inconsistently]? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must remove from the Vinny Fay Sen factory the Chinese section head who beat workers in November? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must stop discriminating against the union? [Para 7]
5. Whether the arbitration proceedings should continue at all while the strike goes on? [Para 25]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the four demands stood as filed; the worker party filed no documents with the Council at all [Para 19], and told the secretariat by telephone that the workers would not resume normal work until the employer agreed a suitable settlement with them first [Para 17][Para 36].
**Employer (respondent):** it disagreed with each demand and denied any discrimination against the union [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8]; the strike begun on 1 December 2015 was seriously affecting its production chain, so the Council should order it suspended [Para 15].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel ordered that the arbitration in case No. 319/15 be terminated (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការ អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល), and decided none of the four disputed points. [Para 39][Para 40]

The panel took jurisdiction under Chapter 12, Section 2(b) (Articles 309 to 317) of the 1997 Labour Law, Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 and the term-13 appointment Prakas No. 321 of 27 July 2015, and noted that conciliation had been attempted under Chapter 12, Section 2(a) before the file reached it. [Para 9][Para 10] It recorded that it held no hearing, and so made no finding of fact on any of the disputed points. [Para 24]

Its reasoning turned on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, which requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lock-out under Article 318, or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — throughout the arbitration, and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 25][Para 26] The panel read that provision as barring it from proceeding while a strike is running, the position it had taken in earlier orders. [Para 27][Para 28] It recited a line of those orders — Nos. 5806, 3908, 13709 and 13809, 103/10, 110/11, 104/13 (Sabrina (Cambodia)) and 019/14 (Dongdu Tech Stai (Cambodia)) — and the notice of 29 January 2004 in case 04/04, which held that a party has no right to strike during the arbitration and that the Council may neither resolve the dispute nor issue an order on it, but may take the case up again once all the union's workers stop striking. [Para 29][Para 30] It also cited the second orders of 22 April 2004 in case 18/04 and of 24 December 2005 in case 77/05, and its own order in case 040/15 (FY Cambodia Fashion Limited), each of which declined to proceed for the same reason. [Para 31][Para 33][Para 34] It stated its agreement with that interpretation. [Para 35]

On the facts, the panel relied on telephone confirmations from both parties, taken after order 038/15, that the workers were still striking in front of the company and were not complying with the order unless the employer first offered a settlement; on that basis it did not summon the parties to a pre-hearing meeting. [Para 17][Para 36] It found that the workers were continuing to strike in disregard of its order, and that it could not therefore hold a hearing to resolve the collective dispute. [Para 37][Para 38] Applying Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, it decided to close the proceedings. [Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers of an enterprise keep striking after the panel has ordered the strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not hear the collective dispute at all: it terminates the arbitration instead of ruling on the merits, and the case may be brought again only once every worker of the unions concerned has stopped striking. [Para 28][Para 30][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's explanation that it dispensed with a pre-hearing meeting because both parties' accounts by telephone were consistent is a procedural observation, not part of the ground of the termination. [Para 36] So too is its remark that it made no factual finding on the disputed points because no hearing was held. [Para 24]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The dismissal date in issue 2 reads "ថ្ងៃទី៣០ ខែធ្នូ វិច្ឆិកា" — the 30th of both December and November — at [Para 3]; the brief reproduces the ambiguity rather than choosing a month.
- The non-conciliation report is numbered 2231/15 and dated 3 December 2015 at [Para 11] and [Para 19], but 2231 K.B/K.N dated 2 December 2015 at [Para 14]; the conciliation minute itself is dated 2 December 2015 at [Para 20].
- The header block is heavily corrupted by OCR: "អ-- :-20006-==:=-" for the Council's seal line, a stray "]" inside the worker party's address, and the union representatives' names run together without separators [Para 1]. The signature block is worse, printing "strum", "mago Azer)" and "Nowng ARBITR" for the three signatures [Para 41]; the arbitrators' names survive only in the header.
- Paragraph [Para 14] carries a block of unrelated text ("ការពារ កម្ពុជា ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា ស្រុក ស្អាត ... ៣០០០ @-@") spliced into the sentence, and [Para 36] repeats a clause twice and leaves a quotation and a bracket unclosed; [Para 35] carries a stray "៤".
- The company is printed "វីននី ហ្វេ សិន ខូអិលធីឌី" throughout but "វីនី ហ្វេ សិន ខូ អិលធីឌី" in the operative order [Para 40]; the earlier case numbers at [Para 29] are garbled and one party name there survives only as "ហ្វ្រង់កូ នីត ធីង".
- The document is styled an order (សេចក្តីបង្គាប់), not an award, and carries no award number in the Council's usual "NN/YY" form; it is identified only by the case number 319/15. The employer's representative and the section head named in issue 3 are reduced to initials here, as are the union officers, per briefing convention.
