# Award No. 307/15 — New Ark Kit Garment Factory Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Sokh MaTheung (president) · Doc ID 139479

## 1. Facts
The Free Women Workers' Union (សហជីព សេរី ស្ត្រី កាត់ដេរ) at New Ark Kit Garment Factory Ltd, a garment manufacturer employing about 4,300 workers, brought a collective dispute over working conditions; conciliation at the Kandal provincial labour department settled one of four points, leaving three. [Para 10][Para 47][Para 12] The disputed acts were the non-renewal of a warehouse worker's successive six-month fixed-term contracts as of 1 October 2015, and the employer's unilateral withdrawal of a 2008 table-cutting agreement and of overtime pay, meal allowance and target bonuses for two union officers. [Para 55][Para 72][Para 77]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr A, a warehouse worker, into normal employment and pay his wages from the date of dismissal until reinstatement? [Para 52]
2. Whether the employer must honour the conditions of the 2008 agreement with the table-cutting section — the calculation of two additional hours of overtime pay and the target bonus? [Para 66]
3. Whether the employer must pay arrears and continue providing overtime pay, meal allowance during overtime, and the target bonus to Mr C and Mr V? [Para 75]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Under Article 67 of the Labour Law Mr A's contract became one of indefinite duration because he had worked more than two years; he had committed no serious misconduct, and the employer acted out of anti-union discrimination because he was a union activist. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 60]**Employer (respondent):** This was not a dismissal but the expiry of a fixed-term contract that was simply not renewed, at a time when the company had little work and was cutting costs and retained only essential staff; there was no union discrimination. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 90]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to take Mr A back into normal work and pay his wages from the date of dismissal until his return; to maintain the 2008 table-cutting conditions (two hours' overtime calculation and the target bonus); and to continue and pay arrears of overtime pay, overtime meal allowance and target bonus to Mr C and Mr V from 24 June 2015. No aggregate sum was quantified in the award. [Para 196][Para 197][Para 199]

On issue 1 the panel asked first what type of contract Mr A held. Article 67(2) caps fixed-term contracts at two years, and Article 73(5) provides that absent prior notice a contract continues for an equal term or is treated as indefinite where the aggregate exceeds the Article 67 limit. [Para 92][Para 96] The panel adopted its earlier reasoning that the two-year limit is measured by the total duration of all successive contracts, not each renewal, and cited ILO Recommendation No. 166 of 1982 to the same effect. [Para 97][Para 104] On the facts, Mr A served from 7 February 2013 to 1 October 2015 — 2 years, 7 months and 23 days — under six-month contracts renewed without any fair interval, so his contract became indefinite. [Para 119][Para 120][Para 121] The termination was therefore a dismissal under an indefinite contract, not a non-renewal at expiry. [Para 122] Article 74 permits such dismissal only on a valid reason concerning the worker's aptitude or character or the necessity of the enterprise's operation; the employer produced no document supporting its claim of falling orders and cost-cutting, and advanced no reason of that character. [Para 134][Para 140][Para 141] The dismissal was thus contrary to the spirit of Article 74. [Para 143]

The discrimination plea under Articles 12 and 279 failed separately: the party alleging discrimination bears the burden of proof, and the workers adduced no evidence linking the dismissal to union membership or activity. [Para 123][Para 125][Para 128][Para 130] The panel expressly confined the award to the union and the four workers present at the hearing. [Para 51]

On issue 2 the panel applied Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code: a contract arises from mutual consent and can only be modified or extinguished by that consent. [Para 147][Para 149][Para 151] The 2008 table-cutting agreement was proved, and the employer withdrew the two-hour overtime calculation on 1 September 2015 without the agreement of the workers who were party to it and without stating any reason. [Para 157][Para 160][Para 161] Parties wishing to alter their obligations must consult each other first, so the employer was bound to maintain the 2008 terms. [Para 162][Para 165]

On issue 3 the panel applied Article 139 (new) and point 2 of Notification No. 041 of 7 March 2011: voluntary overtime attracts pay at 150 per cent of the normal rate plus a 2,000 riel daily meal allowance. [Para 169][Para 171][Para 172] The employer justified withholding these entitlements by the two workers' failure to sign voluntary-overtime letters, but produced no document showing the 27 April 2015 meeting at which it said this rule was announced, and the workers denied knowing of it. [Para 180][Para 190][Para 192] Attendance was in any event verified by group assistants and fingerprint scanning. [Para 193] The panel concluded the entitlements had been earned and ordered both continuation and arrears. [Para 194][Para 195]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Successive fixed-term contracts that are renewed without a fair interval are measured by their aggregate duration; once that total exceeds the two-year ceiling in Article 67(2), the contract is of indefinite duration regardless of the five per cent indemnity paid at each expiry, and any subsequent termination must satisfy Article 74 — failing which the worker is reinstated with wages from the date of dismissal. [Para 119][Para 120][Para 122]

**Obiter dicta:** An employer may impose administrative procedures on its workforce under its management prerogative, but must explain or publish them publicly so that all workers can clearly know and understand them. [Para 188][Para 189] The panel also observed that Cambodian labour law leans toward indefinite-duration contracts because job security encourages worker commitment. [Para 99][Para 100]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The conciliation report is dated two ways: 12 November 2015 in the procedural narrative and in the list of Ministry documents, but 6 November 2015 where the transmission of the three unresolved issues is described. [Para 7][Para 12][Para 41] The hearing time is printed as 14:30 in one place and as "14 and 00 minutes" in another. [Para 9][Para 13] The employer is named "New Ark Kit Garment Factory Ltd" on the face of the award but shortened to "New Ark Kit" and, in places, "New Ark" in the reasons. [Para 46] The award date of 25 December 2015 follows the parties' agreed extension from 17 December, but the extension is printed with a broken year ("២០ ២០១៥"). [Para 18] The president's name survives only in the header and a partly garbled signature block; the worker-appointed arbitrator's name is legible only as "An Non". [Para 1][Para 201] No aggregate riel figure for back wages or arrears is printed — the orders are framed as continuing obligations. Paragraphs 92–104 and 108–114 are heavily corrupted by OCR, with statutory quotations partly lost.
