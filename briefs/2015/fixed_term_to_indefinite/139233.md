# Award No. 156/15 — Jeneray Sin International (Arbitration Council, 09 July 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Pollak, president appointed by both) · Doc ID 139233 · non-binding award

## 1. Facts
Jeneray Sin International operates at Trapeang Ampil village, Trapeang Kong commune, Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu. [Para 1] On 09 June 2015 the Kampong Speu provincial Labour Department received a complaint from the Khmer Free Workers' Federation raising 13 demands on the workers' behalf; conciliation on 24 June 2015 produced no settlement and the 13 points were referred to the panel. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22]
The company wrote on 25 June 2015 asking the panel to order the workers back to work, reporting that from 23 June 2015 the Khmer Free Workers' Federation had led 47 workers in a strike. The panel issued Order No. 023/15 on 26 June 2015 suspending the strike and requiring return to work from the morning of 27 June 2015; on 30 June 2015 it was told by telephone that about 2,000 workers were still striking. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25] Neither party attended the pre-hearing meeting of 02 July 2015, and no hearing was held. [Para 28][Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration where the worker party, which brought the complaint, is absent? [Para 38]
2. Whether the three conditions for proceeding or terminating in a party's absence — proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid excuse — were satisfied? [Para 46][Para 51]
3. Whether any of the 13 substantive demands was decided? [Para 22][Para 37]

The 13 demands — travel or accommodation allowance of 15 US dollars, no discrimination over sick-leave certificates, attendance bonus of 20 US dollars, extra card-scanning machines in the Building B cutting and leather sections, a 10 US dollar skill allowance, a 3,000 riel overtime meal allowance, no wage deduction for scanning three or four minutes early or late, a nursery as required by the Labour Law, hygienic and adequate midday meals, sufficient medicine in the clinic, a 5 US dollar annual skill increment for supervisors with two years' service, correct end-of-contract payments when documents are changed, and an incentive bonus for material-opening assistants — were not examined. [Para 2][Para 5][Para 7][Para 8][Para 13][Para 14][Para 37]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Neither federation attended. The Khmer Samaki Workers' Federation wrote on 02 July 2015 that it was negotiating with the employer and withdrew all points from case 156/15 by letter No. 281/15. The Khmer Free Workers' Federation, which had brought the complaint, gave the secretariat no reason at all for its non-attendance. [Para 31][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]
**Employer (respondent):** The company did not attend either. Its only substantive position on the record is its written request of 25 June 2015 that the panel order the striking workers back to work. [Para 1][Para 23][Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration of case 156/15 without examining any of the 13 demands on the merits. [Para 68][Para 70]

The panel read Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 as obliging it to summon the parties to state their case in person and to submit the necessary documents, Article 20 as obliging the parties to attend every meeting convened, and Article 21 together with Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules as allowing it either to continue in a party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41][Para 43]

It then tested the three conditions it had identified in earlier awards. On summons, the secretariat had invited the complainant workers by letter No. 809 of 30 June 2015 stating the date, place, telephone and fax for contact, and had also telephoned both federations, which acknowledged the notice. On appearance, the complainant did not attend and did not participate in the pre-hearing meeting through to its close, and appointed no authorised representative; "appearing before the panel" requires both presence at the meeting and participation in it. On excuse, the Khmer Free Workers' Federation, the party that had brought the complaint, offered no valid reason, the Khmer Samaki Workers' Federation having merely withdrawn its points. All three conditions were therefore met. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 56][Para 58][Para 60][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]

The panel accordingly closed the arbitration and issued an award terminating the proceeding, making no finding on any demand. [Para 68][Para 70]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been properly notified of the date, time and place of the arbitration, fails to appear, and gives no valid reason, the panel may either proceed in that party's absence or close the arbitration by issuing an award; closing is lawful once those three conditions are met, and a complainant federation that neither attends nor authorises a representative in writing cannot keep the case alive merely by asserting that it is negotiating with the employer. [Para 41][Para 46][Para 56][Para 65][Para 68]

**Obiter dicta:** A party may appear in person, with an accompanying person, or through a lawyer or another person authorised in writing [Para 57]; the panel stressed that the three conditions must be verified rather than assumed, which is why it examined the summons letters and the telephone notices individually [Para 47][Para 52].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The header carries a garbled OCR string before the case number [Para 1]. The letter from the company is dated "២០២៣ ២០១៥" in the same sentence [Para 23], and the paragraph recording the telephone report names "ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា ២ អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល", an unreadable reference [Para 25]. The strike figures are irreconcilable on the face of the award: 47 workers are said to have struck from 23 June 2015 [Para 23], while about 2,000 were reported still striking on 30 June 2015 [Para 25]. The long list of prior awards is badly corrupted, with fragments such as "វើ៣", "១៣២០៨" and "០៩ នីម បឹស ថិច ថាយ៍" [Para 49]. The employer-appointed arbitrator's colleague is printed as "អន ណន" in the header and "អូន ណន" in the signature block [Para 1][Para 71], and stray figures "(201629" appear beside the president's signature [Para 71]. Because no hearing was held, the award records no evidence at all beyond the summons letters and the two federation letters [Para 37].
