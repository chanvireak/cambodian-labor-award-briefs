# Award No. 112/15 — Sun An Fang Garment Factory (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 10 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chiv Phirom, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Aing Eng Phong, president) · Doc ID 139405

## 1. Facts
The employer, Sun An Fang Garment Factory (ស្យ៊ូ អាន ហ្វាង ហ្គាម៉ិន ហ្វេក ឆើរី), sews garments and sportswear with about 580 workers. [Para 34][Para 35] Three workers — the president, vice-president and secretary elected to lead the factory's union on 12 February 2015 — were dismissed together on 28 February 2015, sixteen days after the ballot, and told at 17:40 that day that they were being let go because they did union work. [Para 36][Para 41][Para 44][Para 85] They had no disciplinary record and no warning, and the employer never gave them a written contract. [Para 45][Para 62] They claim reinstatement and back wages from dismissal to return.

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the three workers' contracts were undetermined-duration contracts rather than fixed-duration contracts with no fixed term? [Para 60]
2. Whether the three workers, as candidates elected to union leadership, were entitled to protection against dismissal, so that the employer needed prior authorisation from the labour inspector? [Para 70]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** All three served a two-month probation, were then kept on normally, and were never given the written contract the law requires; they were dismissed without cause and in discrimination against union membership. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 53]
**Employer (respondent):** The dismissals were ordered because the employer did not want several unions in the factory, multiple unions making management complicated; the employer conceded it had given written contracts to women workers but not to these men, and never produced the contracts it promised to file. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50][Para 52][Para 64]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to take the three workers back into normal work and to calculate back wages from the date of dismissal until the date they return; no sum in riel or USD is printed [Para 104][Para 105]

On issue 1 the panel applied Article 67(1) and (7) of the Labour Law: a fixed-duration contract must state a precise end date, and must be in writing; an oral or unwritten fixed-term engagement becomes a contract of undetermined duration. [Para 65][Para 67][Para 68] Since the employer admitted it never issued written contracts and filed none, the panel held all three to hold undetermined-duration contracts. [Para 63][Para 69]

On issue 2 the panel applied Article 293 of the Labour Law and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 (22 November 2001), which extends to election candidates the same protection against dismissal as staff delegates. [Para 70][Para 73] Protection runs 45 days before the ballot and 45 days after it for candidates who lose; for candidates who win, the Prakas fixes no expiry. [Para 74][Para 77] Following Award No. 40/10 (Meng Yan), the panel held a winning candidate must receive at least the 45 days after the ballot, or a reasonable period continuing until the union registration application is filed. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80] It then applied the three-condition test drawn from Awards 50/05 (Flown), 64/05 (Jean Vay), 07/06 (Da Yang), 09/06 (Grand Diamond City) and 148/07 (Bay Heng): the worker must fall under Prakas 305, the dismissal must fall inside the protected period, and the employer must have been notified. [Para 81][Para 82] Condition 1 was met — all three were elected leaders. [Para 83] Condition 2 was met — dismissal came only 16 days after the 12 February ballot. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 87] Condition 3 was met — the member list and the notice of elected leadership were handed to administration staff on 28 February, and the notice carried a signed receipt dated 17 February, so the employer knew before it acted. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 94] No prior authorisation was sought from the labour inspector. [Para 101][Para 102] Under Article 4 of Prakas 305, measures taken in breach are null and void, and Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 empowers the panel to order reinstatement and payment. [Para 96][Para 103]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that keeps a worker on after probation without issuing a written fixed-duration contract cannot later treat the engagement as fixed-term; the contract is of undetermined duration. [Para 66][Para 69] And an employer that dismisses a worker who has been elected to union leadership, inside the protected window and after receiving notice of the candidacy, without prior labour inspector authorisation, must reinstate the worker with back wages from dismissal to return, because the dismissal is void. [Para 96][Para 100][Para 101]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that Prakas 305 sets no fixed expiry for the protection of a candidate who wins, so the protection cannot run forever but lasts at least 45 days past the ballot or a reasonable period until registration is applied for. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 79] It also noted the employer had dismissed other union leaders in the past, which it treated as context rather than as a separate ground. [Para 49]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's own numbering is inconsistent: the procedural narrative records one non-conciliated issue and "01" issue for decision, while an earlier passage refers to "4 issues in dispute" (៤ ចំណុច វិវាទ), and the reasons then decide two sub-issues lettered (a) and (b). [Para 14][Para 12][Para 15][Para 60][Para 70] The letterhead is badly OCR-damaged, carrying stray Latin fragments, a broken email address and the year "2017" although the award is dated 2015. [Para 2] The Prakas No. 099 date is printed as "21 April 2019 2004" in the reasons, and the signature block is corrupted with Latin noise and a stray "273". [Para 103][Para 105][Para 107] Each of the three workers is rendered in three or more different spellings across the document, and one administrative employee who signed the receipt is named only in the signature line with no stated role, so the workers are designated Ms R, Mr M and Mr D here. [Para 37][Para 41][Para 90][Para 105] No money amount is printed for the back wages, and the union's own name is spelled three ways.
