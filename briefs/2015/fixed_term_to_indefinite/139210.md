# Award No. 138/15 — Double U and D (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 22 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Suthy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Run Saray (president) · Doc ID 139210

## 1. Facts

Workers at ដាប់ប៊ិលយូ អែន ឌី (ខេមបូឌា) — Double U and D (Cambodia), a garment-type enterprise in Meanchey district, Phnom Penh — filed seven demands for improved working conditions through the Federation of Trade Unions of Workers (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត). [Para 1][Para 14] Conciliation on 4 June 2015 left all seven unresolved, and the file reached the Council on 11 June 2015. [Para 15][Para 16] The workers struck from 9 June 2015; on 11 June the Council issued order No. 020/15 K BA requiring the strike to stop immediately and all strikers to return to work from the morning of 13 June 2015. [Para 19][Para 20] The strike continued. [Para 21] The panel was constituted on 12 June 2015 and never held a hearing. [Para 17][Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company must stop deducting pay when workers arrive up to ten minutes late? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company may deduct the regular attendance bonus when a worker takes leave for a legitimate reason? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must deal with leave requests without issuing a warning to the worker? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must read the internal regulations aloud once a week, each Saturday, and play music for workers every day? [Para 6]
5. Whether the company must apply the special leave provisions of the Labour Law? [Para 7]
6. Whether the company must allow a woman with a young child one hour per day to breastfeed? [Para 8]
7. Whether the company must refrain from deducting pay from a sick worker who holds a medical certificate? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The workers' side filed no documents and did not put its case to the panel; its contentions survive only as the seven demands recorded in the conciliation report, each framed as an entitlement under the Labour Law or a previous award. [Para 24][Para 14]

**Employer (respondent):** The company wrote on 11 June 2015 asking the Council to order the strike suspended, and submitted its own file on the dispute; it did not address the seven demands on their merits either. [Para 19][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការ អាជ្ញាកណ្តាល) in Case No. 138/15 without ruling on any of the seven claims; no money was ordered. [Para 44][Para 45]

The panel put the threshold question to itself: whether the arbitration could continue at all. [Para 28] It read article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 as obliging the parties, throughout the proceedings, to suspend every contentious measure — strike, lockout (ឡុកអៅ) or anything else that could aggravate the dispute — and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 28][Para 29] That reading, it said, had been applied in its earlier awards, which it listed by number. [Para 31][Para 32]

It then recalled its own prior practice in more detail: in Case No. 04/04-IMS it had notified the parties on 29 January 2004 that while a party has no right to strike during its arbitration, the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on the dispute; in Case No. 18/04 (the hotel cases) its second order of 22 April 2004 required all strikers to stop and reserved the right to close the proceedings if any party breached the order. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 36] In Case No. 77/05-Franco Nitting it declined to process the dispute while the strike went on, and in Cases Nos 110/11-Simano, 17/13-Internal Fashion-Royal and 066/14-Calisto Apparel (Cambodia) it terminated the arbitration for exactly this reason. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]

Applying that line, the panel found the facts undisputed: order No. 020/15 had directed the workers back to work from 13 June 2015, the Secretariat's calls on 15 June confirmed with both sides that the strike was still running, and the workers had not complied. [Para 20][Para 22][Para 42] Because a strike in progress is incompatible with the conduct of arbitration, the panel held it could neither hear the case nor decide the demands, and closed the file under article 20. [Para 43][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

While a lawful strike or lockout is in progress during arbitration proceedings, the panel will not hear the dispute on its merits; where its order to suspend the stoppage is disobeyed, the panel terminates the arbitration by award instead of adjudicating the claims, which remain undetermined and may be brought again once the stoppage ends. [Para 30][Para 34][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that a party has no right to strike during its own arbitration, and its reservation of a general power to close proceedings for breach of any order, were carried over from the earlier awards it quoted rather than decided afresh here. [Para 33][Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

Demand 2 is OCR-damaged and its heading is duplicated: it asks that the company "cut" the regular attendance bonus, which would be a demand against the workers' own interest, and the negative is probably lost; the issue is framed above as the deduction question the conciliation file supports. [Para 4] Paragraph 27 says the Labour Department sent the report "to 4" (ទៅ ៤), a figure with no referent. [Para 27] The conciliation is dated 4 June 2015 in the narrative but the report transmitting it is dated 11 June 2015, and the report number appears as 589 K/AK/VK in one place and 589 K.B/AK/VK in another. [Para 15][Para 12][Para 16] A stray year "2016" precedes the date 24 December 2005 in the Franco Nitting reference, and "2015" is embedded in the 2004 notification. [Para 37][Para 33] The employer's name is printed in three transliterations (ដាប់ប៊ិលយូ, ដាប់ ប៊ិល យូ, ដាប់ប៊ិល យូ) and the English form is not given. [Para 1][Para 12][Para 19]
