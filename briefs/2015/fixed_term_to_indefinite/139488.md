# Order No. 321/15 — Leadvine Soo (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 18 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer party; An Non, chosen by the worker party; Kong Phal, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139488

## 1. Facts
Leadvine Soo (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a factory at Toul Pongro, Choam Chao, faced a twelve-point collective claim from the Khmer Workers' Union Federation, received by the labour dispute department on 4 December 2015. [Para 1][Para 17] Conciliation of all twelve points failed on 7 December 2015 and the non-conciliation report No. 1549 was transmitted to the Council on 8 December 2015. [Para 18][Para 19] Workers had struck from 3 December 2015; two lawyers acting for the company asked that day for an order suspending the strike, and the Council issued order 040/15 on 8 December 2015 requiring the strike to stop at once and all workers to return to their shifts from the afternoon of 9 December 2015. [Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay a meal allowance of 2,000 riel for each overtime hour? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must stop deducting from workers living in its rented housing a sum paid to the housing committee? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must stop charging workers in that housing a motorbike parking fee? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company must supply soap, light bulbs and door keys for the bathrooms? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must keep paying the annual bonus as before? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 7]
7. Whether the company must reduce seniority from undetermined to fixed from one year's service upward? [Para 8]
8. Whether the company must open all the doors on the water line? [Para 9]
9. Whether the company must pay a travel allowance of USD 20? [Para 10]
10. Whether the company must pay a "prime" of USD 15? [Para 11]
11. Whether the company must stop deducting money from workers who do not join the union? [Para 12]
12. Whether the company must stop deducting money from workers during a strike? [Para 13]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the twelve demands on allowances, deductions and facilities stood as filed; the worker party submitted no documents to the Council at all [Para 25], and told the secretariat by telephone that the workers were still striking. [Para 22][Para 40]
**Employer (respondent):** the strike since 3 December 2015 was seriously disrupting its production chain, so the Council should order it suspended [Para 20]; it reported on 16 December 2015 that the workers had not stopped, that they were letting no water truck in and no goods out, and that tyres had been burned. [Para 23][Para 41]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel ordered the termination of the arbitration in case No. 321/15 (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការអាជ្ញាកណ្តាល), deciding none of the twelve points. [Para 44][Para 45]

The panel took its power from Chapter 12, Section 2(b) (Articles 309 to 317) of the 1997 Labour Law, Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and the term-13 appointment Prakas No. 321 of 27 July 2015, and noted that conciliation had been attempted under Section 2(a) first. [Para 14][Para 15] Because it held no hearing, it made no finding of fact on any disputed point. [Para 28]

Its reasoning was confined to a single question of its own: whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 29] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure during the arbitration — strike or lock-out under Article 318, or anything else that might aggravate the dispute — and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 29][Para 30] The panel read that as meaning it will not proceed while the workers are striking or the employer has locked out, the reading it had adopted in earlier orders. [Para 31][Para 32]

It recited the line of authority: orders Nos. 59/06, 3908, 13709 and 13809, 103/10, 110/11, 104/13 (Sabrina (Cambodia)) and 019/14 (Dongdu Tech Stai (Cambodia)), and the notice of 29 January 2004 in case 04/04, which held that a party has no right to strike during the arbitration and that the Council may neither resolve the dispute nor issue an order on it, but may take it up again once all the union's workers stop striking. [Para 33][Para 34] It added the second orders of 22 April 2004 in case 18/04 and of 24 December 2005 in case 77/05, and its own order in case 040/15 (FY Cambodia Fashion Limited), each declining to proceed for the same reason, and expressed its agreement with that interpretation. [Para 35][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]

On the facts, the panel relied on what the parties told the secretariat: the workers' side confirmed by telephone that the strike went on, and the employer confirmed by telephone and by letter of 16 December 2015 that it had not stopped, adding that no water truck was allowed in, no goods out, and that tyres had been burned. [Para 40][Para 41] It found the workers still on strike in disregard of its order, and so decided it could not hold a hearing; on that basis it did not summon the parties to a further pre-hearing meeting. [Para 42][Para 43] Applying Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, it closed the proceedings. [Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the workers keep striking after the panel has ordered the strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not hear the collective dispute: it terminates the arbitration instead of ruling on the merits, and the case may be brought again only once every worker of the union concerned has stopped striking. [Para 32][Para 34][Para 44]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's explanation that it had made no factual finding because no hearing was held, and that it therefore dispensed with a further pre-hearing meeting, are procedural remarks rather than part of the ground of the termination. [Para 28][Para 42]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The point numbering in section 2 runs to twelve, but the non-conciliation report is said to cover twelve points while the order records conciliation of twelve points at [Para 18] and non-conciliation of twelve at [Para 19]; no dispositive treatment of any point appears, so nothing suggests which were withdrawn.
- The prior orders are cited with inconsistent numbering: "លេខ ៥៩/០៦" at [Para 33] corresponds to "5806" in the parallel order 319/15, and case 040/15 appears both as a precedent and as the number of this employer's strike order [Para 21][Para 38].
- Dates clash: the strike is dated from 3 December 2015 [Para 20] but the company's report of continued strike is dated 16 December 2015 and the order 18 December 2015 [Para 1][Para 24]; the employer's letter is described as received both by telephone and by letter on the same day [Para 23].
- OCR loss is severe: the seal line is reduced to "=-=>@$68" [Para 1]; page numbers and stamps intrude mid-sentence ("-៣-", "-៥-", "2016 .", "mino 2009 , 2007") at [Para 26], [Para 46]; the worker party's representative name is broken by stray brackets at [Para 1]; and the signature block gives only "បន្ទាប់ ខាង 2016" for the worker-party arbitrator's name, which survives from the header alone.
- The brief refers to individual union officers and the company's lawyer by role only; the company name is printed "លីដវីន ស៊ូ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី" throughout but "លីដវីន ស៊ូ" alone in the operative order [Para 45].
