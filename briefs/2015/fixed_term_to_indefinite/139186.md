# Award No. 095/15 — KTC Cable Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 May 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Uk Ri appointed by the employer, An Non appointed by the workers, Pen Bunhea chair) · Doc ID 139186

## 1. Facts
KTC Cable Co., Ltd manufactures electrical wire, cable and equipment; it was registered on 22 August 2005 and had 65 workers at the hearing. [Para 48][Para 49] The company's production line had not operated since July 2014, and it was selling its land and buildings. [Para 55][Para 62][Para 143] Forty-three workers and 13 staff — 56 claimants — transferred their rights to the Khmer Workers' Trade Union Federation. [Para 50][Para 54] Of twelve demands, eight were withdrawn at the hearing, leaving issues 9, 10, 11 and 12; both parties elected a non-binding award. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay statutory severance if the company does not resume operations, and, if it does resume, provide production-line work as before? [Para 55][Para 108]
2. Whether the employer must maintain wages and other benefits during the strike of 07 to 27 April 2015? [Para 152]
3. Whether the three dismissed workers are entitled to severance, and what type of employment contract governed them? [Para 163]
4. Whether the employer must pay the 13th-month bonus to staff in May 2015, having failed to pay it on 10 February as in previous years? [Para 221][Para 235]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer has given no production-line work for nearly a year, has reassigned them to skills they do not have — stripping cable for copper scrap, cleaning, guarding, gardening and washing jars in a pond — pays only base wages, and stopped Saturday work without paying for it; the three dismissed workers were sacked for bathing in a nearby pond on a hot afternoon and should receive severance; and staff have always received the 13th-month bonus on 10 February each year. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 102][Para 104]
**Employer (respondent):** the company has not closed and continues to pay monthly wages; it faces financial difficulty, is selling its property as strategy, and will restart production when it can; the three were dismissed for serious misconduct under Article 83 (theft of working time) and will not be reinstated; and the 13th-month bonus for staff will be paid in October 2015. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 67][Para 94][Para 107]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the parties to negotiate in good faith within 02 months over the lack of production-line work, failing which the workers may claim statutory severance; dismissed the strike-wage demand; ordered severance for the three dismissed workers (notice pay under Article 75, dismissal indemnity under Article 89, unused annual leave, moral damages under Article 91 and any unpaid final wages); and ordered payment of the 13th-month bonus to staff not yet paid in May 2015. [Para 248][Para 249][Para 250][Para 251][Para 252]

**Issue 9 (production line and Saturday work).** Article 2 lets the employer organise and manage the enterprise, including transfers, provided the change is lawful and reasonable — no wage cut, no distant relocation, no day/night shift swap and no transfer far outside the worker's skill, following Awards 17/03, 18/03 and 390/07. [Para 110][Para 113][Para 126] Cutting Saturday work and Saturday wages was a unilateral variation of a contractual term (clause 3: Monday to Saturday, 48 hours), made without the workers' agreement, and was therefore unlawful. [Para 122][Para 123][Para 124][Para 125] The reassignment to unskilled tasks was also unreasonable: it degraded workers' skills and exposed them to losing them. [Para 133][Para 136][Para 139] Because the employer could not say when production would restart, the panel declined to order resumption, but under Article 34(ង) of Prakas No. 099 ordered good-faith negotiation within two months, after which the workers may claim severance. [Para 144][Para 147][Para 148]

**Issue 10 (wages during strike).** Articles 332(1) and 72(1) suspend the contract during a strike and relieve the employer of wages; the panel followed Award 27/10 in holding this applies whether or not the strike was lawful, and dismissed the demand. [Para 152][Para 153][Para 155][Para 157][Para 162]

**Issue 11 (the three dismissed workers).** Article 67(2) caps a fixed-duration contract at two years in total, and successive renewals beyond that convert it into an indefinite-duration contract. [Para 163][Para 164][Para 165][Para 167][Para 174] All three had served more than two years by the time of dismissal. [Para 175][Para 176][Para 177] Article 83 serious misconduct means theft, misappropriation or embezzlement, not using working time to bathe; the fault was minor and dismissal was disproportionate under Article 27, following Awards 134/09, 33/06 and 134/08. [Para 184][Para 188][Para 191][Para 194][Para 195] Since the employer refused reinstatement, the panel awarded notice pay, dismissal indemnity, unused annual leave, moral damages and final wages. [Para 198][Para 204][Para 209][Para 213][Para 217]

**Issue 12 (13th-month bonus).** Past practice is a legal concept under Article 65(2)–(3) requiring a practice that is regular, clear, stable and accepted by both sides; here the bonus was paid on 10 February every year. [Para 224][Para 228][Para 230][Para 235] A past practice can end only by agreement, negotiation or a change of circumstances, and the employer showed none — the workers' claim itself showed they did not accept the October deferral. [Para 236][Para 243][Para 244][Para 245] The employer was therefore ordered to pay the bonus to staff in May 2015. [Para 246][Para 247]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may transfer workers under Article 2 only lawfully and reasonably; removing a contractual working day and its wages, or moving production workers long-term to unskilled tasks outside their trade, is an unlawful unilateral variation even where the factory's production line has stopped. [Para 124][Para 133][Para 139]
Severance is owed to a worker under two years of successive fixed-duration renewals only as an indefinite-duration contract; that conversion happens by operation of Article 67(2) once total duration exceeds two years. [Para 165][Para 174][Para 177]
Using working time to bathe is not theft under Article 83, and dismissal for it is disproportionate under Article 27, entitling the worker to notice pay, dismissal indemnity, leave pay and moral damages. [Para 188][Para 195]
A regularly paid 13th-month bonus is an enforceable past practice that survives until ended by agreement or changed circumstances. [Para 235][Para 236][Para 245]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the company's own statutes (Article 14) would require dissolution if losses reached half of capital with no recovery within a year was not converted into an order. [Para 149][Para 151]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The strike is dated "07 April 2019 2015" [Para 160], and the company statutes "30 December 2019 2010" [Para 149] against "30 December 2010" at [Para 75]; service entry for one worker reads "24 June 202 2010" [Para 175].
- The order is addressed to "4 workers" while naming 03 [Para 220], and elsewhere to "both 03 workers" [Para 216].
- The second worker's name appears as Roeun Doeun, Roeun Kheun and Hun Roeun Doeun across the award [Para 88][Para 175][Para 203].
- Issue 9's reasoning covers both the Saturday-wage question and the reassignment, but the panel's own statement of the issue at [Para 110] splits them into (1) and (2) without numbering in the decision.
