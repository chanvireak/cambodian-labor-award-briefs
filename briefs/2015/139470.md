# Award No. 293/15 — San Feng (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 17 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bunchea presiding; Mar Sambor Na appointed by the employer, Liv Sovanna appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 139470

## 1. Facts
San Feng (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. makes curtains in the Manhattan Special Economic Zone at Bavet, Svay Rieng, and employs about 1,664 workers. [Para 60][Para 61] After a strike in September 2015, the Khmer Youth Voice Trade Union Federation took up the cases of nine workers; three demands went to conciliation on 12 November 2015 and failed. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 62] Only seven workers attended the hearing of 27 November 2015, and the panel treated demands 1 and 2 as a single issue. [Para 15][Para 17][Para 63] The union held its leadership election on 2 September 2015, and the employer ended the contracts of seven workers between 21 September and 19 October 2015, some before the term of their three-month contracts expired. [Para 113][Para 86][Para 187]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate the seven union leaders and pay their wages and benefits from the date of dismissal, because the dismissals were unlawful or anti-union? [Para 63][Para 168]
2. Whether the dismissals were anti-union discrimination contrary to Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law? [Para 188]
3. Whether the employer must stop terminating the contracts of night-shift workers during the night? [Para 290]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the seven were dismissed without valid reason, out of anti-union discrimination and without the procedure the Labour Law requires; they should return to work with back pay [Para 66][Para 165]. They also said the strike was spontaneous, provoked by a USD 10 incentive paid to workers who stayed out, and that their president had tried to calm it [Para 74][Para 75]. Dismissals at night leave them with no way home and endanger them [Para 142][Para 298].
**Employer (respondent):** the president was dismissed on 6 October 2015 for serious misconduct under Article 83 — inciting the strike, telling workers they would lose no pay, letting off smoke that panicked the workforce, forcing entry and throwing stones [Para 64][Para 68][Para 69][Para 71]; the other six simply were not renewed at the end of fixed-term contracts, with notice longer than required, and were paid [Para 77][Para 78]. It denied discrimination and said night notice follows the shift the worker actually works [Para 67][Para 301].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must reinstate the union president and one elected candidate (Mr T and Mr V) with back wages and benefits, and pay the worker Mr M severance, final wages, unused annual leave and damages; reinstatement for four others and the ban on night dismissals are refused. [Para 312][Para 313][Para 314][Para 315]
First the panel fixed the parties: only the seven workers present at the hearing were claimants, and one worker who had not validly transferred rights and did not appear was excluded. [Para 166][Para 167]
On the nature of the terminations, Article 73(1) lets a fixed-duration contract end at its term and be dissolved early only by written agreement before the labour inspector or for serious misconduct. [Para 168][Para 170] For five workers the employer ended the contract 12 days or more before expiry, so the panel held this was dismissal, not non-renewal. [Para 187][Para 268]
On discrimination, Articles 12 and 279 forbid an employer to use union membership or activity as the ground for discipline or termination, but the party alleging it bears the burden; the union produced no evidence that union reasons drove the dismissals. [Para 188][Para 189][Para 192][Para 197][Para 199]
On protected status, Article 293 and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 protect election candidates for 45 days before and 45 days after the vote, and require the labour inspector's prior approval before dismissal. [Para 200][Para 203][Para 254] Three workers did not prove they were candidates, so the first condition failed for them. [Para 214][Para 215][Para 227] The president, elected on 2 September 2015, was dismissed 34 days later and a candidate 36 days later, both inside the window; no approval was sought, so those dismissals were void. [Para 231][Para 233][Para 253][Para 260]
For the four outside protection, Article 73(3) allows the employer to end a fixed-term contract early but requires damages at least equal to wages to the end of the term; three had been paid, and the panel ordered the remaining sums for Mr M. [Para 263][Para 264][Para 273][Para 286]
On night dismissals, Article 65(1) leaves contract terms to the parties; a night-shift worker's working time is the night, so notice given then is lawful, and dismissing such a worker by day would be unreasonable. [Para 290][Para 292][Para 305][Para 307] The panel nevertheless endorsed the employer's undertaking to let dismissed night-shift workers stay on the premises until morning. [Para 310]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that ends a fixed-term contract before its expiry has dismissed the worker, not declined to renew; where that worker is a union election candidate still inside the 45-day post-election protection window, the dismissal is void unless the labour inspector approved it in advance. [Para 187][Para 260]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel accepted that notice of non-renewal may lawfully be given during the night shift, because that is when the worker's contract is performed [Para 307]; and it encouraged the employer to keep housing dismissed night-shift staff until morning so they are not stranded [Para 308][Para 310].

[Para 13][Para 20][Para 272][Para 261][Para 257] The last contract of one worker is dated "31 December October 2015" and others "22 31 October 2015". [Para 62][Para 63][Para 167] The seven claimants are also enumerated inconsistently — nine names in the transfer letter, seven at para 63, six in the operative orders — and each worker's name is OCR-unstable across the award, so individual names are redacted here and only the office held is given.
