# Award No. 045/15 and 057/15 — Quantum Clothing (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 3 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambor Na, appointed by the employer; An Vireak, appointed by the workers; Aing Eng Thong, chair) · Doc ID 139146

## 1. Facts
Two collective disputes between the same parties were heard together: No. 045/15 on working conditions for night-shift sewers, and No. 057/15 on wages and the attendance bonus during the strike. [Para 1][Para 17] Conciliation on the two demands of 045/15 failed on 04 February 2015 and the report reached the secretariat on 10 February 2015; a second report of 20 February 2015 added the strike-related demand. [Para 10][Para 13][Para 15][Para 16] Workers had struck from before the case was filed, and the panel issued order No. 007/15 on 20 February 2015 requiring the strike to stop and all workers back to their shifts from the morning of 23 February 2015. [Para 18][Para 19] The strike continued. [Para 20][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
**Case No. 045/15:**
1. Whether the company must first pay compensation to night-shift workers before moving them to day-shift work? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must top up wages to the base wage where a sewer's output falls short of the quota (ដេរ ស៊ីបុង)? [Para 4]

**Case No. 057/15:**
3. Whether the company must keep wages and the regular attendance bonus unchanged during the strike period? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Night-shift workers transferred to the day shift should be paid the various compensations in advance of the transfer; where a sewer cannot meet the output quota the company must top up to the base wage; and wages plus the attendance bonus must be maintained for the strike period. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6]

**Employer (respondent):** If a night-shift worker asked to move to the day shift wishes to return to nights, the company agrees to place them in the sewing building, paying a 3-month average and training those who lack the skill. [Para 3] It already tops up to the minimum base wage when output falls short, [Para 5] and does not maintain wages or the attendance bonus during a strike, applying the law. [Para 7]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration in both case files without ruling on any of the three demands. [Para 54][Para 55]

The panel framed the only question it decided as whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 39] It applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 (21 April 2004), which requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures — strikes or lockouts, and any other measure aggravating the dispute — while the panel's proceedings run, and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 39][Para 40] Following its own earlier awards, it read that provision as barring it from continuing an arbitration while workers are striking or an employer is locking out. [Para 41][Para 42]

It noted that in case No. 04/04 — IMSI it had held that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during the panel's proceedings, and that it could not settle or issue an award on the collective dispute referred to it, though it could take the dispute up again once the strike ended. [Para 43][Para 44] It also followed the second order of 22 April 2004 in case No. 18/04 — Hotels, whose points 9 and 11 required the strike to stop and reserved the panel's right to terminate proceedings where a party breached the order. [Para 45][Para 46] Several later awards had terminated arbitration on the same ground. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]

On the facts, order No. 007/15 of 20 February 2015 had directed the workers to stop striking and return on 23 February 2015, and at the pre-hearing meeting of 25 February 2015 both parties confirmed the strike was still running. [Para 51][Para 52] Before that meeting the panel had also appointed arbitrator Livo Sovanna to conciliate in an attempt to end the strike, and the conciliation session of 27 February 2015 failed. [Para 23][Para 24] The panel therefore found the strike ongoing in defiance of its order and held it could not proceed to hearings. [Para 53] No hearing on the merits was held and no findings of fact were made on the three demands. [Para 38]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers keep a strike running after the panel has ordered its suspension under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of hearing the collective demands, and will not decide them on the merits. [Para 41][Para 53][Para 54]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the two case files were linked because the parties were identical, which is why they were consolidated into one award. [Para 17] It also recorded that it could take up a collective dispute again once the strike ended, an observation that did not affect this outcome. [Para 44]
