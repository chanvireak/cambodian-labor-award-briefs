# Award No. 255/15 — Great On Near Tech Style Factory Ltd (Arbitration Council, 28 October 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Ing Sothy, employer nominee; An Non, worker nominee) · Doc ID 139437

## 1. Facts
Great On Near Tech Style Factory Ltd cuts and sews garments at Svay Rolum, Sa'ang, Kandal, employing about 1,300 workers, and had previously traded as Vin Near. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 183] The Cambodia Workers' Union Federation complained on 25 August 2015 over working conditions, and conciliation failed on seven of eight points, which reached the Secretariat on 15 September 2015. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 23] The local union was registered while the factory still bore the old name and had no registration certificate under the new one, and the authorisation letters it filed carried names and fingerprints for only three workers, the other fifty-five being identified by fingerprint and employee number alone. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 66][Para 109] Only three workers attended the hearing, and the panel treated them alone as claimants. [Para 65][Para 124]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must continue paying the team-leader role allowance to the five team leaders from whom it stopped the payment in April 2015? [Para 2][Para 67]
3. Whether the employer must add a further 50 per cent, making 100 per cent of wages, for the days in April to September 2015 on which it suspended work in the weaving section without the labour inspector's authorisation? [Para 86][Para 159]
6. Whether the employer must supply at least two sets of work clothing a year to every worker, and not only to those in electrical, laundry, boiler and chemical sections? [Para 12][Para 174]
7. Whether the employer must pay a round of severance on the change of company name from Vin Near to Great On Near? [Para 101][Para 184]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 18, 23 and 30 dollars a month paid to the team leaders since 1999 or 2000 was a role allowance, not a merit bonus, and the employer cut it in April 2015 without notice, reason or consultation; the suspension of weaving-section work without inspector approval entitles them to full wages; and Prakas 307 requires two sets of clothing for every worker. [Para 68][Para 70][Para 86][Para 96][Para 97]
**Employer (respondent):** the sums were a good-work incentive that it may grant and withdraw as productivity allows, and the role allowance for machine checking was never cut; orders have fallen and weaving has no full-year work; it concedes it never sought the inspector's approval to suspend; and clothing is issued only where safety demands it. [Para 71][Para 73][Para 75][Para 92][Para 93][Para 99]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the team-leader allowance restored to the three claimants, the additional 50 per cent paid for every suspended day from April to September 2015, and two sets of work clothing a year issued to all workers, while dismissing issue 7 as already decided. [Para 205][Para 206][Para 207][Para 208]
On issue 1 the panel began from the burden of proof and found the workers had shown a payment that had been made unchanged for fifteen years to workers holding the team-leader post, whereas the employer's description of it as a discretionary merit bonus was unsupported. [Para 128][Para 131][Para 135][Para 138] Applying the doctrine of past practice under Article 65(2) and (3) — a consistent, long-standing practice recognised and accepted by both sides — it held the allowance part of the wage, and since the employer ended it without prior consultation it was not entitled to do so. [Para 143][Para 144][Para 150][Para 156][Para 157] The same doctrine had been applied in Award 95/09 Tak Fhat and elsewhere. [Para 145][Para 151]
On issue 3 the panel applied Article 71(11), which permits suspension of enterprise activity only for serious economic or material difficulty, for no more than two months and under the labour inspector's supervision, and Article 72(1) on suspension of the contract. [Para 160][Para 163][Para 164] Following Award 58/07 Star Skutwear, it held that a suspension properly notified to the inspector relieves the employer of wages, but that a suspension carried out without that step obliges it to pay 100 per cent even though no work was given; the employer had admitted it never applied. [Para 165][Para 166][Para 172][Para 173][Para 174]
On issue 6 the panel read Article 6 of Prakas 307 of 14 December 2007 as requiring two sets a year, free, for every worker, the first within fifteen days of engagement; an established practice of supplying only hazardous sections does not comply, and it followed Award 017/15 YEN PAN ROCK S.E.I. [Para 175][Para 177][Para 178][Para 182][Para 183]
On issue 7 the panel applied res judicata: the parties had litigated the same demand in Award 088/15 of 7 May 2015, which refused severance on the name change, and all three conditions — same parties, same issue, full decision — were met, so it declined to decide the point again. [Para 186][Para 192][Para 196][Para 198][Para 200][Para 202]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A payment made consistently over many years to workers holding a particular post, recognised and accepted by both sides, becomes a past practice that forms part of the wage and cannot be withdrawn unilaterally: the employer must negotiate its ending, and the party asserting that the payment was a discretionary bonus bears the burden of proving it. [Para 138][Para 144][Para 156] Where an employer suspends work for lack of orders without placing the suspension under the labour inspector's supervision, it must pay full wages for the suspended days even though no work was performed. [Para 163][Para 166] Prakas 307 obliges an employer to issue two sets of clothing a year to all workers, not merely to those in the most hazardous sections. [Para 175][Para 182]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel distinguished practices that are not past practice at all — those dictated by the employer's own control of the production line, such as work schedules — a distinction the outcome did not turn on. [Para 146] It also observed that a properly authorised suspension of under two months would have extinguished the wage claim, without deciding whether this one qualified. [Para 165]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel is constituted on "17 December September 2015" and the hearing is dated 29 September 2015. [Para 20][Para 24] The chair is "Pen Bun Chheam" in the header and "Pen Bun Chhea" at the signature block, and the worker nominee appears as "An Non" in the header and "An Lean" at signature. [Para 1][Para 210] The employer's name varies across the file — "Great On Near Tech Style Factory", "Fet On Near" and "Great On Near Tech Style" — and the former name appears as both "Vin Near" and "Vin Near Night Thing Factory". [Para 59][Para 106][Para 200] The date on which the allowance was cut is printed "April 2019 2015". [Para 155] Claimant numbers conflict: 58 authorisations, 55 without names, 3 named, 5 team leaders claimed for but 3 before the panel, and 65 weavers said to be affected. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 88][Para 109] The award date is extended from 8 to 28 October 2015. [Para 32] Individual worker, union and employer-representative names are not reproduced here.
