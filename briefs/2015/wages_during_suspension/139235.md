# Award No. 161/15 — Chinese Government Team for Safeguarding Angkor (CSA) (Arbitration Council, 22 July 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Suonty, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Ang Eng Thong, chair) · non-binding award · unofficial English translation · Doc ID 139235

## 1. Facts
CSA restored the Takeo temple in Siem Reap under a project running in phases from 2011 to 2018 and employed about 61 workers, of whom 31 were found to have authorised BWTUC by letter dated 20 April 2015 to act for them. The workers' latest contracts were six-month fixed terms running from 1 April 2014 to 30 September 2014, the closing date of Phase 1, at which point they were paid their final month's wages and a USD 50 encouragement bonus. The employer did not appear at the hearing, so the panel proceeded by default and issued a non-binding award. [Para 15][Para 18][Para 20][Para 21][Para 44]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must stop suspending the workers' employment contracts and permit them to return to work? [Para 5]
2. Whether the employer must pay full wages for the period of an allegedly unlawful work suspension? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the Takeo project is unfinished — two Chinese staff still occupy the main office and an office at the temple, materials remain on site, and the completion notice the workers received bore no date; a suspension is lawful only if the employer gives proper notice and pays 50 per cent of wages, and this employer gave no adequate notice, breaching article 83 of the Labour Law. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 34][Para 35]

**Employer (respondent):** the employer was absent and offered no submission at the hearing; its position on the record is that if Phase 2 proceeds it will notify workers of the continuity of their contracts and will cooperate with the Apsara Authority on recruitment complying with the law, and that it was a contractor rather than the employer for wage purposes. [Para 6][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both demands were rejected, and no sum was ordered in riel or USD. [Para 59][Para 62]

On the threshold question the panel applied clause 21 of Prakas No. 99 of 21 April 2004, which allows it to proceed where a duly invited party fails to appear without good cause, following earlier awards against absent respondents, and conducted a default hearing on the workers' evidence and statements. Because the employer was absent, the award is non-binding. [Para 39][Para 41][Para 44][Para 13]

On issue 1 the panel set out article 71 of the Labour Law: suspension of the contract arises where the enterprise faces serious economic or material difficulty, may not exceed two months, and operates under the control of the Labour Inspector. Following Award No. 58/07 (8 Star Sportswear) and the line of authority requiring prior notification to and authorisation from the Labour Inspector (72/05 North Gaiety Apparel, 22/05 Ocean Garment, 198/13 Soo Apparel Garment, 160/14 Ocean Garment), it accepted that interpretation. It then held that the facts did not disclose a suspension at all: the fixed-term contracts of six months expired on 30 September 2014, the completion date of Phase 1, and the notification submitted by the Siem Reap Labour and Vocational Department carried a clear date, so the workers' assertion that the notice was undated was not realistic. The non-renewal was therefore the natural end of fixed-term employment, not a suspension, and the suspension provisions did not apply. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 54][Para 57][Para 58]

On issue 2 the panel read article 72(1): suspension affects only the main obligations of the contract, so an employer generally owes no wages to a worker who does not work — but that article operates only where the suspension itself satisfied article 71. Since it had found no suspension, the wage regime for suspension could not be invoked and only the provisions on termination applied. The demand for full wages therefore failed. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a fixed-term contract expires on the completion of a defined project phase, the resulting separation is a natural termination and not a work suspension, so the article 71 conditions and the article 72 rule that no wages are owed during suspension never come into play; and in deciding whether a completion notice specified a date, a panel will prefer the copy filed with the provincial labour department over the copy the workers say they received. [Para 52][Para 55][Para 58][Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's endorsement of the prior-award rule that a lawful suspension requires notification to and authorisation from the Labour Inspector before it is imposed was not necessary to the outcome, since it held that no suspension had occurred. [Para 47][Para 48]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Paragraph 12 records that both parties were present at the hearing, while paragraphs 13 and 39 state that the employer was absent; the absence is repeated throughout the reasoning and is plainly the operative fact. The completion and termination notification is dated 08 September 2014 at paragraph 28 and 08 September 2015 at paragraphs 51 and 55, a year apart. The non-conciliation report is numbered 043 and dated 24 June 2015 at paragraph 8 but 15 May 2015 at paragraph 11, the latter matching the conciliation date. Paragraph 14 sets the evidence-objection deadline at 07 July 2015, earlier than the 10 July 2015 hearing. The translation systematically merges the article into the following noun ("themployer", "thearing", "washared"), drops letters from "the" throughout, and renders "hearing" as "hearing" in some places and "aring" in others; paragraph 18 begins mid-sentence because the date at paragraph 17 was split across lines. The claim that 37 workers authorised BWTUC was reduced on the documentary evidence to 31, and the award nowhere reconciles the 31 claimants with the 61 workers said to be employed. [Para 8][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13][Para 14][Para 16][Para 18][Para 28][Para 51][Para 55]
