# Award No. 202/15 — Capitol (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 6 August 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Virak for the worker party, Kong Phalla chair) · Doc ID 139265

## 1. Facts

Drivers at Capitol (Cambodia) Ltd, a company at Street 182, Sangkat Boeung Prolit, Phnom Penh, filed 15 demands through the Cambodian Labour Confederation seeking improved working conditions; conciliation on 23 July 2015 left 3 of the 15 unresolved. [Para 1][Para 13][Para 14] The three surviving demands were a 10 per cent annual wage increase, reinstatement of five named drivers, and cash reimbursement of annual leave, Kan Ben festival days and Sundays from the date of hire to the present. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6] A strike began on 22 July 2015; the panel issued order 029/15 on 29 July 2015 requiring its immediate suspension and the return of all strikers from the morning of 30 July 2015. [Para 17][Para 18] The workers did not return, the Phnom Penh Municipal Court issued protective injunction 462 on 31 July 2015, and on 5 August 2015 both sides reported that the strikers were still outside and the employer would not take them back. [Para 19][Para 22][Para 29][Para 30]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company must raise wages by 10 per cent each year on top of the existing wage? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must take back and re-employ the five drivers it suspended and whose contracts it suspended? [Para 4][Para 23]
3. Whether the company must reimburse in money the annual leave, Kan Ben public holidays and Sundays accrued from each driver's date of hire to the present? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** all strikers intended to return to work without any condition and without filling in the letter templates the employer demanded, waiting outside the factory for the Council's process to run; they denied any refusal to obey the panel's order. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 57]

**Employer (respondent):** the company had no capacity to grant the 10 per cent increase, would not accept the drivers back, and would not reimburse the leave and holiday pay, applying the new practice only from 2015; the letter templates were an administrative formality only, and the company was still considering whether to take back drivers who had not respected the court's injunction. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 24][Para 25]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការ អាជ្ញាកណ្តាល) its own proceedings in case 202/15 without adjudicating any of the three demands, and recorded that it made no findings of fact on them because no hearing was held. [Para 45][Para 60]

Prakor (ប្រការ) 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to a dispute, while the panel's process is running, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lock-out under Article 318 of the Labour Law, or any other measure that could aggravate the dispute, and to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. [Para 46][Para 47] The panel read that provision as it had in earlier awards, holding that neither the Council nor an arbitrator will continue a process while workers are striking or an employer is locking out. [Para 48][Para 49]

The panel then walked through the earlier authority: case 04/04-IM SI, where the Council held that a party without the right to strike during the process could not have its dispute resolved or decided; case 18/04-Hotel, whose second order of 22 April 2004 required all workers to stop striking and to cease any disruption inside the hotel premises; case 77/05, where a continued strike meant the dispute would not be processed; case 110/11-Simano, where proceedings were terminated for that reason; and cases 17/13 and 066/14. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56]

On the facts the panel found the strikers had still not returned by 16:00 on 5 August 2015, and that the employer in turn refused to take them back — a stand-off on both sides which aggravated the dispute and made it impossible to continue the procedure or hold a hearing. [Para 58][Para 59] It therefore terminated the process under Prakor 20 of Prakas No. 099, leaving the demands undecided and open to be raised again once the strike ended. [Para 51][Para 60]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where a strike or lock-out continues after the panel has ordered its suspension, the panel has no competence to continue: Prakor 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the parties to suspend all dispute measures during the process, and a party that stays out forfeits the adjudication of its demands until the strike ends, at which point the dispute may be taken up again. The panel terminates rather than dismisses, so the merits of the demands — including wage increases and termination-related payments — remain undecided. [Para 46][Para 48][Para 51][Para 59][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the employer's requirement of up to four different letter templates before readmission was no more than the company's internal administrative procedure, and its remark that it could not determine who had failed to respect the court's injunction, were not necessary to the termination and were left unresolved. [Para 22][Para 24]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

- The terminating provision is printed as Prakas No. 099 in the reasoning [Para 46] but as "ប្រកាស លេខ ១០៩៩" in the order itself [Para 60] — the extra digit is an OCR artefact.
- Stray "2015" strings are embedded in quoted passages at [Para 50] and [Para 55], and "2019" appears nowhere; the year is inserted into text that has no year.
- The number of suspended drivers is inconsistent: five are named as those to be reinstated [Para 4] and five dismissal letters are listed [Para 39], yet [Para 23] refers to "អ្នកបើកបរ ទាំង ០៧ នាក់" (all 07 drivers) as suspended, and [Para 39] names five.
- The worker party's address block is printed twice, once for the union and once in the Council's own footer address, at [Para 1]–[Para 2].
- Names are damaged: "លោក ហ៊ុន ហ៊ិន មឿន" at [Para 4] carries an extra element against "លោក ហ៊ុន មឿន" at [Para 2]; the chair appears as "គង់ ផល្ទៈ" on the face [Para 1] and "គង់ ផល្លៈ" at signature [Para 61].
- The court's injunction number carries two different suffixes, "៤៦២ \"ល\" ភ.ព.ភា" [Para 36] and "៤៦២ \"៧\" ភ.ព.កា" [Para 37].
- Heavy OCR noise in the signature and stamp block ("AKP ponlocked", "mpwonthe Azzled25", "22242", "Lage", "HMPSS", "Strarion", "ADAND") [Para 30][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]; no signature date is legible, so only the 6 August 2015 header date is used.
- The employer's street address reads "អគារ លេខ ១៤ AEo" and the district "ខណ្ឌ ៧មក" — both corrupted [Para 1].
