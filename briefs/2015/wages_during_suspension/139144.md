# Award No. 038/15 — Smart Shirts Garments Manufacturing (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kuy Nam, chair) · Doc ID 139144

## 1. Facts
Some 800 workers at the company's Phnom Penh garment factory lodged 13 collective demands to improve working conditions — contract length, wage payment dates, a USD 28 wage increase, maternity and sick-leave entitlements, seniority bonus and welfare items. [Para 3][Para 7][Para 22] Conciliation by the Ministry's labour dispute department on 04 February 2015 failed on all 13 points, and report No. 115 dated 05 February 2015 reached the Council's secretariat on 06 February 2015. [Para 18][Para 23] A strike had begun on 03 February 2015; the panel issued order No. 005/15 on 06 February 2015 requiring its immediate suspension, and the strike continued afterwards. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The panel recorded 13 demands as the issues in dispute (ចំណុច វិវាទ):

1. Whether the company should convert 6-month employment contracts into 1-year contracts? [Para 3]
2. Whether pregnant workers should be allowed to leave 15 minutes early? [Para 4]
3. Whether wages should be paid between the 04th and the 10th of each month? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company should pay 5% severance plus the final month's wage on transfer or termination of the contract instead of holding it for 1 year? [Para 6]
5. Whether monthly wages should be raised by USD 28 on top of existing pay? [Para 7]
6. Whether the seniority bonus should be calculated by a set formula? [Para 8]
7. Whether wages should be paid on the day a worker resigns, as for ordinary workers? [Para 9]
8. Whether a lunch allowance of 2,000 riel should be provided? [Para 10]
9. Whether a worker on certified sick leave should keep wages, premium and other benefits unchanged? [Para 11]
10. Whether workers should be allowed to bring drinking water into the building? [Para 12]
11. Whether at least 3 warnings must precede termination of the contract? [Para 13]
12. Whether maternity leave of 90 days should be paid at 50% wage plus the allowance, totalling 90 days? [Para 14]
13. Whether the company should keep a permanent nurse, medicines and first-aid equipment during working hours? [Para 15]

In the event the panel did not reach these demands; it framed for decision only whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 47]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Through the two federations representing them, the workers pressed all 13 demands and, at the pre-hearing meeting of 11 February 2015, undertook to end the strike and return to work so that arbitration could proceed. [Para 31] On 13 February 2015 they stated that the employer had expelled workers from the company. [Para 59]

**Employer (respondent):** The employer admitted all workers back except 4, whom it feared would incite the others to strike, and undertook to put their reinstatement to the owner. [Para 33][Para 34] It asserted that more than 80% of workers had left the company of their own accord. [Para 60]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 038/15 without ruling on any of the 13 demands. [Para 65][Para 66]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council (21 April 2004), which requires parties to suspend all dispute measures such as strikes or lockouts, and any other measure aggravating the dispute, while the panel's proceedings are running, and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 47][Para 48] It read that provision, consistently with its own earlier awards, as meaning that it will not carry on an arbitration while workers are striking or an employer is locking out. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 55]

On the facts, order No. 005/15 of 06 February 2015 had directed an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the afternoon of 07 February 2015, and the strike nonetheless continued. [Para 27][Para 28] At the pre-hearing meeting the workers promised to return, but on 13 February they reported that the employer had expelled them, while the employer said more than 80% had left voluntarily. [Para 59][Para 60] The panel found the accounts irreconcilable but noted one common element: the workers were still outside the company. [Para 61][Para 62] It further reasoned that if 80% of the approximately 800-strong workforce were inside the factory, that number exceeded the 100-odd who first struck, which meant the situation had not calmed and its order had not been respected. [Para 63][Para 64] It therefore ended the proceedings; no hearing on the merits was held and no findings of fact were made on the 13 demands. [Para 46][Para 65]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party keeps a strike (or lockout) running after the panel has ordered its suspension under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, and the situation at the workplace has not calmed, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of proceeding to a merits award on the collective demands. [Para 47][Para 64][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the parties' conflicting accounts of why workers were outside the factory could not by themselves establish a strike or lockout, and that it could not conclude one way or the other on that question. [Para 64] It also noted that no witnesses or experts appeared and that it took no oral evidence. [Para 38][Para 46]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name is printed several ways across the document — "ស្មាតស៊ើតស៍ ហ្គាមេ នូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង" in the header, "ស្មាត ស៊ើតស៍ ហ្គាមេ នូហ្វេ ក ឆឹរីង (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី" in the body, and "ស្មាត ស៊ីតស៍" at Paras 26 and 38; the award is titled here by the English company name and briefed under it. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 26] The employer's request letter is dated 06 February 2014 while describing a strike of 03–06 February 2015, and is elsewhere dated 06 January 2015 — both dates appear as printed. [Para 26][Para 39] Paragraph 63 states the workforce at approximately 800, which sits awkwardly with the "more than 100" strikers and the 70%–80% return figures. [Para 32][Para 36][Para 63] Stray digits and OCR debris survive in the header ("07 @,@ 0"), in the pre-hearing address ("ផ្លូវ លេខ ឆ្នាំ ២០២ះ"), in the reference to prior awards ("ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល ៤០"), and the signature block is partly illegible. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 55][Para 67] Paragraph 51's list of prior award numbers is corrupted and could not be verified against the text.
