# Award No. 295/15 — Great On Near Tech Style Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 30 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Tang Tri presiding; Ing Sothy appointed by the employer, Huon Chun Dy appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 139472

## 1. Facts
The company cuts, sews and weaves at Svay Rolum, Sa'ang, Kandal, and employs about 1,250 workers. [Para 65][Para 66] Sixty-two weaving workers transferred their rights to the Khmer Youth Union Federation on 1 October 2015; twenty of them attended the hearing. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 72] On 30 September 2015 the employer notified twenty weaving workers that they were being moved permanently to other sections — ironing, sewing labels, packing — from 5 October 2015, and they refused to go. [Para 80] The employer then cut the yarn, and from 10 November 2015 to 10 January 2016 it suspended the contracts of the whole workforce of 1,250 with the labour inspectorate's letter No. 1976/15. [Para 95][Para 98][Para 115]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must give all the weaving workers work in the weaving building and stop moving them to other buildings? [Para 73]
2. Whether the employer must pay wages and benefits from 5 October 2015 onwards, having shut the weaving building and cut the yarn? [Para 99][Para 164]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** their contracts name the weaving section, but the employer moved them to ironing, labelling and packing, which are not their skills [Para 74][Para 75]. They kept coming to the weaving section and clocking in, but the employer cut the yarn and gave them nothing to do, so the loss is the employer's fault, not theirs [Para 100][Para 105][Para 169].
**Employer (respondent):** weaving orders have fallen, so it looked for work elsewhere in the same compound, about 50 metres away, with the same shift and seniority preserved and training of one to two weeks [Para 77][Para 82][Para 83][Para 88]. It notified the transfers in writing on 30 September, and the workers' refusal to go and clocking in without working amounted to a strike, so no wages are owed [Para 102][Para 106][Para 107].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must stop transferring the twenty weaving workers to other sections and must pay their wages and benefits in full from 5 October to 9 November 2015; the demand for work during the suspension, and anything after 10 January 2016, is refused. [Para 185][Para 186][Para 187][Para 188]
The panel first disposed of the transfer. Article 65(1) of the Labour Law and Articles 664, 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code make the contract the source of the parties' obligations, and no party may impose terms the other has not accepted. [Para 131][Para 133][Para 136][Para 139][Para 143] The contracts of the twenty named the weaving section, so moving them permanently to another section required their consent, which they withheld. [Para 150][Para 151][Para 152][Para 153] The panel also relied on the settlement of 14 September 2015 in case 243/15, in which the employer confirmed that the weaving building was operating normally, so transferring the workers while it ran was unreasonable. [Para 154][Para 155] It followed awards 62/06, 17/03 and 18/03, which allow redeployment only where wages, distance, shift and skill are respected. [Para 157][Para 158]
On the demand for work, Article 71(11) permits suspension of the enterprise for serious economic or material difficulty, subject to the labour inspector's oversight and a maximum of two months, and the panel found the suspension lawful and covering the twenty. [Para 111][Para 113][Para 115][Para 117][Para 118] It therefore refused to order work during the suspension, and held that the position after 10 January 2016 was a future dispute it could not decide. [Para 124][Para 125][Para 120]
On wages, the panel asked whether the workers' conduct was a strike under Article 318: a group of workers, a stoppage of work, and an aim of obtaining a settlement. [Para 164][Para 165][Para 166] Only the first element was met. "Work" means the weaving work their contracts describe; they came to the weaving section and clocked in, and it was the employer that supplied no weaving work. [Para 171][Para 172][Para 173][Para 175][Para 176] Withholding work from piece-rate workers who present themselves is listed in Article 83(a)(5) as serious misconduct by the employer. [Para 178][Para 180][Para 181] The panel therefore ordered 100% of wages and benefits from 5 October to 9 November 2015, ending at the date the lawful suspension began. [Para 183][Para 184]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A permanent move of workers out of the section named in their contracts needs their consent and cannot be imposed by managerial prerogative; and where an employer withholds work from piece-rate workers who attend their own section, the workers are not on strike and the employer owes their wages for that period as the author of its own serious misconduct. [Para 152][Para 175][Para 180]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel declined to compare the workers' earnings before and after the proposed transfer, because they had never actually worked in the new sections [Para 160]; and it noted that it cannot decide a dispute that has not yet arisen [Para 121][Para 123].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Para 14 dates the panel's constitution "17 November 2019 2015", para 183 the end of the wage period "9 November 2019 2015", and para 21 the extended award date "11 December 2 2015". [Para 14][Para 183][Para 21] The employer's bundle contains contracts dated between December 2002 and January 2004, although the company's registration certificate is dated 26 May 2011, and the award does not reconcile the two. [Para 30][Para 49][Para 65] The claimant group is unstable: 62 transferred rights, 20 attended and are treated as claimants, and the employer's own list names 19. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 51] The company name is printed in at least six forms ("Great On Near Tech Style", "Great On Near Tech Style Fak Feri", "Gret On Near Tech Stai", "Fret On Near Tek Stay", and others); the title above follows the face of the award but the romanisation is approximate. [Para 1][Para 65][Para 98] The settlement of 14 September 2015 is attributed both to this company and, at para 97, to "company L", and para 179 prefixes it "CC". [Para 96][Para 97][Para 179] Paragraph 108 describes issue 1 as having two limbs (a) and (b) but the panel's own numbering does not carry that division through. [Para 108][Para 185] Individual workers, union officials and employer representatives appear only under partial OCR names and are redacted. [Para 1][Para 2]
