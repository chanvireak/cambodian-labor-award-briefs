# Award No. 132/15 — Cambodian Handlift Product (Arbitration Council, 26 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Run Saray (president) · Doc ID 139209

## 1. Facts

Workers at Cambodian Handlift Product, an enterprise in the Sihanoukville Special Economic Zone, went on strike and pursued four demands through the Khmer Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ). [Para 1] Conciliation on 28 May 2015 settled two of the four; the two that remained were payment for 18 days of annual leave after one year of service and the preservation of other benefits during the strike. [Para 11] The Secretariat fixed the hearing for 25 June 2015 at 14:00; the employer attended, the workers' side did not. [Para 9][Para 15] The panel therefore had to answer a threshold question of its own — whether to continue the arbitration at all in the absence of the claimant — before it could reach the merits. [Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether, once a worker has completed one year with the company, the company must pay compensation for 18 days of annual leave under the Labour Law? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company must keep all other benefits unchanged during the strike? [Para 4]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The workers' side filed no documents at all and did not appear; its only recorded position is the conciliation demand for 18 days of annual leave and for no change to existing benefits while the strike continued. [Para 21][Para 15]

**Employer (respondent):** The company asked the Council by letter of 5 June 2015 to issue an order suspending the strike and submitted its own account of the dispute [Para 19], and it refused the workers' request to move the hearing to another day. [Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the arbitration (បញ្ចប់ ដំណើរការ អាជ្ញាកណ្តាល) in Case No. 132/15 and treated the workers' claims as abandoned; nothing was ordered to be paid. [Para 60][Para 63]

The panel reached neither substantive issue. Reading Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, it held that articles 18, 20 and 21 and rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules leave a party that fails to appear with a choice of consequence: the panel may carry on in that party's absence or close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 32][Para 30] Closing the file, it held, requires three cumulative conditions — proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid excuse — a test it said it had already applied in a long line of earlier awards, which it listed by number. [Para 34][Para 37]

On the first condition, the panel found the claimant properly summoned by letter No. 726 of 17 June 2015, which stated the date, place and the Council's telephone and fax numbers, and it noted a further telephone call on the day itself. [Para 39][Para 40] On the second, it read "appearance before the panel" in article 21 as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages of the proceedings, and found neither. [Para 43][Para 45] On the third, it rejected the excuse relayed by the Federation — that the Federation had forgotten the hearing date and never told the workers — as no valid reason. [Para 49][Para 50]

Finally, the panel reasoned that a claimant carries the burden of presenting its argument and evidence first, and that a respondent's duty to rebut arises only once the claimant has done so; a claimant who never appears therefore abandons its claim, and there is nothing left to arbitrate. [Para 55][Para 58][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where the claimant party has been properly notified of the hearing and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may close the arbitration by award instead of hearing the claim on its merits, and the claim is treated as abandoned — the three conditions of Prakas No. 099 (proper summons, non-appearance, no valid excuse) being cumulative and each to be established on the record. [Para 34][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's account of the four stages of an arbitration hearing, and its observation that a party may appear in person, with an accompanying person, or through a Bar-registered lawyer, were not needed to dispose of the case. [Para 44]

[Para 13] The same conciliation report is numbered 0359/15 in one place, 0359/14 in another and 20359/15 in a third. [Para 7][Para 12][Para 21] The relevant rule appears as "rule 4.7" and later as "rule 4.76". [Para 30][Para 32] The hearing time is printed as 14:00, as 13:50 (the Secretariat's call) and as 14:05 (the Federation's reply). [Para 9][Para 16][Para 49] Paragraph 14 speaks of four unsettled points while the rest of the award says two. [Para 14] The employer's name is rendered both in English and as "ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខេមបូឌាន ហ៊ែន លីហ្វ ប្រដាក់"; the interpreter is recorded only as Lok Nheung Sokha and one employer representative only by the transliteration "Mr. Z".
