# Award No. 010/15 — Strot Sphot Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 23 January 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambona for the employer party, An Nan for the worker party, Tang Tri chair) · Doc ID 139121 · Khmer original

## 1. Facts
Strot Sphot Co., Ltd operates in the Special Economic Zone at Sihanoukville, Preah Sihanouk province, and faces seven collective demands from two federations — the Cambodian Workers' Trade Union Federation and the Khmer Workers' Pride Trade Union Federation — covering incentive bonuses, a doctor on duty, a base-wage increase, union discrimination, dismissals, sick-leave deductions, reinstatement of a worker and wage payments during a strike. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 8][Para 9] Conciliation at the Sihanoukville office of the Ministry of Labour on 05 January 2015 settled none of the seven, and the non-conciliation report reached the Secretariat on 09 January 2015. [Para 16][Para 17] The employer attended the hearing of 16 January 2015; the worker representatives signed the attendance sheet and left without taking part. [Para 14][Para 20][Para 36][Para 41]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must implement the Arbitration Council's award, including a written policy for a monthly incentive bonus and a doctor present every working hour? [Para 3]
2. Whether the company must add USD 28 to the old base wage of USD 110, making a total of USD 138? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must refrain from union discrimination, such as summoning, warning and dismissing without reason? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must refrain from dismissing workers without reason? [Para 6]
5. Whether the company must not deduct the hour's pay when a worker is sick and must rest during working hours? [Para 7]
6. Whether the company must take back a dismissed worker (name redacted) with the same wages and benefits? [Para 8]
7. Whether the company must keep paying regular wages to workers during a strike? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demands were filed as a collective dispute over working conditions and were pressed through conciliation, where the parties failed to agree on any of the seven points. [Para 15][Para 16] No representative appeared before the panel on the hearing date; the two federations' representatives registered their attendance at the Secretariat and then departed, telling staff the panel could proceed without them. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 36][Para 41]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer party attended the hearing as summoned, and the award records no substantive position on the seven demands. [Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel held the claimant had abandoned its claim and terminated the arbitration of case No. 010/15; no order was made on any of the seven demands and nothing was awarded in riel or USD. [Para 56][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]

The panel framed its own question as whether it should continue the proceedings in the absence of the claimant worker party. [Para 30] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council of 21 April 2004, and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules annexed to it, permit the panel to continue or to close the case by award where a properly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason. [Para 30][Para 31] Following Awards No. 16/07 Loutou, 27/07 E&W 3, 138/08 Malin, 11/11 Choun Tich, 131/12 Master & Frank, 108/13 Otto and 332/14 T&K Garment, the panel read Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions: the party was properly summoned, the party did not appear before the panel, and the party gave no valid reason for its absence. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 34]

On condition one, invitation letters No. 045 and No. 044 dated 13 January 2015 went to both federations and to the company with the date, time, place and contact details, and were followed up by telephone at 03:00 p.m. the same day. [Para 34][Para 35] The panel found the claimant had in fact been properly summoned. [Para 38][Para 39] On condition two, it read "appearance before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict-of-interest explanation, explanation of procedure and identification of the issues, renewed conciliation if the parties agreed, and the hearing itself — and found the claimant had merely registered attendance and left. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 44] On condition three, the federations gave no valid reason, saying only that the panel need not wait for their representatives. [Para 45][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] All three conditions being satisfied, the panel treated the claim as abandoned and closed the file. [Para 50][Para 56][Para 60]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a properly summoned claimant party registers attendance but does not remain and does not participate in the four stages of the hearing, it has not "appeared before the panel" within Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Procedural Rules; once the three conditions of proper summons, non-appearance and absence of valid reason are cumulatively met, the panel may treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration without deciding the merits. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 40][Para 44][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general statement that a claimant bears the duty to come and present its own allegations, reasons and evidence, while the respondent's duty to answer arises afterwards, was not necessary to the disposal of the case. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award is in Khmer only; every rendering of a name or demand above is a translation. The company name appears only as ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្ត្រូត ស្ព័ត and is transliterated, not printed in Latin script; it also appears corruptly as ស្ត្រ ត័ត at [Para 61].
- Panel names are inconsistent: the chair is តាំង ទ្រី in the composition [Para 1] but តាំង ទី in the signature block [Para 62], which also breaks off into fragments such as "untion\" - 2012" and "2017-".
- The non-conciliation report date is garbled — "ថ្ងៃទី ០៦ ខែ ម្មូក វ៉ា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" [Para 12] where January is evidently meant, since the report number 0863/15 is repeated with ០៦ ខែមករា at [Para 17] and [Para 24].
- The panel is said to be constituted on "ថ្ងៃទី ១៣ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ ២០ ២០១៥" [Para 18], a duplicated year, and Prakas No. 099 is dated ២៥ ២១ April at [Para 50].
- Large blocks of the reasons are unrecoverable: [Para 30] contains inserted English fragments ("In 2019 2017", "World", "Wesquanwithmation anounturnamany"), and [Para 46] begins with the token "<unk>".
- The seventh demand and the first are internally odd: demand 1 asks the company to implement "the Arbitration Council's award" although no earlier award is identified in the file [Para 3].
