# Award No. 314/15 — Global A Parallel Ltd (Arbitration Council, 30 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothi (employer-appointed), Sin Kim Sean (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 139484

## 1. Facts
The Democratic Union of Cambodian Garment Workers brought two demands for the workers of Global A Parallel Ltd, a garment factory of about 2,570 employees: reinstatement of two men who had stood as candidates in the union election, and deduction of union dues by bank transfer. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 48] Both men worked on the factory's premises in the ironing and stamping section but had been recruited, contracted and paid through Fei Ma, a labour-supply agency with which Global A Parallel had a written temporary-supply agreement paying the agency five per cent of each worker's base wage. [Para 69][Para 88][Para 92] They were told to stop work on 1 October 2015, and were dismissed on 1 October and 9 October 2015, within weeks of the 29 September 2015 union election in which they were candidates. [Para 49][Para 59][Para 63]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate the two union-election candidates to their former posts and pay their wages from the date of dismissal until their return? [Para 55]
2. Whether the employer must deduct union dues from members' wages and pay them to the federation's bank account, on members' written authorisation? [Para 98]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The two are candidates who were dismissed without notice or cause and so enjoy the 45-day protection under Prakas No. 305; their pay slips and staff cards are the factory's, they were fingerprinted on its system, and the factory took part in selecting and testing them, so it is their real employer and must answer for them. [Para 59][Para 65][Para 69][Para 76][Para 80]**Employer (respondent):** Neither man is its employee; both were hired from Fei Ma under a proper supply contract, and recruitment, wages, hours, leave, warnings, absence and dismissal all rest with Fei Ma, so any complaint lies against that company. On the dues, the plant union is unregistered and has no company-level leadership structure, and audits require signed pay records. [Para 4][Para 68][Para 78][Para 102][Para 103][Para 104]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider both claims. [Para 171][Para 172]

Before the merits the panel asked whether the union could represent members at all. Under Article 268 the union acquires the rights the Labour Law recognises only once registered with the Ministry, and the Global A Parallel union was not registered. [Para 108][Para 110][Para 112][Para 113] Under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 a party may appear through a representative only with written authority, and the panel found transfer-of-rights letters from the two workers covering the reinstatement claim alone, with nothing transferring the dues claim. [Para 114][Para 115][Para 120][Para 122]

On issue 1 the panel applied Articles 1, 65 and 664 of the Labour Law and the Civil Code to decide who was the employer. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 126] It found written employment contracts between the two men and Fei Ma and none with Global A Parallel. [Para 127][Para 128] It then applied the control test drawn from earlier awards — recruitment, wage-setting, hours and leave, and dismissal — and found Fei Ma performed each: Fei Ma recruited them directly and decided whom to send; Fei Ma set their base wage of USD 133 and benefits; Fei Ma's contracts fixed their hours, shifts and leave; and Fei Ma's owner alone told them to stop work for lack of work and dealt with warnings, absence and termination. [Para 140][Para 143][Para 145][Para 149][Para 151][Para 161][Para 162] The factory's pay slips and temporary staff cards were issued to verify attendance and minimum-wage compliance against the agency's own list, and its testing of candidates went only to whether they could do the work the agency had sent them for. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 75][Para 142][Para 148] Fei Ma was therefore the employer, and the panel followed Award No. 130/08 (Fuu Hing) in refusing to consider a claim against a respondent that is not the worker's employer. [Para 163][Para 164][Para 165][Para 167]

On issue 2, no worker had transferred rights on that item, so no one was properly before the panel on it and it was not considered. [Para 169][Para 170]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A labour-supply agency that recruits the worker, sets and pays the wage, fixes hours and leave, and alone decides warnings and dismissal is the employer, even where the worker performs the work on a client factory's premises, is fingerprinted on its attendance system and carries its temporary staff card; a claim for reinstatement must be brought against that agency, not the client. [Para 149][Para 162][Para 164]

**Obiter dicta:** A client's contractual power to verify attendance and minimum-wage compliance, and to test whether a supplied worker can do the job, does not by itself make it the employer. [Para 142][Para 148]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated 30 December 2015 in the header, but the parties are recorded as extending the deadline from 21 December to 31 December 2015, which is later than the date of issue. [Para 1][Para 20] The panel's constitution is printed "30 November 2019 2015". [Para 14] The conciliation report is dated 17 November 2015 but said to be transmitted on 27 November, and a separate conciliation minute is dated 23 November. [Para 9][Para 13] The company appears as Global A Parallel, "Kloopbal A Parallel", "Kampu Bal A Parallel" and "Ju A Parallel". [Para 9][Para 47][Para 69] The two workers' transfer-of-rights letters are dated 3 October 2015, before the second of them was dismissed on 9 October 2015, and the panel elsewhere prints that date as "3 October 2019 2015". [Para 59][Para 116][Para 118] The federation's complaint is numbered 115/15, the same number as an unrelated letter in the file. [Para 12][Para 42] The president's name is printed "Kong Phalha" in the header and "Kong Phalla" in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 174]
