# Award No. 053/15 — Meridian In Das Stri Garment (Arbitration Council, 11 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer; An Non, chosen by the workers; Kong Phallak, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139153

## 1. Facts
Meridian In Das Stri Garment (មេរីឌាន អ៊ីន ដាស ស្ទ្រី ហ្គាមេន) operates at Kampong Pring village, Sethbo commune, Saang district, Kandal. [Para 1] On 17 December 2014 two federations — the Federation of Unions for Labour Rights and the Peace Federation for Workers — complained to the Kandal provincial Department of Labour and Vocational Training, demanding improvements to a list of working conditions. [Para 19] Conciliation on 30 January 2015 settled none of the twelve demands, and report No. 408/15 of 17 February 2015 reached the Secretariat the same day; the panel was formed on 19 February 2015 and fixed the hearing for 05 March 2015. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 18] At the hearing only the employer appeared; the Peace Federation filed a letter withdrawing all the claims, and the Federation of Unions for Labour Rights stayed away, saying by telephone that it had decided to withdraw and would send a letter later. [Para 23][Para 25][Para 26] That letter never came: on 10 March 2015 its officials said they could not come to withdraw because they were busy with workers' strikes elsewhere and asked the panel to close the file. [Para 27][Para 53]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must remove a Chinese national (name redacted) from the company? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay USD 10 a month for travel and accommodation? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must pay a USD 15 monthly attendance premium? [Para 4]
4. Whether the employer must pay a daily midday meal allowance of USD 300? [Para 5]
5. Whether the employer must stop dismissing workers who have committed no fault? [Para 6]
6. Whether the employer must refrain from forcing workers to work overtime, overtime being voluntary? [Para 7]
7. Whether the employer must set fair piece rates? [Para 8]
8. Whether the employer must calculate hourly pay correctly? [Para 9]
9. Whether the employer must allow pregnant women one health check a month while preserving their other benefits? [Para 10]
10. Whether the employer must grant pregnant women USD 15 for leaving work early? [Para 11]
11. Whether the employer must apply one-year employment contracts? [Para 12]
12. Whether the employer must reinstate three named workers it previously dismissed? [Para 13]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The twelve demands in the non-conciliation report are the workers' case; they transferred their rights to the two federations by letter of 11 December 2014, and the Peace Federation's letter of 05 March 2015 both transferred its right to appear and, the same day, withdrew the claims. [Para 31][Para 30][Para 32]
**Employer (respondent):** The award records no substantive defence. The employer appeared through its administrative head, holding a written transfer of authority of 05 March 2015, and asked for the hearing to be postponed; the merits of the twelve demands were never argued. [Para 1][Para 28][Para 29][Para 37]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel declared the claim abandoned and terminated the arbitration in case No. 053/15 without ruling on any of the twelve demands. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66]
The panel first recorded that it could establish no facts on the disputed issues at all, because the claimant workers did not attend the hearing. [Para 37] It then framed the question whether it should continue in their absence and applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY of 21 April 2004 — where a party has been properly invited and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may proceed in its absence or close the arbitration by issuing an award — together with Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to the same Prakas, on condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] It confirmed the three-condition test as settled in awards 16/07 Lotus, 27/07 IM & V 3, 138/08 Malin, 11/11 Joun Tech Thai, 131/12 Master & French, 108/13 Auto and 332/14 T&K Garment. [Para 41][Para 42]
On condition one, the Secretariat issued invitation No. 297 to the presidents of both federations and No. 296 to the company's director on 04 March 2015, stating the date, place and contact numbers. [Para 43][Para 36][Para 35][Para 45][Para 46] On condition two, the Federation of Unions for Labour Rights did not appear at the hearing set for 14:00 on 05 March 2015, and so took part in none of the four stages of the proceedings — the introduction on conflicts of interest, the explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation, and the hearing itself. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51] On condition three, its officials gave no valid reason: they announced a withdrawal but sent no letter, and a week later said only that they were too busy with strikes to come and asked the panel to close the case. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] All three conditions being satisfied, the panel held the claimant had abandoned its claim and need not continue. [Para 56][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An announced intention to withdraw a collective claim, unaccompanied by any written notice of withdrawal, is not a withdrawal and does not end the proceedings; and where the claimant federation then fails to appear at a hearing it was properly summoned to, its officials' later explanation that they are too busy with other workers' strikes is not a valid reason, so the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and close the arbitration under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY and Rule 4.7 — a request that the panel "close the file" is not a substitute for appearing. [Para 26][Para 47][Para 54][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's general account of the parties' duty to attend and to present argument and evidence in person, and its remarks that the claimant's burden comes first and the respondent's only afterwards, were not applied to any demand and played no part in the outcome. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is Khmer only and heavily corrupted. The fourth demand is printed as a midday meal allowance of "USD 300 per day" [Para 5], an implausible figure that is plainly a riel amount mis-typed; it is recorded here exactly as printed and no riel equivalent is stated anywhere. The non-conciliation report number is printed as "៤០៨/ ៦ ១៥" and as 408/15 elsewhere [Para 16][Para 20][Para 33], and paragraph 16 contains a garbled repetition with stray years ("ឆ្នាំ ២០១៧", "ឆ្នាំ ២០២២") in place of the referral date. The panel is said to have been formed on "19 February 2023 2015", both years printed [Para 21]. The operative Prakas article is cited as "ប្រការ ២១២" where Article 21 is meant [Para 38]. The worker-chosen arbitrator is printed as "អន ណន" in the header and as "អន លាន" in the signature block [Para 1][Para 66]. The company is spelled មេរីឌាន, មេរី ឌាន and "ហ្គាអែន"; the Latin form in the heading is a rendering, not a printed name [Para 1][Para 16]. Paragraph 54 also carries an intruded fragment of English ("លោក World Championsher") inside the reasoning [Para 54]. No figure of any kind is awarded, and because the merits were never reached, the three named dismissed workers' claims remain unresolved [Para 13][Para 66].
