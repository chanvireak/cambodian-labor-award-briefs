# Award No. 032/15 — E Cheng (Cambodia) Hop Beresin (Arbitration Council, 18 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ri for the employer party, Vaen Pov for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139138

## 1. Facts
E Cheng (Cambodia) Hop Beresin, a garment enterprise at Taing Doung, Bati, Takeo, faced a single collective demand from its own workers: that the company add USD 28 to the base wage they received in previous years, on top of the new 2015 base wage, while keeping all other benefits — a demand expressly limited to workers who are team leaders and deputy team leaders. [Para 1][Para 3] Conciliation before the Takeo provincial department on 21 January 2015 settled nothing on that one point, and the report No. 037 of 23 January 2015 reached the Council on 29 January 2015. [Para 6][Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] The panel was formed on 2 February 2015 and fixed a hearing for 13 February 2015 at 14:00, with invitations No. 153 and No. 154 of 5 February 2015. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 22][Para 23] The employer attended through its general director, general manager and general affairs officer; the claimant workers did not appear, and when the secretariat went to the factory on 10 February 2015 at 15:15 they refused to sign for the invitation. [Para 1][Para 14][Para 15][Para 39] The panel therefore found no facts on the demand. [Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must add USD 28 to the base wage of team leaders and deputy team leaders, on top of the 2015 base wage increase, while preserving their other benefits? [Para 3]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration in the absence of the claimant workers? [Para 25]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the workers demanded that the USD 28 be added to the base wage they had received in earlier years, cumulating with the new 2015 base wage, and that other benefits stay unchanged; the claim was confined to team leaders and deputy team leaders. [Para 3] They then took no part in the proceedings, refused to sign for the invitation, and gave the secretariat no reason for their absence. [Para 39][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent):** the award records no substantive answer to the demand; the company appeared through three representatives and produced no documents. [Para 1][Para 14][Para 16]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed for abandonment — the panel terminated the arbitration of case No. 032/15 — E Cheng (Cambodia) Hop Beresin; nothing was ordered on the wage demand. [Para 63][Para 64]

The panel framed the question itself: whether to continue where the claimant workers are absent. [Para 25] Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 S.K.B.Y. of 21 April 2004 requires the panel to summon the parties to state their claims in person and to table the necessary documents; Article 20 obliges parties to attend every meeting to which they are summoned; Article 21 permits the panel either to proceed in the absence of a duly invited party who fails to appear without valid reason, or to close the file by issuing an award; Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules repeats that choice, on condition of proper notice of date, time and place. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30] Consistent with earlier awards, the panel applied three cumulative conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid reason. [Para 33][Para 36][Para 37]

Condition one was met on a qualified basis: invitation No. 154 of 5 February 2015 specified the date, place and contact details, and the panel held that the claimant was properly summoned even though the workers refused to sign for it when the secretariat attended the factory on 10 February 2015 — the panel treated delivery as effected and the refusal to acknowledge it as the party's own act. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] Condition two was met: "appearance before the panel" requires both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict explanation, procedure and issue clarification, conciliation if agreed, and hearing to verdict — and the claimant did neither, nor appointed a representative authorised in writing as Article 19 permits. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] Condition three was met: no valid reason for the absence was ever given to the secretariat. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]

All three conditions being satisfied, the panel held that a claimant must attend to state its claims and produce its documents, the respondent's burden arising only afterwards. [Para 54][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58] The claimant having defaulted, the panel deemed the claim abandoned and closed the arbitration. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant workers have been summoned by an invitation delivered at the workplace with precise notice of date, time and place, their refusal to sign for it does not defeat service; if they then stay away without a substantiated reason, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration without examining the merits. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the four-stage description of an arbitration hearing and the sequence of burdens between claimant and respondent are general statements of procedure, not steps needed to reach the closure order. [Para 43][Para 57][Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The dispute is said to have been notified to the Takeo department on 21 January 2014 [Para 9], while the conciliation session and the referral report are dated 21 and 23 January 2015 [Para 10][Para 11]; the 2014 date is as printed.
- The venue is given as "ភូមិគ្រឹះ លេខ ៥៩២", dropping the house number 72 used elsewhere in the same file [Para 2][Para 7].
- The jurisdictional paragraph is garbled as "ជំពូក ទី ១២ ផ្នែក ទី២៤ ពីរ(ខ)" [Para 4], and the list of precedent awards is badly corrupted, e.g. "លេខ ១៣៨០៨-ម៉ាលីន", "លេខ ០៩- ដើម បឹងឺ ស ថិច ថាយ៍" and "លេខ ២៧០/១៣-៨ ស្តារ ស្ពត វែរ" [Para 36].
- The final operative paragraph repeats its own opening clause verbatim before the order ("សរេច យោង តាម អង្គហេតុ … សម្រេច ចេញ សេចក្តី បង្គាប់") [Para 64].
- No employer response to the single demand is recorded anywhere in the award, so the employer's position cannot be stated beyond its appearance; no evidence was tendered by either side [Para 16][Para 17].
- The worker-party arbitrator's signature line degenerates into the fragment "man to seas and Chany" [Para 65], and stray marks ("LL", "FI", "Z", "២") interrupt the reasoning at [Para 43], [Para 56] and [Para 13].
