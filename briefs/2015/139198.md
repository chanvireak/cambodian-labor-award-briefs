# Award No. 119/15 — វ៉ូណា មមី (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន (Arbitration Council, 11 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bunchea presiding; Li Tai Seng, employer nominee; Liv Sovanna, worker nominee) · Doc ID 139198

## 1. Facts
The respondent sews garments at Samrong village, Sophi commune, Bati district, Takeo, was registered on 23 January 2014 under Inv. 0319 E/2014, and employs about 578 workers. [Para 2][Para 48][Para 49] Four local unions exist at the factory — Khmer Youth Voice, Khmer Children's Labour, Labour for Cambodia and Cambodian Workers' Labour — but none had yet received its registration certificate from the Ministry. [Para 49] On 5 May 2015 the Takeo labour department received a complaint from those four federations over fourteen demands; conciliation on 11 May 2015 settled eight, one more was settled at the hearing, and two were withdrawn, leaving issues 2 and 3. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] Fifteen workers signed authorisations and twelve attended, giving twenty-six claimants in all, and the panel heard the case on 29 May 2015. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 53][Para 18]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must raise the regular attendance bonus (លុយព្រីម) from USD 10 to USD 15 a month? [Para 5][Para 54]
3. Whether the employer must pay an overtime meal allowance of 2,000 riel for each hour worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 7][Para 65]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the attendance bonus should be 15 dollars because workers live far away, household costs are tight and goods have risen in price, the bonus encourages attendance, Ministry notification sets 10 dollars as a floor so more may be claimed, and other factories pay 18 to 20 dollars; the overtime meal allowance should be 2,000 riel an hour because food and rent have risen, other factories pay it, and workers need to eat enough to avoid fainting. [Para 55][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 66][Para 68]

**Employer (respondent):** it pays the 10 dollars that Notification 230 requires; factories paying 15 to 20 dollars sew for famous brands and can afford it, whereas this company opened recently and has no brand contracts or profit, so it asks for understanding; on overtime it pays 3,000 riel for each two-hour overtime day, having raised the daily 2,000 riel by 500 riel an hour in December 2014. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 64][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both remaining claims were refused: the attendance bonus stays at USD 10 a month and the overtime meal allowance claim for 2,000 riel an hour was refused. [Para 104][Para 105]

On issue 2 the panel applied point 2 of Notification No. 230 of the Ministry of Labour, which requires at least USD 10 a month for a worker who attends on all working days without absence, and found the employer pays exactly that, so the statutory obligation was met. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 75] No law, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or past practice obliged the employer to pay 15 dollars, so the claim sought a benefit above the law. [Para 76][Para 77] It was therefore an interest dispute, and under Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas 305 read with Article 43 of Prakas 099 an interest award replaces the collective agreement and removes the right to claim such benefits in future, so the panel decides one only for the most representative union. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 82][Para 83][Para 85] The complainant was not that union, so the panel refused to consider the claim. [Para 87][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92]

On issue 3 the panel applied point 2 of Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, which gives a worker who volunteers for overtime 2,000 riel a day or one free meal, and found the employer pays 3,000 riel for each two-hour overtime day — more than the law requires. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95][Para 97] No instrument required 2,000 riel an hour, and the claim was in any event an interest dispute that the panel would not decide for a union that was not the most representative. [Para 98][Para 99][Para 100][Para 102][Para 103]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the employer already pays the statutory attendance bonus and an overtime meal allowance above the statutory daily rate, the obligation is discharged and a demand for more is an interest dispute; the panel will not decide such a dispute unless the claimant is the most representative union in the enterprise, or unions together representing more than half the workforce. [Para 75][Para 85][Para 97][Para 102]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted the workers' comparison with factories paying 18 to 20 dollars and with factories paying 2,000 riel an hour, but observed that those employers' greater profitability cannot create an obligation, since Notification 230 fixes only a minimum. [Para 59][Para 61][Para 66]

[Para 1][Para 48][Para 62] The conciliation report number is "35618" in one place and "356" in two others. [Para 16][Para 21][Para 42] The deadline for objecting to documents is printed "04 June 2014" for a case heard in 2015. [Para 29] Notification 230 is dated "2023 July 2012" in the reasoning. [Para 73] The workers present are listed as twelve but the numbering skips from 8 to 10. [Para 51] The operative part repeats itself, and the paragraph on the finality of the award is mangled into fragments. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107] The award records that all four local unions at the factory were still unregistered, yet the four federations that signed the complaint are treated as the complainant and the panel's representativeness finding rests on the local unions' position.
