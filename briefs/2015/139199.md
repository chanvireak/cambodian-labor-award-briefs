# Award No. 117/15 — Ngu Kuri Bak and Lak Gidyu (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 23 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mr M Sambor Na for the employer party, Mr An Non for the worker party, Mr T Tri chair) · Doc ID 139199

## 1. Facts
The employer runs a bag factory in the Sihanoukville Special Economic Zone, employing about 220 workers. [Para 49][Para 50] Mr C was hired on 26 May 2014 as a sewing worker on successive fixed-term contracts of 3 and 6 months, the last of which ran to 26 April 2015, and he was dismissed on 31 March 2015 — before that expiry date. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] The Union Federation of Khmer Workers' Spirit (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) filed a collective complaint of 14 points on 27 April 2015; 8 were settled and 6 remained for the panel. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15] Only Mr C appeared at the hearing. [Para 52]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr C as a union leader, with wage and benefits preserved? [Para 2][Para 54]
2. Whether the employer must provide an adequate medical room and medicines for workers? [Para 81]
3. Whether the employer must stop deducting wages from workers who listen to the radio during working hours? [Para 87]
4. Whether the employer must raise the transport and accommodation allowance by USD 5, to USD 15 per month? [Para 90]
5. Whether the employer must keep the USD 5 monthly health-care allowance in place? [Para 94]
6. Whether the employer must stop discriminating against union representatives and members by wrongful dismissal or transfer of role? [Para 102]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr C was a candidate elected as a union leader and was dismissed without valid reason; the union also sought a clinic and medicines, an end to wage deductions for listening to the radio, a USD 15 travel and lodging allowance, retention of the USD 5 health allowance, and an end to anti-union measures. [Para 57][Para 75][Para 91][Para 103]
**Employer (respondent):** Mr C committed serious misconduct — disobeying supervisors, leading other workers to strike and urging them not to work overtime — and had been warned repeatedly; it also said the USD 10 travel allowance already exceeded the legal minimum, that the USD 5 health allowance had been folded into the basic wage by Notification No. 103 of 09 April 2013, and that no union leader had been dismissed. [Para 65][Para 72][Para 93][Para 96][Para 104]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel dismissed issues 02, 04, 05 and 06 and ordered the employer to pay Mr C (1) termination damages of 5% of the wages paid over the contract period, (2) final wages not yet paid, (3) unused annual leave, and (4) moral damages of at least the wages he would have received to the end of the contract. [Para 229][Para 230][Para 231][Para 232][Para 233][Para 234]

On standing, the panel applied Article 268 of the Labour Law and Prakar 19 of Prakas No. 099/04: a union only holds the rights the law recognises once registered with the Ministry, and may be represented there by a person authorised in writing. [Para 112][Para 113][Para 117][Para 118] The federation had no written transfer of rights from the workers of this employer and did not appear; Mr C produced no letter of authorisation, so he was the only claimant. [Para 121][Para 125][Para 126][Para 128]

On issue 01, protection against dismissal under Article 293 and Prakars 3 and 4 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 requires three cumulative conditions: that the worker holds protected status, that the dismissal fall inside the protection period, and that the union notify the employer of the protected names. [Para 132][Para 134][Para 140][Para 150] The election notice of 17 February 2015 named only Ms K Saron as president, Ms S Ou as vice-president and Ms R Ratha as secretary; Mr C' name was absent, and condition 1 therefore failed, so conditions 2 and 3 were not examined. [Para 154][Para 155][Para 156][Para 158]

On serious misconduct, Article 83 requires gross breach of discipline or safety rules, and following Award No. 107/04 the employer bears the burden of proving it. [Para 160][Para 170] The employer alleged a strike, agitation and refusal of overtime but produced no evidence; the only documented conduct was listening to the radio and using a telephone, already punished by three written warnings with USD 1 and USD 2 deductions, and the internal rules prescribe a warning and then a deduction, not dismissal. [Para 169][Para 173][Para 176][Para 177] The panel also held that deducting wages as a disciplinary measure is itself improper, because the Labour Law forbids measures that reduce wages properly earned. [Para 175] The employer could not convert conduct already sanctioned into a ground of serious misconduct. [Para 178][Para 179]

On the fixed-term contract, Article 73(3) permits the employer to terminate at will before expiry but entitles the worker to damages of at least the wages due to the end of the contract; Article 73(6) adds severance of 5% of wages paid during the contract, Article 116 requires final wages within 45 hours, and Article 166 requires payment for unused annual leave. [Para 180][Para 181][Para 186][Para 190][Para 195][Para 198][Para 202]

Issues 02, 04 and 05 were dismissed because Mr C no longer had any legal employment relationship with the employer and held no transfer of rights from any other worker, so no claimant remained on those points. [Para 208][Para 209][Para 210][Para 211] Issue 06 was dismissed because the party asserting discrimination carries the burden of proof and the workers filed no evidence at all by the deadline. [Para 217][Para 223][Para 224][Para 228]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissed worker claiming the protection attaching to a union leader under Article 293 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 305 must first show that the union notified the employer of his name as an elected leader; where the election notice lists other names, the protection does not attach and the panel will not reach the remaining conditions. [Para 150][Para 155][Para 157] Separately, an employer terminating a fixed-term contract before its expiry may do so at will, but owes the worker damages of at least the wages due to the contract's end, plus 5% severance, final wages and unused leave; conduct already warned and fined cannot afterwards be recharacterised as serious misconduct. [Para 175][Para 178][Para 181][Para 186][Para 190]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that disciplinary wage deductions are improper under the Labour Law, and that the internal rules contain no rule treating these breaches as serious misconduct, are not necessary to the order, which rests on the absence of proof and the fixed-term compensation regime. [Para 175][Para 176]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed in at least five different Khmer spellings across the award — ញូ គួរី ប៊ែក និង ឡាក់ ហ្គីដ្យ at [Para 1], ញូ គ្លរី at [Para 1], ញូគ្លូរី at [Para 15], ពូកូវី at [Para 50] and ញូ គួរ ប៊ែក at [Para 227]. The name in the heading has been used throughout.
- The worker's name appears as ឈួន ប៊ុន ថៃ, ឈូន ប៊ុន ថៃ, ឈួន បុន ឆៃ and once as ឈួន សុខ [Para 156]; he is rendered as "Mr C".
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "18 May 2023 2015", an apparent duplication of two years, with the year repeated alone on the next line [Para 16][Para 17].
- Two hearing dates are given: 22 May 2015 [Para 12] and an invitation to a hearing on 26 May 2015 [Para 18]. Both are reported as printed.
- The heading date of 23 June 2015 [Para 1] matches the parties' agreed extension from 8 June to 23 June 2015 [Para 24].
- Issue 03 disappears from the operative part: the panel records that it was no longer in dispute because the employer had stopped the deductions, and the decision then addresses only issues 01, 02, 04, 05 and 06 [Para 89][Para 233].
- Severance is printed as "05 ភាគរយ" (5%), and the award refers to "14 contracts of 6 months" [Para 188] although the evidence lists terms of 3 and 6 months [Para 54].
- No riel or dollar figure is printed for the amounts ordered; the order fixes only the measure. [Para 229][Para 232]
