# Award No. 027/15 — Orion International Apparel (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 18 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139136

## 1. Facts
Orion International Apparel (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd, at Chamkar Roth, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, was the respondent to a collective dispute brought by two federations, the Union Federation for Workers' Rights and Interests and the Union Federation for Labour Rights, seeking improved working conditions. [Para 1][Para 10] Of fifteen demands conciliated at the provincial department from 14 January 2015, twelve were settled and three were referred to the Council on 26 January 2015. [Para 11][Para 12] The panel was formed on 28 January 2015 and set a hearing for 6 February 2015 at 14:00; invitations No. 124 and No. 125 of 28 January 2015 were issued and confirmed by telephone. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 20][Para 21][Para 36][Para 37] Neither party attended: no employer representative appeared at all, and the claimant workers telephoned to say they would not attend but would come to withdraw the complaint on 9 February 2015 — and had not done so by 10 February 2015. [Para 15][Para 43][Para 46][Para 47] The panel therefore had no evidence before it and could establish no facts on the three demands. [Para 16][Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a lunch allowance of 2,000 riel per day? [Para 2]
2. Whether the "prime" (ព្រីម) payment must be raised above the USD 12 the employer wishes to maintain? [Para 2]
3. Whether the employer must increase the travel and accommodation payment above USD 7 plus the USD 5 allowance it wishes to keep? [Para 4]
4. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration where both parties are absent from the hearing? [Para 23]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two federations asked the employer to improve a set of working conditions, of which the surviving three concern the 2,000 riel daily lunch allowance, an increase in the prime, and the travel and accommodation payment. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 10] At the hearing date they did not appear, saying by telephone that they would withdraw the complaint instead, which they never did. [Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** as recorded in the non-conciliation report, the company asked for one month to consider the lunch allowance, asked to keep the prime unchanged at USD 12, and asked to keep the existing USD 7 travel and accommodation payment plus the USD 5 allowance. [Para 2][Para 4] It sent no representative to the hearing and gave the secretariat no reason for the absence. [Para 1][Para 47]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed for abandonment — the panel terminated the arbitration of case No. 027/15 — Orion International Apparel (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd; nothing was ordered on the three demands. [Para 58][Para 59]

The panel posed the procedural question itself: whether to continue where both parties are absent. [Para 23] Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 S.K.B.Y. of 21 April 2004 requires the panel to summon the parties to present their claims in person and to supply the necessary documents; Article 20 obliges the parties to attend every meeting to which they are summoned; Article 21 permits the panel either to proceed in the absence of a duly invited party who fails to appear without valid reason, or to close the proceedings by issuing an award; Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules repeats that choice, subject to proper notice of date, time and place. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] Following ten earlier awards, the panel treated Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and absence of a valid reason. [Para 31][Para 34][Para 35]

Condition one was met: invitations No. 124 and No. 125 of 28 January 2015 went to the company and to the presidents of both federations with the hearing date, place and contact numbers, and were followed by telephone confirmation that afternoon. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 39][Para 40] Condition two was met: "appearance before the panel" means both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict explanation, procedure and issue clarification, conciliation if agreed, and hearing to verdict — and neither party attended nor appointed a representative authorised in writing, as Article 19 allows. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] Condition three was met: the workers' telephone notice that they would withdraw was not a valid reason and the withdrawal never happened, while the employer offered no reason at all. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49]

All three conditions being satisfied, the panel held that a claimant must come before it to state its claims and produce its documents, and that the respondent's burden arises only afterwards. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54] Neither side having done so, the panel deemed the claimant to have abandoned its claim and its rights, found no need to continue, and closed the arbitration. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both the claimant and the respondent fail to appear at a hearing of which they had precise notice, and neither gives a substantiated reason, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration without reaching the merits; a bare telephone indication that the claimant intends to withdraw the complaint, unaccompanied by an actual withdrawal or an authorised representative, is not a valid reason for non-appearance. [Para 27][Para 41][Para 46][Para 56]

**Obiter dicta:** the description of the four hearing stages and the allocation of the burden of proof between claimant and respondent are general statements of procedure, not steps needed to reach the closure order. [Para 42][Para 53][Para 54]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The hearing is dated 6 February 2015 in the header and procedure [Para 9][Para 14], but the two telephone notifications record it as "06 February 2014" [Para 37][Para 38], and one notification is dated "28 April 2014" where the sequence requires 28 January 2015 [Para 38].
- An unrelated phrase, "in Siem Reap province" (នៅ ខេត្ត សៀមរាប), is inserted mid-sentence in the Article 21 discussion, which concerns a Kampong Speu factory [Para 41].
- The chair's name is printed "Pen Bun Chhea" in the composition [Para 1] but the signature block reads "ប៊ុនណ . Accove" beside "ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា" [Para 60].
- The Prakas No. 099 citation is corrupted with an English OCR fragment ("///mangjn pightpppoom geno gener … Thoug htalandshipmated by យុវនីតិសម្បទា") [Para 23], and the list of ten prior awards is badly garbled, e.g. "លេខ ៩៥៩ ] ០៨យ៉ុង វ៉ា ទី១" and "លេខ ២៧០/១៣-៨ស្តារ ស្ពត វែរ" [Para 34].
- The three surviving demands are recorded only as the employer's responses in the non-conciliation report; neither party appeared, so no evidence exists on any of them [Para 2][Para 22].
- The conciliation report number is printed "១១១/១៥ កប/កស្ត" at [Para 7] and [Para 18] but the referring paragraph adds "ខេត្ត កំពង់ស្ពឺ" inside the company name [Para 12].
