# Award No. 025/15 — Vanny Five Sin (Arbitration Council, 11 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139134

## 1. Facts
Vanny Five Sin, a garment enterprise at Trapeang Krasaing, Ang Snuol, Kandal, faced thirteen demands filed by the Federation of Democratic Union of Lucky Workers (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ សំណាង កម្មករ) on 25 December 2014. [Para 1][Para 28] Conciliation at the Kandal provincial department on 20 January 2015 failed, and the thirteen non-conciliated points reached the Council on 26 January 2015. [Para 25][Para 29] The panel was formed on 28 January 2015, summoned both parties to a hearing on 5 February 2015 at 08:30, and on that day only a federation official appeared — not in the name of the claimant workers but as spokesperson for the federation president, seeking postponement to 23 February 2015 because the president was busy in Preah Vihear province. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 33][Para 59] The claimant workers themselves never appeared, and the panel could not establish any facts on the thirteen disputed points. [Para 53][Para 66]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must stop discriminating against the union the workers voted to create? [Para 4]
2. Whether the company must stop dismissing the leadership of the Democratic Union of Lucky Workers? [Para 6]
3. Whether the company must add USD 28 on top of the existing base wage? [Para 8]
4. Whether the company must pay 4,000 riel as meal money for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 in the evening? [Para 10]
5. Whether the company must extend employment contracts from 6 months to 1 year? [Para 12]
6. Whether the company must provide a eating area with tables, chairs and a television? [Para 14]
7. Whether the company must equip the clinic room and stock sufficient medicine? [Para 16]
8. Whether the company must pay USD 15 per month for accommodation and travel? [Para 17]
9. Whether the company must raise the "prime" (ព្រីម) payment by USD 15 per month? [Para 18]
10. Whether the company must calculate the prime payment in riel by percentage? [Para 19]
11. Whether the company must make the toilets hygienic and provide hand soap? [Para 20]
12. Whether pregnant women must be allowed to leave 15 minutes before the end of the shift? [Para 21]
13. Whether pregnant women must be allowed one day per month for a health check while keeping wages, prime and other benefits? [Para 22]
14. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration in the absence of the claimant workers? [Para 54]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the thirteen demands were put as conditions of work; through its federation official the claimant side sought an adjournment to 23 February 2015, asserting that only the federation president could represent it and that he was unavoidably in Preah Vihear province. [Para 33][Para 37][Para 70]
**Employer (respondent):** the company denies discriminating against any union and says the two individual workers concerned resigned voluntarily; it asks to apply Prakas No. 282 K.B/Prak of 12 November 2014 on the minimum wage, to renew contracts in three-month periods, and states it has already provided tables and chairs in the eating area (without a television). [Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 13][Para 15]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed for abandonment — the panel terminated the arbitration of case No. 025/15 — Vanny Five Sin; nothing was ordered on the thirteen demands. [Para 88][Para 89]

The panel framed the dispositive question itself: whether to continue in the absence of the claimant. [Para 54] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 S.K.B.Y. of 21 April 2004 allows the panel to proceed or to close the file and issue an award where a duly invited party fails to appear without valid reason, and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules repeats the power subject to the party having been properly notified of date, time and place. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] Following its own reading in ten earlier awards (case No. 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-EM and W 3, 138/08-Malin, 11/11-Chun Tech, 131/12-Master & Frank, 108/13-Atto and 332/14-T&K German), the panel treated Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and absence of a valid reason. [Para 57][Para 58]

Condition one was satisfied: invitations No. 115 and No. 114 K.B/A.K/V.K/L.K.A. of 28 January 2015 went to the federation president and the company director with the date, place and contact numbers, followed by a telephone confirmation on the same day. [Para 59][Para 60] Condition two was satisfied on a substantive reading of "appearance": the party must both be present at the hearing and take part through all four stages (introduction and conflict explanation, procedure and issue clarification, conciliation if agreed, and hearing to verdict). [Para 64][Para 65] Only a federation official attended, as spokesperson, not the claimant party, so the claimant did not appear in the sense of Article 21. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 68] Condition three was satisfied: the stated reason — the president's business in Preah Vihear — was never substantiated, the official could not say why no deputy, secretary or other official could substitute, and the requested date of 23 February 2015 fell after the award was due on 18 February 2015. [Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 72][Para 74][Para 75]

The panel then held that a claimant bears the burden of presenting its own allegations, reasons and evidence, with the respondent's burden arising only afterwards; the claimant having defaulted and offered no acceptable excuse, the panel deemed the claim abandoned and closed the proceedings. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 81][Para 82][Para 83][Para 84]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been duly summoned with precise notice of date, time and place, fails to attend either in person or through a representative with authority to act as the party, and offers no substantiated reason for the absence, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration without examining the merits, even if a union official attends to request an adjournment; sending a spokesperson is not appearance. [Para 56][Para 64][Para 67][Para 84][Para 86]

**Obiter dicta:** the four-stage description of an arbitration hearing and the allocation of the burden of proof between claimant and respondent are general statements of procedure, not steps needed to reach the closure order. [Para 65][Para 80][Para 81]
