# Award No. 124/15 — Seed 153 (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mr Ly Tai Seng for the employer party, Mr Tu Si Phan for the worker party, Mr P Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139203

## 1. Facts
Seed 153 (Cambodia) Co., Ltd cuts and sews garments at Trapeang Krasang, Po Sen Chey, Phnom Penh, with about 400 workers. [Para 50][Para 51] The Khmer Workers' Spirit federation filed 13 claims after a strike, and conciliation on 19 May 2015 settled none of them. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] At the hearing of 09 June 2015 issue 02 was settled and the workers withdrew 11 more (01, 03, 05 to 13), leaving only the transport claim. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] On that claim the parties agreed the practice: the company pays USD 7 a month in travel and lodging allowance and provides a car carrying all workers to the factory and home again. [Para 58][Para 59] The local union was not yet registered, and only two workers — Ms S Srey Mom and Mr T — remained as claimants. [Para 52][Para 57]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide transport by car, three-wheeled vehicle or cash (equivalent to USD 380 divided among 15 workers) for workers travelling from Ekareach Road to the company and home again? [Para 58]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer used to run a car from Ekareach Road to the factory and back, stopping it has made travel expensive, and Article 390 of the Labour Law prevents an employer from taking back a benefit more favourable than the law that it has already granted. [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]
**Employer (respondent):** it notified workers in April 2015 that transport for the Ekareach Road area would stop; the company's rule is that a car is provided only where at least 15 workers travel, and the number on that route fell from 20 to 7 and then to 5; the law does not oblige an employer to keep a benefit it once granted, and the USD 7 allowance continues. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67][Para 68][Para 73][Para 74]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to resume providing transport for workers living in the Ekareach Road area to the factory and back home. [Para 110][Para 111]

The panel framed the question as whether the employer's provision of transport from Ekareach Road was a "past practice" (ការអនុវត្ត កន្លងមក). [Para 79] It defined past practice as a course of conduct long followed, consistently, clearly, stably, and recognised and accepted by both workers and employer; it need not be written down. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82][Para 84] Article 65(2) and (3) of the Labour Law were cited in support, together with earlier awards on annual leave, wage payment, bonuses and meal allowances. [Para 83][Para 86]

Applying that test, the panel found the employer had a policy of providing transport to all workers in the company, including the Ekareach Road route, which had existed since the factory opened and continued to the present; the practice was clear, stable and accepted by both sides. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 93] It then asked whether the employer was nonetheless bound to continue it. [Para 94] Following Award No. 95/09, a past practice may lawfully be brought to an end in specific and genuine circumstances: where the parties agree to end it, where they agree a new practice in its place, or where the original circumstances that grounded the practice have changed. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 99]

The panel found none of these. Although the employer notified the workers in April 2015 that transport would stop, it did not consult them to find a new rule to replace the old one. [Para 100] The asserted "15 workers per car" rule had never been reduced to writing, and the company could not point to any document setting its terms and conditions. [Para 101][Para 102] The employer had also kept running transport for another area, Village 8 Mok, where only about 10 workers travelled, which undercut the claim that 5 workers on the Ekareach route justified withdrawal. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107] The employer had not shown that the original circumstances behind the practice had changed, and the change had not been accepted by the workers concerned. [Para 107][Para 108] Article 390 independently forbids an employer from withdrawing a benefit more favourable than the law once granted. [Para 64]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that has consistently and openly provided a benefit since the enterprise opened — here, transport for a group of workers — creates a past practice binding on it under Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 390, which cannot be withdrawn unilaterally: the practice ends lawfully only where the parties agree to end or replace it, or where the original circumstances change, and the employer bears the burden of proving that change. An unwritten internal rule invoked to justify the withdrawal, applied inconsistently to other groups of workers, does not discharge that burden. [Para 84][Para 95][Para 102][Para 108][Para 109]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's catalogue of earlier awards treating annual leave, wage payment, bonuses and meal allowances as past practice, and its remark that production-line arrangements under the employer's management are not past practice, are general illustrations not needed for the order. [Para 86][Para 87]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The complaint is numbered inconsistently: the 13 points run 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, then "១-" again, 7, 8, "១៩-" (for 9), 10, 11, 12, 13 [Para 2]–[Para 14], while the panel's withdrawal list uses 01, 03, 05 to 13 [Para 27].
- The employer's name is printed as ស៊ីដ ១៥៣ (ខេមបូឌា) in the heading but as ស៊ូ ដ ១៥៣ at [Para 20], and the business registration number is given as Inv. 1883E/2012 [Para 50] with no date.
- The number of workers on the Ekareach route is given as 20 falling to 7 then 5 [Para 70][Para 73], but the panel later refers to "១៥ នាក់" and to "០៦ ខែ" of continued service in a sentence that mixes the two [Para 105].
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "25 May 2023 2015" [Para 23], and the employer's notification date is given as April 2015 [Para 65][Para 74] while the panel's findings place the withdrawal from May 2015 [Para 98].
- The chair's name appears as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា in the heading and signature block but as ថៃ ន ប៊ុន ណា immediately above it [Para 113].
- The hearing notice times conflict — "ម៉ោង ១០ … ១៤ និង 00 នាទី រសៀល" [Para 24] — and stray fragments ("The", "VNA", "2-", "អ្នកស្រី") interrupt sentences at [Para 80][Para 91][Para 112].
