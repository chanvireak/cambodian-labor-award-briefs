# Award No. 028/15 — UD S Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vun Hun for the employer party, Suong Sophal for the worker party, Ang Eng Thong chair) · Doc ID 139137

## 1. Facts
UD S Garment Co., Ltd, on National Road 6A, Prek Liep, Phnom Penh, faced a collective dispute filed by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia seeking improved working conditions. [Para 1][Para 14] Nine demands were conciliated at the Ministry's labour dispute department, six settled and three left unresolved at the final session of 21 January 2015, the referral report No. 084 of 27 January 2015 reaching the Council on 28 January 2015. [Para 11][Para 15][Para 16] The panel was formed on 30 January 2015 and set a hearing for 11 February 2015 at 14:00; invitation No. 144 of 2 February 2015 was signed for by the union on the same day. [Para 13][Para 17][Para 27][Para 28][Para 44] The employer attended through its administration chief; the claimant union did not appear and appointed no authorised representative. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 20][Para 51] The panel therefore found no facts on the three demands. [Para 29]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must add USD 28 to the old base wage for all workers from January 2015 onwards? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must stop deducting the regular attendance bonus by percentage when a worker takes lawful leave? [Para 5]
3. Whether the employer must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel per day to each worker? [Para 7]
4. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration in the absence of the claimant union? [Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union demanded the USD 28 wage increase, an end to percentage deductions from the attendance bonus for workers on lawful leave, and a 4,000 riel daily lunch allowance. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7] It did not appear at the hearing and offered the secretariat no reason for the absence. [Para 20][Para 55]
**Employer (respondent):** the company says it cannot afford the wage increase but is discussing a further rise and would inform workers by 6 February 2015 at the latest; it keeps the old attendance-bonus rule — deducting USD 1 on the first day and USD 1 on the second, then the whole bonus on the third, for workers with or without permission to be absent; and it cannot afford the lunch allowance. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed for abandonment — the panel terminated the arbitration of case No. 028/15 — UD S Garment; nothing was ordered on the three demands. [Para 68][Para 69]

The panel framed the question itself: whether to continue where the claimant, the party that brought the case, is absent. [Para 30] Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 S.K.B.Y. of 21 April 2004 requires the panel to summon the parties to state their claims in person and to table the necessary documents; Article 20 obliges parties to attend every meeting to which they are summoned; Article 21 lets the panel either proceed in the absence of a duly invited party who fails to appear without valid reason, or close the file by issuing an award; Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules repeats that choice, on condition of proper notice of date, time and place. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35] Following a long line of earlier awards, the panel applied three cumulative conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid reason. [Para 38][Para 41][Para 42]

Condition one was met: invitation No. 144 of 2 February 2015 gave the date, place and contact details and was signed for by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia on 2 February 2015. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] Condition two was met: "appearance before the panel" means both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict explanation, procedure and issue clarification, conciliation if agreed, and hearing to verdict — and Article 19 allows appearance in person or through a lawyer or a person authorised in writing; the claimant did neither. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53] Condition three was met: the claimant gave the secretariat no valid reason for staying away. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

All three conditions being satisfied, the panel held that a claimant must come before it to state its claims and produce its documents, and that the respondent's burden arises only once the claimant has done so. [Para 58][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] The claimant having defaulted, the panel deemed the claim abandoned, found no need to continue, and closed the arbitration. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the party that brought the collective dispute is duly summoned with precise notice, fails to attend either in person or through a representative authorised in writing, and gives no substantiated reason, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration without examining the merits, however ready the respondent is to be heard. [Para 34][Para 48][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the four-stage description of an arbitration hearing and the sequence of burdens between claimant and respondent are general statements of procedure, not steps needed to reach the closure order. [Para 48][Para 61][Para 62]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The complaint is said to have been received on 29 July 2014 [Para 14], yet the conciliation it led to concluded on 21 January 2015 and the referral report is dated 27 January 2015 [Para 15][Para 16]; the 2014 date is as printed.
- The employer-party arbitrator is "សេង វូន ហ៊ុន" in the composition [Para 1] but "សេង វូច ហ៊ុន", twice on one line, in the signature block [Para 70].
- The company name is abbreviated to "យូ ឌី អេស" and "យូ ឌី អេស ហ្គាមេន ខូអិល ធឺ" in the operative parts, and misspelt "យូរ ឌី អេស" in the fact-finding paragraph [Para 16][Para 29][Para 69].
- The list of precedent awards is badly garbled by OCR, e.g. "លេខ ១៣៨០៨-ម៉ាលីន", "លេខ ៣០/១០-ក្ខូប័ល អាបាំ រែលេខ ១៣០/១៣" and "លេខ ២៧០/១៣-៨ ស្តារ ស្ពត វែរ" [Para 41], as is the quotation of Article 21, which degenerates into "ក្នុង ករណី ដែល ជាតិ ណាមួយ … ត្រូវ ហើយ តែ បែរ ផាន់កាន់ មិន បាន បង្ហាញ ខ្លួន" [Para 54].
- The three demands survive only as described in the non-conciliation report, with no evidence heard on any of them [Para 29].
- Stray page marks and English fragments ("New", "Allia", "hyll", "-៧-", "-៨-") interrupt the text at [Para 3], [Para 43], [Para 59] and [Para 70].
