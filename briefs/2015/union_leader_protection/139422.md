# Award No. 232/15 — Blum Fiel (Cambodia) Knitwear (Arbitration Council, 4 September 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Ven Pov for the worker party, Kong Phalapha chair) · Doc ID 139422

## 1. Facts
Workers of Blum Fiel (Cambodia) Knitwear, a factory in Kampong Tralach district, Kampong Chhnang province, transferred their rights to the Free Trade Union of Workers of the Kingdom of Cambodia, which filed 12 claims — piece rates for ironing, contracting, wage look-ahead, seniority and function allowances, laundry and scanning rates, no wage deductions during the strike, and no discrimination against Muslim applicants. [Para 1][Para 19] Conciliation at the provincial Department of Labour on 21 August 2015 settled none of the 12 claims, which reached the Secretariat on 24 August 2015, and the hearing was set for 1 September 2015 at 08:30. [Para 16][Para 20][Para 40] The employer attended through its administrative assistant; the union did not appear, having telephoned on 26 August 2015 to say it would not take part. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration when the claimant worker party, though duly invited, fails to appear and gives no valid reason? [Para 35][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** by telephone, that the union would not join the proceedings because the case was not its own original complaint and it would bring a separate case with 18 claims later. [Para 26][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent):** attended the hearing through its representative and produced a transfer-of-rights letter; it advanced no position on the 12 claims, and the panel recorded no facts on them because the claimant was absent. [Para 24][Para 27][Para 34]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel held the claimant had abandoned its claims, declined to continue the arbitration and terminated proceedings in case 232/15. No sum was ordered. [Para 61][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]

The panel framed the only question as procedural and began from Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council procedural rules, which allow the panel either to continue in a party's absence or to close the case by award. [Para 35][Para 36] Following earlier awards, it distilled three conditions for proceeding in absentia: the party was duly invited, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason for non-appearance. [Para 38][Para 39]

On the first condition it found that letter No. 1164 of 26 August 2015 had been issued to the union president with the date and place of hearing, contact details and a direction to attend at 08:30 on 1 September 2015. [Para 40] The union official refused to accept the letter, but the panel held notification was still effected, and the union's own telephone call confirmed it knew of the hearing; the condition was met. [Para 41][Para 43][Para 44] On the second, it read "appearance before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction, explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation, and hearing — none of which occurred, so the condition was met. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 49] On the third, it found the union's stated reason — that the complaint was not originally its own — invalid, since the workers had transferred their rights to it and it was the party representing them. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55]

The panel added that a claimant bears the duty to appear, state its case and supply documents and evidence, and that only after it has done so does any burden shift to the respondent; with the claimant absent the panel could establish no facts at all on the 12 claims. [Para 34][Para 56][Para 57][Para 59] It therefore treated the claims as abandoned and closed the file without reaching their merits. [Para 61][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant, having been duly notified of the date, time and place of hearing, does not appear and offers no valid reason, the panel treats the claim as abandoned and terminates the arbitration by award instead of deciding the merits; refusing delivery of the invitation does not defeat notification where the party otherwise knows of the hearing, and a claimant's assertion that the complaint was not originally its own is not a valid reason when the workers transferred their rights to it. [Para 38][Para 43][Para 52][Para 61]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's four-stage description of an arbitration hearing, and its general allocation of the burden of proof between claimant and respondent, were explanatory rather than determinative, since no evidence was heard. [Para 45][Para 56][Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel's constitution date is garbled as "26 August 2022 2019 2015" [Para 22]; other dates place it in 2015.
- The hearing date is printed at [Para 18] amid a duplicated fragment of the Council's address, and the record at [Para 46] and [Para 48] contains stray years ("2017") and broken phrases ("from the date").
- The company name survives only in Khmer transliteration and is rendered here phonetically; it appears variously as "Blum Fiel", "Bloom Fiel" and "Blum Fiel Nit Thoeur", and "Nit Thoeur" is likely the word for knitwear [Para 1][Para 21][Para 65].
- The number of claimant workers is inconsistent: the union is said to represent "10" claimants at [Para 52] while 12 claims and an 18-claim follow-on case are recorded elsewhere [Para 26][Para 51].
- Prakas No. 099 is dated "25th 21 April 2004" at [Para 36] and "12 21 April 2004" at [Para 55]; the correct date, 21 April 2004, appears at [Para 14] and [Para 35].
- The signature block at [Para 67] is partly lost to OCR ("movere as <un> (1997"), leaving the chair's name only in the header.
