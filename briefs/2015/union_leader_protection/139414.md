# Award No. 215/15 — Wai Sin 2 (Arbitration Council, 25 August 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Yu Soun Thy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair, chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139414

## 1. Facts
The employer operates at National Road 21, Kampong Thkol village, Samphov Poun commune, Koh Thom district, Kandal province. [Para 1][Para 2] On 4 August 2015 the Khmer Youth Voice Union Federation asked the provincial labour department to help improve a set of working conditions; conciliation on 7 August 2015 failed on all 10 demands, which were transmitted to the Council on 11 August 2015. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19] The hearing was fixed for 21 August 2015 and both parties were invited by letters of 18 August 2015. [Para 16][Para 30][Para 31] Neither party appeared; the secretariat telephoned the federation's secretary-general, who asked by telephone for the hearing to be moved to another day. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 55]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Ms S, the union president, into work and pay her wages and bonuses up to the date of return? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must provide a mid-day meal allowance of 4,000 riel? [Para 4]
3. Whether the employer must pay an attendance bonus of 15 US dollars while deducting the statutory rate and the days workers take lawful special leave? [Para 5]
4. Whether the employer must build a nursery and a breastfeeding room as required by the Labour Law? [Para 6]
5. Whether the employer must pay 1x2+1 for overtime worked on public holidays and Sundays, plus an 8000 riel meal allowance? [Para 7]
6. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of 15 US dollars? [Para 8]
7. Whether the employer must refrain from dismissing a worker who, having a pressing personal matter, sends word through friends instead of applying for leave? [Para 9]
8. Whether the employer must maintain wages and bonuses unchanged when workers strike to demand better conditions? [Para 10]
9. Whether the employer must deduct 2,000 riel per month from each worker who voluntarily joins the Khmer Youth Voice Union and pay it to the federation? [Para 11]
10. Whether the employer must implement the agreement of 14 July 2015? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union federation pursued all 10 demands to the Council after conciliation failed entirely, seeking wage, meal, bonus, nursery, overtime, travel and check-off terms and enforcement of the 14 July 2015 agreement. [Para 18][Para 19] Its representative, the federation secretary-general, did not attend and asked by telephone for a postponement, explaining that the federation had forgotten the hearing date. [Para 23][Para 55]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer did not attend the hearing and gave no reason for its absence. [Para 22][Para 25]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel deemed the claimant to have abandoned its claims and terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 215/15 Wai Sin 2, without examining any of the 10 demands. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 68]

The panel framed the only question as whether it should continue where the claimant party was absent, and set out Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, requiring parties to be invited to explain their claims in person and to submit documents, Article 20 requiring parties to attend every meeting convened, and Article 21 permitting the Council either to proceed in a party's absence or to close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35] Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to Prakas No. 099 repeats that choice and requires that the party be properly notified of the date, time and place before a decision is taken. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] The panel distilled three conditions for closing a case in a party's absence: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid reason for the non-appearance, and noted that it had applied them consistently in a long line of awards. [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]

On the first condition, it found the claimant had been invited by letter No. 1107 of 18 August 2015 stating the date, place and contact numbers, and that the secretariat telephoned again on the hearing day. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47] On the second, it read "appearance before the panel" in Article 21 to require both presence at the hearing and participation throughout, including the four stages of introduction, procedure, conciliation and hearing, and found the claimant absent from all of them; it also invoked Article 19, under which a party may appear in person, with an accompanying person, a lawyer or another person holding written authority. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] On the third, it held that forgetting the hearing date, the reason the federation gave by telephone, was not a valid reason. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

The panel then reasoned that the burden of setting out the claim, its grounds and its evidence lies on the claimant, the respondent's burden arising only once the claimant has discharged its own. [Para 60][Para 62] Because the claimant never appeared to discharge that burden, and had no valid excuse, the claim was treated as abandoned and there was no need to proceed. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66] It accordingly closed the case by award under Article 21 and rule 4.7. [Para 67][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant party, properly notified of the date, time and place, fails to appear and offers no valid reason, the Council treats the claim as abandoned and closes the arbitration by award without examining the merits; forgetting the hearing date is not a valid reason. [Para 40][Para 56][Para 65][Para 67]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's four-stage description of an arbitration hearing, and its observation that the respondent's evidential burden arises only after the claimant has discharged its own, are general statements about procedure not needed to close this file. [Para 50][Para 62]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's constitution is dated "១៣ ខែសីហា ឆ្នាំ ២០២២ ២០១៥", carrying a stray extra year. [Para 20] Rule 4.7 is cited as dated "ថ្ងៃទី ២៥ ទី២១ ខែមេសា", and the same recital of rule 4.7 at line 38 attaches the word "នាក់" to the rule number. [Para 36][Para 38] The jurisdictional recital reads "ជំពូក ទី ១២ ផ្នែក ទី២៤ ពីរ(ខ)" with a stray numeral inserted. [Para 13] The employer is printed as "វ៉ៃ ស៊ិន ២" on the face of the award and as "វ៉ៃ ស៊ិន II" and "វ៉ៃ ស៊ិន 2" in the recitals. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 28] The award states that both parties were absent without reason, yet its reasoning is devoted entirely to the claimant's absence, and it records that the federation asked for a postponement while elsewhere reciting that no reason was given at all. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 55] The signature block is partly destroyed by OCR, leaving the employer-appointed arbitrator's name legible but reducing the others to noise. [Para 69]
