# Award No. 223/15 — Gold Den Hing (Cambodia) Garment (Arbitration Council, 15 September 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambona for the employer party, Tuon Si Phan for the worker party, Pen Bun Chea chair) · Doc ID 139417

## 1. Facts
Gold Den Hing (Cambodia) Garment sews clothing at Prey Lvea, Chom Chao, Por Sen Chey, Phnom Penh, with about 297 workers. [Para 33][Para 34] Mr S, a machine mechanic, joined in December 2014 on a written two-month probation contract; after it expired the employer let him keep working without any written contract, and he refused to sign two successive three-month fixed-term contracts the employer offered. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 50][Para 51] He was elected president of the local union on 27 July 2015, a union not yet registered with the Ministry, and on 10 August 2015, after the lunch break, he was barred from entering the factory. [Para 35][Para 48] Of ten demands seven were settled and two withdrawn at the hearing, leaving only his reinstatement and back pay. [Para 18][Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take Mr S back to his former job and pay him wages from the day of dismissal until he returns? [Para 42]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the dismissal had no valid reason and broke the procedure for terminating an open-ended contract, since he worked on without any written fixed-term contract after probation; it was also union discrimination because he had just been elected union president. [Para 44][Para 45]
**Employer (respondent):** it cannot take him back or pay back wages; this was the expiry of a fixed-term contract, not a dismissal, he had refused to sign the renewal, and the employer never received any notice about the union election. [Para 44][Para 58][Para 64]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to take Mr S back to the same job and to pay him wages from the date of dismissal until the date he returns. [Para 116][Para 117]

Article 67(1) and (7) requires a fixed-term contract to be in writing and to state a definite end date, and provides that a contract not put in writing becomes a contract without a fixed term; both parties accepted there was no written contract after probation, so his engagement was open-ended. [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63][Para 65]

It followed that ending the relationship was a dismissal under an open-ended contract, not a mere non-renewal at expiry, which is what the employer pleaded. [Para 67]

On protection, Article 293 requires the labour inspector's prior agreement before a staff delegate or a candidate may be dismissed, and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 extends that to union candidates: 45 days before the election, and 45 days after it for those not elected, while an elected leader keeps protection for at least 45 days after the election or for a reasonable period such as until the union applies for registration, following Award No. 40/10 (Meng Yan). [Para 69][Para 72][Para 81][Para 85]

The panel applied its three conditions. Mr S was of the protected category, having been elected president on 27 July 2015; the dismissal on 10 August 2015 fell 15 days after the election, inside the protected period; and the union had given notice of the election and of his name, by handing a letter to company security on 28 July, by distributing 200 leaflets at the gate, and by EMS post on 29 July which the employer refused to accept. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 96][Para 99][Para 101]

On the last point the panel followed Award No. 283/14 (Lian Fa), holding that notification by post is a valid means and that an employer who refuses delivery is treated as having been informed, so the employer's denial of knowledge was not considered. [Para 105][Para 108][Para 110]

All three conditions being met, the employer was bound to seek the labour inspector's authorisation before terminating, which it did not do. [Para 111][Para 115]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed-term contract that is not in writing with a stated end date is an open-ended contract under Article 67(1) and (7), so letting it run out is a dismissal, not a non-renewal, and the employer must justify it and observe the procedure for open-ended contracts. [Para 62][Para 65][Para 67]

An elected union leader dismissed within 45 days of the election without the labour inspector's prior authorisation is protected under Article 293 and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 and must be reinstated with wages for the whole period out of work, whatever the employer's reason for the dismissal; notice of the election sent by registered post is valid even if the employer refuses to accept it, and the employer is deemed to know of it. [Para 85][Para 105][Para 115][Para 116]

**Obiter dicta:** the discussion of Article 4 of Prakas No. 305, which protects founders and members while registration is pending and for 30 days after it, was set out although Mr S relied on his status as an elected leader rather than as a founder or member. [Para 75][Para 82][Para 83]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The employer's name is printed inconsistently: the face reads ហ្គូល ដិន ហ៊ីង (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន with an unexplained "នូហ្វេ ក ធ្វើ រីង" appended [Para 1], while the findings use ហ្គោល ដិន ហ៊ីង [Para 37], ហ្គុល ដិន ហ៊ីង [Para 30] and, in one minute, an unrelated "ហល វិទ" [Para 27].
- The probation term is two months for the workers [Para 45] and three months for the employer [Para 49], and the award never resolves it, resting instead on the absence of any written contract after probation.
- Two dismissal dates appear: an oral announcement of termination on 3 August 2015 [Para 47] and the physical exclusion on 10 August 2015 [Para 48]. The panel counts the 45 days from the later date [Para 96] and orders back pay from "the date of dismissal" without saying which.
- The three unresolved demands are listed in the header as items 1 to 3 [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6] but the reasons use the conciliation report's numbering (issues 02, 03 and 09), and the two withdrawn ones are described as 09 and 03 [Para 18], so the same demand carries two numbers.
- The conciliation report is dated 17 August 2015 in the procedural recital [Para 10] but 14 August 2015 when cited as evidence [Para 14]; the extension of the award deadline is corrupted to "ឆ្នាំ ២ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" [Para 22].
- OCR damage: the Article 293 quotation is garbled ("រាល់ ការណ៍ បញ្ឈប់") [Para 69], Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 is introduced with a stray "ចែង ២០ ថា" [Para 71], a bare "27" sits in the reasoning [Para 115], "2017" appears mid-sentence [Para 75], and the chair's signature block is illegible [Para 119].
