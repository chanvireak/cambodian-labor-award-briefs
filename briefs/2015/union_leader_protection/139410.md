# Award No. 122/15 — Im Vy International Garment (Cambodia), Branches 1 and 3 (Arbitration Council, 27 May 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Chhiv Phirum (employer-appointed), Venh Pov (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (chair, chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139410

## 1. Facts
Eight union federations filed a complaint on 28 April 2015 giving notice of a strike and demanding improvement of 17 points of working conditions at the employer's two branches. [Para 31] Conciliation on 11 May 2015 settled 5 points and left 12 unresolved, which were transmitted to the Council on 19 May 2015. [Para 32][Para 33] The workers struck on 18 May 2015; the Council issued Instruction No. 017/15 on 22 May 2015 ordering an immediate suspension of the strike and return to work from the morning of 23 May 2015. [Para 34][Para 35] The workers did not comply, and the employer reported by telephone that pickets blocked vehicles at the factory gate. [Para 36][Para 38]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay 100% wage and other benefits during the suspension of work from 1 to 28 February 2015, calculated distinctly for piece-rate and statutory-wage workers? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must stop importing semi-finished garment products that cause disruption and shortages of work? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay severance to all workers on unfixed-duration contracts with 5 years' seniority or more? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must raise the piece rate by 28% in line with the minimum-wage increase? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must pay USD 1 per day for lunch and 2,000 riel per hour for evening overtime meals? [Para 10]
6. Whether the company must pay an attendance bonus of USD 15 per month? [Para 12]
7. Whether the company must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 15 per month? [Para 14]
8. Whether the company must pay USD 15 per month as a milk allowance to women who have given birth, until the child is 18 months old? [Para 16]
9. Whether the company must stop deducting special leave from annual leave? [Para 18]
10. Whether the company must pay additional seniority indemnity to 2 workers with 11 years' service or more? [Para 20]
11. Whether the company must pay a USD 2 attendance allowance equally to workers in the automatic building and the ordinary building on public holidays? [Para 22]
12. Whether the company must not impose extra work pressure following the Ministry's wage increase, particularly on workers in the automatic building? [Para 24]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 12 unresolved demands remained outstanding in full, each framed as an entitlement the company must grant. [Para 2][Para 10][Para 20] At the 26 May 2015 pre-hearing the president of the Cambodian Industrial Union Federation confirmed that workers were still striking in front of the factory in disregard of the Council's instruction. [Para 64]
**Employer (respondent):** on every demand the company had no capacity to grant it, offering at most 60% on the wage claim, and said it could not comply with the import demand at all; it also maintained a nursery at the workplace against the milk-allowance claim. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 17] It did not attend the pre-hearing, stating by telephone that the strike had put a picket line across the factory gate so that vehicles could not leave. [Para 65]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — arbitration proceedings in case No. 122/15 Im Vy International Garment (Branches 1 and 3) were terminated, with no ruling on any of the 12 merits demands. [Para 68]

The panel framed the only question it reached as whether arbitration procedure should continue at all. [Para 50] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires disputing parties to suspend all dispute measures, including strikes and lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, during the Council's proceedings, and to attend every meeting convened. [Para 50][Para 51] The panel read that provision, as it had in earlier awards, to mean it will not proceed while workers are striking or the employer is locking out, citing Award No. 59/06-Sang Vo, No. 39/08-Chong Youv, Nos. 137/09 and 138/09-Pouro Kanh, No. 103/10-Vaythith, No. 110/11-Simanou, No. 104/13-Sabrina (Cambodia) and No. 019/14-Dongdu Tek Stay (Cambodia). [Para 52][Para 53] In case No. 04/04-IMSI it had gone further, issuing a notice on 29 January 2004 that where a party has no right to strike during proceedings the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on the dispute, and may take the case up again only when all the unions stop striking. [Para 54][Para 55] The same approach was taken in case No. 18/04 (Kambodiana, Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Hotel Le Royal Angkor and others), point 9 of the second instruction of 22 April 2004 requiring all workers to cease strike and protest, with point 11 reserving the Council's right to terminate if a party breached any term. [Para 56][Para 57] Case No. 77/05-France Nite Thing (point 4, 24 December 2005) and case No. 040/15-FY Cambodia Fashion Limited had likewise ended proceedings where the strike continued. [Para 58][Para 59]

On the facts, the panel found no evidentiary findings to make because it held no hearing. [Para 49] It found the strike still ongoing, on the union president's own statement and the employer's telephone report, and held that a continued strike in breach of Instruction 017/15 barred it from continuing the hearing. [Para 66][Para 67] It therefore terminated the proceedings under Article 20 of Prakas 099 without examining the merits of the 12 demands. [Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party continues a strike, in defiance of the Council's own instruction to suspend it, the Council will not conduct a hearing or rule on the merits: it terminates the arbitration under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, and the dispute may be taken up again once the strike has ended. [Para 50][Para 55][Para 68]

**Obiter dicta:** the recital of seven earlier awards and the reservation in case No. 18/04 that the Council keeps the right to end proceedings on any breach are explanatory background, not necessary to this outcome. [Para 53][Para 57]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 1][Para 69] The employer is printed as "Im Vy" in the style of cause and as "Im Vay"/"Im 5" in the procedural recitals. [Para 33][Para 34] The letter of 21 May 2015 carries the date "២០ 20008" and its company name is reduced to OCR noise ("BeBon Growng Prompole Formanch Assance"). [Para 34] The paragraph at lines 62–63 repeats the same sentence about the Cambodian Industrial Union Federation attending, and line 25 contains untranslatable OCR debris ("Nive to 400 @,@ 093"). [Para 25][Para 62][Para 63] The internal instruction is numbered 017/15 in the reasons and 017/15 in the document list, but the invitation letters are 612 and 613 in one place and 613 alone in another.
