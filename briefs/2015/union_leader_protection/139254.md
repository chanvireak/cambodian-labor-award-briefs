# Award No. 181/15 — Victco Handbags (Cambodia) Industrial Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 24 July 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Kong Phalha chair) · Doc ID 139254

## 1. Facts
The company, a handbag factory, operates on National Road 4 at Trapeang Sang Chek, Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu; the workers were represented by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia and the Labour Movement Union Federation, and no worker representative appeared at the hearing. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 16]
On 9 July 2015 the Kampong Speu Labour Department received their complaint on a single demand: if the company moves payday from the 8th to the 10th of each month, it should compensate by adding lunch money and travel or lodging money up to USD 15. [Para 4][Para 10] Conciliation on 9 July 2015 failed, the one unsettled issue was referred on 14 July 2015, and the hearing was fixed for 23 July 2015 at 08:30. [Para 7][Para 9][Para 12] The employer attended as summoned; the workers did not, and the panel expressly found that it could establish no facts on the demand. [Para 15][Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, where the company changes the monthly payday from the 8th to the 10th, it must add lunch money and travel or lodging money so that the total reaches USD 15 per day? [Para 4]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all when the claimant workers, duly summoned, did not appear? [Para 28]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they pursued the single demand through both unions; on the hearing day the Free Workers' Union officer told the secretariat by telephone that the disputed point had already been agreed with the employer, so they would not attend, while the Labour Movement Union Federation said it was waiting to file a complaint with the buyer instead. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 20]
**Employer (respondent):** it appeared and filed a letter of authority dated 22 July 2015 appointing Mr H Borin as direct assistant, together with minutes of the 9 July 2015 meeting; it put no merits case, because none was reached. [Para 15][Para 21][Para 22]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 181/15, treating the claim as abandoned, and ordered nothing in riel or USD. [Para 54][Para 58][Para 59]

Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules let the panel either continue in a party's absence or close the file by award, provided the absent party was properly notified of the date, time and place of the proceedings. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30] Following its own earlier awards, the panel distilled three cumulative conditions for closing a file in default: proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid excuse. [Para 31][Para 32]

On the first condition it found summons No. 916 of 16 July 2015 addressed to both union presidents, stating the hearing date of 23 July 2015, the venue, and contact telephone and fax numbers. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 36] On the second it read "appearance before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation throughout the four stages — introduction and conflict check, explanation of procedure and framing of the issues, conciliation if agreed, and the hearing itself — none of which occurred. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 41] On the third it found no valid reason: the asserted settlement was unsupported, because the panel never received any written agreement on the disputed point from the union and the employer, and the federation's stated intention to go to the buyer was not an excuse for absenting itself. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 45][Para 46]

Having found all three conditions met, the panel held that a claimant bears the first burden of adducing reasons and evidence in support of its demand, and that a claimant who stays away neither discharges that burden nor shows good cause. [Para 47][Para 49][Para 50][Para 52][Para 53] It therefore treated the claim as abandoned, declined to continue, and terminated the proceedings. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant workers have been properly summoned with the date, time and place of the hearing and fail to appear or to adduce any evidence on their demand, and the settlement they claim in excuse is not evidenced by any written agreement in the panel's hands, the three Article 21 conditions are satisfied: the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration under Rule 4.7 without examining the merits. [Para 31][Para 47][Para 54][Para 55]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general remarks that claimants must appear in person to state their case and supply documents, and that the respondent's burden to answer arises only after the claimant has discharged its own, go beyond what the termination required. [Para 49][Para 51]

- The company name is printed with the third element varying between ស្ទៀល, ស្ទ្រៀល and ស្វ្រៀល, and no Latin-script form appears anywhere; the heading transliterates the face of the award [Para 1][Para 5][Para 58].
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "15 July 2019 2015", a year printed twice [Para 13].
- The conciliation report number appears as 1937/15 កប/កស្ត and as 1937/15 កប/ភស្តុ [Para 7][Para 12].
- The reasoning at the third condition repeats a whole clause twice, and the "decision" passage is interrupted by OCR noise, "ព្រឹត្តិការណ៍ 2016 2017 @-@ 099 @-@ provider many" [Para 43][Para 57].
- Line 26 carries a stray question mark in the date of summons No. 916 (១៦ ខែកក្កដា?), and the workers' address line ends in a bare "N" [Para 2][Para 26].
- The merits of the USD 15 demand were never examined, so the award records no finding on the payday change itself [Para 27].
