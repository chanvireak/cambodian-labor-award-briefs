# Award No. 125/15 — Day Den (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 15 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mr M Sambor Na for the employer party, Mr An Non for the worker party, Mr P Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139201

## 1. Facts
Day Den (Cambodia) Co., Ltd operates at Kbal Damrei village, Kakab commune, Po Sen Chey district, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Three federations — Khmer Workers' Safety, Khmer Workers' Peace and the Cambodian Labour Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ពលកម្ម កម្ពុជា) — complained on 04 May 2015 over working conditions, after the company dismissed a union leader, Ms S Neang, on 30 April 2015. [Para 2][Para 20] Of 8 claims, 1 was settled at conciliation on 11 May 2015 and 7 were referred to the panel. [Para 21][Para 22] At the hearing of 02 June 2015 the Peace federation filed a written withdrawal, and the two others confirmed by telephone that they too were withdrawing, promising letters later; only the employer attended. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] By 03 June 2015 the Safety federation had withdrawn all its claims, but the Cambodian Labour Federation never filed a withdrawal and never appeared. [Para 29][Para 30]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back Ms S Neang, a union leader dismissed on 30 April 2015, with her position and benefits preserved? [Para 2][Para 7]
2. Whether the company must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 7 as required by the Labour Law? [Para 5]
3. Whether the company must stop suppressing the rights of workers who joined the union movement? [Para 7]
4. Whether the company must pay the seniority bonus (prime) pro rata when a worker takes lawful leave? [Para 9]
5. Whether the company must provide a midday meal allowance of 2,000 riel? [Para 11]
6. Whether the company must remove the administrative assistant Ms S Kouch from the company? [Para 13]
7. Whether the company must maintain the prime, the daily allowance and other benefits during the strike? [Para 14]
8. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration in the absence of the claimant federation that neither appeared nor withdrew? [Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the three federations held transfers of rights from the company's workers and pressed the seven claims above, the dismissal of Ms S Neang being the core grievance. [Para 2][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** the company stated that it could not take Ms S Neang back, could pay only USD 5 of the USD 7 travel allowance claimed, and had not suppressed union rights; it appeared at the hearing through its general manager. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 24][Para 31]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration of case 125/15 without ruling on any of the seven claims. [Para 67][Para 68]

The panel identified a single procedural question: whether to continue when the claimant does not appear. [Para 39] Prakar 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 allows the panel either to continue in the party's absence or to close the case by award, and Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules repeats the choice, subject to the party having been properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] Earlier awards (Nos 16/07, 27/07, 138/08, 11/11, 131/12, 108/13 and 332/14) read Prakar 21 as requiring three conditions: proper invitation, non-appearance, and no valid excuse. [Para 42][Para 43]

Condition 1 was satisfied: the federation was invited for 02 June 2015 and the secretariat's letters were proved. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] Condition 2 was satisfied: "appearing before the panel" means both attending the hearing and taking part in all four stages of the arbitration — introduction, explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation, and hearing to final submissions — and the claimant did none of them. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51] Condition 3 was satisfied: a telephone statement that the case would be withdrawn, followed by no written withdrawal by 08 June 2015, is not a valid excuse. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56]

Having found all three conditions met, the panel held that a claimant bears the duty to appear, present its arguments and evidence, and that the respondent's duty arises only afterwards; a claimant that does neither is treated as having abandoned its claim. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63] It therefore closed the proceedings instead of deciding the merits. [Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant federation is properly invited, does not appear at any stage, and offers no valid excuse — a promise to withdraw the case later, unfulfilled by the date the panel fixes, is not a valid excuse — the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and close the arbitration under Prakar 21 of Prakas No. 099/04 rather than rule on the merits. [Para 42][Para 54][Para 63][Para 66]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's description of the four stages of an arbitration hearing, and its statement that the respondent's evidential duty arises only after the claimant has discharged its own, are general observations on procedure; no evidence was taken from either side. [Para 48][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The chair's name is printed as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា in the heading [Para 1] and in the signature block, but once as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ណា [Para 69].
- The hearing date is given as 02 June 2015 at [Para 19] and [Para 44], but the secretariat's invitation is dated at "០២ ខែឧសភា" (2 May) 2015 [Para 23], and the panel's constitution and the invitation appear merged in the same sentence [Para 23].
- The employer's representative list is cut off after "២- តាតី" with no name or function [Para 1], and the worker representatives are recorded only as "អវត្តមាន" (absent).
- The conciliation report is dated 20 May 2015 and transmitted 21 May 2015 [Para 17][Para 22], although conciliation itself is recorded as held on 11 May 2015 [Para 21].
- The number of claims is inconsistent: 8 claims with 1 settled gives 7 referred [Para 21][Para 22], but the panel's own list runs to seven items only because item 6 lost its number and the dismissal of Ms S Neang appears both as claim 1 [Para 2] and again as claim 3 [Para 7].
- Stray figures break the text: "១២៥០០" appears inside the operative sentence [Para 67], and "2017" and "រៀន" appear in the reasoning [Para 61][Para 62].
