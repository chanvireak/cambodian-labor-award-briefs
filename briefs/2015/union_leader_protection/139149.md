# Award No. 049/15 — Jin Win In Das Stri (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, chosen by the employer; An Non, chosen by the workers; Run Saray, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139149

## 1. Facts
Jin Win In Das Stri (Cambodia) Co Ltd, a Phnom Penh factory in Khan Sen Sok employing about 650 workers, was struck by 139 workers from 12 to 19 December 2014 and again on 23–24 December 2014. [Para 50][Para 67] On 24 December the employer settled the strike with lump-sum cash payments; most strikers took the money and left or returned to work, but two union leaders — Mr S, the factory branch president, and Mr N, a board member — refused the settlement. [Para 72][Para 74][Para 52][Para 101] Their last fixed-term contracts ran to 27 January 2015 and 10 January 2015 respectively, and neither had yet received final wages or severance. [Para 63][Para 65][Para 66] They requested 14 days' leave on 25 December, were refused, stayed away from 26 December, and on 9 January 2015 were barred by company security; the federation's complaint of 12 January 2015 led to a failed conciliation and referral of the one unresolved issue. [Para 75][Para 78][Para 79][Para 38][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr S and Mr N, the union leaders, and pay them wages and other benefits from the date they were stopped from working? [Para 53][Para 108]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The two men were president and board member of the company's branch of the union federation, so the dispute concerns rights conferred on a professional organisation and is collective; they were stopped without reason, and because they had come forward to help workers press claims over working conditions. [Para 56][Para 52][Para 59]
**Employer (respondent):** The dispute touches only two workers; the branch has no other members and no worker supports or protests for them, so their claims are purely personal; and the two ignored the protective order (ដីការសម្រេចរក្សាការពារ) No. 449 of 19 December 2014 of the Phnom Penh First Instance Court by not returning to work. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 61]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel refused to consider the claim that Mr S and Mr N be taken back and paid wages and other benefits from the date they were stopped from working. [Para 139][Para 140]
The panel first settled who the claimant was. Only a registered professional organisation enjoys the rights the Labour Law grants, and under Article 268 of the Labour Law of 1997 an organisation whose registration papers are lodged with the Ministry is deemed registered if the Ministry does not respond within two months. [Para 93][Para 94] The federation held a registration application receipt dated 18 December 2014 and the hearing was on 23 February 2015 — more than two months later — so the panel found the union had become a lawful professional organisation, that Mr S had been elected branch president, and that he was entitled to represent its members, following awards 62/06 Khli, 30/08 E Garment, 31/08 Sau Sorn Borey, 94/09 Takfat and 116/13 Tuki Sport. [Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 103][Para 105][Para 98]
Turning to its own competence, the panel held that Article 32 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY of 21 April 2004 confines it to collective labour disputes referred under Article 309, and that Article 302 defines a collective dispute by three cumulative conditions as construed in award 10/03 Zaksintex. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 121][Para 122] Conditions (a) — a dispute between some workers and one or more employers — and (b) — a dispute over working conditions and the contract of employment — were satisfied. [Para 125][Para 126][Para 128][Para 129] Condition (c) — that the dispute may make the enterprise run badly or damage social peace — was not: the workers advanced nothing to show their removal could block the production line or disrupt the company, and both conceded that no other union members or supporters remained to press the claim, the union now consisting of the two of them alone. [Para 134][Para 131][Para 132] Because one of the three conditions failed, the dispute was not a collective labour dispute and the panel had no jurisdiction over it. [Para 135][Para 136][Para 137][Para 138] It added that it ordinarily follows the Labour Inspector's and the Ministry's classification of a dispute, absent a clear reason to depart from it. [Para 110][Para 111]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimants are the last two remaining members of a workplace union, no other worker supports or protests for them, and nothing shows that their removal disrupted production or threatened social peace, the claim fails the third condition of Article 302 and is an individual rather than a collective dispute; the Arbitration Council therefore has no jurisdiction, however lawfully the union was registered and however senior the claimants within it. [Para 131][Para 132][Para 134][Para 135]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statements that it normally defers to the Labour Inspector's and Ministry's characterisation of a dispute, and that the union was validly registered and Mr S entitled to represent its members, were not necessary to the jurisdictional dismissal. [Para 110][Para 111][Para 105]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award survives only in Khmer and the OCR is uneven. Threshold dates are printed two ways: the non-conciliation report No. 156 is dated 16 February 2015 in the procedural history and exhibit list but 13 February 2015 where the referral is described [Para 6][Para 45][Para 11]; the union leadership election is dated 04 August 2012 in the evidence section and 04 August 2014 in the reasoning [Para 87][Para 101]; and 2015 is printed where 2014 must be meant, both for the date the two stopped attending ("from 26 December 2015") and inside the quoted protective order of 19 December 2015 [Para 78][Para 85]. The extension of the award date is garbled ("ថ្ងៃទី ២៣ ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០១ ២០១៥") [Para 18], and the operative paragraph describes the claimants as "ទាំង ៣ ពីរ នាក់" (both three persons) [Para 107]. The worker Mr S's name also appears in one spelling as "សូ សំណាង" in the contract list [Para 33]. The union federation is named only in Khmer (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ) and is rendered here literally, not from any printed Latin form. No monetary sum is printed anywhere in the award; the only figures are 650 workers and 139 strikers. [Para 50][Para 67]
