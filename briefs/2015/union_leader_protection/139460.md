# Award No. 282/15 — W V P Trading Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139460

## 1. Facts
W V P Trading Co., Ltd cuts and sews garments in Phnom Penh with about 568 workers. [Para 45][Para 46] A local union was formed on 21 June 2015 and told the company on 22 June 2015, electing Mr Kh as president, Ms M as vice-president and Mr I as secretary, but it had still not been registered with the Ministry of Labour when the case was heard. [Para 39][Para 47][Para 48] Ms M and Mr I were each on successive three-month fixed-duration contracts, expiring 8 September and 12 September 2015; the company gave notice of non-renewal on 1 and 5 September 2015 and each worked to the expiry date. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 57][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73]

## 2. Issues in dispute
Two of five claims remained unresolved. [Para 12][Para 18]
1. Whether the employer must take Ms M, the union vice-president (staff no. 626), back into work and pay her wages from the day of termination to the day of return? [Para 53][Para 81]
2. Whether the employer must take Mr I, the union secretary, back into work and pay his wages from the day of termination to the day of return? [Para 69][Para 108]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** both were removed because of their union roles — Ms M after joining the union and after the strike found her section head, who had previously been friendly, pressing her to work faster; Mr I had been a union committee member and was elected secretary. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 75] The union was elected on 21 June 2015 and notice was taken to the administration, where the chief, being busy, told the president to leave it on the desk. [Para 68][Para 95]
**Employer (respondent):** the contracts simply reached their end and were not renewed; Ms M worked slowly and had quarrelled with her section head on 6 June 2015, telling him to dismiss her if he was not satisfied, and the company did not know either worker held a union post. [Para 3][Para 62][Para 66][Para 80] From August to late September 2015 there was little work and wages were paid at 50%. [Para 65][Para 77]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both claims for reinstatement and back wages were refused. [Para 124][Para 125]

For Ms M the panel found that her last contract expired on 8 September 2015, that notice of non-renewal was given on 1 September 2015 and that she kept working until the expiry date, so the event was the expiry of a fixed-duration contract, not a dismissal. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 85] Following earlier awards, the panel held that the special protection against dismissal cannot be applied to the expiry or invalidity of a contract. [Para 86][Para 87][Para 89] She had already been paid the statutory severance, which the company had prepared for her to collect. [Para 84]

The panel then tested the discrimination allegation separately. Under arts 12 and 279 of the Labour Law an employer may not use union membership or activity as the ground for a disciplinary decision or for terminating a contract, but the party making the accusation bears the burden of proof, and the panel weighs the hearing explanations against the documents. [Para 90][Para 91][Para 96][Para 98][Para 100] It found no fact showing that the non-renewal was connected with union discrimination, and no evidence that the company knew Ms M was a union leader — the only notice was said to have been left on a desk on an unknown date. [Para 102][Para 103][Para 104] Her contract relationship had come to an end, so the claim failed. [Para 106][Para 107]

For Mr I the panel reasoned identically: his last contract expired on 12 September 2015, notice was given on 5 September, he worked until 10 September and took half wages for the final two days, so this too was a non-renewal, and the protection against dismissal did not apply. [Para 109][Para 110][Para 111][Para 112][Para 114] He had already received the 5% severance, payment in lieu of unused annual leave and his final wage. [Para 121] Again no fact or evidence linked the decision to his union role, and the claim was refused. [Para 116][Para 117][Para 119][Para 122][Para 123]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer gives notice before the end of a fixed-duration contract and the worker continues to work until the contract expires, the resulting end of the relationship is a non-renewal by expiry, not a dismissal, so the special protection against dismissal of staff representatives and union leaders does not apply to it. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 89][Para 111][Para 114]
A claim that a non-renewal was union discrimination under arts 12 and 279 of the Labour Law must be proved by the worker, and that includes proving the employer knew of the union post; an unproved assertion that notice was left on an administrative desk does not discharge that burden. [Para 96][Para 103][Para 119]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's account of Ms M's quarrel with her section head on 6 June 2015, and of the general shortage of work and 50% wages from August to September 2015, were background findings that did not decide either claim. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 65]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "05 November 20 2015" [Para 14], and a three-month contract in the evidence list is dated to expire "08 December 2019" where 2015 is plainly meant [Para 22].
- Ms M's severance is said at [Para 58] not to have been paid to her at all, while the reasons record that the company had prepared the statutory severance for her to collect [Para 84]; the award does not resolve which is right.
- Mr I's post is given as secretary at [Para 48] and [Para 69] but the employer's answer has it as vice-president [Para 80].
- Both workers' names are printed in several forms — "E Meng Sreang", "Ei Meng Sreang" and "E Meng Sreaing"; "Im Phas", "Um Phas" and "Im Phos" [Para 2][Para 50][Para 73][Para 122]. They are redacted here.
- The union is described as not yet registered with the Ministry [Para 47] although it had notified the company of its formation on 22 June 2015 [Para 39]; the panel did not say whether registration affected the claim.
- The reasoning breaks off mid-sentence at [Para 66]–[Para 67] ("និយោជិត នោះ ឡើយ"), leaving the employer's position incomplete.
