# Award No. 019/15 — Chung Fai Knitwear FTVY Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Soung Sophal (worker-appointed), Kuy Neam (president) · Doc ID 139132

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a knitwear factory at Prek Ta Long, Chak Angre Krom, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh. The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia lodged a claim on 26 December 2014 that workers who fail to meet their piece-rate output target should nonetheless be paid the minimum wage set by ministerial prakas, from October 2014 onward. Conciliation on the single unresolved issue failed on 14 January 2015 and the file reached the Council on 20 January. The hearing was set for 6 February 2015; the employer attended, the complainant union did not, although its staff had signed for the summons on 28 January. [Para 6][Para 9][Para 14]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must top up the wages of workers who do not sew the full quantity of product to the minimum wage fixed by ministerial prakas, from October 2014? [Para 3]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all when the complainant party, duly summoned, fails to appear and gives no valid reason? [Para 24]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demand, as recorded in the conciliation report, was that workers paid by output who fall short of the target should receive at least the statutory minimum wage from October 2014. No representative appeared to argue it, and no document was filed in support. [Para 3][Para 16]

**Employer (respondent):** the employer attended the hearing but filed no documents and made no substantive submission on the claim, so the record contains no answer to it. [Para 13][Para 15]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed the arbitration without ruling on the merits, treating the claim as abandoned by the complainant. [Para 62][Para 65]

The panel did not reach the minimum-wage question. It first asked whether it could proceed in the absence of the originating party, and set out the three conditions drawn from Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 and rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules: that the party was properly summoned, that it did not appear, and that it gave no valid reason for its absence. Each was then tested. On the first, the summons was issued on 28 January 2015 with the date, time, place and contact details of the hearing, and was signed for by the union's own staff at 9:03 a.m. that day, so service was regular. On the second, the panel read Article 21 as requiring both physical presence and participation through all four stages of the hearing — introduction, explanation of the issues, conciliation and argument — and found that the complainant had attended none of them and had appointed no representative. On the third, the secretariat had received no explanation at all. The three conditions being satisfied, the panel held that the complainant had abandoned its claim and that it need not continue the proceedings. [Para 37][Para 39][Para 41][Para 53][Para 54]

The panel added that an originating party carries the burden of presenting its allegations, reasons and evidence in person, and that the respondent's burden arises only once the complainant has discharged its own. Since the complainant never appeared to discharge it, there was nothing on which to adjudicate. [Para 60]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a complainant fails to appear at a duly convened hearing and offers no valid reason, the claim is deemed abandoned and the panel will close the file by award without examining the merits; a panel will not decide a collective dispute on the papers alone, because the originating party must present its own case in person before any burden passes to the respondent. [Para 54][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that it could establish no facts at all about the wage claim, since neither party had filed documents and the complainant was absent, leaving the substance of the dispute untouched. [Para 23]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The file is thin by design: both parties' document lists read "none", so the brief rests on the procedural record alone. The company's name is printed inconsistently across the award — "Chung Fai Knitwear FTVY", "Jung Fai", "Chung Fai Nit Wear F T Y" and a garbled form in the operative paragraph. A stray "12017" appears at the head of the reasoning, and the closing paragraphs repeat the company name in corrupted form. The panel's reasoning nowhere addresses the minimum-wage question, so the award records no view on it. [Para 15][Para 33][Para 66]
