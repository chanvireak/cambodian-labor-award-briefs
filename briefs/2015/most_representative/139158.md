# Award No. 062/15 — Crystal Martin (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 6 April 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry, chosen by the employer; Liv Sovanna, chosen by the workers; Aing Eng Thong, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139158

## 1. Facts
Crystal Martin (Cambodia) Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន (ខេមបូឌា)) sews trousers at Sre Chea village, Samrong Krom, Porsenchey, Phnom Penh, and told the panel it employs more than 312 workers. [Para 1][Para 37][Para 38] On 25 February 2015 the Labour Disputes Department received a telephone complaint that the workers were striking over working conditions; conciliation the same day covered six points, settled three and left three, and the hearing was held on 13 March 2015. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 12] The Khmer Workers' Spirit federation received transfers of rights from 181 workers by letter of 17 February 2015, and 184 workers are claimants. [Para 40][Para 43] The disputed acts are the employer's refusal to convert every undetermined-duration contract into a fixed-duration one on payment of compensation, and its refusal to raise the attendance bonus from USD 10 to USD 15 a month and the travel and accommodation allowance from USD 08 to USD 15. [Para 2][Para 50][Para 55]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must terminate the undetermined-duration contracts once, paying all compensation due under the Labour Law, and re-sign the workers on fixed-duration contracts? [Para 44][Para 45]
2. Whether the employer must raise the monthly attendance bonus by USD 5, to USD 15? [Para 50][Para 76]
3. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance to USD 15 a month? [Para 55][Para 108]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They want the 5% end-of-contract compensation that fixed-duration contracts attract and undetermined-duration contracts do not, so the employer should close the undetermined contracts and re-sign them for three or six months; workers who leave after three or six months to care for sick parents in the provinces would then receive it. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48] Travel and rent costs have risen and most workers are migrants in rented rooms, so the bonus and the allowance must rise. [Para 52][Para 57]
**Employer (respondent):** It has applied undetermined-duration contracts since the factory opened and continues to apply the law, and it has no intention of terminating them or changing their type. [Para 49][Para 70] The attendance bonus and travel allowance have already been decided by the panel in an earlier case, and in any event it pays what the law requires. [Para 53][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the first demand refused on the merits, the second and third declined consideration; nothing was awarded. [Para 125][Para 126][Para 127]

On issue 1, the panel applied Article 311 of the Civil Code and Article 65(1) of the Labour Law: a contract is the act by which parties with a common will create, modify or extinguish obligations, so only the two parties to the employment contract may terminate it, and one party cannot compel the other to terminate against its will. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] Following awards 34/13 Papilion Tech Style, 138/13 Sun Industry Handbag, 039/14 E-Garment and 308/14 Hui Jian (Cambodia), it found the employer had issued undetermined-duration contracts since opening and held that the workers could not force termination and re-signing contrary to the employer's will; it added that the demand lacked any legal basis, since the 5% compensation is what the law attaches to fixed-duration contracts. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73][Para 74]

On issues 2 and 3, the employer objected that both had been decided in award No. 258/14 Crystal Martin (Cambodia) Ltd of 13 October 2014. [Para 77][Para 109] The panel set out the res judicata principle (អាជ្ញា អស់ ជំនុំ), noting that no procedural rule of the Council addresses it, and adopted the three conditions from award 42/07 Sethanabek: the parties must have litigated the point before, the same issue must have been brought, and the panel must have decided it fully. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 81][Para 82] It found condition one unmet, because the earlier case had 66 claimants and this one has 184, so the parties differ, and it therefore had jurisdiction to consider the demands afresh without examining conditions two and three. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87][Para 88][Para 110][Para 113]

On the merits, Notification No. 230 K.B/S.C.N of 25 July 2012 requires an attendance bonus of at least USD 10 a month and a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 7; the employer pays USD 10 and USD 08. [Para 89][Para 114][Para 115] The panel found no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice requiring USD 15 for either, held the employer's practice more favourable than the Notification, characterised both demands as exceeding what the law fixes, and so classified them as disputes over interests. [Para 91][Para 92][Para 93][Para 116][Para 117][Para 118][Para 119] Applying Article 96 of the Labour Law, Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 SAK BY and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY — under which an interest award replaces the collective agreement and removes the right to strike — it held that only a most representative union may bring such a demand, and that the 184 individual claimants were not a union with that character. [Para 94][Para 96][Para 97][Para 98][Para 99][Para 100][Para 103][Para 105][Para 106]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The creation, modification and extinguishment of an employment contract flow from the will of both parties, so neither may compel the other to terminate an undetermined-duration contract and re-sign it for a fixed term, whatever advantage the change would bring the workers. [Para 65][Para 67][Para 71][Para 73]

A demand that repeats one decided in an earlier award will be reconsidered where the claimants are not the same parties: with 66 claimants before and 184 now, the first res judicata condition fails and the panel has jurisdiction afresh. [Para 81][Para 85][Para 88]

A demand for a bonus or allowance above the figure fixed by the Ministry's notification is a dispute over interests, and a body of individual workers is not a most representative union capable of bringing it. [Para 92][Para 93][Para 100][Para 106]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that the workers' desire for the 5% fixed-duration compensation supplied no legal basis for the demand, and its observation that it need not examine the second and third res judicata conditions once the first failed, were not necessary to the outcome. [Para 73][Para 87]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The travel-allowance demand is printed as a total of "១៥៦ ដុល្លារ" — USD 156 — at [Para 6], as USD 15 at [Para 55][Para 108][Para 124][Para 127], and as an addition of USD 07 or USD 08 to an existing USD 08 at [Para 56]. The Notification is dated 25 July 2012 but its text carries an intruded "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥" [Para 89][Para 114], and the USD 7 figure is printed "៧N (ប្រាំ ពីរ)" — seven with the words "five two" — at [Para 114]. The first demand is garbled by duplication ("ត្រូវ តាម ច្បាប់ ស្តីពី ការងារ" twice) at [Para 2], and Prakas No. 099 is dated "21 April 2019 2004" at [Para 98]. Paragraphs 83 and 96 carry blocks of intruded English ("thathe for the for the for", "They \" fiffician \" to the movernment"). The chair, Aing Eng Thong, appears in the header [Para 1] but the signature block is corrupted to "upwornso meth Lyyy" with no chair's name [Para 129]. The company is spelled គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទីន, គ្រីស្តាល់ ម៉ាទី and គ្រីស្តាយ ល់ [Para 1][Para 39][Para 110]. Paragraph 43 counts 181 transferred plus three attendees as 184 while four workers attended [Para 41][Para 42], and the local union is said not yet to have received registration [Para 39] although the federation acts on 181 transfers of rights [Para 40].
