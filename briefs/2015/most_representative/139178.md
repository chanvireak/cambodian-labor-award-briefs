# Award No. 085/15 — Ei Cheng (Cambodia) Hop Beresin (Arbitration Council, 28 April 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; An Virak, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139178

## 1. Facts
Ei Cheng (Cambodia) Hop Beresin, a shoe manufacturer in Bati district, Takeo, employing about 2,800 workers, faced 14 demands brought by the Workers' Movement Union. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 48][Para 49] Conciliation on 20 March 2015 settled 7 points, leaving 7 for the panel: issues 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9 and 13. [Para 15][Para 21] The local union was registered and recognised, but its membership of about 600 was less than half the workforce. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 139]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay the lunch allowance and the overtime meal allowance once a week, every Saturday? [Para 2][Para 53]
2. Whether the employer must build extra dining tables and a roofed shelter for bicycles and motorcycles? [Para 3][Para 60][Para 121]
4. Whether the employer must pay a skill allowance of USD 10 for skiving and sole-roughing, USD 5 for chemical spraying and machine 2, and USD 3 for machine 1, per month? [Para 4][Para 69][Para 143]
7. Whether the employer must add a further KHR 1,000 per overtime hour to the existing overtime meal allowance? [Para 5][Para 75][Para 150]
8. Whether the employer must add USD 3 to the existing USD 12 travel and accommodation allowance, making USD 15 per month? [Para 6][Para 80][Para 161]
9. Whether the employer must pay Sunday overtime wages weekly, every Saturday? [Para 7][Para 83][Para 171]
13. Whether the employer must add USD 28 to the existing base wage of workers already receiving USD 128 per month? [Para 8][Para 89][Para 194]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers live hand to mouth and need the allowances on Saturday so they have time to buy food and supplies on Sunday; neighbouring factories with more workers manage to pay on Saturdays, and a worker skilled on machine 1 is sometimes moved to sew on machine 2 without extra pay. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 71][Para 72]
**Employer (respondent):** Payroll for Monday-to-Saturday work cannot be assembled, sent to accounts and processed by the bank before Wednesday, and the practice has operated since 2014 without anyone losing money; the old bicycle shed was converted into a dining area because eating space mattered more, and workers already receive USD 12 travel allowance. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 66][Para 68]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim to move the lunch allowance to Saturday was dismissed, but the employer was ordered to pay the overtime meal allowance once a week, every Saturday; all other claims were dismissed or declined. [Para 210][Para 211][Para 212][Para 213][Para 215][Para 216][Para 217][Para 218]

**Issue 1.** Applying article 2 of the Labour Law and its own prior awards, the panel held that the employer's right to manage the enterprise includes fixing the day on which the lunch allowance is paid, and no law requires weekly Saturday payment, so that claim was dismissed. [Para 94][Para 97][Para 101][Para 102][Para 103][Para 104] For the overtime meal allowance, point 2 of Notification No. 041/11 K.B/S.J.N. of 7 March 2011 entitles a worker doing voluntary overtime at the employer's request to KHR 2,000 per day or a free meal, without fixing the payment date. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107] The panel reasoned that the allowance exists so the worker can buy food when working overtime, and must therefore be paid within a period that actually allows this; it held a weekly Saturday payment reasonable and necessary, and ordered it. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 110][Para 112][Para 119][Para 120]

**Issues 2, 4, 7, 8, 9 and 13.** Each was found to be an interest dispute: no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal regulation or past practice obliged the employer to build a shelter, pay a skill allowance, raise the overtime meal money, increase the travel allowance or add USD 28 to the base wage. [Para 124][Para 125][Para 126][Para 144][Para 145][Para 154][Para 155][Para 156][Para 164][Para 165][Para 166][Para 188][Para 189][Para 204][Para 205] Following Prakas No. 099 article 43 — an interest award replaces the collective agreement for one year and removes the right to strike on that subject — the panel held it may decide such a dispute only where the claimant union is most representative, with membership above half the workforce. [Para 133][Para 134][Para 135] With about 600 members out of 2,800 the union did not qualify, so the panel declined to consider those claims. [Para 137][Para 139][Para 140][Para 141][Para 142] On issue 9 the panel separately noted that Sunday overtime pay is part of wages under article 103 and must be paid at least twice a month with no more than 16 days between payments under article 116, which the employer's practice of paying on the 1st or 2nd and the 15th satisfied. [Para 173][Para 175][Para 181][Para 183][Para 184][Para 186] On issue 13, Prakas No. 283 K.B/Br.K of 12 November 2014 fixes a minimum wage of USD 128 for 2015 and does not require an increase for workers already at that level, which the employer had applied. [Para 194][Para 195][Para 201][Para 202][Para 203]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a ministerial notification requires a meal allowance for voluntary overtime but is silent on timing, the panel will treat the date of payment as governed by the purpose of the allowance — enabling the worker to buy food while working overtime — and will order a weekly payment; but a claim to move an ordinary lunch allowance to a particular weekday is a matter of the employer's lawful managerial discretion and fails.

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that earlier awards required the free meal to be provided on each day of overtime, while accepting a weekly cash payment as adequate here, was not necessary to the operative order. [Para 111][Para 112]
