# Award No. 051/15 — Royal Cambodia Phnom Penh Golf Club (Arbitration Council, 6 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouchhun, chosen by the employer; An Non, chosen by the workers; Aing Eng Thong, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139150

## 1. Facts
Royal Cambodia Phnom Penh Golf Club — operated by Civica Investment Development Co Ltd at Phum Ok Romdual, Sangkat Samrong Krom, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh — runs a golf-caddying service and employs about 181 workers. [Para 28][Para 2] The workforce's union held an election of its leadership but had not lodged any application for registration with the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training. [Para 29][Para 30] On 7 February 2015, 115 workers signed a letter of authority transferring their rights to the Federation of Workers' Trade Unions (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត) to pursue the dispute, and the names of all three union leaders appear on that letter. [Para 23][Para 31][Para 32] Conciliation on 09 February 2015 resolved 7 of 8 issues; the single unresolved issue — a transport and accommodation allowance — was referred to the Secretariat on 17 February 2015 and heard on 24 February 2015. [Para 11][Para 25][Para 12][Para 9]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay each worker a transport and accommodation allowance (ប្រាក់សោហ៊ុយធ្វើដំណើរ និងថ្លៃស្នាក់នៅ) of at least USD 7 per month? [Para 34][Para 42]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Golf-service workers do the same work as textile, garment and footwear workers — eight hours a day, travelling to the workplace in the same way — so they should receive the same USD 7 monthly transport and accommodation allowance; their wages are low and they want the allowance on top of wages. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38]
**Employer (respondent):** It has already raised wages by USD 128 per month; the company provides golf services and is not in textiles, garment or footwear, so Prakas No. 283 K.B/Pr.K and Notice No. 230 K.B/S.J.N of the Ministry, which alone require the allowance, do not apply to it; and takings are down because fewer guests come than before. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the claim that the employer pay USD 7 per month in transport and accommodation allowance to each worker. [Para 63][Para 64]
On the merits the panel first identified both parties to the claim as agreeing that the company has never in practice paid USD 7 per month for travel and accommodation. [Para 34] It then examined Notice No. 230 K.B/S.J.N of 25 July 2012, whose point 1 provides "transport and accommodation allowance of 7 (seven) US dollars per month", and found on the face of the notice that it is addressed to textile, garment and footwear factories and enterprises and applies only to workers in those sectors. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] The company being a golf-service operator, it fell outside the notice's scope and the employer had no obligation to pay. [Para 46][Para 47] Independently, the panel found no provision of law, collective agreement, agreement between the parties, employment contract, internal rules or past practice creating such an obligation, so the claim exceeded what the law fixes and was a dispute of interests (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50] For interest disputes the panel applied Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 SAK BY of 22 November 2001 on the representativeness of professional organisations, read with Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY of 21 April 2004, under which an award on an interest dispute becomes a collective agreement binding every worker in the enterprise and, in the panel's words, removes those workers' right to strike over similar future interest claims. [Para 51][Para 54][Para 57][Para 58] It therefore held it may decide an interest dispute only where the union bringing it is the most representative in the enterprise, or several unions together represent more than half the workforce, following awards 81/04 RV Green, 98/04 Great Union and 169/11 Fourteen Thy, and consistent with 402/11 Puyean, 66/11 Inhansung and 278/13 Meng Da Footwear. [Para 56][Para 59][Para 60] The 115 claimants did not constitute a most-representative union, and their union was not registered at all, so they lacked the legal capacity to bring an interest claim and the panel refused to consider it. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that is not the most representative in the enterprise — and does not, jointly with other unions, represent more than half the workforce — and that is not even registered with the Ministry cannot obtain an award on a dispute of interests: because such an award would operate as a collective agreement binding all workers and would extinguish their right to strike on that subject, the panel will refuse to consider the claim however strong its merits. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 61][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's finding that the employer owed nothing on the merits anyway — the USD 7 allowance being confined to textile, garment and footwear enterprises by Notice No. 230 — was reached before and independently of the standing refusal. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is Khmer only and the OCR is badly degraded. The reasoning paragraphs repeat themselves: the question "តើ និយោជក មាន កាតព្វកិច្ច..." is introduced twice in one sentence and the panel's finding is duplicated verbatim, with "ថ្លៃស្នាក់នៅ" corrupted to "ថ្លៃថ្នូរ ថា ស្នាក់ នៅ" [Para 42]; elsewhere the text re-runs whole clauses ("កំណត់ហេតុ នៃ ការផ្សះផ្សា..." twice in [Para 25], and the exhibit list headings "ឃ- ឯកសារ បានទទួល..." twice in [Para 26]). The signature block omits the employer-chosen arbitrator's name altogether and garbles the worker-chosen arbitrator's title as "អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល ជ្រើសូដោយ" [Para 66], so the composition is taken from the header [Para 2]. The company is named in several transliterations of "CIVICA INVESTMENT DEVELOPMENT CO., LTD" (ស៊ីវីខា / ស៊ិវី ខា / ស៊ីវី ខា, and "ឱវីឡី"/"ឌិវី ឡី"), which are rendered here as Civica Investment Development Co Ltd [Para 2][Para 21][Para 28][Para 46]. Two Prakas references run together ("ប្រកាស លេខ ០៩៩ ... និង ២០១៥ យុវនីតិសម្បទា") [Para 57]. The union federation is named only in Khmer and is translated literally here, not from any printed Latin form. The union's registration status is stated in one line only and is not otherwise evidenced [Para 30], and the award never prints the number of union members, so the "more than half" test cannot be checked against the 181-worker headcount [Para 28][Para 31].
