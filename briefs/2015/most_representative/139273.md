# Award No. 208/15 — Winnie Fashion Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 September 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, chair chosen by both), with a dissenting opinion annexed by Liv Sovanna · non-binding award · Doc ID 139273

## 1. Facts
The company sews garments at Ang Snuol, Kandal, with about 520 workers, and pays a regular-attendance bonus of USD 10 a month. Its internal rules, approved on 21 July 2015, provide that two days' absence is converted into annual leave with the bonus kept, while absence beyond two days forfeits the bonus entirely. The Cambodia Workers' Union of the factory, registered on 3 July 2015 with about 76 members, filed eight demands; six were settled at the hearing, leaving the question how the bonus should be cut for authorised leave and whether it should rise to USD 15. [Para 61][Para 66][Para 74][Para 73][Para 64][Para 31]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must cut the attendance bonus in proportion to the number of days the worker was absent on leave authorised by the employer? [Para 66]
2. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus to USD 15 a month? [Para 75]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Deducting the whole bonus for authorised leave is wrong, and the internal rule that allows it harms workers' benefits; other factories such as Mak Li and Tayong already pay USD 15, and the demand for more is driven by the employer's practice of wiping out the bonus at once. [Para 70][Para 76][Para 77]
**Employer (respondent):** It deducts according to Article 5(b) of its internal rules, which is lawful; the same issue was already decided in case 349/14, so the Council should decline it as res judicata; and it cannot pay USD 15 because the factory has only recently started operating and it already pays what the law requires. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 79]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both demands were refused; the bonus stays at USD 10 and is not cut pro rata, the panel departing from its earlier jurisprudence, with one arbitrator dissenting on issue 1. [Para 151][Para 152][Para 156]

On the res judicata objection the panel set out the three conditions and found the first unmet: in case 349/14 the claimants were 73 individual workers represented by two federations, whereas here the claimant is the factory union itself, so the parties were not the same and the earlier award did not bar the claim. [Para 95][Para 100][Para 101][Para 103][Para 104]

On issue 1 the panel noted that Article 5(b) of the internal rules keeps the bonus for two days' absence by converting them to annual leave but forfeits it beyond that. It reviewed Notification No. 041 Kប/សជណ of 7 March 2011, which set a minimum of USD 7 without any condition of no absence, and its own consistent jurisprudence under that notification ordering pro-rata deduction for leave the employer had authorised. It then found that Notification 041/11 was repealed and replaced by Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, effective 1 September 2012, which added the words "without absence" — words absent from the earlier instrument — and that a decision resting on the repealed text could not be followed. [Para 105][Para 106][Para 107][Para 109][Para 111]

Applying Notification 230, the panel held the bonus is a conditional incentive: the condition is attendance on all the month's working days without absence, the employer may withhold it where the condition fails, and permission to take unpaid personal leave is a separate administrative act that does not convert the absence into attendance. Leave that the law, a contract, internal rules, a collective agreement or an arrangement entitles the worker to take is different, and the panel left open that such leave may preserve the bonus. Because the leave here was personal and unrelated to any contractual or statutory duty, no pro-rata entitlement arose. [Para 113][Para 114][Para 115][Para 116][Para 118][Para 119][Para 130][Para 131]

On issue 2 the panel found the USD 10 payment already conformed to Notification 230 and that no law, collective agreement, internal rule or practice obliged the employer to pay USD 15, so the demand was one for benefits above the legal minimum, an interests dispute. Under Article 96 and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305, only the most representative union may negotiate a collective agreement, and under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interests dispute replaces a collective agreement for a year; the Council will therefore decide such a dispute only for a most representative union, or unions together holding over half the workforce. This union had 76 members and did not qualify, so the panel declined to consider the claim. [Para 133][Para 134][Para 135][Para 137][Para 142][Para 145][Para 148][Para 150]

**Dissent.** Arbitrator Liv Sovanna recorded that he would have granted issue 1. In his view the award ignored the agreement of the Labour Advisory Committee (គណៈកម្មការប្រឹក្សាការងារ) at its meeting of 11 July 2012, whose statement fixed the bonus at USD 10 without adding "without absence"; the notification merely publicised that decision and cannot contradict it, so where the two differ the committee's statement prevails and the prior jurisprudence of pro-rata deduction should stand, the more so as USD 10 equals 10 per cent of the USD 100 base wage. [Para 157][Para 161][Para 163][Para 168][Para 172][Para 175][Para 183]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The attendance bonus under Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 is conditional on attendance on every working day without absence, so once that notification replaced Notification No. 041/11 the Council will no longer order pro-rata deduction of the bonus for unpaid personal leave that the employer merely permitted; and an interests dispute seeking benefits above the statutory minimum will not be decided unless the claimant union is the most representative in the enterprise or unions together represent more than half the workers. [Para 109][Para 111][Para 116][Para 145]

**Obiter dicta:** That leave the worker is entitled to take under law, contract, internal rules, collective agreement or arrangement may stand differently from permitted personal leave; and that res judicata requires the same parties, so a union's claim is not barred by an earlier award won by individual workers represented by federations. [Para 118][Para 103]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is heavily OCR-damaged and self-contradictory in places. The dissent is introduced as directed at "issue 5" although it plainly concerns issue 1, the only issue on which the majority divided. The panel's constitution is dated "10 August 2016 -3 2015", and the deferral of the award is dated "28 August 2019 2015". The procedural summary says six issues were settled but lists five numbers. The order on issue 2 is printed "ចំនួន ២០២៣ ១៥ ដុល្លារ". The company is named both Winnie Fashion and Winnie, and one order paragraph disintegrates into unrelated English words. Notice numbers and dates appear in two forms, and the union's registered name varies by prefix. [Para 156][Para 27][Para 33][Para 30][Para 152][Para 153][Para 21]
