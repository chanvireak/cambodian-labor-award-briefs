# Award No. 315/15 — Chheng Yia In Ther Brai Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 December 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothi (employer-appointed), Tuon Si Phan (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (president) · Doc ID 139483

## 1. Facts
The Khmer Workers' Spirit Union brought eight demands for the workers of Chheng Yia In Ther Brai Co., Ltd, a shoe-sole manufacturer employing 558 workers. [Para 15][Para 40][Para 41] Conciliation left four items, of which the parties settled the third at the hearing and the workers withdrew the first, leaving the travel and accommodation allowance and the deduction of union dues. [Para 16][Para 21][Para 22] The plant-level union had held an election for its leadership and notified the employer of the result, but had not applied to the Ministry of Labour for registration. [Para 42] The federation held transfer-of-rights letters from 62 workers, three of whom attended, and the panel confined the award to those 62. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must pay an additional USD 8 of travel and accommodation allowance, making USD 15 a month? [Para 46]
4. Whether the employer must deduct 2,000 riel a month from the wages of members who volunteer to pay union dues to the federation? [Para 58]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Many factories pay more than USD 7 a month; the allowance is inadequate because travel costs 10 to 12 dollars and lodging 25 to 30 dollars a month; about 150 members have signed and thumbprinted their consent to the deduction, which is their own money. [Para 47][Para 59][Para 60]**Employer (respondent):** It cannot pay more because the minimum wage rises to USD 140 in 2016 and profitability is unknown, while electricity and fuel costs have fallen and the allowance is only a partial subsidy; it will deduct dues once the union is properly registered, since deducting for an unregistered union could expose it to buyer audits and to complaints from workers who belong to two or three unions. [Para 48][Para 51][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — both claims were refused; the panel declined to consider the allowance claim at all. [Para 103][Para 104]

On the allowance the panel applied point 1 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, which sets travel and accommodation allowance at USD 7 a month, disapplies it where the employer provides transport or lodging, and preserves any more favourable practice. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70] The employer was paying exactly USD 7, so the demand for USD 15 exceeded the legal floor and no statute, collective agreement, internal rule, contract or past practice supported it; the claim was therefore a dispute over interests. [Para 74][Para 77][Para 78][Para 79] Because the claimant was not the most representative union — an award on interests becomes a collective agreement binding every worker for a year under Article 96 and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 — the panel held it had no basis to examine the claim and refused to consider it. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 88][Para 89][Para 90]

On the union dues the panel applied Article 268(1), under which a professional organisation acquires the rights the Labour Law recognises only once its statutes and list of officers are deposited with the Ministry, and Article 129(2), under which a worker may authorise deduction of dues for a union of which he is a member, in writing and revocably. [Para 91][Para 94][Para 95] The union had not registered, so it was not yet a legal entity and the workers had no right to require the employer to deduct on its behalf. [Para 99][Para 100][Para 101] The claim was refused. [Para 102]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer cannot be required to deduct union dues from wages at the request of a plant-level union that has not yet deposited its statutes and officers with the Ministry of Labour; until registration the union holds none of the rights the Labour Law confers, and the individual worker's written authorisation under Article 129(2) has nothing to attach to. [Para 94][Para 99][Para 100]

**Obiter dicta:** Where an employer already pays the statutory USD 7 travel and accommodation allowance and the workers seek more, the demand is a dispute over interests that only the most representative union in the enterprise may bring before the panel. [Para 78][Para 88]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The conciliation report is dated 23 November 2015, yet the unresolved items are said to have been transmitted to the Secretariat on 17 November 2015, and the conciliation meeting itself is placed on 20 November 2015. [Para 12][Para 16][Para 17] The panel's constitution is printed "1 November 2019 2015", after the conciliation it supposedly followed. [Para 18] The company's name appears as Chheng Yia In Ther Brai, "Chheng Yia", "Chhing Yia", "Yeung Yia" and "Chheng Yiang Yia". [Para 12][Para 17][Para 39] The claim is said to rest on transfer letters dated 24 October 2015 from 62 workers, while the federation's founding complaint is numbered 314/15 and dated 28 October 2015. [Para 15][Para 43] The workers assert about 150 members consented to the dues deduction, which the panel does not reconcile with the 62 claimants. [Para 59][Para 43] The president's name is printed as both "Pen Bunchhea" and "Pen Bunchheu". [Para 1][Para 106] The final paragraph on the binding effect of the award is largely corrupted by OCR.
