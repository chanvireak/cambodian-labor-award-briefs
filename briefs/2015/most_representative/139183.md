# Award No. 89/15 — Royal Cambodia Phnom Penh Golf Club (registered as Sivy Kha Investment Development Co Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 8 May 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun chosen by the employer, An Nan chosen by the workers, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139183

## 1. Facts
The employer operates a golf service business in Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh — registered as Sivy Kha Investment Development Co Ltd and known as Royal Cambodia Phnom Penh Golf Club — with 180 workers. [Para 44][Para 45] A federation brought 13 demands on behalf of 51 workers who transferred their rights to it; 11 later withdrew, leaving 43 claimants. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 50] Conciliation failed on all 13 points on 24 March 2015, and at the hearing nine points were settled or withdrawn, leaving four for the panel. [Para 21][Para 26][Para 27] The company-level union had not received its registration certificate. [Para 46]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must add USD 28 to the wage of every worker already earning USD 100 or more per month, in addition to raising trainees to USD 123 and full-status workers to USD 128? [Para 51][Para 88]
3. Whether the employer must pay USD 15 per month as a milk allowance to women workers with children aged 3 to 18 months? [Para 60][Para 113]
4. Whether the employer must organise a fresh election of staff delegates? [Para 67][Para 120]
7. Whether the employer must pay overtime to caddies (ឃែនឌី) who work beyond normal hours? [Para 72][Para 135]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** after the February strike the employer agreed at the 24 March conciliation to add USD 28, though the Ministry did not record it in the minutes; mothers returning from three months' maternity leave cannot place a child under 18 months in the crèche and cannot go home to breastfeed, so they need the milk allowance; the staff delegates' term expired long ago because no election has been held in over ten years; caddies who follow a guest beyond eight hours receive no overtime, only 10 riel per round. [Para 54][Para 61][Para 62][Para 68][Para 74]
**Employer (respondent):** it had already raised wages above USD 128 after the strike, Prakas 283 sets a floor of USD 123/128 rather than a USD 28 top-up, and no agreement was reached on the top-up for workers above USD 100; no law requires a milk allowance; the term-7 election was held and is recognised by the Ministry, running from 14 July 2014 to 14 July 2016; caddies work eight hours, are never forced to continue, and receive 10 riel per round plus tips. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 64][Para 66][Para 69][Para 75][Para 78]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all four remaining claims were refused, and the panel declined to consider issues 1 and 3 at all. [Para 155][Para 156][Para 157][Para 158]

On issues 1 and 3 the panel treated the claims as interest disputes (វិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍), because neither rested on an existing legal entitlement: Prakas 283 covers only textile, garment and footwear workers, and the company's main activity is golf services, so it falls outside the Prakas altogether; and no statute, collective agreement or practice provides a milk allowance. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 97][Para 114][Para 115][Para 116] Applying Article 96 of the Labour Law, Prakas 305/01 Article 9 and Prakas 099/04 Article 43 — under which an interest award replaces the collective agreement and binds all workers for a year — the panel held that only a most representative union, with more than half the workforce as members, may bring such a dispute, and that the 43 claimants were not such a union. [Para 100][Para 103][Para 105][Para 108][Para 110] Both claims were therefore dismissed without reaching the merits. [Para 112][Para 119]

On issue 4, Articles 283 and 292 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 2 of Prakas 286 require the employer to hold staff-delegate elections, and the panel found the employer had done so for term 7, running from 14 July 2014 to 14 July 2016; the workers' assertion that no election had been held for ten years could not stand against minutes recognised by the Ministry. [Para 121][Para 122][Para 126][Para 130][Para 131][Para 133]

On issue 7, Article 137 caps normal hours at eight a day and 48 a week and Article 139 new gives a 50% premium for overtime; caddies work an eight-hour day and a round takes four to four and a half hours, continuation is voluntary, and the 10 riel per round plus tips is a separate arrangement. [Para 135][Para 139][Para 140][Para 76][Para 79] The decisive point was proof: a party asserting a claim bears the burden, and the workers never named which caddies worked overtime, on what days or for how long — they said only that 60% of the 51 signatories were caddies. [Para 141][Para 145][Para 151][Para 153]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Prakas No. 283 of 12 November 2014 fixes the minimum wage only for workers in the textile, garment and footwear sectors; an enterprise whose main activity is golf services lies outside it, so no statutory minimum-wage top-up can be claimed there and the demand becomes an interest dispute that only a most representative union may bring. [Para 95][Para 97][Para 108][Para 110] A claim for overtime fails where the claimant does not identify the workers concerned, the dates and the hours: the burden of proof lies on the party asserting the claim, and a bare percentage estimate is not evidence. [Para 141][Para 151][Para 153]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's description of the caddie arrangement — that a caddie may decline a further round and the employer will find a replacement, and that caddies had never previously asked for overtime pay — explains why no entitlement arose but was not necessary to the dismissal, which rested on failure of proof. [Para 82][Para 148][Para 149]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The operative order on the milk allowance reads "USD 156" at [Para 156] where every other reference is USD 15 [Para 5][Para 60][Para 113]; the reasoning and the claim support USD 15.
- The conciliation report is dated 1 April 2015 but said to have been sent on 2 April [Para 18][Para 22], and two different conciliation minutes appear — 9 February 2015 at [Para 36] and 24 March 2015 at [Para 41] — without explanation of which was the final one.
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "06 April 2019 2015" at [Para 23], a duplicated year.
- The registered company name is printed in at least five inconsistent transliterations, none in Latin script, and cannot be confirmed from the source; the award itself equates it with "Royal Cambodia Phnom Penh Golf Club" at [Para 44], and the transliteration in the title is an inference from that.
- The signature block at [Para 160] is largely destroyed; the employer-party arbitrator's name does not survive and only An Nan and Pen Bun Chhea are legible.
- Stray figures corrupt the reasoning: "128 dollars /20" at [Para 57], "03 months to 18 months December" at [Para 113], and "60 per cent per cent" at [Para 151]. The workers are abbreviated here as Mr P and Mr T (the two who attended) and the federation officials as Mr S.
