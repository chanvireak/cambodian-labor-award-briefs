# Award No. 018/15 — CS Goldway Techstyle (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 February 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Kol Vannak (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchea (president) · Doc ID 139129

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment-cutting company in Ang Ta Saom, Trak Kak, Takeo, employing about 923 workers. A collective dispute over 34 demands went to conciliation; 27 were settled and 7 remained unresolved when the file reached the Council. At the hearing the parties settled four more (issues 1, 3, 6 and 7), leaving issues 2, 4 and 5 for the panel. The claimant was the Khmer Workers' Solidarity Federation together with 588 named workers. [Para 15][Para 21][Para 52][Para 57]

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must reduce the monthly attendance bonus proportionally to the number of days a worker was absent on leave authorised by the employer, instead of deducting it at its own discretion? [Para 58][Para 74]
4. Whether the employer must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of 15 USD per month? [Para 117]
5. Whether the employer must pay a regular attendance bonus of 20 USD per month? [Para 137]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the attendance bonus is a wage and may only be reduced in proportion to days taken off with the employer's permission; the company pays only 9 USD of the 15 USD travel allowance claimed; the 10 USD attendance bonus should be doubled to 20 USD. [Para 60][Para 70]

**Employer (respondent):** company practice is to deduct the whole bonus for any absence, though it shows leniency where a medical certificate or wedding is produced; it has no capacity to raise the travel allowance to 15 USD; the 10 USD bonus already complies with the ministerial notification. [Para 63][Para 72]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issue 2 granted (proportional deduction ordered), issues 4 and 5 dismissed for want of jurisdiction, the claimant union not being the most representative in the enterprise. [Para 147][Para 148][Para 149]

On issue 2 the panel held that point 2 of Notification No. 230 (25 July 2012) fixes a minimum attendance bonus of 10 USD per month and is a general legal rule binding all textile, garment and footwear employers, not a discretionary reward the employer may administer as it pleases. Absences that the law itself contemplates — annual, special, maternity, public holiday and weekly rest — do not reduce the bonus. The phrase "without absence" is undefined, and the Labour Advisory Committee has not clarified whether it covers authorised as well as unauthorised absence, so the panel declined to interpret it and said each claim must be assessed on its own facts. Turning to employer-authorised leave for personal reasons, the panel applied Articles 71(6) and 72(1) of the Labour Law: such leave suspends the employment contract, so no wage is due for those days, and the employer may correspondingly reduce the bonus — but only in proportion to the days taken, not in full. [Para 80][Para 83][Para 91][Para 111][Para 115]

On issues 4 and 5 the panel found that the employer's 9 USD travel allowance already exceeded the 7 USD in Notification No. 230, and that no law, collective agreement, internal regulation or past practice required 15 USD; the same was true of the 20 USD attendance bonus claim against the 10 USD actually paid. Both were therefore interest disputes. Under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 an award on an interest dispute becomes a collective agreement binding all workers and extinguishes their right to strike over that interest, so the panel will only entertain such a claim from a union, or coalition of unions, representing more than half the workforce. With 588 claimants against roughly 923 workers, the claimant was not the most representative union. [Para 120][Para 131][Para 132][Para 134]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An attendance bonus set by ministerial notification is a legal minimum owed to every worker in the sector, and where the employer has itself authorised a worker's absence the bonus may be reduced only in proportion to the days absent, never forfeited in full; and an interest dispute may be arbitrated only at the instance of the most representative union in the enterprise, because the resulting award operates as a collective agreement over the whole workforce. [Para 83][Para 132]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that it could not supply a general meaning for "without absence", since doing so would ignore the lawfulness and reasonableness of each absence, and left the question to case-by-case assessment. [Para 91]
