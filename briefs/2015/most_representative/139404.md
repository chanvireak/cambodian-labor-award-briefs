# Award No. 107/15 — CN Prosperous Garment Cambodia Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 3 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom appointed by the employer party, An Non appointed by the worker party, Tang Tri chair) · Doc ID 139404

## 1. Facts
CN Prosperous Garment Cambodia Co., Ltd. (also called PPO) is a garment factory at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, employing about 662 workers; the Khmer Workers' Spirit Trade Union Federation and its factory union, not yet registered with the Ministry of Labour, brought a ten-point collective dispute. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] Seventy-three workers delegated their rights to the federation, one name appearing twice in the delegation, leaving seventy-two plus a worker who attended. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47] Conciliation failed on 23 April 2015 and the panel heard the case on 21 May 2015. [Para 17][Para 15] The employer sent only its administrator, who told the panel he held no written authority from the director, who was abroad, and no power to settle any issue. [Para 22][Para 97][Para 98]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay $15 a month of travel and accommodation money ($8 to workers using company transport)? [Para 48][Para 50]
2. Whether the employer must pay $1 a day of dessert money at lunch? [Para 53]
3. Whether the employer must pay overtime meal money of 2,000 riel per hour? [Para 55]
4. Whether the employer must reduce the attendance bonus only in proportion to the days of leave the employer authorised? [Para 58][Para 150]
5. Whether the employer must let workers offset special leave against annual leave instead of deducting wages? [Para 62][Para 196]
6. Whether the employer must provide hot and cold drinking water in hygienic containers? [Para 67]
7. Whether the employer must provide adequate medicines and first-aid supplies? [Para 76][Para 243]
8. Whether the employer must finish paying wages before 16:00? [Para 78][Para 253]
9. Whether the employer must provide soap in the toilets? [Para 83][Para 263]
10. Whether the employer must make overtime voluntary? [Para 87][Para 278]
## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** rents and petrol have risen, so $7 of travel money is too little and other factories pay $7 on top of transport; neighbours pay 1,000 to 2,000 riel of dessert money and 2,000 riel of overtime meal money an hour. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 54][Para 57] The attendance bonus is wages for 26 days' work, so authorised leave should reduce it proportionately; the employer pays cash for unused annual leave at each contract's end, blocking its use. [Para 61][Para 64] The water tank sits outside in mud and rice waste, insects get in and it smells; the medical box is empty; wages are finished only at 17:00 or 18:00; there is no soap; supervisors threaten and block the door to force overtime. [Para 70][Para 72][Para 76][Para 82][Para 86][Para 88]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer did not attend or file a reply, so the panel recorded that it had abandoned its right to answer. [Para 114][Para 131][Para 155]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 1, 2 and 3 were dismissed for want of standing; the employer was ordered to prorate the attendance bonus (issue 4), to stop cashing out unused annual leave while workers remain employed and to allow special leave to be offset against remaining annual leave (issue 5), to keep drinking water clean and hygienic, to stock adequate medicines, to finish paying wages before 16:00, to provide soap, and to make overtime voluntary. [Para 298][Para 299][Para 300][Para 301][Para 302][Para 305][Para 307][Para 308][Para 309][Para 310]

On procedure, Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 allows representation only by a person authorised in writing, and the administrator held no such letter. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 94] Under Article 21 a panel may proceed where a summoned party fails to appear without good reason, which it did, treating the workers' assertions as uncontested. [Para 101][Para 107][Para 108][Para 115]

Issues 1, 2 and 3 asked for more than Notice No. 230 of 25 July 2012 ($7 travel money), Notice No. 041/11 (2,000 riel of overtime meal money a day) or any agreement gave, so each was an interest dispute; the claimant was 73 individual workers, not a most-representative union, and an award on an interest dispute replaces the collective agreement and binds the whole enterprise under Article 43 of Prakas No. 099, so the panel declined them. [Para 110][Para 117][Para 119][Para 122][Para 123][Para 129][Para 149]

Issue 4 succeeded: the attendance bonus is a wage under Article 103, and absence authorised by the employer suspends the contract under Articles 71(6) and 72(1), so only a sum proportionate to the days absent may be withheld. [Para 186][Para 188][Para 192][Para 194][Para 195] On issue 5, Article 167(3) makes void any arrangement paying cash in lieu of annual leave, and Article 167(2) applies only where the contract has ended, so the employer had to stop cashing out leave during employment; Article 171 and Prakas No. 267 allow special leave to be offset against remaining annual leave, or repayment if the leave is exhausted. [Para 200][Para 201][Para 203][Para 207][Para 218][Para 219][Para 220]

Issues 6 to 9 turned on statutory duties: Article 229(1) and Prakas No. 052 required hygienic toilets with soap, [Para 263][Para 266][Para 277] Article 242 and Prakas No. 330, Article 4, required an infirmary with dressings and medicines at the employer's cost, [Para 243][Para 246][Para 247][Para 251][Para 252] and Articles 115(3) and 137 confined wage payment to working hours, so wages had to be complete before the 16:00 departure. [Para 253][Para 258][Para 261][Para 262] Prakas No. 054, Article 1, required only sufficient hygienic drinking water, not hot and cold containers, so that part of issue 6 was refused while the container's placement was ordered cleaned. [Para 221][Para 238][Para 241][Para 242] Issue 10 succeeded on Article 4 of Prakas No. 80 and Notice No. 030/15: overtime rests on the worker's consent, may not exceed two hours a day, and may not be compelled by discipline or by blocking the exit. [Para 278][Para 286][Para 289][Para 296][Para 297]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that neither attends nor authorises a representative in writing abandons its right to reply, and the panel may decide the case in its absence on the workers' uncontested assertions. [Para 94][Para 101][Para 108] An employer may not pay cash for unused annual leave while the employment contract continues, because Article 167(3) of the Labour Law voids any such arrangement; special leave may instead be set off against annual leave that remains unused, and repaid only once it is exhausted. [Para 203][Para 207][Para 218][Para 219] Statutory welfare duties — hygienic drinking water, a stocked infirmary, soap in the toilets, wages completed within working hours, and voluntary overtime — are enforced as of right whatever the employer's capacity to pay. [Para 241][Para 252][Para 277][Para 262][Para 297]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the words "without absence" in Notice No. 230 remain unexplained by the Labour Advisory Committee, so each claim turns on its own facts, are observations not necessary to the orders on issues 4 and 5. [Para 165][Para 166][Para 170]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Conciliation is recorded as settling two points, issues 3 and 4 [Para 20], yet the panel decided issues 3 and 4 on the merits [Para 139][Para 150]. The settlement record and the award cannot both be right.
- The employer's name appears as "ស៊ី អិន ប្រុស ស្ពើ រើស", "CN PROSPEROUS GARMENT CAMBODIA CO., LTD", "PPO ស៊ី អិន" and "ស៊ី អិន_ប្រសស្ពើ" [Para 1][Para 42][Para 45][Para 13]; the non-conciliation report is No. 451 at [Para 13] but No. 441 at [Para 18] and [Para 36].
- The employer's administrator is "លោកង វ៉ាន់ ណែត" at [Para 1][Para 21] and "លោក កង វ៉ាន់ ណែត" at [Para 98]. Workers' names are redacted; one, "ម៉ម កំសត់", appears twice in the delegation list [Para 46].
- The $1 dessert claim is printed "០១៩ ដុល្លារ" in the issue statement and the order [Para 130][Para 299], against "$1" in the demand [Para 53].
- [Para 151] is corrupted ("លោក Kink Kong តើ និយោជក មាន កាតព្វកិច្ចកាត់ ... តើ និយោជក មាន កាតព្វកិច្ចកាត់"), repeating the question instead of stating it; [Para 9] merges two demands, and [Para 305]–[Para 306] split one order across paragraphs.
- The local union had not applied for registration [Para 44], which is why the standing question arose; no document records its registration.
