# Award No. 270/15 — UDS Garment (Arbitration Council, 13 November 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (In Sothy appointed by the employer, Tuon Si Phan appointed by the workers, Ang Eng Thong chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 139450

## 1. Facts
UDS Garment (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូឌី អេស), a garment factory on National Road 6A, Prek Liep, Phnom Penh, employed about 723 workers. [Para 42][Para 43] The company-level Free Workers' Union raised eight demands; conciliation on 1 October 2015 settled two, and six unresolved points were referred to the Council on 19 October 2015. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22] At the hearing the workers withdrew issue 6 (dismissal of the administrative manager), leaving five issues for decision. [Para 26][Para 27] Both parties agreed the issues were identical to Case No. 097/15 — UDS Garment, decided by award of 27 May 2015. [Para 46][Para 48]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must deduct the regular attendance bonus pro rata, according to the days of authorised absence taken by the worker? [Para 49]
2. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 2,000 riel per day? [Para 52]
3. Whether the employer must raise the piece rate by 28% over the existing rate? [Para 54]
4. Whether the employer must add USD 3 to the existing USD 7 transport and accommodation allowance, making USD 10 per month? [Para 55][Para 56]
5. Whether the employer must pay overtime for public holidays and Sundays forthwith? [Para 58]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the facts are entirely the same as Case No. 097/15 and the same demands are maintained on each point — pro rata attendance-bonus deduction, a 2,000 riel meal allowance, a 28% piece-rate increase, USD 10 total transport allowance and immediate holiday overtime payment. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 56]
**Employer (respondent):** the facts and issues are the same as Case No. 097/15, which the Council already decided on 27 May 2015; on the bonus it added that from 1 November 2015 it would apply a new schedule (0.38 and 0.76 USD deducted for one and two days of leave, the rest of the bonus forfeited from three days), and on each other point it said only that the company had no capacity to pay. [Para 46][Para 51][Para 5][Para 12]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel decided and ordered the refusal to reconsider Case No. 270/15 in its entirety; the award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour. [Para 83][Para 84][Para 28]

The panel first held that no legal provision governs *res judicata* in arbitration procedure, so it applied the principle as developed in its own earlier awards, citing Cases 45/07, 14/08, 64/09, 95/09, 81/11, 42/07, 114/12 and 116/11. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 66] It set three cumulative conditions: the same parties previously joined issue on the point, the same issue was previously brought to the Council, and the Council decided it fully. [Para 64]

On condition one, the claimant union and UDS Garment were the same parties in Case No. 097/15, so it was satisfied. [Para 67][Para 68] On condition two, the workers had raised the same issues there, so it was satisfied as well. [Para 69][Para 70] On condition three, the earlier panel had decided issues 01, 03 and 05 on the merits but had refused to consider issues 02 and 04, so the condition was met only for the first three. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74] *Res judicata* was therefore unavailable for issues 02 and 04. [Para 76]

For those two, the panel turned to the ground the earlier award had used: the claimant was not the most representative union and lacked the legal standing to bring a dispute concerning those interests. [Para 79] It found the position unchanged — the claimant in this case is still not the most representative union — so the workers still had no right to bring the dispute, and it refused to consider issues 02 and 04 exactly as in Case No. 097/15. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 82]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not re-decide an issue already brought by the same parties and fully decided on the merits by an earlier award of the Council, even though *res judicata* is not codified in arbitration procedure. Where the earlier award refused to consider the claim rather than deciding it, *res judicata* does not bar the new case, and the panel must dispose of the claim on its own ground — here, that a union which is not the most representative lacks standing to bring the dispute. [Para 61][Para 64][Para 76][Para 82]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that no legal provision addresses *res judicata* in arbitration procedure, and that its purpose is to avoid contradictory outcomes on the same dispute and to ensure a final settlement, are general remarks on the principle rather than steps in the order. [Para 62][Para 65] Its account of the employer's proposed new bonus schedule from 1 November 2015 is recorded but not ruled on. [Para 51]

- The workers' transport claim is printed as "ប្រាក់ ចំនួន ៤០៣ ដុល្លារ" (USD 403) where the claim and the arithmetic give USD 3 on top of USD 7. [Para 55][Para 56]
- The conciliation report numbers the demands differently from the panel: overtime is demand 4 and transport demand 5 in the report, but the panel decides transport as issue 04 and overtime as issue 05. [Para 8][Para 11][Para 55][Para 58]
- The signature block prints only two of the three arbitrators, naming In Sothy (employer party) and Ang Eng Thong (chair) and leaving the worker-party arbitrator, Tuon Si Phan, unnamed there though he is named on the face of the award. [Para 1][Para 85]
- The company name appears as "យូ ឌី អេស", "យូឌី អេស ហ្គាមេន" and "យូឌី អេស ហ្គាមេ"; a prior-award citation is garbled as "សំណុំរឿង លេខ ៤៤[៤២/០៧". [Para 42][Para 46][Para 64]
