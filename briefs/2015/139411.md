# Award No. 210/15 — Chin Yi Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 28 August 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Aing Eng Thong (chair, chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139411

## 1. Facts
The employer is a garment-cutting and sewing company at Spean Poth village, Sethey commune, Samaki Mean Chey district, Kampong Chhnang province, employing roughly 4,004 workers. [Para 25][Para 26] Three unions — the Federation of Unions for Workers' Rights, the National Khmer Heritage Union and the Free Workers' Union — are the claimants, with membership claimed at 680, 886 and 831 respectively. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 32] Conciliation at the Kampong Chhnang provincial department on 5 August 2015 settled 15 of the 17 points in dispute and left 2, which reached the Council on 7 August 2015. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] At the hearing of 17 August 2015 the parties settled issue 1, leaving only issue 2 for adjudication. [Para 8][Para 15][Para 16]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay 50% of wages to sewing-section workers in Building 8 when there is no work to do? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must keep the existing number of assistants, one assistant per team? [Para 3][Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** each sewing team must retain its own assistant as at present, because assistants already carry more work than team leaders; overload causes confusion and lost goods, for which the employer deducts pay and issues warnings, and any reassignment also costs the assistant the USD 15 functional allowance. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** there is no policy of cutting assistants, but the change to one assistant for two sewing teams is a buyer's requirement, since the buyer inspects the production line before placing orders and considers one assistant per team too costly; a single assistant cannot realistically serve four teams, and the USD 15 allowance is conditional on actually holding the assistant role and bearing responsibility for losses. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 40][Para 47][Para 48]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the demand that the employer keep one assistant for one sewing team, issuing a non-binding award that becomes final 8 days after notification if no party objects in writing to the Minister of Labour through the Council's secretariat. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62]

On issue 1 the panel recorded that the parties reached agreement at the hearing, so it made no ruling and did not consider the 50% wage demand. [Para 15][Para 16]

On issue 2 the panel noted that both sides agreed the current practice is one assistant per sewing team, and that the employer has a project to move to one assistant for two teams which has not yet been implemented or issued as a new policy. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 41][Para 52][Para 57] It relied on case No. 10/03-Zaksintech, where the Council held that it exists to resolve labour disputes and not problems that have not yet arisen, and on a line of awards — No. 68/04-CPL, No. 36/06-Mondochtech, No. 58/07-08 Star Sport Wear and No. 155/13-Cat — in which it declined to consider demands whose subject matter was a future dispute. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] Applying that to the facts, it found the new staffing rule had only been announced as a plan and never put into effect, so the dispute had not yet taken concrete shape and the demand amounted to a concern about a dispute that may or may not arise in future. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59] It therefore refused to consider the claim. [Para 60][Para 61]

Notably, the panel did not engage the workers' substantive arguments about workload, deductions, warnings or the loss of the USD 15 allowance, because the claim failed at the threshold of ripeness. [Para 44][Para 46]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The Council will decline to consider a demand that challenges a workplace change the employer has merely announced as a project and has not yet implemented: while the existing practice remains in force, the dispute has not crystallised and the claim concerns a future dispute the Council has no function to resolve. [Para 53][Para 58][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that a single assistant cannot serve four teams, drawn from the employer's and GMAC's joint study, was recited but played no part in the outcome. [Para 40]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The date on which the panel was constituted is printed twice in one line, as "១០ ខែសីហា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៥" (10 August 2019 / 2015); the award otherwise dates itself 28 August 2015. [Para 1][Para 12] The workforce figure appears as "៤០០៤ នាក់" (4,004), which cannot be reconciled against the three unions' combined claimed membership of 2,397. [Para 26][Para 32] The reasoning at line 51 repeats its own opening clause and prints "ជំនួយ ២០១៦" inside the sentence, and line 56 prints "៤ ០១ នាក់" for one assistant. [Para 51][Para 56] Line 45 is a bare Khmer full stop with no text. [Para 45] The signature block is largely destroyed by OCR, leaving only Ing Sothy's name legible and reducing the chair's name to noise, so the chair is taken from the face of the award. [Para 1][Para 63] The employer's name is written "ជីន យី ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី" and the transfer-of-rights letter of 17 August 2015 transfers authority to Mr Su Ching Chung, but the award does not state the transferee's role beyond factory management head. [Para 18]
