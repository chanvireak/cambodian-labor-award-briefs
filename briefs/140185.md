# Award No. 189/12 — Sien Atrey Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 October 2012)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Virak, Pen Bunhea as chair) · Doc ID 140185

## 1. Facts

Sien Atrey Co., Ltd runs its operation in Tbaes village, Vallis commune, Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu province [Para 1]. On 11 September 2012 the provincial Department of Labour received word by telephone that the company's workers were on strike over conditions of work, and that afternoon it received the complaint of the Solidarity Labour Union Federation of Cambodia [Para 13][Para 16]. Conciliation on 13 September 2012 produced no settlement on the five demands, and the failure report No. 1224/12 of 19 September 2012 reached the Council secretariat on 20 September 2012 [Para 17][Para 18]. The five demands — payment of August wages, annual leave and a 5% bonus, correct recalculation of wages, immediate settlement on dismissal plus 50% of wages for three months for a worker on maternity leave, and an end to coercion of workers — were never reached on the merits, because neither party attended the hearing of 28 September 2012 [Para 3][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7][Para 20]. No documents were filed by either side; the panel sat on the ministry's file alone [Para 21].

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration and decide the five demands, or close the case by order, where both parties were duly notified, neither appeared and neither gave a valid reason for its absence? [Para 26][Para 32][Para 46]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** the two federations filed and pursued the five demands above, but did not appear at the hearing; they told the Council that the workers' wages had already all been paid and that this was why they did not come [Para 43].

**Employer (respondent):** the company had no representative at the hearing either — the award records no appearance and no documents from it — and the person concerned replied that he had not yet received a letter of delegation from the company enabling him to attend [Para 44][Para 21].

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case No. 189/12-Sien Atrey Co., Ltd by order without deciding any of the five demands, on the ground that both parties were absent from the hearing [Para 46][Para 47].

The panel held that it could not establish any of the facts behind the claims, since neither party attended and neither explained its absence [Para 25]. It then framed the question as whether the arbitration should continue, and applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedure: where a party has been properly summoned and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may either continue in that party's absence or close the proceedings by issuing an order [Para 26][Para 27]. Reading its earlier awards — Nos. 30/05-Morea, 416/07, 27/07, 95/08-Young Wa I, 132/08, 138/08, 151/08, 09/09, 01/10 and 30/10-Global Apparel — the panel identified three cumulative conditions for closing a case: the party was properly notified, it did not appear, and it gave no valid reason for not appearing [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31].

On the first condition, the secretariat had issued written invitations to the employer (No. 975) and to the workers (No. 976), both dated 21 September 2012, following its usual procedure, and had confirmed the date, time and place of the hearing by letter and by telephone; the condition was met [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]. On the second, both parties were absent and had appointed no representative such as a federation official or lawyer, as Articles 19 and 20 of Prakas 099 require them to attend [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40]. On the third, the workers' explanation that wages had been paid, and the employer representative's explanation that a delegation letter had not arrived, were both rejected as no valid reason [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]. All three conditions being satisfied, the panel closed the file [Para 46].

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where both parties have been properly notified of the hearing in writing, neither appears and neither advances a valid reason, the panel may close the case by order without ruling on the merits, and a party that treats its own claim as satisfied by payment, or that waits for an internal delegation letter, does not thereby give a valid reason for absence [Para 31][Para 44].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that letters of invitation must follow the secretariat's standing procedure was descriptive of its own practice rather than a condition of the rule [Para 34].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
