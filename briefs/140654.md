# Award No. 109/07 — Khing Lan Garment Cambodia Ltd (Arbitration Council, 12 December 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ying Sothy, An Non, Kong Polha presiding) · Doc ID 140654

## 1. Facts
Khing Lan Garment Cambodia Ltd (ឃីង ឡែន ហ្គាមេន ខេមបូឌា អិលធីឌី), a Phnom Penh factory employing about 600 workers, faced a collective dispute filed by the Coalition of Cambodian Apparel Workers' Democratic Union (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា) [Para 165][Para 166]. The union advanced 46 demands on wages, bonuses, maternity pay, transfers and dismissals; conciliation on 21 September 2007 failed on all 46 points, the employer being absent [Para 59][Para 60]. At five hearings the panel settled 36 by agreement and reserved 10, the trigger events being union-leader dismissals and the 3 September 2007 power cut [Para 63][Para 64].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 7) Whether the company must pay 50% of wages and benefits for three months' maternity leave before the leave begins? [Para 168]
2. (Issue 27) Whether dismissal for a duplicated payroll entry (កាត់បុងជាន់គ្នា) worth under USD 1 was a proportionate sanction? [Para 172]
3. (Issue 31) Whether long-serving cutting-section workers were owed a rise of USD 20 a month? [Para 189]
4. (Issue 35) Whether two dismissed workers had to be reinstated with back pay? [Para 196]
5. (Issue 38) Whether the dismissal of the union's general treasurer rested on adequate evidence? [Para 208]
6. (Issue 39) Whether the company had to dismiss the Chinese workshop chief and the security chief for assaulting workers? [Para 240]
7. (Issue 40) Whether the company had to restore two hours' wages and a USD 2.50 bonus withheld from 17 union members after the power cut? [Para 244]
8. (Issue 41) Whether the warehouse manager had to be dismissed? [Para 262]
9. (Issue 44) Whether the panel could decide the union president's reinstatement while his appeal to the Minister was pending? [Para 473]
10. (Issue 45) Whether the union vice-president and secretary had to be reinstated? [Para 491]
11. (Additional point) Whether the company had to take back seven further workers dismissed during the arbitration? [Para 507]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demands were lawful entitlements; the dismissals retaliated for union activity, the assault by the workshop chief went unpunished, and the company deducted wages from the 17 activists while sparing other workers who stopped work [Para 245][Para 243][Para 260].
**Employer (respondent):** the workers had no legal basis for a raise, a duplicated payroll entry was serious misconduct, the two-hour stoppage was a strike so wages and the bonus were properly withheld, and the union refused to disclose its footage [Para 192][Para 178][Para 433][Para 252].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must pay 50% of wages and benefits for three months' maternity leave in advance (Issue 7), reinstate with back pay the worker dismissed over the payroll entry together with a first written warning (Issue 27), reinstate two dismissed workers (Issue 35), reinstate the general treasurer (Issue 38) and reinstate the union secretary (Issue 45); the raise, assault and management-dismissal demands were dismissed, and Issue 44 and the seven-worker demand were declined [Para 513][Para 514][Para 516][Para 517][Para 522][Para 526].

On Issue 7, Article 182 grants 90 days' maternity leave and Article 183 requires half pay plus benefits during it, which the company was already paying [Para 307][Para 310][Para 312]. Article 115(3) forbids payment on a rest day and requires payment the day before, so the panel followed Award No. 57/06-Evergreen and ordered the three months' half pay in advance [Para 316][Para 318][Para 321][Para 323].

On Issue 27, the internal rules do not rank a duplicated payroll entry as light, medium or serious misconduct [Para 326][Para 328]. Under Article 27 a sanction must match the fault, here under USD 1 with no prior warning, so a written warning and reinstatement replaced dismissal [Para 330][Para 332][Para 335].

On Issue 31, Notice No. 745 set the minimum wage at USD 45 for probationary and USD 50 for full-status workers from 1 January 2007 [Para 336][Para 338]. No worker was paid below USD 50 and no agreement promised a USD 20 rise [Para 342][Para 343]. The union was also not the most representative under Article 277 and Prakas No. 305, so an interest claim failed [Para 346][Para 348].

On Issue 35, the company could not explain the warnings and produced no evidence of the alleged laziness [Para 359][Para 363]. Three warnings issued to one worker in a single day were tied to no internal rule [Para 364][Para 367]. Both dismissals were disproportionate and reinstatement with back pay was ordered [Para 370][Para 371].

On Issue 38, the witnesses who signed the three warnings did not appear, no particulars of time or grade of misconduct were supplied, and on Article 27 the dismissal was disproportionate [Para 400][Para 404][Para 407].

On Issue 39, Article 22 of the 1988 Law on Contracts lets only the parties to a contract terminate it [Para 412][Para 415]. Workers may seek a manager's removal only where he endangers others' safety, and no photograph, medical note or police record was produced [Para 419][Para 421][Para 422].

On Issue 40, the two-hour stoppage was a strike within Article 318, and Article 332 suspends the contract and the wage during a strike [Para 426][Para 427][Para 430]. Withholding the two hours and the USD 2.50 bonus was lawful managerial discretion [Para 433][Para 435][Para 436]. The attendance bonus also failed because the strike did not follow the Article 323 and 324 procedure [Para 451][Para 454][Para 457].

On Issue 41 the same contract-party reasoning applied, and no evidence showed the manager to be dangerous to others' safety [Para 466][Para 471][Para 472].

On Issue 44, Article 293 requires labour-inspector approval to dismiss a union leader; the union appealed the approval of 3 October 2007 to the Minister on 13 November 2007 and the two-month period had not run, so the panel declined to decide [Para 476][Para 481][Para 484][Para 490].

On Issue 45 the vice-president's case was declined for that same reason, but the secretary's dismissal had no evidential support and he was reinstated with back pay [Para 495][Para 504][Para 505].

The additional point failed because Article 312 confines the panel to the conciliation record and its direct consequences, and the seven dismissals were in neither [Para 508][Para 510][Para 512].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissal is unlawful where the internal rules do not rank the conduct as serious misconduct and the proven loss is trivial, because Article 27 requires the sanction to match the fault; the panel then substitutes a written warning plus reinstatement with back pay [Para 330][Para 334].

Wages and attendance bonuses for hours not worked may be withheld where the stoppage amounts to a strike under Article 318, since Article 332 suspends the contract, and the employer's decision to withhold is protected managerial discretion [Para 426][Para 433].

A union that is not the most representative under Article 277 and Prakas No. 305 cannot bring an interest claim such as a wage rise [Para 346][Para 348].

Where a union leader's dismissal has been approved by the labour inspector and is under appeal to the Minister within the two-month period of Article 293, the panel lacks jurisdiction to order reinstatement [Para 484][Para 490].

Maternity half pay must be advanced before the leave begins, because Article 115(3) bars payment on a rest day [Para 316][Para 321].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that no union in the factory enjoyed most-representative status, and refused to view video evidence the company withheld from the workers on the ground that it was reserved for court [Para 167][Para 274].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is printed variously as ឃីង ឡែន, ឃឹង ឡែន and ឃឺង ឡែន within the same document, and the award number on the face is 109/07 while the citation at the maternity reasoning names 57/06-Evergreen and then inserts "2016" [Para 318]. The half-wage figure appears as "៥០៨" where 50% is meant, and Notice No. 745 carries three registration letters [Para 321][Para 341]. The security chief's name was never recorded, both parties stating they did not recall it. The header date of 12 December 2007 follows hearings that ended on 16 November 2007.
