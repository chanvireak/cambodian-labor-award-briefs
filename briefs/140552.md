# Order No. 116/08 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហៃ តិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) អិលធីឌី / Hai Tech Garment (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 19 September 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer party, អន ណន appointed by the worker party, ញាន សុមុន្នីន្ទ chair) · Doc ID 140552

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហៃ តិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) អិលធីឌី (Hai Tech Garment (Cambodia) Ltd), a garment factory at House 536, National Road 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Leu, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employed 463 workers. [Para 1][Para 8] The factory union demanded severance pay under Articles 75, 89, 91, 116 and 166 of the Labour Law on the ground that the company had not given full work, had shipped materials out of the factory, and had repeatedly cut one or two days of annual leave; that demand was never reached in this order. [Para 2] The company replied that it had not closed its doors, that it had no buyers, and that it had suspended work for one month from 29 August to 29 September 2008 by minutes of 26 August 2008. [Para 3] Conciliation failed (non-conciliation report No. 991 of 05 September 2008) and the workers kept striking in front of the factory for the whole of the proceedings, disregarding the panel's instruction of 09 September 2008 to stop by 10 September 2008. [Para 6][Para 12][Para 13][Para 20]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the Arbitration Council may continue to arbitrate the collective dispute when the workers have carried on their strike throughout the proceedings and have disregarded the panel's request to suspend it? [Para 19][Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហៃ តិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) អិលធីឌី workers organised in their factory union:** severance is owed because the employer never provided full employment, removed materials from the premises, and eroded annual leave; at the pre-hearing the union representatives said they could not decide to end the strike on the spot and asked for time to consult the membership. [Para 2][Para 17]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហៃ តិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) អិលធីឌី:** severance cannot be paid because the factory remains in operation and has not closed; the absence of work is caused by the lack of buyers, and work was lawfully suspended for one month under the minutes of 26 August 2008; the company wrote to the Council asking it to resolve the strike. [Para 3][Para 11]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel decided not to continue the arbitration in case No. 116/08 and closed the file, leaving the severance demand undetermined. [Para 43][Para 42]

The panel set out its competence under Chapter 12, Articles 309 to 317 of the Labour Law, Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and the 2007 appointment Prakas No. 076/07. [Para 4] It then applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, which requires the parties, while an arbitration is running, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout (Article 318 of the Labour Law) or any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 33][Para 34] The panel read that provision, as it had done in its earlier order, to mean that it will not carry on an arbitration at a time when the workers are striking or the employer has locked out. [Para 35] It reviewed its own prior jurisprudence: notice of 29 January 2004 in case No. 04/04-IMSI, order No. 26 of 24 December 2005 in case No. 77/05-Frank Co Nitting Garment, and cases No. 28/06-GDM and No. 34/06-Golf Fame, each of which was closed because the strike continued during the proceedings, with a cross-reference to case No. 97/07-Suot Ve. [Para 36][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] On the facts, the panel found the workers had shown no genuine intention to submit the dispute to arbitration, since they went on striking and did not comply with its order and request. [Para 41] Because the hearing could not proceed, the panel made no findings of fact on the non-conciliation report. [Para 32] It therefore decided, in order to comply with the law and with its own earlier orders, not to continue the arbitration. [Para 42][Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the party bringing a collective dispute continues a strike — or the employer a lockout — after the Arbitration Council has been seised of the case and has ordered the dispute measures suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the Council will not continue the arbitration and will close the file; continuing the dispute measure is treated as proof that the party does not genuinely intend to have the dispute decided by arbitration, and the merits are left undetermined rather than decided in that party's favour. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 41][Para 43]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's survey of its earlier orders in cases 04/04-IMSI, 77/05, 28/06 and 34/06/07 and its statement that the parties must attend every meeting are general observations supporting the practice, not findings on these facts. [Para 34][Para 36][Para 39]

- The order recites no issue list of its own; the only disputes stated are those in the non-conciliation report, and the severance demand under six articles is nowhere decided. Issue 1 in §2 is the question the panel actually answered at [Para 41].
- The factory address is printed twice and differently: National Road 2 at [Para 1] and National Road 245 at [Para 8].
- The employer-party arbitrator's name carries two different final spellings — on the face of the order at [Para 1] and in the signature block at [Para 44]; the discrepancy is not resolved in the text.
- The date of the final strike report is garbled: "18 September 2008" appears with the month abbreviated to a form that does not spell September [Para 20].
- OCR losses: stray Latin fragments ("Grover", "C. Phy", "ខែ-") are embedded in the Khmer at [Para 23][Para 24][Para 42], and several sentences retain broken consonant clusters, but the reasoning remains readable throughout.
