# Award No. 82/10 — Ekklat Highther International Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អិក្លាក ហាយ ធើ អ៊ិនធើណេសិនណល ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ខូអិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council, 13 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-nominated), ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ (worker-nominated), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (chair) · Doc ID 140299

## 1. Facts

The Khmer Youth Voice Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ) and its local union at the Ekklat Highther International Garment factory in Kouk Chambok, Choam Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, filed 23 collective demands on 14 June 2010, beginning with the reinstatement of two dismissed union leaders and running through wages on no-work days, overtime, medical checks, canteen and childcare facilities, annual leave, and a US$1,000 monthly union dues deduction. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 25][Para 32] Conciliation failed after the employer failed to attend, and the panel listed the case for hearing on 3 August 2010. [Para 26][Para 29][Para 31] The claimant union then did not appear at the hearing and gave no reason; the employer did appear. [Para 35][Para 36] None of the 23 demands was ever examined on its merits.

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Issue 1 — procedure) Whether the panel must close the file and treat the 23 demands as abandoned where the claimant union, properly summoned, failed to appear and offered no valid reason? [Para 55][Para 60][Para 73]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ and សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អិក្លាត ហាយ ធើ:** the union pressed all 23 demands and asked the company to settle them in accordance with the Labour Law; when the secretariat telephoned before the hearing it said it would not attend and that it had no other representative to send, and asked the panel to proceed as it saw fit. [Para 25][Para 66][Para 68]

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អិក្លាត ហាយ ធើ:** the company had failed to attend conciliation without explanation, but appeared at the hearing through its HR and administration managers as summoned. [Para 26][Para 36]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed file 82/10 and treated the claim as abandoned by the claimant, making no order on any of the 23 demands. [Para 78][Para 79]

The panel took the governing rule to be Article 21 of Prakas 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the arbitration procedure: where a party has been properly summoned and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may either continue in that party's absence or close the proceedings by issuing an award. [Para 55][Para 56] Following Awards 16/07 (Loutus) and 27/07 (IM & W 3), it distilled three conditions — valid notification, non-appearance, and no valid reason. [Para 57]

All three were satisfied. The union had received the written invitation and a telephone reminder, so condition one was met. [Para 60][Para 61] Condition two was met because appearance means both attending and taking part to the end, and the union did neither. [Para 62][Para 64] Condition three was met because the union gave no clear reason and declined the offer to send another representative. [Para 66][Para 71]

The panel recorded that the claimant bears the duty to state reasons and produce evidence before it, and that by staying away the union had given up that opportunity; it characterised the conduct as abandonment of the claim. [Para 76][Para 77][Para 78] It noted that the union had in any event been given a full opportunity to defend its demands. [Para 74][Para 75]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where the claimant union has been validly summoned and stays away without valid reason, the panel will close the file and treat the claim as abandoned rather than decide the demands in the claimant's absence, because the burden of proving the demands lies on the claimant and an unproven demand cannot be granted. [Para 73][Para 76][Para 78]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that Rule 4.7 would equally have allowed it to continue and decide the case in the union's absence, so the choice to close was discretionary. [Para 55]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The employer's name appears in at least five forms — អិក្លាក ហាយ ធើ, អិក្លាត ហាយ ធើ, អិក្តាត ហាយ ធើ, អិក្លាត បរាយ ធើ and រ៉ិំក្ខាត ហាយ — so the entity cannot be fixed from the face of the award. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 44][Para 79] The Prakas is cited once as No. 199 instead of 099. [Para 62] A conciliation minute is dated 15 August 2010 and a hearing invitation 28 August 2010, both after the 3 August hearing they relate to. [Para 48][Para 52] The employer arbitrator's name is printed as អ៉ីង សុឡឺ in the signature block. [Para 80] The two dismissed leaders are named inconsistently between the demand list and the evidence list. [Para 2][Para 37] Individual union officials appear by full name in the header and are not reproduced here.
