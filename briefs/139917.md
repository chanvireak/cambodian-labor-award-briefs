# Order No. 270/13 — 8 Star Sport Wear Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 December 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy appointed by the employer party, An Non appointed by the worker party, Run Saray chair) · Doc ID 139917

## 1. Facts
The Federation of Workers' Unions (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត) filed for the workers of 8 Star Sport Wear Ltd, a factory in Stung Mean Chey, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, claiming six improvements in conditions: a base wage of at least USD 95, a USD 15 attendance bonus treated as a standing entitlement, a USD 10 monthly milk allowance for workers with small children, payment for unused annual leave, a midday meal, and USD 1,500 for travel and accommodation. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12] Conciliation on 4 December 2013 settled five of eleven claims and left six; report No. 1613 of 17 December 2013 referred them to the Council. [Para 20][Para 21] The hearing was set for 25 December 2013 at 08:30; the workers did not appear, and their federation vice-president telephoned to say they were busy striking. [Para 18][Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay workers a base wage of at least USD 95? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must pay a USD 15 attendance bonus and treat it as a standing entitlement? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay USD 10 a month as a milk allowance to workers with small children? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must pay workers for annual leave not taken? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must provide one midday meal to workers? [Para 10]
6. Whether the company must pay USD 1,500 for travel and accommodation? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the six claims above were pressed as improvements to working conditions, but the union sent no representative, filed no documents, and stated by telephone on the day of the hearing only that it was occupied with a strike. [Para 19][Para 23][Para 24]
**Employer (respondent):** it pays what the law requires and cannot grant the wage, milk, meal and travel claims; the attendance bonus has already been paid as a standing item; and it does not agree to paying out unused annual leave. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 13] It added that the strike was called by another union over the 24 December 2013 Labour Advisory Committee statement on the minimum wage, not over these claims. [Para 26]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel deemed the claims abandoned for failure to appear and terminated the arbitration in case No. 270/13, deciding none of the six claims on the merits. [Para 65][Para 68][Para 69]

The panel treated the case as a procedural one: what should a panel do when the worker party is absent? [Para 32] Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 obliges the panel to invite the parties to explain their claims in person and to produce documents, Article 20 obliges them to attend every meeting, and Article 21 permits the panel either to continue in the absence of a party properly summoned who fails to appear without valid reason, or to close the arbitration by order; Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules says the same. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] The panel distilled three conditions from its earlier orders — proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid reason — and applied them in turn. [Para 40][Para 44][Para 45] On condition 1, invitation No. 1344 of 18 December 2013 gave the date, place and contact numbers and was signed for at 11:00 on 20 December 2013 by the federation vice-president, and the secretariat telephoned again on the morning of the hearing. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] On condition 2, "appearing before the panel" means both being present and taking part from start to finish in the four-stage hearing; neither the workers nor any authorised representative attended. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53] On condition 3, the only explanation offered was that the workers were occupied with a strike, which the panel did not accept as valid. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57] Because the claimant bears the burden of setting out reasons and evidence first, its absence meant the claims were abandoned and there was nothing left to continue. [Para 61][Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claimant union that is properly summoned, fails to appear or send an authorised representative, and offers no valid reason — being occupied with a strike is not one — is deemed to have abandoned its claims, and the panel will close the arbitration by order under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY without ruling on the merits. [Para 21][Para 44][Para 56][Para 65]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's four-stage description of a hearing, and its reading of Article 19 that a party may appear in person, with an assistant, or through an enrolled lawyer or a person authorised in writing, are general statements of procedure not required by the outcome [Para 50][Para 51]; so is its acceptance of the employer's account that the strike concerned the 24 December 2013 minimum-wage statement rather than this case. [Para 26]
