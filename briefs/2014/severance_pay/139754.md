# Award No. 304/14 — Long Lead (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 4 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Aing Eng Tong (president) · Doc ID 139754

## 1. Facts
Long Lead (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a shoe factory at Chamkar Svay, Sethey commune, Samaki Meanchey district, Kampong Chhnang, employing about 1,503 workers, had agreed on 21 July 2014 at the provincial labour department to take back Mr K — a storekeeper who had resigned on 21 December 2013 with severance paid — subject to three conditions. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60] The conditions were a pre-employment medical check, a certificate of good conduct from a competent authority, and signature of a new contract. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62] Mr K obtained a health certificate on 01 August 2014 and a conduct certificate No. 477/14 dated 22 July 2014, but the commune chief then issued a letter of 23 July 2014 withdrawing that certificate. [Para 100][Para 105][Para 116]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer is obliged to re-employ Mr K under the agreement of 21 July 2014, he having satisfied its conditions? [Para 85][Para 100]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr K completed conditions 1 and 2 — health certificate of 01 August 2014 from the Ministry's occupational health department and conduct certificate No. 477/14 of 22 July 2014 — and took both to the company adviser on about 04 or 05 August 2014, but was turned away at the gate; the withdrawal letter is not accepted because the commune chief and police chief are friends of the company adviser. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 72][Para 81]
**Employer (respondent):** it never received the documents; it first saw them at the labour department on 24 October 2014, and in any event the commune chief withdrew the conduct certificate by letter of 23 July 2014 stating that Mr K had intentionally injured others, so condition 2 is unmet. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 79][Para 77]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to order re-employment, and no sum of money was awarded. [Para 122][Para 123]

The panel treated the 21 July 2014 instrument as a binding contract. Article 311 of the 2007 Civil Code defines a contract as the meeting of wills creating, modifying or extinguishing obligations; Article 336 gives effect to offer and acceptance; Article 337 ¶¶1 and 3 bind the parties legally once the offer is accepted. [Para 85][Para 86][Para 87][Para 89][Para 90] Following Award 271/14, an employer must perform an agreement it made with its workers, so the conditions governed. [Para 92][Para 93]

Condition 1 was satisfied: both parties accepted the health certificate of 01 August 2014 from the occupational health department. [Para 100][Para 101] Condition 2 was not. The panel found that the conduct certificate No. 477/14 of 22 July 2014 existed, but that the commune chief withdrew it by letter of 23 July 2014, so the certificate lost its validity and no longer proved good conduct. [Para 105][Para 116][Para 117] Because the party asserting a fact bears the burden of proof — Awards 148/07, 34/11 and 203/12 — and the workers produced nothing beyond a certificate that had been withdrawn, the panel held condition 2 unproven. [Para 109][Para 110][Para 118][Para 119] It also found that Mr K never delivered the documents to the employer before 24 October 2014, so condition 2 was not fulfilled in the manner the agreement required. [Para 107][Para 120] Condition 3 was treated as performable only once the first two were met, and never became due. [Para 68][Para 99] The employer was therefore under no obligation to re-employ him. [Para 121]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a settlement makes re-employment conditional, the worker must satisfy every condition, and a certificate of good conduct that its own issuing authority has withdrawn is worthless as proof; the party asserting performance carries the burden, so a claim built on a revoked document fails even where the employer's conduct is open to criticism. [Para 109][Para 117][Para 118][Para 121]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel accepted that the employer's relationship with the commune chief arose only from ordinary administrative contact and rejected the suggestion of improper friendship. [Para 83][Para 82]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The withdrawal letter is dated 23 July 2014 in the reasoning but 23 October 2014 in the employer's account, and the employer's knowledge of the documents is placed at 24 October 2014; the panel cites 25 July 2014 for receipt of the withdrawal. [Para 79][Para 107][Para 108] The worker's name appears as "ឃ្លី ជេត", "ឃី ជេត" and "ឃ្លីប ជីត". [Para 24][Para 29][Para 71] The panel's own framing of the question is partly corrupted by OCR in the middle of the paragraph. [Para 85] The factory is named "ឡុង លីដ", "ឡុង លីដ៍" and once "ទ្រង លីដ". [Para 1][Para 40][Para 43] No riel or dollar figures are printed because nothing was ordered.
