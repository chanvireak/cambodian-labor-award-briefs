# Award No. 085/14 — Y.T.C. Shop Promotion (Arbitration Council, 29 April 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Pen Bun Chhea (president), Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed) · Doc ID 139547

## 1. Facts
Workers at a garment factory at Vallis village, Samraong Tong district, Kampong Speu, raised eleven demands through the Khmer Workers' Voice Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង កម្មករ កម្ពុជា). [Para 1][Para 13] Five points settled at conciliation on 26 March 2014 and six were referred to the Council on 1 April 2014. [Para 14][Para 16] The hearing was listed twice; on 24 April 2014 the employer attended but the federation's president telephoned at 2:10 pm to say he was in Svay Rieng and asked the panel to decide in the workers' absence. [Para 20][Para 22][Para 24] The claimant filed no evidence at all. [Para 32]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The six unresolved points, numbered as in the non-conciliation report:
1. Whether the attendance bonus must be raised to USD 20? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay a USD 20 travel allowance? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must pay 5,000 riel a day in lieu of a midday meal? [Para 4]
4. Whether the employer must pay a USD 20 monthly "cooperation" allowance? [Para 5]
5. Whether the employer must pay a USD 15 monthly milk allowance? [Para 6]
6. Whether the employer must pay 2,000 riel for each overtime hour? [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The six demands were carried into the non-conciliation report; no representative attended and no document or argument was submitted in support. [Para 16][Para 32][Para 61]
**Employer (respondent):** The company appeared through its director, its head of administration and an administrative assistant, and had earlier obtained an adjournment of the 10 April listing because the director was occupied with company business. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration and made no order on any of the six demands. [Para 63][Para 65][Para 66]

Article 21 of Prakas 099 and Rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules let the panel either continue in a party's absence or close the file by issuing an award. [Para 41][Para 42] Following five earlier awards, the panel applied three cumulative conditions: the party was properly summoned, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason for not appearing. [Para 44][Para 45]

On the first condition, invitation No. 332 of 9 April 2014 gave the date, time and place, and Mr P signed for it on 9 April 2014 on the federation's behalf. [Para 46][Para 47] On the second, the panel read "appearing before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation through all four stages — introduction and conflict check, explanation of procedure and issues, conciliation if the parties agree, and the hearing itself; the claimant did neither. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50] On the third, no explanation was offered until the telephone call on the day of the hearing, and the panel did not accept that being in Svay Rieng was a valid reason. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54]

The panel added that the claimant must first set out its grounds and evidence to establish its claim, and that the respondent's duty to answer arises only after the claimant has done so; because the claimant never appeared, that burden was never discharged and the claim was treated as abandoned. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union has been duly summoned with notice of the date, time and place, fails to appear, and gives no valid reason, the panel treats the claim as abandoned and closes the file rather than deciding the demands on their merits. [Para 44][Para 63]

**Obiter dicta:** Appearing means participating through all four stages of the hearing, not mere attendance; being in another province on the day is not a valid reason; and the respondent's burden of answer arises only once the claimant has presented its case. [Para 48][Para 53][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The surviving demands are numbered 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9, the other five having settled, so the award contains no points 1, 5, 7, 10 or 11 and the issues cannot be renumbered from the face of the report. [Para 14][Para 16] The panel's constitution date is garbled as "ថ្ងៃទី ០៣ ខែមេសា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៤". [Para 17] A first hearing fixed for 10 April 2014 was adjourned to 24 April 2014. [Para 18][Para 20] The employer's name is printed in at least three different Khmer spellings and no Latin-script name appears anywhere in the award. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 65] The citation of Prakas 099 appears as "ប្រការ ២១៩៧" where Article 21 is meant. [Para 41] The workers' side is recorded as having no representative. [Para 1]
