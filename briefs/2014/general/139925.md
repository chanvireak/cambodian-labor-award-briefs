# Award No. 280/13 — Maw Sun (Cambodia) Garment (Arbitration Council, 21 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Non, Sokh Ma Theung presiding) · Doc ID 139925

## 1. Facts
Maw Sun (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd is a garment factory at Ang Ta Kiet, Kantok, Dangkor, Phnom Penh [Para 2]. The Federation of Unions for Labour Rights brought twelve demands on behalf of the workforce; conciliation on 21 December 2013 settled eleven and left one, and the file reached the Council on 26 December 2013 [Para 11][Para 12]. The single surviving demand asks the company to remove a Chinese manager, said to oppress Khmer workers; the employer answered that it cannot remove him but will educate and instruct him under the internal rules [Para 3][Para 4]. Thirteen workers had authorised six federations to act for them, and hearings were fixed for 8 and again for 15 January 2014 [Para 16][Para 8][Para 9].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must dismiss the Chinese manager concerned from his post because he oppresses Khmer workers? [Para 3]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The manager's conduct towards Khmer workers is oppressive and he should be removed from his position; the workers maintained the demand after conciliation failed on it [Para 3].
**Employer (respondent):** The company cannot remove the manager from his job, but will educate and advise him in line with the company's internal rules [Para 4]. Neither the employer nor three of the six invited federations attended either hearing, and the employer told the secretariat by telephone that it could not attend [Para 48][Para 44].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel did not decide the claim. It terminated the arbitration proceedings in case 280/13 for the non-appearance of both parties, making no order for or against the employer [Para 55][Para 56].

The panel began by asking whether it should continue at all when the employer and three of the invited federations — the Peace for Workers Federation, the Cambodian Workers' Support Federation and the Cambodian Labour Federation — were absent [Para 26]. Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 provides that where a party has been properly invited and stays away without a valid reason, the panel may either continue in that party's absence or close the proceedings by issuing an award, and Rule 4.7 of the annexed Rules of Procedure says the same [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]. Earlier awards had distilled three conditions for closing a file this way: proper notification, non-appearance, and no valid excuse [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32].

All three were met. On notification, the secretariat issued written invitations to the employer (letter No. 032 of 8 January 2014) and to the workers (No. 033 of the same date), naming the date, time and place, and followed up by telephone [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 39]. On appearance, the panel read "appear before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both presence and participation throughout the four stages of the hearing, and found neither party present nor represented under a written authorisation [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]. On excuse, the employer merely said it could not come, the Labour Federation president said the company would not let the local union out to attend, the Peace for Workers official said he had forgotten the date, and the Support Federation could not be reached; the panel found no valid reason from any of them [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 51][Para 52]. Having found no facts capable of grounding the demand, it closed the proceedings rather than decide it [Para 25][Para 53][Para 55].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where both parties have been properly notified of the hearing and neither appears nor is lawfully represented, without giving a valid reason, the panel will exercise its discretion under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 to terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the claim on its merits [Para 26][Para 44][Para 53][Para 55].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the employer's undertaking to educate the manager under the internal rules was recorded but never tested, since no hearing took place [Para 4][Para 25].
