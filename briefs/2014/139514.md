# Award No. 022/14 — Zhong Yin (Cambodia) B Textile Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 24 February 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chhea (president) · Doc ID 139514

## 1. Facts
Zhong Yin (Cambodia) B Textile Co., Ltd. (ស្យុង យិន), registered under Inv. 0952 E/2013 on 17 May 2013 and employing about 1,077 workers at Prek Traeng, Saang, Kandal, faced twelve demands filed on 29 November 2013 by the Labour Union for Cambodia federation. Conciliation failed on 27 January 2014. At the hearing the parties agreed four issues (2, 4, 6, 12) and the workers withdrew four more (1, 7, 9, 10), leaving issues 3, 5, 8 and 11. Sixty-nine workers had transferred their rights to the federation; after removing two duplicated names the panel counted 67, plus 3 present at the hearing, i.e. 70 claimants. [Para 28][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 58][Para 63][Para 66][Para 70]

## 2. Issues in dispute
3. Whether the employer must pay a USD 5 monthly health-care allowance to workers? [Para 6][Para 96]
5. Whether the employer must pay USD 15 a month in travel and accommodation allowance, i.e. USD 8 on top of the USD 7 already paid? [Para 10][Para 113]
8. Whether the employer must provide one free midday meal, or pay 4,000 riel per worker per day? [Para 16][Para 119]
11. Whether the employer must raise the monthly base wage to USD 150? [Para 22][Para 124]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** wages are too low, workers fall ill for lack of food, and the allowance and meal demands would raise living standards; other factories give two meals and USD 10 of travel allowance. [Para 72][Para 78][Para 86][Para 92]
**Employer (respondent):** it already pays the USD 5 health allowance inside base wage, pays the USD 7 travel and accommodation allowance set by Notification No. 230, and pays the USD 100 minimum wage under Prakas No. 317; it cannot go further. [Para 75][Para 81][Para 83][Para 95][Para 114][Para 127]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider all four surviving demands, making no order for payment and fixing no sum in riel or dollars. [Para 132][Para 133][Para 134][Para 135]

On standing, the Labour Union for Cambodia at the factory was elected on 21 December 2013 but had not received its registration certificate from the Ministry, so it was not yet a lawful professional organisation. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 61][Para 62] The federation, though a signatory of the 3 October 2012 Memorandum of Understanding, therefore could not be treated as a party to the collective agreement through that local union, notwithstanding the transfers of rights from the 70 workers. [Para 44][Para 63][Para 70]

On the merits of each demand, the panel found no legal duty in any of them. On issue 3, Notification No. 103 of 9 April 2013 required the USD 5 health-care allowance to be merged into the base wage, and the panel found the employer had done so. [Para 71][Para 96][Para 97] On issue 5, Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 sets the travel and accommodation allowance at USD 7 a month, which the employer pays; the claim for a further USD 8 had no basis in law, collective agreement, internal rules or past practice. [Para 113][Para 114][Para 115] On issue 8, no provision obliged the employer to supply a free midday meal or 4,000 riel a day. [Para 120] On issue 11, Prakas No. 317 raised the minimum from USD 80 to USD 100 from 1 February 2014 and the employer paid it, so the claim to USD 150 exceeded the legal minimum. [Para 124][Para 126][Para 127][Para 128]

Each of the four was accordingly an interest dispute, not a rights dispute. [Para 99][Para 117][Para 122][Para 130] Applying Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305, and following cases 169/11, 81/04-Evergreen, 98/04-Great Union, 02/11-Pou Yuen and 66/11-Inhansung, the panel held that only the most representative union may bring an interest dispute, because a Prakas 099 Article 43 award replaces the collective agreement for a year and removes the right to strike over the same interest. [Para 102][Para 104][Para 106][Para 107][Para 108] The claimants here were 70 workers, not the most representative union, so the demands were not properly before the panel and it dismissed them without reaching their substance. [Para 109][Para 110][Para 111]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a demand asks for more than the Labour Law, a Prakas or a notification already secures, it is an interest dispute; the panel will decline it unless the claimant is the most representative union in the enterprise, and a transfer of rights from dozens of individual workers does not cure that defect. [Para 106][Para 110]

**Obiter dicta:** An allowance that a notification has merged into the base wage cannot be claimed a second time as a separate monthly payment. [Para 97]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated 24 February 2014, but the panel is recorded as constituted on "០៣ ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០២៣ ២០១៤" (Para 34), and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 is printed as "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០០៤" (Para 104). A stray "២០២៣" corrupts the operative paragraph on issue 3 (Para 132). The president's name appears as "ម៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាម20227" and then correctly as Pen Bun Chhea (Para 137), and the workers' party heading is garbled to "តាតី កម្មករ និយោជិត" (Para 1). The conciliation report number is given as 199/14 Kb/Kandal and 199/14 Kb/Phnom Penh in different places. Para 90 attributes to the workers' side a contention that plainly belongs to the employer; it is read here as an employer argument. [Para 1][Para 34][Para 54][Para 90][Para 104][Para 137]
