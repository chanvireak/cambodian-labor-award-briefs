# Award No. 293/14 — Hong Wah (Cambodia) Garment (Building C) (Arbitration Council, 22 October 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Run Saray, presiding) · Doc ID 139747

## 1. Facts
Hong Wah (Cambodia) Garment, Building C, at Trapeang Thleung, Choam Chao, Por Senchey, Phnom Penh, held an election of worker delegates on 4 October 2014. That afternoon the workers demanded their daily wage for the rest of the day and did not return to work; the stoppage continued as a strike. Conciliation on 16 October 2014 failed on the single demand, and the panel received the file on 17 October 2014. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 6][Para 10]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay the daily wage for the afternoon of 4 October 2014, after the worker-delegate election had been held successfully, when the workers did not return to work? [Para 2][Para 9]

The panel framed its own question as whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demand was for the afternoon's wage of 4 October 2014, and the workers did not resume work afterwards. [Para 2][Para 19]
**Employer (respondent):** it could not grant the demand; on 17 October 2014 it asked the Council to order the workers to stop striking and return immediately, and it reported that the workers had added further demands after the injunction. [Para 3][Para 13][Para 18]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** proceedings terminated — the panel closed the file without deciding the wage claim, so nothing was ordered in riel or USD. [Para 37][Para 38]

No documents were filed by the workers; the record consisted of the company's letter of 17 October 2014, the labour department's non-conciliation report No. 1183 and minutes of 16 October 2014, and the panel's own invitations. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 22][Para 24]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 (21 April 2004), requiring parties to suspend all dispute measures — strikes or lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law — during the arbitration, and to attend every meeting convened. Reading that provision as it had in Awards 59/06, 39/08, 137/09 and 138/09, it held it will not continue an arbitration while workers are striking, and may take the dispute up again only once all the unions stop striking. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]

The panel then recorded that on 17 October 2014 it had issued injunction No. 055/14 ordering an immediate suspension of the strike from the morning of 18 October 2014 and requiring the employer to take the strikers back into normal work, and that the workers did not comply. At the pre-hearing meeting of 21 October 2014 both sides confirmed the strike was still running. The panel concluded the workers had no intention of having the dispute resolved by the Council, and terminated the proceedings under Article 20. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 19][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers keep a strike running after the panel has ordered its suspension under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will not reach the merits and will terminate the arbitration; the file may be re-opened only once the strike has actually ended. [Para 31][Para 36]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that earlier awards (Nos 28/06, 34/06, 110/11, 17/13) had closed or terminated cases on the same ground, and that the employer had reported new demands being added after the injunction was issued. [Para 18][Para 32][Para 33]
