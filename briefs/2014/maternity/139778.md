# Award No. 331/14 — Cambodian Pacific Fashion (Arbitration Council, 19 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Hem Hu Narith (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chea (chair) · Doc ID 139778

## 1. Facts
Cambodian Pacific Fashion (ខេមបូឌាន ផេស៊ីហ្វិក ហ្វេស សិន) makes mattress covers in the Sihanoukville special economic zone and employed 140 workers. [Para 32][Para 33] Four federations filed six collective claims on 31 October 2014; conciliation failed and the non-conciliation report No. 0724/14 of 21 November 2014 reached the Council on 26 November 2014. [Para 14][Para 10][Para 16] The employer stayed away from the first hearing on 4 December 2014 but filed fingerprint statements from seven workers denying they had authorised the four federations to represent them. [Para 18][Para 19] A second hearing was fixed for 11 December 2014, and the claimant workers' party did not appear. [Para 21][Para 47]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, when a worker takes personal leave, the company must prorate the USD 15 monthly attendance bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់ប្រចាំខែ, "prime") rather than forfeit it? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must refrain from deducting wages and the attendance bonus when a worker takes sick leave supported by a valid certificate from a State doctor? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must issue the payroll sheet for workers to inspect seven days before payday? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company must provide a doctor on duty throughout working hours and adequate medicine? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must pay the maternity allowance 90 days before the worker goes on maternity leave? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company must build a nursery and breastfeeding room, or failing that supply three cans of milk powder and USD 20 per month in childcare allowance per worker? [Para 7]
7. (The procedural question the panel actually decided) Whether the panel must continue the arbitration when the claimant workers' party, duly summoned, fails to appear at the second hearing? [Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The six claims stood as recorded in the non-conciliation report; at the first hearing the workers' side asked for an adjournment because it doubted the authenticity of the employer's fingerprint statements, and it later told the secretariat only that the company's workers could not attend the second hearing. [Para 36][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent):** Absent from the first hearing, the employer filed statements from seven workers with employee numbers 002, 0130, 0042, 0036, 0011, 0074 and 0085 dated 16 November 2014, each refusing to place a fingerprint transferring rights to the four federations, and so denied that the 13 alleged transferors were genuine complainants. [Para 19][Para 22][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 331/14 and issued an award; none of the six claims was examined on the merits and no relief was ordered. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58]

The panel framed the only live question as whether to continue in the claimant's absence. Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules allow a panel, where a duly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason, either to continue or to close the case by award, provided notice was properly given. [Para 39][Para 40]

Following earlier awards (16/07, 27/07, 1138/08, 11/11 and 131/12), the panel read Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions: the party was properly summoned, the party did not appear, and the party gave no valid reason. [Para 41][Para 42] It found condition one satisfied: invitation letter No. 1708 of 4 December 2014 fixed the second hearing at 14:00 on 11 December 2014 and was signed for at 15:00 on 4 December by the representative of the Khmer Workers' Pride Federation, and the secretariat telephoned a reminder at about 13:40 on 11 December. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] Condition two was satisfied because no one appeared at the hearing. [Para 47][Para 48] Condition three was satisfied because the party offered no adequate or specific reason, saying only that the company's workers could not attend. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53] With all three met, the panel treated the complaint as abandoned and closed the arbitration. [Para 55][Para 56]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been properly summoned, fails to appear at the hearing, and gives no valid reason for its absence, the panel does not decide the claims: the three conditions of Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 are cumulative and, once all are met, the complaint is treated as abandoned and the arbitration is terminated by award. [Para 41][Para 56]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded that it could not establish the facts of the six claims because the claimant was absent, and that the employer's seven fingerprint statements rejecting the transfer of rights remained disputed by the workers' side. [Para 36][Para 38]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award contradicts itself on who attended the first hearing: paragraph 18 says the workers' party attended while the employer did not, paragraph 20 says no worker of the company attended at all. [Para 18][Para 20] The first hearing date is printed "០៤ ខែធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៤", carrying both 2019 and 2014. [Para 17] Prakas No. 099 is dated "ថ្ងៃទី ២៥ ២១ ខែមេសា ឆ្នាំ ២០០៤" in the closing summary but 21 April 2004 elsewhere. [Para 8][Para 55] The signature block is badly corrupted, giving the worker-side arbitrator as "អន លាន" where the header prints "អន ណន" and the employer-side arbitrator as "ហែម ហួរ ណារិទ្ធ" against "ហែម ហួ ណារិទ្ធ" in the header; stray strings ("The 244 . 24.", "4162", "recollexam") sit in the same block. [Para 1][Para 59] The four federations are named inconsistently across the document, and the non-conciliation report number appears as 0724/14 with varying abbreviations. [Para 1][Para 24] The whole award reflows into 59 sentences because the OCR merged the claim list into the header paragraph.
