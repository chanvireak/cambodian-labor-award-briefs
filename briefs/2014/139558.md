# Award No. 100/14 — Victco Handbags (Cambodia) Industrial Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 May 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; Liv Sovanna, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139558

## 1. Facts
Victco Handbags (Cambodia) Industrial Co., Ltd operates at Trapeang Sang Chiek village, Samraong Tong district, Kampong Speu province. A strike led by the Free Trade Union of Workers of the Kingdom of Cambodia produced six collective demands: a USD 15 transport allowance, a USD 15 midday meal allowance, no wage deduction on 18, 19, 21 and 22 April 2014, reinstatement of a dismissed worker, an end to deductions made for a union that had not assisted the workers, and no deduction from workers on sick leave supported by a medical certificate. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 7] Conciliation at the Kampong Speu provincial department of labour failed, and non-conciliation report No. 763/14 dated 23 April 2014 was forwarded to the Council on 25 April 2014. [Para 10][Para 18] The panel was constituted on 29 April 2014 and sat once, on 8 May 2014; the workers' side was recorded as absent. [Para 12][Para 19][Para 1]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether, where the claimant workers were duly summoned but failed to appear at the hearing without valid reason, the panel should continue the arbitration in their absence or terminate it by award? [Para 37][Para 50]
2. Whether the three conditions for that choice — proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of a valid excuse — were satisfied? [Para 45][Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers pressed the six demands recorded in the non-conciliation report but entered no appearance and appointed no representative for the hearing. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 56]
**Employer (respondent):** Appearing through Mr C under an authority transferred by the company director on 7 May 2014, the company asked on 5 May 2014 to withdraw the complaint, asserting that on 25 April 2014 its representative had met the union and some workers and that the two sides had agreed to end the dispute. [Para 25][Para 21][Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** terminated — the panel closed the arbitration of case No. 100/14; no order was made on the merits and no sum was awarded [Para 69][Para 70]

The panel worked from Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules. Article 21 permits the panel, where a properly summoned party fails to appear without valid reason, either to continue in that party's absence or to close the arbitration by award; Rule 4.7 imposes the same precondition of proper notice of date, time and place. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] The panel restated the three cumulative conditions it had applied in earlier awards and adopted them for this case. [Para 45][Para 48][Para 49]

On notification, invitation No. 513 dated 2 May 2014 was issued to the workers and emailed with the date, place and contact details, and secretariat staff also telephoned them. In that call the workers' side stated that they could not attend and that a union representative would come on the Monday to withdraw the complaint. The panel held that the workers had in fact been properly notified, so condition one was met. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]

On appearance, the panel read "appearing before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation in it from start to finish across its four stages, and noted that a party may appear in person or through a lawyer or other person authorised in writing. The workers were absent and had appointed no authorised representative, so condition two was met. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

On valid reason, the only explanation was the telephone statement of Mr S, deputy secretary-general of the union, that the workers could not attend and that the panel should proceed according to law. The panel held that this was no valid reason for non-appearance, so condition three was met. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61] Having found all three conditions satisfied, and having been unable to establish the facts because of the absence, the panel treated the claimant as having abandoned its claims and closed the proceedings. [Para 36][Para 67][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been properly notified of the hearing and neither appears nor appoints an authorised representative, and gives no valid reason, the panel may treat the claims as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by award without ruling on the merits. [Para 49][Para 67][Para 68]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's general remarks on the parties' duties — that each must attend to state its case and supply documents, and that the respondent's burden arises only after the claimant has first discharged its own — were not applied to any issue. [Para 63][Para 65]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a Khmer original with heavy OCR damage. The employer's withdrawal request of 5 May 2014 is recorded in the procedural narrative, together with the assertion that the parties settled on 25 April 2014, but the panel never addresses it in the reasons or the order, so the record contains an unresolved contradiction between a consensual termination and the termination for abandonment that was actually ordered. [Para 21][Para 68][Para 70] The list of prior awards supporting the three-condition test is badly garbled, with case numbers, dates and party names run together. [Para 48] The president's name appears as "Pen Bun Chhea" in the composition paragraph and as a different spelling in the signature block, and the signature block otherwise carries blank name and date fields. [Para 1][Para 71] The letterhead year "2017" is embedded in the conciliation narrative although the events are from 2014. [Para 17] The sixth demand is ambiguous on its face, referring to deductions for "a union that did not help the workers" without identifying the union. [Para 6] The final order misprints "Arbitration Council" as "អាជ្ញាធរកណ្តាល". [Para 69]
