# Award No. 005/14 — Khen Theren Ei Pheril (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 20 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Somborna, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phalha, president) · Doc ID 139502

## 1. Facts

Workers at the company's factory in Trapeang Thleung, Chom Chao, Po Sen Chey, Phnom Penh struck from 25 December 2013, demanding that the employer pay 100 per cent of wages for the strike period. [Para 2][Para 9] The labour dispute department's conciliation on 09 January 2014 failed on that single point, and report No. 021 of 10 January 2014 sent it to the Council. [Para 10][Para 11] The strike continued after the referral. [Para 12] On the company's application of 10 January 2014 the panel issued injunction No. 002/14 of 14 January 2014 ordering the strike suspended immediately and all strikers back to work from the afternoon of 14 January 2014; the strikers did not comply. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 15] At a pre-hearing meeting on 16 January 2014 both parties attended and confirmed the strike was still running. [Para 39]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company must pay 100 per cent of wages to workers for the strike period from 25 December 2013 to 02 January 2014, and from 02 to 09 January 2014? [Para 2][Para 9]
2. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration at all, given that the workers remained on strike after the referral and disobeyed the panel's injunction? [Para 26][Para 41]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** The workers, represented through CUMW and CCAWDU officials and the Workers' Movement Union, maintained the strike and demanded full wages for the strike days, refusing to return to work notwithstanding the panel's order. [Para 1][Para 39] They gave no documentary evidence to the panel. [Para 20]

**Employer (respondent):** The company said it could not pay wages during the strike, but that it maintained the attendance bonus and other benefits unchanged; it wrote on 10 January 2014 asking the panel to order the strike stopped, and on 17 January 2014 reporting which workers had returned to work under the injunction. [Para 3][Para 18][Para 19]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 005/14 without deciding the wage claim, because the strike continued in defiance of its injunction. [Para 41][Para 42]

The panel began from Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 on the Arbitration Council, which requires the parties, throughout the arbitration, to suspend every dispute measure — strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, or any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute — and to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] It read that provision, consistently with its earlier awards, as meaning that the panel will not continue arbitration while the workers are striking or the employer is locking out. [Para 29][Para 30]

It then surveyed its practice in the same situation: in case No. 04/04 (IMSI) a notification of 29 January 2004 stated that where the parties had no right to strike during the proceedings the panel could neither resolve nor issue an award on the collective dispute, and could take the case up again only once all the unions stopped striking; in case No. 18/04 (hotels) an injunction of 22 April 2004 ordered all strike and protest activity stopped and reserved the panel's right to close proceedings on breach; in case No. 77/05 (Frank Connitty Thing) a second injunction of 24 December 2005 said the panel would not deal with the dispute if the strike continued; and in cases No. 110/11 (Simano) and No. 17/13 (Internal Fashion Royal) the panel closed proceedings for the same reason. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]

Applying that approach, the panel found that the workers were still striking and had not complied with the injunction of 14 January 2014, a fact confirmed by both parties at the 16 January 2014 meeting; it therefore closed the arbitration. [Para 40][Para 41] Because no hearing on the merits was ever held, the panel recorded that it made no finding of fact on the disputed point and decided nothing about the 100 per cent wage claim. [Para 25]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where workers keep striking after the dispute has been referred to the Council and after the panel has issued an injunction ordering the strike suspended, the panel will not arbitrate the merits and will close the case by award, leaving the claim undetermined; the case can be taken up again only once the strike has ended. [Para 29][Para 32][Para 41]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the obligation to suspend dispute measures is reciprocal, binding the employer against lockout as much as the workers against strike, and that non-attendance at summoned meetings is a distinct breach of the same article. [Para 28][Para 30]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The header states the claim as covering 25 December 2013 to 02 January 2014, while the procedural section puts it as 25 to 31 December 2013 plus 02 to 09 January 2014; both are reported and neither is preferred. [Para 2][Para 9] The injunction is dated "14 January 201 2014" and the 16 January meeting as "16 January 20 2014". [Para 14][Para 38] The employer's name is printed in at least five variants — ឃែន ធើរេន អី ភែរឹល, ឃែន ធើរេន អី ភែរីល, ឃែន ធើរេន អ៊ីភែរឹល and ឃែនធើរេន អ៊ីភែរីល — and paras 22 and 23 duplicate the invitation list in garbled form. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 42][Para 22] The worker party is named only as "worker representatives" with individual union officers listed by name in the header, so no single authorised representative can be identified; individual officers are reduced to initials in this brief per redaction policy.
