# Award No. 314/14 — Vai Ful Garmen (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer, An Non for the workers, Tang Tri presiding) · Doc ID 139760

## 1. Facts
Ms N worked as a QC at the garment factory of Vai Ful Garmen (Cambodia) Ltd, which employs about 850 workers, on successive fixed-duration contracts beginning with a two-month probation from 18 March 2013; her final three-month contract expired on 17 August 2014 and the company did not renew it. Three union federations demanded that she be taken back as a union adviser (ទីប្រឹក្សា សហជីព), alleging the non-renewal punished her union activity, while the company said the contract simply ran out and that she had repeatedly left the line without telling her team leader. [Para 39][Para 43][Para 45][Para 58]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the non-renewal of Ms N's fixed-duration contract was connected to union discrimination? [Para 60]
2. Whether the termination of Ms N's fixed-duration contract was lawful under the Labour Law? [Para 79]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The non-renewal was anti-union discrimination — she was a good worker who had never been disciplined, the factory still needed hands and was running overtime, the employer's cameras told it who met the federations, and after a meeting with GMAC on working conditions managers began hunting for her faults and told her to give up union work and she would not be dismissed. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 71]

**Employer (respondent):** The contract expired by its own term, so no discrimination is involved; she left during working hours without telling her team leader, was warned orally once, and repeated the fault; notice of non-renewal was given on 05 August 2014 and her final wages and other benefits were made ready but she did not come to collect them. [Para 72][Para 85][Para 86]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the workers' demand that the company take Ms N back into work; no sum of riel or USD was ordered. [Para 91][Para 92]

On issue 1 the panel began from Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law, which forbid an employer to use union membership or union activity as a ground for discipline, termination or the granting of benefits, and noted that earlier awards had ordered reinstatement on exactly that basis. It then applied its standing rule that the party making an allegation bears the burden of proving it, and weighed the evidence. Ms N herself accepted at the hearing that her contract had ended on 17 August 2014, which was before the 4 September 2014 election that created the base union; the workers produced no witness and no document for the alleged camera surveillance, the manager's remark, or any link between the decision and her union role. The panel therefore found no evidentiary basis for a finding of anti-union discrimination. [Para 62][Para 64][Para 75][Para 76][Para 77][Para 78]

On issue 2 the panel read Article 73(1) of the Labour Law as providing that a fixed-duration contract expires of itself when its term falls due, and that the ten days' prior notice is owed only where the contract runs longer than six months; a contract of six months or less carries no notice requirement at all. Both parties agreed the last contract was for three months and expired on 17 August 2014, and the employer had given notice on 05 August 2014, twelve days before expiry. The panel held the notice obligation satisfied in any event, the contractual relationship at an end, and accordingly no obligation on the employer to reinstate. [Para 79][Para 82][Para 83][Para 84][Para 88][Para 89][Para 90]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed-duration contract ends by effluxion of its term, and a worker who says the non-renewal was anti-union discrimination must bring evidence connecting the decision to union activity; where the contract expired before the union was even constituted and the claimant offers neither witness nor document, the claim under Articles 12 and 279 fails and no order to reinstate follows. [Para 64][Para 76][Para 78]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded, without relying on it, that none of the four federations yet held a base-union registration at the company, and that the parties agreed the aggregate duration of Ms N's successive contracts did not exceed two years. [Para 41][Para 45]

[Para 25][Para 85][Para 88] The worker's name is OCR'd four different ways (ណយ សុផានី, ណយ សូផានី, គណយ សុផានី, and លាយ សុផានី in the operative order), and the company appears once as "វ៉ៃ ហ្គាមេន", dropping ហ្វ៊ុល. [Para 27][Para 49][Para 85][Para 92] The demand section states a single grievance while the reasons decide two numbered questions, and page-break debris ("-៣-", "-៧-", "-១០-", "-១១-") interrupts sentences mid-line.
