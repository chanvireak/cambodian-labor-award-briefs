# Case No. 149/14 — Bray Me Su Manufacturing Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Virak, worker-appointed; Tang Tri, chair) · Doc ID 139606

## 1. Facts
The employer runs a shoe factory at Roueng village, Khnor Doung commune, Cheung Prey district, Kampong Cham, which opened in November 2013 and employs about 500 workers. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 68] Workers struck on 19 and 20 May 2014 over working conditions and 11 demands; conciliation on 5 June 2014 settled one and left 10 unresolved, which came to the panel on 9 June 2014. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 37] At the hearing the parties agreed on issues 6 and 8 and the workers withdrew issues 2, 3, 10 and 11, leaving issues 4, 5, 7 and 9. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must sign three-month fixed-duration contracts with the garden-maintenance workers? [Para 49][Para 90]
2. Whether the employer must provide two sets of work clothing a year, or pay USD 30 in lieu? [Para 64][Para 94]
3. Whether the employer must give a free meal to workers doing overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. instead of the 2,000 riel meal allowance? [Para 104][Para 124]
4. Whether the employer must pay 5 per cent severance each time a three-month fixed-duration contract expires? [Para 125][Para 141]

The panel also had to decide, before these, whether the National Union Confederation had authority to represent the workers. [Para 80][Para 86]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** garden workers do the same work as production-line workers yet are kept on two-month contracts without the seniority bonus, while women get three-month contracts renewed as a matter of course; Prakas 307 requires two sets of clothing a year so that a worker is identifiable after a workplace accident; 2,000 riel no longer buys a meal, and other factories such as Chou Hoy provide one; a 5 per cent payment is owed at each contract expiry, failing which the contract becomes indefinite. [Para 55][Para 57][Para 67][Para 72][Para 76][Para 77]
**Employer (respondent):** the factory is still in training, has little revenue and had goods returned for poor quality, so it cannot yet supply clothing; the labour law has already been complied with on the meal allowance; and it renews the three-month contracts without a break, so no severance is due. [Para 68][Para 73][Para 78][Para 100]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to provide two sets of work clothing a year to the workers; the claims on garden-worker contracts, the free overtime meal and the 5 per cent severance were dismissed. No money sum was ordered. [Para 143][Para 142][Para 144][Para 145]
On representation, the panel applied Article 19 of Prakas 099/04, which lets a party appear through a person holding written authority from that party. [Para 81] The confederation filed authority letters dated 12 May 2014 that concerned demands unrelated to this case; the panel told it to file letters covering these issues and it filed none by the evidence deadline, so the confederation had no standing. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86] Because three workers attended in person, the panel decided the issues for those three only. [Para 87][Para 88][Para 89]
On the garden workers, the panel found that no garden worker was among the complainants and no specific worker claimed the relief, so it declined to consider the demand. [Para 91][Para 92][Para 93] On clothing, the panel applied Paragraph 6 of Prakas No. 307 of 14 December 2007, which requires at least two sets of clothing a year free of charge, each set being at least trousers or skirt plus shirt sized to the worker. [Para 95][Para 97] The employer's training status, weak revenue and returned goods did not excuse it, since a ministerial Prakas is binding law. [Para 101][Para 102] On the overtime meal, the panel read Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, which gives the worker 2,000 riel or one free meal, and held the choice belongs to the employer because the overtime originates in the employer's request; the payment of 2,000 riel therefore discharged the obligation. [Para 106][Para 118][Para 123] It expressly declined to follow award No. 115/08 (Thap Vann), which had required a meal. [Para 117] On the 5 per cent, the panel applied Article 73 of the Labour Law and its own reasoning in award No. 60/06 (Numeca Gamen): severance is payable when the employment relationship is severed, and a contract renewed at expiry does not sever it. [Para 125][Para 133][Para 134][Para 139]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a notification gives the employer a choice between paying a meal allowance and supplying a meal, and the overtime is at the employer's request, paying the stated allowance discharges the obligation, and the panel will depart from a prior award that required the meal. [Para 118][Para 123]
Severance under Article 73 falls due only when the employment relationship is actually severed; successive fixed-duration contracts renewed without a break do not trigger it at each expiry. [Para 134][Para 139]
A trade union that cannot produce written authority covering the specific issues in dispute has no standing, and the panel will decide only for the workers who appear in person. [Para 86][Para 89]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that 1,000 riel set in 2000 could then buy a meal but no longer can, and that workers may buy food with the 2,000 riel as they prefer, did not affect the outcome. [Para 113][Para 122]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 1][Para 43] The claim list at the head of the award numbers 10 demands, whereas the conciliation recital speaks of 11 claims with 10 unresolved, and the panel's issue numbers in the reasons run one higher than that list — its "issue 4" is item 3 of the list. [Para 18][Para 26][Para 49] Notification 041/11 is quoted with the figure "2,000 (two thousand) riel" but discussed elsewhere against 1,000 riel. [Para 106][Para 112] The conciliation report number appears as 55/14 and 55/14 with differing date-formats, and parts of the signature block are lost to OCR noise. [Para 14][Para 147]
