# Award No. 127/14 — Cambodian Hoi Fu Game and Nit Thin Fake Feri Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, appointed by the employer; Tuon Siphan, appointed by the workers; Ben Bun Chhea, chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 139580

## 1. Facts
The company is a weaving enterprise (ក្រុមហ៊ុន តម្បាញ) at Trapeang Krasang, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, registered in 1999 and employing 433 workers. [Para 50][Para 51] Fourteen demands went to conciliation; six were settled there, one was withdrawn at the hearing, and seven remained. [Para 27][Para 28] The union's third-term leadership had been recognised on 23 August 2011 and had never been renewed, so the panel first had to fix who could carry the case; only seven workers appeared. [Para 129][Para 136]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must calculate the attendance bonus pro rata to the days a worker was absent with permission? [Para 55]
2. Whether the employer must pay the statutory rates for work on holidays, on Sundays and on night shift? [Para 62][Para 190]
3. Whether the employer must pay 100% wages for periods when it gave no work but did not lawfully suspend the contract? [Para 78][Para 236]
4. Whether the employer must pay wages for the days workers stopped work in February and March 2014? [Para 84]
5. Whether the March attendance bonus must still be paid for a month in which the employer gave no work? [Para 106][Para 292]
6. Whether the employer must keep paying the USD 5 monthly health allowance separately? [Para 109][Para 314]
7. Whether the employer must dismiss the member of its accounting staff? [Para 118][Para 341]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the bonus should be cut only in proportion to days taken off with permission; the 19:00 to 07:00 shift is night work; the February stoppage answered late payslips rather than being a strike, and they worked the morning of 10 February; the accountant humiliated them over their pay. [Para 57][Para 94][Para 119][Para 283]
**Employer (respondent):** with no work available it paid 50% of wages and nothing more; the stoppages were strikes, so no wages are due; the USD 5 allowance was folded into the basic wage; and the accountant cannot be replaced. [Para 108][Para 251][Para 329]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must reduce the bonus only pro rata, pay 200% for holiday and Sunday work, the night-shift rates below, 100% wages for the unsuspended idle period, the March bonus, and half a day's wage for 10 February; claims 6 and 7 were dismissed, as was the allowance element of claim 3. [Para 366][Para 377]

On standing, Article 268 of the Labour Law requires a union to deposit its statutes and the list of its officers, and Article 3(2) of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 caps a leadership term at two years. [Para 123][Para 128] The third-term mandate expired on 23 August 2013 and no new recognition letter was produced, so those officers could not represent members. [Para 130][Para 133] Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 still lets a party appear in person or through a written agent, and no other workers had assigned their rights to the seven who attended; the panel decided the claims for those seven alone. [Para 134][Para 139]

On the attendance bonus, Article 2 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 gives at least USD 10 a month to workers who attend regularly "without absence". [Para 147] The panel found that phrase ambiguous and unclarified by the Labour Advisory Committee. [Para 158] Statutory absences — annual, special, maternity, holiday and weekly rest — cannot cost the bonus. [Para 161] Leave taken with the employer's permission suspends the contract, so no wage is owed for those days, and the employer may deduct pro rata to them but may not forfeit the whole bonus. [Para 186][Para 189]

On holiday, Sunday and night work the claim was a rights dispute. [Para 191] Article 164 of the Labour Law and Article 4 of Prakas No. 10 of 04 February 1999 give 200% in total for work on a paid holiday; the employer paid 100%, which was non-compliant. [Para 194][Para 200] Article 139 (new) gives 200% for the weekly rest day. [Para 205] Article 144 (new) defines night as at least eleven consecutive hours including 22:00 to 05:00 and sets 130% for night work. [Para 209] The company's 19:00 to 07:00 shift meets that definition, so the panel fixed normal hours at 19:00 to 03:00 at 130%, overtime from 03:00 to 05:00 at a 100% premium of the night wage, and from 05:00 to 07:00 at 50%. [Para 223][Para 232]

On the idle period, Article 71(11) permits suspension only for serious economic difficulty and subject to labour inspectorate supervision. [Para 239] Awards 27/08, 136/08 and 51/09 require notice to the inspector and written verification that the difficulty is real. [Para 242][Para 246] The employer neither asked the inspector nor obtained authorisation, so the suspension was its own act and invalid, and 100% wages were owed. [Para 252][Para 253] The allowance element failed because the workers never identified which allowances. [Para 255]

On the February and March stoppages, the conduct met the Article 318 definition of a strike, [Para 262][Para 267] and Article 332 suspends the contract during a strike so no wages are due. [Para 269] Awards 49/05 and 27/10 hold that strikers receive no wages whether the strike was lawful or not. [Para 271] The morning of 10 February was contested: the employer asserted no work was done but produced no output records, which it was better placed to have, so the panel preferred the workers' account. [Para 285][Para 289]

On the March bonus, the stoppage was the employer's decision, not the workers' absence, so withholding the bonus would punish them, and the employer had also failed to suspend lawfully. [Para 310][Para 312] On the health allowance, Prakas No. 206 of 13 December 2011 gave USD 5, but Prakas No. 103 of 09 April 2013 merged it into the basic wage and Prakas No. 317 fixed USD 100 from 01 February 2014, leaving no separate entitlement. [Para 314][Para 325][Para 333]

On the accountant, Article 65 and Article 311 of the Civil Code reserve termination to the parties to the contract, so a third party cannot demand a dismissal, [Para 345][Para 347] and prior awards allow it only where the person is shown to endanger other workers. [Para 350] Neither humiliation nor danger was proved. [Para 362][Para 365]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union whose leadership term has expired under Prakas No. 305 loses the right to represent its members, but the workers concerned may still bring the dispute in person or through a written agent under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099, and the panel will decide the claims for those workers only. [Para 133][Para 139] An employer that gives no work without obtaining labour inspectorate supervision of the suspension under Article 71(11) must pay full wages for the idle period, and an attendance bonus may be reduced only pro rata to days of leave the employer itself authorised. [Para 189][Para 253]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's advice that workers should route pay complaints through their team leader is practice guidance, not a ruling, and affected no order. [Para 360]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The company's name appears in several damaged transliterations, and its registration is dated "24 June 199" without a final digit [Para 50]; no Latin name is printed.
- The operative paragraph on the idle period orders payment "to 20 workers" although the order itself names no number and only seven workers were parties [Para 256][Para 372]; the March bonus is written as "USD 100" in one place and USD 10 everywhere else [Para 313][Para 376].
- The conciliation report is dated 22 May 2014 in the procedural section but 22 May 2013 where the referral is described [Para 18][Para 24]; the panel's constitution reads "23 May 2023 2014" [Para 25].
- The employer-side arbitrator's name is lost to OCR noise in the signature block [Para 379], and passages of the reasons are printed twice [Para 275].
- Claim 14 named four workers for a USD 30 reimbursement, but one had resigned, which appears only in the employer's evidence list [Para 15][Para 40].
