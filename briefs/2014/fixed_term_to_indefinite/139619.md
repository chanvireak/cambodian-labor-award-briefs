# Award No. 163/14 — Sin Chang Sing Garmen (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 10 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Ven Pov, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139619

## 1. Facts
Sin Chang Sing Garmen (Cambodia) Co., Ltd cuts and sews garments and employs about 800 workers. [Para 24][Para 25] The Democratic Union of Garment Workers at the company had its leadership recognised by Labour Disputes Department letter No. 027 of 9 January 2013, naming Mr C as president, Mr N as vice-president and Mr S as secretary. [Para 26] On 22 May 2014 the employer stopped all three from entering the factory, posting their photographs at the gate with a notice barring them, after they asked about the annual bonus; the employer did not seek the labour inspector's authorisation first. [Para 50][Para 57][Para 98] The employer did not attend the hearing and gave travel to National Road 4 as its reason. [Para 75]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back the three dismissed workers — Mr C, Mr N and Mr S — and pay them back wages from the date of dismissal until their return? [Para 81][Para 83]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The three were union leaders, were dismissed without any fault of their own and without the labour inspector's prior authorisation, and the company was discriminating against the union; a rival slate elected by the confederation had asked the employer to dismiss them, threatening a strike otherwise. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 65][Para 68]

**Employer (respondent):** In its written position it said it could not take the three back, denied union discrimination, and refused to pay any compensation; it then stayed away from the hearing. [Para 4][Para 73]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to take back Mr C, Mr N and Mr S and to pay their back wages from the date of dismissal until the date of return. [Para 113][Para 114]

The panel first held it could hear the case in the employer's absence: Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 allows proceedings to continue where a party duly summoned fails to appear without a valid reason, and travelling on National Road 4 was not one. [Para 70][Para 76][Para 77] A party that stays away forgoes its right to answer. [Para 103]

On the merits, Article 293 of the Labour Law requires the labour inspector's agreement before a staff delegate is dismissed, and Article 4 (chapter 2) of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 extends that protection to union leaders, whose term may not exceed two years. [Para 83][Para 84][Para 89] The panel held letter No. 027 of 9 January 2013 to be sufficient legal proof that the three were recognised leaders, even though the union's registration certificate was not produced — the confederation holds it, and no later letter recognised a replacement leadership. [Para 91][Para 95][Para 96] They were therefore still leaders when dismissed on 22 May 2014. [Para 97][Para 99] Under Article 4 (chapter 4) of Prakas No. 305 a dismissal effected without the inspector's prior authorisation is void, so the dismissals were void. [Para 110][Para 111] Article 34 of Prakas No. 099 empowers the panel to order reinstatement and immediate payment, and the panel followed its earlier awards in doing so. [Para 112][Para 107]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissal of a recognised union leader without the labour inspector's prior authorisation is void, and the panel will order reinstatement with back wages to the date of return. The union need not produce its registration certificate where a Labour Disputes Department letter names the leader, and the protection lapses only on a later recognition of a replacement leadership. [Para 95][Para 105][Para 111]

**Obiter dicta:** That a party which stays away from a hearing it was duly summoned to, without valid reason, gives up its right to answer the other side's case. [Para 103]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The dismissed workers' names are printed inconsistently (ឈឹម សំអន, ឈីម សំអន, ឈីម រ សំអន) and are therefore rendered as Mr C, Mr N and Mr S. [Para 26][Para 59][Para 95] One wage figure is lost to OCR — base wage printed as "១៩ អាមេរិក" with the currency word and a digit missing, against a stated total of "១២០ ទៅ ១៥០" — so no wage is relied on here. [Para 46][Para 47]
