# Award No. 168/14 — Vin Kam Khob Pherer Sen (Arbitration Council, 2 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139621

## 1. Facts
Vin Kam Khob Pherer Sen is a company at Building 62, Street 70, Sangkat Toul Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] Eight demands went to conciliation, which failed on 12 June 2014 because the company insisted it would apply only the law. [Para 24][Para 25] About 850 workers struck for 12 days; on 23 June 2014 the company asked the Council for an order to stop the strike, and the Council issued order No. 036/14 requiring immediate suspension and return to work from the afternoon of 24 June 2014. [Para 28][Para 29] The strike continued after that order. [Para 30][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay 2,000 riel per hour for overtime, or provide a proper meal? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must pay a skills allowance of $30 per month? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must add $5 to the regular attendance bonus? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must add $5 to the travel and accommodation allowance? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must allow pregnant workers one day a month for a health check with pay and benefits preserved? [Para 10]
6. Whether the company must deduct the attendance bonus pro rata when a worker is absent with lawful excuse? [Para 12]
7. Whether the company must preserve wages and the attendance bonus during a strike? [Para 14]
8. Whether the company must convert indefinite contracts into fixed-term contracts and pay 100 per cent of the resulting severance? [Para 16]
9. Whether the panel could continue the arbitration at all, the strike having continued after the Council's suspension order? [Para 42]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The eight demands stood as formulated; at the pre-hearing meeting the federation president and workers confirmed that the strike was continuing after order No. 036/14. [Para 2][Para 34]

**Employer (respondent):** It said it could not grant the overtime meal, skills allowance, extra attendance bonus, extra travel allowance or strike pay, that it would apply only the law, and that it would keep indefinite contracts; it asked the Council to order the strike stopped because production and buyer confidence were being damaged. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 17][Para 28]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration and made no order on any of the eight demands. [Para 64][Para 65]

The panel recorded that it held no hearing and found no facts on the disputed points. [Para 41] It framed the only question it could decide as whether the arbitration should proceed, and applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099: during the panel's proceedings the parties must suspend all dispute measures, including strikes and lockouts, and must attend every meeting to which they are summoned. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] Earlier awards read that provision as barring continuation whenever the workers were striking or the employer was locking out, citing cases including 04/04-IM SI, 18/04-Hotel, 77/05-Franco Nith Ting, 110/11-Simanor and 17/13-International Fashion 2. [Para 45][Para 47][Para 52] On the facts, both sides confirmed at the 26 June 2014 meeting that the strike went on after 24 June; the panel found the order disobeyed and held it could not continue the proceedings or hold a hearing. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 62][Para 63]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a strike continues after the Council has ordered dispute measures suspended, the panel loses the power to proceed: it will terminate the arbitration rather than hear the demands, so a union that keeps striking forfeits the hearing it asked for. [Para 42][Para 63]

**Obiter dicta:** That the same bar applies to a lockout by the employer, and that the Council may take the dispute up again once the strike ends, as the awards quoted record. [Para 45][Para 48]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel states that no hearing was held and that no facts were found on the eight demands, so sections 3 and 4 rest on the parties' written and meeting positions only. [Para 41] The company's name is printed "វីន ខេម ខប ភើរេសិន" throughout, with no Latin-script rendering, so the transliteration here is phonetic. [Para 1] The demand number 6 carries a stray "623", and the address block at Para 21 prints "ផ្លូវ លេខ ឆ្នាំ ២០២៧" in place of Street 327. [Para 12][Para 21] One cited precedent appears as "77/05" here and as "77/06" in the parallel award. [Para 51]
