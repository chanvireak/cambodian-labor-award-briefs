# Arbitration Order, Case No. 322/14 — Chim Ly (Cambodia) PTY LTD (Arbitration Council, 26 November 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — You Sorn Ty (employer-appointed), Huon Chun Dy (worker-appointed), Pen Bun Chea (president) · Doc ID 139767

## 1. Facts
Four workers of Chim Ly (Cambodia) PTY LTD, represented by the Cambodian Workers' Movement Union, struck from 17 November 2014 over eight collective demands; conciliation on 17 November settled none of the eight, and the Ministry's non-conciliation report No. 1306 Kb/Ak/Vk of 18 November 2014 referred the dispute. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17] The panel issued Order No. 059/14 on 20 November 2014 directing the strike to be suspended and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 21 November 2014. [Para 20] The workers did not comply, did not attend the pre-hearing meeting of 24 November 2014, and confirmed by telephone that the strike continued; the employer attended and confirmed the same. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 49]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back Ms S, machine mechanic, into normal employment? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must provide a food allowance of 4,000 riel per day? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay travel and accommodation costs of 15 dollars per month? [Para 5]
4. Whether the company must pay a premium (prum) of 18 dollars? [Para 6]
5. Whether the company must pay 5 per cent every three months when a worker's contract ends? [Para 7]
6. Whether the company must pay women who take three months' maternity leave before the birth? [Para 8]
7. Whether the company must arrange suitable parking for bicycles and motorcycles? [Para 9]
8. Whether the company must apply working hours according to the old policy? [Para 10]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Reinstatement of Ms S was an absolute demand and a condition precedent — if the company would not take her back, no other point could be resolved at all. [Para 2] The remaining seven demands were the allowances, seniority and maternity payments, parking and hours set out above. [Para 4][Para 7][Para 9][Para 10]
**Employer (respondent):** It refused reinstatement outright because it had signed a fixed-duration employment contract with Ms S and had terminated that contract only at its stated expiry. [Para 3] It asked the panel for an immediate order stopping the strike and reported that, after Order No. 059/14, some workers returned but others did not. [Para 19][Para 25]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** proceedings terminated — the panel closed arbitration in Case No. 322/14 without any ruling on the eight demands and without any monetary order. [Para 51][Para 52]

The panel put the only question it decided as whether arbitration should continue at all, and answered it on procedure rather than merits. [Para 35] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBy of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures — strike, lockout or anything else aggravating the dispute — during the panel's proceedings, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 36][Para 37] The panel read that obligation as a precondition: it will not run an arbitration while workers are striking or an employer is locking out. [Para 38]

It followed its own earlier orders: the notice of 29 January 2004 in Case No. 04/04-IMS, holding that parties have no right to strike during arbitration and that the panel cannot resolve the dispute or issue an award while the strike lasts, with the case revivable once all unions stop striking; [Para 40][Para 41] the second order of 22 April 2004 in the hotel case, paragraphs 9 and 11, directing an end to strike, demonstration or disruption and reserving the right to terminate where a party breaches the order; [Para 42][Para 43] and the orders in Cases 77/05, 110/11, 17/13, 066/14 and the awards listed at paragraph 39, each terminated for a strike continued during proceedings. [Para 39][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]

On the facts, Order No. 059/14 had directed suspension and return to work from 21 November 2014; the workers kept striking, stayed away from the 24 November meeting, and their federation official told the secretariat by telephone that the strike went on. [Para 20][Para 24][Para 48] The employer's account at the pre-hearing meeting was the same. [Para 49] Because the workers did not respect the order, the panel held it could neither continue the procedure nor hold a hearing, and terminated. [Para 50][Para 51] Having held no hearing, it made no findings of fact on the demands themselves. [Para 34]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a panel has ordered a strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBy and the workers continue striking, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of hearing the merits; suspension of dispute measures is a precondition to the panel proceeding, not a direction it can enforce by deciding the case. [Para 37][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the dispute can be taken up again once all the workers' unions stop striking, and that it retains a right to terminate where a party breaches its orders — neither point was necessary to closing this file. [Para 41][Para 43]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed three ways: "Chim Ly (Cambodia) PTY LTD" in the header, "Chim Ly" and "Chim Li" in the body — treated as one company. [Para 1][Para 15] The worker's name is OCR-damaged throughout ("Ses_Pisei" with a stray bracket), so she is given as Ms S. [Para 2][Para 3] Order No. 059/14 appears once as "No. 15 059/14" with a stray numeral. [Para 48] Paragraph 39's list of prior cases is badly corrupted — "59/06-Sang Ro", "3908 Chong You", "13709 & 138/09-Plum Kanh" — and those numbers cannot be relied on. [Para 39] Paragraph 12 prints "Chapter Lin 12" for Chapter 12, paragraph 36 inserts "2015" into the Ministry's name, and paragraph 43 inserts a stray "19" before "any party". [Para 12][Para 36][Para 43] The employer says some workers returned after the order while the panel finds the strike continued; the panel resolved this against the workers, but the divergence is recorded. [Para 25][Para 50] The signature block lost the employer-side arbitrator's name to garbled text. [Para 53][Para 54]
