# Award No. 066/14 — Callisto E Peril (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 25 March 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Venh Pov, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, president) · Doc ID 139541

## 1. Facts
Workers at the Callisto E Peril (Cambodia) garment factory struck from 12 March 2014 over fourteen demands; five failed conciliation and were referred to the panel on 18 March 2014. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 21] On 20 March 2014 the panel issued Order No. 014/14 under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 ordering an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the morning of 21 March. [Para 22][Para 36] The workers did not comply: the secretariat was told by both parties on 24 March that some 40 to 50 workers were still striking outside the factory. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 48] No hearing on the merits was ever held. [Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must dismiss the head of administration, Mr N (also called Mr H), for using improper language towards workers? [Para 2][Para 3]
2. Whether the company must pay an attendance bonus of USD 15 and pro-rate that bonus when workers take authorised personal leave? [Para 4][Para 5]
3. Whether the company must pay travel and accommodation money of USD 10 per month? [Para 8][Para 9]
4. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel per day? [Para 10][Para 11]
5. Whether the company must pay wages two days before the normal pay day? [Para 12][Para 13]

The panel answered none of these. It decided a single threshold question instead: whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 36]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The administration head should be removed for speaking improperly to workers; the attendance bonus should be USD 15 and pro-rated for authorised leave; travel money should be USD 10, lunch 4,000 riel a day, and wages paid two days early. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 8][Para 10][Para 12]
**Employer (respondent):** No ground exists to dismiss Mr N, who committed no fault as alleged; the company holds to USD 10 attendance bonus, USD 7 travel money and 1,000 riel lunch money, and cannot fix a firm date for early wage payment. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 9][Para 11][Para 13] It also asked the president to order an immediate strike suspension once the strike began. [Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration in case 066/14 and decided nothing on the five demands. [Para 49][Para 50]

Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure — strike, lockout or anything else aggravating the dispute — throughout the arbitration, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] The panel read that provision as mandatory and as a condition of its own ability to proceed, not merely as a duty on the parties. [Para 38]

It then followed a settled line of its own awards: where workers continue to strike after being ordered to suspend, the panel will not carry on with the arbitration. [Para 39][Para 47] It cited Awards 59/06, 3908, 13709, 110/11, 108/13, 119/13 and 019/14, and, at greater length, Award 04/04 — which declared that a party striking during proceedings forfeits the panel's consideration and may only be heard again once the strike ends — Award 18/04 (points 9 and 11 of the second order of 22 April 2004) and Award 77/05 (point 4, reserving the right to discontinue). [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] In this case both parties confirmed the same facts, so the breach was not in dispute. [Para 48]

Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings of fact on the five demands and gave no reasons on their merits. [Para 35]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the panel has ordered a suspension of strike action under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 and the workers nonetheless continue to strike, the panel will terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the referred demands; the right to have those demands heard revives only when the strike ends. [Para 38][Para 41][Para 42][Para 49]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that Article 20 binds the parties to attend every meeting the panel convenes, and its catalogue of earlier awards treating continued strike action as fatal to the proceedings, are general observations beyond what this termination required. [Para 37][Para 46]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed five different ways in the same document — "កាល់លី ស្គូ អី ភេរីល", "កាលីស្តូ អី ភែរឹល", "កាល់លីស្តូ អ៊ីភែរីល" and "កាល់លី ស្តូ អី ភេរីល" — and the operative order drops a digit, naming the case "៦៦/១៤" where the header reads "០៦៦/១៤". [Para 1][Para 21][Para 22][Para 50] Demand 2 is garbled, reading "១៥ ដុល្លារ ក្នុង មួយ ឆ្នាំ ខែ", which cannot be reconciled with the employer's USD 10 per month; both readings are reported as printed. [Para 4][Para 5] The date of the conciliation record is given as 18 March at one point and the related memorandum as 17 March. [Para 16][Para 29] Paragraph 35 is corrupt ("XXX", "ដែលោ កឹម") but clearly states that no hearing took place.
