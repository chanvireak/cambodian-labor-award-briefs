# Award No. 288/14 — Tru Tham Phnom Penh (ទ្រូ ថា ម ភ្នំពេញ) (Arbitration Council, 30 October 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Aing Eng Thong, presiding) · Doc ID 139744

## 1. Facts
Workers at Tru Tham Phnom Penh, a company at house 184E0, Preah Monireth Boulevard, Tonle Bassac, Chamkarmon, Phnom Penh, demanded that the company dismiss its manager and two other staff, relying on a meeting at which the owner said they had been dismissed from 16 July 2014. On 8 October 2014 the company instead let those three return to work as normal, which the workforce refused to accept, and a strike began on 9 October 2014. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 3]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company should terminate the employment of the manager and the two other staff, as the owner had announced on 16 July 2014? [Para 2][Para 11]

The panel framed the operative question for itself as whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 57]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the employer had dismissed nine of their representatives immediately after the first hearing of 24 October 2014; they would return to normal work only once the company allowed all nine representatives back. [Para 24][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** the owner had not dismissed the three staff, only given them three months' rest while continuing to pay their wages each month; and the nine representatives could not be taken back. [Para 4][Para 26][Para 66]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** proceedings terminated — the panel closed the file on case No. 288/14 without ruling on the demand, so nothing was ordered in riel or USD. [Para 68][Para 69]

The panel heard the parties on 24 October 2014, but the employer-side persons concerned with the case were absent, so a second hearing was fixed for 31 October 2014 to question them directly. [Para 9][Para 18][Para 19] The strike had already been suspended once: injunction No. 052/14 of 16 October 2014 ordered an immediate halt to the strike and a return to work from the morning of 17 October 2014, and the workers complied. [Para 15][Para 16] After the first hearing the panel learned that the employer had dismissed nine worker representatives and that the workers refused to return; at the information meeting of 28 October 2014 both sides confirmed the deadlock, the employer stating it would not take the nine back. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 65][Para 66]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 (21 April 2004), which requires parties to suspend all dispute measures — strikes or lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law — for the duration of the arbitration, and read it, consistently with Awards 59/06, 39/08, 137/09 and 138/09, to bar it from continuing while a strike is running. Because the strike was continuing and the employer would not reinstate the nine representatives so that it could end, the panel terminated the proceedings. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 59][Para 60][Para 67][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a strike continues into the arbitration — whether because the workers persist or because the employer's refusal to reinstate dismissed representatives prevents them from returning — Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 is not satisfied, and the panel will terminate the proceedings rather than decide the merits; the dispute may be brought back once the strike has ended. [Para 61][Para 64]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that earlier awards (Nos 28/06, 34/06, 110/11, 17/13) had closed or terminated files on the same ground, and that the employer's own documents included a transfer of the owner's authority to Mr T dated 22 October 2014. [Para 27][Para 62][Para 63]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is internally inconsistent on whether any hearing occurred: Para 18 and Para 22 record that both sides attended the hearing of 24 October 2014 and the meeting of 28 October 2014, while Para 56 states that no findings of fact were made because the panel "did not conduct a hearing". The dispositive date is 30 October 2014, yet Para 19 fixes a second hearing for 31 October 2014 and the panel relies on a letter of 27 October 2014. The employer's romanised name is uncertain — the face of the award gives only ទ្រូ ថា ម ភ្នំពេញ. The presiding arbitrator's name appears as អាំង អេង ថុង in the header and អាំង អេង ថ្ម in the signature block. Para 37 and Para 70 contain large OCR-garbled Latin fragments. Individual workers, representatives and staff are named in the source and are redacted here.
