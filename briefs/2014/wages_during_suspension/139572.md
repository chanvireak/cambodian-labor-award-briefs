# Award No. 113/14 — Sun Chin Nith Thing (Arbitration Council, 20 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party — dissenting on issues 3 and 4(b), Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139572

## 1. Facts
Sun Chin Nith Thing is an unregistered sewing workshop at Pothi Leu, Traey Sla, Saang, Kandal, making vests from cloth woven elsewhere and employing about 100 workers, with no internal regulation and no infirmary. [Para 52][Para 54][Para 91] Its union, of the Khmer Youth Voice Federation, held a Ministry registration receipt dated 03 December 2013 and a rights-transfer letter of the same date. [Para 55][Para 57] Of eighteen conciliation demands, four were settled at the hearing and eight withdrawn, leaving eight for the panel. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must provide an infirmary staffed by a doctor during working hours? [Para 59]
2. Whether the attendance bonus may be deducted in full, or only pro rata to days of authorised absence? [Para 157]
3. Whether wages must be maintained during sick leave certified by a doctor, and the attendance bonus only proportionally reduced? [Para 201]
4. Whether the employer must let workers who have completed one year take their annual leave? [Para 248]
5. Whether night overtime from 18:00 to 07:00 must be paid at 200 per cent, and continuous overtime of that length be stopped? [Para 266]
6. Whether work on holidays and Sundays must be paid at 200 per cent plus 2,000 riel meal money? [Para 307]
7. Whether the employer must send workers for a pre-employment medical examination and pay for it? [Para 372]
8. Whether the employer must assume liability for work accidents? [Para 389]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the Labour Law requires an infirmary and a doctor, and administration staff have no medical skill to hand out medicine; the company operated for years and never granted annual leave; overtime ran from 18:00 to 07:00 at the ordinary rate. [Para 63][Para 94][Para 111]
**Employer (respondent):** the workshop is too small and its workforce too reduced for an infirmary; the company is new, under a year old, with all-new workers; it cannot pay 200 per cent for the whole night and gives 1,000 riel an hour plus two meals instead. [Para 65][Para 96][Para 99][Para 114]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered an infirmary with one doctor, pro-rata deduction of the attendance bonus, annual leave, statutory overtime rates with an end to 18:00–07:00 continuous overtime, 200 per cent piece rates for holiday and Sunday work plus 2,000 riel Sunday meal money, employer-paid medical examinations and NSSF registration; it refused wages for sick leave and holiday meal money. The award is non-binding. [Para 411][Para 412][Para 413][Para 414][Para 415][Para 416][Para 419][Para 420][Para 421]

On the infirmary the panel applied Article 242 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 3 of Prakas No. 330 of 06 December 2000: any enterprise employing at least fifty workers must have a permanent infirmary with a doctor and nurse. The workshop employed about 100, so the obligation applied whatever its self-description as a handicraft. [Para 150][Para 153][Para 154]

On the attendance bonus the panel held the 10 USD monthly bonus under Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 is wages, not a discretionary gift, and that Articles 71(6) and 72 suspend the contract during authorised absence, so the employer may deduct only in proportion to days absent, not the whole bonus; full forfeiture would contradict the purpose of rewarding regularity and push sick workers to attend. [Para 192][Para 199][Para 241][Para 246]

On sick leave the panel distinguished the two limbs: under Articles 71(3) and 72 no wage is owed for certified sick leave because the contract is suspended, but the attendance bonus may still only be reduced pro rata. [Para 201][Para 208][Para 247]

On annual leave it applied Articles 166 and 167: a worker who has completed one year is entitled to paid leave of one and a half days a month, or 18 days a year, and the panel rejected the employer's claim to be a new company, holding the right independent of the disputed start date. [Para 248][Para 264][Para 265]

On night overtime the panel read Article 144 (night being 22:00 to 05:00) with Article 139 and Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 01 March 1999: the hours 22:00–05:00 attract 200 per cent and the flanking hours 18:00–22:00 and 05:00–07:00 only 150 per cent, so the claim for 200 per cent across the whole span failed. [Para 266][Para 271][Para 281][Para 282] Separately, applying Articles 137, 140, 141 and Prakas No. 143, it held continuous work from 07:00 to 07:00 next day, with one hour for lunch, exceeds permissible hours and endangers health, and ordered the practice stopped. [Para 298][Para 305]

On holiday and Sunday work the panel applied Articles 163, 164 and Prakas No. 10 of 04 February 1999: piece-rate workers are paid, for holiday work, twice the piece wage earned that day, giving 200 per cent; the same calculation governs Sundays, which are the weekly rest day and therefore overtime. [Para 312][Para 321][Para 329][Para 331] Meal money of 2,000 riel was due for Sunday work under Notification No. 041/11 but not for holiday work, where no rule created it. [Para 353][Para 367][Para 370]

On the medical examination and accidents the panel applied Article 247, Prakas No. 09 of 1994 and Inter-ministerial Prakas No. 1191 of 21 November 2006 (12,000 riel per worker): the employer bears the cost even if the worker attends alone. [Para 381][Para 387] Under Articles 248, 249, 256 and 257 with Prakas No. 021/08 and 022/08, any enterprise with eight or more workers must register with the National Social Security Fund and pay occupational-risk contributions; with about 100 workers the company was within that scope. [Para 391][Para 397][Para 408]

**Dissent:** Ing Sothy dissented on issues 3 and 4(b), reasoning from dictionary definitions of "bonus" that a bonus attaches to conditions set by its giver, so a worker who fails the condition of full attendance cannot demand a pro-rata share. [Para 424][Para 429][Para 431][Para 443]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The statutory attendance bonus is wages, not a gratuitous reward, so an employer may reduce it only in proportion to days of authorised absence and never forfeit it entirely; the same proportion applies to certified sick leave, though no wage is owed for those days because the contract is suspended. Piece-rate workers who work a holiday or the weekly rest day are entitled to twice the piece wage earned that day, and night-shift premiums follow the statutory clock — 200 per cent only between 22:00 and 05:00. [Para 199][Para 247][Para 281][Para 321][Para 331]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that full forfeiture would discourage respect for the regularity rule, and that continuous day-and-night work endangers health, supported orders that rested independently on the statutory rates. [Para 243][Para 304]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award carries a **dissent** by the employer-party arbitrator on issues 3 and 4(b); the annexed opinion at [Para 424]–[Para 445] is reported separately above, while the orders at [Para 412] and [Para 413] follow the majority.
- The chair is printed "Pen Bun Chheam" on the face [Para 1] and "Pen Bun Chhea" in the signature block [Para 422]; this brief uses the latter.
- Issue numbering is discontinuous: the source runs 1–18, with disputes 1, 3 and 5 and 7 (in part), 13–16 and 18 withdrawn or settled [Para 31][Para 32]; this brief lists the eight survivors in the panel's order.
- The company is said not to be registered with the Ministry of Commerce [Para 52], yet to employ about 100 workers and to have operated for years [Para 101][Para 407]; the employer's "new company" defence [Para 99] is left unresolved.
- The conciliation report is dated 18 March 2014 but transmitted on 06 May 2014 [Para 22][Para 28], and the complaint is dated 06 December 2013 yet received on 20 December 2013 [Para 26].
- Stray years and folio markers intrude: "2023" in the constitution of the panel [Para 29], "2017" after a Prakas number [Para 150], and fragments at [Para 20][Para 22][Para 237].
- A document dated 03 December 2014 is twice relied on [Para 38], a date after the award.
