# Award No. 303/14 — Green Thine International (Cambodia) PLC (Arbitration Council, 3 November 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouch Hun (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Nhan Somoninth (president) · Doc ID 139752

## 1. Facts
Green Thine International (Cambodia) PLC, at Trapeang Pou village, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Pou Senchey, faced a strike from 20 October 2014 over 14 demands raised by two federations and referred by the employer to the Labour Disputes Department. [Para 1][Para 20][Para 24] Conciliation on 22 October 2014 failed on all 14 points, which reached the Council on 24 October. [Para 21][Para 22] The panel issued Order No. 057/14 on 27 October 2014 suspending the strike until arbitration was complete and requiring all strikers to return by shifts from the morning of 28 October. [Para 25] The strike continued, and both parties admitted this at the pre-hearing meeting of 31 October 2014. [Para 26][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel must terminate the arbitration proceedings because the strike continued after Order No. 057/14, in breach of Article 20 of Prakas No. 099? [Para 40][Para 50]
2. Whether the panel can hear the 14 claims at all, no hearing having been possible while the strike was under way? [Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the two federations demanded measures against Chinese team and section leaders who used improper language toward workers, a 5,000 riel midday meal allowance, a 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance, 5,000 riel for overtime on holidays and Sundays, voluntary-only overtime, a USD 10 attendance incentive, 15 minutes' early departure for pregnant workers, one health-check day a month, a USD 15 housing allowance, and recalculation of seniority. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 14][Para 15] They confirmed through the secretariat that the strike went on. [Para 31]
**Employer (respondent):** represented by counsel, it asked on 24 October 2014 for an order suspending the strike that had begun on 20 October, and attended the pre-hearing meeting to report the strike continuing. [Para 24][Para 28][Para 30]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction to continue — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case 303/14, made no finding on the 14 claims and ordered no payment of any riel or dollar sum. [Para 50][Para 51]

The panel put the only question it could reach as whether to continue at all. [Para 40] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 requires the parties, while a panel is seised, to suspend every dispute measure such as a strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law and to attend all meetings convened. [Para 40][Para 41] Earlier awards read that provision as barring the panel from continuing while a strike or lockout is running: Awards 04/04 (IMSI), 59/06 (Sang Vo), 39/08 (Chong You), 137/09 and 138/09 (Nhou Phanh), 28/06 (GDM) and 34/06 (Gold Fame), and more recently 97/07, 63/08, 110/11 (Simano) and 17/13 (Internal Fashion Royal). [Para 42][Para 43][Para 45][Para 46] The panel also followed Award 066/14 and its companions 092/14, 096/14 and 111/14, which terminated proceedings on the same ground. [Para 47] Order No. 057/14 had already required an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from 28 October 2014. [Para 25] Both parties accepted at the 31 October meeting that the workers kept striking in disregard of that order, and the panel found on that basis that the parties had no intention of arbitrating. [Para 49][Para 32] Because no hearing could be held, the panel recorded that it made no finding of fact on the reported issues. [Para 40]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a panel has ordered a strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 and the workers keep striking through the date fixed for their return, the panel will terminate the proceedings without any finding on the merits; the dispute stays revivable and may be brought again once every union has stopped striking. [Para 44][Para 50][Para 51]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that a terminated file can be reopened at any time once the unions cease striking, so termination is procedural rather than a dismissal of the claims. [Para 44]

[Para 43] The company name appears in several OCR variants ("ហ្គ្រេន ធ្វីន", "ហ្គ្រេន អ៊ិន ណេសិន លាល"). [Para 22][Para 33] No sums were ordered, so no amounts are reproduced.
