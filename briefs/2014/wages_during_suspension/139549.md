# Award No. 092/14 — Kouvol Apparel (Cambodia) In, Branch 2 (Arbitration Council, 28 April 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Pen Bun Chhea (president), Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed) · Doc ID 139549

## 1. Facts
Workers at a garment factory in Choam Chao, Phnom Penh, struck from 4 April 2014 behind eighteen demands raised by two federations, the Khmer Labour Solidarity Federation and the Khmer Workers' Power Federation. [Para 1][Para 25][Para 29] Conciliation on 8 April 2014 failed on all eighteen points and the file reached the Council on 21 April 2014. [Para 26][Para 27] The employer applied the same day for an order stopping the strike; the Council issued Instruction No. 020/14 ordering the strike suspended and all strikers back at their posts from the afternoon of 22 April 2014. [Para 29][Para 30] The workers did not comply, and at the pre-hearing meeting of 24 April 2014 the workers' side did not appear while both federation presidents confirmed by telephone that the strike continued. [Para 31][Para 33][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The eighteen demands in the non-conciliation report — midday meals or 4,000 riel, transport home when work stops at 4 pm, meal money for overtime, no deduction for certified sick leave, work-injury treatment costs, water and sanitation, a permanent nurse and medicines, accurate wage payment, special leave, tax paid by the employer, fifteen minutes' early release and one paid check-up day for pregnant workers, a daycare centre and USD 25 milk money, voluntary overtime, easier personal leave, an annual party, and wages during strike — were never adjudicated. [Para 3][Para 4][Para 5][Para 6][Para 9][Para 11][Para 13][Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 18] The panel decided one procedural question:
1. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration when the workers keep striking in defiance of the Council's order to suspend the strike? [Para 44][Para 62]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** No representative attended the pre-hearing meeting or the hearing; the two federation presidents telephoned at 7:40 am on 24 April 2014 to say the workers remained on strike. [Para 34][Para 58][Para 59]
**Employer (respondent):** It asked for an immediate injunction because the strike had run since 4 April; after the order most workers returned to their shifts, but a minority stayed out and threatened others who tried to return. [Para 29][Para 35][Para 60]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel terminated the arbitration and made no order on any of the eighteen demands. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 64]

Article 20 of Prakas 099 requires the parties to suspend every disputed measure, including strike or lockout, while the panel sits, and to attend all meetings it convenes; Article 318 of the Labour Law is to the same effect. [Para 44][Para 46] The panel noted its own earlier rulings that it will not continue an arbitration while the workers are striking or the employer has locked out, and it followed six prior awards in which files were closed for that reason, including one decided the same year against the same group of companies. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] It also cited a 2004 notification holding that the Council cannot settle or issue an award on a collective dispute while a strike continues, though it may take the file up again once all the unions stop striking. [Para 49][Para 50]

On the facts, the panel found the strike was still running on 24 April 2014 and that the workers had not obeyed Instruction 020/14; it therefore held it could neither continue the proceedings nor hold a hearing, and closed the file. [Para 31][Para 62][Para 63] Because no hearing was held, the panel recorded that it made no findings of fact on any of the eighteen demands. [Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered its suspension for the duration of the arbitration, the panel will close the file without deciding the demands, and will reopen it only if the strike ends. [Para 50][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the duty to suspend disputed measures and to attend every meeting is reciprocal, binding the employer against lockout just as it binds the workers against strike. [Para 45][Para 46]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
No findings of fact could be made because no hearing took place, so the eighteen demands are recorded but undecided. [Para 43] Demand 14 is printed as "២១៤-" and the non-conciliation report number appears both as 404 and as "១៧ ៤- ៤០៤", dated 9 April in one place and 21 April in another. [Para 15][Para 22][Para 38] The employer's name is spelled differently in almost every reference and no Latin-script name is printed anywhere, so the romanisation above is provisional. [Para 1][Para 27][Para 64] The pre-hearing meeting is recorded at 08:00 "រសៀល" (afternoon) while the federation call is logged at 7:40 am. [Para 24][Para 34] The header telephone block degenerates into a repeated digit string. [Para 2] Worker-side representatives are recorded as absent throughout. [Para 1][Para 33]
