# Award No. 157/14 — Win Khem Khob Perfection (Arbitration Council, 20 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3: Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (president) · Doc ID 139611

## 1. Facts
Some 800 workers of the garment employer Win Khem Khob Perfection struck for three days demanding that their indefinite-term contracts be converted into fixed-term contracts, that the contracts be terminated against payment of 15 days' wages per year of service, and that outstanding annual leave be paid. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 17] Conciliation failed and the dispute reached the Council on 16 June 2014. [Para 10] On 16 June 2014 the Council issued order No. 034/14 requiring the strike to be suspended until the arbitration was complete and all workers to return to their shifts from the morning of 17 June 2014. [Para 18] The strike nonetheless continued. [Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must convert the workers' indefinite-term employment contracts into fixed-term contracts? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must terminate the employment contracts and pay an indemnity of 15 days' wages per year of service? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must pay the outstanding annual leave, calculated on wages and other allowances? [Para 6]
4. Whether the arbitration proceedings could continue at all while the strike continued in breach of order No. 034/14? [Para 32][Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The company must convert the contracts to fixed-term and, failing that, terminate them with 15 days' wages per year of service, and must pay the annual leave still owing; after order No. 034/14 the workers kept striking and had no plan to return on any specific date. [Para 4][Para 6][Para 24]
**Employer (respondent):** The company is still applying indefinite-term contracts, has no intention of terminating anyone's contract, and has already paid the base wage for the last month. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7] It asked the Council to order the strike stopped because 800 workers had been out for three days and production and buyer confidence were badly affected. [Para 17]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction (proceedings terminated) — the Council decided and ordered the termination of the arbitration in case No. 157/14, without ruling on any of the three demands. [Para 50][Para 51]
The panel framed the only question it would decide as whether the arbitration should proceed. [Para 32] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires disputing parties to suspend every dispute measure, including strikes and lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and to attend all meetings convened by the panel; the panel read this as barring it from acting while a strike or lockout is under way. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 34] It followed its own earlier orders — No. 5906-Sang Vo, No. 3908-Chong Youv, No. 13709/13809-Plan, No. 110/11-Simano, No. 104/13-Saprina (Cambodia), No. 119/13-Pou Yien and No. 019/14-Dong Du Tek Stay (Cambodia) — and the notification of 29 January 2004 in case No. 04/04-MSI, which held that a party has no right to strike during the panel's proceedings and that the panel may take the case up again only once all the unions stop striking. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] It also cited the second order of 22 April 2004 in case No. 18/04-Hotel (Cambodiana, Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Grand, Angkor, Sunway, InterContinental and Sofitel Angkor), points 9 and 11, and case No. 77/05-Franco Nithing, reserving the right to terminate where a party breaches the order. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]
On the facts, the company's administration head, Mr L, confirmed at the pre-hearing meeting of 19 June 2014 that the strike was still on as of 17 June 2014; on the morning of 19 June 2014, 622 workers were still striking although 293 had returned, and the company could not run production. [Para 22][Para 23] The union president, Mr U, confirmed there was no plan to return to work on any specific date. [Para 24] The panel therefore found that the workers had continued the strike in disregard of order No. 034/14 and that it could not continue the procedure or hold a hearing. [Para 49] It recorded that it made no finding of fact on the disputed issues because no hearing was held. [Para 31]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will terminate the arbitration instead of deciding the merits where the workers continue a strike after the Council has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099; the bar is procedural and the panel says it may take the case up again only when the strike has ended. [Para 34][Para 38][Para 49][Para 50]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it agreed with the decisions of the Council in the earlier cases it cited is an observation on consistent practice, not a ruling on the three demands. [Para 45]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The conciliation report is given as No. 637 Kb/Ak/Vk dated 16 June 2014 in one place and as No. 627 Kb/Ak/Vk dated 16 June 2014 in another. [Para 10][Para 15] Para 10 also has the report dated 16 June 2014 but sent to the secretariat on 13 June 2014, three days earlier. [Para 10] Paragraphs 22–23 and 46–47 repeat the same findings verbatim, and the pre-hearing time is printed both as 13:30 and as "13 and 3000". [Para 22][Para 46] OCR has reduced several passages to noise ("VLL rand to and the", "Chambodia Samer", "AL", a bare "Sonn" for the worker-appointed arbitrator's signature) and the cited award numbers in para 36 are run together without separators; the worker-side union officials appear only as Mr L and Mr U.
