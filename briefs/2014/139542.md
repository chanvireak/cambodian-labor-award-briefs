# Award No. 068/14 — I.M.B. Jewel Kovy (Arbitration Council, 9 April 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Hem Hunarith, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Ang Eng Thong, president) · Doc ID 139542

## 1. Facts
I.M.B. Jewel Kovy is a garment factory employing 190 workers; it was formerly registered as J. Van M. Pro Dry Co., Ltd., a change of name the panel accepted after the employer, absent from the hearing, offered nothing to contradict it. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30] Eight demands went to conciliation on 13 March 2014 and seven were settled, leaving one: the meal allowance paid to workers who volunteer for overtime. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 16] The company pays 2,000 riel a day for voluntary overtime; the workers want 1,000 riel more per hour — 4,000 riel for overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. and 6,000 riel for 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. [Para 35][Para 58][Para 60]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the workers are entitled to an additional 1,000 riel per hour of overtime meal allowance, so that voluntary overtime from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. attracts 4,000 riel and overtime from 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. attracts 6,000 riel? [Para 58][Para 60]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The 2,000 riel paid is too little to live on; rice and dishes cost 3,000 to 5,000 riel a meal, and other factories such as Bery pay 5,000 riel for overtime work. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]
**Employer (respondent):** The company pays only what the Ministry's Notification No. 041 requires and cannot pay above the law; no representative attended the hearing, and the secretariat was told by telephone only that the person responsible was busy. [Para 3][Para 55][Para 56]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider the claim and ordered no payment. [Para 79][Para 80]

Two procedural questions were settled first. On representation, the panel applied Article 19 of Prakas No. 099: a party may be represented by another person only if that person holds written authorisation from the party, and the eight workers had transferred their rights to the federation president in a letter of 24 February 2014; the federation could therefore act for the members of the base union. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] On the employer's absence, Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 allows the panel to continue and hear the case in the absence of a properly summoned party who fails to appear without good reason; the employer had been summoned, telephoned at 2:15 p.m. on the hearing day, and gave no adequate reason, so the panel proceeded without it. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 53][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

On the merits, point 2 of Notification No. 041 of 7 March 2011 requires 2,000 riel a day, or a free meal, for workers who volunteer for overtime at the employer's request, without fixing hours. [Para 61][Para 62] The panel found the company had paid at least that sum, in line with Award 282/13 (Sino Garment), and that no statute, agreement, practice or internal rule obliged it to pay 1,000 riel per hour more. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66] The demand was therefore an interest dispute, and under Article 96 of the Labour Law, Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 only the most representative union — more than half the workforce — may bring one. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 69][Para 71][Para 73] The base union was expressly not the most representative in the company, so the panel followed Awards 81/04, 98/04, 169/11, 02/11 and 66/11 and declined the claim. [Para 32][Para 73][Para 74][Para 76][Para 78]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for overtime meal money above the 2,000 riel a day fixed by Notification No. 041 is an interest dispute, and a base union that is not the most representative in the enterprise — commanding no more than half the workers — cannot bring such a dispute before the panel, even where the federation holds written authorisation from individual members. [Para 66][Para 73][Para 76][Para 78]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's acceptance that a federation may represent members of an affiliated base union on a written transfer of rights, and its ruling that a party who gives only a telephone excuse that the responsible person is busy forfeits the right to be present, were procedural findings not needed to decide the single demand. [Para 49][Para 56][Para 57]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer is named three ways — "អ៉ី អិម ប៊ី ដែល កូវី", "អ៊ី អិម ប៊ី ជែល កូវី" and, in the union's own letter, a sewing factory "អ៊ី អិម ប៊ី ជែល កូវី (ខេមបូឌា)". [Para 1][Para 21][Para 22] The panel date of constitution reads "ថ្ងៃទី ២០ ខែមីនា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៤", printing two years [Para 12]. Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 is dated "២០១ ២០០៤" [Para 71]. The section numbering restarts twice ("ចំណុចវិវាទ" appears at paras 35 and 58) and paragraph 77 is a fragment ("ដូចនេះ តំណាង បំផុត ប្រចាំ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ទេ") that adds nothing. [Para 35][Para 58][Para 77] The union's registration is cited as letter No. 024/13 and certificate No. 2756, both dated 25 January 2013, and the two numbers are not reconciled. [Para 19][Para 31]
