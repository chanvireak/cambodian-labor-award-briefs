# Award No. 344/14 — Jin Win In Das Stri (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 26 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer, An Nan for the workers, Pen Bunhea chair) · Doc ID 139786

## 1. Facts
Jin Win In Das Stri (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a Phnom Penh garment factory, faced a strike by about 20 workers from 13 December 2014 over ten demands on working conditions. [Para 17] Conciliation on 13 December settled nothing, and the Ministry referred the ten unresolved points to the Council on 15 December. [Para 18][Para 19] On 16 December the Council issued injunction 064/14 ordering the strike suspended and all strikers back at their shifts from the morning of 17 December 2014; the workers stayed out, and the panel terminated the proceedings on 26 December 2014. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must remove its administrative staff? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must pay a midday meal allowance of 4,000 riel per day? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must pay an overtime meal allowance of 2,000 riel per hour? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of USD 15? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must pay a regular attendance bonus of USD 18? [Para 6]
6. Whether the 2015 wage increase must be added on top of the existing basic wage? [Para 7]
7. Whether the union leadership must work ordinary overtime? [Para 8]
8. Whether the company must instruct its Chinese managers? [Para 9]
9. Whether the company must stop discriminating against the Cambodian Movement of Workers Union? [Para 10]
10. Whether the company must honour the agreement reached before the Council? [Para 11]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the strike was called to press the ten demands and continued after the case was referred; the workers also said union representatives were barred from the production line. [Para 29][Para 31]
**Employer (respondent):** about 800 workers had returned but some 200 were still out; the company refused entry to three or four representatives for fear they would incite the workforce, and had terminated one union leader's contract. [Para 31][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings without deciding any of the ten claims and made no order on the merits. [Para 59]

The panel framed the only question as whether the arbitration should continue. [Para 44] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure, including strikes and lockouts, while the panel is seised, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 44] Earlier awards had read that provision as barring the panel from proceeding whenever workers are striking or an employer has locked out, and the panel followed them. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 55] It also relied on the notice in case 04/04 MSI, which held that a panel cannot resolve or issue an award on a collective dispute while the parties strike and may take the case up again once the strike ends. [Para 49][Para 50]

Because no hearing was held, the panel made no findings on the facts of the ten demands. [Para 43] It recorded that the workers kept striking on 23 December 2014, after the injunction and after the employer had refused entry to three or four representatives and terminated a union leader's contract, and terminated the proceedings on that basis. [Para 56][Para 57]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended and after being asked to return to their shifts, the panel will not continue the arbitration: it terminates the proceedings and leaves the claims undecided, and may take the dispute up again only once the strike has ended. The refusal of entry to a few union representatives, or the termination of a union leader's contract, does not entitle workers to keep striking during the arbitration. [Para 47][Para 50][Para 56][Para 58]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that it could not make any factual finding because it never reached a hearing, and its catalogue of earlier strike cases, were explanatory rather than determinative of the outcome. [Para 43][Para 48]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The header block carries a stray "June 2017" between the party names [Para 1], and the second page line mixes "secretariat" and an address fragment into the worker-representative list [Para 2].
- The ten demands are reproduced only from the conciliation report; the decision section never restates them, so the numbering in §2 is the report's, not the panel's.
- Demand 1 reads "សុំឱ្យក្រុមហ៊ុនដករដ្ឋបាល" without an object, so it is unclear whether "administration" means the administrative staff or an administrative measure [Para 2]; rendered as printed.
- In case 04/04 the panel is recorded as issuing a "សេចក្តីជូនដំណឹង ២០១៥" dated 29 January 2004, a year inserted into the document type [Para 49].
- Award numbers in the citation list are run together ("១៣៧ ០៩ ១៣៨/០៩"), and "៣៩០៨" appears without a slash [Para 48].
