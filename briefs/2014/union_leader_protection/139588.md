# Award No. 141/14 — O SE ANG GAMING Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 10 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Run Saray, chair) · Doc ID 139588

## 1. Facts
The company, a garment manufacturer at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, suspended the employment contracts of all 1,655 production workers for one month from 26 May to 26 June 2014, paying each a 15 USD accommodation allowance. [Para 3][Para 22] It said repeated strikes had caused shipments to go by air and losses of 1 million USD in 2013 and 1.1 million USD in the first four months of 2014, after which the buyer stopped ordering. [Para 3] Conciliation on four unsettled demands failed and the file reached the Secretariat on 30 May 2014. [Para 17][Para 18] The workers kept meeting and demonstrating after the referral, and the panel convened only a pre-hearing meeting on 4 June 2014; no hearing was held and no evidence was taken. [Para 19][Para 28][Para 38]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel must terminate the arbitration proceedings because the workers continued collective meetings and street processions during the arbitration, contrary to Article 20 of Prakas No. 099? [Para 29][Para 42]
2. Whether the employer must pay 50 per cent of base wage and other benefits during the one-month suspension, and failing that terminate the contracts with statutory payments? [Para 2][Para 5]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they maintained the demand for 50 per cent of base wage and benefits during the suspension, for lawful termination payments if that was refused, for underpaid wages to be made good by 1 June 2014, and for payment of workers left "floating"; they would keep meeting and marching to other institutions for a solution. [Para 2][Para 7][Para 9][Para 23]
**Employer (respondent):** a 50 per cent payment was impossible given the losses and the loss of its buyer; underpaid amounts would be folded into the May 2014 payroll payable on 7 June 2014, and the same date applied to the floating workers' money. [Para 3][Para 8][Para 10]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration proceedings in case No. 141/14 without ruling on any of the four demands. [Para 45]
The panel never reached the merits. It read Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 as requiring the parties, while arbitration is running, to suspend every dispute measure such as strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, and to attend all meetings to which the panel summons them. [Para 29][Para 30] It found that the workers' continued gatherings and processions after the referral were exactly such an aggravating measure inside the arbitration window. [Para 42] It added that the workers showed no will to resolve the collective dispute at the Arbitration Council at all. [Para 43]
The panel followed its own earlier jurisprudence: in Case No. 194/12 — An Ful Gaming it ended proceedings where the employer refused strikers' return to work and skipped the pre-hearing meeting, and in Case No. 49/10 — Wing Ying it treated the dismissal of the whole union leadership after an order to suspend the strike as an aggravating measure. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34] On that basis, and expressly relying on Article 20, it declined to continue. [Para 35][Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party keeps up collective action — meetings, processions or other pressure — after the dispute has been referred, and so aggravates the dispute during the arbitration, the panel will terminate the proceedings instead of ruling on the demands, whichever party is responsible and whatever the merits of the underlying claims. [Para 42][Para 44]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted, without deciding anything, that the 15 USD accommodation allowance exceeded the 7 USD set by Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, and that it had taken no evidence because no hearing was held. [Para 4][Para 28]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The enabling Prakas is printed as No. 1099 in the jurisdiction paragraph but as No. 099 in the reasoning and the dispositive article. [Para 11][Para 29][Para 44] The award refers to the company as "VOA Ou Se Ang" in the procedural history and "Ou Se Ang Gaming" elsewhere. [Para 16] The signature block is heavily corrupted, the worker-side arbitrator's line reduced to unreadable Latin fragments. [Para 46] No statement of facts section exists: the panel recorded that it made no factual findings because it never held a hearing. [Para 28]
