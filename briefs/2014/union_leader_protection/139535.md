# Award No. 055/14 — Moun Ei Pheril (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 13 March 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 139535

## 1. Facts
Moun Ei Pheril (Cambodia), a factory in Chrongruk village, Khan Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh, faced a strike by its workers over 17 demands; conciliation on 3 March 2014 settled 7 and left 10, which reached the Council on 5 March 2014 under report No. 261 Kb/Ak/Vk. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] On 5 March 2014 the company asked for an order suspending the strike, and on 6 March 2014 the Council issued Order No. 009/14 requiring all strikers back at their shifts from the morning of 7 March 2014, except three dismissed union leaders. [Para 28][Para 30] The strike continued: the union's coordinator said workers would stay out until the three were reinstated, and on 11 March 2014 he reported that about 99 per cent of the workforce was still striking. [Para 48][Para 50] No hearing was ever held, so the panel made no findings of fact on any demand. [Para 36]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must dismiss a workers' representative (name redacted) who is said to have threatened and insulted workers? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must pay a regular attendance bonus of 15 dollars a month, pro-rated for days of lawful leave and other permitted absence? [Para 4]
3. Whether the company must provide a midday meal allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 6]
4. Whether the company must pay a travel and accommodation allowance of 10 dollars a month? [Para 8]
5. Whether the company must pay an overtime meal allowance of 3,000 riel for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 10][Para 11]
6. Whether the company must build an eating area and a bicycle and motorcycle shed by a fixed date? [Para 12]
7. Whether the company must install loudspeakers so workers can listen during working hours, on a fixed schedule? [Para 14]
8. Whether the company must organise dancing and a prize draw on International Women's Day? [Para 16]
9. Whether the company continues to discriminate against the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union? [Para 18]
10. Whether the company must take back three union leaders, Mr M, Ms B and Ms K, into employment? [Para 20]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the ten demands stood as recorded in the non-conciliation report, and the strike would continue until the company took back the three dismissed union leaders. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 48]
**Employer (respondent):** it cannot dismiss the representative but will counsel him on his language and conduct; it pays a 10-dollar attendance bonus, deducting it for absence beyond two days of other business a month and cancelling it entirely beyond that; it pays 100 riel a day for meals and 7 dollars travel; it pays 2,500 riel for overtime meals from 16:00 to 18:00; it will discuss building works with the building owner but has no date; it has no plan for loudspeakers or a Women's Day event; it does not discriminate against any union; and it cannot rehire the three leaders because their three-month fixed-duration contracts ended and statutory payments were made. [Para 3][Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 11][Para 13][Para 15][Para 17][Para 19][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel terminated the arbitration in case No. 055/14 without deciding any of the ten demands and without making findings of fact. [Para 52][Para 53]

The panel treated one question as logically prior: whether the arbitration should continue at all. [Para 37] Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend every disputing measure, including a strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, for the duration of the panel's work, and to attend all meetings to which it summons them. [Para 37][Para 38] Prior awards had read that provision as barring the panel from proceeding while a strike is under way, and the panel followed Award No. 04/04-IM SI, Award No. 18/04 concerning the Cambodian hotels, and Award No. 77/05-Franco Nitting, each of which had refused to continue and reserved the right to end proceedings where its order was breached. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]

On the facts, the panel's order of 6 March 2014 required a return to work on 7 March 2014, and the secretariat's telephone checks that day and again on 11 March 2014 confirmed that the strike was continuing at about 99 per cent of the workforce. [Para 30][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50] The panel therefore found that the workers had not complied with Order No. 009/14 and terminated the proceedings; because no hearing took place it recorded no findings of fact and reached no conclusion on the merits of any of the ten demands. [Para 36][Para 51][Para 52] The award does not say whether the case may be reopened; the earlier decisions it followed had said the panel could take the dispute up again once the strike ended. [Para 41]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a panel has ordered a strike suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 and the workers continue striking through the period of arbitration, the panel will terminate the arbitration without a hearing and without ruling on any of the demands; the precondition for arbitrating a collective dispute is that the disputing measures have actually stopped. [Para 37][Para 44][Para 51][Para 52]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's exclusion of the three dismissed union leaders from the return-to-work order, on the ground that their contracts had ended before the strike and their case was awaiting legal resolution, is an observation on the scope of that order rather than a finding on demand 10. [Para 30][Para 47]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Order No. 009/14 is dated "06 March 2023 2014" at [Para 30], an OCR intrusion of 2023; the order and the file are elsewhere dated 6 March 2014 [Para 35][Para 47].
- The company name is printed four ways: មូន អី ភេរឹល, មូន អី ភេរីល, មូន អូន អី ភេរីល and មូន អ៊ីភេរឹល [Para 1][Para 27][Para 33][Para 34]; no Latin-script form appears, so "Moun Ei Pheril (Cambodia)" is a transliteration.
- The conciliation is said to have produced 7 settled and 10 unsettled points out of 17 demands [Para 26], but only the ten unsettled demands are reproduced [Para 2]–[Para 21], so the settled seven cannot be identified.
- The overtime claim's start time is printed "ម៉ោង ២៦៖០០០" [Para 10], unintelligible; the employer's figure is 16:00 to 18:00 [Para 11].
- The file names the individual workers and union leaders concerned [Para 2][Para 20][Para 30]; they are redacted here.
- The worker-party arbitrator's name is set beside the employer-party slot in the signature block, which also carries stray English fragments and the year "2017" [Para 54].
