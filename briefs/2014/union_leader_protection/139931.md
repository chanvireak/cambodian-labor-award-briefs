# Award No. 284/13 — Ming Ya Garment (Arbitration Council, 23 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (យូ សួន ទី for the employer, អន ណន for the workers, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 139931

## 1. Facts
The respondent is a garment factory employing slightly more than 200 workers. [Para 33] Mr C began work as a sewing-machine operator on 6 August 2013 without any written contract, on a basic wage of USD 80, an attendance bonus of USD 20 and a transport and accommodation allowance of USD 7 a month. [Para 38][Para 39] A union was formed by election on 20 October 2013, at which Mr C was elected president, Mr N deputy and Mr V secretary; the federation notified the employer of the three on 21 October 2013, and again by EMS post on 7 November 2013. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 52] On 4 November 2013 the director dismissed Mr C at his own house next to the factory, telling him the company did not want a union, and dismissed the two other elected leaders the same day; only Mr C claims reinstatement. [Para 40][Para 42][Para 43][Para 94]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must take back Mr C as union president, and Mr N and Mr V in their posts, and cease anti-union discrimination? [Para 2][Para 36]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Mr C committed no misconduct, was never warned in writing, and was dismissed hours after the election purely because he had become union president; the employer sought no authorisation from the labour inspector, and the two other elected leaders were dismissed the same day. [Para 41][Para 44][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** absent. It did not attend conciliation on 19 December 2013 or the hearing on 10 January 2014, gave no valid reason, and its security staff accepted the invitation but refused to sign for it. [Para 13][Para 19][Para 58]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to take Mr C back in his job and to cease all anti-union discrimination. The award is not binding and becomes final 8 days after notification absent written objection to the Minister. [Para 103][Para 104]

The panel first confirmed it could hear the case in the employer's absence: Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 permits the panel to continue, or to close the file by award, where a party duly invited fails to appear without valid reason, and earlier awards had proceeded in absentia on that basis. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 59]

On the merits, Article 293 of the Labour Law allows the dismissal of a shop steward, or of any candidate for that office, only with the agreement of the labour inspector. [Para 60][Para 61] Point 3 of Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 extends the same protection to every candidate in a union leadership election, running from 45 days before the poll and ending 45 days after it unless the candidate is elected, and it requires the union to notify the employer of the candidacy by any means available. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65] Following Award No. 40/10 (Meng Yan), the panel held that an elected candidate's protection runs from the day of election until the union is registered, since otherwise the candidate could be dismissed precisely in the interval before registration. [Para 69][Para 71]

The panel then applied the three conditions it had set in earlier awards: that the worker be a protected person, that the dismissal fall within the protected period, and that the union have notified the employer of the names. [Para 72] All three were met. Mr C was an elected candidate, the dismissal of 4 November 2013 fell 15 days after the election of 20 October 2013, and the notification of 21 October 2013 was handed to the director through the factory's security staff, who returned it when he refused to accept it, and was then sent by EMS on 7 November 2013 — so the employer knew the names from 21 October 2013. [Para 75][Para 78][Para 83][Para 84][Para 86]

Because the employer never sought the labour inspector's authorisation, the dismissal breached Article 293 procedure. [Para 87] On discrimination, the panel weighed the director's statement that the company did not want a union together with the dismissal of all three elected leaders on the same day, and found that this showed an intention to prevent the union being formed; the employer, by staying away, forwent the chance to answer the accusation or produce contrary evidence. [Para 97][Para 98][Para 100] Discrimination contrary to Articles 12 and 279 was therefore established, and reinstatement plus a cessation order followed. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 102][Para 103]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An elected union leader is protected from dismissal from the day of the election until the union is registered, so that the gap between election and registration cannot be used to remove him; the protection is lost only if the union fails to notify the employer of the candidacy, and notice given to security staff at the factory gate, refused by the director and then repeated by post, satisfies the "any means available" requirement. [Para 65][Para 71][Para 83][Para 84] Dismissing every elected leader on the same day, coupled with a statement that the company does not want a union, establishes anti-union discrimination under Articles 12 and 279 without more. [Para 97][Para 98]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that a dismissal under Article 293 also requires the agreement of the Minister, and its remark that the employer had abandoned its opportunity to rebut, were general statements that did not alter the relief ordered. [Para 61][Para 100]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The worker's name is printed in at least five forms — ជុំ តឿភត្រា, ជុំ ត្រឿភត្រា, ជុំ ត្សឿភត្រា, ជុំ ត្បឿក ត្រា and ជុំ តឿភ្ញូ តា — so he is recorded here as Mr C. [Para 40][Para 80][Para 97][Para 102]
- Column 3 of the reasoning reads "លក្ខខណ្ឌ ទី៣ បាន បំពេញ ទេ", omitting the negative, and so contradicts the findings at [Para 83] and the conclusion at [Para 86] that the third condition was satisfied. [Para 85]
- The dismissal time is given as "about 8:00 or 9:00 in the morning", two times printed for one act. [Para 40][Para 94]
- The panel is constituted on "02 January 20 2014", with a stray year fragment. [Para 15]
- The chair's name appears as ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា in the header but is garbled to "ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ណា2" in the signature block, where the employer-side arbitrator's name is lost entirely. [Para 1][Para 106]
- The union's registration receipt is dated 2 December 2013, after the dismissal, and no registration certificate appears in the file. [Para 22][Para 53]
