# Award No. 021/14 — Vik Co Hen Bak (Cambodia) In Das Stial (Arbitration Council, 24 February 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Nhan Somuninth (president) · Doc ID 139513

## 1. Facts
Vik Co Hen Bak (Cambodia) In Das Stial (វីក កូ ហែន ប៊ែក), a bag manufacturer at Trapeang Kong, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, began operating in July 2012 and employs about 1,482 workers. The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia filed five demands; conciliation on 23 January 2014 failed and the file reached the Council on 30 January 2014. At the 17 February 2014 hearing only one worker, Mr T, appeared claiming to represent the workers, and the union's letters of authority were unclear. [Para 9][Para 16][Para 47][Para 51]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must raise wages to USD 160 a month? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must take back Mr T of the cutting section, employee No. 0830, to his former job? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must stop deducting pay from workers absent on sick leave with a medical certificate? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company must provide a nursery for workers' children? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must pay a USD 15 lunch allowance? [Para 6]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the five demands as filed, pressed by the Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia and by Mr T personally; neither produced a written authority to represent the workforce. [Para 2][Para 51][Para 59]
**Employer (respondent):** attended through its administration head and an administrative officer, holding a written transfer of authority dated 17 February 2014, and did not concede the demands. [Para 26][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to consider any of the five demands and made no order for payment, holding that the case was not a collective labour dispute at all. [Para 97][Para 98][Para 99]

The panel first fixed who was properly before it. The Free Workers' Union at the factory was still being organised and had no registration certificate, so under Article 268 of the Labour Law it had not yet acquired the rights the law recognises for a professional organisation, including the right to represent members before the panel. [Para 23][Para 69][Para 70][Para 71][Para 76] The federation's staff member who attended had filed no document showing his authority from the union president, and the letters of request relied on were defective: the first page listed workers' names but carried no legible thumbprints, pages two and three were bare name lists with no heading and no indication which company employed them, and page four had names and thumbprints but nothing saying the workers transferred their rights to the federation. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 60] The panel explained the legal necessity of a clear written transfer, set a deadline of 5.00 pm on 18 February 2014, and no valid document was filed by then. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 67]

Mr T, the worker elected union president, had no lawful leadership status for the same reason, and filed no transfer of rights to himself either. [Para 77][Para 78][Para 79] He was, however, the only worker who both claimed the demands and appeared in person. [Para 81][Para 82][Para 93]

On jurisdiction, Prakas No. 099, Article 32, limits the panel to collective labour disputes under Article 309. [Para 83][Para 84] Article 302 defines a collective dispute as one arising between one or more employers and a number of their workers, and the panel, following case 10/03, held that all three conditions must be met. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 92] With a single worker as complainant, condition (a) — several workers — was not satisfied, so the panel did not need to reach conditions (b) and (c), and the case as a whole was not a collective labour dispute. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 96]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dispute brought by one worker alone is not a collective labour dispute, whatever its subject matter; the panel therefore has no jurisdiction and will not examine the merits, and an unregistered factory union cannot supply the missing plurality because it cannot yet represent anyone. [Para 70][Para 94]

**Obiter dicta:** Thumbprint letters that name workers but show no legible prints, no employer, no heading and no words of transfer are not a valid transfer of rights, even after the panel grants an extension to cure them. [Para 55][Para 67]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated 24 February 2014 but the panel is recorded as constituted on "០៣ ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៣", a year before the dispute, and Para 57 gives the hearing year as "២០១៦ ២០១៤". The employer's name varies throughout (វីក កូ ហែន ប៊ែក, វឹកកូ, វីកកូ, "ហ៊ុន ប៊ែក", "ខាមបូឌា"), and the sole claimant's name is printed three ways (តាត់ គឹម ឈិន, តាក់ គីម ឈិន, តាត់ គីម ឈិន), so he is given as Mr T here. A signed agreement of 17 February 2014 making the award binding on all issues (Para 45) contradicts the closing statement that this is a non-binding award (Para 100); both are reported. Stray numerals ("៣២០" at Para 26, "៣២" at Para 99) and a duplicated signature block remain. [Para 1][Para 17][Para 45][Para 57][Para 99][Para 100]
