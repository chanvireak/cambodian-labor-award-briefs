# Order No. 357/14 — Double U Win Holding P.L.C (Arbitration Council, 9 January 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Uk Ri, chosen by the employer party; Huon Chun Di, chosen by the worker party; Pen Bun Chea, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139796

## 1. Facts
Two federations — the Federation of Trade Unions of Khmer Democratic Workers and the Federation of Trade Unions of Free Workers — brought a claim on behalf of workers of Double U Win Holding P.L.C, Street 169, Sangkat Veal Vong, Khan 7 Makara, Phnom Penh, for wages owed, the last month's wage and severance pay under the Labour Law. [Para 1][Para 11] Conciliation on 22 December 2014 left one of seven demands unresolved, and the non-conciliation report No. 1472 of 26 December 2014 reached the Secretariat on 29 December 2014. [Para 12][Para 13] The panel was constituted on 31 December 2014 and set the hearing for 6 January 2015 at 14:00; the employer appeared, the workers' federations did not. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay the wages owed, the last month's wage and the severance pay due on termination of the employment contracts under the Labour Law, together with the final wage? [Para 2]
2. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all, the claimant workers having failed to appear at the hearing? [Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company owes them wages and the final month's wage and must pay severance under the Labour Law in a single settlement; they asked the conciliator not to refer this demand to arbitration at all, on the ground that it falls within the competence of the labour inspectorate under Articles 116 and 117 of the Labour Law, and gave notice of a right to strike from 23 December 2014 to press it. [Para 2][Para 4] By telephone on the day of the hearing the president of the Khmer Democratic Workers federation said he would not attend and asked the Council to close the file, while the president of the Free Workers federation said he was busy and repeated the Articles 116–117 objection. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 47][Para 48]

**Employer (respondent):** the company undertook to pay the wages owed and the final month's wage on 25 December 2014 at 15:00, and asked for time to consider the severance pay. [Para 5] It appeared at the hearing through its lawyer and staff. [Para 1][Para 16]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the claim as abandoned for non-appearance and terminated the arbitration proceedings, so nothing was ordered on the wages or severance claimed. [Para 59][Para 63][Para 64]

The panel identified the only question it would decide as whether the arbitration should continue where the claimant party was absent, and started from Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004: where a party has been duly invited and fails to appear before the panel without showing a valid reason, the Council may either continue in that party's absence or terminate the arbitration by issuing an order. [Para 30] Rule 4.7 of the annexed Rules of Procedure says the same, subject to the condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place of the proceedings. [Para 31][Para 32]

On the authority of its earlier orders in cases No. 16/07, No. 27/07, No. 138/08, No. 11/11, No. 131/12, No. 108/13 and No. 332/14, the panel treated Article 21 as requiring three cumulative conditions: that the party was duly invited, that it did not appear, and that it gave no valid reason for the absence. [Para 33][Para 34]

It then applied each condition. On the first, the Secretariat had summoned the claimant by letter No. 1856 of 31 December 2014 to the hearing of 6 January 2015 at 14:00, and the summons was signed for on 2 January 2015 at 15:11; the panel found the invitation genuine and the condition met. [Para 35][Para 37][Para 38] On the second, it read "appearance before the panel" as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation in the arbitration until its conclusion, and found that the workers had done neither at any of the four stages of the proceedings. [Para 39][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] On the third, neither federation gave any specific or reasonable excuse, the Khmer Democratic Workers federation having refused to attend and asked for the file to be closed and the Free Workers federation pleading only that it was busy. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51]

Having found all three conditions satisfied, the panel held that the claimant bears the duty to appear and to present its arguments and evidence in support of its claim, and that a respondent's duty to answer arises only once the claimant has done so. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] The claimant having failed in that duty without excuse, the panel treated the claim as abandoned, held that it need not continue the arbitration, and terminated it by order. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been duly summoned and fails to appear before the panel without a valid reason, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and terminate the arbitration by order rather than proceed in the claimant's absence; the three conditions of Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY — valid invitation, non-appearance, and absence of valid excuse — must each be established, and "appearance" means attending the hearing and taking part in the proceedings to their conclusion. [Para 33][Para 39][Para 59]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that a respondent's duty to answer arises only after the claimant has presented its case, and its account of the four stages of an arbitration, are general descriptions of procedure rather than part of the rule applied. [Para 40][Para 56]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The complaint is said to have been received on 2 December 2014 but to be dated 30 December 2014, a date after receipt [Para 11]; the conciliation is dated 22 December 2014 and the report 26 December 2014 [Para 12].
- The summons at [Para 45] is dated "6 January 2014" while the hearing throughout is 6 January 2015 [Para 10][Para 35].
- Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 is printed as "ប្រការ ២១២" at [Para 30] and as "ប្រការ និង ២១" at [Para 39]; the reference to Rule 4.7 is corrupted at [Para 31] ("ភាព្លោីះ", "X \" prover \"").
- The summons is recorded as signed for by the secretary of the National Union Confederation, a body that is not a party to this case, rather than by either claimant federation [Para 35].
- No figure is given anywhere for the wages owed, the final month's wage or the severance claimed, so the amounts in dispute cannot be stated [Para 2][Para 5].
- The company name is printed with inconsistent spacing ("វីដាប់បែលយូ" and "វីដាប់ បែល យូ"), and the chair's signature at [Para 64] is lost to OCR ("muchesev"); the reasoning at [Para 36] repeats a sentence twice.
