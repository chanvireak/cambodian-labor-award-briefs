# Award No. 179/14 — Meng Da Footwear Industrial Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 12 August 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Ven Pov (worker-appointed), Roun Saray (president) · Doc ID 139637

## 1. Facts
Meng Da Footwear Industrial is a shoe factory at Chom Chao, Phnom Penh, employing about 4,100 workers, of whom more than 1,000 are women, and it has no nursery. [Para 57][Para 58][Para 116] In 2010 it took over 107 workers from Ming Da, a factory that closed in May 2013 after its owner fled, and recognised their seniority from their first day at Ming Da while paying wages itself from 1 August 2010. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 81][Para 133] The Khmer Youth Voice Federation, registered at the factory but not the most representative union, filed 12 collective demands in May 2014; one was settled at the hearing, leaving eight for decision. [Para 24][Para 30][Para 31][Para 61]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether Meng Da must pay severance, notice and moral damages to the 107 workers for their service at Ming Da up to 1 August 2010? [Para 62]
2. Whether the employer must offset special leave against overtime in three instalments over three months rather than all at once? [Para 82]
4. Whether the employer must pay a skill bonus of USD 10, USD 20 or USD 35 according to how many machines a worker can operate? [Para 91]
5. Whether the employer must pay 300 per cent of the normal day's wage for work on public holidays? [Para 187]
6. Whether the employer must raise the regular attendance bonus from USD 10 to USD 15 a month? [Para 204]
7. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance from USD 7 to USD 15 a month? [Para 214]
8. Whether the employer must build a nursery, or pay USD 40 a month towards childcare? [Para 222]
9. Whether the employer must continue paying the USD 5 health-care allowance on top of the minimum wage? [Para 238]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Meng Da took over the workforce, so it should answer for the severed Ming Da relationship and pay notice, dismissal and moral damages counted from each worker's first day at Ming Da; skilled workers operating several machines deserve an extra bonus, holiday work deserves 300 per cent, and housing and travel costs have risen. [Para 65][Para 70][Para 94][Para 113][Para 118]
**Employer (respondent):** Ming Da itself paid the 5 per cent termination allowance and lawful benefits when the relationship ended on 1 August 2010, and Meng Da is still operating; it gives 10 USD attendance bonus, 7 USD travel and accommodation, 200 per cent for holiday work and 5 USD milk money, all as the law and practice require, and has no space for a nursery. [Para 67][Para 95][Para 99][Para 121][Para 229]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the nursery claim succeeded and the employer was ordered to set up a nursery, or where it cannot, to pay the actual invoices of an outside nursery for children aged 18 to 36 months; every other claim was dismissed, with no sum in riel or USD fixed. [Para 264][Para 265]

On the severance claim the panel applied Art 664 of the Civil Code: a labour contract arises from the promise to work for wages and ends when work and wages stop. [Para 128][Para 130] Because Ming Da stopped paying wages and Meng Da began on 1 August 2010, the old contract ended and a new one began that day; the recognition of seniority was a benefit to be realised when the Meng Da contract itself ends, not a transfer of liability for the Ming Da closure. [Para 134][Para 135][Para 139][Para 140]

On special leave the panel applied Art 171 and Arts 2 and 3 of Prakas 267, which allow special leave to be deducted from annual leave, or repaid within 90 days, within a 10-hour day and 54-hour week; repaying at the overtime rate is more favourable than the law requires and so is permitted under Art 13, following award 49/04. [Para 141][Para 145][Para 151][Para 159][Para 161] The claim to spread the offset failed. [Para 166]

On the skill bonus, holiday pay, attendance bonus, travel allowance and health allowance the panel found either that the employer already met the legal floor or that nothing in law, agreement or practice supported the higher figure. [Para 196][Para 207][Para 216][Para 248] Prakas 230 of 25 July 2012 fixed travel and accommodation at USD 7 and the attendance bonus at 10 USD, both paid. [Para 205][Para 214] Prakas 103 of 2013 merged the USD 5 health allowance into the minimum wage and was itself superseded by Prakas 317 of 31 December 2013, which raised the minimum to USD 100 without a separate health line. [Para 238][Para 245][Para 248] Each surviving claim was an interest dispute, and because the union was not most representative the panel refused to consider them. [Para 171][Para 199][Para 210][Para 218][Para 253]

On the nursery, Art 186 requires any enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a nursing room and a nursery; award 81/09 was followed, and where a nursery cannot be arranged for children over 18 months the employer pays the actual outside invoice. [Para 222][Para 225][Para 230] Because the factory had over 1,000 women and no nursery, and because the 40 USD figure was a flat rate unsupported by any invoice, the panel ordered the nursery with the invoice-based fallback rather than the sum claimed. [Para 227][Para 233][Para 236]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a workforce is taken over by a new employer which recognises prior seniority but begins paying wages itself on a fixed date, the earlier contract ended on that date and the new employer is not liable for termination payments owed for the earlier period; the seniority it recognises counts only when the new contract ends. [Para 134][Para 139]

**Obiter dicta:** that an employer may adopt a practice more generous than the law, such as repaying special leave at the overtime rate, without breaching the Labour Law. [Para 155][Para 159]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The union's registration certificate is dated 21 October 2010 in the evidence list and 21 November 2010 in the findings. [Para 46][Para 59] The number of transferred workers is 113 in one recital and 107 wherever the panel actually decides. [Para 63][Para 81] The date of Prakas 103 appears as "09 December April 2013", "09 April 2013" and "09 September 2013" in different passages. [Para 124][Para 238][Para 248] The company and its predecessor are printed interchangeably as Ming Da and Meng Da, and the employer's own name appears in several spellings. [Para 64][Para 122] The final order on issue 9 repeats its "05 dollars a month" line twice, and the closing paragraph on the binding effect of the award is duplicated mid-sentence. [Para 266][Para 267][Para 268] The award date on the face is 12 August 2014, the original date of 28 July 2014 having been deferred by agreement. [Para 33]
