# Award No. 038/14 — Wai Full Garment (Cambodia) Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 21 March 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, An Non, Kong Phally presiding) · Doc ID 139524

## 1. Facts
A garment factory in Ang Snuol district, Kandal, employing about 880 workers, faced 18 claims from the Cambodia Union federation, of which conciliation settled 10 and referred 8. One of those was settled at the hearing itself, when the employer agreed to build a nursery within two months, leaving seven for decision. The union had been registered since 29 August 2013 and was not the most representative in the enterprise. [Para 16][Para 44][Para 46][Para 62]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a 2,000 riel target bonus to workers in a team that met its target and who worked on either of the two days? [Para 108]
2. Whether the employer must establish a standard nursery for workers' children? [Para 62]
3. Whether the employer must pay 50 per cent of wages and allowances for the whole 90 days of maternity leave before the leave begins? [Para 143]
4. Whether the employer must raise transport and accommodation allowance from 8 to 15 US dollars a month? [Para 161]
5. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus from 10 to 20 US dollars a month? [Para 179]
6. Whether the employer must pay a 15 dollar lunch allowance or provide one free meal a day? [Para 186]
7. Whether the employer must pay a 15 dollar skills allowance to workers who can operate three machines? [Para 199]
8. Whether the employer must pay a 10 dollar monthly incentive to workers who worked two hours' overtime daily? [Para 204]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** teams lose the target bonus because one or two absent members break the target, so payment should follow the individual worker's attendance; wages were cut for the five-day nationwide strike; the employer also discontinued a 10 dollar overtime incentive that had run for six or seven months; skills pay is given for one machine but not for three, which is unreasonable. [Para 55][Para 56][Para 92][Para 88]
**Employer (respondent):** the target bonus is a deliberate productivity policy conditioned on full attendance, and a team that misses its target earns nothing; the 8 dollar transport payment and the 10 dollar attendance bonus already exceed Ministry Notification 230; a 15 dollar skills or lunch increase is beyond its means. [Para 53][Para 60][Para 74][Para 78][Para 83]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the maternity-pay claim was granted; the target-bonus claim was dismissed on the merits, the nursery claim was resolved by agreement, and the remaining five claims were dismissed for want of jurisdiction as interest disputes. [Para 223][Para 225][Para 228]
On issue 1 the panel held the claim to be a rights dispute because the target bonus is set by the employer under Article 2's management right, but it then found for the employer on the substance: the law neither requires nor forbids such a scheme, the condition of full attendance is rational and tied to productivity, and no agreement, collective agreement or practice contradicts it. Workers who meet both conditions still receive 4,000 riel. [Para 112][Para 122][Para 127][Para 130]
On issue 3 the panel read Articles 182 and 183 with Articles 103 and 115(3): maternity pay is wages, wages may not be paid on a rest day, and payment must therefore be made at least one day before the leave begins rather than monthly during it. The employer's monthly practice did not comply, and the panel ordered 50 per cent of wages and allowances for the whole 90 days to be paid at least one day before the leave starts. [Para 143][Para 153][Para 157][Para 159][Para 160]
On issues 4 to 8 the panel found no law, agreement, internal rule or established practice entitling the workers to the higher sums, held each to be an interest dispute, and dismissed them because the claimant union was not the most representative in the enterprise, following earlier awards to the same effect. [Para 165][Para 174][Para 178][Para 220][Para 222]
On issue 8 the panel added that it could not even find a clear and continuous past practice, since neither party could date the start or the end of the 10 dollar incentive. [Para 217][Para 218]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Maternity pay under Articles 182 and 183 is wages, and under Article 115(3) it must be paid at least one day before the leave begins; paying it month by month during the leave is unlawful. An employer may set the conditions of a productivity bonus under its management right, and a condition requiring full team attendance is lawful and enforceable while the underlying entitlement is contractual. Interest claims may be arbitrated only by the most representative union in the enterprise. [Para 157][Para 127][Para 174]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that where a long-standing practice gives workers a benefit beyond the law it can ripen into a binding oral agreement, but it declined to apply that to the 10 dollar incentive because the practice was not shown to be clear and continuous. [Para 209][Para 218]

The presiding arbitrator's name appears both as Kong Phallath and Kong Phally, and the company as Wai Full Garment (Cambodia) Ltd. and Wai Full Garment. [Para 1][Para 235] The disposition describes the existing lunch payment as 10 dollars where the reasoning records 6.5 dollars. [Para 187][Para 191][Para 230] Prakas 099 is dated "21 April 2019 2004", and the internal rules approval is printed as "30 November 2 2012". [Para 104][Para 30] The nursery issue is numbered 02 but decided only by consent, and the award records an issue settled without an operative order. [Para 62][Para 63]
