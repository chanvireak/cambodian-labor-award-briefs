# Award No. 264/13 — Kho Sik Garmen (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 10 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kong Phal presiding, appointed by both parties; You Soun Ty, employer-appointed; Vong Vanna, worker-appointed) · Doc ID 139912

## 1. Facts
Kho Sik Garmen is a garment factory at Toul Sangke, Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, registered on 21 November 2012 and employing about 250 workers. [Para 38][Para 39] The Federation of Trade Union for the Development of Workers filed a complaint on 2 December 2013 after a strike over working conditions; conciliation the same day settled 16 of 20 demands and left four, which reached the secretariat on 11 December 2013 as case No. 264/13. [Para 15][Para 16][Para 17][Para 12] The local union at the company was not yet registered as a professional organisation. [Para 22][Para 40] The hearing was held on 19 December 2013; the workers withdrew demand 4, leaving three, and 54 workers had given written authority to the federation president, with three more attending without it. [Para 14][Para 20][Para 21][Para 41][Para 44]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance of 4,000 riel to workers who work overtime from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm? [Para 2][Para 45]
2. Whether the employer must keep the full skill allowance (ប្រាក់មុខជំនាញ) for workers who take authorised leave? [Para 4][Para 85]
3. Whether the employer must pay a meal allowance of 2,000 riel to workers who work on public holidays or Sundays? [Para 6][Para 102]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the 2,000 riel the company pays for overtime meals no longer covers food because market prices have risen, so the allowance should be 4,000 riel for the 4:00 to 6:00 pm shift; and the skill allowance should not be cut when a worker takes leave the law allows. [Para 45][Para 66][Para 67][Para 85] On holiday and Sunday work they asked for 2,000 riel. [Para 102]
**Employer (respondent):** it pays 1,000 riel per hour of overtime, so 2,000 riel for the two hours, consistently and in line with Notification No. 041/11; the skill allowance is an incentive tied to completing a full month of work, so it is reduced in proportion to leave days taken. [Para 46][Para 68][Para 95][Para 96] It gave 1,000 riel to workers who came in on Sundays. [Para 134]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — demand 1 is dismissed, demand 2 is dismissed, and demand 3 is dismissed as to public holidays but granted as to Sundays, the employer being ordered to pay 2,000 riel to workers who voluntarily work on a Sunday; the award is non-binding and becomes final 08 days after notification if no party objects to the Minister. [Para 138][Para 139][Para 140][Para 141][Para 142]

The panel first established that the union could not bring an interest dispute. The Federation and the company signed the memorandum of understanding of 3 October 2012, but the local union was unregistered, so the federation's agreement to represent the workers did not make the parties to this case the signatories of that memorandum, and the award is therefore non-binding. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 24] Reading Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, the panel held that only a union with most-representative status — more than half the workers in the enterprise — may conclude a collective agreement and bring an interest dispute to it. [Para 71][Para 73][Para 74][Para 78] It followed awards 81/04 and 98/04, and 169/11, 02/11 and 66/11, which refused interest disputes brought by unions lacking that status. [Para 78][Para 79] Since the local union was unregistered, and 54 of 250 workers had authorised the federation, the panel found the claimant lacked the legal standing to have an interest dispute heard and dismissed demand 1 on that ground. [Para 81][Para 83][Para 84]

On demand 1 the panel also found no law, regulation, collective agreement, past practice or internal rule requiring 4,000 riel; the practice was 2,000 riel, matching point 2 of Notification No. 041/11 of 7 March 2011, and the company had already applied that notification, so the claim was an interest dispute and failed. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70]

On demand 2 the panel held the skill allowance to be an incentive paid on two conditions — good performance and a full month of work — and found the employer's practice of reducing it in proportion to leave days to be a reasonable exercise of the managerial right under Article 2 of the Labour Law, consistent with awards 62/06, 108/06, 33/07 and 119/09. [Para 88][Para 91][Para 93][Para 95][Para 99] It reasoned that workers lose the allowance only for the time they did not actually work, and that no law or standard requires the employer to keep it during leave. [Para 97][Para 98][Para 100][Para 101]

On demand 3 the panel split the claim. Work on a public holiday is not overtime: Article 137 and Article 139 new, with Articles 2 and 4 of Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999, give a worker who works a holiday one day's wage in compensation on top of the monthly wage, and the panel followed awards 115/08, 52/12 and 245/13 in holding that holiday work is not overtime work, so the 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance was refused. [Para 104][Para 109][Para 110][Para 113][Para 114][Para 115][Para 116] Sunday, by contrast, is the company's weekly rest day and not a normal working day, so a worker who voluntarily works on Sunday works beyond normal hours; by point 2 of Notification No. 041/11 such a worker must receive a 2,000 riel meal allowance for the day regardless of how many hours are worked. [Para 124][Para 125][Para 126][Para 133] The employer paid only 1,000 riel, so the panel ordered the full 2,000. [Para 134][Para 136][Para 137]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that is not registered, and that cannot show it represents more than half the workers in the enterprise, lacks the standing to bring an interest dispute to the Arbitration Council, and the panel will decline to consider it even where the federation to which it belongs signed the memorandum of understanding — the award in such a case being non-binding. [Para 24][Para 78][Para 83]

Separately, work on a public holiday is not overtime but attracts one day's wage in compensation, whereas work on the weekly rest day is work beyond normal hours and carries the 2,000 riel daily meal allowance under Notification No. 041/11 without regard to the number of hours worked. [Para 113][Para 115][Para 133]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that the skill allowance is an incentive whose reduction is a reasonable managerial choice, and that the union's 54 authorising workers fell far short of the majority required, were not necessary to the orders on demands 1 and 2 once standing and the absence of any legal basis had been found. [Para 81][Para 93][Para 99]

[Para 10][Para 75] The conciliation report is dated 6 December 2013 at paras 12 and 31 but 4 December 2013 at para 17. [Para 133][Para 137] The company's registration number is printed "7012" at para 29 and "Inv. 2991 E/2012" at para 38. [Para 29][Para 38] The order at para 138 gives the start of the overtime period as "04:00 pm" where the rest of the award says 4:00 pm. [Para 45][Para 138] Long passages are duplicated by the OCR at paras 117, 127 and 130, and the signature block at para 143 is reduced to fragments.
