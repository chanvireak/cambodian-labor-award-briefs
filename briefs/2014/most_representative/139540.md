# Award No. 064/14 — Cambo Unisole L.T.D. (Arbitration Council, 23 April 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed, dissenting in part; Kong Phalha, president) · Doc ID 139540

## 1. Facts
Cambo Unisole L.T.D. began garment production in October 2013 and employs about 700 workers. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 63] Fourteen demands went to conciliation; nine were settled at the hearing, issue 13 was withdrawn, and the panel considered only issues 1, 7, 8 and 11. [Para 22][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] Only two workers — Ms M and Ms K — appeared and were treated as the claimants, because the union's authorisation letters were defective. [Para 45][Para 49][Para 54][Para 55]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must raise the daily lunch allowance by USD 6, from USD 4 to USD 10 per month? [Para 56][Para 92]
2. Whether the employer must preserve the entire attendance bonus when a worker takes sick leave supported by a proper medical certificate? [Para 67][Para 109]
3. Whether the employer must pro-rate the attendance bonus by the number of days of authorised personal leave? [Para 76][Para 118]
4. Whether the employer must add USD 4 to the USD 6 child-care allowance paid in lieu of a crèche, making USD 10 per month? [Para 148][Para 152]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Living costs, especially food, outrun the USD 4 allowance, and the February 2014 agreement with the Cambodia Labour Union Federation already lifted it from USD 3 to USD 4, so a further increase is due; sick leave is not laziness and the company should show understanding; the USD 6 child-care payment is too small to pay a child-minder. [Para 57][Para 61][Para 71][Para 88][Para 150]
**Employer (respondent):** No law obliges a lunch allowance, the company has run only five months without profit, and the February agreement's USD 4 is already more favourable than law; a bonus paid for regular attendance cannot survive repeated absence, and equal treatment of absent workers would be unjust; the budget does not permit a child-care increase. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 74][Para 82][Para 91]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all four claims declined, with no payment ordered. [Para 108][Para 117][Para 147][Para 169][Para 171][Para 173]

On issue 1 the panel found no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliging a lunch allowance beyond the USD 4 already paid under the 14 February 2014 agreement, and classified the demand as an interest dispute (ទំនាស់ផលប្រយោជន៍). [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95] Applying Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305, it held that an interest award becomes a collective agreement binding all workers and extinguishing their right to strike on the same demand, so only a most-representative union may bring one — over half the workforce. [Para 96][Para 99][Para 103][Para 104] Two claimants out of 700 fell far short; the panel followed Awards 81/04, 98/04, 169/11, 02/11 and 66/11. [Para 104][Para 105][Para 107]

Issue 7 failed on the same jurisdictional ground: the panel recorded that wages are preserved during certified sick leave but that no rule preserves the bonus, making the claim "more favourable than law" and hence an interest dispute. [Para 113][Para 114][Para 115]

On issue 8 the panel read point 2 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012 as making the bonus conditional on attendance "without absence", a phrase absent from the repealed Notification No. 041. [Para 109][Para 125][Para 129] Authorised unpaid personal leave is not attendance, and the employer's separate administrative power to grant such leave does not imply the bonus survives it; the employer may withhold or pro-rate it. [Para 133][Para 138][Para 139][Para 145] Arbitrator An Non dissented, arguing the earlier line of awards pro-rated the bonus rather than cancelling it, and that the majority departed from that jurisprudence without good reason. [Para 178][Para 179][Para 180]

Issue 11 was again an interest dispute: no legal provision, agreement or practice required more than USD 6, so the panel declined it under the same standing rule. [Para 166][Para 167][Para 169] The panel noted Article 186 of the Labour Law requires a crèche but that the crèche itself was not the subject of this claim. [Para 152][Para 162][Para 163]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that commands only a handful of the enterprise's workers cannot put an interest dispute before the panel, because the resulting award would bind the whole workforce and extinguish the right to strike; the most-representative threshold of more than half the employees is jurisdictional. [Para 104][Para 107] Separately, the attendance bonus under point 2 of Notification No. 230 is earned only by attending every working day of the month without absence, so authorised unpaid personal leave defeats the condition and the employer may pro-rate the bonus accordingly. [Para 129][Para 133][Para 145]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it will follow its own earlier awards save where a new legal basis or good reason exists, and its refusal to follow the pre-2012 line decided under Notification No. 041, exceed what the dismissal required. [Para 126][Para 127] Its remark that Article 186 obliges the employer to provide a crèche, while declining to order one because the crèche was not claimed, is likewise collateral. [Para 162][Para 163]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel records nine settled points but lists only eight (2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12 and 14) [Para 26]. The company's commercial registration is given as "Inv.1252 E/2013" yet dated 20 June 2004 [Para 43]. Two unrelated dates are corrupted to "2023" and "202": the award year in the issue 1 reasoning [Para 92] and the child's age band in issue 11 [Para 148]. The workers' authorisation letter is described as lacking thumbprints and then as rejected because no letter could be "taken as valid" [Para 49][Para 53], and one claimant's name is lost to "NVOA" [Para 53]. The award is dated 23 April 2014 in the header and at the signature [Para 1][Para 181].
