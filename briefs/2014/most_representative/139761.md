# Award No. 313/14 — Asia Dong Run Import Export Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 16 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; Tuon Si Phan, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139761

## 1. Facts
The Federation of Trade Unions for Solidarity of Cambodia filed a collective dispute against Asia Dong Run Import Export Co., Ltd., a garment manufacturer in Trapeang Chheu Neang, Ang Snuol, Kandal, employing about 486 workers. [Para 47][Para 48] Ten demands went to conciliation; three settled there and seven were referred to the Council, of which two (demands 5 and 7) settled at the hearing, leaving five for decision. [Para 22][Para 27] The local union held only a receipt for its registration application of 4 September 2014, not a completed certificate. [Para 50]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Issue 1 — Whether the workers were entitled to a lunch allowance of 2,000 riel per day? [Para 107]
2. Issue 2 — Whether workers hired from September 2014 were entitled to the same benefits as longer-serving workers, namely a 15 USD monthly attendance bonus, a 10 USD monthly travel and accommodation allowance and a 5 USD monthly health allowance? [Para 125]
3. Issue 3 — Whether the employer had to pay annual leave in lieu six months at a time, twice a year, at Khmer New Year and at Pchum Ben? [Para 146]
4. Issue 4 — Whether the employer had to pay the 90 days of maternity allowance, at 50 per cent, in a single sum one day before maternity leave began? [Para 161]
5. Issue 6 — Whether the employer had to deduct union dues from the wages of members of the solidarity union? [Para 175]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** other factories paid a lunch allowance — Gran East 1,500 riel, New Archite 2,000 riel, Vinson 2,000 riel — and the 4,000 riel meal money paid only to overtime workers was no substitute for it. [Para 57][Para 59] All of them were workers of the same company, so old and new hires should receive identical benefits, and benefits once granted could not be withdrawn. [Para 62][Para 64]

**Employer (respondent):** it applied the Ministry of Labour's Prakas rather than matching neighbouring factories, and had reduced benefits to the Prakas minimum for new hires only, keeping the higher rates for those engaged before September 2014 to avoid claims from them. [Para 58][Para 65] It feared that deducting dues where a thumbprint was disputed would provoke complaints and disrupt production. [Para 83]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — issues 1, 2 and 3 refused; issue 4 granted, the employer ordered to pay the full 90 days of maternity allowance one day before leave began; issue 6 granted, the employer ordered to deduct dues on each member's written request. [Para 190][Para 191][Para 192][Para 193][Para 194]

On standing, the panel held that the union filed its registration papers on 4 September 2014 and, more than two months having passed without objection from the Ministry, Article 268 of the Labour Law made it a lawful trade organisation notwithstanding the missing certificate. [Para 88][Para 92][Para 93] Three of the four workers attending had not proved they were union leaders, but the federation held written authorisations from 24 workers, so the panel considered the claims of those 24 plus one attending worker, 25 in all. [Para 100][Para 104][Para 106]

On issue 1, no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliged the employer to pay 2,000 riel a day for lunch; the panel classified the demand as an interest dispute. [Para 108][Para 109] Because the claimants were not a union with majority representativeness in the enterprise, the panel declined to consider it at all. [Para 122][Para 124]

On issue 2, the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law (the employer's right to manage, exercised lawfully and reasonably), Notification 230/12 of 25 July 2012 fixing a 10 USD attendance bonus and 7 USD travel allowance, and Notification 103 of 2013 absorbing the 5 USD health allowance into the base wage. [Para 128][Para 133][Para 139] The employer had therefore met the legal minimum for new hires, and paying more to older workers was a lawful management choice. [Para 141][Para 143]

On issue 3, Article 167(3) voids any payment in lieu of annual leave made while the worker is still employed; such compensation is payable only on termination. The claim was refused. [Para 147][Para 151][Para 160]

On issue 4, Articles 182 and 183 require half wages and allowances across 90 days, Article 103 counts maternity pay as wages, and Article 115(3) requires wages falling due on a rest day to be paid the day before. Paying month by month was therefore unlawful. [Para 162][Para 165][Para 167][Para 172]

On issue 6, Article 129(2) and Prakas 305 require the employer to deduct dues where a member asks in writing, and the union had filed letters and thumbprints for about 400 workers. [Para 176][Para 180][Para 186]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that has lodged registration papers and received no response from the Ministry for two months is a lawful trade organisation for representing its members, but it may bring an interest dispute — a claim to a new benefit such as a lunch allowance — only where it is the most representative union in the enterprise, alone or with others covering more than half the workforce; absent that, the panel will decline to consider the claim whatever its subject matter. [Para 118][Para 123]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that paying annual leave in lieu once a year ran against the spirit of Article 167(3), and that employers should encourage workers to carry leave over to the following year rather than buy it out. [Para 158][Para 159]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name is printed four ways across the award ("អេស៊ា ដុង វុន", "អេស៊ា ដុង រុន", "អេស៊ាន ដុង រុង", "អេង ស៊ា ដុង រុង"); it is treated here as one entity, Asia Dong Run Import Export. [Para 1][Para 22][Para 91] Page markers and year digits have corrupted dates: the hearing-date extension reads "០៥ ខែធ្នូ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ -៣- ២០១៤" and the panel's creation date reads "ឆ្នាំ ២ ២០១៤". [Para 23][Para 30] The maternity provision is cited once as "$07 ថ្ងៃ" and once with a stray "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩" inside the sentence; the operative figure in the reasons and the order is 90 days. [Para 167][Para 168][Para 174] The signature block names a third arbitrator "ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ណា" where the face of the award gives "ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា"; the face of the award has been followed. [Para 1][Para 196] Article 167 is printed throughout as "១៦៧ ក ឋាខ ណ្ខ" (for ខាណ្ឌ, paragraph). Worker names are individuals and are not reproduced here.
