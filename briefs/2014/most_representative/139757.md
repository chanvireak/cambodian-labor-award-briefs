# Award No. 309/14 — Chu Yu In Das Sri Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 December 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouchhun for the employer party, An Virak for the worker party, Pen Bunhea chair) · Doc ID 139757

## 1. Facts
Chu Yu In Das Sri Co Ltd, a shoe factory at Phnom Penh Thmey, Sen Sok, Phnom Penh, employed about 220 workers, roughly 70 per cent of them women, and its in-house union was not registered with the Ministry. [Para 46][Para 48][Para 49][Para 90] Of eighteen demands filed by the Khmer Workers' Union Federation, ten were settled in conciliation on 27 October 2014 and eight came to the Council on 31 October 2014 under report No. 1253; at the hearing of 17 November 2014 the parties agreed issues 5 and 8, leaving six. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 30][Para 31] One hundred and fifty-three workers delegated their rights to the federation by letter of 1 October 2014, and the panel rejected the employer's objection that the thumbprints were unreliable after comparing the original. [Para 51][Para 55][Para 56]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the attendance bonus must be reduced pro rata to the days a worker was absent with the employer's authorisation? [Para 57]
2. Whether the employer must provide enough medicine and a doctor on duty during every working hour? [Para 64]
3. Whether the employer must pay an overtime meal allowance of 3,000 riel for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 71]
4. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 2,000 riel a day? [Para 76]
5. Whether the employer must supply one drinking cup per worker, replaced every six months? [Para 81]
6. Whether the employer must provide a nursery with childminders and adequate toys? [Para 87]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** it is unjust that a worker who works almost the whole month loses the entire USD 10 bonus for two or three days of authorised leave; the factory has had electric shocks and a machine fall on a worker's hand, so a doctor and medicines are needed on site; the base wage has risen but the meal allowances have not while food prices have; and neighbouring factories pay 3,000 riel and 2,000 riel. [Para 63][Para 68][Para 69][Para 72][Para 73][Para 74][Para 79]
**Employer (respondent):** the deduction rule exists to stop workers taking too much leave and disrupting production; the company lacks the budget for a resident doctor, medicines, day-care or higher meal allowances; the nursery has no plan; and it already allows workers to bring their own water bottles. [Para 64][Para 70][Para 75][Para 80][Para 85][Para 86][Para 93]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the attendance bonus to be cut pro rata to authorised absences, ordered a nurse and doctor or assistant doctor present at least two hours each working day plus adequate medicines, and ordered a nursery or reimbursement of outside day-care on invoices; the 3,000 riel overtime meal allowance, the 2,000 riel lunch allowance and the individual six-monthly cup were refused, though the employer was ordered to provide hygienic drinking vessels. [Para 217][Para 218][Para 219][Para 220][Para 222][Para 223]

Issue 1: because the claim turned on the bonus fixed by point 2 of Notification No. 230 of 25 July 2012, the panel classified it as a rights dispute, not an interest dispute. [Para 94][Para 96] The words "regular attendance without absence" were ambiguous and the panel declined to read them as covering every kind of absence; leave the employer has authorised suspends the contract under Articles 71(6) and 72, so no wage is due for those days, but the bonus may only be withheld in proportion to the days absent, not in full. [Para 110][Para 116][Para 135][Para 140][Para 141]

Issue 2: Article 238 requires primary care on site and Article 242 requires a permanent infirmary once an enterprise reaches fifty workers, staffed according to the table in article 3 of Prakas No. 330 of 6 December 2000, which for 50 to 300 workers means a nurse on duty and a doctor present two hours; the panel followed award 62/08 and ordered compliance at the company's actual headcount, with medicines under article 4. [Para 142][Para 144][Para 149][Para 157][Para 158][Para 163]

Issues 3 and 4: the employer already paid 2,000 riel overtime meal allowance, which matched point 2 of Notification No. 041 of 7 March 2011, and no law, collective agreement, internal rule or practice required 3,000 riel or a lunch allowance. [Para 164][Para 167][Para 168][Para 185] Both claims therefore exceeded the law and were interest disputes; under Article 96 and article 9 of Prakas No. 305 only the most representative union may bring such a claim, and the complainants were not that union. [Para 171][Para 176][Para 179][Para 182][Para 190][Para 191]

Issue 5: article 4 of Prakas No. 054 of 10 February 2000 requires the employer to provide cups or another hygienic means, not one cup per worker every six months, so the specific demand was refused but the general obligation was ordered. [Para 194][Para 200][Para 201][Para 204][Para 205]

Issue 6: Article 186 requires an enterprise employing at least one hundred women to set up a nursing room and a nursery, and where a nursery for children over eighteen months cannot be provided the employer must pay for outside care on production of invoices; with 220 workers the obligation applied. [Para 207][Para 208][Para 214][Para 215]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an employer has itself authorised a worker's absence, the contract is suspended for those days and the attendance bonus set by ministerial notification may be reduced in proportion to the days absent but not forfeited altogether. An enterprise of fifty or more workers must maintain a permanent infirmary staffed to the Prakas No. 330 table for its actual headcount, and one employing a hundred or more women must provide a nursery or pay for outside day-care against invoices. A claim for a benefit above the legal minimum is an interest dispute which the panel will not hear from a union that is not the most representative in the enterprise, however many workers have delegated their rights to it. [Para 141][Para 158][Para 179][Para 214]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel said it could not itself supply a meaning for "without absence" in Notification No. 230 because the Labour Advisory Committee had not done so, and each claim must be assessed on its facts. [Para 110][Para 116]
