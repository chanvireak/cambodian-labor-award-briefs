# Award No. 137/14 — Zhu Crown Manufacture (Cambodia) Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 7 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Kong Polla (president) · Doc ID 139586

## 1. Facts
Zhu Crown Manufacture (Cambodia) Ltd., a trouser factory at Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, employed about 1,200 workers. [Para 83][Para 84] A factory union was elected on 01 May 2014 and the federation notified the employer of the three elected leaders on 02 May, the letter being signed for by a security guard. [Para 133][Para 135] Between 24 April and 06 May 2014 the employer ended the contracts of Ms L (president), Ms P (vice-president) and Ms S. [Para 90][Para 109][Para 125] Of 29 demands, 20 were settled, one was withdrawn, and seven remained. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the dismissal of Ms L and the non-renewal of the contracts of Ms P and Ms S were linked to anti-union discrimination, and what each is owed? [Para 169]
2. Whether the employer must stop using three-month fixed-duration contracts and engage all workers on indefinite contracts? [Para 315]
9. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance to 15 US dollars per month? [Para 343]
16. Whether the employer must pay a 3,000 riel overtime meal allowance for two hours of overtime? [Para 351]
19. Whether the employer must pay a 15-dollar monthly health-care allowance? [Para 359]
23. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus to 15 US dollars per month? [Para 373]
25. Whether the employer must pay a 3,000 riel daily midday meal allowance? [Para 380]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the three were union candidates and leaders, so their removal is anti-union discrimination; three-month contracts leave workers insecure and should be replaced by indefinite contracts; allowances should rise because prices have risen. [Para 143][Para 293][Para 303]
**Employer (respondent):** it never received the notice of the election and did not know a union existed, so there was no discrimination; Ms L was dismissed for serious misconduct under Article 83; Ms P and Ms S simply reached the end of their fixed-term contracts; it pays what the law requires — 8 dollars travel, 2,000 riel meal money, 10 dollars attendance bonus — and cannot pay more. [Para 91][Para 145][Para 294][Para 346][Para 374]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to reinstate Ms L; reinstatement was refused for Ms P and Ms S, who instead received 5% termination indemnity, final wages, outstanding annual leave and (for Ms S) damages; the fixed-duration-contract claim and the five allowance claims were refused. [Para 386][Para 387][Para 388][Para 389][Para 390][Para 392][Para 397]

**The protected dismissal.** Article 293 allows the dismissal of a staff representative or candidate only with the labour inspector's agreement, and Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 gives union-election candidates the same protection. [Para 170] The panel held that the 45-day window in that Prakas cuts off only for candidates who lose; for those elected, the protection does not expire at 45 days but continues at least that long and, following Award No. 40/10 Meng Yan, until the union files its registration application. [Para 172][Para 173][Para 175] It applied three conditions — the worker holds protection, the dismissal falls within it, and the union notified the employer — and found each satisfied: Ms L was elected president on 01 May 2014, dismissed on 06 May, and the notice reached a security guard on 02 May. [Para 177][Para 181][Para 186][Para 188] The employer's denial that the guard was its employee was unsupported by any evidence, so the employer had not discharged the burden and the panel presumed receipt. [Para 196][Para 200][Para 203] Because no labour-inspector approval was sought, the dismissal breached Article 293 and reinstatement followed. [Para 207][Para 208][Para 209]

**Discrimination.** On Ms P and Ms S the panel found no proof of anti-union motive: the union had not specified any concrete fact or reason, and the claim failed for want of evidence. [Para 307][Para 308]

**Ms P.** Her last three-month contract ran from 06 February to 05 May 2014 and expired on its own terms, so under Article 73(1) it terminated normally and no labour-inspector approval was needed for non-renewal. [Para 218][Para 220][Para 221][Para 216][Para 217] The panel awarded the 5% termination indemnity under Article 73(6), payment for unused annual leave under Article 166, and final wages under Article 116, but refused notice pay because Article 73(5) requires notice only for contracts of more than six months. [Para 225][Para 231][Para 234][Para 243][Para 246]

**Ms S.** The employer produced only an unsigned "resignation" and no Khmer-language evidence of the dismissal date, and Prakas No. 099 Article 23 requires Khmer; the panel therefore accepted that she was dismissed on 24 April 2014, before her contract's end. [Para 253][Para 254][Para 258] Under Article 73(3) the employer owed damages at least equal to wages to the contract's end, plus the 5% indemnity, annual leave and final wages; her extra 300-dollar damages claim had no legal source and was an interest dispute it refused to hear. [Para 262][Para 266][Para 268][Para 279]

**Fixed-duration contracts.** Article 65 leaves the form of the employment contract to the parties' agreement, and Articles 311, 336 and 337 of the Civil Code require offer and acceptance; neither party may impose a contract type on the other. [Para 315][Para 322][Para 326][Para 337] The claim was refused. [Para 342]

**Allowances.** Each demand exceeded what Notification No. 230 (7 dollars travel, 10 dollars attendance bonus) or Notification No. 041/11 (2,000 riel overtime meal) requires, while the employer already paid 8 dollars, 10 dollars and 2,000 riel; on health care, point 1 of Notification No. 103 had folded the 5-dollar allowance into the minimum wage from 01 May 2013, so nothing further was owed. [Para 344][Para 346][Para 352][Para 366][Para 373][Para 375] The panel treated all five as interest disputes and refused them because the claimants were not a most representative union. [Para 290][Para 291]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An elected union leader or candidate dismissed without the labour inspector's prior agreement acts in breach of Article 293, and reinstatement follows once the three conditions are met — protected status, dismissal within the protected period, and notice to the employer — even where the union notified only by a letter received by a security guard and the employer denies receipt without evidence. [Para 177][Para 203][Para 209]

The protection of Prakas No. 305 Article 3(3) does not lapse at 45 days for a candidate who wins: it continues at least that long and until the union applies for registration. [Para 175]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the choice between fixed-duration and indefinite contracts belongs to the parties' freedom of contract, so a demand that all contracts be converted is not arbitrable as a rights claim. [Para 326][Para 337]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The corporate name appears in Khmer in several OCR variants (ហ្ស៊ូ ក្រោន មេនូហ្វេ ក ឆឺរីង, មេនូហ្វេ ក ធឺរីង, មេនូហ្វាក់ ធើរីង) with no Latin form printed, so the transliteration in the title is provisional. [Para 1][Para 40][Para 83] The award states that 20 of 29 points were settled and one withdrawn, leaving seven, but the list of settled points names twenty-one numbers — including point 28 — and the seven remaining are 1, 2, 9, 16, 19, 23 and 25, so the arithmetic holds only if point 28 is counted twice or one of the 21 is a misprint. [Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] Ms S's final contract is dated 03 February to 02 May 2014 in the document list and described in the reasons as a "30-month" contract, and her dismissal date is 24 April in one passage and 23 April in another. [Para 130][Para 249][Para 250][Para 253] The employer's 5% indemnity is printed once as "50 per cent" and the 300-dollar damages claim as "30 dollars" in the decision text. [Para 260][Para 278][Para 314] OCR has corrupted the operative order: paragraph 388 reads "ខែ- បង្គាប់" where "ខ-" is meant, and paragraph 386 records the award as a "សេចក្តីជូនបង្គាប់" rather than an award. [Para 386][Para 388]
