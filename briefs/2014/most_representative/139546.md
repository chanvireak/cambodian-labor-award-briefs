# Award No. 074/14 — D & L Altimate (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 6 May 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Kong Phalla (president), Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed) · Doc ID 139546

## 1. Facts
D & L Altimate (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. ("D & L") sews garments in Dangkao, Phnom Penh, began operating on 1 December 2012 and leased about 370 workers at the hearing. [Para 1][Para 49][Para 50] The Khmer Conscience Workers' Federation filed ten demands on 20 February 2014; conciliation failed and the file reached the Council on 25 March 2014, with a hearing on 7 April 2014. [Para 17][Para 19][Para 21] Four points settled and point 1 was withdrawn, leaving five: the attendance bonus (3), a midday meal (4), pro-rating the bonus for authorised leave (5), salary-tax withholding (7) and the reinstatement of a dismissed union president (10). [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25] Only one worker remained a claimant. [Para 58]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must add USD 5 to the existing USD 10 monthly attendance bonus, bringing it to USD 15? [Para 60]
2. Whether the employer must provide one midday meal a day, or 4,000 riel a day in lieu? [Para 67]
3. Whether the attendance bonus must be pro-rated to the days of authorised personal absence instead of being forfeited? [Para 72]
4. Whether the employer must stop deducting salary tax from workers' wages? [Para 82]
5. Whether the employer must take back Mr H, elected president of the factory union, into normal work? [Para 92]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Market and rent prices have risen, other factories pay USD 18–20 bonuses and 70–75 per cent provide lunch, the tax deduction further cuts take-home pay, and Mr H was dismissed without any serious misconduct and because of his union role. [Para 62][Para 68][Para 85][Para 101]
**Employer (respondent):** It already pays the USD 10 fixed by Notification 230 and USD 14 in travel and lodging, above what the law requires; it cannot afford lunch after a change of director; withholding tax is a legal obligation; and Mr H left the factory during working hours without approval and refused three written warnings. [Para 64][Para 71][Para 86][Para 104][Para 128]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer is ordered to take Mr H back into normal work; claims 3, 4, 5 and 7 are refused. [Para 236][Para 240]

The panel first narrowed the parties. The delegation letter of 20 February 2014 bore no names or thumbprints in the copy filed, the workers did not produce the original by the 9 April deadline, and their evidence lodged on 10 April was out of time; Mr H, present at the hearing, was therefore the only claimant. [Para 53][Para 55][Para 56][Para 58] On issue 3 the panel found the employer paying USD 10 exactly as Notification 230 requires, and no law, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliging USD 15; the demand was an interests dispute, and since the sole claimant was not a most-representative union it was refused. [Para 129][Para 131][Para 145][Para 147] Issue 4 failed for the same reason, no instrument requiring a meal or 4,000 riel a day. [Para 149][Para 150] On issue 7 the panel held it had no jurisdiction at all: Article 302 requires a dispute between workers and their employer, whereas withholding tax is a duty between the worker and the State, represented by the Ministry of Economy and Finance, so the first condition of a collective labour dispute was not met. [Para 183][Para 186][Para 187][Para 189]

On issue 5 the panel weighed the words "without absence" added by Notification 230 against the earlier Notification 041, which had set only USD 7 and contained no such condition. It reasoned that the 2012 notification did no more than raise the sum from USD 7 to USD 10 and did not attach a new condition, so an authorised personal absence still disqualifies a worker from the bonus; pro-rating was not required, and the company's own practice of keeping the full USD 10 where leave is reasonable and taken no more than two or three times a month was more favourable than the law and lawful. [Para 156][Para 157][Para 179][Para 180]

Issue 10 succeeded. Article 293 forbids dismissing a shop steward or a candidate for that office without the labour inspector's prior agreement, and Article 3(3) of Prakas 305 extends 45-day protection to all union election candidates. [Para 191][Para 194] The panel found all three conditions met: Mr H was elected president on 15 February 2014, the dismissal fell within the protected window, and the federation notified the employer of the successful candidates by letter received on 18 February 2014. [Para 214][Para 220][Para 224] No authorisation was sought from the labour inspector, so under Article 4 of Prakas 305 the dismissal was void and reinstatement followed. [Para 228][Para 233][Para 236]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A dismissal of a worker who has been elected to union office, or who stood as a candidate, is void where the employer did not obtain the labour inspector's prior agreement, and the panel will order reinstatement even though the union has not yet been registered; conversely, a demand for more than the amount fixed by a Ministry notification is an interests dispute that only a most-representative union may bring. [Para 145][Para 228]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that without that protection an elected candidate would face a high risk of anti-union dismissal, contrary to Articles 266, 12 and 279, and noted that Prakas 305 says nothing about dismissing candidates before the union is registered. [Para 207][Para 202]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The dismissal date is given as 7 March 2014 by the employer and 5 March 2014 by the workers; the panel recorded the two-day gap and placed the act 18 to 20 days after the election without choosing a date. [Para 99][Para 100][Para 218] The attendance bonus appears as "១០០០ ដុល្លារ" where USD 10 is meant. [Para 130] Notification 230 is dated 25 July 2012 in the reasons but cited as a 2002 instrument in the employer's account. [Para 64][Para 129] The panel's own account of the delegation letter says it carries no thumbprints, while the record notes the workers' assertion of 59. [Para 53][Para 54] The constitution date is garbled as "២០២២ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៤". [Para 20] Arbitrator Liv Sovanna appended a dissent on issue 5 urging pro-rating. [Para 245]
