# Award No. 134/14 — Nobel Ei Pheril Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 25 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Pen Bunhea (president) · Doc ID 139582

## 1. Facts
Nobel Ei Pheril Ltd., a garment subcontractor at Steung Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employed about 570 workers. [Para 47][Para 48] The Khmer Youth Voice Union, which had held a most-representative certificate from 27 September 2010 valid to 27 September 2012, brought six demands on wages and allowances after conciliation failed on six of seven points. [Para 52][Para 20][Para 21] The employer disputed nothing about the merits and said only that it could not afford the claims; the panel's own inquiry turned on whether the union could bring an interest dispute at all. [Para 3][Para 55]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 2,000 riel per day? [Para 56]
2. Whether the employer must raise the regular attendance bonus from 10 to 15 US dollars per month? [Para 62]
3. Whether the employer must raise the travel and accommodation allowance from 7 to 15 US dollars per month? [Para 69]
4. Whether the employer must raise the payment in lieu of a nursery from 10 to 15 US dollars per month? [Para 74]
5. Whether the employer must pay holiday work at 100% x 300% of the normal daily wage? [Para 135]
6. Whether the employer must add 2,000 riel to the existing 4,000 riel Sunday overtime meal allowance? [Para 146]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** food, rent and transport costs have risen sharply (a plate of food from 500 to 1,000 riel, rent from 25 to 55 dollars, transport from 7 to 10 dollars), other factories pay these benefits, and the law sets a 10-dollar attendance floor that the employer should better. [Para 57][Para 59][Para 71][Para 64]
**Employer (respondent):** it already pays what the law and Prakas require — 10 dollars attendance bonus, 7 dollars travel, 10 dollars in lieu of a nursery, 2,000 riel Sunday meal money and 100% + 100% for holiday work — and cannot afford more, the company being a subcontract sewer with thin margins. [Para 5][Para 7][Para 9][Para 83][Para 60]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all six claims were refused without consideration on the merits ("បដិសេធ ពិចារណា"), because the claimant union lacked standing to bring an interest dispute; the award was binding by the parties' agreement of 06 June 2014. [Para 155][Para 160][Para 161]

The panel treated each demand as an interest dispute (ទិវាទអំពីផលប្រយោជន៍) rather than a rights dispute, because each sought more than the Labour Law, the collective agreement, the internal rules or past practice already secured. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 104][Para 112] For each item it searched the law, the collective agreement of 05 May 2006 and the internal rules approved on 16 February 2004 and found no obligation beyond what was paid, so the demand was "in excess of what the law provides". [Para 32][Para 88][Para 103][Para 111]

On standing, Article 96 defines the collective agreement and Prakas No. 305 (22 November 2001) Article 9 gives the most representative union the right to request one; the panel read those together to hold that only a most representative union may bring an interest dispute, since such an award substitutes for a collective agreement. [Para 91][Para 92][Para 94] Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 (21 April 2004) makes an interest award a substitute collective agreement effective one year from the date it becomes final, and the panel reasoned that this extinguishes the workers' right to strike over those demands — a consequence it would not visit on a workforce at the suit of a minority union. [Para 95][Para 96][Para 97]

Applying that, the panel found the union's certificate of most-representative status had expired on 27 September 2012 and no replacement had been produced, so the union was not most representative despite its roughly 430 members. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55] It followed earlier awards (No. 81/04 Evergreen, No. 98/04 Fre Trade Union, No. 169/11 Fortune) in which interest disputes were refused for the same reason. [Para 97][Para 98] Because standing failed, the panel declined to consider each of the six claims, and did so in identical terms for all six. [Para 100][Para 109][Para 117][Para 134][Para 145][Para 154]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An interest dispute may be arbitrated only at the instance of a union holding current most-representative status: a certificate that has lapsed and not been renewed defeats standing even where the union's membership exceeds half the workforce, and the panel will then refuse the claims without examining their merits. [Para 55][Para 97]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that an interest award operates as a collective agreement for one year and so removes the right to strike on the same demands — an observation about consequences, not part of the standing holding. [Para 95][Para 96]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company's Khmer name is printed three ways — ណូបែល អី ភែរឹល in the header, ណូបែល អី ភែរីល in the document list and ណូបែល អី ភែ អីល in the employer's submissions — and is rendered here as "Nobel Ei Pheril Ltd." on the face of the header only, without preference among the variants. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 60] There is an internal contradiction the panel did not address: the company's statutes were registered on 11 July 2011 but its internal rules are said to have been approved by the labour inspectorate on 16 February 2004, seven years before the company existed. [Para 30][Para 31] The certificate of most-representative status is also dated 27 September 2010 and said to expire 27 September 2012, while a separate document dated 27 September 2010 is described as confirming that status, so the two cannot both be the certificate described. [Para 41][Para 52] Six of seven conciliated points are said to have been unsettled, yet six issues are listed and the seventh is never identified. [Para 20] The OCR lost whole clauses: the Article 164 quotation opens with a bare ellipsis and breaks mid-sentence, the Article 186 text is interrupted by stray tokens ("AS", "ឆ្នាំ"), and the demand as printed at paragraph 80 reads "១០០% x ៣០០%" whereas the employer's actual practice is "១០០% + ១០០%", so the two multipliers do not match anywhere in the document; the signature block is corrupted beyond reading and the presiding arbitrator's name survives only in the header as Pen Bunhea. [Para 135][Para 118][Para 162] Worker and official names are reduced to initials or fragments and are not used here.
