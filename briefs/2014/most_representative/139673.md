# Award No. 212/14 — Huy Chian (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 September 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Tang Tri (president) · Doc ID 139673

## 1. Facts
Huy Chian (Cambodia) Co., Ltd manufactures footwear and employed about 1,250 workers, roughly 90 per cent of them women, when the Khmer Union Federation filed fifteen demands on their behalf. [Para 67][Para 68][Para 86] Six demands were settled at the hearing and demand 10 was merged into demand 13, leaving seven for decision. [Para 27][Para 28][Para 30] The parties had signed a memorandum of understanding on 3 October 2012 on improving professional relations in the garment industry, which made awards on rights disputes binding between them. [Para 31] The company was also about to change its registered name to Chien Li. [Para 206]

## 2. Issues in dispute
3. Whether the employer must add 2 US dollars to the 10-dollar monthly attendance bonus, making 12 dollars a month? [Para 135]
4. Whether the employer must build a nursery (ទារកដ្ឋាន) and breastfeeding room for workers' children? [Para 159]
5. Whether the employer must pay workers who work on public holidays a total of three times the ordinary daily wage? [Para 174]
6. Whether the employer must provide two sets of work clothing every six months? [Para 182]
11. Whether the employer must pay the whole 90 days of maternity allowance in one lump sum before the worker takes maternity leave? [Para 221]
13. Whether the employer may rename the company from Huy Chian to Chien Li, and must pay severance before any renaming? [Para 198]
14. Whether the employer must pay worker representatives at Huy Chian the same support allowance as representatives at the six-storey factory? [Para 237]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The 17 March 2011 company letter fixed attendance bonus at 7 dollars and the 30 December 2011 agreement at 10 dollars, so 12 dollars is owed; holiday work should attract 300 per cent, not the 100 per cent actually paid; clothing has never been supplied; and the 90-day maternity allowance should be paid in one sum. [Para 77][Para 79][Para 91][Para 96][Para 110]
**Employer (respondent):** It already applies the law and lacks capacity because orders are scarce; it pays 10 dollars attendance bonus and 100 per cent for holiday work under Prakas No. 10; it agreed to build a nursery once the union moved its meeting room, having no other site. [Para 83][Para 87][Para 89][Para 176][Para 98]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — two awards were issued. For members of the Khmer Federation, a binding award ordered a nursery (or an outside nursery paid by the employer against actual invoices), at least two sets of work uniform a year, and the 90-day maternity allowance paid in one lump sum before leave; the renaming was permitted with seniority preserved, and the severance demand was not considered. [Para 244][Para 245][Para 246][Para 247][Para 250] For non-members the same terms were issued as a non-binding award, and the interest claims were refused. [Para 256][Para 257][Para 251]

The panel first classified each demand as a rights or an interest dispute. [Para 128][Para 133] On the bonus, it found the applicable instrument was Notification No. 230 Kb/S.J.N. of 25 December 2012, requiring at least 10 dollars a month for full attendance; the employer pays 10 dollars, so nothing more is owed and the extra 2 dollars is an interest claim. [Para 142][Para 143][Para 145][Para 146] It then held that an interest dispute can only be decided where the claimant union is the most representative in the enterprise, which this applicant was not, and refused to consider the claim. [Para 155][Para 156][Para 158] The same reasoning disposed of the 300 per cent holiday pay demand, since Prakas No. 10 of 4 February 1999 guarantees only 100 per cent on top of the holiday wage already received, that is 200 per cent in total, and the employer pays exactly that. [Para 175][Para 177][Para 181] The clothing and representative-allowance claims fell the same way as interest disputes. [Para 186][Para 191][Para 240][Para 243]

On the nursery, article 186 of the Labour Law requires an enterprise with at least 100 women workers to provide a breastfeeding room and a nursery; the panel found the breastfeeding room existed but no nursery had been built, and ordered one, with an outside nursery at the employer's expense where children over 18 months cannot be accommodated. [Para 159][Para 167][Para 171][Para 172] On clothing, Prakas No. 307 Kb/Pr.K of 14 December 2007 requires two sets free of charge at least once a year, and the employer had never supplied any; the demand for two sets every six months was refused but two sets a year were ordered. [Para 182][Para 192][Para 193][Para 197] On maternity, articles 182 and 183 give 90 days at half pay, and article 115(3) read with article 103 requires payment before the leave begins, so monthly instalments were held unlawful and a lump sum was ordered. [Para 224][Para 227][Para 230][Para 236]

On the renaming, article 87 keeps all contracts in being between the new employer and the workforce where the legal status of the employer changes; because Chien Li kept every worker's seniority and benefits, the renaming was lawful and the demand to forbid it failed. [Para 199][Para 209][Para 210] The panel declined jurisdiction over the demand for severance before renaming, as it was a new issue not in the non-conciliation record and not a direct consequence of the recorded dispute. [Para 216][Para 218][Para 219]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An arbitration panel will only decide an interest dispute — a demand for more than the law, a collective agreement, internal regulations or established practice already provides — where the claimant is the most representative union in the enterprise, or unions together representing more than half the workforce; a minority union's interest claim is not considered at all. [Para 155][Para 157]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel explained that an interest award replaces the collective agreement for one year from the date it becomes final, and that this is why workers lose the right to strike over that interest once the panel has ruled on it. [Para 154]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
OCR is badly degraded and several figures are printed twice or inconsistently. The bonus passages give the company practice as "20 10 dollars" and then describe the current bonus as both 18 and 10 dollars while the total is stated as 12. [Para 143][Para 145] The workforce figure appears as "1,250 100" and the number of worker representatives as "1 05" in one place and 105 in another. [Para 71][Para 166] The date the panel was constituted is printed as "06 August 2022 2023 2014". [Para 24] The company name shifts between Huy Chian, Chien Li and several Khmer spellings, sometimes within one paragraph. [Para 201][Para 206] Finally, the award is structured as two separate awards for members and non-members, yet paragraph 267 certifies a single non-binding award, which cannot be reconciled with paragraphs 250 and 256. [Para 250][Para 256][Para 267]
