# Award No. 191/14 — Cambo Kothap Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 14 August 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers, dissenting in part; Kong Phallak, president) · Doc ID 139650

## 1. Facts
Cambo Kothap Co., Ltd is a garment factory on National Road 4 at Kantouk Cheung, Phnom Penh, employing about 2,400 workers. Its plant union, of the Labour Movement Federation, was registered on 4 December 2012 and is not the most representative union in the enterprise; on 4 May 2014 workers signed transfers of rights to the federation for the purpose of this case. The union filed a six-point demand in May 2014; conciliation on 9 July 2014 settled point 6 only, and five points went to the hearing of 28 July 2014. [Para 21][Para 28][Para 48][Para 49][Para 51][Para 52]
The employer holds a registered collective agreement of 21 September 2012 and internal regulations registered on 7 August 2012. [Para 33][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must raise the attendance bonus from USD 13 to USD 23 a month? [Para 54][Para 55]
2. Whether the employer must raise the transport and accommodation allowance from USD 10 to USD 20 a month? [Para 60][Para 61]
3. Whether the employer must raise the midday meal allowance from 2,000 to 4,000 riel a day? [Para 67][Para 126]
4. Whether the employer must raise the overtime meal allowance from 3,200 to 5,200 riel a day for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 73][Para 135]
5. Whether the employer must pro-rate the attendance bonus, rather than deduct it in fixed steps, for authorised absences? [Para 80][Para 155]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** workers live in hardship, rent has risen and market prices have pushed up the cost of food; other factories on National Road 3 pay at least 3,000 riel for the midday meal, and several named factories pro-rate the attendance bonus by days absent; the union asks for USD 10 more on each allowance. [Para 56][Para 62][Para 70][Para 86]
**Employer (respondent):** it applies its collective agreement and Prakas 230/12; it already pays USD 13 attendance bonus against the USD 10 required, USD 10 transport against USD 8 in the agreement and USD 7 in the Prakas, and 3,200 riel overtime meal against 2,000 riel; the bonus is an incentive payable only to workers who attend regularly, so fixed deductions of 3 and 5 dollars are lawful. [Para 57][Para 63][Para 92][Para 97][Para 148]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all five remaining claims were refused, four of them without any consideration on the merits; the award is non-binding and becomes final after 8 days absent written objection to the Minister. [Para 186][Para 187][Para 188][Para 189][Para 190][Para 191]
Points 1, 2 and 3 were each categorised as an interest dispute: no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice obliged the employer to pay more than it already paid, and its current payments exceeded both Prakas 230/12 and its own collective agreement. Because the claimant union was not the most representative in the enterprise, it had no standing to bring interest claims, and the panel refused to consider them. [Para 97][Para 113][Para 118][Para 128]
Point 4 was split. For union members, the panel applied res judicata: the same parties had already litigated the same overtime meal claim in Award No. 102/13 of 19 June 2013, where it was refused on the same facts, and the three conditions for barring re-litigation were met, so the panel declined to decide it again. For the workers who had transferred their rights, the panel reached the merits and found the employer's 3,200 riel payment more favourable than the 2,000 riel required by Prakas 041/11, making the claim an interest demand that the non-representative union could not bring. [Para 136][Para 138][Para 141][Para 146][Para 148][Para 153]
Point 5 was refused on the merits. Prakas 041/11 does not specify how many working days count as "regular", and the panel departed from its earlier line of awards because the later Prakas 230/12 added the words "without absence", which the older awards had not addressed. It held that leave authorised by the employer and payment of the bonus are two separate matters, that attendance is the contractual condition of the bonus, and that the employer's scale — USD 13 for full attendance, USD 9 after one day, USD 7 after two, nothing from three days — was more favourable than the Prakas and therefore lawful. [Para 156][Para 158][Para 162][Para 169][Para 182][Para 183]
Arbitrator An Non dissented on point 5, recording that the majority had departed from the Council's settled jurisprudence and from six prior awards without adequate reason, and that the panel should have ordered the bonus pro-rated by days of authorised absence. [Para 192][Para 197][Para 198]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a later Prakas adds a condition the earlier one lacked — here "without absence" in Prakas 230/12 — the panel may depart from its earlier awards that were decided under the superseded text, and it will treat an employer's paid attendance bonus as an incentive earned only by full attendance, with authorised leave and bonus entitlement being separate questions. A claim that merely seeks more than an employer already pays under a Prakas or collective agreement is an interest dispute that a non-representative union cannot bring. [Para 162][Para 169][Para 113]

**Obiter dicta:** that res judicata is available to the panel even though no provision of the Prakas regulates it, and the observation that the employer's scheme of deducting fixed sums rather than pro-rating by days is more generous than the Prakas requires. [Para 137][Para 183]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel's own list of surviving issues says five points remain but enumerates only four (1, 2, 4 and 5); point 3 was in fact decided at [Para 126], so the enumeration lost a number at [Para 28]. The number of workers who transferred rights is given as 72 at [Para 52] and as 20 at [Para 53], and the two passages conflict. Scrambled years appear in the procedural history ("2023 2014") and in the extension of the award date ("24 14 August") [Para 24][Para 29]. The company's name is OCR-unstable across the file (ខេមបូ ខូថ ប, ខេមបូ ខូច ប, ខេមបូ ខូថប), so the Latin form used above is an inference from the Khmer, not a printed rendering [Para 1][Para 46]. The disposition records the overtime meal claim as "20 riel" where the reasons use 2,000 riel [Para 154]. Several paragraphs are duplicated verbatim, and the signature block is partly replaced by OCR noise [Para 192].
