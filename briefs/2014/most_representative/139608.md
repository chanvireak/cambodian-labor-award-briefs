# Case No. 145/14 — 8 Star Sport Wear (Arbitration Council, 8 July 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Vong Vanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139608

## 1. Facts
The employer is a garment factory in Stung Mean Chey, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, registered in 2006, employing about 900 workers of whom some 95 per cent are women. [Para 45][Para 46][Para 99] The factory union registered with the Ministry on 25 September 2013 and is the complainant, but it is not the most representative union in the enterprise. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 49] Conciliation on 29 May 2014 settled 7 of 15 demands and left 8 unresolved, which reached the panel on 3 June 2014. [Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must convert unused annual leave into cash each year during the employment relationship, calculated on the average of the last 12 months' total wage? [Para 51][Para 168]
2. Whether the overtime rate must be calculated on basic wage plus seniority bonus, role allowance, skill allowance and other incentives? [Para 68][Para 196]
3. Whether the employer must pay 50 per cent of wage and allowances for the whole 90 days of maternity leave before the leave begins? [Para 94][Para 251]
4. Whether the overtime meal allowance must be paid every Saturday instead of every Wednesday? [Para 106][Para 270]
5. Whether the employer must provide one free lunch each working day? [Para 131][Para 300]
6. Whether the transport and accommodation allowance must rise from USD 7 to USD 15 a month? [Para 139][Para 317]
7. Whether the attendance bonus must rise from USD 10 to USD 15 a month? [Para 145][Para 325]
8. Whether the employer must stop deducting annual leave, and must maintain wages, for the day a pregnant worker attends an antenatal check? [Para 151][Para 333]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** paying out unused leave at the basic wage short-changes workers, since "wage" includes skill pay and other benefits and a leave day is the same as a working day; overtime should therefore be computed on the full wage; staggered maternity payments cause hardship because most workers give birth in the provinces; 2,000 riel no longer buys a meal and other factories such as Meng Da Footwear provide one; rents, transport and fuel have risen; and deducting annual leave for antenatal checks punishes a legal obligation. [Para 56][Para 60][Para 72][Para 101][Para 132][Para 141][Para 153]
**Employer (respondent):** it has always used the basic wage for these calculations, no law requires the 12-month average, it has no means to provide meals or higher allowances and asks to apply only what the law requires, and the Wednesday meal payment already complies with award No. 223/12. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 73][Para 137][Para 144][Para 148][Para 287]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to include the seniority bonus in the overtime base, to pay 50 per cent of wage and allowances across the 90 days of maternity leave before the leave starts, to pay the overtime meal allowance every Saturday, and to stop deducting annual leave for antenatal check days; the other claims were dismissed or not considered. No money sum was fixed. [Para 361][Para 364][Para 365][Para 366][Para 362][Para 363][Para 368][Para 371]
On the first issue the panel applied Articles 166, 167(3) and 168 of the Labour Law: Article 167(3) voids any agreement converting annual leave into cash while the worker is still employed, and Article 168's 12-month average governs only the payment made when the contract ends, so the claim was refused and the employer was encouraged instead to carry leave forward. [Para 169][Para 171][Para 184][Para 185][Para 195]
On the overtime base, Articles 139 (as amended) and 144 and Paragraph 5 of Prakas No. 80 leave the term "wage at normal working time" undefined. [Para 198][Para 203][Para 205] Reading Articles 102 and 103, the panel held that normal-time wage means the wage strictly earned for the work done, excluding benefits. [Para 206][Para 210] Applying item 3 of Notification No. 041/11 and its reasoning in award No. 255/13 (Pou Yien), it held the seniority bonus is earned in return for experience and loyalty and so joins the base. [Para 211][Para 213][Para 214][Para 220] Role allowance, skill allowance and unspecified incentives failed for want of evidence, the burden lying on the party asserting them. [Para 232][Para 238][Para 240][Para 250]
On maternity pay, Articles 182 and 183 require half pay including allowances for 90 days, Article 115(3) requires payment before a rest day, and Article 103 counts maternity pay as wages, so the employer's staged payment was unlawful and the full 50 per cent was ordered in advance. [Para 252][Para 255][Para 257][Para 260][Para 264][Para 269]
On the meal allowance, item 2 of Notification No. 041/11 allows 2,000 riel or one free meal without fixing the payment day; since the workers wanted Saturday for weekend needs and the employer can compute Wednesday-to-Wednesday while keeping three days to prepare, Saturday was ordered. [Para 271][Para 277][Para 293][Para 297][Para 299]
The panel classified issues 5, 6, 7 and 8(b) as disputes over interests, because no law, collective agreement, internal rule or practice creates them. [Para 301][Para 321][Para 329][Para 356] Under Article 312(2) and Paragraph 43 of Prakas No. 099, an interest award replaces the collective agreement for a year and removes the right to strike, so only the most representative union may bring such a claim; this union was not, and the panel declined jurisdiction. [Para 309][Para 310][Para 311][Para 315][Para 316][Para 324][Para 332] On issue 8(a), Article 170 requires agreement for annual leave timing, and the deduction rested on a deal with a different, non-representative union that was never produced, so the practice was ordered stopped. [Para 334][Para 341][Para 343][Para 347][Para 348]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Because Article 167(3) voids any conversion of annual leave into money while the worker is still employed, a claim for yearly cash payment of unused leave fails, and the 12-month average in Article 168 applies only on termination. [Para 185][Para 195]
The seniority bonus is part of the wage at normal working time for computing overtime, while role and skill allowances are not, absent proof they were actually paid. [Para 214][Para 240]
Half pay for maternity leave must be paid in full before the leave begins, since Article 115(3) bars payment on a rest day. [Para 257][Para 264]
An interest dispute may be decided only in favour of a most representative union, because the resulting award displaces the collective agreement and removes the right to strike. [Para 310][Para 311]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that 80 per cent of factories pay the overtime meal allowance on Saturday, naming Younient Mel, Lucky Sika and Kat, which informed but did not decide the timing order. [Para 115][Para 116]

**Dissenting opinion:** arbitrator Ing Sothy, appointed by the employer, dissented on issue 2, holding that including the seniority bonus in the overtime base has no legal foundation and rests only on equity, and that if the base is enlarged it should equally enlarge the base for wage deductions. [Para 375][Para 379][Para 380]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is followed by the annexed dissent signed the same day, and the header labels the document a dissenting opinion by Ing Sothy before reciting the award. [Para 1][Para 373][Para 381] The number of unresolved points is stated as 08 but the recital also reads "មិន សះ ជា ចំនួន ០៨ ចំនួន ០៨", a duplication. [Para 23] The panel is said to have been constituted on "03 June 2023 / 2019 / 2014". [Para 24] Notification No. 230/12 is dated 25 July 2012 in one place and 25 December 2012 in another, and its attendance bonus appears as both 10 and 15 dollars. [Para 317][Para 325][Para 326] The employer's name is OCR'd variously as 8 Star Sport Wear, 8 Star Sport War and 8 Star Stout Wear. [Para 1][Para 313][Para 314]
