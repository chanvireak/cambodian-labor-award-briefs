# Award No. 123/14 — Chevron (Cambodia) Limited (Arbitration Council, 12 June 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Sambona, appointed by the employer; An Nan, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalha, chair appointed by both) · Doc ID 139577

## 1. Facts
Chevron (Cambodia) Limited supplies and distributes petroleum products, was registered on 03 August 1995 and employs about 530 workers at Phnom Penh Centre. [Para 44][Para 45] Conciliation of five demands settled two and left three, of which the surviving two concern an annual staff party and a thirteenth-month bonus. [Para 14][Para 15] The wage demand was dropped at the hearing after the company agreed to raise pay by USD 20 a month from June 2014 and not to deduct May 2014 wages for the strike period. [Para 57][Para 82] At that hearing both sides recorded identical positions: keep the existing "Eagles' Night" programme and the existing VBP incentive scheme as they are. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 65][Para 66]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must organise a party for all workers every year? [Para 48]
2. Whether the employer must pay a thirteenth-month bonus at least equal to the last month's basic wage? [Para 52]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company had held such a party in the past and should continue every year; the party and incentive scheme currently in force should simply be maintained. [Para 2][Para 48][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent):** the company runs an incentive party only for workers who perform well, and asks that the present programmes — Eagles' Night and the VBP project incentive — be kept as they are. [Para 3][Para 48][Para 52]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the workers abandoned both surviving demands at the hearing and the panel ordered each party to perform the positions recorded in the hearing minutes of 03 June 2014: Eagles' Night retained, the VBP incentive retained, the USD 20 monthly raise from June 2014, and no deduction of May 2014 wages. [Para 51][Para 55][Para 75][Para 76][Para 82]

The panel began with its own jurisdiction. Article 312(1) of the Labour Law confines it to the matters stated in the non-conciliation minutes or arising after them as a direct consequence of the dispute, and Article 33 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 says the same. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60] It therefore took only the two issues listed in report No. 509. [Para 70]

On the party, the hearing minutes of 03 June 2014 show the employer asking to retain Eagles' Night and the workers agreeing to retain it; the workers stated expressly that they no longer demanded an annual party for all staff. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 51][Para 62][Para 63][Para 64] Because the word "Eagles' Night" is foreign, the panel adopted the Khmer rendering "កម្មវិធី អ៊ីហ្គល ណៃ ធ៍" for the rest of the award. [Para 50]

On the thirteenth-month bonus the same alignment appears: the employer asked to keep the current VBP incentive, the workers agreed, and the workers withdrew the demand for a bonus at least equal to the last month's basic wage. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 55][Para 65][Para 66][Para 67] The panel again substituted the Khmer "គម្រោង វីប៊ី ភី" for "VBP". [Para 54]

Having found the parties in the same position on both issues, the panel held the dispute resolved by the record rather than by adjudication. [Para 68][Para 70][Para 71] It relied on Article 5 of the Civil Code 2007, which requires rights to be exercised and obligations performed in good faith and honesty, and concluded that the parties must honour the positions they signed. [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74] Its order was accordingly that each party perform its recorded position. [Para 75][Para 76]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the parties record the same position in the hearing minutes and the claimant abandons the demand, the panel does not adjudicate the merits: the dispute is treated as settled by consent and the panel orders each party to perform the position it signed, under Article 5 of the Civil Code. [Para 70][Para 73][Para 75] Jurisdiction is in any event fixed by the non-conciliation minutes, so a demand not listed there, or not arising directly from the dispute, cannot be brought before the panel by later agreement of the parties. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 76]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's substitution of Khmer terms for "Eagles' Night" and "VBP" is a translation convention for the award's own text, not a finding about either scheme. [Para 50][Para 54] Its remark that an employer may run an incentive party for high-performing workers only was recorded from the employer's position and was not decided. [Para 3]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The conciliation report is numbered 509 and dated 21 May 2014 in the procedural section [Para 10][Para 15][Para 39] but 20 May 2014 where the ministry's copy is listed [Para 39]; the conciliation minutes are dated 20 May 2014 in one list [Para 26] and 21 May 2014 in another [Para 40].
- The dispute summary is dated both 03 June 2014 and 27 May 2014 within a single line [Para 30].
- The panel's constitution reads "23 May 2023 2014" [Para 16]; the Civil Code article 5 quotation is garbled in the original ("ការអនុវត្ត ករណី យកិច្ច") [Para 72].
- The company is transliterated four different ways in Khmer across the evidence list, and one transfer letter gives authority to "លោក ហេង សុវណ្ណ" although the header names her as the (female) human-resources director [Para 1][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25][Para 44].
- Counsel appears as "Tip Janvibol" in the attendance record and as ទិព្វ ច័ន្ទ វិបុល in the header [Para 1][Para 78]; the minimum wage figure carries a corrupted "ហុកសិបន្ត" for sixty [Para 6].
