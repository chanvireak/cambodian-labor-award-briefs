# Award No. 253/14 — Total Cambodia (Arbitration Council, 21 October 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom, An Non, chair Ang Eng Thong) · Doc ID 139714

## 1. Facts
Total Cambodia, a fuel storage and distribution company registered in 1997 with about 140 staff, was the sole shareholder of CAMDIS Co., Ltd, a service company created in 2000 that manages Total's seven filling stations for a fee and has its own clients and management. [Para 105] [Para 106] [Para 135] [Para 250] [Para 252] On 26 August 2014 staff representatives gave notice of a peaceful strike over nine demands; conciliation on 2 September 2014 failed on five, which reached the panel by report No. 1037 of 5 September 2014, with hearings on 17 September and 3 October 2014. [Para 21] [Para 22] [Para 23] The parties consolidated demands 1, 4 and 5 into one issue and demands 2 and 3 into another. [Para 28] [Para 175] [Para 190] Of the 23 claimants only one, Mr M, held a contract with Total Cambodia; seven were former Total staff now with CAMDIS and the rest were CAMDIS employees. [Para 116] [Para 119] [Para 120]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the Arbitration Council has jurisdiction at all, the employer contending that the dispute is an individual one and not a collective labour dispute under Article 302 of the Labour Law? [Para 216] [Para 224]
2. Whether the employer must implement point 1 of the agreement of 03 April 2014, answer at law for all drivers and fuel attendants, and stop the abusive practice of placing staff under the CAMDIS subcontractor so that seniority and benefits are lost (demands 1, 4 and 5, consolidated)? [Para 175] [Para 15]
3. Whether the employer must stop terminating long-serving, skilled workers without valid reason and reinstate Mr M with back pay from the date of dismissal (demands 2 and 3, consolidated)? [Para 190] [Para 8]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that Total Cambodia and CAMDIS are one company, since Total Cambodia is CAMDIS's sole shareholder and CAMDIS's managers were Total staff paid by Total, so the April agreement must apply equally; that staff wear Total uniforms and carry cards bearing both names, that no work stopped on transfer, and that CAMDIS benefits are lower — no 13th-month salary, no evaluation bonus, no pension, medical cover of US$120 against US$200 to US$360. [Para 156] [Para 159] [Para 174] [Para 181] [Para 183] [Para 188]
**Employer (respondent):** that the two are separate legal entities registered separately, CAMDIS being a service subsidiary with its own management that bills Total and other clients; that the transfers were signed and severance paid; that Mr M's post was abolished in a 2014 restructuring and no suitable alternative existed; and that these are individual disputes for the individual procedure. [Para 127] [Para 133] [Para 149] [Para 196] [Para 207] [Para 209] [Para 10]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to consider all five demands, holding they were not a collective labour dispute; no sum ordered. [Para 261] [Para 262] [Para 263]

The panel raised jurisdiction on its own motion and noted that it ordinarily respects the labour inspector's or Minister's classification of a dispute unless there is a clear reason to depart, following awards No. 10/03, 41/04, 07/05, 45/07, 13/08 and 14/11. [Para 216] [Para 217] [Para 218] [Para 221] Article 302 requires three conditions, the first being a dispute between several workers and one or more employers. [Para 224] [Para 225] [Para 226] On the evidence that condition failed: of the 23 claimants only Mr M was a Total Cambodia employee, the seven transferred staff had become CAMDIS employees, and the remainder had never worked for Total. [Para 186] [Para 236] [Para 246] [Para 247] [Para 259] Because the first condition was unmet the panel did not need to reach the second and third. [Para 260]

The panel found the employer of each worker to be the entity with which that worker contracted, reading Articles 1, 2 and 3 of the Labour Law — the relationship arises from the labour contract and is identified by who recruits, pays wages, fixes hours and disciplines — and following award No. 53/07 (Ega Men). [Para 228] [Para 229] [Para 235] It accepted that Total Cambodia and CAMDIS were distinct legal persons registered separately with the Ministry of Commerce, that CAMDIS was a wholly owned subsidiary with its own authority over hiring, wages and dismissal, and that Total had not placed its own staff under CAMDIS. [Para 248] [Para 250] [Para 152] [Para 188] On the 2001 transfers it held that a challenge brought in 2014 to consent given more than ten years earlier was unreasonable, and that the workers' real remedy had been to protest at the time; one transferred worker's own evidence was that his pay and benefits had not fallen. [Para 239] [Para 240] [Para 243] [Para 245] The panel also held it had no jurisdiction to decide whether creating CAMDIS was bad faith, since Article 309 limits it to labour disputes. [Para 256] [Para 258]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where several workers claim against a parent company but only one of them holds a contract with it, the first condition of Article 302 is not satisfied and the panel has no jurisdiction over the claim as a collective dispute; the employer of a worker is the entity that contracted with, paid, directed and disciplined that worker, so staff of a wholly owned service subsidiary are not employees of the parent however close the commercial and managerial links, and the panel will not reopen a transfer to such a subsidiary that went unchallenged for over a decade. [Para 229] [Para 247] [Para 259]

**Obiter dicta:** that a worker challenging a transfer of employer must protest when it happens, delay of more than ten years making the claim of defective consent unreasonable; and that the lawfulness or good faith of incorporating a registered company is outside the panel's competence. [Para 240] [Para 258]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel is recorded as constituted on "08 September 2023 2014". [Para 24] The award says Total Cambodia and CAMDIS were registered with different ministries and later that both were registered with the Ministry of Commerce. [Para 248] [Para 254] The claimant group is not stable: transfer letters carry 7, 6, 5 and 23 thumbprints, the panel noted duplicated names, and the employer's list of 23 included 7 former staff. [Para 109] [Para 110] [Para 112] [Para 114] The agreement is dated "03 December April 2014" in the issue list and 03 April 2014 elsewhere. [Para 2] [Para 176] The release date was postponed from 01 to 17 October and then to 21 October, the last matching the header. [Para 30] Article 302 is quoted with the employer count garbled as "one or 20 more", and the three conditions are numbered 1, 2 and 4. [Para 224] [Para 226] Exhibit numbering repeats item 3 and jumps from 62 to 66. [Para 84] [Para 95] The parent is printed as Total Cambodia, TOTAL CAMBODGE and Totale; CAMDIS appears five ways. [Para 129] Party representatives and individual workers are reduced to initials per the briefing convention.
