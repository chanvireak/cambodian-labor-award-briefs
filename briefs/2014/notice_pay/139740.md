# Award No. 276/14 — DKSH (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 21 November 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun for the employer party, An Vireak for the worker party, Tang Tris chair) · Doc ID 139740

## 1. Facts
DKSH (Cambodia) Co Ltd distributes pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, food, consumer products and machinery at Monivong Boulevard, Chamkar Mon, Phnom Penh, and employs approximately 1,058 workers. [Para 65][Para 66] The local union at DKSH had not yet obtained registration from the Ministry of Labour, so the Cambodian Labour and Services Workers' Federation received a transfer of rights from 67 of its employees by letter dated 29 July 2014. [Para 67][Para 68] Mr N, a warehouse employee since 1997 earning an average of USD 204 a month, was dismissed in January 2014 for an alleged serious offence involving loss of company property; he did not sign a transfer of rights but attended the hearing, making 68 claimants in all. [Para 70][Para 71][Para 78][Para 79][Para 80] The parties chose a non-binding award. [Para 28]

## 2. Issues in dispute
6. Whether the employer must pay Mr N compensation in lieu of notice, dismissal damages, compensation for unused annual leave, together with wages and medical expenses, under Articles 75, 89, 91, 166 and 167 of the Labour Law? [Para 72][Para 86]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the allegation of theft has no clear basis, appears nowhere in the company's internal rules, and the charge under Article 83 is not specific; Mr N worked for the company for more than 16 years and had received a gold-hand award, so the dismissal was unlawful and the statutory compensation is owed. [Para 82][Para 84][Para 85]
**Employer (respondent):** the dismissal rests on Article 83 of the Labour Law for a serious offence concerning loss of company property, supported by Mr N's own confession report; the last month's wage and the savings payment were already made, and the dispute is an individual one already being processed by the Ministry, not a collective dispute. [Para 72][Para 81][Para 83][Para 93]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction — the panel declined to consider the demand for Mr N's notice pay, dismissal damages, annual-leave and medical compensation. [Para 106][Para 107]

The panel framed the question as one of jurisdiction, since the Ministry's referral does not bind it. [Para 86] It noted that, in principle, the labour inspector and the Minister decide whether a dispute is individual or collective before referring it, and that the panel generally respects that classification unless there is a clear objection; where a party objects, that party — here the employer — bears the burden of proving its contention. [Para 87][Para 91][Para 92]

It then took Article 302 of the Labour Law, which defines a collective labour dispute as one between one or more employers and some of their workers over working conditions, the exercise of rights granted to a professional organisation, recognition of such an organisation, or relations between employer and workers, and which may make the enterprise operate poorly or harm social peace. [Para 94] Reading that definition as three cumulative conditions, the panel tested each. [Para 95][Para 96]

The first condition was satisfied: the demand is brought by 67 employees who transferred their rights plus Mr N against their employer. [Para 97][Para 98] The second was satisfied too, because the claim concerns working conditions — compensation in lieu of notice, dismissal damages and annual-leave payment. [Para 99][Para 100] The third failed: both parties accepted that the dismissal did not obstruct the company's operations, and the panel found no evidence that the dispute had harmed social peace. [Para 102][Para 103][Para 104] Since a single missing condition is fatal, the panel concluded the dispute is not a collective labour dispute and declined to consider it, leaving Mr N to the individual dispute procedure. [Para 105][Para 106]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A compensation claim brought on behalf of a group of employees is a collective labour dispute only if all three conditions of Article 302 of the Labour Law are met at once: a plurality of workers against one or more employers, a subject matter of working conditions or organisational rights, and a real capacity to disrupt the enterprise or harm social peace. Where both parties agree the dismissal did not obstruct operations and no evidence of harm to social peace is produced, the third condition fails however many workers join the claim, and the panel has no jurisdiction over it. [Para 96][Para 102][Para 104][Para 105]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it will generally defer to the labour inspector's and the Minister's classification of a dispute as individual or collective, absent clear objection, is a general practice note rather than a ground of the order. [Para 87][Para 91]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The claim is pleaded under Articles "74, 89, 91, 166 and 167" of the Labour Law in the statement of issues [Para 12] but under Articles 75, 89, 91, 166 and 167 in the reasons and the order [Para 72][Para 86]; the first article number cannot be reconciled.
- The company's registration is given as "Co.1067 E/2001" dated 15 January 2014 [Para 65], an E/2001 number carrying a 2014 date, while the certificate in evidence is No. 0392 dated 15 January 2014 [Para 33].
- The summons letters are dated 30 October 2014 [Para 63][Para 64], thirteen days after the hearing they convened, which was held on 17 October 2014 [Para 18]; the panel is said to have been constituted on "03 October 2019 2014" [Para 22].
- The arithmetic of the issues does not close: eight issues were conciliated, two resolved, six referred [Para 20]; two more (issues 2 and 3) were settled at the hearing and issues 1 and 4 withdrawn [Para 25][Para 26], which leaves two, yet only issue 6 is considered [Para 27]. One unresolved issue is dropped without explanation.
- Names of the three arbitrators appear only in Khmer script and are transliterated here; the signature block is illegible ("upponive 2014 / AL/", "Su Ammiheaks", "2017") [Para 109]. Several sentences are duplicated verbatim [Para 3][Para 36][Para 55][Para 73].
