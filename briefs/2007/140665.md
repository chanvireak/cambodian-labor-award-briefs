# Award No. 126/07 — Heng Yu Knitting Factory Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 19 November 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry, An Nan, Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140665

## 1. Facts
Members of the Free Trade Union of Workers of Kingdom of Cambodia (FTUWKC) at Heng Yu Factory, a Phnom Penh plant of 1,200 workers, demanded that the company dismiss the team leader of the sewing unit because she had sworn at team members. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 7]
The employer, Heng Yu Knitting Factory Co., Ltd., refused, saying the conduct alleged did not amount to serious misconduct and that dismissal was the company's right. [Para 4]
Conciliation on 8 November 2007 failed and the dispute reached the panel, but the union refused to select an arbitrator (one was drawn by lot on 12 November 2007) and the workers went on strike. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 9]
The panel ordered the strike stopped on 12 November 2007; the strike continued, and the union told the Secretariat it did not want the panel to resolve the dispute. [Para 13][Para 14]
The worker party was absent from the hearing of 15 November 2007. [Para 13][Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must dismiss the sewing unit team leader for swearing at her team members, as the union demanded? [Para 3][Para 4]
2. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration when the referring worker party is on strike and has refused to attend? [Para 16][Para 20]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** FTUWKC members at Heng Yu Factory demanded the dismissal of the team leader of the sewing unit on the ground that she had sworn at her team members, and by their absence and continued strike indicated they no longer wanted the panel to decide the dispute. [Para 3][Para 14]
**Employer (respondent):** Heng Yu Knitting Factory Co., Ltd. declined to dismiss her, denying serious misconduct and asserting that dismissal is the company's own right. [Para 4]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel discontinued the arbitration process in case 126/07 Heng Yu without ruling on the dismissal demand. [Para 21]
Clause 20 of Prakas 099 dated 21 April 2004 requires the parties to abstain from strikes or lockouts and to attend all meetings to which the panel calls them. [Para 15][Para 16]
The panel read that clause, as it had before, to bar it from continuing the process while the worker party is striking. [Para 16][Para 20]
It relied on Arbitral Award 04/04 (MSI), where a notification of 29 January 2004 held the Council cannot resolve or issue an award on a collective dispute while a party is on strike, and on the second order in Franco Knitting Garment of 24 December 2005. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19]
It also cited cases 28/06 (GDM), 34/06 (Gold Fame) and Arbitral Award 97/07 (South Bay), in which awards closed the file because the worker parties kept striking. [Para 19]
On the facts it found nothing showing the union wanted its dispute settled, so it stopped the process rather than deciding the merits. [Para 19][Para 20]
The panel had earlier issued Order No. 015 dated 12 November 2007 directing the workers to stop the strike and return on 13 November 2007, which was not obeyed. [Para 12][Para 13]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the referring worker party keeps striking during the arbitration and does not attend, Clause 20 of Prakas 099 obliges the panel to discontinue the process instead of issuing an award on the merits. [Para 15][Para 20][Para 21]
The panel may take the dispute up again only once the strike ends or the employer asks it to continue. [Para 18]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that it acted to comply with the law and to stay consistent with its earlier rulings, rather than for any reason touching the merits of the dismissal demand. [Para 20]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award's fact section is printed as "FACT: N/A", so the facts in §1 are drawn from the procedural history and the non-conciliation report rather than from a findings section. [Para 3][Para 13]
Two dates in the procedural recital read "12 November 2008" and "14 November 2008" where the surrounding text and the 19 November 2007 award date require 2007; both readings are reported as printed. [Para 13]
This is an unofficial English translation of the Khmer original with dropped "th" throughout ("themployer", "thearing", "the"); the worker party's address is printed "N/A" and its representatives are recorded only as "Absent". [Para 2][Para 9][Para 13]
The individual the union sought to have dismissed is a worker and is referred to by initial only; the source prints her full name. [Para 3]
