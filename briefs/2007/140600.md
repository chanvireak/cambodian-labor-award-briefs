# Award No. 30/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឌីយូណាយធីត នីត ធីង អេម អេហ្វជ្ជី (ខេមបូឌា) (Arbitration Council, 20 April 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ចង់ for the employer party, អន ណន for the worker party, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140600

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឌីយូណាយធីត នីត ធីង អេម អេហ្វជ្ជី (ខេមបូឌា), a weaving factory at Village 05, Sangkat 04, Mittapheap, Sihanoukville, employing about 929 workers, ran out of buyers' orders and suspended the employment contracts, asking the Sihanoukville Department of Labour and Vocational Training for permission on 06 February and again on 26 February 2007. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 28][Para 59] The parties agreed at the hearing that the suspension ran from 01 February to 19 March 2007, excluding 15–16 March, which were strike days. [Para 20][Para 50] Workers organised in សហជីពសេរីកម្មករនៃព្រះរាជាណាច្រកកម្ពុជា and សហជីពសេរីកម្មករកម្ពុជានៃអង្គចលនឌីយូណាយធីត នីត ធីង demanded 50% of wages for February and the following months, relying on point 6 of a collective agreement of 04 November 2004. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 23] Conciliation settled two of three demands and referred the remaining one to the panel. [Para 9][Para 10]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the suspension of the employment contracts was in accordance with the Labour Law, and whether the employer must pay 50% of wages while it had no work for the workers from 01 February 2007 onwards? [Para 52][Para 19][Para 75]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីពសេរីកម្មករនៃព្រះរាជាណាច្រកកម្ពុជា and សហជីពសេរីកម្មករកម្ពុជានៃអង្គចលនឌីយូណាយធីត នីត ធីង:** point 6 of the 04 November 2004 collective agreement promises that where the company has no work for the workers it must pay 50%, and in the past 50% was paid even for stoppages of ten days, twenty days, a month and two months. [Para 23][Para 25][Para 51]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឌីយូណាយធីត នីត ធីង អេម អេហ្វជ្ជី (ខេមបូឌា):** the 50% promise covers only short stoppages of 01 to 10 days, and where the factory has no work for longer than 10 days no wage is owed; the plant faced genuine hardship with no orders and no income. [Para 22][Para 24][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to pay 50% of wages, as claimed, to the workers whose contracts it suspended from 01 February to 19 March 2007, excluding 15–16 March 2007, which were strike days. [Para 77][Para 76]

The panel confirmed the company did face serious economic difficulty: it had no buyers, no work for the workers and no income, which is the ground Article 71(11) recognises for suspending an enterprise's activity for no more than two months. [Para 52][Para 59][Para 60][Para 62] The suspension of 01 February to 19 March 2007 fell inside that ceiling. [Para 61][Para 62] Article 71(11) also requires the suspension to be placed under the supervision of the labour inspector, which the panel read as meaning the inspector must, after inspecting the factory, give or withhold agreement to the request. [Para 53][Para 54] Inspectors of the Sihanoukville Department visited on 07 February 2007 and their report, signed by the head and deputy head of the labour disputes office and the company director, contains no point granting that agreement — its points 11, 12 and 13 only record the documents inspected and advise the company to chase customers. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 45][Para 64][Para 65][Para 66] An employer representative argued that under the office's practice, no reply after an inspection means the suspension is agreed, but the panel refused to build a legal conclusion on an oral assertion and required documentary proof. [Para 44][Para 68][Para 69][Para 70] It therefore held the suspension never received the agreement of the Sihanoukville Department and was not lawful under the Labour Law, following awards 22/05 and 46/05. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 67][Para 71] The panel then restated its earlier position that a lawful suspension relieves the employer of wages while an unlawful one requires full wages, citing award 31/07 (Park Yoo). [Para 72][Para 73] Noting that its jurisdiction arises only where a dispute and a demand exist, it confined itself to the 50% claimed and ordered that sum for the suspension period. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 76]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A suspension of employment contracts under Article 71(11) of the Labour Law is complete only when the labour inspector, after inspecting the enterprise, agrees to it; the inspector's silence, or a local office practice of treating silence as consent, cannot substitute for that agreement, and the employer bears the consequences of failing to obtain it in writing. [Para 54][Para 64][Para 70][Para 71]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that a lawful suspension releases the employer from paying wages whereas an unlawful one obliges it to pay in full does not decide this case, because the panel went on to award the 50% that was claimed, not 100%. [Para 72][Para 76]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The same non-conciliation report, No. 068, is dated 19 March 2007 in the procedural section but 05 October 2006 in the list of documents received from the Ministry; both dates are reported as printed. [Para 6][Para 10][Para 15]
- The reasons state the two-month ceiling is "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៧", a year that does not exist in this case; the suspension is elsewhere fixed as 2007. [Para 62][Para 61]
- The reasoning at [Para 72] says an unlawful suspension requires full wages and cites award 31/07, yet the operative order at [Para 76] awards only the 50% claimed; the brief reports both as printed rather than reconciling them.
- The section on the inspection report is duplicative, points 11–13 and their advice being set out twice in different numbering; and the letter of 06 February 2007 appears both as requesting suspension to 26 February and to 25 February. [Para 28][Para 31][Para 45]
- Individual names of the departmental officials and of the employer's representative survive in the source; they are described by office rather than reproduced here, and the worker-party arbitrator's name is printed as អន ណន in the header but អន មាន at the signature block. [Para 1][Para 79]
