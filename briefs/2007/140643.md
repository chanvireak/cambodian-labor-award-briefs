# Award No. 97/07 — Suit Way & La Rissaca Fashion Factory Limited (Arbitration Council, 21 September 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · Ing Sothy (employer-chosen), Ann Vireak (worker-chosen), Kong Phallack (chair) · Doc ID 140643

## 1. Facts
Suit Way & La Rissaca Fashion Factory Limited, Sangkat Toul Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, employed 1,050 workers [Para 1][Para 30]. The Federation Union of Development Workers Rights (FUDWR) at Suit Way Garment brought four demands through non-conciliation report No. 996 of 14 September 2007: reinstatement of dismissed FUDWR members, implementation of the agreement of 26 June 2007 signed at the Arbitration Council in case 60/07 and of the related award, dismissal of two named administrative staff, and registration of the worker delegate election minutes [Para 23][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 30]. On 14 September 2007 the Secretariat learned the workers were on strike, and the panel issued Order No. 009 that day requiring a return to work on 15 September 2007 [Para 30][Para 31][Para 38]. The workers stayed out until the afternoon of 20 September 2007, and the company refused to admit the 12 dismissed workers [Para 32].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration of the four non-conciliated demands where the worker party remains on strike and has asked that the case be closed for resolution at the court? [Para 34][Para 38][Para 44]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, FUDWR at Suit Way Garment):** the company discriminated against the union and the 12 dismissed members must be reinstated; the 26 June 2007 agreement and the award in case 60/07 must be implemented; the two administrative staff who ignored that agreement and dismissed workers without valid reason must themselves be dismissed; and the election minutes must be registered because the Labour Inspector witnessed the election [Para 23][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]. They would not return to work while the 12 were excluded, and asked the panel to close the case [Para 32][Para 34].
**Employer (respondent, Suit Way & La Rissaca Fashion Factory Limited):** there was no union discrimination and any misconduct is handled under the Internal Work Rules and the Labour Law; the agreement and award were already implemented; it would follow the Labour Law on the dismissal demand; and the Administration Department knew nothing of the election [Para 24][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]. It would admit all workers except the 12 dismissed ones [Para 33].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel ordered the arbitration process in case 97/07 Suit Way discontinued while the workers continue their strike [Para 44][Para 45].
Clause 20 of Prakas 099/04 requires the parties to abstain from strikes or lockouts, as defined in Article 318 of the Labour Law, and from any action aggravating the situation, and to attend all meetings [Para 38]. The panel read that clause, with Award 04/04 MSI and its notification of 29 January 2004, Award 18/04 Cambodiana and the Raffles, Sunway, Intercontinental and Sofitel Angkor hotels order of 22 April 2004, Award 77/05 Franco Knitting Garment, and Awards 28/06 GDM, 34/06 Gold Fame, 50/06 Genuine, 59/06 Sang Woo and 22/07 Park View, as barring it from proceeding while a strike continues [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]. It warned the parties repeatedly at the pre-hearing meeting of 21 September 2007 that arbitration could not proceed unless the strike stopped, and the worker party maintained both the strike and its request that the panel close the case [Para 33][Para 35][Para 38][Para 43]. No evidence was tendered by either side, the employer or the worker party marking none [Para 37].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party to a collective dispute continues a strike or lockout during the arbitration process, in breach of Clause 20 of Prakas 099/04, the panel will not continue to hear the case and will discontinue the arbitration process until the strike or lockout ends [Para 38][Para 44]. A party cannot ask the panel to decide a dispute and simultaneously withdraw it to the court while maintaining industrial action [Para 43][Para 44].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that it may resolve the collective dispute again once the union and workers stop the strike, following the MSI notification [Para 39].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Unofficial English translation only; no Khmer original survives in the file, so the Khmer names of the parties cannot be checked [Para 1]. The document is a termination order, not a merits award: no findings on any of the four demands appear, and no evidence was filed [Para 37]. The date of the return-to-work order is printed as Order No. 009 dated 14 September 2006 in the evidence list and as 14 September 2007 in the procedural account [Para 31][Para 38]. The employer's name is given as Suit Way & La Rissaca Fashion Factory Limited in the title and as Suit Way Company or Suit Way Garment in the body. OCR drops initial letters throughout, and the worker party's representative list runs to eighteen names across broken lines, several of them union assistants whose roles carry no distinguishing detail.
