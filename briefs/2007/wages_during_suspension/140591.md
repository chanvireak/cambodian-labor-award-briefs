# Award No. 22/07 — Park View Cambodia Garment Factory (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផាក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី) v Free Workers' Union, Khmer Youth Union and National Union of Textile Workers (Arbitration Council, 13 March 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Eng Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 140591

## 1. Facts
Park View Cambodia Garment Factory (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផាក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី) employs 660 workers at Trea, Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, and three unions appear for the workers: the Independent and Democratic Union, the Khmer Youth Union and the National Union of Textile Workers. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 10]
The workers demanded that the company terminate their contracts and pay the statutory compensation because they had lost faith in the company, leaving any rehiring to its discretion; the company answered that it had no policy of dismissing anyone and that all existing benefits were preserved. [Para 3][Para 4]
The case reached the Secretariat on 27 February 2007 while a strike was running, and the panel ordered everyone back to work on 28 February 2007; it did not happen, and the company then shut the factory from 9 March 2007 and applied for a two-month suspension of work that the ministry had not yet authorised. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may continue the arbitration while the workers keep striking and the employer keeps the factory closed during the proceedings? [Para 26]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Independent and Democratic Union, Khmer Youth Union and National Union of Textile Workers / សហជីពឯករាជ្យ និង ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ, សហជីពយុវជនខ្មែរ, សហជីពជាតិឧស្សាហកម្មវាយនភ័ណ្ឌកាត់ដេរកម្ពុជា): they wanted the contracts ended with statutory compensation because they had lost faith in the company, and they said they came to work but the company gave them too little work to do. [Para 3][Para 15][Para 20]
**Employer (respondent, Park View Cambodia Garment Factory / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ផាក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី):** it had arranged work but the workers refused to do it, it had no policy of dismissing anyone, and it closed the gate from 9 March because no orders were coming in. [Para 4][Para 16][Para 22][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to continue the arbitration in case 22/07 and closed it without deciding the demand. [Para 40]
The panel relied on Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend every contentious measure — strike, lockout or anything aggravating the dispute — for the duration of the proceedings, and to attend all sittings. [Para 29][Para 30]
At the pre-hearing meetings of 2 and 12 March 2007 the panel told both sides repeatedly that it could act only if the strike ended and no lockout was maintained, but the strike continued and the company shut the factory. [Para 28][Para 26]
It followed its own earlier rulings — Award No. 04/04 (MSI), Award No. 18/04 (Cambodiana and other hotels), Award No. 77/05 (Franco Nithing Garment), Award No. 28/06 (GDM), Award No. 34/06 (Gold Dream) and Award No. 59/06 (Sang Vo) — all of which closed the file for the same reason. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 34][Para 36][Para 37]
Finding no sign that either side wanted the dispute resolved by arbitration, the panel held that both had disregarded its procedure and terminated the case. [Para 38][Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The panel will not arbitrate a collective dispute while a strike or lockout continues during the proceedings; suspension of such measures is a precondition to jurisdiction, and a party that keeps striking, or an employer that keeps its gate shut, loses the arbitration without any decision on the merits. [Para 29][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel framed non-compliance as a matter of the parties' own intent to have the dispute arbitrated rather than as a sanction. [Para 38]
