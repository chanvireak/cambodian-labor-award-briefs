# Award No. 104/07 — Global Apparel (ក្លូបល អាប៉ា រ៉ែល) (Arbitration Council, 24 October 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, An Nan, Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140649

## 1. Facts
The employer is ក្រុមហ៊ុន ក្លូប ល អាបាំ រែលី មីធីត, the factory known in the case name as ក្លូបល អាប៉ា រ៉ែល (Global Apparel), at Trapeang Toul, Kampoul, Ang Snuol, Kandal, employing about 2,600 workers. [Para 1][Para 19]
The claimants are សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ និង ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ, សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព ខ្មែរ; a fourth union, សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព, was an original claimant but did not appear at the hearing. [Para 1][Para 20][Para 21]
Conciliation failed and non-conciliation report No. 198/07 of 27 September 2007 was sent to the Secretariat on 1 October 2007; a strike followed, which the panel ordered stopped by order No. 010 of 2 October 2007, and the hearing took place on 6 October 2007. [Para 7][Para 13][Para 14][Para 9]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (ចំណុច ១) Whether the company must pay an additional 100 per cent of wages to workers who did not take leave between February and April 2007, because it paid 100 per cent to workers whom it suspended during those months? [Para 23]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ និង ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ, សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព ខ្មែរ:** the company paid 100 per cent of wages to suspended workers for February–April 2007, so those who kept working should receive the same in addition; a 5 June 2006 agreement obliged the company to pay 100 per cent when there was no work, and many workers signed the suspension list without understanding it. [Para 23][Para 32][Para 33]
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ក្លូប ល អាបាំ រែលី មីធីត:** about 790 suspended workers received 50 per cent wage, 50 per cent attendance bonus and 100 per cent seniority pay, as notified to them and as permitted by point 1 of Award 74/07 of 30 August 2007. [Para 25][Para 28][Para 33]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel rejected the demand that the company pay a further 100 per cent of wages to workers who did not take leave between February and April 2007; the award was non-binding and would become final eight days after notification absent written opposition. [Para 39][Para 40]
Both parties agreed at the hearing that the employer had already paid the workers who worked in February–April 2007 in full and in accordance with the law. [Para 34]
The panel found no provision of the Labour Law granting a further 100 per cent in these circumstances. [Para 35]
The worker party itself confirmed that no internal work rules, collective agreement or other agreement conferred such a benefit on the workers. [Para 36]
The panel therefore held that the workers had no right to an additional 100 per cent, and rejected the demand. [Para 37][Para 38]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A claim to a wage supplement that is found in neither the Labour Law nor the enterprise's internal rules, a collective agreement or an individual agreement fails, even where the employer has granted more generous terms to a different group of workers; parity with that group creates no entitlement. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified; the panel reasoned only to the single demand before it. [Para 35]
