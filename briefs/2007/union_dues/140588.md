# Award No. 16/07 — Loutous Tech Spy Garment (Cambodia) (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឡូទូសតិ៍ តិច ស្ពាយ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)) v Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) (Arbitration Council, 28 March 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, employer-appointed; Tuon Si Phan, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 140588

## 1. Facts
Loutous Tech Spy Garment (Cambodia) (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឡូទូសតិ៍ តិច ស្ពាយ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)) operates on National Road 4 at Kantok, Ang Snuol, Kandal, and the claimant before the panel was the Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ). [Para 1]
Of eleven demands conciliated at the Kandal provincial labour office on 23 January 2007, three were settled and eight were referred: playing music in the cutting section, keeping the attendance bonus for three days' lawful absence, annual instead of three-monthly contracts, keeping role and incentive pay during absence, leniency for lost company property, a USD 1 allowance for two hours' overtime, three card swipes a day, and a 1,000 riel union dues deduction. [Para 20][Para 21][Para 2][Para 12][Para 15]
Two hearings were fixed, on 24 February and 17 March 2007; the company attended both, the workers' party attended neither, and all its representatives had taken leave. [Para 20][Para 28][Para 41][Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel must close the file and terminate the arbitration where the claimant party was properly summoned, did not appear, and gave no valid reason for its absence? [Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Khmer Youth Union Federation / សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ):** advanced no position at either hearing; its eight demands stand as recorded in the non-conciliation report and were never argued before the panel. [Para 23][Para 2]
**Employer (respondent, Loutous Tech Spy Garment (Cambodia) / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឡូទូសតិ៍ តិច ស្ពាយ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)):** attended both hearings, said it had itself tried to bring the workers' representatives to the hearing, and asked the panel to close the file. [Para 40][Para 43]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case 16/07 without reaching the merits. [Para 63]
The panel applied Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules, which allow proceedings to continue or to be closed by order where a party neither appears nor sends a representative, provided the party was duly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45]
It followed Award No. 30/05 (Moria) in treating three cumulative conditions as necessary: proper notification, non-appearance, and absence of any valid reason. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48]
Each was found satisfied — the Secretariat issued formal summons to both parties by letter and confirmed by telephone, the claimants did not appear and sent no representative, and they offered no reason to the Secretariat. [Para 50][Para 52][Para 57][Para 59]
Article 19 and Article 20 of the same Prakas, requiring parties to attend all sittings, were also cited, and the panel closed the file on that basis. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel may close a case by order instead of deciding the merits only where all three conditions are cumulatively met: the party was duly notified of the date, time and place, it neither appeared nor sent an authorised representative, and it gave no valid reason for the absence. [Para 46][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified. [Para 61]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is issued on 28 March 2007 although the parties agreed at para 31 to postpone issuance to 2 April 2007. [Para 31] The case name in the header uses a different spelling of the company from the name printed for the respondent in the body. [Para 1] The claimant's address is given twice in conflicting forms, once as the Phnom Penh Center office and once as a house on Street 265 in Toul Kork. [Para 1][Para 2] Paragraphs 44, 45, 49 and 59 are heavily corrupted by OCR, and the text of rule 4.7 had to be read across two fragments. [Para 44][Para 49] Individual union officials appear under initials.
