# Award No. 25/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជា ក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី (Cha K VU Cambodia Garment Factory) (Arbitration Council, 3 April 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom for the employer party, Tuon Siphan for the worker party, Nhan Somunthend chair) · Doc ID 140596

## 1. Facts
Cha K VU Cambodia Garment Factory (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជា ក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី), Tra village, Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, employs more than 600 workers. [Para 1][Para 17]
On 04 January 2007 the Khmer Youth Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ) and its union at the factory complained that the company should deduct 1,000 riel in union dues from each union member and hand it to the Khmer Youth Union. [Para 2][Para 9] The employer answered that the company could not make the deduction because it has too few office staff to do the work. [Para 3]
Conciliation on 31 January 2007 failed on this single point, the file reached the panel on 12 March 2007 and the hearing was set for 23 March 2007. [Para 6][Para 10][Para 11][Para 8] The employer attended; the worker party sent no representative, having told the secretariat shortly before the hearing opened that the workers would not come, and the union never asked for an adjournment. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 20][Para 23] The panel therefore never examined the dues demand on its merits. [Para 31][Para 32]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must deduct 1,000 riel in union dues from each union member and pay the amount to the Khmer Youth Union? [Para 2][Para 9]
2. Whether the panel should close the case when the claimant union was properly summoned, sent no representative and gave no valid reason for its absence? [Para 25][Para 28][Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ and its factory union:** the company should check off 1,000 riel per member as union dues and pay it over to the union; the union then refused to attend the hearing and gave no reason to the panel. [Para 2][Para 14][Para 24]
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជា ក វីយូ ខេមបូឌា ហ្គា មិន ហ្វាក តូរី:** it cannot make the deduction because it has insufficient office staff for the task; it attended the hearing through its general manager, interpreter and secretary. [Para 1][Para 3][Para 13]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case No. 25/07 (Park Vyu) and treated the union's demand as abandoned, making no order on the 1,000 riel dues. [Para 31][Para 32]

The panel set out Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, under which a disputing party may appear in person, with an accompanying person, through an avocat, or through another person holding written authority, and found the union had done none of these. [Para 22][Para 24] It then applied Article 21 of the same Prakas, which allows the panel either to continue in the absence of a party properly summoned and without valid excuse, or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an award, together with Rule 4.7 of the procedure annexed to Prakas No. 099. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]
The panel found that both parties had been properly notified of the date, time and place of the hearing, and that the secretariat had even telephoned to remind them. [Para 28][Para 21] Only the employer appeared. [Para 29] Following Awards 30/05 (Mo Ria) and 16/07 (Lo Tou Sot), the panel held that where the claimant worker party neither attends nor gives a valid reason for its absence, the file is closed, and it inferred that the union had abandoned its claim. [Para 30][Para 31]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been properly notified of the hearing, sends no representative and offers no valid excuse for its absence, the panel may close the file and treat the demand as abandoned under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 and Rule 4.7 of its annexed procedure, without examining the merits of the claim. [Para 25][Para 30][Para 31]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that the secretariat telephoned the parties to remind them of the hearing time supports the finding of proper notification but is not itself a condition of the rule. [Para 21][Para 28]
