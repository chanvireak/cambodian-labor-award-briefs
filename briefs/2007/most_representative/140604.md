# Award No. 37/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេ អរ ប៊ី អាកសិន (Arbitration Council, 23 May 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី for the employer party, ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់ for the worker party, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140604

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេ អរ ប៊ី អាកសិន, a factory at Sngoun Vathana Building C, Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, with 905 workers and two unions, neither of them most representative, put every new worker on a three-month probation contract, then a six-month contract, and paid only 50% of wages when orders or materials ran short. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 31][Para 33][Para 71] Workers organised in សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ and សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ filed eight demands in January and February 2007; conciliation on 19 March 2007 settled two, and six came to the panel. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21] Issue 2, a demand to post the payroll table three days before payday, was dismissed because no union at the factory is most representative. [Para 117][Para 119][Para 197]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether sewing, cutting, trimming, ironing and quality-control workers who have served more than two months of probation must be made permanent? [Para 92][Para 196]
2. (Issue 3) Whether section heads and line managers must receive and annotate leave applications they refuse? [Para 121][Para 198]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the employer must pay 100% of wages for short stoppages and repay the 50% withheld in the past? [Para 70][Para 199]
4. (Issue 5) Whether past annual leave taken during stoppages must be restored? [Para 163][Para 201]
5. (Issue 6) Whether 50% maternity pay for three months must be paid in advance? [Para 178][Para 202]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ and សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព-សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** probation should run two months for skilled sections; section heads sometimes refuse even to look at a leave application or annotate it; the 13 October 2006 agreement paying 50% during stoppages is worse than the Labour Law; and maternity money is needed before the birth because of medical and care costs. [Para 35][Para 63][Para 75][Para 89]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ជេ អរ ប៊ី អាកសិន:** every worker is tested for three months, the payroll table cannot be posted early for security reasons, and maternity pay is released monthly rather than in advance because a lump sum risks losing skilled workers who do not return. [Para 3][Para 45][Para 90]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must make permanent the skilled sewing, cutting, trimming, ironing and quality-control workers who completed two months of probation, order its supervisors to receive and annotate leave applications, pay 50% back pay for three years of irregular stoppages, and pay 50% of wages and allowances for three months before maternity leave. [Para 196][Para 198][Para 199][Para 202]

On issue 1 the panel found Article 68 of the Labour Law fixes three ceilings — three months for employees, two for specialised workers, one for ordinary workers — so a uniform three-month probation for all staff does not comply, and sewing staff are specialised workers limited to two months. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 97][Para 100][Para 105] On issue 4 it held the 13 October 2006 agreement cannot stand because it gives less than the law, since the employer never asked the labour inspector to approve the stoppages as required by Article 71(11), so Article 13(1) strikes the agreement down and 100% is owed. [Para 71][Para 147][Para 153][Para 154][Para 159] Recovery was then limited by Article 120, which bars wage claims after three years from the due date, so the panel ordered the missing 50% for the three years preceding the demand. [Para 160][Para 162][Para 200] On issue 3 it found supervisors failed to perform their role by refusing to read or annotate applications, which denied workers access to the director's decision. [Para 135][Para 136][Para 140][Para 142] On issue 5 it held that workers who accepted the company's leave schedule without objection agreed to it, and that leave coinciding with stoppage days cost them nothing because they were paid in full for those days. [Para 170][Para 175][Para 176] On issue 6 it applied Articles 182 and 183 and ordered the 50% for three months to be paid before the leave begins, since the employer's fear of losing staff is not a legal reason to withhold it. [Para 182][Para 183][Para 192][Para 195]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective agreement that pays only half wages during a work stoppage the employer never submitted to the labour inspector is void under Article 13(1) of the Labour Law as less favourable than the law, and the employer must pay the full wage for that irregular suspension; the arrears recoverable are those of the three years before the demand, by operation of Article 120. [Para 147][Para 159][Para 160][Para 162]

**Obiter dicta:** the employer's concern that women paid maternity money in advance will not return to work may be true or false, but it is not a legal ground for escaping a statutory duty. [Para 192]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Article 120 is quoted with the limitation period printed as "បី ឆ្នាំ ក្រោយ ២០១៥", a year that has no place in a 2007 award, and Article 115 is misprinted as "មាត្រា ១៩១០". [Para 160][Para 189]
- The employer's name is printed ជេ អរ ប៊ី អាកសិន in the header, ជេ អរ ប៊ី អាក សិន in the findings, and "AKP" once. [Para 1][Para 29][Para 30]
- One union is described as "សហជីព យុវជន ២០១៥ ខ្មែរ" in the procedural section, while the header names only two federations. [Para 19][Para 2]
- The signature block records no name for the worker-party arbitrator and only "អ៊ុក" for the employer-party arbitrator, whose full name appears at the header. [Para 1][Para 203]
- Page markers and stray characters ("-២០-", "-២១-", "AS", ".5") interrupt the reasoning, and the operative paragraphs on issue 4 run across a page break with duplicated words. [Para 191][Para 199]
