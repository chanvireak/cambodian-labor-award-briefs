# Award No. 08/07 — Sauv Kinh (IM FY) Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ាវ គីញ (អិម អិហ្វ យី) អិលធីឌី) v Khmer Youth Union of Sauv Kinh Factory (សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊ាវ គីញ) (Arbitration Council, 20 February 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirem, employer-appointed; Ven Pov, worker-appointed; Pang Tri, president) · Doc ID 140587

## 1. Facts
Sauv Kinh (IM FY) Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ាវ គីញ (អិម អិហ្វ យី) អិលធីឌី) employs 580 workers at Kva village, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, and no union there has yet acquired most-representative status, including the Khmer Youth Union of Sauv Kinh Factory (សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊ាវ គីញ). [Para 30][Para 31]
The workers filed a ten-point demand on 16 January 2007; the last conciliation on 17 January 2007 settled three points and left seven, which came to the panel. [Para 21][Para 22]
One point — the request for a nursing room and nursery — was raised only at the hearing, and several of the remaining demands asked for conditions better than the law requires rather than for rights it grants. [Para 175][Para 139]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must pay out unused annual leave in January each year, and keep daily wages and the attendance bonus while a worker takes annual leave? [Para 32]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay 100 per cent of wages while there is no work for a short period? [Para 36]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the attendance bonus must be kept where a worker is absent three days a month? [Para 42]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the attendance bonus must be kept where a worker arrives fifteen minutes late? [Para 125]
5. (Issue 5) Whether wages and the attendance bonus must be kept for a pregnant worker taking one day a month for a pregnancy check? [Para 133]
6. (Issue 6) Whether maternity leave of 90 days must exclude Sundays and public holidays? [Para 142]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the company must give one hour a day for breastfeeding to a woman who has given birth? [Para 155]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, Khmer Youth Union of Sauv Kinh Factory / សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊ាវ គីញ):** the company should pay annual leave in January and keep wages and bonus when leave is used, pay full wages during stoppages, keep the bonus despite lawful absences and late arrival, and grant the pregnancy, maternity and breastfeeding benefits claimed. [Para 51][Para 40][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent, Sauv Kinh (IM FY) Ltd / ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ាវ គីញ (អិម អិហ្វ យី) អិលធីឌី):** it pays leave in April and does not keep the bonus during leave, pays only 50 per cent during stoppages, applies the law on attendance bonuses, and had already supplied tinned milk in place of a nursery under the 2005 agreement. [Para 52][Para 80][Para 8][Para 161]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel refused payment of leave in January but ordered the company to pay the full attendance bonus while annual leave is used, ordered full wages during short stoppages where the company fails its Article 71 duties, dismissed issues 3 to 6 and ordered one hour a day for breastfeeding together with a nursing room and nursery. [Para 185][Para 186][Para 187][Para 190]
On leave, the panel held under Articles 166 and 167 and Article 13 that leave may be paid in lieu only when the contract ends, so the company's April payout was lawful; the bonus is different, since Article 168 requires wages including the attendance bonus while leave is taken. [Para 64][Para 74][Para 75]
On stoppages it read Article 71(11) with Article 72: suspension of a section's work for economic reasons is lawful, but the employer is freed from paying wages only if it has notified and obtained the labour inspector's prior authorisation, which the company admitted it never did. [Para 86][Para 91][Para 94]
Issues 3 to 6 were dismissed because the demands exceeded what the law grants and, being interest disputes, could only be brought by a union with most-representative status under Article 277 and Prakas No. 305; this union lacked it. [Para 112][Para 119][Para 123]
On issue 7 the panel applied Articles 184, 185 and 186 as public-order rules: the 2005 agreement giving tinned milk was no substitute for the hour, the room and the nursery, so the company must provide them even if it stops the milk. [Para 170][Para 182][Para 190]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that suspends part of its workforce for economic reasons and pays half wages must pay the full wage for the whole period unless it notified the labour inspector and obtained prior authorisation under Article 71; and a benefit bought in place of a statutory breastfeeding entitlement, however agreed, does not discharge the employer's duty. [Para 94][Para 182]

**Obiter dicta:** demands that merely exceed the statutory floor are interest disputes, not rights disputes, and cannot be arbitrated at the instance of a union without most-representative status. [Para 117][Para 123]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 1][Para 19] The presiding arbitrator's name is spelled differently in the signature block than in the header.
