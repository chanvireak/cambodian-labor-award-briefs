# Award No. 122/07 — Genuine Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 21 December 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Sin Kim Sean, Ang Eng Thong chairing) · Doc ID 140658

## 1. Facts
Genuine Garment (Cambodia) Co., Ltd, a Kandal Province factory of about 1,000 workers, faced a collective dispute filed by the Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (KYFTU) and the Khmer Youth Trade Union (KYTU) at the Factory, which had 573 members and was registered on 13 July 2007 [Para 18][Para 4]. Six demands were conciliated down to four, and at the hearing Issues 1 and 3 were withdrawn, leaving Issue 2 (an extra US$10 for a month of overtime) and Issue 4 (a seniority bonus of US$10 at nine years) [Para 14][Para 16]. Both parties agreed the single union in the factory lacked most-representative status [Para 18].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay an additional US$10 a month on top of overtime wages and meal allowance when it requires a month of overtime? [Para 18]
2. (Issue 4) Whether the company must raise the seniority bonus to US$10 for workers who have completed nine years? [Para 19]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the extra US$10 was warranted because prices had risen sharply and workers had always cooperated with overtime requests; the seniority bonus should reach US$10 at nine years for the same reason, the company having so far paid only the Notification 017 scale of US$2 to US$5 [Para 18][Para 19][Para 21].
**Employer (respondent):** it already paid an incentive bonus of 900 to 1,000 riels a day plus a 1,000-riel meal allowance, and it applied Notification 017 in full, promising to follow any new notification [Para 18][Para 19].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to consider both demands, the first because the union is not the most representative and the claim is an interest demand beyond the law, the second because no worker has yet completed nine years [Para 24][Para 34].

On Issue 2, Point 4 of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000 fixes meal allowance for voluntary overtime at 1,000 riels a day or one free meal, and the company was paying both that allowance and overtime wages [Para 24]. The demand therefore exceeded what the law provides, and the panel follows its practice of declining interest disputes brought by a union without most-representative status, that status being the basis for collective bargaining under Article 96(2B) and Article 9(1) of Prakas 305 [Para 24][Para 25]. Registration and the other conditions in Article 277 were not met, following Awards United Art, 89/04 and 109/04-Great Fortune [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28].

On Issue 4, Clause 3 of Notification 745 of 23 October 2006 preserves the benefits in Points 3, 5 and 6 of Notification 017, whose Clause 5 caps the seniority bonus at US$5 a month after four years [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31]. The company had paid that scale, and only 138 workers had more than four years' service while none had nine [Para 23][Para 32]. The demand looked to a future event, and the panel does not decide disputes that have not yet arisen, following Awards 10/03-Jacqsintex, 14/06 and 42/07 [Para 33][Para 34].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand for more than a notification or the Labour Law already secures is an interest demand, and the panel will decline it where the claimant union lacks most-representative status under Article 277 and Prakas 305 [Para 24][Para 26].

A claim contingent on service that no worker has yet completed is not a present dispute, so the panel declines it rather than deciding it on the merits [Para 33][Para 34].

**Obiter dicta:** both parties informed the panel that the factory's only union did not hold most-representative status, a point the panel recorded without deciding it [Para 18].

## 6. Source defects disclosed

Several passages are duplicated by OCR — the phrase on consistency with previous rulings appears twice in one sentence, and the list of awards at Issue 4 is printed twice [Para 28][Para 34].
