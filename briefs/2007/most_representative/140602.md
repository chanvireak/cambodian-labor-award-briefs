# Award No. 33/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើទ្រាយ ស៊ីស នីត ធើរលី មីតធីត (Arbitration Council, 30 April 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី for the employer party, អន ណន for the worker party, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140602

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើទ្រាយ ស៊ីស នីត ធើរលី មីតធីត, a factory at Kampong Pring, Setbo, Saang, Kandal, operating since 1997 with about 5,000 workers and four unions, none of them most representative, faced a 16-point demand lodged on 06 March 2007 by workers organised in សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រហ្គោល ហ្វេម. [Para 1][Para 41][Para 43][Para 44] Conciliation on 22 March 2007 settled three points, thirteen went to the panel, and point 9 was settled again at the hearing, leaving twelve. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 37][Para 38] Points 2, 8, 11, 12 and 13 were then not considered, on representativity, on absence of specific evidence, on the union's lack of a written mandate, and because they concerned future transfers. [Para 145][Para 146][Para 164][Para 279][Para 301][Para 313]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer may deduct the whole regularity bonus for a single day's authorised absence, rather than USD 1? [Para 45][Para 128]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must resume thumbprinting workers out at the gate? [Para 147][Para 160]
3. (Issue 4) Whether fixed-duration contracts must be converted into contracts of undetermined duration? [Para 162][Para 167]
4. (Issue 5) Whether past annual leave must be paid back, and whether annual leave rises by one day every three years? [Para 168][Para 306]
5. (Issue 6) Whether workers already earning USD 50 or more must receive a further USD 5? [Para 212][Para 218]
6. (Issue 7) Whether maternity pay is 50% of average wages including overtime? [Para 219][Para 233]
7. (Issue 10) Whether pay slips must be issued to monthly-paid workers as well? [Para 248][Para 268]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រហ្គោល ហ្វេម:** a one-day authorised absence should cost only USD 1 of the regularity bonus, seniority pay should rise USD 1 a year after four years, the company should thumbprint workers out, and maternity pay should be 50% of the last twelve months' wages including overtime. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 6][Para 220]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើទ្រាយ ស៊ីស នីត ធើរលី មីតធីត:** the regularity bonus rewards attendance and is not a penalty; self-thumbprinting is a buyer requirement; Article 166 gives 18 days of leave; the minimum wage notice guarantees only USD 50 a month and does not raise everyone by USD 5. [Para 7][Para 120][Para 122][Para 169][Para 215]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must deduct only USD 1 of the regularity bonus for one authorised day's absence, give one extra day of annual leave for every three years of service, pay maternity benefit of 50% of average wages including overtime for three months, and issue pay slips to all workers; the thumbprinting, contract-conversion, USD 5 raise and back-leave claims were dismissed. [Para 302][Para 307][Para 309][Para 311][Para 304]

On issue 1 the panel held that notice No. 745 of 23 October 2006 keeps the benefit of a regularity bonus of at least USD 5, which rewards a full month's attendance and penalises only unjustified absence, so an absence authorised by the employer cannot forfeit it and only USD 1 may be deducted. [Para 119][Para 122][Para 124][Para 128] On issue 3 it applied Article 2 of the Labour Law, which gives the employer the right to manage and direct the enterprise within law and reason, and found the thumbprint queue of 3 to 5 minutes for 120 to 150 workers per machine not excessive. [Para 150][Para 154][Para 157][Para 158][Para 160] On issue 4 the union produced no names, contract types or seniority, so the claim failed for want of evidence. [Para 163][Para 164][Para 167] On issue 5 it held Article 166(4) raises annual leave by one working day per three years of service, reading "seniority" consistently with Articles 73, 75, 89 and 95, so that an entitlement once acquired cannot fall back, but past leave could not be reconstructed without names and seniority. [Para 179][Para 184][Para 196][Para 199][Para 210][Para 173][Para 175] On issue 6 it found notice No. 745 guarantees a floor of USD 50 and does not entitle those already above it to a USD 5 increase. [Para 215][Para 216][Para 218] On issue 7 it held Article 183 gives 50% of wage and allowances, and Article 103 counts overtime as wage, so the last twelve months' total is divided by twelve and by two, then multiplied by three. [Para 223][Para 228][Para 230][Para 231] On issue 10 it found Article 112(b) requires clear notice of wages whenever they change, that a pay slip is the practical means, and ordered slips for all. [Para 251][Para 258][Para 268]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The seniority-based increase of annual leave in Article 166(4) of the Labour Law — one extra working day for every three years of service — is measured by continuous service in the enterprise and, once earned, can never be reduced, because "seniority" carries the same meaning as in Articles 73, 75, 89 and 95 and those provisions never lower an acquired right. [Para 184][Para 196][Para 199][Para 210]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remarks that pay slips are transparent, remove workers' suspicion and improve professional relations, and that issuing them for monthly-paid staff is a heavy but manageable administrative burden, go beyond the order it made. [Para 263][Para 266][Para 267]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- Maternity pay is printed as "៥០០០ ភាគរយ" in the issue heading against 50% everywhere else, and issue 1 is printed as a deduction of "១ ដុល្លារ ១" at the operative paragraph. [Para 219][Para 128]
- The conciliation report is dated "ឆ្នាំ ២ មក" and the same report number 057 carries 27 March in one passage and 23 March in another. [Para 30][Para 34]
- The award skips issue 9 in the reasoning and in the operative list, because it was settled at the hearing; the numbering gap is the panel's, not an omission in this brief. [Para 37][Para 91][Para 310]
- The employer's name appears in four forms — ហ្គោល ហ្វេម, ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើរ ប្រាយ ស៊ីស, ហ្គោល ហ្វេម and ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើទ្រាយ — and the chair's name is printed three ways at the signature block, one of them a Khmer misrendering of the same name. [Para 1][Para 41][Para 316]
- Several reasoning passages are duplicated word for word by OCR, for example the Article 2 quotation and the annual-leave table, and page markers ("-១៣-", "-២៣-", "-២៨-") sit inside sentences. [Para 150][Para 210][Para 261]
