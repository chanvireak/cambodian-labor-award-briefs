# Award No. 55/07 — Siu Quinh Garment (MFG) Ltd. (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ាវ គ័ញ (អិម អិហ្វ យី) អិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council, 17 July 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី appointed by the employer, លីវ សុវណ្ណា appointed by the workers, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140614

## 1. Facts
Siu Quinh Garment (MFG) Ltd. (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ាវ គ័ញ (អិម អិហ្វ យី) អិលធីឌី) employs about 480 workers at Khva village, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, and has two in-house unions. [Para 1][Para 17] The Khmer Youth Union at Siu Quinh Factory (សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ស៊ាវ គីញ) filed seven demands on 10 May 2007; five were settled in conciliation and two came to the panel — milk for nursing mothers and a canteen. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 11] A collective agreement of 28 April 2005 had promised milk in place of a factory nursery, and after Award No. 08/07 on the same factory the company built a nursery that no mother has used. [Para 19][Para 20] About 100 workers bring food from home and eat outside the factory fence, which is locked at break time, beside a drain and food stalls 30 to 100 metres away. [Para 25][Para 27]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must build a roofed canteen where workers can eat and shelter during work breaks? [Para 25][Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the nursery built after Award No. 08/07 has heat-trapping corrugated walls, no breastfeeding room and no interior conditions shown in the photographs, so the union wants milk restored as before rather than the building. [Para 21][Para 24] On the canteen they say workers eat in dust, sun and rain, are chased away from neighbouring houses, and the chairs provided outside the fence are too few and uncovered. [Para 26][Para 28]

**Employer (respondent):** the company has already complied with the earlier order and asks that the labour doctor inspect the nursery and recommend improvements if it falls short of standards. [Para 22][Para 23] It says the drain is properly covered and so has no smell, and it has put chairs outside the fence for workers to rest. [Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to build a properly roofed canteen for eating and shelter during breaks, and the panel declined to consider the milk demand. [Para 40][Para 41]

The panel refused to reopen the milk question. Issue 7 of Award No. 08/07 concerned the same factory and the same parties and had already held that where the company meets its duties under Articles 184, 185 and 186 of the Labour Law it need not follow the 2005 agreement, only provide milk, one hour a day for breastfeeding and a nursery. [Para 29][Para 30] Applying res judicata, the panel held it could not decide the same issue a second time, citing Awards 10/06, 24/06, 106/06 and 45/07. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33]

On the canteen the panel read Article 229 of the Labour Law, which requires every establishment to keep premises clean and to maintain hygiene and health conditions for workers. [Para 34] It found the current arrangements — no shelter from sun or rain, eating in dust, workers returning to work soaked — to be conditions harmful to health, and followed Award No. 35/04 which held that the absence of a canteen for storing food, eating and resting breaches Article 229. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38] It therefore ordered the employer to provide a roofed canteen suitable for eating and shelter during breaks. [Para 39][Para 41]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A demand already decided between the same parties on the same facts cannot be arbitrated again: where an earlier award has ruled on the point, the panel will not reconsider it, so a union that lost on an issue in one award cannot relitigate it in the next. [Para 30][Para 31][Para 33] Separately, Article 229 of the Labour Law obliges an employer to provide a covered place where workers can eat and shelter during breaks, and the absence of one is a breach even where the factory yard is otherwise orderly. [Para 34][Para 38][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the invitation to have the labour doctor inspect the nursery, and the panel's remark that no mother has yet used it, played no part in either order. [Para 20][Para 22]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed four ways: the Khmer face form at [Para 1], "SIU QUINH GAMENT (MFG) LTD." in the findings [Para 17], and two further variants of the Khmer transliteration later in the text. The citation for the earlier award gives its date as "ឆ្នាំ ៤", the year truncated [Para 15]. The judgments paragraph refers to the "National Constitutional Council" (ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា ធម្មនុញ្ញ ជាតិ កណ្តាល) as the earlier deciding body, which is not the Arbitration Council [Para 32]. Worker numbers conflict: 480 employees, 100 bringing food, about 30 nursing [Para 17][Para 25]. The headcount sentence at [Para 17] breaks off at "ឆ្នាំ ១៩៧" mid-figure.
