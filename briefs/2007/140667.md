# Award No. 134/07 — Wonrex (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 19 January 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 140667

## 1. Facts
The Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (KYFTU) and Khmer Youth Trade Union (KYTU) at Wonrex Factory complained on 1 October 2007 over 19 working conditions at Wonrex (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., a factory of 2,870 workers. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 38]
Nine issues were conciliated at the Department of Labour Disputes and ten were remitted in report No. 1266; at the hearings of 12 and 25 December 2007 six more were conciliated, leaving Issues 1, 5, 6 and 8. [Para 22][Para 23][Para 25]
At the second hearing only a KYFTU official attended — no local union leader and no worker — and KYFTU never produced written authority from KYTU at the factory to act for it. [Para 23][Para 45][Para 46][Para 50]
The underlying demand was that KYFTU settle the dispute for the local union and its members. [Para 54]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay 100 percent of wages when it has no work for the workers, rather than 50 percent with the workers staying at home? [Para 6][Para 7]
2. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must deduct the 1,000 riel union contribution fee from members who agreed to the deduction? [Para 10][Para 11]
3. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must announce piece rates one week before releasing a new design? [Para 11][Para 12]
4. (Issue 8) Whether the employer must pay US$ 20 per month for the workers' cooperation in working overtime? [Para 14][Para 15]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** KYFTU and KYTU at Wonrex Factory pursued the surviving demands on wages during work shortages, the union dues deduction, advance piece-rate notice and the overtime cooperation payment, and said the local leaders could not attend because the employer would not allow them to. [Para 6][Para 10][Para 50]
**Employer (respondent):** Wonrex (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. said it pays 50 percent when there is no work and the workers can stay home, cannot deduct dues until its payroll is computerised, announces piece rates when a new design appears, and cannot afford the US$ 20 overtime payment; it also insisted the KYFTU representative had no written authority. [Para 7][Para 11][Para 12][Para 15][Para 48]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 134/07 - Wonrex, holding that the claimant had given up its demands. [Para 73][Para 74]
Article 268(1) requires a union to file its statutes and management list for registration, and Article 266(2) names workers' organisations as workers' unions; from these the panel held that only a registered local union can represent its members. [Para 50][Para 51][Para 52]
The local KYTU at Wonrex was registered on 8 August 2003 and so had standing, but it had not authorised KYFTU to settle the dispute, and Clause 19 of Prakas 099 dated 21 April 2004 requires any representative to be expressly authorised in writing. [Para 38][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55]
KYFTU produced no authorisation letter by the 28 December 2007 deadline, following Arbitral Award 20/07 (Star Knitting). [Para 49][Para 58]
The panel then applied Clause 21 of Prakas 099 and Point 4.7 of the Procedural Rules, which let it proceed in absence or terminate by award. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]
Following Arbitral Awards 16/07 (M&V) and 73/07 (Sun Sin), it tested three conditions — proper notice, non-appearance, and no valid reason — and found all met: the local union knew the date, did not attend, and never itself told the panel that the employer blocked attendance. [Para 62][Para 67][Para 69][Para 71]
It therefore treated the claimant as giving up its demands and closed the file without reaching Issues 1, 5, 6 and 8 on the merits. [Para 72][Para 73]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A parent federation cannot settle a local union's collective dispute without a written authorisation from that local union, and where the local union itself fails to appear without a valid reason told to the panel, the panel treats the demands as given up and closes the case. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 72]
Only a registered local union may represent the workers who are its members. [Para 52][Para 53]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel recorded KYFTU's explanation that the employer had prevented the local leaders from attending, but held it unproven because the local union never advanced it. [Para 68][Para 70]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is dated "19 January 2007" on its face although it recites hearings of 12 and 25 December 2007 and conciliation on 27 November 2007; the printed date is retained here and is presumably a year error. [Para 1][Para 21]
Paragraph 26 lists the non-conciliated issues as 1, 6, 7 and 8, but paragraph 25 records Issue 7 as conciliated, so the surviving issues are reported as 1, 5, 6 and 8. [Para 25][Para 26]
The precedent M&V is cited as "16/07 - M&V" once and "27/07 M&V" twice, and paragraph 73 has a citation fragment spliced into the middle of its sentence. [Para 62][Para 73][Para 74]
This is an unofficial English translation of the Khmer original with dropped "th" throughout ("themployer", "thearing"), and the registration dates of the local union are printed three ways (8 August 2003, 18 September 2007, 2 October 2006). [Para 29][Para 35][Para 38]
Union leaders and the KYFTU official are individuals and appear here by role only; the source prints their full names. [Para 30][Para 44]
