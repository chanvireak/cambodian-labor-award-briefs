# Award No. 110/07 — Now Corp (Arbitration Council, 12 November 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Tuon Siphann, Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 140650

## 1. Facts
Now Corp operates at Choeung Ork Village, Lom Hach Commune, Angk Snuol District, Kandal, and employs 1,553 workers; the claimant is the Khmer Youth Trade Union (KYTU) at Now Corp Factory. [Para 1][Para 7][Para 31]
A complaint from striking workers on 21 September 2007 went to conciliation, where 13 of 20 issues were settled; seven non-conciliated issues reached the Arbitration Council in non-conciliation report No. 203/07, and four more (issues 4, 5, 6 and 7) were conciliated at the hearing, leaving Issues 1, 2 and 3. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 25]
In July, August and September 2007 the company had no work for 70 per cent of its workers for periods of two to seven days, sending them home with 50 per cent of wages without suspending their contracts or giving advance notice, and on 19 September 2007 it dismissed 36 workers. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 35][Para 36]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must pay full wages for the periods when it had no work for the workers, and back pay for earlier suspensions? [Para 31]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must reinstate the KYTU leaders and the other workers dismissed on 19 September 2007? [Para 38]
3. (Issue 3) Whether workers are entitled to wages and the attendance bonus for the days of the strike? [Para 57]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — Khmer Youth Trade Union (KYTU) at Now Corp Factory:** where the company had no work and did not lawfully suspend the contracts, it owed 100 per cent of wages; the five dismissed workers were union leaders removed for union activity; and wages plus attendance bonus were owed for the strike days. [Para 36][Para 53][Para 57]
**Employer (respondent) — Now Corp:** the dismissals of 36 workers followed the loss of purchasing orders, not union discrimination, and severance was paid under the Labour Law; the company had informed workers and given 50 per cent of wages when work ran out. [Para 35][Para 39][Para 53][Para 95]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to pay an additional 50 per cent of wages to make up full wages for the days in July, August and September 2007 when there was no work, within 15 days of the award taking effect; the reinstatement demand and the demand for strike-day wages and attendance bonus were rejected. [Para 115][Para 116]
Issue 1: Article 71(11) of the Labour Law allows suspension only for serious economic or material difficulty, for no more than two months and under the control of the Labour Inspector; following Awards 21/03 – Loyal Cambodia (Issue 8), 46/04 – M&A Cambodia (Issue 1) and 01/04 – New Point (Issue 1), the panel held that the company never suspended the contracts and gave no advance notice, so Article 72(1) did not apply and full wages were owed — the 50 per cent already paid was short by 50 per cent. [Para 59][Para 67][Para 70][Para 71]
Issue 2: Article 293 of the Labour Law and Clause 4 of Prakas 305 of 22 November 2001 protect shop stewards and union founders, and Award 28/07 – De Fang requires four conditions, the first being that the workers were already in a protected category when dismissed. [Para 75][Para 77][Para 82][Para 91] The union said the committee election was held at about 9:00 or 10:00 a.m., while its own minutes put it at 4:20 p.m., so the panel could not find the three leaders were elected before the dismissal; on Article 12 and Article 279, and Awards 93/06 – River Rich and 01/07 – Supreme, the union also proved no link between the 36 dismissals and union membership, the company having lost its orders. [Para 46][Para 87][Para 93][Para 96][Para 99]
Issue 3: under Articles 332 and 334 wages and allowances are not payable during a strike unless replacements are hired, which was not claimed; and because the strike began without the secret ballot required by Article 323, the seven working days' notice required by Article 324, or the prior peaceful settlement required by Article 320(4), the workers did not work regularly and lost the attendance bonus under Point 3 of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000, consistently with Awards 04/03 – Lida, 03/05 – Flying Dragon, 15/04 – Lucky Zone, 22/04 – Raffles le Royal and 63/07 – Phnom Penh. [Para 101][Para 103][Para 105][Para 109][Para 111][Para 114]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that stops giving work without suspending the labour contracts in the manner and under the supervision required by Article 71(11) remains bound to pay full wages; a partial payment made while work is withheld is only a part-payment of that obligation. [Para 66][Para 70]
Special protection for union leaders under Clause 4 of Prakas 305 attaches only if the worker already held protected status when dismissed, so an election held after the dismissal creates no protection and no duty to seek the Labour Inspector's authorisation. [Para 82][Para 91]
A strike staged without a secret ballot, without seven days' notice and before the statutory conciliation and arbitration steps are exhausted is unlawful, and the strikers forfeit both wages and the attendance bonus for those days, whether or not the employer hired replacements. [Para 105][Para 111][Para 114]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that a separate dispute arose from the union's letter of 7 November 2007, which required new proceedings rather than decision in this case. [Para 26][Para 27]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The strike dates are printed inconsistently: the strike is said to have run from 20 September to 22 October 2007, but the decision is expressed for "20 to 22 October 2007" and one reason paragraph gives "20 to 22 September 2007". [Para 57][Para 101][Para 108][Para 116] The number of dismissed workers is given as 200 by the union and 36 by the employer throughout the reasons. [Para 38][Para 96] OCR drops the letter "t" from many words ("themployer", "thearing"), and the paragraph stating the company's receipt of the election notice breaks off mid-sentence ("at"), so the timing of that notice cannot be fixed from the text. [Para 52] Individual workers and union officers are identified in this brief by role only.
