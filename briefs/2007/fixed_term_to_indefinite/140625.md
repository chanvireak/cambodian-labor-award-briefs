# Award No. 66/07 — Jia Fung Garment Company (Arbitration Council, 9 August 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (MAR SAMBORANA, ANAN, PEN BUNCHHEA) · Doc ID 140625

## 1. Facts
Jia Fung Garment Company, at Toul Sangke, Khan Russei Keo, Phnom Penh, employed two sewing-unit chiefs, Ms V and Ms C, on probationary contracts from 1 December 2006 and 12 December 2006 respectively, each at a minimum wage of US$55 [Para 22][Para 24][Para 28][Para 46]. Both were told on 12 May 2007 that their contracts would end that day; both refused to sign for their wages, and neither returned to work afterwards [Para 30][Para 41]. The Federation Union of Development Workers Rights (FUDWR) elected Ms V as its secretary on 27 May 2007 [Para 59][Para 63]. Of nine demands, seven were conciliated and the payment-date issue was conciliated at the hearing, leaving reinstatement [Para 13][Para 16].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must reinstate Ms V and Ms C, whose dismissal as union election candidates was unlawful? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Federation Union of Development Workers Rights (FUDWR):** both women had helped organise the union's election and were dismissed before it was held; Ms V was verbally terminated on 12 May 2007 and then barred from the factory by the guard, while Ms C was never shown any valid reason [Para 58][Para 42][Para 54].

**Employer (respondent), Jia Fung Garment Company:** it will not reinstate them because they abandoned their employment [Para 11]. Ms V did nothing wrong but refused to sign for her wages and never came back [Para 36][Para 38]; Ms C had forged her ID card, one of about 20 such cases found in an April 2007 document review [Para 46][Para 47].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — reinstatement refused, but the company ordered to pay Ms V 28.5 days' wages at her average daily rate plus her last month's wage, and to pay Ms C her wages to 31 July 2007 plus 7.5 days' compensation for annual leave [Para 141][Para 142][Para 143].

On special protection, Clause 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 protects election candidates for 45 days before and after the poll, but only where the union has notified the employer of the candidacy by all reliable means [Para 73][Para 77]. Following Awards 07/06 Dai Young and 09/06 Grand Diamond City, the panel required three elements: protected status, dismissal within the protected period, and prior notice to the employer [Para 78][Para 79]. The first two were met, the dismissal of 12 May 2007 falling within 45 days of the election, but FUDWR never gave the company the candidate list or notice, so protection failed [Para 82][Para 83][Para 85].

On procedure, Article 67(7) provides that a fixed-duration contract not put in writing becomes one of undetermined duration, so both women held undetermined contracts terminable only for a valid reason under Article 74 [Para 93][Para 96]. The panel rejected the abandonment plea for Ms V: the company conceded she had done nothing wrong, and it found she was refused entry to the factory [Para 101][Para 107]. Termination without valid reason engages Article 91, entitling her to damages, and following Awards 29/05 Kang Ning and 111/04 Sun City the panel awarded notice under Article 73, dismissal indemnity under Article 89, annual leave under Article 167 and seven days' damages, totalling 28.5 days [Para 111][Para 112][Para 131][Para 133].

The panel declined to order reinstatement because the employer would not accept it, substituting compensation; the daily rate is her total wage over the last five months, including overtime and attendance bonuses, divided by five months and 26 working days [Para 131][Para 134][Para 136].

For Ms C the panel found the forgery proved and admitted, and Article 83(B2) makes presentation of false documentation a serious offence justifying dismissal, so the company was legally entitled to dismiss her, but only from the hearing day when the reason was first given; she therefore received her wages to 31 July 2007 and 7.5 days of annual leave [Para 119][Para 122][Para 126][Para 140].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union election candidate enjoys the 45-day protection of Clause 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 only if the union has notified the employer of the candidacy in advance; without that notice the dismissal is judged on ordinary grounds [Para 78][Para 85]. A worker engaged without a written fixed-duration contract holds an undetermined-duration contract, and dismissal without a valid reason under Article 74 entitles her to notice, dismissal indemnity, annual leave and damages under Article 91, computed as 28.5 days for five months of service [Para 96][Para 111][Para 133]. Forgery of an identity document is a serious offence under Article 83(B2), but a dismissal on that ground takes effect only when the reason is communicated, leaving wages payable up to that date [Para 122][Para 126].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the union had not re-elected its leaders only because it did not know whether the two women would be reinstated [Para 63].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
An unofficial English translation with systematic loss of the leading "t" and "th" and frequent word fusion ("themployer", "wastaged", "annualeave"), plus duplicated phrases where the scan repeated a line [Para 77][Para 96]. The union election is dated 27 May 2007 in the evidence list but 30 May 2007 in the reasoning [Para 59][Para 83]. Several exhibit dates are wrong by a decade: the business licence reads 22 August 206, the conciliation minutes 29 June 2006 and one hearing invitation 23 July 2006 [Para 18][Para 24]. The worker party is called both the Federation Union of Development Workers Rights and the Federation Trade Union of Development Workers Rights [Para 3][Para 13]. Both dismissed workers' names are printed in two variants, one of them a transliteration of the other [Para 9][Para 20].
