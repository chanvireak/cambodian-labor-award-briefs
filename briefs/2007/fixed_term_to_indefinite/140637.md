# Award No. 82/07 — Ark Khit Garment Factory (Cambodia) Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គាមិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត) v Khmer Youth Union at Ark Khit (សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត) (Arbitration Council, 26 September 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Mar Sambor Na (employer-appointed), Liv Sovanna (worker-appointed), Ang Eng Thong (president) · Doc ID 140637

## 1. Facts
Ark Khit Garment Factory (Cambodia) Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត ហ្គាមិន ហ្វេក ធើរី ខេមបូឌា លីមីធីត) faced a collective dispute brought by the Khmer Youth Union at Ark Khit (សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន អាក ឃីត), a union registered at the factory on 21 February 2007 [Para 1][Para 33].
Of eight demands, four were conciliated at the Ministry and four others were forwarded to the Council on 20 August 2007 [Para 18][Para 19].
Hearings were held on 28 August and 11 September 2007 [Para 15][Para 16].
The union objected that the ministry had wrongly treated two further points as already conciliated, asking the panel to add them; the ministry sent no clarification, and the panel ruled that only the four referred points fell within its jurisdiction [Para 23][Para 28][Para 29].
The union then refused to present facts or evidence and did not attend the second hearing, which was closed at once [Para 30][Para 37].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether a fixed-duration contract running longer than two years must be converted into a contract of undetermined duration? [Para 2]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must issue one-year fixed-duration contracts because the earlier contracts were too short and disrupted workers' livelihoods? [Para 5]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must keep the attendance bonus for workers who clock in more than ten minutes late? [Para 7]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must pay out outstanding annual leave and allow it to be taken each year before Khmer New Year? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Khmer Youth Union at Ark Khit:** contracts exceeding two years should be treated as of undetermined duration, and the company should issue one-year contracts because short terms forced workers to look for work afresh and hurt their families [Para 2][Para 5]. It also sought to keep the attendance bonus despite lateness of over ten minutes, and payment of remaining annual leave each year before Khmer New Year [Para 7][Para 9].
**Employer (respondent), Ark Khit Garment Factory (Cambodia) Ltd:** the company refused each demand, saying it applies Articles 670 and 73 of the Labour Law on fixed-duration contracts, that contract terms follow its production chain, that Notification No. 017 of 18 July 2000 governs the attendance bonus, and that annual leave depends on each worker's seniority [Para 4][Para 6][Para 8][Para 10].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 82/07 without considering any of the four demands, treating the union's refusal to take part as abandonment of the complaint [Para 45][Para 48].
Before reaching the merits the panel settled its own jurisdiction. Prakas No. 099 Article 21 and Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules let it proceed or close the file where a duly summoned party fails to appear without cause [Para 38][Para 39], and Article 312 together with Rules 24 to 27 give it wide powers to organise hearings and gather information [Para 40].
The panel stressed that a hearing is an opportunity for the parties to produce evidence and argument, and that mere attendance is not enough: a party must participate and explain to give the panel a basis for decision [Para 41][Para 42].
It recorded that the union was given every chance to appear and to support the claims of the workers who had entrusted it with the case [Para 43].
Instead the union chose to refuse to supply facts and evidence, and gave no reason beyond its demand that two points outside the panel's jurisdiction be added to the four referred by the ministry [Para 44].
The panel therefore held the union had abandoned its complaint and closed the file, following Award 62/04 E Sin, points 17 to 19 [Para 36][Para 45].
It also declined to add the two extra points, since they were neither in the non-conciliation report nor a direct consequence of the dispute and did not arise after the report was made [Para 34][Para 35].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A party that has been properly summoned and then refuses to attend, or to produce facts and evidence in support of its own claims, is treated as having abandoned its complaint, and the panel may close the file without deciding the demands on their merits [Para 38][Para 45].
Under Prakas No. 099 Article 21 and Rule 4.7 the panel has the choice, where a party is absent without cause, to continue in that party's absence or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an order [Para 38][Para 39].
Attendance alone does not preserve a claim: a party must participate and explain, since the hearing exists to give the panel a basis for decision [Para 41][Para 42].
The panel will not add issues to those referred by the ministry unless they appear in the non-conciliation report or arise directly from the dispute after it [Para 34][Para 35].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the union's three representatives in the staff-delegate election had been appointed by the company as team leaders, a point it recorded without ruling on it because the election issue was outside its jurisdiction [Para 24].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a Khmer original with heavy OCR noise. The award number cited at Para 46 is printed as "2707" with a garbled case name, so the precedent relied on cannot be identified with confidence. The number of unresolved points is printed inconsistently: five ("០៥ ចំណុច") at Para 20 where the rest of the award speaks of four, and Para 22 adds that only four can be resolved. Arbitrator names differ between the face of the award and the signature page: the employer-side arbitrator's name loses its final consonant, the worker-side arbitrator's name is printed two different ways in the signature block, and the president's name changes its last syllable [Para 49]. The email address in the letterhead is corrupted ("arbitrationcouncif.org") [Para 2]. The exhibit list is truncated mid-item and one registration number has stray digits spliced into the date [Para 33].
