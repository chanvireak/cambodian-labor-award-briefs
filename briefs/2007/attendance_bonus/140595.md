# Award No. 27/07 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ឹម វ៉េ កាត់ដេរ អន្តរជាតិ (សាខា ឌី៣) (IM & W Garment International, D3 branch) (Arbitration Council, 10 April 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Thach for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Kong Chhala chair) · Doc ID 140595

## 1. Facts
IM & W Garment International, D3 branch (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ឹម វ៉េ កាត់ដេរ អន្តរជាតិ (សាខា ឌី៣)), on National Road 2, Prek Ta Long, Chak Angre Krom, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, faced a ten-point complaint filed on 17 January 2007 by the Cambodian Democratic Garment Workers Union Federation (សម្ព័ន្ឋ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា) and its union at the factory. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 10]
Ten points went unresolved at conciliation, but the Labour Dispute Department recorded that seven of them had already been dealt with in earlier agreements and awards, so only three were referred to the panel: a demand that the company dismiss a named worker for inciting serious misconduct, a demand to re-run the election of worker representatives, and a demand that the company stop deducting the regular USD 5 attendance bonus from workers who took leave properly. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 11][Para 12][Para 13]
At the hearing of 26 March 2007 the union demanded that the panel decide all ten points afresh; the panel explained that it would hear only the three points in the non-conciliation report, whereupon the union refused to take part and walked out before the proceedings finished. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18][Para 21][Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel could decide all ten demands when only the three points listed in the non-conciliation report had been referred to it? [Para 17][Para 18][Para 42]
2. Whether a claimant union that attended the opening stages but left before the hearing concluded had "appeared" under Article 21 of Prakas No. 099, so that the case could be closed in its absence? [Para 34][Para 37][Para 39]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សម្ព័ន្ឋ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and its factory union:** all ten demands should be resolved again by the panel, and unless they were added back the union would not continue to take part in the hearing. [Para 16][Para 41][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ឹម វ៉េ កាត់ដេរ អន្តរជាតិ (សាខា ឌី៣):** it attended through its administration chief and the Garment Manufacturers Association officer, filed its documents and statement on the disputed points, and asked the panel to proceed on the three referred points. [Para 1][Para 23]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case No. 27/07 and made no finding on the three demands, because the claimant union left the hearing and abandoned its opportunity to present evidence. [Para 49][Para 50]

The panel began from Rule 4.7 of the procedure annexed to Prakas No. 099, which allows it either to continue in the absence of a party or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an award, and from Article 21 of the same Prakas on the same option. [Para 27][Para 30] It adopted the reading in Award 115/06 (Ak Khi Da) that walking out of the hearing room before the proceedings begin counts as failure to appear, and the three conditions set out in Award 16/07 (Lo Tou Sot): proper notification, non-appearance, and no valid reason. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 31]
Condition one was satisfied, since the union had been properly summoned by the secretariat. [Para 32][Para 33] Condition two was satisfied because the union was present for the introductory and procedural stages but took no part in the conciliation or merits stages, so it did not participate through to the end as Article 21 requires. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 39][Para 40] Condition three was satisfied because the union's stated reason — that the panel would not add back the seven settled points — was not a valid excuse, the panel having no power to decide disputes not recorded in the non-conciliation minute. [Para 42][Para 44][Para 45] All three conditions met, the panel closed the file. [Para 46][Para 49]
The panel added that by walking out the union had forfeited the chance to present evidence on its own demands, a course that did not show good faith in bringing the dispute for settlement. [Para 47][Para 48]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A party that is properly summoned, attends the opening procedural stages and then withdraws rather than take part in the merits has not "appeared" within Article 21 of Prakas No. 099, and where that condition plus proper notification and the absence of a valid reason are all met, the panel may close the case without deciding the demands; a refusal to proceed unless the panel revives points already settled is not a valid reason, because the panel cannot decide demands outside the non-conciliation report. [Para 37][Para 42][Para 44][Para 46]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the union's conduct did not show good faith in seeking settlement, and its description of the four hearing stages, are observations that did not affect the three-condition test. [Para 36][Para 48]
