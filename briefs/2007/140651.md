# Award No. 102/07 — Teratex Knitting and Garment International Factory (តេរ៉ាតិច្ស) (Arbitration Council, 24 October 2007)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, Liv Sovanna, Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140651

## 1. Facts
The employer is ក្រុមហ៊ុន តេរ៉ាតិច្ស នីត ធីង អ៊ន (ហ្គាមេន) អ៉ីនធីណាស្សូណាល់ ហ្វាក់តូរី លីមីតធីត (Teratex Knitting and Garment International Factory Ltd) on National Road No. 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Krom, Khan Meanchey, Phnom Penh, employing about 3,200 workers; the claimant is សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា, with about 400 members at the factory. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 26][Para 27]
The union complained on 23 August 2007; conciliation settled 4 of 8 demands on 11 September 2007, non-conciliation report No. 1013 was filed on 18 September 2007, and the hearing was held on 5 October 2007. [Para 12][Para 14][Para 15][Para 16][Para 17]
At the hearing the worker party withdrew Issue 1 (a demand that output prices be issued within two days of sewing), so the panel considered only Issues 2, 3 and 4. [Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must accept a private doctor's certificate for sick leave of fewer than five days? [Para 29]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must raise the meal allowance for overtime from 1,000 riels to 2,000 riels? [Para 39]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must raise the basic wage of the nine workers in the water-tank section from US$ 50 to US$ 60 a month? [Para 49]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រ កម្ពុជា:** state medical services are ineffective and costly, so private certificates should be accepted for short illness; food prices have risen, so the meal allowance should rise to 2,000 riels; and the nine water-tank workers do several jobs and deserve US$ 60. [Para 29][Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 49]
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន តេរ៉ាតិច្ស នីត ធីង អ៊ន (ហ្គាមេន) អ៉ីនធីណាស្សូណាល់ ហ្វាក់តូរី លីមីតធីត:** the 4 December 2002 agreement with the union requires the company doctor to verify the illness, and a state doctor's certificate on return; it pays the 1,000 riel meal allowance fixed by Point 4 of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000; and US$ 50 is the lawful minimum for the water-tank workers. [Para 5][Para 31][Para 35][Para 44][Para 51][Para 55]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the Issue 2 demand was rejected, and the panel declined to consider Issues 3 and 4; the award was non-binding and would become final eight days after notification absent written opposition. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 109][Para 110]
Issue 2: Article 71 of the Labour Law treats absence certified by an official doctor as suspending the contract, and earlier awards (62/04 – Ecin, 68/04 – Cithu, 81/05 – Sokhprim) read "official doctor" to include a licensed private doctor; but Article 4 of the company's internal rules and Point 4 of the 4 December 2002 agreement require the company doctor's examination and, for illness outside the factory, a state doctor's certificate on return, a regime the panel found more favourable than the law. [Para 58][Para 60][Para 61][Para 68][Para 75][Para 80] The demand therefore had no legal basis and was rejected. [Para 82][Para 83][Para 84]
Issue 3: Article 4 of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000 requires 1,000 riels or a free meal for voluntary overtime, and the company pays that, so a demand for 2,000 riels exceeds what the law provides and is an interest demand. [Para 45][Para 85][Para 86][Para 88]
Issue 4: Point 1 of Notification 745 of 23 October 2006 fixes US$ 45 for probationary and US$ 50 for regular workers, which guarantees the minimum only; following Award 33/07 – Gold Fame (Issue 6), workers already at US$ 50 are not entitled to an increase under it. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 100][Para 101]
Both interest demands were declined because the union, with 400 members among 3,200 workers, lacked most representative status under Article 277 of the Labour Law, Article 96 and Prakas 305 Clause 9(1), consistent with Awards 57/04 – Evergreen, 60/04 – Unitex and 08/07 – Sau Khin. [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91][Para 92][Para 106] Arbitrator Liv Sovanna dissented on Issue 2, holding that Article 4 of the internal rules allows a private doctor's prescription and that the demand was therefore valid. [Para 149][Para 150]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an internal rule or a collective agreement sets a stricter proof of illness than the Labour Law — requiring the company doctor's examination and a state doctor's certificate — and confers a benefit greater than the statute, the rule governs and a demand to accept private certificates fails for want of legal basis. [Para 80][Para 82][Para 83]
A demand that exceeds an entitlement fixed by Notification 017, such as a doubled meal allowance, is an interest demand and cannot be decided in favour of a union that does not hold most representative status at the enterprise. [Para 88][Para 89][Para 106]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that Point 1 of Notification 745 guarantees the minimum wage only, so it creates no general right to a rise for workers already paid US$ 50. [Para 100][Para 101]
