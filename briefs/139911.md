# Award No. 265/13 — Lan Global Tech Style (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 3 January 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Pen Bun Chhea presiding; Ly Tai Seng, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed) · Doc ID 139911

## 1. Facts
Lan Global Tech Style (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. operates at Prey Ta village, Kab commune, Pou Senchey, Phnom Penh. [Para 2] The Khmer Workers' Spirit Union Federation and its local union filed a complaint on 21 November 2013; of three demands conciliation on 3 December 2013 settled one, leaving two, and the conciliation report No. 1568 reached the secretariat on 11 December 2013. [Para 12][Para 13][Para 14] A hearing was fixed for 23 December 2013 at 14:00 and the workers were notified by letter No. 1332 of 16 December 2013, received on 20 December 2013. [Para 11][Para 22][Para 23][Para 39]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay a midday meal allowance of 5,000 riel per day to each worker? [Para 3]
2. Whether the employer must stop deducting tax from workers' wages and reimburse the amounts already deducted? [Para 5]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union maintained both demands — the 5,000 riel daily meal allowance, and an end to wage tax deductions with repayment of what had been withheld. [Para 3][Para 5] No worker, and no union officer with written authority, appeared at the hearing; only the federation's coordinator attended, and he held no letter transferring authority from the local union. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 44]
**Employer (respondent):** the company said it had no capacity to pay the meal allowance, and that it deducted tax from salaries in accordance with the law on taxation. [Para 4][Para 6] Its administration head and accounting head attended. [Para 2][Para 19]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel found the claimants had abandoned their demands and terminated the arbitration, issuing an award closing case No. 265/13 without deciding either demand on the merits. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58][Para 59]

The panel asked only one question, of procedure: how to proceed when the claimant is absent. [Para 25] Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the panel to invite the parties to explain their claims in person and to furnish documents (Article 18), requires the parties to attend every meeting convened (Article 20), and allows the panel either to continue in the absence of a party properly summoned who fails to appear without valid reason, or to close the arbitration by issuing an award (Article 21); Rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules repeats that choice and adds that the absent party must have been properly notified of date, time and place. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28][Para 29]

Following its earlier awards in cases 30/05, 16/07, 27/07, 95/08, 132/08, 138/08, 151/08, 01/10, 30/10, 130/13, 167/13 and 270/13, the panel identified three conditions for closing a case in the claimant's absence: proper summons, non-appearance, and no valid reason for it. [Para 34][Para 36][Para 37] It found the first satisfied — the invitation carried the date, place and contact numbers and was signed for on 20 December 2013. [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] It found the second satisfied: appearing "before the panel" means both being present at the hearing and taking part in the proceedings from start to finish, and neither the workers nor an authorised representative attended. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44][Para 45] It found the third satisfied: the reasons given — the local president was away in his home province, the vice-president was ill, and the secretary's whereabouts were unknown — were not valid reasons. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]

The panel added that a claimant bears the duty to appear and to supply the arguments and evidence supporting its claim, and that the respondent's duty arises only once the claimant has done so; since the claimant appeared not at all and offered no valid excuse, the panel treated the demands as abandoned and closed the case. [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant union neither attends the hearing nor is represented by a person holding written authority from the local union or the workers, and offers only reasons the panel finds invalid, the three conditions of Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 are met and the panel may treat the demands as abandoned and close the arbitration by an award without hearing the merits, the respondent having no case to answer until the claimant has presented its own. [Para 37][Para 44][Para 55][Para 57]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed generally that it is the respondent's duty to answer only after the claimant has discharged its own burden, and that an absent claimant leaves nothing for the panel to weigh. [Para 54]
