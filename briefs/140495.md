# Award No. 34/08 — Quint Major Industrial Co., Ltd v Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (Arbitration Council, 19 March 2008)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Kong Phallack chair) · Doc ID 140495

## 1. Facts
Quint Major Industrial Co., Ltd, at Phum Tror Yeoung, Perk Commune, Angsnol District, Kandal, employs about 2,100 workers. [Para 18] The Khmer Youth Federation Trade Union (KYFTU) and the local KYTU at the factory brought sixteen demands covering a daycare centre and breastfeeding room, a 1,500 riel overtime meal allowance, double pay on holidays and Sundays, pro rata bonuses for leave, a monthly health-check day, US$10 for breastfeeding time, a 24-hour doctor, a sewing-target bonus, a thirteenth-month payment, accident treatment costs, payment within 48 hours of termination, and 100 days of maternity leave. [Para 4][Para 7] The union did not attend either hearing, on 7 and 17 March 2008, and the employer attended only the first. [Para 10][Para 15][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Clause 21 / Rule 4.7) Whether the panel should proceed or close the case where the claimant union, twice invited, failed to appear and gave no prior notice? [Para 24][Para 32]

None of the sixteen substantive demands was reached, the case being closed on the procedural question. [Para 32][Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, KYFTU and local KYTU at Quint Major Industrial):** no position was presented at either hearing; the union's only explanation, given by telephone, was that it could not find a worker representative because it was pay day, and later that no worker could be found to testify, and it told the Secretariat to decide according to its procedure. [Para 11][Para 16][Para 28]
**Employer (respondent, Quint Major Industrial Co., Ltd):** it attended the first hearing, said the choice of response was up to the panel, and after failing to attend the second asked the panel to decide according to legal procedure, explaining that no vehicle was available. [Para 9][Para 14][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed case 34/08-Quint Major Industrial, treating the claimant union as having withdrawn its demand. [Para 32][Para 33]

The panel grounded its power to act on Clause 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which allows a party to appear in person or through a lawyer or a person expressly authorised in writing. [Para 22] KYFTU had assigned no representative and no lawyer, and had told the Secretariat before both hearings that it could not find anyone to attend. [Para 23] Clause 21 of the same Prakas and Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules permit the panel either to proceed in the absence of a duly invited party or to terminate the proceedings by award, provided it is satisfied the party was properly notified of the date, time and venue. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] The panel found proper notification by letters of 3 and 14 March 2008, plus telephone reminders, for both hearings. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 27] It rejected the union's explanations: the union had time to prepare, and if it could not find a representative it could have sought a postponement in advance rather than waiting for the reminder call. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31] Following Awards 30/05-Maure and 16/07-Lotus, it closed the case. [Para 32]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a claimant union, properly notified of two successive hearings, fails to appear without good cause and gives no prior notice or request for postponement, the panel will close the case and treat the demand as withdrawn rather than proceed in the claimant's absence; the same rule permits proceeding, but the panel will not decide a collective dispute on the employer's evidence alone where the claimant has abandoned it. [Para 24][Para 26][Para 32]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations on the employer's own absence from the second hearing and its stated lack of transport were not relied on, since the employer had asked the panel to follow its procedure and the case failed for the claimant's non-appearance. [Para 21][Para 32]

- The first hearing is dated 7 March 2008 in the header at [Para 6] and in the body, but "07 March 2007" at [Para 8].
- The list of demands at [Para 4] is truncated in the source itself, ending mid-word at demand 13, and demands 14 to 16 are not recoverable; the text also loses the numbering of demands 1–2 and 7–9.
- The two hearings are each recorded as lasting from 2:00 to 2:30 p.m. at [Para 6], which cannot cover a contested hearing with a postponement decision at [Para 15].
- The employer's representatives are named in full at [Para 2] and [Para 3]; they are given by role here.
