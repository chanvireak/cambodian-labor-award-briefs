# Award No. 69/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ី ភី ខេមបូឌា (CP Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 12 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: សេង វូច ហ៊ុន (employer-nominated), អាន វិរៈ (worker-nominated), សុខ ម៉ាធឿង (chair) · Doc ID 140286

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ី ភី ខេមបូឌា has made animal feed in Cambodia since 1996 at Trapeang Tuol, Ang Snuol district, Kandal, employing about 200 people of whom 50 are workers. [Para 52][Para 53] Six workers in the raw-material unloading section handled dried corn bought from farmers between September and February, earning 10,500 riel a day plus a tonnage bonus; when the company installed a silo machine in February 2009 the manual handling of corn largely disappeared and their monthly earnings fell from 1.1–1.5 million riel to about 270,000 riel by May 2010. [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63][Para 66][Para 67] សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចំរើន កម្មករ filed on 24 June 2010 on behalf of សហជីព ចំរើន កម្មករ រោងចក្រ ស៊ី ភី ខេមបូឌា; four demands failed conciliation, the third was withdrawn and the fourth settled by agreement at the third hearing on 27 July 2010, leaving two. [Para 13][Para 25][Para 30]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must transfer the six corn-unloading workers to the feed-mixing section, where work is steadier? [Para 55][Para 96]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must repay the tax it deducted from the severance payments of a worker it dismissed? [Para 84][Para 121]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចំរើន កម្មករ (សហជីព ចំរើន កម្មករ រោងចក្រ ស៊ី ភី ខេមបូឌា):** only six of the twelve raw-material handlers remain and they were promised orally that they would handle corn only; there is no corn left for them, the company stockpiles corn and brings in contract labour instead, so they should be moved to feed mixing where the work is continuous; and the company deducted tax from a terminated colleague's severance and other terminal payments, though only wages are taxable. [Para 59][Para 64][Para 65][Para 89]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស៊ី ភី ខេមបូឌា:** the silo machine is modern technology that cuts cost and raises productivity, corn is seasonal, and the six have been offered 2,000–3,000 tons a month of other raw materials to unload at wages no lower than before, which they refused; the feed-mixing section is fully staffed with contract workers and cannot absorb them, and mixing requires skill or the product is damaged; the tax deduction followed the Tax Law. [Para 68][Para 71][Para 81][Para 92][Para 98]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the demand to transfer the six workers to feed mixing was refused; the company was ordered to repay to the dismissed worker the money it wrongly withheld as tax from his severance and terminal payments. [Para 146][Para 147]

On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 2 §2 of the Labour Law and earlier awards (62/06 and 119/09) for the proposition that the employer may organise and direct the enterprise so long as the exercise is lawful and reasonable. [Para 100][Para 103][Para 106] It found that other raw materials — rice bran, cassava, broken rice and imported goods — were available for the six to unload, that the section's work was still being done by other workers, that the feed-mixing section needed no additional labour, and that the company had guaranteed earnings no lower than before the silo was installed; on those facts the refusal to transfer was lawful and reasonable. [Para 108][Para 112][Para 114][Para 118][Para 119] The panel also held that adopting labour-saving technology to cut costs and raise output is ordinary conduct for an employer. [Para 116]

On Issue 2 the panel held that Article 44 of the Tax Law of 8 January 1997, unamended by the 2003 amending law, exempts severance paid within the limits set by the Labour Law. [Para 128][Para 129][Para 142] It treated the terminal payments — pay in lieu of notice under Article 77, dismissal damages under Article 89 and moral damages under Article 91 — as severance for that purpose, and held the company should not have withheld tax from them; repayment was ordered. [Para 138][Para 140][Para 141][Para 144][Para 145] Wages themselves were taxable and were not in dispute. [Para 126][Para 136]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may reorganise production around labour-saving machinery, and may decline to move workers to another section, where the change is lawful and reasonable and the workers are offered alternative work at earnings no lower than before; the managerial right under Article 2 is not defeated by a fall in piece-rate earnings caused by the employer's own modernisation. [Para 106][Para 118][Para 119] Severance and terminal payments made within the Labour Law's limits are exempt income under Article 44 of the Tax Law, so tax withheld from them must be repaid to the worker. [Para 129][Para 142]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel set out that "wage" under Article 102 includes bonuses, payment for unused annual leave and seniority pay, a definition it used to classify the terminal payments but which was not itself contested. [Para 131][Para 132][Para 134]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award names the dismissed worker in full at every stage of the reasons and in the operative order, and names the two probation workers by name; they appear here only as roles. [Para 5][Para 84][Para 145] The reasoning is internally inconsistent about the scope of the tax claim: it records that wages were not in dispute and then orders repayment of tax withheld "from his wages" as well as from severance. [Para 126][Para 145] The evidence list is misnumbered and one item begins with a stray year fragment. [Para 31] The union's name and the address block are garbled in the header, and the employer is spelled in several Khmer variants. [Para 1][Para 2] Three hearing dates were fixed, one adjourned because the factory president was abroad and one because an arbitrator had a personal matter; the award does not say which arbitrator, and the signature block prints the employer-nominated arbitrator's name differently from the header. [Para 10][Para 21][Para 149] The award is recorded as binding because the parties accepted it on 27 July 2010. [Para 148]
