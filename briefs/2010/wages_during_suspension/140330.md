# Order No. 125/10 — IMP International Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អឹម ភី អេ អ៊ិនធើណេស្សុនណល់) and its security staff represented by the Cambodian Labour Union (Arbitration Council, 13 December 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun សេង វួច ហ៊ុន; An Non អន ណន; Ang Eng Thong អាំង អេង ថុង presiding) · Doc ID 140330

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អឹម ភី អេ អ៊ិនធើណេស្សុនណល់ (IMP International Co., Ltd), a Phnom Penh security-services employer with about 550 staff [Para 105], faced an eleven-point collective demand from its guards, filed on 9 August 2010 and referred to the Arbitration Council after the Ministry's Labour Disputes Department failed to conciliate any of the eleven points [Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]. The workers were represented by the Cambodian Labour Union (សហភាព ការងារ កម្ពុជា) on behalf of the company's security personnel [Para 1][Para 106]. At the hearing of 30 November 2010 the parties settled ten of the eleven points; only Issue 2, the deduction of money from wages as a disciplinary fine, remained for the panel to decide [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37]. The disputed practice is a deduction of between USD 1 and USD 40 per month, graduated by the seriousness of the fault [Para 109]. In the instance put to the panel, a guard who also served as union vice-president had USD 30 deducted in September 2010 for allegedly mocking a manager and, after he protested, USD 20, so that he received USD 70 instead of his usual USD 90 [Para 110][Para 116][Para 139].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must stop deducting money from workers' wages as a penalty whenever a worker commits a fault, because such deductions are contrary to the spirit of Article 28 of the Labour Law, which forbids employers to impose monetary penalties? [Para 107]

The panel recorded that the ten other demands — disciplinary notice to the Ministry, overtime and holiday pay, working hours and leave, equal pay, death and workplace-accident liability, severance and anti-union interference — were settled by agreement at the hearing and were not decided on the merits [Para 31][Para 35][Para 36].

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហភាព ការងារ កម្ពុជា for the security staff of ក្រុមហ៊ុន អឹម ភី អេ:** Article 28 forbids monetary penalties outright, and the scale applied here (USD 1 to USD 40) bore no relation to the seriousness of any fault [Para 107][Para 109]. Deductions were made without prior notice, and the schedule of punishable faults was shown to the workers only after they protested [Para 115]. On wages of USD 55 to USD 100 the cuts left guards unable to pay water, electricity and school costs [Para 118].

**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន អឹម ភី អេ អ៊ិនធើណេស្សុនណល់:** Article 28 does not prohibit an employer from deducting wages, and the company's internal rules do not forbid it either [Para 120][Para 121]. The employer therefore has the right to deduct where a worker repeats a fault without correcting it, and written guidance had been given before any deduction was made [Para 6][Para 122].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to stop deducting fines from workers' wages when workers commit faults [Para 143]

The panel read Article 28 as an unqualified prohibition: the employer must not impose a monetary penalty or a double disciplinary sanction for one and the same fault [Para 126]. It defined a monetary penalty functionally, as any measure that reduces the wage a worker would normally earn by performing the work, which covers a deduction made while the worker is still working [Para 127][Para 129][Para 136]. It then observed that the Labour Law treats wage deductions strictly, subjecting them to the conditions of Articles 126 to 129 [Para 130].

Turning to the internal rules, the panel found Article 10 of the company's rules provided only for a written warning, an unpaid suspension of up to seven days, and dismissal for repeated faults — no provision authorised a deduction from wages [Para 131][Para 132][Para 133][Para 142]. It distinguished suspension from deduction: suspension suspends the contract, so no wage is earned, whereas Article 28 targets deductions from a wage earned by normal work [Para 135][Para 136]. On the facts, the employer had in truth applied a deduction as a disciplinary sanction [Para 137][Para 140]. The panel added that the employer should use the disciplinary measures in its own internal rules, but must not apply any sanction that contradicts the law or other applicable regulations [Para 141].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A deduction from a worker's wage imposed as a disciplinary sanction for a fault is a prohibited monetary penalty under Article 28 of the Labour Law, even where the worker keeps working normally, even where the internal rules are silent on deductions, and even where the employer asserts a contractual right to punish repeat faults; an unpaid suspension is different because no wage is earned during it [Para 127][Para 135][Para 141].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general remark that the Labour Law is strict about wage deductions, pointing to Articles 126 to 129, and its observation that ten of the eleven demands had been settled in conciliation [Para 35][Para 130].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The instrument is headed សេចក្តីបង្គាប់ (order) rather than award, though it decides the merits of Issue 2 with reasons. The employer's name is OCR'd in at least five different Khmer spellings across the document (អឹម ភី អេ, អ៊ីម ភី អេ, អ៉ីម ភី អេ, អី ម ភី អេ, អ៉ឹម ភី អេ) [Para 1][Para 39][Para 40][Para 44]. The internal rules are dated 11 December 2006 in the exhibit list but 11 December 2010 in the reasoning [Para 42][Para 131]. The deduction imposed on the named guard appears as USD 30 and then as USD 20 in the same account, and the USD 30 figure is tied to "September 2010" while the wage outcome is USD 70 against a normal USD 90 [Para 110][Para 116][Para 138][Para 139]. In the signature block the worker-appointed arbitrator's name is garbled and the presiding arbitrator's name appears in a corrupted form (the header form is used above), and a stray figure marker "2017" and repeated "ATIO" strings are OCR noise [Para 2][Para 85][Para 145]. Individual workers and union officers are given initials or role only.
