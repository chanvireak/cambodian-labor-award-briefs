# Award No. 137/10 — Ei Sang (Arbitration Council, 12 January 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, presiding) · Doc ID 140341

## 1. Facts
Ei Sang (ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ី សរ៉ាំង), a plastic shoulder-pad factory that began operating on 15 March 2010 with about 199 workers over three shifts, declined to renew the fixed-duration contracts of ten workers when they expired on 18, 19, 22 and 24 November 2010. [Para 3][Para 107][Para 109] The claimant is the Cambodia Asia Union Confederation (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្ពុជា អាស៊ី) and its Ei Sang union, registered on 3 August 2010. [Para 1][Para 46] At the hearing the panel consolidated the two unresolved demands into one issue. [Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1 and Issue 2) Whether the company must renew the ten workers' contracts because work remains and because their non-renewal was anti-union discrimination? [Para 116]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant — Cambodia Asia Union Confederation and its Ei Sang union):** the factory still had work in shift one, including overtime, so the ten contracts should be renewed; and the company refused to renew them to cut union membership just as the union was preparing to apply for most-representative status on 26 October 2010, while it renewed probationary workers and left the union president alone non-renewed. [Para 2][Para 118][Para 120][Para 133]
**Employer (respondent — the Ei Sang company, represented by its vice general manager and administrative staff):** orders had fallen sharply, production lines were cut, and the ten contracts simply reached their term; notice was given lawfully, no new workers were hired, no discrimination occurred, and thirteen contracts were in fact renewed, eight of them union members. [Para 3][Para 121][Para 129][Para 131]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to order renewal of the ten fixed-duration contracts and found no anti-union discrimination. [Para 185]

The panel began with Article 73(1) of the Labour Law, under which a fixed-duration contract expires of its own force when its term falls due. [Para 147] Following its award in Case No. 100/07 (Hou Yia Cambodia), it held that expiry extinguishes the parties' obligations and neither side can compel the other to renew without agreement. [Para 148][Para 150][Para 152] The ten contracts had reached their term and notice had been given, so the employment relationship ended. [Para 153][Para 155]

That did not dispose of the discrimination claim, since a lawful-looking non-renewal is unlawful if union membership or activity caused it. [Para 160] The panel cited Article 12 and Article 279 of the Labour Law, which forbid an employer to use union membership or union activity in decisions on hiring and dismissal, and award No. 10/03 (Zack Sin Tich), which treats a non-renewal based on union activity as contrary to the Labour Law and the Council's jurisprudence. [Para 157][Para 158][Para 159]

On evidence, the panel applied its usual rule that the party alleging discrimination carries the burden of proving it, and that it decides the question by weighing the hearing explanations against the documents. [Para 161][Para 163][Para 165] It found the workers' case internally inconsistent: the twenty explanatory letters said work continued in the shoulder-pad section, but the pleadings and hearing answers did not align with them. [Para 174][Para 176] Against that, the company produced supervisor assessments dated 2 November 2010 rating each worker's performance and conduct and recommending renewal or not, renewed thirteen contracts including eight union members, hired no new staff, and had also ended ten contracts in September and about ten in October. [Para 137][Para 140][Para 173][Para 178][Para 179] The panel therefore could not conclude that union discrimination caused the ten non-renewals, and dismissed. [Para 180][Para 183][Para 184]

The award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification absent written objection to the Minister of Labour. [Para 186]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed-duration contract ends by operation of Article 73(1) at its term, so the Council will not compel renewal; but a non-renewal is unlawful where union membership or activity was the reason, and the worker who alleges that bears the burden of proving it on the documents and hearing evidence. [Para 150][Para 159][Para 165]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that an employer which renewed eight union members while cutting contracts across three consecutive months had visibly assessed workers on performance rather than union status. [Para 179][Para 180]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name is printed three ways — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ី សរ៉ាំង in the party block, អ៊ី ស៊ាំង throughout the findings, and អ៊ីសាំង in one witness statement — with no correction anywhere. [Para 1][Para 108][Para 144]

Union membership is given as 115 on the union's own notification and as 88 after the employer's November deduction, and the award proceeds without reconciling the two. [Para 114][Para 115] The evidence list collapses two numbered items into a single line, so the item numbering runs from 2 straight into 3 without a break. [Para 46] An English fragment is embedded in the Khmer at the membership passage, and another sits in the passage citing award No. 10/03. [Para 114][Para 159] The conciliation report is dated 17 November 2010 but recorded as received at the secretariat on 8 December 2010, an unexplained gap the award does not address. [Para 9] In the signature block the employer-side arbitrator's name is corrupted, though the header names him as Ing Sothy. [Para 187] Worker names appearing in the evidence schedule are given here by role.
