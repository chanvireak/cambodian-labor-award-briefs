# Award No. 30/10 — Global Apparel (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 9 April 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom for the employer party, Liv Sovanna for the worker party, Kong Phalla chair) · Doc ID 140257

## 1. Facts
The Khmer Youth Trade Union at the Global Apparel factory in Ang Snuol district, Kandal province, filed a complaint with the provincial Department of Labour on 28 January 2010 seeking improved working conditions; conciliation on 3 March 2010 settled 8 of the 10 points in issue, and non-conciliation report No. 140/10 of 17 March 2010 reached the secretariat on 18 March 2010. [Para 11][Para 12][Para 8] Two points stayed unresolved: the workers asked the company to cancel the fixed-duration labour contracts, and to define the role and the limits of the work of Mr R; the company refused to cancel the contracts and asked the panel first to observe Mr R's actual conduct. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 4][Para 5]

The hearing was fixed for 5 April 2010 at 14:00. The employer attended through two human-resources staff holding an authorization of 29 March 2010; the workers' party was absent and had appointed no representative at all. [Para 10][Para 14][Para 15][Para 37] The panel therefore examined only whether it could close the file, and expressly found itself unable to establish any fact relating to the two demands because of that absence. [Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the three conditions for terminating the arbitration by award were satisfied — that the party was duly notified of the date, time and place of the proceedings, that it failed to appear and appoint a representative, and that it gave no valid reason for its absence? [Para 28]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company should cancel the fixed-duration contracts, and should state the role and the boundaries of Mr R's work; the union never appeared to argue either point. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 37]
**Employer (respondent):** it will not agree to cancel fixed-duration contracts, and on Mr R it asked the panel to observe his conduct in practice before ruling. [Para 3][Para 5]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel ordered the closure of case No. 30/10-Global Apparel, the workers' party having been absent from the hearing without reason. [Para 43][Para 44]

The panel grounded the decision on Prakar 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, which allows it either to continue in the absence of a party or to terminate the arbitration by issuing an award, subject to that party having been properly notified. [Para 23][Para 24] It read rule 4.7 of the Council's procedural rules, annexed to the same Prakas, as imposing the same condition. [Para 23] Drawing on its earlier awards — Nos. 30/05, 16/07, 27/07, 95/08, 132/08 and 09/09 — it identified three cumulative conditions that must be met before a case can be closed. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27]

On the first condition, the secretariat had issued formal written invitations, No. 175 to the employer and No. 176 to the workers, both dated 25 March 2010, in the ordinary course, and had in addition contacted the parties by telephone about the date, time and place; the panel found both parties duly notified. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33] On the second, Prakar 19 lets a party appear in person, with an accompanying person, through a member of the Bar, or through any other person holding written authorization, and Prakar 20 obliges the parties to attend every meeting to which the panel summons them; the workers neither attended nor appointed a federation or a lawyer to represent them. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 37][Para 38] On the third, the workers gave the secretariat no valid reason whatever for staying away. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41] All three conditions being fulfilled, the panel closed the case. [Para 42][Para 43] The two unresolved demands were never examined on their merits.

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel may terminate a collective dispute by award rather than deciding it where three cumulative conditions are each established on the record: written notification of the date, time and place of the hearing, absence of the party and of any properly authorized representative, and the absence of any valid reason for that absence. Notification by telephone is corroborative only; the formal written invitation is what satisfies the first condition. Once the case is closed on this ground the demands are never reached. [Para 25][Para 29][Para 31][Para 34][Para 39][Para 42]

**Obiter dicta:** the observation that Prakar 20 obliges parties to attend every meeting convened by the panel adds a duty of attendance but does not itself supply the power to close a file. [Para 35]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The complaint is described as containing 20 demands at [Para 11] but the conciliation as covering 10 points at [Para 12]; the number actually unresolved, two, is consistent only with the second figure.
- Evidence item 3 is the internal regulations of "Fasca (Cambodia)" dated 7 December 2004, an enterprise that is not a party to this case, listed among the respondent's documents. [Para 17]
- The Prakas date is printed as 21 April 2004 in most places but corrupted to "2019" and "2004" doubled in the quotations at [Para 23] and [Para 34], and the Prakas is mis-titled there as one on the "company employing arbitrators".
- The non-conciliation report number appears as "140/10" at [Para 19] and as an OCR-mangled "14()/10" at [Para 8]; the hearing time is 14:00 at [Para 10] but printed as 02:00 in the afternoon at [Para 13].
- The precedent list at [Para 25] has garbled party names and doubled numbers ("លេខ ២៤ ១៦/០៧"), so the awards are cited by number only.
- The signature block at [Para 45] is overwritten by Latin scribbles; the chair's and worker-party arbitrator's names are taken from the face of the award, and the worker-party representative line records only "absent".
