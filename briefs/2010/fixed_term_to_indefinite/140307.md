# Order No. 91/10 — Nue Vy D (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd v. Khmer Democratic Workers Union Federation (Arbitration Council, 30 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Eing Sothy, An Vireak, Kong Phalla) · Doc ID 140307

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូ វាយ ដ៏ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន — Nue Vy D (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd — employs more than 1,000 workers at Tuol Pongro, Dangkao, Phnom Penh, and has a collective agreement registered on 4 January 2010 with the Khmer Democratic Workers Union (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ ខ្មែរ) and its factory union. [Para 48][Para 56][Para 57] Of 16 demands, two were settled at conciliation on 27 July 2010 and ten more at the hearing of 17 August 2010, leaving issues 3, 7, 10 and 13: the conversion of long-serving workers' fixed-term contracts, pay and attendance bonus for two days of strike, the date wages are paid, and seating at meetings. [Para 38][Para 42][Para 43] The conciliation report reached the secretariat on 10 August 2010. [Para 39]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must convert to indefinite-term contracts the fixed-term contracts of workers whose successive contracts total more than two years? [Para 58][Para 114]
2. (Issue 7) Whether the employer must pay wages and the regular attendance bonus for 7 and 8 July 2010, when the workers struck? [Para 82][Para 158]
3. (Issue 10) Whether the employer must pay monthly wages on the 5th of the following month rather than at any point between the 5th and the 10th? [Para 96][Para 177]
4. (Issue 13) Whether the employer must provide adequate seating when it convenes meetings with section heads, team leaders and worker representatives? [Para 104][Para 182]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), Khmer Democratic Workers Union Federation and the Nue Vy D factory union:** three workers present at the hearing had each served more than two years — one just over two years, one three years, one four — so their contracts must become indefinite-term under the law, the collective agreement's Article 11 notwithstanding. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 75] They also seek wages and the attendance bonus for the two strike days, a fixed pay date of the 5th because payday banditry follows the predictable 10th, and proper chairs, since workers at the cramped ground-floor meeting room must kneel on bricks or stand out to the doorway. [Para 97][Para 98][Para 107]

**Employer (respondent), Nue Vy D (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd:** it hires on six-month fixed-term contracts, renews them and pays 5% termination allowance at each renewal, and under Article 11 of the collective agreement each renewal stays within the two-year maximum, so the contracts remain fixed-term however long the worker has served. [Para 59][Para 71][Para 72][Para 73] It also says no wages or bonus are owed for 7 and 8 July 2010 because the workers did not come to work, and the strike was called while the case was still before the Council; wages are in fact paid somewhere between the 5th and 10th under Article 22 and it cannot guarantee payment by the 5th because payroll is technical and funds arrive from abroad; the upstairs meeting room cannot be used without disturbing technical and accounting staff, and splitting sessions wastes working time. [Para 88][Para 93][Para 101][Para 109]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — Issue 3 granted by ordering conversion of the three workers' fixed-term contracts to indefinite-term; Issue 7 refused; Issue 10 refused; Issue 13 granted by ordering the employer, within one month, to hold meetings in the larger upstairs room, or split them into two shifts, or find another suitable means of providing proper seating. [Para 196][Para 197][Para 198][Para 199]

On Issue 3 the panel set out Article 67(2) of the Labour Law, capping a fixed-term contract and any renewal at two years, and Article 73(5), deeming a contract indefinite where the aggregate renewal period exceeds that limit, and followed Awards 10/03 (Zacksintic), 155/09 (USA), 21/10 and 70/10 (Menhattan). [Para 116][Para 119][Para 121][Para 122] It then found that Article 11 of the collective agreement — which allows renewal so long as each renewal is within two years — contradicts Articles 67(2) and 73(5), because the statute speaks of the aggregate and not each renewal, and under Article 5 of the 1988 Law on Contracts any agreement contrary to law is void. [Para 130][Para 131][Para 133][Para 136][Para 140] The employer conceded the three workers had each served over two years, so their contracts are indefinite-term. [Para 124][Para 125] On the related 5% point, the panel held the employer owes no 5% allowance for fixed-term contracts once the contract is indefinite, and may even recover the 5% already paid. [Para 155][Para 156]

On Issue 7 the panel applied Article 332(1), which suspends the employment contract during a strike and provides that no work and no wages are given, and Article 72(1) on the effect of suspension, and followed Award 49/05 (Oceangate Garment). [Para 159][Para 161][Para 164] It held that whether the strike was lawful or not, no wages or bonus are owed for strike days, and added that this strike, called while arbitration was running, breached Article 320(4)'s requirement to exhaust peaceful means. [Para 162][Para 168][Para 169] Article 3 of Notice No. 017 of 18 July 2000 conditions the attendance bonus on working the required days in the month, so the bonus fell away too. [Para 170][Para 171][Para 175]

On Issue 10 the panel construed Article 22 of the collective agreement, which requires payment between the 5th and the 10th, found the employer had paid within that window, and refused a demand that would rewrite the agreement. [Para 177][Para 179][Para 181] On Issue 13 the panel applied Article 1 of Prakas No. 053 of 10 February 2000, requiring suitable seating wherever the work calls for a sitting posture, accepted from photographs that the ground-floor room is too cramped for 30 to 40 people at meetings lasting 30 to 60 minutes four to six times a month, and ordered one of three remedies within a month. [Para 183][Para 187][Para 188][Para 191][Para 195] The order is stated to be binding rather than provisional. [Para 201]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A clause in a collective agreement that permits successive fixed-term contracts so long as each individual renewal stays within two years is void as contrary to Articles 67(2) and 73(5) of the Labour Law, which measure the two-year ceiling by the aggregate duration of the successive contracts; once the aggregate passes two years the contract is indefinite-term and no 5% fixed-term allowance is owed. [Para 133][Para 140][Para 155] Wages and the monthly attendance bonus are not payable for days on which workers strike, whether the strike is lawful or not, because Article 332 suspends the contract. [Para 162][Para 169]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel said the employer might claim back the 5% allowances it had already paid; that the 7–8 July 2010 strike, called mid-arbitration, breached Article 320(4); and that meetings of an hour or less are not so frequent as to make upstairs use disruptive. [Para 156][Para 168][Para 192]

The arbitrator selected by the employer, Eing Sothy, appended a dissent on Issue 3, reasoning that the employer may choose the contract type, that paying 5% at each renewal protects the worker, and that conversion would make the employer pay twice. [Para 204][Para 206][Para 210][Para 214]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's name appears in at least five variant spellings, including forms that drop the final consonant and forms inserting an extra syllable, and one passage prints "New" in Latin script in the middle of a Khmer sentence. [Para 1][Para 43][Para 179] Dates are unstable: the strike year is printed as both 2010 and 2019 in the Issue 7 heading, and one Article citation is rendered as "19 28 October 1988". [Para 136][Para 158] The signature block names a fourth arbitrator who is not on the panel as constituted and corrupts the employer-side arbitrator's name into a glyph cluster, while the dissent annexure spells that same name three different ways. [Para 202][Para 216] Several paragraphs are duplicated verbatim with one clause repeated inside a single sentence, and the residual 5% discussion restarts mid-thought. [Para 122][Para 158][Para 199] The three workers seeking conversion are named in full with their exact service lengths; this brief uses their roles. [Para 68][Para 124]
