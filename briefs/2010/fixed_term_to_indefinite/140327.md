# Order No. 122/10-Meng Yan — Meng Yan Garment New Factory & Wet Processing Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង យ៉ាន់ ហ្គាមេន ញូហ្វាក់ ថួរី & វែត ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង អិលធីឌី), Kandal (Arbitration Council, 22 December 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding; ម៉ារ សំបូរ ណា, employer nominee; ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់, worker nominee) · Doc ID 140327

## 1. Facts
Meng Yan Garment New Factory & Wet Processing Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង យ៉ាន់ ហ្គាមេន ញូហ្វាក់ ថួរី & វែត ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង អិលធីឌី) at village 3, Svay Rolum commune, Sa'ang district, Kandal, employs about 500 workers, more than 90 per cent of them on three-month fixed-duration contracts. [Para 1][Para 61][Para 69] The claimant was the Cambodian Democratic Garment Workers' Union Federation (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា) and its Meng Yan factory union, which said it had about 200 members; the company said it knew of no members because the union held no registration and no dues were deducted. [Para 1][Para 62][Para 63] Its complaint of 22 September 2010 raised three demands which conciliation on 22 October 2010 left unsettled and which the panel merged into one point: that three workers be taken back with back wages, and that discrimination against the union's leaders, activists and members stop. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 15][Para 64]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issues 1, 2 and 3) Whether the company must take back the three dismissed workers, pay them back wages, and cease discriminating against the union's leaders, activists and members? [Para 64][Para 122]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា and its factory union:** the three workers' three-month contracts were allowed to lapse because of their union role; the company went on renewing other workers in the same sections — three in the ironing section who were not members, and others elsewhere — so the failure to renew theirs was anti-union discrimination; the union also represented the absent third claimant, who was a member but ill. [Para 67][Para 71][Para 75][Para 97][Para 99][Para 109][Para 149]
**Employer (respondent) — Meng Yan Garment New Factory & Wet Processing Co., Ltd:** it dismissed nobody; the three-month contracts simply expired, and the company did not renew 37 fixed-duration contracts at once, among them members and non-members alike, because there was no work; it did not discriminate against any union and had no reason to, since two other unions with proper registration already existed in the factory; it added that one claimant thumbprinted an agreement ending his contract and then refused the compensation, and that new administration staff were still checking the renewal lists. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 70][Para 72][Para 74][Para 95][Para 100][Para 101][Para 102][Para 112][Para 150][Para 152][Para 153]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand that the company take back the two remaining claimants and pay them back wages, and refused the demand that it be found to have discriminated against the union. [Para 171][Para 172][Para 173]
First the panel examined whether the claimant union could act at all. Article 268 requires a professional organisation to deposit its statutes and the list of its responsible officers with the Ministry in order to be registered, and provides that if the Ministry does not respond within two months of a complete application the organisation is deemed registered. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129][Para 131] The panel found the founding election was held on 23 August 2010 and the registration application receipt issued on 8 November 2010, so at the hearing of 29 November 2010 less than two months had run and the union was not registered and could not represent its members. [Para 132][Para 133][Para 134][Para 135] It also found that the absent claimant, who was ill, had given the union no written authority, which Article 19 of Prakas 099 requires of a representative, and so it considered the demand only for the two workers who attended. [Para 136][Para 138][Para 139]
On discrimination the panel applied Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law, which forbid an employer to use union membership or union activity as a ground for discipline or termination, and followed Orders 155/09-USA, 10/03 and 112/06 in holding that the worker bears the burden of proving the discrimination. [Para 142][Para 143][Para 144][Para 146] It found the company had declined to renew 37 fixed-duration contracts at the same time, including members of this union and workers who were not members, so the inference of anti-union motive was not made out. [Para 153][Para 154][Para 155]
On the non-renewal itself the panel applied Article 73, under which a fixed-duration contract expires automatically at its term and neither party can force renewal without agreement, and Article 73(k)(5), which requires ten days' notice only where the contract exceeds six months. [Para 156][Para 157][Para 160][Para 162] Because these were three-month contracts, no notice was owed, and the panel found the employer had complied with the Labour Law in not renewing. [Para 165][Para 168][Para 169][Para 170] The order is not binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects to the Minister. [Para 174]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union that has applied for registration but is still inside the Ministry's two-month response period under Article 268 is not yet registered and cannot represent its members before the panel; and a fixed-duration contract of less than six months expires by itself under Article 73, so non-renewal is lawful without notice, and is not anti-union discrimination where the employer let lapse a batch of contracts covering members and non-members alike. [Para 131][Para 135][Para 165][Para 170]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted the employer's evidence that two other unions — the Khmer Youth Voice Union and the Khmer Union — were operating with proper registration in the factory, and its statement that new staff were still checking the renewal lists; neither was needed once the claimants' own union was found unregistered. [Para 63][Para 88][Para 100]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case is numbered 122/10 on the face but the employer's defence and its covering letter are titled "case No. 522/10", and the Ministry's conciliation report carries 629/10. [Para 1][Para 17][Para 31][Para 55] The number of workers whose contracts were not renewed is printed as 37 in the reasoning and the pleadings but as "237 workers in August 2010" in one answer. [Para 70][Para 74][Para 111][Para 153] The date of the claimants' filing is printed with two years spliced together, "06 December 2019 ... 2010". [Para 132] The panel is constituted under Prakas 190 of 2 September 2009 (seventh term), not the eighth-term Prakas cited in the neighbouring orders of this batch. [Para 4] The employer nominee's name is printed "ម៉ារ សំបូរ ណា" in the header of this order and a variant spelling elsewhere in the library. [Para 1][Para 175] The evidence list reproduces the full names of some thirty individual workers in dismissal notices and complaints; those names are not reproduced here, and no worker, union official or employer representative is named in this brief. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]
