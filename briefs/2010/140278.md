# Award No. 55/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន (ខាំបូឌា) អែល ធី ឌី (Arbitration Council, 6 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Li Tai Seng, appointed by the employer; An Virak, appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 140278

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន (ខាំបូឌា) អែល ធី ឌី, a garment factory at Prek Ta Nu, Chak Angre Leu, Meanchey, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1995 and employs about 1,166 workers under seven unions, none of them most representative. [Para 53][Para 54][Para 55][Para 56] The claimant is សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន, with about 777 members. [Para 57][Para 58] Since June 2009 the company has withheld a day's wage and the USD 5 attendance bonus from any worker whose timecard lacks the Chinese group leader's signature, even where the worker clocked in and out properly. [Para 61][Para 62][Para 63] Nine demands went through failed conciliation on 25 May 2010; at the hearing of 10 June 2010 the union withdrew Issue 3, leaving eight for decision. [Para 29][Para 33][Para 34]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must abolish the requirement of a Chinese group leader's signature on the timecards of workers who have already clocked in and out? [Para 61][Para 150]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must stop using transfers, half-pay suspension and forced annual leave to push workers into resigning? [Para 77][Para 171]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must pay 15 minutes of wage and the USD 5 bonus to a worker who clocked out on 9 March 2010 but was recorded as leaving early? [Para 80][Para 178]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the company must pay half an hour of wage and the USD 5 bonus to twelve packing-section workers who left their workstations to ask about the union president? [Para 96][Para 201]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the company must restore annual leave deducted from four workers for 25 to 31 March 2010? [Para 107][Para 221]
6. (Issue 7) Whether the company is liable for a group leader's statement that a named worker would be beaten? [Para 119][Para 236]
7. (Issue 8) Whether the company must pay one day's wage and the USD 5 bonus for 26 March 2010 to the worker whose leave request had been approved? [Para 129][Para 245]
8. (Issue 9) Whether the company must pay one worker's March 2010 wage inside the ordinary payroll table? [Para 142][Para 260]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព សេរី កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន:** the timecard signature rule is pressure, since workers who properly clocked out still lose pay; the company manoeuvres workers into resigning to escape statutory settlements; one worker was docked for 15 minutes although she had finished her day; and the packing workers only asked about their union president. [Para 66][Para 67][Para 77][Para 101][Para 201]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន តាក ហ្វាត ហ្គាមិន:** the signature requirement is internal management to check who leaves the machines, announced by microphone and applied under Notice No. 017 and the internal rules; the twelve packing workers left their posts without permission, which is irregular work; and transfers follow production needs. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 76][Para 103][Para 172]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must abolish the group-leader-signature condition; pay 15 minutes of wage and the USD 5 bonus to the worker concerned, and one day's wage plus bonus for 26 March 2010 to another; and restore the annual leave deducted from four workers; the other claims were dismissed. [Para 273][Para 275][Para 277][Para 279][Para 280]

On Issue 1 the panel held that Article 2(2) lets the employer manage the enterprise and set rules for smooth production only where those rules are lawful and reasonable. [Para 154][Para 156] The internal rules do forbid leaving the workplace without notice, but they attach no penalty, and the company turned the missing signature into a deduction of a full day's wage and the attendance bonus — a fine, which Article 28 forbids as a double punishment for one fault and Article 27 requires to be proportionate. [Para 161][Para 162][Para 163][Para 166][Para 167] The rule was therefore unlawful and ordered abolished. [Para 169][Para 273]

On Issues 2 and 7 the panel held that both complaints concerned feared future conduct rather than an existing dispute — no worker had yet been forced out, and the named worker was still working normally with no incident — and that the Council exists to settle existing labour disputes, not hypothetical ones, so it declined to decide them. [Para 174][Para 176][Para 177][Para 239][Para 242][Para 244]

On Issue 4 the employer alleged the worker left 15 minutes early, but its own evidence showed group leaders distribute timecards 20 minutes before the end of work and that other workers were at the clock machines at the same time; it could not show she left early, so she was paid in full attendance and recovered the 15 minutes and the USD 5. [Para 89][Para 185][Para 189][Para 191][Para 200] On Issue 5, by contrast, the twelve packing workers left their stations together to press a demand about their union president — conduct the panel found equivalent to a strike, and one made without the seven days' prior notice Article 324 requires; under Articles 318 and 332 a strike suspends the contract and wages are not paid, so the deduction stood. [Para 202][Para 203][Para 208][Para 216][Para 220]

On Issue 6 the panel applied Article 170: annual leave is taken at the Khmer New Year unless the parties agree otherwise and the employer notifies the labour inspector in advance. [Para 221][Para 226][Para 233] The parties had agreed a half-and-half split year by year, and the company had already used its half, so it could not cut further into the workers' remaining days, especially as the inspector was never notified. [Para 230][Para 232][Para 234][Para 235] On Issue 8 the worker's leave had been authorised in writing by her group leader and the administration head and orally by accounts; the company's later discovery that she had no 2009 balance left was its own error and could not be charged to her. [Para 252][Para 255][Para 256][Para 258][Para 259]

On Issue 9 the panel held that the employer's power to organise includes choosing how wages are paid, provided that choice is reasonable and lawful, and paying one worker separately from the payroll table cost her no statutory benefit; the claim was refused. [Para 268][Para 270][Para 272]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer's management right under Article 2(2) does not extend to turning an administrative rule into a wage penalty: where the internal rules prescribe no sanction, withholding a day's wage and the attendance bonus for a missing group-leader signature is a fine contrary to Articles 27 and 28 and must be abolished. Workers who leave their stations together to press a collective demand without the seven days' notice required by Article 324 are treated as striking, and their wages for that time are not owed under Article 332. [Para 156][Para 162][Para 167][Para 216]

**Obiter dicta:** the remarks that annual leave exists so families can gather at the Khmer New Year, and that the employer should verify leave balances before authorising absence, are general guidance rather than holdings. [Para 223][Para 255]

- Issue 3 exists in the demand list but is absent from the findings and the decision; only the withdrawal at the hearing explains the gap [Para 9][Para 33].
- The decision on Issue 6 orders restoration for five workers but names only four, while the claim was brought for four [Para 107][Para 277].
- Page furniture interrupts the text at [Para 160] and [Para 161], and a passage at [Para 164] is left with unclosed brackets and stray syllables; the section on Issue 1 refers to "វិផាន" for "វិធានការ" [Para 156][Para 164].
