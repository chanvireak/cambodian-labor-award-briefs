# Award No. 61/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ុយ ចៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ខប (Arbitration Council, 5 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Eng Sothy, appointed by the employer; An Non, appointed by the workers; Kong Phallha, chair) · Doc ID 140280

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ុយ ចៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ខប is a garment factory at Chum Ampil, Kbal Koh, Kien Svay, Kandal province, registered in 2006. [Para 1][Para 51] The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ and its factory union, which filed 29 demands on 25 May 2010; the provincial labour office failed to conciliate them on 11 June 2010 and forwarded them to the Council on 16 June 2010. [Para 2][Para 35][Para 36][Para 37] The hearing was set for 1 July 2010 at 14:00. [Para 35][Para 38] The employer attended with a written authorisation for its accompanying person, Mr M, who was also an official of the Khmer Federation — the claimant's own federation — and the claimant's representatives walked out of the hearing because they would not accept that person, so the panel decided none of the 29 demands on their merits. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 59][Para 64]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Procedural question) Whether the panel could proceed or must close the file where the claimant union left the hearing because it objected to the employer's written authorised accompanying person, and refused any settlement? [Para 67][Para 68][Para 74][Para 75]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** their 29 demands concern working conditions, maternity protection, annual leave, wages, transport and discrimination; they refused to sit through the hearing while the employer was accompanied by an official of their own federation, whom they had not authorised, and withdrew. [Para 2][Para 39][Para 59]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ុយ ចៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ខប:** it attended on proper invitation and produced a written authorisation for its accompanying person, as the Council's rules allow; the claimant's absence was its own choice. [Para 64][Para 67]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed Case No. 61/10 without ruling on any of the 29 demands. [Para 75][Para 76]

The panel began with Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which lets a party appear in person or be accompanied by a lawyer of the Bar or by any other person holding that party's written authorisation. [Para 62][Para 63] It found the employer had given exactly such an authorisation to its accompanying person and that the Prakas nowhere prescribes what kind of person an accompanying person must be, so the employer's representation was regular. [Para 64][Para 65]

Article 21 of the same Prakas and rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules allow a panel, where a properly invited party fails to appear without good reason, either to continue in that party's absence or to bring the arbitration to an end by issuing an award; the condition is that the party was correctly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70] Following Award No. 147/09, the panel held that a claimant's non-attendance before the proceedings begin can be treated as a failure to appear or to be represented. [Para 71][Para 73] Here the claimant had been invited, appeared, and then chose to leave rather than accept the panel's handling of the case; it therefore declined the panel's settlement, and the panel closed the file. [Para 74][Para 75][Para 76]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A party's right to be accompanied under Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 is satisfied by any person holding that party's written authorisation, and the opposing party's objection to the identity of that person does not make the representation irregular. Where the claimant has been properly notified and then walks out, refusing both the hearing and any settlement, the panel may close the file by award under Article 21 and rule 4.7 rather than decide the demands in the claimant's absence. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 69][Para 73]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the Prakas does not specify what kind of person an accompanying person must be, and its reference to its own earlier ruling in Award No. 147/09 on pre-hearing absence, go beyond the single order closing this file. [Para 65][Para 71]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The company name is printed as ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ុយ ចៀន (ខេមបូឌា) ខប in the party list, and elsewhere as ហ៊ុយ ចៀន alone or with "Li" prefixed; the union's name is truncated as "សហជី(សេរ មួង យុវជន ខ្មែរ" [Para 1][Para 50].
- The employer's director's name is printed in Latin as "Chat Lie Change" in the party list, and the accompanying person's role is the only description of him given [Para 1][Para 60].
- The signature block survives only as Latin noise ("unpines . 20142", "mpires . Shalace"), so the individual signatures cannot be verified [Para 77].
- The chair's name appears in the header as ឥង់ ផល្អៈ, an unusual rendering that is probably a misreading of a Khmer name; the employer-appointed arbitrator is printed អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី [Para 1].
- Dates are corrupted in several places: the conciliation minute is dated "ថ្ងៃទី ១១ ២០១០៩" [Para 57], and Prakas No. 099 is dated 2019 in one quotation of Article 19 while the same Prakas is dated 2004 in the surrounding text [Para 62].
- The award carries no reasons on the 29 demands themselves, so the brief cannot report any findings of fact beyond the procedural history.
