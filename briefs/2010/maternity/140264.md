# Award No. 43/10 — Meng Jian (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉ឺង ជៀន) (Arbitration Council, 2 June 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun; Vann Pov; chair Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 140264

## 1. Facts
Meng Jian (Cambodia) has operated since 2004 and employs 570 workers; the local union of C.CAWDU (សម្ព័ន្ធសហជីពប្រជាធិបតេយ្យកម្មករកាត់ដេរកម្ពុជា) is the claimant and has about 297 members. [Para 22] [Para 23] [Para 24] [Para 25] Of eleven demands, one survived conciliation on 2 April 2010: that the company pay maternity allowance of 50 per cent for the three months of maternity leave, calculated on the average of the total wage received in the last 12 months, including overtime pay. [Para 2] [Para 9] [Para 26] [Para 29] The employer says it already applies the Labour Law on maternity pay, at 50 per cent of base wage plus regular-attendance bonus, living allowance and seniority pay, paid monthly on the 10th, but without overtime. [Para 3] [Para 27] [Para 28] Both employer representatives attended the hearing without a written authority from the director, who was abroad, and the panel therefore heard the case in the employer party's absence. [Para 13] [Para 37] [Para 45] [Para 47]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must pay the 50 per cent maternity allowance for the 90 days of maternity leave in advance, before the leave begins, calculated on the average total monthly wage of the preceding 12 months and including overtime pay in that calculation? [Para 26] [Para 63] [Para 67]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that the allowance should be paid in a lump sum before the leave starts, because the women live far away and the monthly journey to collect it costs them the money they collect, and that the calculation must take in overtime pay as part of total wage. [Para 29] [Para 31] [Para 32] [Para 33] They expressly disclaimed any claim for 50 per cent of wages during the three months before the application is filed. [Para 26]
**Employer (respondent):** that it pays the allowance lawfully each month at 50 per cent of base wage plus attendance bonus, living allowance and seniority pay, but does not include overtime pay in the base. [Para 3] [Para 27] [Para 28] Its two representatives had no written authority to represent the company at the hearing. [Para 37] [Para 45]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the company is ordered to pay the 50 per cent maternity allowance for the 90 days of maternity leave before the worker begins her leave, calculated by taking the total wages of the last 12 months before the leave, dividing by 12 for the average monthly wage, dividing that by two, and multiplying by three, with overtime pay included in the base. [Para 67] [Para 68] [Para 69] [Para 70]

On representation, the panel applied clause 19 of Prakas 99 of 21 April 2004, which lets a party appear in person, through a representative or through any person expressly authorised in writing, and read it with Award 161/09-Prek Treng and Award 71/08-River Rich to mean that a person without written authority from the employer cannot be a lawful representative; it therefore proceeded in the absence of the employer party after the workers asked it to continue. [Para 38] [Para 39] [Para 40] [Para 42] [Para 43] [Para 46] [Para 47]

On the substantive claim, Article 182 of the Labour Law gives women 90 days of maternity leave, and Article 183 gives half the wage, including subsidiary benefits, for that period, but Article 183 paragraph 4 confines the entitlement to women with at least one year's service in the enterprise. [Para 48] [Para 49] [Para 50] [Para 51] [Para 52] [Para 61] Finding Article 183 silent on what the wage is composed of, the panel turned to Article 103, which lists the elements of wage and expressly includes overtime pay (ប្រាក់បន្ថែមម៉ោង), and held that the 50 per cent must therefore be computed on a base that includes overtime. [Para 54] [Para 55] [Para 56] [Para 57] It followed its earlier awards 33/07-Gold Fame, 06/08-King Vann, 68/04 and 18/06 on the 12-month averaging method. [Para 58] [Para 59] [Para 60]

On timing, Article 115 paragraph 3 forbids paying wages on the very day the worker becomes entitled and requires payment one day before where the entitlement falls on a rest day; the panel read this with Awards 08/08, 114/08 and 139/08-Sub Prime Garment to require the whole 50 per cent to be paid before the leave begins. [Para 63] [Para 64] [Para 65] [Para 66]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Article 183 of the Labour Law fixes the maternity entitlement at half the wage including subsidiary benefits, and because Article 103 defines wage to include overtime pay, the 50 per cent must be computed on the average monthly total of the 12 months preceding the leave, overtime included, and not on base wage plus a hand-picked set of allowances; the entitlement belongs only to women with at least one year's service. Under Article 115 the whole sum must be paid before the leave starts rather than month by month, and an employer whose representatives appear without written authority may be treated as absent under clause 19 of Prakas 99. [Para 52] [Para 56] [Para 59] [Para 65] [Para 46]

**Obiter dicta:** that the workers' practical reasons — distance from the factory and the cost of monthly collection — were not the basis of the order, and that the workers had dropped any claim relating to the three months before the application was filed. [Para 26] [Para 31] [Para 32]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a Khmer original; the arbitrator names appear only in Khmer and the Latin forms here are transliterations, one of them partly legible only in the signature block, where it is printed twice in different forms. [Para 1] [Para 72] The reasoning states the entitlement as "ប្រាក់ឈ្នួល ៥០០ ភាគរយ" — 500 per cent — at the point where the rest of the award, including the operative order, says 50 per cent. [Para 57] [Para 67] The employer company is spelled five different ways (ម៉ឺង ជៀន, ម៉ីង ជៀន, មឹង ជៀន, មីង ជាន, ប៉ឹង ជៀន) and the union name is also unstable. [Para 1] [Para 14] [Para 22] [Para 24] The panel records that it decided "in the absence of the employer party" although two representatives attended, the absence being one of authority rather than of persons. [Para 36] [Para 47] The patent number's year is printed as "២០១()" and the award date carries a doubled zero. [Para 1] [Para 15] The procedural section states the union complaint was received on 4 March 2010 but describes the final conciliation as 2 April 2010 while a later passage gives the hearing invitation as 19 April and the hearing as 19 May 2010. [Para 8] [Para 9] [Para 11] OCR corrupts particles and headings throughout ("ប្រវេទេ" for "ប្រភេទ", "មាត្រា" as "មាត្រ" / "ហត្រា"), and the article number 115 is printed as "១១៥ ក" in one place and as a bare "១១៥" in the operative reasoning. [Para 63] [Para 65] [Para 71] Employer representatives are described by role only.
