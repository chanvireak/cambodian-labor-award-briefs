# Award No. 63/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង យ៉ាន់ ហ្គាមេន ញូហ្វាក់ ថូរី & វែត ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង អិលធីឌី (Meng Yan Garment New Frock Tori & Wet Process Sing Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 2 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: អ៊ុក រី (employer-nominated), ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន (worker-nominated), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140283

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង យ៉ាន់ ហ្គាមេន ញូហ្វាក់ ថូរី & វែត ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង អិលធីឌី is a laundry and garment operation at Sway Rolum, Sa'ang district, Kandal, running since November 2007 with about 600 workers. [Para 131][Para 132] Three unions compete there: សហជីព ខ្មែរ, which holds the most-representative certificate, សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ with about 90 members, and សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ with about 32 members. [Para 133][Para 148] សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា (C.CAWDU) filed the complaint on 31 May 2010 alleging that the company had transferred and dismissed its activists; conciliation failed on all three demands on 14 June 2010 and demand 3 was withdrawn at the hearing, leaving two. [Para 18][Para 19][Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must immediately stop all discrimination against សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ, having transferred and dismissed its leaders and members after the union election? [Para 139]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must take back the dismissed activists — Mr M and Mr S — and pay their wages for the period of dismissal? [Para 183]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា (សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ):** after the union election the president was moved from laundry supervision to a pressing machine and reproved when he objected, the vice-president was moved from cutting to pushing carts, and both were left unrenewed at contract expiry; the company twice objected to the union's registration, including on the youth of one candidate, which shows its motive; both men had no warnings and a passing score of 27 on the first evaluation. [Para 139][Para 141][Para 143][Para 144][Para 160][Para 203]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ម៉េង យ៉ាន់ ហ្គាមេន ញូហ្វាក់ ថូរី & វែត ប្រូសេស ស៊ីង អិលធីឌី:** it never discriminated against any union, since three unions now exist and it objects only where a candidate does not meet the law's conditions; the men's three-month contracts simply expired on 5 May 2010 and no one can compel renewal; the two were lazy and disobedient after becoming leaders, and thirteen workers in total were not renewed in May 2010 for poor performance. [Para 3][Para 149][Para 168][Para 171][Para 189]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the company was ordered to stop transferring or dismissing workers on the ground of their union activity, and to take back Mr M and Mr S and pay their wages from the date of dismissal until the date they return to work. [Para 210][Para 211]

On Issue 1 the panel read Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law as barring any use of union membership or union activity in decisions on hiring, discipline or termination, and followed Awards 28/07 (De Fang), 123/07 (E Garment) and 56/09 (Santich). [Para 175][Para 176][Para 179] The panel found as fact that the two leaders were reassigned to different work and then separated after the election, and held that conduct unlawful. [Para 180][Para 181][Para 182]

On Issue 2 the panel accepted that a fixed-term contract expires by effluxion of time and that neither party can compel renewal, following Award 100/07 (Houyia Cambodia) and Article 73(a) §1. [Para 184][Para 185][Para 187] But it also held, following Award 10/03 (Zaksintich), that a decision not to renew taken because of union activity is contrary to the Labour Law, and it placed the burden of proving discrimination on the workers. [Para 193][Para 194][Para 195] That burden was discharged: both men had passed the first evaluation with 27 points, neither had ever been warned, the company's list of thirteen non-renewed workers did not name them, and the company failed to send the evaluation records the panel had ordered on 8 July 2010. [Para 160][Para 171][Para 172][Para 203][Para 207] The panel therefore found the non-renewals were a reaction to the strike of 30 April 2010 and the union election, and ordered reinstatement with back wages. [Para 201][Para 204][Para 208][Para 209]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A fixed-term contract still ends by effluxion of time and its renewal cannot be compelled; but where the workers prove that the decision not to renew was taken because of their union membership or activity, that decision is unlawful and the remedy is reinstatement with wages for the whole period out of work. [Para 187][Para 194] An employer that pleads poor performance or indiscipline as the reason for non-renewal must produce the evaluation and warning records it relies on; a bare assertion that is contradicted by a passing appraisal and the absence of any warning does not rebut the inference of anti-union motive. [Para 195][Para 206]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed generally that proof of anti-union discrimination is assessed from the parties' answers at the hearing and from the documents touching the case, a statement of method rather than of result. [Para 197]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award records that សហជីព ស៊ី ខៅ ឌូ had still not obtained a registration receipt from the Ministry of Labour, the first election having been blocked over a candidate's age and the second over litigation against three of its leaders, yet nowhere addresses whether an unregistered union could bring the complaint. [Para 136][Para 137][Para 138] The employer's name is spelled in at least two Khmer variants across the header and the evidence schedule. [Para 1][Para 31] The evidence list runs to ninety-two items and prints numerous individual workers' full names, which appear here only as initials; several item numbers in that list are misnumbered. [Para 34][Para 115] The decision section repeats each issue heading inside its own operative sentence. [Para 210][Para 211]
