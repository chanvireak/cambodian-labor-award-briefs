# Award No. 106/10 — Hai Fei Sin Co Ltd and Cham Ti Tha Y Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាយ ហ្វេ សិន ខូ អិលធីឌី; ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាម ថិ ថា យ ខូ អិលធីឌី) (Arbitration Council, 29 September 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (You Suon Ty for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Pen Bun Chhea chair) · Doc ID 140313

## 1. Facts
Two companies, Hai Fei Sin Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាយ ហ្វេ សិន ខូ អិលធីឌី) and Cham Ti Tha Y Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាម ថិ ថា យ ខូ អិលធីឌី), hold separate commercial registrations at Village Banla S'at, Sangkat Khmounh, Khan Sen Sok, but their workers are managed by one administration and paid from one accounts section. [Para 59][Para 60] The Khmer Worker Movement Union (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ) and its Cham Ti Tha Y factory union brought ten demands on 31 August 2010; all ten were unresolved at conciliation, but by the hearing of 17 September 2010 issues 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 had been agreed, issue 2 was folded into issue 1, and issue 10 was withdrawn, leaving issues 1 and 5. [Para 17][Para 19][Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] The union was elected on 8 August 2010 but had still not been registered. [Para 63][Para 64]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must take back the union president it dismissed on 28 August 2010 and let him work normally again, as a union leader protected against dismissal? [Para 1][Para 65]
2. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must pay a further 500 riel of meal money, on top of the 1,000 riel already paid, when overtime runs from 19:00 to 21:00? [Para 6][Para 92]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the Khmer Worker Movement Union (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ) and the Cham Ti Tha Y factory union (សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ ឆាម ថិច ថា យ):** the dismissed man was elected union president and the company was notified the next day, so his dismissal was union discrimination; and prices have risen, so all workers doing overtime from 7:00 to 9:00 pm need 500 riel more meal money. [Para 65][Para 69][Para 70][Para 95]
**Employers (respondent), Hai Fei Sin Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហាយ ហ្វេ សិន ខូ អិលធីឌី) and Cham Ti Tha Y Co Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឆាម ថិ ថា យ ខូ អិលធីឌី):** his work as a machine repairer was poor, he repeatedly made mistakes, and the company was reducing staff anyway, so no notice of his union candidacy was ever received; and the extra 500 riel cannot be granted. [Para 76][Para 77][Para 96]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — on issue 1 the panel ordered the employer to take the dismissed union president back into work after the award takes effect; on issue 5 it declined to consider the demand for a further 500 riel of meal money. [Para 169][Para 170]

On issue 1 the panel applied point 3 of Prakas No. 305 SKBY of 22 November 2001, which protects candidates in a union leadership election from dismissal like staff delegates, for 45 days before the poll and 45 days after it if they are not elected, and requires the union to notify the employer of the candidacies by any reliable means. [Para 97][Para 98][Para 99] Following Award 40/10-Meng Yan, it held that for a candidate who is elected the protection is not cut off at 45 days but continues for a reasonable period, at least until the union applies for registration. [Para 101][Para 102][Para 104][Para 105]

The protection still failed, however, because the union said it notified the company on 9 August 2010 through a security guard with badge number 079, and the employer's own security attendance list for that day shows no guard of that number on duty or signing for anything; the workers did not contest that evidence, so the panel found the employer did not know of the candidacy. [Para 107][Para 111][Para 113][Para 115][Para 116]

The dismissal then had to be tested against the internal rules and Article 74. His contract was indefinite, so Article 74 allowed termination only with written notice and a proper reason going to aptitude, conduct or the needs of the enterprise. [Para 117][Para 118][Para 120] Rule 10 of the company's internal regulations requires oral education, then written warning, then dismissal; the employer gave neither education nor warning, admitted it never warned him, and did not notify the labour inspector. [Para 83][Para 121][Para 128][Para 129] Its claim of staff reduction also failed, since the workforce was not in fact being cut and the same employer had been asking him to work overtime. [Para 131][Para 132][Para 133][Para 134] Under point (a) of article 34 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004 the panel ordered his reinstatement in his old or a suitable post. [Para 135]

On issue 5 the panel first asked whether the union could claim for all workers. Under Article 268 a professional organisation acquires the rights the Labour Law recognises only when registered at the Ministry, and this union had applied on 9 August 2010 but held no certificate at the hearing, so it could not represent its members; it also filed no transfer of authority from the workers, so the panel decided the claim for one worker only. [Para 138][Para 140][Para 142][Para 143][Para 147][Para 148]

On the merits, point 3 of Notification No. 745 KKBV of 23 October 2006 preserves the benefits of Notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000, whose point 4 gives 1,000 riel a day or one free meal for voluntary overtime. [Para 149][Para 150][Para 151] The employer already paid 1,000 riel for overtime from 4:30 to 6:30 pm and a further 500 riel where overtime continued to 9:00 pm, a total of 1,500 riel. [Para 156][Para 157][Para 158][Para 159] No law or regulation requires 2,000 riel, so the demand exceeded the law, was an interests dispute, and could not be considered from a union without most-representative status. [Para 160][Para 161][Para 162][Para 163][Para 166][Para 168]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that dismisses a union leader must show both that it knew of the leadership role at the time of dismissal and that it followed its own disciplinary procedure; the statutory protection in point 3 of Prakas No. 305 does not attach where the union's notification never reached the employer, but the dismissal still fails if the internal rules required education and warnings that were never given, and reinstatement follows under article 34(a) of Prakas No. 099. A union not yet registered under Article 268 cannot bring a claim on behalf of its members, and a demand for meal money above the 1,000 riel or one meal fixed by Notification No. 017 is an interests dispute that such a union cannot bring at all. [Para 116][Para 128][Para 135][Para 143][Para 160][Para 162]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the two respondent companies keep separate registrations while sharing one administration and one payroll, and that the union's demand was said to cover every worker in the enterprise, neither of which changed the outcome. [Para 59][Para 60][Para 137]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The dismissed worker is named in full throughout the source; his name is withheld here and he appears by his post. [Para 65] One passage inserts a Latin "Lin" before the first company's name when restating the union's position. [Para 166] A year "២០១៧" intrudes into a quoted paragraph of Prakas No. 099 in the reasoning on representation, and another appears in the party block. [Para 1][Para 145] The evidence list breaks its numbering, running ១៩ and ១០ where 9 and 10 belong, and repeats the two companies' attendance sheets. [Para 34][Para 35] One sentence describing the shared management carries an unexplained Latin acronym. [Para 60] The employer's name is printed both "ខូ អិលធីឌី" and "ខូច អិលធីឌី". [Para 29][Para 30]
