# Award No. 67/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហុង វ៉ាំ កម្ពុជា ហ្គាមីន ម៉ាញូ ហ្វាក់ ឆឺរីង លីមីធីត (ហុង វាំ ថ្មី) (Arbitration Council, 30 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel: អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-nominated), ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់ (worker-nominated), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140285

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហុង វ៉ាំ កម្ពុជា ហ្គាមីន ម៉ាញូ ហ្វាក់ ឆឺរីង លីមីធីត, known as ហុង វាំ ថ្មី, is the 2005 branch of a factory running since 1995, employing about 1,100 workers of whom 1,050 are women. [Para 51][Para 52][Para 53][Para 55] Two unions exist there; the complainant, សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ ហុង វ៉ា ថ្មី, claims about 830 members and asserted most-representative status without ever producing the certificate. [Para 54] សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត filed on 12 May 2010; one of five demands was settled in conciliation, leaving four, and the hearing was held on 6 July 2010. [Para 16][Para 17][Para 18] The employment relationship is built on eight-month fixed-term contracts renewed back to back, with 5 per cent severance and unused annual leave paid at every expiry. [Para 87][Para 88][Para 89]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must allow workers five minutes' grace before deducting wages for lateness on public holidays and Sundays? [Para 67]
2. (Issue 3) Whether maternity pay must be calculated at 50 per cent of average actual earnings over the last twelve months, and paid three months before the leave begins? [Para 81]
3. (Issue 4) Whether the company must pay seniority allowance to workers whose fixed-term contracts have run continuously for one year or more? [Para 87][Para 151]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ ហុង វ៉ា ថ្មី:** a worker six or seven minutes late has thirty minutes of wages taken, which is punishment far heavier than the fault; the company has sixteen attendance scanners and only about ten latecomers a month, so lateness is not a congestion problem; maternity pay must be averaged over the last twelve months' real earnings and paid three months early because workers need the money to travel and to cross the river; and eight-month contracts renewed without a break should count as continuous seniority. [Para 73][Para 74][Para 79][Para 86][Para 91]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហុង វ៉ាំ កម្ពុជា ហ្គាមីន ម៉ាញូ ហ្វាក់ ឆឺរីង លីមីធីត:** ordinary-day lateness is tolerated up to 130 minutes a month and only the actual minutes are cut, but on Sundays and holidays, where overtime pay is generous and the payroll system works in half-hour blocks, a flat thirty minutes is deducted; maternity pay is already paid monthly at 50 per cent of base, seniority and attendance bonus plus the full $6 living allowance, and no law requires payment three months early; and each contract ended with severance paid, so seniority restarts. [Para 76][Para 77][Para 83][Para 85][Para 92]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to deduct wages only for the actual minutes a worker is late when coming in for voluntary Sunday and holiday work, to pay women three months of maternity wages before their leave begins, calculated as half the average of the last twelve months' actual earnings, and to pay the seniority allowance to members of the complainant union whose renewed fixed-term contracts exceed one year; the claim to retain full pay during antenatal check-ups was refused. [Para 168][Para 169][Para 170][Para 171][Para 172]

On Issue 2 the panel applied Articles 27 and 28 of the Labour Law: a penalty must be proportionate to the fault, and an employer may not use wage deduction as a pecuniary disciplinary measure. [Para 112][Para 117] It found the internal rules set no sanction for lateness beyond an obligation to respect working hours, so a flat thirty-minute cut for six or seven minutes was unlawful, and ordered deduction of the actual minutes only. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 125]

On Issue 3 the panel held that Article 183 gives half wages including supplementary pay to a woman with at least one year's service, that Article 115(3) requires payment before the leave rather than on the day it starts, and that the calculation must average the twelve preceding months of actual earnings and halve them, following Awards 57/06, 97/06, 70/09 and 109/09. [Para 127][Para 133][Para 136][Para 138][Para 143] The company's method — adding base wage, seniority and attendance bonus and halving, plus $6 — was held incorrect and replaced by the twelve-month average. [Para 148][Para 149][Para 150]

On Issue 4 the panel applied point 5 of Notification No. 017 SKBY of 18 July 2000, preserved by point 3 of Notification No. 745 KKBV of 23 October 2006, which fixes $2 to $5 a month by length of service and imposes no condition as to contract type. [Para 151][Para 156][Para 158] Paying 5 per cent severance and unused leave at each expiry does not extinguish seniority, which ends only when the worker leaves the enterprise; the panel followed Award 60/10 (Kin Tay) and held that 16 months of back-to-back eight-month contracts give over one year's seniority. [Para 162][Para 165][Para 166] The remedy was confined to members of the complainant union. [Para 167]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may not use deduction from wages as a pecuniary penalty: where a worker is late, only the wages attributable to the minutes actually not worked may be withheld, and a flat block deduction for a few minutes' lateness is unlawful for want of proportionality. [Para 117][Para 125] Seniority runs from continuous service to the enterprise and is not restarted by successive fixed-term contracts or by the payment of 5 per cent severance at each expiry, so a worker on renewed eight-month contracts accrues seniority allowance after one year. [Para 163][Para 165]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel recorded that the factory has sixteen attendance scanners and that only about ten of 1,100 workers arrive late in a month, mostly because they stop at the washroom or canteen before scanning — observations that were not necessary once the legal rule was settled. [Para 79][Para 80]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The panel refused even to consider Issue 1 because the union never produced a most-representative certificate, yet went on to decide Issues 2 to 4 in that union's favour and limited the seniority remedy to its members, which leaves the award internally inconsistent about the union's standing. [Para 54][Para 106][Para 109][Para 167] The attendance bonus is printed as $5 in one place and as the $2.5 deducted in another. [Para 56][Para 93] One citation of Notification No. 017 gives the year as "200" instead of 2000. [Para 152] The evidence schedule lists twenty-one named workers' maternal health books; they appear here only through the issues, and no individual is named. [Para 23][Para 43] The signature block prints the chair's name twice in variant spellings. [Para 174]
