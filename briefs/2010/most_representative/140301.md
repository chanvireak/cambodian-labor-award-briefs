# Award No. 86/10 — Nhu Ming Da (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូ មីង ដា (ខេមបូឌា)) (Arbitration Council, 25 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three: សេង វូច ហ៊ុន (employer-nominated), អន ណន (worker-nominated), អាំង អេង ថុង (chair) · Doc ID 140301

## 1. Facts

The Labour Movement Union Federation (សម្ព័ន្ធសហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ) and its local at the Nhu Ming Da garment factory in Trapeang Thleung, Choam Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh filed three collective demands on 2 July 2010. [Para 2][Para 13] The factory began operating on 27 April 2007 and employs about 1,175 workers; the local union registered on 12 July 2010 with about 239 members and holds no certificate of most-representative status. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 33] Conciliation settled nothing, and the panel heard all three demands on 10 August 2010. [Para 14][Para 18] At the hearing the union narrowed demand one: it asked for a free meal, or 1,500 riel, for overtime worked from 16:00 to 18:00, and abandoned the claim for the 18:00 to 20:30 period. [Para 34][Para 35]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must give a free meal, rather than the 1,000 riel meal allowance, to workers who volunteer for overtime from 16:00 to 18:00? [Para 34][Para 70]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must pay a US$4 monthly housing and transport allowance to every worker in the factory? [Para 47][Para 83]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay US$12 a month in place of providing a nursery, and must in any event let workers place their children in the nursery it has built? [Para 56][Para 96]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant) — សម្ព័ន្ធសហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ and សហជីព ចលនា កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ ញ៉ូ មីង ដា:** 1,000 riel no longer buys a meal now that prices have risen, so the company should give the meal in kind if it will not pay 1,500 riel; the US$4 allowance is paid only to workers renting in blocks the company designates, though everyone must still pay to travel to work; and the company has a nursery it refuses to open because fewer than 15 children have been enrolled. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 52][Para 56][Para 65]

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូ មីង ដា (ខេមបូឌា):** it pays the 1,000 riel that Notification 017 requires and cannot pay 1,500 riel or supply meals because it is about to raise the basic wage; the US$4 allowance is a deliberate incentive for workers to live near the factory so they can do overtime, and no agreement extends it to everyone; and a nursery with fewer than 15 children cannot be run. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 43][Para 51][Para 57][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to provide a free meal to workers doing voluntary overtime from 16:00 to 18:00 and to operate its nursery, allowing workers with children of the qualifying age to place them there, or failing that to pay for an outside nursery; the US$4 monthly allowance was refused without examination. [Para 113][Para 114][Para 115][Para 116]

On the meal, the panel read point 4 of Notification 017 of 18 July 2000, preserved by point 3 of Notification 745 of 23 October 2006, which entitles a volunteer for overtime to 1,000 riel a day or one free meal. [Para 70][Para 71] The text does not say who chooses between the two, so the panel looked to its content and purpose and to Award 47/07 (Jung Fei), which held that the employer must supply the meal. [Para 72][Para 74] It accepted that the employer retains the option of paying the 1,000 riel, but found that the money option is not the primary purpose of the notification. [Para 77] Since 1,000 riel can no longer buy a meal as it could in 2000, the worker may demand the meal in kind, which is not contrary to Notification 017. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 81]

The US$4 allowance failed: no law, agreement or collective agreement provides it, so the demand was an interest dispute, and an interest dispute is not decided for a union that is not the most representative in the enterprise. [Para 84][Para 85][Para 86] With 239 members out of 1,175 and no certificate, this union did not qualify, and Article 277 was applied to confirm it. [Para 87][Para 92][Para 93][Para 94]

On the nursery, Article 186 requires any enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a breastfeeding room and a nursery, and, where it cannot take children over eighteen months, to let the mother place the child in another nursery at the employer's expense. [Para 98][Para 99] The panel held the duty unconditional: nothing in Article 186 sets a minimum number of children before a nursery may operate, so the employer's 15-child rule was wrong. [Para 107][Para 110][Para 111] It ordered the existing nursery to be opened, preserving the outside-nursery alternative. [Para 112]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Where a ministerial notification gives the worker a choice between a cash meal allowance and a meal in kind but fixes the cash sum in a currency that has since lost its purchasing power, the employer may not rely on the cash option alone, and the worker may insist on the meal; and the Article 186 duty to run a nursery for an enterprise employing 100 or more women admits no minimum enrolment threshold, so an employer who has built a nursery must open it. [Para 79][Para 81][Para 107][Para 111]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the 1,000 riel fixed in 2000 no longer buys a meal, a fact it used to justify the meal order but which does not itself vary the notification. [Para 78]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The employer's name appears as ញូ មីង ដា, ញ៉ូ មីង ដា, ញ្ញូ មីង ដា, ញ្ជូំ មីង ដា and ញូ ឆ្នាំ មីង ដា across the award. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 31][Para 94] The demand list records a two-tier claim of 400 riel and a further 500 riel, whereas the claim argued at the hearing and decided was a single meal for the 16:00 to 18:00 period valued at 1,500 riel. [Para 2][Para 34] The age band for children is given as 18 months to 3 years in one place and "over eighteen months" elsewhere, and Article 186 is quoted with the words "eighteen February" in place of "eighteen months". [Para 56][Para 99] The findings refer to the company having four nurseries where the rest of the award speaks of one. [Para 104] One sentence in the employer's account is printed twice verbatim. [Para 64] A stray "2015" appears in the demand summary. [Para 5] The employer arbitrator's name is absent from the signature block. [Para 118]
