# Award No. 81/10 — San Li Fong Garment & Wool Knitting Factory (ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាន់ លីហ្វុង ហ្គាមេន & វូល ឡិនី ធីង ហ្វេក ធើរី) (Arbitration Council, 12 August 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of three: អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer-nominated), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker-nominated), រុន សារ៉ាយ (chair) · Doc ID 140298

## 1. Facts

The San Li Fong garment and wool knitting factory on National Road 2, Chark Angre Leu, Meanchey, Phnom Penh, has operated since 1997 and employs about 550 workers. [Para 23] A single union, សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ សាន់ លី ហ្វុង, is the claimant; it has 320 members, but its certificate of most-representative status expired in 2005 and has not been renewed. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26][Para 27] On 21 June 2010 it filed a single collective demand for a seniority payment (ប្រាក់អតីតភាពការងារ) of US$80 per year of service, payable on resignation, for the 200 workers on indefinite contracts. [Para 9][Para 28][Para 30] Conciliation failed on that one point and the panel heard it on 30 July 2010. [Para 10][Para 14]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. (Sole issue) Whether the employer must pay US$80 per year of service to the 200 workers on indefinite contracts when they resign? [Para 28][Para 40]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ សាន់ លី ហ្វុង:** workers have served for ten to twenty years and should be encouraged to leave with something for their livelihood, so the company should pay US$80 for each year of service on resignation. [Para 29] They acknowledged at the hearing that this was only a request of theirs and rested on no provision of law. [Para 34]

**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាន់ លីហ្វុង ហ្គាមេន & វូល ឡិនី ធីង ហ្វេក ធើរី:** it pays seniority as the Labour Law requires and would pay this further sum if any legal provision required it, but none does. [Para 3][Para 31][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning

**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused to consider the demand and made no order for payment. [Para 49]

The panel took the question as whether any legal provision imposes a duty to pay US$80 per year of service on resignation, and answered that none does. [Para 40] Both sides further agreed that they had never had an agreement or collective agreement providing such an incentive payment. [Para 41][Para 35] The demand therefore exceeded what the law gives, which made it an interest dispute rather than a rights dispute. [Para 42]

On an interest dispute the panel will not decide unless the union bringing it is the most representative in the factory. [Para 43][Para 45] Here the union had 320 members out of about 550 workers, but its most-representative certificate had lapsed in 2005 and no new one had been issued, so it could not obtain a decision on an interest claim. [Para 46][Para 47] The panel followed its earlier awards 84/07 (Yong Wah 2), 143/08 (Cham Thetha), 152/08 (Wilson) and 48/09 (Rosing) on that point. [Para 43]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

A demand for a benefit that no statute, Prakas or collective agreement grants is an interest dispute, and the panel will decline to decide it where the claimant union holds no current certificate of most-representative status, however large its membership; only a current certificate unlocks a decision on interest claims. [Para 42][Para 46][Para 47]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the union's 320 members out of 550 workers was a substantial share, but treated the formal certificate, not the arithmetic, as decisive. [Para 46]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

The employer's name is printed in at least three forms — សាន់ លីហ្វុង, សាន់ លី ហ្វុង and សាន់ លី ហ្វូង — so the entity cannot be fixed from the face of the award alone. [Para 1][Para 24][Para 25] The worker count is given as "46, approximately 550", the "46" being OCR debris. [Para 23] The employer is once called ភាគី និយោជិក (worker party) instead of និយោជក. [Para 33] The reasons repeat the parties' positions twice in near-identical wording, and two lines are visibly duplicated from the recital into the reasoning. [Para 34][Para 37][Para 38] The employer arbitrator's name appears as អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី on the face and អ៊ឹង សុខ្លី in the signature block, and the chair's as រុន សារ៉ាយ and រន សារាយ. [Para 1][Para 52] The last page is largely destroyed. [Para 53]
