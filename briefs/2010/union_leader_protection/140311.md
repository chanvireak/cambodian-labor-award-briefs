# Award No. 103/10 — Vai Thich Garment (Cambodia) (ក្រុមហ៊ុន វាយ ថិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)) (Arbitration Council, 13 September 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, Tuon Sy Chan for the worker party, Run Saray chair) · Doc ID 140311

## 1. Facts
Vai Thich Garment (Cambodia) (ក្រុមហ៊ុន វាយ ថិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)) operates at Village Trapeang Thlang, Sangkat Chom Chao, Khan Dangkao, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] The Cambodian Union Federation for Workers' Rights (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ) and its factory union complained by telephone on 31 August 2010 that workers were striking to have the factory's administration chief removed from the enterprise altogether. [Para 2][Para 8] The employer said it had already agreed to remove him as administration chief but had moved him to purchaser-relations work. [Para 3] Conciliation that day failed on the single demand, and the strike went on despite a Council order of 3 September 2010 to stop and return on 4 September 2010. [Para 9][Para 12][Para 13]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must dismiss the administration chief from the factory entirely, as the striking workers demanded? [Para 2][Para 8]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), the Cambodian Union Federation for Workers' Rights (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្ពុជា ដើម្បី សិទ្ធិ កម្មករ) and its Vai Thich factory union:** the strike would end only if the employer let the eight suspended union leaders return to normal work and withdrew its court complaint against them for incitement to strike. [Para 17][Para 18][Para 19]
**Employer (respondent), Vai Thich Garment (Cambodia) (ក្រុមហ៊ុន វាយ ថិច ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)):** it had already conceded the removal of the administration chief from that post, reassigning him elsewhere, and could not meet the remaining demands without authority from its head office, which had not been given. [Para 3][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel ordered that arbitration proceedings in case 103/10-Vai Thich not continue, so the demand that the administration chief be removed from the factory was left undecided on its merits. [Para 39][Para 40]

The panel began from Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties during the panel's proceedings to suspend every contentious measure, including strikes and lockouts under Article 318 of the Labour Law, and to attend all meetings the panel convenes. [Para 29][Para 30] It read that article, as earlier panels had, to mean that where workers strike while arbitration is running the panel will not carry on with the arbitration. [Para 31][Para 32]

It then reviewed the Council's own line of awards — 04/04-MSI, 77/05-Frank Cooney Thing Garment, 28/06-GDM and 34/06-Gold Fame, with 97/07-Sout Vey, 63/08-Sovann Bery and Golden Golden — in which proceedings were closed because the strike continued during the panel's procedure. [Para 33][Para 35][Para 36]

Applying that practice, the panel found that neither party had shown a genuine intention to have the collective dispute settled by arbitration: the workers kept striking after the order of 3 September 2010 and the employer kept the eight union leaders suspended and pressed its court complaint. [Para 37][Para 38] Because the panel's own hearing could not proceed and no findings of fact on the disputed issue had been made, it decided not to continue. [Para 28][Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers continue a strike after the panel has ordered it suspended and have not returned to work, the panel will not continue the arbitration and the case is closed without any ruling on the merits; the same applies where the employer keeps the disputed suspensions and court proceedings alive, since neither party is then genuinely submitting the dispute to arbitration. A later panel may take the dispute up again once the strike has actually stopped, which is why closing the file is not a decision for or against the demand. [Para 31][Para 34][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that the employer had already given up the administration chief's post while keeping him in the enterprise, and that the union's remaining conditions concerned the eight suspended leaders rather than the demand in the reference — observations it did not need to decide. [Para 3][Para 17]

[Para 1][Para 24][Para 25] One arbitrator's name is printed differently in the header and in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 41] The paragraph numbering breaks down mid-file: the procedural history reuses "4" and the signature block is followed by a page marker only.
