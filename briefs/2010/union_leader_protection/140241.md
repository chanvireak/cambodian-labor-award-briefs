# Award No. 09/10 — Jung Hao Garment Factory (Arbitration Council, 15 February 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Seng Vouchhun (employer-appointed), Ven Pov (worker-appointed), Kong Phalla (president) · Doc ID 140241

## 1. Facts
Jung Hao is a garment factory on Veng Sreng road, Steung Mean Chey, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employing roughly 400 to 500 workers and hosting two plant unions, of which the Democratic Garment Workers Union is the claimant here [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]. Its president, a mechanic engaged on 26 March 2008 at an average wage of USD 150 to 160, was among the leaders elected at the union election of 15 November 2009 [Para 33][Para 34][Para 36]. The union notified the company of the election record before 8 a.m. on 16 November 2009 and a security officer signed for the letter [Para 37][Para 65]. That day the company met him twice, told him it would dismiss him, offered USD 420, and dismissed him; he took the money [Para 39][Para 42][Para 43]. Conciliation failed on 14 January 2010 and the hearing was held on 2 February 2010 [Para 10][Para 9].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the dismissal of the elected plant union president complied with the legal procedure for dismissing a protected worker, and whether he must be reinstated with full wages from dismissal to return? [Para 33][Para 51][Para 47]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the dismissal was anti-union discrimination, coming one day after the election, and the company knew of his office [Para 44][Para 64]. He never wanted to leave, asked the company to reconsider the USD 420, and now asks for reinstatement to the same post with wages for the whole period out [Para 40][Para 47].

**Employer (respondent):** there was no discrimination; he was an ordinary worker, not a union leader, because the plant union had no registration certificate, and he had already taken the compensation paid to him [Para 3][Para 4][Para 45]. The real reason was his poor health and frequent nosebleeds [Para 46].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer was ordered to take him back in the same post and to pay his full wages from the date of dismissal until the date of return; the award was issued as non-binding, becoming final after eight days without objection [Para 71][Para 72][Para 73].

The panel began with Article 293 paragraph 1 of the Labour Law, under which the dismissal of a staff delegate, or of any candidate for that role, requires the prior agreement of the labour inspector, and it treated that consent as the mechanism by which the inspector examines and allows or refuses a request to dismiss a union leader [Para 51][Para 53][Para 54]. It then applied Article 3 paragraph 3 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, which extends the protection against dismissal enjoyed by staff delegates to every worker who stands as a candidate in a union leadership election, running 45 days before the poll and 45 days after it where the candidate is not elected [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58].

Drawing on Awards 07/06-Daiyang, 09/06-Grand Diamond and 148/07-Bay Hotel, the panel stated three cumulative conditions for that protection: the worker must fall in the protected category, the dismissal must occur inside the protection window, and the union must have notified the employer of the candidacy by any practicable means [Para 61][Para 62]. It found all three satisfied. He was a candidate at the election, so condition one held [Para 63]; he was dismissed one day after the poll, so condition two held [Para 64]; and the election record had been delivered to the company's security officer, who signed for it, so condition three held [Para 65]. Because the company never sought the labour inspector's agreement, the dismissal was unlawful [Para 67][Para 68].

On the employer's stated reason, the panel noted that the nosebleed explanation was unsupported by any evidence and could not, in any event, have justified dismissal without the inspector's consent [Para 49][Para 50]. The panel therefore followed the remedy ordered in Awards 148/07-Bay Hotel and 71/09, reinstating the worker rather than leaving him to damages, which is why the USD 420 already paid did not bar the order [Para 69][Para 70].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker who stands as a candidate in a union leadership election acquires the staff delegate's protection against dismissal for 45 days before and, if not elected, 45 days after the vote, and dismissal inside that window without the labour inspector's prior agreement is unlawful whatever reason the employer advances [Para 57][Para 58][Para 67]. Notification of candidacy by any practicable means, including delivery of the election record to a security officer who signs for it, satisfies the union's side of the test [Para 65]. The remedy for such a dismissal is reinstatement with wages for the period out, not damages, and acceptance of a severance payment does not extinguish the right [Para 69][Para 71].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the employer's health explanation was unsupported by evidence was made although the point had already been disposed of by the absence of the inspector's consent [Para 49][Para 50].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award prints its issue heading twice, back to back, without any numeral, so "(Issue 1)" is this brief's label for the single issue referred and not the panel's numbering [Para 33][Para 48]. The worker's name is spelled three different ways across the reasoning and the operative order, and in §4 he is identified by office only [Para 44][Para 45][Para 63]. The claimant's representative is recorded as an officer of a differently named union confederation from the one pleaded as claimant, leaving the union's identity unclear [Para 2][Para 32]. Stray English and Latin fragments interrupt the Khmer at several points, including a corrupted passage in the middle of the dismissal facts and a garbled signature block [Para 40][Para 29][Para 74]. Article references are repeatedly damaged, appearing as "Article 293, paragraph 1" in one place and as a fragment of the same phrase elsewhere [Para 51][Para 66]. The employer's offer of USD 420 and the worker's acceptance sit uneasily with the reinstatement order and are not reconciled in the text [Para 43][Para 71].
