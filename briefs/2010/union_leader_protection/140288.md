# Award No. 64/10 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សិន តៃ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) / Sen Tai Garment (Cambodia) (Arbitration Council, 30 July 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី, អន ណន, គង់ ផល្លៈ (president) · Doc ID 140288

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សិន តៃ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា) ("Sen Tai Garment") employed about 1,500 workers at Damnak, Sen Sok [Para 93]. The claimant was the federation សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ ("Khmer Youth Voice"), which had filed for registration but held no certificate [Para 95]. On 8 May 2010 a union election at a worker's house elected a president, deputy and secretary among three candidates [Para 100][Para 103]. After the election the administration called two of the elected leaders, Ms P and Ms I, to a meeting on 11 May and, on their refusing to resign from the union, dismissed both on 12 May 2010 without prior warning [Para 130][Para 132][Para 138]. A third elected leader, Ms O, withdrew her reinstatement claim at the hearing, and the panel declined to consider her case [Para 97][Para 98]. Nineteen demands were referred after conciliation failed on 16 June 2010 [Para 25][Para 28].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must reinstate the dismissed union leaders with full wages and benefits from dismissal to return? [Para 97]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must keep adequate medicines permanently available? [Para 144]
3. (Issue 3) Whether a free meal or 4,000 riel is owed for Sunday and holiday work? [Para 147]
4. (Issue 4) Whether special leave must be granted more easily? [Para 151]
5. (Issue 5) Whether a day-care centre built to Ministry standards is required? [Para 156]
6. (Issue 6) Whether annual leave must be usable, and payable in lieu? [Para 161]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the company is wholly liable for workplace accidents? [Para 164]
8. (Issue 8) Whether toilets and drinking water must be improved? [Para 168]
9. (Issue 9) Whether a sick-leave certificate from a private clinic preserves wages? [Para 171]
10. (Issue 10) Whether 100% wages are due in periods without work? [Para 173]
11. (Issue 11) Whether an extra US$5 attendance bonus is owed, and whether that bonus may be cut for lawful absences? [Para 175]
12. (Issue 12) Whether final wages and benefits are owed on termination? [Para 184]
13. (Issue 13) Whether occasional personal leave must be facilitated? [Para 186]
14. (Issue 14) Whether overtime must be organised on a voluntary basis? [Para 188]
15. (Issue 15) Whether two raincoats per worker per season are owed? [Para 191]
16. (Issue 16) Whether pregnant workers may leave 20 minutes early on full pay? [Para 193]
17. (Issue 17) Whether a US$10 monthly overtime bonus is owed? [Para 195]
18. (Issue 18) Whether workers may leave early once the production quota is met? [Para 199]
19. (Issue 19) Whether the 1,000 riel deduction from non-members must stop and be refunded? [Para 202]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សម្លេង យុវជន ខ្មែរ):** the dismissals of 12 May 2010 punished union leadership — the administration threatened them with dismissal unless they resigned from the union — so both must return with back pay [Para 128][Para 131]. On benefits, the workers relied on Prakas 017 and on the argument that 1,000 riel no longer buys a meal [Para 147][Para 312].
**Employer (respondent, ក្រុមហ៊ុន សិន តៃ ហ្គាមេន (ខេមបូឌា)):** the dismissals had nothing to do with the union, which the company said it did not know existed; the two were dismissed as ordinary workers for inciting others to refuse overtime, and were paid statutory compensation plus moral damages, which they declined to collect [Para 133][Para 140][Para 142]. It denied every benefits claim as already compliant or unsupported [Para 146][Para 148][Para 170].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company must take back Ms P and Ms I and pay full wages and other benefits from the date of dismissal until their return; must give one free meal to workers doing Sunday overtime; must let workers take annual leave at Khmer New Year (other times by agreement); must accept medical certificates from qualified public or private practitioners; must pay wages and benefits on termination; and may dock attendance bonus for lawfully authorised absences. The remaining demands were dismissed or not considered [Para 495][Para 498][Para 502][Para 506][Para 510].

On Issue 1 the panel first tested protected status. Prakas 305 (22 November 2001) protects founders and members from the date of the registration application until 30 days after registration, but only if the union notifies its candidates to the employer by every available means [Para 208][Para 210]. The panel found the union never delivered the election minutes: security refused to sign, and the workers did not then use the administration window, a route they knew and did not contest [Para 216][Para 218][Para 219]. Protected status therefore failed, and the dismissals were judged as ordinary dismissals [Para 221]. Applying Article 91, the panel held an employer still needs a valid reason; the company admitted it had none and instead paid compensation [Para 223][Para 226][Para 228][Para 229]. Weighing the 11 May meeting, the election four days earlier and the absence of any warning, the panel found the dismissals tied to union activity and unlawful, following Awards 19/04 and 22/04 [Para 233][Para 243][Para 245][Para 247].

On Issue 3 the panel held holiday work is not "overtime" under Article 139 or Prakas 080, so the 1,000 riel meal allowance already paid exceeded the law and stood [Para 271][Para 273]. The 4,000 riel claim had no legal base and was refused; but because 1,000 riel no longer buys a meal, a free meal for Sunday overtime was ordered under Prakas 017 [Para 293][Para 313][Para 318].

On Issue 6 the panel held Article 167(3) voids pay in lieu of annual leave during employment, so that demand failed; it ordered leave at Khmer New Year under Article 170 [Para 345][Para 349][Para 364]. On Issue 9 the company's refusal of private-clinic certificates was wrong: Article 71(3) requires only a certificate from an officially qualified doctor [Para 396][Para 403][Para 405]. Issue 11(b) allowed the attendance bonus to be docked for authorised absences because the bonus is earned by attendance [Para 433][Para 444]. Issues 2 and 10 were refused as claims about disputes not yet arisen [Para 252][Para 410]; Issues 4, 5, 8, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18 and 19 failed for want of evidence [Para 327][Para 339][Para 389][Para 469][Para 490][Para 494].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that dismisses workers it believes to be union leaders still needs a valid reason under Article 91; failure to prove the statutory notification that would confer protected status removes the special protection but does not license a dismissal without cause, and a dismissal that follows union elections and a demand to resign from the union is unlawful discrimination, entitling the workers to reinstatement with full wages from dismissal to return [Para 216][Para 223][Para 247].

**Obiter dicta:** that the 1,000 riel meal allowance fixed in 2000 no longer reflects the price of a meal, and that the company should encourage leave rather than buy it out [Para 312][Para 351].
