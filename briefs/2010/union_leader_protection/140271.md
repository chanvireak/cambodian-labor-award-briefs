# Award No. 48/10 — Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 June 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (In Sothy, An Non, Pen Bun Chhea presiding) · Doc ID 140271

## 1. Facts
Seng Yong Garment Factory Co., Ltd, on National Road 5 in Phnom Penh, has operated since 1997, suspended for about three months from January to March 2009, and employs roughly 860 workers, of whom about 424 belong to the claimant union [Para 40][Para 41][Para 42][Para 45]. Of fifteen demands, twelve were settled by agreement before the hearing of 28 May 2010, leaving three: reinstatement of one dismissed man from a group of eighteen, a change of working hours, and re-election of shop stewards before their term ended [Para 21][Para 26][Para 27][Para 101]. In early May 2010 the company ended the fixed-duration contracts of 33 laundry-section workers, including the union president, Mr S K H; seventeen of the eighteen union members affected accepted the end-of-contract payment, leaving him alone in his claim [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50]. His contract ran one year, from 16 May 2009 to 16 May 2010, after a two-month probation begun on 16 March 2009, and he was told on 6 May 2010 that it would not continue [Para 62][Para 64][Para 121].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must take back the union president, whose one-year fixed-duration contract expired on 16 May 2010 and who was given notice of non-renewal on 6 May 2010? [Para 48][Para 112]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must move the working day from 07:30–17:00 to 07:00–16:00, so that workers can catch shared motorcycle taxis that only run from 16:00? [Para 84][Para 143]
3. (Issue 11) Whether the company must hold a fresh shop-steward election before the current mandate ends in April 2011, because the workforce has grown from about 350 to about 800 since the April 2009 ballot? [Para 101][Para 156]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the washing section still had work, the company refused to renew only the union president's contract and had taken on new workers in spraying, trimming and laundry, so the non-renewal was anti-union discrimination; a later shift would leave workers stranded because neighbouring factories release at 16:00; and the stewards no longer match the workforce, some are inactive and one is ill [Para 70][Para 76][Para 88][Para 107][Para 173].
**Employer (respondent):** laundry work fell off in early May 2010, so it let the 33 contracts run to their term; the other Chinese translator, Mr H L, could cover the section, no new translator was hired, and the decision had nothing to do with the union; the 07:30 start and 90-minute lunch were fixed after workers arrived late at 07:00 and complained of short breaks, and are in the internal rules; and re-election can only occur within 15 days before the mandate expires [Para 74][Para 75][Para 93][Para 110][Para 175][Para 177].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three remaining demands were refused: no reinstatement, no change of hours, no early election [Para 181][Para 182][Para 183].

On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 73(1), under which a fixed-duration contract expires on its stated date, and Article 73(5), which requires ten days' notice of expiry or non-renewal for contracts longer than six months [Para 116][Para 119]. Notice on 6 May 2010 for a 16 May 2010 expiry met that requirement, so the ending was regular [Para 120][Para 122]. Turning to union protection, the panel read Article 293 and Article 4 of Prakas No. 305 as applying to dismissal, and held that no provision requires the labour inspector's authorisation where a union leader's fixed-term contract simply runs out, following Awards Nos 10/09 and 155/09 [Para 123][Para 126][Para 127][Para 128]. On discrimination under Articles 12 and 279, where the workers carry the evidential burden, the panel found no new translator hired, only one translator left, and insufficient evidence of anti-union motive [Para 131][Para 135][Para 138][Para 140].

On Issue 2 the panel applied Article 137 (eight hours a day, 48 a week) and Article 4 of the internal rules of 15 July 2009: the existing 07:30–11:30 and 13:00–17:00 day totals eight hours and complies, so the employer was not obliged to adopt the workers' preferred timetable [Para 145][Para 148][Para 152][Para 154].

On Issue 11 the panel applied Article 292 and Articles 2(9) and 7 of Prakas No. 286 (5 November 2001): election is the employer's duty, but a re-election must be held within the 15 days before the current mandate ends, and delegates may be removed earlier only by a vote of the workers [Para 158][Para 162][Para 167]. As the mandate runs to April 2011 and a rise or fall in headcount does not trigger a new ballot, the demand was premature [Para 169][Para 171][Para 178][Para 180].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The protection that Article 293 and Prakas No. 305 give union leaders is protection against dismissal, not a right to renewal: where a fixed-duration contract expires on its stated date and the ten days' notice required by Article 73(5) is given, no labour-inspector authorisation is needed, and the panel will not infer discrimination from non-renewal alone [Para 126][Para 127][Para 122]. Likewise, shop-steward re-election is tied to the calendar of the mandate, not to changes in headcount, and may be brought forward only by a removal vote under Prakas No. 286 [Para 167][Para 171].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that a rise or fall in the workforce is normal and that one delegate's illness does not by itself justify an early ballot go beyond what the order required [Para 173][Para 179].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The claimant's name is printed in at least five different spellings across the award, and one Latin-script personal name has been inserted by the OCR into the middle of a sentence in the notice discussion, so this brief uses initials [Para 53][Para 129][Para 137][Para 141]. The workforce figure is given as about 860 in the findings and about 800 in the discussion of the stewards, and the headcount at the 2009 ballot as about 350 [Para 42][Para 104][Para 101]. Article 4 of the internal rules is quoted as ending the afternoon at 17:30, while both parties and the panel treat the practised day as ending at 17:00 — the panel decided on the practice, and the discrepancy is reported, not resolved [Para 149][Para 152]. The award jumps from Issue 2 to Issue 11 with no Issues 3–10 in the reasons, those having been settled, and the chair's name differs between header and signature block [Para 27][Para 1][Para 185].
