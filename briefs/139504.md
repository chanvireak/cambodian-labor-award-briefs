# Award No. 007/14 — ALIM (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 20 February 2014)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី appointed by the employer party, អន ណន appointed by the worker party, តាំង ទ្រី chair) · award made binding by the parties' agreement, with a dissenting opinion by arbitrator អន ណន annexed · Doc ID 139504

## 1. Facts
ALIM (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., a garment factory at Street 224, Trapaing Thleung, Chom Chao, Porsenchey, Phnom Penh, employed 408 workers. [Para 45][Para 46] Workers struck from 11 to 15 November 2013 and again from 20 to 22 November 2013; on 14 November 2013 the Phnom Penh Municipal Court issued Order No. 22/c requiring all workers to return to their posts within 48 hours of notice, failing which absence would count as serious misconduct under Article 337. [Para 71][Para 79][Para 88] The workers ended the strike on 22 November and presented themselves at 07:00 on 23 November 2013, but the employer refused them entry and dismissed 14 union leaders and activists that same day; eight of them pursued the claim, and six of those later dropped it. [Para 49][Para 50][Para 86][Para 109]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The single non-conciliated issue was whether the employer must take the 14 activists back and pay their wages from dismissal until reinstatement; the panel decided it under two heads. [Para 49]
1. Whether the employer lawfully dismissed the six workers (Ms S Lom, Ms S Y Sopheak, Mr M Makara, Mr Ch Dara, Mr Ph Chen and Ms Si Cheyma) for serious misconduct in not returning to work within the 48 hours set by Order No. 22/c? [Para 111][Para 116]
2. Whether the employer could dismiss the two union leaders, Mr C Sopheary (vice-president) and Mr T Rambo (secretary), without the prior consent of the labour inspector, and whether they are owed back pay? [Para 122][Para 132][Para 140]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the eight were union leaders and activists, their dismissal was contrary to the Labour Law, and they had in fact decided to return on 23 November but the company refused to let them in; they asked for reinstatement and wages from the date of dismissal. [Para 36][Para 70][Para 75]
**Employer (respondent):** the eight kept striking after the court injunction and so committed serious misconduct, and the company therefore cannot take them back. [Para 79][Para 95]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the claim for the six ordinary workers was dismissed, and the employer was ordered to take back Mr C Sopheary and Mr T Rambo into normal work. No back pay was ordered by the majority. [Para 134][Para 135][Para 137]

On the six, the panel first fixed when the 48 hours began. Order No. 22/c binds from the moment both parties have notice, and the delivery report signed by federation president Mr P Sina at 08:30 on 20 November 2013 — not his oral account of 21 November — was the operative notice, so the deadline expired at 08:30 on 22 November 2013. [Para 100][Para 104][Para 106] The six stopped striking on 22 November but returned only at 07:00 on 23 November, after the deadline and without valid excuse, which is serious misconduct under Article 337. [Para 107][Para 108][Para 110] Article 74(2) lets an employer terminate an indefinite-duration contract only for a reason related to the worker's aptitude or conduct and to the needs of the enterprise, and the panel followed Awards 64/05, 5707 and 178/11 in holding that the misconduct supplied that reason. [Para 111][Para 114][Para 115] Article 26(2) requires dismissal within seven days of the employer learning of the misconduct; the dismissal on 23 November was one day later and so in time. [Para 117][Para 120][Para 121]

On the two leaders, the panel held that Article 293 requires the labour inspector's consent before dismissing a workers' delegate, that Article 295 allows only immediate suspension pending that decision, and that Article 4(2) of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001 extends the same protection to union leaders, following Awards 14908, 17309 and 48/13. [Para 123][Para 126][Para 127][Para 128] The employer dismissed them on 23 November and only notified the Labour Disputes Department on 25 November, never requesting permission, so the procedure was not followed and the two must be reinstated. [Para 129][Para 131][Para 132][Para 133]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The 48-hour period in a court injunction ordering strikers back to work runs from the moment notice of the order is delivered to the party's representative, proved by the signed delivery report rather than by recollection, and a return after that moment is serious misconduct supporting dismissal under Articles 337, 74(2) and 26(2). But a union leader or workers' delegate may not be dismissed at all without the labour inspector's prior consent under Article 293; notifying the authority after the dismissal does not cure the defect and reinstatement follows. [Para 104][Para 110][Para 130][Para 133]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's discussion of Article 26(2)'s seven-day window as a limit on the employer's right to punish, and its review of the earlier awards on reinstatement, are general statements beyond what the six dismissals required. [Para 118][Para 119][Para 128]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award carries a dissent: arbitrator អន ណន would have added back pay for the two reinstated leaders because the contract was never lawfully severed, so wages never stopped. [Para 140][Para 141][Para 142]
- The injunction is dated 14 November 2013 throughout, but at [Para 96] it is dated 14 November 2014; the delivery report number appears both as 22/c/14-11-13 and 22/c/14-11-14, and the report's own despatch date is 14 November while receipt is recorded as 20 November 2013. [Para 93][Para 96][Para 102]
- The union leadership recognition letter is dated 22 July 2013 at [Para 122] and 22 July 2010 at [Para 48].
- The company appears both as "ALIM (CAMBODIA) CO.,LTD" and as "CAMBODLA" in quoted court text. [Para 87][Para 89]
- Names are unstable across the document: "ឆឹង សុផារី" also appears as "ឆ្អឹង សុផារី" [Para 51]; "ស ឡន" as "ស ឡូន" [Para 59]; the count of dismissed workers shifts between 14 activists (claim), 8 claimants, and a party list that names nine representatives. [Para 2][Para 49][Para 86]
- Dates in the procedural recitals are corrupted ("ថ្ងៃទី ១៥ ខែមករា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៩ ២០១៤" for constitution of the panel) [Para 12]; a stray "២០១៥" appears in the reasoning [Para 127]; the hearing attendance year is printed as 2013 [Para 47].
