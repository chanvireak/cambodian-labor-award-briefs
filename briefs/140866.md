# Award No. 012/22 — Yak Jin (Cambodia) Inc. 2 (Arbitration Council, 18 May 2022)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun for the employer, An Non for the workers, Kong Phalla presiding) · Doc ID 140866

## 1. Facts
Yak Jin (Cambodia) Inc. 2 (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ីន ២) is a garment factory in Phnom Penh employing 1,151 workers, 949 of them women. [Para 69] The Khmer Workers' Spirit Union (សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ) raised sixteen demands on 23 February 2022; eight were settled in conciliation on 10 March 2022 and two more at the hearing, leaving Issues 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 8. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 34][Para 35] The dispute turns on the company's purchase of the former Cambodia Han Sam factory in January 2021 and on the piece-rate bonus it inherited there. [Para 87][Para 92]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must restore the 100 per cent piece-rate bonus across all sections, as paid before? [Para 145]
2. (Issue 2) Whether pregnant workers must be allowed one full day a month for antenatal checks with pay and benefits kept? [Para 156]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must pay 15 USD a month in transport and accommodation allowance? [Para 179]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must provide a nursery and a breast-feeding room, or 20 USD a month in milk money instead? [Para 189][Para 190]
5. (Issue 7) Whether the employer must pay a skill allowance of 5, 10 or 15 USD by machine grade? [Para 197]
6. (Issue 8) Whether a team's piece-rate bonus must be paid to that team alone rather than shared across the section? [Para 205]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), through the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union (សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ):** the 100 per cent bonus used to bring over 30 USD a month, but since the change to an 80/20 split a sewer earns only 1 to 2 USD a day, or 7 to 15 USD a month; the sharing of one team's bonus with teams that missed target destroys effort. [Para 80][Para 81][Para 138]
**Employer (respondent), Yak Jin (Cambodia) Inc. 2 (ក្រុមហ៊ុន យ៉ាក ជីន (ខេមបូឌា) អ៊ីន ២):** the 100 per cent practice belonged to the predecessor company Cambodia Han Sam, which paid severance and whose staff were rehired as new employees; the present rate is the company's own policy, applied since September 2021, and equal sharing inside a section rewards the whole team. [Para 85][Para 88][Para 143]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer was ordered to set up a nursery, with the cost of an outside nursery falling on it where it cannot; the bonus, antenatal-day, transport, skill-allowance and bonus-sharing claims were all refused. [Para 222][Para 223]
On Issue 1 the panel applied Article 2 of the Labour Law, holding that an employer may fix and vary a bonus scheme outside the statutory bonus provided it does not offend the law or reasonableness, and that the predecessor's scheme bound nobody after severance was paid. [Para 148][Para 153][Para 155]
On Issues 2, 3 and 7 the panel found no statute, collective agreement, internal rule or past practice granting one day a month for pregnancy checks, 15 USD instead of the 8 USD paid, or the skill allowance claimed. Each demand was therefore for better than the law — an interest dispute — and, because the claimant union was neither most representative nor a negotiating council, Article 54 (new) of the Union Law and Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 barred the panel from deciding them. [Para 157][Para 173][Para 183]
On Issue 5, Article 186 of the Labour Law requires any enterprise employing at least 100 women to provide a breast-feeding room and a nursery; with 949 women, the nursing room already provided did not discharge the second duty, so the employer was ordered to open a nursery, or to pay a real invoice for an outside one. [Para 190][Para 195][Para 196]
On Issue 8 the panel again rested on Article 2: equal distribution of a section's bonus among its teams is a managerial choice, and the workers had produced no evidence that the old 30 USD practice existed. [Para 214][Para 215]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A statutory duty to provide a nursery for an establishment with at least one hundred women workers is not satisfied by providing the separate breast-feeding room; the employer must either build the nursery or bear the invoiced cost of placing children in an outside one. [Para 190][Para 196]

**Obiter dicta:** an arbitration award on an interest dispute stands in place of a collective agreement for one year, which is why a union without most-representative status cannot obtain one. [Para 171][Para 172]

The section example counts five sewing teams in one place and four in another [Para 122][Para 210]. The acquired company's name is printed three different ways, once with a corrupted province syllable [Para 145]. Several paragraphs carry untranslated Latin OCR debris in place of text [Para 123][Para 155]. Names of the employer's representatives and of the worker delegates survive only as initials and fragments, so individuals are identified by role [Para 63][Para 70].
