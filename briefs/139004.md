# Award No. 062/16 — Dy Vit Chem Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 22 March 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (អ៊ីង សុទ្ធី), appointed by the employer; An Non (អន ណន), appointed by the workers; Pen Bun Chha (ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា), chair · Doc ID 139004

## 1. Facts
The C.CAWDU federation filed a collective complaint for workers of Dy Vit Chem Co., Ltd (ឌី វ៉ាយ តិច ខេម ខូអិលធីឌី) at Romduol, Trapeang Kong, Samraong Tong, Kampong Speu, raising six demands to improve working conditions. [Para 1][Para 13] Conciliation on 29 February 2016 settled nothing, so all six points came to the Council. [Para 14][Para 15] The hearing was fixed for 17 March 2016 at 14:00; the employer had no representative, and the union telephoned on the day to say the workers would not attend because the hearing fell on payday, promising a postponement request that never arrived. [Para 12][Para 17][Para 18][Para 19] Neither party filed any document. [Para 20][Para 21]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay wages no later than the 10th of each month? [Para 1]
2. Whether the company must provide sufficient toilet facilities? [Para 2]
3. Whether the company must provide a suitable canteen for the workers? [Para 3]
4. Whether the company must pay wages before the Khmer New Year holiday? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must supply one can of milk powder a month to nursing mothers? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company must add USD 5 to the midday meal allowance? [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the six demands were pursued through the union federation, which confirmed on the hearing day that the workers would not attend because the date was payday and said it would apply to postpone. [Para 18][Para 44] No written position, reasons or evidence were ever filed. [Para 20][Para 58]
**Employer (respondent):** absent and unrepresented; no documents and no answer to any of the six claims were submitted. [Para 1][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel treated the complaint as abandoned and terminated the arbitration in case 062/16, making no order on any of the six claims. [Para 60][Para 62][Para 63]

The panel framed the only question as whether it should continue the arbitration in the absence of the complainant, and answered it under Article 18 (parties must appear and explain their claims in person and lodge documents), Article 20 (parties must attend every meeting convened) and Article 21 of Prakas 099 SAPK of 21 April 2004, together with rule 4.7 of the annexed procedural rules permitting the panel to proceed or to close the case by award where a notified party fails to appear. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]

It drew from prior awards 138/08, 11/11, 131/12, 108/13, 332/14 and 232/15 a three-condition test: the party was properly invited, it did not appear, and it gave no valid reason. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36] Condition one was met — invitation letter 342 KAP/AK/VK/LKA of 9 March 2016 named the date, place and contact numbers, and a reminder call was made at about 13:59 on 17 March 2016. [Para 37][Para 40][Para 41] Condition two was met, since appearing means both attending the hearing and taking part in all four stages of the process, and the workers did neither. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 45][Para 46][Para 47] Condition three was met: "the hearing is on payday" is not a valid reason, and no postponement request was ever filed as promised, the union officer's telephone becoming unreachable afterwards. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53]

Having found all three conditions satisfied, the panel held the complainant had abandoned its complaint and closed the arbitration by award. [Para 54][Para 55][Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a complainant has been properly notified of the hearing and fails to attend, to take part in the process, or to give a valid reason — and in particular where it files no evidence of its claims — the panel treats the complaint as abandoned and may terminate the arbitration by issuing an award without ruling on the merits. [Para 21][Para 28][Para 52][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general statement that a complainant normally bears the burden of first presenting reasons and evidence, with the respondent answering only afterwards, was not necessary to the outcome. [Para 56][Para 57][Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed

[Para 15][Para 24][Para 38] The first claim is truncated after "no later than the 10th", the day of the month being lost. [Para 1] The panel writes of "two conditions" in its summary after setting out three, and cites "Article 219" where Article 21 is meant. [Para 34][Para 54] The signature block carries stray digits ("42017 - 2017") and the chair's Latin signature is illegible, so his name can be read only in Khmer. [Para 64]
