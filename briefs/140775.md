# Award No. 30/05 — Maurea Garment and the Federation Union of Solidarity (Arbitration Council, 7 June 2005)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Tach, An Nan, chair Kong Phallack) · Doc ID 140775

## 1. Facts
Maurea Garment Factory, on National Road 5, Sangkat Svay Pak, Khan Rousey Keo, Phnom Penh, employs 1,200 workers. [Para 26] On 4 May 2005 the district labour inspector received a telephone complaint about a strike over 14 working conditions; conciliation settled six of the 14, leaving eight, which reached the Secretariat on 17 May 2005 under a non-conciliation report dated 12 May 2005. [Para 27] [Para 28] [Para 16] A hearing was fixed for 20 May 2005 at 9:00 a.m. at the Secretariat, and the employer party appeared with its documents. [Para 29] [Para 19] The worker party, the Federation Union of Solidarity (FUS), did not appear, gave no reason and sent no representative. [Para 30] [Para 37]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must set up a mixed commission with the union to examine and decide the recruitment of new workers? [Para 4]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the company must terminate six recently recruited workers in the cutting section because they took the company's side? [Para 5]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must remove the security guard from the sewing section? [Para 6]
4. (Issue 4) Whether the company must grant, beyond the Ministry's Prakas, one extra day of holiday before and after Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben, made up by working extra hours? [Para 7]
5. (Issue 5) Whether the company must allow a union representative to accompany injured workers sent to hospital? [Para 8]
6. (Issue 6) Whether the company must pay 5,000 riel to workers who reach the production target? [Para 9]
7. (Issue 7) Whether the company must terminate its general manager, Mr T, and its administrative chief, Ms L, for inappropriate conduct toward workers? [Para 10] [Para 12]
8. (Issue 8) Whether the company must provide transportation when overtime runs from 6:00 p.m. to 8:30 p.m.? [Para 14]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** that recruitment should pass through a mixed commission with the union; that six cutting-section recruits and the sewing-section guard should be removed; that two extra holidays be added around Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben; that a union representative accompany injured workers to hospital; that 5,000 riel be paid on reaching the production target; that the general manager and administrative chief be dismissed for their conduct; and that transport be provided for overtime. [Para 4] [Para 5] [Para 7] [Para 8] [Para 9] [Para 12] [Para 14] They did not appear to argue any of it at the hearing. [Para 30]
**Employer (respondent):** that recruitment is the company's own right; that the six recruits were openly hired; that without a guard in the sewing section workers do not work hard; that it would comply with the Ministry's Prakas on paid holidays; that escorting the injured is for a doctor and the company to manage; that it would apply the old policy on the 5,000 riel and on transport; and that it would warn, not dismiss, its two managers, asking the union to behave appropriately as well. [Para 4] [Para 5] [Para 6] [Para 7] [Para 8] [Para 9] [Para 13] [Para 15]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed case 30/05, rejecting the worker party's claims on the ground that it had given up its claim by not appearing at the hearing. [Para 38] [Para 39]
The panel reasoned from Article 18 of the Prakas on the Arbitration Council 99/04, which requires the panel to invite the parties to present arguments and documents, and Article 19, which allows a party to appear in person, through a Bar Association lawyer, or through a person authorised in writing. [Para 31] [Para 32] Article 20 of the same Prakas obliges the parties to abstain from strikes and lockouts and to attend every meeting to which the panel calls them. [Para 33] Article 21 and provision 4.7 of the annex to that Prakas allow the panel, where a duly invited party fails to appear without good cause, either to proceed in its absence or to terminate the proceedings by means of an award, provided the panel is satisfied that the party was properly notified of the date, time and venue. [Para 34] [Para 35] [Para 36]
Applying those provisions, the panel found the FUS had been invited yet stayed away without proper reasons, that notification had been regular, and that it could therefore reject the claims as abandoned. [Para 33] [Para 36] [Para 38] It made no finding on the merits of any of the eight demands. [Para 38]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been properly notified of the date, time and venue of a hearing and fails to appear without good cause, the panel may terminate the arbitration by an award and treat that party's claim as abandoned, after satisfying itself on the record that notification was regular and that no valid excuse was given; it need not examine the merits. [Para 34] [Para 36] [Para 38] A party's attendance obligation includes abstaining from strikes and lockouts during the arbitration, and it may appear only in person, through a Bar Association lawyer, or through a person authorised in writing. [Para 32] [Para 33]

**Obiter dicta:** none identified.

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The employer's internal work rules are dated 20 November 2000 but said to have been approved by the Labour Inspector on 22 November 2005, and the shop-steward recognition letter dated 3 November 2000 is said to be signed on 8 November 2005, both impossible on their face and reported as printed. [Para 19] The non-conciliation report is dated 12 May 2005 although the conciliation minute is dated 6 May 2005 and the Ministry's request letter 25 May 2005. [Para 16] [Para 24] [Para 22] The district is spelled three ways across the text, and the employer is called Maurea Garment in the style of cause but Maurea Garment Factory in the body. [Para 26] [Para 27] OCR runs words together and drops "t" throughout ("themployer", "withe", "athearing"), and repeats "therefore, therefore" in the operative passage. [Para 31] [Para 33] The two managers and the employer's representatives named in the demands are reduced to initials per the briefing convention. [Para 10] [Para 12]
