# Award No. 017/16 — T&K German Industry (2) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 17 February 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Run Saray (president); dissenting opinion by Ing Sothy annexed · Doc ID 138972

## 1. Facts
T&K German Industry (2) (ធី & ខេ ហ្គាមេន អ៊ីន ដាស្ទ្រី (២)) is branch 2 of the T&K German Industry garment operation, employing about 830 workers, and the case is treated as a case of T&K 2, not of the parent company [Para 38][Para 40][Para 41]. Of eight demands, four (points 1, 3, 5 and 6) were settled at the hearing and four remained: points 2, 4, 7 and 8 [Para 23][Para 28][Para 29]. Twenty-nine workers transferred their rights to the Khmer Workers' Union Federation on 7 December 2015 [Para 43][Para 47]. The disputed acts are the docking of a half-day's wage and the whole attendance bonus from union representatives who attended conciliation and hearings on 6 November, 1 and 28 December 2015, and the employer's method of calculating overtime and supplying water bottles and uniforms [Para 49][Para 84][Para 136][Para 151].

## 2. Issues in dispute
2. Whether the employer must repay the daily wage and attendance bonus it deducted for the days the union representatives attended conciliation at the Ministry and hearings at the Council? [Para 84]
4. Whether the seniority bonus and the effort bonus must be added to the basic wage as the base for calculating overtime pay? [Para 108]
7. Whether the employer must give each worker one durable lidded water bottle a year? [Para 136]
8. Whether the employer must provide three uniforms a year? [Para 151]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Attending to settle a labour dispute is work like any other, and a union may use working time to resolve workers' problems; Article 306 of the Labour Law and Article 10 of Prakas No. 305 require the employer to keep paying wages to those who attend [Para 54]. The effort and seniority bonuses are part of wages, so they must be included in the overtime base; two reusable bottles a year are unhygienic and cannot hold hot water; uniforms protect clothing and should number three a year [Para 71][Para 76][Para 82].

**Employer (respondent):** It is willing to keep wages and the attendance bonus for representatives who request leave properly, since it too has internal rules [Para 55]. The rate of overtime pay is set by law and practice has always used the basic wage [Para 66][Para 64]. It cannot give individual bottles or glasses because the workforce turns over constantly and used bottles cannot be passed on, and it has no capacity to supply uniforms [Para 79][Para 83].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer must repay one half-day's wage and the attendance bonus for the three representatives who attended conciliation on 6 November 2015; it must add the seniority bonus to the basic wage when computing overtime; and it must supply two free uniforms a year. The claims for 1 and 28 December 2015, for including the effort bonus, and for a water bottle and three uniforms are dismissed [Para 161][Para 162][Para 163][Para 164][Para 165][Para 167]. The award is non-binding and becomes final if no party objects in writing within 08 days [Para 168].

On point 2 the panel applied Article 306 of the Labour Law, which requires parties to attend every meeting convened during conciliation, and Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, whose Articles 14 and 20 protect representatives from any adverse effect on their rights when they attend hearings during working hours, read with Article 10 of Prakas No. 305 of 22 November 2001, which bars cutting the wages, bonus and other benefits of negotiating representatives [Para 84][Para 87][Para 89][Para 90]. It then separated the dates by procedure: on 6 November 2015 the three representatives filed written leave requests under the company's own procedure, though the form gave "personal business" as the reason at the administration's suggestion, so the employer had to keep their wage and attendance bonus [Para 95][Para 97][Para 98][Para 100]; on 1 and 28 December 2015 they gave only oral notice and filed no written request, so the employer owed nothing, the panel holding that the leave formality is genuinely necessary for record-keeping and discipline [Para 102][Para 103][Para 105][Para 106][Para 107].

On point 4 the panel read the amended Article 139 (50% and 100% loading on the normal wage) and Article 5 of Prakas No. 80 of 1 March 1999, found that neither defines the base, and held that "wage during normal work" means the basic wage under Article 102, excluding benefits under Article 103 [Para 108][Para 110][Para 113][Para 117][Para 120]. It nevertheless followed its reasoning in Award No. 74/08 (Generation) on skill and position allowances to hold that the seniority bonus — granted under Notice No. 041/11 for length of service and experience — may be added to the base, while the effort bonus, being conditional and variable with each worker's output, may not [Para 121][Para 125][Para 127][Para 128][Para 133][Para 134][Para 135].

On point 7 the panel applied Article 4 of Prakas No. 054 of 10 February 2000, which requires the employer to provide clean drinking glasses or another hygienic means, and found that the employer supplies drinking water, two bottles a year and permission to bring personal bottles, which satisfies the Prakas, whose text does not require a bottle per worker [Para 137][Para 139][Para 145][Para 147][Para 150].

On point 8 the panel applied Article 6 of Prakas No. 307 of 14 December 2007, which requires at least two free uniforms a year, and ordered two; the demand for three was refused, as was the employer's plea of incapacity [Para 151][Para 152][Para 156][Para 160][Para 167].

Ing Sothy dissented on point 4-1, holding that adding the seniority bonus to the overtime base has no legal foundation and rests only on an equity argument, so the same base should apply whether wages are being paid or deducted [Para 171][Para 175][Para 176].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A union representative who attends conciliation or arbitration during working hours is protected from any deduction of wage, bonus or benefit, but only where the worker has complied with the employer's written leave procedure; oral notice alone leaves the employer free to deduct [Para 98][Para 106][Para 90]. The overtime base is the basic wage under Article 102, to which a seniority bonus tied to length of service may be added, but not an effort bonus that varies with individual output [Para 120][Para 128][Para 135]. Prakas No. 307 requires at least two free uniforms a year whatever the employer's capacity, while Prakas No. 054 requires only a hygienic means of drinking, not an individual bottle [Para 145][Para 152].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that the leave procedure serves record-keeping, supervision, discipline and the employer's other rights goes beyond what was needed to refuse the December claims [Para 104].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award says the case concerns T&K 2, branch 2, but the header, the party names and much of the reasoning name T&K German Industry without the branch suffix, and the report is cited both as "លេខ ០៨៧" and "លេខ ០១៧០" [Para 1][Para 20][Para 40][Para 46]. The award date is 17 February 2016 on the face and in the dissent, but the parties' agreed extension is printed as running to 18 February 2016, and the document-deadline paragraph gives the objection date as 3 March 2016 where January was plainly meant [Para 1][Para 31][Para 32][Para 177]. The number of complainants is not stable: 29 transferred rights, five workers attended, and the panel states that only one of those five was absent from the transfer letter, then treats all 29 plus that one as complainants [Para 43][Para 44][Para 46][Para 47]. The three settled points are listed as 1, 3, 5 and 6 — four points — while the text says four were settled and four remained [Para 28][Para 29]. The operative paragraphs repeat themselves and are partly corrupted, printing "ចំនួន ២០៣ ០៣" for three uniforms and duplicating the point 8 order [Para 160][Para 166][Para 167]. The union's name is given as "សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត" while an earlier name, "សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សាមគ្គីភាព", appears in the demand list [Para 1][Para 2][Para 14]. Prior-award numbers are OCR-mangled, e.g. "សំណុំរឿង លេខ ៧៤០៩ ហ្វ័រ ធ្យូន" for Award No. 74/08 [Para 154].
