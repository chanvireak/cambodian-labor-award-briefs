# Award No. 187/15 — Lida Knitting (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 18 August 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy for the employer party, An Non for the worker party, Kong Phalha chair) · Doc ID 139256

## 1. Facts
The company weaves sweaters and employed about 270 workers. [Para 69][Para 70] Eleven workers transferred their rights to the president of the Khmer Youth Voice Union Federation on 6 July 2015 after an internal union election on 1 July 2015 elected eleven leaders, whose recognition the company refused and against whom it had issued warnings. [Para 70][Para 81][Para 52] Fourteen demands went to conciliation on 7 July 2015, none settled, and all fourteen were referred on 17 July 2015; the hearing was held on 28 July 2015. [Para 23][Para 24][Para 21] None of the eleven workers attended — only the federation's secretary-general, on their written mandate. [Para 71][Para 72] By a thumbprinted letter dated 29 July 2015 the eleven workers confirmed they had been paid and asked to end the dispute and withdraw the mandate; the union did not contest the letter. [Para 73][Para 127]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must accept the eleven elected union leaders, let them work again and keep their wages unchanged? [Para 75]
2. Whether the company must grant fixed-term contracts of one year rather than three months? [Para 101]
3. Whether the company must pay an overtime meal allowance of 2,000 riel an hour? [Para 105]
4. Whether the company must keep wages and bonuses for workers on sick leave certified by a private clinic? [Para 108]
5. Whether the company must pay double wages plus one for work on paid public holidays? [Para 112]
6. Whether the company must pay a lunch allowance of 4,000 riel a day? [Para 117]

Seven further issues were settled at the hearing and two were withdrawn by the workers, leaving these six. [Para 28][Para 29][Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the eleven leaders were dismissed on 4 July 2015 before their fixed-term contracts expired and because of their union activity, contrary to Article 279 of the Labour Law and Prakas No. 305; the company's pay and allowance practices fell short of what the law and other factories provide. [Para 89][Para 94][Para 95][Para 118]
**Employer (respondent):** the dismissals had nothing to do with union discrimination, the other workers did not want the eleven back, they had incited colleagues to refuse overtime and strike and had been warned in writing, and severance was paid on 8 July 2015 without coercion. [Para 77][Para 92][Para 93][Para 96][Para 98]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel declined to consider case No. 187/15 at all, holding that the eleven workers had ended the dispute and that the union no longer represented them, so it made no order on any of the six issues, in riel or USD. [Para 128][Para 129][Para 130]

Before the merits, the panel addressed who the claimant was. Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 allows a party to appear in person or through a representative holding written authority, and the panel read that as requiring written authorisation before anyone may act for a party, following awards 161/09, 43/10, 144/12 and 120/15. [Para 121][Para 122] On the transfer-of-rights letter of 6 July 2015 it found the eleven workers to be the claimants, represented by the federation. [Para 123][Para 124]

It then weighed the letter of 29 July 2015, filed by the employer before the deadline for objecting to evidence, bearing the workers' thumbprints and names and stating that they had received their lawful wages on 8 July 2015, wished to end the dispute and withdrew their mandate from the federation; the workers filed no objection to it. [Para 126][Para 127] The panel held that withdrawing the mandate meant the federation no longer had the right to represent the eleven before it, and that the same letter was also a request by the workers to end the dispute, so no dispute remained. [Para 128][Para 129] It therefore decided not to consider the case. [Para 130]

The award is non-binding. [Para 131]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the named claimants themselves, by a signed and uncontested letter filed before the deadline for objecting to evidence, withdraw the mandate of the union acting for them and ask that the dispute be ended, the union loses standing before the panel and no dispute remains, so the panel will decline to consider the case whatever the strength of the demands. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 130]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's general reading of Article 19 of Prakas No. 099 — that representation requires written authority — and its survey of earlier awards on that point were preliminary observations, since the case was disposed of on the withdrawal alone. [Para 121][Para 122]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The termination date is not settled in the award: the workers say 4 July 2015, the employer says it does not recall and would check the file, and severance is recorded as paid on 8 July 2015 [Para 77][Para 89][Para 92].
- The union's notification of the election result is dated 1 July 2015 but was signed for by the company's security officer on 1 June 2015; the workers attribute this to a clerical error by the guard [Para 82][Para 83].
- The panel is said to have been constituted on "21 July 2022 2015" [Para 25], the award date was extended from "10 August 2 2015" [Para 33], and the hearing is given as 08:30 "រសៀល" (afternoon) in one place and 08:30 morning in another [Para 21][Para 26].
- Individual names of the eleven workers are spelled differently from list to list (for example ឆិល កិល against ឆិល កុល, ផូ ជុន against ផូ ផុន, ទី ហូន against ទី ហួន), and one letter warns a different worker from the one named in the charge [Para 2][Para 40][Para 57][Para 70].
- The company is once printed as "លីដា Chan នីត ធីង (ខេមបូឌា)"; the award carries no Latin-script form and the heading follows the catalogue title, "Lida Knitting (Cambodia) Co., Ltd" [Para 64], and the federation's name is garbled as សហព័ន្ធ ឥសាហជីព សម្តែង យុវជន ខ្មែរ in the quoted withdrawal letter [Para 126].
- The conciliation report number appears as 1082/15 កប/កណ and as 1082/15 ក.ប/ក.ណ, and the number of workers is given as 11 in the referral notice but 270 for the factory [Para 19][Para 24][Para 22][Para 70].
- No finding was made on any demand: the panel's reasons address only representation and the withdrawal, so the dismissal and allowance claims are untested on the record [Para 120][Para 130].
