# Award No. 052/15 — Global Fashion Garment (Arbitration Council, 11 March 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Li Tai Seng, chosen by the employer; An Non, chosen by the workers; Pen Bun Chhea, chair chosen by the two) · Doc ID 139151

## 1. Facts
The employer is a garment factory at Sangkat Toul Sangke, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, which the workers say has traded under successive names — "សាម ហ្វត", then "ខេ ទៅ ស៊ីណូ ម៉ាក", then Global Fashion Garment (ក្លូប៊ល ហ្វេ ហ្គាមេន). [Para 1][Para 2] On that renaming the workers claimed severance pay for accrued seniority for all workers, and separately claimed 50% of wages during a two-month suspension of work. [Para 2][Para 4] The employer's position was that the renaming preserved every worker's seniority and no factory was closed, and that during suspension it pays USD 40 per worker per month. [Para 3][Para 5] The employer asked for the file to go to the Arbitration Council. [Para 6] Conciliation on 10 February 2015 settled neither of the two issues, and report No. 158 dated 16 February 2015 reached the Secretariat on 17 February 2015; the hearing was set for 06 March 2015. [Para 13][Para 14][Para 11]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay severance for seniority (ប្រាក់បំណាច់អតីតភាពការងារ) to all workers because it changed the company's name from "សាម ហ្វត" to "ខេ ទៅ ស៊ីណូ ម៉ាក" and then to Global Fashion Garment? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer must pay 50% of wages to workers during the two months in which it suspended work, as demanded by the two further unions to which the workers transferred their rights? [Para 4]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** A change of company name is a break in the employment relationship, so all workers are owed severance for their past seniority; and during the two-month suspension of work the company should pay half of each worker's wage rather than the flat sum it offers. [Para 2][Para 4]
**Employer (respondent):** The renaming from "សាម ហ្វត" to "ខេ ទៅ ស៊ីណូ ម៉ាក" to Global Fashion Garment preserved the seniority of all workers and no factory was closed, so no severance is due; during suspension it pays USD 40 per worker per month. [Para 3][Para 5]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel declared the claim abandoned and terminated the arbitration in case No. 052/15 without ruling on either issue. [Para 60][Para 61][Para 62][Para 63]
The panel began by recording that it could establish no facts on either claim, because the claimant workers were not present at the hearing. [Para 23] It then framed the question whether it should continue in the claimants' absence, and applied Article 18 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY of 21 April 2004 — parties must be summoned to state their case in person and to file documents — together with Article 20 (parties must attend every meeting convened) and Article 21 (where a party has been properly invited and stays away without valid reason, the panel may proceed in its absence or close the arbitration by issuing an award). [Para 24][Para 25][Para 26] Rule 4.7 of the procedural rules annexed to the same Prakas was applied to the same effect, subject to the condition that the party was properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 27][Para 28] The panel confirmed this three-condition test as settled in its earlier awards, citing 30/05 Mouria, 16/07 Lotus, 27/07 IM & V, 95/08 Yong Va (1), 132/08 JGH, 138/08 Malin, 151/08 Wilson, 09/09 Nim Piseth Thai, 01/10 Dinnitech, 30/10 Global A Park, 130/13 Chen Theron I Kerril, 167/13 Queen Th Measer In Das Striol and 270/13 8 Star Sport Wear. [Para 29][Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34]
On condition one, the Secretariat issued invitation No. 264 K.B/A.K/V.K/L.K.A. to the employer and No. 265 to the workers, both of 25 February 2015, stating the date, place and contact numbers, and telephoned each of the three federations — the Khmer Workers' Rights Federation at 15:40 on 25 February, the Workers' Security Federation at 07:46 on 02 March and the Workers' Rights and Benefits Federation at 07:44 on 02 March — before calling them again on the hearing day. [Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] On condition two, the claimants neither attended the hearing nor sent an authorised representative, so they took part in none of the four stages of the proceedings. [Para 43][Para 44][Para 45][Para 46] On condition three, no valid reason was given: the vice-president of the Khmer Workers' Rights Federation said by telephone at 13:37 on the hearing day that he was in Svay Rieng province; the president of the Workers' Security Federation said at 14:05 that he would not attend and would come to the Secretariat to withdraw the complaint; and the general secretary of the Workers' Rights and Benefits Federation said at 14:19 that she would not attend and asked the panel to proceed according to law. [Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51][Para 52] All three conditions being met, the panel held the claimant had abandoned its claim and that it need not continue the arbitration. [Para 53][Para 59][Para 60][Para 61]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant has been properly summoned — in writing with the date, time and place, and by telephone — fails to appear at the hearing either in person or through an authorised representative, and offers no valid reason for the absence, the panel will treat the claim as abandoned and close the arbitration by award under Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 SAK BY and Rule 4.7 of its procedural rules, without reaching the merits; a federation officer's mere statement that he is in another province is not a valid reason, and an announced intention to withdraw the complaint is not a withdrawal. [Para 26][Para 28][Para 43][Para 48][Para 51][Para 60]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's general remarks on the burden of proof — that the claimant must first present arguments and evidence, and the respondent's burden to answer arises only after that — and its account of the four stages of an arbitration hearing played no part in the outcome, which rested on non-appearance alone. [Para 42][Para 56][Para 57][Para 58]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The Khmer original is badly corrupted. The company is printed under six different spellings (ក្លូប៊ល, គ្លូប៊ល, គ្រូ បិល, គ្លូបិល, and with the second element sometimes "ហ្វេ សិន" without "ហ្គាមេន") and the Latin form used in this brief is a rendering, not a printed one. [Para 1][Para 9][Para 14][Para 19][Para 20][Para 23] The earlier trading names "សាម ហ្វត" and "ខេ ទៅ ស៊ីណូ ម៉ាក" survive only as OCR strings and cannot be reliably romanised. [Para 2][Para 3] Paragraph 22 is an orphaned fragment ("ខែកុម្ភៈ ឆ្នាំ ២០១៥") standing where the second Secretariat exhibit — the workers' invitation No. 265 — should be, so that letter is known only from the reasoning [Para 22][Para 36]. Paragraph 12 is truncated mid-sentence ("បាន បញ្ជាក់ ថា ការងារ មួយចំនួន") and cannot be relied on for the date conciliation began [Para 12]. The quoted text of Article 21 contains an intruded year ("ការអញ្ជើញ ត្រឹម ២០១៥ ត្រឹមត្រូវ") [Para 26], and the Prakas 099 quotation names a Ministry that does not exist ("ក្រសួង សង្គមកិច្ច ការងារ បណ្តុះបណ្តាល វិជ្ជាជីវៈ និង ៤០ យុវនីតិសម្បទា") [Para 24]. The signature block omits the employer-chosen arbitrator's name entirely, leaving only An Non and Pen Bun Chhea [Para 64]. No severance figure is printed anywhere; the only sums are the USD 40 per month the employer says it pays and the 50% of wages demanded [Para 4][Para 5].
