# Award No. 13/11 — Gold Kamvimex Garment Factory Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 23 February 2011)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kao Thach, An Nan; chair Pen Bunchhea) · Doc ID 139975

## 1. Facts
Gold Kamvimex Garment Factory Ltd., a 575-worker factory, employed four workers on successive three-month fixed duration contracts. All four were founders or members of the newly formed Local Union of C.CAWDU, whose registration application was filed on 27 October 2010 and granted on 24 January 2011. On 18 and 19 November 2010 the head of administration told each of them the contract had expired and would not be renewed; the union says the separations were union discrimination, and seeks reinstatement, back pay and conversion of the contracts to undetermined duration. [Para 2][Para 21][Para 22][Para 65]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must reinstate the four workers and pay them back pay from the date of dismissal to the date of reinstatement, the workers alleging discrimination against the Local Union? [Para 2]
2. Whether the employer complied with the working conditions of the Labour Law and did not discriminate against the Local Union's leaders? [Para 3]
3. Whether the employer must convert the fixed duration contracts to undetermined duration contracts? [Para 4]

The panel combined issues 1 and 2, which it found related, and decided them together as a single rights dispute. [Para 12][Para 23]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the four were dismissed because of their union roles, after the employer was notified of the leadership election; the employer's practice of issuing three-month contracts lets it terminate workers easily, so the contracts should be converted. [Para 36][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent):** it does not recognise the union because it was unregistered and had notified discrepant election results; the fixed duration contracts simply expired, so there was no dismissal and no discrimination; the three-month practice follows purchase orders and the production line and has always been in place. [Para 3][Para 23][Para 45][Para 55]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the employer is ordered to reinstate Mr T, Ms P and Ms S with back pay from the date of their dismissal to the date of reinstatement (no sum quantified in the award); the claim for Ms K is rejected, and Issue 3 is rejected. [Para 81][Para 95][Para 96]

On classification, the panel applied Article 312 paragraph 2 of the Labour Law and Clause 43 of Prakas No. 099, following Awards 119/09-SL Garment, 86/10-New Mingda and 02/11-Pou Yuen: an interests dispute has no basis in law, an agreement or a collective agreement, while a rights dispute concerns entitlements under them. Because termination is governed by the Labour Law, the reinstatement claim was a rights dispute decided on law, not equity. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 53]

The employer's central defence — expiry — failed on the facts: the panel found the contracts were terminated before they expired, so the real question was whether premature termination of fixed duration contracts complied with the Labour Law. [Para 56][Para 57]

On protection, Article 293 requires Labour Inspector authorisation before dismissing a shop steward, and Clause 4 paragraph 1 of Prakas No. 305 extends the same protection to founding members from the moment the registration application is filed until 30 days after registration. Following Awards 71/09-Hytex and 09/10-Chung Hao, the panel required three conditions: protected status, dismissal inside the protection window, and notice to the employer of the candidates. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 61] All four were founders, satisfying the first condition; the application was filed on 27 October 2010, the dismissals occurred on 18 and 19 November 2010 and registration came on 24 January 2011, satisfying the second. [Para 63][Para 64][Para 65]

The third condition split the group. The employer had been notified on 22 September 2010 of the six leadership candidates, including Mr T, Ms P and Ms S, and a security guard acknowledged receipt, so the employer had known of their status since that date; for Ms K there was no evidence of such knowledge. [Para 66][Para 69][Para 71][Para 72] The employer dismissed the three protected founders without seeking Labour Inspector approval, which the panel held unlawful and remedied by reinstatement and back pay. [Para 74][Para 75]

For Ms K, special protection being unavailable, the panel turned to Articles 12 and 279 of the Labour Law on union discrimination. Following Awards 93/06-Evergreen, 112/06-River Rich and 01/07-Supreme, it placed the burden of proof on the worker party and found the evidence insufficient, so the claim was rejected. [Para 76][Para 78][Para 79][Para 80]

Issue 3 was also a rights dispute, since conversion is stipulated in the Labour Law and Decree 38. Applying Article 65, Articles 1 and 22 of Decree 38, and Awards 131/09-Medcrest Textiles and 54/10-USA, the panel held that no third party can force parties to sign a labour contract. Article 67(2) nonetheless converts a fixed duration contract to undetermined duration where renewals push total service past two years (Awards 57/06-Evergreen, 10/03 Jacqsintex, 36/06-Mondotex); the longest service here was one year and two months, so the demand was rejected. [Para 83][Para 90][Para 92][Para 93][Para 94]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Union founders acquire shop-steward protection against dismissal from the date the registration application is filed, not from the date of registration, and that protection survives the employer's refusal to recognise the union — so a dismissal without Labour Inspector authorisation is unlawful even where the employer contends the contracts merely expired, provided the employer was notified of the candidacy. [Para 59][Para 64][Para 74]

**Obiter dicta:** that the panel, as a third party, cannot order any party to sign a contract; that the union received its certificate late because the Ministry failed to notify it; and that no worker here had over two years of service. [Para 22][Para 92][Para 94]
