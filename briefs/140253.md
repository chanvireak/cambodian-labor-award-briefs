# Award No. 25/10 — Hai Bun Inter Brai Weaving Factory (Arbitration Council, 26 March 2010)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · panel of 3 (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Nan, worker-appointed; Run Saray, president) · Doc ID 140253

## 1. Facts
The factory, operating since December 2001 with about 650 workers, recognised a single union of 380 members as the most representative union on 3 April 2008. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 37] The union's complaint of 26 January 2010 raised 14 demands; 13 were settled in conciliation on 26 February 2010, leaving one point of non-conciliation certified on 2 March 2010. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 13] That last point concerned the meal allowance paid for the two hours of overtime worked daily from 04:00 to 06:00. [Para 3][Para 38] A collective agreement of 22 July 2008 between the company, the worker delegation and the union had already fixed the allowance. [Para 17][Para 45]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the workers may obtain an additional 1,000 riel of overtime meal allowance — making 2,000 riel for two hours of overtime — by collective demand, where a 2008 agreement with their representatives fixed 1,000 riel for two hours and 2,500 riel for four hours? [Para 38][Para 45][Para 55]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union argued that market prices had risen so that 1,000 riel no longer buys a meal, that a higher allowance would encourage workers to accept overtime, and that other factories already pay more. [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41]
**Employer (respondent):** the company relied on Notice No. 017 of 18 July 2000, which requires 1,000 riel per day of voluntary overtime, and asked the panel to apply the law as written; it did not answer the comparison with other factories. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 44]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claim for an additional 1,000 riel meal allowance for overtime from 04:00 to 06:00 was rejected, leaving the 2008 rate in force. [Para 69][Para 70]

The panel began with point 4 of Notice No. 017, which grants 1,000 riel per day, or one free meal, to workers who volunteer for overtime at the employer's request, and observed that the notice says nothing about how many hours of overtime attract that sum. [Para 49][Para 50] It then turned to the agreement of 22 July 2008, recorded in the employer's own documents, under which 1,000 riel is paid for two hours and 2,500 riel for four hours of overtime. [Para 52][Para 53] Because the company had in fact been paying exactly 1,000 riel to workers who volunteered for two hours, the question became whether the union could revise that agreement by demand. [Para 54][Para 55] On Article 65 of the Labour Law and Articles 1 and 22 of the Law on Contracts (Kret-Chbab No. 38), the panel held that a contract is the law of the parties and can be modified only with the consent of both; only a party to the contract may terminate or amend it. [Para 56][Para 58][Para 63] Since the employer expressly refused the increase, the demand failed. [Para 67][Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective agreement that fixes the overtime meal allowance binds both sides and cannot be raised by unilateral demand: the panel will not rewrite the agreed rate where the employer withholds consent, and the statutory minimum in Notice No. 017 of 18 July 2000 is a floor, not a variable entitlement. [Para 60][Para 67]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that Notice No. 017 does not specify how many overtime hours earn the 1,000 riel is not needed for the result, which rests on the 2008 agreement alone. [Para 50][Para 53]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan is noisy and the money figures are unreliable. The claim is pleaded as 2,000 riel per two hours, but the same sum is printed as "2000/" in one passage, as "20 riel" in the reasoning section and as "10 riel" where the panel restates the agreed rate, so the agreed 1,000 riel is not consistently reproduced. [Para 38][Para 48][Para 54][Para 66] The overtime window appears both as 04:00–06:00 and as "hours 4 to 6" and "2 hours", and the hearing date is given as 14:00 while the invitation letter is described as setting 02:00 p.m., the same time written two ways. [Para 14][Para 38][Para 49] The company name is printed in at least four transliterations, including one that renders it as a weaving rather than a garment plant. [Para 2][Para 17][Para 34] The non-conciliation report is numbered 231 in the procedural account and in the ministry's report list, while the union's complaint carries 137/010 and a duplicate of the same letter is numbered 020/010. [Para 7][Para 22][Para 30] Several individual names appear in the party lists; they are not reproduced here, and the union officers and employer representatives are identified by role only. [Para 3] The signature block is partly overwritten by scanner artefacts. [Para 72]
