# Award Nos. 111/15 and 118/15 — Than Yann International Nail (Cambodia) Fashion Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Seng Vouch Hun, employer-appointed; Liv Sovanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chea, president) · Doc ID 139406

## 1. Facts
The employer, Than Yann International Nail (Cambodia) Fashion Co., Ltd, cuts and sews garments with about 850 workers. [Para 93][Para 94] A C.CAWDU-affiliated union, elected on 20 March 2015 and not yet registered with the Ministry, brought 17 demands on working conditions for 294 workers. [Para 94][Para 95][Para 97] In a joined file the same union sought reinstatement of Mr K, a cut-table team leader and its own president, whose last three-month fixed-term contract expired on 3 May 2015 and was not renewed. [Para 122][Para 125][Para 177]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the claimant union is the most representative union in the enterprise, so that the panel may decide the interest claims on a skills allowance and wage increases (issues 13, 15 and 16)? [Para 149][Para 153]
2. Whether Mr K's employment ended by expiry of his fixed-term contract rather than by dismissal? [Para 173][Para 179]
3. Whether the non-renewal was discrimination against his union role, so that reinstatement and back wages are owed? [Para 189][Para 199]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Workers able to sew three or more machine types should receive a USD 6 skills allowance, cutters a USD 20 base increase (from the USD 133–163 now paid) and team assistants USD 15, because their pay is below the cost of living and equal to ordinary sewers; Mr K was let go for his union presidency. [Para 99][Para 106][Para 111][Para 135]
**Employer (respondent):** It cannot pay: since 28 April 2015 it grants USD 3–5 incentives to one or two multi-machine workers per team at the team leader's choice, and USD 10 to assistants, all from May; Mr K was not dismissed but simply not renewed for disobeying his section head and demanding USD 10 to lift goods, and it never received the union notice. [Para 101][Para 117][Para 131][Para 132][Para 191]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — all three interest claims (13, 15, 16) refused for want of jurisdiction, and reinstatement plus back wages refused; nothing is awarded in riel or USD [Para 155][Para 201][Para 204]

On the interest claims the panel found no provision of the Labour Law, collective agreement, internal rules or past practice obliging the employer to pay a USD 6 skills allowance, a USD 20 cutter increase or a USD 15 assistant increase, so the demands were "better than the law" and therefore interest disputes. [Para 140][Para 141][Para 157][Para 165] Under Article 96 of the Labour Law and Article 9 of Prakas No. 305, only the most representative union may conclude a collective agreement; Article 43 of Prakas No. 099 makes an interest award itself a collective agreement binding all workers for one year and stripping their right to strike over the same demands. [Para 143][Para 147][Para 148] The panel therefore accepts an interest dispute only from the most representative union, or several unions together representing more than half the workforce, following Awards 81/04 (Evergreen) and 113/14, and declining jurisdiction as in Award 169/11 (Fort Tayon Thi Trang). [Para 149][Para 150] Eleven of the 17 demands were settled at conciliation and three withdrawn, leaving 13, 15 and 16. [Para 37][Para 38] On the evidence the claimant was not the most representative union, so the panel refused to consider all three. [Para 153][Para 155][Para 163][Para 172]

On Mr K the panel applied Article 73(1): a fixed-duration contract expires at its term, and whether to renew is the parties' own decision, following Awards 100/07 (Houyia Cambodia) and 64/09 (Cino Mekong 2). [Para 173][Para 175][Para 176] His probation ran 3 December 2013 to 3 February 2014 and five successive three-month contracts followed, a total of 1 year 5 months, the last ending 3 May 2015; notice of non-renewal was given on 23 April 2015 and settlement on 30 April 2015 because 1–3 May were a public holiday and Sunday. [Para 125][Para 130][Para 177][Para 178] That being expiry, not dismissal, the panel held the protection in Article 293 and Article 3 of Prakas No. 305 inapplicable, following Awards 34/05 (Zaksintic) and 53/06 (Hong Mi). [Para 180][Para 182][Para 186][Para 188] On discrimination the panel placed the burden of proof on the party alleging it. [Para 192][Para 194] Aside from a notice of the elected leadership handed to a security guard on 20 March 2015 — which the employer says never reached administration and which the guard had no authority to accept — no specific evidence of anti-union motive was produced. [Para 127][Para 129][Para 198] The claim failed for want of proof. [Para 199][Para 200]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An interest dispute — a demand for more than the law, a collective agreement, internal rules or established practice require — will not be heard unless the union bringing it is the most representative in the enterprise, or the unions together represent more than half the workers, because an interest award becomes a collective agreement binding the whole workforce. [Para 148][Para 149] Separately, the protected status of a union leader under Article 293 does not convert the natural expiry of a fixed-term contract into a dismissal, and a claim of anti-union non-renewal fails unless the claimant proves the motive. [Para 186][Para 188][Para 199]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the employer had introduced its own incentive schemes from May 2015 and treated that as confirming the demands were policy, not legal entitlement — a point not needed once jurisdiction was declined. [Para 102][Para 117][Para 141]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award date is 11 June 2015 and the parties' extension from 3 June to 11 June is recorded, so the header and reasons agree. [Para 1][Para 41] The union election is dated 19 March 2015 in the reasons but 20 March 2015 in the findings, and the notice of leadership is dated 20 March throughout. [Para 94][Para 126][Para 197] Mr K's start date is printed as both 5 December 2013 and 3 December 2013, the employer's own statement gives 3 December 2015, and the contract expiry appears as both 3 May and 5 May 2015. [Para 121][Para 123][Para 125] The 17 demands are numbered erratically in the OCR, with items 17, 18 and 20 out of sequence, and the withdrawal of issues 6, 8 and 12 leaves a count that only reconciles at 3 remaining. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 37][Para 38] Amounts are printed as "២០១៩" and "២០១៦" dollars for the USD 20 claim, i.e. years bled into figures. [Para 156][Para 202] A letter dated 18 October 2015 is cited although the award predates it. [Para 64] The signature block is corrupted, the employer arbitrator's name is lost from the signature page, and the individual worker is designated Mr K here. [Para 206]
