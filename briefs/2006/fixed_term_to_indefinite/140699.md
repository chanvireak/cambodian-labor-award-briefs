# Award No. 33/06 — Sau Th Bay Enterprise Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 25 May 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី for the employer party, ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន for the worker party, តាំង ទ្រី chair) · Doc ID 140699

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅ ធ៍ បាយ អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយ ខូ អិលធីឌី, a garment factory at Soun Vuthana Building E, Choam Chao, Dangkao, employing about 670 workers, was the scene on 13 March 2006 at 09:00 of a stand-up by the workers of the sewing and ironing sections — about 40 per cent of the workforce according to the union, about 500 according to the employer, of whom 326 signed a fingerprint list — lasting between two and five minutes. [Para 1][Para 19][Para 23][Para 24][Para 26][Para 29] The union gave management no prior notice; it later explained the reasons and apologised. [Para 30][Para 31] The company moved the union president of សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន សោធ័ បាយ (Mr H) from the cutting section to finishing, then suspended him by notice dated 4 April 2006 for a suspension it described as dating from 20 March 2006, and set his reinstatement on repayment of a USD 3,500 bonus while paying him 50 per cent of wages meanwhile. [Para 1][Para 32][Para 33][Para 35][Para 38]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must take Mr H back as union president and work normally, its suspension of him being unlawful? [Para 2][Para 47]
2. (Issue 2) Whether annual leave must be counted by adding the months worked before August, and whether workers may choose when to take that leave? [Para 3][Para 122]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the suspension of Mr H was not lawful under the Labour Law, so he must be reinstated, and annual leave days must be computed for every month worked from the start of employment or paid in cash instead. [Para 2][Para 3][Para 41][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន សៅ ធ៍ បាយ អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយ ខូ អិលធីឌី cannot accept Mr H back because he incited other workers into a five-minute strike, he must be disciplined, and he must repay the USD 3,500 bonus the company had paid out; suspension continues until the Inspector's decision or repayment. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36][Para 48]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the company to take Mr H back as union president from the date the award takes effect, and to add annual leave days for every month worked from the start of employment, but refused payment in cash for annual leave and refused to let workers fix the timing of leave themselves. [Para 137][Para 138][Para 139]

On Issue 1 the panel worked through four sub-questions. [Para 49] Under Article 10 of the registered internal rules, misconduct is graded light, medium and serious, and the panel found the stand-up did interrupt the production chain and cause loss, so the conduct was a fault. [Para 49][Para 51][Para 52][Para 53] It then asked whether that fault was serious under Article 83(b): the list in Article 83(b) covers theft, fraud, violence, threats and incitement to serious misconduct, and the panel found nothing in the stand-up corresponding to items 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6. [Para 55][Para 61] On incitement under Article 83(b)(5) it held that two elements are required — an act of incitement, and serious misconduct by other workers caused by that incitement — and found the second element missing. [Para 62][Para 63][Para 65][Para 81] It also found the gesture was not a strike within Article 318, because no demand was made and everyone returned to work after two to five minutes, so Article 330 could not be applied. [Para 111][Para 112][Para 115][Para 116] The fault was therefore medium-grade under Article 10. [Para 91] On sanction, Article 27 requires the penalty to be proportionate to the fault: the company produced no basis at all for the USD 3,500 figure and proved no financial loss, and it had deducted nothing from the bonuses of the other 326 workers, so singling out Mr H was punitive and disproportionate. [Para 93][Para 94][Para 95][Para 98][Para 99][Para 100] Indefinite suspension was also outside the internal scale — warning, then suspension without pay of no more than seven days, then dismissal — so the suspension was unlawful, following awards 08/05 (Vennie Nighting) and 76/05 (Double Foot Way). [Para 102][Para 103][Para 104][Para 105][Para 106][Para 107][Para 108]

On Issue 2 the panel held the old practice of counting leave only to August unlawful: Article 166 gives one and a half days per month worked and Article 167(1) makes the right vest after one year, so the months before August must be added. [Para 124][Para 125][Para 126] Payment in lieu of leave is void under Article 167(3) and Article 13, following cases 62/04, 94/04 and 45/05 (B&N), so that claim was refused. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129][Para 130] Neither side may fix leave unilaterally; Article 170 requires the employer to allow leave at Khmer New Year, following cases 21/05, 27/05 and 39/05 (Wing Tai 2). [Para 131][Para 133][Para 135][Para 136]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A brief stand-up or sit-down by workers that states no demand and ends within minutes is not a strike under Article 318 and not serious misconduct under Article 83(b); incitement under Article 83(b)(5) requires both an act of incitement and serious misconduct actually committed by the incited workers, so a medium-grade fault cannot be punished as serious. A penalty must be proportionate under Article 27 and must follow the graduated scale in the registered internal rules, which makes an indefinite suspension unlawful, and an employer that claims a cash sum from a worker must prove the loss and its calculation. Annual leave accrues at one and a half days per month for every month worked, including the first incomplete year, and payment in lieu is void. [Para 63][Para 91][Para 100][Para 108][Para 126][Para 129]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel noted that its award itself serves as a written warning to Mr H, an observation on his record that does not affect the orders. [Para 121]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award is printed as case "៣៣/០៦ - សៅ ធ៍ បាយ" but the employer appears under at least five spellings (សៅ ធ៍ បាយ, សៅ ធ័ បាយ, សោធ័ បាយ, សៅរ៍ បាយ, សៅធិ៍ បាយ), so the entity name is uncertain at [Para 1][Para 18][Para 137].
- The suspension date is printed as 20 March "ឆ្នាំ ២០១៦" at [Para 33] although the events and the award are of 2006.
- The number of participants is given three ways — 40 per cent, about 500, and 326 on the fingerprint list — and the duration two ways (two minutes by the union, five by the company), resolved by the panel at [Para 29][Para 23][Para 24][Para 26].
- The operative paragraph at [Para 139] is corrupt: the words after "បដិសេធ ការទាមទារ" are interleaved with the following order, so the scope of the refusal can only be reconstructed from the reasoning at [Para 130] and [Para 136].
- The employer's arbitrator is printed អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី in the composition and អ៉ីង សុទ្លី at signature, and the worker-party arbitrator ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន versus ស៊ីន គឹម ស៊ាន [Para 1][Para 141].
- Internal-rule numbering and headings are fragmentary throughout the quotation at [Para 49], and the precedent names are truncated ("វីណឹ ណៃ ធីង", "ធ្នូប៊ល ហ្វូត វៃ").
