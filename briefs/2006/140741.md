# Award No. 102/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ ខេមបូឌា ខូអិលធីឌីជី (Sang Vo Cambodia Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 17 November 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, Houn Choun Dy, Trang Tri) · Doc ID 140741

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ ខេមបូឌា ខូអិលធីឌីជី (Sang Vo Cambodia Co., Ltd), a garment factory on National Road 4 in Samrong Tong district, Kampong Speu, employed at least 900 workers and had no union yet registered with the Ministry of Labour [Para 1][Para 37]. On 28 September 2006 the Kampong Speu labour office received a complaint from សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Khmer Youth Federation of Unions) raising 11 demands, including overtime pay for July 2006, an extra gate, fixed working hours, reinstatement of 64 workers dismissed on 24 July and 60 more on 26 September with back pay, a nursery and breastfeeding room or $20 plus three 1-kg tins of milk, adequate medicine and a doctor, sick-leave pay, accident compensation and premium, a 100-riel health-check refund, strike pay, and removal of the administrative chief [Para 28][Para 2][Para 5][Para 6][Para 10][Para 11][Para 12]. Conciliation on 17 October 2006 settled none of the 11 points, and the case reached the Council on 20 October 2006 [Para 29][Para 30]. The hearing was set for 10 November 2006; the company attended but the worker party did not, sending only an official to ask for a postponement [Para 31][Para 32].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the hearing should be postponed at the request of a federation official who attended without written authorisation to represent the workers? [Para 32][Para 56]
2. Whether the panel may close the case, without considering the 11 demands, where the claimant party failed to appear after proper notice and gave no valid reason? [Para 50][Para 71]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ (Khmer Youth Federation of Unions):** The workers did not attend. Thirty minutes before the hearing a federation official telephoned to ask for an adjournment, and at the hearing the federation sent Mr O, who said he had no written authority to represent the Sang Vo workers and had come only to request postponement [Para 47][Para 39][Para 56]. He said two worker representatives were absent through illness and a third could not be reached by telephone [Para 40][Para 58].
**Employer (respondent) — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ ខេមបូឌា ខូអិលធីឌីជី (Sang Vo Cambodia Co., Ltd):** The company opposed any adjournment because its shareholders had travelled to attend and had to return early the following week, and asked that the hearing proceed that day [Para 48][Para 68].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the postponement and closed case No. 102/06 without considering the 11 demands [Para 70][Para 71].
The panel treated the worker party's absence as the threshold issue [Para 46]. Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, Article 21, allows the panel to continue in the absence of a party who was properly summoned and gave no valid reason, or to terminate the proceedings by award; Rule 4.7 of the annex says the same [Para 49][Para 50][Para 51]. The precondition is valid notice of date, time and place, and the panel found that invitations No. 475 and No. 476 of 10 November 2006 had been properly served on both sides, who had also agreed the panel composition and hearing date [Para 52][Para 53][Para 54].
The federation's explanation was held insufficient: the illness of two representatives and the unreachable third were not valid reasons under Article 21, and a request made only 30 minutes before the hearing was not timely [Para 58][Para 60]. Because Rule 4.9 permits an adjournment only where all parties agree, and the company expressly refused, the panel had no basis to postpone [Para 61][Para 69]. It also noted that the federation official carried no written authorisation and could not bind the workers [Para 56][Para 57]. The case was therefore closed with no ruling on the merits of any of the 11 demands [Para 71].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been properly notified and fails to appear without a valid reason, the panel may proceed or close the proceedings by award; an adjournment requires the agreement of all parties, and a union official without written authority cannot represent the workers [Para 50][Para 56][Para 61].
**Obiter dicta:** None identified.
