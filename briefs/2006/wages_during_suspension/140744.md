# Award No. 108/06 — ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កុមារា (Trinunggal Komara Garment Industry Co., Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 13 December 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, Liv Sovanna, Kong Phalla) · Doc ID 140744

## 1. Facts
ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កុមារា (Trinunggal Komara Garment Industry Co., Ltd), a garment factory on Russian Federation Boulevard, Sangkat Tuek Thla, Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, employed workers in several buildings on the same site [Para 1]. On 13 November 2006 the company moved the workers of building D to buildings A and B because it had no goods for them to sew in building D [Para 17]. It had earlier obtained ministerial permission to suspend the building D workers for two months, from 30 August to 30 October 2006 [Para 22]. From 1 to 9 November 2006 those workers had no work and received no notice of any transfer [Para 24]; the company issued announcements of the move on 10 and 11 November 2006, the second of which the workers learned of through their representatives [Para 25][Para 27][Para 28]. The transfer did not affect their wages or their jobs [Para 29]. Conciliation failed and the non-conciliation report No. 1690 of 21 November 2006 was sent to the Council on 22 November 2006 [Para 7]. The complaint had raised 16 demands, of which all but one were resolved or fell away, leaving the objection to the transfer as the single unresolved point [Para 11][Para 12].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company may transfer the workers from building D to buildings A and B over their objection? [Para 10][Para 30]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant) — representatives of the workers of ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កុមារា (Trinunggal Komara Garment Industry Co., Ltd):** They accepted that there was no work, but refused to move: they had previously asked to be sent to buildings A and B and the company had refused and suspended them instead; they wanted the company to terminate their contracts and pay statutory compensation under the record of 25 August 2006; and they asked why some building D workers had been dismissed while they were not [Para 19][Para 20][Para 21].
**Employer (respondent) — ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កុមារា (Trinunggal Komara Garment Industry Co., Ltd):** Building D had no goods to sew, so the company was suspending work there temporarily and had no policy of dismissing the building D workers [Para 3][Para 4]. It had ministry permission for the two-month suspension, and the transfer to buildings A and B did not affect wages or the workers' jobs [Para 22][Para 29].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand that the company be barred from moving the workers from building D to buildings A and B [Para 39][Para 40][Para 41].
The panel framed the question as whether the workers had the right to refuse to work as directed by the company [Para 30]. Article 2 of the Labour Law provides that one enterprise may have several establishments, each being a group of people working together at a definite place under the management and direction of an employer [Para 30][Para 32]. Following its Award No. 06/06 (Im V), the panel held that an employer has the right and authority to manage and direct labour, human resources and operations within the company, so long as that management is lawful and reasonable [Para 33].
It applied its earlier Awards No. 17/03 and No. 18/03 (Ho Hing), which set the limits of that authority: the Council will not interfere with an employer's ordinary business management, and a reassignment is lawful where it does not reduce wages, does not move the worker to a workplace far from the original one, does not switch the shift from day to night or night to day, and does not affect the worker's core professional skills; a transfer that breaches those limits is a breach of the employment contract [Para 35][Para 36].
On the facts, the move from building D to buildings A and B did not affect the workers' wages, their role, or their working hours [Para 37]. The panel therefore found that the change did not affect any individual right the workers derived from their employment contracts, and refused the demand [Para 38][Para 39][Para 40].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may reassign workers between buildings of the same enterprise without their consent where the transfer does not reduce wages, does not move them to a distant workplace, does not switch day and night shifts, and does not affect their core professional skills; such a transfer is a lawful exercise of managerial authority, not a breach of contract [Para 33][Para 35][Para 36][Para 38].
**Obiter dicta:** The Council will not interfere with an employer's ordinary business management decisions [Para 35].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name appears both as "ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល កុមារា" and "ទ្រី នុង ហ្គាល ក្រុមារា" [Para 1]. The Prakas reference is corrupted to "ផ្លូវការ ០៩៩" for "ប្រកាស លេខ ០៩៩", and the same passage dates the fourth mandate to 2009 instead of 2006 [Para 5]. The number of unresolved points is printed as 016 in one paragraph and 01 in the next, and the hearing paragraph adds a stray "៤" before "២១ ចំណុច" [Para 11][Para 12][Para 15]. Paragraph 31 is a bare "ល ។" and the award alternates "អាគារ" and "អគារ" [Para 31][Para 24]. The signature block omits the worker-side arbitrator's name [Para 42].
