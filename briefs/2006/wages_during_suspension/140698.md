# Award No. 34/06 — Gold Fame Enterprise (Arbitration Council, 9 May 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ច for the employer party, វ៉ែន ពៅ chosen by lot for the workers after they declined to appoint, កុយ នាម chair) · Doc ID 140698

## 1. Facts
The collective dispute at ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយ, a factory at Kampong Pring village, Setbo commune, Sa'ang district, Kandal, was not settled in conciliation, and the non-conciliation report No. 070/06 K.B.V/Kan. of 2 May 2006 reached the Council's secretariat the same day, together with news that the workers were on strike. [Para 1][Para 5][Para 6] Representing the workers was សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា; its representatives were recorded as absent. [Para 1][Para 3] The workers refused to choose an arbitrator, so one was drawn by lot, and they then refused to attend the pre-hearing meeting of 5 May 2006 and a second conciliation meeting on 8 May 2006. [Para 7][Para 8][Para 11][Para 15] The panel issued two orders to end the strike and return to work, on 3 and 5 May 2006, warning that it would not continue if the strike went on; the strike continued through the hearing date of 9 May 2006. [Para 9][Para 12][Para 13][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel may continue the arbitration of this collective dispute while the workers remain on strike and have refused to appoint an arbitrator and to attend its hearings? [Para 7][Para 15][Para 18][Para 25]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union did not appoint an arbitrator when asked, did not attend the pre-hearing or conciliation meetings, and gave the panel no indication that it wanted the dispute resolved by the Council at all. [Para 7][Para 11][Para 26]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយ appeared through its two representatives, but the award records no contention by it on the substance of the dispute, which the panel left unresolved. [Para 1][Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel ordered the case file No. 34/06 Gold Fame closed, without examining the merits, so that the parties could use another method of dispute resolution. [Para 28][Para 29]

The panel began from Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004, which requires the parties to suspend all dispute measures such as a strike or lockout during the panel's proceedings, and to attend every meeting the panel convenes. [Para 16][Para 17] It read that provision, as it had in an earlier order, to mean that the Council will not continue an arbitration while the worker party is striking. [Para 18] It relied on four precedents: order No. 04-IMS of 29 January 2004, which held that a party to a labour dispute has no right to strike during arbitration and that the Council cannot then decide the dispute; order No. 2 of 22 April 2004 in case 18/04-Hotel, points 9 and 11, which reserved the right to terminate proceedings where a party breached the order; order No. 24 of 24 December 2005 in case 77/05-Franconia Knitting; and case 28/06-G.D.I.M., where the file was closed for the same reason. [Para 19][Para 21][Para 22][Para 23][Para 24]

Applying those rulings, the panel decided that it would not carry out the arbitration process while the workers were on strike. [Para 25] It added that the union had shown no interest in the Council as a body for resolving the collective dispute, and that keeping the file open would damage the interests of both parties and prevent them from using other means of settlement, so closing the file was the only option. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A panel will not arbitrate a collective dispute while the worker party is conducting a strike: Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 obliges the parties to suspend strike action and to attend hearings during the proceedings, and where the strike continues after an order to return to work the panel closes the file rather than decide the dispute, leaving the parties to other lawful methods of resolution. [Para 16][Para 18][Para 25][Para 28][Para 29]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's remark that the union had displayed no wish to have its collective dispute settled by the Council is an observation on the party's conduct, not a ground of the order. [Para 26]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award contains no `ចំណុច វិវាទ` section and no statement of the workers' demands, so the single issue in §2 is framed from the panel's own jurisdictional reasoning rather than from its numbering. [Para 6][Para 25]
- The case number is printed as "៣៤/០៦-ហ្គោល ហ្វេម" in the header but garbled as "សំណុំរឿង ២០ ៣៤/០៦" at [Para 6]; the operative paragraph names the deciding body "ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញាធរ កណ្តាល" instead of ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល [Para 29].
- The arbitrator chosen by lot appears as `វ៉ែន "ពៅ` in the composition and as វ៉ែន ពៅ at signature, and the chair's name is doubled in the signature block [Para 1][Para 30].
- The employer's registered name is inconsistent — "ហ្គោល ហ្វេម អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយ" at [Para 1] and "ហ្គោល ហ្វេម" elsewhere — and the cited precedent names are partly corrupted (e.g. "០៤-អិម អេស អាយ") [Para 19][Para 23][Para 29].
- The address of the union federation is repeated in broken fragments across [Para 1] to [Para 3], and the secretariat's contact block is OCR noise.
