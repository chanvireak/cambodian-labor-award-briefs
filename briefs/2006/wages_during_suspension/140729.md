# Award No. 82/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ីម និង វ៉េ កាតដេរ អន្តរជាតិ សាខា ទី៣ (Arbitration Council, 20 October 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators កៅ ថា ច (employer side), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker side), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140729

## 1. Facts
On 1 September 2006 the សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា នៃ រោងចក្រ អ៊ឹម វ៉េ complained on behalf of the workers of ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ីម និង វ៉េ កាតដេរ អន្តរជាតិ សាខា ទី៣, asking for 18 improvements [Para 1][Para 21]. Conciliation on 11 September 2006 settled two of the nine points then in issue, and seven unresolved points reached the Council on 29 September 2006 [Para 21][Para 22]. At the hearing of 5 October 2006 five more points were settled — points 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7 — leaving points 1 and 6 [Para 21][Para 25][Para 26]. Point 1 sought 100% wages during short periods without work and delivery of work to the workers' own section; point 6 sought Sunday and public-holiday pay calculated on piece-rate output at 200% [Para 2][Para 13][Para 47].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must pay 100% wages when there is no work for short periods, and bring production to the workers' own section? [Para 2][Para 30]
2. (Issue 6) Whether Sunday and public-holiday wages must be computed on the piece-rate actually earned, and at what increased rate? [Para 13][Para 46][Para 89]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The workers of សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា នៃ រោងចក្រ អ៊ឹម វ៉េ demanded full wages whenever they came to the factory and found no work, and asked that work be brought to their section so that they would not lose attendance bonuses or be threatened by other section chiefs [Para 2][Para 40]. On Sundays and holidays they asked for 200% of the piece rate [Para 47].
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៉ីម និង វ៉េ កាតដេរ អន្តរជាតិ សាខា ទី៣ said it tried to redistribute work and paid 50% when none was available [Para 3]. It said production lines differ, so work could not be brought to each section, and that it would stop the threats and bonus deductions [Para 42][Para 43][Para 45]. It paid Sunday and holiday work by adding $1.73 to the piece rate, following past practice [Para 46][Para 48].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered 100% wages for periods without work and recalculation of Sunday and holiday pay, but refused the demand to bring work to each section [Para 111][Para 112].
On Issue 1(a) the panel held that a short stoppage for lack of work is a suspension of the employment contract under Article 71(11) of the Labour Law, since it flows from a special difficulty of the enterprise [Para 63][Para 64]. Such a suspension must be placed under the Labour Inspector's supervision, and the company neither sought that authorisation nor had any internal rule on suspension, so the suspension was unlawful [Para 65][Para 66][Para 67]. It followed awards 21/03, 01/04, 46/04 and 60/04 for the rule that workers are entitled to 100% wages during an unlawful suspension [Para 68][Para 72][Para 73]. The 5 December 2002 agreement providing only 50% therefore gave less than the law, and under Article 13 it was void [Para 75][Para 76][Para 78][Para 80]. On Issue 1(b) the panel held that Article 2 gives the employer the right to organise work and move workers to other sections, provided safety and health are unaffected and the work is within their skills [Para 82][Para 84][Para 86]. The workers showed no evidence of any such impact, so the demand was refused [Para 87][Para 88]. On Issue 6 the panel applied Article 139, which requires a 100% increase for work on weekly rest days, and Article 163 on public holidays [Para 90][Para 91][Para 102][Para 103]. The base is the piece-rate wage actually earned, not the minimum wage [Para 93][Para 98][Para 99]. It therefore ordered Sunday and holiday pay to be the piece-rate earned plus 100%, following its calculation in case 72/05 [Para 95][Para 100][Para 101][Para 108].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A short stoppage for lack of work is a suspension of the employment contract under Article 71(11) and is lawful only if placed under the Labour Inspector's supervision; without that authorisation the employer must pay full wages, and any agreement giving less is void under Article 13 [Para 64][Para 65][Para 78]. Sunday and public-holiday work must be paid on the piece rate actually earned plus the statutory increase, not on the minimum wage [Para 98][Para 100].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that the employer's managerial right under Article 2 allows it to reassign workers to other sections while work is short, so long as health, safety and skill are respected [Para 84][Para 86].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan is degraded in the procedural section: the number of original demands and of settled points is garbled ("១៨ ឆ្នាំ ២០១", "០៩ ចំនួ ៣០"), so the count of 18 demands and 9 unresolved points is reconstructed from the surrounding text [Para 21][Para 22]. The 2002 agreement is dated 5 December 2002 in one place and carries a stray "២០១៥" in another [Para 26][Para 74]. The employer's name shifts between "អ៊ឹម និង វ៉េ" and "អ៊ឹម វ៉េ" and once appears as "អ៉ីម និង វ៉េ" [Para 1][Para 21][Para 28]. The union's name loses "កម្មករ" in one reference [Para 21]. A stray "៩។" stands alone as a paragraph [Para 83], Article 139 is quoted twice in one sentence [Para 90], and the chair's name is printed differently in the signature block from the panel list [Para 114].
