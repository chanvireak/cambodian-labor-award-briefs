# Award No. 24/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វរ ធ្យូន ហ្គាមែនិង វូលែន ណៃ ធីង ហ្វាក់ តូរី (Four Dimension Garment and Woollen Knitting Factory) (Arbitration Council, 11 April 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី / Ouk Ry, ស៊ីន គីម ស៊ាន / Sin Kim San, តាំង ទ្រី / Tang Tri as chair) · Doc ID 140695

## 1. Facts
The employer is ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វរ ធ្យូន ហ្គាមែនិង វូលែន ណៃ ធីង ហ្វាក់ តូរី, at Prek Khsev Village, Roka Khpos Commune, S'ang District, Kandal, employing about 2,700 workers, some 85 percent of them women. [Para 1][Para 22]
The worker party is សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្វរ ធ្យូន, the democratic garment workers' union at the company, affiliated to សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា. [Para 1][Para 2]
Eleven demands were conciliated on 10 March 2006, seven were settled and four were referred to the Council on 17 March 2006; the panel declined to revisit the annual-leave demand because it had already been decided in the same company's earlier case. [Para 14][Para 15][Para 33]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the dismissal of the cut-section worker holding employee number BM13 was lawful, and whether he must be reinstated in his original job? [Para 2][Para 34]
2. (Issue 2) Whether maternity leave pay must be calculated on the worker's average wage over twelve months, allowances included, or only on the base wage under Article 183? [Para 29][Para 63]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the company must build a day-care centre and a nursing room and grant one hour a day for breastfeeding, or may substitute a monthly cash allowance? [Para 30][Para 106]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, ភាគី កម្មករ និយោជិត):** The cut-section worker was dismissed without reason and because of his union activity, so he must be taken back; maternity pay must equal 50 percent of the average of twelve months' wages including the 5 dollar attendance bonus, the 2 to 4 dollar incentive, the 10 dollar responsibility allowance, seniority pay and overtime; and the company must build a day-care centre or pay 12 dollars a month for 20 months plus one hour a day for breastfeeding. [Para 6][Para 34][Para 58]
**Employer (respondent, ភាគី និយោជក):** The dismissal was an exercise of its own will on an indefinite contract and it would pay the statutory benefits; it already pays 50 percent of the 45 dollar base wage plus 100 percent seniority pay, and the extra allowances depend on attendance, task and employer capacity; it cannot yet build a day-care centre and offers 5 dollars a month for 12 months plus 5 dollars a month for children over 18 months. [Para 3][Para 7][Para 61][Para 71]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the company was ordered to reinstate the dismissed worker in his former position from the date of the award, to pay maternity leave at 50 percent of the average monthly wage including all allowances over the previous twelve months, and to build a day-care centre (or pay the actual external childcare fees against invoices) and a nursing room with one hour a day for breastfeeding; reconsideration of the annual-leave demand was refused. [Para 119][Para 120][Para 122][Para 123][Para 124]

On Issue 1 the panel found no evidence of the union discrimination asserted under Article 279, but held that the employer's stated reasons were unfounded: the worker had nearly six years' service and had risen to cut-section chief, his February 2006 pay included regular wage, seniority, responsibility and incentive components, the company had not followed Article 10 of its own internal rules on warnings, and it dismissed the only "touch" worker while keeping the two inspectors. [Para 35][Para 39][Para 45][Para 49][Para 52] Article 74 permits dismissal on an indefinite contract only for a reason concerning the worker's aptitude or conduct and proportionate to the employer's needs, and none was shown, so the dismissal breached Article 74; the panel ordered reinstatement under Article 34(a) of Prakas 099/04, following 41/04-MiCasa Hotel Apartment. [Para 42][Para 55][Para 56][Para 57]

On Issue 2 the panel read Article 183 as requiring half pay including allowances, and Article 103 as defining wages to include overtime, bonuses, allowances and maternity payments; the 12-month seniority condition in the article was satisfied. [Para 63][Para 67][Para 74][Para 76] Because Article 183 does not say whether the half is taken from base wage or total wage, the panel adopted the total: average of the previous twelve months' wages and allowances, divided by twelve and then by two, as in 49/04-Hou Hing, 68/04-CTU and 18/06-JHJ. [Para 73][Para 78][Para 80][Para 81]

On Issue 3 the panel held that the nursing room and the day-care centre are separate duties under Articles 184 and 186, that Article 186 obliges any enterprise employing at least one hundred women to provide a day-care centre and allows substitution only by paying real external childcare costs against invoices, and that Article 184's one hour a day cannot be bought out for 5 dollars a month. [Para 88][Para 90][Para 98][Para 104][Para 106][Para 108]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Dismissal on an indefinite contract is unlawful unless grounded in the worker's aptitude or conduct and proportionate to the employer's needs; where the employer's stated reasons are unsupported and its own disciplinary rules were not followed, reinstatement is ordered. [Para 42][Para 55][Para 57]
The half-wage payable during maternity leave under Article 183 is one half of the worker's total average monthly wage over the previous twelve months, allowances and bonuses included, not one half of the base wage alone. [Para 78][Para 81]
The day-care centre and the nursing room are mandatory duties of any employer with at least one hundred women: the centre may be substituted only by paying the actual cost of external care, and the one-hour breastfeeding entitlement is not exchangeable for cash. [Para 90][Para 98][Para 106]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's statement that it found no proof of anti-union discrimination, while still finding the dismissal substantively unjustified, and its observation that cash cannot replace breastfeeding for the child's health, were not necessary to the orders. [Para 39][Para 107][Para 109]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The company name is spelt three different ways in the Khmer text (with the initial consonants of "Four" rendered variously), and the ministry report reference garbles the case number. [Para 1][Para 20] The six-year service period is printed with a stray figure before the start date, which otherwise reads 1 June 2000 to 22 February 2006. [Para 24] One reason line states the maternity provision as "50 years" instead of 50 percent. [Para 63] The dismissed worker's name is spelt two ways across the document, and his pay-advance document carries a third variant; he is redacted here as Mr S. [Para 23][Para 38][Para 54] The panel repeats whole sentences in the findings and leaves the operative numbering running beyond the four issues it decided. [Para 41][Para 66][Para 118]
