# Award No. 91/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លី មីធិត (Ho (Cambodia) Garment Ltd) (Arbitration Council, 8 November 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី, អាន វីរៈ, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈាន) · Doc ID 140735

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លី មីធិត (Ho (Cambodia) Garment Ltd) began operating on 1 August 2005 and employs about 856 workers at No. 1223, National Road 2, Chak Angre Leu, Mean Chey, Phnom Penh [Para 23][Para 24]. The claimant is សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រ ហូ យៀ, representing 154 listed workers [Para 1][Para 25]. Of five demands, two were settled in conciliation and two more at the hearing, leaving only the claim for reimbursement of the health-check fee [Para 13][Para 14][Para 17][Para 18]. The company required candidates who passed its test to undergo a health check before hiring, and the labour contract made that cost the worker's burden [Para 27][Para 28].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must reimburse the health-check fee of 100 riel paid by workers who were examined, and bear the fee for workers still to be examined? [Para 2]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ ប្រចាំ រោងចក្រ ហូ យៀ:** the health-check fee is the employer's burden under the Labour Law, and the company should repay the 10,100 riel each worker paid [Para 2][Para 29]. Other companies already repay it [Para 29].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហូ (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាមេន លី មីធិត:** no other company has yet applied this, but if most companies do, this company will follow [Para 3][Para 30][Para 31]. The health check is a condition of recruitment and the labour contract places the cost on the worker [Para 28][Para 30]. The company has never reimbursed the fee since it began operating [Para 32], and it objected to eight names on the list [Para 33].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the company was ordered to pay the health-check fee of 110,100 riel per worker to 154 listed workers within 30 days of the award taking effect [Para 60], with the eight disputed workers limited to the three years preceding their examination [Para 61].
On issue 1, Article 247(c) of the Labour Law requires the employer to bear the cost of health checks, including checks at the time of recruiting new staff [Para 35][Para 37]. Prior awards applied the same reading [Para 38][Para 46]. The panel acknowledged that some earlier awards questioned the continuing force of Prakas No. 09 of 1994, but held that Article 247 of the 1997 Law is itself a sufficient legal basis [Para 40][Para 42][Para 45].
Although the labour contract made the worker bear the cost, the panel held that such a clause cannot release the employer from its legal duty under Article 247(c) [Para 48]. Under Article 13 of the Labour Law, contractual terms contrary to public-order provisions are void [Para 49][Para 50]. The panel therefore ordered reimbursement of 10,100 riel per worker to the 154 workers on the list [Para 52].
On the eight workers whose names the company disputed, the panel applied Article 120: wage-related claims lapse three years after the payment fell due [Para 54][Para 57]. Those eight are entitled only for the three years preceding their health check [Para 61]. The company produced no evidence to support its objection to their names [Para 53].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
The employer must bear health-check costs under Article 247(c) of the Labour Law, including pre-hiring checks, and a contract clause shifting that cost to the worker is void under Article 13 [Para 37][Para 49]. A reimbursement claim is nonetheless cut off three years after the payment under Article 120 [Para 54][Para 57].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel's remark that the employer's willingness to follow other companies' practice is not a legal standard [Para 31].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The health-check sum is stated as 100 riel in the demand and in several references, 10,100 riel in the reasoning, and 110,100 riel per worker in the operative order [Para 2][Para 52][Para 60]. The company name is printed in variants including ហូ យៀ and ហូ យៀរ [Para 1][Para 23]. The Article 120 quotation includes a stray "២០១៥" that is not in the statutory text [Para 54]. From about Para 65 the OCR collapses into unrecoverable character strings across several pages, destroying most of the signature block and annexes [Para 65][Para 66][Para 80]. The worker-side arbitrator's name appears as both អាន វីរៈ and អាន វរៈ [Para 1][Para 64].
