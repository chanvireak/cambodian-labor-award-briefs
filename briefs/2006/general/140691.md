# Award No. 21/06 — Zheng Yong (Arbitration Council, 17 March 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · KONG Phallack, Chhiv Phyrum, Tuon Siphann · Doc ID 140691

## 1. Facts
The Arbitration Council issued an arbitral decision in Phnom Penh on 17 March 2006. [Para 1] [Para 2]
The decision recites the Constitution, R. D. NS/RKM/0704/001 dated 31 July 2004, R. D. NS/RKM/0704/124 dated 15 July 2004, R. D. 0397/01 promulgating the Labour Law dated 13 March 1997, and Prakas 099/04 on the Arbitration Council. [Para 1]
It refers to non-conciliation report number 358/MoLVT dated 10 March 2006, the Secretariat referral 21/06-Zheng Yong dated 13 July 2004, Interim Order No. 002/KBA dated 14 March 2006, and a conciliation report dated 14 March 2006. [Para 1]
It also refers to an agreement dated 21 February 2006 concerning a work-related accident involving a worker. [Para 1]
At the 17 March 2006 hearing, the worker party verbally withdrew Issue 2. [Para 1]
The panel was KONG Phallack, Chhiv Phyrum and Tuon Siphann. [Para 2]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether, after the worker party verbally withdrew Issue 2 at the hearing on 17 March 2006, that issue remained for decision? [Para 1]
2. Whether the referral 21/06-Zheng Yong should be dismissed in consequence? [Para 1]
3. Whether the decision should take effect from the date of signature? [Para 1]

## 3. Positions of the parties
The worker party’s recorded position was that Issue 2 was verbally withdrawn at the hearing on 17 March 2006. [Para 1]
The decision records that the withdrawal was mentioned in non-conciliation report number 358/MoLVT dated 10 March 2006, although the withdrawal itself is described as occurring later. [Para 1]
The employer’s position is not stated in the supplied text; no pleading, answer, or argument by the employer is reproduced. [Para 1]
No witness testimony or union position is recorded. [Para 1]
The panel did not set out any party’s characterization of the 21 February 2006 agreement concerning the work-related accident beyond its recital. [Para 1]
The only explicit procedural position supporting the result is the worker party’s withdrawal, followed by the panel’s dismissal of 21/06-Zheng Yong. [Para 1]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel dismissed 21/06-Zheng Yong; no riel or USD amount was stated; the decision was effective from the date of signature. [Para 1]
The panel decided: “Dismiss 21/06-Zheng Yong”. [Para 1]
The stated procedural basis was the worker party’s verbal withdrawal of Issue 2 at the 17 March 2006 hearing. [Para 1]
The decision also recited non-conciliation report number 358/MoLVT dated 10 March 2006, the Secretariat referral 21/06-Zheng Yong dated 13 July 2004, Interim Order No. 002/KBA dated 14 March 2006, and the conciliation report dated 14 March 2006. [Para 1]
It referred to the agreement dated 21 February 2006 concerning the work-related accident, but did not explain how that agreement affected the claim. [Para 1]
No analysis of the Constitution, Royal Decrees, Labour Law, or Prakas 099/04 is supplied beyond the initial recitals. [Para 1]
The panel signed in Phnom Penh on 17 March 2006. [Para 2]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
When a party verbally withdraws an issue at the hearing and that withdrawal is recorded, the panel may treat the issue as disposed of and dismiss the referral to the extent it depends on that issue. [Para 1]
This arbitral decision is not binding precedent. [Para 2]
**Obiter dicta:** The statutory and Prakas recitals do not establish a substantive accident-compensation rule without reasoned application. [Para 1]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The heading is OCR-damaged, including “CIL THE ARBITRATION COUN” and “KINGDOM OF CAMBODIA NATION RELIGION KING”. [Para 1]
The text chronologically conflicts by linking non-conciliation report number 358/MoLVT dated 10 March 2006 to a withdrawal made at the 17 March 2006 hearing. [Para 1]
It omits the employer’s position, the full issues, any reasoning beyond recitals, and any monetary amount. [Para 1]
