# Award No. 10/06 — North Gaiety Apparel Company (Arbitration Council, 24 February 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tayseng for the employer party, Tuon Siphann for the worker party, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140682

## 1. Facts
North Gaiety Apparel Company, at Building 636, National Road No. 2, Sangkat Chak Angre Kroam, Khan Mean Chey, Phnom Penh, employs 1,103 workers. [Para 12][Para 4] On 12 January 2006 the Department of Labour Disputes received a petition dated 10 January 2006 from the National Industrial Federation Trade Union of Cambodia (NIFTUC) and the worker delegate, giving notice of an intention to strike and demanding wages and 50 per cent of bonuses during the suspension of work from 1 December 2005 to 31 January 2006. [Para 13] Conciliation on 16 January 2006 failed and the issue reached the Council on 26 January 2006. [Para 15][Para 16] At the hearing both parties acknowledged that this demand was the same one raised in award 72/05-North Gaiety, issued on 27 December 2005. [Para 18][Para 19]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must pay wages and 50 per cent of bonuses to the workers for the suspension from 1 December 2005 to 31 January 2006, that demand having already been decided in award 72/05-North Gaiety? [Para 13][Para 18][Para 19]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the National Industrial Federation Trade Union of Cambodia (NIFTUC) and the worker delegate demanded payment of wages and 50 per cent of bonuses for the suspension period from 1 December 2005 to 31 January 2006, together with their notice of an intention to strike. [Para 13]
**Employer (respondent):** the company did not contest the substance of the demand before the panel; both sides accepted that it was identical to the demand decided in award 72/05-North Gaiety. [Para 18][Para 19]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — no jurisdiction, the panel declining to hear a demand already decided in award 72/05-North Gaiety. [Para 25][Para 26]

The panel began from Clause 18 of the Prakas on the Arbitration Council 99/04, which requires the parties to be invited to present oral arguments, and found that both had attended the hearing of 10 February 2006. [Para 16][Para 17] It then applied Clause 40 of the same Prakas: a party to a dispute may lodge an objection to a non-binding arbitral award with the competent authority and take action as permitted by law, so the Council has no jurisdiction to settle an objection or complaint about its own award. [Para 19][Para 20] It added that the settlement of a dispute, judicial or otherwise, must respect res judicata — the rule that the same issue between the same parties, once decided on the merits, may not generally be reconsidered — and followed 18/04-Raffles Le Royal and Raffles Grand D'Angkor. [Para 21][Para 22][Para 25][Para 26] On that basis it dismissed case 10/06. [Para 26]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a new collective dispute repeats a demand already decided on the merits in an earlier award between the same parties, Clause 40 of the Prakas on the Arbitration Council 99/04 and the principle of res judicata deprive the panel of jurisdiction to hear it; the dissatisfied party's remedy is an objection lodged with the competent authority against the earlier award, not a second arbitration of the same demand. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 25]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's explanation of why res judicata is a matter of public policy — to prevent inconsistent results and to bring disputes to finality — is general reasoning that supports, but is not needed for, the dismissal. [Para 23][Para 24]
