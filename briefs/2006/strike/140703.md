# Award No. 45/06 — Cambodia Spot Wear MFG Ltd (Arbitration Council, 20 June 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ហែម ហួ ណារិទ្ធ for the employer party, ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ for the worker party, អាំង អេង ថុង chair) · Doc ID 140703

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខាំបូឌា ស្ពត វែរ អិម អែហ្វ ហ្ស៉ី អែល ធី ឌី, a garment factory at National Road 2, Chak Angre Krom, Mean Chey, faced a strike called on 25 May 2006 by the workers of សហជីព ជាតិ នៃ ឧស្សាហកម្ម វាយនភ័ណ្ឌ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា ប្រចាំ ក្រុមហ៊ុន ស្ពត វែរ, who demanded that the company comply with the panel's earlier award in case 30/06, which had ordered it to pay a piece rate whenever it required rework and to pay at least the statutory minimum wage. [Para 1][Para 2][Para 23] The company objected to that award on 23 May 2006. [Para 24][Para 31] Of the six demands recorded in the non-conciliation report, the federation withdrew issues 2 to 6 by letter of 9 June 2006, leaving only the demand for compliance. [Para 15][Para 17][Para 26][Para 28]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the panel may order the employer to comply with its award in case 30/06 when that award has been objected to, and the workers have struck to obtain its enforcement? [Para 2][Para 30][Para 32]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company must apply and respect the panel's award in case 30/06 of 18 May 2006, and their strike of 25 May 2006 was called to force that compliance. [Para 2][Para 25][Para 30]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន ខាំបូឌា ស្ពត វែរ អិម អែហ្វ ហ្ស៉ី អែល ធី ឌី has lodged an objection against that award, on 23 May 2006, and asked the Council to intervene so that the workers return to work. [Para 19][Para 24]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed the file in case 45/06 Spot Wear and Sup Better, declaring the demand outside its jurisdiction rather than ordering compliance. [Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]

The panel first recorded that the six demands had been reduced to one, the others having been withdrawn by written request of 9 June 2006, and that a withdrawn demand is not considered again, following case 47/05 (Hou Y). [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28] On the remaining demand it noted that the workers wanted the award in case 30/06 of 18 May 2006 applied. [Para 29][Para 30]

It then turned to Article 40 of Prakas No. 099 SK.B.Y. of 21 April 2004, which provides that a party seeking to enforce an award that the other party has already objected to must bring the matter before the competent court for a final ruling, or may strike under Article 319 of the 1997 Labour Law. [Para 32] Because the employer had objected to the award in case 30/06 on 23 May 2006, the panel held that a request to apply that award lay outside its jurisdiction and declined to consider it, closing the first issue and the file. [Para 33][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Once a party has objected to an award within the period allowed, the panel loses the power to enforce it: under Article 40 of Prakas No. 099 the other party's remedy is an application to the competent court or a strike under Article 319, so a fresh collective dispute whose only demand is that the earlier award be applied falls outside the panel's jurisdiction and the file is closed rather than decided. [Para 32][Para 33][Para 36]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observation that demands withdrawn by the union federation are not reopened is a procedural aside, applied here to issues 2 to 6, and does not bear on the jurisdictional ground of the closure. [Para 27]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award has no substantive findings section: the `អង្គហេតុ` paragraph breaks off mid-sentence at [Para 19], and the narrative resumes only at the pre-hearing meeting, so the facts of the underlying piece-rate dispute come solely from the summary of case 30/06 [Para 23].
- The case name is corrupted in the header — "ស្ពត វែរ និង [ស៊ីុប ភើតិច្សា" — and the employer appears as ស្ពត វែរ, ស្គុត វែរ and ខាំបូឌា ស្ពត វែរ អិម អែហ្វ ហ្ស៉ី អែល ធី ឌី [Para 1][Para 12][Para 19].
- The panel composition is unreliable: the worker-party arbitrator ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់ named in the header does not appear in the signature block, where only the employer-party arbitrator and the chair sign, the latter printed as អាំង អេង ផុង instead of អាំង អេង ថុង [Para 1][Para 37].
- The signature block carries a stray "2016" date fragment, and the sentence at [Para 14] is reduced to a fragment ending "នាយកដ្ឋាន វិធានការងារ".
- The award is dated 20 June 2006 in the header while the record mentions a panel letter of 7 June 2006 and a secretariat note of 8 June 2006; no hearing date is given, only a pre-hearing meeting [Para 1][Para 11][Para 19].
