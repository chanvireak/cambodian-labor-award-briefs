# Award No. 83/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន រ៉ូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន លីមីតធីត (Arbitration Council, 18 October 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី (employer side), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker side), គង់ ផល្លៈ (chair) · Doc ID 140730

## 1. Facts
On 31 August 2006 the សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ filed 16 demands for improved conditions at ក្រុមហ៊ុន រ៉ូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន លីមីតធីត, a Phnom Penh garment factory employing about 4,000 workers [Para 23][Para 36][Para 2]. Conciliation settled none of them, and all 16 came to the Council on 29 September 2006 [Para 24][Para 25]. The panel held hearings on 5 and 12 October 2006, both parties present [Para 23][Para 27]. At the first hearing the employer objected that the union was not registered and that the 28 complainants included workers who had resigned and some duplicated signatures; the panel therefore required written transfers of representation to the federation [Para 28][Para 29]. Only 5 of the 28 did so [Para 30]. At the second hearing 9 further points were settled, leaving points 1, 2, 6, 8, 12 and 15 [Para 30][Para 31]. The demands included reimbursement of a 10,100 riel health-check fee, retention of the attendance bonus for three days' authorised absence, a nursery or three cans of milk plus $20 a month, and one hour a day for breastfeeding or $20 a month [Para 44][Para 4][Para 9][Para 13].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Jurisdiction) Whether the 16 demands of five workers out of about 4,000, brought by an unregistered union, constitute a collective labour dispute within Article 302 of the Labour Law? [Para 65][Para 66][Para 70]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ pressed the 16 demands on behalf of the workers of ក្រុមហ៊ុន រ៉ូ ស៊ីង ហ្គាមិន លីមីតធីត, including the health-check refund, the nursery and the bonus demands [Para 2][Para 4][Para 9][Para 44].
**Employer (respondent):** The company objected that the factory union was not yet registered, having only applied, and that the complainant group included former employees and duplicated signatures [Para 28]. It said the health-check payment was a condition of recruitment under its internal rules, and that it had no capacity to fund the nursery and bonus demands [Para 50][Para 62].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel held it had no jurisdiction and refused to consider all the demands [Para 80].
The panel first examined whether the union properly represented the complainants, and required each to transfer representation in writing; only five did [Para 29][Para 30]. It then set out Article 302 and its three conditions for a collective dispute [Para 66][Para 67][Para 68]. Conditions (a) and (b) were satisfied, since five workers had filed and the subject concerned working conditions and employment relations [Para 70]. Condition (c) was not: the five were a very small number against about 4,000 employees, were not key skilled staff even though they worked in the same sewing section, some demands concerned only one worker, others had not yet arisen at the factory, and no evidence showed the dispute could obstruct the enterprise or disturb social peace [Para 72][Para 73][Para 74]. The panel followed its awards in cases 20/05, 57/06 and 77/06, which had refused to consider claims failing condition (c) [Para 69][Para 75]. It held the dispute individual rather than collective, to proceed under Article 301, and because Chapter 12 gives the Council power only over collective disputes, it declined to consider the demands [Para 76][Para 77][Para 78][Para 79].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Five workers out of a workforce of several thousand, represented by a union that is not yet registered and whose demands are partly individual or not yet arisen, cannot satisfy the third condition of Article 302; the dispute is individual, and the Council must decline jurisdiction and leave the workers to the procedure under Article 301 [Para 72][Para 76][Para 78].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that it ordinarily follows the Labour Inspector's and the Minister's classification of a dispute absent a clear reason to differ [Para 65].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The scan is badly degraded in the procedural section. The company name appears as "រ៉ូ ស៊ីង", "រូ ស៊ីង", "រ៉ូស៊ីង" and "Roo Hsing" in different paragraphs [Para 1][Para 31][Para 38]. The employer-side arbitrator's name is printed "អ៉ីង សុទ្ធី" on the face and "អ៉ីង សុទ្លី" in the signature block [Para 1][Para 82]. The transfer-of-representation letters are dated 12 October 2016 instead of 2006 [Para 32]. The number of demands is unstable: 16 are filed [Para 23], the reasoning speaks of 14 [Para 65], and the operative section speaks of 7 while the panel listed 6 unresolved points [Para 31][Para 73][Para 79]. The Article 302 quotation is garbled by inserted words ("ប្រគល់ ឬ ច្រើន នាក់") [Para 66], and paragraph 71 uses "ឧបត្ថម្ភ" where "ខូចសន្តិភាព" is meant [Para 71].
