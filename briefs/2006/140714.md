# Award No. 59/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី / Sang Vo (Cambodia) Co Ltd (Arbitration Council, 8 August 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ច; ទួន ស៊ី ផាន់; គង់ ផល្លៈ presiding) · Doc ID 140714

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី runs a factory on National Road 4 at Trapeang Kong, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, employing 875 workers organised through សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ [Para 1][Para 7]. The workers demanded the reinstatement of 18 workers the company had dismissed [Para 2]. Conciliation failed on 21 July 2006 and the case reached the Council on 26 July 2006, while the workers were already on strike [Para 5][Para 8]. On 28 July 2006 the panel ordered the strike and lockout to stop and all workers to be readmitted, and on 1 August 2006 it issued a second order warning that it would not proceed if either side persisted [Para 9][Para 10]. At the 4 August 2006 hearing both sides confirmed that more than 40 workers were still outside the factory [Para 15].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel should continue to arbitrate the collective dispute where the workers remained on strike and the employer maintained a lockout in breach of the panel's orders? [Para 19][Para 33]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), កម្មករ និយោជិត represented by សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ:** they were no longer striking; it was the company that refused to let them back in, and 15 workers wrote on 1 August 2006 that they had not disobeyed the Council [Para 12][Para 16].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន សាង វ៉ូ (ខេមបូឌា) ខូ អិលធីឌី:** it would take back all workers it selected but did not want workers who cause problems, and it would not under any circumstances reinstate the 18 dismissed workers [Para 17][Para 18].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel terminated the proceedings without deciding the reinstatement demand, because both parties continued the strike and lockout in breach of its orders [Para 34][Para 33].

Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 requires the parties to suspend every dispute measure, including strike or lockout under Article 318 of the Labour Law, while the panel sits, and to attend its meetings [Para 22][Para 23]. The panel found that more than 40 workers remained outside the factory, so the strike and lockout were continuing [Para 19][Para 21]. It had warned in Order No. 017 of 1 August 2006 that it would stop hearing the case in that event [Para 10][Para 20], and it followed earlier awards in Cases 04/04 (MSI), 18/04 (hotels), 77/05 (Frontco Knitting Garment), 28/06 (GEM), 34/06 (Gold Fame) and 50/06 (Yen Yu Internet), where proceedings were closed for the same reason [Para 25][Para 27][Para 29][Para 30]. Finding that neither side had the will to settle, it declined to continue [Para 32][Para 33].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the parties to a collective dispute persist in a strike or lockout during the panel's proceedings, in breach of the panel's orders and Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, the panel will terminate the proceedings without deciding the merits [Para 22][Para 33]. The dispute may be brought again once the strike has stopped [Para 26].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the lockout continued even on the workers' account that they had stopped striking, so neither party's conduct permitted resumption [Para 16][Para 21].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
Paragraph 8 misprints the case number as "១០ ៥៩/០៦" and paragraph 2 duplicates several worker-delegate names with OCR artifacts. The demand concerns 18 dismissed workers, while paragraph 12 refers to 19 probationary contracts terminated. Paragraphs 19 and 24 are garbled, the hearing time at paragraph 7 is printed incoherently ("ម៉ោង ១០០ នាទី ដល់ ម៉ោង ១០:៣០នាទី"), and the signature block at paragraph 35 contains "unk" tokens where signatures were scanned.
