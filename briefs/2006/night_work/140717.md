# Award No. 63/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេហ្វ៉ាយ ខេមបូឌា ហ្វា ស្ហិន លីមីធីត / FY Cambodia Fashion Ltd (Arbitration Council, 16 August 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (ឈីវ ភិរម្យ; វ៉ែន ពៅ; ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា presiding) · Doc ID 140717

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេហ្វ៉ាយ ខេមបូឌា ហ្វា ស្ហិន លីមីធីត operates a garment factory at 1A Preah Norodom Boulevard, Tonle Basak, Chamkar Morn, Phnom Penh, employing about 600 workers organised in សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ អេហ្វាយ កាំបូឌា ហ្វេ សិន [Para 1][Para 2][Para 23][Para 24]. The factory's internal rules, registered on 21 April 2003, set working hours of 07:30 to 11:30 and 13:00 to 17:00 [Para 25][Para 26]. On 24 January 2006 the company announced new hours of 07:30 to 11:30 and 12:30 to 16:30 for building 1, effective 1 February 2006, shortening the midday break by half an hour, without completing the legal procedure for amending internal rules [Para 26][Para 27]. Conciliation on 25 July 2006 settled 11 of 16 demands, and at the hearing of 31 July 2006 three of the five remaining issues were settled, leaving issues 3 and 5 [Para 16][Para 17][Para 20][Para 21]. Issue 5 concerns a single ironing worker, Mr. S.S.A., employed since 7 October 1998 in building 1 at USD 110 a month, whom the company moved to building 2, about 100 metres away, where work starts at 07:00 rather than 07:30 [Para 32][Para 33][Para 35][Para 39][Para 40].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 3) Whether the company must allow the workers a midday meal break until 13:00, as the internal rules provide, rather than the 12:30 resumption it introduced? [Para 6][Para 7]
2. (Issue 5) Whether the company must refrain from changing a team leader's role and workplace without reason? [Para 10][Para 32]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), represented by សហជីព ឯករាជ្យ កម្មករ កម្ពុជា រោងចក្រ កាត់ដេរ អេហ្វាយ កាំបូឌា ហ្វេ សិន:** the shortened break is too short for workers who live far away, must collect children from school, prepare family meals and queue at the card scanner, so the former hours should be restored [Para 28][Para 31]; and the company should not move a team leader's post without reason [Para 10][Para 32].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន អេហ្វ៉ាយ ខេមបូឌា ហ្វា ស្ហិន លីមីធីត:** the new hours align it with the embroidery and printing subcontractors, ease contact with overseas buyers, and follow the one-hour midday break common among Cambodian factories [Para 7][Para 9][Para 27]; and it may reassign team leaders under its internal rules [Para 11].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the company to apply the working hours in its 2003 internal rules until those rules are lawfully amended, and refused the demand concerning the team leader's transfer [Para 78][Para 79].

On Issue 3 the panel began from Articles 2 and 138 of the Labour Law, which place the organisation of working time within the employer's management of the enterprise [Para 42][Para 44], and from Award 11/05, which treats the fixing of hours as the employer's prerogative [Para 45]. It held that this prerogative covers the midday meal break [Para 46][Para 47]. The Labour Law does not fix the length of that break, but the internal rules of 1 April 2003 set it at one and a half hours [Para 48]. Article 30 requires any amendment of internal rules to follow the same procedure as their adoption [Para 49], and Notice No. 14 of 16 August 2002 point 6 says the same [Para 50]. Under Article 24 the rules must be drawn up after consultation with worker representatives elected under Article 283, with opinions recorded in writing [Para 51][Para 57][Para 58], must receive the labour inspector's visa within 60 days [Para 52][Para 53], and must be posted where workers can read them [Para 54]. None of these steps was taken: the company consulted team leaders who were not elected representatives, obtained no written opinions, sought no visa and did not post the change [Para 55][Para 56][Para 59]. The panel therefore ordered the 2003 hours to remain in force until a lawful amendment [Para 66][Para 78], noting that the one-hour break is not unreasonable in itself but must be introduced by the proper procedure [Para 60][Para 61].

On Issue 5 the panel relied on Article 65 of the Labour Law and Article 22 of Decree Law No. 38 of 1988, under which a contract may be modified only by agreement of the parties [Para 67][Para 68][Para 71], and on Article 2, which gives the employer the right to manage and direct the enterprise [Para 72]. Following Awards 20/06 (New Star), 17/03 and 18/03 (Ho Hing), a transfer is lawful where it does not cut wages, does not move the worker far away, does not switch day and night shifts and does not radically change the worker's skill [Para 73][Para 74]. The move here was 100 metres, changed the start time by 30 minutes, and left wages, duties, skills and contract untouched [Para 75][Para 76][Para 41]. The panel refused the demand [Para 77][Para 79].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may fix working hours, including the midday break, as part of its management right, but where internal rules already fix those hours it must amend them through the full statutory procedure: consultation with elected worker representatives and written opinions under Article 24, the labour inspector's visa, and posting under Article 29 [Para 44][Para 49][Para 51][Para 54][Para 78]. A modest reassignment that leaves wages, duties, skills and contract intact and moves the worker only a short distance does not require the worker's consent [Para 74][Para 76][Para 77].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that the one-hour midday break is neither too long nor too short and is already common practice, but that this did not excuse skipping the amendment procedure [Para 60].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The issue numbering is corrupted at paragraph 10, where issue 5 is printed as "៥៥". Paragraph 16 repeats and garbles the procedural narrative, paragraph 21 breaks off mid-list of exhibits, and paragraph 54 states that "four" conditions must be met while enumerating three. Paragraph 74 contains a stray numeral after "ក្រុមប្រឹក្សា អាជ្ញា កណ្តាល", and the signature block at paragraph 81 misprints both the employer-side arbitrator's name and the presiding arbitrator's name, with a stray Latin fragment "Luxaman Vi". The company name is also spelled three different ways across paragraphs 1, 23 and 24.
