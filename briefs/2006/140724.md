# Award No. 76/06 — Sau Thy Buy International Trading Co., Ltd (សៅ ធ៍ បាយ អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយស៍ កូ អិលធីឌី)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ly Tai Seng, employer-appointed; Tuon Siphann, worker-appointed; Kong Phalha, chair) · heard as non-binding award, 18 September 2006 · Doc ID 140724

## 1. Facts

Sau Thy Buy International Trading Co., Ltd (សៅ ធ៍ បាយ អ៉ិន ធើ ប្រាយស៍ កូ អិលធីឌី) operates at South Vuthy, Chom Chao, Dangkao, Phnom Penh. [Para 1] The claimants are the Coalition of Cambodian Apparel Workers' Democratic Union (សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា, C.CAWDU) and its local union at the Sau Thy Buy factory (សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ នៃ រោងចក្រ សៅ ធ៍ បាយ). [Para 2]

The company began operating in 1998 and started using written employment contracts in 2000. [Para 18] Its first contracts ran for two years, and thereafter were renewed for one year at a time, in some cases repeatedly. [Para 19] The company never paid 5% severance when a worker chose not to renew a fixed-duration contract [Para 20], but it had a policy of paying 5% when the company itself ended the contract [Para 21], and paid nothing where it dismissed a worker for fault [Para 22].

The single demand went through conciliation, which failed on 17 August 2006 [Para 9], and was referred by report No. 1231 dated 30 August 2006 [Para 5][Para 10]. The hearing was held on 07 September 2006 [Para 7][Para 11], and the award issued on 18 September 2006. [Para 1]

## 2. Issues in dispute

1. Whether the company must pay 5% severance at the expiry of each fixed-duration contract under Article 73 of the Labour Law, even where the employment relationship continues? [Para 2][Para 26]

## 3. Positions of the parties

**Workers (claimant):** each fixed-duration contract ends at its term, and Article 73 entitles them to 5% of the wages paid during that contract at each such ending, whether or not a new contract follows. [Para 23]

**Employer (respondent):** Article 73 does not say that; the 5% is payable only when the company ends the contract, which is what its practice already provides. [Para 24][Para 25][Para 27]

## 4. Award and reasoning

The panel read Article 73(1) as providing that a fixed-duration contract expires by its own terms when the agreed date arrives [Para 28], and Article 73(6) as requiring severance on termination of the contract, set at not less than 5% of the wages paid during the contract where no collective agreement fixes a higher figure [Para 30][Para 31]. It noted that the phrase "on termination of the contract" is ambiguous, because it is unclear whether it refers to the ending of each worker's contract or to the severing of the employment relationship. [Para 33]

Following its earlier award in case 60/06, the panel held that severance is payable when the employment relationship itself is severed, not at each contractual milestone. [Para 32][Para 34] It therefore concluded that an employer is not obliged to pay 5% at the expiry of each fixed-duration contract while the employment relationship continues [Para 36], that the 5% falls due when a fixed-duration contract ends and the relationship is cut [Para 37], and that where a contract is renewed at its end the relationship is not cut, so the benefit carries over to the new contract rather than being paid out [Para 38].

The panel then applied that rule to the present contracts. Because the workers' engagements had run beyond two years, they had become contracts of indefinite duration [Para 39], and their termination must therefore follow the rules for indefinite-duration contracts rather than those for fixed-duration contracts [Para 40][Para 42]. On that footing the demand for 5% at each fixed-term expiry was refused [Para 43]; the panel relied on its awards in cases 10/03, 36/06 and 57/06 for the conversion rule. [Para 39]

**Outcome:** dismissed — the demand that the employer pay 5% severance at the end of each fixed-duration contract, where the contract was renewed, was refused. [Para 43] The award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification if no party objects in writing. [Para 44]

## 5. Ratio decidendi

Severance under Article 73(6) falls due when the employment relationship is severed, not each time a fixed-duration contract reaches its term; renewal of the contract carries the entitlement forward instead of triggering payment. [Para 34][Para 36][Para 38] A contract renewed beyond two years becomes one of indefinite duration, so its termination is governed by the indefinite-duration rules. [Para 39][Para 40]
