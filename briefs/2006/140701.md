# Award No. 38/06 — Bes Tan Garment Ltd (Arbitration Council, 7 June 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (អ៊ុក រី for the employer party, អន ណន for the worker party, ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា chair) · Doc ID 140701

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន បេស តាន ហ្គាមេន អិលធីឌី, a garment factory at Building 233, Preah Norodom Boulevard, Tonle Basak, Chamkar Morn, employs about 400 workers, who are represented by សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព កម្មករ កម្ពុជា and the plant union សហជីព កម្មករ និយោជិត រោងចក្រ បេស តាន. [Para 1][Para 16][Para 33] Of seventeen demands, seven were settled in conciliation and six more (issues 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10) at the hearings, leaving issues 1, 3, 7 and 9. [Para 17][Para 20][Para 22] The workers are hired on fixed-duration contracts of two, three or four months, and each paid 10,100 riel for the medical check the company requires, either before hiring or shortly after starting work; the company has never paid that fee. [Para 25][Para 36][Para 45][Para 46][Para 49] The company also posts workers temporarily between sections and charges workers who lose their identity card. [Para 51][Para 60]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company must replace the fixed-duration contracts with contracts of indefinite duration because the contracts omitted an end date? [Para 2][Para 34]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must reimburse the 10,100 riel medical-check fee to the workers who paid it themselves? [Para 4][Para 44]
3. (Issue 7) Whether the company must stop moving workers from one section to another? [Para 8][Para 49]
4. (Issue 9) Whether the company must stop deducting money from workers who lose their identity card? [Para 10][Para 58]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** their contracts carried a start date only, so they were never validly time-limited and must be rewritten as indefinite; the company must refund the 10,100 riel medical check each worker paid; transfers between sections should end, and losing a card should not cost 5 USD when wages are low. [Para 2][Para 4][Para 36][Para 57][Para 62]
**Employer (respondent):** ក្រុមហ៊ុន បេស តាន ហ្គាមេន អិលធីឌី will not pay the medical-check fee, though it accepts each worker paid it; it agreed to convert future contracts but asked workers to repay the severance and leave pay already received; transfers are temporary and necessary for the production line, and the card rule protects security. [Para 40][Para 45][Para 51][Para 59]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel refused the demand to convert the contracts and to stop transfers, ordered the employer to repay 10,100 riel in medical-check fees to every worker who paid it, and set the charge for a lost identity card at USD 3.50. [Para 120][Para 121][Para 122][Para 123]

On Issue 1 the panel held that Article 65 makes the labour contract an agreement of its parties and that nothing in it lets the panel compel either side to sign, or to convert, a contract — fixed-term or indefinite. [Para 70][Para 74][Para 75] It noted Article 67(1) requires an end date in a fixed-duration contract, but found the workers had produced no evidence that their contracts lacked one, while the sample contracts shown at the hearing carried clear dates; the claim was therefore not considered, following cases 04/03 (Lida) and 02/05 (Bu Min). [Para 76][Para 77][Para 78]

On Issue 3 the panel held that Article 247(3)(c) of the 1997 Labour Law obliges the employer to arrange and bear the cost of medical checks, including checks on recruitment, and that this is a sufficient legal basis even without a new Prakas; Article 7 of Prakas No. 09 of 19 January 1994 says the same, though the panel recorded its own disagreement over whether that Prakas remains in force, following 23/06 (Mak Phal), 60/04 (Union Textile), 64/04, 98/04, 106/04, 05/05 and 05/06. [Para 80][Para 82][Para 83][Para 85][Para 86][Para 88] The employer was therefore ordered to repay 10,100 riel per worker. [Para 90]

On Issue 7 the panel held that Article 2 gives the employer the right to organise and manage the enterprise, which includes temporary transfers that keep the same wage and the same job grade; the moves were temporary and no evidence showed illegality. [Para 91][Para 98][Para 99][Para 100][Para 101][Para 102] It added that a rights dispute about a future event cannot be decided, following 42/05 (Yung Va), so the demand was refused. [Para 103][Para 106]

On Issue 9 the panel found the identity-card requirement a reasonable measure for order and security, that workers must take care of the card, that the card costs 5 USD and the company had reduced its charge to 4 USD, and that the union asked for 3 USD; under Article 27 the penalty must match the fault, and it fixed the sum at USD 3.50. [Para 108][Para 109][Para 112][Para 113][Para 114][Para 119]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Under Article 247(3)(c) of the 1997 Labour Law the employer must pay the cost of medical examinations, including those required before hiring, and that obligation does not depend on any new Prakas being issued, so a factory that has charged workers for the check must refund them. By contrast, Article 65 leaves the making and the form of a labour contract to its parties, so a panel cannot order an employer to convert fixed-term contracts into indefinite ones, and it will not decide a demand about transfers that may or may not occur. A charge imposed for a lost identity card is a disciplinary measure that must be proportionate to the fault under Article 27. [Para 74][Para 82][Para 87][Para 103][Para 114]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's note that its own awards disagree on whether Prakas No. 09 of 19 January 1994 still has legal effect, and that the issue would need a further Prakas, is an observation on the state of the law rather than a ground of the order. [Para 86]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The award names only four of the original seventeen demands as decided, and the numbering of the decided issues is corrupted in the reasons — "វិវាទ ទី ២៣" at [Para 79] and "បញ្ហា ទី១៦" style fragments elsewhere — though the operative part restores issues 1, 3, 7 and 9 [Para 79][Para 120][Para 121][Para 122][Para 123].
- The employer's name shifts between បេស តាន ហ្គាមេន, ក្រុមហ៊ុន កាត់ដេរ បេស តាន and ប៉េសតាន [Para 1][Para 27][Para 33].
- The medical-check fee appears both as "១០.១០០ រៀល" and with a garbled "១០.១០០" repeated in the operative paragraph [Para 4][Para 90].
- The card charge is printed four ways — 5 USD, 4 USD, 3 USD and "៣.៥០ ដុល្លារ" — and the operative order rounds it as "៣,៥០ ដុល្លារ" [Para 60][Para 64][Para 62][Para 119][Para 123].
- Paragraphs [Para 42] and [Para 108] contain stray fragments naming the company as "ក្រុមហ៊ុន Li", evidently a transliteration left in the text; three sentences at [Para 93] to [Para 97] are reduced to single syllables.
