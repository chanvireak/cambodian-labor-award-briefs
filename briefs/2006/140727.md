# Award No. 80/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គាវ័ន្ត អី ភែរិល (Arbitration Council, 19 October 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · arbitrators សេង វូច ហ៊ុន (employer side), លីវ សុវណ្ណា (worker side), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (chair) · Doc ID 140727

## 1. Facts
On 16 August 2006 the សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ complained for the workers of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ្គាវ័ន្ត អី ភែរិល, a Phnom Penh factory of 450 workers, raising 10 demands [Para 8][Para 25]. Conciliation settled 9, leaving one: that the company keep the $5 monthly attendance bonus for July 2006 for workers who did not work half a day because their wages had not been paid on time [Para 2][Para 8][Para 9]. Wages were normally paid on the 10th at 4pm; on 10 July 2006 the company postponed payment for a bank delay, promised 12 July at 4pm without deducting the bonus for the two hours missed that day, then failed to pay again on 11 and 12 July [Para 27][Para 28][Para 31][Para 34]. On the afternoon of 12 July 2006 the workers stopped work and waited for their wages; the company cut the power at 2pm and paid at 4pm, deducting a half-day's wage and the $5 bonus [Para 37][Para 41][Para 42]. Hearings were held on 29 September and 12 October 2006 [Para 8].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the workers' half-day stoppage on the afternoon of 12 July 2006 was a strike within Article 318 of the Labour Law? [Para 59]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the workers are entitled to the $5 monthly attendance bonus for July 2006 despite not working that half day? [Para 59][Para 68]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** They said they stopped work because the company had repeatedly broken its promise to pay and they had lost confidence in it, and that they waited for their wages and would resume once paid [Para 62][Para 40]. They maintained that deducting the bonus was unlawful.
**Employer (respondent):** The company said it could not pay the bonus because the workers had not worked the half day of 12 July 2006 [Para 3][Para 60], and that it did not treat the stoppage as a strike or as a demand [Para 61][Para 71].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel refused the demand for the $5 attendance bonus for July 2006 [Para 85].
On issue 1 the panel applied Article 318 of the Labour Law, which defines a strike as a group of workers agreeing to stop work in order to obtain a just solution to their demands [Para 59]. It found the three elements present: a group of 450 workers, a stoppage of half a day on the afternoon of 12 July 2006, and a purpose of obtaining payment of wages, following Award 33/06 [Para 65][Para 67][Para 68]. On issue 2 the panel applied Article 332, under which the employment contract is suspended during a strike and neither work nor wages are owed, and Article 334, which requires payment only where the employer hires replacements [Para 68][Para 69][Para 70]. The company hired no replacements [Para 71]. Prior awards hold that strikers forfeit wages during a strike even where it is lawful, but keep the attendance bonus pro rata to days worked where the strike is lawful [Para 72][Para 74][Para 77]. Article 324 requires seven working days' notice before a strike, and none was given [Para 78][Para 79]. Article 333's bar on sanctions does not protect a strike that breaches the law [Para 80][Para 82]. The panel therefore held that deducting the bonus was not a punishable sanction and the demand was unfounded [Para 83][Para 84].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A collective stoppage to compel payment of overdue wages is a strike under Article 318 even where the workers do not call it one, and where it proceeds without the seven working days' notice required by Article 324 the participants lose the pro-rata attendance bonus for that month; the employer's deduction is then not a sanction barred by Article 333 [Para 67][Para 78][Para 83].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that had the strike been lawful the bonus would have been payable pro rata to days actually worked [Para 74][Para 75], and that Article 334 would have required full wages had the company hired replacements [Para 70][Para 73].
