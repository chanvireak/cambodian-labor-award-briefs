# Award No. 08/06 — Himawari Hotel Apartments (Arbitration Council, 3 March 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ouk Ry for the employer party, An Nan for the worker party, Koy Neam chair) · Doc ID 140680

## 1. Facts
Himawari Hotel Apartments, at 313 Sisowath Quay, Phnom Penh, is the Micasa Hotel under a new name from 1 January 2005. [Para 42][Para 43][Para 61] Micasa dismissed four union leaders in March and April 2004; on 6 August 2004 the Council issued award 41/04-Micasa ordering their reinstatement, and the hotel's representative objected to that award on 12 August 2004. [Para 46][Para 50][Para 51] The Cambodian Tourism and Service Workers Federation (CTSWF) then complained on 18 April 2005, asking Himawari to reinstate the four with back pay to the end of the dispute; the hotel answered that they were never its employees and refused to select an arbitrator or appear. [Para 11][Para 14][Para 16][Para 30] One issue was left after conciliation failed on 20 January 2006. [Para 23][Para 24]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether Himawari Hotel Apartments must reinstate the four dismissed union leaders and pay them back pay, given that the Council had already ordered their reinstatement in award 41/04-Micasa? [Para 11][Para 52][Para 57]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Micasa and Himawari are the same firm trading under a new name, the Himawari employment contract recognises staff seniority from 2000, and the Council should therefore apply its own award 41/04 of 6 August 2004 ordering reinstatement of the four, with back pay until reinstatement. [Para 52][Para 56][Para 60]
**Employer (respondent):** Himawari is not a party to the dispute and the four are not its employees, as stated in its letter of 19 December 2005; the hotel declined to choose an arbitrator or attend the hearing. [Para 16][Para 27][Para 28][Para 32]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel declined to decide the issue again between the same parties and held that it has no power to enforce award 41/04. [Para 69]

The panel first asked whether Himawari was a party at all, and found that it was: at the conciliation of 26 April 2005 the hotel's representative took the request and said he would consult management, and the client newsletter of 12 July 2004 recorded nothing more than a change of name from Micasa to Himawari from 1 January 2005, the same four workers and the same subject. [Para 59][Para 61] Case 08/06 therefore concerned the same issue and the same party as case 41/04. [Para 64]

On that footing the panel applied Article 40 paragraph 2 of Prakas 99/04: an award opposed within the time limit becomes unenforceable, and a dispute over the application of a rule of law — including an arbitral award that takes the place of a collective agreement — may be taken to the competent court. [Para 65][Para 67] Award 41/04 was opposed on 12 August 2004 and ceased to have effect from that date, and the Council has no power to order a party to implement its own award, as held in 54/04-Cambodia Sportswear. [Para 65][Para 66] Res judicata barred it from reconsidering the same issue between the same parties, following 18/04-Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Grand D'Angkor and 10/06-North Gaiety. [Para 68]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where an earlier award between the same parties was opposed within the statutory time limit, it became unenforceable under Article 40 paragraph 2 of Prakas 99/04; a fresh collective dispute raising the same issue against the same employer cannot revive it, because res judicata bars the panel from deciding the issue again and the panel has no power to compel compliance with its own award — the claimant's route is the competent court. [Para 65][Para 66][Para 68][Para 69] A change of the employer's trading name alone does not make the dispute a new one: identity of the four workers, of the subject matter and of the undertaking keeps the parties the same. [Para 61][Para 64]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it did not believe the hotel's letters because the hotel sent no witness or evidence to the hearing was not necessary to its conclusion, party status having been established from the conciliation admission. [Para 59][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
- The complaint is dated 18 April 2005 [Para 18] and received on 26 April 2005 [Para 18], yet the conciliation minute is dated 29 April 2005 in the evidence list [Para 33] and 26 April 2005 in the reasons [Para 58]; a later minute in the same list is dated 20 January 2006 [Para 36][Para 41].
- The hotel's address is given as #313 Sisowath Blvd, Chamkamorn, in the header [Para 4] and as 313 Sisowath Quay, Chaktomok, Daun Penh, in the facts [Para 42].
- The resident manager's surname is printed with three different spellings across [Para 5], [Para 41] and [Para 51]; individuals are identified by role here.
- OCR damage is pervasive: sentences are duplicated ("The hotel only wrote" [Para 28], "In this case the employee party" [Para 55]), words lose their first letter ("thearing", "receiving", "bargaing", "Micasand"), and citations of the Prakas and of award numbers are garbled at [Para 64]–[Para 67].
- The Prakas on the Appointment of Arbitrators is dated 9 April 2005 at [Para 16], where the Council's other 2006 awards print 19 April 2005.
