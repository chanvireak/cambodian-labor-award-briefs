# Award No. 60/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូមេ ក ហ្គាមេន ខូ. លីមីធិត / Nume K Garment Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 31 August 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (យូ សួន ទី; ហួន ជុន ឌី; តាំង ទ្រី presiding) · Doc ID 140715

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូមេ ក ហ្គាមេន ខូ. លីមីធិត runs a garment factory at Damnak Thom, Stung Meanchey, Meanchey, Phnom Penh, employing 1,000 workers and producing mainly POLO shirts; the workers are organised in សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ញូ មេក [Para 1][Para 34][Para 35][Para 36]. Conciliation on 18 July 2006 settled 13 of 19 demands, leaving six; at the hearings of 11 and 22 August 2006 two more were settled, leaving four [Para 19][Para 23]. The company accepted that production lines stop for one to three days, at most a week, each month, affecting part of the plant rather than the whole factory [Para 37][Para 38].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 2) Whether the company must pay full wages to workers when it has no work for them, rather than the 50% basic wage it currently pays? [Para 4]
2. (Issue 3) Whether the company must keep the attendance bonus unchanged for workers who are occasionally late? [Para 6]
3. (Issue 5) Whether the company must continue the previous practice of overtime on Wednesdays and Saturdays? [Para 10]
4. (Issue 6) Whether the company must pay 5% contract-end indemnity at the expiry of each fixed-duration contract even where the contract is renewed? [Para 12]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), កម្មករ និយោជិត represented by សហជីព យុវជន ខ្មែរ នៃ រោងចក្រ ញូ មេក:** where the company has no work it should pay full wages, since the stoppage is the company's doing; occasional lateness from traffic or family matters should not cost the attendance bonus; the earlier overtime pattern on Wednesdays and Saturdays should continue; and 5% should be paid at each contract expiry [Para 4][Para 6][Para 10][Para 12][Para 92].
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន ញូមេ ក ហ្គាមេន ខូ. លីមីធិត:** it pays 50% during work shortages as it always has, and will cut bonuses for unjustified lateness only from now on; it refuses the overtime and indemnity demands [Para 5][Para 7][Para 11][Para 13][Para 50][Para 99].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the panel ordered the company to notify the labour inspector before suspending any part of production and to pay full wages for the suspension if it fails, while refusing the demands on occasional lateness, Wednesday and Saturday overtime, and 5% indemnity at each renewal [Para 156][Para 157][Para 158][Para 159][Para 160].

On Issue 2 the panel applied Article 71(11) of the Labour Law, which permits suspension of enterprise activity for special hardship, and Article 72, which excuses the wage obligation only where a rule provides otherwise [Para 63][Para 64][Para 72][Para 79]. It found real difficulties from customer model changes, bottlenecks and late raw materials [Para 68][Para 72], but held that Article 71(11) makes notification to and consultation with the labour inspector a mandatory procedural condition [Para 84][Para 85][Para 87]. The company had not complied [Para 88], so it was ordered to do so and to pay full wages for any future suspension where it fails [Para 156][Para 157]. Since the workers claimed only for the future and proved no past loss, no arrears were awarded [Para 89][Para 90][Para 91].

On Issue 3, Notice No. 017 Article 3 provides a bonus of at least USD 5 for regular attendance, and neither the notice nor the internal rules define lateness or provide for forfeiture [Para 101][Para 102][Para 104]. The panel held the matter is an interest dispute, and that the Khmer Youth Union lacked most-representative status under Article 277, so no order could issue [Para 106][Para 113][Para 116][Para 118][Para 143].

On Issue 5, Articles 137 and 139 and Prakas No. 80 of 1999 let the employer organise working time and request overtime within the legal limits, on a voluntary basis [Para 121][Para 123][Para 126]. The demand was refused [Para 159].

On Issue 6, Article 73(1) makes a fixed-duration contract expire at its term, and the 5% indemnity is due when the employment relationship is severed [Para 147][Para 149][Para 150][Para 153]. A renewed contract does not sever that relationship, so the panel refused the demand [Para 155][Para 160].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer that suspends part of its production under Article 71(11) must notify and consult the labour inspector beforehand; if it does not, it owes full wages for the suspension period even though Article 72 otherwise excuses wages [Para 84][Para 156][Para 157]. The 5% contract-end indemnity under Article 73 is payable only when the employment relationship is severed, not on each renewal of a fixed-duration contract [Para 155][Para 160].

**Obiter dicta:** although no law requires advance notice to workers of a line stoppage, the panel observed that the company should give it as good industrial-relations practice [Para 83].
