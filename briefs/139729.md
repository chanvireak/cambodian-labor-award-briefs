# Award No. 108/13 — ATTO (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 11 June 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-chosen; An Non, worker-chosen; Mae Nimmith, chair) · Doc ID 139729

## 1. Facts
ATTO (Cambodia) Co., Ltd is a garment factory at Prey Doun Ok, Trapeang Krasang, Pur Senchey, Phnom Penh. [Para 1][Para 20] The claimant is the local union of the Khmer Workers' Spirit Union Federation (សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព ស្មារតី កម្មករ ខ្មែរ). [Para 1][Para 9] On 27 March 2013 the union complained to the Department of Labour Disputes with eight demands on working conditions; conciliation on 8 May 2013 left one issue unresolved, referred to the Secretariat on 23 May 2013 under non-conciliation report No. 595 of 22 May 2013. [Para 9][Para 10][Para 11][Para 21] The outstanding issue was whether the company must pay the regular attendance bonus pro rata when a worker takes paid personal leave, the employer keeping the bonus for a half-day absence in a month but cutting it entirely beyond that. [Para 2][Para 3] The hearing was fixed for 6 June 2013 at 8:30 a.m.; the secretariat telephoned the union at 8:10 a.m., was told the workers would attend on time, and by 9:00 a.m. no representative had appeared. [Para 14][Para 16][Para 31][Para 36]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the panel should continue the arbitration at all, given the absence of the claimant union from the hearing? [Para 26]
2. Whether the employer must reduce the regular attendance bonus pro rata, rather than in full, when a worker takes paid personal leave? [Para 2][Para 3]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the union maintained its demand that the company cut the regular attendance bonus only in proportion to the leave taken for personal business, rather than withdrawing the whole bonus. [Para 2] It did not appear at the hearing and filed no documents, so its position rests on the non-conciliation report alone. [Para 21][Para 25]
**Employer (respondent):** the company keeps the regular attendance bonus intact where a worker is absent half a day in a month, but deducts the entire bonus where the absence exceeds half a day. [Para 3] It attended through its accountant and administration officer under a written authorisation of 4 June 2013. [Para 19][Para 20]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel found the claimant had abandoned its claim and ordered the termination of the arbitration proceedings in case 108/13-ATTO; it made no order on the attendance bonus. [Para 48][Para 50][Para 51]

The panel framed the question as whether it should continue in the absence of the claimant. [Para 26] Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 provides that where a party has been duly summoned and fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may either continue in that party's absence or end the proceedings by issuing an award; Rule 4.7 of the Procedural Rules, annexed to the same Prakas, is to the same effect and requires in any case that the parties have been properly notified of the date, time and place. [Para 26][Para 27][Para 28]

The panel followed its earlier interpretation in Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M&V 3, 138/08-Malin, 11/11-John Tixay and 131/12-Master & Frank, which identified three cumulative conditions for ending proceedings in a party's absence: the party was properly summoned, the party did not appear before the panel, and the party gave no valid reason for its non-appearance. [Para 29][Para 30]

On condition one, the secretariat issued summons No. 541 K.B/A.K/V.K/L.K.A. of 30 May 2013 fixing the hearing for 6 June 2013 at 8:30 a.m., and telephoned again at 8:10 a.m. on the day; the panel found the claimant had genuinely been summoned. [Para 31][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34] On condition two, the panel read "appearance before the panel" in Article 21 as requiring both presence at the hearing and participation throughout the four stages of the arbitration — introduction and explanation of conflicts of interest, explanation of procedure and identification of the issues, conciliation if the parties consent, and the hearing on the merits; the union was still absent at about 9:00 a.m. and took part in none of them, so it had abandoned its claim. [Para 35][Para 36][Para 37][Para 38][Para 39][Para 40] On condition three, the union had promised by telephone to attend on time and then gave no reason at all for failing to appear, so no valid reason was shown. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 45][Para 46]

All three conditions being met, the panel held the claimant had abandoned its complaint and that it could close the arbitration by award, and it accordingly terminated the proceedings. [Para 47][Para 48][Para 49][Para 50][Para 51] Because the union never appeared, the panel recorded that it could not establish any facts on the disputed issue beyond the non-conciliation report. [Para 25]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant union, duly summoned in writing and reminded by telephone, fails to appear at the hearing and gives no valid reason, the panel treats the claim as abandoned and closes the case by award without reaching the merits, provided the three conditions of Prakas No. 099 Article 21 are met. [Para 29][Para 40][Para 46][Para 48]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that appearing before the panel means more than being physically present at the hearing; it requires participation through all four stages of the arbitration, from the introduction to the closing hearing on the substance. [Para 35][Para 36]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is a Khmer original with frequent OCR loss, and Paragraphs 13 and 24 are badly corrupted ("Premangent Brenvion miggoo", a stray "ឧសភា ឆ្នាំ ២០១៣" line), so the record of the summons is partly reconstructed from Paragraphs 31 and 32. [Para 13][Para 24][Para 31] The case number appears as 108/13-ATTO in the header and as "108/13-1 ATTO" at Paragraph 50, and the company as "អាត់ តូ ខេមបូឌា ខូអិលធីឌី" in the party list and only as "អាត់ តូ" elsewhere. [Para 1][Para 50] The award states no type of award, unlike the Council's usual closing paragraph identifying it as binding or non-binding, and the signature block is partly lost to OCR. [Para 51][Para 52] The non-conciliation report number is printed 595 in the procedural section and the evidence list. [Para 6][Para 11][Para 21]
