# Award No. 45/09-Whitex Garments — Whitex Garments (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 3 April 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Mar Samborana, Huon Chundy, Pen Bunchhea chair) · Doc ID 140378

## 1. Facts
Whitex Garments (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. employs workers at Trapang Thleung Village, Chom Chao Commune, Dangkor District, Phnom Penh. The claimant is the Khmer Youth Trade Union Federation (KYFTU) Local Union of KYFTU, which filed a complaint on 10 February 2009 raising five demands; conciliation on 10 March 2009 settled three, and two went to the panel — that the US$5 monthly attendance bonus be split into four weekly bonuses, and that full wages be paid where the employer suspends work without following the legal procedure. The union did not attend the hearing of 30 March 2009 and told the Secretariat by telephone that it would not attend a second one, so the panel made no findings of fact on either demand and instead examined whether the case could be closed. [Para 6][Para 7][Para 2][Para 3][Para 15]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the worker party was properly notified of the date, time and venue of the hearing? [Para 21]
2. Whether the worker party appeared at the hearing, in the sense of being present and participating in the whole process? [Para 25]
3. Whether the worker party provided proper reasons for its absence? [Para 31]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant, KYFTU Local Union):** the union advanced no position at the hearing; its filed demands were a four-part weekly split of the US$5 attendance bonus and full wages whenever the employer suspends work without permission from the Ministry. [Para 2][Para 3]

**Employer (respondent, Whitex Garments (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.):** it refuses the four-part split because it already divides the bonus in two, paying US$2.50 to workers no more than 30 minutes late, and says that where it has no work for a short period and has no Ministry permission it pays 50% of wages; it attended as summoned and asked the panel to apply the law. [Para 3][Para 34]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** withdrawn — the panel closed Case 45/09-Whitex Garments, holding that the worker party had dropped its claim by failing to appear. [Para 39][Para 40]

The panel applied Rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council Procedural Rules (Annex to Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004), which allows it to proceed in a party's absence or to terminate the proceedings by award, read with Clause 21 of the same Prakas, which requires the party to have been duly invited and to have shown no good cause. [Para 18][Para 19]

It then took the three conditions from Awards 16/07-Lotus, 27/07-M & V (Branch 3), 95/08-Yung Wah, 132/08-GHG and 138/08-Marlyn and tested each. Notification was proved by the Secretariat's dispatch and its reminder call on the morning of the hearing; appearance was measured against the four steps of the arbitral process — conflict disclosure, explanation and confirmation of issues, conciliation, arbitration — none of which the union joined; and no reason was given for the absence. All three conditions were met. [Para 19][Para 20][Para 24][Para 30][Para 36]

On the consequences, the panel held that the claimant must argue its claim with reasons and evidence, that the union had been given ample opportunity and had passed it up, and that its refusal to bring the dispute before the panel showed a lack of good will. It therefore treated the claim as dropped and closed the case without any ruling on the attendance bonus or on 50% pay during unauthorised suspensions. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 39]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where a party has been duly notified and neither appears nor shows good cause, the panel may terminate the arbitration by award; a claimant that stays away has not discharged its burden of reasons and evidence, and its claim is treated as dropped rather than decided on the merits. [Para 19][Para 36][Para 39]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel could make no findings of fact at all because the worker party was absent, and non-attendance in the face of a reminder call indicates a lack of good will in resolving the dispute. [Para 15][Para 40]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The case number is printed in the header as "45109- Whitex Garments", the slash having been lost, while the operative order closes "Case 45/09-Whitex Garments". The text is an unofficial English translation with the article fused into the following word ("themployer", "thearing"), and the evidence section was omitted from the English version.
