# Award No. 083/19 — Golden Gian Shoes Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 3 October 2019)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (In Sothy, appointed by the employer; Tun Si Phan, appointed by the workers; Kong Phalla, president) · Doc ID 139218

## 1. Facts
Golden Gian Shoes Co., Ltd makes footwear at Prey Ta, Chaom Chau, Por Sen Chey, Phnom Penh, with about 1,000 workers. [Para 1][Para 64][Para 65] On 31 May 2017 the company agreed with the Cambodian Workers Union to pay seniority compensation of USD 80 or USD 120 per year of service and, from 31 July 2017, to convert consenting workers' unlimited-duration contracts to fixed-duration contracts. [Para 27][Para 69][Para 70] Some 2,000 workers accepted and thumbprinted; six refused and kept their unlimited-duration contracts. [Para 71][Para 72][Para 89] Of six claims, five were settled, leaving only the demand that the company pay severance, damages and moral compensation again. [Para 16][Para 21][Para 22]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the employer must pay, a second time, compensation in lieu of notice, dismissal damages and moral damages (សោហ៊ុយជំងឺចិត្ត), where it had already paid seniority compensation under the agreement of 31 May 2017? [Para 66][Para 82]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The agreement was concluded with the union rather than with each worker, paid roughly USD 300 to USD 800 less per person than the law required, and so should be annulled under article 360 of the Civil Code and struck down as contrary to article 13 of the Labour Law; 160 workers are said to be affected. [Para 73][Para 75][Para 77][Para 82]
**Employer (respondent):** Nobody was forced; each worker thumbprinted if they agreed, roughly 2,000 did so and twenty staff delegates among them, and those who refused kept their unlimited-duration contracts and their seniority. [Para 78][Para 79][Para 80][Para 91]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the claims for pay in lieu of notice, dismissal damages and moral damages are refused; the award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification. [Para 127][Para 128][Para 129]
The panel split the question in two. On validity of consent it applied articles 359 and 360 of the Civil Code, under which a contract may be annulled for mistake, fraud, misrepresentation, duress, undue influence or exploitation, and found the workers had thumbprinted voluntarily, that the company set aside 10 July to 4 August 2017 for collection, and that no coercion or threat was shown; the request to annul therefore failed. [Para 82][Para 88][Para 89][Para 92][Para 93] It also observed that the annulment request came about five months after the agreement and two months after performance. [Para 100]
On conformity with the law it applied article 354 of the Civil Code and article 13 of the Labour Law: the Labour Law is public order, so any rule, contract or collective agreement giving less than it provides is void, but the law fixes severance only where the employer terminates with valid reason or on its own initiative under article 89, and says nothing about a mutually agreed seniority payment. [Para 106][Para 108][Para 109][Para 113][Para 114] Because no statutory amount was displaced, the agreement did not contravene article 13 or article 354, and the employer owed nothing further. [Para 123][Para 124][Para 125][Para 126]
The panel added that the claimants supplied no evidence of what each had actually received against the statutory scale, so the assertion of a shortfall was unproven. [Para 120][Para 121]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An agreement converting unlimited-duration contracts to fixed-duration contracts against a lump-sum seniority payment is not void merely because the Labour Law fixes no such payment: article 13 voids only terms that fall below a specific statutory entitlement, and consent shown by voluntary thumbprinting without duress will not be annulled on a bare assertion of disadvantage. [Para 88][Para 113][Para 124]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that where a worker resigns of his own accord the employer owes no dismissal damages, and that a party alleging a fact bears the burden of proving it. [Para 116][Para 117]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award gives the workforce as about 1,000 in the findings but about 2,000 in the account of who accepted the payment. [Para 65][Para 71] The president's name appears as Kong Phalla in the header and as Kong Phally in the signature block. [Para 1][Para 130] The claim numbering jumps from 2 to 4 because claim 3 was settled at conciliation, so the panel's single surviving issue does not track the original list. [Para 2][Para 5][Para 8] The company name is rendered inconsistently in Khmer and appears in Latin only in a quoted letter. [Para 39]
