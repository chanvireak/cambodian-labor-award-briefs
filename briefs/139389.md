# Award No. 085/16 — Hong Sen Thek Stai (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 22 April 2016)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — Ing Sothy (employer-appointed), An Non (worker-appointed), Pen Bunchhea (chair) · Doc ID 139389

## 1. Facts
Ms C, a sewing worker, entered the company on 1 August 2014 on successive six-month fixed-duration contracts, the last running 1 August 2015 to 1 February 2016. [Para 35][Para 36] She stood as a candidate in the 20 November 2015 union election and was elected union treasurer rather than a leadership post. [Para 42][Para 68] On 2 January 2016 the administration chief, Mr H, told her she was dismissed, before her contract expired; she has received no contract-termination indemnity. [Para 37][Para 38][Para 40]

## 2. Issues in dispute
The reconciliation report listed two disputed points; the workers withdrew point 2 at the hearing, leaving one numbered issue. [Para 16][Para 17]
1. Whether the employer is obliged to take Ms C back into work and pay her wages from the date of dismissal until the date of return? [Para 65]
2. Preliminary, unnumbered: whether the panel may hear the case in the employer's absence? [Para 55]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the dismissal was union discrimination — she was a candidate and elected treasurer, her contract had not expired, and notice of the election result was posted to the employer on 23 November 2015 and refused. [Para 40][Para 43]
**Employer (respondent):** did not attend and filed no response, thereby abandoning its right of reply to the workers' assertions. [Para 69]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the employer is ordered to take Ms C back into work and to pay her wages from the date of dismissal until the date she returns; no sum is printed. [Para 101][Para 102] Arbitrator Ing Sothy dissented in part, agreeing on reinstatement but objecting to the wage order. [Para 107]

On the absence, Article 21 of Prakas No. 099 SKBY permitted the panel to continue where a properly invited party fails to appear without valid reason; the employer had a week's notice, had been telephoned again on the hearing day, and had asked that the hearing proceed in its absence. [Para 55][Para 58][Para 60][Para 62]

On the substance, Article 293 of the Labour Law makes dismissal of a protected worker conditional on prior authorisation from the Labour Inspector, and the panel found no document showing any request for or grant of that authorisation. [Para 53][Para 70][Para 96] Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305 SKBY of 22 November 2001 protects candidates for union leadership for 45 days before the poll and 45 days after it where they are not elected. [Para 71][Para 72] The panel applied the three conditions settled in its earlier awards — protected status, dismissal inside the protection window, and notice of protected names to the employer. [Para 76][Para 77] Status: although defeated for leadership, Ms C was a candidate and was elected treasurer, so she qualified. [Para 80][Para 94] Timing: 20 November 2015 to 2 January 2016 is 43 days, inside the 45-day post-election window. [Para 83][Para 85] Notice: the letter of 23 November 2015 was sent by post and marked refused by the postal service; refusal by the addressee did not defeat service by all available means, and the employer did not contest the postal documents, so the third condition was met. [Para 88][Para 90][Para 91]

The dissent reasoned that her fixed-duration contract entitled her only to wages up to its 1 February 2016 end date under Article 73, and that she had suffered no loss of earning opportunity during the interval. [Para 109][Para 112][Para 116]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A worker who stands as a candidate in a union leadership election and is elected to a union office below leadership is a protected worker under Article 3(3) of Prakas No. 305; a dismissal inside the 45 days after the poll, made without the Labour Inspector's authorisation, is unlawful and the panel will order reinstatement and wages from dismissal to return even though the contract was fixed-duration. [Para 80][Para 84][Para 97][Para 99]

**Obiter dicta:** non-appearance after proper invitation was treated as abandonment of the right of response, so the workers' assertions were accepted unopposed; the panel also recorded that the employer had disobeyed Award No. 014/16 of 16 February 2016 ordering reinstatement of the three elected leaders. [Para 49][Para 69]
