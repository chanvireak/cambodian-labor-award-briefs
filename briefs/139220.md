# Award No. 145/15 — A & J Kat Phoeur (Cambodia) Ltd (Arbitration Council, 29 June 2015)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Chhiv Phirom, employer-appointed; Vong Vanna, worker-appointed; Pen Bun Chhea, chair) · Doc ID 139220

## 1. Facts
The Free Workers' Union of the Kingdom of Cambodia (សហជីពសេរីកម្មករ នៃ ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា) filed a collective complaint with the Kampong Speu provincial labour department on 11 June 2015 raising eight demands about working conditions at the respondent company. [Para 15][Para 16] Conciliation on 12 June 2015 failed on all eight points, and report No. 1753/15 of 15 June 2015 reached the Council the same day. [Para 12][Para 17][Para 22] The hearing was set for 25 June 2015; the employer attended through its administrative assistant, the union did not attend at all, and the panel therefore never reached the merits. [Para 14][Para 20][Para 48]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the company must take back three workers (Mr S, badge 1982, team A2; Mr S, badge 2073, sewing team; and Mr N) and allow them to work at their original posts? [Para 2]
2. Whether the company must add 8 USD to the workers' midday meal money? [Para 3]
3. Whether the company must not force workers to work overtime? [Para 4]
4. Whether the company must arrange sufficient medicine for the workers? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must not dismiss workers at will? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company must provide a hot-water machine and cold water for the workers? [Para 7]
7. Whether the company must pay the annual seniority indemnity to workers twice a year? [Para 8]
8. Whether the company must take back workers whom it dismissed without reason? [Para 9]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the demands are recorded in the conciliation report of 15 June 2015 and were never elaborated before the panel, because the union neither appeared nor sent an authorised representative; the secretariat did receive a telephone message that the workers' party would not attend, with no reason given. [Para 21][Para 48][Para 52]
**Employer (respondent):** the employer attended the hearing through Mr L (name redacted), its administrative assistant, but the award records no substantive answer to the eight demands, the panel having found itself unable to establish any facts about them. [Para 1][Para 28]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the panel closed the arbitration of case No. 145/15 without examining the eight claims, treating the claimant as having abandoned them; no money was ordered. [Para 61][Para 63][Para 64]

The panel framed the only question it would decide as whether it should continue the arbitration when the workers' party is absent on the hearing date. [Para 29] It applied Articles 18, 20 and 21 of Prakas No. 099 of 21 April 2004 and rule 4.7 of the Arbitration Council procedural rules annexed to it: parties must be invited to present their claims in person and to file the necessary documents; the parties must attend every session the panel convenes; and where a party, duly invited, fails to appear without valid reason, the panel may either continue in that party's absence or close the arbitration by issuing an award. [Para 29][Para 30][Para 31][Para 32]

From those provisions the panel derived three cumulative conditions: the party was properly notified of the date, time and place; the party failed to appear before the panel; and the party gave no valid reason for its absence. [Para 36][Para 40] It followed a long line of its own awards reaching the same reading, cited in a list running from No. 30/05 Murea and No. 16/07 Lotus to No. 052/15 Thnou Bil Five Sen Garment. [Para 39]

On condition 1, the secretariat issued invitation No. 740 of 22 June 2015 to the union president with the date, time, place and contact details attached, telephoned the union at 16:30 on 22 June 2015 to confirm the 25 June hearing, and delivered the letter to the union directly on 24 June 2015 against the signature of its deputy secretary-general. [Para 41][Para 42][Para 43][Para 44] On condition 2, the panel held that "appearing before the panel" in Article 21 means both being present at the hearing and taking part in the proceedings from beginning to end through all four stages; the workers' party was present at neither and had appointed no authorised representative. [Para 46][Para 47][Para 48][Para 49] On condition 3, the secretariat was told by telephone only that the party would not come, with no valid reason offered. [Para 52][Para 53] All three conditions being met, the panel considered the claimant to have abandoned its claims and its rights, held that it need not continue, and closed the file by award. [Para 55][Para 61][Para 62]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where the claimant party has been properly notified of the hearing and fails to appear without a valid reason, the panel treats that failure as abandonment of the claim and closes the arbitration by award without reaching the merits; the panel will act on that basis only where all three conditions are satisfied — valid notification, non-appearance, and absence of any valid excuse — and "appearing" requires participation throughout the proceedings, not mere presence at the opening. [Para 36][Para 46][Para 61][Para 62]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's observations that the claimant bears the burden of presenting argument and evidence first, and that the respondent's duty to answer arises only once the claimant has discharged that burden; hence no answer was required here. [Para 58][Para 59][Para 60]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The award is short but badly OCR-damaged, and the substantive positions of both parties are largely lost: paragraph 21 records that no documents were received from either party, and the recital of the employer's case never states an answer to any of the eight demands. [Para 21][Para 28] The chair is printed as Pen Bun Chhea in the header and as "ថៃ ប៊ុន ណា" (Thai Bunna) in the signature block, and the employer-appointed arbitrator's name appears a second time as "ឈឺ វី ភីរមនោ"; the composition above follows the header. [Para 1][Para 65] Paragraph 18 prints the panel's constitution date as "17 June 2021 2015", the year being corrected in the text. [Para 18] The company name is transliterated inconsistently (ខាត ផើរ in the header, ខាត ធើរ in the body) and is rendered here from the header. [Para 1][Para 17] The list of prior awards at paragraph 39 is garbled, mixing numbers and names ("លេខ ២៧០៧-អ៊ីម", "លេខ ០៩ និមប៊ីស ថិច ថាយ៍"). The three workers in issue 1 and the employer's representative are referred to by initial.
