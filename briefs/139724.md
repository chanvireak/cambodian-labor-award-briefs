# Award No. 101/13 — Bright Sun International Corporation (Arbitration Council, 29 May 2013)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed Kao Tha Ch; worker-appointed Liv Sovanna; president Pen Bun Chhea) · Doc ID 139724

## 1. Facts
Bright Sun International Corporation, on National Road 41 at Trapeang Kong, Samrong Tong, Kampong Speu, faced a strike that began on 16 May 2013 over six unresolved demands covering wages, allowances and working conditions. [Para 1][Para 6][Para 16] Conciliation on 9 May 2013 failed on all six points. [Para 13][Para 14] Because the strike continued after the case reached the panel, the panel issued Order No. 020/13 on 17 May 2013 requiring an immediate suspension of the strike and a return to work from the morning of 18 May 2013; the workers did not comply. [Para 16][Para 17]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. Whether the base wage must be raised to USD 100 per month so that workers can live suitably? [Para 2]
2. Whether the transport or accommodation allowance must be increased by USD 5 over the existing amount? [Para 3]
3. Whether the overtime meal allowance must be raised to 3,000 riel for overtime worked from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m.? [Para 4]
4. Whether the premium must be increased by USD 5 over the existing premium? [Para 5]
5. Whether the company must install or upgrade water-tank heating so that hot and cold water are available as the workers request? [Para 6]
6. Whether the company must pay normal wages for days lost to the strike or demonstrations, because it failed to honour an agreement? [Para 7]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** they maintained every demand without concession and kept striking after the panel's order; their representative filed a letter of 23 May 2013 and confirmed orally that the strike was continuing. [Para 13][Para 17][Para 22]
**Employer (respondent):** represented by counsel, it held to its existing policy, asked the panel on 16 May 2013 to order the strike suspended, and reported on 23 May 2013 that the strike was still running despite Order 020/13. [Para 1][Para 18][Para 21]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** no jurisdiction to proceed — the panel declined to continue the arbitration and closed case 101/13 without deciding any of the six demands; no payment or other order was made. [Para 38][Para 39]

The panel applied Article 20 of Prakas No. 099, under which parties must suspend all dispute measures such as strikes or lockouts, and any other measure capable of aggravating the dispute, for the duration of the arbitration, and must attend every meeting convened. [Para 29] Reading the article with its earlier awards, it held that the panel will not arbitrate while a strike is in progress. [Para 29][Para 36]

It relied on Award 04/04 MSI, where a party striking during arbitration was held to have no right to strike and the panel declined to settle or issue an award until the strike ended; on Award 18/04 (the hotel cases: Cambodiana, Raffles Le Royal, Raffles Le Grand, Sokha Angkor, Sunway, InterContinental and Sofitel Angkor), where the panel ordered the strike stopped and reserved the right to close proceedings on breach; on Award 77/06 Franco Night Thing; and on Award 110/11 Simano, where the panel declined to continue because workers kept striking. [Para 30][Para 32][Para 33][Para 34][Para 35]

On the facts, the employer's request of 16 May 2013, its report No. 054 of 23 May 2013 and the workers' own oral confirmation all showed the strike continuing after Order 020/13, so the panel closed the file. [Para 18][Para 17][Para 37] The panel made no findings on the merits because it held no hearing. [Para 28]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A strike that continues after the panel has ordered it suspended under Article 20 of Prakas No. 099 removes the basis for arbitration: the panel closes the case without ruling on the demands, and the dispute may be pursued again only when the strike has ended. [Para 29][Para 31][Para 38]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel's statement that it retains the right to terminate proceedings where a party breaches its order, and that a closed case can be reopened once the strike stops [Para 31][Para 33].
