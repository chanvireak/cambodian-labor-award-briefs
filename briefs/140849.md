# Award No. 031/21 — ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូ គួន (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន ឯ.ក / Huu Koun (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd. (Arbitration Council, 22 December 2021)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Ing Sothy, employer-appointed; An Non, worker-appointed; Kong Phalla, chair, with a written dissent by Ing Sothy) · non-binding award · Doc ID 140849

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន ហ៊ូ គួន (ខេមបូឌា) ហ្គាម៉ិន ឯ.ក (Huu Koun (Cambodia) Garment Co., Ltd.) runs a garment factory employing 430 workers, and it acquired the workforce of ក្រុមហ៊ុន ឌីហ្សាំង ហៅ វ៉ៃ (Dey Sang Way), an insolvent company it bought, with every transferred worker signing a fresh contract on 6 February 20.. [Para 81][Para 88][Para 89][Para 87]
The worker representatives (ភាគីតំណាងកម្មករ និយោជិត) filed 23 collective demands on 27 September 2021; 14 were settled at conciliation on 20 October 2021 and three more (Issues 4, 7 and 8) at the hearing, leaving six issues for the panel. [Para 25][Para 26][Para 33][Para 34]
The disputed acts are the non-payment of seniority bonus before June 2021, the exclusion of that bonus from the overtime base, and four welfare or pay demands. [Para 84][Para 119][Para 131][Para 156]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must pay retroactive seniority bonus (ប្រាក់រង្វាន់អតីតភាពការងារ) for the period before June 2021 to the workers who thumbprinted the demand? [Para 84][Para 160]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the annual seniority bonus must be added to the base wage used to calculate overtime pay? [Para 119][Para 234]
3. (Issue 3) Whether the employer must provide a hygienic drinking glass or water bottle to each worker? [Para 131][Para 271]
4. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must post up its rules on entitlement to the USD 5 cooperation bonus? [Para 141][Para 280]
5. (Issue 6) Whether the employer must build a roof over the motorbike parking area? [Para 152][Para 296]
6. (Issue 9) Whether Sunday overtime must be paid at twice the piece rate actually earned that day? [Para 156][Para 318]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** Long-serving workers were owed seniority bonus back to their real hire dates because ឌីហ្សាំង ហៅ វ៉ៃ never paid them anything on transfer, and they still carry that company's ID cards; seniority bonus is part of wage under Article 103, so it must enter the overtime base; they also pressed the drinking water, the USD 5 cooperation bonus notice, the parking roof and double piece rate for Sundays. [Para 93][Para 100][Para 121][Para 132][Para 144][Para 158]
**Employer (respondent):** The company only began operating on 6 February 20.., all transferred workers signed new contracts so seniority runs from that date, ឌីហ្សាំង ហៅ វ៉ៃ had already paid termination benefits, it agreed to pay bonus for February to May 2021 but pleaded economic difficulty, and it said Article 109 requires posting only the minimum wage and that the industrial park owner forbids a roof. [Para 87][Para 102][Para 103][Para 122][Para 149][Para 154]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — the retroactive bonus claim was rejected for Mr Y and Mr So but the employer was ordered to pay seniority bonus to Mr H for the last three years (2019, 2020 and 2021) and to Mr Se according to his actual seniority; to add seniority bonus to the base wage for overtime calculation; and to pay Sunday overtime at twice the piece rate earned; the water, USD 5 notice and parking roof demands were rejected. [Para 334][Para 335][Para 336][Para 340][Para 337][Para 338][Para 339]

On Issue 1 the panel first had to identify the claimants: although the submission spoke of 114 thumbprints, only a list bearing 28 names and thumbprints was produced, so only those 28 were original complainants. [Para 179][Para 180][Para 181] It then applied Article 87-new of the Labour Law, under which a new employer taking over an enterprise by sale or succession must maintain the contracts in force, including accrued seniority. [Para 214][Para 221] Because the employer proved no termination payment by ឌីហ្សាំង ហៅ វ៉ៃ, seniority carried over. [Para 220][Para 225] Only four workers had provable hire dates, and Article 120-new limits wage claims to three years, so Mr Y and Mr So, hired in 2020, had already been paid from June 2021 and got nothing more, while Mr H recovered 2019–2021 and Mr Se recovered in full. [Para 203][Para 226][Para 231][Para 232]

On Issue 2 the panel read "ប្រាក់ឈ្នួលពេលធ្វើការធម្មតា" in Article 139-new as the wage defined by Article 102, not the aggregate income of Article 103, so the bonus is an addition to normal wage that must be consolidated into the overtime base. [Para 241][Para 245][Para 266][Para 269] It expressly departed from Award No. 111/08 (Young Min), which had held the opposite. [Para 264][Para 265]

On Issue 3 the panel found a duty under Prakas No. 054 to arrange clean glasses or other hygienic means, but no duty to give each worker a bottle, so it rejected the demand as framed while noting the employer's practice of supplying water alone was insufficient. [Para 272][Para 276][Para 278][Para 279]

On Issue 5 the panel held Article 109 requires posting of the statutory minimum wage only, and that Article 2 managerial prerogative lets the employer choose how to publicise a unilateral bonus scheme. [Para 285][Para 288][Para 291][Para 294]

On Issue 6 the panel treated the roof as a demand above the law, an interest dispute, which it could not decide because the claimant was not the most representative union under Article 54-new of the Union Law. [Para 300][Para 309][Para 315][Para 317]

On Issue 9 the panel applied Article 139-new with Prakas No. 100, article 5, holding Sunday work payable at 200 per cent of the piece rate earned that day, not merely piece rate plus one day's base wage. [Para 319][Para 321][Para 330][Para 333]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A new employer that buys an insolvent enterprise inherits the accrued seniority of the transferred workers and must pay seniority bonus on it, unless it proves the old employer settled those entitlements at transfer; the claim is nonetheless cut off three years back by Article 120-new. [Para 221][Para 225][Para 231]
Seniority bonus is a recurrent addition to normal wage, so it must be consolidated into the base for overtime and Sunday premiums, and the panel will depart from its earlier contrary award where the statutory text supports it. [Para 266][Para 269][Para 264]

**Obiter dicta:** The employer's practice of supplying drinking water without any hygienic glass or bottle was insufficient even though the demand for one bottle per worker failed. [Para 276]

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The OCR repeatedly truncated the year in dates, printing "ឆ្នាំ ២០" where 2020 or 2021 was meant, so the company's opening date and several hire dates survive without a full year. [Para 86][Para 186][Para 205] Hire dates for Mr H are printed as both 2015 and "2023" in the same reasoning, and one paragraph gives his start date twice with different years. [Para 230][Para 222] The number of claimants is given as 114 thumbprints in the submission but 28 on the only list produced, and 17 ID cards were filed of which three matched. [Para 162][Para 180][Para 185] The conciliation report is cited as No. 709 in one place and No. 708 in another, and the union's name is printed once as "ប៊ូ គួន" instead of "ហ៊ូ គួន". [Para 117][Para 67] Issues 4, 7 and 8 are missing from the panel's list because the parties settled them at the hearing. [Para 33]
