# Award No. 107/06 — ក្រុមហ៊ុនូវ ខប (Nouv Khob) garment factory (Arbitration Council, 6 December 2006)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (employer-appointed កៅ ថា ច; worker-appointed វ៉ែន ពៅ; chair អាំង អេង ថុង) · Doc ID 140746

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុនូវ ខប, a garment factory on National Road 51 in Ang Snuol district, Kandal province, suspended the contracts of all 964 workers from 11 September to 10 November 2006 with the approval of the Kandal provincial labour department [Para 18][Para 19]. It then obtained a second suspension covering 503 workers from 11 November 2006 to 11 January 2007, authorised by letter 195 KbV/KCh of 8 November 2006 [Para 23]. The workers' federations, NIFTUC and CFWR, claimed 100% of wages for the two months of that second suspension [Para 2][Para 24]. Conciliation failed and the case was referred to the panel [Para 6].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the second suspension of the employment contracts was lawful and, if not, whether the workers were entitled to 100% of their wages for the two months concerned? [Para 17][Para 27]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** The 503 affected workers contended that the second suspension was not carried out in accordance with the procedure laid down in the Labour Law, so 100% wages were owed for the period [Para 24].
**Employer (respondent):** The company relied on the labour department's reply letter 195 KbV/KCh of 8 November 2006 authorising the suspension, and stated that the $7 monthly accommodation payment had to be met from its own funds because it held no reserve [Para 3][Para 25].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to pay 100% of wages for the two-month suspension from 11 November 2006 to 11 January 2007 [Para 64].

The panel held the first suspension lawful under Article 71-11 of the Labour Law, treating it as a "special difficulty" within that article and noting that it had been authorised by the Kandal labour department and was not contested by the workers [Para 29][Para 30]. Article 71-11 places the suspension under the supervision of the labour inspector [Para 31][Para 55], and the panel found that element satisfied because the employer had applied to the labour inspector and received authorisation [Para 32][Para 57]. On the two-month limit, the panel preferred the reading that Article 71-11 allows suspension only once, following its earlier award in case 95/04 SD Cambodia Ltd [Para 48][Para 49]. It relied on the French text "Cette suspension devrait être inférieure ou égale à deux mois" and contrasted Article 71-3, which expressly permits repeated suspension [Para 50][Para 52][Para 53][Para 54]. Because the second suspension satisfied only elements 1 and 3 of Article 71-11 and not element 2, the panel held it contrary to the spirit of that article [Para 58]. On entitlement, it followed prior awards 46/04, 21/03, 1/03 and 60/04 holding that workers whose suspension is unlawful receive 100% wages for the period [Para 59][Para 62][Para 63], and ordered payment accordingly [Para 64]. The award is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification [Para 65].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may suspend employment contracts under Article 71-11 of the Labour Law only once, for a period not exceeding two months; a second suspension on the same ground is unlawful and obliges the employer to pay full wages for the whole period [Para 46][Para 48][Para 58].

**Obiter dicta:** The panel observed that an authorisation or interpretation given by the labour inspector does not bind the Arbitration Council in its own reading of the Labour Law [Para 33].
