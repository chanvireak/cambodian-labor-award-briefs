# Award No. 77/09 — Im Way Garment International, Branch 4 (Arbitration Council, 9 July 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators — កៅ ថា ច (employer-appointed), ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់ (worker-appointed), ប៉ែន ប៊ុន ឈា (chair) · Doc ID 140398

## 1. Facts
ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៊ឹម វ៉េ កាត់ដេរ អន្តរជាតិ (សាខា ទី៤ ខេត្ត កំពង់ឆ្នាំង) ("Im Way Garment International, Branch 4") employs about 2,873 workers at Kampong Chhnang [Para 34]. The claimant is សហព័ន្ធ សហជីព សហជីព អភិវឌ្ឍន៍ ការងារ រោងចក្រ អ៊ឹម វ៉េ, the factory local of the Labour Development Union Federation (LDUF) [Para 1][Para 36]. Ms O C T, a pressing-section worker, joined on 2 June 2008 on two months' probation and then signed successive three-month fixed-duration contracts, the last running from 2 February to 30 April 2009 [Para 39][Para 40]. On 23 April 2009 the company gave her seven days' notice of non-renewal, which she thumbprinted, and on 30 April it ended the contract and paid the fixed-duration severance, which she also accepted by thumbprint [Para 41][Para 42]. She was elected deputy union president in a vote on 17 May 2009; the union gave notice on 19 May and the company received it on 27 May [Para 46][Para 47].

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the company's refusal to renew Ms O C T's fixed-duration contract was discrimination against a union leader, and whether it must take her back into work? [Para 37][Para 70]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the company knew she was a candidate for union president and that she had led 15 workers to the International Labour Day event on 21 April 2009, and refused renewal for that reason; another union in the factory was the one keeping the attendance list at that event [Para 44][Para 49][Para 51]. She had worked well, had never been warned or disciplined, and had been made a team leader after two or three months; when she returned on 7 or 8 May the company offered her two teams to supervise and, when she said she lacked the capacity and asked to return as an ordinary worker, told her to wait while a new post was found [Para 38][Para 56][Para 57][Para 60].
**Employer (respondent):** the renewal decision was taken on 30 April 2009, before the union notified it of the new leadership on 27 May; it did not know she was a union leader at the time [Para 64][Para 65]. She declined the team-leader role, so no alternative post could be found, and the contract simply ran to its term with the severance paid under Article 73 [Para 59][Para 69]. It granted her ordinary personal leave for the 21 April event and did not know she was leading workers there [Para 52][Para 54].

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** dismissed — the union's demand that the company renew Ms O C T's fixed-duration contract so that she could return to normal work was refused, and nothing was ordered [Para 101][Para 102]. The order is non-binding and becomes final eight days after notification [Para 103].

**Discrimination.** Articles 12 and 279 forbid an employer to base discipline, termination, assignment, promotion or pay on union membership or union activity, and earlier awards order reinstatement where a dismissal is shown to be anti-union [Para 70][Para 71][Para 74]. The claimant carries the burden of proving the discrimination, and the panel weighs the statements at hearing against the documents (Awards 93/06, 112/06, 01/07) [Para 75][Para 76]. Here the union asserted that the company knew of her candidacy and of her role at the 21 April event, but never explained how the company knew, and produced no argument or evidence that it did [Para 81][Para 82][Para 89]. The panel also noted that the union's own account put the notice of the new leadership at 19 May, received 27 May, whereas the non-renewal notice was issued on 23 April and the decision taken on 30 April — before any notification reached the employer [Para 83][Para 84][Para 86]. The company's willingness to keep her on as a team leader, and her refusal of that role, confirmed that the ending was not motivated by her union position [Para 87].

**Legality of the non-renewal.** Article 73(1) provides that a fixed-duration contract expires at its term; following Award 100/07 the panel held that the expiry extinguishes the parties' obligations automatically and that neither party can compel the other to renew without agreement [Para 91][Para 93][Para 95]. The employer may therefore decide not to renew, unless it acts on a discriminatory ground [Para 96][Para 97]. The panel found no such ground here, and the severance required by Article 73 had been paid and collected on 13 May 2009 [Para 99][Para 100].

## 5. Ratio decidendi
An employer may lawfully decline to renew a fixed-duration contract at its term, since Article 73(1) brings the relationship to an end automatically and renewal depends on agreement; that right is lost only where the union proves, on evidence and not merely assertion, that the non-renewal was grounded in the worker's union membership or activity — and a decision taken before the employer was notified of the worker's union office cannot rest on that office [Para 84][Para 89][Para 96].

**Obiter dicta:** the panel observed that a claimant union must explain how the employer came to know of a worker's union role; asserting knowledge without identifying its source is not enough, however plausible the timing may look [Para 82][Para 89].

## 6. Source defects disclosed
The worker's name is printed in at least six different spellings across the file — with variant forms of each of her three name elements, including one version beginning with a different initial letter — so she is identified here by initials only [Para 2][Para 46][Para 54]. The award's name in the header reads ៧៧/០៩-អ៊ឹម វ៉េ ទី៤ while the employer is named in four further ways in the body, including ក្រុមហ៊ុន អ៊ឹម និង វ៉េ សា ខេត្ត កំពង់ឆ្នាំង [Para 1][Para 9][Para 34]. Paragraph 14 records the number of non-conciliated points as "២០១៦ ចំណុច" where the context requires one [Para 14]. The conciliation minutes in the Ministry's file are dated 16 June 2008 although the conciliation was held on 16 June 2009, and the evidence list breaks its numbering with an entry marked "D-" instead of 10 [Para 24][Para 31]. The worker-appointed arbitrator's name appears as ទូន ស៊ី ផាន់ on the face of the award and as ទួន សុផាន at the signature block, whose scrawl is otherwise unreadable [Para 1][Para 104].
