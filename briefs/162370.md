# Award No. 009/26 — Yupi Aluminum Menu Furniture (Cambodia) Co., Ltd (Arbitration Council, 28 August 2026)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (Kuy Neam presiding; Ung Sothy appointed by the employer; An Non appointed by the workers) · Doc ID 162370

## 1. Facts
The employer, ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូភី អាលុយ មីនៀម មេនូហ្វេ ក ធឺរីង (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក — Yupi Aluminum Menu Furniture (Cambodia) Co., Ltd — processes aluminium and employed 603 workers, 79 of them women. [Para 60] Thirty-two workers, represented by ភាគី តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូភី អាលុយ មីនៀម មេនូហ្វេ ក ធឺរីង (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក and by the president of the Cambodian Labour Union Federation, lodged eleven demands on 13 July 2026; conciliation on 20 July 2026 settled five and left two for the panel. [Para 10][Para 11][Para 12][Para 62] The company pays wages twice a month, on the 10th and on the 25th of the following month. [Para 70]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 1) Whether the employer must issue payslips to the workers five days before the payday? [Para 138]
2. (Issue 2) Whether the employer must provide a nutritional supplement — a sugary drink or a sweet cake — to workers at every overtime session? [Para 155]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant), តំណាង កម្មករ និយោជិត នៃ ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូភី អាលុយ មីនៀម មេនូហ្វេ ក ធឺរីង (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក:** five days lets them verify days worked, overtime hours and benefits, query anything wrong and have it corrected before the money is paid. [Para 73][Para 74] They also rely on a ministerial notification directing sugary food at overtime, and ask the panel to enforce it. [Para 97][Para 99]
**Employer (respondent), ក្រុមហ៊ុន យូភី អាលុយ មីនៀម មេនូហ្វេ ក ធឺរីង (ខេមបូឌា) ឯ.ក:** payslips two days before payday are a reasonable period, complaints are settled within one hour, and it already serves a midday meal plus a dinner to those working overtime, so the demand is met. [Para 79][Para 80][Para 100][Para 101]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** partly granted — Issue 1 dismissed; the employer ordered to provide sugary food or drink, or sweet cakes, at every overtime session. [Para 171][Para 172]
The panel first settled the representation question, holding that a representative acting for workers needs written authority, and that under Article 54(2) of the Civil Procedure Code an authorised representative may appoint a sub-representative, so the federation president validly represented the 32 claimants. [Para 114][Para 121][Para 135][Para 137]
On Issue 1 the panel read Article 112 of the Labour Law as obliging the employer to tell workers clearly and understandably what their wages are, and to repeat that notice whenever the wage changes, but as fixing no deadline before payday. [Para 138][Para 146] Following its Award 05/06, notice of a changed wage is what the article requires. [Para 139][Para 147] The employer's existing practice therefore already complied and no duty to issue payslips five days ahead was found. [Para 148][Para 152][Para 153]
On Issue 2 the panel applied Notification No. 003 SKBY of 10 February 2004, which directs owners of factories and enterprises to give workers a supplement of sugary food — a sugary drink or sweet cake — at every overtime session, over and above overtime pay. [Para 155] Following Award 031/22 it treated that as a binding duty, and held that providing a rice meal under Notification No. 041/11 satisfies a different obligation only. [Para 156][Para 167][Para 168] The panel also rejected the argument that an aluminium plant falls outside the notification, reading the word "special" as emphasis on garment and shoe factories rather than as a limit on scope. [Para 163][Para 164][Para 165]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
A wage-information duty is discharged by clear notice of the wage actually payable for each pay period; it does not require the payslip to be handed over a fixed number of days before payday. [Para 138][Para 146] Where a ministerial notification requires sugary food or sweet cakes at every overtime session, supplying a rice meal instead does not discharge that duty, and the duty applies to any factory or enterprise, not only to garment and shoe plants. [Para 155][Para 163][Para 167]

**Obiter dicta:** The panel noted that the company had moved from one day to two days of advance payslip notice in July 2026 without that change meeting the five-day demand. [Para 72][Para 148]
