# Award No. 80/09-សិប្បកម្មត្បាញភូមិព្រែកត្រែង — សិប្បកម្ម ត្បាញ ភូមិ ព្រែក ត្រែង (Arbitration Council, 14 July 2009)
Forum: Arbitration Council of Cambodia · 3 arbitrators (កៅ ថា ចង់ for the employer, វ៉ែន ពៅ for the workers, chair អាំង អេង ថុង) · Doc ID 140402

## 1. Facts
សិប្បកម្ម ត្បាញ ភូមិ ព្រែក ត្រែង ("Prek Treng weaving workshop") weaves cloth at Prek Treng village, Sitbo commune, Saang district, Kandal, employing about 1,200 workers, and it had not yet registered itself as an enterprise. [Para 37][Para 54] The 884 workers who thumbprinted were represented by សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា (C.CAWDU), a union that was also not yet registered with the Ministry. [Para 38][Para 40] Of 22 collective demands, 11 more were settled by agreement before the hearing, leaving only Issue 5 for the panel. [Para 24][Para 25] Workers are paid by the piece ("តម្លៃឡូត៍") in checking, ironing, measuring, punching, labelling and sewing sections. [Para 41]

## 2. Issues in dispute
1. (Issue 5) Whether the employer must issue the piece rate for each lot to the workers before they perform the work, and within how many days must that rate be disclosed? [Para 41][Para 63]

## 3. Positions of the parties
**Workers (claimant):** the workers represented by សម្ព័ន្ធ សហជីព ប្រជាធិបតេយ្យ កម្មករ កាត់ដេរ កម្ពុជា said the employer never announces the piece rate in advance and only reveals it at month end when wages are paid, and asked for a three-day trial followed by disclosure of the rate on the fourth day, citing nearby factories that do it within three to four days. [Para 42][Para 44][Para 46]
**Employer (respondent):** the workshop said the rates sit only in the accounts office, that it cannot disclose them earlier because it must first weigh water and electricity costs and security and know whether a lot is profitable, and that no law obliges advance disclosure; it asked for seven days. [Para 43][Para 45][Para 51]

## 4. Award and reasoning
**Outcome:** granted — the panel ordered the employer to issue the piece rate so that workers know it in advance, within 3 to 7 days depending on the actual case, and never only after the workers have finished the lot. [Para 83]

The panel first examined whether the parties were properly constituted, found that the company and the local union were both unregistered, and held that the employer must register the company in accordance with the law of the Kingdom of Cambodia. [Para 54][Para 56][Para 58] It then turned to Article 112 of the Labour Law, which requires an employer to take measures so that workers are clearly and understandably informed of wage conditions before they take up their post and at every change of those conditions, and of the wages due for each pay period. [Para 63][Para 64] Reading that purpose, the panel held that whenever wage conditions change, in particular the wage level, the employer must tell the workers clearly what they will earn. [Para 65][Para 66] It found that a new lot with a small number of pieces can be finished in a short time, so a late rate announcement defeats the protection Article 112 intends. [Para 67][Para 68]

On timing, the panel noted that Article 112(a) fixes no number of days, that Awards 62/04, 14/03 and 71/08 accepted that the employer must notify, and that the usual period in earlier awards is 3 to 7 days, subject to the employer's practical needs. [Para 70][Para 71][Para 73][Para 75] Weighing the employer's costing needs against the workers' need to know, it ordered disclosure before the work is done, within 3 to 7 days according to the circumstances. [Para 81][Para 82]

## 5. Ratio decidendi
Where workers are paid by the piece, Article 112 of the Labour Law obliges the employer to disclose the piece rate for a lot before the workers perform the work, not after they have finished it, and the disclosure period is 3 to 7 days according to the actual circumstances of the enterprise rather than at the employer's convenience. [Para 65][Para 81][Para 83]

**Obiter dicta:** the panel added that both the workshop and the local union should complete their registration, although neither defect barred it from hearing the demand. [Para 56][Para 58]
