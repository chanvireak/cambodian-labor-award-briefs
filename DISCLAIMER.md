# Disclaimer

These are **machine-generated research notes** on public awards of the Arbitration Council of Cambodia. They
are not legal advice, not a substitute for reading the award, and not an authoritative statement of Cambodian
labour law.

Read this before you rely on anything in this repository.

## What you are reading

- Each brief summarises a public arbitration award. The award itself is the only authoritative text. Where a
  brief and an award disagree, **the award controls** and the brief is wrong.
- The briefs were written by AI models reading OCR'd Khmer and English documents. Two models draft, a third
  edits, a fourth grades twice, and the published text is the product of that chain — not of a lawyer reading
  the file.
- OCR damage is common in these documents. Each brief discloses in §6 the defects it worked around rather than
  silently repairing them, but a defect nobody noticed may still be present.
- Pin cites (`[Para N]`) point into a numbered working text of the award, not to a printed page. They were
  machine-verified to resolve against the source. Resolution is not proof that the cited paragraph supports
  the sentence.
- Outcomes and amounts are reported as the award prints them. Where an award contradicts itself, both readings
  are recorded in §6.
- These awards are first-instance decisions of an arbitration body. They are **not binding precedent**.

## Briefs that are known to be imperfect

Many briefs carry this in their frontmatter:

```yaml
review_required: true
review_action: "correct-citations"
review_reason: "…"
```

That is not a warning added defensively to every file. It means the grading pipeline found a specific defect
in that brief and no person has corrected it yet. Everything under `review_required: true` should be treated
as an unreviewed draft, and `review_action` tells you what is wrong with it.

Briefs marked `review_required: false` passed both grading passes with no open gap. That still does not make
them legal advice; it means no defect was found by the checks that exist.

## Privacy

Parties, witnesses, union officials and employer representatives are reduced to initials or roles; arbitrators
are named as the award names them. If you find an individual named who should not be, that is the most serious
class of error in this repository — open an issue marked `privacy` and it is handled before anything else.
