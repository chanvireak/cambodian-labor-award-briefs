# Disclaimer

These are **machine-generated research notes**, not legal advice, not a substitute for reading the award,
and not an authoritative statement of Cambodian labour law.

- Each brief summarises a public arbitration award of the Arbitration Council of Cambodia. The award
  itself is the only authoritative text; where a brief and an award disagree, the award controls.
- The briefs were written by an AI agent reading OCR'd Khmer and English documents. OCR damage is common
  and is disclosed in §6 of each brief rather than silently repaired, but a defect the agent did not notice
  may still be present.
- Pin cites (`[Para N]`) refer to the numbered working text of the award, not to a printed page. They were
  machine-verified to resolve against the source, but resolution is not proof that the cited paragraph
  supports the sentence.
- Outcomes and amounts are reported as the award prints them. Where the award contradicts itself, both
  readings are recorded in §6.
- These awards are first-instance decisions of an arbitration body. They are not binding precedent.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Obtain advice from a qualified Cambodian lawyer before acting.

Corrections are welcome and expected — see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
